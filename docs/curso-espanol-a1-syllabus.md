# Syllabus — Spanish A1 (English-speaking learners)

60 units × theory article ≥ 2.000 words × exercise article ≥ 2.000 words.  
**Can-do frame:** CEFR A1 + DELE A1 + PCIC *Inventarios* (aproximación).  
**Reference variety:** Peninsular standard; *vosotros* taught; *ustedes* shown as Latin American default.

**Category:** `curso-espanol-a1`  
**Folder:** `src/content/blog/curso-espanol-a1/`

Slug prefix: `unidad-{N}-{slug}` and `unidad-{N}-{slug}-ejercicios-soluciones`.

## Module 1 — First contacts (U1–10)

| U | Title | Grammar | Vocabulary / situation | Can-do | Slug | EN→ES pitfall |
|---|-------|---------|------------------------|--------|------|---------------|
| 1 | Greetings and introductions | *ser* (yo/tú): *soy, eres*; *Me llamo…*; *¿Cómo te llamas?* | *hola, buenos días, adiós, mucho gusto, nombre* | Introduce yourself in 20 seconds (name). | `unidad-1-greetings-introductions-ser` | *I am called* → *Soy llamado* |
| 2 | Alphabet, sounds, stress | Vowels; *ñ*; *j/g*; *rr/r*; written accent | Letters, spelling names, *tilde* | Spell your name; hear *pero* vs *perro*. | `unidad-2-alphabet-pronunciation-stress` | Reading Spanish as English vowels |
| 3 | Numbers 0–20, age, phone | *tener* + years; *¿Cuántos años tienes?* | Numbers, *año, teléfono, WhatsApp* | Say age and a phone number. | `unidad-3-numbers-age-tener` | *Soy 20* instead of *Tengo 20 años* |
| 4 | Origin and nationality | *ser de*; gender of nationalities | Countries, *español/a, estadounidense, británico/a* | Say where you are from. | `unidad-4-origin-nationality-ser-de` | Invariable English adjectives → forget gender |
| 5 | Gender: *el/la, un/una* | Noun endings *-o/-a*; common exceptions (*el problema, la mano*) | Classroom and everyday nouns | Choose the right article. | `unidad-5-gender-articles-el-la` | No gender in English → skip articles |
| 6 | Plurals *los/las, unos/unas* | *-s / -es*; *un lápiz → unos lápices* | Classroom, bag contents | Name several objects. | `unidad-6-plurals-los-las` | *the books* → *el libros* |
| 7 | Subject pronouns and *ser* (all) | *yo, tú, él, ella, usted, nosotros, vosotros, ellos, ustedes* | Identity sentences | Describe other people with *es/somos/son*. | `unidad-7-subject-pronouns-ser` | Dropping *usted* vs overusing *tú* |
| 8 | Jobs | *ser* + profession (usually **no** indefinite article) | *profesor, médico, estudiante, ingeniero* | Say what you do. | `unidad-8-jobs-ser-profession` | *Soy un profesor* (calque of *I am a teacher*) |
| 9 | *Tú* vs *usted*; time-of-day greetings | Formal *es/está* intro in greetings; *¿Cómo está usted?* | *señor, señora, disculpe, por favor* | Choose informal vs formal greeting. | `unidad-9-tu-usted-formal-greetings` | Using *tú* with officials |
| 10 | Module 1 review | Integrates U1–9 | Personal profile | Write a 6-line self-introduction. | `unidad-10-module-1-review` | Mixed *ser/tener* for age |

## A1 lexical budget
