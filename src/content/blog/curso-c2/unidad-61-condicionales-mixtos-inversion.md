## Unidad 61: Condicionales mixtos e inversión

Esta unidad cierra el curso en la estructura que el nivel C2 más a menudo confunde con un tercer condicional corriente: el mixto y su inversión. *Had I known about the delay, I would have changed plans* es un tercer condicional invertido. *Had the warning been clearer, the risk would be lower now* es mixto: condición pasada, resultado presente.

### Cómo se construye

*Were it not for the clause* sustituye a *if it were not for*. *But for the clause* hace un trabajo parecido con un sustantivo. *Were I to leave* es una hipótesis formal de futuro.

La fórmula de esta unidad es **Had + sujeto + participio… would be / Were + sujeto + to… would have**.

La inversión no lleva *if*. *Had if I known* es la mezcla que delata el ensayo.

### Ejemplos comentados

1. If I had known about the delay, I would have changed plans.
   Con **KNOWN**: Had I known about the delay, I would have changed plans.
   Inversión del tercer condicional: Had + sujeto + participio, sin if.

2. If the warning had been clearer, the risk would be lower now.
   Con **HAD**: Had the warning been clearer, the risk would be lower now.
   Mixto: condición pasada, resultado presente.

3. If it were not for the clause, the contract would have failed.
   Con **WERE**: Were it not for the clause, the contract would have failed.
   Were it not for sustituye if it were not for.

### Errores frecuentes

- If I would have known, I would have changed plans. If + had known, o Had I known, sin would en la condición.
- Had if the warning been clearer. Sin if: Had the warning been clearer.
