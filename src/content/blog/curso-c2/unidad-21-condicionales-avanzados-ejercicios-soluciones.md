Este cuaderno reúne la práctica de la **Unidad 21** (*Condicionales avanzados*, crimen y castigo).

> **Guía:** [Unidad 21: Condicionales avanzados](/blog/curso-c2/unidad-21-condicionales-avanzados)  
> **Curso:** [Inglés C2](/blog/curso-c2)

Haz cada bloque sin mirar la solución.

## Lección 1 — Transformaciones

Completa la segunda frase con la palabra clave. Usa entre tres y ocho palabras cuando la estructura lo permita. El sentido debe ser el mismo.

### Ejercicio 1

**Frase:** The court did not admit the letter, so the conviction is unsafe to challenge now.

**Palabra clave:** HAD

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**If the court had admitted the letter, the conviction would be easier to challenge now.**

Had admitted (pasado) + would be (presente).

</details>

### Ejercicio 2

**Frase:** She is isolated, and that is why she took the risk.

**Palabra clave:** WERE

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**If she were less isolated, she would not have taken the risk.**

Were (presente hipotético) + would not have (pasado).

</details>

### Ejercicio 3

**Frase:** They did not disclose the deal. A retrial is now unlikely.

**Palabra clave:** DISCLOSED

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**Had they disclosed the deal, a retrial would now be likely.**

Inversión: Had they disclosed… would now be.

</details>

### Ejercicio 4

**Frase:** The sentence is harsh only because the record was incomplete.

**Palabra clave:** BEEN

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**If the record had been complete, the sentence would be less harsh.**

Had been + would be.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Huecos

Una palabra, o la forma breve que se indica, por hueco.

### Ejercicio 5

If the jury _____ heard the recording, the doubt would still be in the room.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**had**

Had heard: condición pasada.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6

Were the precedent clearer, the court _____ have split.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**would**

Would have: el resultado ya ocurrió.

</details>

### Ejercicio 7

Had the warning been recorded, the defence _____ be stronger today.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**would**

Would be today: resultado presente.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Writing

Escribe 80–110 palabras sobre crimen y castigo y usa la estructura de la unidad al menos dos veces.

<details><summary>Modelo</summary>

If the court had admitted the letter, the conviction would be easier to challenge now. Had the warning been recorded, the defence would be stronger today. If she were less isolated, she would not have taken the risk. The mixed conditional is accurate only when each clause sits in its own time.

</details>

Cuando el modelo te salga sin mirar, sigue con la guía de la unidad siguiente o repasa [la explicación de la Unidad 21](/blog/curso-c2/unidad-21-condicionales-avanzados).
