Este cuaderno reúne la práctica de la **Unidad 33** (*Estructura del argumento*, libre albedrío y determinismo).

> **Guía:** [Unidad 33: Estructura del argumento](/blog/curso-c2/unidad-33-estructura-del-argumento)  
> **Curso:** [Inglés C2](/blog/curso-c2)

Haz cada bloque sin mirar la solución.

## Lección 1 — Transformaciones

Completa la segunda frase con la palabra clave. Usa entre tres y ocho palabras cuando la estructura lo permita. El sentido debe ser el mismo.

### Ejercicio 1

**Frase:** The choice had a cause. That does not make it meaningless.

**Palabra clave:** FOLLOW

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**Granted that the choice was caused, it does not follow that it was meaningless.**

It does not follow that frena un salto lógico.

</details>

### Ejercicio 2

**Frase:** Someone will say the feeling of freedom is an illusion.

**Palabra clave:** OBJECTION

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**The objection is that the feeling of freedom is an illusion.**

The objection is that nombra la réplica.

</details>

### Ejercicio 3

**Frase:** The feeling is not the whole story, and the agent still had reasons.

**Palabra clave:** EVEN

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**The feeling is not the whole story. Even so, the agent had reasons.**

Even so concede y sigue.

</details>

### Ejercicio 4

**Frase:** If every event is caused, blame becomes hard to place. That is the premise.

**Palabra clave:** PREMISE

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**The premise is that if every event is caused, blame is hard to place.**

The premise is that declara el punto de partida.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Huecos

Una palabra, o la forma breve que se indica, por hueco.

### Ejercicio 5

Granted _____ the act was caused, responsibility is still argued.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**that**

Granted that + oración.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6

It does not _____ that a cause erases a reason.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**follow**

It does not follow that.

</details>

### Ejercicio 7

The _____ is that 'free' has been left undefined.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**objection**

The objection is that.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Writing

Escribe 80–110 palabras sobre libre albedrío y determinismo y usa la estructura de la unidad al menos dos veces.

<details><summary>Modelo</summary>

The premise is that if every event is caused, blame is hard to place. Granted that the choice was caused, it does not follow that it was meaningless. The objection is that the feeling of freedom is an illusion. Even so, the agent had reasons. A visible argument lets the reader check the step instead of the tone.

</details>
