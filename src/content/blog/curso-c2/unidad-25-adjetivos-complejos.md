## Unidad 25: Sintagmas adjetivales

El sintagma adjetival comprime un juicio: *a widely held view*, *a deeply rooted preference*, *an apparently simple form*. El adverbio afina el participio y el conjunto modifica al sustantivo sin una oración de relativo.

### Cómo se construye

El adverbio tiene que poder modificar ese participio. *A very held view* no funciona. *Widely, deeply, seemingly, fiercely* sí, según el participio.

La fórmula de esta unidad es **adverbio de grado + participio + sustantivo (a widely held, a deeply rooted)**.

En estética, el sintagma evita decir *people think* y deja el juicio como propiedad del objeto.

### Ejemplos comentados

1. Many people hold the view that beauty is proportion.
   Con **HELD**: Beauty as proportion is a widely held view.
   Widely held view condensa many people hold.

2. The preference for symmetry is rooted deep in the tradition.
   Con **DEEPLY**: The preference for symmetry is a deeply rooted tradition.
   Deeply rooted + sustantivo.

3. The form looks simple, but the look may deceive.
   Con **APPARENTLY**: It is an apparently simple form.
   Apparently simple no afirma que lo sea.

### Errores frecuentes

- A held widely view. El adverbio va delante del participio: a widely held view.
- An apparent simple form. Si apparent modifica a simple, apparently simple. Apparent simple significaría otra cosa.
