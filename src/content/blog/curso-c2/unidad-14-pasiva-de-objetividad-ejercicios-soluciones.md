Este cuaderno reúne la práctica de la **Unidad 14** (*Pasiva de objetividad*, inteligencia artificial y ética).

> **Guía:** [Unidad 14: Pasiva de objetividad](/blog/curso-c2/unidad-14-pasiva-de-objetividad)  
> **Curso:** [Inglés C2](/blog/curso-c2)

Haz cada bloque sin mirar la solución.

## Lección 1 — Transformaciones

Completa la segunda frase con la palabra clave. Usa entre tres y ocho palabras cuando la estructura lo permita. El sentido debe ser el mismo.

### Ejercicio 1

**Frase:** Engineers trained the model on scraped text.

**Palabra clave:** TRAINED

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**The model was trained on scraped text.**

La pasiva deja el modelo como tema.

</details>

### Ejercicio 2

**Frase:** The board decided to ship the system.

**Palabra clave:** DECIDED

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**It was decided to ship the system.**

It was decided to oculta el agente. Úsala solo si el agente no es la cuestión.

</details>

### Ejercicio 3

**Frase:** Nobody reached agreement on the appeals process.

**Palabra clave:** REACHED

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**No agreement was reached on the appeals process.**

No agreement was reached tematiza el vacío, no a las personas.

</details>

### Ejercicio 4

**Frase:** Reviewers found that the dataset under-represented certain speakers.

**Palabra clave:** FOUND

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**The dataset was found to under-represent certain speakers.**

Was found to + infinitivo presenta el hallazgo como resultado.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Huecos

Una palabra, o la forma breve que se indica, por hueco.

### Ejercicio 5

The weights _____ released without a model card.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**were**

Were released: pasiva en pasado.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6

It _____ argued that scale does not settle responsibility.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**is**

It is argued that + oración.

</details>

### Ejercicio 7

Users _____ not told how the output was ranked.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**were**

Were not told: la falta de información es el tema.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Writing

Escribe 80–110 palabras sobre inteligencia artificial y ética y usa la estructura de la unidad al menos dos veces.

<details><summary>Modelo</summary>

The model was trained on scraped text and the weights were released without a model card. It was decided to ship the system before an appeals process existed. No agreement was reached on who answers when the output harms someone. The passive is useful for the process and evasive for the responsibility. A C2 text knows which one it is doing.

</details>
