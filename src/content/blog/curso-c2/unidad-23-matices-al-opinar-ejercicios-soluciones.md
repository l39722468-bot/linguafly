Este cuaderno reúne la práctica de la **Unidad 23** (*Matices al opinar*, sociedad civil y protesta).

> **Guía:** [Unidad 23: Matices al opinar](/blog/curso-c2/unidad-23-matices-al-opinar)  
> **Curso:** [Inglés C2](/blog/curso-c2)

Haz cada bloque sin mirar la solución.

## Lección 1 — Transformaciones

Completa la segunda frase con la palabra clave. Usa entre tres y ocho palabras cuando la estructura lo permita. El sentido debe ser el mismo.

### Ejercicio 1

**Frase:** The problem was the permit, not the size of the crowd.

**Palabra clave:** MUCH

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**It was not so much the size of the crowd as the permit.**

Not so much X as Y corrige el énfasis.

</details>

### Ejercicio 2

**Frase:** I am not saying the march was illegal. I am saying the route was not negotiated.

**Palabra clave:** THAT

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**It is not that the march was illegal; it is that the route was never negotiated.**

It is not that…; it is that… separa las dos lecturas.

</details>

### Ejercicio 3

**Frase:** You are right about the footage, and the caption still misleads.

**Palabra clave:** POINT

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**I take the point about the footage, but the caption still misleads.**

I take the point reconoce y luego limita.

</details>

### Ejercicio 4

**Frase:** The organisers did not reject debate. They rejected a closed room.

**Palabra clave:** REFUSE

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**The organisers did not so much refuse debate as refuse a closed room.**

Not so much refuse A as refuse B.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Huecos

Una palabra, o la forma breve que se indica, por hueco.

### Ejercicio 5

It is not _____ the slogan was violent; it is that it was vague.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**that**

It is not that… it is that…

</details>

### Ejercicio 6

Not so much the noise _____ the blockage drew the complaint.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**as**

Not so much X as Y.

</details>

### Ejercicio 7

I _____ your point about the permit, but the deadline was published.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**take**

I take your point.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Writing

Escribe 80–110 palabras sobre sociedad civil y protesta y usa la estructura de la unidad al menos dos veces.

<details><summary>Modelo</summary>

It is not that the march was illegal; it is that the route was never negotiated. The complaint was not so much about the noise as about the blockage. I take the point about the footage, but the caption still misleads. A nuanced sentence tells the reader which charge is being refused and which one is being kept.

</details>

Cuando el modelo te salga sin mirar, sigue con la guía de la unidad siguiente o repasa [la explicación de la Unidad 23](/blog/curso-c2/unidad-23-matices-al-opinar).
