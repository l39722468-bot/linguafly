## Unidad 23: Matices al opinar

Una opinión C2 distingue qué se afirma y qué no. *It is not that the march was illegal; it is that the route was never negotiated* separa la acusación fácil de la objeción real. *Not so much X as Y* corrige el énfasis.

### Cómo se construye

*I take the point* reconoce el argumento ajeno antes de limitarlo. Sin esa concesión, el contraste parece sordera.

La fórmula de esta unidad es **not so much X as Y / it is not that / I take the point, but**.

El matiz no es debilidad. Es decir exactamente qué parte del hecho se discute.

### Ejemplos comentados

1. The problem was the permit, not the size of the crowd.
   Con **MUCH**: It was not so much the size of the crowd as the permit.
   Not so much X as Y corrige el énfasis.

2. I am not saying the march was illegal. I am saying the route was not negotiated.
   Con **THAT**: It is not that the march was illegal; it is that the route was never negotiated.
   It is not that…; it is that… separa las dos lecturas.

3. You are right about the footage, and the caption still misleads.
   Con **POINT**: I take the point about the footage, but the caption still misleads.
   I take the point reconoce y luego limita.

### Errores frecuentes

- Not so much the crowd but the permit was the issue. La pareja es not so much X as Y: Not so much the crowd as the permit.
- It is not that they protested, but they blocked the exit. Falta la segunda estructura: It is not that they protested; it is that they blocked the exit.
