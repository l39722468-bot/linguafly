## Unidad 17: Construcciones impersonales

La construcción impersonal evalúa sin poner un *yo* en el centro. *It is worth noting*, *there is reason to believe*, *it would be unwise to conclude*. En un texto sobre sesgo, el impersonal evita fingir que el juicio es una preferencia personal, pero no lo convierte en un hecho.

### Cómo se construye

*There is little reason to* niega con educación. *It remains to be seen* aplaza. *One might object that* anticipa la crítica.

La fórmula de esta unidad es **It is worth / There is reason to / It would be unwise to + infinitivo**.

Si detrás del impersonal no hay argumento, es una muleta. La frase siguiente tiene que pagar la cautela.

### Ejemplos comentados

1. We should notice that the raters shared a background.
   Con **WORTH**: It is worth noting that the raters shared a background.
   It is worth noting that + oración.

2. The distribution gives us a ground for doubt.
   Con **REASON**: There is reason to doubt the distribution.
   There is reason to + infinitivo.

3. Concluding causation from this table would be unwise.
   Con **UNWISE**: It would be unwise to conclude causation from this table.
   It would be unwise to + infinitivo.

### Errores frecuentes

- It is worth note the bias. Worth + -ing: It is worth noting the bias.
- There is reason believing the sample is skewed. Reason to + infinitivo: reason to believe.
