## Unidad 7: Tiempos narrativos

En una narración literaria el past simple lleva la línea principal. El past perfect abre un antecedente: *By the time the letter arrived, she had already left*. *Would* + infinitivo no es futuro aquí: es el hábito del pasado, *she would read until the light failed*.

### Cómo se construye

El past continuous fija el marco (*it was raining*) mientras el simple introduce el suceso (*the door opened*). Mezclar los tres sin función produce un párrafo plano.

La fórmula de esta unidad es **past simple como eje, past perfect para lo anterior y would para el hábito**.

En crítica, nombra el tiempo y su trabajo: el perfect no es más elegante; es anterior.

### Ejemplos comentados

1. She left before the letter arrived.
   Con **ALREADY**: By the time the letter arrived, she had already left.
   Had already left es anterior a arrived.

2. It rained while she opened the last page.
   Con **READING**: She was reading the last page when the rain started.
   Was reading es el marco; started es el corte.

3. Every winter she read until midnight.
   Con **WOULD**: Every winter she would read until midnight.
   Would read describe un hábito, no una promesa.

### Errores frecuentes

- When the letter arrived, she already left. Lo anterior al eje va en past perfect: she had already left.
- She would read every night when she was a child, and yesterday she would finish the novel. Would de hábito no sirve para un hecho único de ayer: she finished.
