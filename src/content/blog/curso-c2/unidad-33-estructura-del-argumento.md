## Unidad 33: Estructura del argumento

Un argumento visible tiene premisa y paso. *Granted that the choice was caused, it does not follow that it was meaningless*. *It follows that* solo se usa si el paso es realmente lógico. Si no lo es, di *this suggests*.

### Cómo se construye

*The objection is that* nombra la réplica. *Even so* la supera sin insultarla.

La fórmula de esta unidad es **premisa, it follows that, granted that, the objection is**.

En este tema, el vocabulario ya está cargado. Define *free* o *caused* una vez y no los deslices.

### Ejemplos comentados

1. The choice had a cause. That does not make it meaningless.
   Con **FOLLOW**: Granted that the choice was caused, it does not follow that it was meaningless.
   It does not follow that frena un salto lógico.

2. Someone will say the feeling of freedom is an illusion.
   Con **OBJECTION**: The objection is that the feeling of freedom is an illusion.
   The objection is that nombra la réplica.

3. The feeling is not the whole story, and the agent still had reasons.
   Con **EVEN**: The feeling is not the whole story. Even so, the agent had reasons.
   Even so concede y sigue.

### Errores frecuentes

- It follows that she felt free, because the scan lit up. Un scan no implica la conclusión. This suggests, o It does not follow that.
- Granted the choice was caused but meaningless. Falta la estructura: Granted that the choice was caused, it does not follow that it was meaningless.
