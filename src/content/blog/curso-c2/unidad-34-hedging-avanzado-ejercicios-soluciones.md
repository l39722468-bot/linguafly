Este cuaderno reúne la práctica de la **Unidad 34** (*Hedging avanzado*, la identidad a lo largo del tiempo).

> **Guía:** [Unidad 34: Hedging avanzado](/blog/curso-c2/unidad-34-hedging-avanzado)  
> **Curso:** [Inglés C2](/blog/curso-c2)

Haz cada bloque sin mirar la solución.

## Lección 1 — Transformaciones

Completa la segunda frase con la palabra clave. Usa entre tres y ocho palabras cuando la estructura lo permita. El sentido debe ser el mismo.

### Ejercicio 1

**Frase:** A possible view is that the self is a story we maintain.

**Palabra clave:** ARGUED

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**It could be argued that the self is a story we maintain.**

It could be argued that presenta la tesis como discutible.

</details>

### Ejercicio 2

**Frase:** Some philosophers say memory is not enough for identity.

**Palabra clave:** CONTEND

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**One might contend that memory is not enough for identity.**

One might contend that atribuye la tesis sin firmarla del todo.

</details>

### Ejercicio 3

**Frase:** We are not at all sure the teenager and the adult are one agent.

**Palabra clave:** MEANS

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**It is by no means clear that the teenager and the adult are one agent.**

By no means clear niega la obviedad.

</details>

### Ejercicio 4

**Frase:** There are grounds for calling the diary a constructed self.

**Palabra clave:** CASE

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**There is a case for calling the diary a constructed self.**

A case for + -ing.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Huecos

Una palabra, o la forma breve que se indica, por hueco.

### Ejercicio 5

It could be _____ that continuity is practical, not metaphysical.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**argued**

It could be argued that.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6

One _____ contend that a promise binds the later self.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**might**

One might contend that.

</details>

### Ejercicio 7

It is by no _____ obvious that a name proves identity.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**means**

By no means obvious.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Writing

Escribe 80–110 palabras sobre la identidad a lo largo del tiempo y usa la estructura de la unidad al menos dos veces.

<details><summary>Modelo</summary>

It could be argued that the self is a story we maintain. One might contend that memory is not enough for identity, and it is by no means clear that the teenager and the adult are one agent. There is a case for calling the diary a constructed self. The hedge marks the claim as arguable. The next sentence should say what follows if we accept it.

</details>
