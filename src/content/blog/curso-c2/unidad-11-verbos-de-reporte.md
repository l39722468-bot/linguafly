## Unidad 11: Verbos de reporte y evaluación

No todo verbo de crítica va con *that*. *Claim, argue, suggest, deny* aceptan *that*. *Accuse* pide *of + -ing*. *Urge* y *encourage* piden objeto + *to*. *Blame* pide *for*. Elegir el verbo ya es evaluar.

### Cómo se construye

*Suggest* no es *state*. *Acknowledge* admite un punto incómodo. *Dismiss* lo aparta. En una reseña, el verbo dice la postura antes que el adjetivo.

La fórmula de esta unidad es **verbo de reporte + that / objeto / to-infinitivo, según el verbo**.

En estilo indirecto, los tiempos y los deícticos se ajustan si el verbo de reporte está en pasado.

### Ejemplos comentados

1. The critic said the ending was sentimental, and she considered that a fault.
   Con **DISMISSED**: The critic dismissed the ending as sentimental.
   Dismiss as evalúa y aparta en un solo verbo.

2. The author admitted that the middle was slow.
   Con **ACKNOWLEDGED**: The author acknowledged that the middle was slow.
   Acknowledge admite el defecto sin dramatizarlo.

3. Reviewers told the poet to trust the image.
   Con **URGED**: Reviewers urged the poet to trust the image.
   Urge + objeto + to-infinitivo.

### Errores frecuentes

- The review accused the novel that it was sentimental. Accuse of + -ing: accused the novel of being sentimental.
- She suggested him to cut the last chapter. Suggest no lleva objeto + to. She suggested that he cut the last chapter, o suggested cutting it.
