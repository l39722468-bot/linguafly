## Unidad 16: Sintagmas nominales complejos

Un sintagma nominal complejo acumula información antes del verbo: *the extent to which the sample represents the population*. El núcleo es *extent*. Lo demás lo modifica. La oración todavía necesita un verbo principal.

### Cómo se construye

Piezas útiles: *a growing body of evidence*, *the claim that*, *the extent to which*, *the conditions under which*. Sirven en investigación porque comprimen la pregunta.

La fórmula de esta unidad es **núcleo + complemento (of / that / to which) sin perder el verbo de la oración**.

Si el sintagma ocupa tres líneas, el lector olvida el verbo. Parte la frase o adelanta el verbo.

### Ejemplos comentados

1. We do not know how far the sample represents the city.
   Con **EXTENT**: The extent to which the sample represents the city is unknown.
   The extent to which condensa how far.

2. More and more studies point to a bias in self-report.
   Con **BODY**: A growing body of evidence points to a bias in self-report.
   A growing body of evidence + verbo en singular.

3. Researchers asked when the effect disappears.
   Con **CONDITIONS**: Researchers asked about the conditions under which the effect disappears.
   The conditions under which especifica la pregunta.

### Errores frecuentes

- The extent which the sample represents the city is unknown. Falta to: the extent to which.
- A growing body of evidence suggest a bias. Body es singular: suggests.
