Este cuaderno reúne la práctica de la **Unidad 61** (*Condicionales mixtos e inversión*, condición pasada y resultado presente).

> **Guía:** [Unidad 61: Condicionales mixtos e inversión](/blog/curso-c2/unidad-61-condicionales-mixtos-inversion)  
> **Curso:** [Inglés C2](/blog/curso-c2)

Haz cada bloque sin mirar la solución.

## Lección 1 — Transformaciones

Completa la segunda frase con la palabra clave. Usa entre tres y ocho palabras cuando la estructura lo permita. El sentido debe ser el mismo.

### Ejercicio 1

**Frase:** If I had known about the delay, I would have changed plans.

**Palabra clave:** KNOWN

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**Had I known about the delay, I would have changed plans.**

Inversión del tercer condicional: Had + sujeto + participio, sin if.

</details>

### Ejercicio 2

**Frase:** If the warning had been clearer, the risk would be lower now.

**Palabra clave:** HAD

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**Had the warning been clearer, the risk would be lower now.**

Mixto: condición pasada, resultado presente.

</details>

### Ejercicio 3

**Frase:** If it were not for the clause, the contract would have failed.

**Palabra clave:** WERE

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**Were it not for the clause, the contract would have failed.**

Were it not for sustituye if it were not for.

</details>

### Ejercicio 4

**Frase:** If I left tomorrow, the deadline would be missed.

**Palabra clave:** WERE

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**Were I to leave tomorrow, the deadline would be missed.**

Were I to + infinitivo: hipótesis formal.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Huecos

Una palabra, o la forma breve que se indica, por hueco.

### Ejercicio 5

_____ for the footnote, the claim would have stood.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**But**

But for + sustantivo equivale a if it had not been for.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6

Had she seen the log, she _____ not be defending the launch today.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**would**

Would not be: el resultado es presente.

</details>

### Ejercicio 7

Were the precedent _____ be followed, the fine would not have been issued.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**to**

Were… to be followed.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Writing

Escribe 80–110 palabras sobre condición pasada y resultado presente y usa la estructura de la unidad al menos dos veces.

<details><summary>Modelo</summary>

Had I known about the delay, I would have changed plans. Had the warning been clearer, the risk would be lower now. Were it not for the clause, the contract would have failed. Were I to leave tomorrow, the deadline would be missed. But for the footnote, the claim would have stood. Mixed conditionals and their inversions fail when if and had are stacked, or when both clauses are forced into the same time.

</details>

Cuando el modelo te salga sin mirar, sigue con la guía de la unidad siguiente o repasa [la explicación de la Unidad 61](/blog/curso-c2/unidad-61-condicionales-mixtos-inversion).
