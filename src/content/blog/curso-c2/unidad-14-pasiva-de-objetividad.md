## Unidad 14: Pasiva de objetividad

La pasiva de objetividad pone el resultado delante del agente. *The model was trained on scraped text* importa más que quién pulsó el botón, hasta que la ética exige el agente. Entonces la pasiva se vuelve una ocultación y hay que reponer el sujeto.

### Cómo se construye

Estructuras: *was trained, was found to, it was decided that, no agreement was reached*. Elige la pasiva si el proceso es el tema. Elige la activa si la responsabilidad es el tema.

La fórmula de esta unidad es **pasiva o it is + participio cuando el agente no es el tema**.

No apiles pasivas: *It was decided that it was considered that the risk was accepted* no es objetivo; es opaco.

### Ejemplos comentados

1. Engineers trained the model on scraped text.
   Con **TRAINED**: The model was trained on scraped text.
   La pasiva deja el modelo como tema.

2. The board decided to ship the system.
   Con **DECIDED**: It was decided to ship the system.
   It was decided to oculta el agente. Úsala solo si el agente no es la cuestión.

3. Nobody reached agreement on the appeals process.
   Con **REACHED**: No agreement was reached on the appeals process.
   No agreement was reached tematiza el vacío, no a las personas.

### Errores frecuentes

- The risk was decided to ignore. El verbo no rige así. It was decided to ignore the risk, o The risk was ignored.
- It was trained the model on public text. Orden: The model was trained on public text.
