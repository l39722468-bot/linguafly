## Unidad 45: Futuros complejos

*Will have done* sitúa un hecho antes de un momento futuro: *By 2035 the archive will have been duplicated*. *Is to* anuncia un plan oficial. *Be about to* es inminencia. *Be due to* es calendario.

### Cómo se construye

No uses *will have* para un plan vago. Necesita un punto de referencia futuro.

La fórmula de esta unidad es **will have done, is to, be about to, be due to**.

*Is to* en un ensayo suena a comunicado. Si no hay decisión institucional, usa *is expected to*.

### Ejemplos comentados

1. The archive will be fully copied before 2035.
   Con **HAVE**: By 2035 the archive will have been duplicated.
   Will have been + participio antes del punto futuro.

2. The agency has scheduled the standard for March.
   Con **DUE**: The standard is due to be published in March.
   Is due to be: calendario.

3. The trial is on the point of starting.
   Con **ABOUT**: The trial is about to start.
   Is about to: inminencia.

### Errores frecuentes

- By 2035 the archive will be duplicated already. Si el punto es la anterioridad: will have been duplicated.
- The launch is to starting tomorrow. Is to + infinitivo: is to start. Si es inminencia real, is about to start.
