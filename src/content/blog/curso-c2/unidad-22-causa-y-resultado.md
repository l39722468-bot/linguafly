## Unidad 22: Causa y resultado

*Thereby* une una acción con su efecto en la misma frase: *The amendment passed, thereby delaying the vote*. *Hence* concluye. *So + adjetivo + that* marca grado y consecuencia. *On the grounds that* da una razón formal.

### Cómo se construye

La causa no debe quedar implícita si el lector puede imaginar otra. En un texto sobre gobierno, la razón es el contenido político.

La fórmula de esta unidad es **thereby, hence, so… that, on the grounds that**.

*Thereby* modifica la acción ya dicha. No abre la frase como *Therefore*.

### Ejemplos comentados

1. The amendment passed and as a result the vote was delayed.
   Con **THEREBY**: The amendment passed, thereby delaying the vote.
   Thereby + -ing engancha el efecto a la acción.

2. The quorum failed. For that reason the session closed.
   Con **HENCE**: The quorum failed. Hence the session closed.
   Hence introduce la consecuencia en una frase nueva.

3. The objection was serious enough to force a recount.
   Con **THAT**: The objection was so serious that it forced a recount.
   So serious that + consecuencia.

### Errores frecuentes

- The bill passed, hence delaying the audit. Hence no va con -ing así. Thereby delaying, o Hence the audit was delayed.
- The debate was so heated so that the vote was moved. Un solo so: so heated that.
