## Unidad 40: Pasiva institucional

La pasiva institucional presenta la norma como resultado: *the tax was levied*, *the relief was withdrawn*, *it was held that the scheme fell outside the statute*. El agente (parlamento, tribunal, agencia) aparece solo si la autoría es el tema.

### Cómo se construye

*It was held that* introduce un criterio judicial. No lo uses para una opinión de artículo.

La fórmula de esta unidad es **was enacted / was levied / it was held that, con la institución como marco y no como héroe**.

Si criticas quién decidió, vuelve a la activa. La pasiva no puede ser el lugar donde escondes al responsable.

### Ejemplos comentados

1. Parliament levied a tax on empty homes.
   Con **LEVIED**: A tax was levied on empty homes.
   Was levied: la norma es el tema.

2. The agency withdrew the relief in March.
   Con **WITHDRAWN**: The relief was withdrawn in March.
   Was withdrawn deja el alivio como tema.

3. The court decided that the scheme sat outside the statute.
   Con **HELD**: It was held that the scheme fell outside the statute.
   It was held that es fórmula judicial.

### Errores frecuentes

- It was held the scheme to be unlawful. It was held that the scheme was unlawful.
- The tax levied on empty homes last year by. Fragmento. The tax was levied on empty homes.
