Este cuaderno reúne la práctica de la **Unidad 24** (*Repaso: sociedad y justicia*, el módulo de sociedad).

> **Guía:** [Unidad 24: Repaso: sociedad y justicia](/blog/curso-c2/unidad-24-repaso-sociedad)  
> **Curso:** [Inglés C2](/blog/curso-c2)

Haz cada bloque sin mirar la solución.

## Lección 1 — Transformaciones

Completa la segunda frase con la palabra clave. Usa entre tres y ocho palabras cuando la estructura lo permita. El sentido debe ser el mismo.

### Ejercicio 1

**Frase:** The law obliges the agency to give reasons.

**Palabra clave:** REQUIRED

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**The agency is required to give reasons.**

Is required to.

</details>

### Ejercicio 2

**Frase:** The average improved, but the lowest quintile did not.

**Palabra clave:** ALTHOUGH

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**Although the average improved, the lowest quintile did not.**

Although concede el promedio.

</details>

### Ejercicio 3

**Frase:** Notice was not given, so the fine is unlawful today.

**Palabra clave:** HAD

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**If notice had been given, the fine would be lawful today.**

Mixto: pasado → presente.

</details>

### Ejercicio 4

**Frase:** The order was published and therefore the deadline started.

**Palabra clave:** THEREBY

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**The order was published, thereby starting the deadline.**

Thereby + -ing.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Huecos

Una palabra, o la forma breve que se indica, por hueco.

### Ejercicio 5

It is not _____ the protest was large; it is that the route was closed.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**that**

It is not that…

</details>

### Ejercicio 6

Applicants are _____ to a written decision.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**entitled**

Entitled to.

</details>

### Ejercicio 7

Much _____ the figure is accurate, it excludes informal work.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**as**

Much as.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Writing

Escribe 80–110 palabras sobre el módulo de sociedad y usa la estructura de la unidad al menos dos veces.

<details><summary>Modelo</summary>

The agency is required to give reasons, and applicants are entitled to a written decision. Although the average improved, the lowest quintile did not. If notice had been given, the fine would be lawful today. The order was published, thereby starting the deadline. It is not that the protest was large; it is that the route was closed.

</details>
