## Unidad 21: Condicionales avanzados

El condicional mixto cruza tiempos. *If the court had admitted the letter, the conviction would be unsafe now*: la condición es pasada y el resultado es presente. *If she were less isolated, she would not have taken the risk*: estado presente, resultado pasado.

### Cómo se construye

No es un tercer condicional con un verbo mal puesto. El tiempo de cada cláusula tiene que coincidir con el momento real de esa parte.

La fórmula de esta unidad es **mixed conditional: condición pasada, resultado presente, o al revés**.

La inversión (*Had the court admitted the letter…*) es opcional y más formal. El sentido no cambia.

### Ejemplos comentados

1. The court did not admit the letter, so the conviction is unsafe to challenge now.
   Con **HAD**: If the court had admitted the letter, the conviction would be easier to challenge now.
   Had admitted (pasado) + would be (presente).

2. She is isolated, and that is why she took the risk.
   Con **WERE**: If she were less isolated, she would not have taken the risk.
   Were (presente hipotético) + would not have (pasado).

3. They did not disclose the deal. A retrial is now unlikely.
   Con **DISCLOSED**: Had they disclosed the deal, a retrial would now be likely.
   Inversión: Had they disclosed… would now be.

### Errores frecuentes

- If the court would have admitted the letter, the verdict was different. Would have no va en la cláusula con if. If the court had admitted the letter, the verdict would have been different.
- If she was less isolated, she would not have taken the risk. Para un estado presente hipotético, were: If she were less isolated.
