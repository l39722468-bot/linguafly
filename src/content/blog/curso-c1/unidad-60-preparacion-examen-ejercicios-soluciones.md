## Parte II — Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1
Completa la transformación (CAE key word transformation) con la palabra HAVE: It's a pity I didn't revise more. → I wish ______ more.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
I wish I had revised
</details>

### Ejercicio 2
Reescribe con inversión emphática: She had never encountered such a demanding test before.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
Never before had she encountered such a demanding test.
</details>

### Ejercicio 3
Elige el conector C1 adecuado: The policy is popular; ______, its long-term effects remain uncertain.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
albeit (also acceptable: granted / however / nevertheless, depending on the intended nuance). "Albeit" or "granted" work best for concession.
</details>

### Ejercicio 4
Completa con una colocación de alta frecuencia: The theory is ______ acknowledged, though recent evidence has challenged it.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
widely
</details>

### Ejercicio 5
Identifica el distractor trampa: El texto dice "The decline began in 2008, though it accelerated after 2012." ¿Cuál de estas opciones es correcta?
a) The decline started after 2012.
b) The decline began in 2008 and sped up after 2012.
c) The decline was caused by events in 2012.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
b is correct. Option a is a distractor that confuses "began" with "accelerated"; option c is a distractor that infers causation not stated in the text.
</details>

### Ejercicio 6
Completa con la condicional mixta adecuada: If I ______ (start) revising earlier, I ______ (be) better prepared now.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
had started ... would be
</details>

### Ejercicio 7
Reescribe con un phrasal verb formal (to bring about): The reform caused significant improvements.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
The reform brought about significant improvements.
</details>

### Ejercicio 8
Completa la transformación con SO + inversión: The impact was so significant that the committee reconsidered. → ______ that the committee reconsidered.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
So significant was the impact that the committee reconsidered.
</details>

### Ejercicio 9
Elige el léxico C1 adecuado para sustituir "very important": The findings are ______ important for future research.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
profoundly (also acceptable: considerably, markedly)
</details>

### Ejercicio 10
Completa con la preposición correcta (to stem from): Many of these problems ______ poor regulation.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
stem from
</details>

### Ejercicio 11
Reescribe con un subjuntivo mandativo: The examiner insists that every candidate ______ (arrive) ten minutes early.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
arrive
</details>

### Ejercicio 12
Elige la técnica de lectura adecuada: Para localizar el año en que se fundó una empresa en un texto largo, debes usar...
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
scanning. Skimming is for the general idea; scanning is for specific information like a date or name.
</details>

### Ejercicio 13
Completa con no sooner... than + inversión: He had no sooner finished speaking than the audience erupted.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
No sooner had he finished speaking than the audience erupted.
</details>

### Ejercicio 14
Reescribe con un conector de evaluación C1: Some people believe that the exam is unfair. → ______ that the exam is unfair.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
It is arguable (also acceptable: Some are of the opinion)
</details>

### Ejercicio 15
Completa con la colocación: The two studies are broadly ______, despite minor methodological differences.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
consistent
</details>

### Ejercicio 16
Explica en inglés C1 (60-80 palabras) cómo gestionarías el tiempo en la sección de escritura del IELTS.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
In the IELTS Academic Writing paper, I would allocate twenty minutes to Task 1 and forty to Task 2, reserving two to three terminal minutes for proofreading. Task 2 carries more weight, so the imbalance is justified. During Task 1, I would analyse the visual data, draft a concise overview and select key features. For Task 2, I would outline my thesis and arguments before writing, ensuring each paragraph develops a single coherent point.
</details>

### Ejercicio 17
Reescribe con una cláusula concesiva formal (albeit): The proposal was accepted, although with reservations.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
The proposal was accepted, albeit with reservations.
</details>

### Ejercicio 18
Completa con el phrasal verb adecuado (to rule out): The investigators could not ______ foul play.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
rule out
</details>

### Ejercicio 19
Redacta en inglés C1 (60-80 palabras) un breve plan de estrategia para los tres días previos al examen CAE.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
In the three days preceding the CAE, I would taper active study and prioritise consolidation and rest. Day one: a full timed mock under authentic conditions, followed by systematic error review. Day two: targeted revision of fossilised errors and high-yield collocations, with light speaking practice. Day three: minimal review, mental rehearsal of time-management strategy and controlled breathing exercises, ensuring cognitive freshness. Deliberate practice gives way to psychological readiness.
</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

En esta unidad has integrado toda la preparación lingüística del curso C1 en una estrategia orientada a los exámenes CAE, IELTS y TOEFL. Has aprendido a gestionar el tiempo por sección, a combinar skimming y scanning en la comprensión lectora, a identificar y evitar distractores y a exhibir las estructuras complejas (inversión, condicionales mixtas, subjuntivo mandativo) que los examinadores esperan. Has consolidado léxico de alta rentabilidad (to stem from, to bring about, broadly consistent, widely acknowledged) y conectores formales (albeit, notwithstanding, it is arguable that). Has comprendido la psicología del examen y la importancia de los simulacros cronometrados. Los 19 ejercicios te han entrenado en transformaciones, colocaciones y redacción estratégica. Con esta unidad cierras el bloque temático del curso; en la siguiente, la 61, el Language Lab te llevará a perfeccionar los phrasal verbs en contextos argumentativos, un registro esencial para la excelencia C1.
