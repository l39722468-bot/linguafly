## Parte II — Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Los tres condicionales canónicos

Completa con la forma verbal adecuada.

1. If we ______ (close) this round, we ______ (scale) into three markets. (tipo 1)
2. If we ______ (have) more runway, we ______ (expand) faster. (tipo 2)
3. If we ______ (raise) earlier, we ______ (launch) by now. (tipo 3)
4. If we ______ (raise) earlier, we ______ (be) market leader today. (mixed)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. If we **close** this round, we **will scale** into three markets.
2. If we **had** more runway, we **would expand** faster.
3. If we **had raised** earlier, we **would have launched** by now.
4. If we **had raised** earlier, we **would be** market leader today.

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Unless vs. if...not

Reescribe con *unless*.

1. We will not scale if we do not raise Series A.
2. If we do not pivot, burn rate will outlast runway.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. We will **not scale unless** we raise Series A.
2. **Unless** we pivot, burn rate will outlast runway.

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Provided that vs. as long as

Elige la forma más formal (*provided that*) o más coloquial (*as long as*).

1. ______ we hit Q4 targets, we will open the next round. (formal, estricta)
2. ______ the team stays intact, we can ship on time. (coloquial)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Provided that** we hit Q4 targets, we will open the next round.
2. **As long as** the team stays intact, we can ship on time.

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Inversión con should (formal)

Reescribe invirtiendo con *should*.

1. If you need further detail, do not hesitate to ask.
2. If we close this round, we will scale into three markets.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Should you need** further detail, do not hesitate to ask.
2. **Should we close** this round, we will scale into three markets.

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Inversión con were y had

Reescribe invirtiendo con *were* (tipo 2) o *had* (tipo 3).

1. If we were to raise five million more, we would accelerate the roadmap.
2. If we had launched six months earlier, we would have captured the segment.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Were we to raise** five million more, we would accelerate the roadmap.
2. **Had we launched** six months earlier, we would have captured the segment.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — In the event that / assuming that / in case

Elige la conjunción adecuada.

1. ______ funding falls short, we will pivot to a leaner model. (contingencia formal)
2. ______ the market grows at 8% CAGR, TAM reaches 12B by 2030. (presuposición)
3. We keep six months runway ______ the round delays. (precaución)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **In the event that** funding falls short, we will pivot to a leaner model.
2. **Assuming that** the market grows at 8% CAGR, TAM reaches 12B by 2030.
3. We keep six months runway **in case** the round delays.

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Modales de proyección

Elige el modal que mejor calibra el compromiso (will / expect to / could / projected to).

1. We ______ hit $4M ARR by year-end. (compromiso razonable)
2. We ______ expand into Latin America within twelve months. (posibilidad abierta)
3. The market ______ reach $12B by 2030. (atribuido a fuentes)
4. We ______ scale into three markets if we close this round. (compromiso fuerte, condicionado)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. We **expect to** hit $4M ARR by year-end.
2. We **could** expand into Latin America within twelve months.
3. The market **is projected to** reach $12B by 2030.
4. We **will** scale into three markets if we close this round.

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Corrige los errores

Cada frase tiene un error. Corrígelo.

1. We will scale if we don't raise.
2. If we will raise, we would scale.
3. If we had raised, we would have be leader.
4. In case we raise, we will scale. (contexto: condición futura)
5. We would could might scale.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. We will **not** scale **unless** we raise. (sentido contrario corregido)
2. If we **raise**, we **will** scale. (tipo 1, no *will* en el if-clause)
3. If we had raised, we would have **been** leader. (o *we would **be** leader today*, mixed)
4. **If** we raise, we will scale. (condición futura, no *in case*)
5. Un solo modal: *We **would** scale.* (o *We **could** scale.*)

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Transformación (CAE-style)

Reescribe empezando con las palabras dadas.

1. "If we don't pivot, we'll run out of runway." → *Unless...*
2. "If we had launched earlier, we would have won the segment." → *Had we...*
3. "If you need further detail, call me." → *Should you...*
4. "We will scale provided that we secure eight million." → *Provided that...*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Unless** we pivot, we'll run out of runway.
2. **Had we** launched earlier, we would have won the segment.
3. **Should you** need further detail, call me.
4. **Provided that** we secure eight million, we will scale.

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Vocabulario de startups

Relaciona cada término con su definición.

1. Runway   a. Ventaja competitiva defendible.
2. Moat     b. Meses de caja antes de needing más capital.
3. MVP      c. Producto mínimo viable.
4. Pivot    d. Cambio de estrategia o producto.
5. Burn rate  e. Ritmo de gasto mensual.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1-b, 2-a, 3-c, 4-d, 5-e.

</details>

### Ejercicio 11 — Colocaciones de pitch

Completa con el verbo adecuado (raise / extend / gain / capture / build).

1. ______ a round at a fair valuation.
2. ______ runway by cutting non-essential spend.
3. ______ traction through a pilot programme.
4. ______ market share in the enterprise segment.
5. ______ a moat around proprietary data.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Raise** a round at a fair valuation.
2. **Extend** runway by cutting non-essential spend.
3. **Gain** traction through a pilot programme.
4. **Capture** market share in the enterprise segment.
5. **Build** a moat around proprietary data.

</details>

### Ejercicio 12 — Lenguaje persuasivo cuantificado

Mejora estas afirmaciones vagas con cifras precisas (inventa datos realistas).

1. Most of our clients renew.
2. We grew a lot last year.
3. The market is huge.
4. We have enough money.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo:
1. 87% of our enterprise clients renewed in 2025.
2. We grew ARR 140% year-on-year, from $1.2M to $2.9M.
3. The TAM is projected at $12B by 2030, growing at an 8% CAGR.
4. We have 14 months of runway at a burn rate of $180K per month.

</details>

### Ejercicio 13 — Reading

Lee el extracto y responde.

> *Should we close this round at the proposed valuation, we would be positioned to capture a first-mover advantage in a market projected to reach twelve billion by 2030. Assuming a conservative 8% CAGR and our current traction, we expect to hit four million ARR within eighteen months. In the event that funding falls short, we will pivot to a leaner model and conserve runway.*

1. Which inverted conditional opens the paragraph, and what does it mean in plain if-form?
2. What two projections are made, and with what modals?
3. What is the downside plan, and with what conjunction?

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. *Should we close this round...* = *If we close this round...* (inversión con should, tipo 1 formal).
2. (a) Market projected to reach $12B by 2030 (*is projected to*); (b) we expect to hit $4M ARR within 18 months (*expect to*).
3. Pivot to a leaner model and conserve runway, introduced by *in the event that* (contingencia formal).

</details>

### Ejercicio 14 — Listening (paráfrasis)

Parafrasea esta declaración del fundador en registro de pitch C1.

> "If you guys give us the money, we'll get really big. The market is massive. If we don't get the cash, we'll just do less and survive."

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *Should we close this round, we would be positioned to scale aggressively in a market projected to reach twelve billion by 2030. In the event that funding falls short, we will pivot to a leaner model and conserve runway, so that downside risk remains bounded.*

</details>

### Ejercicio 15 — Writing

Escribe el cierre de un pitch (80–100 palabras). Usa al menos: una inversión con *should*, una proyección con *expect to*, una contingencia con *in the event that* y una condición suficiente con *provided that*.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *Should we close this round at the proposed valuation, we would be positioned to capture a first-mover advantage in a market projected to reach twelve billion by 2030. Assuming a conservative 8% CAGR and our current traction, we expect to hit four million ARR within eighteen months. In the event that funding falls short, we will pivot to a leaner model and conserve runway, so that downside risk remains bounded. Provided that we secure eight million, we will scale into three new markets and build the data moat that makes us defensible.* (90 palabras)

</details>

### Ejercicio 16 — Speaking (monólogo)

Prepara un monólogo de 2 minutos presentando un pitch ante inversores, usando condicionales e inversión formal.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo de inicio: *We're solving a twelve-billion-dollar problem with a product that already shows 140% year-on-year growth. Should we close this round at the proposed valuation, we would be positioned to capture a first-mover advantage and hit four million ARR within eighteen months. Assuming an 8% CAGR, our addressable market reaches twelve billion by 2030. In the event that funding falls short, we will pivot to a leaner model and conserve runway, so that downside risk remains bounded. Provided that we secure eight million, we will scale into three new markets and build the data moat that makes us defensible...*

</details>

### Ejercicio 17 — Registro: pitch oral, deck o term sheet

Clasifica cada extracto.

1. *Should you require further detail, do not hesitate to request the data room.*
2. *Look, if you back us, we'll crush it — the market is massive.*
3. *Subject to satisfactory due diligence, the investment shall close within thirty days.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Pitch escrito / deck** (inversión con should, registro denso).
2. **Pitch oral** (coloquial, condicional tipo 1, adjetivo vago).
3. **Term sheet** (legal-formal, *subject to*, *shall*).

</details>

### Ejercicio 18 — Consolidación

Completa el párrafo con una palabra por hueco.

> *(1)______ we close this round, we would be positioned to capture a first-mover advantage. (2)______ an 8% CAGR, we (3)______ to hit $4M ARR. (4)______ the event that funding falls short, we will pivot. (5)______ that we secure eight million, we will scale.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Should** (inversión tipo 1)
2. **Assuming** (presuposición)
3. **expect** (expect to)
4. **In** (in the event that)
5. **Provided** (provided that)

</details>

### Ejercicio 19 — Reescribe el párrafo con registro de pitch

Eleva el registro del párrafo coloquial con condicionales, inversión y cuantificación.

> *If you give us money we'll get huge. The market is really big. If we don't get cash we'll just survive somehow.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *Should we close this round at the proposed valuation, we would be positioned to capture a first-mover advantage in a market projected to reach twelve billion by 2030. Assuming a conservative 8% CAGR, we expect to hit four million ARR within eighteen months. In the event that funding falls short, we will pivot to a leaner model and conserve runway, so that downside risk remains bounded; provided that we secure eight million, we will scale into three new markets.*

</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

- Los **condicionales canónicos** (tipos 0–3 y mixed) proyectan escenarios de hecho, futuro posible, hipotético e irreal; el C1 los combina con **unless** (negación condicional formal, condición necesaria) y **provided that / as long as** (condición suficiente, estricta vs. flexible).
- Las **conjunciones de contingencia** (*in the event that, assuming that, in case*) matizan la probabilidad: *in the event that* (contingencia formal), *assuming that* (presuposición), *in case* (precaución, no condición futura).
- Las **inversiones con should / were / had** elevan el registro formal (*should we close, were we to raise, had we launched*) y son la firma del pitch e informe avanzados.
- Los **modales de proyección** calibran el compromiso: *will* (fuerte), *expect to / anticipate* (razonable), *would / could* (condicionado), *projected to / forecast to* (atribuido a fuentes).
- El **lenguaje persuasivo cuantificado** (cifras precisas, no adjetivos vagos) y el **vocabulario de startups** (*startup, runway, pivot, scale, traction, bootstrapped, disrupt, Series A, valuation, burn rate, MVP, go-to-market, moat, CAGR, TAM*) con sus colocaciones (*raise a round, extend runway, gain traction, capture market share, build a moat, ship an MVP, pivot the model, achieve product-market fit*) permiten construir pitches que argumentan bajo incertidumbre con la credibilidad y el matiz que el C1 exige.
