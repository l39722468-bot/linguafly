## Parte II — Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1
Distingue los tres términos en una frase: refugee, asylum seeker, migrant.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

A refugee has already been granted international protection, an asylum seeker is someone whose protection claim is still under review, and a migrant is a broader term for anyone who moves, without implying protection or a specific motive.
</details>

### Ejercicio 2
Reformula "illegal immigrants" con lenguaje respetuoso y preciso.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Undocumented migrants —or, in a legal context, irregular migrants— since 'illegal' criminalises the person rather than describing the status.
</details>

### Ejercicio 3
Eleva un detalle personal a lo emblemático con un conector adecuado.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Her journey —three borders, eleven days, one smuggler— is at once deeply personal and emblematic of a wider pattern of climate-driven displacement.
</details>

### Ejercicio 4
Sustituye la metáfora deshumanizadora: "waves of migrants."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Large numbers of people on the move —or, more precisely, families and individuals seeking safety— since 'waves' reduces human beings to a natural phenomenon.
</details>

### Ejercicio 5
Completa el principio jurídico: "The principle of ______ prohibits returning a person to a territory where their life or freedom would be at risk."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

The principle of non-refoulement prohibits returning a person to a territory where their life or freedom would be at risk.
</details>

### Ejercicio 6
Nominaliza para el análisis de políticas: "When states criminalise irregular entry, they push people into smugglers' hands."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

The criminalisation of irregular entry drives people into the hands of smugglers, as legal routes are foreclosed.
</details>

### Ejercicio 7
Añade un hedge sobre una cifra: "X people were displaced last year."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

An estimated X people were displaced last year, though the true figure is likely higher as many movements go unrecorded.
</details>

### Ejercicio 8
Distingue push factors y pull factors en una frase sobre una región.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

The push factors —conflict, drought and economic collapse— drove people out, while the pull factors —relative stability and family networks— drew them towards specific destinations.
</details>

### Ejercicio 9
Reformula eligiendo voz activa para devolver agencia: "she was forced to leave" → alternativa que reconozca la decisión.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

She had little choice but to leave, though the decision, once taken, was her own —a nuance that 'was forced to flee' obscures by erasing the agency that remains even under coercion.
</details>

### Ejercicio 10
Cita una fuente con atribución precisa sobre desplazamiento global.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

According to UNHCR, there were over 110 million forcibly displaced people worldwide, though the figure includes several overlapping categories and the true number of movements is difficult to ascertain.
</details>

### Ejercicio 11
Completa el léxico de políticas: "The government introduced a ______ programme allowing undocumented residents to acquire legal status."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

The government introduced a regularisation programme allowing undocumented residents to acquire legal status.
</details>

### Ejercicio 12
Nombra y critica una narrativa contrapuesta: "migrant crisis."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

The framing of migration as a 'crisis' says as much about the host society's institutions as about the movements themselves, since the numbers have often been predictable for years.
</details>

### Ejercicio 13
Construye una frase que combine detalle concreto y marco estructural.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

When the rains failed for a third year, Amina did not leave in search of a better life so much as in flight from an unliveable one —a fate now shared by millions across the region.
</details>

### Ejercicio 14
Completa el léxico del viaje: "They were ______ off the coast and transferred to a processing centre."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

They were intercepted off the coast and transferred to a processing centre.
</details>

### Ejercicio 15
Reformula con el tono de equilibrio crítico: ni fronteras abiertas ni selladas.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

To insist that borders must be entirely open is as detached from political reality as to insist that they must be sealed; serious debate begins between those two extremes.
</details>

### Ejercicio 16
Usa "internally displaced" con precisión en una frase.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

The majority of those affected were internally displaced —having fled their homes but not crossed an international border— which leaves them outside the refugee protection regime.
</details>

### Ejercicio 17
Distingue "deportation" de "removal" en un contexto jurídico.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

In some jurisdictions, 'deportation' refers to a formal order following a criminal conviction, whereas 'removal' denotes an administrative process; the distinction carries different legal consequences and avenues of appeal.
</details>

### Ejercicio 18
Reformula "the policy displaced communities" en pasiva y comenta el cambio de encuadre.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Communities were displaced by the policy —a passive construction that diffuses responsibility, which is precisely why the active voice is often preferred when attributing causation.
</details>

### Ejercicio 19
Redacta un párrafo breve que combine narrativa humana y análisis de política migratoria.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

When Amina reached the border, she was not turned back by geography but by policy —a distinction that matters. Her case is emblematic of a wider tension: while non-refoulement obliges states not to return people to danger, the externalisation of border control increasingly pushes that obligation beyond formal jurisdiction, leaving people like her in a legal and humanitarian limbo that neither narrative nor statute has yet resolved.
</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

En esta unidad has aprendido a moverte entre el registro narrativo y el normativo del discurso migratorio, combinando el detalle concreto humano con el marco estructural de las políticas. Has distinguido con precisión técnica "refugee", "asylum seeker" y "migrant", y has evitado el lenguaje deshumanizador sustituyendo "illegal immigrant" por "undocumented migrant" y rechazando metáforas como "waves" o "floods". Has trabajado el léxico de las políticas migratorias —regularisation, non-refoulement, Dublin Regulation, pushback, resettlement—, el de las causas del desplazamiento —push y pull factors, forced displacement, climate-induced migration— y el principio de la Convención de 1951. Has practicado la elevación del detalle personal a lo emblemático, el uso consciente de voz activa y pasiva para encuadrar la agencia, y el tono de equilibrio crítico que rechita melodrama e indiferencia. Has aprendido a nombrar y criticar las narrativas contrapuestas y a citar fuentes con atribución precisa. En la próxima unidad, la 45, cerrarás este subbloque con el desarrollo y la desigualdad, donde los indicadores y los matices serán los ejes del trabajo.
