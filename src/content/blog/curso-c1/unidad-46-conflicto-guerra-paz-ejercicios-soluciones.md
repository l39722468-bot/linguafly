## Parte II — Ejercicios

**Ejercicio 1.** Identifica y explica la función retórica del eufemismo en: *The operation resulted in collateral damage and the neutralisation of several high-value targets.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*Collateral damage* suaviza las bajas civiles presentándolas como un efecto secundario no intencional, y *neutralisation* reemplaza *killing* con un término aséptico que evoca desactivación más que muerte. Juntos, el eufemismo distancia al lector de la realidad humana de las víctimas y enmarca la operación en un registro clínico que facilita su aceptación pública. Su función retórica es gestionar la percepción, no describir con precisión.
</details>

**Ejercicio 2.** Reescribe la frase siguiente con un léxico más preciso del conflicto, distinguiendo tipos de víctimas: *Many people died in the war, including soldiers and normal people.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*The armed conflict resulted in a substantial number of casualties, comprising both combatant fatalities and a significant civilian toll, with independent estimates suggesting that non-combatants accounted for a disproportionate share of the dead.*
</details>

**Ejercicio 3.** Explica la diferencia entre *ceasefire*, *armistice* y *peace treaty* en una frase cada uno.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Un *ceasefire* es una suspensión temporal de hostilidades, a menudo frágil y reversible, sin abordar las causas del conflicto. Un *armistice* es un acuerdo formal entre partes beligerantes para detener la guerra, generalmente como paso previo a negociaciones de paz. Un *peace treaty* es un acuerdo definitivo que resuelve las causas subyacentes del conflicto y establece las condiciones duraderas de la paz.
</details>

**Ejercicio 4.** Reformula con una pregunta retórica que invite a la crítica sin formular una acusación directa: *The government calls them insurgents. Their supporters call them freedom fighters.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*Is it not telling that the same individuals are branded "insurgents" in official communiqués yet described as "freedom fighters" by their sympathisers, a lexical divergence that already encodes the political judgment one is invited to make about the legitimacy of their cause?*
</details>

**Ejercicio 5.** Reescribe la afirmación categórica usando hedging para ejercer crítica prudente: *This is propaganda.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*The consistent reliance on clinical terminology to describe operations with documented civilian casualties, combined with the near-total absence of independent verification in official messaging, invites the question of whether the language serves less to inform public understanding than to manage public perception.*
</details>

**Ejercicio 6.** Completa con el término preciso del derecho internacional humanitario: *The scale of civilian harm raises serious questions about compliance with the principle of ________, which prohibits attacks where incidental harm to non-combatants would be ________ in relation to the anticipated military advantage.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*proportionality* and *excessive* — el principio de proporcionalidad es una piedra angular del derecho internacional humanitario y prohíbe ataques donde el daño incidental a civiles sea excesivo respecto a la ventaja militar anticipada.
</details>

**Ejercicio 7.** Distingue los siguientes pares explicando la carga ideológica de cada elección: (a) *regime* / *government*; (b) *liberation* / *invasion*; (c) *targeted killing* / *assassination*.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

(a) *Regime* connota ilegitimidad y autoritarismo, mientras que *government* es neutral. (b) *Liberation* enmarca una intervención como liberadora y positiva, mientras que *invasion* la enmarca como agresión no consentida. (c) *Targeted killing* enmarca el acto como operación militar precisa y legitimada, mientras que *assassination* lo caracteriza como asesinato, sugiriendo ilegitimidad moral o legal.
</details>

**Ejercicio 8.** Reescribe usando vocabulario de escalada y desescalada: *The fighting got worse, then it got a bit better, but nobody thinks it will last.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*After weeks of escalating tit-for-tat attacks that deepened the spiral of violence, a fragile calm has settled over the region; yet few observers believe the lull will hold without sustained diplomatic engagement to address the underlying grievances driving the conflict.*
</details>

**Ejercicio 9.** Completa con vocabulario de justicia transicional: *The peace agreement, while a landmark, is only the beginning; its success will hinge on the credibility of the ________ process, particularly whether ________ combatants are genuinely ________ and whether victims feel that truth, reparations and accountability have been pursued in earnest.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*transitional justice*, *demobilised*, *reintegrated* — la justicia transicional abarca verdad, reparaciones y rendición de cuentas; la reintegración genuina de combatientes desmovilizados es un indicador clave de que el proceso va más allá del papel.
</details>

**Ejercicio 10.** Escribe una frase que critique prudentemente una narrativa oficial usando el léxico de *official narrative* o *spin*.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*The official narrative has consistently emphasised the defensive and precision-guided nature of the operation, a framing reinforced across government messaging and sympathetic outlets, though independent reporting has painted a notably more complex picture that raises legitimate questions about the gap between the stated aims and the documented outcomes.*
</details>

**Ejercicio 11.** Reformula con un léxico de mediación internacional: *A person went back and forth between the two sides to help them talk.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*The envoy's shuttle diplomacy between the principal parties yielded a preliminary framework, though whether the contact group can sustain momentum remains uncertain given the entrenched positions on both sides and the limited appetite for meaningful compromise.*
</details>

**Ejercicio 12.** Reescribe manejando la incertidumbre de las cifras: *10,000 people died.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*Fatality estimates range from 8,000 to over 12,000, with independent verification hampered by restricted access to the affected area, a constraint that itself complicates any definitive accounting of the toll and underscores the contested nature of the figures circulating in both official and independent reporting.*
</details>

**Ejercicio 13.** Completa con la colocación adecuada del campo bélico: *The operation was described as a ________ strike, though subsequent reporting revealed ________ civilian harm that called into question the ________ of the "surgical" framing.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*surgical*, *disproportionate*, *plausibility* — *surgical strike* es la colocación eufemística estándar; *disproportionate civilian harm* es el hallazgo que la desmonta; y *plausibility* cuestiona si el enmarcado quirúrgico sigue siendo creíble a la luz de la evidencia.
</details>

**Ejercicio 14.** Escribe una frase que critique tanto el belicismo triunfalista como el pacifismo ingenuo, manteniendo una posición matizada.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*Just as the "mission accomplished" rhetoric proved spectacularly misplaced in underestimating the complexity of the post-conflict landscape, so too does a reflexive pacifism that treats every use of force as inherently illegitimate risk underestimating the genuine moral dilemmas that policymakers face when confronting mass atrocities with no other available lever.*
</details>

**Ejercicio 15.** Reescribe en un tono sobrio y analítico, evitando el lenguaje emocional excesivo: *The attack was horrific and appalling — they butchered innocent people.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*The attack resulted in significant civilian casualties, with independent observers characterising the harm as disproportionate to any discernible military objective, raising serious questions about compliance with the principles of distinction and proportionality under international humanitarian law.*
</details>

**Ejercicio 16.** Completa con un conector de concesión y uno de conclusión: *________ the ceasefire has reduced the immediate bloodshed, ________ without genuine engagement with the underlying grievances, it risks becoming merely a pause in a cycle that shows few signs of being broken.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*Admittedly* (o *While*) y *yet* (o *nevertheless*) — *Admittedly, the ceasefire has reduced the immediate bloodshed; yet, without genuine engagement with the underlying grievances, it risks becoming merely a pause in a cycle that shows few signs of being broken.* La concesión reconoce el logro real; la conclusión matizada reintroduce la complejidad.
</details>

**Ejercicio 17.** Reformula con vocabulario de instituciones y actores internacionales: *The UN people tried to help, and some countries got together to talk.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*The UN Secretary-General's special envoy facilitated preliminary talks, while a contact group of key member states convened a high-level meeting to coordinate diplomatic pressure, though the Security Council remained divided on the question of a binding resolution.*
</details>

**Ejercicio 18.** Escribe una frase que distinga *refugee* de *internally displaced person* (IDP) usando el contexto de un conflicto.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*Whereas those who fled across the international border have been registered as refugees under the UNHCR's mandate, the substantially larger number who abandoned their homes but remained within national territory are classified as internally displaced persons, a status that affords them markedly less international protection and assistance.*
</details>

**Ejercicio 19.** Escribe un párrafo breve (4-5 frases) en inglés C1 que analice críticamente un comunicado oficial sobre una operación militar, usando al menos tres elementos del léxico de esta unidad (eufemismo, hedging, derecho internacional humanitario o crítica prudente).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*The official communiqué characterised the operation as a "surgical strike" resulting only in "collateral damage," a framing that, while doubtless intended to reassure domestic opinion, sits uneasily alongside independent reports of a markedly higher civilian toll. One might reasonably ask whether the clinical terminology serves less to describe the operational reality than to manage public perception of it. More substantively, the scale of documented civilian harm raises serious questions about compliance with the principle of proportionality under international humanitarian law, which prohibits attacks where incidental harm to non-combatants would be excessive in relation to the anticipated military advantage. To be sure, the fog of war complicates any definitive judgment, and the government's account cannot be dismissed outright; yet the gap between the rhetoric of precision and the available evidence is wide enough to warrant sustained scrutiny rather than credulous acceptance. Ultimately, the credibility of the official narrative will hinge less on the elegance of its phrasing than on its willingness to accommodate the uncomfortable findings of independent verification.*
</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

En esta unidad hemos construido el andamiaje lingüístico para hablar de conflicto, guerra y paz con la sofisticación crítica que exige el nivel C1. Has aprendido a desmontar el eufemismo bélico —de *collateral damage* a *enhanced interrogation*— reconociendo su función retórica de gestionar la percepción pública. Has incorporado un léxico preciso del conflicto que distingue *ceasefire* de *armistice* de *peace treaty*, *refugee* de *internally displaced person*, *combatant* de *non-combatant*. Has explorado el *framing* y la carga ideológica de elecciones léxicas como *insurgent* frente a *freedom fighter*. Has ejercitado la crítica prudente mediante *hedging*, preguntas retóricas y concesiones que equilibran el juicio sin caer en el dogmatismo. Has manejado el vocabulario del derecho internacional humanitario, de la diplomacia de paz, de la justicia transicional y de las dinámicas de escalada y desescalada. La destreza central de esta unidad es la crítica prudente: la capacidad de cuestionar narrativas oficiales con proporcionalidad a la evidencia, mantener un tono sobrio que refuerce la credibilidad y reconocer la complejidad sin renunciar al juicio. Esa elegancia crítica es la marca distintiva del hablante C1 cuando se enfrenta al terreno espinoso donde el lenguaje y la violencia se entrelazan inevitablemente.
