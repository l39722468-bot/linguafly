Este cuaderno reúne los **ejercicios de la Unidad 16 del curso C1** con soluciones. Haz cada bloque sin mirar la respuesta.

> **Guía teórica:** [Teoría unidad 16 C1: Pasiva Avanzada en Inglés C1](/blog/curso-c1/unidad-16-pasiva-musica)
> **Más unidades de este nivel:** [Curso C1](/blog/curso-c1)

### Cómo usar este cuaderno

Responde primero y comprueba después. La explicación está pensada para el estudiante hispanohablante.

## Parte II — Ejercicios con soluciones

### Ejercicio 1 — Pasiva básica

Transforma las frases.

1. The orchestra performed the symphony.
2. The technicians are preparing the stage.
3. The conservator has restored the violin.
4. The archive had stored the score for decades.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **The symphony was performed by the orchestra.**
2. **The stage is being prepared by the technicians.**
3. **The violin has been restored by the conservator.**
4. **The score had been stored by the archive for decades.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Modal passive

Completa.

1. The score must ___ (perform) from memory.
2. The instruments should ___ (store) in stable conditions.
3. The recording may ___ (edit) after the broadcast.
4. The violin should ___ ___ ___ (inspect) before the tour.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **be performed**; 2. **be stored**; 3. **be edited**; 4. **have been inspected**. El modal presente usa *be + V3*; el pasado usa *have been + V3*.

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Passive reporting

Transforma.

1. People believe that the premiere will attract international attention.
2. Critics say that the conductor changed the interpretation.
3. The press reports that the presenter was misquoted.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **It is believed that the premiere will attract international attention.** / **The premiere is believed to attract international attention.**
2. **It is said that the conductor changed the interpretation.** / **The conductor is said to have changed the interpretation.**
3. **It is reported that the presenter was misquoted.** / **The presenter is reported to have been misquoted.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Causative have

Completa con la forma correcta de **have**.

1. She ___ her violin ___ (restore) before the tour.
2. The theatre is ___ its acoustics ___ (test) this week.
3. They will ___ the costumes ___ (repair) tomorrow.
4. She had ___ the piano ___ (tune) before the concert began.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **had her violin restored**; 2. **having its acoustics tested**; 3. **have the costumes repaired**; 4. **had had the piano tuned**.

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Have, get o pasiva normal

Elige la opción adecuada.

1. The musician ___ her microphone repaired before the show. (*had / was*)
2. The microphone ___ repaired by a technician. (*had / was*)
3. The theatre ___ its roof damaged in the storm. (*had / was*)
4. The theatre ___ its roof repaired after the storm. (*had / was*)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **had** — la músico organizó el servicio.
2. **was** — pasiva normal: el micrófono recibió la acción.
3. **had** — experiencia adversa: el techo sufrió daños.
4. **had** — la institución organizó la reparación.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Get causative

Transforma usando **get + object + past participle**.

1. The singer arranged for her costume to be altered.
2. The company arranged for the contract to be translated.
3. We need the stage to be inspected.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **The singer got her costume altered.**
2. **The company got the contract translated.**
3. **We need to get the stage inspected.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Causative activa

Completa con **had, got** o **made**.

1. The director ___ the actors repeat the scene.
2. The director ___ the actors to repeat the scene after explaining the problem.
3. The director ___ the actors repeat the scene as a punishment.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **had**; 2. **got**; 3. **made**. *Have* dispone que alguien haga algo; *get* implica conseguirlo y lleva *to*; *make* expresa obligación o imposición.

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Música y vocabulario

Completa con **pacing, range, timbre, leitmotif** o **phrasing**.

1. The singer’s vocal ___ allowed her to move from pianissimo to fortissimo.
2. The recurring theme functioned as a ___ for the protagonist.
3. The performance’s ___ created tension without rushing the music.
4. The instrument’s distinctive ___ made it immediately recognisable.
5. The pianist’s ___ gave each musical sentence a clear direction.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **range**; 2. **leitmotif**; 3. **pacing**; 4. **timbre**; 5. **phrasing**.

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Reading

Lee el texto y responde.

> The new production is believed to have been developed over three years. It is reported that the score was substantially revised after the first workshop, and the orchestra is said to have been given additional rehearsal time. The director had the acoustic properties of the theatre tested before the opening, while the performers got their costumes altered to allow greater movement. What distinguishes the production is not technical novelty alone, but the way the leitmotif is gradually transformed as the protagonist’s understanding changes. The pacing is deliberately restrained in the opening scenes, although the final movement is performed with considerable expressive range.

1. How long is the production believed to have been developed?
2. What happened to the score?
3. What additional time was the orchestra given?
4. What did the director have tested?
5. What distinguishes the production?

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Three years.**
2. **It was substantially revised after the first workshop.**
3. **Additional rehearsal time.**
4. **The acoustic properties of the theatre.**
5. **The transformation of the leitmotif and the expressive relationship between music and character, not technical novelty alone.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Listening guiado

Guion: “The premiere is expected to attract a large audience, but the production is not being presented as a conventional revival. The director is said to have had the score reorchestrated, and several scenes are believed to have been shortened. What matters is that the original emotional arc has not been lost.”

Identifica una pasiva de presente, una pasiva continua, una estructura con infinitivo perfecto y una causativa.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Pasiva de presente: **is expected to attract**. Pasiva continua: **is not being presented**. Infinitivo perfecto: **is said to have had**. Causativa: **to have had the score reorchestrated**; el director encargó que se hiciera el trabajo.

</details>

### Ejercicio 11 — Pasiva con preposición

Completa la preposición.

1. The source was referred ___ in the programme notes.
2. The performer was spoken ___ after the rehearsal.
3. The proposal was objected ___ by several musicians.
4. The audience was provided ___ translated subtitles.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **to**; 2. **to**; 3. **to**; 4. **with**. Las preposiciones forman parte de la construcción verbal y no pueden eliminarse.

</details>

### Ejercicio 12 — Infinitivo perfecto

Completa con **to be**, **to have**, **to have been** o **to have been + -ing**.

1. The young conductor is said ___ extraordinary promise.
2. The score is believed ___ discovered in an archive.
3. The presenter is reported ___ misquoted.
4. The orchestra is thought ___ negotiating a new contract.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **to show** extraordinary promise; 2. **to have been** discovered; 3. **to have been** misquoted; 4. **to have been** negotiating.

</details>

### Ejercicio 13 — Observación o interpretación

Clasifica cada frase como **observación**, **atribución** o **interpretación**.

1. *The tempo was reduced in the final movement.*
2. *The conductor is believed to have changed the phrasing.*
3. *The slower tempo may have emphasised the movement’s reflective quality.*
4. *The score was revised after the workshop.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Observación**; 2. **Atribución**; 3. **Interpretación**; 4. **Observación atribuida al registro del proceso**, si el texto no especifica quién lo revisó.

</details>

### Ejercicio 14 — Corrección gramatical

Corrige las frases.

1. *It is believed the premiere to be revolutionary.*
2. *She had restored her violin by a luthier.*
3. *The director got the actors repeat the scene.*
4. *The score was referred.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **It is believed that the premiere is revolutionary** o **The premiere is believed to be revolutionary.**
2. **She had her violin restored by a luthier.**
3. **The director got the actors to repeat the scene.**
4. **The score was referred to.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 15 — Reformulación crítica

Reescribe de forma más prudente: *The performance was emotionally perfect.*

<details><summary>Modelo</summary>

**The performance appears to have achieved an unusually effective balance between technical precision and emotional expression.**

La nueva frase evita una evaluación absoluta y especifica qué aspectos sostienen la valoración.

</details>

### Ejercicio 16 — Writing

Escribe 150–180 palabras sobre: **How can a conductor modernise a classical work without losing its identity?** Incluye una pasiva impersonal, una estructura personal con infinitivo perfecto, una causativa y vocabulario musical.

<details><summary>Modelo</summary>

A classical work can be modernised without losing its identity if the conductor distinguishes essential structures from historically variable conventions. It is often argued that a new interpretation should respect the original score, but the work is also believed to have survived because performers have continually made different expressive choices. A conductor might have the orchestration revised while preserving the main melodic relationships and leitmotifs. The pacing could be adjusted, although the tempo should not be changed merely to create novelty. It is important that the acoustic balance be tested before the premiere and that the performers be given sufficient rehearsal time. A successful production is likely to refresh the work’s public image when its changes are motivated by interpretation rather than fashion. In my view, the most convincing modernisation allows the audience to hear familiar material differently while retaining the emotional arc, structural coherence and expressive range that make the composition recognisable.

</details>

### Ejercicio 17 — Speaking

Habla durante un minuto sobre una actuación que recuerdes. Usa **was performed**, **is believed to have**, **had the instrument tuned** y dos palabras de vocabulario musical.

<details><summary>Pista y modelo</summary>

**The concerto was performed in a small hall with remarkable acoustic resonance. The soloist is believed to have changed the phrasing during the final movement, and she had her instrument tuned immediately before the performance. The pacing was restrained, but the expressive range was extraordinary.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 18 — Mini-test final

Completa el texto.

> The new opera ___ ___ ___ (believe) to have been revised after the first workshop. The director ___ (have) the stage ___ (inspect) and the singers ___ (get) their costumes ___ (alter). Several scenes ___ (shorten), but the central leitmotif ___ (retain).

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**is believed; had; inspected; got; altered; were shortened; was retained.** El texto completo es: **The new opera is believed to have been revised after the first workshop. The director had the stage inspected and the singers got their costumes altered. Several scenes were shortened, but the central leitmotif was retained.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 19 — Elige el foco adecuado

Escribe una versión apropiada para cada intención.

1. Quieres destacar la información atribuida: “People believe the premiere will change contemporary opera.”
2. Quieres destacar el estreno y mantener distancia: “People believe the premiere changed the composer’s reputation.”
3. Quieres decir que la pianista encargó el servicio a otra persona: “A specialist tuned her piano.”
4. Quieres describir una acción que los actores realizaron por orden del director: “The actors repeated the scene.”

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **It is believed that the premiere will change contemporary opera.**
2. **The premiere is believed to have changed the composer’s reputation.**
3. **The pianist had her piano tuned by a specialist.**
4. **The director had the actors repeat the scene.**

La primera frase enfoca la proposición completa; la segunda enfoca el estreno y usa infinitivo perfecto para la anterioridad. La tercera es una causativa pasiva: la pianista organiza el servicio. La cuarta es una causativa activa con *have + person + bare infinitive*, porque los actores son quienes realizan la acción.

</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

Usa la pasiva para destacar el resultado o el elemento afectado: **be + participio**. Con modales, utiliza **must be performed** para obligación o necesidad presente y **must have been performed** para una conclusión sobre el pasado. En reporting, elige entre **It is believed that...** y **The work is believed to...** según quieras enfocar la información o el sujeto.

Las causativas **have/get + object + past participle** indican que alguien organiza un servicio: *She had her violin restored*. Con una persona, usa **have + person + bare infinitive** o **get + person + to-infinitive**: *The director had the actors repeat the scene* y *got the actors to repeat it*. Recuerda que el mismo verbo *have* también puede describir una experiencia adversa, como *have equipment stolen*.

En crítica musical, combina observaciones concretas con atribución y evaluación prudente. Las estructuras pasivas organizan el foco; el vocabulario de *pacing, phrasing, timbre, range* y *interpretive judgement* aporta precisión. Una reseña C1 no necesita afirmar que una actuación es “perfecta”: debe explicar qué decisiones fueron tomadas, qué efecto produjeron y qué evidencia permite sostener esa valoración.
