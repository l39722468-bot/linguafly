## Parte II — Ejercicios con soluciones

### Ejercicio 1 — Relative pronouns

Elige **whose, which, where** o **that**.

1. The catchment, ___ headwater forests have been cleared, now suffers flash floods.
2. The estuary ___ the birds breed is protected.
3. The migration, ___ spans two countries, is difficult to manage.
4. The habitat ___ the survey identified has already been restored.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **whose** — expresa la relación entre la cuenca y sus bosques.
2. **where** — introduce el lugar de reproducción.
3. **which** — non-defining clause después de coma.
4. **that** — defining clause; funciona como objeto de *identified*.

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Preposiciones

Completa con **in which, on which, to which** o **from which**.

1. The dunes ___ rare orchids depend are being built over.
2. The framework ___ the report refers is widely used.
3. The river ___ the samples were taken is heavily polluted.
4. The laboratory ___ the results were sent confirmed the analysis.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **on which** (*depend on*).
2. **to which** (*refer to*).
3. **from which** (*samples were taken from*).
4. **to which** (*results were sent to*).

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Reduced relatives

Reduce las relative clauses cuando sea posible.

1. The communities that live near the forest requested compensation.
2. The samples that were collected during the expedition were contaminated.
3. The researchers who are studying the glacier published their findings.
4. The wetland which was restored last year now attracts more birds.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **The communities living near the forest requested compensation.**
2. **The samples collected during the expedition were contaminated.**
3. **The researchers studying the glacier published their findings.**
4. **The wetland restored last year now attracts more birds.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Defining o non-defining

Añade comas solo cuando sean necesarias.

1. The Amazon which contains extraordinary biodiversity faces multiple pressures.
2. The river that supplies the village is drying up.
3. Dr Rivera whose fieldwork changed the policy now advises the ministry.
4. The species that the survey identified is endangered.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **The Amazon, which contains extraordinary biodiversity, faces multiple pressures.** Es un nombre ya identificado.
2. **The river that supplies the village is drying up.** La relativa identifica el río.
3. **Dr Rivera, whose fieldwork changed the policy, now advises the ministry.** El nombre ya identifica a la persona.
4. **The species that the survey identified is endangered.** La relativa especifica la especie.

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Hedging

Elige la opción más adecuada para un informe con datos todavía limitados: **may, must, appears to** o **definitely**.

1. The population ___ be recovering, although further monitoring is required.
2. The decline ___ reflect habitat loss, but other causes remain possible.
3. The policy ___ have reduced runoff according to the latest figures.
4. The new reserve will ___ solve the problem. Reescribe la frase para evitar una certeza injustificada.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **may** o **appears to**.
2. **may**.
3. **appears to**.
4. Una opción es: **The new reserve may reduce pressure, although its success will depend on enforcement.** *Definitely* es demasiado categórico sin evidencia suficiente.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Perfect infinitive

Completa con **to have + participio**.

1. The species appears ___ (adapt) to warmer conditions.
2. The policy seems ___ (reduce) illegal fishing.
3. The researchers are believed ___ (identify) a new contaminant.
4. The coral reefs are thought ___ (suffer) from a marine heatwave.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **to have adapted**.
2. **to have reduced**.
3. **to have identified**.
4. **to have suffered**.

El infinitivo perfecto muestra que la adaptación, reducción, identificación o daño precede a la valoración actual.

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Vocabulario natural

Completa con **catchment, estuary, wetland, apex predator** o **runoff**.

1. A river’s ___ collects water from surrounding land.
2. An ___ forms where fresh water meets the sea.
3. A ___ can store water and support migratory birds.
4. An ___ controls populations lower in the food chain.
5. Agricultural ___ may carry fertilisers into rivers.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **catchment**; 2. **estuary**; 3. **wetland**; 4. **apex predator**; 5. **runoff**.

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Colocaciones

Elige el verbo correcto: **monitor, restore, reduce, trigger** o **assess**.

1. ___ biodiversity
2. ___ a wetland
3. ___ runoff
4. ___ coral bleaching
5. ___ ecological impact

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **monitor biodiversity**.
2. **restore a wetland**.
3. **reduce runoff**.
4. **trigger coral bleaching**.
5. **assess ecological impact**.

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Reading

Lee el texto y responde.

> The Marlow estuary, which supports several migratory species, has experienced a gradual decline in water quality. Researchers whose samples were collected over five years have linked part of the problem to agricultural runoff, although the evidence does not establish a single cause. A new scheme whereby farmers receive advice and financial support has recently been introduced. The first measurements appear to show a modest improvement, but it is too early to conclude that the estuary has recovered. The wetlands surrounding the estuary, many of which were drained decades ago, are also being restored.

1. What does the estuary support?
2. What may have contributed to the decline?
3. How does the new scheme work?
4. What do the first measurements show?
5. Why is a strong conclusion not yet justified?

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Several migratory species.**
2. **Agricultural runoff may have contributed, although it is not the only confirmed cause.**
3. **Farmers receive advice and financial support.**
4. **They appear to show a modest improvement.**
5. **Because it is too early to conclude that the estuary has recovered.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Listening guiado

Guion: “The reef, whose tourism value is considerable, has suffered repeated bleaching. Scientists say that marine heatwaves are likely to be a major factor, although local pollution could also be contributing. The monitoring programme, which began last year, has identified some areas wherein coral cover appears to be more stable.”

Identifica tres relative clauses y dos expresiones de hedging.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Relative clauses: **whose tourism value is considerable**, **which began last year** y **wherein coral cover appears to be more stable**. Hedging: **are likely to be** y **could also be contributing**; **appears to be** también expresa cautela.

</details>

### Ejercicio 11 — Corrige la estructura

Corrige estas frases.

1. *The habitat where the species depends is shrinking.*
2. *The samples collecting yesterday were damaged.*
3. *The policy, that was introduced in June, may reduce runoff.*
4. *The scientists studying the data, the report was published.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **The habitat on which the species depends is shrinking.**
2. **The samples collected yesterday were damaged.**
3. **The policy, which was introduced in June, may reduce runoff.**
4. **The scientists studying the data published the report.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 12 — Registro académico

Reescribe con hedging: *The new dam has destroyed the migration route and caused the population decline.*

<details><summary>Modelo</summary>

**The new dam appears to have disrupted the migration route and may have contributed to the population decline.**

La frase distingue una interpretación basada en evidencia de una causa que todavía no puede afirmarse como única.

</details>

### Ejercicio 13 — whereby o wherein

Elige la opción correcta.

1. The programme introduced a system ___ farmers are paid to restore habitats. (*whereby / wherein*)
2. The report describes the conditions ___ the policy was implemented. (*whereby / wherein*)
3. The agreement created a process ___ local communities could report illegal fishing. (*whereby / wherein*)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **whereby** — describe el mecanismo de pago.
2. **wherein** — describe las condiciones o el marco en el que ocurrió algo.
3. **whereby** — describe el procedimiento mediante el cual las comunidades pueden informar.

</details>

### Ejercicio 14 — Writing

Escribe 140–170 palabras sobre esta cuestión: **Should governments expand protected areas?** Incluye una defining relative clause, una non-defining clause, *may* o *might*, *appears to* y dos colocaciones ambientales.

<details><summary>Modelo</summary>

Governments should expand protected areas that contain habitats under serious pressure, although expansion alone may not guarantee recovery. A reserve, which can protect species from immediate land-use change, needs adequate funding and effective enforcement. Recent evidence appears to show that protected wetlands store water and support biodiversity, but results vary according to local governance. Areas whose boundaries are enlarged without consulting nearby communities might generate conflict rather than conservation benefits. A policy whereby residents receive training and financial support could improve long-term cooperation. Governments should therefore assess ecological impact before selecting new sites and monitor biodiversity after designation. Protected areas are likely to be valuable when they are connected to wider migration routes and when local people can participate in their management. In short, expansion may be necessary, but it should form part of a broader strategy rather than be treated as a solution in itself.

</details>

### Ejercicio 15 — Speaking

Habla durante un minuto sobre un ecosistema amenazado. Usa **whose**, una relativa reducida y tres expresiones de cautela.

<details><summary>Pista y modelo</summary>

**I would like to discuss a wetland whose biodiversity is under pressure. Surrounded by expanding farmland, it appears to be losing water during the dry season. The decline may have been accelerated by runoff, although other explanations could be relevant. The communities living nearby might support restoration if they received practical assistance.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 16 — Traducción controlada

Traduce al inglés.

1. El estuario, cuya biodiversidad es excepcional, necesita protección urgente.
2. Las muestras recogidas durante la expedición pueden haber sido contaminadas.
3. El acuerdo creó un sistema mediante el cual los agricultores reciben apoyo.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **The estuary, whose biodiversity is exceptional, needs urgent protection.**
2. **The samples collected during the expedition may have been contaminated.**
3. **The agreement created a system whereby farmers receive support.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 17 — Edición avanzada

Mejora este párrafo para que sea gramatical y académicamente prudente:

> The forest that its trees were removed is recovering definitely. The researchers which visited it proved that the restoration works. The communities living there, the project has helped them.

<details><summary>Modelo</summary>

**The forest, whose trees were removed several decades ago, appears to be recovering. The researchers who visited it found evidence suggesting that the restoration work may be effective. The project has also supported the communities living in the area.**

Se corrige *that its* por **whose**, *which* por **who** para personas, y se reemplaza *proved* por una formulación que distingue evidencia de certeza absoluta.

</details>

### Ejercicio 18 — Mini-test final

Completa el texto.

> The coral reef, ___ supports several fisheries, has suffered bleaching. The species ___ habitat depends on healthy reefs may be declining. The monitoring data ___ have been collected since 2022 suggest a gradual change, but the cause ___ be a combination of warming and pollution. A programme ___ local fishers receive training has now begun.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**which; whose; that/which; may/might/could; whereby.** El texto combina una non-defining clause, posesión con *whose*, una relativa reducida o completa, hedging y un mecanismo formal.

</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

Recuerda estas decisiones:

1. Usa **whose** para expresar posesión o relación, también con lugares y cosas.
2. Usa **which** entre comas para añadir información; reserva **that** para defining clauses.
3. Conserva la preposición exigida por el verbo: *depend on* → *on which*.
4. Reduce a **-ing** cuando el sustantivo realiza la acción y a **V3** cuando la recibe.
5. Usa **whereby** para mecanismos y **wherein** para marcos o condiciones formales.
6. Usa **may, might, could, appears to, seems to** y **tends to** para ajustar la fuerza de tus conclusiones.
7. Separa hechos observados de interpretaciones y añade una limitación cuando la evidencia lo requiera.

Una buena prosa C1 no intenta sonar segura en todo momento. Suena precisa porque muestra qué relación existe entre las ideas, qué pruebas sostienen una afirmación y qué parte sigue abierta a revisión. Las relative clauses aportan estructura; el hedging aporta honestidad intelectual. Juntos permiten describir el mundo natural con claridad, densidad y rigor.
