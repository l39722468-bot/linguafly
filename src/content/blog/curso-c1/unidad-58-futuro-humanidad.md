El futuro de la humanidad es el territorio donde la especulación se encuentra con la responsabilidad moral. Razonar sobre él en inglés C1 exige más que imaginación: requiere hedging, es decir, matizar las afirmaciones según el grado de incertidumbre, y un léxico preciso para hablar de riesgos existenciales, trayectorias civilizatorias y largo plazo. Esta unidad te prepara para especular con prudencia, ni dogmático ni evasivo, sobre los escenarios que configuran el porvenir humano.

Cada concepto clave se presenta en tres niveles. El A2 fija la idea esencial, el B2 añade matices y conectores, y el C1 presenta el léxico futurológico y las estructuras de hedging avanzadas que necesitas dominar.

A2: We can think about the future. Some things might happen. We are not sure, but we can guess carefully.

B2: Speculating about the future requires caution. We must distinguish likely trends from mere possibilities, using hedging language such as may, could and is likely to. Responsible futurology avoids both alarmism and complacency.

C1: Speculation concerning humanity's long-run trajectory demands calibrated hedging, the judicious deployment of modal markers and probabilistic qualifiers that calibrate claims to the available evidence. Responsible futurology neither collapses into alarmist determinism nor retreats into complacent agnosticism; instead, it triangulates between divergent scenarios by foregrounding the epistemic uncertainty inherent in long-range projection.

## Parte I — Teoría: Futuro de la humanidad y especulación prudente

### 1. La futurología como disciplina cautelosa

La futurología o futures studies no pretende predecir el futuro sino mapear los escenarios plausibles. Los futurólogos distinguen entre predicciones, proyecciones y proyecciones condicionales. La especulación prudente reconoce que el futuro es radicalmente incierto y que nuestra tarea es reducir la incertidumbre, no eliminarla.

A2: People who study the future do not say exactly what will happen. They think about different possible futures.

B2: Futures studies, or futurology, does not aim to predict but to map plausible scenarios. Futurologists distinguish predictions from projections and conditional forecasts. Prudent speculation accepts that the future is radically uncertain.

C1: Futures studies eschews the chimera of deterministic prediction in favour of scenario mapping across a distribution of plausible trajectories. Practitioners rigorously demarcate unconditional predictions from conditional projections, acknowledging that the epistemic warrant for long-range claims attenuates sharply with temporal distance. Prudent speculation thus consists not in extinguishing uncertainty but in rendering it tractable for decision-making under radical Knightian uncertainty.

### 2. Hedging: el corazón lingüístico de la especulación C1

El hedging es el conjunto de recursos lingüísticos que matizan las afirmaciones para indicar incertidumbre, probabilidad o tentatividad. A nivel C1, domina expresiones como it is plausible that, the evidence tentatively suggests, may well, cannot be ruled out, there are grounds for supposing, in all likelihood, it is conceivable that, on present trends, barring unforeseen developments.

A2: When we are not sure, we say "maybe" or "I think." We do not say things are certain if we are not sure.

B2: Hedging means softening claims to reflect uncertainty. Useful C1 phrases include it is plausible that, the evidence tentatively suggests, may well, and cannot be ruled out. Hedging signals intellectual honesty when speculating.

C1: Hedging constitutes the linguistic infrastructure of calibrated speculation, deploying an array of modal auxiliaries, evidential adverbs and tentative predicates to modulate the force of propositions in proportion to their evidential support. C1 mastery encompasses formulations such as it is plausible that, the evidence tentatively suggests, may well, cannot be ruled out, there are grounds for supposing, in all likelihood, it is conceivable that, on present trends, and barring unforeseen developments.

### 3. Riesgos existenciales: el marco de Bostrom

Nick Bostrom clasifica los riesgos existenciales en categorías: naturales (asteroides, supervolcanes, pandemias), antropogénicos (guerra nuclear, cambio climático extremo) y emergentes (inteligencia artificial general, ingeniería biológica maliciosa). Su argumento central es que, dado el número de vidas futuras posibles, la reducción de riesgos existenciales tiene una utilidad esperada extraordinariamente alta.

A2: Some things could end humanity, like big rocks from space or very bad diseases. We should try to stop them.

B2: Nick Bostrom categorises existential risks as natural (asteroids, pandemics), anthropogenic (nuclear war, extreme climate change), and emerging (advanced AI, engineered pathogens). He argues that reducing these risks has enormous expected value because of the vast number of future lives at stake.

C1: Bostrom's taxonomy partitions existential risks into natural hazards (asteroidal impacts, supervolcanic eruptions, naturally arising pandemics), anthropogenic catastrophes (nuclear exchange, runaway climate change) and emerging technological threats (unaligned artificial general intelligence, malevolent bioengineering). His central contention is that, given the astronomical cardinality of possible future lives, the expected utility of existential-risk mitigation vastly dominates that of comparatively near-term interventions.

### 4. Longtermism y sus críticas

El longtermism sostiene que influir positivamente en el largo plazo es una prioridad moral clave. Sus defensores, asociados al movimiento de altruismo eficaz, argumentan que las generaciones futuras merecen igual consideración moral. Los críticos objetan que el largo plazo es demasiado incierto para guiar decisiones y que el longtermism puede descuidar los sufrimientos presentes.

A2: Some people think we should care a lot about people who will live far in the future. Other people think we should focus on people who need help now.

B2: Longtermism holds that positively shaping the long-run future is a key moral priority. Its proponents, linked to effective altruism, argue that future generations deserve equal moral consideration. Critics counter that the distant future is too uncertain to guide action and that longtermism can neglect present suffering.

C1: Longtermism, as articulated within the effective altruism movement, maintains that positively influencing the long-run trajectory of civilisation constitutes a paramount moral priority, grounded in the equal moral considerability of future persons. Its detractors retort that the epistemic opacity of the distant future precludes action-guiding predictions and that longtermist reasoning risks instrumentalising present suffering in service of speculative downstream benefits.

### 5. Trayectorias civilizatorias: colapso, estancamiento y florecimiento

Los futurólogos describen tres grandes trayectorias: el colapso (civilización que se autodestruye), el estancamiento (civilización que sobrevive sin avanzar) y el florecimiento (civilización que realiza su potencial). La probabilidad relativa de cada trayectoria es objeto de intenso debate, y el hedging es indispensable al discutirlas.

A2: The future could go three ways: things get very bad, things stay the same, or things get much better.

B2: Futurologists sketch three macro-trajectories: collapse, in which civilisation destroys itself; stagnation, in which it survives without progressing; and flourishing, in which it realises its potential. Estimating the relative likelihood of each trajectory remains deeply contested.

C1: Futurologists delineate three canonical macro-trajectories: collapse, wherein civilisation precipitates its own disintegration; stagnation, wherein it subsists without substantive advancement; and flourishing, wherein it approximates its developmental potential. The conditional probabilities assigned to each trajectory remain acutely contested, and calibrated hedging is indispensable when adjudicating among them.

### 6. Singularidad tecnológica y superinteligencia

La hipótesis de la singularidad tecnológica sostiene que el progreso tecnológico se acelerará de manera incontrolable tras la creación de inteligencia general artificial. I. J. Good y posteriormente Ray Kurzweil argumentan que una máquina que se mejora a sí misma podría superar rápidamente la inteligencia humana. La especulación prudente reconoce tanto la posibilidad como la profunda incertidumbre de este escenario.

A2: Some people think machines could get very smart, smarter than us. Then things might change very fast.

B2: The technological singularity hypothesis holds that progress will accelerate uncontrollably once artificial general intelligence is created. I. J. Good and Ray Kurzweil argue that a self-improving machine could swiftly surpass human intelligence. Prudent speculation acknowledges both the possibility and the deep uncertainty of this scenario.

C1: The technological singularity hypothesis posits an irrevocable acceleration of technological change precipitated by the advent of artificial general intelligence. Good and, latterly, Kurzweil contend that a recursively self-improving intelligence could, via an intelligence explosion, comprehensively eclipse human cognitive capacities. Calibrated speculation demands that one hold this scenario neither as imminent certainty nor as dismissable fantasy, but as a high-stakes low-confidence prospect warranting serious preparatory analysis.

### 7. Colonización espacial y multiplanetariedad

La colonización espacial se propone como mitigación de riesgos existenciales: una civilización multiplanetaria sobreviviría a catástrofes que aniquilaran un único planeta. Elon Musk y otros defensores argumentan que convertirse en especie multiplanetaria reduce la fragilidad existencial. Los escépticos cuestionan la viabilidad técnica, el coste de oportunidad y la prioridad moral frente a problemas terrestres urgentes.

A2: If humans live on more than one planet, we are safer. If something bad happens to Earth, we still survive.

B2: Space colonisation is proposed as a hedge against existential risk: a multiplanetary civilisation would survive a catastrophe that destroyed a single planet. Proponents like Elon Musk argue it reduces existential fragility. Skeptics question its feasibility, opportunity cost, and moral priority over urgent terrestrial problems.

C1: Space colonisation is advanced as an existential-risk mitigant, the rationale being that a multiplanetary civilisation would be robust to single-planet annihilation. Proponents such as Musk contend that achieving multiplanetary status constitutes civilisational redundancy against catastrophic fragility. Skeptics counter on grounds of prohibitive technical intractability, formidable opportunity cost, and contested moral prioritisation relative to exigent terrestrial welfare deficits.

### 8. Cambio climático: escenarios y hedging

El cambio climático ilustra la especulación prudente en acción. Los modelos climáticos proyectan rangos de calentamiento, no valores únicos, porque dependen de supuestos sobre emisiones y retroalimentaciones. El lenguaje científico emplea hedging sistemáticamente: likely, very likely, cannot be excluded, consistent with. Distinguir between projections and predictions es esencial.

A2: The climate is getting warmer, but we do not know exactly how much. Scientists give a range, not one number.

B2: Climate change illustrates prudent speculation. Models project warming ranges, not single values, because outcomes depend on emission scenarios and feedback loops. Scientific language uses systematic hedging: likely, very likely, cannot be excluded. Distinguishing projections from predictions is essential.

C1: Anthropogenic climate change exemplifies calibrated speculation in practice. Climate models yield probabilistic distributions of warming rather than point estimates, contingent on emission pathways and feedback parameterisations. The associated scientific register deploys systematic hedging, likely, very likely, cannot be excluded, consistent with, and scrupulously distinguishes conditional projections from unconditional predictions, a demarcation indispensable to responsible communication.

### 9. Demografía: el declive poblacional y el envejecimiento

Las proyecciones demográficas sugieren que la población humana podría estabilizarse o incluso declinar durante este siglo, con profundo envejecimiento en muchas economías desarrolladas. Las implicaciones para el crecimiento económico, los sistemas de pensiones y la innovación son objeto de especulación cautelosa. El hedging es crucial porque las tendencias pueden revertirse.

A2: The number of people in the world might stop growing soon. Many countries have more old people than before.

B2: Demographic projections suggest the global population may stabilise or decline this century, with profound ageing in many developed economies. The implications for growth, pensions and innovation are debated with caution. Hedging is crucial because trends may reverse.

C1: Demographic projections intimate that the global population may plateau or contract during the present century, accompanied by pronounced ageing transitions across numerous developed economies. The downstream implications for aggregate economic growth, pension-system solvency and innovation rates are subject to calibrated conjecture, hedging being indispensable in light of the demonstrable reversibility of erstwhile secular trends.

### 10. La paradoja de Jevons y la eficiencia

La paradoja de Jevons observa que aumentar la eficiencia en el uso de un recurso puede elevar su consumo total, porque el uso efectivo se abarata. Esta paradoja complica las proyecciones sobre el futuro energético y desafía la intuición de que la eficiencia reduce automáticamente el consumo. Es un ejemplo paradigmático de por qué la especulación sobre el futuro requiere humildad.

A2: Sometimes when we use something better, we end up using more of it, not less. This is surprising.

B2: The Jevons paradox observes that improving the efficiency of a resource can increase its total consumption, because effective use becomes cheaper. This complicates projections about the energy future and challenges the intuition that efficiency automatically reduces consumption.

C1: The Jevons paradox, or rebound effect, observes that augmenting the efficiency of resource utilisation may paradoxically escalate aggregate consumption, as the effective price of use-dependent services declines. This complication bedevils energy-future projections and frustrates the intuitive supposition that efficiency gains mechanically curtail consumption, illustrating why futurological reasoning demands epistemic humility.

### 11. Bioética del futuro: edición genética y extensión de la vida

La edición genética con CRISPR y las terapias de extensión de vida plantean dilemas futuros. ¿Deberíamos editar el genoma germline, transmitiendo cambios a las generaciones futuras? ¿La extensión radical de la vida agravaría las desigualdades? La especulación prudente requiere distinguir entre lo técnicamente posible, lo probablemente próximo y lo meramente concebible.

A2: In the future, we might change genes to make people healthier. We might help people live much longer. But we must think carefully about what is fair.

B2: Gene editing with CRISPR and life-extension therapies pose future dilemmas. Should we edit the germline, passing changes to future generations? Could radical life extension worsen inequality? Prudent speculation distinguishes the technically possible from the soon probable and the merely conceivable.

C1: Germline CRISPR editing and life-extension interventions furnish a proliferating class of future-facing bioethical dilemmas. Whether to authorise heritable genomic modification, with its cascading intergenerational ramifications, and whether radical longevity amplification would exacerbate distributive inequity, are questions that demand a tripartite distinction between the technically feasible, the proximately probable and the merely conceivable.

### 12. Gobernanza global y cooperación a largo plazo

Los retos de largo plazo, desde el cambio climático hasta los riesgos de la IA, sobrepasan la capacidad de los estados individuales. La especulación prudente sobre el futuro de la gobernanza global contempla escenarios de fragmentación, de cooperación reforzada y de nuevas instituciones transnacionales. El hedging es esencial porque las trayectorias institucionales son notoriamente difíciles de predecir.

A2: Big problems need all countries to work together. But we do not know if they will.

B2: Long-term challenges like climate change and AI risk exceed any single state's capacity. Prudent speculation about global governance considers scenarios of fragmentation, strengthened cooperation, and novel transnational institutions. Hedging is essential because institutional trajectories are hard to predict.

C1: Long-horizon challenges, spanning climate destabilisation to AI-related existential risk, decisively outrun the jurisdictional competence of individual states. Calibrated speculation concerning the evolution of global governance encompasses scenarios of institutional fragmentation, reinforced multilateral cooperation and the emergence of qualitatively novel transnational architectures. Given the notorious unpredictability of institutional trajectories, robust hedging is non-negotiable.

### 13. Modales de probabilidad en inglés C1

A nivel C1, debes dominar los modales de probabilidad: it is highly probable that, there is a non-negligible chance, the probability is not negligible, it would be rash to conclude, the evidence is suggestive but not conclusive, we cannot discount the possibility. Estas formulaciones cuantifican verbalmente la incertidumbre, un rasgo distintivo del registro académico-futuroológico.

A2: We can say "it is very likely" or "maybe it will happen" when we talk about what could be.

B2: Useful probability phrases include it is highly probable that, there is a non-negligible chance, and the evidence is suggestive but not conclusive. These formulations verbalise uncertainty, a hallmark of academic futurology.

C1: C1 proficiency in futurological discourse entails mastery of calibrated probability expressions: it is highly probable that, there is a non-negligible chance, the probability is not negligible, it would be rash to conclude, the evidence is suggestive but not conclusive, and we cannot discount the possibility. These formulations verbalise uncertainty with granular precision, a distinguishing register of academic futurology.

### 14. Conectores para el razonamiento prospectivo

El razonamiento sobre el futuro emplea conectores específicos: assuming that, in the event that, should current trends persist, were this scenario to materialise, even granting that, for the sake of argument, conditional on, subject to the caveat that. Estos conectores anclan las especulaciones a supuestos explícitos, una práctica esencial del pensamiento cauteloso.

A2: When we guess about the future, we can say "if this happens" or "let's imagine that."

B2: Speculative reasoning uses connectors like assuming that, in the event that, should current trends persist, and were this scenario to materialise. These anchor speculation to explicit assumptions, a hallmark of careful thinking.

C1: Prospective reasoning deploys a distinctive connector set, assuming that, in the event that, should current trends persist, were this scenario to materialise, even granting that, for the sake of argument, conditional on, and subject to the caveat that, each of which tethers speculative claims to explicitly articulated suppositions, an indispensable discipline of epistemically responsible futurology.

El léxico futuroológico del inglés C1 incluye colocaciones estables: to map plausible scenarios, to hedge one's bets, to avert an existential catastrophe, to reap the dividends of, to plateau and decline, to overshoot a threshold, to precipitate a collapse, to instrumentalise future persons, to discount the distant future, to foreground epistemic uncertainty, to calibrate claims to the evidence.

A2: Useful phrases: to think about the future, to be careful, to try to avoid bad things.

B2: Useful collocations include to map plausible scenarios, to hedge one's bets, to avert an existential catastrophe, and to plateau and decline. These fixed expressions make your futurological vocabulary precise.
