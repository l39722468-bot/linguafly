Este cuaderno reúne los **ejercicios de la Unidad 34 del curso C1** con soluciones. Haz cada bloque sin mirar la respuesta.

Responde primero y comprueba después. La explicación está pensada para el estudiante hispanohablante.

## Parte II — Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Retroceso de tiempos

Reporta estas frases en pasado (verbo de reporte en pasado).

1. "The budget is tight." → She said the budget ______.
2. "We will circulate the draft." → They said they ______.
3. "I can present next week." → He said he ______.
4. "We must act now." → They said they ______.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. She said the budget **was** tight.
2. They said they **would** circulate the draft.
3. He said he **could** present the following week.
4. They said they **had to** act then.

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Cambios de deícticos

Reporta cambiando los deícticos temporales y espaciales.

1. "We will decide tomorrow." → They said they would decide ______.
2. "I reviewed the proposal yesterday." → He said he had reviewed the proposal ______.
3. "Bring the report here." → She told them to bring the report ______.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. They said they would decide **the following day**.
2. He said he had reviewed the proposal **the previous day**.
3. She told them to bring the report **there**.

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Verbos de reporte

Elige el verbo más adecuado (noted / proposed / queried / undertook / flagged).

1. Mr. Chen ______ that procurement savings had been identified.
2. Ms. Patel ______ to present a revised budget by Friday.
3. A team member ______ whether the timeline was realistic.
4. The chair ______ a risk in the supply chain.
5. It was ______ that costs should be reduced by five percent.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Mr. Chen **noted** that procurement savings had been identified.
2. Ms. Patel **undertook** to present a revised budget by Friday.
3. A team member **queried** whether the timeline was realistic.
4. The chair **flagged** a risk in the supply chain.
5. It was **proposed / recommended** that costs should be reduced by five percent. (o *It was noted that...*)

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Preguntas reportadas (orden afirmativo)

Reporta estas preguntas con orden afirmativo.

1. "When will the report be ready?" → She asked when ______.
2. "Has the budget been approved?" → He asked whether ______.
3. "Can we defer this item?" → She asked if ______.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. She asked when the report **would be** ready.
2. He asked whether the budget **had been** approved.
3. She asked if they **could** defer that item.

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Órdenes reportadas (to + infinitivo)

Reporta con *tell* o *instruct* + objeto + to-infinitivo.

1. "Circulate the draft." → He told them ______.
2. "Prepare a revised budget." → She instructed the team ______.
3. "Confirm attendance by Friday." → The chair asked all heads ______.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. He told them **to circulate** the draft.
2. She instructed the team **to prepare** a revised budget.
3. The chair asked all heads **to confirm** attendance by Friday.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Pasivas impersonales y subjuntivo mandativo

Reescribe con pasiva impersonal + subjuntivo.

1. The committee recommends circulating the proposal. → It was recommended that the proposal ______.
2. They agreed to reduce costs by 5%. → It was agreed that costs ______.
3. Someone noted that the timeline was tight. → It was noted that the timeline ______.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. It was recommended that the proposal **be circulated**.
2. It was agreed that costs **be reduced** by 5%. (o *should be reduced*)
3. It was noted that the timeline **was** tight. (reporte de hecho, no mandato)

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Action items (responsable + plazo)

Redacta el action item para cada acuerdo.

1. Ms. Patel — circulate revised KPIs — by Friday.
2. Finance team — present cost-saving options — by 15 November.
3. All heads — confirm attendance at offsite — by end of week.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Action: Ms. Patel to circulate the revised KPIs by Friday.**
2. **Action: The finance team to present cost-saving options by 15 November.**
3. **Action: All heads of department to confirm attendance at the strategy offsite by end of week.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Conectores de secuencia y causalidad

Inserta el conector adecuado (in light of / consequently / thereafter / however / furthermore).

1. ______ the revised forecast, costs should be reduced.
2. Mr. Chen undertook to present a revised budget; ______, the committee endorsed the timeline.
3. Concerns were raised about staffing; ______, no redundancies were contemplated.
4. The minutes were ratified; ______, the chair moved to item 3.2.
5. Procurement savings were identified; ______, the proposal was circulated.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **In light of** the revised forecast, costs should be reduced.
2. Mr. Chen undertook to present a revised budget; **furthermore**, the committee endorsed the timeline.
3. Concerns were raised about staffing; **however**, no redundancies were contemplated.
4. The minutes were ratified; **thereafter**, the chair moved to item 3.2.
5. Procurement savings were identified; **consequently**, the proposal was circulated.

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Corrige los errores

Cada frase tiene un error. Corrígelo.

1. She said them to circulate the draft.
2. She asked when would the report be ready.
3. It was recommended that the proposal is circulated.
4. The minutes are ratified at the start.
5. John was like, we gotta cut costs.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. She **told** them to circulate the draft. (tell + objeto + to-infinitivo)
2. She asked when the report **would be** ready. (orden afirmativo)
3. It was recommended that the proposal **be** circulated. (subjuntivo mandativo)
4. The minutes **were** ratified at the start. (pasado)
5. It was noted that costs **should be reduced**. (registro de minuta)

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Transformación (CAE-style)

Reescribe empezando con las palabras dadas.

1. "We will circulate the draft tomorrow," she said. → *She said they...*
2. "Let's defer the item," he proposed. → *He proposed deferring...*
3. "Can we reduce costs by 5%?" she asked. → *She asked whether...*
4. The committee recommends that the proposal be circulated. → *It was recommended...*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. She said they **would circulate the draft the following day**.
2. He proposed **deferring the item**. (o *that they defer the item*)
3. She asked whether they **could reduce** costs by 5%.
4. It was recommended **that the proposal be circulated**.

</details>

### Ejercicio 11 — Vocabulario de gestión

Relaciona cada término con su definición.

1. Ratify   a. Cuello de botella.
2. Bottleneck  b. Aprobar formalmente.
3. Escalate  c. Subir un problema a nivel superior.
4. Buy-in    d. Adhesión, compromiso de los implicados.
5. AOB       e. Ruegos y preguntas.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1-b, 2-a, 3-c, 4-d, 5-e.

</details>

### Ejercicio 12 — Colocaciones de gestión

Completa con el verbo adecuado (chair / table / defer / secure / escalate).

1. ______ a meeting and call it to order.
2. ______ an item for discussion.
3. ______ a point to the next meeting.
4. ______ buy-in from all stakeholders.
5. ______ a concern to the steering committee.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Chair** a meeting and call it to order.
2. **Table** an item for discussion.
3. **Defer** a point to the next meeting.
4. **Secure** buy-in from all stakeholders.
5. **Escalate** a concern to the steering committee.

</details>

### Ejercicio 13 — Reading

Lee el extracto de minuta y responde.

> *3.2 Cost Reduction. It was noted that, in light of the revised forecast, operating costs should be reduced by a further five percent. Mr. Chen highlighted that procurement savings had already been identified, and undertook to liaise with the finance team and to present a revised budget by the end of the following week. Concerns were raised about staffing; however, the chair reiterated that no redundancies were contemplated.*

1. What two things did Mr. Chen do (verbos de reporte)?
2. What concern was raised, and how was it addressed?
3. Rewrite "It was noted that... costs should be reduced" as a direct quote from the chair.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. He **highlighted** that procurement savings had been identified, and **undertook** to liaise with finance and present a revised budget by the end of the following week.
2. Concerns were raised about staffing (possible redundancies); the chair **reiterated** that no redundancies were contemplated.
3. The chair said, "Operating costs should be reduced by a further five percent." (o "In light of the revised forecast, we should reduce operating costs by a further five percent.")

</details>

### Ejercicio 14 — Listening (paráfrasis)

Parafrasea esta conversación de reunión en registro de minuta.

> "John said we need to cut costs. He'll talk to finance. Someone asked about layoffs but the boss said no redundancies for now."

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *It was noted that operating costs should be reduced. Mr. Chen undertook to liaise with the finance team and to present a revised budget. Concerns were raised about staffing; however, the chair reiterated that no redundancies were contemplated at this stage.*

</details>

### Ejercicio 15 — Writing

Escribe una sección de minuta (80–100 palabras) para el punto "3.3 Vendor Selection". Usa al menos: una pasiva impersonal, un verbo de reporte con compromiso, una concesiva con contraste y un action item con responsable y plazo.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *3.3 Vendor Selection. It was noted that three vendors had been shortlisted following the RFP process. Ms. Patel highlighted that Vendor A had scored highest on technical criteria, and undertook to conduct reference checks by the end of the following week. Concerns were raised about Vendor A's pricing structure; however, the committee agreed that the technical advantage offset the marginal cost premium. It was recommended that the final decision be deferred pending the reference checks. Action: Ms. Patel to report back on reference checks by 7 November.* (89 palabras)

</details>

### Ejercicio 16 — Speaking (monólogo)

Prepara un monólogo de 2 minutos en el que, como chair, recapitules los acuerdos de una reunión al cierre, usando reported speech y registro de gestión.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo de inicio: *To summarise, it was noted that, in light of the revised forecast, operating costs should be reduced by a further five percent. Mr. Chen undertook to liaise with finance and to present a revised budget by the end of the following week. Concerns were raised about staffing; however, I would reiterate that no redundancies are contemplated at this stage. Regarding vendor selection, Ms. Patel agreed to conduct reference checks by 7 November, and it was recommended that the final decision be deferred pending those checks. Our next meeting is scheduled for...*

</details>

### Ejercicio 17 — Registro: minuta, memo o email de seguimiento

Clasifica cada extracto.

1. *3.2 Cost Reduction. It was noted that... Action: Mr. Chen to present by 30 October.*
2. *Decision: the vendor contract is awarded to Supplier A, subject to satisfactory references.*
3. *Hi all — just to recap what we agreed: John's handling the budget, and we'll revisit vendor selection next week.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Minuta** (formal impersonal, reported speech, action items numerados).
2. **Memo de decisión** (formal conciso, registra una decisión y su condición).
3. **Email de seguimiento** (semiformal, primera persona, recapitula acuerdos).

</details>

### Ejercicio 18 — Consolidación

Completa el extracto de minuta con una palabra por hueco.

> *It was (1)______ that, in light of the revised forecast, costs should be reduced. Mr. Chen (2)______ to present a revised budget by the end of the (3)______ week. Concerns were raised about staffing; (4)______, no redundancies were contemplated. It was recommended that the proposal (5)______ circulated for comment.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **noted** (pasiva impersonal)
2. **undertook** (verbo de reporte con compromiso)
3. **following** (deíctico temporal retrocedido)
4. **however** (concesiva con contraste)
5. **be** (subjuntivo mandativo)

</details>

### Ejercicio 19 — Reescribe el párrafo con registro de minuta

Eleva el registro del párrafo coloquial a una sección de minuta con reported speech, pasivas y action item.

> *John said we gotta cut costs and he'll talk to finance. Someone asked about layoffs but the boss said no redundancies for now. We'll send the proposal around before next time.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *It was noted that, in light of the revised forecast, operating costs should be reduced by a further five percent. Mr. Chen undertook to liaise with the finance team and to present a revised budget by the end of the following week. Concerns were raised about staffing; however, the chair reiterated that no redundancies were contemplated at this stage. It was recommended that the proposal be circulated for comment prior to the next meeting. Action: Mr. Chen to present a revised budget by 30 October.*

</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

- El **reported speech en pasado** retrocede los tiempos (*is→was, will→would, can→could, must→had to*), cambia pronombres (*I→he/she, we→they*) y deícticos (*tomorrow→the following day, here→there*); las preguntas reportadas llevan orden afirmativo y las órdenes, *to* + infinitivo.
- Los **verbos de reporte** matizan la intervención: observación (*noted, highlighted, emphasised*), propuesta (*proposed, suggested, recommended*), acuerdo (*agreed, endorsed*), compromiso (*undertook to, committed to, pledged to*), duda (*queried, questioned, raised concerns*) y señalización (*flagged, raised the issue of*).
- Las **pasivas impersonales** (*it was noted/agreed/recommended*) proyectan autoridad colectiva y, con el **subjuntivo mandativo** (*be circulated, be reduced*), formulan recomendaciones vinculantes.
- Los **action items** (responsable + *to* + infinitivo + plazo) y los **conectores de secuencia y causalidad** (*in light of, consequently, thereafter, however*) estructuran la minuta canónica (encabezado, ratificación, puntos, actions, cierre).
- El **vocabulario de gestión** (*agenda, minutes, chair, action item, stakeholder, delegate, accountable, escalate, ratify, mandate, buy-in, bottleneck, remediation, offsite, AOB*) y sus colocaciones (*chair a meeting, table an item, defer a point, secure buy-in, escalate a concern, liaise with, ratify the minutes, address a bottleneck*) permiten convertir conversación en decisión rastreable con la precisión y el registro que el C1 exige.
