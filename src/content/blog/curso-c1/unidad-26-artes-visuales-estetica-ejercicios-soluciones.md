## Parte II — Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Cláusulas de participio (pasado)

Condensa la subordinada en una cláusula de participio pasado al frente.

1. Because it was painted in oils, the canvas has a rich texture.
2. After it was restored, the fresco regained its original hues.
3. As it is composed asymmetrically, the painting feels dynamic.
4. Because it was rendered in low-key tones, the scene looks sombre.
5. When it is viewed from a distance, the portrait seems almost abstract.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Painted in oils,** the canvas has a rich texture.
2. **Restored,** the fresco regained its original hues.
3. **Composed asymmetrically,** the painting feels dynamic.
4. **Rendered in low-key tones,** the scene looks sombre.
5. **Viewed from a distance,** the portrait seems almost abstract.

El participio pasado implica voz pasiva y condensa la subordinada de causa, tiempo o condición.

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Cláusulas de participio (presente)

Condensa con participio presente (voz activa).

1. As it draws the eye to the centre, the focal point organises the composition.
2. Because it suggests depth, the diagonal line enlivens the scene.
3. As it leads the viewer's gaze, the perspective creates movement.
4. Since it invites touch, the impasto texture adds a tactile quality.
5. As it contrasts with the background, the figure stands out.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Drawing the eye to the centre,** the focal point organises the composition.
2. **Suggesting depth,** the diagonal line enlivens the scene.
3. **Leading the viewer's gaze,** the perspective creates movement.
4. **Inviting touch,** the impasto texture adds a tactile quality.
5. **Contrasting with the background,** the figure stands out.

El participio presente implica voz activa: el sujeto de la cláusula es el sujeto del verbo principal.

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Inversión concesiva

Reescribe con *adjective + as/though + subject + verb*.

1. Although the composition is striking, it lacks emotional depth.
2. Even though the palette is vibrant, the brushwork feels hurried.
3. Although the piece is technically flawless, it leaves the viewer cold.
4. Even though the scale is impressive, the subject matter is pedestrian.
5. Although the texture is rich, the colour is inert.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Striking as** the composition **is,** it lacks emotional depth.
2. **Vibrant though** the palette **is,** the brushwork feels hurried.
3. **Technically flawless as** the piece **is,** it leaves the viewer cold.
4. **Impressive though** the scale **is,** the subject matter is pedestrian.
5. **Rich as** the texture **is,** the colour is inert.

El adjetivo se adelanta para dar peso a la concesión; *as* o *though* introducen la cláusula. Se admite un mérito y se señala un límite.

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Otras concesivas formales

Reescribe con *for all its / admittedly...yet / much as / that said*.

1. Although it is technically brilliant, the piece lacks a focal point.
2. The palette is luminous, but the composition feels static.
3. The artist intends to shock, but the result feels derivative.
4. The brushwork conveys energy; however, the drawing is weak.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **For all its** technical brilliance, the piece lacks a focal point.
2. **Admittedly,** the palette is luminous, **yet** the composition feels static.
3. **Much as** the artist intends to shock, the result feels derivative.
4. The brushwork conveys energy; **that said,** the drawing is weak.

*For all its + sustantivo* y *much as + cláusula* son concesivas formales de C1 que matizan sin el básico *although*.

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Adjetivos evaluativos

Sustituye el adjetivo B2 por el C1 adecuado.

1. The painting is nice and makes you feel calm. → It is ___ and ___.
2. The colours are too strong and obvious. → The palette is ___ and ___.
3. The work is interesting but copied from others. → It is ___ yet ___.
4. The sculpture is simple and severe. → It is ___ and ___.
5. The portrait is full of light. → It is ___.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. It is **contemplative** and **restful** (o *serene*).
2. The palette is **garish** (o *cloying*) and **heavy-handed**.
3. It is **thought-provoking** yet **derivative**.
4. It is **stark** and **austere**.
5. It is **luminous**.

Los adjetivos C1 nombran el modo del efecto estético, no solo si gusta.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Verbos de relación visual

Completa con un verbo de relación (*lead / heighten / underscore / overwhelm / invite*).

1. The diagonal line ___ the eye towards the horizon.
2. The chiaroscuro ___ the drama of the scene.
3. The muted palette ___ the figure's isolation.
4. The scale of the canvas ___ the viewer.
5. The texture ___ touch even as the frame forbids it.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The diagonal line **leads** the eye towards the horizon.
2. The chiaroscuro **heightens** the drama of the scene.
3. The muted palette **underscores** the figure's isolation.
4. The scale of the canvas **overwhelms** the viewer.
5. The texture **invites** touch even as the frame forbids it.

Estos verbos relacionan un recurso formal con su efecto, núcleo de la interpretación visual.

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Vocabulario descriptivo (definiciones)

Empareja cada término con su definición.

Términos: *chiaroscuro / impasto / negative space / foreshortening / patina / still life*
1. Técnica de aplicar pintura muy espesa para crear relieve.
2. Espacio vacío alrededor y entre los sujetos de la composición.
3. Contraste fuerte entre luz y sombra.
4. Representación de objetos inanimados.
5. Técnica de perspectiva que acorta un objeto visto de frente.
6. Capa superficial que adquiere una obra con el tiempo.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **impasto** — Técnica de aplicar pintura muy espesa para crear relieve.
2. **negative space** — Espacio vacío alrededor y entre los sujetos de la composición.
3. **chiaroscuro** — Contraste fuerte entre luz y sombra.
4. **still life** — Representación de objetos inanimados.
5. **foreshortening** — Técnica de perspectiva que acorta un objeto visto de frente.
6. **patina** — Capa superficial que adquiere una obra con el tiempo.

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Colocaciones del discurso artístico

Completa con la colocación adecuada.

1. The painting ___ attention. (capta la atención)
2. The landscape ___ a sense of nostalgia. (transmite nostalgia)
3. The composition ___ depth to the scene. (aporta profundidad)
4. The figure seems to ___ on the sentimental. (roza lo sentimental)
5. The contrast is ___. (fuerte/duro)
6. The palette is ___. (apagada)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The painting **commands** attention.
2. The landscape **conveys** a sense of nostalgia.
3. The composition **lends** depth to the scene.
4. The figure seems to **border** on the sentimental.
5. The contrast is **stark**.
6. The palette is **muted**.

*Command attention, convey a sense of, lend depth to, border on, a stark contrast, a muted palette* son colocaciones habituales del registro artístico.

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Corrige los errores

Cada frase tiene un error de registro. Corrígelo.

1. The painting is beautiful and good. (demasiado genérico)
2. The picture is painted and it is composed and it is shown. (pasivo repetido)
3. It is striking the composition is balanced. (falta concesión correcta)
4. The medium are oils. (error de número)
5. The colours are very vibrantly. (error de categoría)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The painting is **evocative and contemplative** (adjetivos C1 concretos).
2. Alternar pasivos con cláusulas de participio: *Painted in oils and composed asymmetrically, the picture...* (evita la repetición de *it is*).
3. **Striking as** the composition **is,** it is balanced (inversión concesiva correcta) o *it is striking that the composition is balanced* (subordinada de comentario).
4. The **medium is** oils (singular; *medium* es singular, *media* plural).
5. The colours are very **vibrant** (adjetivo, no adverbio).

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Transformación (CAE-style)

Reescribe con la palabra dada.

1. Although the brushwork is striking, the drawing is weak. (STRIKING) → Striking ___
2. Because it was rendered in muted tones, the scene felt sombre. (RENDERED) → Rendered ___
3. The muted palette emphasises the figure's isolation. (UNDERSCORES) → The muted palette ___
4. The painting evokes nostalgia despite its modern subject. (FOR ALL) → For all ___
5. The composition is balanced, but it lacks a focal point. (BALANCED) → Balanced ___

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Striking as** the brushwork **is,** the drawing is weak.
2. **Rendered in muted tones,** the scene felt sombre.
3. The muted palette **underscores** the figure's isolation.
4. **For all its** modern subject, the painting evokes nostalgia.
5. **Balanced as** the composition **is,** it lacks a focal point.

Cada reescritura conserva el significado usando la estructura objetivo (inversión concesiva o cláusula de participio).

</details>

### Ejercicio 11 — Vocabulario descriptivo (gap-fill)

Completa con: *palette / foreground / saturation / glaze / contour / motif*.

1. The figure in the ___ draws the eye immediately.
2. The artist used a cool, muted ___ of blues and greys.
3. A recurring ___ of birds appears throughout the series.
4. The high ___ of the reds makes them almost vibrate.
5. A final ___ unifies the surface and deepens the tones.
6. The sharp ___ of the face contrasts with the soft background.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The figure in the **foreground** draws the eye immediately.
2. The artist used a cool, muted **palette** of blues and greys.
3. A recurring **motif** of birds appears throughout the series.
4. The high **saturation** of the reds makes them almost vibrate.
5. A final **glaze** unifies the surface and deepens the tones.
6. The sharp **contour** of the face contrasts with the soft background.

</details>

### Ejercicio 13 — Reading

Lee el fragmento y responde.

> *Rendered in a muted, low-saturation palette, the canvas conveys a quiet melancholy. The asymmetric composition draws the eye to the solitary figure in the foreground, while the vast expanse of negative space underscores her isolation. Striking as the brushwork is, it is the restraint of the chiaroscuro —a single, cool light source from the upper left— that lends the scene its contemplative gravity.*

1. ¿Qué cláusula de participio abre el fragmento y qué implica?
2. ¿Qué verbo de relación sitúa a la figura en el primer plano?
3. ¿Qué función cumple el espacio negativo según el texto?
4. ¿Qué estructura concesiva reconoce un mérito del pincel y señala un límite?
5. ¿Qué recurso final presta gravedad contemplativa a la escena?

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. *Rendered in a muted, low-saturation palette*: cláusula de participio pasado que condensa una subordinada de causa (voz pasiva).
2. *draws the eye to*: verbo de relación visual que sitúa la atención en la figura del primer plano.
3. *underscores her isolation*: el espacio negativo refuerza visualmente el aislamiento de la figura.
4. *Striking as the brushwork is*: inversión concesiva que admite el mérito del pincel y a la vez lo relativiza (lo importante es el claroscuro).
5. *the restraint of the chiaroscuro*: la contención del claroscuro (una sola fuente de luz fría) presta gravedad contemplativa.

</details>

### Ejercicio 14 — Listening (paráfrasis)

Imagina que escuchas a un conservador decir: *"The artist's late work trades the vibrant palette of his youth for an increasingly austere restraint; the result is a gravity that creeps up on you."* Parafrasea en una frase con una cláusula de participio y una inversión concesiva.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *Traded for an austere restraint, the artist's late palette sacrifices youthful vibrancy; striking as the early canvases are, it is this later gravity that lingers.*

Elementos: cláusula de participio (*Traded for an austere restraint*), inversión concesiva (*striking as the early canvases are*) y adjetivo evaluativo (*austere, vibrant, gravity*).

</details>

### Ejercicio 15 — Writing

Escribe una descripción e interpretación breves (120-150 palabras) de una obra real o imaginada que incluya: una cláusula de participio, una inversión concesiva, dos nominalizaciones y un verbo de relación visual.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo (147 palabras):

*Rendered in a muted, almost monochromatic palette, the canvas conveys a hushed melancholy that belies its modest scale. The asymmetric composition draws the eye to a solitary figure hunched in the foreground, while the vast expanse of negative space underscores her isolation. Striking as the gestural brushwork is, it is the restraint of the chiaroscuro —a single, cool light from the upper left— that lends the scene its contemplative gravity. Where an earlier canvas might have spelled out the figure's grief, here the artist lets the viewer infer it, and that understatement earns the quiet devastation of the final glance. If the drawing of the hands wavers slightly, it is a minor flaw in a work that otherwise commands attention through what it refuses to show. A stark, luminous piece that lingers.*

Rúbrica: cláusula de participio (*Rendered in...*), inversión concesiva (*Striking as the gestural brushwork is*), nominalizaciones (*the asymmetric composition, the restraint of the chiaroscuro*), verbo de relación (*draws the eye, underscores, lends*).

</details>

### Ejercicio 16 — Speaking (monólogo)

Habla durante dos minutos describiendo e interpretando una obra que conozcas, usando al menos: una cláusula de participio, una inversión concesiva y dos adjetivos evaluativos C1. Grábate y comprueba que primero describes un recurso formal y luego le atribuyes un efecto.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo de respuesta oral:

*Painted in a restricted palette of greys and browns, the canvas feels austere almost to the point of severity. The composition is balanced but not symmetrical — the figure sits off-centre, which actually draws the eye and creates tension. Striking as the scale is, it's the negative space that does the heavy lifting: it isolates the subject and underscores a sense of solitude. I'd call it a contemplative, restrained piece. The brushwork is gestural, almost rough, yet the chiaroscuro is controlled — a single light source that lends the face an unexpected luminosity. What stays with you is the gravity of what isn't shown.*

Criterios de la rúbrica: cláusula de participio (*Painted in a restricted palette*), inversión concesiva (*Striking as the scale is*), adjetivos C1 (*austere, contemplative, restrained, gestural, luminous*) y descripción que fundamenta la interpretación (*the negative space isolates the subject and underscores solitude*).

</details>

### Ejercicio 17 — Registro: etiqueta de museo vs. ensayo crítico

Reescribe la etiqueta de museo en estilo de ensayo crítico (más nominalizado, concesivo e interpretativo).

Etiqueta: *Oil on canvas, 1882. Landscape with solitary figure. The artist's last landscape.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo de ensayo: *The artist's final landscape, rendered in an increasingly muted palette, betrays a waning confidence in the representational project that had defined his career. Striking as the composition's classical balance remains, it is the vast expanse of negative space —and the solitary figure it isolates— that lends the canvas its quiet, contemplative gravity.*

Cambios: paso de datos factuales a interpretación; nominalizaciones (*the composition's classical balance, the vast expanse of negative space*); cláusula de participio (*rendered in an increasingly muted palette*); inversión concesiva (*Striking as the composition's classical balance remains*).

</details>

### Ejercicio 18 — Consolidación

Completa el párrafo con una palabra por hueco.

> *___(1) in a muted, low-saturation palette, the canvas conveys a quiet melancholy. The asymmetric composition ___(2) the eye to the figure in the foreground, while the negative space ___(3) her isolation. ___(4) as the brushwork is, it is the ___(5) of the chiaroscuro that ___(6) the scene its contemplative gravity.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Rendered** (o *Painted*)
2. **draws** (o *leads*)
3. **underscores** (o *heightens / reinforces*)
4. **Striking** (o *Remarkable / Notable*)
5. **restraint** (o *control / measure*)
6. **lends** (o *gives / imparts*)

El párrafo integra cláusula de participio, nominalización, inversión concesiva y verbo de relación visual.

</details>

### Ejercicio 19 — Reescribe el párrafo con registro C1

Eleva el registro del párrafo neutro con una cláusula de participio, una inversión concesiva y un verbo de relación visual.

> *The painting is nice. The colours are dark and it makes you feel sad. The brushwork is good but the composition is boring.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *Rendered in a muted, low-saturation palette, the canvas conveys a quiet melancholy; striking as the gestural brushwork is, the static composition denies the viewer an easy focal point, and the negative space, vast and unresolved, underscores a sense of inertia rather than isolation.*

Rúbrica: cláusula de participio (*Rendered in a muted, low-saturation palette*), inversión concesiva (*striking as the gestural brushwork is*), verbo de relación (*underscores*), adjetivos C1 (*muted, gestural, static, unresolved*) y eliminación de *nice, good, boring* en favor de valoraciones fundamentadas en recursos formales.

</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

- El discurso sobre arte oscila entre **descripción** (nombrar lo que está en la obra) e **interpretación** (atribuirle sentido o efecto); el buen texto no confunde ambos y ancla siempre la interpretación en un recurso formal descrito.
- Las **cláusulas de participio** condensan una subordinada en una frase inicial densa: el participio pasado (*Rendered in muted tones, ...*) implica voz pasiva; el presente (*Drawing the eye, ...*), voz activa.
- La **inversión concesiva** (*Striking as the composition is, ...*) reconoce una cualidad y a la vez limita su valoración, operación central de la crítica matizada; otras concesivas formales (*for all its, admittedly...yet, much as, that said*) enriquecen el tono reflexivo.
- El **léxico evaluativo** nombra el modo del efecto estético: adjetivos matizados (*evocative, luminous, stark, austere, muted, derivative, inert, cloying*) y verbos de relación visual (*draw the eye, heighten, underscore, overwhelm, lend depth to*).
- El **vocabulario de artes visuales** (*composition, chiaroscuro, perspective, texture, brushwork, palette, medium, negative space, focal point, impasto, patina, foreshortening, motif*) y sus colocaciones (*command attention, convey a sense of, border on the sentimental, a stark contrast, a muted palette*) constituyen el sustrato técnico de toda descripción precisa.
