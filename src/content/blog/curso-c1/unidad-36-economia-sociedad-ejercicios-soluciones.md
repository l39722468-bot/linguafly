## Parte II — Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Estructuras evaluativas de atribución

Reescribe con la estructura indicada.

1. People generally argue that austerity deepens recessions. → It is widely argued that...
2. Targeted stimulus is a reasonable option. → There is a case for...
3. Someone might say the stimulus came too late. → One might contend that...
4. Is austerity really the right approach? → It is debatable whether...

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. It is **widely argued that** austerity deepens recessions.
2. There is **a case for** targeted stimulus.
3. One **might contend that** the stimulus came too late.
4. It is **debatable whether** austerity is the right approach.

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Adverbios de postura

Elige el adverbio adecuado (arguably / admittedly / indisputably / paradoxically / inevitably).

1. ______, the stimulus came too late. (probable pero discutible)
2. ______, debt was already high. (concesión previa)
3. ______, inflation eroded real wages. (hecho innegable)
4. ______, low unemployment coexisted with stagnant growth. (aparente contradicción)
5. ______, the tariff raised consumer prices. (consecuencia necesaria)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Arguably**, the stimulus came too late.
2. **Admittedly**, debt was already high.
3. **Indisputably**, inflation eroded real wages.
4. **Paradoxically**, low unemployment coexisted with stagnant growth.
5. **Inevitably**, the tariff raised consumer prices.

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Hedges económicos

Reescribe con un *hedging* adecuado.

1. The policy stabilised prices. → The policy ______ stabilised prices. (probabilidad)
2. The data support the stimulus thesis. → The data ______ support the stimulus thesis. (tendencia)
3. The effect is temporary. → The effect ______ temporary. (postura tentativa)
4. The link is positive. → The link is ______ positive. (amplio pero no uniforme)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The policy **appears to have** stabilised prices.
2. The data **tend to** support the stimulus thesis.
3. The effect **would seem to be** temporary.
4. The link is **broadly** positive, **though not uniformly** so.

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Concesión y contraste

Reescribe integrando el contraargumento con concesión + contraste.

1. Debt was high. The multiplier justified spending. → Admittedly... nevertheless...
2. Inflation ticked up. It stayed within the target band. → Although...

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Admittedly**, debt was already high; **nevertheless**, the multiplier justified targeted spending.
2. **Although** inflation ticked up, it remained within the target band.

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Verbos de opinión

Elige el verbo adecuado (contend / maintain / concede / dispute / qualify).

1. Critics ______ that austerity deepened the recession.
2. Defenders ______ that it restored market confidence.
3. Both sides ______ that the recovery was uneven.
4. Skeptics ______ the claim that stimulus was ineffective.
5. The author ______ her earlier stance in the revised paper.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Critics **contend** that austerity deepened the recession.
2. Defenders **maintain** that it restored market confidence.
3. Both sides **concede** that the recovery was uneven.
4. Skeptics **dispute** the claim that stimulus was ineffective.
5. The author **qualifies** her earlier stance in the revised paper.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Nominalización

Reescribe convirtiendo el verbo en sustantivo.

1. The service sector grew steadily. → The steady ______ of the service sector.
2. Prices fluctuated wildly. → Wild ______ in prices.
3. The economy overheated. → The ______ of the economy.
4. Governments stimulated demand. → Government ______ of demand.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The steady **growth** of the service sector.
2. Wild **fluctuation** in prices.
3. The **overheating** of the economy.
4. Government **stimulation** of demand.

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Conectores de causa y consecuencia

Inserta el conector adecuado (owing to / consequently / in a bid to / provided that).

1. ______ supply bottlenecks, inflation surged.
2. Inflation surged; ______, the central bank tapered stimulus.
3. ______ curb inflation, the bank raised rates.
4. Prices will stabilise ______ credibility is restored.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Owing to** supply bottlenecks, inflation surged.
2. Inflation surged; **consequently**, the central bank tapered stimulus.
3. **In a bid to** curb inflation, the bank raised rates.
4. Prices will stabilise **provided that** credibility is restored.

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Corrige los errores

Cada frase tiene un error. Corrígelo.

1. I think austerity is bad.
2. Indisputably, austerity helped. (contexto: es discutible)
3. Arguably presumably admittedly, the policy failed.
4. The economy is like totally tanking.
5. Recession is when economy grows a lot. (definición incorrecta)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. It is **widely argued that** austerity is counterproductive. (registro formal)
2. **Arguably**, austerity helped. (discutible, no innegable)
3. Un adverbio por afirmación: **Arguably**, the policy failed.
4. The economy **entered a sharp downturn**. (registro formal)
5. A **recession** is a sustained contraction, not growth. (o: *A recession is typically defined as two consecutive quarters of negative growth.*)

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Transformación (CAE-style)

Reescribe empezando con las palabras dadas.

1. "People generally argue that austerity deepens recessions." → *It is widely...*
2. "Although debt was high, spending was justified." → *Admittedly...*
3. "The stimulus probably came too late." → *Arguably...*
4. "Critics say austerity deepened the recession, but defenders say it restored confidence." → *While defenders maintain...*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. It is widely **argued that** austerity deepens recessions.
2. **Admittedly**, debt was high; **nevertheless**, spending was justified.
3. **Arguably**, the stimulus came too late.
4. **While defenders maintain** that austerity restored confidence, critics contend that it deepened the recession.

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Vocabulario macroeconómico

Relaciona cada término con su definición.

1. Stagflation   a. Crecimiento excesivo que genera inflación.
2. Overheating   b. Estancamiento + inflación.
3. Soft landing  c. Frenar la economía sin causar recesión.
4. Austerity     d. Recorte de gasto público.
5. Yield         e. Rentabilidad de un bono.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1-b, 2-a, 3-c, 4-d, 5-e.

</details>

### Ejercicio 11 — Colocaciones económicas

Completa con el verbo adecuado (curb / spur / weather / taper / erode).

1. ______ inflation before it becomes entrenched.
2. ______ growth through targeted investment.
3. ______ a downturn without mass layoffs.
4. ______ stimulus as the economy recovers.
5. Inflation can ______ real wages silently.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Curb** inflation before it becomes entrenched.
2. **Spur** growth through targeted investment.
3. **Weather** a downturn without mass layoffs.
4. **Taper** stimulus as the economy recovers.
5. Inflation can **erode** real wages silently.

</details>

### Ejercicio 12 — Refutación estructurada

Estructura una refutación en tres pasos (concesión → contraste → postura propia) al argumento: *"Austerity restored market confidence."*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *Admittedly, austerity may have restored short-term market confidence (concesión); nevertheless, the cost in lost output and eroded wages was disproportionately high (contraste); there is therefore a strong case for a more balanced fiscal approach that pairs credibility with targeted growth investment (postura propia).*

</details>

### Ejercicio 13 — Reading

Lee el extracto y responde.

> *Arguably, the austerity measures adopted in the wake of the crisis exacerbated the downturn rather than curbing it. Admittedly, the fiscal room for manoeuvre was limited; yet there is a strong case for targeted stimulus over indiscriminate cuts, particularly given the multiplier effect of public investment during a liquidity trap. While defenders maintain that austerity restored market confidence, critics contend that it deepened the recession and eroded real wages.*

1. Which adverb of stance opens the paragraph, and what does it signal?
2. What concession is made, and with what word?
3. Identify the two opposing verbs of opinion and their subjects.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. *Arguably* — signals a probable but debatable claim.
2. That fiscal room for manoeuvre was limited; introduced by *admittedly*.
3. Defenders **maintain** that austerity restored confidence; critics **contend** that it deepened the recession.

</details>

### Ejercicio 14 — Listening (paráfrasis)

Parafrasea esta declaración del economista en registro C1.

> "I reckon austerity made things worse. Sure, debt was bad, but spending more on the right things would've helped because of the multiplier. Some say it fixed confidence but I think it just made the recession deeper."

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *Arguably, the austerity measures exacerbated the downturn rather than curbing it. Admittedly, the fiscal room for manoeuvre was limited; yet there is a strong case for targeted stimulus over indiscriminate cuts, given the multiplier effect of public investment. While defenders maintain that austerity restored market confidence, critics contend that it deepened the recession and eroded real wages.*

</details>

### Ejercicio 15 — Writing

Escribe un editorial de opinión (80–100 palabras) sobre si el banco central debería subir tipos. Usa al menos: una estructura evaluativa, un adverbio de postura, una concesión con contraste y un verbo de opinión.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *It is widely argued that the central bank should raise rates to curb inflation; arguably, however, doing so prematurely risks tipping the economy into recession. Admittedly, inflation has breached the target band; nevertheless, the drivers are predominantly supply-side, and monetary tightening alone cannot resolve bottlenecks. Critics maintain that inaction entrenches inflationary expectations, while others contend that a patient, data-dependent approach is warranted. There is therefore a case for a measured hike paired with clear forward guidance, so that credibility is preserved without choking the recovery.* (88 palabras)

</details>

### Ejercicio 16 — Speaking (monólogo)

Prepara un monólogo de 2 minutos en el que opines sobre la austeridad vs. el estímulo, usando estructuras evaluativas y registro C1.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo de inicio: *It is widely argued that austerity deepens recessions rather than shortening them, and I would broadly agree. Admittedly, fiscal room for manoeuvre is often limited in the aftermath of a crisis; nevertheless, there is a strong case for targeted stimulus, particularly given the multiplier effect of public investment during a liquidity trap. While defenders maintain that austerity restores market confidence, critics contend — and the data tend to support — that it deepens the downturn and erodes real wages. Paradoxically, the very credibility that austerity seeks to restore can be undermined by the output loss it causes...*

</details>

### Ejercicio 17 — Registro: editorial, informe o análisis técnico

Clasifica cada extracto.

1. *The yield curve inverted in Q2, historically a reliable recession signal.*
2. *There is a strong case for measured hikes; admittedly, supply-side inflation resists monetary tools.*
3. *GDP contracted 0.4% in Q3, with services accounting for 60% of the decline.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Análisis técnico** (terminología densa, mecanismo explicado).
2. **Editorial de opinión** (estructuras evaluativas, adverbios de postura).
3. **Informe económico** (datos formales, pasiva implícita, sin opinión).

</details>

### Ejercicio 18 — Consolidación

Completa el párrafo con una palabra por hueco.

> *(1)______, the austerity measures exacerbated the downturn. (2)______, fiscal room was limited; (3)______, there is a strong case for targeted stimulus. While defenders (4)______ that austerity restored confidence, critics (5)______ that it deepened the recession. The data (6)______ to support the latter view.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Arguably** (adverbio de postura)
2. **Admittedly** (concesión previa)
3. **nevertheless / yet** (contraste)
4. **maintain** (verbo de opinión, sostener)
5. **contend** (verbo de opinión, refutar)
6. **tend** (tend to, hedge)

</details>

### Ejercicio 19 — Reescribe el párrafo con opinión matizada

Eleva el registro del párrafo coloquial con estructuras evaluativas, adverbios de postura y léxico macroeconómico.

> *I reckon austerity made things worse. Sure, debt was bad, but spending more on the right stuff would've fixed it because of the multiplier. Some say it fixed confidence but I think it just tanked the recession deeper.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *Arguably, the austerity measures adopted in the wake of the crisis exacerbated the downturn rather than curbing it. Admittedly, the fiscal room for manoeuvre was limited; yet there is a strong case for targeted stimulus over indiscriminate cuts, particularly given the multiplier effect of public investment during a liquidity trap. </details>

## Resumen de la unidad

- Las **estructuras evaluativas** atribuyen la postura y calibran su fuerza: atribución a la comunidad (*it is widely argued that, there is a growing consensus that*), postura razonable (*there is a case for*), tentativa (*one might contend that*), cuestión abierta (*it is debatable whether*).
- Los **adverbios de postura** posicionan al hablante: *arguably* (probable pero discutible), *presumably* (presuposición), *admittedly* (concesión previa), *conceivably* (posibilidad), *indisputably* (innegable), *paradoxically* (aparente contradicción), *inevitably* (consecuencia necesaria); los **hedges** (*appears to, tends to, would seem to*) templan la certeza.
- La **concesión y el contraste** integran el contraargumento (*admittedly ... nevertheless, although ... yet*); los **verbos de opinión** sostienen o refutan (*contend, maintain, dispute, concede, qualify*); la **refutación estructurada** sigue tres pasos: concesión → contraste → postura propia.
- La **nominalización** densifica el razonamiento (*growth, inflation, recession, stimulus, overheating*); los **conectores de causa y consecuencia** (*owing to, consequently, in a bid to, provided that*) encadenan el argumento.
- El **vocabulario macroeconómico** (*inflation, recession, stagnation, austerity, stimulus, fiscal policy, monetary policy, GDP, yield, downturn, overheating, soft landing, trade deficit, productivity*) y sus colocaciones (*curb inflation, spur growth, weather a downturn, taper stimulus, erode real wages, restore confidence, deepen a recession, bottom out*) permiten opinar sobre economía y sociedad con la precisión, el rigor y el matiz que el C1 exige.
