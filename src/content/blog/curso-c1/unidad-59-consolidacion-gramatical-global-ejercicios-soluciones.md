Este cuaderno reúne los **ejercicios de la Unidad 59 del curso C1** con soluciones. Haz cada bloque sin mirar la respuesta.

> **Guía teórica:** [Repaso Global y Ejercicios de Gramática Inglesa C1](/blog/curso-c1/unidad-59-consolidacion-gramatical-global)

### Cómo usar este cuaderno

Responde primero y comprueba después. La explicación está pensada para el estudiante hispanohablante.

## Parte II — Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1
Completa con el tiempo narrativo correcto: By the time the ambulance arrived, the patient ______ (lose) consciousness for several minutes.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
had been losing (also acceptable: had lost). "had been losing" foregrounds the durative continuity up to the arrival; "had lost" foregrounds the completed state.
</details>

### Ejercicio 2
Reescribe con inversión emphática: She had never seen such devastation before.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
Never before had she seen such devastation.
</details>

### Ejercicio 3
Completa la condicional mixta: If she ______ (take) that job offer last year, she ______ (live) in Berlin now.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
had taken ... would be living
</details>

### Ejercicio 4
Transforma con despite: Although the evidence was weak, the jury convicted him.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
Despite the weak evidence, the jury convicted him. (Also acceptable: Despite the evidence being weak, the jury convicted him.)
</details>

### Ejercicio 5
Completa con una cláusula partitiva: The committee rejected the proposals, ______ (many of which / many of that) were politically sensitive.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
many of which
</details>

### Ejercicio 6
Reescribe con so... that invertido: The impact was so profound that the policy was revised.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
So profound was the impact that the policy was revised.
</details>

### Ejercicio 7
Completa con el subjuntivo mandativo: The board insists that every employee ______ (be) present at the meeting.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
be
</details>

### Ejercicio 8
Elige el conector adecuado y completa: The model is theoretically elegant; ______, it fails to account for the empirical anomalies.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
nevertheless (also acceptable: nonetheless). A contrastive connector is required.
</details>

### Ejercicio 9
Reescribe con una cláusula nominal con what: Her sudden resignation surprised everyone.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
What surprised everyone was her sudden resignation. (Or: Her sudden resignation was what surprised everyone.)
</details>

### Ejercicio 10
Completa con el modal pasado adecuado (deducción de imposibilidad): He ______ (can't have / mustn't have) known about the meeting; he was abroad that day.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
can't have
</details>

### Ejercicio 11
Transforma con un relativo reducido: The bridge which was built in 1890 is still in use.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
The bridge built in 1890 is still in use.
</details>

### Ejercicio 12
Completa con until, once o as soon as: ______ the new legislation takes effect, all existing permits will lapse.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
Once
</details>

### Ejercicio 13
Reescribe con such... that invertido: The outcry was such that the minister resigned.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
Such was the outcry that the minister resigned.
</details>

### Ejercicio 14
Completa la condicional mixta (presente → pasado): If he ______ (be) more disciplined, he ______ (finish) the project by now.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
were ... would have finished
</details>

### Ejercicio 15
Reescribe con un conector reformulativo C1: The theory is wrong. In other words, it cannot explain the data.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
The theory is untenable; to put it differently, it cannot account for the data.
</details>

### Ejercicio 16
Elige el artículo correcto (the, a, o cero): She went to ______ university to study law, and later spent three months in ______ hospital after an accident.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
zero article ... the (or zero article in British English for institutional reference: "She went to university to study law, and later spent three months in hospital after an accident." The first blank is zero; the second is zero in BrE institutional use or "the" if referring to a specific building.)
</details>

### Ejercicio 17
Completa con future perfect o future perfect continuous: By 2050, the company ______ (operate) in over fifty countries for three decades.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
will have been operating
</details>

### Ejercicio 18
Reescribe con much as: Although I sympathise with your position, I cannot support the motion.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
Much as I sympathise with your position, I cannot support the motion.
</details>

### Ejercicio 19
Redacta en inglés C1 (60-80 palabras) un breve párrafo narrativo que combine past perfect, past perfect continuous, inversión emphática y una cláusula concesiva.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
Never before had the village witnessed such flooding. For three days the rain had been falling without relent, and by dawn the river had burst its banks. Although the townsfolk had reinforced the levee the previous summer, the waters overwhelmed it within hours. Despite their preparations, the destruction was total.
</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

En esta unidad has consolidado de manera sistémica las estructuras gramaticales más exigentes del nivel C1. Has repasado los tiempos narrativos (simple past, past perfect, past perfect continuous) y los futuros perfectos para la prospectión. Has dominado las condicionales mixtas que combinan tipos distintos, la inversión emphática con adverbios negativos y con so y such, las cláusulas concesivas con although, despite y much as, las cláusulas de relativo reducidas y partitivas, el subjuntivo mandativo y los modales en pasado para la deducción. A nivel discursivo, has refinado el uso de conectores de contraste, adición y reformulación, y la precisión determinativa del artículo. Los 19 ejercicios te han permitido integrar estas estructuras en un repertorio coherente. En la siguiente unidad, la 60, aplicarás esta consolidación gramatical a la preparación estratégica para los exámenes CAE, IELTS y TOEFL, donde la gestión del tiempo y la estrategia son tan importantes como el dominio lingüístico.
