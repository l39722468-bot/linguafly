Responde primero y comprueba después. La explicación está pensada para el estudiante hispanohablante.

## Parte II — Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1
Reformula la siguiente frase con un marcador de paráfrasis empática seguido de validación: "You are tired and stressed."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

It sounds as though you're both tired and stressed, which must be hard to sustain over time. I can see why that would weigh on you.
</details>

### Ejercicio 2
Completa con el marcador de reformulación más adecuado al contexto: "______ you're saying that the workload has become unmanageable. Is that right?"

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

If I understand correctly, you're saying that the workload has become unmanageable. Is that right?
</details>

### Ejercicio 3
Reescribe usando un hedge para suavizar la prescripción "You need to see a therapist."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

It might be worth speaking to a therapist who can help you work through what you're feeling, if that's something you'd be open to.
</details>

### Ejercicio 4
Traduce manteniendo el tono asertivo y respetuoso, con un hedge final colaborativo: "Necesito poner un límite con los mensajes fuera del horario laboral."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

I'd appreciate it if we could keep messages to working hours, if that works for you.
</details>

### Ejercicio 5
Elige la opción con person-first language y justifica brevemente: (a) a depressive, (b) a person living with depression, (c) a depression sufferer.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

(b) a person living with depression. Coloca a la persona antes que el diagnóstico y evita reducir su identidad a la condición, mientras que (a) y (c) la reducen a la enfermedad.
</details>

### Ejercicio 6
Completa la collocation con el verbo correcto: "She has been ______ from burnout for several months now and is only just starting to recover."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

She has been suffering from burnout for several months now and is only just starting to recover.
</details>

### Ejercicio 7
Reformula con el marcador "In other words" para reempaquetar: "I feel like I can't switch off, even at weekends."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

In other words, your mind stays in work mode even during the time that's supposed to be yours, so rest never really arrives.
</details>

### Ejercicio 8
Une cada verbo con su patrón correcto: (struggle / confide / open up / reach out) + (in a friend / with insomnia / to a therapist / about one's anxiety).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

struggle with insomnia; confide in a friend; open up about one's anxiety; reach out to a therapist.
</details>

### Ejercicio 9
Reescribe con una estructura de concesión que humanice el argumento: "Therapy is expensive. It changes lives."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Admittedly, therapy can be expensive, but for many people it is genuinely life-changing and worth the investment.
</details>

### Ejercicio 10
Expresa reticencia cortés para no hablar de un tema personal delicado: "No quiero hablar de eso."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

I'd rather not go into that, if you don't mind. I'm not quite ready to talk about it yet.
</details>

### Ejercicio 11
Sustituye "think" por un verbo de opinión matizada más preciso: "Many experts think that early intervention matters in mental health."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Many experts contend that early intervention matters in mental health.
</details>

### Ejercicio 12
Completa con un hedge que suavice sin restar credibilidad: "______ it's worth considering whether your current routine is sustainable in the long run."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Perhaps it's worth considering whether your current routine is sustainable in the long run.
</details>

### Ejercicio 13
Reformula con "What I'm hearing is" y añade validación final: "I'm overwhelmed by deadlines and I can't sleep."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

What I'm hearing is that the deadline pressure has spilled over into your sleep, and you're feeling overwhelmed on both fronts. That must be exhausting.
</details>

### Ejercicio 14
Reescribe en modo optativo (no prescriptivo) esta afirmación tajante: "You must take a holiday immediately."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

You might find it helpful to take some time off soon, even a long weekend, to reset before things get any harder.
</details>

### Ejercicio 15
Completa la collocation del autocuidado maduro: "I've started to ______ a digital sunset at 9 p.m. so my mind can wind down before bed."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

I've started to enforce a digital sunset at 9 p.m. so my mind can wind down before bed.
</details>

### Ejercicio 16
Redacta una frase que distinga burnout de stress usando ambos términos con precisión clínica.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Stress is the pressure itself, which can be acute or chronic; burnout is what happens when that pressure is sustained for so long that you're left emotionally depleted, detached and unable to function, even after the pressure eases.
</details>

### Ejercicio 17
Reformula empáticamente con reconocimiento, reformulación y validación: "My team doesn't appreciate me."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

It sounds like you're feeling unseen by your team, which must be incredibly demoralising. In other words, your effort isn't being recognised, and I can see why that would make you question whether it's worth it.
</details>

### Ejercicio 18
Completa con un marcador de escucha activa que estructure la información en dos frentes: "______ there are two things going on here: the workload itself and the lack of recognition."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

It sounds like there are two things going on here: the workload itself and the lack of recognition.
</details>

### Ejercicio 19
Redacta un límite asertivo sobre las reuniones tardías, usando una estructura de preferencia y un hedge final colaborativo.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

I'd prefer it if we could avoid scheduling meetings after 5 p.m., at least while I'm working on this project — if that works for the team.
</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

En esta unidad has aprendido a reformular con empatía usando marcadores como "It sounds like…", "What I'm hearing is…", e "If I understand correctly…", combinándolos con validación emocional para que la persona se sienta genuinamente escuchada. Has incorporado hedges para suavizar afirmaciones sensibles y has practicado el modo optativo frente al prescriptivo, respetando así la autonomía del interlocutor. Has trabajado las estructuras para marcar límites con asertividad respetuosa —"I'd appreciate it if…", "I'm not comfortable with…", "I'd rather we didn't…", "I'd prefer it if…"— y has dominado el person-first language para hablar de salud mental sin estigmatizar. El léxico del bienestar —resilience, coping, burnout, grounding, emotional regulation— junto con las collocations terapéuticas y los verbos de opinión matizada te permiten ahora participar en conversaciones serias sobre salud mental con la precisión y la sensibilidad que el nivel C1 exige. En la próxima unidad, la 38, darás un paso más al explorar la neurociencia divulgativa, donde la simplificación controlada de terminología técnica pondrá a prueba tu recién adquirida capacidad de reformular y matizar.
