Responde primero y comprueba después. La explicación está pensada para el estudiante hispanohablante.

## Parte II — Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Pasiva básica e institucional

Reescribe estas frases activas en pasiva, manteniendo el tiempo verbal.

1. UNESCO inscribed the site in 1999.
2. The committee must maintain funding.
3. Specialists have restored the façade.
4. The state party should adopt a conservation plan.
5. Encroaching development threatens the setting.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The site **was inscribed** in 1999.
2. Funding **must be maintained**.
3. The façade **has been restored**.
4. A conservation plan **should be adopted**.
5. The setting **is threatened** by encroaching development.

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Pasiva con verbo de reporte

Reescribe con la estructura *It is believed that...* y luego con sujeto pasivo (*...is believed to...*).

1. Scholars believe that the mosaic dates from the third century.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

- *It is believed that the mosaic dates from the third century.*
- *The mosaic is believed to date from the third century.*

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Cláusula mandativa con subjuntivo

Completa con el verbo en forma base (subjuntivo) o *should* + infinitivo.

1. It is recommended that the site ______ (monitor) annually.
2. The charter stipulates that all alterations ______ (document).
3. It is essential that funding ______ (remain) stable.
4. The Committee urges that the buffer zone ______ (expand).

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. It is recommended that the site **be** monitored annually. (o *should be monitored*)
2. The charter stipulates that all alterations **be** documented. (o *should be documented*)
3. It is essential that funding **remain** stable. (o *should remain*)
4. The Committee urges that the buffer zone **be** expanded. (o *should be expanded*)

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Nominalización

Reescribe convirtiendo el verbo o adjetivo subrayado en un sustantivo.

1. The site must be **preserved**; this requires investment.
2. The frescoes have **deteriorated** rapidly.
3. The state party **inscribed** the property; this was welcomed.
4. We must **conserve** the wall; a plan was adopted.
5. Urban areas **encroach** on the setting.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The **preservation** of the site requires investment.
2. The **deterioration** of the frescoes has been rapid.
3. The **inscription** of the property was welcomed.
4. A **conservation** plan for the wall was adopted.
5. Urban **encroachment** on the setting [is a concern].

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Hedges

Reescribe cada frase con un *hedging* del nivel indicado.

1. (probabilidad) The damage stems from rising damp.
2. (atribución a la comunidad) The mosaic dates from the third century.
3. (postura tentativa) Reburial is the best interim measure.
4. (tendencia) The findings support the erosion hypothesis.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The damage **appears to** stem from rising damp.
2. It is **widely held that** the mosaic dates from the third century.
3. It **would seem that** reburial is the most viable interim measure.
4. The findings **tend to** support the hypothesis of gradual erosion.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Verbos de postura institucional

Elige el verbo de postura adecuado (endorse / urge / pledge / caution against / affirm) y conjúgalo.

1. The Committee ______ the proposed strategy and calls for its implementation.
2. The Foundation ______ to underwrite half the costs.
3. The Advisory Body ______ further commercial development in the buffer zone.
4. The state party ______ that all interventions will be reversible.
5. The Committee ______ the state party to act without delay.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The Committee **endorses** the proposed strategy and calls for its implementation.
2. The Foundation **pledges** to underwrite half the costs.
3. The Advisory Body **cautions against** further commercial development in the buffer zone.
4. The state party **affirms** that all interventions will be reversible.
5. The Committee **urges** the state party to act without delay.

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Conectores formales

Inserta el conector adecuado (moreover / consequently / nevertheless / namely / thereafter).

1. The site meets criteria (i) and (iii); ______, it retains high authenticity.
2. ______, the Advisory Body recommends inscription.
3. The Committee should caution against development; ______, limited visitor access may be permitted.
4. Two threats are identified: ______, encroachment and underfunding.
5. The plan was adopted in 2020; ______, annual reports were submitted.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The site meets criteria (i) and (iii); **moreover**, it retains high authenticity.
2. **Consequently**, the Advisory Body recommends inscription.
3. The Committee should caution against development; **nevertheless**, limited visitor access may be permitted.
4. Two threats are identified: **namely**, encroachment and underfunding.
5. The plan was adopted in 2020; **thereafter**, annual reports were submitted.

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Modales: shall / must / should / ought to

Elige el modal que mejor refleja la fuerza indicada.

1. (obligación normativa, charter) The state party ______ submit periodic reports.
2. (obligación fuerte) All interventions ______ be reversible.
3. (recomendación) The buffer zone ______ be expanded.
4. (recomendación formal) It ______ to be advisable to commission a survey.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The state party **shall** submit periodic reports.
2. All interventions **must** be reversible.
3. The buffer zone **should** be expanded.
4. It **ought to** be advisable to commission a survey. (nota: más natural *It would be advisable to...*; *ought to* marca recomendación formal)

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Corrige los errores

Cada frase tiene un error de registro institucional. Corrígelo.

1. It is recommended that the site is monitored annually.
2. The state party will submit reports. (contexto: charter normativo)
3. We think you should preserve the old quarter.
4. The implementation of the conservation of the restoration of the wall began.
5. It might perhaps arguably seem to possibly suggest that funding is low.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. It is recommended that the site **be** monitored annually. (subjuntivo)
2. The state party **shall** submit reports. (obligación normativa, no futuro)
3. It is recommended that the old quarter **be preserved**. (impersonalizar)
4. **Restoring the wall and conserving it** began. (desnominalizar el exceso)
5. It **would seem that** funding is low. (un solo *hedging*)

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Transformación (CAE-style)

Reescribe la frase empezando con las palabras dadas, conservando el sentido.

1. "They should expand the buffer zone." → *It is recommended that...*
2. "People believe that the tower dates from 1100." → *The tower is believed...*
3. "Because the site has deteriorated, the Committee wants action." → *In light of the deterioration...*
4. "We pledge to fund the restoration." → *The Foundation pledges...*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. It is recommended that the buffer zone **be expanded**.
2. The tower is believed **to date** from 1100.
3. In light of the **deterioration** of the site, the Committee **urges** action.
4. The Foundation **pledges to fund** the restoration.

</details>

### Ejercicio 11 — Vocabulario del patrimonio

Relaciona cada término con su definición.

1. Authenticity   a. Área que protege el entorno del bien.
2. Buffer zone    b. Criterio que evalúa si el bien es genuino.
3. Intangible heritage  c. Tradiciones, rituales y saberes vivos.
4. Stewardship    d. Gestión responsable a largo plazo.
5. Encroachment   e. Invasión de construcción o actividad en el entorno.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1-b, 2-a, 3-c, 4-d, 5-e.

</details>

### Ejercicio 12 — Colocaciones

Completa con el verbo adecuado (meet / inscribe / retain / halt / allocate).

1. ______ the criteria for outstanding universal value.
2. ______ the site on the World Heritage List.
3. ______ a high degree of authenticity.
4. ______ the deterioration before it becomes irreversible.
5. ______ sustained funding to the conservation plan.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Meet** the criteria for outstanding universal value.
2. **Inscribe** the site on the World Heritage List.
3. **Retain** a high degree of authenticity.
4. **Halt** the deterioration before it becomes irreversible.
5. **Allocate** sustained funding to the conservation plan.

</details>

### Ejercicio 13 — Reading

Lee el extracto y responde.

> *The reactive monitoring mission concluded that the property's integrity had been compromised by unregulated construction within the buffer zone. It is therefore recommended that the historic centre be inscribed on the List of World Heritage in Danger, and that the state party be requested to submit, by 1 December, a corrected map of the property's boundaries.*

1. What two problems does the mission identify?
2. What are the two recommended actions?
3. Identify one nominalization and one passive construction.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Integrity compromised by unregulated construction within the buffer zone (y, implícitamente, boundaries incorrectas que exigen un mapa corregido).
2. (a) Inscribe the historic centre on the List of World Heritage in Danger; (b) request the state party to submit a corrected map of boundaries by 1 December.
3. Nominalización: *construction* / *monitoring*. Pasiva: *had been compromised* / *be inscribed* / *be requested*.

</details>

### Ejercicio 14 — Listening (paráfrasis)

Escucha (o lee) esta declaración y parafrasea en registro institucional.

> "We, the Committee, back the plan. We also warn that building near the site is bad. We promise to help pay for it."

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *The Committee endorses the proposed conservation plan and cautions against further construction within the buffer zone. Furthermore, it pledges to contribute financially to the restoration effort.*

</details>

### Ejercicio 15 — Writing

Escribe un párrafo (80–100 palabras) de argumento institucional recomendando la inscripción de un sitio imaginario ("Old Quarter of Vila Nova"). Usa al menos: una pasiva, una nominalización, un *hedging*, un verbo de postura y un conector formal.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *In light of its outstanding universal value, it is recommended that the Old Quarter of Vila Nova be inscribed on the World Heritage List. The property retains a remarkable degree of authenticity and integrity; moreover, its architectural fabric appears to have survived largely intact. Consequently, the Committee endorses the nomination and urges the state party to designate an adequate buffer zone without delay. Furthermore, it is essential that sustained funding be allocated to long-term stewardship, so that the deterioration presently observed may be halted before it becomes irreversible.* (92 palabras)

Rúbrica: pasiva (*be inscribed, be allocated*), nominalización (*inscription, deterioration, stewardship, funding*), *hedging* (*appears to have*), verbo de postura (*endorses, urges*), conectores (*moreover, consequently, furthermore*).

</details>

### Ejercicio 16 — Speaking (monólogo)

Prepara un monólogo de 2 minutos en el que, asumiendo el rol de un delegado ante el Comité, argumentes a favor de ampliar la zona de amortiguamiento de un sitio. Usa registro institucional.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo de inicio: *Distinguished members of the Committee, in light of recent encroachment documented along the eastern boundary, it is strongly recommended that the buffer zone be enlarged without delay. The integrity of the inscribed property is, I would argue, at considerable risk; moreover, the current boundary fails to reflect the historical extent of the settlement. Consequently, I urge the Committee to endorse the proposed revision and to call upon the state party to adopt it forthwith...*

Rúbrica: impersonalización, subjuntivo (*be enlarged*), *hedging* (*I would argue*), verbo de postura (*urge, call upon*), coherencia y tono formal sostenido.

</details>

### Ejercicio 17 — Registro: formal, técnico o normativo

Clasifica cada extracto como formal, técnico o normativo.

1. *Carbon dating places the structure between 1180 and 1220.*
2. *All interventions shall be documented and reversible.*
3. *The site retains considerable authenticity and warrants further study.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Técnico** (datos especializados, sin obligación).
2. **Normativo** (*shall* = obligación).
3. **Formal** (expositivo, sin imperativo).

</details>

### Ejercicio 18 — Consolidación

Completa el párrafo institucional con una palabra por hueco.

> *In light of the accelerated (1)______ of the inscribed property, it is (2)______ that the site (3)______ inscribed on the List in Danger. The Committee (4)______ the state party to adopt a conservation plan; (5)______, it is essential that funding (6)______ sustained.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **deterioration** (nominalización)
2. **recommended** (pasiva / verbo de recomendación)
3. **be** (subjuntivo mandativo)
4. **urges** (verbo de postura)
5. **moreover** (conector formal de adición)
6. **remain** (subjuntivo mandativo)

</details>

### Ejercicio 19 — Reescribe el párrafo con argumento institucional

Eleva el registro del párrafo coloquial a un argumento institucional con pasiva, nominalización, *hedging*, verbo de postura y conector formal.

> *The old quarter is falling apart because nobody looks after it and people keep building too close. We really think UNESCO should put it on the danger list and make the government write a proper plan and give it money.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *Owing to the accelerated deterioration of the historic quarter — attributable in part to insufficient stewardship and unregulated encroachment — it is recommended that the property be inscribed on the List of World Heritage in Danger. The Committee urges the state party to adopt a comprehensive conservation plan; furthermore, it is essential that sustained funding be allocated so that further loss may be halted.*

Rúbrica: pasiva (*be inscribed, be allocated*), nominalización (*deterioration, stewardship, encroachment, inscription, funding, loss*), *hedging* (*attributable in part*), verbo de postura (*urges*), conector formal (*furthermore*), y registro impersonal coherente.

</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

- El **argumento institucional** es el discurso formal que una organización formula en su propio nombre; su marca es la impersonalidad, la autoridad colectiva y el registro cuidado.
- La **voz pasiva** desplaza el foco del agente al objeto o proceso, creando distancia y objetividad; sus variantes avanzadas incluyen pasiva con verbo de reporte (*it is believed that / ...is believed to*) y la **cláusula mandativa con subjuntivo** (*it is recommended that the site be monitored*).
- La **nominalización** (*preservation, deterioration, inscription, conservation, designation, encroachment*) densifica y abstrae el texto, pero debe dosificarse para no ahogar el sentido.
- Los **hedges** (*appears to, tends to, it is widely held that, it would seem that, there is a strong case for*) calibran la certeza y proyectan prudencia institucional.
- Los **verbos de postura** (*endorse, advocate, urge, call upon, pledge, undertake, caution against, affirm*) toman posición en nombre de la entidad; los **conectores formales** (*moreover, consequently, nevertheless, namely, thereafter*) encadenan el razonamiento; y los **modales** (*shall* normativo, *must* obligación, *should/ought to* recomendación) gradúan la fuerza de la directriz.
- El **vocabulario del patrimonio** (*heritage site, inscription, conservation, restoration, encroachment, authenticity, integrity, intangible heritage, stewardship, safeguard, designation, deterioration, buffer zone, state party, outstanding universal value*) y sus colocaciones (*meet the criteria, inscribe on the List, retain authenticity, combat encroachment, halt deterioration, allocate funding, submit a periodic report, adopt a conservation plan, designate a buffer zone*) permiten escribir textos institucionales autoritativos, formales y precisos sobre el patrimonio y la preservación.
