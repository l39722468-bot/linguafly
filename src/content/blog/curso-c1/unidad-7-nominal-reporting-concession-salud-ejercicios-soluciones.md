## Parte II — Ejercicios con soluciones

### Ejercicio 1 — That, whether o what

Completa.

1. ___ chronic stress affects sleep is well established.
2. ___ digital therapy will prove cost-effective remains uncertain.
3. ___ is most striking is the gap between symptom scores and recovery.
4. The study investigated ___ participants had improved.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **That** — presenta una proposición como sujeto.
2. **Whether** — introduce una incertidumbre.
3. **What** — significa “lo que” y focaliza la información posterior.
4. **whether** — funciona como objeto de *investigated*.

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Nominal clause como sujeto

Combina las frases.

1. The trial was not preregistered. This should give readers pause.
2. Early intervention can alter illness trajectories. Longitudinal studies support this.
3. Effects were heterogeneous across sites. This complicates the meta-analysis.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **That the trial was not preregistered should give readers pause.**
2. **That early intervention can alter illness trajectories is supported by longitudinal studies.**
3. **The fact that effects were heterogeneous across sites complicates the meta-analysis.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Orden indirecto

Corrige las preguntas integradas.

1. *The researchers asked whether had the participants improved.*
2. *The review examines how does social isolation affect wellbeing.*
3. *It is unclear what did the intervention change.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **The researchers asked whether the participants had improved.**
2. **The review examines how social isolation affects wellbeing.**
3. **It is unclear what the intervention changed.**

Una nominal clause mantiene el orden declarativo: sujeto antes del verbo.

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Reporting verbs

Elige **acknowledged, contended, conceded, disputed** o **maintained**.

1. The authors ___ that the sample was too small to support a definitive conclusion.
2. The review ___ that prevention should begin before symptoms become severe.
3. The researchers ___ that the effect was modest but clinically relevant.
4. The critics ___ that the correlation proved causation.
5. Despite the criticism, the team ___ that the intervention remained useful.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **acknowledged** — reconocieron una limitación.
2. **contended** — defendieron una posición formal.
3. **conceded** — admitieron un punto parcialmente desfavorable.
4. **disputed** — rechazaron una interpretación.
5. **maintained** — siguieron defendiendo su postura.

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Patrones de reporting

Completa con la forma correcta.

1. The committee recommended ___ (offer) the intervention early.
2. The clinicians insisted that every participant ___ (receive) follow-up care.
3. The therapist advised patients ___ (keep) a sleep diary.
4. The witness denied ___ (ignore) the warning.
5. The speaker claimed ___ (identify) a new risk factor.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **offering** o **that the intervention be offered**.
2. **receive** — subjuntivo mandativo; también *should receive*.
3. **to keep**.
4. **having ignored**.
5. **to have identified**.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Concesión con cláusula

Completa con **although, even though, while** o **whereas**.

1. ___ the sample was small, the pattern was consistent.
2. ___ symptom scores improved, functional recovery remained limited.
3. ___ the treatment was brief, participants reported less distress.
4. The digital group improved quickly, ___ the control group changed gradually.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Although/Even though**.
2. **While**.
3. **Although/Even though**.
4. **whereas**.

*While* puede señalar contraste entre dos resultados; *whereas* contrasta grupos o proposiciones de forma explícita.

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Despite e in spite of

Reescribe sin cambiar el significado.

1. Although the evidence was limited, the finding deserved attention.
2. Although participants experienced attrition, the analysis remained informative.
3. Although the intervention was expensive, it improved follow-up care.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Despite the limited evidence, the finding deserved attention.**
2. **Despite participant attrition, the analysis remained informative.**
3. **In spite of the intervention’s expense, it improved follow-up care.**

Después de *despite/in spite of* usamos un sustantivo, posesivo o gerundio, no una oración finita sin *the fact that*.

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — However y nevertheless

Puntúa correctamente.

1. *The cohort was small however the data were valuable.*
2. *The evidence was preliminary nevertheless the policy changed.*
3. *The treatment was demanding. However participants completed it.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **The cohort was small; however, the data were valuable.**
2. **The evidence was preliminary; nevertheless, the policy changed.**
3. **The treatment was demanding. However, participants completed it.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Vocabulario

Completa con **attrition, wellbeing, cognitive load, effect size** o **follow-up care**.

1. The study measured the ___ rather than relying only on symptom scores.
2. High ___ made the digital exercises difficult to complete.
3. Participant ___ reduced the final sample.
4. The researchers reported a small but meaningful ___ .
5. Patients received structured ___ after the intervention.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **wellbeing**; 2. **cognitive load**; 3. **attrition**; 4. **effect size**; 5. **follow-up care**.

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Reading

Lee el texto y responde.

> That chronic stress affects sleep is well established, but the relationship is not simple. A longitudinal cohort found that participants reporting poor sleep also showed lower emotional resilience six months later. The authors acknowledged that the study could not establish causation, since unmeasured social factors may have influenced both outcomes. What is most striking is that functional impairment persisted even when symptom scores had improved. Although the digital programme reduced reported distress, its effect size was modest. The researchers nevertheless contend that the intervention may be cost-effective because it can provide support to people who cannot attend regular appointments.

1. What is well established?
2. What did the cohort find?
3. What limitation did the authors acknowledge?
4. What remained even when symptom scores improved?
5. Why do the researchers defend the programme?

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **That chronic stress affects sleep.**
2. **Participants with poor sleep also showed lower emotional resilience six months later.**
3. **The study could not establish causation because unmeasured social factors may have affected both outcomes.**
4. **Functional impairment persisted.**
5. **They contend that it may be cost-effective because it can support people who cannot attend regular appointments.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 11 — Listening guiado

Guion: “The committee conceded that the intervention was demanding, yet it maintained that the benefits justified the effort. It recommended that participants receive additional support, although it disputed the claim that everyone needed weekly sessions. What matters most, the chair argued, is whether people can function more independently after treatment.”

Identifica dos reporting verbs, una concesión y una nominal clause.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Reporting verbs: **conceded, maintained, recommended, disputed, argued**. Concesión: **although it disputed the claim...** o **yet it maintained...**. Nominal clause: **whether people can function more independently after treatment**; también **What matters most** funciona como sujeto focalizado.

</details>

### Ejercicio 12 — Completa un argumento

Completa con una nominal clause adecuada.

1. ___ the trial was not preregistered should give readers pause.
2. The main question is ___ the benefits will last.
3. ___ the intervention changes in daily life is still unclear.
4. The fact ___ effects varied across sites complicates interpretation.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **That**; 2. **whether**; 3. **What**; 4. **that**.

Las respuestas construyen respectivamente una proposición como sujeto, una incertidumbre, una what-clause y el complemento de *the fact*.

</details>

### Ejercicio 13 — Reformulación académica

Reescribe con distancia y precisión: *The treatment works for everyone, even though the study only had twenty participants.*

<details><summary>Modelo</summary>

**Although the study included only twenty participants, the authors suggest that the treatment may benefit a wider population; however, the available evidence does not establish that it works for everyone.**

La versión distingue la afirmación de los autores de lo que los datos permiten concluir y evita una generalización absoluta.

</details>

### Ejercicio 14 — Writing

Escribe 150–180 palabras sobre: **Should digital mental-health interventions be offered as a first-line option?** Incluye una that-clause, una whether-clause, dos reporting verbs y dos estructuras de concesión.

<details><summary>Modelo</summary>

That digital interventions can improve access to support is increasingly recognised, particularly for people who live far from specialist services. Several reviews suggest that guided programmes may reduce mild distress, and one committee has recommended that patients receive professional follow-up when necessary. Nevertheless, whether these tools should be offered as a first-line option remains debatable. Although digital therapy can be cost-effective, it may not meet the needs of people experiencing severe symptoms or limited digital access. Despite the convenience of remote sessions, some users may require a relationship that is easier to establish face to face. The authors acknowledge that the evidence is heterogeneous, but they maintain that carefully designed programmes can complement, rather than replace, clinical care. It is therefore reasonable to expand access gradually while monitoring outcomes, protecting privacy and ensuring that users can move to more intensive support when required.

</details>

### Ejercicio 15 — Speaking

Habla durante un minuto sobre un hábito que puede mejorar el bienestar. Usa *What matters is...*, *although*, *despite* y un reporting verb.

<details><summary>Pista y modelo</summary>

**What matters is whether the habit can be maintained in daily life. Although short walks may seem simple, they can reduce cognitive load and support wellbeing. Despite the limited evidence for one individual, researchers suggest that regular movement may improve sleep. I would nevertheless acknowledge that people have different health needs.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 16 — Traducción controlada

Traduce al inglés.

1. Que los efectos fueran distintos complica el metaanálisis.
2. Los autores reconocieron que no podían demostrar causalidad.
3. A pesar de que la muestra era pequeña, el resultado merece atención.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **That the effects were different complicates the meta-analysis.**
2. **The authors acknowledged that they could not demonstrate causation.**
3. **Despite the fact that the sample was small, the result deserves attention.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 17 — Corrección avanzada

Corrige el párrafo.

> What the study shows it is uncertain. The researchers suggested participants to complete more sessions, despite the evidence was limited. They claimed that the intervention helped everyone, however they acknowledged the attrition was high.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**What the study shows is uncertain. The researchers suggested that participants complete more sessions, although the evidence was limited. They claimed that the intervention helped everyone; however, they acknowledged that attrition was high.**

Se elimina el *it* innecesario, se corrige el patrón de *suggest*, se cambia *despite* por *although* ante una cláusula y se puntúa *however* correctamente.

</details>

### Ejercicio 18 — Mini-test final

Completa el texto.

> ___ early psychological intervention can alter illness trajectories is supported by longitudinal cohorts. The committee ___ that the evidence remains heterogeneous, ___ it recommends that services expand access. ___ matters most is whether patients receive appropriate follow-up care. It remains unclear ___ the benefits will last, and the researchers ___ that further data are needed.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**That; acknowledges; although; What; whether; maintain/contend.** Una versión completa es: **That early psychological intervention can alter illness trajectories is supported by longitudinal cohorts. The committee acknowledges that the evidence remains heterogeneous, although it recommends that services expand access. What matters most is whether patients receive appropriate follow-up care. It remains unclear whether the benefits will last, and the researchers maintain that further data are needed.**

</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

Usa **that-clauses** para presentar proposiciones, **whether-clauses** para expresar incertidumbre y **what-clauses** para focalizar “lo que” importa o sigue sin explicarse. Usa **the fact that** cuando quieras convertir un hecho explícito en el sujeto o complemento de la oración.

Elige los reporting verbs por su significado y por su patrón: *acknowledge that*, *recommend doing* o *recommend that someone do*, *advise someone to do*, *deny having done* y *claim to have done*. No combines estructuras por traducción literal.

Para la concesión, recuerda la diferencia formal: **although/even though + clause**, **despite/in spite of + noun o gerund**, y **however/nevertheless + oración independiente**. Estas formas te permiten admitir una limitación sin abandonar una conclusión matizada.

En un texto sobre salud y mente, la precisión gramatical debe ir acompañada de prudencia. Una muestra pequeña, un efecto modesto o una posible variable de confusión no invalidan necesariamente un estudio, pero sí condicionan lo que podemos afirmar. El inglés C1 te permite expresar exactamente esa relación entre evidencia, postura y límite.
