### Cómo usar este cuaderno

Responde primero y comprueba después. La explicación está pensada para el estudiante hispanohablante.

## Parte II — Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1
Calibra la fuerza de la evidencia reformulando con un hedge: "Sugar causes diabetes."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

High added-sugar intake is associated with an increased risk of type 2 diabetes, though the evidence is not entirely uniform.
</details>

### Ejercicio 2
Completa la expresión de consenso: "There is ______ agreement that vegetables are beneficial, though the optimal quantity is less clear."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

There is broad agreement that vegetables are beneficial, though the optimal quantity is less clear.
</details>

### Ejercicio 3
Formula una recomendación prudente con contexto: "reduce processed meat."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

For most adults, it may be advisable to limit processed meat, though what counts as a reasonable amount varies from person to person.
</details>

### Ejercicio 4
Distingue "association" de "causation" en una frase sobre un estudio observacional.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

The study found an association between coffee consumption and reduced liver-disease risk, but as an observational design, it cannot establish causation.
</details>

### Ejercicio 5
Sustituye el término sensacionalista por uno riguroso: "Blueberries are a superfood."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Blueberries are a nutrient-dense food whose consumption has been associated with certain health benefits, though the term 'superfood' has no strict scientific definition.
</details>

### Ejercicio 6
Completa la collocation de macronutrientes: "______ carbohydrates are associated with more stable blood-sugar responses than refined ones."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Complex carbohydrates are associated with more stable blood-sugar responses than refined ones.
</details>

### Ejercicio 7
Cita un estudio con su tipo y limitación: un ensayo controlado pequeño sobre ayuno intermitente.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

A small, short-term randomised controlled trial suggested that intermittent fasting may aid weight management, though the sample was limited and the findings await confirmation in larger studies.
</details>

### Ejercicio 8
Reformula "the Mediterranean diet prevents heart disease" respetando la evidencia agregada.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

The Mediterranean dietary pattern has been associated with a reduced risk of cardiovascular disease in numerous observational and interventional studies.
</details>

### Ejercicio 9
Completa con un verbo de recomendación graduado: "Current guidelines ______ limiting added sugar to well below the daily recommended ceiling."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Current guidelines recommend limiting added sugar to well below the daily recommended ceiling.
</details>

### Ejercicio 10
Expresa la variabilidad individual con la estructura adecuada: "cada persona responde distinto a los carbohidratos."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Individual responses to carbohydrates vary considerably, so what works for one person may not suit another.
</details>

### Ejercicio 11
Reformula con un hedge de controversia: "Intermittent fasting is the best diet."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

The evidence on intermittent fasting is mixed but leans towards modest benefits for some people, though experts remain divided on whether it outperforms other approaches.
</details>

### Ejercicio 12
Completa la tríada de pilares del estilo de vida: "Lifestyle factors —diet, ______ and ______— collectively shape long-term health outcomes."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Lifestyle factors —diet, physical activity and sleep— collectively shape long-term health outcomes.
</details>

### Ejercicio 13
Formula una recomendación condicional para alguien con colesterol alto.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

For those with elevated cholesterol, it may be worth discussing saturated-fat intake with a clinician before making major dietary changes.
</details>

### Ejercicio 14
Calibra el tamaño del efecto con un adjetivo adecuado: "Processed meat is associated with a ______ increased risk of colorectal cancer."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Processed meat is associated with a modestly increased risk of colorectal cancer.
</details>

### Ejercicio 15
Reformula "detox cleans work" con distanciamiento crítico.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

So-called 'detox' regimes lack robust evidence; the body's own organs —the liver and kidneys— already perform that function continuously.
</details>

### Ejercicio 16
Completa con la expresión de evidencia provisional: "The jury is ______ on whether very-low-carb diets outperform balanced ones over the long term."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

The jury is still out on whether very-low-carb diets outperform balanced ones over the long term.
</details>

### Ejercicio 17
Cita una revisión sistemática con prudencia: la fibra reduce el riesgo cardiovascular.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

A meta-analysis of several cohort studies indicates that higher dietary fibre intake is associated with a reduced risk of cardiovascular disease, though the optimal intake remains debated.
</details>

### Ejercicio 18
Reformula "supplements are necessary" con una recomendación prudente y condicional.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Unless you have a diagnosed deficiency, supplementation is generally unnecessary; for most healthy adults, a varied diet provides adequate nutrients.
</details>

### Ejercicio 19
Redacta una frase de consenso cauteloso que combine certeza y humedad: ultra-procesados.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Few would dispute that ultra-processed foods should be limited; the real debate is how, pragmatically, to do so without making healthy eating feel punitive.
</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

En esta unidad has aprendido a presentar evidencia nutricional con prudencia usando hedges y expresiones graduadas como "the evidence suggests", "a growing body of research indicates", y "findings are mixed but lean towards". Has practicado la gramática de la recomendación prudente con modalidad y condicionales contextualizados, has distinguido consenso de controversia y de especulación, y has evitado el sensacionalismo sustituyendo términos como "superfood" por lenguaje riguroso. Has trabajado el léxico de macronutrientes y micronutrientes, los patrones dietéticos frente a alimentos aislados, y la gramática del riesgo con adjetivos que calibran el tamaño del efecto. Has aprendido a citar estudios con precisión metodológica, a reconocer los límites de la evidencia observacional y a formular recomendaciones individualizadas que respetan la variabilidad metabólica. En la próxima unidad, la 40, cerrarás este bloque temático con la longevidad y el envejecimiento, donde el tono respetuoso y el manejo de datos serán los ejes centrales.
