## Parte II — Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1
Define el término técnico con una aposición entre guiones: "The amygdala governs the fear response."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

The amygdala —an almond-shaped cluster deep in the temporal lobe— governs the fear response.
</details>

### Ejercicio 2
Introduce una analogía controlada con su matiz para explicar la neuroplasticidad.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Neuroplasticity is, broadly speaking, the brain's ability to rewire itself. Think of it as a network of footpaths: the more you walk one, the clearer it becomes. That said, the real picture is rather more complex than the analogy suggests.
</details>

### Ejercicio 3
Elige el verbo de función cognitiva correcto: "The hippocampus ______ new memories during sleep."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

The hippocampus consolidates new memories during sleep.
</details>

### Ejercicio 4
Completa distinguiendo brain, mind o cognition: "______ declines with age, but the rate varies enormously between individuals."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Cognition declines with age, but the rate varies enormously between individuals.
</details>

### Ejercicio 5
Reformula de forma accesible con "to put it simply": "The brain prunes underused synapses during sleep."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

The brain prunes underused synapses during sleep. To put it simply, it clears away the connections you no longer use, which is thought to make the remaining ones more efficient.
</details>

### Ejercicio 6
Añade un hedge para evitar la sobreinterpretación: "Sleep consolidates memory."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Evidence suggests that sleep plays a key role in consolidating memory.
</details>

### Ejercicio 7
Une cada neurotransmisor con su efecto asociado: (dopamine / serotonin / cortisol / GABA) + (reward and motivation / mood and wellbeing / stress response / inhibition and calm).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

dopamine — reward and motivation; serotonin — mood and wellbeing; cortisol — stress response; GABA — inhibition and calm.
</details>

### Ejercicio 8
Reescribe la frase para evitar el determinismo neurologicista: "My brain made me do it."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

While brain chemistry plays a role in behaviour, saying 'my brain made me do it' is, at best, an oversimplification that ignores the wider picture.
</details>

### Ejercicio 9
Completa la collocation del aprendizaje: "______ repetition is one of the most effective ways to consolidate what you've studied."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Spaced repetition is one of the most effective ways to consolidate what you've studied.
</details>

### Ejercicio 10
Describe el mecanismo con un verbo de causalidad graduada: "Chronic stress → inflammation → depression."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Chronic stress drives inflammation, which in turn is thought to contribute to depression.
</details>

### Ejercicio 11
Cita una fuente sin sobreinterpretarla: un estudio de 2020 sugiere que la meditación cambia la estructura cerebral.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

A 2020 study suggests that long-term meditation may be associated with changes in brain structure, though the findings are preliminary and await replication.
</details>

### Ejercicio 12
Completa con la estructura cerebral correcta: "The ______ handles planning, decision-making and impulse control."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

The prefrontal cortex handles planning, decision-making and impulse control.
</details>

### Ejercicio 13
Reformula "the glymphatic system clears waste during deep sleep" con un marcador de reformulación y una analogía.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

During deep sleep, the glymphatic system clears metabolic waste from the brain. In other words, think of it as a night-time cleaning crew that flushes out the debris left by a day's activity.
</details>

### Ejercicio 14
Expresa el matiz de los límites de la analogía cerebro-ordenador.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

The brain is often compared to a computer, but the analogy only goes so far: unlike a computer, the brain is constantly rewiring itself in response to experience.
</details>

### Ejercicio 15
Completa la collocation del sueño: "After a week of short nights, she was severely ______ and struggling to concentrate."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

After a week of short nights, she was severely sleep-deprived and struggling to concentrate.
</details>

### Ejercicio 16
Reescribe con un hedge de evidencia provisional: "Meditation rewires the brain."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Preliminary research suggests that sustained meditation may be associated with measurable changes in brain structure, though the field is still young.
</details>

### Ejercicio 17
Explica "working memory" con definición entre comas y reformulación accesible.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Working memory, the mental workspace that holds and manipulates information in the short term, is, to put it simply, the part of your mind you use when you hold a phone number in your head just long enough to dial it.
</details>

### Ejercicio 18
Completa con la estructura causal adecuada (gradúa la fuerza): "Genetics ______ a role in temperament, but environment shapes it further."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Genetics plays a role in temperament, but environment shapes it further.
</details>

### Ejercicio 19
Redacta una frase divulgativa sobre el sueño REM que combine término técnico, definición y matiz.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

REM sleep —the stage associated with vivid dreaming— is thought to play a key role in processing emotions and consolidating certain types of memory, though precisely how remains a matter of ongoing research.
</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

En esta unidad has aprendido a manejar terminología neurocientífica esencial —estructuras cerebrales, neurotransmisores, procesos cognitivos— y a presentarla de forma accesible mediante aposiciones entre guiones, analogías controladas y reformulaciones con marcadores como "in other words" y "to put it simply". Has trabajado los verbos de función cognitiva con sus sujetos correctos, has distinguido "brain", "mind" y "cognition", y has practicado la gramática de la causalidad graduada para describir mecanismos sin sobreinterpretar. Has incorporado hedges para presentar la evidencia como provisional, has evitado el determinismo neurologicista y has calibrado el registro divulgativo entre lo académico y lo infantil. En la próxima unidad, la 39, aplicarás estas destrezas al ámbito de la nutrición y el estilo de vida, donde la presentación prudente de la evidencia y la formulación de recomendaciones meditadas serán el centro del trabajo.
