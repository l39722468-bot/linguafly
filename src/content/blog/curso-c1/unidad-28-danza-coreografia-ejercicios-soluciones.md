## Parte II — Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Relativo restrictivo vs. explicativo

Añade o quita comas según corresponda y elige *that* o *which*.

1. The dancer who leads sets the tempo. (delimita)
2. The lead who trained in Paris sets the tempo. (información adicional)
3. The phrase that closes the piece dissolves into stillness. (delimita)
4. The duet which unfolds as a single breath builds slowly. (información adicional)
5. The dancers who were tired sat down. (solo los cansados)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The dancer **who** leads sets the tempo. (restrictiva, sin comas; *that* también válido)
2. The lead**,** **who** trained in Paris**,** sets the tempo. (explicativa, con comas; solo *who/which*, no *that*)
3. The phrase **that** closes the piece dissolves into stillness. (restrictiva, sin comas)
4. The duet**,** **which** unfolds as a single breath**,** builds slowly. (explicativa, con comas; no *that*)
5. The dancers **who** were tired sat down. (restrictiva, sin comas: solo los cansados se sentaron).

La coma distingue restrictiva (delimita) de explicativa (añade información prescindible); *that* solo vale en restrictivas.

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — whose / whom / preposición + which

Completa con el relativo adecuado.

1. The dancer ___ control never wavers. (posesivo)
2. The choreographer ___ the critics admire. (objeto formal)
3. The phrase ___ which the piece hinges. (preposición + relativo)
4. The ensemble, ___ motif recurs throughout, anchors the work. (explicativa posesiva)
5. The critic by ___ the piece was reviewed praised the partnering. (objeto con preposición)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The dancer **whose** control never wavers.
2. The choreographer **whom** the critics admire.
3. The phrase **on** which the piece hinges.
4. The ensemble, **whose** motif recurs throughout, anchors the work.
5. The critic by **whom** the piece was reviewed praised the partnering.

*Whose* es posesivo (vale para personas y cosas); *whom* es objeto formal; la preposición adelantada (*on which, by whom*) es marca de C1.

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Cláusulas de relativo reducidas

Reduce el relativo a participio o adjetivo.

1. The dancer who is leading sets the tempo.
2. The phrase that closes the piece dissolves.
3. The corps de ballet, which is arranged in a semicircle, advances.
4. The lift that suspends gravity is the climax.
5. The solo, which was performed with restraint, lingered.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The dancer **leading** sets the tempo.
2. The phrase **closing** the piece dissolves.
3. The corps de ballet, **arranged in a semicircle,** advances.
4. The lift **suspending gravity** is the climax.
5. The solo, **performed with restraint,** lingered.

La reducción elimina el relativo y el auxiliar, dejando el participio; densifica el texto, propio del registro expositivo avanzado.

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Metáfora del movimiento

Asocia el movimiento a una metáfora precisa controlada.

1. The solo develops gradually like one continuous exhalation. → The solo ___
2. The corps de ballet moves as if it were one body. → The corps ___
3. The lift makes gravity seem to pause. → The lift ___
4. The duet is like a dialogue made of gestures. → The duet ___
5. The phrase gathers energy and then releases it. → The phrase ___

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The solo **unfolds as a single sustained breath.**
2. The corps de ballet **moves as a single organism.**
3. The lift **seems to suspend gravity itself.**
4. The duet **reads like a conversation conducted in gestures.**
5. The phrase **builds to a surge and then dissolves into stillness.**

La metáfora C1 es precisa y se ancla en un recurso técnico (*breath* → control del adagio; *organism* → conjunto unificado); no es ornamento, sino interpretación.

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Verbos de movimiento y transformación

Sustituye *moves* por el verbo de movimiento preciso.

1. The phrase develops gradually. → The phrase ___
2. The ensemble gathers energy suddenly. → The ensemble ___
3. The solo ends by becoming stillness. → The solo ___
4. The group comes together in the centre. → The group ___
5. The tempo gets faster. → The tempo ___

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The phrase **unfolds.**
2. The ensemble **surges.**
3. The solo **dissolves into stillness.**
4. The group **converges.**
5. The tempo **accelerates.**

*Unfold* (desarrollo gradual), *surge* (acceso de energía), *dissolve* (disolución), *converge* (convergencia), *accelerate* (aceleración) nombran el modo del movimiento con precisión.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Vocabulario técnico de la danza (definiciones)

Empareja cada término con su definición.

Términos: *adagio / arabesque / partnering / corps de ballet / motif / plié*
1. Secuencia lenta y sostenida que prueba el control y el equilibrio.
2. Figura en la que una pierna se extiende hacia atrás mientras la otra sostiene el peso.
3. Trabajo de pareja que implica lifts, apoyo y contacto.
4. Frase o gesto recurrente que estructura la coreografía.
5. Flexión de rodillas, base de gran parte del vocabulario clásico.
6. Conjunto que se mueve coordinado, distinto de los solistas.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **adagio** — Secuencia lenta y sostenida que prueba el control y el equilibrio.
2. **arabesque** — Figura en la que una pierna se extiende hacia atrás mientras la otra sostiene el peso.
3. **partnering** — Trabajo de pareja que implica lifts, apoyo y contacto.
4. **motif** — Frase o gesto recurrente que estructura la coreografía.
5. **plié** — Flexión de rodillas, base de gran parte del vocabulario clásico.
6. **corps de ballet** — Conjunto que se mueve coordinado, distinto de los solistas.

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Adjetivos evaluativos de la danza

Sustituye el adjetivo B2 por el C1 adecuado.

1. The movement was smooth and continuous. → It was ___.
2. The dancing was stiff and bad. → It was ___ and ___.
3. The turn was full of skill. → It was ___.
4. The phrase was done without care. → It was ___.
5. The presence was light and otherworldly. → It was ___.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. It was **seamless** (o *fluid*).
2. It was **wooden** (o *laboured*) and **perfunctory**.
3. It was **virtuosic**.
4. It was **telegraphed** (o *perfunctory*).
5. It was **ethereal**.

Los adjetivos C1 nombran la cualidad del movimiento, no solo si gusta.

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Colocaciones del discurso dancístico

Completa con la colocación adecuada.

1. The solo ___ a phrase for an impossible length. (sostiene)
2. The piece ___ to a climax. (construye hasta)
3. The movement ___ into stillness. (se disuelve)
4. The ensemble ___ as one. (se mueve unido)
5. The whole piece ___ on a single gesture. (depende de)
6. The dancer ___ a balance. (mantiene el equilibrio)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The solo **sustains** a phrase.
2. The piece **builds** to a climax.
3. The movement **dissolves** into stillness.
4. The ensemble **moves** as one.
5. The whole piece **hinges** on a single gesture.
6. The dancer **holds** a balance.

*Sustain a phrase, build to a climax, dissolve into stillness, move as one, hinge on, hold a balance* son colocaciones consolidadas de la crítica de danza.

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Corrige los errores

Cada frase tiene un error de registro. Corrígelo.

1. The duet, that unfolds as a breath, builds slowly. (relativo en explicativa)
2. The dancer who leads, sets the tempo. (coma innecesaria en restrictiva)
3. The dance was good and beautiful. (demasiado genérico)
4. The dancer who is leading is the lead. (relativo no reducido)
5. The phrase moves nice. (error de categoría)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The duet**,** **which** unfolds as a breath**,** builds slowly. (las explicativas no admiten *that*; solo *which/who*).
2. The dancer **who leads** sets the tempo. (sin coma: la restrictiva no se separa con comas).
3. The dance was **seamless and ethereal** (adjetivos C1 concretos).
4. The dancer **leading** is the lead (relativo reducido a participio).
5. The phrase moves **nicely** (adverbio) o, mejor, *the phrase unfolds smoothly* (verbo preciso + adverbio).

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Transformación (CAE-style)

Reescribe con la palabra dada.

1. The dancer's control never wavers. (WHOSE) → The dancer ___
2. The solo unfolds as a single breath and it is devastating. (WHICH) → The solo, ___
3. It is the restraint of the phrasing that makes the arabesque devastating. (WHAT) → What ___
4. The phrase on which the piece hinges is the adagio. (HINGES) → The piece ___
5. The dancer who is leading sets the tempo. (LEADING) → The dancer ___

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The dancer **whose** control never wavers.
2. The solo**,** **which unfolds as a single breath,** is devastating.
3. **What** makes the arabesque devastating **is** the restraint of the phrasing.
4. The piece **hinges** on the phrase that is the adagio (o *hinges on the adagio*).
5. The dancer **leading** sets the tempo.

Cada reescritura conserva el significado usando el relativo o la reducción objetivo.

</details>

### Ejercicio 11 — Vocabulario técnico (gap-fill)

Completa con: *tempo / lift / count / ensemble / alignment / allegro*.

3. The ___ of eight dancers moved as a single organism.
4. The ___ accelerated sharply in the final phrase.
5. The dancers waited for the ___ before the leap.
6. The ___ at the climax seemed to suspend gravity.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The **allegro** section showcased rapid jumps and turns.
2. The dancer maintained perfect **alignment** throughout the adagio.
3. The **ensemble** of eight dancers moved as a single organism.
4. The **tempo** accelerated sharply in the final phrase.
5. The dancers waited for the **count** before the leap.
6. The **lift** at the climax seemed to suspend gravity.

</details>

### Ejercicio 13 — Reading

Lee el fragmento y responde.

> *The solo, which unfolds as a single sustained breath, builds from a whisper of movement to a surge that seems to suspend gravity itself. The dancer, whose control never wavers, turns the body into a metaphor for resistance; the adagio, measured and grounded, earns its climactic lift not through virtuosity alone but through the patience of everything that precedes it. It is the restraint of the phrasing —the refusal to telegraph the climax— that makes the final arabesque, held a beat longer than expected, so devastating.*

1. ¿Qué tipo de cláusula de relativo es *which unfolds as a single sustained breath* y cómo lo sabes?
2. ¿Qué metáfora interpreta el solo al inicio y cuál lo cierra?
3. ¿Qué relativo posesivo describe al bailarín?
4. ¿Qué cleft relaciona la contención con el efecto del arabesque final?
5. ¿Por qué el lift final es efectivo según el texto?

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Es **explicativa**: va entre comas y añade información prescindible sobre un antecedente ya identificado (el solo); admite *which*, no *that*.
2. Inicia con *a whisper of movement* (movimiento susurrado, mínimo) y cierra con *a surge that seems to suspend gravity* (acceso que suspende la gravedad): metáforas que interpretan la graduación de la energía.
3. *whose control never wavers*: relativo posesivo *whose*.
4. El cleft *it is the restraint of the phrasing ... that makes the final arabesque ... so devastating*.
5. Porque se gana *through the patience of everything that precedes it*, no solo por la virtuosidad: la contención previa hace merecedor al clímax.

</details>

### Ejercicio 14 — Listening (paráfrasis)

Imagina que escuchas a un crítico decir: *"It's the patience of the phrasing that gets you; the dancer holds everything back until the lift, and then the whole room exhales. I've rarely seen a body so in command of its own stillness."* Parafrasea en una frase con un relativo explicativo y un cleft.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *The solo, which holds everything back until the climactic lift, is defined by the patience of its phrasing; it is that restraint, more than any display of virtuosity, that makes the body's command of its own stillness so devastating.*

Elementos: relativo explicativo (*which holds everything back*), cleft (*it is that restraint... that*) y registro evaluativo (*patience, restraint, devastating*).

</details>

### Ejercicio 15 — Writing

Escribe una reseña de danza breve (120-150 palabras) que incluya: un relativo explicativo, un relativo con *whose*, una metáfora del movimiento y un cleft.

### Ejercicio 16 — Speaking (monólogo)

Habla durante dos minutos valorando una pieza de danza que hayas visto, usando al menos: un relativo explicativo, una metáfora del movimiento y dos adjetivos evaluativos C1. Grábate y comprueba que describes el movimiento e interpretas su sentido, no que cuentas una historia.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo de respuesta oral:

*The solo, which unfolds as a single sustained breath, is the heart of the evening. The dancer, whose control is extraordinary, holds everything back — there's a real patience to the phrasing. The adagio is measured and grounded, and it builds to a lift that seems to suspend gravity. What gets me is the restraint: she refuses to telegraph the climax, so when the final arabesque comes, held a beat longer than you expect, it's devastating. I'd call it a seamless, ethereal performance. The ensemble, by contrast, moves as one organism — the partnering is virtuosic without ever showing off. If anything, the second piece feels a bit laboured, but the first is a masterclass in what you can say with stillness.*

Criterios de la rúbrica: relativo explicativo (*which unfolds as a single sustained breath*), metáfora (*suspend gravity, moves as one organism, say with stillness*), adjetivos C1 (*measured, grounded, seamless, ethereal, virtuosic, laboured*) y descripción del movimiento con interpretación (*the restraint makes the arabesque devastating*) en lugar de relato.

</details>

### Ejercicio 17 — Registro: reseña vs. análisis coreográfico

Reescribe la reseña en estilo de análisis coreográfico (más nominalizado, sin primera persona, más abstracto).

Reseña: *The solo, which unfolds as a single breath, is devastating. The dancer's control is great and the lift seems to suspend gravity. It is the restraint that makes it work.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo de análisis: *The solo's sustained phrasing, whose internal logic hinges on a gradual accumulation of tempo, produces a climactic effect that reframes the piece's governing motif; the dancer's unwavering control generates the illusion of suspended gravity, and it is the restraint of the phrasing —the deliberate withholding of the climax— that accounts for the final arabesque's devastating impact.*

Cambios: eliminación de primera persona, paso a nominalizaciones (*the solo's sustained phrasing, the dancer's unwavering control, the restraint of the phrasing*) y registro más abstracto (*reframes the piece's governing motif, accounts for... impact*).

</details>

Eleva el registro del párrafo neutro con un relativo explicativo, una metáfora del movimiento y un cleft.

> *The dance was nice. The dancer moved well and the lift was impressive. The ending was good because of the control.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *The solo, which unfolds as a single sustained breath, builds from a whisper of movement to a lift that seems to suspend gravity itself; it is the restraint of the phrasing —the dancer's refusal to telegraph the climax— that makes the final arabesque, held a beat longer than expected, so devastating, and the whole, seamless and ethereal, lingers long after the curtain.*

</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

- Escribir sobre danza exige **traducir un lenguaje no verbal a uno verbal** sin traicionarlo: describir el movimiento con precisión técnica e interpretarlo con metáfora controlada.
- Las **cláusulas de relativo restrictivas** delimitan el antecedente (sin comas, admiten *that*); las **explicativas** añaden información prescindible (con comas, solo *which/who*); una coma cambia el significado. Los relativos avanzados (*whose, whom, preposición + which*) y las **reducciones** a participio densifican el registro.
- La **metáfora del movimiento** es precisa y controlada, no ornamental: asocia el gesto a una imagen que ilumina su sentido (*unfolds as a single sustained breath, reads like a conversation conducted in gestures*) y se ancla en un recurso técnico.
- Los **verbos de movimiento** nombran el modo de la transformación (*unfold, surge, dissolve, converge, accelerate, suspend*); el **léxico evaluativo** nombra la cualidad del movimiento (*seamless, fluid, grounded, ethereal, virtuosic, laboured, wooden, telegraphed*).
- El **vocabulario técnico** (*choreography, arabesque, pirouette, plié, adagio, allegro, partnering, ensemble, corps de ballet, motif, count, tempo, lift, alignment*) y sus colocaciones (*sustain a phrase, build to a climax, dissolve into stillness, move as one, hinge on, hold a balance*) constituyen el sustrato técnico de toda reseña precisa.
