### Cómo usar este cuaderno

Responde primero y comprueba después. La explicación está pensada para el estudiante hispanohablante.

## Parte II — Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Modificadores del comparativo

Añade un intensificador o atenuador adecuado.

1. Sales grew ______ faster than costs. (intenso)
2. Margins were ______ higher than last quarter. (apenas perceptible)
3. The new system is ______ more efficient than the legacy one. (notable)
4. Domestic growth was ______ positive. (muy modesto)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Sales grew **considerably / far / substantially** faster than costs.
2. Margins were **marginally / slightly** higher than last quarter.
3. The new system is **far / substantially / much** more efficient than the legacy one.
4. Domestic growth was **only marginally / slightly** positive.

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — as...as reforzado

Reescribe con una forma reforzada de *as...as*.

1. Domestic growth is not as volatile as international. (casi nada)
2. The churn rate is not as high as feared. (ni mucho menos)
3. The cloud platform is as efficient as the market leader. (el doble)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Domestic growth is **not nearly as** volatile as international.
2. The churn rate is **nothing like as** high as feared.
3. The cloud platform is **twice as** efficient as the market leader.

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — La estructura the more... the more...

Completa con *the + comparativo*.

1. ______ we automate, ______ we scale. (more / fast)
2. ______ we invest in R&D, ______ it becomes to stay competitive. (less / hard)
3. ______ we diversify suppliers, ______ our single-point risk. (more / low)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **The more** we automate, **the faster** we scale.
2. **The less** we invest in R&D, **the harder** it becomes to stay competitive.
3. **The more** we diversify suppliers, **the lower** our single-point risk.

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Superlativos reforzados

Reescribe añadiendo refuerzo.

1. Cloud is the most profitable segment. (by far)
2. It is a fast-growing market. (one of the)
3. Enterprise is the largest contributor. (the single)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Cloud is **by far the most** profitable segment.
2. It is **one of the fastest-growing** markets in the region.
3. Enterprise is **the single largest** contributor.

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — whereas / by contrast

Reescribe usando *whereas* (cláusula) o *by contrast* (punto y coma + cláusula nueva).

1. Domestic growth stagnated. International revenue surged.
2. Costs rose. Margins widened thanks to efficiency gains.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Domestic growth stagnated, **whereas** international revenue surged. (o: Domestic growth stagnated; **by contrast**, international revenue surged.)
2. Costs rose, **whereas** margins widened thanks to efficiency gains. (o: Costs rose; **by contrast**, margins widened thanks to efficiency gains.)

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Concesivas (although / despite)

Reescribe con *although* o *despite*.

1. The market contracted, but we gained share. → ______ the market contracted, we gained share.
2. There was a downturn, but margins held. → ______ the downturn, margins held.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Although** the market contracted, we gained share.
2. **Despite** the downturn, margins held.

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Conectores de causa y consecuencia

Inserta el conector adecuado (owing to / consequently / in order to / given).

1. ______ supply-chain disruptions, revenue fell.
2. Costs rose; ______, margins narrowed.
3. We diversified suppliers ______ reduce single-point risk.
4. ______ the volatility, a cautious forecast is warranted.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Owing to** supply-chain disruptions, revenue fell.
2. Costs rose; **consequently**, margins narrowed.
3. We diversified suppliers **in order to** reduce single-point risk.
4. **Given** the volatility, a cautious forecast is warranted.

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Corrige los errores

Cada frase tiene un error. Corrígelo.

1. Sales are more than last year.
2. We have less customers than Q1.
3. More we automate, faster we scale.
4. Sales are like way up.
5. Domestic stagnated whereas by contrast international surged.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Sales are **considerably higher** than last year. (modificador graduado)
2. We have **fewer customers** than Q1. (contable → fewer)
3. **The more** we automate, **the faster** we scale.
4. Sales **rose substantially**. (registro formal)
5. Domestic stagnated, **whereas** international surged. (un solo conector: whereas o by contrast, no ambos)

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Transformación (CAE-style)

Reescribe empezando con las palabras dadas.

1. "International revenue grew faster than domestic." → *Domestic growth was...*
2. "Because of the downturn, margins narrowed." → *Owing to...*
3. "We gained share although the market contracted." → *Despite...*
4. "Investment should be reallocated to high-growth territories." → *It is recommended that...*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Domestic growth was **considerably slower than** international revenue.
2. **Owing to the downturn,** margins narrowed.
3. **Despite the contraction of the market,** we gained share.
4. **It is recommended that** investment **be reallocated** to high-growth territories.

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Vocabulario de tendencias

Relaciona cada término con su definición.

1. Headwind   a. Tasa de abandono de clientes.
2. Churn      b. Factor adverso que frena el crecimiento.
3. Tailwind   c. Resistente, que se recupera bien.
4. Resilient  d. Factor favorable que impulsa el crecimiento.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1-b, 2-a, 3-d, 4-c.

</details>

### Ejercicio 11 — Colocaciones de informe

Completa con el verbo adecuado (surged / narrowed / outperform / offset / weather).

1. Revenue ______ 14% year-on-year.
2. Rising costs caused margins to ______.
3. We managed to ______ the downturn without layoffs.
4. International gains ______ the domestic shortfall.
5. We continued to ______ the market benchmark.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Revenue **surged** 14% year-on-year.
2. Rising costs caused margins to **narrow**.
3. We managed to **weather** the downturn without layoffs.
4. International gains **offset** the domestic shortfall.
5. We continued to **outperform** the market benchmark.

</details>

### Ejercicio 12 — fewer vs. less

Elige la forma correcta.

1. We had ______ churn events than Q1. (contable)
2. There was ______ volatility than expected. (incontable)
3. ______ customers upgraded this quarter. (contable)
4. The upgrade generated ______ revenue. (incontable)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **fewer** churn events (contable)
2. **less** volatility (incontable)
3. **fewer** customers (contable)
4. **less** revenue (incontable)

</details>

### Ejercicio 13 — Reading

Lee el extracto y responde.

> *Q3 revenue climbed 14% YoY, considerably outpacing the sector average of 6%. The more aggressively the firm expanded into emerging markets, the more its blended acquisition cost fell, so that operating margins widened by 230 basis points. Domestic growth, by contrast, remained only marginally positive. Although headwinds persist in supply, international momentum is expected to offset the domestic shortfall.*

1. By how much did revenue and the sector average grow? What does the comparison show?
2. What proportional relationship is described, and with what structure?
3. Identify one concesiva and one contrast connector.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Revenue climbed 14% YoY; the sector average, 6%. The comparison shows the firm outperformed the sector by a considerable margin (more than double).
2. The more aggressively it expanded into emerging markets, the more its blended acquisition cost fell. Structure: *the more... the more...*
3. Concesiva: *although headwinds persist*. Contrast: *by contrast*.

</details>

### Ejercicio 14 — Listening (paráfrasis)

Parafrasea esta declaración del CFO en registro de informe.

> "We made more money this quarter, way more than the market. Going abroad really helped bring costs down. Home market is basically flat though. Supply issues are still there but we'll cover the gap."

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *Q3 revenue grew considerably faster than the sector average. International expansion materially lowered blended acquisition costs; by contrast, domestic growth remained only marginally positive. Although supply headwinds persist, international momentum is expected to offset the domestic shortfall.*

</details>

### Ejercicio 15 — Writing

Escribe el cuerpo de un informe (80–100 palabras) comparando dos productos (Producto A y Producto B). Usa al menos: un comparativo con modificador, *the more... the more...*, un conector de contraste y un conector de consecuencia.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *Product A outperformed Product B considerably in Q3, generating 31% more revenue at a fraction of the support cost. The more aggressively we promoted Product A through targeted campaigns, the faster its adoption curve steepened, so that its margins widened by 180 basis points. Product B, by contrast, remained stagnant amid pricing pressure and rising churn. Consequently, it is recommended that marketing spend be reallocated toward Product A, and that a discontinuation review of Product B be commissioned for Q4.* (88 palabras)

</details>

### Ejercicio 16 — Speaking (monólogo)

Prepara un monólogo de 2 minutos presentando los resultados trimestrales a un comité, usando comparativos y registro de informe.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo de inicio: *Turning to Q3 performance, revenue grew fourteen percent year-on-year, considerably outpacing the sector average of six percent. The more aggressively we expanded into emerging markets, the more our blended acquisition cost fell, so that operating margins widened by two hundred and thirty basis points. Domestic growth, by contrast, remained only marginally positive, stagnant amid pricing pressure. Although supply headwinds persist, international momentum is expected to offset the domestic shortfall; consequently, we recommend reallocating investment toward high-growth territories...*

</details>

### Ejercicio 17 — Registro: informe, memo o email

Clasifica cada extracto.

1. *To: Board / From: CFO / Subject: Q3 Review. This report examines...*
2. *Hi team — quick note: the deck is ready for tomorrow's call.*
3. *Re: supplier policy. Please note that all new vendors must complete KYC within five business days.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Informe** (encabezado formal, propósito explícito, registro denso).
2. **Email profesional** (semiformal, conciso, primera persona).
3. **Memo** (formal-corto, propósito único, directriz).

</details>

### Ejercicio 18 — Consolidación

Completa el párrafo con una palabra por hueco.

> *Q3 revenue grew (1)______ faster than costs, and margins improved (2)______ on the previous year. The (3)______ we expanded abroad, the (4)______ our acquisition cost fell. Domestic growth, (5)______ contrast, remained only (6)______ positive.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **considerably** (intensificador)
2. **markedly / substantially** (intensificador)
3. **more** (the more)
4. **lower / more** (the lower / the more... fell)
5. **by** (by contrast)
6. **marginally** (atenuador)

</details>

### Ejercicio 19 — Reescribe el párrafo con registro de informe

Eleva el registro del párrafo coloquial con comparativos, *the more... the more...*, contraste y consecuencia.

> *We did way better this quarter, like loads more than the market. Going abroad really cut costs. Home market is basically flat though. Supply is still a pain but we'll cover the gap.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *Q3 revenue grew considerably faster than the sector average. The more aggressively we expanded into emerging markets, the more our blended acquisition cost fell, so that operating margins widened substantially. Domestic growth, by contrast, remained only marginally positive, stagnant amid pricing pressure. Although supply headwinds persist, international momentum is expected to offset the domestic shortfall; consequently, a cautious but optimistic forecast is warranted.*

</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

- Los **comparativos con modificadores** gradúan la diferencia: intensificadores (*far, considerably, substantially, much, even, still*) y atenuadores (*marginally, slightly, narrowly, only modestly*); con sustantivos (*more/less/fewer ... than*) y *as...as* reforzado (*not nearly as, nothing like as, twice as*).
- La estructura **the + comparativo ... the + comparativo** expresa proporcionalidad manteniendo el orden sujeto-verbo; los **superlativos** se refuerzan con *by far, one of the, the single*.
- El **contraste** se reparte en subordinado (*whereas, while, whilst*), adverbial independiente (*by contrast, in contrast, conversely*) y argumentativo (*on the other hand, however, nevertheless*); las concesivas (*although/even though* con cláusula; *despite/in spite of* con sustantivo) añaden el matiz de obstáculo no impedidor.
- Los **conectores de causa y consecuencia** (*owing to, due to, given / consequently, accordingly, therefore*) y la **estructura canónica del informe** (encabezado, introducción, secciones, conclusiones, recomendaciones) enmarcan el análisis.
- El **vocabulario de tendencias** (*revenue, margin, forecast, downturn, scale, disrupt, benchmark, leverage, streamlined, volatile, resilient, stagnant, headwind, churn, YoY*) y sus colocaciones (*revenue surged, margins narrowed, gain/lose market share, outperform the market, offset the decline, weather the downturn, flat year-on-year*) permiten escribir informes que convierten datos en argumento accionable con la precisión y el registro que el C1 exige.
