Responde primero y comprueba después. La explicación está pensada para el estudiante hispanohablante.

## Parte II — Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1
Marca el estatus epistémico reformulando como opinión lo que se presenta como hecho: "Acupuncture cures chronic pain."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

It is widely held among some practitioners that acupuncture can relieve chronic pain, though the clinical evidence for a curative effect remains limited.
</details>

### Ejercicio 2
Yuxtapón evidencia y opinión con un conector de contraste: la evidencia es débil / la popularidad es alta.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Whereas the clinical evidence for homeopathy is weak, its popularity remains considerable in several countries.
</details>

### Ejercicio 3
Distingue evidencia anecdótica de clínica en una frase sobre un suplemento herbal.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Anecdotal reports of its benefits are abundant, but clinical evidence from controlled trials remains scarce and methodologically limited.
</details>

### Ejercicio 4
Formula el consenso médico con la fórmula adecuada: la acupuntura como complemento, no cura, para cierto dolor.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

There is broad consensus that acupuncture may serve as a conditional adjunct for certain chronic pain conditions, though not as a curative therapy.
</details>

### Ejercicio 5
Sustituye "say" por un verbo de postura intelectual: "Skeptics say the benefits are just placebo."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Skeptics contend that the benefits are largely attributable to placebo effects and regression to the mean.
</details>

### Ejercicio 6
Construye una estructura concesión-reserva sobre los remedios herbales.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Admittedly, some herbal preparations contain active compounds with demonstrable pharmacological effects; that said, the evidence for their curative claims remains weak and quality control is inconsistent.
</details>

### Ejercicio 7
Añade un hedge que calibre la eficacia: "Acupuncture relieves pain."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Acupuncture may offer modest relief for certain types of chronic pain, though the effect size appears small and varies between individuals.
</details>

### Ejercicio 8
Distingue complementary, alternative e integrative medicine en una frase.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Complementary medicine is used alongside conventional care, alternative medicine is used in its place, and integrative medicine combines both under coordinated clinical supervision.
</details>

### Ejercicio 9
Reformula el placebo sin descalificarlo: "It's just placebo."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Rather than dismissing the benefit as 'just placebo', it is more accurate to say that placebo responses are a real, if non-specific, mechanism of improvement that trials must control for.
</details>

### Ejercicio 10
Cita un estudio con su calidad de evidencia: una revisión Cochrane sobre acupuntura y dolor.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

A Cochrane review found moderate-certainty evidence that acupuncture reduces certain types of chronic pain, though the effect size was small and the clinical significance debated.
</details>

### Ejercicio 11
Formula una advertencia sobre interacciones herbales con anticoagulantes.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Patients on anticoagulants should consult a clinician before taking certain herbal supplements, as the interactions can be clinically significant.
</details>

### Ejercicio 12
Desmonta la ecuación "natural igual a seguro" con precisión.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

The fact that a substance is natural does not entail that it is safe; many potent toxins are entirely natural, and plant-derived compounds can interact with medications just as pharmaceuticals do.
</details>

### Ejercicio 13
Reformula el recurso a la tradición como falacia con respeto.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Long-standing traditional use can be a useful starting point for research, but it is not, in itself, proof of efficacy —bloodletting, too, has a long history.
</details>

### Ejercicio 14
Completa con la postura del "ni credulismo ni desdén": "One need not be either ______ or ______."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

One need not be either credulous or dismissive: the question is which specific interventions, for which conditions, have what quality of evidence.
</details>

### Ejercicio 15
Completa el léxico regulatorio: "In some jurisdictions, herbal supplements are regulated as ______ rather than medicines."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

In some jurisdictions, herbal supplements are regulated as foods rather than medicines, which means quality control is less stringent.
</details>

### Ejercicio 16
Reformula con un verbo de precaución: "Don't delay real medicine for unproven alternatives."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Clinicians caution against delaying conventional treatment in favour of unproven alternatives, as this can carry serious risks in progressive conditions.
</details>

### Ejercicio 17
Construye un contraste con "despite": falta de datos / popularidad.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Despite the lack of robust clinical data, the therapy enjoys considerable popularity, driven largely by anecdotal reports and cultural familiarity.
</details>

### Ejercicio 18
Cita un estudio débil con su limitación metodológica: un piloto pequeño sobre un suplemento.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

A small, methodologically weak pilot study suggested possible benefits, but the sample was too limited to draw firm conclusions and the findings await replication.
</details>

### Ejercicio 19
Redacta una postura matizada completa sobre la meditación como terapia complementaria.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

While mindfulness-based interventions have a reasonable evidence base for stress reduction and are generally safe, the evidence for their efficacy in treating specific clinical conditions is more mixed, which is why they are best regarded as a complement to, rather than a substitute for, conventional care.
</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

En esta unidad has aprendido a contrastar evidencia y opinión marcando el estatus epistémico de cada afirmación con fórmulas como "studies show" frente a "some practitioners believe", y a yuxtaponer ambos planos con conectores de contraste como "whereas", "although" y "despite". Has distinguido la evidencia anecdótica de la clínica, has formulado el consenso médico con expresiones como "there is broad consensus that", y has usado verbos de postura intelectual —"contend", "maintain", "dispute", "concede"— para graduar tu acuerdo. Has practicado la estructura concesión-reserva, has calibrado la eficacia con hedges y has desmontado falacias frecuentes como "natural igual a seguro" o el recurso a la tradición. Has aprendido a citar estudios con su calidad metodológica, a formular advertencias sin alarmismo y a sostener la postura del escéptico respetuoso. En la próxima unidad, la 42, consolidarás lo aprendido en el bloque de salud con una síntesis interdisciplinar que integrará el léxico y las destrezas de las unidades anteriores.
