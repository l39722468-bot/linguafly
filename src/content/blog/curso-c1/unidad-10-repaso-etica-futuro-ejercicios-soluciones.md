## Parte II — Ejercicios con soluciones

### Ejercicio 1 — Distancing

Transforma.

1. People say that the white paper was drafted months ago.
2. Analysts believe that the dataset was collected without meaningful consent.
3. The press reports that the chair was pressured to withdraw the findings.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **The white paper is said to have been drafted months ago.**
2. **The dataset is believed to have been collected without meaningful consent.**
3. **The chair is reported to have been pressured to withdraw the findings.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Impersonales con there

Completa con **is/are thought to be** o **appears/appear to be**.

1. There ___ a regulatory gap.
2. There ___ several unresolved ethical questions.
3. There ___ little agreement on liability.
4. There ___ two competing frameworks.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is thought to be**; 2. **are thought to be**; 3. **appears to be**; 4. **appear to be**. La concordancia depende del sustantivo posterior.

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — It-clefts

Reescribe destacando lo indicado.

1. The lack of transparency triggered opposition. (lack of transparency)
2. The regulator requested an audit. (the regulator)
3. The rules changed after the accident. (after the accident)
4. Critics questioned the failure rate. (the failure rate)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **It was the lack of transparency that triggered opposition.**
2. **It was the regulator who requested an audit.**
3. **It was after the accident that the rules changed.**
4. **It was the failure rate that critics questioned.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — What-clefts

Completa.

1. ___ the report reveals ___ a regulatory gap.
2. ___ remains uncertain ___ whether users gave meaningful consent.
3. ___ the panel demanded ___ independent oversight.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **What the report reveals is**.
2. **What remains uncertain is**.
3. **What the panel demanded was**.

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Directo, atribuido o especulativo

Clasifica cada frase como **directa**, **atribuida** o **especulativa**.

1. *The system caused harm.*
2. *The system is alleged to have caused harm.*
3. *The system may have caused harm.*
4. *The available evidence appears to suggest that the system caused harm.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Directa**; 2. **Atribuida**; 3. **Especulativa**; 4. **Interpretación cautelosa basada en evidencia**. La última no es una afirmación directa del escritor.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Vocabulario ético

Completa con **accountability, autonomy, liability, oversight** o **consent**.

1. Users should give meaningful ___ before their data are collected.
2. Human ___ is required when a system makes high-stakes decisions.
3. The organisation must accept ___ for the consequences of its design.
4. Individuals should retain control over their personal ___ .
5. The company may face legal ___ after the breach.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **consent**; 2. **oversight**; 3. **accountability**; 4. **autonomy**; 5. **liability**.

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Especulación sobre el futuro

Elige **is likely to, may, might well, could conceivably** o **remains unclear whether**.

1. Automation ___ change routine employment over the next decade.
2. The technology ___ reduce costs, but evidence is limited.
3. The reform ___ improve public trust if reporting is transparent.
4. A negotiated international standard ___ be reached, although many obstacles remain.
5. It ___ autonomous systems will gain public trust.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is likely to**; 2. **may**; 3. **might well**; 4. **could conceivably**; 5. **remains unclear whether**. Las opciones pueden variar si cambia el grado de certeza que quieres expresar.

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Reading

Lee el texto y responde.

> The consortium is reportedly developing an autonomous system for allocating emergency resources. It is claimed that the system will improve efficiency, but there appears to be little agreement about who should be accountable when its recommendations cause harm. What concerns civil-rights groups is the absence of a transparent appeals process. The developers are believed to have tested the system on historical data, although it remains unclear whether those data contained algorithmic bias. The technology could conceivably save time during a crisis, but it may also reproduce existing inequalities unless human oversight is guaranteed.

1. What is the consortium reportedly developing?
2. What is the claimed benefit?
3. What concerns civil-rights groups?
4. What remains unclear about the testing data?
5. What two possibilities does the final sentence present?

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **An autonomous system for allocating emergency resources.**
2. **It is claimed that it will improve efficiency.**
3. **The absence of a transparent appeals process.**
4. **Whether they contained algorithmic bias.**
5. **It could save time, but it may reproduce existing inequalities without human oversight.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Listening guiado

Guion: “The policy was ostensibly introduced to protect privacy, but it is widely believed to have increased surveillance. What the minister has not explained is why independent oversight was reduced. There are thought to be several regulatory gaps, and the measure might well face legal challenges.”

Identifica una expresión de distancing, una what-cleft, una construcción con *there* y una especulación.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Distancing: **ostensibly** o **it is widely believed to have increased**. What-cleft: **What the minister has not explained is...**. There-construction: **There are thought to be several regulatory gaps.** Especulación: **might well face legal challenges.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Patrones de infinitivo

Completa con **to be**, **to have**, **to have been** o **to have been + -ing**.

1. The system is believed ___ reliable.
2. The company is alleged ___ concealed the breach.
3. The whistle-blower is said ___ pressured.
4. The developers are thought ___ testing the model privately.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **to be**; 2. **to have** concealed; 3. **to have been** pressured; 4. **to have been testing**. El infinitivo perfecto muestra anterioridad; *been + -ing* añade una actividad en progreso.

</details>

### Ejercicio 11 — Puntuación

Corrige la puntuación.

1. *The evidence is limited however the question deserves attention.*
2. *The system may be efficient nevertheless it could undermine autonomy.*
3. *The policy was ostensibly neutral, but it increased surveillance.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **The evidence is limited; however, the question deserves attention.**
2. **The system may be efficient; nevertheless, it could undermine autonomy.**
3. La tercera ya está correctamente puntuada. *But* puede unir las dos cláusulas en una oración coordinada.

</details>

### Ejercicio 12 — Corrección de errores

Corrige el párrafo.

> It is alleged the dataset to contain private information. There are thought to be a serious breach. What did the review reveal was that the company is believed having ignored consent requirements.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**It is alleged that the dataset contains private information. There is thought to be a serious breach. What the review revealed was that the company is believed to have ignored consent requirements.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 13 — Reformulación ética

Reescribe con un registro más responsable: *The company built an unethical system that will harm everyone.*

<details><summary>Modelo</summary>

**The company is alleged to have developed a system whose design may pose risks to vulnerable users. It remains unclear whether independent oversight will be sufficient to prevent unintended harm.**

La reformulación atribuye la valoración, evita *everyone* y separa riesgo posible de daño demostrado.

</details>

### Ejercicio 14 — Cleft y contraste

Combina las frases con una cleft y una concesión.

*The lack of accountability worries critics. The system may improve efficiency.*

<details><summary>Modelo</summary>

**It is the lack of accountability that worries critics, although the system may improve efficiency.** También: **What worries critics is the lack of accountability, even though the system could improve efficiency.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 15 — Writing

Escribe 150–180 palabras sobre: **Should autonomous systems make decisions that affect people’s rights?** Incluye dos estructuras de distancing, una it-cleft, una what-cleft, dos expresiones especulativas y vocabulario ético.

<details><summary>Modelo</summary>

It is widely argued that autonomous systems could improve public services, but their use in rights-sensitive decisions remains controversial. The systems are reportedly becoming more accurate, and some developers are believed to have introduced independent testing. Nevertheless, what matters most is whether affected people can understand and challenge an automated decision. It is the absence of meaningful accountability that concerns civil-rights groups, rather than automation itself. An autonomous tool might well reduce administrative delays, and it could conceivably identify patterns that human officials miss. However, it may also reproduce algorithmic bias if its training data reflect existing inequality. It is therefore essential that human oversight remain available and that institutions accept legal liability when harm occurs. The technology should be used only where consent, transparency and a clear appeals process are guaranteed. Efficiency can be valuable, but it must not replace autonomy or procedural fairness.

</details>

### Ejercicio 16 — Speaking

Habla durante un minuto sobre una consecuencia ética de una tecnología futura. Usa *It is often claimed that*, *what concerns me is*, *may*, *might well* y *it remains unclear whether*.

<details><summary>Pista y modelo</summary>

**It is often claimed that predictive systems will make society safer. What concerns me is whether vulnerable groups will be monitored more heavily. The technology may prevent some harm, but it might well increase surveillance. It remains unclear whether citizens will be able to challenge decisions made by an opaque system.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 17 — Traducción controlada

Traduce al inglés.

1. Se cree que el informe fue redactado por el comité de ética.
2. Lo que sigue sin estar claro es quién debe asumir la responsabilidad.
3. El sistema podría mejorar la eficiencia, pero también podría aumentar la desigualdad.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **The report is believed to have been drafted by the ethics committee.**
2. **What remains unclear is who should accept responsibility.**
3. **The system could improve efficiency, but it might also increase inequality.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 18 — Mini-test final

Completa el texto.

> The white paper ___ ___ ___ ___ drafted months before the vote. ___ is alleged that its authors ignored public consultation. ___ surprised the panel was the lack of a risk assessment. There ___ thought to be several unresolved issues, and the proposal ___ well face legal challenges.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**is said to have been; It; What; are; might.** Una versión completa es: **The white paper is said to have been drafted months before the vote. It is alleged that its authors ignored public consultation. What surprised the panel was the lack of a risk assessment. There are thought to be several unresolved issues, and the proposal might well face legal challenges.**

</details>

### Ejercicio 19 — Integración de registro

Reescribe este argumento en cuatro frases formales. Debes incluir una estructura impersonal, una estructura personal con infinitivo perfecto, una cleft y una expresión de especulación.

> People say that the company collected data without consent. The company probably ignored the warning. Critics are worried about accountability. The system could create more problems in the future.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Una respuesta posible es: **It is reported that the company collected data without meaningful consent. The company is believed to have ignored the warning. It is the lack of accountability that concerns critics. The system might well create further problems in the future.**

La primera oración atribuye la información de forma impersonal. La segunda enfoca a la empresa y usa *to have ignored* para mostrar anterioridad. La tercera utiliza una it-cleft para destacar la causa de la preocupación. La última especula sin presentar el resultado como seguro.

</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

Para expresar distancia, usa **allegedly, reportedly, supposedly, it is believed that** y las estructuras personales con **be + past participle + infinitive**. Usa **to have + participle** cuando el hecho es anterior y **to have been + -ing** cuando quieres mostrar una actividad previa en desarrollo.

Para presentar existencia o ausencia de problemas, usa **there is/are thought to be** y **there appears/appear to be**, respetando la concordancia. Para guiar el foco, usa **It is/was X that/who...** y **What... is/was...**, sin invertir el orden dentro de la what-clause.

Al hablar de ética y futuro, distingue hechos, atribuciones y posibilidades. **May** expresa posibilidad, **might well** una posibilidad razonable, **could conceivably** una opción imaginable y **it remains unclear whether** una cuestión abierta. La precisión C1 consiste en combinar gramática, registro y responsabilidad: un texto bien construido no solo suena formal, también deja claro qué sabemos, qué suponemos y quién debe responder por las consecuencias.
