Este cuaderno reúne los **ejercicios de la Unidad 31 del curso C1** con soluciones. Haz cada bloque sin mirar la respuesta.

> **Guía teórica:** [Modalidad y Discurso Profesional C1](/blog/curso-c1/unidad-31-carreras-ambicion-laboral)
> **Más unidades de este nivel:** [Curso C1](/blog/curso-c1)

### Cómo usar este cuaderno

Responde primero y comprueba después. La explicación está pensada para el estudiante hispanohablante.

## Parte II — Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Must vs. have to

Elige la forma correcta y justifica brevemente.

1. I really ______ update my CV this weekend. (obligación interna del hablante)
2. You ______ submit the application by Friday. (regla externa)
3. Last year I ______ relocate for the role. (pasado)
4. All candidates ______ arrive ten minutes early. (instrucción directa)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **must** (obligación interna del hablante).
2. **have to** (obligación externa, plazo impuesto).
3. **had to** (pasado; must no tiene forma de pasado léxico).
4. **must** (instrucción directa y autoritaria, típica de normas).

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Should vs. ought to

Reescribe con *should* o *ought to* según el tono.

1. It's a good idea to tailor your CV to each role. (recomendación neutra)
2. You have a moral duty to inform your referee before naming them. (tono moral)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. You **should** tailor your CV to each role.
2. You **ought to** inform your referee before naming them.

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Be supposed to vs. be to

Elige la construcción adecuada (expectativa percibida vs. instrucción acordada).

1. Candidates ______ dress formally, but some show up in jeans. (expectativa que se incumple)
2. The panel ______ convene at noon sharp. (instrucción oficial)
3. I ______ hear back by Tuesday, but nobody called. (expectativa incumplida)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Candidates **are supposed to** dress formally, but some show up in jeans.
2. The panel **is to** convene at noon sharp.
3. I **was supposed to** hear back by Tuesday, but nobody called.

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Be expected to / be required to

Completa con la forma más fuerte (required) o de expectativa (expected).

1. Senior managers ______ mentor junior staff. (presupuesto por el entorno)
2. Applicants ______ provide three references. (obligación vinculante, reglamento)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Senior managers **are expected to** mentor junior staff. (presupuesto del entorno)
2. Applicants **are required to** provide three references. (obligación vinculante)

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Would y could (discurso tentativo)

Reescribe suavizando con *would* o *could*.

1. I suggest highlighting your metrics. → I ______ suggest...
2. You can frame that gap as a sabbatical. → You ______ frame...
3. I am happy to relocate. → I ______ be happy to relocate.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **would** suggest highlighting your metrics.
2. You **could** frame that gap as a sabbatical.
3. I **would** be happy to relocate.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Perfectos modales (reproche y posibilidad)

Reescribe con el perfecto modal adecuado.

1. It was a mistake not to ask about the promotion criteria earlier. → I ______ ...
2. It was possible for her to apply for the VP role (but she didn't). → She ______ ...
3. I prepared slides, but they only wanted a conversation (unnecessary). → I ______ ...

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **should have asked** about the promotion criteria earlier.
2. She **could have applied** for the VP role.
3. I **needn't have prepared** slides; they only wanted a conversation.

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Corrige los errores

Cada frase tiene un error. Corrígelo.

1. You should to update your CV.
2. I must submit it yesterday.
3. You should could must prepare.
4. The offer is to be signed, but they haven't yet. (contexto: expectativa que se incumple)
5. You ought to updating your LinkedIn profile.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. You should **update** your CV. (sin *to* tras modal)
2. I **had to** submit it yesterday. (pasado)
3. Un solo modal por cláusula: *You should prepare.* (o *You must prepare.*)
4. The offer **is supposed to be** signed, but they haven't yet. (expectativa incumplida, no instrucción)
5. You ought to **update** your LinkedIn profile. (infinitivo sin *-ing*)

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Transformación (CAE-style)

Reescribe empezando con las palabras dadas.

1. "It would be a good idea to prepare competency-based answers." → *You would be well advised...*
2. "They expect senior managers to mentor juniors." → *Senior managers are expected...*
3. "I regret not negotiating a higher salary." → *I should have...*
4. "It wasn't necessary to worry; the panel was friendly." → *I needn't have...*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. You would be well advised **to prepare competency-based answers**.
2. Senior managers are expected **to mentor juniors**.
3. I should have **negotiated a higher salary**.
4. I needn't have **worried; the panel was friendly**.

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Vocabulario de carreras

Relaciona cada término con su definición.

1. Plateau     a. Canales de candidatos potenciales.
2. Upskill     b. Estancamiento profesional.
3. Pipeline    c. Mejorar competencias.
4. Headhunt    d. Cazar talentos activamente.
5. Appraisal   e. Evaluación de desempeño.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1-b, 2-c, 3-a, 4-d, 5-e.

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Colocaciones profesionales

Completa con el verbo adecuado (climb / land / hand in / negotiate / demonstrate).

1. ______ the ladder to reach senior management.
2. ______ a role at a leading firm.
3. ______ one's notice and leave on good terms.
4. ______ a competitive salary package.
5. ______ quantifiable impact in your CV.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Climb** the ladder to reach senior management.
2. **Land** a role at a leading firm.
3. **Hand in** one's notice and leave on good terms.
4. **Negotiate** a competitive salary package.
5. **Demonstrate** quantifiable impact in your CV.

</details>

### Ejercicio 11 — Perfil de CV

Mejora este perfil débil con registro C1 (sin pronombre, verbos de acción, cuantificación).

> *I am a marketing person. I have done marketing for a while. I like working with teams and I am good at social media.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *Data-driven marketing manager with seven years' experience scaling B2B SaaS brands across EMEA. Orchestrated a multi-channel campaign that grew qualified leads by 41% and reduced cost-per-acquisition by 18%. Skilled at aligning brand strategy with revenue goals; proven track record of building and mentoring high-performing teams.*

</details>

### Ejercicio 12 — Lenguaje de entrevista (STAR)

Estructura una respuesta STAR a: *"Tell me about a time when you led a team through ambiguity."*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: **(Situation)** *In my previous role, a key product launch was thrown into uncertainty when our lead engineer left three weeks before release.* **(Task)** *I needed to stabilise the team and keep the launch on track.* **(Action)** *I redistributed responsibilities transparently, introduced daily stand-ups to surface blockers, and negotiated a two-week extension with stakeholders by presenting a revised risk plan.* **(Result)** *We launched on the new date with zero critical bugs, and team morale, measured in our pulse survey, recovered to pre-departure levels within a month.*

</details>

### Ejercicio 13 — Reading

Lee el extracto y responde.

> *The role requires a candidate who is expected to evidence leadership at every level. Applicants must demonstrate quantifiable impact, and they are supposed to have managed cross-functional teams of at least eight. You would be well advised to frame any career gap as intentional development; moreover, candidates need to provide three references, one of which ought to be a former direct report.*

1. Which three obligations/expectations are listed?
2. Which modal signals the strongest obligation? Which signals a recommendation?
3. Rewrite "they are supposed to have managed cross-functional teams" with *be expected to*.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Evidence leadership, demonstrate quantifiable impact, have managed cross-functional teams of 8+, provide three references (one a former direct report).
2. Strongest: **must** (demonstrate). Recommendation: **would be well advised to** / **ought to**.
3. They **are expected to have managed** cross-functional teams.

</details>

### Ejercicio 14 — Listening (paráfrasis)

Parafrasea esta declaración del reclutador en registro C1.

> "You've gotta send the CV by Friday, and you should really prep for the interview. We expect you to know the company."

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *Applications must be submitted by Friday, and you would be well advised to prepare thoroughly for the interview. Candidates are expected to demonstrate a solid understanding of the company.*

</details>

### Ejercicio 15 — Writing

Escribe una cover letter (80–100 palabras) para un puesto de *Senior Project Manager*. Usa al menos: un modal de obligación, una construcción de expectativa, un modal tentativo y un conector formal.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *Dear Ms. Alvarez, I am writing to express my interest in the Senior Project Manager position. Having led cross-functional teams of up to twelve across three markets, I am confident I can deliver the impact you require. Senior project managers are expected to align delivery with commercial strategy, and I have consistently done so, most recently cutting time-to-market by eight weeks. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my approach to stakeholder management could benefit your roadmap; moreover, I would be happy to provide references from former direct reports. Yours sincerely, ...* (94 palabras)

</details>

### Ejercicio 16 — Speaking (monólogo)

Prepara un monólogo de 2 minutos en el que expliques tu trayectoria profesional y ambición, usando modales y registro profesional.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo de inicio: *I began my career in operations, where I quickly realised that I had to develop a stronger commercial lens to progress. Over the next five years I upskilled through a part-time MBA while leading two regional rollouts; I would say that experience taught me to evidence impact rather than effort alone. I am expected, in my current role, to mentor juniors, and I've come to see that as the most rewarding part of the job. Looking ahead, I would like to step into a director-level position within three years...*

</details>

### Ejercicio 17 — Registro: CV, carta o entrevista

Clasifica cada extracto.

1. *Results-driven analyst with six years' experience in fintech.*
2. *I am writing to apply for the Data Lead role.*
3. *I would say my biggest strength is building consensus across silos.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **CV** (sin pronombre, verbos de acción implícitos).
2. **Cover letter** (pronombre *I*, registro formal escrito).
3. **Entrevista** (oral, modal tentativo *I would say*).

</details>

### Ejercicio 18 — Consolidación

Completa el párrafo con una palabra por hueco.

> *Senior managers are (1)______ to mentor junior staff, and applicants (2)______ demonstrate quantifiable impact. You (3)______ be well advised to tailor your CV; (4)______, you (5)______ to provide three references. Last year I (6)______ to relocate for the role.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **expected** (be expected to)
2. **must** (obligación fuerte)
3. **would** (would be well advised to)
4. **moreover** (conector formal)
5. **need** (need to)
6. **had** (had to, pasado)

</details>

### Ejercicio 19 — Reescribe el párrafo con discurso profesional

Eleva el registro del párrafo coloquial con modales, construcciones de expectativa y léxico profesional.

> *You've gotta fix your CV and you should go to the interview. They expect you to know the company and you have to show you're a leader. I think you could ask for more money.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *You must revise your CV before submitting it, and you would be well advised to prepare thoroughly for the interview. Candidates are expected to demonstrate a deep understanding of the company, and you are required to evidence leadership with quantifiable results. Furthermore, you could consider negotiating a more competitive remuneration package, particularly given the seniority of the role.*

</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

- La **modalidad** es la gramática de la obligación, el permiso, la posibilidad y la recomendación; en el discurso profesional calibra la fuente y la fuerza de la presión.
- **Must** (obligación interna, inferencia) frente a **have to** (obligación externa, regla; única forma de pasado y futuro léxico); **should** (recomendación neutra) frente a **ought to** (tono moral); **need to** (necesidad objetiva) y **needn't / don't need to** (ausencia de necesidad).
- Las **construcciones de expectativa** gradúan la presión institucional: *be supposed to* (expectativa percibida, a menudo incumplida), *be to* (instrucción acordada), *be expected to* (presupuesto del entorno), *be required to* (obligación vinculante).
- Los **modales tentativos** (*would, could, would be well advised to*) suavizan consejo y feedback; los **perfectos modales** (*should have, could have, needn't have, was supposed to*) evalúan el pasado con reproche, posibilidad o innecesariedad.
- El **vocabulario de carreras** (*career path, promotion, appraise, relocate, headhunt, succession planning, plateau, upskill, pipeline, remuneration, benchmark, competency, KPI, probation, notice*) y sus colocaciones (*climb the ladder, make a lateral move, hand in one's notice, land a role, negotiate a package, demonstrate impact, evidence leadership*) permiten hablar de trayectorias y ambición con la autoridad y el matiz que el C1 exige, tanto en CV (sin pronombre, verbos de acción, cuantificación), en cover letter (con pronombre, cohesión narrativa) como en entrevista (oral, modales tentativos, método STAR).
