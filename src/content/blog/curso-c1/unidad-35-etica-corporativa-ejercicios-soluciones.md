Este cuaderno reúne los **ejercicios de la Unidad 35 del curso C1** con soluciones. Haz cada bloque sin mirar la respuesta.

### Cómo usar este cuaderno

Responde primero y comprueba después. La explicación está pensada para el estudiante hispanohablante.

## Parte II — Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — responsible for / accountable for / liable for

Elige la estructura adecuada.

1. The CFO ______ financial reporting. (tarea de rol)
2. The CEO ______ the company's overall conduct. (rendición de cuentas por el resultado)
3. The company ______ the cost of remediation. (responsabilidad legal, con sanción)
4. The board ______ the shareholders. (responder ante)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The CFO **is responsible for** financial reporting.
2. The CEO **is accountable for** the company's overall conduct.
3. The company **is liable for** the cost of remediation.
4. The board **is accountable to** the shareholders.

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Obligación moral

Elige la estructura que mejor gradúa la fuerza (ought to / have a duty to / be obliged to / be required to).

1. The board ______ act in the interest of all stakeholders. (obligación fiduciaria fuerte)
2. The company ______ honour its settlement agreement. (contractual)
3. The firm ______ have disclosed the conflict earlier. (consejo moral, reproche pasado)
4. All employees ______ disclose any conflict of interest. (reglamentaria, código)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The board **has a duty to** act in the interest of all stakeholders.
2. The company **is obliged to** honour its settlement agreement.
3. The firm **ought to have** disclosed the conflict earlier.
4. All employees **are required to** disclose any conflict of interest.

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Condicionales de consecuencia ética

Completa con la forma verbal adecuada.

1. If a company pollutes, it ______ (must) remediate.
2. Should a breach ______ (occur), the firm is obliged to disclose it promptly.
3. Were a director to act in breach of fiduciary duty, they ______ (be) personally liable.
4. Unless the company reforms its supply chain, reputational damage ______ (follow).

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. If a company pollutes, it **must** remediate.
2. **Should** a breach **occur**, the firm is obliged to disclose it promptly.
3. **Were** a director to act in breach of fiduciary duty, they **would be** personally liable.
4. Unless the company reforms its supply chain, reputational damage **will** follow.

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Hedges de atribución

Reescribe con un *hedging* de atribución.

1. Companies must put stakeholders first. → It is widely held that...
2. Boards should be accountable for ESG failures. → There is a strong case for...
3. Short-termism undermines long-term value. → It is arguable that...

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. It is **widely held that** companies should put stakeholders first.
2. There is **a strong case for** holding boards accountable for ESG failures.
3. It is **arguable that** short-termism undermines long-term value.

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Conectores de causa, consecuencia y contraste

Inserta el conector adecuado (insofar as / accordingly / nevertheless / whereas / on the grounds that).

1. ______ the corporation knowingly breached its policy, it is liable.
2. The company is liable; ______, it has a duty to remediate.
3. Mitigating factors may reduce the penalty; ______, no mitigation absolves the board.
4. The board owes a duty to shareholders, ______ a growing consensus holds that it also owes a duty to stakeholders.
5. The claim was dismissed ______ the plaintiff lacked standing.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Insofar as** the corporation knowingly breached its policy, it is liable.
2. The company is liable; **accordingly**, it has a duty to remediate.
3. Mitigating factors may reduce the penalty; **nevertheless**, no mitigation absolves the board.
4. The board owes a duty to shareholders, **whereas** a growing consensus holds that it also owes a duty to stakeholders.
5. The claim was dismissed **on the grounds that** the plaintiff lacked standing.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Corrige los errores

Cada frase tiene un error. Corrígelo.

1. The CFO is liable for reporting. (es su función, no ilegal)
2. The board is accountable. (¿ante quién?)
3. Companies must always put stakeholders first. (demasiado absoluto, sin atribución)
4. The company was really bad and should say sorry.
5. The firm ought to must disclose the conflict.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The CFO **is responsible for** reporting. (función de rol, no responsabilidad legal)
2. The board **is accountable to** the shareholders.
3. **A growing consensus holds that** companies should put stakeholders first. (hedge de atribución)
4. The company **is liable for the damage and has a duty to compensate** affected stakeholders.
5. The firm **ought to have disclosed** the conflict. (un solo modal; perfecto para reproche pasado)

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Transformación (CAE-style)

Reescribe empezando con las palabras dadas.

1. "The company is responsible for the spill and should pay." → *Insofar as...*
2. "If a director breaches fiduciary duty, they will be personally liable." → *Were a director...*
3. "People think companies owe duties beyond shareholders." → *It is widely held that...*
4. "The company must remediate and prevent recurrence." → *The company has a duty...*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Insofar as** the company is responsible for the spill, it should pay (for remediation).
2. **Were a director** to breach fiduciary duty, they would be personally liable.
3. **It is widely held that** companies owe duties beyond shareholders.
4. **The company has a duty** to remediate and to prevent recurrence.

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Vocabulario de ética corporativa

Relaciona cada término con su definición.

1. Whistleblowing  a. Falsa apariencia ecológica.
2. Greenwashing    b. Denuncia interna de irregularidades.
3. Due diligence   c. Debida diligencia (verificación previa).
4. Compliance      d. Cumplimiento normativo.
5. ESG             e. Criterios ambientales, sociales y de gobernanza.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1-b, 2-a, 3-c, 4-d, 5-e.

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Colocaciones éticas

Completa con el verbo adecuado (owe / bear / disclose / remediate / prevent).

1. ______ a duty to all stakeholders.
2. ______ the cost of remediation.
3. ______ a conflict of interest promptly.
4. ______ the damage before it spreads.
5. ______ recurrence by implementing safeguards.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Owe** a duty to all stakeholders.
2. **Bear** the cost of remediation.
3. **Disclose** a conflict of interest promptly.
4. **Remediate** the damage before it spreads.
5. **Prevent** recurrence by implementing safeguards.

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Fiduciary duty: shareholder vs. stakeholder

Reescribe el enunciado equilibrando ambas doctrinas con un conector de contraste.

> "The board has a fiduciary duty to shareholders. A growing consensus says it also has a duty to stakeholders."

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *The board has a fiduciary duty to shareholders; **nevertheless**, a growing consensus holds that it also has a duty to stakeholders, and that the two are not irreconcilable.* (o con *whereas*: *The board has a fiduciary duty to shareholders, whereas a growing consensus holds that it also owes a duty to stakeholders.*)

</details>

### Ejercicio 11 — Reading

Lee el extracto y responde.

> *Insofar as the corporation knowingly discharged pollutants in breach of its own environmental policy, it is not merely responsible but fully accountable to the affected communities and liable for the cost of remediation. Accordingly, it has a duty to compensate affected stakeholders and to implement safeguards against recurrence. Nevertheless, it is widely held that mitigating factors — notably the self-reporting of the breach — ought to be weighed when determining the scale of the penalty.*

1. Distinguish the three levels of responsibility used in the text.
2. What two duties does the company have, according to the passage?
3. What mitigating factor is mentioned, and with what hedge?

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. *Responsible* (la tarea/incumplimiento), *accountable to* the affected communities (rendición de cuentas), *liable for* the cost of remediation (responsabilidad legal con sanción).
2. A duty to **compensate** affected stakeholders and a duty to **implement safeguards against recurrence**.
3. The self-reporting of the breach; introduced by the hedge *it is widely held that*.

</details>

### Ejercicio 12 — Listening (paráfrasis)

Parafrasea esta declaración del CEO en registro ético-formal.

> "We messed up with the spill. We'll pay to clean it and make sure it doesn't happen again. But we did tell on ourselves, so that should count for something."

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *Insofar as the corporation discharged pollutants in breach of its environmental policy, it is liable for the cost of remediation. Accordingly, it has a duty to compensate affected stakeholders and to implement safeguards against recurrence. Nevertheless, it is widely held that the self-reporting of the breach ought to be weighed as a mitigating factor.*

</details>

### Ejercicio 13 — Writing

Escribe un párrafo de argumentación ética (80–100 palabras) sobre un caso de greenwashing. Usa al menos: una estructura de responsabilidad (liable/accountable), una obligación moral (have a duty to), un condicional con *should* y un conector de contraste.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *Insofar as the firm marketed its product as carbon-neutral while knowingly relying on unverifiable offsets, it is accountable to consumers and liable for misleading claims. Accordingly, it has a duty to retract the campaign and to disclose its actual emissions transparently. Should the regulator find the practice systematic, the firm would face not only penalties but a lasting loss of trust; nevertheless, a prompt, good-faith correction may mitigate reputational damage and demonstrate genuine commitment to ESG principles.* (81 palabras)

</details>

### Ejercicio 14 — Speaking (monólogo)

Prepara un monólogo de 2 minutos en el que argumentes, como miembro de un comité de ética, a favor de reforzar la whistleblower policy. Usa registro ético-formal.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo de inicio: *Insofar as the current whistleblower framework affords inadequate protection, the company is failing in its duty to employees and, by extension, to the stakeholders it claims to serve. It is widely held that robust whistleblowing channels are not a discretionary perk but a fiduciary safeguard: were a material breach to go unreported for fear of retaliation, the board would be accountable for the resulting harm. Accordingly, I would argue that we have a duty to strengthen protections, to guarantee anonymity, and to implement safeguards against recurrence. Should we fail to act, the reputational and legal exposure would, I believe, be considerable...*

</details>

### Ejercicio 15 — Registro: código ético, informe de CSR o declaración pública

Clasifica cada extracto.

1. *All employees shall disclose any conflict of interest to the compliance officer without delay.*
2. *It is estimated that Scope 1 emissions were reduced by 18% year-on-year, owing to the electrification of the fleet.*
3. *We are committed to remediating the damage, to compensating affected communities, and to preventing recurrence.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Código ético** (normativo, *shall*).
2. **Informe de CSR** (formal-expositivo, pasiva, datos).
3. **Declaración pública** (formal-retórico, *we are committed to*).

</details>

### Ejercicio 16 — Consolidación

Completa el párrafo con una palabra por hueco.

> *(1)______ as the corporation knowingly breached its policy, it is (2)______ for the cost of remediation. (3)______, it has a duty to compensate affected stakeholders. (4)______, mitigating factors ought to be weighed. Should the breach (5)______ systematic, the firm would face severe penalties.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Insofar** (insofar as)
2. **liable** (be liable for)
3. **Accordingly** (consecuencia)
4. **Nevertheless** (contraste)
5. **prove** (should the breach prove systematic)

</details>

### Ejercicio 17 — Reescribe el párrafo con registro ético-formal

Eleva el registro del párrafo coloquial con estructuras de responsabilidad, obligación moral y conectores.

> *The company did a bad thing with the spill. They should pay to clean it and make sure it doesn't happen again. But they did tell on themselves so maybe go easy on them.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *Insofar as the corporation knowingly discharged pollutants in breach of its environmental policy, it is accountable to the affected communities and liable for the cost of remediation. Accordingly, it has a duty to compensate affected stakeholders and to implement safeguards against recurrence. Nevertheless, it is widely held that the self-reporting of the breach ought to be weighed as a mitigating factor when determining the scale of the penalty.*

</details>

### Ejercicio 18 — Distinción fina: responsible vs. accountable vs. liable

Para cada contexto, elige la estructura correcta y justifica.

1. Contexto: el CFO firma los informes financieros. (rol)
2. Contexto: el CEO explica ante el board por qué los ingresos cayeron. (rendición)
3. Contexto: la empresa paga una indemnización por un derrame. (sanción legal)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The CFO **is responsible for** financial reporting. (rol/tarea asignada)
2. The CEO **is accountable to** the board for the revenue decline. (rendición de cuentas, responder ante)
3. The company **is liable for** the cost of the spill. (responsabilidad legal con sanción)

</details>

### Ejercicio 19 — Debate shareholder vs. stakeholder (writing)

Escribe un párrafo (80–100 palabras) que presente ambas doctrinas y postule una reconciliación, usando al menos un conector de contraste y una estructura de obligación.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *The board has a fiduciary duty to maximise shareholder value; nevertheless, a growing consensus holds that it also has a duty to stakeholders — employees, customers, communities and the environment. Insofar as short-termism erodes trust and, with it, long-term returns, the two doctrines are not irreconcilable but complementary. Accordingly, it is widely held that robust ESG governance serves not to dilute shareholder value but to safeguard it, so that a company accountable to all affected parties is, in the long run, also the one that best serves its shareholders.* (85 palabras)

</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

- Las **estructuras de responsabilidad** se distinguen con precisión: *be responsible for* (la tarea o función asignada), *be accountable for/to* (la rendición de cuentas por el resultado y ante quién), *be liable for* (la responsabilidad legal o financiera, con posible sanción). Confundirlas tergiversa la exigencia ética y legal del texto.
- La **obligación moral** se gradúa con *have a duty to* (fiduciaria/ética fuerte), *be obliged to* (contractual), *ought to* (consejo moral, con perfecto para reproche), *be required to* (reglamentaria).
- Los **condicionales de consecuencia ética** (*if, should, were, unless*) encadenan acción y consecuencia; la **inversión con should/were** eleva el registro del razonamiento ético-formal.
- Los **hedges de atribución** (*it is widely held that, there is a strong case for, it is arguable that, a growing consensus holds*) proyectan prudencia y evitan el dogmatismo; los **conectores de causa, consecuencia y contraste** (*insofar as, accordingly, nevertheless, whereas, on the grounds that*) estructuran el argumento.
- El debate **fiduciary duty / shareholder vs. stakeholder value** y el **vocabulario ético** (*stakeholder, shareholder, accountability, liability, CSR, due diligence, whistleblowing, fiduciary duty, transparency, greenwashing, remediation, compliance, recurrence, conflict of interest, ESG*) con sus colocaciones (*owe a duty to, act in the interest of, bear the cost of, disclose a conflict, remediate the damage, prevent recurrence, hold accountable, breach fiduciary duty, implement safeguards, compensate affected parties*) permiten argumentar sobre ética corporativa con la precisión, el matiz y el registro que el C1 exige.
