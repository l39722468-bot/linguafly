## Parte II — Ejercicios

**Ejercicio 1.** Completa con conectores de síntesis: *taken together*, *what emerges from this*, *the through-line*, *seen in this light*.

1. ______, the phenomena examined are not separate crises but facets of one transformation.
2. ______ that connects these otherwise disparate units is the renegotiation of trust.
3. ______, the deontological strains are not an independent crisis but the ethical expression of the same dynamic.
4. ______ is a claim that none of the individual analyses makes on its own.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Taken together
2. The through-line
3. Seen in this light
4. What emerges from this

These connectors do not merely link sentences; they perform the intellectual work of synthesis \u2014 showing convergence (*taken together*), identifying the connecting thread (*the through-line*), reframing a phenomenon (*seen in this light*), and naming the emergent claim (*what emerges from this*).
</details>

**Ejercicio 2.** Reescribe esta enumeración plana como una síntesis C1: "We studied media, privacy, AI and journalism. They are all about information and trust."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*Taken together, the transformations examined across this block \u2014 the restructuring of media authority, the rise of surveillance capitalism, the epistemic challenges of generative AI, and the deontological strains on verification \u2014 converge on a single question that none of them poses in isolation: whether the informational commons can sustain the shared trust on which democratic deliberation depends, a through-line that recasts what might appear as four separate crises as four perspectives on one underlying renegotiation of the relationship between information and credibility.*

The rewrite performs three synthesising moves: it names the convergence (*taken together... converge on*), identifies the through-line (trust as the common question), and reframes the four as perspectives on one transformation rather than separate crises.
</details>

**Ejercicio 3.** Escribe una frase en C1 que jerarquice \u2014que muestre qué es lo fundamental debajo de los detalles\u2014 usando al menos una expresión de jerarquización.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*Beneath the specifics of platform design, data collection, algorithmic bias and editorial ethics lies a more fundamental question about the conditions under which a society can sustain a shared informational reality, and it is at that level \u2014 rather than at the level of any individual remedy \u2014 that the most consequential decisions about the future of the public sphere will, in all likelihood, be taken.*

The hierarchising expression *beneath the specifics... lies a more fundamental question* does the synthesis work: it distinguishes the surface phenomena from the underlying dynamic and asserts that the latter is where the stakes are highest.
</details>

**Ejercicio 4.** Traduce al inglés C1: "En conjunto, la transformación de los medios, la desinformación, el capitalismo de vigilancia y la IA convergen en una pregunta que ninguno plantea aislado: si el procomún informativo puede sostener la confianza compartida."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*Taken together, the transformation of the media, the challenge of disinformation, the rise of surveillance capitalism and the implications of AI converge on a question that none of them poses in isolation: whether the informational commons can sustain the shared trust on which democratic deliberation depends.*

Key synthesising vocabulary: *taken together*, *converge on*, *none of them poses in isolation*, *informational commons*, *shared trust*. The construction *converge on a question that none poses alone* is the signature move of synthesis: the whole produces a question the parts do not.
</details>

**Ejercicio 5.** Reescribe esta afirmación dual como una síntesis que sostenga la tensión: "Democratising publishing is good. But it also removed filtering."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*The synthesis must hold the tension between two truths that the individual analyses each lean towards: that the democratisation of publishing has, on the one hand, given voice to perspectives that the gatekeeping model excluded, and that it has, on the other hand, removed the filtering that, for all its biases, supplied a common baseline of verified fact \u2014 a trade-off whose net effect on the public sphere is not captured by narratives that celebrate only the first or lament only the second.*

The synthesising moves: *hold the tension between*, *on the one hand / on the other hand*, *for all its biases* (concession that the filtering was imperfect), *a trade-off whose net effect... is not captured by narratives that...*. The sentence refuses to resolve the tension falsely.
</details>

**Ejercicio 6.** Completa con léxico de evaluación integrada: *arguably*, *on balance*, *the net effect*, *in all likelihood*.

1. ______, the cumulative impact is more ambiguous than either narrative allows.
2. The evidence, taken together, suggests that ______ is a restructuring of the conditions of trust.
3. ______, the same mechanisms that democratised access have eroded the conditions of shared credibility.
4. ______, the most consequential decisions will be taken at the structural rather than the individual level.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. On balance
2. the net effect
3. arguably (or in all likelihood)
4. in all likelihood

These expressions calibrate the integrated judgement: *on balance* weighs competing considerations, *the net effect* names the cumulative outcome, *arguably* signals a defensible but contestable claim, and *in all likelihood* signals probability without certainty. Synthesis without calibration becomes assertion.
</details>

**Ejercicio 7.** Escribe una frase en C1 que integre la transformación mediática y la economía de la vigilancia, revelando una conexión que ninguna muestra sola.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*The synthesis of the privacy and media transformations reveals a connection that neither analysis foregrounds on its own: the same extraction of behavioural surplus that funds the platforms to which editorial authority has migrated means that the economic model of the new information ecosystem is one in which the user is simultaneously the audience and the raw material, a dual role whose tension the advertising model of the legacy press, for all its limitations, did not embody.*

The synthesising move is to identify the *connection that neither foregrounds on its own* \u2014 that the platform economy fuses the audience and the raw material in a way the legacy press did not. This is emergent insight: a claim produced by the integration, not present in either part.
</details>

**Ejercicio 8.** Reescribe con síntesis jerárquica: "Fact-checking won't solve disinformation. Algorithms cause it. Bubbles reinforce it."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*Viewed holistically, the disinformation challenge is not adequately framed as a problem of individual credulity correctable by better fact-checking, for the structural conditions \u2014 algorithmic curation that rewards engagement, filter bubbles that insulate belief from contestation, and the collapse of shared gatekeeping that once supplied a common baseline of verified fact \u2014 interact in ways that make the individual-psychology framing a diagnosis of symptoms rather than of the disease, which is to say that the remedy, if this analysis is correct, lies at the structural level rather than at the level of correcting any single falsehood.*

The synthesising moves: *viewed holistically* reframes, the list of structural conditions shows interaction, *diagnosis of symptoms rather than of the disease* hierarchises, and *the remedy... lies at the structural level* moves from description to prescription. The rewrite integrates three claims into one structural diagnosis.
</details>

**Ejercicio 9.** Escribe una frase en C1 que muestre cómo la IA y la ética periodística se intersectan en el problema de la verificación, usando vocabulario de síntesis.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*At the intersection of the AI and media transformations lies a complication that compounds the trust crisis: the prospect that synthetic media, by eroding the commonsense presumption that audiovisual evidence is by default trustworthy, could undermine the very evidentiary foundation on which verification \u2014 the discipline that distinguishes journalism from assertion \u2014 has historically rested, a degradation whose full consequences may, over time, prove more corrosive than any single fabricated clip.*

The synthesising expression *at the intersection of* identifies the point where two transformations compound each other, and the sentence shows how the AI challenge (synthetic media) attacks the foundation (evidentiary trust) on which the ethical discipline (verification) rests. This is synthesis as the identification of compounding effects.
</details>

**Ejercicio 10.** Traduce al inglés C1: "Lo que une a estos no es un tema compartido sino una pregunta compartida: si el procomún informativo puede sostener la confianza."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*What unifies these is not a shared topic but a shared question \u2014 the question of whether the informational commons can sustain the trust that democratic deliberation requires \u2014 and it is against this backdrop that the adequacy of any proposed remedy must, in the final analysis, be assessed.*

The key construction *what unifies these is not a shared topic but a shared question* performs the synthesising move of distinguishing surface commonality (topic) from deep commonality (question). The phrase *against this backdrop* then uses the synthesis as the frame for evaluating remedies.
</details>

**Ejercicio 11.** Reescribe esta conclusión plana como una síntesis C1 que pase de la descripción a la prescripción: "Things are bad. We should do something."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*If this analysis is correct, the remedy is not to restore the gatekeeping model whose biases the democratisation of publishing rightly contested, but rather to build the institutional and architectural conditions under which the benefits of open access and the discipline of verification can coexist \u2014 a project whose difficulty is, admittedly, considerable, but whose necessity the synthesis of these transformations makes difficult to deny.*

The synthesising moves: *if this analysis is correct* (grounds the prescription in the synthesis), *the remedy is not... but rather...* (distinguishes false remedy from genuine one), and *whose necessity the synthesis... makes difficult to deny* (closes the loop by deriving the prescription from the integrated analysis). The move from description to prescription is the hallmark of mature synthesis.
</details>

**Ejercicio 12.** Completa con conectores de recapitulación jerárquica: *pulling these threads together*, *in the final analysis*, *what this comes down to*, *the thread that runs through*.

1. ______, these are not five crises but one restructuring of trust.
2. ______ all of this is a renegotiation of the information-credibility relationship.
3. ______, the adequacy of any remedy will be measured by whether it grasps the underlying dynamic.
4. ______ these units is the question of shared informational reality.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Pulling these threads together
2. What this comes down to
3. In the final analysis
4. The thread that runs through

These connectors close the synthesis by recapitulating at a higher level: *pulling these threads together* gathers, *what this comes down to* distils, *in the final analysis* delivers the ultimate judgement, and *the thread that runs through* names the through-line in closing. Together they form the architecture of a synthesising conclusion.
</details>

**Ejercicio 13.** Escribe una frase en C1 que use la metáfora de la confianza como bien común (*commons*) para unificar los temas del bloque.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*The overarching point is that trust functions in the informational ecosystem as a commons \u2014 a resource that each actor draws upon but that none alone can sustain \u2014 and the tragedy that the convergence of these transformations risks producing is the tragedy of that commons: a situation in which the rational individual incentives of platforms, outlets and users combine, without any of them intending it, to degrade the very shared reality on which all of them, including the individual rational actors, ultimately depend.*

The metaphor of the *commons* (and the *tragedy of the commons*) unifies the block by providing a single explanatory frame: each transformation (media, privacy, AI, ethics) is a vector through which individual rational incentives degrade a shared resource. The synthesising expression *the overarching point is* introduces the unifying frame.
</details>

**Ejercicio 14.** Reescribe con evaluación integrada calibrada: "The internet destroyed journalism and democracy. Everything is worse now."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*The evidence, taken together, suggests that the net effect of these transformations on the capacity of the public sphere to sustain informed deliberation is, on balance, more ambiguous than either the celebratory or the catastrophic narrative allows, for the same mechanisms that have democratised access have, in the same motion, eroded the conditions of shared trust under which that access translates into collective sense-making \u2014 a dual character that a synthesis, unlike a polemic, is bound to hold rather than resolve.*

The calibration moves: *the evidence, taken together, suggests* (grounded, not asserted), *more ambiguous than either narrative allows* (rejects both poles), *on balance* (weighs), *a dual character that a synthesis... is bound to hold rather than resolve* (names the discipline of synthesis). The rewrite replaces polemic with calibrated integrated judgement.
</details>

**Ejercicio 15.** Escribe una frase en C1 que muestre cómo las presiones éticas sobre el periodismo son expresión de la misma transformación estructural, usando vocabulario de síntesis.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*Seen in this light, the deontological strains on journalism are not an independent crisis but the ethical expression of the same transformation that has restructured the economic and technological conditions of the profession, for the pressure of engagement metrics on editorial decisions, the erosion of the church-state separation by native advertising, and the compression of verification time by the demand for immediacy are all manifestations of a single dynamic in which the economic model of the attention economy is misaligned with the deontological duty to inform.*

The synthesising moves: *seen in this light* reframes, *not an independent crisis but the ethical expression of the same transformation* identifies the unity, and the list of three manifestations shows they are *all manifestations of a single dynamic*. The sentence integrates ethical, economic and technological phenomena under one structural diagnosis.
</details>

**Ejercicio 16.** Usa la estructura *having examined X, we are in a position to Y* para sintetizar el bloque entero en una frase.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*Having examined the transformation of media, the challenge of disinformation, the economics of surveillance, the implications of AI and the strains on journalistic ethics, we are in a position to see that what unifies these is not a shared topic but a shared question \u2014 the question of whether the informational commons can sustain the trust that democratic deliberation requires \u2014 and it is against this backdrop that the adequacy of any proposed remedy must, in the final analysis, be assessed.*

The structure *having examined X, we are in a position to Y* performs the synthesising move of deriving the conclusion from the cumulative examination: the list acknowledges the parts, *we are in a position to see* signals the emergent insight, and *what unifies these* names the through-line that the integration reveals.
</details>

**Ejercicio 17.** Reescribe esta afirmación sobre remedios con la precisión sintetizadora de un C1: "We need to fix the internet. Regulation will solve everything."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*If the foregoing synthesis is correct, the adequacy of any remedy will be measured not by how well it addresses any single vector \u2014 disinformation, surveillance, algorithmic bias or editorial pressure \u2014 but by whether it grasps the underlying dynamic that connects them, which is to say that a regulatory response confined to individual symptoms, however well-intentioned, risks reproducing the fragmentation that the diagnosis identifies as the problem, for a structural condition is not adequately addressed by a catalogue of unrelated fixes.*

The synthesising moves: *if the foregoing synthesis is correct* (grounds the claim in the integration), *not by how well it addresses any single vector... but by whether it grasps the underlying dynamic* (hierarchises remedy by structural grasp), and *a structural condition is not adequately addressed by a catalogue of unrelated fixes* (derives the prescription from the diagnosis). The rewrite replaces slogan with structurally grounded prescription.
</details>

**Ejercicio 18.** Escribe un párrafo breve (4-5 frases) en C1 que sintetice el bloque entero \u2014medios, desinformación, privacidad, IA y ética\u2014 identificando el through-line y moviéndose hacia una prescripción.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*Taken together, the transformations examined across this block \u2014 the restructuring of media authority, the challenge of disinformation, the rise of surveillance capitalism, the epistemic implications of AI and the deontological strains on verification \u2014 converge on a question that none of them poses in isolation: whether the informational commons can sustain the shared trust on which democratic deliberation depends. The through-line that connects these is not a shared topic but a shared dynamic \u2014 the renegotiation of the relationship between information and credibility \u2014 and seen in this light, what might appear as five separate crises are more adequately understood as five perspectives on a single transformation. To be sure, the synthesis must hold the tension between the democratisation that these mechanisms have enabled and the erosion of shared trust they have, in the same motion, produced, for a narrative that celebrates only the former or laments only the latter fails to capture the dual character that the evidence, on balance, sustains. If this analysis is correct, the remedy is not to restore a gatekeeping model whose biases the democratisation of publishing rightly contested, but rather to build the institutional and architectural conditions under which open access and the discipline of verification can coexist \u2014 a project whose difficulty is, admittedly, considerable, but whose necessity the synthesis makes difficult to deny.*

Synthesising features: *taken together... converge on*, *the through-line that connects these*, *seen in this light*, *the synthesis must hold the tension*, *on balance*, *if this analysis is correct... the remedy is not... but rather*. The paragraph moves from convergence to through-line to tension to prescription \u2014 the full arc of a synthesising argument.
</details>

**Ejercicio 19.** Escribe un párrafo breve (4-5 frases) en inglés C1 que evalúe de forma integrada el estado del ecosistema mediático, usando al menos tres conectores de síntesis y la metáfora de la confianza como bien común.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

*The overarching point that emerges from pulling these threads together is that trust functions in the informational ecosystem as a commons \u2014 a resource that each actor draws upon but that none alone can sustain, and whose degradation, once it passes a certain threshold, is liable to become self-reinforcing. Against this backdrop, the convergence of the transformations examined across this block takes on a character that the individual analyses do not fully convey: the migration of authority to platforms whose business model treats trust as an externality, the assault of disinformation on the presumption of good faith, the complication of evidentiary reliability by synthetic media, and the ethical strains on the profession of verification are, in the final analysis, not parallel phenomena but mutually reinforcing vectors of a single erosion. What this comes down to is that the adequacy of any response will be measured not by its effect on any one vector but by whether it grasps the dynamic that connects them, for a commons cannot be defended by measures that address its depletion in one quadrant while leaving the mechanisms of its depletion in the others intact. The honest synthesis, then, is neither the celebratory narrative that counts the gains of democratisation and stops there, nor the catastrophic one that counts the losses and stops there, but the more demanding recognition that the same transformation has produced both \u2014 and that the question of whether the net effect can be made to favour the public is one that the architecture of the ecosystem, not the goodwill of any individual actor within it, will ultimately decide.*

Synthesising connectors: *the overarching point that emerges from pulling these threads together*, *against this backdrop*, *in the final analysis*, *what this comes down to*. Metaphor: *trust as a commons*, *self-reinforcing degradation*, *mutually reinforcing vectors of a single erosion*, *defended by measures that address its depletion in one quadrant*. The paragraph enacts the culminating C1 skill: integrated evaluation that refuses both polemic and complacency, and grounds its prescription in the structural diagnosis that the synthesis produces.
</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

En esta unidad hemos consolidado el andamiaje lingüístico del bloque dedicado a los medios, la información y la transformación digital, elevando las destrezas de las unidades anteriores al nivel de la síntesis argumentativa. Has aprendido a manejar los conectores de síntesis \u2014*taken together*, *what emerges from this*, *the through-line that connects these*, *seen in this light*\u2014, las expresiones de jerarquización \u2014*beneath the specifics lies*, *the more fundamental point is*, *the underlying dynamic*\u2014 y las de evaluación integrada \u2014*on balance*, *the net effect*, *the evidence, taken together, suggests*\u2014. Has incorporado el léxico de la convergencia \u2014*informational commons*, *shared reality*, *trust as a commons*, *mutually reinforcing vectors*\u2014 y el de la prescripción madura \u2014*the remedy is not... but rather*, *if this analysis is correct*, *the adequacy of any remedy will be measured by*\u2014. Has ejercitado la identificación del *through-line* que convierte la enumeración en narrativa, el sostén de las tensiones sin resolución falsa, y el paso de la descripción a la prescripción que la síntesis madura sustenta. La destreza central de esta unidad es la integración: la capacidad de mostrar cómo los temas aparentemente dispersos del bloque convergen en una pregunta compartida, de jerarquizar lo fundamental debajo de los detalles, y de derivar del análisis integrado una prescripción que ningún análisis individual habría fundamentado. Esa arquitectura sintetizadora es la marca distintiva del hablante C1 cuando cierra un bloque temático: no quien acumula, sino quien integra; no quien enumera, sino quien revela las relaciones que la enumeración oculta.
