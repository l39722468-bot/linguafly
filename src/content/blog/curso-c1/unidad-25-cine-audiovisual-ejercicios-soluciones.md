## Parte II — Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Nominalización (verbo → sustantivo)

Transforma la cláusula en una frase nominal con sustantivo abstracto.

1. The director decided to shoot in black and white. → The director's ___
2. The film is edited very fast. → The ___
3. The story is coherent. → The ___
4. How the cinematographer lit the scene matters. → The ___
5. The cast performs well. → The ___

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The director's **decision to shoot in black and white**.
2. The **fast editing of the film** (o *the film's rapid editing*).
3. The **coherence of the story**.
4. The **cinematographer's lighting of the scene** matters.
5. The **cast's strong performance** (o *the strong performances of the cast*).

La nominalización condensa la cláusula en una frase nominal densa y formal.

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Sustantivos abstractos del discurso crítico

Sustituye la formulación de B2 por el sustantivo abstracto de C1.

1. The way the film is paced is effective. → The ___ is effective.
2. The director handles silence very well. → The director's ___ of silence is remarkable.
3. The film treats grief in an interesting way. → The film's ___ of grief is compelling.
4. The ending is restrained. → The ending's ___ is its strength.
5. The film builds tension gradually. → The gradual ___ of tension is effective.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The **pacing** is effective.
2. The director's **handling** of silence is remarkable.
3. The film's **treatment** of grief is compelling.
4. The ending's **restraint** is its strength.
5. The gradual **building** (o *build-up*) of tension is effective.

Los sustantivos abstractos (*pacing, handling, treatment, restraint, build-up*) elevan el registro al nombrar cualidades formales.

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Subordinadas de comentario con it

Reescribe empezando con *it + be + adjetivo + that*.

1. The film avoids melodrama, and that is striking.
2. The subplot mirrors the main plot; this is worth noting.
3. The climax occurs in near-silence, which is no coincidence.
4. The film subverts genre conventions, and that is significant.
5. The director refuses to explain the ending; this is remarkable.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **It is striking that** the film avoids melodrama.
2. **It is worth noting that** the subplot mirrors the main plot.
3. **It is no coincidence that** the climax occurs in near-silence.
4. **It is significant that** the film subverts genre conventions.
5. **It is remarkable that** the director refuses to explain the ending.

El *it* anticipa la valoración; la *that*-clause aporta el contenido. Otros adjetivos válidos: *noteworthy, notable, telling*.

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Adjetivos evaluativos

Sustituye el adjetivo B2 por el adjetivo C1 adecuado.

1. The thriller is very good. (sostiene la tensión) → It is a ___ thriller.
2. The film is bad and obvious. → It is ___ and ___.
3. The film is interesting and makes you think. → It is ___.
4. The direction is subtle and controlled. → It is ___ and ___.
5. The horror film uses too many obvious tricks. → It is ___.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. It is a **taut / gripping** thriller.
2. It is **derivative** and **on-the-nose** (o *heavy-handed*).
3. It is **thought-provoking**.
4. It is **understated** and **measured** (o *assured*).
5. It is **heavy-handed** (o *contrived*).

Los adjetivos C1 nombran el modo en que la película funciona, no solo si gusta.

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Verbos de valoración causal

Completa con un verbo de valoración causal (*subvert / elevate / undermine / reinforce / earn*).

1. A stellar cast cannot ___ a thin script.
2. The minimalist score ___ the sense of dread.
3. The climax ___ its emotional payoff.
4. The film ___ the conventions of the whodunit.
5. Sluggish pacing ___ the tension.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. A stellar cast cannot **elevate** a thin script.
2. The minimalist score **reinforces** the sense of dread.
3. The climax **earns** its emotional payoff.
4. The film **subverts** the conventions of the whodunit.
5. Sluggish pacing **undermines** the tension.

Estos verbos expresan la relación causal entre un recurso y su efecto, núcleo del argumento crítico.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Vocabulario cinematográfico (definiciones)

Empareja cada término con su definición.

Términos: *cinematography / montage / diegetic sound / character arc / voiceover / foreshadowing*
1. Sonido cuya fuente está dentro del mundo de la película.
2. Secuencia de imágenes yuxtapuestas para condensar tiempo o ideas.
3. Narración en off de un personaje.
4. Evolución interna de un personaje a lo largo de la película.
5. Arte y técnica de la fotografía en movimiento.
6. Pistas que anticipan un evento posterior.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **diegetic sound** — Sonido cuya fuente está dentro del mundo de la película.
2. **montage** — Secuencia de imágenes yuxtapuestas para condensar tiempo o ideas.
3. **voiceover** — Narración en off de un personaje.
4. **character arc** — Evolución interna de un personaje a lo largo de la película.
5. **cinematography** — Arte y técnica de la fotografía en movimiento.
6. **foreshadowing** — Pistas que anticipan un evento posterior.

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Colocaciones de la crítica

Completa con el verbo o sustantivo que forma colocación.

1. The film ___ the payoff. (merece el desenlace emocional)
2. The romantic subplot ___. (no funciona, decepciona)
3. The dialogue ___. (suena auténtico)
4. A ___ thriller. (tenso, compacto)
5. The plot ___ on a single coincidence. (depende de)
6. The film ___ expectations. (subvierte las expectativas)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The film **earns** the payoff.
2. The romantic subplot **falls flat**.
3. The dialogue **rings true**.
4. A **taut** thriller.
5. The plot **hinges** on a single coincidence.
6. The film **subverts** expectations.

*Earn the payoff, fall flat, ring true, taut thriller, hinge on, subvert expectations* son colocaciones consolidadas del registro crítico.

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Género y convención

Reescribe con vocabulario de género (*subvert / adhere to / depart from / pay homage to / pastiche*).

1. The film follows the rules of film noir exactly.
2. The director copies classical Westerns out of admiration.
3. The movie twists the conventions of the slasher.
4. The comedy ignores the usual rom-com formula.
5. The film is a self-aware imitation of the spy genre.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The film **adheres to** the conventions of film noir.
2. The director **pays homage to** classical Westerns.
3. The movie **subverts** the conventions of the slasher.
4. The comedy **departs from** the usual rom-com formula.
5. The film is a self-aware **pastiche** of the spy genre.

*Adhere to, pay homage to, subvert, depart from, pastiche* sitúan la obra respecto a su tradición de género.

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Corrige los errores

Cada frase tiene un error típico de registro. Corrígelo.

1. The film is good and interesting. (demasiado genérico)
2. I think the film is gripping. (primera persona innecesaria)
3. The story and the plot are the same. (confusión story/plot)
4. It is striking the film avoids melodrama. (falta that)
5. The editing of the film is very fastly. (error de categoría)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The film is **gripping and thought-provoking** (adjetivos C1 concretos).
2. **The film is gripping** (se elimina *I think*; la afirmación ya implica valoración).
3. La **story** (eventos) y el **plot** (su disposición) no son lo mismo; reformular distinguiéndolos: *the plot rearranges the chronology of the story*.
4. **It is striking that** the film avoids melodrama (la *that*-clause es obligatoria).
5. The editing of the film is very **fast** (adjetivo, no adverbio).

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Transformación (CAE-style)

Reescribe la frase con la palabra dada para que el significado sea equivalente.

1. The film's restraint is what makes the climax so powerful. (IT) → It is ___
2. The director's handling of silence is remarkable. (HANDLED) → The way ___
3. Nobody expected the film to subvert the genre so boldly. (EXPECTATIONS) → The film ___
4. The plot depends entirely on one coincidence. (HINGES) → The plot ___
5. The film resembles a Hitchcock thriller but diverges in its ending. (WHILE) → While ___

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **It is the film's restraint that** makes the climax so powerful. (cleft con *it*)
2. The way **the director handled silence** is remarkable.
3. The film **subverted expectations by** defying the genre so boldly.
4. The plot **hinges** entirely on one coincidence.
5. **While the film resembles** a Hitchcock thriller, it diverges in its ending.

Cada reescritura conserva el significado usando la estructura objetivo.

</details>

</details>

### Ejercicio 13 — Reading

Lee el fragmento y responde.

> *It is striking that the film resists the temptation to explain itself. The measured pacing of the first act and the understated cinematography produce a tension that more heavy-handed thrillers cannot match. Where a lesser director might have spelled out the protagonist's guilt, here the editing lets the audience infer it — a restraint that earns the quiet devastation of the climax.*

1. ¿Qué recurso gramatical introduce la valoración en la primera frase?
2. ¿Qué dos nominalizaciones describen elecciones formales?
3. ¿Qué adjetivo critica a otros thrillers?
4. ¿Qué verbo de valoración cierra el fragmento y qué implica?
5. ¿Por qué la crítica no explica la trama?

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Una **subordinada de comentario**: *it is striking that*.
2. *the measured pacing* y *the understated cinematography* (nominalizaciones).
3. *heavy-handed* (censura a los thrillers que lo explican todo).
4. *earns*: implica que el clímax merece la inversión emocional previa.
5. Porque la crítica explica **cómo** funciona la película (elecciones formales y efecto), no **qué** ocurre; resumir la trama no es criticar.

</details>

### Ejercicio 14 — Listening (paráfrasis)

Imagina que escuchas esta afirmación de un crítico: *"The picture's chief virtue is its refusal to sentimentalise; the score is deployed sparingly, and the result is a cumulative dread that creeps up on you."* Parafrasea en una frase con nominalización y subordinada de comentario.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *It is remarkable that the film refuses to sentimentalise; the sparing use of the score produces a cumulative dread that builds gradually.*

Elementos: subordinada de comentario (*it is remarkable that*), nominalización (*the sparing use of the score*), adjetivo evaluativo (*cumulative*) y verbo causal (*produces*).

</details>

### Ejercicio 15 — Writing

Escribe una crítica breve (120-150 palabras) de una película real o imaginada que incluya: una subordinada de comentario, dos nominalizaciones, un adjetivo evaluativo C1 y un verbo de valoración causal.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo (148 palabras):

*It is striking that the film trusts its audience as rarely as it does. The slow pacing of the first hour and the understated cinematography — naturalistic, low-key, almost documentary — create a disquiet that no jump scare could match. Where a more heavy-handed director might have spelled out the protagonist's guilt, here the cross-cutting lets us infer it, and that restraint earns the quiet devastation of the final shot. The screenplay is taut, the score is deployed sparingly, and the cast's measured performances elevate material that, in lesser hands, might have felt thin. If the third act leans slightly on coincidence, it is a minor flaw in a film that otherwise subverts the conventions of the psychological thriller with confidence. A gripping, thought-provoking piece that lingers long after the credits.*

Rúbrica: *it is striking that* (comentario), *the slow pacing, the understated cinematography* (nominalizaciones), *heavy-handed, measured, taut, gripping* (adjetivos C1), *earns, elevate, subverts* (verbos causales).

</details>

### Ejercicio 16 — Speaking (monólogo)

Habla durante dos minutos valorando una película que hayas visto, usando al menos: una subordinada de comentario, una nominalización y dos adjetivos evaluativos C1. Grábate y comprueba que no narres la trama, sino que valores las elecciones formales.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo de respuesta oral:

*It is worth noting that the film avoids easy answers. The cinematography is restrained — lots of static wide shots — and the pacing is deliberate, almost slow, but that's the point: it builds tension through what it refuses to show. The score is haunting but used sparingly, which actually reinforces the unease. I'd call it a measured, thought-provoking piece rather than a conventional thriller. What earns the climax is precisely the patience of everything that came before.*

Criterios de la rúbrica: presencia de *it is worth noting that* (comentario), *the cinematography, the pacing* (nominalizaciones), *restrained, haunting, measured, thought-provoking* (adjetivos C1) y valoración de elecciones formales (*avoids easy answers, builds tension through what it refuses to show*) en lugar de resumen de trama.

</details>

### Ejercicio 17 — Registro: reseña vs. análisis académico

Reescribe la reseña en estilo de análisis académico (más nominalizado, sin primera persona, más abstracto).

Reseña: *It is a gripping thriller and I think the director handles silence really well. The ending is understated and it earns its payoff.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo académico: *The film's sustained tension derives in large part from the director's handling of silence; the understatement of the final sequence produces a resonance that more conventionally climactic thrillers fail to achieve, thereby earning the sequence's emotional payoff.*

Cambios: eliminación de *I think* y de *it is a gripping thriller* (valoración directa), paso a nominalizaciones (*the director's handling of silence, the understatement of the final sequence*) y registro más abstracto (*derives from, produces a resonance, fail to achieve*).

</details>

### Ejercicio 18 — Consolidación

Completa el párrafo con una palabra por hueco.

> *It is ___(1) that the film subverts the conventions of the whodunit. The ___(2) pacing of the first act and the understated ___(3) produce a tension that more ___(4) counterparts cannot match. The climax ___(5) its emotional payoff, and the sparing use of a non-diegetic ___(6) reinforces the sense of dread.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **striking** (o *significant / noteworthy*)
2. **measured** (o *deliberate / slow*)
3. **cinematography** (o *lighting / staging*)
4. **heavy-handed** (o *conventional / formulaic*)
5. **earns**
6. **score** (o *soundtrack / music*)

El párrafo integra subordinada de comentario, nominalizaciones, adjetivo evaluativo, verbo causal y vocabulario cinematográfico.

</details>

### Ejercicio 19 — Reescribe el párrafo con registro crítico C1

Eleva el registro del párrafo neutro con una subordinada de comentario, dos nominalizaciones y un adjetivo evaluativo C1.

> *The film is good. The director films it well and the music helps. The ending is quiet and it works. Other thrillers are too obvious.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

Modelo: *It is striking that the film resists the temptation to spell everything out. The measured pacing and the understated cinematography produce a tension that more heavy-handed thrillers cannot match, and the climax's quiet restraint earns its emotional payoff.*

Rúbrica: subordinada de comentario (*it is striking that*), nominalizaciones (*the measured pacing, the understated cinematography, the climax's quiet restraint*), adjetivo evaluativo C1 (*heavy-handed, understated, measured*) y verbo de valoración causal (*earns*). Se elimina la primera persona y la valoración genérica (*good*).

</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

- La **crítica fílmica** es un discurso evaluativo fundamentado: juzga cómo una obra construye sentido mediante recursos formales, no qué ocurre en ella, y la sitúa en una tradición de género.
- La **nominalización** convierte verbos y adjetivos en sustantivos abstractos (*the pacing, the cinematography, the restraint, the handling of silence*) y densifica el texto, elevándolo de B2 a C1.
- Las **subordinadas de comentario** (*it is striking that, it is worth noting that, it is no coincidence that*) anticipan la valoración antes de revelar su contenido, creando peso y expectativa.
- El **léxico evaluativo** distingue al crítico del espectador casual: adjetivos matizados (*gripping, thought-provoking, understated, heavy-handed, derivative, on-the-nose*) y verbos de valoración causal (*subvert, elevate, undermine, reinforce, earn*).
- El **vocabulario cinematográfico** (*cinematography, soundtrack, pacing, character arc, plot, subplot, climax, still, framing, voiceover, screenplay, editing, montage, diegetic sound, close-up, cross-cutting*) y sus colocaciones (*earn the payoff, fall flat, ring true, taut thriller, hinge on, subvert expectations*) constituyen el sustrato técnico del discurso crítico.
- El **género** aporta el marco comparativo: *adhere to / depart from / subvert the conventions of*, *pay homage to*, *pastiche*, *trope* permiten medir la obra contra su tradición y juzgar qué hace con ella.
