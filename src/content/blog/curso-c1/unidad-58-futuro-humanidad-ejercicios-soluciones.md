## Parte II — Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1
Completa con una expresión de hedging C1: ______ artificial general intelligence will be developed within the next fifty years, though the evidence is far from conclusive.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
It is conceivable that (also acceptable: It is plausible that / There are grounds for supposing that)
</details>

### Ejercicio 2
Traduce al inglés con hedging: "Es probable que la población mundial se estabilice durante este siglo, aunque no puede descartarse un declive."
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
It is likely that the global population will stabilise this century, though a decline cannot be ruled out.
</details>

### Ejercicio 3
Reescribe con un conector prospectivo C1: If current trends continue, warming could exceed 2 degrees.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
Should current trends persist, warming could exceed 2 degrees.
</details>

### Ejercicio 4
Completa con la colocación correcta: The longtermist argues that we have a moral duty to ______ existential catastrophes.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
avert
</details>

### Ejercicio 5
Identifica la categoría de riesgo existencial según Bostrom: Una pandemia provocada por un patógeno ingenierizado en un laboratorio.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
It is an emerging technological risk (anthropogenic, specifically an engineered pathogen), as opposed to a naturally arising pandemic, which would be a natural existential risk.
</details>

### Ejercicio 6
Reescribe con hedging más fuerte: Artificial intelligence will definitely destroy humanity.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
It is conceivable that advanced artificial intelligence could pose an existential threat to humanity, though the evidence is suggestive but far from conclusive. (The key change: replace the dogmatic "will definitely" with calibrated hedging.)
</details>

### Ejercicio 7
Traduce al inglés: "Bostrom sostiene que la reducción de los riesgos existenciales tiene una utilidad esperada extraordinariamente alta."
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
Bostrom argues that mitigating existential risks has an extraordinarily high expected utility.
</details>

### Ejercicio 8
Completa la frase: The Jevons paradox observes that improving resource efficiency may ______ total consumption rather than reduce it.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
increase (also acceptable: escalate)
</details>

### Ejercicio 9
Construye una frase C1 usando "subject to the caveat that" sobre el futuro de la energía.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
Renewable energy could plausibly meet the bulk of global demand by mid-century, subject to the caveat that battery storage scales faster than current trajectories suggest.
</details>

### Ejercicio 10
Reescribe con un modulador de probabilidad C1: Maybe space colonisation will reduce existential risk.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
There is a non-negligible chance that space colonisation would meaningfully reduce existential risk.
</details>

### Ejercicio 11
Traduce al inglés: "Una civilización multiplanetaria sería robusta frente a catástrofes de un solo planeta."
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
A multiplanetary civilisation would be robust to single-planet catastrophes.
</details>

### Ejercicio 12
Identifica el error de razonamiento: "No podemos predecir el futuro a largo plazo, por tanto no debemos preocuparnos por él."
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
This commits a non sequitur: the inability to predict the long-term future with precision does not entail that we should disregard it. Longtermists argue that even under radical uncertainty, some interventions (such as existential-risk reduction) have sufficiently high expected value to warrant prioritisation.
</details>

### Ejercicio 13
Completa con el conector adecuado: ______ this scenario were to materialise, the downstream consequences would be profound.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
Were
</details>

### Ejercicio 14
Reescribe con registro C1: We don't know if people will live much longer, but it might happen.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
Whether radical life extension will materialise remains uncertain, though it cannot be discounted. (Also acceptable: The prospect of radical life extension is speculative but not dismissable.)
</details>

### Ejercicio 15
Traduce al inglés con hedging: "La evidencia sugiere tentativamente que la curva de Kuznets ambiental podría no generalizarse a todos los contaminantes."
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
The evidence tentatively suggests that the environmental Kuznets curve may not generalise across all pollutants.
</details>

### Ejercicio 16
Explica en inglés C1 (60-80 palabras) por qué el hedging es esencial al especular sobre los riesgos de la inteligencia artificial general.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
Hedging is indispensable when speculating about artificial general intelligence because the relevant evidence is sparse, the timelines are deeply contested and the underlying mechanisms remain theoretically unsettled. Asserting that catastrophe is imminent would overstate the warrant; dismissing the risk entirely would understate it. Calibrated formulations, it is conceivable that, there is a non-negligible chance, preserve epistemic honesty while still licensing serious preparatory action under uncertainty.
</details>

### Ejercicio 17
Completa con la colocación: The futurologist attempts to ______ plausible scenarios rather than predict a single outcome.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
map
</details>

### Ejercicio 18
Reescribe con un conector concesivo C1: Longtermism has powerful arguments. Many ethicists remain unconvinced.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
Notwithstanding the powerful arguments advanced for longtermism, a substantial number of ethicists remain unconvinced.
</details>

### Ejercicio 19
Redacta en inglés C1 (60-80 palabras) un breve argumento sobre si la colonización espacial debería ser una prioridad moral frente a los problemas terrestres.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
Whether space colonisation merits moral prioritisation over terrestrial welfare deficits turns on contested empirical and normative assumptions. Proponents argue that existential-risk mitigation via multiplanetary redundancy safeguards an astronomical number of possible future lives, yielding extraordinary expected value. Critics counter that the opportunity costs are prohibitive and the technical feasibility uncertain, whilst present suffering commands more immediate moral salience. A defensible position may hedge: pursue both, but not at the expense of urgent welfare today.
</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

En esta unidad has aprendido a especular con prudencia sobre el futuro de la humanidad en inglés C1. Has explorado el marco de los riesgos existenciales de Bostrom, el debate entre longtermism y sus críticos, las trayectorias civilizatorias y la singularidad tecnológica. A nivel lingüístico, has consolidado el hedging con expresiones como it is plausible that, cannot be ruled out y there are grounds for supposing, junto con conectores prospectivos como should current trends persist y were this scenario to materialise. Has dominado colocaciones futuroológicas como to map plausible scenarios, to avert an existential catastrophe y to foreground epistemic uncertainty. Los 19 ejercicios te han entrenado en el razonamiento prospectivo con la humildad epistémica que define el registro avanzado. En la siguiente unidad, la 59, consolidarás de manera sistémica toda la gramática del nivel C1 en un repaso global antes de la preparación para el examen.
