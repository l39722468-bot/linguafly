## Parte II — Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1
Sustituye el sinónimo latino por el phrasal verbal formal adecuado: The reforms caused a profound transformation of the sector.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
The reforms brought about a profound transformation of the sector.
</details>

### Ejercicio 2
Completa la colocación: The committee will ______ a proposal at the next meeting.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
put forward
</details>

### Ejercicio 3
Reescribe con to rule out: The detective excluded the possibility of suicide.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
The detective ruled out the possibility of suicide.
</details>

### Ejercicio 4
Elige la opción gramaticalmente correcta (separabilidad): The committee turned ______ the proposal. / The committee turned the proposal ______.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
Both are correct: "The committee turned down the proposal" and "The committee turned the proposal down." However, with a pronoun, only "The committee turned it down" is correct (not "turned down it").
</details>

### Ejercicio 5
Completa con to account for: How do you ______ the discrepancy between the two datasets?
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
account for
</details>

### Ejercicio 6
Sustituye por el phrasal verb formal: The problem originates in a lack of regulation.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
The problem stems from a lack of regulation.
</details>

### Ejercicio 7
Reescribe con to spell out: The report did not make the implications explicit.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
The report did not spell out the implications.
</details>

### Ejercicio 8
Identifica el phrasal verb inapropiado para un ensayo académico y propón una alternativa formal: The government chucked out the proposal.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
"Chuck out" is too informal for academic prose. Formal alternative: The government discarded the proposal (or: The government rejected the proposal).
</details>

### Ejercicio 9
Completa con to boil down to: The whole debate ______ a question of priorities.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
boils down to
</details>

### Ejercicio 10
Reescribe con to set out: The minister presented the reasons for the policy in a structured manner.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
The minister set out the reasons for the policy.
</details>

### Ejercicio 11
Completa con el pronombre correcto (separabilidad obligatoria): They put the offer forward → They put ______ forward.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
it (They put it forward. Separable phrasal verbs must split with pronouns: not "put forward it.")
</details>

### Ejercicio 12
Sustituye por el phrasal verb formal: The evidence confirmed the hypothesis.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
The evidence bore out the hypothesis.
</details>

### Ejercicio 13
Completa con to point to: Several indicators ______ a forthcoming recession.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
point to
</details>

### Ejercicio 14
Reescribe con un phrasal verb de tres partes (formal-neutro): The team could not devise a solution in time.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
The team could not come up with a solution in time.
</details>

### Ejercicio 15
Elige entre el phrasal verb y el sinónimo latino según el registro (ensayo académico denso): "The findings ___ a reconsideration of the theory."
a) point to   b) indicate
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
Both are acceptable, but in a lexically dense academic essay, "indicate" (b) may be stylistically preferred for its compactness. In oral debate, "point to" (a) would be more idiomatic. Either is grammatically correct at C1.
</details>

### Ejercicio 16
Explica en inglés C1 (60-80 palabras) cuándo preferirías to put forward sobre to propose.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
I would prefer to put forward in oral debate or semi-formal discussion, where its idiomatic fluency lends the argument a natural, persuasive rhythm without sacrificing register. To propose, by contrast, carries a more markedly formal, even bureaucratic tone, better suited to dense academic prose or official documentation. The choice is ultimately registeral: to put forward for spoken fluency, to propose for written formality, though both remain grammatically impeccable at C1.
</details>

### Ejercicio 17
Completa con to build on o to draw on: The second study ______ the findings of the first.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
builds on (to build on = to extend or develop further; to draw on = to use as a resource. "Builds on" fits extending findings; "draws on" would fit using findings as a resource.)
</details>

### Ejercicio 18
Reescribe con to follow through on: The government did not complete its commitment to reform.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
The government did not follow through on its commitment to reform.
</details>

### Ejercicio 19
Redacta en inglés C1 (60-80 palabras) un breve argumento que use al menos tres phrasal verbs formales distintos.
<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
The current crisis stems from a decade of regulatory neglect, and no partial measure can bring about the transformation required. The government must put forward a comprehensive proposal that sets out the reasons for reform and rules out the dilatory compromises of the past. Unless policymakers spell out the implications of inaction, the public will not grasp the stakes, and the opportunity for meaningful change will be forfeited.
</details>

## Resumen de la unidad

En esta unidad del Language Lab has entrenado el uso de phrasal verbs de registro formal en contextos argumentativos. Has aprendido la anatomía del phrasal verb, la distinción entre separables e inseparables, la existencia de phrasal verbs de tres partes y la frontera entre phrasal verbs formales e informales. Has consolidado colocaciones como to bring about a change, to put forward a proposal, to rule out a possibility, to account for a discrepancy y to stem from a problem. Has practicado la alternancia con sinónimos latinos (to bring about / to cause, to put forward / to propose, to rule out / to exclude) según el registro, una habilidad distintiva del C1. Los 19 ejercicios te han entrenado en la reformulación y el uso idiomático. En la siguiente unidad, la 62, el Language Lab se centra en los patrones preposicionales de verbo y adjetivo, otro territorio donde la precisión C1 se gana o se pierde.
