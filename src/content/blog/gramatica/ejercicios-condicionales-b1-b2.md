## Bloque 1 — Second Conditional (hipótesis presentes)

Estructura: **If + past simple, would + infinitivo**

### Ejercicio 1: Completa con la forma correcta

1. If I _______ (be) you, I would apologize.
2. She would travel more if she _______ (have) time.
3. If we _______ (win) the lottery, we would buy a house.
4. What would you do if you _______ (lose) your phone?

### Ejercicio 2: Corrige y completa

5. If I would have more money, I would study abroad. → _______________
6. If he were more confident, _______.

### Ejercicio 3: Transforma usando Second Conditional

7. She is shy, so she does not speak in class.
8. I do not know her number, so I cannot call her.

**Soluciones:**
1. were
2. had
3. won
4. lost
5. If I had more money, I would study abroad.
6. he would apply for that job
7. If she were not shy, she would speak in class.
8. If I knew her number, I could call her.

---

## Bloque 2 — Third Conditional (hipótesis pasadas)

Estructura: **If + past perfect, would have + past participle**

### Ejercicio 4: Completa con la forma correcta

1. If they _______ (leave) earlier, they would have caught the train.
2. She would have passed if she _______ (study) more.
3. If I had seen you, I _______ (say) hello.
4. If we had known, we _______ (not come).

### Ejercicio 5: Transforma usando Third Conditional

5. He missed the alarm. He arrived late.
6. I did not save money, so I did not buy the laptop.

### Ejercicio 6: Corrige y escribe

7. If you would have told me, I would have helped. → _______________
8. Escribe una oración en Third Conditional sobre un arrepentimiento del pasado.

**Soluciones:**
1. had left
2. had studied
3. would have said
4. wouldn't have come
5. If he had not missed the alarm, he would have arrived on time.
6. If I had saved money, I would have bought the laptop.
7. If you had told me, I would have helped.
8. Respuesta abierta (ej.: *If I had studied harder, I would have passed the exam.*)

## Resumen rápido

| Tipo | Estructura | Uso |
|------|-----------|-----|
| Second | if + past simple → would + inf. | Hipótesis presente irreal |
| Third | if + past perfect → would have + pp. | Hipótesis pasada irreal |
