## Bloque 1 — Inversión condicional y registro formal

Estructura: **Had / Were / Should + sujeto + verbo** (sin *if*)

### Ejercicio 1: Reescribe usando inversión condicional

1. If I had known, I would have acted differently.
2. If you need any help, contact me.
3. If it were not for your support, we would fail.

### Ejercicio 2: Completa con Had, Were o Should

4. _______ I known about the delay, I would have changed plans.
5. _______ you require further details, let us know.
6. _______ it not for this grant, the project would collapse.

### Ejercicio 3: Corrige y elige la opción formal

7. Had I knew this, I would not accept. → _______________
8. Elige la opción formal: *if / should / gonna* → _______________

**Soluciones:**
1. Had I known, I would have acted differently.
2. Should you need any help, contact me.
3. Were it not for your support, we would fail.
4. Had
5. Should
6. Were
7. Had I known this, I would not have accepted.
8. should

## Bloque 2 — Mixed conditionals y equivalentes avanzados

Estructuras: **Third + Second / Second + Third / but for / otherwise**

### Ejercicio 4: Identifica el tipo de mixed conditional

1. If I had studied medicine, I would be a doctor now.
2. If she were more organized, she would have finished yesterday.

### Ejercicio 5: Reescribe con la estructura indicada

3. If it had not been for your help, we would have failed. → (usa *but for*)
4. If you do not submit today, your place will be lost. → (usa *otherwise*)

### Ejercicio 6: Completa y transforma

5. If he _______ (not be) so stubborn, he would have listened yesterday.
6. If they had invested in 2020, they _______ (be) profitable now.
7. I am not patient, so I argued last night. → (mixed conditional)
8. Escribe una oración con *Were I to...*

**Soluciones:**
1. Third + Second (pasado irreal → consecuencia presente)
2. Second + Third (condición presente → consecuencia pasada)
3. But for your help, we would have failed.
4. Submit today; otherwise, your place will be lost.
5. were not
6. would be
7. If I were more patient, I would not have argued last night.
8. Respuesta abierta (ej.: *Were I to accept the offer, I would relocate immediately.*)

### Criterio de revisión avanzada

Subraya condición y consecuencia en cada respuesta y anota si el efecto es
pasado, presente o futuro. *If I had accepted that job, I would be living
abroad now* mezcla pasado y presente; no la confundas con *would have been
living*, que sitúa el resultado en el pasado. En un ensayo, alterna
*provided that*, *on condition that*, *unless* y *otherwise* solo si cambia la
relación lógica. Revisa también el registro: la inversión (*Had I known...*)
es especialmente natural en informes, pero *If I had known...* es correcta en
conversación. Lee el párrafo en voz alta para comprobar claridad y ritmo.

Un último reto: reescribe una condición con *but for* y otra con *should*. Si
el significado cambia de probabilidad o de registro, explica el matiz en una
frase en español antes de entregar el ejercicio.

## Reto avanzado: inversión y matiz

En escritura formal puedes eliminar *if*: *Had I known the risks, I would have
refused*; *Were the plan to fail, we would need a backup*; *Should you require
assistance, contact reception*. **But for** equivale a “de no ser por” y
suena más formal. Completa: 1) *Had they ______ (check) the figures, they
______ (avoid) the error.* 2) *If I had taken the course, I ______ (feel) more
confident now.* 3) *If demand rises, the company ______ expand* (modal de
posibilidad).

**Soluciones:** 1) *checked, would have avoided*. 2) *would feel* (mixed
conditional). 3) *might/may/could*. *Will* expresaría más certeza. Comprueba
que la inversión solo aparece con auxiliares adecuados y que *would* no va en
la cláusula de condición. En un texto C1-C2 alterna *unless*, *provided that*
y *otherwise* solo cuando cada conector representa exactamente la relación
lógica.
