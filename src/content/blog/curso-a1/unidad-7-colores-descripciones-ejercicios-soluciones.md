## Lección 1 — Gramática: adjetivos y *have got*

**Objetivo:** Adjetivo delante del nombre; *to be* + adjetivo; *have/has* para pelo y ojos.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. She ____ tall. → *is / are / am*  
2. He ____ got long brown hair. → *has / have / is*  
3. My car ____ red. → *is / are / am*  
4. She is ____ for her age. → *young / tall / short / old*  
5. They ____ blue eyes. → *have / has / is*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is** — *to be* + adjetivo con *she*.  
2. **has** — *he/she/it* → *has (got)*.  
3. **is**  
4. **young**  
5. **have** — *they* → *have*.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. Which sentence is correct?  
   a) She has long hair. · b) She has hair long. · c) She is long hair.  
7. Where do adjectives go in English?  
   a) After the noun. · b) Before the noun. · c) At the end.  
8. He ____ short black hair.  
   a) has · b) is · c) have  
9. The sky ____ blue.  
   a) is · b) are · c) have  
10. She ____ green eyes.  
    a) has · b) is · c) are

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) She has long hair.**  
7. **b) Before the noun.**  
8. **a) has**  
9. **a) is**  
10. **a) has**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. He / is / tall.  
12. She / has / long / brown / hair.  
13. The / car / is / blue.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. He is tall.  
12. She has long brown hair.  
13. The car is blue.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. In English we put adjectives before nouns. → ?  
15. We use have got for hair and eyes in descriptions. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: colores y rasgos físicos

**Objetivo:** Colores básicos y adjetivos de descripción física.

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige la palabra correcta

1. What color is the sky on a sunny day? → a) red · b) blue · c) green · d) yellow  
2. The opposite of tall is ____. → a) young · b) short · c) old · d) long  
3. We use ____ to describe hair length. → a) tall · b) long · c) old · d) blue  
4. The opposite of young is ____. → a) short · b) tall · c) old · d) long  
5. What word describes the colour of grass? → a) red · b) blue · c) green · d) yellow  
6. Brown hair is ____ in Spanish. → a) rubio · b) castaño · c) negro · d) pelirrojo

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b) blue**  
2. **b) short**  
3. **b) long**  
4. **c) old**  
5. **c) green**  
6. **b) castaño**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–10 — Une con su significado

**7.** tall · short · young · old · long  
→ alto · bajo · joven · mayor/viejo · largo

**8.** red · blue · green · yellow · black  
→ rojo · azul · verde · amarillo · negro

**9.** white · brown · hair · eyes · dark  
→ blanco · marrón/castaño · pelo · ojos · oscuro

**10.** She has long hair. · He is tall. · Her eyes are blue. · My brother is young. · The car is red.  
→ Ella tiene el pelo largo. · Él es alto. · Sus ojos son azules. · Mi hermano es joven. · El coche es rojo.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

7. tall→alto · short→bajo · young→joven · old→mayor/viejo · long→largo  
8. red→rojo · blue→azul · green→verde · yellow→amarillo · black→negro  
9. white→blanco · brown→marrón/castaño · hair→pelo · eyes→ojos · dark→oscuro  
10. She has long hair.→Ella tiene el pelo largo. · He is tall.→Él es alto. · Her eyes are blue.→Sus ojos son azules. · My brother is young.→Mi hermano es joven. · The car is red.→El coche es rojo.

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Completa

11. She has ____ hair. It is not short. → *long / tall / old / blue*  
12. My grandfather is ____. He is seventy years old. → *young / tall / old / short*  
13. His ____ are green. → *hair / eyes / hand / face*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **long**  
12. **old**  
13. **eyes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Contexto

14. You describe a person who is not short. She ____.  
    a) is short · b) is tall · c) is old · d) is young  
15. You say the colour of snow. It is ____.  
    a) red · b) blue · c) white · d) black

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **b) is tall**  
15. **c) white**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: My best friend

**Objetivo:** Leer una descripción física corta de una amiga.

### Texto

2. What color is the friend's hair? → a) black · b) brown · c) blonde  
3. What color are the friend's eyes? → a) blue · b) brown · c) green  
4. How old is the friend? → a) thirty · b) twenty · c) eighteen  
5. Is the hair long or short? → a) short · b) long · c) medium

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **c) tall**  
2. **b) brown**  
3. **c) green**  
4. **b) twenty**  
5. **b) long**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–9 — Verdadero o falso

6. The person described is a man. → ?  
7. She has green eyes. → ?  
8. She is forty years old. → ?

## Lección 4 — Listening: Lisa and Tom

**Objetivo:** Escuchar un diálogo con descripciones físicas de familiares.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-7/listening-lisa-tom.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Lisa and Tom"></audio>

> **Lisa:** Who is that woman with the long red hair?  
> **Tom:** She is my sister Emma.  
> **Lisa:** She is very tall. How old is she?  
> **Tom:** She is twenty-five years old. She has blue eyes too.  
> **Lisa:** My brother is short and he has brown hair.  
> **Tom:** Is he young?  
> **Lisa:** Yes, he is only eighteen.

### Ejercicios 1–8 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What color is Emma's hair? → a) brown · b) red · c) black · d) blonde  
2. Who is Emma? → a) Tom's friend · b) Tom's sister · c) Tom's mother · d) Lisa's sister  
3. Is Emma tall or short? → a) short · b) tall · c) medium  
4. How old is Emma? → a) twenty · b) twenty-five · c) thirty · d) eighteen  
5. What color are Emma's eyes? → a) green · b) brown · c) blue · d) black  
6. Lisa's brother is ____. → a) tall · b) short · c) old  
7. What color is Lisa's brother's hair? → a) black · b) red · c) brown · d) blonde  
8. How old is Lisa's brother? → a) twenty-five · b) twenty · c) eighteen · d) thirty

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b) red**  
2. **b) Tom's sister**  
3. **b) tall**  
4. **b) twenty-five**  
5. **c) blue**  
6. **b) short**  
7. **c) brown**  
8. **c) eighteen**

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Verdadero o falso

9. Emma has black hair. → ?  
10. Tom and Lisa talk about physical descriptions. → ?  
11. Lisa's brother is tall. → ?  
12. Emma is Tom's sister. → ?

<details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: Physical descriptions

**Objetivo:** Escribir descripciones cortas con *to be*, colores y *have/has*.

### Modelo

> I have long brown hair.  
> My eyes are green. / I have green eyes.  
> I am tall. I am twenty-two years old.  
> My sister is tall. She has long black hair and brown eyes.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. The sky is ____. (*blue*)  
2. She ____ long brown hair. (*has / have*)  
3. My brother is ____. He is not tall. (*short*)  
4. Her eyes ____ green. (*is / are*)  
5. She is ____ — only twenty years old. (*young*)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **blue**  
2. **has**  
3. **short**  
4. **are**  
5. **young**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. and / beautiful. / She / is / tall  
7. He / has / blue / eyes.  
8. My / car / is / red.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. She is tall and beautiful.  
7. He has blue eyes.  
8. My car is red.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. Describe your hair (*I have … hair*).  
10. Describe your eyes (*My eyes are … / I have … eyes*).  
11. Height + age (*I am … / I am … years old*).  
12. A family member or friend: height, hair, eyes.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I have long brown hair.  
10. My eyes are brown. / I have green eyes.  
11. I am tall. I am twenty-two years old.  
12. My sister is tall. She has long black hair and brown eyes.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Describe your best friend (4–6 sentences).  
14. Describe two family members.  
15. Colours you like and colours of things you have.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
My best friend is Ana. She is tall and young. She has long brown hair. Her eyes are green. She is twenty years old. She is very beautiful.

**14.**  
My mother is tall. She has short brown hair and blue eyes. My brother is short. He has black hair and brown eyes. He is young — only eighteen years old.

**15.**  
I like blue and green. My room is white. My bag is black. I have brown hair and green eyes. My favourite colour is blue.

</details>
