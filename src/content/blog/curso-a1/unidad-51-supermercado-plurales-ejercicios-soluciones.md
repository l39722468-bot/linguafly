## Lección 1 — Gramática: plurales

**Objetivo:** plurales regulares (*apples, tomatoes, potatoes*) e irregulares básicos (*children, people*); *fish*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I need two ____ for the salad. → *tomatoes / tomatos / tomato*  
2. We have three ____ in the fridge. → *potatoes / potatos / potato*  
3. There are many ____ in the fruit section. → *apples / apple / appls*  
4. I want two ____ of fish. → *pieces / piece / fishs*  
5. How many ____ do you need? → *eggs / egg / egges*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **tomatoes** — *tomato → tomatoes* (*-es*).  
2. **potatoes** — *potato → potatoes*.  
3. **apples** — plural regular *-s*.  
4. **pieces** — *two pieces of fish*.  
5. **eggs**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Plural of *child* = ____. → a) children · b) childs · c) childes  
7. Correct plural of *person*: ____. → a) people · b) persons (usual A1) · c) persones  
8. We add *-es* for plural when ____. → a) the word ends in *-s, -ch, -sh, -x* (or *-o* like tomato) · b) the word is short · c) always  
9. *Fish* as food (plural) = ____. → a) fish (same) · b) fishs · c) fishes only  
10. *Vegetable* plural = ____. → a) vegetables · b) vegetable · c) vegetabls

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) children**  
7. **a) people**  
8. **a) the word ends in *-s, -ch, -sh, -x* (or *-o* like tomato)**  
9. **a) fish (same)**  
10. **a) vegetables**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. The / apples / are / in / aisle / five.  
12. I need / some / meat / and / fish.  
13. Where are / the / vegetables?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. The apples are in aisle five.  
12. I need some meat and fish.  
13. Where are the vegetables?

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *Tomato* and *potato* add *-es* for the plural. → True / False  
15. We say *two fish* for two pieces of fish. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: supermercado

**Objetivo:** *fruit, vegetables, meat, fish, aisle, trolley, checkout, basket, shelf*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *Fruit* = ____. → a) apples, bananas, oranges · b) meat · c) bread  
2. *Vegetables* = ____. → a) carrots, tomatoes, potatoes · b) fish · c) milk  
3. *Meat* = ____. → a) chicken, beef, pork · b) fish · c) eggs  
4. *Fish* = ____. → a) salmon, tuna, cod · b) chicken · c) bread  
5. *Aisle* = ____. → a) space between shelves in a supermarket · b) checkout · c) basket

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) apples, bananas, oranges**  
2. **a) carrots, tomatoes, potatoes**  
3. **a) chicken, beef, pork**  
4. **a) salmon, tuna, cod**  
5. **a) space between shelves in a supermarket**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Empareja

6. Empareja: apple / carrot / chicken / salmon / aisle → fruit · vegetable · meat · fish · between shelves  
7. Empareja: banana / tomato / beef / tuna / checkout → fruit · vegetable · meat · fish · pay here

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **apple → fruit** · **carrot → vegetable** · **chicken → meat** · **salmon → fish** · **aisle → between shelves**  
7. **banana → fruit** · **tomato → vegetable** · **beef → meat** · **tuna → fish** · **checkout → pay here**

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–13 — Completa / elige

8. The ____ are in aisle three. → *vegetables / meat / fish*  
9. I need some ____ and chicken. → *beef / apples / bread*  
10. Where is the ____ section? → *fruit / meat / fish*  
11. *Basket* = ____. → a) a container to carry shopping · b) an aisle · c) checkout  
12. *Trolley* = ____. → a) a cart for many items · b) a basket · c) a shelf  
13. *Shelf* = ____. → a) a flat surface for products · b) an aisle · c) a basket

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **vegetables**  
9. **beef**  
10. **fruit**  
11. **a) a container to carry shopping**  
12. **a) a cart for many items**  
13. **a) a flat surface for products**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. An aisle is where you find products on shelves. → True / False  
15. Meat and fish are usually in different sections. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Every Saturday

**Objetivo:** leer sobre una compra en el supermercado.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-51/reading-supermarket.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Every Saturday"></audio>

> I go to the supermarket every Saturday. First I get a trolley. The fruit and vegetables are in aisle one. I buy apples, bananas and tomatoes. The meat and fish are at the back. I need some chicken and two pieces of fish. Then I pay at the checkout.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. When does the writer go to the supermarket? → a) Every Saturday · b) Every Sunday · c) Every day  
2. What does he/she get first? → a) A trolley · b) A basket · c) Fruit  
3. Where are fruit and vegetables? → a) In aisle one · b) At the back · c) At checkout  
4. Which fruit/veg does he/she buy? → a) Apples, bananas, tomatoes · b) Only apples · c) Oranges  
5. Where are meat and fish? → a) At the back · b) In aisle one · c) At the entrance

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Every Saturday**  
2. **a) A trolley**  
3. **a) In aisle one**  
4. **a) Apples, bananas, tomatoes**  
5. **a) At the back**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. What meat does he/she need? → a) Chicken · b) Beef · c) Pork  
7. How many pieces of fish? → a) Two · b) One · c) Three  
8. Where does he/she pay? → a) At the checkout · b) In aisle one · c) At the back  
9. Which word means *pasillo*? → a) Aisle · b) Checkout · c) Trolley  
10. *Trolley* means ____. → a) shopping cart · b) basket · c) shelf

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Chicken**  
7. **a) Two**  
8. **a) At the checkout**  
9. **a) Aisle**  
10. **a) shopping cart**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text is about ____. → a) shopping at the supermarket · b) cooking · c) a restaurant  
12. *Checkout* means ____. → a) where you pay · b) aisle · c) fruit section  
13. *At the back* means ____. → a) at the end of the shop · b) at the entrance · c) in aisle one  
14. *Vegetables* means ____. → a) carrots, tomatoes, etc. · b) meat · c) fish  
15. The main topic is ____. → a) a supermarket shopping trip · b) cooking at home · c) eating at a restaurant

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) shopping at the supermarket**  
12. **a) where you pay**  
13. **a) at the end of the shop**  
14. **a) carrots, tomatoes, etc.**  
15. **a) a supermarket shopping trip**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Where are the vegetables?

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo para encontrar productos.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-51/listening-supermarket.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Where are the vegetables?"></audio>

> **A:** Where are the vegetables?  
> **B:** In aisle two.  
> **A:** And the fruit?  
> **B:** Aisle one, next to the entrance.  
> **A:** I need some chicken and fish.  
> **B:** Meat and fish are at the back.  
> **A:** Thank you. Where is the checkout?  
> **B:** Over there. Get a trolley if you have many things.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What does A ask first? → a) Where are the vegetables? · b) Where is the fruit? · c) Where is the checkout?  
2. Which aisle for vegetables? → a) Aisle two · b) Aisle one · c) Aisle three  
3. Where is the fruit? → a) Aisle one, next to the entrance · b) At the back · c) Aisle two  
4. What does A need? → a) Chicken and fish · b) Vegetables only · c) Fruit only  
5. Where are meat and fish? → a) At the back · b) Aisle one · c) At checkout

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Where are the vegetables?**  
2. **a) Aisle two**  
3. **a) Aisle one, next to the entrance**  
4. **a) Chicken and fish**  
5. **a) At the back**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. What does A say to B? → a) Thank you · b) Goodbye · c) Sorry  
7. Where is the checkout? → a) Over there · b) Aisle one · c) At the back  
8. What does B suggest if A has many things? → a) Get a trolley · b) Get a basket · c) Go to aisle one  
9. The dialogue is at ____. → a) the supermarket · b) a restaurant · c) home  
10. *Aisle* means ____. → a) space between shelves · b) checkout · c) entrance

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Thank you**  
7. **a) Over there**  
8. **a) Get a trolley**  
9. **a) the supermarket**  
10. **a) space between shelves**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Trolley* means ____. → a) shopping cart · b) basket · c) shelf  
12. *At the back* means ____. → a) at the end of the shop · b) at the entrance · c) at checkout  
13. *Checkout* means ____. → a) where you pay · b) aisle · c) fruit section  
14. *Next to the entrance* means ____. → a) near the door · b) at the back · c) at checkout  
15. The dialogue is about ____. → a) finding things in the supermarket · b) cooking · c) eating out

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) shopping cart**  
12. **a) at the end of the shop**  
13. **a) where you pay**  
14. **a) near the door**  
15. **a) finding things in the supermarket**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My shopping

**Objetivo:** escribir sobre una compra con plurales y vocabulario de tienda.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. The ____ are in aisle one. → *vegetables / vegetable / vegetabls*  
2. I need two ____ of fish. → *pieces / piece / fishs*  
3. We have three ____ and four tomatoes. → *potatoes / potatos / potato*  
4. Where are the ____? → *apples / apple / fish*  
5. Meat and fish are ____ the back. → *at / in / on*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **vegetables**  
2. **pieces**  
3. **potatoes**  
4. **apples**  
5. **at**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. The / vegetables / are / in / aisle / two.  
7. I need / some / chicken / and / fish.  
8. Where is / the / checkout?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. The vegetables are in aisle two.  
7. I need some chicken and fish.  
8. Where is the checkout?

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Dónde está la fruta (*aisle one*).  
10. Qué necesitas comprar (*chicken, fish* o *vegetables*).  
11. Dos frases: qué compras y dónde pagas.  
12. Pregunta *Where are the vegetables?* y respuesta *In aisle two.*  
13. Párrafo corto (3–4 frases): trolley, pasillos, fruta/verdura, caja.  
14. 2–3 frases: qué hay en cada pasillo.  
15. Lista de la compra (3–4 cosas) y dónde las encuentras.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. The fruit is in aisle one.  
10. I need some chicken and two pieces of fish.  
11. I buy apples and tomatoes. I pay at the checkout.  
12. Where are the vegetables? In aisle two.  
13. I go to the supermarket and get a trolley. The fruit and vegetables are in aisle one. I pay at the checkout.  
14. The fruit is in aisle one. Vegetables are in aisle two. Meat and fish are at the back.  
15. I need apples, tomatoes, chicken and fish. Fruit is in aisle one. Meat is at the back.

</details>
