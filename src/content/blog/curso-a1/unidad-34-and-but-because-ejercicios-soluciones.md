## Lección 1 — Gramática: and, but, because

**Objetivo:** unir ideas con *and* (suma), *but* (contraste) y *because* (razón).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I like coffee ____ tea. → *and / but / because*  
2. I like football ____ I don't play it. → *but / and / because*  
3. I stay at home ____ I am tired. → *because / but / and*  
4. She is tall ____ her brother is short. → *but / and / because*  
5. We eat pasta ____ we like it. → *because / but / and*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **and** — suma dos gustos.  
2. **but** — contraste.  
3. **because** — razón (*because* + sujeto + verbo).  
4. **but**  
5. **because**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *And* adds ____. → a) another idea (same direction) · b) a contrast · c) a reason  
7. *But* shows ____. → a) contrast or difference · b) addition · c) reason  
8. *Because* introduces ____. → a) a reason or cause · b) a contrast · c) addition  
9. I like tea ____ I don't like coffee. → a) but · b) and · c) because  
10. I'm tired ____ I didn't sleep well. → a) because · b) but · c) and

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) another idea (same direction)**  
7. **a) contrast or difference**  
8. **a) a reason or cause**  
9. **a) but**  
10. **a) because**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. I / like / pizza / and / pasta.  
12. She / is / tired / because / she / worked / hard.  
13. I / like / swimming / but / I / don't / like / running.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I like pizza and pasta.  
12. She is tired because she worked hard.  
13. I like swimming but I don't like running.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use *because* + subject + verb: *I stay home because I am tired.* → True / False  
15. *But* connects contrasting ideas. *And* connects similar or adding ideas. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True** — no digas *because tired*.  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: conectores

**Objetivo:** significado y uso de *and*, *but*, *because* (y palabras relacionadas).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *And* = ____. → a) y / add ideas · b) pero / contrast · c) porque / reason  
2. *But* = ____. → a) pero / contrast · b) y / add · c) porque / reason  
3. *Because* = ____. → a) porque / reason · b) pero / contrast · c) y / add  
4. After *because* we need ____. → a) subject + verb · b) only an adjective · c) nothing  
5. For contrast we use ____. → a) but · b) and · c) or

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) y / add ideas**  
2. **a) pero / contrast**  
3. **a) porque / reason**  
4. **a) subject + verb**  
5. **a) but**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Empareja

6. Empareja conector → función: and / but / because / so / or → add ideas · contrast · reason · result · alternative  
7. Empareja EN → ES: and / but / because / reason / contrast → y / pero / porque / razón / contraste

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **and → add ideas** · **but → contrast** · **because → reason** · **so → result** · **or → alternative**  
7. **and → y** · **but → pero** · **because → porque** · **reason → razón** · **contrast → contraste**

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. I like dogs ____ cats. → *and / but / because*  
9. I like tea ____ I don't like coffee. → *but / and / because*  
10. She is sad ____ she lost her phone. → *because / but / and*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **and**  
9. **but**  
10. **because**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Definiciones

11. *Reason* means ____. → a) why something happens · b) a difference · c) an alternative  
12. *Contrast* means ____. → a) difference between two things · b) the same idea · c) a result  
13. Which word means "porque"? → a) because · b) but · c) and

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) why something happens**  
12. **a) difference between two things**  
13. **a) because**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena

14. I / like / tea / and / coffee.  
15. She / is / sad / because / she / lost / her / phone.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. I like tea and coffee.  
15. She is sad because she lost her phone.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Leo

**Objetivo:** leer un texto con *and*, *but* y *because*.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-34/reading-leo.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Leo"></audio>

> My name is Leo. I like sport and music. I play football but I don't like watching it on TV. I prefer to play outside. I like pizza because it is easy to make and tasty. I don't like fish but I eat it sometimes because it's healthy. At weekends I meet friends and we go to the cinema or we play video games.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the writer's name? → a) Leo · b) Liam · c) Luke  
2. What does Leo like? → a) Sport and music · b) Only sport · c) Only music  
3. Does he like watching football on TV? → a) No · b) Yes · c) Sometimes  
4. Why does he like pizza? → a) Because it's easy to make and tasty · b) Because it's cheap · c) Because it's healthy  
5. Does he like fish? → a) No, but he eats it sometimes · b) Yes, always · c) No, never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Leo**  
2. **a) Sport and music**  
3. **a) No**  
4. **a) Because it's easy to make and tasty**  
5. **a) No, but he eats it sometimes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Why does he eat fish sometimes? → a) Because it's healthy · b) Because he loves it · c) The text doesn't say  
7. What does he do at weekends? → a) Meet friends and go to the cinema or play video games · b) Only study · c) Only work  
8. Which connector shows contrast? → a) But · b) And · c) Or  
9. Which connector introduces a reason? → a) Because · b) And · c) Or  
10. Leo ____ play football. → a) likes to · b) doesn't · c) never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Because it's healthy**  
7. **a) Meet friends and go to the cinema or play video games**  
8. **a) But**  
9. **a) Because**  
10. **a) likes to**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. Connectors are used ____. → a) to connect ideas · b) only for food · c) only for sport  
12. Which word means "porque"? → a) Because · b) But · c) And  
13. What is the main topic? → a) Leo's likes and reasons, using and/but/because · b) Leo's job · c) Leo's family  
14. He prefers ____. → a) to play outside · b) to watch TV · c) to stay home  
15. At weekends he ____ friends. → a) meets · b) meet · c) meeting

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) to connect ideas**  
12. **a) Because**  
13. **a) Leo's likes and reasons, using and/but/because**  
14. **a) to play outside**  
15. **a) meets**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: New job

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo con *and*, *but* y *because*.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-34/listening-job.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: New job"></audio>

> **A:** Do you like your new job?  
> **B:** Yes, I like it because it's interesting and I meet nice people. But I don't like getting up early.  
> **A:** What about the team?  
> **B:** They are friendly and helpful. I am happy because we work well together.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Does B like his new job? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
2. Why does B like the job? → a) Because it's interesting and he meets nice people · b) Because he gets up late · c) The audio doesn't say  
3. What does B not like? → a) Getting up early · b) The team · c) The people  
4. How are the team? → a) Friendly and helpful · b) Busy · c) Strict  
5. Why is B happy? → a) Because they work well together · b) Because he gets up early · c) The audio doesn't say

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Yes**  
2. **a) Because it's interesting and he meets nice people**  
3. **a) Getting up early**  
4. **a) Friendly and helpful**  
5. **a) Because they work well together**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Conectores y detalles

6. Which connector does B use for contrast? → a) But · b) And · c) Because  
7. B says the job is ____ and ____. → a) interesting, meets nice people · b) easy, boring · c) long, tiring  
8. The dialogue is ____. → a) about job and reasons (and/but/because) · b) about food · c) about sport  
9. B ____ getting up early. → a) doesn't like · b) likes · c) loves  
10. Which word means "levantarse"? → a) Getting up · b) Going to bed · c) Working

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) But**  
7. **a) interesting, meets nice people**  
8. **a) about job and reasons (and/but/because)**  
9. **a) doesn't like**  
10. **a) Getting up**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma y significado

11. They ____ well together. → a) work · b) works · c) working  
12. B uses "because" ____ times. → a) twice (reasons) · b) once · c) never  
13. The team is ____ and ____. → a) friendly, helpful · b) strict, busy · c) tired, sad  
14. A ____ about the job and the team. → a) asks · b) ask · c) asking  
15. B is ____ because they work well together. → a) happy · b) tired · c) sad

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) work**  
12. **a) twice (reasons)**  
13. **a) friendly, helpful**  
14. **a) asks**  
15. **a) happy**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My ideas

**Objetivo:** escribir frases y un párrafo corto con *and*, *but* y *because*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I like tea ____ coffee. → *and / but / because*  
2. I like dogs ____ I don't have one. → *but / and / because*  
3. She is tired ____ she worked hard. → *because / but / and*  
4. We eat fruit ____ vegetables every day. → *and / but / because*  
5. He is tall ____ his sister is short. → *but / and / because*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **and**  
2. **but**  
3. **because**  
4. **and**  
5. **but**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. I / like / pizza / and / pasta.  
7. I / stay / at home / because / I / am / tired.  
8. She / likes / swimming / but / she / doesn't / like / running.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I like pizza and pasta.  
7. I stay at home because I am tired.  
8. She likes swimming but she doesn't like running.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Escribe una frase con *and* (dos cosas que te gustan).  
10. Escribe una frase con *but* (algo que te gusta y algo que no).  
11. Escribe una frase con *because* (razón).  
12. Escribe dos frases: una con *and* y otra con *but*.  
13. Escribe un párrafo corto (4–5 frases) usando *and*, *but* y *because*.  
14. Escribe: *I like… and… but…* (tres ideas).  
15. Escribe una frase con *He/She is… because…*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I like coffee and tea.  
10. I like football but I don't like running.  
11. I stay at home because I am tired.  
12. I like pizza and pasta. I like swimming but I don't like running.  
13. I like sport and music. I play football but I don't like watching TV. I like pizza because it is tasty.  
14. I like tea and coffee but I don't like milk.  
15. She is happy because she passed the exam.

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Conector | Trabajo | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **and** | suma | I like pizza **and** pasta. |
| **but** | contraste | I like tea **but** I don't like coffee. |
| **because** | razón | I stay home **because** I am tired. |
| Regla | *because* + sujeto + verbo | because **I am** tired |
