## Lección 1 — Gramática: negativa e interrogativa

**Objetivo:** Formar la negativa (*am not / isn't / aren't*) y las preguntas sí/no con *to be*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa con la forma correcta

1. I ____ tired. I am fine. → *am not / isn't / aren't*  
2. She ____ sad. She is happy. → *am not / isn't / aren't*  
3. They ____ hungry. They just ate. → *am not / isn't / aren't*  
4. ____ you angry? Yes, I am. → *Is / Am / Are*  
5. ____ he bored? No, he isn't. → *Are / Is / Am*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **am not** — Con *I* la negativa es *am not*.  
2. **isn't** — Con *she* usas *isn't* (*is not*).  
3. **aren't** — Con *they* usas *aren't* (*are not*).  
4. **Are** — Pregunta con *you*: *Are* + *you* + adjetivo?  
5. **Is** — Pregunta con *he*: *Is* + *he* + adjetivo?

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. Which sentence is correct?  
   a) I not am scared. · b) I am not scared. · c) I isn't scared.  
7. Choose the correct question for "She is excited":  
   a) Are she excited? · b) Am she excited? · c) Is she excited?  
8. Short answer for "Are you hungry?" (no):  
   a) No, I am not. · b) No, I isn't. · c) No, I aren't.  
9. Complete: "We ____ cold. We are warm."  
   a) are not · b) is not · c) am not  
10. Which sentence is correct?  
    a) Is they tired? · b) Am they tired? · c) Are they tired?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **b) I am not scared.**  
7. **c) Is she excited?**  
8. **a) No, I am not.**  
9. **a) are not** (*aren't*)  
10. **c) Are they tired?**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. am not / I / sad.  
12. she / Is / happy?  
13. are not / They / hungry.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I am not sad.  
12. Is she happy?  
13. They are not hungry.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. "She am not tired" is a correct sentence in English. → ?  
15. "Are you angry?" is correct in English. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Falso** — Correcto: *She isn't tired* / *She is not tired*.  
15. **Verdadero**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: estados y emociones

**Objetivo:** Emociones (*happy, sad, tired…*) y respuestas a *How are you?*

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige la palabra correcta

1. Which word means "feliz"? → a) sad · b) tired · c) happy · d) angry  
2. You haven't eaten in six hours. How do you feel? → a) excited · b) hungry · c) bored · d) cold  
3. What word means "cansado"? → a) thirsty · b) scared · c) tired · d) angry  
4. "How are you?" asks about: → a) age · b) name · c) how someone feels · d) job  
5. The opposite of "happy" is: → a) tired · b) sad · c) hungry · d) excited  
6. It is very cold outside. No coat. How do you feel? → a) thirsty · b) excited · c) cold · d) bored

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **c) happy**  
2. **b) hungry**  
3. **c) tired**  
4. **c) how someone feels**  
5. **b) sad**  
6. **c) cold**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–10 — Une la palabra con su significado

**7.** Happy · Sad · Tired · Hungry · Angry  
→ Feliz · Triste · Cansado · Hambriento · Enfadado

**8.** Excited · Bored · Scared · Thirsty · Cold  
→ Emocionado · Aburrido · Asustado · Con sed · Con frío

**9.** How are you? · I am fine. · I am not well. · I am great!  
→ ¿Cómo estás? · Estoy bien. · No estoy bien. · ¡Estoy genial!

**10.** Yes, I am. · No, I'm not. · Not bad. · Pretty good.  
→ Sí, lo estoy. · No, no lo estoy. · No está mal. · Bastante bien.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

7. Happy → Feliz · Sad → Triste · Tired → Cansado · Hungry → Hambriento · Angry → Enfadado  
8. Excited → Emocionado · Bored → Aburrido · Scared → Asustado · Thirsty → Con sed · Cold → Con frío  
9. How are you? → ¿Cómo estás? · I am fine. → Estoy bien. · I am not well. → No estoy bien. · I am great! → ¡Estoy genial!  
10. Yes, I am. → Sí, lo estoy. · No, I'm not. → No, no lo estoy. · Not bad. → No está mal. · Pretty good. → Bastante bien.

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Completa con la emoción correcta

11. He has been working for ten hours. He is very ____. → *bored / excited / tired*  
12. She hasn't drunk anything all day. She is ____. → *tired / thirsty / happy*  
13. He is watching a great film. He is not bored; he is ____. → *sad / angry / excited*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **tired**  
12. **thirsty**  
13. **excited**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Responde en contexto

14. Someone asks "How are you?" and you feel good. You reply:  
    a) I am sad. · b) I am fine, thank you. · c) I am angry. · d) I am scared.  
15. A friend asks "Are you hungry?" and you are. You reply:  
    a) No, I'm not. · b) I am sad. · c) Yes, I am. · d) I am bored.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **b) I am fine, thank you.**  
15. **c) Yes, I am.**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Laura and Tom

**Objetivo:** Leer un texto corto sobre sentimientos con *to be* en negativa y pregunta.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-3/reading-laura.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Laura"></audio>

> Hi! My name is Laura. Today is Monday and I am very tired. I worked all weekend. I am not happy about that, but it is OK.  
> My friend Tom is excited today. He is not tired — he slept ten hours! Is he hungry? Yes, he is — he wants breakfast.  
> We are both students. Are we bored at school? No, we are not. We love our class!

### Ejercicios 1–8 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is her name? → a) Maria · b) Ana · c) Laura · d) Sofia  
2. How does Laura feel today? → a) happy · b) excited · c) bored · d) tired  
3. Why is Laura tired? → a) She was sick · b) She worked all weekend · c) She did not sleep · d) She is bored  
4. How does Tom feel today? → a) tired · b) sad · c) angry · d) excited  
5. How many hours did Tom sleep? → a) eight · b) nine · c) ten · d) eleven  
6. Is Tom hungry? → a) He is thirsty · b) No, he isn't · c) He is not hungry or thirsty · d) Yes, he is  
7. What does Tom want? → a) lunch · b) dinner · c) breakfast · d) a snack  
8. What are Laura and Tom? → a) teachers · b) workers · c) doctors · d) students

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **c) Laura**  
2. **d) tired**  
3. **b) She worked all weekend**  
4. **d) excited**  
5. **c) ten**  
6. **d) Yes, he is**  
7. **c) breakfast**  
8. **d) students**

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Verdadero o falso

9. Laura is happy about working all weekend. → ?  
10. Tom is tired today. → ?  
11. Laura and Tom are bored at school. → ?  
12. Tom slept more than Laura. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. **Falso** (*I am not happy about that*)  
10. **Falso** (*He is not tired*)  
11. **Falso** (*No, we are not*)  
12. **Verdadero** (Tom slept ten hours; Laura worked all weekend)

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Comprensión global

13. Main topic of this text:  
    a) A morning at school · b) A weekend trip · c) Two friends describing how they feel today · d) Breakfast habits  
14. How do Laura and Tom feel about school?  
    a) Both bored · b) Both tired · c) They don't like school · d) They are not bored — they love their class  
15. What structure is "I am not happy about that"?  
    a) Affirmative to be · b) Negative to be · c) Question with to be · d) Past tense

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

13. **c) Two friends describing how they feel today**  
14. **d) They are not bored — they love their class**  
15. **b) Negative to be**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Ben and Ana

**Objetivo:** Escuchar un diálogo sobre emociones y estados con *to be*.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-3/listening-ben.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Ben"></audio>

> **Receptionist:** Good morning! How are you today?  
> **Ben:** Hi! I am not great, actually. I am very tired.  
> **Receptionist:** Oh, I am sorry. Is your friend Ana here with you today?  
> **Ben:** Yes, she is over there. She is excited — she is not tired at all.  
> **Receptionist:** Is she hungry? We have snacks here.  
> **Ben:** Actually, she isn't hungry, but she is thirsty. Are there any drinks here?  
> **Receptionist:** Of course! And what about you, Ben? Are you hungry?  
> **Ben:** Yes, I am a little hungry. I am not angry or sad — just tired and hungry.

### Ejercicios 1–8 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. How does Ben feel today? → a) excited · b) happy · c) angry · d) tired  
2. Is Ana tired? → a) Yes, she is · b) She is a little tired · c) No, she isn't · d) She is very tired  
3. How does Ana feel? → a) tired · b) sad · c) scared · d) excited  
4. Is Ana hungry? → a) Yes, very · b) A little · c) She is not thirsty · d) No, she isn't hungry  
5. What does Ana want? → a) food · b) snacks · c) coffee · d) a drink  
6. Is Ben hungry? → a) No · b) Very hungry · c) Thirsty, not hungry · d) Yes, a little  
7. How does Ben feel (all emotions)? → a) angry and sad · b) excited and thirsty · c) tired and hungry · d) happy and excited  
8. Who are Ben and Ana talking to? → a) A friend · b) A teacher · c) A doctor · d) A receptionist

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **d) tired**  
2. **c) No, she isn't**  
3. **d) excited**  
4. **d) No, she isn't hungry**  
5. **d) a drink**  
6. **d) Yes, a little**  
7. **c) tired and hungry**  
8. **d) A receptionist**

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Verdadero o falso

9. Ben is angry today. → ?  
10. Ana is excited. → ?  
11. Both Ben and Ana are hungry. → ?  
12. The receptionist offers drinks. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. **Falso** (*I am not angry or sad*)  
10. **Verdadero**  
11. **Falso** (solo Ben tiene un poco de hambre; Ana no)  
12. **Verdadero**

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Comprensión global

13. Main topic of this audio:  
    a) A job interview · b) A restaurant order · c) A school lesson · d) Two people describing their state and emotions  
14. Who is the most positive person?  
    a) Ben · b) The receptionist · c) Both equally · d) Ana (excited and not tired)  
15. What structure is "I am not angry or sad"?  
    a) Affirmative to be · b) Past tense · c) Negative to be · d) Question with to be

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

13. **d) Two people describing their state and emotions**  
14. **d) Ana (excited and not tired)**  
15. **c) Negative to be**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: How I feel

**Objetivo:** Escribir negativas, preguntas y frases cortas sobre cómo te sientes.

### Modelo

> I am tired today. I am not angry.  
> Is she happy? Yes, she is.  
> He is excited. He is not sad.  
> How are you? — I am fine. I am not bored.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ not tired. I am fine.  
2. She ____ hungry. She is thirsty. (*isn't / is not*)  
3. They ____ bored. They are excited. (*aren't / are not*)  
4. ____ you angry? Yes, I am.  
5. ____ he scared? No, he isn't.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **am**  
2. **isn't** / **is not**  
3. **aren't** / **are not**  
4. **Are**  
5. **Is**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. am not / I / angry.  
7. she / Is / happy?  
8. are not / They / tired.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I am not angry.  
7. Is she happy?  
8. They are not tired.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. How you feel today (1–2 sentences): *I am … / I am not …*  
10. Question + short answer about a friend: *Is she happy? Yes, she is.*  
11. Two sentences about a friend (positive + negative): *He is excited. He is not sad.*  
12. Mini-dialogue: *How are you? — I am …. I am not ….*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I am tired today. I am not angry.  
10. Is she happy? Yes, she is. / Is he hungry? No, he isn't.  
11. He is excited. He is not sad.  
12. How are you? — I am fine. I am not bored.

Otras respuestas correctas según tus datos también son válidas.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Paragraph about you and a friend today (4–6 sentences; include one negative and one question).  
14. Message to a friend: ask how they feel and say how you feel (4–6 sentences).  
15. Typical Monday morning: how you feel (4–6 sentences; at least two negatives and one question).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
Today I am tired. I am not angry. My friend Ana is excited. Is she hungry? No, she isn't. We are happy at school.

**14.**  
Hi! How are you? Are you tired? I am fine, thank you. I am not sad. I am a little hungry. See you soon!

**15.**  
On Monday morning I am tired. I am not excited. Am I hungry? Yes, I am. I am not angry. I want breakfast and coffee.

</details>

|------|----------|

| Saludo | *How are you? — I'm fine, thank you.* |
