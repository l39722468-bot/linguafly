En la [Unidad 49](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-49-maleta-need-to) cerraste el viaje con la maleta. La **Unidad 50 del curso A1** (*Module 5 Review*) consolida todo el **Módulo 5: Places & Travel**. No introduce gramática nueva: **repasa** las unidades 41–49 para que puedas narrar un viaje completo en inglés A1.

Esta guía es un **mapa de repaso** con tablas, checklist, audios y ejercicios. Úsala para detectar huecos y volver a la [unidad concreta](/blog/curso-a1) si algo falla.

## Qué deberías poder hacer al terminar el Módulo 5

- Usar ***must / should*** en el aeropuerto (*passport, check-in, gate*).
- Elegir bien ***take the*** + tren/bus vs ***go by*** + car/plane.
- Hablar de **horarios** y verbos de viaje (*arrive, leave, depart*).
- Describir el **tiempo**: ***It is*** sunny / rainy / hot / cold.
- Usar ***in*** + estación (*in summer, in winter*).
- Pedir en el **hotel**: ***Can I have…?*** (*single room, key, room service*).
- Hablar de **vacaciones** con ***was / were*** y pasado regular.
- Describir lugares con **superlativos**: ***the most famous***.
- Preparar la **maleta** con ***need to / don't need to***.

## 1. Aeropuerto: *must / should* (U41)

| Forma | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **must** | obligación fuerte | You **must** show your passport. |
| **should** | consejo | You **should** go to the gate early. |

Vocabulario: *check-in, boarding pass, gate, luggage, passport, ticket*.

> You must show your passport at check-in.  
> Passengers should go to the gate before boarding.  
> I must have my ticket ready.

## 2. Transporte: *by* / *take the* (U42)

| Patrón | Cuándo | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **take the** + train/bus/metro | transporte público con *the* | I **take the** train to London. |
| **go by** + car/plane/taxi | medio genérico (sin *the*) | We **go by** bus to the airport. |

Regla rápida del curso: *take the train / take the bus*; *go by car / go by plane*.

> I take the train to work.  
> We go by bus to the airport.  
> How did you go to the airport? — I took the bus.

## 3. Verbos de viaje y horarios (U43)

| Verbo | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **arrive** | llegar | The train arrives at 9. |
| **leave / depart** | salir | The bus leaves at 10. |
| **catch** | coger (a tiempo) | I catch the 8 o'clock train. |

Present Simple para horarios fijos: *The flight leaves at 7*.

> The train arrives at nine.  
> The bus leaves at ten.  
> Don't miss your flight.

## 4. El tiempo atmosférico: *It is…* (U44)

Patrón fijo: ***It is*** + adjetivo de clima.

| Clima | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| sunny | It is sunny today. |
| rainy | It is rainy — take an umbrella. |
| hot / cold | It is hot in summer. / It is cold today. |
| cloudy / windy | It is cloudy. / It is windy. |

En pasado del viaje: *The weather **was** sunny* / *It **was** rainy yesterday*.

> It is sunny today.  
> It is rainy today — take an umbrella.  
> The weather was rainy yesterday.

## 5. Estaciones: *in* + season (U45)

| Estación | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| spring | It is warm **in spring**. |
| summer | It is hot **in summer**. |
| autumn / fall | Leaves fall **in autumn**. |
| winter | It is cold **in winter**. |

Siempre ***in*** + estación (sin artículo en A1): *in summer*, no *\*in the summer* como forma prioritaria del curso.

> It is hot in summer.  
> We go to the beach in summer.  
> It is cold in winter.

## 7. Vacaciones: *was / were* (U47)

Pasado de *to be*:

| Sujeto | Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I / he / she / it | **was** | I **was** on the beach last week. |
| you / we / they | **were** | We **were** in the mountains last year. |

Pregunta: ***Were** you at the hotel last week?* → *Yes, I **was**.*

> I was on the beach last week.  
> We were in the mountains last year.  
> Were you at the hotel last week? — Yes, I was there.

También practicas pasado regular de viaje: *I **visited** the museum* / *I **went** on holiday*.

## 8. Sightseeing: superlativos (U48)

Patrón A1 prioritario con adjetivos largos: ***the most*** + adjetivo.

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| the most famous | This is **the most famous** monument in the city. |
| the most beautiful | It is **the most beautiful** museum. |
| the biggest / the oldest | (formas cortas también aparecen en el curso) |

Vocabulario: *sightseeing, guided tour, souvenir, museum, monument, statue*.

> This is the most famous monument in the city.  
> I visited the most famous museum.  
> We did sightseeing yesterday.

## 9. Maleta: *need to / don't need to* (U49)

| Forma | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **need to** + base | necesario | I **need to** pack my suitcase. |
| **don't need to** | no necesario | I **don't need to** take so many clothes. |
| **doesn't need to** | he/she | She **doesn't need to** bring so many clothes. |

Vocabulario: *suitcase, pack, clothes, toiletries, essential items, passport, toothbrush*.

> I need to pack my suitcase tonight.  
> You need to take your passport.  
> I don't need to take so many things — only essential items.

## 10. Grammar check del módulo

![Grammar check Module 5 A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-50/a1-unit50-grammar-check.png)

| Estructura | Recuerda |
| :--- | :--- |
| must / should | must = obligación; should = consejo |
| take the / go by | *take the train*; *go by car* |
| It is + weather | It is sunny / rainy |
| in + season | in summer |
| Can I have…? | petición educada en hotel |
| was / were | pasado de *be* |
| the most + adj. | superlativo largo |
| need to + base | necesidad; don't need to = no hace falta |

Errores típicos a evitar:

1. *\*I go by the bus* → **I take the bus** / **I go by bus**.  
2. *\*It are sunny* → **It is sunny**.  
3. *\*We was on holiday* → **We were on holiday**.  
4. *\*most famous museum* (sin *the*) → **the most famous museum**.  
5. *\*I need pack* → **I need to pack**.

## 11. Una historia de viaje (todo junto)

Une el módulo en un solo relato —como hace el reading del curso:

> Last month I went on holiday. I took the train to the airport. The weather was sunny. I was at the hotel for one week. I did sightseeing — I visited the most famous museum. I needed to pack my suitcase with clothes and toiletries. I didn't need to take so many things. It was great.

## 14. Errores frecuentes del Módulo 5

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I go by the bus | I take the bus / I go by bus | *the* con *take the*; no con *go by* |
| It are sunny | It is sunny | sujeto *It* + *is* |
| We was at the hotel | We were at the hotel | *we* → *were* |
| most famous museum | the most famous museum | falta *the* |
| I need pack | I need to pack | *need to* + verbo base |
| in the summer (prioridad A1) | in summer | patrón del curso: *in* + season |
| Can I having a room? | Can I have a room? | *have* (base), no *-ing* |

## 17. Consejos antes del Módulo 6

1. No memorices listas sueltas: **cuenta un viaje** (aeropuerto → transporte → clima → hotel → museo → maleta).  
2. Si fallas solo en *take the / go by*, vuelve a [U42](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-42-transporte-by-take-the).  
3. Si fallas en *was/were*, vuelve a [U47](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-47-vacaciones-was-were).  
4. Si fallas en superlativos, vuelve a [U48](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-48-sightseeing-superlativos).  
5. Si fallas en *need to*, vuelve a [U49](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-49-maleta-need-to).  
6. Practica en la [Unidad 50 del curso A1](/curso-a1/unit-50).

El Módulo 6 empieza en el supermercado (*At the Supermarket*): plurales, frutas, verduras y pasillos. Llevarás contigo el vocabulario de viaje y la capacidad de narrar experiencias en pasado.
