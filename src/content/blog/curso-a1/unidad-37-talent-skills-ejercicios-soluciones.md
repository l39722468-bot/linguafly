## Lección 1 — Gramática: very well / quite well / not at all

**Objetivo:** niveles de habilidad con *can* + *very well / quite well / not at all*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I can sing ____. → *very well / not at all / quite slow*  
2. She cooks ____. → *quite well / at all / very bad*  
3. He can't dance ____. → *at all / very well / quite well*  
4. I play the guitar ____. → *quite well / at all / very nothing*  
5. She speaks English ____. → *well / wells / at*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **very well**  
2. **quite well**  
3. **at all** — *can't … at all*.  
4. **quite well**  
5. **well**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. High ability = ____. → a) very well · b) not at all · c) never well  
7. Medium ability = ____. → a) quite well · b) not at all · c) very never  
8. I ____ very well. → a) can draw · b) drawing · c) draws  
9. *Not at all* means ____. → a) nothing / no ability · b) medium · c) high  
10. She can ____ English very well. → a) speak · b) speaks · c) speaking

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) very well**  
7. **a) quite well**  
8. **a) can draw**  
9. **a) nothing / no ability**  
10. **a) speak**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. I / can / sing / very well.  
12. She / cooks / quite well.  
13. He / can't / dance / at all.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I can sing very well.  
12. She cooks quite well.  
13. He can't dance at all.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *Very well* = high level; *quite well* = medium; *not at all* = zero. → True / False  
15. We say *I can't dance at all* (not *I can't dance not at all*). → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: talentos

**Objetivo:** *creative, hardworking, skill, talent, good at, bad at*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *Creative* = ____. → a) has new ideas · b) works slowly · c) can't do anything  
2. *Hardworking* = ____. → a) works a lot · b) lazy · c) only creative  
3. *Fast* = ____. → a) quick · b) slow · c) creative  
4. *Skill* = ____. → a) something you can do well · b) only music · c) only sport  
5. *Talent* = ____. → a) natural ability · b) homework · c) a room

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) has new ideas**  
2. **a) works a lot**  
3. **a) quick**  
4. **a) something you can do well**  
5. **a) natural ability**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Empareja

6. Empareja: very well / quite well / not at all / creative / hardworking → high · medium · no ability · new ideas · works hard  
7. Empareja actividad → área: sing / draw / cook / drive / swim → music · art · food · car · water

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **very well → high** · **quite well → medium** · **not at all → no ability** · **creative → new ideas** · **hardworking → works hard**  
7. **sing → music** · **draw → art** · **cook → food** · **drive → car** · **swim → water**

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. She is ____ — she has new ideas. → *creative / slow / bad*  
9. He works ____. → *hard / hardly never / soft*  
10. I can't ____ at all. → *dance / dancing / dances*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **creative**  
9. **hard**  
10. **dance**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Definiciones

11. *Slow* means ____. → a) not fast · b) creative · c) hardworking  
12. *Good at* means ____. → a) can do well · b) can't do · c) never try  
13. *Bad at* means ____. → a) can't do well · b) excellent · c) creative

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) not fast**  
12. **a) can do well**  
13. **a) can't do well**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena

14. She / is / good / at / drawing.  
15. He / can / run / very / fast.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. She is good at drawing.  
15. He can run very fast.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Lisa

**Objetivo:** leer sobre talentos en la familia de Lisa.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-37/reading-lisa.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Lisa"></audio>

> My name is Lisa. I can sing very well and I play the piano quite well. My brother can cook very well — he is creative. I can't draw at all. My sister is hardworking and she speaks English very well. We all have different skills.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the writer's name? → a) Lisa · b) Lara · c) Lucy  
2. Can Lisa sing? → a) Yes, very well. · b) No · c) Quite well only  
3. How well does she play the piano? → a) Quite well. · b) Not at all. · c) Very badly.  
4. Who can cook very well? → a) Her brother. · b) Her sister. · c) Lisa  
5. Can Lisa draw? → a) No, not at all. · b) Yes, very well. · c) Quite well.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Lisa**  
2. **a) Yes, very well.**  
3. **a) Quite well.**  
4. **a) Her brother.**  
5. **a) No, not at all.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. How is her sister? → a) Hardworking. · b) Lazy. · c) Bad at English.  
7. Does her sister speak English well? → a) Yes, very well. · b) No · c) Not at all  
8. The text is about ____. → a) different skills in a family · b) shopping · c) instruments only  
9. Her brother is ____. → a) creative · b) bad at cooking · c) not creative  
10. Which word means "creativo"? → a) creative · b) hardworking · c) slow

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Hardworking.**  
7. **a) Yes, very well.**  
8. **a) different skills in a family**  
9. **a) creative**  
10. **a) creative**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. We all ____ different skills. → a) have · b) has · c) having  
12. Lisa ____ draw at all. → a) can't · b) can · c) cans  
13. Lisa sings ____. → a) Very well. · b) Not at all. · c) Never  
14. How many people are mentioned? → a) Three: Lisa, her brother, and her sister. · b) One · c) Five  
15. *Skills* means ____. → a) things you can do well · b) only sports · c) only music

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) have**  
12. **a) can't**  
13. **a) Very well.**  
14. **a) Three: Lisa, her brother, and her sister.**  
15. **a) things you can do well**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Skills

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo sobre habilidades.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-37/listening-skills.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Skills"></audio>

> **A:** Can you sing?  
> **B:** Yes, I can. I sing very well.  
> **A:** What about cooking?  
> **B:** I cook quite well. My brother can't dance at all. He is good at drawing, though.  
> **A:** Are you creative?  
> **B:** Yes, we are. We all have different skills.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Can B sing? → a) Yes, very well. · b) No · c) Not at all  
2. How well does B cook? → a) Quite well. · b) Not at all. · c) Very badly.  
3. Can B's brother dance? → a) No, not at all. · b) Yes, very well. · c) Quite well.  
4. What is the brother good at? → a) Drawing. · b) Singing. · c) Cooking.  
5. Are they creative? → a) Yes. · b) No. · c) The audio doesn't say.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Yes, very well.**  
2. **a) Quite well.**  
3. **a) No, not at all.**  
4. **a) Drawing.**  
5. **a) Yes.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. We all ____ different skills. → a) have · b) has · c) having  
7. The dialogue is ____. → a) about skills and abilities · b) about food only · c) about school  
8. B sings ____. → a) Very well. · b) Not at all. · c) Never  
9. A ____ about singing, cooking and creativity. → a) asks · b) ask · c) asking  
10. The brother is good at ____. → a) drawing · b) dancing · c) singing

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) have**  
7. **a) about skills and abilities**  
8. **a) Very well.**  
9. **a) asks**  
10. **a) drawing**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. B cooks ____. → a) quite well · b) at all · c) very never  
12. The brother can't ____ at all. → a) dance · b) dancing · c) dances  
13. Which word adds contrast? → a) Though. · b) And only. · c) Because.  
14. Does B give ability levels? → a) Yes; “very well” and “quite well.” · b) No · c) Only “not at all”  
15. They all have different ____. → a) skills · b) skillses · c) skillful

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) quite well**  
12. **a) dance**  
13. **a) Though.**  
14. **a) Yes; “very well” and “quite well.”**  
15. **a) skills**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My skills

**Objetivo:** escribir sobre tus habilidades y las de otros.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I can sing ____. → *very well / at all / quite never*  
2. She cooks ____. → *quite well / at all / very nothing*  
3. He ____ draw very well. → *can / cans / canning*  
4. I am ____ at drawing. → *good / well / goods*  
5. She can't ____ at all. → *dance / dancing / dances*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **very well**  
2. **quite well**  
3. **can**  
4. **good**  
5. **dance**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. I / can / cook / quite well.  
7. She / is / creative / and / hardworking.  
8. He / can't / swim / at all.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I can cook quite well.  
7. She is creative and hardworking.  
8. He can't swim at all.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Escribe algo que haces *very well*.  
10. Escribe algo que *can't … at all*.  
11. Dos frases: *very well* + *quite well*.  
12. Usa *good at* y *bad at*.  
13. Párrafo corto (4–5 frases) sobre tus skills.  
14. Dos preguntas *Can you…?* con respuesta.  
15. Escribe sobre un amigo/a.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I can sing very well.  
10. I can't dance at all.  
11. I can cook very well. I play the guitar quite well.  
12. I am good at drawing. I am bad at singing.  
13. I can speak English quite well. I can cook very well. I can't sing at all. I am good at drawing. I am creative.  
14. Can you swim? Yes, I can. Can you drive? No, I can't.  
15. My friend can play the piano very well. He is good at sports.

</details>
