## Lección 1 — Gramática: Present Simple y *What time…?*

**Objetivo:** Present Simple con horarios, *What time do/does…?* y *at* + hora.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I get up ____ seven o'clock. → *at / in / on*  
2. He ____ up at half past seven. → *gets / get / getting*  
3. What time ____ you have breakfast? → *do / does / are*  
4. She always ____ lunch at one. → *have / has / having*  
5. I ____ to bed at ten o'clock. → *go / goes / going*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **at** — *at* + hora.  
2. **gets** — *he/she/it* + *-s*.  
3. **do** — *What time do you…?*  
4. **has** — *she* + *-s*.  
5. **go** — *I* + forma base.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. Which preposition with the time? → a) at · b) in · c) on  
7. My brother ____ up at six. → a) get · b) gets · c) getting  
8. What time ____ she go to work? → a) do · b) does · c) is  
9. I usually ____ breakfast at eight. → a) have · b) has · c) having  
10. We ____ dinner at half past eight. → a) have · b) has · c) having

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) at**  
7. **b) gets**  
8. **b) does**  
9. **a) have**  
10. **a) have**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. I / get / up / at / seven / o'clock.  
12. What / time / do / you / have / breakfast?  
13. She / goes / to / work / at / nine / o'clock.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I get up at seven o'clock.  
12. What time do you have breakfast?  
13. She goes to work at nine o'clock.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use "at" before the time in English (at 7 o'clock). → ?  
15. With he/she/it we add -s to the verb in present simple (he gets up). → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: rutinas y la hora

**Objetivo:** Verbos de rutina, partes del día, *o'clock / half past* y adverbios de frecuencia.

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige la palabra correcta

1. You ____ at seven in the morning. You leave the bed. → a) get up · b) go to bed · c) have lunch  
2. The ____ is from 12 to about 5 pm. → a) morning · b) afternoon · c) evening  
3. At seven ____ = 7:00. → a) o'clock · b) half past · c) quarter  
4. I ____ at ten at night. I sleep. → a) get up · b) go to work · c) go to bed  
5. Half ____ eight = 8:30. → a) o'clock · b) past · c) past eight  
6. I ____ at eight. I breakfast in the morning. → a) have breakfast · b) have dinner · c) go to bed

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) get up**  
2. **b) afternoon**  
3. **a) o'clock**  
4. **c) go to bed**  
5. **b) past**  
6. **a) have breakfast**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–10 — Une con su significado

**7.** get up · have lunch · have dinner · go to bed · go to work  
→ morning · afternoon · evening · night · morning

**8.** seven o'clock · half past seven · twelve o'clock · half past three · nine o'clock  
→ 7:00 · 7:30 · 12:00 · 3:30 · 9:00

**9.** get up · have breakfast · go to work · go to bed · have dinner  
→ Levantarse · Desayunar · Ir a trabajar · Acostarse · Cenar

**10.** always · usually · sometimes · never · every day  
→ 100% · most of the time · not always · 0% · daily

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

7. get up→morning · have lunch→afternoon · have dinner→evening · go to bed→night · go to work→morning  
8. seven o'clock→7:00 · half past seven→7:30 · twelve o'clock→12:00 · half past three→3:30 · nine o'clock→9:00  
9. get up→Levantarse · have breakfast→Desayunar · go to work→Ir a trabajar · go to bed→Acostarse · have dinner→Cenar  
10. always→100% · usually→most of the time · sometimes→not always · never→0% · every day→daily

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Completa

11. I get ____ at seven o'clock. → *up / to / in*  
12. She ____ to work at nine. → *goes / go / going*  
13. What time ____ you have breakfast? → *do / does / are*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **up**  
12. **goes**  
13. **do**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Contexto

14. Someone asks "What time do you get up?". You say: ____.  
    a) I get up at seven. · b) I am seven. · c) I have seven.  
15. You want to ask when someone eats breakfast. You ____.  
    a) say "What time is it?" · b) say "What time do you have breakfast?" · c) say "Do you have breakfast?"

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **a) I get up at seven.**  
15. **b) say "What time do you have breakfast?"**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Mark's day

**Objetivo:** Leer la rutina diaria de Mark con horarios.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-10/reading-mark.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Mark"></audio>

> My name is Mark. I get up at seven o'clock in the morning. I have breakfast at half past seven. I go to work at nine o'clock. I work in an office. I have lunch at one o'clock. In the afternoon I finish work at half past five. I have dinner at eight o'clock in the evening. I go to bed at ten o'clock. I always read a book before sleep.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What time does Mark get up? → a) At seven o'clock · b) At nine o'clock · c) At half past seven  
2. What time does he have breakfast? → a) At seven o'clock · b) At half past seven · c) At eight o'clock  
3. What time does Mark go to work? → a) At seven o'clock · b) At nine o'clock · c) At one o'clock  
4. Where does Mark work? → a) In a school · b) In an office · c) In a hospital  
5. What time does he have lunch? → a) At twelve o'clock · b) At one o'clock · c) At half past five

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) At seven o'clock**  
2. **b) At half past seven**  
3. **b) At nine o'clock**  
4. **b) In an office**  
5. **b) At one o'clock**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–9 — Verdadero o falso

6. Mark finishes work at half past five. → ?  
7. Mark has dinner at seven o'clock. → ?  
8. He goes to bed at ten o'clock. → ?  
9. Mark has breakfast at nine o'clock. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **Verdadero**  
7. **Falso** (eight o'clock)  
8. **Verdadero**  
9. **Falso** (half past seven)

</details>

### Ejercicios 10–15 — Comprensión

10. Main topic:  
    a) Mark's daily routine and times · b) An office · c) Food  
11. What does Mark do before sleep?  
    a) He watches TV · b) He reads a book · c) He has dinner  
12. What does Mark describe?  
    a) His daily routine (from get up to bed) · b) Only his work · c) He doesn't have breakfast  
13. What time does he have dinner?  
    a) At seven o'clock · b) At eight o'clock · c) At ten o'clock  
14. How many meals a day (breakfast, lunch, dinner)?  
    a) One · b) Two · c) Three  
15. Does Mark read before bed?  
    a) Yes, he always reads a book · b) No, he never reads · c) Only in the morning

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

10. **a) Mark's daily routine and times**  
11. **b) He reads a book**  
12. **a) His daily routine (from get up to bed)**  
13. **b) At eight o'clock**  
14. **c) Three**  
15. **a) Yes, he always reads a book**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My daily routine

**Objetivo:** Escribir frases y párrafos sobre rutinas con *at* + hora.

### Modelo

> I get up at seven o'clock.  
> I have breakfast at half past seven.  
> What time do you get up? I get up at seven o'clock.  
> She goes to work at nine o'clock.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I get up ____ seven o'clock. (*at / in / on*)  
2. He ____ up at six o'clock. (*gets / get / getting*)  
3. What time ____ you have breakfast? (*do / does / are*)  
4. She ____ breakfast at half past eight. (*have / has / having*)  
5. I ____ to bed at ten o'clock. (*go / goes / going*)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **at**  
2. **gets**  
3. **do**  
4. **has**  
5. **go**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. I / get / up / at / seven / o'clock.  
7. What / time / do / you / have / breakfast?  
8. She / goes / to / work / at / nine / o'clock.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I get up at seven o'clock.  
7. What time do you have breakfast?  
8. She goes to work at nine o'clock.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. Say when you get up (*I get up at … o'clock*).  
10. Say when you have breakfast.  
11. Two sentences: get up + go to work/school.  
12. Question + answer (*What time do you get up?*).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I get up at seven o'clock.  
10. I have breakfast at eight o'clock.  
11. I get up at seven o'clock. I go to work at nine o'clock.  
12. What time do you get up? I get up at seven o'clock.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Your morning routine.  
14. Your full day with times (get up → bed).  
15. Someone you know (He/She gets up…).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
I get up at seven o'clock in the morning. I have breakfast at half past seven. I usually have toast and coffee. I go to work at nine o'clock. I start my day early every day.

**14.**  
I get up at seven o'clock. I have breakfast at half past seven. I go to work at nine o'clock. I have lunch at one o'clock. I have dinner at eight o'clock in the evening. I go to bed at ten o'clock.

**15.**  
My sister gets up at six o'clock. She has breakfast at half past six. She goes to work at eight o'clock. She finishes work at five o'clock. She has dinner at seven o'clock. She goes to bed at ten o'clock.

</details>
