## Lección 1 — Gramática: How much / How many

**Objetivo:** *How much* (precio e incontables); *How many* (contables plural).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. How ____ is the bread? → *much / many / many much*  
2. How ____ eggs do you need? → *many / much / much many*  
3. How ____ water do you want? → *much / many / many much*  
4. How ____ does the milk cost? → *much / many / many much*  
5. How ____ apples are there? → *many / much / much many*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **much** — precio / incontable.  
2. **many** — contable plural.  
3. **much** — incontable.  
4. **much** — precio.  
5. **many**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. We use *How much* for ____. → a) price and uncountable · b) countable plural only · c) always price only  
7. We use *How many* for ____. → a) countable nouns (plural) · b) uncountable · c) price  
8. *How much is it?* = ____. → a) asking for price · b) asking for quantity · c) asking for number only  
9. Correct: ____. → a) How much is the bread? · b) How many is the bread? · c) How much are the bread?  
10. Correct: ____. → a) How many eggs do you need? · b) How much eggs do you need? · c) How many egg do you need?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) price and uncountable**  
7. **a) countable nouns (plural)**  
8. **a) asking for price**  
9. **a) How much is the bread?**  
10. **a) How many eggs do you need?**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. How much / is / the / milk?  
12. How many / oranges / do you want?  
13. How much / does / it / cost?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. How much is the milk?  
12. How many oranges do you want?  
13. How much does it cost?

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *How much* for price and uncountable; *How many* for countable plural. → True / False  
15. We say *How much is…?* with a singular verb for price. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: precio y cantidad

**Objetivo:** *pound, dollar, euro, cost, price, cheap, expensive, total*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *Pound* (£) = ____. → a) British money · b) American money · c) euro  
2. *Dollar* ($) = ____. → a) US and other countries' money · b) British only · c) euro  
3. *Euro* (€) = ____. → a) European currency · b) dollar · c) pound  
4. *Cost* = ____. → a) to have a price · b) to buy · c) to sell  
5. *Price* = ____. → a) the amount you pay · b) quantity · c) number only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) British money**  
2. **a) US and other countries' money**  
3. **a) European currency**  
4. **a) to have a price**  
5. **a) the amount you pay**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Empareja

6. Empareja: pound / dollar / euro / how much / how many → £ · $ · € · price, uncountable · countable  
7. Empareja: cost / price / cheap / expensive / total → have a price · amount to pay · low price · high price · all together

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **pound → £** · **dollar → $** · **euro → €** · **how much → price, uncountable** · **how many → countable**  
7. **cost → have a price** · **price → amount to pay** · **cheap → low price** · **expensive → high price** · **total → all together**

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–13 — Completa / elige

8. How ____ is the bread? → *much / many / cost*  
9. The ____ is five pounds. → *total / much / many*  
10. How ____ apples do you want? → *many / much / cost*  
11. *Cheap* = ____. → a) low price · b) high price · c) free  
12. *Expensive* = ____. → a) high price, costs a lot · b) cheap · c) free  
13. *It costs* means ____. → a) it has a price · b) it is free · c) it is cheap

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **much**  
9. **total**  
10. **many**  
11. **a) low price**  
12. **a) high price, costs a lot**  
13. **a) it has a price**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *How much* is used for price and uncountable. → True / False  
15. *How many* is used for countable things. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: At the market

**Objetivo:** leer sobre compras, precios y cantidades.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-54/reading-prices.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: At the market"></audio>

> I am at the market. I want to buy some fruit and vegetables. How much is the bread? It costs two pounds. How many oranges do I need? Maybe six. How much water do we have? We have a lot. The total for my shopping is fifteen pounds. That is not expensive. The milk costs one pound fifty.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Where is the writer? → a) At the market · b) At home · c) At a restaurant  
2. What does he/she want to buy? → a) Some fruit and vegetables · b) Only bread · c) Only milk  
3. How much is the bread? → a) Two pounds · b) One pound · c) Fifteen pounds  
4. How many oranges does he/she need? → a) Maybe six · b) Two · c) Fifteen  
5. What is the total for the shopping? → a) Fifteen pounds · b) Six pounds · c) Two pounds

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) At the market**  
2. **a) Some fruit and vegetables**  
3. **a) Two pounds**  
4. **a) Maybe six**  
5. **a) Fifteen pounds**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Is the total expensive? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The text doesn't say  
7. How much does the milk cost? → a) One pound fifty · b) One pound · c) Fifty pounds  
8. Do they have much water? → a) Yes, a lot · b) No · c) A little  
9. Which question asks for price? → a) How much is the bread? · b) How many oranges? · c) How much water?  
10. *Pound* means ____. → a) British currency · b) dollar · c) euro

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) No**  
7. **a) One pound fifty**  
8. **a) Yes, a lot**  
9. **a) How much is the bread?**  
10. **a) British currency**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text is about ____. → a) shopping, prices, how much/many · b) cooking · c) a restaurant  
12. *Total* means ____. → a) all together, full amount · b) cheap · c) how many  
13. *Expensive* means ____. → a) costs a lot · b) cheap · c) free  
14. *How many* is for ____. → a) countable (oranges) · b) price · c) uncountable  
15. The main topic is ____. → a) shopping at the market, prices, how much/many · b) cooking · c) at home

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) shopping, prices, how much/many**  
12. **a) all together, full amount**  
13. **a) costs a lot**  
14. **a) countable (oranges)**  
15. **a) shopping at the market, prices, how much/many**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: How much is the milk?

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo de precios y cantidades.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-54/listening-prices.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: How much is the milk?"></audio>

> **A:** How much is the milk?  
> **B:** It's one pound fifty.  
> **A:** How many eggs do you need?  
> **B:** Six please.  
> **A:** How much does the bread cost?  
> **B:** Two pounds. The total is five pounds fifty.  
> **A:** That's cheap. Thank you.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. How much is the milk? → a) One pound fifty · b) Two pounds · c) Five pounds  
2. How many eggs does B need? → a) Six · b) Five · c) Two  
3. How much does the bread cost? → a) Two pounds · b) One pound · c) Five pounds  
4. What is the total? → a) Five pounds fifty · b) Six pounds · c) Two pounds  
5. Does A think it's expensive? → a) No, cheap · b) Yes · c) The dialogue doesn't say

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) One pound fifty**  
2. **a) Six**  
3. **a) Two pounds**  
4. **a) Five pounds fifty**  
5. **a) No, cheap**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. What does A say at the end? → a) Thank you · b) Goodbye · c) How much?  
7. The dialogue is at ____. → a) a shop or market · b) a restaurant · c) home  
8. *How much* is for ____. → a) price (milk, bread) · b) quantity of eggs only · c) total only  
9. *How many eggs* = ____. → a) countable, quantity · b) price · c) uncountable  
10. *Pound* means ____. → a) British money · b) dollar · c) euro

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Thank you**  
7. **a) a shop or market**  
8. **a) price (milk, bread)**  
9. **a) countable, quantity**  
10. **a) British money**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Total* means ____. → a) full amount to pay · b) cheap · c) how many  
12. *Cheap* means ____. → a) low price · b) expensive · c) total  
13. *It's one pound fifty* means ____. → a) the price is 1.50 · b) one pound and fifty pounds · c) fifty pounds  
14. *Cost* means ____. → a) to have a price · b) to buy · c) to sell  
15. The dialogue is about ____. → a) asking prices and quantity, paying · b) cooking · c) eating

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) full amount to pay**  
12. **a) low price**  
13. **a) the price is 1.50**  
14. **a) to have a price**  
15. **a) asking prices and quantity, paying**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: Prices

**Objetivo:** escribir preguntas y respuestas de precio y cantidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. How ____ is the bread? → *much / many / cost*  
2. How ____ eggs do you need? → *many / much / cost*  
3. It ____ two pounds. → *costs / cost / is*  
4. The ____ is five pounds. → *total / much / many*  
5. How ____ water do you want? → *much / many / total*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **much**  
2. **many**  
3. **costs**  
4. **total**  
5. **much**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. How much / is / the / milk?  
7. How many / oranges / do you want?  
8. The / total / is / ten / pounds.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. How much is the milk?  
7. How many oranges do you want?  
8. The total is ten pounds.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Pregunta: ¿Cuánto cuesta la leche?  
10. Respuesta: el pan cuesta dos libras.  
11. Pregunta *How many eggs?* y respuesta *Six please.*  
12. Pregunta por el total y respuesta *The total is five pounds.*  
13. Párrafo corto (3–4 frases): mercado, *How much*, *How many*, total.  
14. Diferencia *How much* y *How many* con ejemplos.  
15. Diálogo corto: precio + total (2–3 réplicas).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. How much is the milk?  
10. The bread costs two pounds.  
11. How many eggs do you need? Six please.  
12. How much is the total? The total is five pounds.  
13. I am at the market. How much is the milk? It costs two pounds. The total is five pounds.  
14. How much is the bread? How many eggs do you need? The bread costs two pounds.  
15. How much is the milk? It costs one pound fifty. The total is three pounds.

</details>
