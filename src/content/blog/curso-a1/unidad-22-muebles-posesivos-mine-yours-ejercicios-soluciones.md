## Lección 1 — Gramática: *mine, yours, his, hers*

**Objetivo:** pronombres posesivos sin nombre detrás.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. This book is ____. → *mine / my / me*  
2. That chair is ____. → *yours / your / you*  
3. The bed is ____. → *his / he / him*  
4. This bag is ____. → *hers / her / she*  
5. Is this pen ____? Yes, it's mine. → *yours / your / you*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **mine** — *mine = mío/a*.  
2. **yours** — *yours = tuyo/a*.  
3. **his** — *his = de él*.  
4. **hers** — *hers = de ella*.  
5. **yours** — *Is this pen yours?*

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. This is ____ sofa. → a) my · b) mine · c) me  
7. That table ____ ours. → a) is · b) are · c) be  
8. Whose is this? It's ____. → a) hers · b) her · c) she  
9. The ____ are mine. → a) keys · b) key · c) a key  
10. This room is ____ (my room). → a) mine · b) my · c) me

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) my** — *my + noun*; *mine* solo.  
7. **a) is**  
8. **a) hers**  
9. **a) keys**  
10. **a) mine**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. This / sofa / is / mine.  
12. That / chair / is / yours.  
13. The / bed / is / his.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. This sofa is mine.  
12. That chair is yours.  
13. The bed is his.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use mine, yours, his, hers without a noun after. → ?  
15. His and hers are for he and she. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero** — *This is mine.* (no *this is my*).  
15. **Verdadero** — *his = de él; hers = de ella*.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: muebles y objetos de casa

**Objetivo:** *bed, sofa, table, chair, fridge, wardrobe, desk, lamp, shelf*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la opción correcta

1. You sleep in a ____. → a) bed · b) sofa · c) table  
2. Sofa = ____. → a) comfortable seat for living room · b) where you sleep · c) where you eat  
3. Table = ____. → a) flat surface with legs · b) to sleep · c) to keep food cold  
4. Chair = ____. → a) seat for one person · b) big seat for three · c) where you put clothes  
5. Fridge = ____. → a) keeps food cold · b) to sit · c) to sleep

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) bed**  
2. **a) comfortable seat for living room**  
3. **a) flat surface with legs**  
4. **a) seat for one person**  
5. **a) keeps food cold**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Relaciona

**6.** Mueble → habitación  

| Mueble | Habitación |
|--------|------------|
| bed | ? |
| sofa | ? |
| fridge | ? |
| wardrobe | ? |
| table | ? |

**7.** Objeto → uso  

| Objeto | Uso |
|--------|-----|
| wardrobe | ? |
| fridge | ? |
| chair | ? |
| table | ? |
| bed | ? |

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**6.** bed → bedroom · sofa → living room · fridge → kitchen · wardrobe → bedroom · table → dining room  

**7.** wardrobe → clothes · fridge → food, milk · chair → sit · table → eat, work · bed → sleep

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. Wardrobe = where you ____ clothes. → *put / sleep / eat*  
9. There is a ____ in the kitchen. → *fridge / bed / sofa*  
10. I sit on the ____. → *sofa / fridge / wardrobe*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **put** (también *keep / store*)  
9. **fridge**  
10. **sofa**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Elige

11. Desk = ____. → a) table for work · b) to sleep · c) to cook  
12. Lamp = ____. → a) gives light · b) keeps cold · c) for sitting  
13. Shelf = ____. → a) put things on · b) to sleep on · c) to sit on

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) table for work**  
12. **a) gives light**  
13. **a) put things on**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena las palabras

14. There / is / a / bed / and / a / wardrobe / in / the / bedroom.  
15. The / fridge / is / in / the / kitchen.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. There is a bed and a wardrobe in the bedroom.  
15. The fridge is in the kitchen.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: My bedroom and living room

**Objetivo:** leer sobre muebles y de quién son.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-22/reading-bedroom.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Bedroom"></audio>

> My bedroom has a big bed and a wardrobe. The bed is mine. I have a small table next to the bed. In the living room we have a sofa and two chairs. The red chair is mine; the blue one is my brother's. In the kitchen there is a table and a fridge. The plates on the table are ours.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is in the bedroom? → a) A bed and a wardrobe · b) A sofa · c) A fridge  
2. Whose is the bed? → a) Mine (the writer's) · b) His brother's · c) Ours  
3. What is next to the bed? → a) A small table · b) A wardrobe · c) A chair  
4. How many chairs are in the living room? → a) Two · b) One · c) Three  
5. Which chair is the writer's? → a) The red one · b) The blue one · c) Both

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) A bed and a wardrobe**  
2. **a) Mine (the writer's)**  
3. **a) A small table**  
4. **a) Two**  
5. **a) The red one**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Whose is the blue chair? → a) The brother's · b) The writer's · c) Ours  
7. What is in the kitchen? → a) A table and a fridge · b) A bed · c) A sofa  
8. Whose are the plates? → a) Ours · b) Mine · c) His  
9. The text talks about ____. → a) furniture and whose things are · b) food · c) family  
10. Which word means "armario"? → a) Wardrobe · b) Fridge · c) Table

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) The brother's**  
7. **a) A table and a fridge**  
8. **a) Ours**  
9. **a) furniture and whose things are**  
10. **a) Wardrobe**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. Which word means "nuestros"? → a) Ours · b) Mine · c) Yours  
12. The ____ is in the bedroom. → a) wardrobe · b) fridge · c) sofa  
13. In the living room there ____. → a) is a sofa and two chairs · b) is a bed · c) is a fridge  
14. The writer uses ____ to say whose things are. → a) mine, ours, my brother's · b) I, we, he · c) this, that  
15. Which room has a fridge? → a) The kitchen · b) The bedroom · c) The living room

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) Ours**  
12. **a) wardrobe**  
13. **a) is a sofa and two chairs**  
14. **a) mine, ours, my brother's**  
15. **a) The kitchen**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Whose is this?

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo sobre muebles y posesión.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-22/listening-whose.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Whose"></audio>

> **A:** Whose is this chair?  
> **B:** It's mine.  
> **A:** And the table?  
> **B:** That one is yours. We have a new sofa in the living room. The old one was my sister's — now she has a bed in her room. In the kitchen there is a big fridge. The plates are ours.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Whose is the chair? → a) B's (mine) · b) A's · c) Nobody's  
2. Whose is the table? → a) A's (yours) · b) B's · c) Ours  
3. What do they have in the living room? → a) A new sofa · b) A new table · c) A new bed  
4. Whose was the old sofa? → a) The sister's · b) B's · c) A's  
5. What is in the kitchen? → a) A big fridge · b) A bed · c) A wardrobe

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) B's (mine)**  
2. **a) A's (yours)**  
3. **a) A new sofa**  
4. **a) The sister's**  
5. **a) A big fridge**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Whose are the plates? → a) Ours · b) Mine · c) Hers  
7. B says "It's ____" for the chair. → a) mine · b) yours · c) ours  
8. The sister ____ a bed in her room. → a) has · b) have · c) having  
9. The dialogue is ____. → a) about furniture and whose things are · b) about food · c) about work  
10. Which word means "mía"? → a) Mine · b) Yours · c) Ours

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Ours**  
7. **a) mine**  
8. **a) has**  
9. **a) about furniture and whose things are**  
10. **a) Mine**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. That one refers to ____. → a) the table · b) the chair · c) the sofa  
12. Where is the fridge? → a) In the kitchen · b) In the living room · c) In the bedroom  
13. The fridge is ____. → a) big · b) small · c) old  
14. A ____ about the chair and the table. → a) asks · b) ask · c) asking  
15. B uses ____ to say possession. → a) mine, yours, ours, my sister's · b) I, you, we · c) this, that

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) the table**  
12. **a) In the kitchen**  
13. **a) big**  
14. **a) asks**  
15. **a) mine, yours, ours, my sister's**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: Furniture and possession

**Objetivo:** escribir sobre muebles y de quién son.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. This book ____ mine. → *is / are / be*  
2. There ____ a sofa in the living room. → *is / are / have*  
3. The ____ is in the kitchen. → *fridge / bed / wardrobe*  
4. That ____ is yours. → *chair / fridge / wardrobe*  
5. I put my ____ in the wardrobe. → *clothes / food / books*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is**  
2. **is**  
3. **fridge**  
4. **chair**  
5. **clothes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. This / table / is / mine.  
7. There / is / a / bed / in / my / bedroom.  
8. The / plates / are / ours.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. This table is mine.  
7. There is a bed in my bedroom.  
8. The plates are ours.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. Furniture in your living room + whose it is.  
10. What is in your bedroom (*bed, wardrobe…*).  
11. One thing that is *mine* and one that is *his/hers*.  
12. *Whose is this?* + answer (*It's mine / yours*).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. We have a sofa in the living room. It is ours.  
10. There is a bed, a wardrobe and a small table in my bedroom.  
11. This chair is mine. That table is his.  
12. Whose is this? It's mine.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Short paragraph (4–5 sentences): furniture in a room + whose.  
14. 3 sentences: kitchen furniture + whose plates/fridge.  
15. *There is/are* + furniture in a room (2–3 sentences).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
My bedroom has a bed and a wardrobe. The bed is mine. In the living room we have a sofa. The sofa is ours.

**14.**  
There is a table and a fridge in the kitchen. The fridge is ours. The plates are ours too.

**15.**  
There is a sofa and a table in the living room. There are two chairs.

</details>
