## Lección 1 — Gramática: posición

**Objetivo:** *always, usually, often, sometimes, never* con Present Simple y *be*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ have breakfast in the morning. → *always / never / sometimes*  
2. She ____ eats meat. → *never / always / usually*  
3. I ____ watch TV in the evening. → *often / always / never*  
4. He is ____ late. → *never / always / often*  
5. We ____ go to the cinema at weekends. → *sometimes / always / never*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **always** — *Always* = siempre.  
2. **never** — *Never* = nunca.  
3. **often** — *I often watch TV.* (antes del verbo)  
4. **never** — *He is never late.* (después de *be*)  
5. **sometimes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. With Present Simple (not *be*), the adverb goes ____ the verb. → a) before · b) after · c) at the end  
7. With *be* (am/is/are), the adverb goes ____. → a) after am/is/are · b) before am/is/are · c) at the start  
8. I ____ ____ late. → a) am never · b) never am · c) never is  
9. From most to least frequent: ____. → a) always, usually, often, sometimes, never · b) never, sometimes, often, usually, always · c) sometimes, always, never  
10. Usually = ____. → a) most of the time · b) not often · c) never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) before**  
7. **a) after am/is/are**  
8. **a) am never**  
9. **a) always, usually, often, sometimes, never**  
10. **a) most of the time**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. I / always / have / breakfast / at 8.  
12. She / is / never / late.  
13. We / sometimes / go / to the cinema.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I always have breakfast at 8.  
12. She is never late.  
13. We sometimes go to the cinema.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We put *always/usually/often/sometimes/never* before the main verb. → V / F  
15. With *be*, we put the adverb after *am/is/are*. → V / F

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: always…never

**Objetivo:** significado de cada adverbio de frecuencia.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige

1. Always = ____. → a) every time · b) no times · c) some times  
2. Never = ____. → a) not at any time · b) every time · c) often  
3. Usually = ____. → a) most of the time · b) never · c) sometimes  
4. Often = ____. → a) many times · b) never · c) one time  
5. Sometimes = ____. → a) on some occasions · b) always · c) never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) every time**  
2. **a) not at any time**  
3. **a) most of the time**  
4. **a) many times**  
5. **a) on some occasions**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Relaciona (escribe la pareja)

6. always · usually · often · sometimes · never → 100% / most of the time / many times / some times / 0%  
7. always · never · usually · often · sometimes → siempre / nunca / normalmente / a menudo / a veces

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. always → 100% · usually → most of the time · often → many times · sometimes → some times · never → 0%  
7. always → siempre · never → nunca · usually → normalmente · often → a menudo · sometimes → a veces

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. I ____ drink coffee in the morning. (casi siempre) → *usually / never / sometimes*  
9. She ____ forgets her keys. (nunca) → *never / always / often*  
10. We ____ eat out at weekends. (algunas veces) → *sometimes / always / never*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **usually**  
9. **never**  
10. **sometimes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Elige

11. Which is stronger (more frequent)? → a) Always · b) Sometimes · c) Never  
12. I ____ go to the gym. = Three times a week. → a) often · b) never · c) sometimes  
13. He ____ eats fish. = Zero times. → a) never · b) always · c) usually

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) Always**  
12. **a) often**  
13. **a) never**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena las palabras

14. I / usually / get up / at 7.  
15. They / are / always / busy.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. I usually get up at 7.  
15. They are always busy.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Emma's routine

**Objetivo:** leer una rutina con adverbios de frecuencia.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-33/reading-emma.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Emma"></audio>

> My name is Emma. I always get up at 7 in the morning. I usually have breakfast with my family. I often go running before work. I sometimes meet friends for lunch. I never eat late at night because I like to sleep early. At weekends I always relax and read books.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the writer's name? → a) Emma · b) Eva · c) Ella  
2. When does she get up? → a) At 7 · b) At 8 · c) At 6  
3. Does she have breakfast with family? → a) Usually yes · b) Never · c) Sometimes  
4. What does she do often before work? → a) Go running · b) Have breakfast · c) Meet friends  
5. Does she meet friends for lunch? → a) Sometimes · b) Always · c) Never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Emma**  
2. **a) At 7**  
3. **a) Usually yes**  
4. **a) Go running**  
5. **a) Sometimes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Does she eat late at night? → a) Never · b) Always · c) Often  
7. Why doesn't she eat late? → a) She likes to sleep early · b) She is busy · c) The text doesn't say  
8. What does she do at weekends? → a) Relax and read books · b) Go running · c) Meet friends  
9. At weekends she ____ relax. → a) always · b) sometimes · c) never  
10. Which adverb does she use for get up? → a) Always · b) Sometimes · c) Never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Never**  
7. **a) She likes to sleep early**  
8. **a) Relax and read books**  
9. **a) always**  
10. **a) Always**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text is ____. → a) about daily habits and frequency · b) about work · c) about family  
12. Which word means "nunca"? → a) Never · b) Always · c) Sometimes  
13. Which word means "a menudo"? → a) Often · b) Always · c) Never  
14. She ____ has breakfast with family. → a) usually · b) never · c) always  
15. What is the main topic? → a) Emma's daily routine and how often she does things · b) Emma's job · c) Emma's family

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) about daily habits and frequency**  
12. **a) Never**  
13. **a) Often**  
14. **a) usually**  
15. **a) Emma's daily routine and how often she does things**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: How often…?

**Objetivo:** escuchar hábitos y preguntas de frecuencia.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-33/listening-habits.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Habits"></audio>

> **A:** Do you always have breakfast?  
> **B:** Yes, I usually have breakfast at 8. I never skip it.  
> **A:** How often do you go to the gym?  
> **B:** I often go three times a week. Sometimes I go four times.  
> **A:** Do you always go to bed early?  
> **B:** Not always. I sometimes watch TV late. But I usually sleep well.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Does B have breakfast? → a) Yes, usually · b) No, never · c) Sometimes  
2. When does B have breakfast? → a) At 8 · b) At 7 · c) At 9  
3. Does B skip breakfast? → a) Never · b) Always · c) Sometimes  
4. How often does B go to the gym? → a) Often, three or four times a week · b) Never · c) Once a month  
5. Does B go to bed early always? → a) Not always · b) Yes, always · c) Never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Yes, usually**  
2. **a) At 8**  
3. **a) Never**  
4. **a) Often, three or four times a week**  
5. **a) Not always**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. What does B do sometimes late? → a) Watch TV · b) Go to the gym · c) Have breakfast  
7. Does B sleep well usually? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
8. The dialogue is ____. → a) about habits and how often · b) about work · c) about food  
9. B ____ skips breakfast. → a) never · b) always · c) sometimes  
10. Which word means "saltarse"? → a) Skip · b) Have · c) Go

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Watch TV**  
7. **a) Yes**  
8. **a) about habits and how often**  
9. **a) never**  
10. **a) Skip**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. B goes to the gym ____ three times a week. → a) often · b) never · c) always  
12. Sometimes B ____ four times a week. → a) goes · b) go · c) going  
13. B says "Not always" for ____. → a) going to bed early · b) breakfast · c) gym  
14. Which adverb does B use for breakfast? → a) Usually and never (skip) · b) Always · c) Sometimes  
15. A ____ about breakfast, gym and sleep. → a) asks · b) ask · c) asking

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) often**  
12. **a) goes**  
13. **a) going to bed early**  
14. **a) Usually and never (skip)**  
15. **a) asks**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My habits

**Objetivo:** escribir hábitos con adverbios de frecuencia.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ get up at 7. → *always / never / sometimes*  
2. She is ____ late. → *never / always / often*  
3. We ____ have lunch together. → *usually / never / always*  
4. He ____ goes to the gym three times a week. → *often / never / always*  
5. They ____ eat fish. (algunas veces) → *sometimes / always / never*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **always**  
2. **never**  
3. **usually**  
4. **often**  
5. **sometimes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. I / always / have / breakfast / at 8.  
7. She / is / never / late.  
8. We / sometimes / go / to the cinema.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I always have breakfast at 8.  
7. She is never late.  
8. We sometimes go to the cinema.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. Something you always do (*I always…*).  
10. Something you never do (*I never…*).  
11. One *usually* + one *sometimes*.  
12. *How often do you…?* + your answer.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I always have breakfast in the morning.  
10. I never skip breakfast.  
11. I usually get up at 7. I sometimes go to the cinema.  
12. How often do you go to the gym? I often go there.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Short paragraph (4–5 sentences): your routine with all five adverbs.  
14. 3 sentences: *always*, *usually*, *never*.  
15. *He/She is* + adverb + adjective (*never late*).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
I always get up early. I usually have breakfast at 8. I often go running. I sometimes meet friends. I never go to bed very late.

**14.**  
I always brush my teeth. I usually have lunch at home. I never forget my keys.

**15.**  
He is never late.

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Tema | Recuerda |
|------|----------|
| **Escala** | always → usually → often → sometimes → never |
| **Verbo** | subject + **adv** + verb |
| ***be*** | subject + **be** + adv |
| **Negativo** | *never* (sin *don't*) |
| **Pregunta** | *How often…?* |
| **Tip** | *-s* en el verbo, no en el adverbio |
