## Lección 1 — Gramática: *on* + día y Present Simple

**Objetivo:** Preposición *on* con días, hábitos en Present Simple y *What do you do on…?*

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I work ____ Monday. → *on / at / in*  
2. She ____ English on Tuesdays. → *teaches / teach / teaching*  
3. What ____ you do on Saturdays? → *do / does / are*  
4. I don't ____ on Sunday. → *work / works / working*  
5. We rest ____ the weekend. → *on / at / in*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **on** — *on* + día.  
2. **teaches** — *she/he/it* + *-s*.  
3. **do** — *What do you do on…?*  
4. **work** — *don't* + infinitivo.  
5. **on** — *on the weekend*.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. He ____ football on Fridays. → a) play · b) plays · c) playing  
7. On ____ I have class. → a) Wednesday · b) weekend · c) morning  
8. Do you ____ on Thursdays? → a) study · b) studies · c) studying  
9. Weekdays ____ Monday to Friday. → a) is · b) are · c) be  
10. I ____ shopping on Saturday. → a) go · b) goes · c) going

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **b) plays**  
7. **a) Wednesday**  
8. **a) study**  
9. **b) are**  
10. **a) go** — *go shopping*

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. I / work / on / Mondays.  
12. What / do / you / do / on / Sunday?  
13. She / has / class / on / Tuesdays.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I work on Mondays.  
12. What do you do on Sunday?  
13. She has class on Tuesdays.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use the preposition "on" with days of the week (on Monday). → ?  
15. The weekend is Saturday and Sunday. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero** — *on Monday, on Tuesday, on the weekend*.  
15. **Verdadero** — *weekend* = sábado y domingo.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: días, weekend y weekdays

**Objetivo:** Los siete días, *weekend*, *weekdays* y hábitos con *on*.

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige la opción correcta

1. The ____ day after Sunday is Monday. → a) first · b) last · c) middle  
2. Saturday and Sunday are the ____. → a) weekdays · b) weekend · c) week  
3. From Monday ____ Friday we work. → a) to · b) at · c) on  
4. Wednesday is ____ Tuesday and Thursday. → a) between · b) after · c) before  
5. I don't ____ on Sundays. → a) work · b) works · c) working  
6. On ____ I go to the gym. → a) weekdays · b) weekend · c) today

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) first**  
2. **b) weekend**  
3. **a) to** — *from Monday to Friday*  
4. **a) between**  
5. **a) work**  
6. **a) weekdays**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–10 — Relaciona

**7.** Día → traducción  

| Inglés | Español |
|--------|---------|
| Monday | ? |
| Tuesday | ? |
| Wednesday | ? |
| Thursday | ? |
| Friday | ? |

**8.** Día / concepto → traducción  

| Inglés | Español |
|--------|---------|
| Saturday | ? |
| Sunday | ? |
| weekend | ? |
| weekdays | ? |
| week | ? |

**9.** Día → actividad típica  

| Día | Actividad |
|-----|-----------|
| on Monday | ? |
| on Friday | ? |
| on the weekend | ? |
| on weekdays | ? |
| on Sunday | ? |

**10.** Frase → significado  

| Frase | Significado |
|-------|-------------|
| What do you do on Mondays? | ? |
| Do you work on Saturday? | ? |
| When do you have class? | ? |
| I study on Tuesdays. | ? |
| On the weekend I relax. | ? |

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**7.** Monday → Lunes · Tuesday → Martes · Wednesday → Miércoles · Thursday → Jueves · Friday → Viernes  

**8.** Saturday → Sábado · Sunday → Domingo · weekend → Fin de semana · weekdays → Días entre semana · week → Semana  

**9.** on Monday → I have class · on Friday → we finish early · on the weekend → I rest · on weekdays → I work · on Sunday → we don't work  

**10.** What do you do on Mondays? → Pregunta por hábito · Do you work on Saturday? → Pregunta sí/no · When do you have class? → Pregunta por día · I study on Tuesdays. → Hábito semanal · On the weekend I relax. → Actividad en weekend

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Completa

11. I ____ yoga on Wednesdays. → *do / does / doing*  
12. She ____ to school on weekdays. → *goes / go / going*  
13. We ____ on Saturday. We don't work. → *rest / rests / resting*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **do**  
12. **goes**  
13. **rest**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Contexto

14. Someone asks "What do you do on Fridays?". You go to the gym. You ____.  
    a) I go to the gym. · b) I am a teacher. · c) I have breakfast at eight.  
15. You want to ask if someone works on Saturday. You ____.  
    a) Do you work on Saturday? · b) What time is it? · c) Where do you work?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **a) I go to the gym.**  
15. **a) Do you work on Saturday?**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Paul's week

**Objetivo:** Leer la agenda semanal de Paul.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-12/reading-paul.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Paul"></audio>

> My name is Paul. On Monday and Wednesday I work from nine to five. On Tuesday and Thursday I have English class in the evening. On Friday I finish early and meet my friends. On the weekend I don't work. On Saturday I go shopping or run. On Sunday I rest at home. I like my week.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the writer's name? → a) Paul · b) Peter · c) John  
2. On ____ does he work from nine to five? → a) Monday and Wednesday · b) Tuesday and Thursday · c) Friday  
3. When does he have English class? → a) Monday and Wednesday · b) Tuesday and Thursday · c) On the weekend  
4. On Friday he ____. → a) works late · b) finishes early and meets friends · c) has class  
5. Does he work on the weekend? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Paul**  
2. **a) Monday and Wednesday**  
3. **b) Tuesday and Thursday**  
4. **b) finishes early and meets friends**  
5. **b) No**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Verdadero o falso

6. On Saturday he goes shopping or runs. → ?  
7. On Sunday he works. → ?  
8. He likes his week. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **Verdadero**  
7. **Falso** (he rests at home)  
8. **Verdadero**

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Comprensión

9. English class is in ____. → a) the morning · b) the evening · c) the night  
10. Main topic:  
    a) What the writer does each day of the week · b) A job · c) Food  
11. Where does he rest on Sunday? → a) At home · b) At work · c) In the gym  
12. How many days does he work? → a) Two · b) Four · c) Seven  
13. On Friday he ____ his friends. → a) meets · b) meet · c) meeting  
14. What does Paul do on Tuesday? → a) He has English class. · b) He works. · c) He goes shopping.  
15. On Saturday he doesn't ____. → a) rest · b) work · c) go shopping

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. **b) the evening**  
10. **a) What the writer does each day of the week**  
11. **a) At home**  
12. **a) Two** (Monday and Wednesday)  
13. **a) meets**  
14. **a) He has English class.**  
15. **b) work**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Anna and Ben

**Objetivo:** Escuchar un diálogo sobre la semana de Ben.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-12/listening-anna-ben.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Anna and Ben"></audio>

> **Anna:** What do you do on Mondays?  
> **Ben:** I work from nine to six.  
> **Anna:** Do you have class on Tuesdays?  
> **Ben:** Yes, I have English in the evening.  
> **Anna:** What about the weekend?  
> **Ben:** On Saturday I go to the gym. On Sunday I rest. I don't work on the weekend.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What does Anna ask first? → a) What do you do on Mondays? · b) Do you have class? · c) What about the weekend?  
2. Ben works on Monday ____. → a) from nine to six · b) from eight to five · c) in the evening  
3. Does Ben have class on Tuesdays? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
4. When does he have English? → a) In the morning · b) In the evening · c) On Sunday  
5. On Saturday Ben ____. → a) works · b) goes to the gym · c) has class

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) What do you do on Mondays?**  
2. **a) from nine to six**  
3. **a) Yes**  
4. **b) In the evening**  
5. **b) goes to the gym**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. On Sunday he works. → a) True · b) False  
7. He doesn't work on the weekend. → a) True · b) False  
8. Main topic of the dialogue:  
   a) What Ben does each day of the week · b) Food · c) A job interview  
9. Who asks about the weekend? → a) Anna · b) Ben · c) Nobody  
10. Ben has English on ____. → a) Mondays · b) Tuesdays · c) Sundays

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **b) False** (he rests)  
7. **a) True**  
8. **a) What Ben does each day of the week**  
9. **a) Anna**  
10. **b) Tuesdays**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. On ____ he rests. → a) Saturday · b) Sunday · c) Monday  
12. Ben works on weekdays. → a) True · b) False  
13. What does he do on Saturday? → a) He goes to the gym. · b) He works. · c) He has class.  
14. What does Ben do on Saturday? → a) He goes to the gym. · b) He works. · c) He has English class.  
15. Ben works ____ nine ____ six on Mondays. → a) from / to · b) at / at · c) on / on

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **b) Sunday**  
12. **a) True**  
13. **a) He goes to the gym.**  
14. **a) He goes to the gym.**  
15. **a) from / to**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My week

**Objetivo:** Escribir frases y párrafos cortos sobre tu semana.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I work ____ Monday. → *on / at / in*  
2. She ____ class on Wednesdays. → *have / has / having*  
3. I ____ work on the weekend. → *don't / doesn't / not*  
4. On ____ I rest. → *weekend / weekdays / week*  
5. From Monday ____ Friday I work. → *to / at / on*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **on**  
2. **has**  
3. **don't**  
4. **weekend** — *On the weekend / On weekend*  
5. **to**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. I / work / on / Mondays.  
7. What / do / you / do / on / Sunday?  
8. On / Saturday / I / go / shopping.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I work on Mondays.  
7. What do you do on Sunday?  
8. On Saturday I go shopping.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. Say what you do on one weekday (*On Monday I…*).  
10. Say what you do on the weekend (*On the weekend I…*).  
11. Two sentences: weekdays + Sunday.  
12. Question + answer (*What do you do on Saturday?*).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. On Monday I work. On Tuesdays I have English class.  
10. On the weekend I rest. I meet my friends on Saturday.  
11. From Monday to Friday I work. On Sunday I stay at home and read.  
12. What do you do on Saturday? I go shopping and I meet my family.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Your week: weekdays and weekend (*on Monday*, *on the weekend*).  
14. From Monday to Friday + Saturday and Sunday.  
15. Compare a weekday and Saturday or Sunday.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
On Monday and Wednesday I work. On Tuesday I have class. On the weekend I don't work. I go shopping on Saturday. On Sunday I rest at home.

**14.**  
From Monday to Friday I get up early and work. On Saturday I get up late. I go to the park. On Sunday I have lunch with my family.

**15.**  
On Monday I get up at seven and go to work. I have lunch at one. On Saturday I get up at ten. I don't work. I go shopping and meet my friends. Sunday is quiet. I stay at home.

</details>
