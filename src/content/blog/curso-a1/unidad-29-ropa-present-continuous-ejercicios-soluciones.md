## Lección 1 — Gramática: Present Continuous

**Objetivo:** *am / is / are + wearing* con ropa.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ wearing a shirt. → *am / is / are*  
2. She ____ wearing a dress. → *is / am / are*  
3. They ____ wearing hats. → *are / is / am*  
4. I am wearing blue ____. → *trousers / hat / shirt*  
5. He is wearing a ____ and shoes. → *jacket / dress / trousers*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **am** — *I am wearing* = llevo puesto.  
2. **is** — *She is wearing* = lleva puesto.  
3. **are** — *They are wearing* = llevan puesto.  
4. **trousers** — *Trousers* = pantalones.  
5. **jacket** — *Jacket* = chaqueta.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. We ____ wearing white shirts. → a) are · b) is · c) am  
7. What ____ you wearing today? → a) are · b) is · c) am  
8. She is wearing a ____ and a skirt. → a) shirt · b) shoes · c) hat  
9. I am wearing ____ shoes. → a) black · b) shirt · c) dress  
10. Present ____ is used for what someone is wearing now. → a) Continuous · b) Simple · c) Perfect

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) are**  
7. **a) are**  
8. **a) shirt**  
9. **a) black**  
10. **a) Continuous**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. I / am / wearing / a / red / shirt.  
12. She / is / wearing / a / dress / and / shoes.  
13. He / is / wearing / trousers / and / a / jacket.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I am wearing a red shirt.  
12. She is wearing a dress and shoes.  
13. He is wearing trousers and a jacket.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use *am/is/are + wearing* to say what clothes someone has on. → V / F  
15. *Trousers* and *shoes* are plural in English but one item. → V / F

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: ropa

**Objetivo:** *shirt, trousers, dress, shoes, hat, jacket*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige

1. He is wearing a ____. → a) shirt · b) dress · c) jacket  
2. I am wearing ____ trousers. → a) blue · b) shirt · c) hat  
3. She is wearing a ____ to the party. → a) dress · b) trousers · c) shirt  
4. My ____ are black. → a) shoes · b) hat · c) dress  
5. It is cold. Wear a ____. → a) jacket · b) dress · c) shirt

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) shirt**  
2. **a) blue**  
3. **a) dress**  
4. **a) shoes**  
5. **a) jacket**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Relaciona (escribe la pareja)

6. shirt · trousers · dress · jacket → top for upper body / clothes for legs / one piece for women / outer layer, often warm  
7. shoes · hat · shirt · trousers → on your feet / on your head / on your upper body / on your legs

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. shirt → top for upper body · trousers → clothes for legs · dress → one piece for women · jacket → outer layer, often warm  
7. shoes → on your feet · hat → on your head · shirt → on your upper body · trousers → on your legs

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. I am wearing a ____ on my head. → *hat / shoes / dress*  
9. She is wearing a ____ and white shoes. → *dress / jacket / trousers*  
10. He is wearing a ____ over his shirt. → *jacket / hat / dress*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **hat**  
9. **dress**  
10. **jacket**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Elige

11. Trousers = ____. → a) pantalones · b) camisa · c) vestido  
12. Shoes go ____ your feet. → a) on · b) in · c) at  
13. A ____ is for women often. → a) dress · b) shirt · c) trousers

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) pantalones**  
12. **a) on**  
13. **a) dress**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena las palabras

14. I / am / wearing / a / shirt / and / trousers.  
15. She / is / wearing / a / hat / and / a / jacket.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. I am wearing a shirt and trousers.  
15. She is wearing a hat and a jacket.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Ready for the party

**Objetivo:** leer descripciones de ropa con Present Continuous.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-29/reading-party.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Party clothes"></audio>

> Today I am wearing a blue shirt and grey trousers. I am wearing black shoes. My sister is wearing a red dress and white shoes. She is wearing a small hat. My brother is wearing a jacket and jeans. He is wearing a green shirt under the jacket. We are all ready for the party.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the writer wearing? → a) A blue shirt and grey trousers · b) A red dress · c) A jacket  
2. What colour are the writer's shoes? → a) Black · b) White · c) Blue  
3. What is the sister wearing? → a) A red dress and white shoes · b) A blue shirt · c) A jacket  
4. Is the sister wearing a hat? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say  
5. What is the brother wearing? → a) A jacket and jeans and green shirt · b) A dress · c) A hat only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) A blue shirt and grey trousers**  
2. **a) Black**  
3. **a) A red dress and white shoes**  
4. **a) Yes**  
5. **a) A jacket and jeans and green shirt**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. What colour is the brother's shirt? → a) Green · b) Blue · c) Red  
7. Where are they going? → a) To a party · b) To school · c) To work  
8. Which tense does the text use for clothes? → a) Present Continuous (am/is/are wearing) · b) Present Simple · c) Past Simple  
9. Which word means "camisa"? → a) Shirt · b) Dress · c) Jacket  
10. Which word means "pantalones"? → a) Trousers · b) Dress · c) Shoes

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Green**  
7. **a) To a party**  
8. **a) Present Continuous (am/is/are wearing)**  
9. **a) Shirt**  
10. **a) Trousers**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. Who is wearing a dress? → a) The sister · b) The writer · c) The brother  
12. The brother has a ____ under the jacket. → a) green shirt · b) red dress · c) hat  
13. The main topic is ____. → a) what the family is wearing · b) food · c) animals  
14. Which word means "chaqueta"? → a) Jacket · b) Shirt · c) Dress  
15. We use ____ to describe what someone is wearing now. → a) am/is/are wearing · b) have got · c) there is

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) The sister**  
12. **a) green shirt**  
13. **a) what the family is wearing**  
14. **a) Jacket**  
15. **a) am/is/are wearing**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: What are you wearing?

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo sobre ropa.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-29/listening-wearing.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Wearing"></audio>

> **A:** What are you wearing today?  
> **B:** I am wearing a white shirt and blue trousers.  
> **A:** Nice. Is your sister wearing a dress?  
> **B:** Yes, she is wearing a red dress and black shoes.  
> **A:** What about your brother?  
> **B:** He is wearing a jacket and a hat. He is wearing brown shoes.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is B wearing? → a) A white shirt and blue trousers · b) A red dress · c) A jacket  
2. Is B's sister wearing a dress? → a) Yes, a red dress · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
3. What colour are the sister's shoes? → a) Black · b) White · c) Brown  
4. What is the brother wearing? → a) A jacket and a hat · b) A dress · c) A shirt only  
5. What colour are the brother's shoes? → a) Brown · b) Black · c) Blue

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) A white shirt and blue trousers**  
2. **a) Yes, a red dress**  
3. **a) Black**  
4. **a) A jacket and a hat**  
5. **a) Brown**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. What does A ask first? → a) What are you wearing today? · b) Where is your sister? · c) What colour is the jacket?  
7. Which tense do they use for clothes? → a) Present Continuous (am/is/are wearing) · b) Present Simple · c) Past  
8. B says I ____ wearing a white shirt. → a) am · b) is · c) are  
9. The dialogue is ____. → a) about what people are wearing · b) about food · c) about animals  
10. Which word means "vestido"? → a) Dress · b) Shirt · c) Jacket

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) What are you wearing today?**  
7. **a) Present Continuous (am/is/are wearing)**  
8. **a) am**  
9. **a) about what people are wearing**  
10. **a) Dress**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. She ____ wearing a red dress. → a) is · b) am · c) are  
12. He ____ wearing a jacket. → a) is · b) are · c) am  
13. B's trousers are ____. → a) blue · b) white · c) black  
14. The brother is wearing ____ and a hat. → a) a jacket · b) a dress · c) a shirt  
15. Wearing means ____. → a) llevar puesto · b) comprar · c) lavar

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) is**  
12. **a) is**  
13. **a) blue**

## Lección 5 — Writing: Describe clothes

**Objetivo:** escribir qué llevas puesto con Present Continuous.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ wearing a blue shirt. → *am / is / are*  
2. She ____ wearing a dress. → *is / am / are*  
3. They ____ wearing hats. → *are / is / am*  
4. He is wearing a ____ and trousers. → *jacket / dress / shoes*  
5. I am wearing black ____. → *shoes / shirt / hat*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **am**  
2. **is**  
3. **are**  
4. **jacket**  
5. **shoes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. I / am / wearing / a / red / shirt.  
7. She / is / wearing / a / hat / and / a / dress.  
8. What / are / you / wearing / today?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I am wearing a red shirt.  
7. She is wearing a hat and a dress.  
8. What are you wearing today?

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. What are you wearing today? (*I am wearing…*)  
10. What is another person wearing? (*He/She is wearing…*)  
11. Your clothes + a friend's clothes (two sentences).  
12. *What are you wearing?* + your answer.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I am wearing a white shirt and blue trousers.  
10. She is wearing a red dress and black shoes.  
11. I am wearing a shirt and trousers. My friend is wearing a jacket.  
12. What are you wearing? I am wearing a shirt and jeans.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Short paragraph (4–5 sentences): you and another person.  
14. 3 sentences: clothes for a party.  
15. *What is he/she wearing?* + answer with 2–3 items.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
I am wearing a blue shirt and grey trousers. I am wearing black shoes. My sister is wearing a red dress. She is wearing a hat.

**14.**  
I am wearing a dress. I am wearing white shoes. I am wearing a small hat.

**15.**  
What is she wearing? She is wearing a jacket, a shirt and trousers.

</details>
