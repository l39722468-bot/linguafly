En la [Unidad 52](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-52-contables-incontables) mediste cantidad con *much / many / a lot of*. En la **Unidad 53 del curso A1** (*Some & Any*) eliges la forma correcta según el tipo de frase: ***some*** en afirmativas, ***any*** en negativas y preguntas — más ***bottle, box, bag, can***.

Incluye **audios**, **esquemas** y **ejercicios**.

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 53 — Some & Any](/curso-a1/unit-53)  
> **Repaso útil:** [Unidad 52 — Contables](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-52-contables-incontables) · [Unidad 51 — Supermercado](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-51-supermercado-plurales)

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 53 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Usar ***some*** en oraciones afirmativas.
- Usar ***any*** en negativas y preguntas.
- Ofrecer con ***Would you like some…?***
- Combinar *some/any* con **incontables** y **plurales**.
- Nombrar contenedores: ***bottle, box, bag, can***.
- Formar: ***a bottle of water / a box of cereal / a bag of apples / a can of cola***.

---

## 1. Regla rápida: *some* vs *any*

| Tipo de frase | Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Afirmativa** | **some** | I have **some** milk in the fridge. |
| **Negativa** | **any** | There isn't **any** bread left. |
| **Pregunta** | **any** | Do you have **any** eggs? |
| **Oferta / petición** | **some** (frecuente) | Would you like **some** tea? |

> I have some milk in the fridge.  
> There isn't any bread left.  
> Do you have any eggs?  
> She doesn't have any rice.

## 2. *Some* en afirmativas

*Some* = “algo de / algunos” (cantidad no exacta).

| Con incontable | Con plural contable |
| :--- | :--- |
| some milk | some eggs |
| some water | some apples |
| some rice | some cans |
| some bread | some bottles |

> We need some water and some apples.  
> There is some milk and some water in bottles.  
> I have some rice in a box.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-53/need-some-water-apples.mp3" title="🔊 We need some water and some apples"></audio>

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-53/some-rice-in-box.mp3" title="🔊 I have some rice in a box"></audio>

No digas normalmente *\*I have any milk* en afirmativa.

---

## 3. *Any* en negativas y preguntas

| Negativa | Pregunta |
| :--- | :--- |
| There isn't **any** bread. | Do you have **any** bread? |
| I don't have **any** eggs. | Do we have **any** fruit? |
| She doesn't have **any** rice. | Is there **any** milk? |

> Do you have any bread? — No, I don't have any.  
> There isn't any rice left.  
> Do you want any? — No, thanks.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-53/dont-have-any.mp3" title="🔊 No, I don't have any"></audio>

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-53/isnt-any-rice.mp3" title="🔊 There isn't any rice left"></audio>

Respuesta corta típica: *I don't have any* (sin repetir el sustantivo).

---

## 4. Ofertas: *some* en preguntas

Cuando **ofreces** o **pides** algo, *some* suena natural:

| Oferta / petición | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| Would you like **some**…? | Would you like some tea? |
| Can I have **some**…? | Can I have some water? |
| Do you need **some**…? | Do you need some milk? |

> Do you need a bottle of water or some milk? — Some milk, please.  
> Would you like some tea?

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-53/some-milk-please.mp3" title="🔊 Some milk, please"></audio>

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-53/would-like-some-tea.mp3" title="🔊 Would you like some tea?"></audio>

En A1 del curso, para preguntas de información neutras prioriza ***any***; guarda ***some*** para ofertas y peticiones.

## 5. Contenedores: *bottle, box, bag, can*

Patrón: ***a + contenedor + of + producto***

| Contenedor | Uso típico | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **bottle** | líquidos | a bottle of water / milk |
| **box** | cartón / caja | a box of cereal |
| **bag** | bolsa | a bag of apples / potatoes |
| **can** | lata | a can of cola / beans |

> I need a bottle of water.  
> There is a box of cereal on the table.  
> Buy a can of cola.  
> There are some apples in the bag.

## 8. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I have any milk. | I have **some** milk. | afirmativa → *some* |
| I don't have some bread. | I don't have **any** bread. | negativa → *any* |
| Do you have some eggs? (neutra) | Do you have **any** eggs? | pregunta neutra → *any* |
| a bottle water | a bottle **of** water | falta *of* |
| box of cereal (sin artículo) | **a** box of cereal | *a* + contenedor |
| There isn't some rice. | There isn't **any** rice. | negativa → *any* |

## 9. Mini prácticas orales

1. Abre tu nevera mental y di 3 frases con *some*.  
2. Di 2 cosas que no hay: *There isn't any…*  
3. Pregunta a alguien: *Do you have any…?*  
4. Ofrece: *Would you like some…?*  
5. Nombra 4 contenedores con *a … of …*

> Do we have any milk? — Yes, we have some.  
> There aren't any eggs left.  
> I need a bag of potatoes.

## 10. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — *some* o *any*

1. I have \_\_\_\_ milk in the fridge.  
2. There isn't \_\_\_\_ bread left.  
3. Do you have \_\_\_\_ eggs?  
4. We need \_\_\_\_ water and \_\_\_\_ apples.  
5. She doesn't have \_\_\_\_ rice.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **some** · 2. **any** · 3. **any** · 4. **some**, **some** · 5. **any**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Tipo de frase

Elige **afirmativa / negativa / pregunta**:

1. I have some milk. → \_\_\_\_  
2. Do you have any bread? → \_\_\_\_  
3. There isn't any rice. → \_\_\_\_  
4. Would you like some tea? → \_\_\_\_ (oferta)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **afirmativa** · 2. **pregunta** · 3. **negativa** · 4. **pregunta (oferta)**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Contenedores

1. I need a \_\_\_\_ of water.  
2. There is a \_\_\_\_ of cereal on the table.  
3. Buy a \_\_\_\_ of cola.  
4. There are some apples in the \_\_\_\_.  
5. I have some rice in a \_\_\_\_.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **bottle** · 2. **box** · 3. **can** · 4. **bag** · 5. **box**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Empareja

1. bottle  
2. box  
3. bag  
4. can  
5. some  
6. any  

a) cola, beans · b) water, milk · c) affirmative · d) cereal · e) apples, potatoes · f) negative / question

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **d** · 3 → **e** · 4 → **a** · 5 → **c** · 6 → **f**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrige

1. *I have any milk.*  
2. *I don't have some bread.*  
3. *Do you have some eggs?* (pregunta neutra)  
4. *a bottle water*  
5. *There isn't some rice left.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I have **some** milk.  
2. I don't have **any** bread.  
3. Do you have **any** eggs?  
4. a bottle **of** water  
5. There isn't **any** rice left.
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa el diálogo

A: Do you have \_\_\_\_ bread?  
B: No, I don't have \_\_\_\_.  
A: I have \_\_\_\_ in the bag. Do you need a \_\_\_\_ of water or \_\_\_\_ milk?  
B: \_\_\_\_ milk, please.  
A: There are \_\_\_\_ cans of cola. Do you want \_\_\_\_?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**any** · **any** · **some** · **bottle** · **some** · **Some** · **some** · **any**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Completa el texto

I open the fridge. There is \_\_\_\_ milk and \_\_\_\_ water in bottles. There isn't \_\_\_\_ bread left. I have \_\_\_\_ rice in a \_\_\_\_ and \_\_\_\_ cans of beans. Do we have \_\_\_\_ fruit? Yes, there are \_\_\_\_ apples in the bag.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**some** · **some** · **any** · **some** · **box** · **some** · **any** · **some**
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Elige la correcta

1. ____  
   a) I have any milk · b) I have some milk · c) I have many milk  
2. ____  
   a) Do you have any eggs? · b) Do you have much eggs? · c) Have you some eggs?  
3. ____  
   a) a can of cola · b) a can cola · c) can of a cola  
4. We use any in ____.  
   a) affirmatives only · b) negatives and questions · c) never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b** · 2. **a** · 3. **a** · 4. **b**
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Traduce

1. Tengo algo de leche en la nevera.  
2. No queda pan.  
3. ¿Tienes huevos?  
4. Necesito una botella de agua.  
5. Hay una caja de cereales en la mesa.  
6. ¿Quieres un poco de té?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I have some milk in the fridge.  
2. There isn't any bread left. / There is no bread left.  
3. Do you have any eggs?  
4. I need a bottle of water.  
5. There is a box of cereal on the table.

### Ejercicio 10 — Escribe tu nevera

Escribe 8–10 frases. Incluye:

- 3 × *some* (afirmativa)  
- 2 × *any* (negativa)  
- 2 × *any* (pregunta)  
- 1 oferta con *some*  
- 3 contenedores distintos (*bottle / box / bag / can*)

## 12. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Pregunta mental: ¿afirmativa? → *some*. ¿negativa/pregunta? → *any*.  
2. Si ofreces algo, *some* suena más natural.  
3. Contenedores ayudan a “contar” incontables: *a bottle of milk*.  
4. Practica en la [Unidad 53 del curso A1](/curso-a1/unit-53).

La **Unidad 54** trabaja ***How much & How many*** con precios y cantidades.
