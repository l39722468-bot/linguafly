## Lección 1 — Gramática: *like/love/hate* y *have breakfast*

**Objetivo:** Gustos con comida, *have breakfast/lunch/dinner* e intro a contables/incontables.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ cheese. → *like / likes / to like*  
2. She ____ pasta. → *love / loves / loving*  
3. They ____ broccoli. → *don't like / doesn't like / not like*  
4. He ____ fish. → *hates / hate / hating*  
5. Do ____ like milk? → *you / he / she*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **like** — *I like* + nombre.  
2. **loves** — *she* + *-s*.  
3. **don't like** — *they don't like*.  
4. **hates** — *he* + *-s*.  
5. **you** — *Do you like…?*

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. We ____ eggs for breakfast. → a) like · b) likes · c) to like  
7. "Milk" is ____ (countable/uncountable). → a) uncountable · b) countable · c) plural  
8. "Apple" is ____. → a) countable · b) uncountable · c) both  
9. I ____ breakfast at eight. → a) have · b) eat · c) has  
10. She ____ lunch at one. → a) has · b) have · c) having

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) like**  
7. **a) uncountable**  
8. **a) countable**  
9. **a) have** — *have breakfast*  
10. **a) has**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. I / like / fruit / for / breakfast.  
12. She / doesn't / like / meat.  
13. Do / you / like / vegetables?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I like fruit for breakfast.  
12. She doesn't like meat.  
13. Do you like vegetables?

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use like/love/hate + noun (I like cheese). → ?  
15. We usually say "I have breakfast", not "I eat breakfast". → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero** — *have breakfast/lunch/dinner*

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: comidas y alimentos

**Objetivo:** *breakfast, lunch, dinner*, fruta, verdura, carne, lácteos.

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige la opción correcta

1. I have ____ at eight. → a) breakfast · b) dinner · c) lunch  
2. Lunch is at ____. → a) midday / one o'clock · b) eight in the morning · c) nine at night  
3. An ____ is a fruit. → a) apple · b) bread · c) meat  
4. ____ is from animals. → a) Meat · b) Fruit · c) Milk  
5. Cheese and ____ are dairy. → a) milk · b) meat · c) bread  
6. Broccoli, carrots are ____. → a) vegetables · b) fruits · c) meat

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) breakfast**  
2. **a) midday / one o'clock**  
3. **a) apple**  
4. **a) Meat**  
5. **a) milk**  
6. **a) vegetables**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–9 — Relaciona

**7.** Comida / grupo → significado  

| Inglés | Significado |
|--------|-------------|
| breakfast | ? |
| lunch | ? |
| dinner | ? |
| fruit | ? |
| vegetables | ? |

**8.** Alimento → tipo  

| Alimento | Tipo |
|----------|------|
| chicken | ? |
| rice | ? |
| yogurt | ? |
| fish | ? |
| bread | ? |

**9.** Expresión → uso  

| Expresión | Uso |
|-----------|-----|
| I like | ? |
| I love | ? |
| I hate | ? |
| have breakfast | ? |
| have dinner | ? |

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**7.** breakfast → morning meal · lunch → midday meal · dinner → evening meal · fruit → apple, banana, orange · vegetables → carrots, broccoli  

**8.** chicken → meat · rice → grain / food · yogurt → dairy · fish → from the sea · bread → baked food  

**9.** I like → + food name · I love → + food name · I hate → + food name · have breakfast → in the morning · have dinner → in the evening

</details>

### Ejercicios 10–12 — Completa

10. I ____ eggs and toast for breakfast. → *have / has / eat*  
11. She ____ salad for lunch. → *likes / like / to like*  
12. We ____ dinner at eight. → *have / has / having*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

10. **have**  
11. **likes**  
12. **have**

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Contexto

13. You want to say you really like pizza. You ____.  
    a) I love pizza. · b) I hate pizza. · c) I have pizza.  
14. What do you have for breakfast? Common ____.  
    a) Toast, cereal, fruit, eggs. · b) Pizza and pasta. · c) Only coffee.  
15. Countable: ____. Uncountable: ____.  
    a) apple / milk · b) milk / apple · c) bread / water

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

13. **a) I love pizza.**  
14. **a) Toast, cereal, fruit, eggs.**  
15. **a) apple / milk**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Leo's meals

**Objetivo:** Leer sobre las comidas y gustos de Leo.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-17/reading-leo.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Leo"></audio>

> My name is Leo. I have breakfast at seven thirty. I like fruit and toast. I don't like eggs. For lunch I have a sandwich or salad. I love pasta for dinner. I have dinner at eight thirty. I don't like fish or broccoli.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the writer's name? → a) Leo · b) Anna · c) Tom  
2. What time does he have breakfast? → a) At seven thirty · b) At eight · c) At six  
3. Does he like eggs? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The text doesn't say  
4. What does he have for lunch? → a) A sandwich or salad. · b) Pasta · c) Fish  
5. What does he love for dinner? → a) Pasta · b) Fish · c) Eggs

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Leo**  
2. **a) At seven thirty**  
3. **a) No**  
4. **a) A sandwich or salad.**  
5. **a) Pasta**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. He likes fish. → a) True · b) False  
7. What time does he have dinner? → a) At eight thirty · b) At seven · c) At nine  
8. He ____ fruit for breakfast. → a) likes · b) like · c) to like  
9. Main topic:  
   a) Leo's meals and food likes/dislikes · b) Sports · c) Work  
10. Does he like broccoli? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The text doesn't say

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **b) False**  
7. **a) At eight thirty**  
8. **a) likes**  
9. **a) Leo's meals and food likes/dislikes**  
10. **a) No**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. Which meal does he mention first? → a) Breakfast · b) Lunch · c) Dinner  
12. He has ____ and toast for breakfast. → a) fruit · b) meat · c) fish  
13. What verb does he use for love? → a) love · b) like · c) have  
14. How many meals does he mention? → a) Three: breakfast, lunch, dinner · b) One · c) Two  
15. The text uses ____ to say what he doesn't like. → a) don't like · b) like · c) love

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) Breakfast**  
12. **a) fruit**  
13. **a) love**  
14. **a) Three: breakfast, lunch, dinner**  
15. **a) don't like**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Food preferences

**Objetivo:** Escuchar un diálogo sobre comidas y gustos.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-17/listening-meals.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Meals"></audio>

> **A:** What do you have for breakfast?  
> **B:** I have cereal and milk. I like fruit too.  
> **A:** Do you like eggs?  
> **B:** No, I don't. I hate eggs. For lunch I have a sandwich. For dinner I love pasta or rice.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What does A ask first? → a) What do you have for breakfast? · b) Do you like eggs? · c) What is your name?  
2. What does B have for breakfast? → a) Cereal and milk · b) Eggs · c) Pasta  
3. Does B like eggs? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The audio doesn't say  
4. What word does B use for eggs? → a) hate · b) like · c) love  
5. What does B have for lunch? → a) A sandwich · b) Pasta · c) Cereal

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) What do you have for breakfast?**  
2. **a) Cereal and milk**  
3. **a) No**  
4. **a) hate**  
5. **a) A sandwich**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. B loves ____ for dinner. → a) pasta or rice · b) eggs · c) only salad  
7. B likes fruit. → a) True · b) False  
8. Main topic:  
   a) Meals and food preferences · b) Sports · c) Work  
9. Does B mention dinner? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
10. B ____ cereal for breakfast. → a) has · b) have · c) having

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) pasta or rice**  
7. **a) True**  
8. **a) Meals and food preferences**  
9. **a) Yes**  
10. **a) has**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. Which meal does B mention last? → a) Dinner · b) Breakfast · c) Lunch  
12. I ____ eggs. → a) hate · b) love · c) like  
13. What does B have for lunch? → a) A sandwich · b) Rice · c) Milk  
14. B says he/she loves ____. → a) pasta or rice · b) eggs · c) cereal  
15. The dialogue is ____. → a) about food and meals · b) about time · c) about work

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My meals

**Objetivo:** Escribir sobre tus comidas y gustos.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ breakfast at eight. → *have / has / eat*  
2. She ____ pasta. → *loves / love / loving*  
3. Do ____ like fruit? → *you / he / she*  
4. They ____ like fish. → *don't / doesn't / not*  
5. I have ____ at one. → *lunch / breakfast / dinner*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **have**  
2. **loves**  
3. **you**  
4. **don't**  
5. **lunch**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. I / like / fruit / for / breakfast.  
7. She / has / dinner / at / eight.  
8. Do / you / like / vegetables?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I like fruit for breakfast.  
7. She has dinner at eight.  
8. Do you like vegetables?

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. What you have for breakfast (2–3 sentences).  
10. One food you like and one you don't.  
11. What time you have lunch and dinner.  
12. *Do you like cheese?* + your answer.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I have breakfast at eight. I have coffee and toast. Sometimes I have fruit.  
10. I love pasta. I don't like fish.  
11. I have lunch at one. I have dinner at eight.  
12. Do you like cheese? Yes, I do.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Four sentences: breakfast, lunch, dinner + one like/don't like.  
14. Three foods: love / like / don't like.  
15. *She loves pasta but she doesn't like fish.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
I have breakfast at seven. I have lunch at one. I have dinner at eight. I love fruit and I don't like broccoli.

**14.**  
I love chocolate. I like bread. I don't like fish.

**15.**  
She loves pasta but she doesn't like fish.

</details>
