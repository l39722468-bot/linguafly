En la [Unidad 53](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-53-some-any) elegiste *some/any*. En la **Unidad 54 del curso A1** (*How Much & How Many*) preguntas **precio** y **cantidad**: ***How much is it?*** / ***How many eggs do you need?***

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 54 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Preguntar **precio** con ***How much is…?*** / ***How much does it cost?***
- Preguntar **cantidad de incontables** con ***How much + milk/water…?***
- Preguntar **cantidad de contables** con ***How many + plural?***
- Responder con **precios** (*two pounds*, *one pound fifty*).
- Usar vocabulario: ***pound, dollar, euro, cost, price, cheap, expensive, total***.

---

## 1. Regla rápida: *How much* vs *How many*

| Pregunta | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **How much…?** | precio | How much is the bread? |
| **How much…?** | incontable | How much water do you want? |
| **How many…?** | contable plural | How many eggs do you need? |

> How much is the bread?  
> How many eggs do you need?  
> How much water do you want?  
> How many apples are there?

## 2. *How much* para precio

Patrones A1:

| Pregunta | Respuesta típica |
| :--- | :--- |
| **How much is** the milk? | It's one pound fifty. |
| **How much is** it? | Two pounds. |
| **How much does** the bread **cost**? | It costs two pounds. |
| **How much does** it **cost**? | One euro. |

> How much is the milk? — It's one pound fifty.  
> How much does the bread cost? — Two pounds.  
> How much does it cost?

Recuerda: *How much **is**…?* (verbo singular) es la forma más frecuente para un producto.

## 3. *How many* para contables

Siempre + **plural**:

| Correcto | Incorrecto típico |
| :--- | :--- |
| How many **eggs** do you need? | *How much eggs?* |
| How many **oranges** do you want? | *How many orange?* |
| How many **apples** are there? | *How much apples?* |

> How many eggs do you need? — Six, please.  
> How many oranges do you want? — Maybe six.  
> How many apples are there?

## 4. *How much* + incontable (cantidad)

No es solo precio: también cantidad de *milk, water, rice, bread…*

> How much water do we have? — We have a lot.  
> How much milk do you want?  
> How much rice do we need?

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-54/how-much-water-have.mp3" title="🔊 How much water do we have?"></audio>

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-54/we-have-a-lot.mp3" title="🔊 We have a lot"></audio>

Truco: si no puedes decir *two ___* fácilmente → *How much*. Si dices *two/six ___* → *How many*.

## 5. Moneda y vocabulario de precio

| Palabra | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **pound** (£) | libra | two pounds |
| **dollar** ($) | dólar | three dollars |
| **euro** (€) | euro | five euros |
| **cost** | costar | It costs two pounds. |
| **price** | precio | The price is high. |
| **cheap** | barato | That's cheap. |
| **expensive** | caro | That is not expensive. |
| **total** | total | The total is five pounds fifty. |

> The total for my shopping is fifteen pounds.  
> That is not expensive.  
> The milk costs one pound fifty.  
> That's cheap. Thank you.

## 8. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| How many is the bread? | How **much** is the bread? | precio → *much* |
| How much eggs do you need? | How **many** eggs…? | contable plural → *many* |
| How many water…? | How **much** water…? | incontable → *much* |
| How much are the bread? | How much **is** the bread? | *bread* + *is* |
| How many egg…? | How many **eggs**…? | plural obligatorio |
| It cost two pounds. | It **costs** two pounds. | 3.ª persona *-s* |

---

## 9. Mini prácticas orales

1. Pregunta el precio de 3 productos de tu cocina.  
2. Pregunta cantidades: *How many…?* / *How much…?*  
3. Di un total: *The total is…*  
4. Opina: *That's cheap* / *That's expensive*.

> How much is the bread? — Two pounds.  
> How many apples do you want? — Six.  
> The total is five pounds fifty.

2. How \_\_\_\_ eggs do you need?  
3. How \_\_\_\_ water do you want?  
4. How \_\_\_\_ does the milk cost?  
5. How \_\_\_\_ apples are there?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **much** · 2. **many** · 3. **much** · 4. **much** · 5. **many**
</details>

<details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Precio y moneda

1. It costs two \_\_\_\_. (£)  
2. The \_\_\_\_ is five pounds fifty.  
3. That's \_\_\_\_. (barato)  
4. That is not \_\_\_\_. (caro)  
5. The milk \_\_\_\_ one pound fifty.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Empareja

1. How much is it?  
2. How many eggs…?  
3. pound  
4. cheap  
5. expensive  
6. total  

a) £ · b) precio bajo · c) precio · d) contable · e) precio alto · f) todo junto

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **c** · 2 → **d** · 3 → **a** · 4 → **b** · 5 → **e** · 6 → **f**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrige

1. *How many is the bread?*

3. *How many water do you want?*

<details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Completa el texto

I am at the market. How \_\_\_\_ is the bread? It \_\_\_\_ two pounds. How \_\_\_\_ oranges do I need? Maybe six. How \_\_\_\_ water do we have? We have a lot. The \_\_\_\_ for my shopping is fifteen pounds. That is not \_\_\_\_.

### Ejercicio 8 — Elige la correcta

1. ____  
   a) How many is the milk? · b) How much is the milk? · c) How much are the milk?  
2. ____  
   a) How many eggs…? · b) How much eggs…? · c) How many egg…?  
3. Currency in the UK often:  
   a) dollar · b) pound · c) only euro  
4. We use How much for ____.  
   a) price and uncountable · b) only countable · c) never price

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b** · 2. **a** · 3. **b** · 4. **a**
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Traduce

1. ¿Cuánto cuesta el pan?  
2. ¿Cuántos huevos necesitas?  
3. ¿Cuánta agua quieres?  
4. Cuesta dos libras.  
5. El total es cinco libras cincuenta.  
6. Eso es barato.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

### Ejercicio 10 — Escribe tu compra

Escribe 8–10 frases de una compra. Incluye:

- 2 × *How much is…?* o *How much does… cost?*  
- 2 × *How many…?*  
- 1 × *How much* + incontable (cantidad)  
- *costs / total / cheap* o *expensive*  
- al menos una moneda (*pounds / euros / dollars*)

**Modelo:** el texto de la sección 6.

## 12. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Precio → casi siempre ***How much***.  
2. Contable plural → ***How many***.  
3. Practica números de precio en voz alta (*one pound fifty*).  
4. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 54](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-54-how-much-how-many-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 54 del curso A1](/curso-a1/unit-54).
