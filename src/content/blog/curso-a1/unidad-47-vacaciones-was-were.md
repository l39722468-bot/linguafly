## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 47 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Usar ***was / were*** para lugares y situaciones pasadas.
- Formar pasado regular con ***-ed*** (*visited, played, stayed*).
- Hablar de ***beach, mountains, sightseeing, souvenir***.
- Preguntar: ***Where were you last week?***
- Contar unas vacaciones cortas en pasado.

---

## 1. Was / were

| Sujeto | Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I / he / she / it | **was** | I **was** on the beach. |
| we / you / they | **were** | We **were** in the mountains. |
| Negativa | was not / weren't | I **was not** at home. |

![Was were A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-47/a1-unit47-was-were.png)

```
I/he/she/it + was
we/you/they + were
+ last week / last year / yesterday / last summer
```

### Tip

*Was/were* = pasado de *am/is/are*. Para vacaciones: *I was on holiday* / *We were in Spain*.

---

## 2. Verbos regulares: + -ed

| Presente | Pasado | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| visit | **visited** | She **visited** Paris. |
| play | **played** | We **played** volleyball. |
| stay | **stayed** | I **stayed** at a hotel. |
| walk | **walked** | They **walked** in the old town. |

![Regular past A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-47/a1-unit47-regular-past.png)

```
regular verb + -ed
→ visited · played · stayed · walked
```

### Tip

En A1 empieza con regulares. Combina: *I was on the beach. I played volleyball.*

### Comprueba

1. ¿Cuándo? → **Last summer**.  
2. ¿Dónde? → **Spain / on the beach**.  
3. ¿Qué visitaron los amigos? → **The mountains**.  
4. ¿Qué hicieron en Barcelona? → **Sightseeing**.  
5. ¿Qué compró? → **A souvenir**.  
6. ¿Cómo fue? → **Great** (sunny weather).

## 8. Errores típicos de hispanohablantes

| ❌ Incorrecto | ✅ Correcto | Explicación |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *I **were** on the beach* | *I **was** on the beach* | I → was |
| *We **was** in Spain* | *We **were** in Spain* | we → were |
| *She **visit** Paris* | *She **visited** Paris* | *-ed* |
| *I **play** volleyball yesterday* | *I **played** volleyball yesterday* | pasado |
| *Where **was** you?* | *Where **were** you?* | you → were |
| *on the mountains* | *in the mountains* | *in* + mountains |

## 10. Mini-diálogos para memorizar

> **B:** Yes — a small statue.

> **A:** Was the weather sunny?  
> **B:** Yes, it was. We played volleyball. It was great.

Cambia solo el lugar: fijas ***was/were*** y ***-ed***.

5. I \_\_\_\_ not at home yesterday.

## 12. Plan de práctica (15 minutos)

1. **3 min** — Di 5 frases *was/were* + lugar.  
2. **3 min** — Di 5 con *-ed* (*visited, played, stayed*).  
3. **4 min** — Reescribe el reading con tu viaje.  
4. **5 min** — Mini-entrevista *Where were you…?*

## 13. Consejos para practicar

1. **Foto de viaje:** 5 frases en pasado por foto (*I was… / We visited…*).  
2. **Pareja:** uno pregunta *Where were you?* / *Did you visit…?*; el otro responde.  
3. **Combina** U46: *I stayed at a hotel. The room was clean.*  
4. Prepárate para la [Unidad 48](/curso-a1/unit-48): *Sightseeing* (superlativos: *the biggest, the most famous*).  
5. Practica en la [Unidad 47 del curso A1](/curso-a1/unit-47).
