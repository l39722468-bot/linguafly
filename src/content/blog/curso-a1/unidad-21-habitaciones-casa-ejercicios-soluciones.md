## Lección 1 — Gramática: plurales y adjetivos

**Objetivo:** plurales (*rooms, bathrooms*) y adjetivos (*bright, cozy, small*).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. We have three ____ in our house. → *rooms / room / roomes*  
2. The kitchen is ____ and modern. → *bright / small / cozy*  
3. The living room is very ____. → *cozy / bright / small*  
4. My house ____ four bedrooms. → *has / have / having*  
5. There are two ____ upstairs. → *bathrooms / bathroom / bathroomes*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **rooms** — *room → rooms*.  
2. **bright** — *bright = luminoso*.  
3. **cozy** — *cozy = acogedor*.  
4. **has** — *My house = it → has*.  
5. **bathrooms** — *bathroom → bathrooms*.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. We use ____ before a noun to describe a room. → a) an adjective · b) a verb · c) a pronoun  
7. The ____ is small. → a) bathroom · b) bathrooms · c) bathroomes  
8. We ____ two bedrooms. → a) have · b) has · c) having  
9. The garden ____ big. → a) is · b) are · c) have  
10. How many ____ does your flat have? → a) rooms · b) room · c) roomes

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) an adjective** — *The kitchen is bright.*  
7. **a) bathroom**  
8. **a) have**  
9. **a) is**  
10. **a) rooms**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. The / kitchen / is / bright / and / modern.  
12. We / have / three / bedrooms.  
13. The / living room / is / very / cozy.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. The kitchen is bright and modern.  
12. We have three bedrooms.  
13. The living room is very cozy.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We add -s to make the plural of room: rooms. → ?  
15. Adjectives like bright, small, cozy go after is (The room is bright). → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero** — *The room is bright / small / cozy.*

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: habitaciones de la casa

**Objetivo:** *kitchen, living room, bedroom, bathroom, garden, hall, dining room*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la opción correcta

1. Where do we cook? → a) In the kitchen · b) In the bedroom · c) In the bathroom  
2. Living room = ____. → a) the room with sofa and TV · b) the room to sleep · c) the room to cook  
3. Bedroom = ____. → a) the room with a bed · b) the room with a bath · c) the room with a table  
4. Bathroom = ____. → a) where you have a shower · b) where you eat · c) where you sleep  
5. Garden = ____. → a) outside with plants and flowers · b) inside the house · c) under the house

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) In the kitchen**  
2. **a) the room with sofa and TV**  
3. **a) the room with a bed**  
4. **a) where you have a shower**  
5. **a) outside with plants and flowers**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Relaciona

**6.** Habitación → uso  

| Habitación | Uso |
|------------|-----|
| kitchen | ? |
| bedroom | ? |
| bathroom | ? |
| living room | ? |
| garden | ? |

**7.** Adjetivo → significado  

| Adjetivo | Significado |
|----------|-------------|
| bright | ? |
| small | ? |
| cozy | ? |
| modern | ? |
| large | ? |

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**6.** kitchen → cook food · bedroom → sleep · bathroom → shower, toilet · living room → sofa, TV · garden → plants, outside  

**7.** bright → a lot of light · small → not big · cozy → comfortable, warm · modern → new style · large → big

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. The ____ is where you sleep. → *bedroom / kitchen / garden*  
9. Hall = the ____ near the door. → *hall / room / garden*  
10. Dining room = where you ____ dinner. → *have / cook / sleep*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **bedroom**  
9. **hall**  
10. **have** (también *eat*)

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Elige

11. Stairs = ____. → a) to go up and down · b) to cook · c) to sleep  
12. Balcony = ____. → a) small area outside a room · b) room under the house · c) room for the car  
13. Garage = ____. → a) for the car · b) for cooking · c) for sleeping

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) to go up and down**  
12. **a) small area outside a room**  
13. **a) for the car**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena las palabras

14. We / have / a / kitchen / and / a / living room.  
15. The / bathroom / is / small / but / clean.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. We have a kitchen and a living room.  
15. The bathroom is small but clean.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Tom's house

**Objetivo:** leer la descripción de la casa de Tom.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-21/reading-tom.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Tom"></audio>

> My name is Tom. I live in a house with my family. We have a big kitchen. It is bright and modern. We have a living room too. It is very cozy. We have three bedrooms and two bathrooms. The bathroom upstairs is small but clean. We have a garden at the back. My favourite room is the living room because we watch TV there.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the writer's name? → a) Tom · b) Tim · c) John  
2. How is the kitchen described? → a) Bright and modern · b) Small and old · c) Cozy and dark  
3. How many bedrooms do they have? → a) Three · b) Two · c) Four  
4. How many bathrooms are there? → a) Two · b) One · c) Three  
5. The bathroom upstairs is ____. → a) small but clean · b) big and bright · c) cozy

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Tom**  
2. **a) Bright and modern**  
3. **a) Three**  
4. **a) Two**  
5. **a) small but clean**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Do they have a garden? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say  
7. What is his favourite room? → a) The living room · b) The kitchen · c) The bedroom  
8. Why does he like the living room? → a) Because they watch TV there · b) Because it is big · c) The text doesn't say  
9. The living room is ____. → a) very cozy · b) small · c) dark  
10. Where is the garden? → a) At the back · b) Upstairs · c) Next to the kitchen

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Yes**  
7. **a) The living room**  
8. **a) Because they watch TV there**  
9. **a) very cozy**  
10. **a) At the back**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. Tom ____ with his family. → a) lives · b) live · c) living  
12. The kitchen is ____. → a) big · b) small · c) cozy  
13. Main topic: → a) Description of a house and its rooms · b) Tom's family · c) What they eat  
14. Which word means "luminosa"? → a) Bright · b) Cozy · c) Small  
15. Which word means "acogedor"? → a) Cozy · b) Bright · c) Clean

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) lives**  
12. **a) big**  
13. **a) Description of a house and its rooms**  
14. **a) Bright**  
15. **a) Cozy**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: A big house

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo sobre una casa y sus habitaciones.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-21/listening-house.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: House"></audio>

> **A:** Do you have a big house?  
> **B:** Yes, we have four bedrooms and two bathrooms.  
> **A:** Is the kitchen big?  
> **B:** Yes, it is bright and we have a dining room next to it.  
> **A:** Do you have a garden?  
> **B:** Yes, a small garden at the back. My favourite room is the living room because it is so cozy.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Does B have a big house? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
2. How many bedrooms does B have? → a) Four · b) Three · c) Two  
3. How many bathrooms? → a) Two · b) One · c) Three  
4. Is the kitchen described as ____? → a) Bright · b) Small · c) Dark  
5. What room is next to the kitchen? → a) Dining room · b) Living room · c) Bedroom

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Yes**  
2. **a) Four**  
3. **a) Two**  
4. **a) Bright**  
5. **a) Dining room**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Do they have a garden? → a) Yes, a small one · b) No · c) A big one  
7. Where is the garden? → a) At the back · b) Upstairs · c) Next to the kitchen  
8. What is B's favourite room? → a) The living room · b) The kitchen · c) The bedroom  
9. Why does B like the living room? → a) Because it's cozy · b) Because it's big · c) The audio doesn't say  
10. The dialogue is ____. → a) about a house and its rooms · b) about work · c) about food

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Yes, a small one**  
7. **a) At the back**  
8. **a) The living room**  
9. **a) Because it's cozy**  
10. **a) about a house and its rooms**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. B ____ four bedrooms. → a) has · b) have · c) having  
12. Which adjective describes the living room? → a) Cozy · b) Bright · c) Small  
13. A ____ about the house and rooms. → a) asks · b) ask · c) asking  
14. The garden is ____. → a) small · b) big · c) bright  
15. B says "we have" ____ times. → a) several (bedrooms, bathrooms, dining room, garden) · b) once · c) twice

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) has**  
12. **a) Cozy**  
13. **a) asks**  
14. **a) small**  
15. **a) several (bedrooms, bathrooms, dining room, garden)**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My house or flat

**Objetivo:** describir tu casa o piso con habitaciones y adjetivos.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. We have ____ bedrooms. → *three / bedroom / room*  
2. The ____ is bright. → *kitchen / garden / bathroom*  
3. My ____ room is the living room. → *favourite / small / big*  
4. We have a ____ at the back. → *garden / bedroom / kitchen*  
5. The bathroom ____ small but clean. → *is / are / have*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **three**  
2. **kitchen**  
3. **favourite**  
4. **garden**  
5. **is**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. We / have / a / living room / and / a / kitchen.  
7. The / living room / is / very / cozy.  
8. My / house / has / three / bedrooms.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. We have a living room and a kitchen.  
7. The living room is very cozy.  
8. My house has three bedrooms.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. How many rooms does your house/flat have?  
10. Describe the kitchen or living room (*bright, cozy, small, big*).  
11. Which rooms do you have + your favourite room.  
12. *Do you have a garden?* + your answer.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. We have three bedrooms and two bathrooms.  
10. The kitchen is bright and modern.  
11. We have a living room, a kitchen and two bedrooms. My favourite room is the living room.  
12. Do you have a garden? Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Short paragraph (4–5 sentences): your house/flat and how the rooms are.  
14. 3–4 sentences: rooms + one quality each.  
15. *How many rooms does your house have?* + your answer.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
I live in a flat. We have two bedrooms, a kitchen and a living room. The kitchen is small but bright. The living room is cozy. We don't have a garden.

**14.**  
We have a kitchen — it is bright. We have a living room — it is cozy. We have two bedrooms.

**15.**  
How many rooms does your house have? We have four rooms.

</details>
