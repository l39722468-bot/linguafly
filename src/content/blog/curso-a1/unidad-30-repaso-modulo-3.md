La **Unidad 30 del curso A1** (*Module 3 Review*) cierra el Módulo 3: **mi mundo** (casa, barrio, direcciones, animales, cuerpo y ropa). No introduce gramática nueva: **consolida** las unidades 21–29 para que puedas describir tu entorno y a ti mismo sin dudar.

Esta guía es un **mapa de repaso** con tablas, checklist, audios y ejercicios. Úsala para detectar huecos y volver a la [unidad concreta](/blog/curso-a1) si algo falla.

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 30 — Module 3 Review](/curso-a1/unit-30)  
> **Guías del módulo:** [U21](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-21-habitaciones-casa) · [U23](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-23-there-is-there-are) · [U26](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-26-direcciones-imperativos) · [U28](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-28-partes-cuerpo-have-got) · [U29](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-29-ropa-present-continuous)

## Qué deberías poder hacer al terminar el Módulo 3

- Describir **habitaciones y muebles** (*living room, sofa, desk…*).
- Usar ***There is / There are*** + preposiciones (*in, on, under, behind, next to, between, opposite*).
- Hablar del **barrio** con *a/an/the* y *some/any*.
- Dar **direcciones**: *Turn left, Go straight, Past the…*.
- Clasificar **animales** (*pet / farm / wild*) y adjetivos (*friendly, cute, dangerous*).
- Describir el **cuerpo** con ***have / has got*** y *this/these*.
- Decir qué **llevas puesto**: ***I am wearing…***.

## 2. *There is / There are* + preposiciones (U23–U24)

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| There **is** | There is a sofa in the living room. |
| There **are** | There are two chairs in the room. |
| There **isn’t** / **aren’t** | There isn’t a TV. There aren’t any chairs. |
| **Is/Are** there…? | Is there a bathroom? Are there any shops? |

Preposiciones del módulo:

| Grupo | Preposiciones |
| :--- | :--- |
| U23 | in, on, under, behind |
| U24 | next to, between, in front of, opposite |

> The cat is **under** the table.  
> The bank is **next to** the pharmacy.  
> The supermarket is **between** the bank and the library.

## 3. Barrio: *a/an/the* y *some/any* (U25)

| Regla | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| Primera mención | There is **a** park near my house. |
| Conocido | I go to **the** park every weekend. |
| Afirmativa | There are **some** shops in my street. |
| Negativa / pregunta | There aren’t **any** pharmacies. Are there **any** cafes? |

## 9. Texto modelo de repaso (todo junto)

> My name is Anna. I live in a small flat. There is a living room, a kitchen, two bedrooms and a bathroom. There is a sofa in the living room and a table next to the window. My room is small but I have got a bed and a desk. I have got a pet cat. She is very friendly. Today I am wearing a blue shirt and jeans. Near my flat there are a park and a supermarket. Go straight and turn left at the corner.

## 10. Diálogo modelo de cierre

> **A:** What is in your living room?  
> **B:** There is a sofa and there are two chairs. The table is next to the window.  
> **A:** Do you have got a pet?  
> **B:** Yes, I have got a dog. He is very friendly.  
> **A:** What are you wearing?  
> **B:** I am wearing a shirt and trousers.  
> **A:** How do I get to the park?  
> **B:** Go straight and turn left.

## 11. Errores típicos del Módulo 3 (repaso)

| ❌ | ✅ |
| :--- | :--- |
| *There is two chairs* | *There **are** two chairs* |
| *I has got blue eyes* | *I **have** got blue eyes* |
| *I wearing a shirt* | *I **am** wearing a shirt* |
| *Turn left in the corner* | *Turn left **at** the corner* |
| *This are my feet* | ***These** are my feet* |
| *two foots* | *two **feet*** |
| *a blue trousers* | ***blue trousers*** |
| *The bank is between the pharmacy* | *… **between** A **and** B* |

## 12. Mini-diálogos por bloque

**Casa**  
> A: Is there a sofa in the living room?  
> B: Yes, there is. And there are two chairs.

**Barrio**  
> A: Where is the supermarket?

**Direcciones**  
> A: How do I get to the park?

**Animales**

**Cuerpo**

> B: These are my feet.

**Ropa**

### Rutina de repaso de 10 minutos

1. **2 min** — 4 frases *There is/are* + preposición.  
2. **2 min** — 3 pasos de direcciones.  
3. **2 min** — *have got* (cuerpo o mascota).  
4. **2 min** — *I am wearing…* (outfit de hoy).  
5. **2 min** — Lee el texto de Anna sin mirar y resume.

### Checklist mental final

- Singular/plural → *is/are*  
- Lugar → preposición correcta  
- Posesión muebles → *mine/yours*  
- Calle → imperativo sin *you*  
- Cuerpo → *have/has got* · *feet*  
- Ahora ropa → *am/is/are wearing*  
- Barrio → *a* luego *the* · *some/any*

2. There \_\_\_\_ two chairs in the room.  
3. She \_\_\_\_ wearing a dress.  
4. I \_\_\_\_ wearing a shirt.

2. **are**
