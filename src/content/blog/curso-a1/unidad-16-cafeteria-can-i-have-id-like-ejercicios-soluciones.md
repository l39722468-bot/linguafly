## Lección 1 — Gramática: *Can I have…?* e *I'd like…*

**Objetivo:** Pedir con educación y ofrecer con *Would you like…?*

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. Can ____ have a coffee, please? → *I / you / we*  
2. I ____ like a tea, please. → *would / can / do*  
3. I'd ____ a croissant, please. → *like / have / want*  
4. Can ____ have the bill, please? → *I / you / he*  
5. Would ____ like something to drink? → *you / I / we*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **I** — *Can I have…?*  
2. **would** — *I would like…*  
3. **like** — *I'd like = I would like*  
4. **I** — *Can I have the bill?*  
5. **you** — *Would you like…?*

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. To order politely we say ____. → a) Can I have…? / I'd like… · b) Give me… · c) I want…  
7. I ____ like a juice. → a) would · b) can · c) do  
8. After "Can I have…?" we often add ____. → a) please · b) now · c) quickly  
9. Would you like…? is ____ than "Do you want…?". → a) more polite · b) the same · c) less polite  
10. Can I ____ a water? → a) have · b) want · c) need

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Can I have…? / I'd like…**  
7. **a) would**  
8. **a) please**  
9. **a) more polite**  
10. **a) have**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. Can / I / have / a / coffee, / please?  
12. I'd / like / a / tea, / please.  
13. Would / you / like / something / to eat?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. Can I have a coffee, please?  
12. I'd like a tea, please.  
13. Would you like something to eat?

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use "Can I have…?" to ask for something politely. → ?  
15. "I'd like" is short for "I would like". → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero** — *I'd = I would*

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: bebidas, snacks y precios

**Objetivo:** *coffee, tea, juice, croissant, sandwich, bill, How much…?*

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige la opción correcta

1. I'd like a ____, please. → a) coffee · b) book · c) pen  
2. A ____ is a hot drink. → a) tea · b) juice · c) water  
3. Orange ____ is a drink. → a) juice · b) fruit · c) cake  
4. A ____ is sweet and you eat it with coffee. → a) croissant · b) soup · c) salad  
5. The ____ is where you pay in a cafe. → a) counter · b) table · c) chair  
6. How ____ is the coffee? → a) much · b) many · c) often

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) coffee**  
2. **a) tea**  
3. **a) juice**  
4. **a) croissant**  
5. **a) counter**  
6. **a) much**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–9 — Relaciona

**7.** Bebida → descripción  

| Bebida | Descripción |
|--------|-------------|
| coffee | ? |
| tea | ? |
| juice | ? |
| water | ? |
| milk | ? |

**8.** Snack → descripción  

| Snack | Descripción |
|-------|-------------|
| croissant | ? |
| sandwich | ? |
| cake | ? |
| cookie | ? |
| toast | ? |

**9.** Pregunta → respuesta  

| Pregunta | Respuesta |
|----------|-----------|
| Can I have a coffee? | ? |
| Would you like something to eat? | ? |
| How much is it? | ? |
| Can I have the bill? | ? |
| Anything else? | ? |

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**7.** coffee → hot drink, often with milk · tea → hot drink with leaves · juice → from fruit · water → no sugar, no flavour · milk → white drink  

**8.** croissant → French pastry · sandwich → bread with filling · cake → sweet dessert · cookie → small sweet biscuit · toast → grilled bread  

**9.** Can I have a coffee? → Yes, of course. · Would you like something to eat? → Yes, a croissant please. · How much is it? → Two euros fifty. · Can I have the bill? → Sure, one moment. · Anything else? → No, that's all thanks.

</details>

### Ejercicios 10–12 — Completa

10. Can I ____ the bill, please? → *have / want / like*  
11. I'd ____ a tea and a sandwich. → *like / have / want*  
12. How ____ is the tea? → *much / many / often*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

10. **have**  
11. **like**  
12. **much**

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Contexto

13. You are at the counter. You ____.  
    a) Can I have a coffee and a croissant, please? · b) I am from Madrid. · c) How are you?  
14. The ____ has tables and chairs. → a) cafe · b) hospital · c) airport  
15. Would you like something to drink? → a) Yes, I'd like an orange juice. · b) I am fine thanks. · c) My name is Anna.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

13. **a) Can I have a coffee and a croissant, please?**  
14. **a) cafe**  
15. **a) Yes, I'd like an orange juice.**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Cafe Sunny

**Objetivo:** Leer la información de una cafetería (menú y precios).

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-16/reading-cafe-sunny.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Cafe Sunny"></audio>

> Welcome to Cafe Sunny. We have coffee, tea, juice, water and milk. For snacks we have croissants, sandwiches, cake and cookies. Coffee is two euros. Tea is one euro fifty. A croissant is two euros twenty. You can order at the counter or sit at a table. We accept cards and cash.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the name of the cafe? → a) Cafe Sunny · b) Cafe Madrid · c) Cafe Big  
2. How much is the coffee? → a) Two euros · b) One euro · c) Three euros  
3. How much is the tea? → a) One euro fifty · b) Two euros · c) One euro  
4. What drinks does the cafe have? → a) Coffee, tea, juice, water, milk. · b) Only coffee. · c) Beer and wine.  
5. What snacks are on the menu? → a) Croissants, sandwiches, cake, cookies. · b) Pizza and pasta. · c) Only cake.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Cafe Sunny**  
2. **a) Two euros**  
3. **a) One euro fifty**  
4. **a) Coffee, tea, juice, water, milk.**  
5. **a) Croissants, sandwiches, cake, cookies.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. A croissant costs ____. → a) two euros twenty · b) one euro · c) three euros  
7. Where can you order? → a) At the counter or at a table. · b) Only at the counter. · c) Only outside.  
8. Do they accept cards? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say  
9. The text is ____. → a) a cafe menu / information · b) a hotel list · c) a school timetable  
10. Which is cheaper? → a) Tea · b) Coffee · c) Croissant

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) two euros twenty**  
7. **a) At the counter or at a table.**  
8. **a) Yes**  
9. **a) a cafe menu / information**  
10. **a) Tea** (1.50€)

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. They accept ____ and cash. → a) cards · b) only cash · c) only cards  
12. Can you sit at a table? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say  
13. What phrase can you use to order politely? → a) Can I have…? / I'd like… · b) Give me… · c) I want…  
14. How much would a coffee and a croissant cost? → a) Four euros twenty · b) Three euros · c) Five euros  
15. Main topic:  
    a) A cafe: drinks, snacks, prices, how to order. · b) A school. · c) A hospital.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) cards**  
12. **a) Yes**  
13. **a) Can I have…? / I'd like…**  
14. **a) Four euros twenty** (2 + 2.20)  
15. **a) A cafe: drinks, snacks, prices, how to order.**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Ordering

**Objetivo:** Escuchar un pedido en la cafetería.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-16/listening-order.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Ordering"></audio>

> **Waiter:** Hi. What can I get you?  
> **Customer:** I'd like a coffee and a croissant, please.  
> **Waiter:** Anything else?  
> **Customer:** No, that's all thanks.  
> **Waiter:** That's four euros twenty please.  
> **Customer:** Here you are.  
> **Waiter:** Thank you. Enjoy your coffee!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Where is the dialogue? → a) In a cafe · b) In a school · c) In a hospital  
2. What does the customer order? → a) A coffee and a croissant. · b) A tea and a sandwich. · c) Only water.  
3. Does the customer want anything else? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The audio doesn't say  
4. How much is the total? → a) Four euros twenty · b) Three euros · c) Five euros  
5. What does the waiter say first? → a) What can I get you? · b) That's four euros twenty. · c) Anything else?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) In a cafe**  
2. **a) A coffee and a croissant.**  
3. **a) No**  
4. **a) Four euros twenty**  
5. **a) What can I get you?**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. The customer uses "I'd like" to order. → a) True · b) False  
7. What does the customer reply when the waiter asks "Anything else?"? → a) No, that's all thanks. · b) Yes, a tea please. · c) How much is it?  
8. The waiter says "Enjoy your coffee". → a) True · b) False  
9. How does the customer order? → a) Politely with I'd like and please. · b) Give me a coffee. · c) I want coffee.  
10. Main topic:  
    a) Ordering at a cafe · b) Buying a ticket · c) At the doctor

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) True**  
7. **a) No, that's all thanks.**  
8. **a) True**  
9. **a) Politely with I'd like and please.**  
10. **a) Ordering at a cafe**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. Does the customer say "please"? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
12. Who pays? → a) The customer · b) The waiter · c) Nobody  
13. What phrase could you use to ask for the bill? → a) Can I have the bill, please? · b) Give me money. · c) I don't pay.  
14. When does the waiter say "Anything else?"? → a) After the customer orders. · b) Before the customer orders. · c) When giving the bill.  
15. How many times does the customer say "thanks"? → a) Once (that's all thanks). · b) Never · c) Twice

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) Yes**  
12. **a) The customer**  
13. **a) Can I have the bill, please?**  
14. **a) After the customer orders.**  
15. **a) Once (that's all thanks).**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My order

**Objetivo:** Escribir pedidos, ofertas y la cuenta.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. Can ____ have a coffee? → *I / you / we*  
2. I'd ____ a tea, please. → *like / have / want*  
3. How ____ is the coffee? → *much / many / often*  
4. Can I have ____ bill, please? → *the / a / an*  
5. Would ____ like something to eat? → *you / I / we*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **I**  
2. **like**  
3. **much**  
4. **the**  
5. **you**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. Can / I / have / a / coffee, / please?  
7. I'd / like / an / orange juice, / please.  
8. No, / that's / all / thanks.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. Can I have a coffee, please?  
7. I'd like an orange juice, please.  
8. No, that's all thanks.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. Order a coffee politely (*Can I have… please?*).  
10. Order a drink and a snack (*I'd like…*).  
11. *Would you like something to drink?* + your answer.  
12. Ask for the bill.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. Can I have a coffee, please?  
10. I'd like a tea and a sandwich, please.  
11. Would you like something to drink? Yes, I'd like an orange juice.  
12. Can I have the bill, please?

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos / mini-diálogos (ejemplos)

13. Mini-dialogue: waiter asks → you order coffee + croissant → price.  
14. Three things you can order in a cafe.  
15. Reply to "Anything else?" when you don't want more.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
What would you like? I'd like a coffee and a croissant, please. That's five pounds, please.

**14.**  
A coffee. A tea. A cake.

**15.**  
No, that's all thanks.

</details>
