## Lección 1 — Gramática: must / should

**Objetivo:** *must* (obligación) y *should* (consejo) en el aeropuerto.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. You ____ show your passport at check-in. → *must / should / canning*  
2. You ____ go to the gate early. → *should / musts / going*  
3. You ____ have your ticket. → *must / shoulding / are*  
4. You ____ arrive early for your flight. → *should / musts / do*  
5. You ____ show your boarding pass. → *must / shoulds / am*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **must** — obligación.  
2. **should** — consejo.  
3. **must**  
4. **should**  
5. **must**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *Must* shows ____. → a) obligation · b) only a wish · c) past tense  
7. *Should* shows ____. → a) advice or recommendation · b) only past · c) a sport  
8. After *must* / *should* we use ____. → a) base verb · b) -ing only · c) past participle only  
9. You ____ your passport. → a) must show · b) must showing · c) musts show  
10. Check-in is ____. → a) where you get your boarding pass · b) the plane seat · c) the hotel

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) obligation**  
7. **a) advice or recommendation**  
8. **a) base verb**  
9. **a) must show**  
10. **a) where you get your boarding pass**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. You / must / show / your / passport / at check-in.  
12. Go / to / gate / number / ten.  
13. You / should / arrive / early / for / your / flight.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. You must show your passport at check-in.  
12. Go to gate number ten.  
13. You should arrive early for your flight.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *Must* + base verb: *You must show your passport.* → True / False  
15. *Should* gives advice: *You should go early.* → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: aeropuerto

**Objetivo:** *passport, ticket, check-in, boarding pass, gate, boarding, luggage, suitcase, flight*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *Passport* = ____. → a) document for travel · b) a gate number · c) a suitcase only  
2. *Ticket* = ____. → a) document for the flight · b) only luggage · c) a gate  
3. *Check-in* = ____. → a) where you get your boarding pass · b) the plane seat · c) customs only  
4. *Gate* = ____. → a) where you board the plane · b) the hotel desk · c) a restaurant  
5. *Boarding* = ____. → a) getting on the plane · b) buying food · c) sleeping

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) document for travel**  
2. **a) document for the flight**  
3. **a) where you get your boarding pass**  
4. **a) where you board the plane**  
5. **a) getting on the plane**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Empareja

6. Empareja: passport / boarding pass / gate / luggage / flight → travel document · get at check-in · board the plane · bags · plane trip  
7. Empareja: ticket / check-in / boarding / suitcase / passport → for the flight · desk · get on the plane · luggage · show at the airport

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **passport → travel document** · **boarding pass → get at check-in** · **gate → board the plane** · **luggage → bags** · **flight → plane trip**  
7. **ticket → for the flight** · **check-in → desk** · **boarding → get on the plane** · **suitcase → luggage** · **passport → show at the airport**

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. You must show your ____. → *passport / passportsing / gate*  
9. Gate ____ ten. → *number / numbers / numbering*  
10. Have a good ____! → *flight / flightsing / gate*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **passport**  
9. **number**  
10. **flight**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Definiciones

11. *Luggage* means ____. → a) bags you take when you travel · b) only a ticket · c) a gate  
12. A *boarding pass* is ____. → a) a document to get on the plane · b) a hotel key · c) a menu  
13. Where do you get your boarding pass? → a) At check-in. · b) On the plane seat only · c) At home never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) bags you take when you travel**  
12. **a) a document to get on the plane**  
13. **a) At check-in.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. You show your passport and ticket at check-in. → True / False  
15. The gate is where you board the plane. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: At the airport

**Objetivo:** leer un texto sobre el aeropuerto con *must* y *should*.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-41/reading-airport.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: At the airport"></audio>

> I am at the airport today. My flight is at ten o'clock. I must show my passport and my ticket at check-in. After that, I get my boarding pass. I should go to the gate early. Boarding starts at half past nine. I have one suitcase. My luggage is not heavy.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Where is the writer? → a) At the airport. · b) At home. · c) At school.  
2. What time is the flight? → a) At ten o'clock. · b) At nine o'clock. · c) At midnight.  
3. What must the writer show? → a) Passport and ticket. · b) Only a book. · c) Only food.  
4. Where does the writer show them? → a) At check-in. · b) At the hotel. · c) At school.  
5. When does boarding start? → a) At half past nine. · b) At ten. · c) At noon.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) At the airport.**  
2. **a) At ten o'clock.**  
3. **a) Passport and ticket.**  
4. **a) At check-in.**  
5. **a) At half past nine.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. How many suitcases does the writer have? → a) One. · b) Five. · c) Zero.  
7. Is the luggage heavy? → a) No. · b) Yes. · c) The text doesn't say.  
8. The writer should go to the gate ____. → a) Early. · b) Late. · c) Never.  
9. Where do you get the boarding pass? → a) Check-in. · b) The plane seat. · c) The park.  
10. A boarding pass is ____. → a) A document to board the plane. · b) A suitcase. · c) A gate number only.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) One.**  
7. **a) No.**  
8. **a) Early.**  
9. **a) Check-in.**  
10. **a) A document to board the plane.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text is about ____. → a) The airport and a flight. · b) Only food. · c) Only sports.  
12. *Must show* means ____. → a) An obligation to show something. · b) A wish only. · c) Past tense.  
13. The *gate* is ____. → a) The place where you board. · b) The hotel room. · c) The ticket price.  
14. *Luggage* means ____. → a) Bags and suitcases. · b) Only tickets. · c) Only gates.  
15. The main topic is ____. → a) What to do at the airport. · b) Cooking. · c) School subjects.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) The airport and a flight.**  
12. **a) An obligation to show something.**  
13. **a) The place where you board.**  
14. **a) Bags and suitcases.**  
15. **a) What to do at the airport.**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Check-in

**Objetivo:** escuchar indicaciones en el aeropuerto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-41/listening-check-in.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Check-in"></audio>

> **A:** Where is the check-in?  
> **B:** Over there. You must show your passport and ticket.  
> **A:** Thank you. What gate is the flight to London?  
> **B:** Gate twenty-two. You should go now; boarding starts in ten minutes.  
> **A:** I have my boarding pass.  
> **B:** Perfect. Have a good flight!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. A's first question is ____. → a) “Where is the check-in?” · b) “Where is the hotel?” · c) “What time is lunch?”  
2. What must A show? → a) Passport and ticket. · b) Only food. · c) Only a book.  
3. What gate is the London flight? → a) Gate twenty-two. · b) Gate two. · c) Gate ten.  
4. When does boarding start? → a) In ten minutes. · b) In one hour. · c) Tomorrow.  
5. Does A have a boarding pass? → a) Yes. · b) No. · c) The audio doesn't say.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) “Where is the check-in?”**  
2. **a) Passport and ticket.**  
3. **a) Gate twenty-two.**  
4. **a) In ten minutes.**  
5. **a) Yes.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. B says A should ____. → a) Go now. · b) Wait three hours. · c) Go home.  
7. Where is check-in? → a) Over there. · b) Outside the city. · c) At school.  
8. “Have a good flight!” is ____. → a) A wish for a good trip. · b) A refusal. · c) A ticket.  
9. The dialogue is ____. → a) At the airport. · b) At home. · c) In a park.  
10. The flight is ____. → a) A plane to London. · b) A bus to London. · c) A train only.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Go now.**  
7. **a) Over there.**  
8. **a) A wish for a good trip.**  
9. **a) At the airport.**  
10. **a) A plane to London.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Must* here shows ____. → a) An obligation. · b) Only a hobby. · c) Past simple.  
12. *Boarding* means ____. → a) Getting on the plane. · b) Buying lunch. · c) Sleeping.  
13. After the gate information, A says ____. → a) “Thank you.” · b) “Goodbye forever.” · c) “I refuse.”  
14. The *gate* is ____. → a) The place where you board. · b) The suitcase. · c) The passport.  
15. The dialogue gives ____. → a) Airport instructions. · b) Cooking recipes. · c) Sports scores.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) An obligation.**  
12. **a) Getting on the plane.**  
13. **a) “Thank you.”**  
14. **a) The place where you board.**  
15. **a) Airport instructions.**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: Airport tips

**Objetivo:** escribir instrucciones y tips con *must* / *should*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. You ____ show your passport. → *must / shoulds / am*  
2. You ____ go to the gate early. → *should / musts / are*  
3. You ____ your ticket at check-in. → *must show / must showing / musts*  
4. Go ____ the gate now. → *to / for / at*  
5. You ____ have your boarding pass. → *must / shoulding / do*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **must**  
2. **should**  
3. **must show**  
4. **to**  
5. **must**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. You / must / show / your / passport / at check-in.  
7. My / flight / is / at / ten o'clock.  
8. You / should / go / to / the / gate / early.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. You must show your passport at check-in.  
7. My flight is at ten o'clock.  
8. You should go to the gate early.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Escribe una obligación con *must* en el aeropuerto.  
10. Escribe un consejo con *should*.  
11. Dos frases: gate + hora del vuelo.  
12. Pregunta y respuesta: *Where is check-in?*  
13. Párrafo corto (3–4 frases) sobre check-in y gate.  
14. Dos tips: *must* + *should*.  
15. Lista: qué *must* / *should* llevar o hacer.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. You must show your passport at check-in.  
10. You should arrive early for your flight.  
11. My gate is number fifteen. My flight is at nine o'clock.  
12. Where is check-in? Over there.  
13. I must show my passport and ticket at check-in. Then I get my boarding pass. I should go to the gate early.  
14. You must show your passport. You should go to the gate now.  
15. I must have my passport and ticket. I should arrive early.

</details>
