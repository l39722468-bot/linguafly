## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 52 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Distinguir **countable** (*apple, egg*) e **uncountable** (*milk, water, bread, rice*).
- Usar ***many*** con plurales contables.
- Usar ***much*** con incontables (sobre todo en negativas y preguntas).
- Usar ***a lot of*** con ambos tipos.
- Preguntar: ***How many…?*** / ***How much…?***
- Hablar de una **lista de compra** con cantidades naturales.

---

## 1. Countable vs uncountable

| Tipo | Idea | Ejemplos | Cantidad |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Countable** | se pueden contar | apple, egg, orange, banana | one apple, two eggs, many apples |
| **Uncountable** | sin plural normal | milk, water, bread, rice, sugar, coffee | some milk, much water, a lot of rice |

> Apples and eggs are **countable** — we say two apples.  
> Milk, water, bread and rice are **uncountable**.  
> We don't usually say *two milks* or *three rices* in A1.

## 2. *Many* + contable plural

Usa ***many*** con cosas que puedes contar (plural):

| Correcto | Incorrecto típico |
| :--- | :--- |
| How **many** eggs do you need? | *How much eggs?* |
| I need **many** apples. | *I need much apples.* |
| There aren't **many** oranges. | *There isn't many oranges.* |

> How many eggs do you need?  
> How many apples do you want?  
> She has a lot of apples in the basket. / She has many apples.

## 3. *Much* + incontable

Usa ***much*** con incontables. En A1 es muy frecuente en **negativas** y **preguntas**:

| Correcto | Incorrecto típico |
| :--- | :--- |
| I don't have **much** milk left. | *I don't have many milk.* |
| There isn't **much** bread left. | *There aren't much bread.* |
| Do we have **much** milk? | *Do we have many milk?* |

> I don't have much milk left.  
> There isn't much bread left.  
> Do we have much milk? — No, we don't have much.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-52/isnt-much-bread.mp3" title="🔊 There isn't much bread left"></audio>

Pregunta con incontable: ***How much*** milk / water / rice / sugar…?

> How much milk do we have?  
> How much water do you want?

## 4. *A lot of* — contables e incontables

***A lot of*** funciona con **ambos**. Es la opción más cómoda en afirmativas:

| Con contable | Con incontable |
| :--- | :--- |
| a lot of apples | a lot of water |
| a lot of eggs | a lot of bread |
| a lot of oranges | a lot of rice / milk |

> We have a lot of water in the fridge.  
> We have a lot of water and bread.  
> I'll buy water and many apples. / I'll buy a lot of apples.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-52/alot-water-bread.mp3" title="🔊 We have a lot of water and bread"></audio>

### Tabla rápida

| Cuantificador | Contable plural | Incontable | Uso típico A1 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **many** | ✅ many eggs | ❌ | preguntas / negativas / afirmativas |
| **much** | ❌ | ✅ much milk | preguntas / negativas |
| **a lot of** | ✅ a lot of eggs | ✅ a lot of milk | especialmente afirmativas |

## 5. Vocabulario esencial

| Uncountable | Countable |
| :--- | :--- |
| milk | apple / apples |
| water | egg / eggs |
| bread | orange / oranges |
| rice | banana / bananas |
| sugar | tomato / tomatoes |
| coffee | carrot / carrots |

> I need some milk and bread.  
> How many eggs do you want?  
> There is a lot of water in the fridge.  
> We say some rice, a lot of bread — not two rices.

## 8. *Too much* (aviso A1)

En el reading aparece ***too much*** = demasiado (incontable):

> We have **too much** sugar at home.  
> Don't buy **too much** sugar.

Con contables, más adelante verás *too many* (*too many apples*). En esta unidad prioriza *too much* + incontable.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-52/too-much-sugar.mp3" title="🔊 Don't buy too much sugar"></audio>

## 9. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| How much eggs? | How **many** eggs? | *eggs* = contable plural |
| How many milk? | How **much** milk? | *milk* = incontable |
| I don't have many milk. | I don't have **much** milk. | incontable → *much* |
| two rices / three breads | some rice / some bread | incontables sin plural normal |
| a lot of of water | **a lot of** water | una sola *of* |
| There aren't much bread. | There **isn't much** bread. | incontable → *isn't* + *much* |

## 11. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — ¿Contable o incontable?

1. milk → \_\_\_\_  
2. apple → \_\_\_\_  
3. rice → \_\_\_\_  
4. eggs → \_\_\_\_  
5. bread → \_\_\_\_  
6. water → \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **uncountable** · 2. **countable** · 3. **uncountable** · 4. **countable** · 5. **uncountable** · 6. **uncountable**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — *much* / *many* / *a lot of*

1. I don't have \_\_\_\_ milk left.  
2. How \_\_\_\_ eggs do you need?  
3. We have \_\_\_\_ water in the fridge.  
4. There isn't \_\_\_\_ bread left.  
5. She has \_\_\_\_ apples in the basket.  
6. How \_\_\_\_ milk do we have?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **much**  
2. **many**  
3. **a lot of**  
4. **much**  
5. **a lot of** / **many**  
6. **much**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Elige la correcta

1. ____  
   a) How many milk? · b) How much milk? · c) How much milks?  
2. ____  
   a) How many eggs? · b) How much eggs? · c) How many egg?  
3. ____  
   a) a lot of rice · b) many rice · c) much rices  
4. Which is uncountable?  
   a) apple · b) egg · c) rice

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b** · 2. **a** · 3. **a** · 4. **c**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Completa con vocabulario

1. I need some \_\_\_\_ and bread. (leche)  
2. How many \_\_\_\_ do you want? (huevos)  
3. There is a lot of \_\_\_\_ in the fridge. (agua)  
4. We have some \_\_\_\_ but not much water. (arroz)  
5. Don't buy too much \_\_\_\_. (azúcar)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **milk** · 2. **eggs** · 3. **water** · 4. **rice** · 5. **sugar**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrige

1. *How much eggs do you need?*  
2. *I don't have many milk.*  
3. *We have two rices.*  
4. *How many water do you want?*  
5. *There aren't much bread left.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. How **many** eggs do you need?  
2. I don't have **much** milk.  
3. We have **some** rice. / We have **a lot of** rice.  
4. How **much** water do you want?  
5. There **isn't much** bread left.
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Empareja

1. many  
2. much  
3. a lot of  
4. How many…?  
5. How much…?  

a) milk / water / bread  
b) eggs / apples (pregunta)  
c) contable e incontable  
d) eggs / apples (afirm./neg./preg.)  
e) milk / rice (pregunta)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **d** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **c** · 4 → **b** · 5 → **e**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Completa el diálogo

A: Do we have \_\_\_\_ milk?  
B: No, we don't have \_\_\_\_.  
A: How \_\_\_\_ eggs do we need?  
B: Six eggs and \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_ bread.  
A: I'll buy water and \_\_\_\_ apples.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**much** · **much** · **many** · **a lot** · **of** · **many** / **a lot of**
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Completa el texto

I need to buy some food today. We don't have \_\_\_\_ milk or bread left. I want \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_ water and some rice. For fruit I need \_\_\_\_ apples and six \_\_\_\_. We have too \_\_\_\_ sugar at home. How \_\_\_\_ oranges do we need?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**much** · **a lot** · **of** · **many** · **eggs** · **much** · **many**
</details>

## 13. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Si dudas en afirmativa, usa ***a lot of***: funciona casi siempre.  
2. Pregunta mental: ¿puedo decir *two ___?* → contable → *many*.  
3. *Some / any* se practican a fondo en la Unidad 53.  
4. Practica en la [Unidad 52 del curso A1](/curso-a1/unit-52).

La **Unidad 53** trabaja ***some & any*** en afirmativas, negativas y preguntas.
