## Lección 1 — Gramática: *don't* y *Do you…?*

**Objetivo:** Negativa con *don't*, preguntas *Do you/they…?* y respuestas cortas.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ like coffee. I prefer tea. → *don't / doesn't / not*  
2. We ____ watch TV in the morning. → *don't / doesn't / aren't*  
3. ____ you play football? → *Do / Does / Are*  
4. Do you cook? Yes, I ____. → *do / don't / does*  
5. They ____ eat meat. They are vegetarian. → *don't / doesn't / not*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **don't** — *I/you/we/they* + *don't*.  
2. **don't** — *don't* + verbo en forma base.  
3. **Do** — *Do you…?*  
4. **do** — *Yes, I do.*  
5. **don't** — *They don't eat.*

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. Do you ____ housework? No, I don't. → a) do · b) does · c) doing  
7. We ____ read books every day. → a) don't · b) doesn't · c) not  
8. ____ they like music? Yes, they do. → a) Do · b) Does · c) Are  
9. Do you speak English? No, I ____. → a) do · b) don't · c) doesn't  
10. I ____ drink alcohol. → a) don't · b) doesn't · c) am not

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) do** — *Do you do housework?* (auxiliar + verbo)  
7. **a) don't**  
8. **a) Do** — *Do they like…?*  
9. **b) don't** — *No, I don't.*  
10. **a) don't**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. I / don't / like / getting up early.  
12. Do / you / play / tennis?  
13. No, / we / don't / watch / TV.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I don't like getting up early.  
12. Do you play tennis?  
13. No, we don't watch TV.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. With I, you, we, they we use "don't" for the negative. → ?  
15. To ask about habits we say "Do you…?" and answer "Yes, I do" or "No, I don't". → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: ocio y tareas domésticas

**Objetivo:** *watch TV, cook, play football, do the housework, like, read* y más.

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige la opción correcta

1. I ____ TV every evening. → a) watch · b) watches · c) watching  
2. We ____ the housework on Saturdays. → a) do · b) does · c) doing  
3. Do you ____ music? Yes, I do. → a) like · b) likes · c) liking  
4. I ____ dinner. My husband washes the dishes. → a) cook · b) cooks · c) cooking  
5. They ____ football at weekends. → a) play · b) plays · c) playing  
6. I ____ read books in the evening. → a) don't · b) doesn't · c) not

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) watch**  
2. **a) do**  
3. **a) like**  
4. **a) cook**  
5. **a) play**  
6. **a) don't**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–10 — Relaciona

**7.** Acción → traducción  

| Inglés | Español |
|--------|---------|
| watch TV | ? |
| do the housework | ? |
| cook dinner | ? |
| play football | ? |
| read books | ? |

**8.** Acción → traducción  

| Inglés | Español |
|--------|---------|
| wash the dishes | ? |
| clean the house | ? |
| listen to music | ? |
| go shopping | ? |
| meet friends | ? |

**9.** Pregunta → respuesta  

| Pregunta | Respuesta |
|----------|-----------|
| Do you like music? | ? |
| Do you cook? | ? |
| Do they play tennis? | ? |
| Do we have time? | ? |
| Do you do housework? | ? |

**10.** Hábito → tipo  

| Hábito | Tipo |
|--------|------|
| watch TV | ? |
| do the housework | ? |
| play sport | ? |
| read | ? |
| cook | ? |

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**7.** watch TV → Ver la tele · do the housework → Hacer las tareas · cook dinner → Cocinar la cena · play football → Jugar al fútbol · read books → Leer libros  

**8.** wash the dishes → Lavar los platos · clean the house → Limpiar la casa · listen to music → Escuchar música · go shopping → Ir de compras · meet friends → Quedar con amigos  

**9.** Do you like music? → Yes, I do. · Do you cook? → No, I don't. · Do they play tennis? → Yes, they do. · Do we have time? → Yes, we do. · Do you do housework? → Yes, I do.  

**10.** watch TV → Ocio en casa · do the housework → Tarea doméstica · play sport → Actividad física · read → Ocio tranquilo · cook → Tarea en la cocina

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Completa

11. ____ you like reading? → *Do / Does / Are*  
12. We ____ play video games. We prefer sport. → *don't / doesn't / not*  
13. Do they ____ the bathroom? Yes, they do. → *clean / cleans / cleaning*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **Do**  
12. **don't**  
13. **clean** — tras *Do* el verbo va en forma base.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Contexto

14. Someone asks "Do you watch TV in the evening?". You don't watch TV. You ____.  
    a) Yes, I do. · b) No, I don't. · c) I am fine.  
15. You want to ask if someone likes cooking. You ____.  
    a) Do you like cooking? · b) What time is it? · c) Where do you work?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **b) No, I don't.**  
15. **a) Do you like cooking?**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Sara's habits

**Objetivo:** Leer sobre hábitos con *don't* y *like*.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-14/reading-sara.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Sara"></audio>

> My name is Sara. I don't like getting up early. I get up at eight. I don't watch TV in the morning. I have breakfast and go to work. In the evening I cook dinner. I don't eat in restaurants often. I like reading and listening to music. I don't play sport. On the weekend I meet my friends or stay at home.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the writer's name? → a) Sara · b) Emma · c) Anna  
2. Does she like getting up early? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say  
3. What time does she get up? → a) At seven · b) At eight · c) At nine  
4. Does she watch TV in the morning? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say  
5. Does she cook dinner? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Sara**  
2. **b) No**  
3. **b) At eight**  
4. **b) No**  
5. **a) Yes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–9 — Comprensión

6. She eats in restaurants often. → a) True · b) False  
7. She likes reading and listening to music. → a) True · b) False  
8. Does she play sport? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say  
9. On the weekend she ____. → a) meets friends or stays at home · b) works · c) gets up early

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **b) False**  
7. **a) True**  
8. **b) No**  
9. **a) meets friends or stays at home**

</details>

### Ejercicios 10–15 — Detalles

10. Main topic:  
    a) Sara's habits: what she does and doesn't do · b) Food · c) A job  
11. How many things doesn't she do? → a) Two · b) Three or more · c) None  
12. She ____ dinner in the evening. → a) cooks · b) cook · c) cooking  
13. Does she go to work? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say  
14. When does she cook dinner? → a) In the evening. · b) In the morning. · c) She doesn't cook.  
15. The text uses ____ to say what she doesn't do. → a) "don't" · b) always · c) very

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

10. **a) Sara's habits: what she does and doesn't do**  
11. **b) Three or more** (getting up early, TV in the morning, restaurants often, sport)  
12. **a) cooks**  
13. **a) Yes**  
14. **a) In the evening.**  
15. **a) "don't"**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Mike

**Objetivo:** Escuchar una entrevista sobre hábitos (*Do you…?*).

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-14/listening-mike.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Mike"></audio>

> **Interviewer:** Do you watch TV in the evening?  
> **Mike:** Yes, I do. I like watching films.  
> **Interviewer:** Do you cook dinner?  
> **Mike:** No, I don't. I don't like cooking. My wife cooks.  
> **Interviewer:** Do you do the housework?  
> **Mike:** Yes, I do. I wash the dishes and clean the bathroom. I don't iron. My wife irons.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Does Mike watch TV in the evening? → a) Yes, he does. · b) No, he doesn't. · c) The audio doesn't say.  
2. Does he cook dinner? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
3. Who cooks in his family? → a) Mike · b) His wife · c) Nobody  
4. Does he do the housework? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
5. What housework does he do? → a) He washes the dishes and cleans the bathroom. · b) He cooks. · c) He irons.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Yes, he does.**  
2. **b) No**  
3. **b) His wife**  
4. **a) Yes**  
5. **a) He washes the dishes and cleans the bathroom.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. He irons. → a) True · b) False  
7. He likes watching films. → a) True · b) False  
8. Main topic of the dialogue:  
   a) Mike's habits: TV, cooking, housework · b) Food · c) A job  
9. Does he like cooking? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
10. Who irons? → a) Mike · b) His wife · c) Nobody

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **b) False** (his wife irons)  
7. **a) True**  
8. **a) Mike's habits: TV, cooking, housework**  
9. **b) No**  
10. **b) His wife**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. Mike answers "Yes, I do" to ____ questions. → a) two · b) one · c) three  
12. He washes the dishes. → a) True · b) False  
13. What does the interviewer ask first? → a) Do you watch TV in the evening? · b) Do you cook dinner? · c) Do you do the housework?  
14. Does he clean the bathroom? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
15. The interviewer uses ____ to ask about habits. → a) Do you…? · b) What time…? · c) Where…?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

12. **a) True**  
13. **a) Do you watch TV in the evening?**

## Lección 5 — Writing: My habits

**Objetivo:** Escribir negativas, preguntas y respuestas cortas sobre hábitos.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ like coffee. → *don't / doesn't / not*  
2. ____ you play football? → *Do / Does / Are*  
3. Do you cook? Yes, I ____. → *do / don't / does*  
4. Do they watch TV? No, they ____. → *don't / do / doesn't*  
5. We don't ____ TV. → *watch / watches / watching*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **don't**  
2. **Do**  
3. **do**  
4. **don't**  
5. **watch** — tras *don't* → forma base.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. I / don't / like / getting up early.  
7. Do / you / play / tennis?  
8. No, / we / don't / watch / TV.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I don't like getting up early.  
7. Do you play tennis?  
8. No, we don't watch TV.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. *Do you like music?* + your answer.  
10. One thing you don't do (*I don't…*).  
11. Two *Do you…?* questions: one *Yes*, one *No*.  
12. *Do you cook?* + your answer.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. Do you like music? Yes, I do.  
10. I don't watch TV in the morning. I don't like getting up early.  
11. Do you play football? Yes, I do. Do you cook? No, I don't.  
12. Do you cook? No, I don't.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Your habits: what you like and don't like (*I don't*, *Do you…?*).  
14. Free time: what you do and don't do.  
15. Mini-dialogue: three *Do you…?* with Yes/No answers.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
I like reading and swimming. I don't like getting up early. Do you play tennis? I don't. I prefer football. We watch TV in the evening.

**14.**  
I like reading and listening to music. I play football on Saturdays. I don't watch TV much. I don't like cooking. I meet my friends at the weekend.

**15.**  
Do you like coffee? Yes, I do. Do you play tennis? No, I don't. Do you work on Saturday? No, I don't.

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Tema | Recuerda |
|------|----------|
| Negativa | *I/you/we/they* + **don't** + verbo |
| Pregunta | **Do** + *you/we/they* + verbo…? |
| Respuestas | *Yes, I do.* / *No, I don't.* |
| Vocabulario | *watch TV, cook, play, do the housework, like, read* |
| Atención | Tras *don't/Do* → verbo **sin -s** |
