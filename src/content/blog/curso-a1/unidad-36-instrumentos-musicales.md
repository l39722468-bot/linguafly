En la [Unidad 35](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-35-free-time-activities) hablaste de tiempo libre (*go for a walk, watching TV*). En la **Unidad 36 del curso A1** (*Musical Instruments*) aprendes a hablar de **instrumentos** con la fórmula ***play the + instrument***.

Conecta con *can* de la [Unidad 31](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-31-can-habilidad) y con *play* de la [Unidad 32](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-32-deportes-hobbies-play-go-do) (¡ojo con *the*!). Incluye **audios**, **esquemas** y **ejercicios**.

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 36 — Musical Instruments](/curso-a1/unit-36)  
> **Repaso útil:** [Unidad 35 — Free Time](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-35-free-time-activities) · [Unidad 31 — Can](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-31-can-habilidad)

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 36 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Usar ***play the*** + instrumento.
- Nombrar: ***piano, guitar, drums, violin, flute***.
- Formar 3.ª persona: *She **plays** the guitar*.
- Combinar con ***can***: *Can you play the violin?*
- Hablar de música en familia (texto de Noah).

---

## 1. La regla *play the + instrument*

| Patrón | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| **play the** + instrument | I play **the** piano. |
| he/she **plays the** + instrument | She plays **the** guitar. |
| **can play the** + instrument | He can play **the** flute. |

![Esquema play the A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-36/a1-unit36-play-the.png)

```
play the piano
play the guitar
play the drums
play the violin
play the flute
```

### Tip clave: *the*

Con instrumentos musicales en A1 (estilo británico del curso): **casi siempre *the***.

| ✅ | ❌ (A1 del curso) |
| :--- | :--- |
| play the piano | play piano |
| play the guitar | play a guitar |
| play the drums | play drums |

---

## 2. Contraste: deportes vs instrumentos

En la U32 viste *play football* **sin** *the*. Aquí es al revés:

| Tipo | Estructura | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Deporte (pelota) | play + sport | play football, play tennis |
| Instrumento | play **the** + instrument | play **the** guitar |

> I **play football** on Saturdays.  
> I **play the piano** every evening.

No mezcles: *play the football* ❌ · *play guitar* ❌ (en este curso).

## 3. Vocabulario: cinco instrumentos

![Vocabulario instrumentos A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-36/a1-unit36-instruments-vocab.png)

| Inglés | Español | Tipo (extra) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **piano** | piano | teclado |
| **guitar** | guitarra | cuerda |
| **drums** | batería | percusión (plural) |
| **violin** | violín | cuerda + arco |
| **flute** | flauta | viento |

### *Drums* es plural

Decimos ***the drums*** (no *the drum* en A1 habitual):

> He plays **the drums** in a band.

### *Do you play…?* vs *Can you play…?*

| Pregunta | Matiz A1 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Do you play** the piano? | ¿Lo tocas (hábito / sí o no)? |
| **Can you play** the piano? | ¿Sabes tocarlo (habilidad)? |

Ambas son útiles. Respuestas:

> Yes, I do. / No, I don't.  
> Yes, I can. / No, I can't.

## 7. Reading modelo: Noah

![Familia musical de Noah A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-36/a1-unit36-noah-family.png)

> My name is Noah. I play **the piano** and **the guitar**. I started when I was eight. My sister plays **the violin**. She is very good. My brother plays **the drums** in a band. We sometimes play together at home. My mum can play **the flute** but she doesn't practice often. Music is important in our family.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-36/noah-intro.mp3" title="🔊 My name is Noah. I play the piano and the guitar"></audio>

### Comprueba

1. ¿Qué toca Noah? → **The piano and the guitar**.  
2. ¿Cuándo empezó? → **When he was eight**.  
3. ¿Qué toca su hermana? → **The violin**.  
4. ¿Dónde toca la batería su hermano? → **In a band**.  
5. ¿Qué puede tocar su madre? → **The flute** (pero no practica a menudo).

Cuenta los *the*: verás que **cada instrumento** lo lleva.

## 8. Diálogo modelo

> **A:** Do you play the piano?  
> **B:** Yes, I do — and the guitar. And you?  
> **A:** I can play the flute, but I don't practice often.  
> **B:** Nice! Does your sister play an instrument?  
> **A:** Yes, she plays the violin. She's very good.  
> **B:** My brother plays the drums in a band.  
> **A:** Cool. We sometimes play together at home.

## 9. Errores típicos de hispanohablantes

| ❌ Incorrecto | ✅ Correcto | Explicación |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *I play piano* | *I play **the** piano* | *the* + instrumento |
| *I play a guitar* | *I play **the** guitar* | No *a* en A1 del curso |
| *She play the violin* | *She **plays** the violin* | 3.ª persona |
| *play the football* | *play football* | Deporte ≠ instrumento |
| *He plays the drum* | *He plays **the drums*** | Plural habitual |
| *Can you plays…?* | *Can you **play**…?* | Tras *can* → base |

---

## 9b. Pronunciación útil

| Forma | Tip A1 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **piano** | *pi-A-nou* (3 sílabas). |
| **guitar** | *gui-TAR* (acento al final). |
| **drums** | *dramz*. |
| **violin** | *vai-o-LIN*. |
| **flute** | *fluut*. |
| **the** | /ðə/ antes de consonante: *the piano*. |

Practica: *the piano → the guitar → the drums → the violin → the flute*.

---

## 9c. Mini-diálogos para memorizar

> **A:** Do you play the guitar?  
> **B:** Yes, I play the guitar and the piano.  
> **A:** Can you play the drums?  
> **B:** No, I can't — but my brother can.

> **A:** What does your sister play?  
> **B:** She plays the violin. She's very good.  
> **A:** And your mum?  
> **B:** She can play the flute but she doesn't practice often.

> **A:** Do you play any instruments?  
> **B:** No, I don't play any instruments.  
> **A:** Do you like music?  
> **B:** Yes — music is important to me.

Cambia solo el instrumento: fijas ***play the***.

### Rutina de 2 minutos

1. Di los 5 instrumentos con *the*.  
2. Di 3 frases *I play the…*.  
3. Di 2 con *plays*.  
4. Pregunta *Can you play the…?* y responde.

Si lo unes a U33: *I often play the piano. She sometimes plays the violin.*

### Combinar U31 + U32 + U36

| Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| Deporte | I play football. |
| Instrumento | I play **the** guitar. |
| Habilidad | I **can** play the piano. |
| Free time | In my free time I play the guitar. |

### Mini-historia para reescribir

> I play the piano and the guitar. My sister plays the violin. My brother plays the drums. My mum can play the flute. We sometimes play together at home. Music is important in our family.

Cambia los instrumentos y mantén ***the***.

### Tour guiado (modelo Noah)

> My name is Noah. I play the piano and the guitar. I started when I was eight. My sister plays the violin. She is very good. My brother plays the drums in a band. We sometimes play together at home. My mum can play the flute but she doesn't practice often. Music is important in our family.

Grábate leyéndolo. Luego improvisa con **tu** familia.

### Frases extra para practicar en voz alta

> Do you play the piano?  
> Yes, I play the guitar.  
> Can you play the violin?  
> She plays the flute.  
> He plays the drums in a band.  
> We sometimes play together at home.  
> Music is important in our family.  
> I don't play any instruments.

Personaliza con los instrumentos que conozcas.

### Checklist mental

- Instrumento → **play the** …  
- Deporte (pelota) → **play** … (sin *the*)  
- he/she → **plays**  
- *drums* → plural  
- *Can you play the…?*

## 11. Plan de práctica (15 minutos)

1. **3 min** — Lista en voz alta: *the piano, the guitar, the drums, the violin, the flute*.  
2. **3 min** — 5 frases *I play the…* + 3 *She/He plays…*.  
3. **4 min** — Reescribe el texto de Noah.  
4. **5 min** — Mini-entrevista *Do/Can you play the…?*

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *play the* + instrumento  
- [ ] No pongo *the* con football/tennis  
- [ ] Formo *plays* con he/she  
- [ ] Uso *the drums* (plural)  
- [ ] Pregunto con *Do/Can you play…?*

## 12. Consejos para practicar

1. **Etiquetas en casa:** pega *the piano / the guitar* en flashcards.
