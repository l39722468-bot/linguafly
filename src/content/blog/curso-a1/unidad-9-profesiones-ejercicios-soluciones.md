## Lección 1 — Gramática: *to be*, *a/an* y *Where do you work?*

**Objetivo:** Decir la profesión (*I am a teacher*), elegir *a/an* y preguntar el lugar de trabajo.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ a teacher. → *am / is / are*  
2. She is ____ engineer. → *a / an / the*  
3. He ____ a doctor. → *am / is / are*  
4. My brother is ____ teacher. → *a / an / the*  
5. Where ____ you work? → *do / does / are*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **am** — *I am a teacher.*  
2. **an** — *engineer* empieza por vocal.  
3. **is**  
4. **a** — *teacher* empieza por consonante.  
5. **do** — *Where do you work?*

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. Which article before engineer? → a) a · b) an · c) the  
7. Which article before doctor? → a) a · b) an · c) the  
8. They ____ students. → a) am · b) is · c) are  
9. Where ____ she work? → a) do · b) does · c) is  
10. I work ____ a school. → a) in · b) at · c) on

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **b) an**  
7. **a) a**  
8. **c) are**  
9. **b) does**  
10. **a) in**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. I / am / a / teacher.  
12. Where / do / you / work?  
13. She / works / in / a / hospital.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I am a teacher.  
12. Where do you work?  
13. She works in a hospital.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use "a" before professions that start with a consonant. → ?  
15. We ask "Where do you work?" to know the place of work. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: profesiones y lugares

**Objetivo:** Nombrar trabajos básicos y relacionarlos con lugares de trabajo.

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige la palabra correcta

1. Someone who works in a school is ____. → a) teacher · b) doctor · c) waiter  
2. Someone who helps sick people in a hospital is ____. → a) chef · b) doctor · c) driver  
3. A ____ works in a restaurant kitchen. → a) waiter · b) chef · c) nurse  
4. A ____ drives buses or taxis. → a) engineer · b) driver · c) pilot  
5. A ____ studies at school or university. → a) student · b) teacher · c) engineer  
6. A ____ helps doctors in a hospital. → a) nurse · b) waiter · c) chef

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) teacher**  
2. **b) doctor**  
3. **b) chef**  
4. **b) driver**  
5. **a) student**  
6. **a) nurse**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–10 — Une con su significado

**7.** teacher · doctor · chef · waiter · engineer  
→ school · hospital · restaurant · restaurant · office

**8.** nurse · driver · student · pilot · teacher  
→ hospital · bus · university · airport · school

**9.** engineer · waiter · artist · pilot · doctor  
→ Ingeniero · Camarero · Artista · Piloto · Médico

**10.** I work in a school · I work in a hospital · I work in a restaurant · I study at university · I drive a bus  
→ I am a teacher · I am a doctor or nurse · I am a chef or waiter · I am a student · I am a driver

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

7. teacher→school · doctor→hospital · chef→restaurant · waiter→restaurant · engineer→office  
8. nurse→hospital · driver→bus · student→university · pilot→airport · teacher→school  
9. engineer→Ingeniero · waiter→Camarero · artist→Artista · pilot→Piloto · doctor→Médico  
10. school→teacher · hospital→doctor/nurse · restaurant→chef/waiter · university→student · bus→driver

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Completa

11. My mother is ____ teacher. She works in a school. → *a / an / the*  
12. He is ____ engineer. He works in an office. → *a / an / the*  
13. Where ____ your sister work? She works in a hospital. → *do / does / is*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a**  
12. **an**  
13. **does**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Contexto

14. Someone asks your job. You say: ____.  
    a) I am a teacher. · b) I work. · c) How old are you?  
15. You want to ask where someone works. You ____.  
    a) say "What is your name?" · b) say "Where do you work?" · c) say "How old are you?"

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **a) I am a teacher.**  
15. **b) say "Where do you work?"**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Laura's family

**Objetivo:** Leer un texto corto sobre las profesiones de una familia.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-9/reading-laura.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Laura"></audio>

> My name is Laura. I am a teacher. I work in a school in Madrid. My brother is an engineer. He works in an office. My sister is a nurse. She works in a hospital. My father is a doctor. He works in the same hospital as my sister. We are a family of workers.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is Laura's job? → a) Teacher · b) Engineer · c) Nurse  
2. Where does Laura work? → a) In a hospital · b) In a school · c) In an office  
3. What is Laura's brother's job? → a) Teacher · b) Engineer · c) Doctor  
4. Where does the brother work? → a) In a school · b) In an office · c) In a hospital  
5. What is Laura's sister's job? → a) Nurse · b) Doctor · c) Teacher

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Teacher**  
2. **b) In a school**  
3. **b) Engineer**  
4. **b) In an office**  
5. **a) Nurse**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–9 — Verdadero o falso

6. Laura's father is a doctor. → ?  
7. The sister works in a school. → ?  
8. The father and the sister work in the same hospital. → ?  
9. Laura works in Barcelona. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **Verdadero**  
7. **Falso** (hospital)  
8. **Verdadero**  
9. **Falso** (Madrid)

</details>

### Ejercicios 10–15 — Comprensión

10. Main topic:  
    a) A family and their jobs · b) A school in Madrid · c) Hospitals  
11. Which professions are mentioned?  
    a) Only teacher · b) Teacher, engineer, nurse, doctor · c) Waiter and chef  
12. What does Laura introduce?  
    a) Her family and their jobs · b) Schools · c) A hospital  
13. Where does Laura's sister work?  
    a) In a school · b) In a hospital · c) In an office  
14. What does "a family of workers" mean?  
    a) Everyone has a job · b) No one works · c) Only one person works  
15. How many people in Laura's family have a job mentioned?  
    a) Two · b) Three · c) Four

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

10. **a) A family and their jobs**  
11. **b) Teacher, engineer, nurse, doctor**  
12. **a) Her family and their jobs**  
13. **b) In a hospital**  
14. **a) Everyone has a job**  
15. **c) Four**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Tom the teacher

**Objetivo:** Escuchar una entrevista corta sobre profesiones.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-9/listening-tom.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Tom"></audio>

> **Interviewer:** What do you do?  
> **Tom:** I am a teacher. I work in a primary school.  
> **Interviewer:** Where is the school?  
> **Tom:** It is in the centre of town.  
> **Interviewer:** Do you like your job?  
> **Tom:** Yes, I love it. My sister is a nurse. She works in a hospital. We both like our jobs.

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is Tom's job? → a) Doctor · b) Teacher · c) Engineer  
2. Where does Tom work? → a) In a hospital · b) In a primary school · c) In an office  
3. Where is the school? → a) In the centre of town · b) In a village · c) Near the hospital  
4. Does Tom like his job? → a) Yes · b) No · c) He doesn't say  
5. What is Tom's sister's job? → a) Teacher · b) Nurse · c) Doctor  
6. Where does Tom's sister work? → a) In a school · b) In a hospital · c) In an office

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b) Teacher**  
2. **b) In a primary school**  
3. **a) In the centre of town**  
4. **a) Yes**  
5. **b) Nurse**  
6. **b) In a hospital**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–10 — Verdadero o falso

7. Tom is a secondary school teacher. → ?  
8. Tom and his sister like their jobs. → ?  
9. The interviewer asks "What do you do?". → ?  
10. Tom's sister works in a school. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

7. **Falso** (primary school)  
8. **Verdadero**  
9. **Verdadero**  
10. **Falso** (hospital)

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Comprensión global

11. Main topic:  
    a) Food · b) A person talks about his job and his sister's job · c) Sports  
12. What is Tom's job?  
    a) Teacher · b) Nurse · c) Doctor  
13. Which questions does the interviewer ask?  
    a) Only Where do you work? · b) What do you do?, Where is the school?, Do you like your job? · c) How old are you?  
14. What type of school does Tom work in?  
    a) Secondary · b) Primary · c) University  
15. Does Tom mention his sister?  
    a) No · b) Yes, she is a nurse · c) Yes, she is a teacher

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **b) A person talks about his job and his sister's job**  
12. **a) Teacher**  
13. **b) What do you do?, Where is the school?, Do you like your job?**  
14. **b) Primary**  
15. **b) Yes, she is a nurse**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: Jobs

**Objetivo:** Escribir frases y párrafos sobre profesiones y lugares de trabajo.

### Modelo

> I am a teacher. I work in a school.  
> He is an engineer. She is a doctor.  
> Where do you work? I work in a hospital.  
> My mother is a nurse. She likes her job.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ a teacher. (*am / is / are*)  
2. He is ____ engineer. (*a / an*)  
3. She is ____ doctor. (*a / an*)  
4. Where ____ you work? (*do / does*)  
5. I work ____ a hospital. (*in / at / on*)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **am**  
2. **an**  
3. **a**  
4. **do**  
5. **in**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. I / am / a / teacher.  
7. Where / do / you / work?  
8. She / works / in / a / hospital.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I am a teacher.  
7. Where do you work?  
8. She works in a hospital.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. Say a job (*I am a/an …* or *My … is a/an …*).  
10. Say a workplace (*I work in a/an …*).  
11. Job + workplace (two sentences).  
12. Question + answer (*What do you do?* / *Where do you work?*).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I am a teacher. / My sister is a nurse.  
10. I work in a school. / She works in a hospital.  
11. I am a teacher. I work in a school.  
12. What do you do? I am a doctor. / Where do you work? I work in a hospital.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Your job or a family member's job (4–6 sentences).  
14. Jobs of 2–3 people you know.  
15. Short presentation: name, job, workplace, like.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
My mother is a nurse. She works in a hospital. She likes her job. My father is an engineer. He works in an office. I am a student.

**14.**  
My brother is a driver. He works on a bus. My friend is a chef. She works in a restaurant. My uncle is a doctor. He works in a hospital.

**15.**  
My name is Ana. I am a teacher. I work in a school. I like my job. My sister is a nurse. She works in a hospital.

</details>
