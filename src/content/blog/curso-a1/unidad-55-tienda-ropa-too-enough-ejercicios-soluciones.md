## Lección 1 — Gramática: too y enough

**Objetivo:** *too* + adjetivo; adjetivo + *enough*; *not … enough*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. This shirt is ____ big. → *too / enough / to*  
2. It isn't ____ enough. → *big / too / enough*  
3. The trousers are ____ small. → *too / enough / not*  
4. Is it ____ enough? → *long / too / enough*  
5. This jacket is ____ expensive. → *too / enough / cheap*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **too** — *too* + adjetivo.  
2. **big** — adjetivo + *enough*.  
3. **too**  
4. **long**  
5. **too**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *Too big* = ____. → a) more than needed, doesn't fit · b) just right · c) not enough  
7. *Big enough* = ____. → a) sufficient size, it fits · b) too big · c) too small  
8. *Not big enough* = ____. → a) too small · b) too big · c) just right  
9. Word order: *enough* ____. → a) comes after the adjective (*big enough*) · b) comes before the adjective · c) same as *too*  
10. Word order: *too* ____. → a) comes before the adjective (*too big*) · b) comes after the adjective · c) same as *enough*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) more than needed, doesn't fit**  
7. **a) sufficient size, it fits**  
8. **a) too small**  
9. **a) comes after the adjective (*big enough*)**  
10. **a) comes before the adjective (*too big*)**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. This / shirt / is / too / big.  
12. It isn't / big / enough.  
13. Where is / the / changing room?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. This shirt is too big.  
12. It isn't big enough.  
13. Where is the changing room?

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *Too* + adjective = demasiado; adjective + *enough* = suficientemente. → True / False  
15. *Not big enough* means *too small*. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: tienda de ropa

**Objetivo:** *S, M, L, XL, size, changing room, sale, fit*.

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige el significado

1. *S* = ____. → a) small size · b) medium · c) large  
2. *M* = ____. → a) medium size · b) small · c) large  
3. *L* = ____. → a) large size · b) small · c) medium  
4. *XL* = ____. → a) extra large · b) small · c) medium  
5. *Changing room* = ____. → a) a place to try on clothes · b) the shop entrance · c) the cash desk  
6. *Sale* = ____. → a) reduced prices · b) closed · c) a new collection

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) small size**  
2. **a) medium size**  
3. **a) large size**  
4. **a) extra large**  
5. **a) a place to try on clothes**  
6. **a) reduced prices**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–8 — Empareja

7. Empareja: S / M / L / XL / changing room → small · medium · large · extra large · try on clothes  
8. Empareja: sale / too big / big enough / size / fit → cheaper prices · doesn't fit · it fits · S, M, L, XL · right size

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

7. **S → small** · **M → medium** · **L → large** · **XL → extra large** · **changing room → try on clothes**  
8. **sale → cheaper prices** · **too big → doesn't fit** · **big enough → it fits** · **size → S, M, L, XL** · **fit → right size**

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–13 — Completa / elige

9. Do you have this in ____ M? → *size / sale / changing room*  
10. Where is the ____ room? → *changing / sale / size*  
11. This is ____ small. → *too / enough / size*  
12. *Size* = ____. → a) S, M, L, XL · b) price · c) colour  
13. *It doesn't fit* = ____. → a) wrong size (too big or too small) · b) it's perfect · c) it's on sale

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. **size**  
10. **changing**  
11. **too**  
12. **a) S, M, L, XL**  
13. **a) wrong size (too big or too small)**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *S, M, L, XL* are clothing sizes. → True / False  
15. *Sale* means lower prices. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: A new shirt

**Objetivo:** leer sobre comprar una camisa con *too/enough*.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-55/reading-clothes.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: A new shirt"></audio>

> I am at the clothing store. There is a sale this week. I want a new shirt. Do you have this in size M? The shop assistant says yes. I go to the changing room. The shirt is too big. I need size S. She brings a smaller. It is big enough now. I buy it. The total is twenty pounds because it's on sale.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Where is the writer? → a) At the clothing store · b) At home · c) At the supermarket  
2. Is there a sale? → a) Yes, this week · b) No · c) The text doesn't say  
3. What does he/she want? → a) A new shirt · b) Trousers · c) A jacket  
4. What size first? → a) Size M · b) Size S · c) Size L  
5. Where does he/she go to try it? → a) To the changing room · b) Home · c) To the cash desk

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) At the clothing store**  
2. **a) Yes, this week**  
3. **a) A new shirt**  
4. **a) Size M**  
5. **a) To the changing room**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Is size M too big or too small? → a) Too big · b) Too small · c) Just right  
7. What size does he/she need? → a) Size S · b) Size M · c) Size L  
8. Is the smaller shirt big enough? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say  
9. How much is the total? → a) Twenty pounds · b) Fifteen pounds · c) Ten pounds  
10. *Changing room* means ____. → a) a place to try on clothes · b) a sale · c) the cash desk

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Too big**  
7. **a) Size S**  
8. **a) Yes**  
9. **a) Twenty pounds**  
10. **a) a place to try on clothes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. *Too big* means ____. → a) doesn't fit, need smaller · b) just right · c) too small  
12. *Big enough* means ____. → a) it fits · b) too big · c) too small  
13. *Sale* means ____. → a) reduced prices · b) closed · c) new  
14. *Size S, M, L* = ____. → a) small, medium, large · b) price · c) colour  
15. The main topic is ____. → a) buying a shirt, sizes, changing room, too/enough · b) supermarket · c) restaurant

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) doesn't fit, need smaller**  
12. **a) it fits**  
13. **a) reduced prices**  
14. **a) small, medium, large**  
15. **a) buying a shirt, sizes, changing room, too/enough**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Do you have this in size L?

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo en la tienda de ropa.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-55/listening-clothes.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Do you have this in size L?"></audio>

> **A:** Do you have this in size L?  
> **B:** Yes, here you are.  
> **A:** Where is the changing room?  
> **B:** Over there.  
> **A:** This is too small. Do you have it in XL?  
> **B:** Yes. Try this.  
> **A:** It's big enough now. I'll take it. Is it on sale?  
> **B:** Yes, twenty pounds.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What size does A ask for first? → a) Size L · b) Size M · c) Size XL  
2. Where is the changing room? → a) Over there · b) Here · c) At the back  
3. Is size L too big or too small? → a) Too small · b) Too big · c) Just right  
4. What size does A try next? → a) XL · b) L · c) M  
5. Is XL big enough? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The dialogue doesn't say

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Size L**  
2. **a) Over there**  
3. **a) Too small**  
4. **a) XL**  
5. **a) Yes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Does A buy it? → a) Yes · b) No · c) Maybe  
7. Is it on sale? → a) Yes, twenty pounds · b) No · c) The dialogue doesn't say  
8. The dialogue is at ____. → a) a clothing store · b) a supermarket · c) a restaurant  
9. *Too small* means ____. → a) not big enough · b) too big · c) just right  
10. *Big enough* means ____. → a) it fits · b) too small · c) too big

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Yes**  
7. **a) Yes, twenty pounds**  
8. **a) a clothing store**  
9. **a) not big enough**  
10. **a) it fits**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Changing room* means ____. → a) a place to try on clothes · b) a sale · c) the cash desk  
12. *I'll take it* means ____. → a) I will buy it · b) I don't want it · c) I need another size  
13. *Sale* means ____. → a) reduced prices · b) closed · c) new  
14. *Size L, XL* = ____. → a) large, extra large · b) small · c) medium  
15. The dialogue is about ____. → a) trying clothes, sizes, too/enough, buying · b) cooking · c) eating

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) a place to try on clothes**  
12. **a) I will buy it**  
13. **a) reduced prices**  
14. **a) large, extra large**  
15. **a) trying clothes, sizes, too/enough, buying**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: Trying clothes

3. Do you have this in ____ M? → *size / sale / changing room*  
4. Where is the ____ room? → *changing / sale / big*  
5. The trousers are ____ small. → *too / enough / size*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **too**  
2. **big**  
3. **size**  
4. **changing**  
5. **too**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. This / shirt / is / too / big.  
7. It isn't / big / enough.  
8. Where is / the / changing room?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. This shirt is too big.  
7. It isn't big enough.  
8. Where is the changing room?

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Esta camisa es demasiado grande (*too big*).  
10. No es suficientemente grande (*not big enough*).  
11. Preguntar talla M y dónde está el probador.  
12. Decir que es demasiado pequeña y pedir talla L.  
13. Párrafo corto (3–4 frases): tienda, probar, *too/enough*, comprar.  
14. Diferencia *too big* y *not big enough* con ejemplos.  
15. Preguntar talla, probador y si está en rebajas.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. This shirt is too big.  
10. It isn't big enough.  
11. Do you have this in size M? Where is the changing room?  
12. This is too small. Do you have it in size L?  
13. I am at the clothing store. I try a shirt in size M. It is too big. I need size S. It is big enough now. I buy it.  
14. This shirt is too big. That one isn't big enough.  
15. Do you have this in size L? Where is the changing room? Is it on sale?

</details>
