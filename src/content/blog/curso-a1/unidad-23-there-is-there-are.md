## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 23 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Usar ***There is*** (singular) y ***There are*** (plural).
- Formar negativas: ***There isn't / There aren't***.
- Preguntar: ***Is there…? / Are there…?***.
- Responder: *Yes, there is / No, there aren't…*.
- Situar objetos con ***in, on, under, behind***.

---

## 1. Afirmativa: *There is / There are*

| Forma | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **There is** | 1 cosa (singular) | There is a book on the table. |
| **There are** | 2+ cosas (plural) | There are two chairs in the room. |

```
There is + a/an + singular
There are + número/plural
```

> There is a window in the room.  
> There is a lamp on the table.  
> There are three books under the bed.  
> There are two pillows on the bed.

### Tip

En habla rápida, *There is* a menudo suena *there's*. En A1 practica primero la forma completa.

## 2. Negativa: *There isn't / There aren't*

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| **There isn't** (+ singular) | There isn't a pen in my bag. |
| **There aren't** (+ plural) | There aren't any apples. |
| Con incontables | There isn't any milk in the fridge. |

> There isn't a bathroom upstairs.  
> There aren't any chairs in the hall.  
> There isn't any milk in the fridge.

*Any* aparece mucho en **negativas** y **preguntas** (intro A1; más adelante en U25/U53).

## 3. Preguntas: *Is there…? / Are there…?*

| Pregunta | Sí | No |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Is there** a bathroom upstairs? | Yes, **there is**. | No, **there isn't**. |
| **Are there** any chairs? | Yes, **there are**. | No, **there aren't**. |

```
Is there + a/an + singular…?
Are there + (any) + plural…?
```

### No confundir con *Do you have…?*

| There is/are | Have |
| :--- | :--- |
| Hay algo en un lugar | Alguien posee algo |
| Is there a sofa in the room? | Do you have a sofa? |
| There is a fridge in the kitchen. | We have a fridge. |

Ambas son útiles; en esta unidad el foco es **existencia en un sitio**.

## 4. Preposiciones: *in, on, under, behind*

| Preposición | Significado | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **in** | dentro de | The book is **in** the bag. |
| **on** | encima de | The cup is **on** the table. |
| **under** | debajo de | The ball is **under** the bed. |
| **behind** | detrás de | The cat is **behind** the sofa. |

### Combinar *There is/are* + preposición

> There is a pen **on** the desk.  
> There is a book **in** my bag.  
> There are three books **under** the bed.  
> The bag is **behind** the chair.  
> There is a lamp **on** the table.

También: *The book is on the table* (sujeto = el objeto). *There is* presenta; *The book is…* localiza algo ya conocido.

## 5. Más ejemplos en contexto (casa)

> In my bedroom there is a bed and a wardrobe. There is a lamp on the desk. There are two pillows on the bed. There isn't a TV in the bedroom.  
> Is there a bathroom upstairs? — Yes, there is.  
> Are there any chairs in the hall? — No, there aren't.  
> The cat is under the table. My bag is behind the sofa. The keys are in the bag.

### Diálogo modelo

> **Ana:** Is there a sofa in your living room?  
> **Luis:** Yes, there is. There is a table too.  
> **Ana:** Are there any chairs?  
> **Luis:** Yes, there are four chairs.  
> **Ana:** Where is your bag?  
> **Luis:** It's behind the chair. And my book is on the table.  
> **Ana:** Is there any milk in the fridge?  
> **Luis:** No, there isn't. There aren't any apples either!

## 6. Errores típicos de hispanohablantes

| ❌ Incorrecto | ✅ Correcto | Explicación |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *There is two chairs* | *There **are** two chairs* | Plural → are |
| *There are a book* | *There **is** a book* | Singular → is |
| *It is a book on the table* (presentar) | *There is a book on the table* | Presentar con *There* |
| *Is there chairs?* | *Are there (any) chairs?* | Plural → Are |
| *There isn't any chairs* | *There **aren't** any chairs* | Plural negativo |
| *The book is in the table* | *The book is **on** the table* | Encima → on |
| *The cat is under of the bed* | *The cat is **under** the bed* | Sin *of* |

---

## 7. Pronunciación útil

| Forma | Tip A1 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **There is / There's** | *There's* = *therz* (contracción). |
| **There are** | A menudo *ther-er* rápido. |
| **Is there…?** | Empieza por *Is*, no por *There*. |
| **under / behind** | *UN-der* · *bi-HAIND*. |
| **any** | *E-ni* /ˈeni/. |

Escucha afirmativa → negativa → pregunta del mismo objeto: *There is a lamp* → *There isn't a lamp* → *Is there a lamp?*

## 7b. Mini-diálogos para memorizar

> **A:** Is there a bathroom upstairs?  
> **B:** Yes, there is.  
> **A:** Are there any towels?  
> **B:** No, there aren't.

> **A:** Where is the book?  
> **B:** It's on the table.  
> **A:** And the keys?  
> **B:** They're in the bag.

> **A:** Is there a cat in the room?  
> **B:** Yes — it's under the table!  
> **A:** Oh! And the bag?  
> **B:** Behind the sofa.

Cambia solo el objeto y la preposición: fijas la estructura en minutos.

## 7c. Plantillas para copiar

1. There is a \_\_\_\_ on/in/under/behind the \_\_\_\_.  
2. There are \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_ in the \_\_\_\_.  
3. There isn't a \_\_\_\_ in the \_\_\_\_.  
4. There aren't any \_\_\_\_.  
5. Is there a \_\_\_\_? — Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.  
6. Are there any \_\_\_\_? — Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.

### Más frases para memorizar

> There is a book on the table.  
> There are two chairs in the room.  
> There isn't a pen in my bag.  
> There aren't any apples.  
> Is there a bathroom upstairs? — Yes, there is.  
> Are there any chairs? — No, there aren't.  
> The book is in the bag.  
> The cup is on the table.  
> The ball is under the bed.  
> The cat is behind the sofa.

Lee la lista dos veces. Luego describe **tu** habitación con 6 frases *There is/are* + 4 preposiciones distintas.

### Mapa rápido U11 → U23

| Unidad | Enfoque |
| :--- | :--- |
| U11 | There is/are en la ciudad (bank, park…) |
| U21–22 | Habitaciones y muebles |
| **U23** | There is/are completo + in/on/under/behind |
| U24 | Más preposiciones de lugar |

### Rutina de 2 minutos

1. Di 3 frases *There is…* y 3 *There are…*.  
2. Di 2 negativas (*isn't / aren't*).  
3. Haz 2 preguntas (*Is there…? / Are there…?*) y respóndelas.  
4. Sitúa 4 objetos: *in / on / under / behind*.

Si lo haces mirando tu escritorio real, el vocabulario y la gramática se unen solos.

### Tour guiado (modelo)

> In my room there is a bed. There is a lamp on the desk. There are three books on the shelf. There isn't a TV. Is there a chair? Yes, there is — behind the desk. My bag is under the bed. The keys are in the bag.

Grábate leyéndolo y luego improvísarlo sin mirar. Ese es el speaking típico de esta unidad.

### Frases extra para practicar en voz alta

> There is a window in the kitchen.  
> There are four chairs around the table.  
> There isn't any milk in the fridge.  
> Are there any pillows on the bed? — Yes, there are.  
> Where is the clock? — It's on the wall.  
> The shoes are under the bed.  
> The car is behind the tree.

Personaliza con tu casa. Cuanto más concreto el lugar, mejor recuerdas la preposición correcta.

### Checklist mental

- Singular → *is / isn't / Is there…?*  
- Plural → *are / aren't / Are there…?*  
- Negativa/pregunta plural → suele ir *any*  
- Encima de superficie → *on* (no *in the table*)  
- Dentro → *in*; debajo → *under*; detrás → *behind*

Si fallas solo en preposiciones, practica con objetos reales 5 minutos; si fallas en *is/are*, vuelve a contar: ¿uno o más?

3. There \_\_\_\_ a window in the kitchen.  
4. There \_\_\_\_ three books under the bed.

4. **are**

2. The cup is \_\_\_\_ the table. (encima)  
3. The ball is \_\_\_\_ the bed. (debajo)  
4. The cat is \_\_\_\_ the sofa. (detrás)

<details>

4. **behind**

### Ejercicio 4 — Traduce

1. Hay un libro en la mesa.  
2. Hay dos sillas en la habitación.  
3. No hay un bolígrafo en mi bolso.  
4. ¿Hay un baño arriba? — Sí.  
5. ¿Hay sillas? — No.  
6. El gato está detrás del sofá.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrige

1. *There is two chairs.*  
2. *There are a book on the desk.*  
3. *Is there any chairs?*  
4. *The book is in the table.*  
5. *There isn't any chairs.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. There **are** two chairs.  
2. There **is** a book on the desk.  
3. **Are** there any chairs?  
4. The book is **on** the table.  
5. There **aren't** any chairs.
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Empareja pregunta y respuesta

1. Is there a lamp?  
2. Are there any apples?  
3. Where is the ball?  
4. Where are the keys?  

a) Under the bed.  
b) Yes, there is.  
c) In the bag.  
d) No, there aren't.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **d** · 3 → **a** · 4 → **c**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Escribe tu habitación

Escribe 7–9 frases: qué hay, qué no hay, y 3 ubicaciones con preposiciones distintas.

## 9. Plan de práctica (15 minutos)

1. **3 min** — 6 frases afirmativas (*is/are*).  
2. **3 min** — 4 negativas + 4 preguntas.  
3. **4 min** — Preposiciones con objetos reales.  
4. **5 min** — Escribe y lee tu descripción de habitación.

### Checklist

- [ ] Sé elegir *There is / There are*  
- [ ] Formo *isn't / aren't*  
- [ ] Pregunto *Is there…? / Are there…?*  
- [ ] Respondo con *there is/are/isn't/aren't*  
- [ ] Uso *in, on, under, behind* con confianza

1. **Foto de habitación:** escribe 8 frases *There is/are*.  
2. **Esconde** un objeto y di dónde está (*under/behind…*).

4. Prepárate para la [Unidad 24](/curso-a1/unit-24): más preposiciones de lugar.
