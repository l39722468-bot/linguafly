## Lección 1 — Gramática: play the

**Objetivo:** *play the + instrument*, *plays*, *can play*, *the drums*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I play ____ piano. → *the / a / —*  
2. She plays ____ guitar. → *the / a / —*  
3. He plays ____ violin. → *the / a / —*  
4. Can you ____ the flute? → *play / plays / playing*  
5. They play ____ drums. → *the / a / —*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **the** — *play the piano*.  
2. **the**  
3. **the**  
4. **play** — *can + play*.  
5. **the** — *the drums* (plural).

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. The pattern is ____. → a) play the + instrument · b) play instrument · c) do the instrument  
7. Correct sentence: ____. → a) I play the piano. · b) I play piano. · c) I play a piano always  
8. She ____ guitar. → a) plays the · b) play the · c) playing the  
9. I can ____ the flute. → a) play · b) plays · c) playing  
10. With drums we say ____. → a) the drums · b) the drum · c) a drums

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) play the + instrument**  
7. **a) I play the piano.**  
8. **a) plays the**  
9. **a) play**  
10. **a) the drums**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. I / play / the / piano.  
12. She / plays / the / guitar.  
13. He / can / play / the / flute.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I play the piano.  
12. She plays the guitar.  
13. He can play the flute.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. In A1 we usually say *play the piano* (with *the*). → True / False  
15. *Drums* takes *the* and is plural: *play the drums*. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: instrumentos

**Objetivo:** piano, guitar, drums, violin, flute.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *Piano* = ____. → a) keyboard instrument · b) wind instrument · c) only drums  
2. *Guitar* = ____. → a) string instrument with six strings · b) wind instrument · c) keyboard only  
3. *Drums* = ____. → a) You hit them with sticks. · b) You blow them. · c) You only press keys.  
4. *Violin* = ____. → a) small string instrument with a bow · b) a big flute · c) a piano  
5. *Flute* = ____. → a) wind instrument you blow · b) string instrument · c) drums

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) keyboard instrument**  
2. **a) string instrument with six strings**  
3. **a) You hit them with sticks.**  
4. **a) small string instrument with a bow**  
5. **a) wind instrument you blow**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Empareja

6. Empareja: piano / guitar / drums / violin / flute → play the piano · play the guitar · play the drums · play the violin · play the flute  
7. Empareja EN → ES: piano / guitar / drums / violin / flute → piano · guitarra · batería · violín · flauta

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **piano → play the piano** · **guitar → play the guitar** · **drums → play the drums** · **violin → play the violin** · **flute → play the flute**  
7. **piano → piano** · **guitar → guitarra** · **drums → batería** · **violin → violín** · **flute → flauta**

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. I play ____ piano. → *the / a / —*  
9. She ____ the guitar. → *plays / play / playing*  
10. He plays ____ drums. → *the / a / —*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **the**  
9. **plays**  
10. **the**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Definiciones

11. The pattern is ____. → a) play the + instrument · b) play + instrument without the · c) make the instrument  
12. Which is a wind instrument? → a) flute · b) piano · c) drums  
13. Which is a keyboard instrument? → a) piano · b) violin · c) flute

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) play the + instrument**  
12. **a) flute**  
13. **a) piano**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena

14. I / play / the / piano / and / the / guitar.  
15. She / can / play / the / violin.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

15. She can play the violin.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Noah

**Objetivo:** leer sobre los instrumentos de la familia de Noah.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-36/reading-noah.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Noah"></audio>

> My name is Noah. I play the piano and the guitar. I started when I was eight. My sister plays the violin. She is very good. My brother plays the drums in a band. We sometimes play together at home. My mum can play the flute but she doesn't practice often. Music is important in our family.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the writer's name? → a) Noah · b) Nick · c) Neil  
2. What does Noah play? → a) The piano and the guitar · b) Only the drums · c) Only the flute  
3. When did he start? → a) When he was eight · b) When he was seven · c) Two years ago  
4. What does his sister play? → a) The violin · b) The drums · c) The flute  
5. What does his brother play? → a) The drums · b) The piano · c) The violin

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Noah**  
2. **a) The piano and the guitar**  
3. **a) When he was eight**  
4. **a) The violin**  
5. **a) The drums**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Where does his brother play the drums? → a) In a band · b) Only at school · c) Never  
7. What can mum play? → a) The flute · b) The piano · c) The drums  
8. Does mum practice often? → a) No · b) Yes · c) Every day  
9. When do they play together? → a) Sometimes at home · b) Every day at school · c) Never  
10. We say play ____ piano. → a) the · b) a · c) —

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) In a band**  
7. **a) The flute**  
8. **a) No**  
9. **a) Sometimes at home**  
10. **a) the**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

12. Which word means "batería"? → a) drums · b) flute · c) violin  
13. His sister ____ the violin. → a) is very good at · b) hates · c) never plays  
14. Music ____ in their family. → a) is important · b) is boring · c) is not important  
15. Noah can also ____. → a) play the guitar · b) play the drums · c) play the flute

## Lección 4 — Listening: Instruments

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo sobre instrumentos.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-36/listening-instruments.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Instruments"></audio>

> **A:** Do you play any instrument?  
> **B:** Yes, I play the piano and the guitar.  
> **A:** How long?  
> **B:** I started the piano when I was seven. I started the guitar two years ago.  
> **A:** Can your sister play any instrument?  
> **B:** She plays the violin. She is very good. My brother plays the drums. We all like music.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What does B play? → a) The piano and the guitar · b) Only the drums · c) Only the flute  
2. When did B start the piano? → a) When he was seven · b) When he was eight · c) Last year  
3. When did B start the guitar? → a) Two years ago · b) When he was seven · c) Yesterday  
4. What does his sister play? → a) The violin · b) The drums · c) The piano  
5. What does his brother play? → a) The drums · b) The flute · c) The guitar

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) The piano and the guitar**  
2. **a) When he was seven**  
3. **a) Two years ago**  
4. **a) The violin**  
5. **a) The drums**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Is his sister good? → a) Yes, she is very good. · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
7. The dialogue is ____. → a) about musical instruments · b) about shopping · c) about sports  
8. She ____ the violin. He ____ the drums. → a) plays; plays · b) play; play · c) playing; playing  
9. We say play ____ piano. → a) the · b) a · c) —  
10. They ____ music. → a) all like · b) don't like · c) hate

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Yes, she is very good.**  
7. **a) about musical instruments**  
8. **a) plays; plays**  
9. **a) the**  
10. **a) all like**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. A ____ about instruments. → a) asks · b) ask · c) asking  
12. "How long?" asks about ____. → a) How long (ago) · b) Where · c) Who  
13. B started ____ when he was seven. → a) the piano · b) the drums · c) the flute  
14. How many instruments are mentioned? → a) Four: piano, guitar, violin and drums · b) Two · c) One  
15. His brother can ____. → a) play the drums · b) play the flute · c) play the piano

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

12. **a) How long (ago)**  
13. **a) the piano**  
14. **a) Four: piano, guitar, violin and drums**

## Lección 5 — Writing: My instruments

**Objetivo:** escribir sobre instrumentos con *play the*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I play ____ piano. → *the / a / —*  
2. She ____ the guitar. → *plays / play / playing*  
3. He plays ____ drums. → *the / a / —*  
4. Can you ____ the flute? → *play / plays / playing*  
5. They play ____ violin. → *the / a / —*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **the**  
2. **plays**  
3. **the**  
4. **play**  
5. **the**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. I / play / the / piano.  
7. She / plays / the / guitar / and / the / violin.  
8. He / can / play / the / flute.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I play the piano.  
7. She plays the guitar and the violin.  
8. He can play the flute.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Escribe qué instrumentos tocas (usa *play the*).  
10. Pregunta y respuesta: *Do you play any instrument?*  
11. Escribe: algo que tocas + algo que te gustaría tocar.  
12. Escribe sobre un familiar: *My sister/brother plays…*  
13. Párrafo corto (4 frases) sobre música en tu familia o sobre ti.  
14. Tres frases: *I / She / He play(s) the…*  
15. Pregunta con *Can you…?* y respuesta corta.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I play the piano and the guitar.  
10. Do you play any instrument? Yes, I play the piano.  
11. I play the guitar. I would like to play the piano.  
12. My sister plays the violin.  
13. I play the piano. I started when I was eight. My brother plays the drums. We like music.  
14. I play the piano. She plays the guitar. He plays the drums.  
15. Can you play the guitar? No, I can't.

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Patrón | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| **play the + instrument** | I play **the** piano. |
| **plays** (he/she) | She **plays** the guitar. |
| **can play** | He **can play** the flute. |
| **the drums** | They play **the drums**. |
