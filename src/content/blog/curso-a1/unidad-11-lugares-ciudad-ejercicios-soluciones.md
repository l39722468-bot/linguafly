## Lección 1 — Gramática: There is/are y Where is…?

**Objetivo:** *There is / There are*, *Where is…?*, *Is there a…?* y preposiciones de lugar.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. There ____ a bank near here. → *is / are / be*  
2. There ____ two parks in my town. → *is / are / be*  
3. Where ____ the supermarket? → *is / are / be*  
4. Is there ____ pharmacy near? → *a / an / the*  
5. The bank ____ next to the post office. → *is / are / be*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is** — *There is* + singular.  
2. **are** — *There are* + plural (*two parks*).  
3. **is** — *Where is the…?* (singular).  
4. **a** — *Is there a…?*  
5. **is** — *The bank is next to…*

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. There ____ a library in the centre. → a) is · b) are · c) be  
7. Where ____ the shops? → a) is · b) are · c) be  
8. The café ____ between the bank and the pharmacy. → a) is · b) are · c) be  
9. There ____ three cafés in this street. → a) is · b) are · c) be  
10. Is there a park? Yes, there ____. → a) is · b) are · c) be

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) is**  
7. **b) are** — *Where are the shops?* (plural)  
8. **a) is**  
9. **b) are**  
10. **a) is** — *Yes, there is.*

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. There / is / a / supermarket / near / here.  
12. Where / is / the / post office?  
13. The / bank / is / next to / the / library.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. There is a supermarket near here.  
12. Where is the post office?  
13. The bank is next to the library.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use "There is" for one thing (singular). We use "There are" for two or more. → ?  
15. We ask "Where is the…?" to find the location of a place. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero** — *There is* + singular; *There are* + plural.  
15. **Verdadero** — *Where is the bank?* = ¿Dónde está el banco?

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: lugares y preposiciones

**Objetivo:** *bank, supermarket, library, post office, pharmacy, park, café, street* y *next to / opposite / between / near*.

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige el lugar correcto

1. You go to ____ to get money or pay bills. → a) the bank · b) the park · c) the library  
2. You buy food and drinks at ____. → a) the post office · b) the supermarket · c) the pharmacy  
3. You read books and study in ____. → a) the café · b) the library · c) the bank  
4. You send letters and buy stamps at ____. → a) the post office · b) the park · c) the pharmacy  
5. You buy medicine at ____. → a) the café · b) the pharmacy · c) the bank  
6. The ____ is opposite the bank. You walk there in one minute. → a) street · b) park · c) supermarket

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) the bank**  
2. **b) the supermarket**  
3. **b) the library**  
4. **a) the post office**  
5. **b) the pharmacy**  
6. **a) street**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–10 — Relaciona

**7.** Lugar → traducción  

| Inglés | Español |
|--------|---------|
| bank | ? |
| supermarket | ? |
| library | ? |
| post office | ? |
| pharmacy | ? |

**8.** Preposición → significado  

| Inglés | Español |
|--------|---------|
| next to | ? |
| opposite | ? |
| between | ? |
| near | ? |
| in front of | ? |

**9.** Lugar → qué hay / qué haces  

| Lugar | Asociación |
|-------|------------|
| park | ? |
| café | ? |
| library | ? |
| pharmacy | ? |
| bank | ? |

**10.** Pregunta → respuesta  

| Pregunta | Respuesta |
|----------|-----------|
| Where is the bank? | ? |
| Is there a supermarket? | ? |
| Where are the shops? | ? |
| Is there a park? | ? |
| Where is the post office? | ? |

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**7.** bank → Banco · supermarket → Supermercado · library → Biblioteca · post office → Oficina de correos · pharmacy → Farmacia  

**8.** next to → Al lado de · opposite → Enfrente de · between → Entre · near → Cerca de · in front of → Delante de  

**9.** park → trees and grass · café → coffee and snacks · library → books · pharmacy → medicine · bank → money  

**10.** Where is the bank? → Next to the library. · Is there a supermarket? → Yes, there is. · Where are the shops? → In the centre. · Is there a park? → Yes, there is a big park. · Where is the post office? → Opposite the bank.

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Completa

11. There is ____ supermarket in my street. → *a / an / the*  
12. The library ____ next to the school. → *is / are / be*  
13. Where ____ the pharmacy? It is opposite the bank. → *is / are / be*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a**  
12. **is**  
13. **is**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Contexto

14. Someone asks "Where is the post office?". You know it is next to the bank. You say: ____.  
    a) It is next to the bank. · b) I don't know. · c) There is a bank.  
15. You want to ask if there is a pharmacy near. You ____.  
    a) say "Where is the pharmacy?" · b) say "Is there a pharmacy near here?" · c) say "There is a pharmacy."

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **a) It is next to the bank.**  
15. **b) say "Is there a pharmacy near here?"**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: My town

**Objetivo:** Leer un texto corto sobre los lugares de un pueblo.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-11/reading-town.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: My town"></audio>

> My town is small but there are many places. There is a supermarket in the main street. Next to the supermarket is the library. The library is open from nine to five. Opposite the library there is a park. There is a pharmacy between the bank and the post office. I like my town.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Is the town big or small? → a) Small · b) Big · c) Very big  
2. Where is the supermarket? → a) In the main street · b) Next to the park · c) Opposite the bank  
3. What is next to the supermarket? → a) The bank · b) The library · c) The park  
4. The library is open from ____ to five. → a) eight · b) nine · c) ten  
5. What is opposite the library? → a) A park · b) The bank · c) The pharmacy

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Small**  
2. **a) In the main street**  
3. **b) The library**  
4. **b) nine**  
5. **a) A park**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–9 — Verdadero o falso

6. There is a pharmacy in the text. → ?  
7. The pharmacy is next to the park. → ?  
8. The writer likes his/her town. → ?  
9. There is a restaurant in the text. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **Verdadero**  
7. **Falso** (between the bank and the post office)  
8. **Verdadero**  
9. **Falso** (no se menciona un restaurante)

</details>

### Ejercicios 10–15 — Comprensión

10. Main topic:  
    a) Places in a town · b) A person · c) Food  
11. The pharmacy is ____ the bank and the post office.  
    a) between · b) next to · c) opposite  
12. How many places does the text mention?  
    a) Three · b) Five or more · c) One  
13. Where can you read books?  
    a) In the park · b) In the library · c) In the bank  
14. Where is the pharmacy in the text?  
    a) Between the bank and the post office. · b) Next to the park. · c) Next to the supermarket.  
15. The park is ____.  
    a) big · b) small · c) closed

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

10. **a) Places in a town**  
11. **a) between**  
12. **b) Five or more** (supermarket, library, park, pharmacy, bank, post office)  
13. **b) In the library**  
14. **a) Between the bank and the post office.**  
15. **b) small**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Anna and Ben

**Objetivo:** Escuchar un diálogo pidiendo direcciones en la ciudad.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-11/listening-anna-ben.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Anna and Ben"></audio>

> **Anna:** Where is the post office?  
> **Ben:** It is next to the bank. Go straight and turn left.  
> **Anna:** Is there a pharmacy near?  
> **Ben:** Yes, there is. It is between the library and the supermarket. There are two pharmacies in the street.  
> **Anna:** Thank you.  
> **Ben:** You're welcome.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What does Anna ask first? → a) Where is the post office? · b) Where is the bank? · c) Where is the pharmacy?  
2. Where is the post office? → a) Next to the bank · b) Opposite the library · c) Between the pharmacy and the supermarket  
3. Anna asks about a pharmacy. → a) True · b) False  
4. The pharmacy ____ between the library and the supermarket. → a) is · b) are · c) be  
5. How many pharmacies are there in the street? → a) One · b) Two · c) Three

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Where is the post office?**  
2. **a) Next to the bank**  
3. **a) True**  
4. **a) is**  
5. **b) Two**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Ben says "You're welcome". → a) True · b) False  
7. To go to the post office, Anna must ____.  
   a) go straight and turn left · b) turn right · c) go to the pharmacy  
8. Main topic of the dialogue:  
   a) Asking for directions to places in town · b) Buying food · c) A bank  
9. Is there a pharmacy near? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
10. Who answers Anna's questions? → a) Ben · b) Anna · c) Nobody

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) True**  
7. **a) go straight and turn left**  
8. **a) Asking for directions to places in town**  
9. **a) Yes**  
10. **a) Ben**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The post office is ____ the bank. → a) next to · b) opposite · c) between  
12. Anna says "Thank you". → a) True · b) False  
13. Where are the pharmacies? → a) In the street · b) In the bank · c) In the library  
14. What does Anna ask for first? → a) Where the post office is. · b) Where the bank is. · c) Where the park is.  
15. To find the post office, you go ____ and ____.  
    a) straight / turn left · b) turn right / straight · c) to the pharmacy / to the library

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) next to**  
12. **a) True**  
13. **a) In the street**  
14. **a) Where the post office is.**  
15. **a) straight / turn left**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My town

**Objetivo:** Escribir frases y párrafos cortos sobre lugares y ubicaciones.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. There ____ a bank near. → *is / are / be*  
2. There ____ two parks in my town. → *is / are / be*  
3. Where ____ the pharmacy? → *is / are / be*  
4. The library is ____ the school. → *next to / opposite / between*  
5. The café is ____ the bank and the pharmacy. → *next to / between / opposite*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is**  
2. **are**  
3. **is**  
4. **next to**  
5. **between**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. There / is / a / supermarket / near / here.  
7. Where / is / the / post office?  
8. The / bank / is / next to / the / library.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. There is a supermarket near here.  
7. Where is the post office?  
8. The bank is next to the library.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. Say what there is in your street (*There is a … / There are …*).  
10. Say where a place is (*The … is next to / opposite / between …*).  
11. Two sentences: what there is + where one place is.  
12. Question + answer (*Where is the pharmacy?*).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. There is a supermarket and a pharmacy in my street. There are two cafés.  
10. The bank is next to the library. The café is between the pharmacy and the post office.  
11. There is a park and a school in my town. The supermarket is opposite the bank.  
12. Where is the pharmacy? It is next to the supermarket.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Your town or street: places and where they are (*There is/are*, *next to*, *opposite*, *between*).  
14. Directions: how to get to a place (*go straight*, *turn left/right*).  
15. Compare two streets or towns: what there is in each one.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
In my town there is a library and a park. There are two supermarkets. The bank is next to the post office. The café is between the pharmacy and the school. The bus stop is opposite the library.

**14.**  
Go straight. Turn left at the bank. The pharmacy is next to the supermarket. It is between the café and the post office.

**15.**  
In my street there is a supermarket and a café. There are two parks. In the next street there is a library. There are three shops. The bank is in my street but the post office is in the next street.

</details>
