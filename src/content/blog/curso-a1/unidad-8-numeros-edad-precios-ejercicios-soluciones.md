## Lección 1 — Gramática: edad y precio

**Objetivo:** *How old…?* para la edad, *How much…?* para el precio y números 20–100.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. How ____ are you? → *old / much / many*  
2. How ____ is the coffee? → *old / much / many*  
3. I am ____ years old. → *twenty-five / twenty / five*  
4. The book ____ forty-five euros. → *is / are / am*  
5. How old ____ she? → *are / is / am*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **old** — *How old are you?*  
2. **much** — *How much is…?*  
3. **twenty-five**  
4. **is** — singular.  
5. **is** — *How old is she?*

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. Which question do we use for age?  
   a) How much is it? · b) How old are you? · c) How many?  
7. Which question do we use for price?  
   a) How old are you? · b) How much is it? · c) Where is it?  
8. My sister ____ 30 years old.  
   a) has · b) is · c) have  
9. These shoes ____ £50.  
   a) is · b) are · c) am  
10. 100 = ____.  
    a) one hundred · b) ten · c) one thousand

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **b) How old are you?**  
7. **b) How much is it?**  
8. **b) is**  
9. **b) are**  
10. **a) one hundred**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. How / old / are / you?  
12. I / am / twenty-eight / years / old.  
13. How / much / is / this / book?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. How old are you?  
12. I am twenty-eight years old.  
13. How much is this book?

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use "How old" to ask about age in English. → ?  
15. We use "How much" to ask the price of something. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: números, precios y edad

**Objetivo:** Números 20–100, monedas, *cheap/expensive* y preguntas de edad/precio.

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige la opción correcta

1. Forty = ____. → a) 14 · b) 40 · c) 44  
2. Eighty = ____. → a) eighteen · b) eighty · c) eight  
3. What symbol is for dollars? → a) £ · b) $ · c) €  
4. What word means *barato*? → a) expensive · b) cheap · c) small  
5. How do you say 90 in English? → a) nineteen · b) ninety · c) nine  
6. The opposite of cheap is ____. → a) expensive · b) small · c) old

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b) 40**  
2. **b) eighty**  
3. **b) $**  
4. **b) cheap**  
5. **b) ninety**  
6. **a) expensive**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–10 — Une con su significado

**7.** twenty · fifty · seventy · one hundred · thirty → 20 · 50 · 70 · 100 · 30  

**8.** How old are you? · How much is it? · years old · expensive · cheap  
→ Pregunta por edad · Pregunta por precio · Expresión para edad · Precio alto · Precio bajo

**9.** £ · $ · € · cents · bill → Libra (UK) · Dólar · Euro · Parte de dólar/euro · La cuenta  

**10.** sixty-four · twenty-one · ninety-nine · fifteen · eighty → 64 · 21 · 99 · 15 · 80

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

7. twenty→20 · fifty→50 · seventy→70 · one hundred→100 · thirty→30  
8. How old are you?→edad · How much is it?→precio · years old→edad · expensive→precio alto · cheap→precio bajo  
9. £→libra · $→dólar · €→euro · cents→céntimos · bill→cuenta  
10. sixty-four→64 · twenty-one→21 · ninety-nine→99 · fifteen→15 · eighty→80

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Completa

11. I am ____ years old. (25) → *twenty-five / fifty-two / fifteen*  
12. The total ____ ninety-nine pounds. → *is / are / am*  
13. How ____ are these apples? → *much / many / old*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **twenty-five**  
12. **is**  
13. **much**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Contexto

14. You want to ask the price of a coffee. You ____.  
    a) say "How old are you?" · b) say "How much is it?" · c) say "Where is it?"  
15. Someone asks your age. You answer: ____.  
    a) I am twenty-five years old. · b) I have twenty-five euros. · c) The book is twenty-five.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **b) say "How much is it?"**  
15. **a) I am twenty-five years old.**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: At the supermarket

**Objetivo:** Leer un texto corto de compras con cantidades y precios.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-8/reading-supermarket.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Supermarket"></audio>

> I am at the supermarket. I need twenty apples and ten oranges. The apples are five euros. The oranges are three euros. I also need a bottle of milk. The milk is one euro and fifty cents. The total is nine euros and fifty cents. I have twenty euros. I am happy.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. How many apples does the person need? → a) ten · b) twenty · c) five  
2. How much are the oranges? → a) five euros · b) three euros · c) one euro  
3. How much is the milk? → a) 1.00€ · b) 1.50€ · c) 2.00€  
4. What is the total? → a) nine euros · b) nine euros fifty · c) twenty euros  
5. How much money does the person have? → a) ten euros · b) twenty euros · c) nine fifty

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b) twenty**  
2. **b) three euros**  
3. **b) 1.50€**  
4. **b) nine euros fifty**  
5. **b) twenty euros**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–9 — Verdadero o falso

6. The apples cost five euros. → ?  
7. The person is at the restaurant. → ?  
8. The total is more than ten euros. → ?  
9. The person has enough money to pay. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **Verdadero**  
7. **Falso** (supermarket)  
8. **Falso** (9.50€)  
9. **Verdadero** (20€ > 9.50€)

</details>

### Ejercicios 10–15 — Comprensión

10. Main topic:  
    a) A restaurant · b) Shopping at the supermarket with prices · c) A family  
11. Which numbers appear in the text?  
    a) Only five and three · b) Twenty, ten, five, three, one, fifty, nine, twenty · c) One hundred  
12. How much money do they have?  
    a) 20€ · b) 9.50€ · c) 10€  
13. How many oranges does the person need?  
    a) twenty · b) ten · c) five  
14. What does the person need besides fruit?  
    a) A bottle of milk · b) Bread · c) Water  
15. How does the person feel at the end?  
    a) Sad · b) Happy · c) Tired

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

10. **b) Shopping at the supermarket with prices**  
11. **b) Twenty, ten, five, three, one, fifty, nine, twenty**  
12. **a) 20€**  
13. **b) ten**  
14. **a) A bottle of milk**  
15. **b) Happy**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: At the shop

**Objetivo:** Escuchar un diálogo de tienda sobre precios y edades.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-8/listening-shop.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Shop"></audio>

> **Maria:** How much is this book?  
> **Shop:** It is twenty-five euros.  
> **Maria:** And how much are the notebooks?  
> **Shop:** They are three euros each.  
> **Maria:** How old is your daughter?  
> **Shop:** She is twelve years old.  
> **Maria:** My son is fifteen. I need two books and one notebook.  
> **Shop:** That is fifty-three euros in total.

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. How much is the book? → a) twenty euros · b) twenty-five euros · c) thirty euros  
2. How much are the notebooks? → a) one euro each · b) three euros each · c) five euros each  
3. How old is the shop assistant's daughter? → a) ten · b) twelve · c) fifteen  
4. How old is Maria's son? → a) twelve · b) fifteen · c) twenty-five  
5. What does Maria need? → a) One book and one notebook · b) Two books and one notebook · c) Two notebooks and one book  
6. What is the total? → a) fifty-three euros · b) thirty-three euros · c) twenty-eight euros

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b) twenty-five euros**  
2. **b) three euros each**  
3. **b) twelve**  
4. **b) fifteen**  
5. **b) Two books and one notebook**  
6. **a) fifty-three euros**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–10 — Verdadero o falso

7. Maria asks about the price of the book. → ?  
8. The daughter is fifteen years old. → ?  
9. The notebooks cost five euros each. → ?  
10. The dialogue is about prices and ages. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

7. **Verdadero**  
8. **Falso** (twelve)  
9. **Falso** (three)  
10. **Verdadero**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Comprensión global

11. Main topic:  
    a) Sports · b) A customer asks prices and talks about ages with shop · c) Food  
12. What does the woman buy?  
    a) Books and a notebook · b) Only a notebook · c) Only books  
13. Which questions are asked?  
    a) Only How much · b) How much is the book, how much are the notebooks, how old is your daughter · c) Where is the shop  
14. How many books does Maria buy?  
    a) one · b) two · c) three  
15. In what currency are the prices?  
    a) Dollars · b) Euros · c) Pounds

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **b) A customer asks prices and talks about ages with shop**  
12. **a) Books and a notebook**  
13. **b) How much is the book, how much are the notebooks, how old is your daughter**  
14. **b) two**  
15. **b) Euros**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: Ages and prices

**Objetivo:** Escribir frases sobre edad y precio con números en inglés.

### Modelo

> I am twenty-five years old.  
> How much is this book? It is twenty euros. It is cheap.  
> How old is your sister? She is thirty years old.  
> The total is twenty-five euros.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ____ old are you? (*How*)  
2. How ____ is the coffee? (*much / many / old*)  
3. The book is ____ euros. (40) → *forty / fourteen / four*  
4. The total ____ fifty euros. (*is / are / am*)  
5. I ____ twenty-eight years old. (*am / is / are*)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **How**  
2. **much**  
3. **forty**  
4. **is**  
5. **am**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. How / much / is / this / book?  
7. I / am / thirty / years / old.  
8. The / total / is / twenty-five / euros.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. How much is this book?  
7. I am thirty years old.  
8. The total is twenty-five euros.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. Say your age (*I am … years old*).  
10. Ask the price of something (*How much is this …?*).  
11. Price + expensive/cheap.  
12. Ask someone's age + answer.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I am twenty-five years old.  
10. How much is this book?  
11. It is twenty euros. It is cheap.  
12. How old is your sister? She is thirty years old.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. A shopping trip: what you bought, prices, total.  
14. Ages of family or friends.  
15. Mix prices and ages (something you bought + ages + expensive/cheap).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
I go to the shop. How much is this book? It is twenty euros. How much are the notebooks? They are three euros. The total is twenty-three euros. It is cheap.

**14.**  
I am twenty-eight years old. How old is your brother? He is thirty years old. How old is your sister? She is twenty-five years old. My friend is twenty-two years old.

**15.**  
I am twenty-five years old. My sister is thirty years old. I buy a book. How much is this book? It is forty euros. It is expensive. The total is forty euros.

</details>
