## Lección 1 — Gramática: First, Then, After that

**Objetivo:** Secuenciadores y Present Simple para contar la rutina en orden.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ____ I wake up at seven. → *First / Then / After that*  
2. First I brush my teeth. ____ I have breakfast. → *Then / First / After that*  
3. I ____ up at half past six. → *wake / wakes / waking*  
4. After that ____ go to work. → *I / he / they*  
5. She ____ to bed at ten o'clock. → *goes / go / going*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **First** — empieza la secuencia.  
2. **Then** — siguiente paso.  
3. **wake** — *I* + forma base.  
4. **I** — *After that I go to work.*  
5. **goes** — *she* + *-es* (*go → goes*).

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. Which word comes first in a routine? → a) Then · b) First · c) After that  
7. First I get up. ____ I brush my teeth. → a) Then · b) First · c) Last  
8. He ____ breakfast at eight. → a) have · b) has · c) having  
9. After that we ____ home. → a) go · b) goes · c) going  
10. I ____ my teeth every morning. → a) brush · b) brushes · c) brushing

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **b) First**  
7. **a) Then**  
8. **b) has**  
9. **a) go**  
10. **a) brush**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. First / I / wake up. / Then / I / have breakfast.  
12. After that / I / go / to / work.  
13. She / goes / to bed / at / ten / o'clock.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. First I wake up. Then I have breakfast.  
12. After that I go to work.  
13. She goes to bed at ten o'clock.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use First, Then and After that to order the steps of a routine. → ?  
15. "Go to bed" means go to sleep or get into bed. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero** — *go to bed* = irse a la cama.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: acciones de la rutina

**Objetivo:** *wake up, brush my teeth, have breakfast, have a shower, go to bed* y más.

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige la opción correcta

1. In the morning I ____ at seven. → a) wake up · b) go to bed · c) have dinner  
2. I ____ my teeth every morning. → a) brush · b) wash · c) clean  
3. At eight I ____ breakfast. → a) have · b) has · c) having  
4. At night I ____ at ten. → a) wake up · b) go to bed · c) have lunch  
5. I ____ a shower in the morning. → a) have · b) has · c) do  
6. At one I ____ lunch. → a) have · b) go · c) brush

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) wake up**  
2. **a) brush**  
3. **a) have**  
4. **b) go to bed**  
5. **a) have** — *have a shower*  
6. **a) have**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–10 — Relaciona

**7.** Acción → traducción  

| Inglés | Español |
|--------|---------|
| wake up | ? |
| brush my teeth | ? |
| have breakfast | ? |
| go to bed | ? |
| have a shower | ? |

**8.** Acción → traducción  

| Inglés | Español |
|--------|---------|
| have lunch | ? |
| have dinner | ? |
| get dressed | ? |
| go to work | ? |
| go home | ? |

**9.** Expresión → significado  

| Inglés | Significado |
|--------|-------------|
| First | ? |
| Then | ? |
| After that | ? |
| in the morning | ? |
| at night | ? |

**10.** Orden lógico  

| Secuencia | Acción |
|-----------|--------|
| First | ? |
| Then | ? |
| After that | ? |
| In the evening | ? |
| At night | ? |

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**7.** wake up → Levantarse · brush my teeth → Lavarme los dientes · have breakfast → Desayunar · go to bed → Irse a la cama · have a shower → Ducharse  

**8.** have lunch → Almorzar · have dinner → Cenar · get dressed → Vestirse · go to work → Ir a trabajar · go home → Ir a casa  

**9.** First → Primer paso · Then → Siguiente · After that → Después · in the morning → Por la mañana · at night → Por la noche  

**10.** First → I wake up · Then → I brush my teeth · After that → I have breakfast · In the evening → I go home · At night → I go to bed

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Completa

11. I ____ up at seven. → *wake / wakes / waking*  
12. She ____ to bed at ten. → *goes / go / going*  
13. First ____ have a shower. → *I / he / they*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **wake**  
12. **goes**  
13. **I**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Contexto

14. Someone asks "What do you do first in the morning?". You wake up and brush your teeth. You ____.  
    a) First I wake up. Then I brush my teeth. · b) I go to bed. · c) I have dinner.  
15. You want to say your routine in order. You ____.  
    a) First... Then... After that... · b) I am tired. · c) What time is it?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **a) First I wake up. Then I brush my teeth.**  
15. **a) First... Then... After that...**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Emma's day

**Objetivo:** Leer la rutina diaria de Emma con secuenciadores.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-13/reading-emma.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Emma"></audio>

> My name is Emma. This is my day. First I wake up at seven o'clock. Then I brush my teeth and have a shower. After that I have breakfast at half past seven. I go to work at nine. At one I have lunch. I finish work at six. Then I go home. At night I have dinner and go to bed at ten.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the writer's name? → a) Emma · b) Anna · c) Lisa  
2. What does she do first? → a) She wakes up at seven. · b) She has breakfast. · c) She goes to work.  
3. After waking up, she ____. → a) has breakfast · b) brushes her teeth and has a shower · c) goes to work  
4. What time does she have breakfast? → a) At seven o'clock · b) At half past seven · c) At eight o'clock  
5. She goes to work ____. → a) at nine · b) at eight · c) at ten

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Emma**  
2. **a) She wakes up at seven.**  
3. **b) brushes her teeth and has a shower**  
4. **b) At half past seven**  
5. **a) at nine**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–9 — Comprensión

6. She has lunch at twelve. → a) True · b) False  
7. She finishes work at six. → a) True · b) False  
8. After work she ____. → a) goes home · b) has breakfast · c) goes to bed  
9. What time does she go to bed? → a) At nine · b) At ten · c) At eleven

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **b) False** (at one)  
7. **a) True**  
8. **a) goes home**  
9. **b) At ten**

</details>

### Ejercicios 10–15 — Detalles

10. Main topic:  
    a) Emma's daily routine · b) Food · c) A job  
11. Which words order the routine steps?  
    a) First, Then, After that · b) Big, Small · c) Yes, No  
12. She ____ dinner at night. → a) has · b) have · c) having  
13. How many meals does she mention? → a) Two · b) Three · c) Four  
14. What does Emma do before breakfast?  
    a) She brushes her teeth and has a shower. · b) She goes to bed. · c) She has dinner.  
15. The text uses ____ to order the steps.  
    a) First, Then, After that · b) Monday, Tuesday · c) Big, Small

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

10. **a) Emma's daily routine**  
11. **a) First, Then, After that**  
12. **a) has**  
13. **b) Three** (breakfast, lunch, dinner)  
14. **a) She brushes her teeth and has a shower.**  
15. **a) First, Then, After that**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Jake's routine

**Objetivo:** Escuchar una entrevista corta sobre la rutina de Jake.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-13/listening-jake.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Jake"></audio>

> **Interviewer:** What do you do first in the morning?  
> **Jake:** First I wake up at six. Then I brush my teeth and have a shower.  
> **Interviewer:** What about breakfast?  
> **Jake:** After that I have breakfast at half past six. I go to work at eight.  
> **Interviewer:** When do you go to bed?  
> **Jake:** I go to bed at ten o'clock.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What does the interviewer ask first? → a) What do you do first in the morning? · b) When do you go to bed? · c) What about breakfast?  
2. Jake wakes up ____. → a) at six · b) at seven · c) at eight  
3. After waking up he ____. → a) has breakfast · b) brushes his teeth and has a shower · c) goes to work  
4. What time does he have breakfast? → a) At six o'clock · b) At half past six · c) At seven o'clock  
5. He goes to work ____. → a) at seven · b) at eight · c) at nine

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) What do you do first in the morning?**  
2. **a) at six**  
3. **b) brushes his teeth and has a shower**  
4. **b) At half past six**  
5. **b) at eight**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. He goes to bed at nine. → a) True · b) False  
7. He uses First, Then and After that to describe his routine. → a) True · b) False  
8. Main topic of the dialogue:  
   a) Jake's morning and evening routine · b) Food · c) A job interview  
9. Jake has ____ before breakfast. → a) a shower · b) lunch · c) dinner  
10. When does he go to bed? → a) At nine · b) At ten · c) At eleven

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

7. **a) True**  
8. **a) Jake's morning and evening routine**

10. **b) At ten**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The first ____ is wake up. → a) step · b) meal · c) day  
12. Jake brushes his teeth in the morning. → a) True · b) False  
13. What comes after "Then"? → a) Brush teeth and have a shower · b) Go to bed · c) Have lunch  
14. What time does Jake have breakfast? → a) At half past six. · b) At eight. · c) At ten.  
15. Which sequence words does Jake use? → a) First, Then, After that · b) Monday, Tuesday · c) Big, Small

<summary>Ver solución</summary>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My daily routine

**Objetivo:** Escribir frases y párrafos cortos con *First, Then, After that*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ____ I wake up at seven. → *First / Then / After that*  
2. First I brush my teeth. ____ I have breakfast. → *Then / First / Last*  
3. I ____ up at six o'clock. → *wake / wakes / waking*  
4. I ____ to bed at ten. → *go / wake / have*  
5. I ____ breakfast at eight. → *have / has / having*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **First**  
2. **Then**  
3. **wake**  
4. **go**  
5. **have**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. First / I / wake up / at / seven.  
7. Then / I / have / breakfast.  
8. She / goes / to bed / at / ten.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. First I wake up at seven.  
7. Then I have breakfast.  
8. She goes to bed at ten.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. Two sentences: first and then in the morning (*First… Then…*).  
10. Wake-up time + bedtime.  
11. Three morning steps (*First, Then, After that*).  
12. *I brush my teeth every morning* + what you do next.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. First I wake up at seven. Then I have breakfast.  
10. I wake up at seven o'clock. I go to bed at ten.  
11. First I wake up at six. Then I brush my teeth. After that I have breakfast.  
12. I brush my teeth every morning. Then I have breakfast.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Your full day (5–6 sentences): wake up → bed with *First / Then / After that*.  
14. Morning routine until you leave home.  
15. Compare morning and night.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
First I wake up at seven. Then I have breakfast. After that I go to work. I have lunch at one. I have dinner at eight. I go to bed at ten.

**14.**  
First I wake up at half past six. Then I brush my teeth and have a shower. After that I have breakfast. I have coffee and toast. Then I go to work.

**15.**  
In the morning I wake up at seven. I have breakfast and go to work. In the evening I have dinner at eight. I watch TV. I go to bed at ten. The morning is busy but the evening is quiet.

</details>
