## Lección 1 — Gramática: Present Simple afirmativo

**Objetivo:** Usar Present Simple en afirmativa y la *-s* de 3.ª persona.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa con la forma correcta

1. I ____ every day. → *work / works / working*  
2. She ____ breakfast at seven. → *eat / eats / eating*  
3. They ____ football on weekends. → *plays / play / playing*  
4. He ____ to school every morning. → *go / goes / went*  
5. We ____ coffee in the morning. → *drinks / drink / drinking*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **work** — Con *I* forma base.  
2. **eats** — Con *she* + *-s*.  
3. **play** — Con *they* forma base.  
4. **goes** — Con *he*: *go → goes*.  
5. **drink** — Con *we* forma base.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. Which sentence is correct?  
   a) She work every day. · b) She works every day. · c) She working every day.  
7. Correct form of "study" for "he":  
   a) study · b) studys · c) studies  
8. Which is correct?  
   a) I plays tennis on Sundays. · b) I play tennis on Sundays. · c) I playes tennis on Sundays.  
9. Correct form of "go" for "she":  
   a) go · b) gos · c) goes  
10. Which sentence is correct?  
    a) You sleeps eight hours. · b) You sleep eight hours. · c) You sleeping eight hours.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **b) She works every day.**  
7. **c) studies** (*y → ies*)  
8. **b) I play tennis on Sundays.**  
9. **c) goes**  
10. **b) You sleep eight hours.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. early. / He / gets up  
12. every day. / She / studies  
13. go / They / to school.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. He gets up early.  
12. She studies every day.  
13. They go to school.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. With "he", we add -s to the verb: "he works". → ?  
15. "I plays football" is correct English. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Falso** — Correcto: *I play football*.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: verbos de rutina

**Objetivo:** Verbos de rutina diaria (*work, study, eat, drink, sleep, play, wake up, get up, go, come*).

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige el verbo correcto

1. What verb means doing your job? → a) sleep · b) play · c) work · d) eat  
2. You do this at night to rest. → a) drink · b) study · c) come · d) sleep  
3. You do this at mealtime. → a) go · b) eat · c) wake up · d) study  
4. Students do this to learn and prepare for exams. → a) play · b) drink · c) study · d) get up  
5. You are thirsty. Which verb? → a) sleep · b) work · c) come · d) drink  
6. Children do this with a ball for fun. → a) go · b) eat · c) wake up · d) play

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **c) work**  
2. **d) sleep**  
3. **b) eat**  
4. **c) study**  
5. **d) drink**  
6. **d) play**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–10 — Une la palabra / frase con su significado

**7.** Work · Study · Eat · Drink · Sleep → Trabajar · Estudiar · Comer · Beber · Dormir  

**8.** Play · Wake up · Get up · Go · Come → Jugar · Despertarse · Levantarse · Ir · Venir  

**9.** I work. · She studies. · He eats. · They drink. · We sleep.  
→ Yo trabajo. · Ella estudia. · Él come. · Ellos beben. · Nosotros dormimos.

**10.** She eats breakfast. · He drinks coffee. · They go to school. · I wake up early. · He comes home.  
→ Ella desayuna. · Él bebe café. · Ellos van a la escuela. · Yo me despierto temprano. · Él llega a casa.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

7. Work→Trabajar · Study→Estudiar · Eat→Comer · Drink→Beber · Sleep→Dormir  
8. Play→Jugar · Wake up→Despertarse · Get up→Levantarse · Go→Ir · Come→Venir  
9. I work.→Yo trabajo. · She studies.→Ella estudia. · He eats.→Él come. · They drink.→Ellos beben. · We sleep.→Nosotros dormimos.  
10. She eats breakfast.→Ella desayuna. · He drinks coffee.→Él bebe café. · They go to school.→Ellos van a la escuela. · I wake up early.→Yo me despierto temprano. · He comes home.→Él llega a casa.

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Completa

11. She ____ every morning at seven. → *wakes up / goes / drinks*  
12. They ____ lunch together every day. → *sleep / eat / study*  
13. He ____ to work by bus every morning. → *comes / plays / goes*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **wakes up**  
12. **eat**  
13. **goes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Contexto

14. Ana is a student. Every evening, she ____ for her exams.  
    a) sleeps · b) plays · c) studies · d) drinks  
15. Every morning, Pedro opens his eyes and gets out of bed. He ____ and gets up.  
    a) eats · b) works · c) wakes up · d) goes

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **c) studies**  
15. **c) wakes up**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: María's daily routine

**Objetivo:** Leer la rutina diaria de María en Present Simple.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-5/reading-maria.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: María"></audio>

> María wakes up every day at seven. She gets up and goes to the bathroom. Then, she eats breakfast in the kitchen. She drinks a cup of coffee every morning.  
> After breakfast, María goes to work. She works in an office in the city. She studies English at lunchtime. She eats lunch with her colleagues.  
> In the evening, María comes home at six. She plays with her dog and then she eats dinner. She goes to sleep at ten o'clock. María loves her daily routine.

### Ejercicios 1–8 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What time does María wake up? → a) At six · b) At seven · c) At eight · d) At nine  
2. What does María drink every morning? → a) tea · b) juice · c) water · d) coffee  
3. Where does María work? → a) At home · b) In a school · c) In an office in the city · d) In a shop  
4. When does María study English? → a) In the evening · b) At lunchtime · c) In the morning · d) At night  
5. Who does María eat lunch with? → a) Her family · b) Alone · c) Her dog · d) With her colleagues  
6. What time does María come home? → a) At five · b) At six · c) At seven · d) At four  
7. What does María do in the evening? → a) She goes to the gym · b) She plays with her dog · c) She studies English · d) She works late  
8. What time does María go to sleep? → a) At nine · b) At ten · c) At eleven · d) At eight

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b) At seven**  
2. **d) coffee**  
3. **c) In an office in the city**  
4. **b) At lunchtime**  
5. **d) With her colleagues**  
6. **b) At six**  
7. **b) She plays with her dog**  
8. **b) At ten**

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Verdadero o falso

9. María eats breakfast in the kitchen. → ?  
10. María works at home. → ?  
11. María has a cat. → ?  
12. María loves her daily routine. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. **Verdadero**  
10. **Falso** (works in an office)  
11. **Falso** (she has a dog)  
12. **Verdadero**

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Comprensión global

13. Main topic:  
    a) María's job · b) María's daily routine · c) María's family · d) María's hobbies  
14. Which verbs use -s in this text?  
    a) wake, get, go · b) wakes, gets, goes · c) woke, got, went · d) waking, getting, going  
15. Best summary:  
    a) María only works in the morning. · b) María follows a regular daily routine from morning to night. · c) María studies all day. · d) María never eats breakfast.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

13. **b) María's daily routine**  
14. **b) wakes, gets, goes**  
15. **b) María follows a regular daily routine from morning to night.**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: David and Sara

**Objetivo:** Escuchar un diálogo sobre rutinas diarias.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-5/listening-david-sara.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: David and Sara"></audio>

> **David:** Hi Sara! What time do you wake up every day?  
> **Sara:** I wake up at seven. Then I get up and eat. What about you?  
> **David:** I wake up at six. I go to work early. I work in a school.  
> **Sara:** Do you eat breakfast before work?  
> **David:** Yes, I eat breakfast at home. I drink coffee every morning.  
> **Sara:** I go to the gym after breakfast. Then I study English at home.  
> **David:** What time do you come home in the evening?  
> **Sara:** I come home at five. I eat dinner and then I go to sleep at ten.  
> **David:** I come home at four. I play football with my friends. Then I eat dinner and sleep at eleven.

### Ejercicios 1–8 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What time does David wake up? → a) At five · b) At six · c) At seven · d) At eight  
2. What time does Sara wake up? → a) At six · b) At seven · c) At eight · d) At nine  
3. Where does David work? → a) In an office · b) At home · c) In a gym · d) In a school  
4. What does David drink every morning? → a) tea · b) juice · c) water · d) coffee  
5. What does Sara do after breakfast? → a) She goes to work · b) She goes to the gym · c) She sleeps · d) She plays football  
6. What time does Sara come home? → a) At four · b) At five · c) At six · d) At seven  
7. What time does Sara go to sleep? → a) At nine · b) At ten · c) At eleven · d) At eight  
8. What does David do in the evening? → a) He studies English · b) He goes to the gym · c) He plays football · d) He works late

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b) At six**  
2. **b) At seven**  
3. **d) In a school**  
4. **d) coffee**  
5. **b) She goes to the gym**  
6. **b) At five**  
7. **b) At ten**  
8. **c) He plays football**

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Verdadero o falso

9. David wakes up at seven. → ?  
10. David eats breakfast at home. → ?  
11. Sara studies English at home. → ?  
12. David goes to sleep at ten. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My daily routine

**Objetivo:** Escribir frases y párrafos en Present Simple sobre rutinas.

### Modelo

> Every morning, I wake up at seven. I get up and eat breakfast.  
> She works every day. She also studies English.  
> I work in an office. He studies at university.  
> In the evening, I play football. I go to sleep at ten.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa con el verbo indicado

1. I ____ every day. (*work*)  
2. She ____ to school at eight. (*go*)  
3. We ____ lunch at one o'clock. (*eat*)  
4. He ____ coffee every morning. (*drink*)  
5. They ____ at ten o'clock every night. (*sleep*)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **work**  
2. **goes**  
3. **eat**  
4. **drinks**  
5. **sleep**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. She / gets up / early / every day.  
7. He / goes / to work / at eight.  
8. They / play / football / in the evening.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. She gets up early every day.  
7. He goes to work at eight.  
8. They play football in the evening.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. 1–2 sentences about your morning (*wake up / get up / eat*).  
10. 2 sentences about a friend/family member (*he/she*).  
11. 2 sentences with *work* and *study* (*I* + *he/she*).  
12. Evening–night (*play / eat / drink / sleep*).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. Every morning, I wake up at seven. I get up and eat breakfast.  
10. She works every day. She also studies English.  
11. I work in an office. He studies at university.  
12. In the evening, I play football. I go to sleep at ten.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. My daily routine (4–6 sentences; 4–5 different routine verbs).  
14. A friend’s / family member’s routine in 3rd person (≥4 verbs).  
15. My ideal daily routine (≥5 verbs).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
I wake up at seven every day. I get up and eat breakfast. I go to work at eight. I come home at six. In the evening, I play football. I go to sleep at ten.

**14.**  
My friend Ana wakes up at six. She gets up early and drinks coffee. She goes to work by bus. She studies English at lunchtime. She comes home at five. She goes to sleep at eleven.

**15.**  
In my ideal routine, I wake up at eight. I get up slowly and eat a big breakfast. I study English in the morning. I play tennis in the afternoon. I eat dinner with my family. I go to sleep at eleven.

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Tema | Recuerda |
|------|----------|
| *I / you / we / they* | forma base (*work, play, go*) |
| *he / she / it* | *-s / -es / -ies* (*works, goes, studies*) |
| Rutina | *wake up, get up, eat, drink, go, come, sleep* |
| Tiempo | *every day / every morning / at seven* |
