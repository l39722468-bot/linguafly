## Lección 1 — Gramática: *like + -ing* y *Why? Because…*

**Objetivo:** *like / don't like + -ing*, 3.ª persona y *Why? Because…*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ reading. → *like / likes / to like*  
2. She ____ watching films. → *likes / like / liking*  
3. They ____ like dancing. → *don't / doesn't / not*  
4. I like ____ music. → *listening to / listen to / listens to*  
5. ____ do you like it? Because it's fun. → *Why / Because / What*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **like** — *I like + -ing*.  
2. **likes** — *she* + *-s*.  
3. **don't** — *They don't like dancing.*  
4. **listening to** — *like + -ing*.  
5. **Why** — *Why? = ¿Por qué?*

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. After "like" we use ____. → a) verb + -ing · b) infinitive · c) past  
7. I ____ playing football. → a) like · b) likes · c) to like  
8. He ____ like cooking. → a) doesn't · b) don't · c) not  
9. Why do you like it? ____ I love it. → a) Because · b) Why · c) So  
10. We ____ swimming in summer. → a) like · b) likes · c) to like

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) verb + -ing** — *I like reading, I like swimming.*  
7. **a) like**  
8. **a) doesn't**  
9. **a) Because**  
10. **a) like**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. I / like / reading / books.  
12. Why / do / you / like / it? / Because / it's / relaxing.  
13. She / doesn't / like / running.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I like reading books.  
12. Why do you like it? Because it's relaxing.  
13. She doesn't like running.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use -ing after like (I like swimming). → ?  
15. We answer "Why" with "Because". → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero** — *Like + -ing.*  
15. **Verdadero** — *Why? Because…*

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: hobbies, música, películas

**Objetivo:** hobbies, géneros musicales y tipos de películas.

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige la opción correcta

1. I like ____ books. → a) reading · b) read · c) reads  
2. She ____ to music. → a) listens · b) listen · c) listening  
3. Watching ____ is a hobby. → a) films · b) film · c) a film  
4. Pop, rock and ____ are music genres. → a) jazz · b) book · c) sport  
5. A ____ film has action and fighting. → a) action · b) comedy · c) romance  
6. I ____ playing the guitar. → a) like · b) likes · c) to like

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) reading**  
2. **a) listens**  
3. **a) films**  
4. **a) jazz**  
5. **a) action**  
6. **a) like**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–9 — Relaciona

**7.** Hobby → complemento  

| Hobby | Complemento |
|-------|-------------|
| reading | ? |
| listening to | ? |
| watching | ? |
| playing | ? |
| dancing | ? |

**8.** Género musical → descripción  

| Género | Descripción |
|--------|-------------|
| pop | ? |
| rock | ? |
| jazz | ? |
| classical | ? |
| reggae | ? |

**9.** Tipo de película → idea  

| Tipo | Idea |
|------|------|
| comedy | ? |
| horror | ? |
| romance | ? |
| action | ? |
| documentary | ? |

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**7.** reading → books · listening to → music · watching → films · playing → the guitar · dancing → salsa  

**8.** pop → popular music · rock → guitars and drums · jazz → improvisation · classical → orchestra · reggae → from Jamaica  

**9.** comedy → funny · horror → scary · romance → love story · action → fights, chases · documentary → real facts

</details>

### Ejercicios 10–12 — Completa

10. I ____ swimming. Why? Because it's fun. → *like / don't / love*  
11. She doesn't ____ cooking. → *like / likes / to like*  
12. ____ do you like it? → *Why / Because / What*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

10. **like**  
11. **like** — *doesn't like* (sin *-s* tras *doesn't*)  
12. **Why**

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Contexto

13. Why do you like reading? → a) Because it's relaxing. · b) Yes, I do. · c) I like it.  
14. I ____ dancing. I go to a class every week. → a) like · b) don't like · c) hate  
15. What kind of music do you like? → a) I like pop and rock. · b) I am fine. · c) At eight.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

13. **a) Because it's relaxing.**  
14. **a) like**  
15. **a) I like pop and rock.**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Nina's likes

**Objetivo:** Leer sobre los gustos y hobbies de Nina.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-18/reading-nina.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Nina"></audio>

> Hi. My name is Nina. I like reading books and listening to music. I love pop and rock. I don't like classical music. I like watching films too. I prefer comedy and romance. I don't like horror films because they're scary. My hobby is playing the guitar. Why do I like it? Because it's relaxing.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the writer's name? → a) Nina · b) Anna · c) Leo  
2. What does she like doing? → a) Reading and listening to music · b) Only watching films · c) Only playing guitar  
3. Does she like classical music? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The text doesn't say  
4. What music does she love? → a) Pop and rock · b) Jazz · c) Classical  
5. What type of films does she prefer? → a) Comedy and romance · b) Horror · c) Action

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Nina**  
2. **a) Reading and listening to music**  
3. **a) No**  
4. **a) Pop and rock**  
5. **a) Comedy and romance**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. She likes horror films. → a) True · b) False  
7. What is her hobby? → a) Playing the guitar · b) Reading · c) Watching films  
8. Why does she like playing the guitar? → a) Because it's relaxing. · b) Because it's scary. · c) The text doesn't say  
9. Why doesn't she like horror films? → a) Because they're scary. · b) Because they're boring. · c) The text doesn't say  
10. The text uses ____ to give reasons. → a) Because · b) Why · c) So

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **b) False**  
7. **a) Playing the guitar**  
8. **a) Because it's relaxing.**  
9. **a) Because they're scary.**  
10. **a) Because**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. She ____ reading books. → a) likes · b) like · c) to like  
12. Does she like watching films? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say  
13. Main topic: → a) Nina's likes and dislikes (music, films, hobby) · b) Her job · c) Her family  
14. She says "I don't like" ____ times. → a) two (classical, horror) · b) one · c) three  
15. After like we see ____ in the text. → a) verb + -ing (reading, listening, watching) · b) infinitive · c) past

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) likes**  
12. **a) Yes**  
13. **a) Nina's likes and dislikes (music, films, hobby)**  
14. **a) two (classical, horror)**  
15. **a) verb + -ing (reading, listening, watching)**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Reading, music and films

**Objetivo:** Escuchar un diálogo sobre gustos.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-18/listening-likes.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Likes"></audio>

> **A:** Do you like reading?  
> **B:** Yes, I do. I love reading novels.  
> **A:** What about music? Do you like music?  
> **B:** Yes. I like listening to music. I like pop and jazz. I don't like heavy metal. Why? Because it's too loud.  
> **A:** Do you like watching films?  
> **B:** Yes, I like comedy films.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Does B like reading? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
2. What does B love reading? → a) Novels · b) Magazines · c) Newspapers  
3. What music does B like? → a) Pop and jazz · b) Heavy metal · c) Only classical  
4. Does B like heavy metal? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The audio doesn't say  
5. Why doesn't B like heavy metal? → a) Because it's too loud. · b) Because it's boring. · c) The audio doesn't say

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Yes**  
2. **a) Novels**  
3. **a) Pop and jazz**  
4. **a) No**  
5. **a) Because it's too loud.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Does B like watching films? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
7. What type of films does B like? → a) Comedy · b) Horror · c) Action  
8. B uses "Because" to ____. → a) give a reason · b) ask a question · c) say goodbye  
9. Main topic: → a) B's likes: reading, music, films · b) Work · c) Family  
10. A ____ about reading, music and films. → a) asks · b) ask · c) asking

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Yes**  
7. **a) Comedy**  
8. **a) give a reason**  
9. **a) B's likes: reading, music, films**  
10. **a) asks**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. B says "I don't like" ____. → a) for heavy metal · b) for reading · c) for comedy  
12. B ____ listening to music. → a) likes · b) like · c) to like  
13. Which question does A ask first? → a) Do you like reading? · b) Do you like music? · c) Do you like films?  
14. B gives a ____ for not liking heavy metal. → a) reason (because it's too loud) · b) no reason · c) a question  
15. The dialogue is ____. → a) about likes and dislikes · b) about the time · c) about work

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) for heavy metal**  
12. **a) likes**  
13. **a) Do you like reading?**  
14. **a) reason (because it's too loud)**  
15. **a) about likes and dislikes**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My likes and dislikes

**Objetivo:** Escribir gustos, disgustos y razones con *because*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ reading. → *like / likes / to like*  
2. She ____ like dancing. → *doesn't / don't / not*  
3. ____ do you like it? Because it's relaxing. → *Why / Because / What*  
4. They like ____ music. → *listening to / listen to / listens to*  
5. I ____ watching films. → *love / loves / loving*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **like**  
2. **doesn't**  
3. **Why**  
4. **listening to**  
5. **love**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. I / like / playing / the / guitar.  
7. Why / do / you / like / it? / Because / it's / fun.  
8. She / doesn't / like / horror / films.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I like playing the guitar.  
7. Why do you like it? Because it's fun.  
8. She doesn't like horror films.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. Two things you like doing (*I like …ing*) + one reason with *because*.  
10. Something you don't like + reason with *because*.  
11. *What kind of music do you like?* + your answer.  
12. A hobby you like and why (*Why? Because…*).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I like reading because it's relaxing. I like swimming because it's fun.  
10. I don't like cooking. Because it takes a long time.  
11. What kind of music do you like? I like pop and rock.  
12. I like playing the guitar. Why? Because it's relaxing.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Four sentences: music, films, a hobby + one *because*.  
14. *I like… / I don't like… / Because…*  
15. *She likes listening to pop music. He doesn't like heavy metal because it's too loud.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
I like pop and rock music. I like comedy films. My hobby is reading. I like it because it's relaxing.

**14.**  
I like dancing. I don't like horror films. Because they're scary.

**15.**  
She likes listening to pop music. He doesn't like heavy metal because it's too loud.

</details>
