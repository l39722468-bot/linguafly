## Lección 1 — Gramática: can/can't

**Objetivo:** afirmativa, negativa y preguntas con *can*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. She ____ speak three languages. → *can / can't / cans*  
2. I ____ drive. I don't have a licence. → *can't / can / not can*  
3. ____ you swim? → *Can / Do / Are*  
4. Penguins ____ fly. → *can't / can / could*  
5. My brother ____ cook very well. → *can / can't / to can*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **can** — *Can* + verbo en infinitivo.  
2. **can't** — *Can't* = *cannot*.  
3. **Can** — *Can you* + verbo?  
4. **can't** — *Penguins can't fly.*  
5. **can** — *Can* + infinitivo sin *to*.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. After *can* we use ____. → a) the base form of the verb · b) -ing · c) to + verb  
7. The negative of *can* is ____. → a) can't / cannot · b) don't can · c) not can  
8. To ask about ability: ____ you swim? → a) Can · b) Do · c) Are  
9. He ____ speak French. → a) can · b) cans · c) can to  
10. Correct short answer: Can you cook? Yes, ____. → a) I can · b) I do · c) I am

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) the base form of the verb**  
7. **a) can't / cannot**  
8. **a) Can**  
9. **a) can**  
10. **a) I can**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. I / can / swim / very / well.  
12. Can / you / speak / English?  
13. She / can't / drive.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I can swim very well.  
12. Can you speak English?  
13. She can't drive.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use *can* + base verb (no *-s*, no *to*). Example: *He can swim*. → V / F  
15. For questions we put *can* before the subject: *Can you…?* → V / F

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: habilidades

**Objetivo:** *swim, cook, drive, speak*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige

1. Swim = ____. → a) to move in water · b) to fly · c) to cook  
2. Cook = ____. → a) to prepare food · b) to drive · c) to swim  
3. Drive = ____. → a) to use a car · b) to speak · c) to cook  
4. Speak a language = ____. → a) to use words in that language · b) to cook · c) to drive  
5. I can swim = ____. → a) I know how to move in water · b) I can cook · c) I can drive

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) to move in water**  
2. **a) to prepare food**  
3. **a) to use a car**  
4. **a) to use words in that language**  
5. **a) I know how to move in water**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Relaciona (escribe la pareja)

6. swim · cook · drive · speak · can → in the pool, in the sea / in the kitchen / a car, a bus / English, Spanish, French / ability  
7. swim · cook · drive · speak · can't → nadar / cocinar / conducir / hablar / no poder

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. swim → in the pool, in the sea · cook → in the kitchen · drive → a car, a bus · speak → English, Spanish, French · can → ability  
7. swim → nadar · cook → cocinar · drive → conducir · speak → hablar · can't → no poder

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. I can ____ very well. (en el agua) → *swim / cook / drive*  
9. She can ____ Italian. → *speak / swim / cook*  
10. My dad ____ drive a lorry. → *can / can't / cans*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **swim**  
9. **speak**  
10. **can**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Elige

11. Ability = ____. → a) something you can do · b) something you like · c) something you have  
12. Language = ____. → a) English, Spanish, French, etc. · b) swim, cook · c) car, bus  
13. Can you cook? Yes, ____. → a) I can · b) I do · c) I am

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) something you can do**  
12. **a) English, Spanish, French, etc.**  
13. **a) I can**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena las palabras

14. I / can / cook / pasta.  
15. She / can't / drive.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. I can cook pasta.  
15. She can't drive.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: A talented family

**Objetivo:** leer sobre habilidades de una familia con *can/can't*.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-31/reading-lisa.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Lisa"></audio>

> My name is Lisa. I can swim very well because I go to the pool every week. My brother can cook. He makes pasta and salad. My mum can speak three languages: English, Spanish and French. My dad can drive a bus. I can't drive yet because I am only fifteen. We are a talented family.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the writer's name? → a) Lisa · b) Lina · c) Lucy  
2. Can Lisa swim? → a) Yes, very well · b) No · c) The text doesn't say  
3. What can her brother do? → a) Cook · b) Drive · c) Speak three languages  
4. How many languages can her mum speak? → a) Three · b) Two · c) Four  
5. What can her dad drive? → a) A bus · b) A car · c) A lorry

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Lisa**  
2. **a) Yes, very well**  
3. **a) Cook**  
4. **a) Three**  
5. **a) A bus**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Can Lisa drive? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The text doesn't say  
7. Why can't she drive? → a) She is only fifteen · b) She doesn't like it · c) The text doesn't say  
8. Where does Lisa go to swim? → a) To the pool · b) To the sea · c) To the lake  
9. What does the brother make? → a) Pasta and salad · b) Pizza · c) Soup  
10. Which languages does the mum speak? → a) English, Spanish, French · b) English, German · c) Spanish, Italian

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) No**  
7. **a) She is only fifteen**  
8. **a) To the pool**  
9. **a) Pasta and salad**  
10. **a) English, Spanish, French**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text says they are ____. → a) a talented family · b) a big family · c) a happy family  
12. How often does Lisa go to the pool? → a) Every week · b) Every day · c) Once a month  
13. What is the main topic? → a) What each person in the family can do · b) Lisa's school · c) Food  
14. Which word means "puede"? → a) Can · b) Can't · c) Drive  
15. Which word means "conducir"? → a) Drive · b) Bus · c) Dad

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) a talented family**  
12. **a) Every week**  
13. **a) What each person in the family can do**  
14. **a) Can**  
15. **a) Drive**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Can you…?

**Objetivo:** escuchar preguntas y respuestas con *can/can't*.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-31/listening-abilities.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Abilities"></audio>

> **A:** Can you swim?  
> **B:** Yes, I can. I go to the pool every Saturday.  
> **A:** Can your sister cook?  
> **B:** No, she can't. But she can speak Spanish and English.  
> **A:** Can you drive?  
> **B:** No, not yet. I am seventeen next month. Then I can start driving lessons.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Can B swim? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
2. When does B go to the pool? → a) Every Saturday · b) Every Sunday · c) Every week  
3. Can B's sister cook? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The audio doesn't say  
4. What can the sister do? → a) Speak Spanish and English · b) Cook · c) Drive  
5. Can B drive? → a) No, not yet · b) Yes · c) The audio doesn't say

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Yes**  
2. **a) Every Saturday**  
3. **a) No**  
4. **a) Speak Spanish and English**  
5. **a) No, not yet**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. When can B start driving lessons? → a) When he is seventeen · b) Next week · c) Next year  
7. How many languages can the sister speak? → a) Two · b) One · c) Three  
8. The dialogue is ____. → a) about abilities (can/can't) · b) about family · c) about food  
9. A ____ B about swimming, cooking and driving. → a) asks · b) ask · c) asking  
10. B says "not yet" for ____. → a) driving · b) swimming · c) cooking

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) When he is seventeen**  
7. **a) Two**  
8. **a) about abilities (can/can't)**  
9. **a) asks**  
10. **a) driving**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. Correct short answer: Can you swim? ____. → a) Yes, I can · b) Yes, I do · c) Yes, I am  
12. Which word means "aún no"? → a) Not yet · b) No · c) Yes  
13. B's sister ____ cook. → a) can't · b) can · c) cans  
14. B is seventeen ____. → a) next month · b) now · c) last year  
15. What does B do after he is seventeen? → a) Start driving lessons · b) Go to the pool · c) Cook

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My abilities

**Objetivo:** escribir con *can / can't* y *Can you…?*

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ swim well. → *can / can't / cans*  
2. She ____ drive. She has no licence. → *can't / can / not can*  
3. ____ you cook? → *Can / Do / Are*  
4. My dad ____ speak three languages. → *can / can't / to can*  
5. Birds ____ fly. → *can / can't / could*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **can**  
2. **can't**  
3. **Can**  
4. **can**  
5. **can**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. I / can / swim / and / cook.  
7. Can / you / speak / Spanish?  
8. She / can't / drive / yet.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I can swim and cook.  
7. Can you speak Spanish?  
8. She can't drive yet.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. What can you do? (*I can…*)  
10. What can't you do? (*I can't…*)  
11. *Can you…?* + your answer (*Yes, I can. / No, I can't.*)  
12. One ability of a family member + one they don't have.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I can swim and cook.  
10. I can't drive yet.  
11. Can you swim? Yes, I can.  
12. My sister can speak English. She can't drive.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Short paragraph (4–5 sentences): you and your family (*can/can't*).  
14. 3 Q&A: *Can you swim/cook/drive?* + answers.  
15. *He/She can…* + *He/She can't…*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
I can swim and cook. I can't drive yet. My mum can speak two languages. My dad can drive a bus.

**14.**  
Can you swim? Yes, I can. Can you cook? Yes. Can you drive? No, I can't.

**15.**  
He can cook. She can't drive.

</details>
