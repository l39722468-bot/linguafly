## Lección 1 — Gramática: *the* vs *a/an*; *some/any*

**Objetivo:** artículos y *some/any* para hablar del barrio.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I go to ____ park every day. → *the / a / an*  
2. There is ____ supermarket near my house. → *a / an / the*  
3. There is ____ apple on the table. → *an / a / the*  
4. There are ____ shops in my street. → *some / any / the*  
5. Are there ____ cafes near here? → *any / some / the*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **the** — *the* = el que conocemos.  
2. **a** — *a* + consonante.  
3. **an** — *an* + vocal.  
4. **some** — *some* en afirmativa.  
5. **any** — *any* en preguntas.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. We use ____ for specific or known things. → a) the · b) a · c) some  
7. There aren't ____ pharmacies here. → a) any · b) some · c) the  
8. I want ____ go to ____ cinema. → a) to, the · b) to, a · c) at, the  
9. There is ____ library in the centre. → a) a · b) an · c) the  
10. Do you have ____ bread? → a) any · b) some · c) the

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) the**  
7. **a) any**  
8. **a) to, the** — *I want to go to the cinema.*  
9. **a) a**  
10. **a) any** — *any* en preguntas.

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. There / is / a / supermarket / near / the / park.  
12. I / need / some / milk / from / the / supermarket.  
13. There / aren't / any / cafes / in / this / street.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. There is a supermarket near the park.  
12. I need some milk from the supermarket.  
13. There aren't any cafes in this street.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use some in affirmative and any in negative and questions. → ?  
15. A before consonant; an before vowel sound. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero** — *a book, an apple*.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: lugares del barrio

**Objetivo:** *park, supermarket, cinema, library, pharmacy*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la opción correcta

1. Where do you walk or play outside? → a) In the park · b) In the supermarket · c) In the pharmacy  
2. Supermarket = ____. → a) big shop for food and things · b) place to see films · c) place with books  
3. Cinema = ____. → a) place to watch films · b) place to buy medicine · c) place with trees  
4. Library = ____. → a) place to borrow books · b) place to buy food · c) place to see a film  
5. Pharmacy = ____. → a) place to buy medicine · b) place to watch films · c) place to play

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) In the park**  
2. **a) big shop for food and things**  
3. **a) place to watch films**  
4. **a) place to borrow books**  
5. **a) place to buy medicine**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Relaciona

**6.** Lugar → descripción  

| Lugar | Descripción |
|-------|-------------|
| park | ? |
| supermarket | ? |
| cinema | ? |
| library | ? |
| pharmacy | ? |

**7.** Necesidad → lugar  

| Necesidad | Lugar |
|-----------|-------|
| I need milk | ? |
| I need medicine | ? |
| I want to read | ? |
| I want to see a film | ? |

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**6.** park → trees, grass, play · supermarket → buy food · cinema → watch films · library → borrow books · pharmacy → medicine  

**7.** I need milk → supermarket · I need medicine → pharmacy · I want to read → library · I want to see a film → cinema

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. There is a ____ near my house. I buy food there. → *supermarket / cinema / library*  
9. I go to ____ to borrow books. → *the library / the park / the pharmacy*  
10. We watch films at ____. → *the cinema / the park / the supermarket*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **supermarket**  
9. **the library**  
10. **the cinema**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Elige

11. Where do you buy medicine? → a) At the pharmacy · b) At the cinema · c) In the park  
12. The ____ has books and you can read there. → a) library · b) supermarket · c) pharmacy  
13. Children play in ____. → a) the park · b) the pharmacy · c) the library

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) At the pharmacy**  
12. **a) library**  
13. **a) the park**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena las palabras

14. There / is / a / pharmacy / next to / the / supermarket.  
15. I / like / the / park / because / it / is / quiet.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. There is a pharmacy next to the supermarket.  
15. I like the park because it is quiet.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: My neighbourhood

**Objetivo:** leer una descripción del barrio con *a/the/some*.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-25/reading-neighbourhood.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Neighbourhood"></audio>

> My neighbourhood is quiet and nice. There is a park near my house. I go there every weekend. There is a supermarket in the street. I buy some food there. There is a cinema opposite the park. We sometimes watch films. There is a library next to the school. There isn't a pharmacy in my street but there is one in the next street.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. How is the neighbourhood described? → a) Quiet and nice · b) Big and noisy · c) Small and dark  
2. What is near the writer's house? → a) A park · b) A supermarket · c) A cinema  
3. When does the writer go to the park? → a) Every weekend · b) Every day · c) Once a month  
4. Where is the supermarket? → a) In the street · b) Next to the school · c) Opposite the park  
5. What is opposite the park? → a) The cinema · b) The library · c) The supermarket

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Quiet and nice**  
2. **a) A park**  
3. **a) Every weekend**  
4. **a) In the street**  
5. **a) The cinema**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Where is the library? → a) Next to the school · b) In the street · c) Opposite the park  
7. Is there a pharmacy in the writer's street? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The text doesn't say  
8. Where is the pharmacy? → a) In the next street · b) Next to the school · c) Opposite the park  
9. The writer buys ____ at the supermarket. → a) some food · b) books · c) medicine  
10. Which word means "barrio"? → a) Neighbourhood · b) Street · c) Park

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Next to the school**  
7. **a) No**  
8. **a) In the next street**  
9. **a) some food**  
10. **a) Neighbourhood**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The main topic is ____. → a) description of the writer's neighbourhood · b) the writer's family · c) shopping  
12. Do they watch films at the cinema? → a) Sometimes · b) Never · c) Every day  
13. Which places are mentioned? → a) Park, supermarket, cinema, library, pharmacy · b) School only · c) House only  
14. There ____ a library next to the school. → a) is · b) are · c) have  
15. The writer uses ____ before food in affirmative. → a) some · b) any · c) the

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) description of the writer's neighbourhood**  
12. **a) Sometimes**  
13. **a) Park, supermarket, cinema, library, pharmacy**  
14. **a) is**  
15. **a) some**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Places near here

**Objetivo:** escuchar preguntas sobre lugares del barrio.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-25/listening-places.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Places"></audio>

> **A:** Is there a park near here?  
> **B:** Yes, there is. The park is next to the library.  
> **A:** Are there any supermarkets?  
> **B:** Yes, there are some. There is a big one in the street.  
> **A:** And a pharmacy?  
> **B:** There is a pharmacy opposite the cinema. The cinema is between the park and the supermarket.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Is there a park near? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
2. Where is the park? → a) Next to the library · b) Opposite the cinema · c) Between the park and the supermarket  
3. Are there any supermarkets? → a) Yes, some · b) No · c) Only one  
4. Where is the big supermarket? → a) In the street · b) Next to the library · c) Opposite the pharmacy  
5. Where is the pharmacy? → a) Opposite the cinema · b) Next to the park · c) In the street

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Yes**  
2. **a) Next to the library**  
3. **a) Yes, some**  
4. **a) In the street**  
5. **a) Opposite the cinema**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Where is the cinema? → a) Between the park and the supermarket · b) Next to the library · c) Opposite the pharmacy  
7. B uses ____ for supermarkets in affirmative. → a) some · b) any · c) the  
8. A ____ about places in the area. → a) asks · b) ask · c) asking  
9. The dialogue is ____. → a) about places in the neighbourhood · b) about food · c) about family  
10. Which place is next to the park? → a) The library · b) The cinema · c) The pharmacy

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Between the park and the supermarket**  
7. **a) some**  
8. **a) asks**  
9. **a) about places in the neighbourhood**  
10. **a) The library**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. B says "there ____ some" for ____. → a) are / supermarkets · b) is / park · c) is / pharmacy  
12. The supermarket is ____. → a) big · b) small · c) new  
13. What does A ask about the pharmacy? → a) If there is a pharmacy · b) Where the pharmacy is · c) When the pharmacy opens  
14. The ____ is between the park and the supermarket. → a) cinema · b) library · c) pharmacy  
15. B gives ____ about locations. → a) information · b) food · c) money

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) are / supermarkets**  
12. **a) big**

14. **a) cinema**

## Lección 5 — Writing: Describe your neighbourhood

**Objetivo:** escribir sobre el barrio con *a/an/the* y *some/any*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. There is ____ supermarket in my street. → *a / an / the*  
2. I go to ____ park every weekend. → *the / a / some*  
3. There are ____ shops in the centre. → *some / any / the*  
4. Are there ____ cafes near here? → *any / some / the*  
5. There is ____ library next to the school. → *a / an / the*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a**  
2. **the**  
3. **some**  
4. **any**  
5. **a**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. There / is / a / pharmacy / opposite / the / cinema.  
7. I / need / some / milk / from / the / supermarket.  
8. The / cinema / is / between / the / park / and / the / library.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. There is a pharmacy opposite the cinema.  
7. I need some milk from the supermarket.  
8. The cinema is between the park and the library.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. What is in your neighbourhood (*There is a… / There are some…*).  
10. Where is a place (*next to / between / opposite*).  
11. *I need some… from the…* + *There isn't a…*  
12. *Is there a…?* or *Are there any…?* + your answer.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. There is a park and a supermarket in my street.  
10. The pharmacy is next to the supermarket.  
11. I need some bread from the supermarket. There isn't a pharmacy in my street.  
12. Are there any cafes? Yes, there are some.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Short paragraph (4–5 sentences): your neighbourhood with *a/the/some*.  
14. 3 sentences: *a/an/the* + one with *some* or *any*.  
15. *Where is the…?* + answer with a preposition.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
My neighbourhood is nice. There is a park near my house. There is a supermarket in the street. There are some shops. The library is next to the school.

**14.**  
There is an apple on the table. There are some books in the library. I don't have any milk.

**15.**  
Where is the pharmacy? It is opposite the cinema.

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Tema | Recuerda |
|------|----------|
| **a / an** | Primera mención; *an* + sonido vocálico |
| **the** | Específico / conocido / “el de siempre” |
| **some** | Afirmativa |
| **any** | Negativa y pregunta |
| **Vocab** | *park · supermarket · cinema · library · pharmacy* |
| **Tip** | *There is a park* → *I go to the park* |
