## Lección 1 — Gramática: *is / are*

**Objetivo:** Usar *is* y *are* con *he / she / it / we / they* y decir de dónde eres.

### Ejercicio 1 — Completa con *is* o *are* (10 ítems)

1. He ___ from Spain.  
2. She ___ a teacher.  
3. They ___ students.  
4. We ___ from Italy.  
5. It ___ a big city.  
6. He ___ Brazilian.  
7. They ___ happy.  
8. She ___ from Mexico.  
9. We ___ friends.  
10. It ___ cold today.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. is  
2. is  
3. are  
4. are  
5. is  
6. is  
7. are  
8. is  
9. are  
10. is  

**Regla:** *he / she / it* → **is** · *we / they* → **are**
