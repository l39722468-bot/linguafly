## Lección 1 — Gramática: posesivos y *'s*

**Objetivo:** Usar *my / your / his / her / our / their* y el genitivo sajón (*Tom's sister*).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. This ____ my book. → *is / are / am*  
2. ____ mother lives in Madrid. → *My / Your / His / Her*  
3. That is ____ sister. → *his / her / their / our*  
4. ____ grandfather is seventy years old. → *Our / Their / Your / My*  
5. They love ____ parents. → *their / our / your / his*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is** — Con *this* usamos *is*.  
2. **My** — Corresponde a *I*.  
3. **her** — Posesivo de *she*.  
4. **Our** — Posesivo de *we*.  
5. **their** — Posesivo de *they*.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. Which sentence is correct?  
   a) That is Tom's sister. · b) That is Tom sister. · c) That is Toms sister.  
7. Choose the correct form for "la hermana de Ana":  
   a) Ana's sister · b) Ana sister · c) Anas sister  
8. He has a brother. ____ brother is tall.  
   a) His · b) Her · c) Their · d) Our  
9. What is correct?  
   a) My father's car · b) My fathers car · c) My father car  
10. We have a grandmother. ____ grandmother lives here.  
    a) Our · b) Their · c) Your · d) My

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) That is Tom's sister.**  
7. **a) Ana's sister**  
8. **a) His**  
9. **a) My father's car**  
10. **a) Our**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. Tom's / sister. / That / is  
12. tall. / Her / brother / is  
13. here. / Our / grandmother / lives

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. That is Tom's sister.  
12. Her brother is tall.  
13. Our grandmother lives here.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use 's after a name to show possession. → ?  
15. 'Your' is the possessive for 'she'. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Falso** — *your* = *you*; *her* = *she*.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: miembros de la familia

**Objetivo:** Nombrar miembros de la familia y usarlos con posesivos.

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige la palabra correcta

1. Who is the female parent? → a) mother · b) father · c) sister · d) grandmother  
2. Who is the male parent? → a) mother · b) father · c) brother · d) grandfather  
3. A female sibling is your ____. → a) brother · b) sister · c) cousin · d) aunt  
4. Your mother's mother is your ____. → a) grandmother · b) aunt · c) mother · d) sister  
5. Your father's father is your ____. → a) uncle · b) father · c) grandfather · d) brother  
6. The child of your aunt or uncle is your ____. → a) son · b) daughter · c) cousin · d) nephew

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) mother**  
2. **b) father**  
3. **b) sister**  
4. **a) grandmother**  
5. **c) grandfather**  
6. **c) cousin**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–10 — Une con su significado

**7.** mother · father · sister · brother · grandmother  
→ madre · padre · hermana · hermano · abuela

**8.** grandfather · son · daughter · uncle · aunt  
→ abuelo · hijo · hija · tío · tía

**9.** My mother cooks. · His father works. · Her sister is young. · Our grandmother lives here. · Their son studies.  
→ Mi madre cocina. · Su padre trabaja. · Su hermana es joven. · Nuestra abuela vive aquí. · Su hijo estudia.

**10.** My brother is tall. · Your aunt is kind. · His cousin plays football. · Our grandfather is seventy. · Their daughter sings.  
→ Mi hermano es alto. · Tu tía es amable. · Su primo juega al fútbol. · Nuestro abuelo tiene setenta. · Su hija canta.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

7. mother→madre · father→padre · sister→hermana · brother→hermano · grandmother→abuela  
8. grandfather→abuelo · son→hijo · daughter→hija · uncle→tío · aunt→tía  
9. My mother cooks.→Mi madre cocina. · His father works.→Su padre trabaja. · Her sister is young.→Su hermana es joven. · Our grandmother lives here.→Nuestra abuela vive aquí. · Their son studies.→Su hijo estudia.  
10. My brother is tall.→Mi hermano es alto. · Your aunt is kind.→Tu tía es amable. · His cousin plays football.→Su primo juega al fútbol. · Our grandfather is seventy.→Nuestro abuelo tiene setenta. · Their daughter sings.→Su hija canta.

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Completa

11. My ____ is a teacher. → *mother / father / brother / grandfather*  
12. His ____ works in Madrid. → *sister / father / grandmother / daughter*  
13. Their ____ is five years old. → *uncle / aunt / son / cousin*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **mother**  
12. **father**  
13. **son**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Contexto

14. You introduce the sister of your mother. She is your ____.  
    a) grandmother · b) aunt · c) cousin · d) sister  
15. You talk about the child of your uncle. He is your ____.  
    a) son · b) cousin · c) brother · d) grandfather

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **b) aunt**  
15. **b) cousin**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Carlos and his family

**Objetivo:** Leer una descripción familiar con *my / our / his*.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-6/reading-carlos.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Carlos"></audio>

> My name is Carlos. I have a big family. My mother is Linda. She works in a school. My father is Juan. He is a doctor. My sister Ana is twenty years old. She studies at the university. My brother Pablo is fifteen. He plays football. Our grandmother lives in Valencia. Our grandfather is in Madrid. We love our family.

### Ejercicios 1–8 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is his name? → a) Carlos · b) Juan · c) Pablo · d) Ana  
2. What is his mother's name? → a) Ana · b) Linda · c) Maria · d) Elena  
3. What does his mother do? → a) She is a doctor · b) She works in a school · c) She studies · d) She plays football  
4. What is his father? → a) teacher · b) doctor · c) student · d) driver  
5. How old is Ana? → a) fifteen · b) twenty · c) twenty-five · d) thirty  
6. What does Pablo play? → a) basketball · b) football · c) tennis · d) volleyball  
7. Where does his grandmother live? → a) Madrid · b) Barcelona · c) Valencia · d) Seville  
8. Where is his grandfather? → a) Valencia · b) Barcelona · c) Madrid · d) Seville

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Carlos**  
2. **b) Linda**  
3. **b) She works in a school**  
4. **b) doctor**  
5. **b) twenty**  
6. **b) football**  
7. **c) Valencia**  
8. **c) Madrid**

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Verdadero o falso

9. Carlos has a small family. → ?  
10. His father is a doctor. → ?  
11. Ana is his brother. → ?  
12. Their grandmother lives in Valencia. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. **Falso** (*a big family*)  
10. **Verdadero**  
11. **Falso** (Ana is his sister)  
12. **Verdadero**

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Comprensión global

13. Main topic:  
    a) A school · b) Carlos and his family · c) A doctor · d) Football  
14. Which possessives are in the text?  
    a) Only "my" · b) My, our, his · c) Your and their · d) No possessives  
15. What does Carlos talk about?  
    a) His family (mother, father, sister, brother, grandparents) · b) His job · c) Only Valencia

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

13. **b) Carlos and his family**  
14. **b) My, our, his**  
15. **a) His family (mother, father, sister, brother, grandparents)**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Ana and Pedro

**Objetivo:** Escuchar un diálogo sobre las familias de Ana y Pedro.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-6/listening-ana-pedro.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Ana and Pedro"></audio>

> **Ana:** Hi Pedro! How are you?  
> **Pedro:** I am fine, thanks. And you?  
> **Ana:** I am good. I have two sisters and one brother.  
> **Pedro:** Nice! My family is big too. My mother is a teacher. My father works in an office.  
> **Ana:** Where does your grandmother live?  
> **Pedro:** She lives in Seville. Our grandfather lives there too.  
> **Ana:** I love my family.  
> **Pedro:** Me too. We love our family.

### Ejercicios 1–8 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. How many sisters does Ana have? → a) one · b) two · c) three · d) four  
2. What is Pedro's mother? → a) doctor · b) teacher · c) nurse · d) driver  
3. Where does Pedro's father work? → a) at school · b) in an office · c) at home · d) in a hospital  
4. Where does Pedro's grandmother live? → a) Madrid · b) Barcelona · c) Valencia · d) Seville  
5. Where does his grandfather live? → a) Madrid · b) Seville · c) Barcelona · d) Valencia  
6. Does Ana have a brother? → a) Yes, she does · b) No, she doesn't · c) One brother · d) Two brothers  
7. What do they say about family? → a) They love their family · b) They don't like family · c) Small family · d) No family  
8. Is Pedro's family big? → a) No, small · b) Yes, big · c) No family · d) Very small

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b) two**  
2. **b) teacher**  
3. **b) in an office**  
4. **d) Seville**  
5. **b) Seville**  
6. **a) Yes, she does**  
7. **a) They love their family**  
8. **b) Yes, big**

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Verdadero o falso

9. Ana has three brothers. → ?  
10. Pedro's mother is a teacher. → ?  
11. His grandmother lives in Madrid. → ?  
12. Both love their family. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. **Falso** (two sisters and one brother)  
10. **Verdadero**  
11. **Falso** (Seville)  
12. **Verdadero**

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Comprensión global

13. Main topic:  
    a) School and work · b) Ana and Pedro talk about family · c) Sports · d) Food  
14. Where do Pedro's grandparents live?  
    a) In different cities · b) Both in Seville · c) In Madrid · d) We don't know  
15. What do Ana and Pedro talk about?  
    a) Their families · b) Work · c) Shopping

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

13. **b) Ana and Pedro talk about family**  
14. **b) Both in Seville**  
15. **a) Their families**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My family

**Objetivo:** Escribir frases y párrafos con posesivos y *'s* sobre la familia.

### Modelo

> My mother is a teacher. She works in a school.  
> I have one brother and one sister. My brother is tall.  
> My sister's name is Ana.  
> My grandmother lives in Valencia. She is seventy years old.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ____ mother is a teacher. (*my*)  
2. ____ father works in an office. (*his*)  
3. ____ sister is twenty years old. (*her*)  
4. ____ parents live in Madrid. (*their*)  
5. Ana____ book is on the table. (*'s*)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **my**  
2. **his**  
3. **her**  
4. **their**  
5. **'s** → *Ana's*

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. has / My / sister / two / children.  
7. Madrid. / His / grandmother / lives / in  
8. every / day. / Their / parents / work

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. My sister has two children.  
7. His grandmother lives in Madrid.  
8. Their parents work every day.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. 1–2 sentences about mother/father.  
10. 1–2 sentences about siblings.  
11. One sentence with *'s* (name / car / house).  
12. 2–3 sentences about grandmother/grandfather.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. My mother is a teacher. She works in a school.  
10. I have one brother and one sister. My brother is tall.  
11. My sister's name is Ana.  
12. My grandmother lives in Valencia. She is seventy years old. Our grandfather lives in Madrid.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Describe your family (members, parents, siblings, grandparents).  
14. Introduce your family to a friend (possessives + *'s*).  
15. My family: who they are, what they do, where they live.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
I have a big family. My mother's name is Linda. She works in a school. My father is a doctor. My sister Ana is twenty years old. Our grandmother lives in Valencia.

**14.**  
This is my mother. Her name is Carmen. She is a teacher. That is my father. His name is Juan. My brother's wife is Maria. We love our family.

**15.**  
My family is busy every day. My father works in an office. My mother teaches at school. My brother's school is near our house. Their friends play football. I love my family.

</details>

| Tema | Recuerda |
|------|----------|
| Posesivos | *my, your, his, her, our, their* |
| Genitivo | *Tom's sister* · *My father's car* |
| Familia | *mother, father, sister, brother, grandmother, grandfather…* |
| Cuidado | *My mother's name is…* ≠ *My mother is…* |
