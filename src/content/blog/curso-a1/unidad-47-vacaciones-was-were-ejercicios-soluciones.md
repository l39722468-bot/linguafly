## Lección 1 — Gramática: was/were

**Objetivo:** *was/were* y pasado regular (*visited, played*).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ on the beach last week. → *was / were / am*  
2. We ____ in the mountains. → *were / was / are*  
3. She ____ the museum yesterday. → *visited / visit / visiting*  
4. They ____ volleyball on the beach. → *played / play / playing*  
5. It ____ sunny. → *was / were / are*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **was** — *I/he/she/it was*.  
2. **were** — *you/we/they were*.  
3. **visited** — pasado regular *-ed*.  
4. **played**  
5. **was**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *I ____ happy.* → a) was · b) were · c) are  
7. *You ____ on holiday.* → a) were · b) was · c) is  
8. Past of *visit*: ____. → a) visited · b) visit · c) visiting  
9. *She ____ at the hotel.* → a) was · b) were · c) are  
10. *Did* is used for ____. → a) past questions · b) only present · c) only future

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) was**  
7. **a) were**  
8. **a) visited**  
9. **a) was**  
10. **a) past questions**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. I / was / on / the / beach / last week.  
12. She / visited / the / museum / yesterday.  
13. We / were / in / the / mountains.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I was on the beach last week.  
12. She visited the museum yesterday.  
13. We were in the mountains.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *Was* goes with I/he/she/it; *were* goes with you/we/they. → True / False  
15. Regular past verbs often end in *-ed*: *visited, played*. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: vacaciones

**Objetivo:** *beach, mountains, sightseeing, souvenir, holiday*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *Beach* = ____. → a) a sandy place by the sea · b) high land · c) only a museum  
2. *Mountains* = ____. → a) high land or hills · b) the sea · c) only a hotel  
3. *Sightseeing* = ____. → a) visiting places as a tourist · b) only sleeping · c) only cooking  
4. *Souvenir* = ____. → a) something you buy to remember a place · b) only a ticket · c) only weather  
5. *Holiday* = ____. → a) time off or a trip · b) only work · c) only school

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) a sandy place by the sea**  
2. **a) high land or hills**  
3. **a) visiting places as a tourist**  
4. **a) something you buy to remember a place**  
5. **a) time off or a trip**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Empareja

6. Empareja: beach / mountains / sightseeing / souvenir / last year → sea and sand · hiking and snow · visiting monuments · something bought as a reminder · past time  
7. Empareja: visited / played / was / were / yesterday → past of visit · past of play · past of is/am · past of are · a past day

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **beach → sea and sand** · **mountains → hiking and snow** · **sightseeing → visiting monuments** · **souvenir → something bought as a reminder** · **last year → past time**  
7. **visited → past of visit** · **played → past of play** · **was → past of is/am** · **were → past of are** · **yesterday → a past day**

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–13 — Completa / elige

8. I was on the ____ every day. → *beach / beachesing / mountain*  
9. I bought a ____. → *souvenir / souvenirsing / weather*  
10. We did ____ in Barcelona. → *sightseeing / sightsee / sights*  
11. *Yesterday* is ____. → a) past time · b) future time · c) never  
12. Past of *visit*: ____. → a) visited · b) visit · c) visiting  
13. Sightseeing often includes ____. → a) museums and monuments · b) only sleeping · c) only cooking

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **beach**  
9. **souvenir**  
10. **sightseeing**  
11. **a) past time**  
12. **a) visited**  
13. **a) museums and monuments**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. A souvenir helps you remember a place. → True / False  
15. *Last summer* talks about the past. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Last summer

**Objetivo:** leer sobre unas vacaciones en España.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-47/reading-holiday.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Last summer"></audio>

> Last summer I was on holiday in Spain. I was on the beach every day. My friends visited the mountains too. We did sightseeing in Barcelona. I bought a souvenir—a small statue. The weather was sunny. We played volleyball on the beach. It was great.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Where was the writer? → a) In Spain, on holiday · b) At school · c) At work only  
2. Where was the writer every day? → a) On the beach · b) In the mountains · c) At home  
3. Where did friends visit? → a) The mountains · b) Only the hotel · c) Nowhere  
4. Where did they do sightseeing? → a) In Barcelona · b) In Paris · c) In London  
5. What souvenir? → a) A small statue · b) A car · c) A plane

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) In Spain, on holiday**  
2. **a) On the beach**  
3. **a) The mountains**  
4. **a) In Barcelona**  
5. **a) A small statue**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. What was the weather like? → a) Sunny · b) Snowy · c) Cold  
7. What sport did they play? → a) Volleyball · b) Football only · c) Tennis only  
8. *Sightseeing* means ____. → a) visiting places as a tourist · b) only sleeping · c) only cooking  
9. A *souvenir* is ____. → a) something you buy to remember a place · b) only a ticket · c) only weather  
10. *It was great* means ____. → a) It was very good · b) It was terrible · c) It was never fun

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Sunny**  
7. **a) Volleyball**  
8. **a) visiting places as a tourist**  
9. **a) something you buy to remember a place**  
10. **a) It was very good**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text is about ____. → a) a holiday in Spain · b) only food · c) only work  
12. *Was* is the past of ____. → a) is/am · b) are · c) will  
13. *Visited* is ____. → a) the past of visit · b) the present · c) the future  
14. *Mountains* means ____. → a) high land · b) the sea · c) only a hotel  
15. The main topic is ____. → a) a summer holiday with beach, mountains and sightseeing · b) cooking · c) music

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) a holiday in Spain**  
12. **a) is/am**  
13. **a) the past of visit**  
14. **a) high land**  
15. **a) a summer holiday with beach, mountains and sightseeing**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Last week

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo sobre unas vacaciones recientes.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-47/listening-holiday.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Last week"></audio>

> **A:** Where were you last week?  
> **B:** I was on the beach. I played volleyball and swam.  
> **A:** Nice. Did you do sightseeing?  
> **B:** Yes. I visited the old town. I bought a souvenir.  
> **A:** Were you in the mountains too?  
> **B:** No, I was not. Next year I will go to the mountains.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Where was B last week? → a) On the beach · b) In the mountains · c) At school  
2. What did B do? → a) Played volleyball and swam · b) Only worked · c) Only slept  
3. Did B do sightseeing? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
4. What did B visit? → a) The old town · b) Only a hotel · c) Nowhere  
5. Did B buy a souvenir? → a) Yes · b) No · c) Never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) On the beach**  
2. **a) Played volleyball and swam**  
3. **a) Yes**  
4. **a) The old town**  
5. **a) Yes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Was B in the mountains? → a) No · b) Yes · c) Always  
7. Where will B go next year? → a) To the mountains · b) To school · c) Nowhere  
8. *Old town* means ____. → a) the historic centre of a city · b) only a beach · c) only a hotel  
9. *Was* is ____. → a) the past of I am · b) the present · c) the future  
10. *Swam* is ____. → a) the past of swim · b) the present · c) the future

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) No**  
7. **a) To the mountains**  
8. **a) the historic centre of a city**  
9. **a) the past of I am**  
10. **a) the past of swim**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. The dialogue is about ____. → a) a holiday, the beach, and sightseeing · b) only food · c) only work  
12. *Bought* is ____. → a) the past of buy · b) the present · c) the future  
13. *Nice* here is ____. → a) an expression of approval · b) a refusal · c) a ticket  
14. *Were you…?* is ____. → a) a past-tense question with you · b) a present question · c) a future question  
15. The dialogue uses ____. → a) the past tense · b) only present · c) only future

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) a holiday, the beach, and sightseeing**  
12. **a) the past of buy**  
13. **a) an expression of approval**  
14. **a) a past-tense question with you**  
15. **a) the past tense**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My holiday

**Objetivo:** escribir sobre vacaciones en pasado.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ on the beach last week. → *was / were / am*  
2. She ____ the museum yesterday. → *visited / visit / visiting*  
3. We ____ in the mountains. → *were / was / are*  
4. I ____ a souvenir. → *bought / buy / buying*  
5. They ____ volleyball. → *played / play / playing*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **was**  
2. **visited**  
3. **were**  
4. **bought**  
5. **played**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. I / was / on / the / beach / last week.  
7. We / did / sightseeing / in / Barcelona.  
8. She / visited / the / museum / yesterday.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I was on the beach last week.  
7. We did sightseeing in Barcelona.  
8. She visited the museum yesterday.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Escribe dónde estuviste la semana pasada.  
10. Dos actividades: visit + play.  
11. Dos frases: tiempo + souvenir.  
12. Pregunta y respuesta con *Were you…?*  
13. Párrafo corto (4 frases) sobre unas vacaciones.  
14. Dos frases: *I was…* + *My friends were…*  
15. Dos frases sobre ayer.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I was on the beach last week.  
10. I visited the old town and played volleyball.  
11. The weather was sunny. I bought a souvenir—a small statue.  
12. Were you on the beach? Yes, I was.  
13. Last summer I was on the beach. I played volleyball and did sightseeing. The weather was sunny. I bought a souvenir.  
14. I was on the beach last week. My friends were in the mountains.  
15. Yesterday I was in town. I visited the museum.

</details>
