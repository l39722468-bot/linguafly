## Lección 1 — Gramática: should / shouldn't

**Objetivo:** consejos con *should / shouldn't* + verbo base.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. You ____ see a doctor if you have a fever. → *should / shouldn't / to*  
2. You ____ go to work if you are very ill. → *shouldn't / should / must to*  
3. He ____ go to the pharmacy for medicine. → *should / shouldn't / to*  
4. You should ____ a lot of water. → *drink / to drink / drinking*  
5. You shouldn't ____ on the computer all day. → *work / to work / working*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **should** — consejo.  
2. **shouldn't** — consejo negativo.  
3. **should**  
4. **drink** — *should* + verbo base (sin *to*).  
5. **work** — *shouldn't* + verbo base.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *Should* is used for ____. → a) advice · b) past facts · c) only questions about food  
7. After *should* we use ____. → a) base verb · b) *to* + verb · c) *-ing* only  
8. *You shouldn't go* is ____. → a) correct · b) needs *to* · c) needs *-s*  
9. *He shoulds rest* is ____. → a) wrong (*should* has no *-s*) · b) correct · c) past tense  
10. *You should to rest* is ____. → a) wrong · b) correct · c) better than *should rest*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) advice**  
7. **a) base verb**  
8. **a) correct**  
9. **a) wrong (*should* has no *-s*)**  
10. **a) wrong**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *You should to rest.*  
12. *He shoulds see a doctor.*  
13. *You shouldn't to go to work.*  
14. *I have headache.*  
15. *Go to pharmacy.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. You should **rest**.  
12. He **should** see a doctor.  
13. You shouldn't **go** to work.  
14. I have **a** headache.  
15. Go to **the** pharmacy.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: salud y farmacia

**Objetivo:** *headache, fever, cold, medicine, pharmacy, doctor, rest, ill*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja

1. headache  
2. fever  
3. cold  
4. pharmacy  
5. medicine  

a) place to buy medicine · b) pain in your head · c) something you take when ill · d) high body temperature · e) illness with cough / runny nose

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **d** · 3 → **e** · 4 → **a** · 5 → **c**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. I have a ____. (dolor de cabeza) → *headache / fever / pharmacy*  
7. I have a ____. (fiebre) → *fever / cut / oven*  
8. I think I have a ____. (resfriado) → *cold / pan / bill*  
9. Buy medicine at the ____. → *pharmacy / restaurant / oven*  
10. You should ____ at home. → *rest / fry / bake*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **headache**  
7. **fever**  
8. **cold**  
9. **pharmacy**  
10. **rest**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Ill / sick* means ____. → a) not well · b) hungry · c) happy  
12. *Doctor* = ____. → a) a person who helps when you are ill · b) a chef · c) a waiter  
13. *Pill* is a type of ____. → a) medicine · b) food · c) clothes  
14. *I feel ill* = ____. → a) I don't feel well · b) I am hungry · c) I am at work  
15. *See a doctor* means ____. → a) visit a doctor · b) buy clothes · c) cook pasta

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) not well**  
12. **a) a person who helps when you are ill**  
13. **a) medicine**  
14. **a) I don't feel well**  
15. **a) visit a doctor**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: I feel ill today

**Objetivo:** leer un texto con síntomas y consejos.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-58/reading-health.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: I feel ill today"></audio>

> I feel ill today. I have a headache and a slight fever. I think I have a cold. I should rest at home and drink a lot of water. I should take some medicine from the pharmacy. I shouldn't go to work today. If the fever gets worse I should see a doctor.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. How does the person feel? → a) Ill · b) Great · c) Hungry  
2. What symptoms does the person have? → a) Headache and slight fever · b) Only a cough · c) No symptoms  
3. What does the person think they have? → a) A cold · b) A broken leg · c) Nothing  
4. Should the person go to work? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The text doesn't say  
5. When should they see a doctor? → a) If the fever gets worse · b) Never · c) Only for food

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Ill**  
2. **a) Headache and slight fever**  
3. **a) A cold**  
4. **a) No**  
5. **a) If the fever gets worse**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The person should ____ at home. → a) rest · b) work · c) fry  
7. Medicine comes from the ____. → a) pharmacy · b) bakery · c) cinema  
8. The person should drink ____. → a) a lot of water · b) oil · c) coffee only  
9. *Slight fever* means ____. → a) a little fever · b) no fever · c) a big party  
10. The main topic is ____. → a) feeling ill and what you should do · b) cooking · c) shopping

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) rest**  
7. **a) pharmacy**  
8. **a) a lot of water**  
9. **a) a little fever**  
10. **a) feeling ill and what you should do**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *I have a headache* needs ____. → a) *a* · b) no article · c) *the* only  
12. *Should* is followed by ____. → a) base verb · b) *to* + verb · c) past  
13. *Shouldn't go* is ____. → a) correct · b) needs *to* · c) wrong  
14. *Take some medicine* means ____. → a) use medicine · b) cook medicine · c) sell medicine  
15. *See a doctor* = ____. → a) visit a doctor · b) phone a chef · c) go shopping

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) *a***  
12. **a) base verb**  
13. **a) correct**  
14. **a) use medicine**  
15. **a) visit a doctor**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: I have a bad headache

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo de salud y consejos.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-58/listening-health.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: I have a bad headache"></audio>

> **A:** I have a bad headache.  
> **B:** You should take some medicine.  
> **A:** Where can I get it?  
> **B:** At the pharmacy. Or if you have a fever you should see a doctor.  
> **A:** I don't have a fever. Just a headache.  
> **B:** Then you should rest and drink water. You shouldn't work on the computer all day.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What is A's problem? → a) A bad headache · b) A broken arm · c) No problem  
2. What does B suggest first? → a) Take some medicine · b) Go to work · c) Cook pasta  
3. Where can A get medicine? → a) At the pharmacy · b) At the cinema · c) At school  
4. Does A have a fever? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The dialogue doesn't say  
5. Should A work on the computer all day? → a) No · b) Yes · c) Only at night

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) A bad headache**  
2. **a) Take some medicine**  
3. **a) At the pharmacy**  
4. **a) No**  
5. **a) No**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. If you have a fever you should ____. → a) see a doctor · b) fry onions · c) buy clothes  
7. B also says A should ____. → a) rest and drink water · b) run a marathon · c) bake bread  
8. *Bad headache* means ____. → a) a strong headache · b) no pain · c) a cold only  
9. The dialogue is about ____. → a) health advice · b) a restaurant order · c) a recipe  
10. *Shouldn't* appears with ____. → a) work on the computer · b) take medicine · c) rest

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) see a doctor**  
7. **a) rest and drink water**  
8. **a) a strong headache**  
9. **a) health advice**  
10. **a) work on the computer**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *You should take* uses ____. → a) base verb · b) *to take* · c) *taking* only  
12. *Pharmacy* is where you buy ____. → a) medicine · b) tickets · c) shoes  
13. *Just a headache* means ____. → a) only a headache · b) fever and cold · c) nothing  
14. *Rest* means ____. → a) relax / not work hard · b) cook · c) pay  
15. The dialogue practices ____. → a) should / shouldn't · b) First / Then / Finally · c) too / enough

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) base verb**  
12. **a) medicine**  
13. **a) only a headache**  
14. **a) relax / not work hard**  
15. **a) should / shouldn't**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: Advice for a friend

**Objetivo:** escribir consejos de salud con *should / shouldn't*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. You ____ rest at home. → *should / shouldn't / to*  
2. You ____ go to work today. → *shouldn't / should / must to*  
3. I have ____ headache. → *a / – / the*  
4. Go to ____ pharmacy. → *the / – / a*  
5. You should ____ some medicine. → *take / to take / taking*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **should**  
2. **shouldn't**  
3. **a**  
4. **the**  
5. **take**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. You / should / see / a doctor.  
7. You / shouldn't / go / to work.  
8. I / have / a / fever.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. You should see a doctor.  
7. You shouldn't go to work.  
8. I have a fever.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Tienes dolor de cabeza: consejo con *should*.  
10. Estás enfermo: no vayas al trabajo (*shouldn't*).  
11. Pregunta dónde comprar medicina.  
12. Di que debes beber agua y descansar.  
13. Párrafo corto (4–6 frases): síntomas + *should* / *shouldn't*.  
14. Usa *pharmacy* y *doctor* en dos frases.  
15. Si la fiebre empeora, ve al médico.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. You should take some medicine. / You should rest.  
10. You shouldn't go to work.  
11. Where can I get medicine? / Where is the pharmacy?  
12. You should drink water and rest.  
13. I feel ill. I have a headache and a cold. I should rest at home. I should take medicine. I shouldn't go to work.  
14. Go to the pharmacy for medicine. You should see a doctor.  
15. If the fever gets worse, you should see a doctor.

</details>
