## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 37 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Usar ***very well / quite well / not at all***.
- Colocarlos **después del verbo**.
- Describir personas: ***creative, hardworking, fast***.
- Usar ***good at + -ing***.
- Preguntar ***How well can you…?***

---

## 1. La escala de habilidad

| Expresión | Significado | Nivel |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **very well** | muy bien | alto |
| **quite well** | bastante bien | medio-alto |
| **well** | bien | básico positivo |
| **not at all** | nada / en absoluto | cero |

![Escala very well quite well not at all A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-37/a1-unit37-skill-levels.png)

```
very well  >  quite well  >  well  >  not at all
```

### Tip

Estas expresiones van **al final** de la idea verbal: *I can sing **very well**.* No digas *I very well can sing*.

### *Very* vs *quite*

| Frase | Matiz |
| :--- | :--- |
| I sing **very well**. | Excelente / muy alto. |
| I sing **quite well**. | Bien, pero no perfecto. |

> He can't dance **at all**.

> Not at all.

### Patrones

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| can't + verb + **at all** | I can't swim at all. |
| don't / doesn't + verb + **well** | They don't sing well. |

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-37/dont-sing-well.mp3" title="🔊 They don't sing well"></audio>

Con *not at all* el foco es **cero habilidad**. Con *don't … well* es “no lo hago bien” (puede ser un poco, pero mal).

### *Good at + -ing*

> She is **good at drawing**.  
> He is **good at cooking**.  
> I'm **bad at dancing**.

Tras *good at / bad at* → **verbo + -ing** (como *like + -ing* en U18).

## 7. Reading modelo: Lisa

> My name is Lisa. I can sing **very well** and I play the piano **quite well**. My brother can cook **very well** — he is **creative**. I can't draw **at all**. My sister is **hardworking** and she speaks English **very well**. We all have different **skills**.

### Comprueba

1. ¿Canta Lisa bien? → **Very well**.  
2. ¿Cómo toca el piano? → **Quite well**.  
3. ¿Quién cocina muy bien? → **Her brother** (creative).  
4. ¿Sabe dibujar? → **Not at all**.  
5. ¿Cómo es su hermana? → **Hardworking**; speaks English very well.

## 8. Diálogo modelo

> **A:** How well can you sing?  
> **B:** Very well — and I play the piano quite well. And you?  
> **A:** I can't sing at all! But my brother can cook very well.  
> **B:** Is he creative?  
> **A:** Yes, he is. My sister is hardworking and she speaks English very well.  
> **B:** Nice. We all have different skills.

## 9. Errores típicos de hispanohablantes

| ❌ Incorrecto | ✅ Correcto | Explicación |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *I very well can sing* | *I can sing **very well*** | Adv. al final |
| *I can to sing very well* | *I can **sing** very well* | Sin *to* tras *can* |
| *She is good at draw* | *She is good at **drawing*** | *good at + -ing* |
| *I can't draw not at all* | *I can't draw **at all*** | Un solo negativo |
| *quite very well* | *very well* **o** *quite well* | No combines ambos |

---

## 9b. Pronunciación útil

| Forma | Tip A1 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **very well** | *VE-ri wel* — une las dos palabras. |
| **quite well** | *kwait wel*. |
| **at all** | *ət OL* (acento en *all*). |
| **creative** | *kri-EI-tiv*. |
| **hardworking** | *HARD-wer-king*. |

Practica la escala: *very well → quite well → not at all*.

## 9c. Mini-diálogos para memorizar

> **A:** How well can you cook?  
> **B:** Quite well, thanks.  
> **A:** Can you draw?  
> **B:** Not at all.

> **A:** Does she sing well?  
> **B:** Yes — very well.  
> **A:** And the piano?  
> **B:** She plays the piano quite well.

> **A:** Is your brother creative?  
> **B:** Yes. He can cook very well.  
> **A:** Is your sister hardworking?  
> **B:** Yes — and she speaks English very well.

Cambia solo la habilidad: fijas ***very well / quite well / not at all***.

## 9d. Plantillas para copiar

1. I can \_\_\_\_ very well.  
2. I \_\_\_\_ quite well.  
3. I can't \_\_\_\_ at all.  
4. She / He is creative / hardworking.  
5. She is good at \_\_\_\_ing.  
6. How well can you \_\_\_\_?  
7. We all have different skills.

### Más frases para memorizar

> I can speak English very well.  
> She cooks quite well.  
> He can't dance at all.  
> I play the guitar quite well.  
> They don't sing well.  
> I can sing very well.  
> She can draw very well.  
> I can't draw at all.  
> My brother can cook very well — he is creative.  
> My sister is hardworking and she speaks English very well.  
> She is good at drawing.  
> He can run very fast.  
> My name is Lisa. I can sing very well and I play the piano quite well.  
> We all have different skills.  
> How well can you cook?  
> Quite well, thanks.  
> Not at all.  
> Very well.  
> She is creative — she paints beautifully.  
> He works hard.

Lee la lista dos veces. Luego escribe **tus** skills con la escala completa.

### Rutina de 2 minutos

1. Di 2 frases *very well*.  
2. Di 2 *quite well*.  
3. Di 2 *not at all* / *can't … at all*.  
4. Di 1 *good at + -ing* y 1 adjetivo (*creative / hardworking*).

Si lo unes a U36: *I play the piano quite well. She plays the violin very well.*

### Combinar U31 + U36 + U37

| Unidad | Aporta | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| U31 | can / can't | I can sing |
| U36 | play the + instrument | I play the piano |
| **U37** | degree | …**very well** / **quite well** |

Modelo: *I can play the piano quite well. I can't draw at all.*

### Mini-historia para reescribir

> I can sing very well. I play the piano quite well. My brother can cook very well — he is creative. I can't draw at all. My sister is hardworking and she speaks English very well. We all have different skills.

Cambia las habilidades y mantén la escala.

### Tour guiado (modelo Lisa)

> My name is Lisa. I can sing very well and I play the piano quite well. My brother can cook very well — he is creative. I can't draw at all. My sister is hardworking and she speaks English very well. We all have different skills.

Grábate leyéndolo. Luego improvisa con **tu** familia.

### Frases extra para practicar en voz alta

> How well can you cook?  
> Very well.  
> Quite well, thanks.  
> Not at all.  
> I can sing very well.  
> I play the guitar quite well.  
> I can't dance at all.  
> She is good at drawing.  
> He is creative.  
> She is hardworking.

Personaliza con tus talentos reales.

### Checklist mental

- Escala: very well → quite well → not at all  
- Posición: **después** del verbo  
- *can't … at all*  
- *good at + -ing*  
- Adjetivos: creative, hardworking, fast

## 10. Ejercicios prácticos (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige el nivel

1. I can sing \_\_\_\_. (muy bien)  
2. She cooks \_\_\_\_. (bastante bien)  
3. He can't dance \_\_\_\_. (nada)  
4. I play the guitar \_\_\_\_. (bastante bien)  
5. They don't sing \_\_\_\_. (bien)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **very well**  
2. **quite well**  
3. **at all**  
4. **quite well**  
5. **well**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Ordena

1. very well / I / can / sing  
2. quite well / she / cooks  
3. at all / he / can't / dance  
4. drawing / she / is / good at  
5. fast / he / can / run / very

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **I can sing very well.**  
2. **She cooks quite well.**  
3. **He can't dance at all.**  
4. **She is good at drawing.**  
5. **He can run very fast.**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Traduce

1. Puedo cantar muy bien.  
2. Cocina bastante bien.  
3. No sé dibujar nada.  
4. Es creativo.  
5. Es trabajadora y habla inglés muy bien.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **I can sing very well.**  
2. **She/He cooks quite well.**  
3. **I can't draw at all.**  
4. **He/She is creative.**  
5. **She is hardworking and she speaks English very well.**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — *Good at* o *very well*

1. She is good at \_\_\_\_. (draw)  
2. She can draw \_\_\_\_.  
3. He is good at \_\_\_\_. (cook)  
4. He can cook \_\_\_\_.  
5. I'm bad at \_\_\_\_. (dance)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **drawing**  
2. **very well** / **quite well**  
3. **cooking**  
4. **very well** / **quite well**  
5. **dancing**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Reading corto

Lee el texto de Lisa (sección 7). Contesta:

1. How well can Lisa sing?  
2. How well does she play the piano?  
3. Who is creative?  
4. Can Lisa draw?  
5. How well does her sister speak English?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Very well**  
2. **Quite well**  
3. **Her brother**  
4. **No — not at all**  
5. **Very well**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Une con conectores (U34)

1. I can sing very well. I play the piano quite well. → …  
2. My brother can cook very well. He is creative. → …  
3. I can't draw. (nada) → …

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **I can sing very well and I play the piano quite well.**  
2. **My brother can cook very well — he is creative.** / *…because he is creative.*  
3. **I can't draw at all.**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Escribe sobre ti

Escribe 8–10 frases. Incluye:

- *very well*, *quite well*, *not at all*  
- 1 adjetivo (*creative / hardworking / fast*)  
- 1 × *good at + -ing*  
- 1 pregunta *How well…?*

**Modelo:** el texto de Lisa (sección 7).

### Ejercicio 8 — Elige la opción correcta

1. I can sing \_\_\_\_. (very well / well very / at all)  
2. He can't dance \_\_\_\_. (very well / at all / quite)  
3. She is good at \_\_\_\_. (draw / drawing / draws)  
4. How well \_\_\_\_ you cook? (can / cans / are)  
5. She cooks \_\_\_\_. (quite well / well quite / quite)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **very well**  
2. **at all**  
3. **drawing**  
4. **can**  
5. **quite well**
</details>

## 11. Plan de práctica (15 minutos)

1. **3 min** — Escala en voz alta: very well → quite well → not at all.  
2. **3 min** — 6 frases con *can* + grado.  
3. **4 min** — Reescribe el texto de Lisa.  
4. **5 min** — Entrevista *How well can you…?* × 6.

### Checklist

- [ ] Distingo very well / quite well / not at all  
- [ ] Los pongo después del verbo  
- [ ] Uso *can't … at all*  
- [ ] Uso *good at + -ing*  
- [ ] Describo con creative / hardworking

---

## 12. Consejos para practicar

1. **Tabla personal:** 3 skills *very well*, 3 *quite well*, 3 *not at all*.  
2. **Pareja:** *How well can you…?* + respuesta corta.  
3. **Combina** U36: *I play the guitar quite well.*  
4. Prepárate para la [Unidad 38](/curso-a1/unit-38): *Asking for Permission* (*Can I…? / May I…?*).  
5. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 37](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-37-talent-skills-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 37 del curso A1](/curso-a1/unit-37).
