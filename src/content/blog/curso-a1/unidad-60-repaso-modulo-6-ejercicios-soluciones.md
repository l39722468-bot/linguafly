## Lección 1 — Gramática: repaso

**Objetivo:** *some/any*, *much/many*, *too/enough*, *would like*, *First/Then/Finally*, *should*, *pay for / spend on*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I have ____ apples. → *some / any / much*  
2. I don't have ____ milk. → *any / some / many*  
3. How ____ water do you want? → *much / many / some*  
4. How ____ apples do you need? → *many / much / any*  
5. This shirt is ____ small. → *too / enough / some*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **some** — afirmativa.  
2. **any** — negativa.  
3. **much** — incontable.  
4. **many** — contable.  
5. **too** — *too* + adjetivo.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. It isn't big ____. → *enough / too / some*  
7. I would ____ the chicken please. → *like / likes / liking*  
8. ____ boil the water. Then add the pasta. → *First / Finally / Any*  
9. You ____ rest when you are ill. → *should / shouldn't / to*  
10. I paid ____ dinner with cash. → *for / on / to*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **enough** — adj. + *enough*.  
7. **like** — *I would like*.  
8. **First**  
9. **should** + verbo base.  
10. **for** — *pay for*.

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige / corrige

11. Correct: ____. → a) I spend money on food · b) I spend money for food · c) I pay on food  
12. Correct: ____. → a) You should rest · b) You should to rest · c) You shoulds rest  
13. Correct order: ____. → a) First, Then, Finally · b) Finally, First, Then · c) Then, Finally, First  
14. *I have any milk* (afirmativa) → corrige  
15. *How many water?* → corrige

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) I spend money on food**  
12. **a) You should rest**  
13. **a) First, Then, Finally**  
14. I have **some** milk.  
15. How **much** water?

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: repaso

**Objetivo:** supermercado, ropa, restaurante, cocina, salud, dinero.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja

1. aisle  
2. changing room  
3. bill  
4. pharmacy  
5. receipt  

a) place to try clothes · b) supermarket corridor · c) restaurant account · d) proof of payment · e) place to buy medicine

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **c** · 4 → **e** · 5 → **d**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. Apples are ____. → *countable / uncountable / too*  
7. Milk is ____. → *uncountable / countable / receipt*  
8. Starter comes ____ the main course. → *before / after / never*  
9. Fry in a ____. Bake in the ____. → *pan / oven · pot / pharmacy*  
10. Cash or ____ card? → *credit / fever / boil*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **countable**  
7. **uncountable**  
8. **before**  
9. **pan** · **oven**  
10. **credit**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Headache* = ____. → a) pain in your head · b) a receipt · c) a recipe  
12. *Change* (in a shop) = ____. → a) money you get back · b) a cold · c) an aisle  
13. *Ingredients* = ____. → a) what you need for a recipe · b) the bill · c) size M  
14. *Dessert* = ____. → a) sweet course at the end · b) starter · c) medicine  
15. *Sale* = ____. → a) reduced prices · b) pharmacy · c) fever

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) pain in your head**  
12. **a) money you get back**  
13. **a) what you need for a recipe**  
14. **a) sweet course at the end**  
15. **a) reduced prices**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Last week at the supermarket

**Objetivo:** leer un texto que une el Módulo 6.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-60/reading-review.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Last week at the supermarket"></audio>

> Last week I went to the supermarket and bought some apples, some milk and bread. I don't have much money this month so I didn't buy any dessert. On Saturday I cooked pasta. First I boiled the water. Then I added the pasta. Finally I served it with sauce. I feel a bit ill today — I have a headache. I should rest and take some medicine.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Where did the person go last week? → a) The supermarket · b) The cinema · c) The airport  
2. What did they buy? → a) Apples, milk and bread · b) Only clothes · c) Only medicine  
3. Why didn't they buy dessert? → a) Not much money · b) They hate dessert · c) The shop was closed  
4. What did they cook on Saturday? → a) Pasta · b) Cake only · c) Nothing  
5. How do they feel today? → a) A bit ill / headache · b) Great · c) Hungry only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) The supermarket**  
2. **a) Apples, milk and bread**  
3. **a) Not much money**  
4. **a) Pasta**  
5. **a) A bit ill / headache**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. *Some* appears with ____. → a) apples and milk · b) only dessert · c) only headache  
7. *Any* appears with ____. → a) dessert (negative) · b) apples (affirmative) · c) pasta only  
8. Recipe order uses ____. → a) First, Then, Finally · b) only Finally · c) only should  
9. Advice uses ____. → a) should rest · b) pay for · c) too small  
10. The main topic is ____. → a) a shopping/cooking/health day · b) only weather · c) only travel

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) apples and milk**  
7. **a) dessert (negative)**  
8. **a) First, Then, Finally**  
9. **a) should rest**  
10. **a) a shopping/cooking/health day**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Much money* is correct because *money* is ____. → a) uncountable · b) countable · c) a verb  
12. *Didn't buy any dessert* uses ____. → a) any in negative · b) some in negative · c) many water  
13. *Boiled* is past of ____. → a) boil · b) bake · c) cut  
14. *I have a headache* needs ____. → a) *a* · b) no article · c) *any*  
15. *Take some medicine* uses ____. → a) some · b) any · c) too

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) uncountable**  
12. **a) any in negative**  
13. **a) boil**  
14. **a) *a***  
15. **a) some**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Review dialogue

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo que une restaurante, dinero y salud.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-60/listening-review.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Review dialogue"></audio>

> **A:** I would like the chicken for main course please.  
> **B:** Anything else?  
> **A:** Can I have the bill please? I paid for dinner with cash yesterday but today I only have credit card.  
> **B:** No problem. Here is your receipt.  
> **A:** I feel a bit ill — headache.  
> **B:** You should rest and take medicine.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What does A order? → a) Chicken for main course · b) Only dessert · c) Only water  
2. What does A ask for? → a) The bill · b) A changing room · c) An aisle  
3. How did A pay yesterday? → a) With cash · b) Only with card · c) Not at all  
4. How does A pay today? → a) Credit card · b) Only cash · c) Nothing  
5. What advice does B give? → a) Rest and take medicine · b) Go to work · c) Fry onions

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Chicken for main course**  
2. **a) The bill**  
3. **a) With cash**  
4. **a) Credit card**  
5. **a) Rest and take medicine**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. B gives A a ____. → a) receipt · b) fever · c) pan  
7. A has a ____. → a) headache · b) recipe · c) sale  
8. *I would like* is ____. → a) a polite order · b) a past tense · c) a superlative  
9. *Paid for dinner* uses ____. → a) pay for · b) spend on · c) too enough  
10. The dialogue reviews ____. → a) restaurant + money + health · b) only weather · c) only packing

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) receipt**  
7. **a) headache**  
8. **a) a polite order**  
9. **a) pay for**  
10. **a) restaurant + money + health**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Can I have the bill?* is ____. → a) a polite request · b) a recipe step · c) a size  
12. *Should* + ____. → a) base verb · b) *to* + verb · c) *-ing* only  
13. *Main course* = ____. → a) the main dish · b) starter · c) receipt  
14. *No problem* means ____. → a) it's OK · b) never · c) too small  
15. The listening mixes Module 6 topics: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Unit 41

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) a polite request**  
12. **a) base verb**  
13. **a) the main dish**  
14. **a) it's OK**  
15. **a) yes**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My shopping day

**Objetivo:** escribir un día que una el Módulo 6.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I bought ____ apples. → *some / any / much*  
2. I don't have ____ dessert. → *any / some / many*  
3. This shirt is ____ big. → *too / enough / some*  
4. ____ boil water. Then cook the pasta. → *First / Finally / Any*  
5. I paid ____ the dinner with cash. → *for / on / to*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **some**  
2. **any**  
3. **too**  
4. **First**  
5. **for**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. I / would like / the chicken / please.  
7. You / should / rest.  
8. Can I / have / the receipt / please?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I would like the chicken please.  
7. You should rest.  
8. Can I have the receipt please?

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Compra con *some* y negativa con *any*.  
10. Pregunta *how much* y *how many*.  
11. Ropa con *too* / *enough*.  
12. Pedido educado + la cuenta.  
13. Receta corta con *First / Then / Finally*.  
14. Consejo con *should / shouldn't*.  
15. Párrafo (6–8 frases) que una supermercado + cocina + salud o pago.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I bought some apples. I didn't buy any dessert.  
10. How much milk? How many apples?  
11. This shirt is too small. It isn't big enough.  
12. I would like the chicken please. Can I have the bill please?  
13. First boil the water. Then add the pasta. Finally serve.  
14. You should rest. You shouldn't go to work.  
15. Last week I went to the supermarket. I bought some milk and bread. On Saturday I cooked pasta. First I boiled water. Then I added the pasta. I feel ill today. I should rest. Yesterday I paid for dinner with cash.

</details>
