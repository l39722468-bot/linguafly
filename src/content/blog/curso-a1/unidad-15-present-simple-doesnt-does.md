En la [Unidad 14](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-14-present-simple-dont-do-you) aprendiste *don't* y *Do you…?* con **I, you, we, they**. En la **Unidad 15 del curso A1** (*Present Simple: He, She, It*) pasas a la **3.ª persona**: *doesn't* y *Does he/she/it…?*. Es el complemento natural de la unidad anterior.

Esta guía cubre la **negativa**, las **preguntas**, las **respuestas cortas**, la **ortografía** (*goes, watches, studies, has*) y vocabulario de **animales** y **rutinas profesionales**. Incluye **audios**, **esquemas** y **ejercicios** (más de 2000 palabras).

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 15 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Formar la **negativa** con *doesn't* (*He doesn't like…*).
- Hacer **preguntas** con *Does he / she / it…?*.
- Responder con ***Yes, he does*** / ***No, she doesn't***.
- Aplicar la **-s / -es / -ies** en afirmativa (y quitarla tras *does/doesn't*).
- Hablar de **hábitos de animales** y **rutinas de trabajo**.

### Tips de uso

- Con **he/she/it** el verbo de la afirmativa lleva **-s** (o **-es / -ies**).  
- **have** → **has** (irregular en 3.ª persona).  
- Animales = *it*: *The dog barks. It doesn't like cats.*  
- Nombres propios = he/she: *Maria works. Does Maria cook?*

---

## 2. Negativa: *doesn't*

Con **he / she / it** la negativa se forma así:

```
Sujeto + doesn't + verbo (forma base)
```

| Afirmativa | Negativa |
| :--- | :--- |
| He likes coffee. | He **doesn't** like coffee. |
| She studies English. | She **doesn't** study English. |
| It eats meat. | It **doesn't** eat meat. |
| She goes to work at nine. | She **doesn't** go to work at nine. |
| He plays football. | He **doesn't** play football. |

**Regla clave:** tras *doesn't* el verbo **pierde la -s**. No digas *He doesn't likes*.

### *don't* vs *doesn't* (mapa rápido)

| Sujeto | Negativa |
| :--- | :--- |
| I / you / we / they | **don't** + verbo |
| he / she / it | **doesn't** + verbo |

> I don't like coffee.  
> He **doesn't** like coffee.  
> We don't watch TV.  
> She **doesn't** watch TV.

## 3. Preguntas: *Does he / she / it…?*

```
Does + he/she/it + verbo (forma base)…?
```

| Pregunta | Significado |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Does** he play football? | ¿Juega al fútbol? |
| **Does** she cook? | ¿Cocina? |
| **Does** the cat like milk? | ¿Al gato le gusta la leche? |
| **Does** Maria work on Sunday? | ¿María trabaja el domingo? |
| **Does** your brother study? | ¿Tu hermano estudia? |

Tras *Does* el verbo va **sin -s**: *Does she like…?* (no *likes*).

### *Do* vs *Does*

| Sujeto | Pregunta |
| :--- | :--- |
| I / you / we / they | **Do** + sujeto + verbo…? |
| he / she / it | **Does** + sujeto + verbo…? |

> Do you cook?  
> **Does** she cook?  
> Do they like music?  
> **Does** he like music?

## 4. Respuestas cortas

| Pregunta | Sí | No |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Does he play football? | Yes, he **does**. | No, he **doesn't**. |
| Does she cook? | Yes, she **does**. | No, she **doesn't**. |
| Does it like milk? | Yes, it **does**. | No, it **doesn't**. |

No respondas solo *Yes* o *Yes, she likes*. En A1 la respuesta corta estándar es *Yes, she does* / *No, she doesn't*.

## 5. Ortografía de la 3.ª persona (afirmativa)

En afirmativa, he/she/it añaden terminación. En negativa y pregunta **esa -s desaparece** porque ya está en *does/doesn't*.

| Regla | Infinitivo → 3.ª pers. | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| + **-s** (la mayoría) | work → works, like → likes | He works. |
| + **-es** (-ch, -sh, -ss, -x, -o) | watch → watches, go → goes | She watches TV. She goes. |
| **-y** tras consonante → **-ies** | study → studies, fly → flies | He studies. The bird flies. |
| Irregular | have → **has** | She has breakfast. |

### Triple salto (muy útil)

| Afirmativa | Negativa | Pregunta |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| She likes tea. | She doesn't like tea. | Does she like tea? |
| He goes to work. | He doesn't go to work. | Does he go to work? |
| It eats meat. | It doesn't eat meat. | Does it eat meat? |
| She watches TV. | She doesn't watch TV. | Does she watch TV? |
| He studies English. | He doesn't study English. | Does he study English? |

## 6. Más ejemplos en contexto

### Personas y trabajo

> My sister is a nurse. She **wakes up** at six. She **goes** to the hospital. She **helps** patients. She **doesn't** work on Sunday.  
> **Does** she work on Saturday? — Yes, she **does**.

> Tom is an engineer. He **writes** reports every week. He **doesn't** teach.  
> **Does** he design buildings? — Yes, he **does**.

### Animales

> The dog **barks** when it sees a stranger. It **doesn't** meow.  
> The cat **meows**. **Does** the cat like milk? — Yes, it **does**.  
> The cow **doesn't** eat meat. It **eats** grass.  
> The bird **flies**. The fish **swims**. The owl **hunts** at night.

### Comparación U14 vs U15

> I don't like coffee. / He **doesn't** like coffee.  
> Do you cook? / **Does** she cook?  
> Yes, I do. / Yes, she **does**.  
> No, I don't. / No, he **doesn't**.

## 7. Errores típicos de hispanohablantes

| ❌ Incorrecto | ✅ Correcto | Explicación |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *He don't like coffee* | *He **doesn't** like coffee* | he → *doesn't* |
| *She doesn't likes tea* | *She doesn't **like** tea* | Sin -s tras *doesn't* |
| *Does she likes music?* | *Does she **like** music?* | Forma base tras *Does* |
| *Do he play football?* | ***Does** he play football?* | he → *Does* |
| *Yes, she like* | *Yes, she **does*** | Respuesta corta con *does* |
| *He go to work* | *He **goes** to work* | Afirmativa: -s/-es |
| *She study English* | *She **studies** English* | study → studies |

---

## 8. Pronunciación de *doesn't* y *Does he*

| Forma | Tip A1 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **doesn't** | Dos sílabas: *DUZ-nt*. |
| **Does he** | *Does* suena como *duz*. |
| **Yes, he does** | Acentúa *does*. |
| **No, she doesn't** | Acentúa *doesn't*. |

Practica en voz alta: *Does she cook?* → *No, she doesn't.* Empieza despacio y luego un poco más rápido, sin saltarte el auxiliar. Escucha los audios del artículo y repite dos veces cada frase.

### Ortografía vs sonido (nota rápida)

En afirmativa, la *-s* de *likes / goes / watches* a veces suena /s/, /z/ o /ɪz/. No hace falta memorizar todas las reglas aún: imita el audio. Lo importante en esta unidad es **cuándo** usas *-s* (afirmativa he/she/it) y **cuándo no** (tras *does/doesn't*).

## 8b. Mini-diálogos para memorizar

> **A:** Does he like coffee?  
> **B:** No, he doesn't. He prefers tea.  
> **A:** Does he cook?  
> **B:** Yes, he does. In the evening he cooks dinner.

> **A:** Does your sister work in a hospital?  
> **B:** Yes, she does. She is a nurse.  
> **A:** Does she work on Sunday?  
> **B:** No, she doesn't.

> **A:** Does the cat like milk?  
> **B:** Yes, it does.  
> **A:** Does the dog like cats?  
> **B:** No, it doesn't. It barks a lot!

Cambia el verbo y el sujeto: *Does Maria…? Does your brother…? Does the bird…?*

## 8c. Tabla resumen U14 + U15

| | I / you / we / they | he / she / it |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Afirmativa | I like tea. | He like**s** tea. |
| Negativa | I **don't** like tea. | He **doesn't** like tea. |
| Pregunta | **Do** you like tea? | **Does** he like tea? |
| Sí | Yes, I **do**. | Yes, he **does**. |
| No | No, I **don't**. | No, he **doesn't**. |

Cuando dudes, pregunta: ¿el sujeto es *he/she/it*? → *does / doesn't* + verbo base. Si no → *do / don't*.

### Frases para copiar y personalizar

1. He doesn't like \_\_\_\_.  
2. She doesn't \_\_\_\_ on Sunday.  
3. Does he \_\_\_\_? — Yes, he does.  
4. Does she \_\_\_\_? — No, she doesn't.  
5. The dog barks. It doesn't \_\_\_\_.  
6. My sister wakes up at \_\_\_\_. She goes to \_\_\_\_.

Rellena con tu vida real: así el Present Simple deja de ser “teoría” y se convierte en frases que sí dirías.

### Ejercicio 6 — Escribe sobre alguien

Escribe 5–7 frases sobre un amigo, familiar o mascota: qué hace, qué no hace, y 2 preguntas con *Does…?*.

## 11. Plan de práctica (15 minutos)

1. **3 min** — Repite: *He doesn't… / She doesn't… / It doesn't…*  
2. **4 min** — Haz 6 preguntas *Does he/she/it…?* y respóndelas.  
3. **4 min** — Escucha los audios y repite.  
4. **4 min** — Escribe el “triple salto” con 4 verbos (*like, go, watch, study*).

### Checklist

- [ ] Sé formar *doesn't* + verbo base.  
- [ ] Sé preguntar con *Does he/she/it…?*.  
- [ ] Uso *Yes, he does / No, she doesn't*.  
- [ ] No mezclo *don't* con *he/she/it*.  
- [ ] Tras *does/doesn't* no añado -s al verbo.

---

## 12. Consejos para practicar

1. **Elige una persona** (hermano, compañero) y escribe 5 hábitos con *-s* y 3 con *doesn't*.  
2. **Pregunta sobre mascotas:** *Does your dog bark? Does your cat sleep a lot?*  
3. **Contrasta U14 y U15:** *I don't…* vs *He doesn't…*; *Do you…?* vs *Does she…?*.  
4. Repasa la [Unidad 14](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-14-present-simple-dont-do-you) si dudas con *don't/Do*.  
5. Practica en la [Unidad 15 del curso A1](/curso-a1/unit-15).
