## Lección 1 — Gramática: pay for / spend on

**Objetivo:** *pay for*, *spend on*, *paid*, *spent*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I paid ____ the dinner with cash. → *for / on / to*  
2. She spends money ____ clothes. → *on / for / at*  
3. We ____ too much on food last month. → *spent / spend / paid*  
4. Yesterday I ____ for the book. → *paid / payed / spent*  
5. Can I ____ for this with credit card? → *pay / spend / change*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **for** — *pay for* + cosa.  
2. **on** — *spend on* + categoría.  
3. **spent** — pasado de *spend*.  
4. **paid** — pasado de *pay* (no *payed*).  
5. **pay**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *Pay for* = ____. → a) pay for a specific thing · b) spend in a category · c) get change only  
7. *Spend on* = ____. → a) use money on a category · b) only ask for receipt · c) cook  
8. Past of *pay* = ____. → a) paid · b) payed · c) paying  
9. Past of *spend* = ____. → a) spent · b) spended · c) spend  
10. *I paid on the coffee* is ____. → a) wrong (*pay for*) · b) correct · c) better

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) pay for a specific thing**  
7. **a) use money on a category**  
8. **a) paid**  
9. **a) spent**  
10. **a) wrong (*pay for*)**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I paid on the coffee.*  
12. *She spends money for books.*  
13. *I payed for dinner.*  
14. *We spended too much.*  
15. *Can I to pay with card?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I paid **for** the coffee.  
12. She spends money **on** books.  
13. I **paid** for dinner.  
14. We **spent** too much.  
15. Can I **pay** with card?

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: dinero y caja

**Objetivo:** *cash, credit card, change, receipt, pounds*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja

1. cash  
2. credit card  
3. change  
4. receipt  
5. pounds  

a) paper proof of payment · b) money back when you pay more · c) plastic card to pay · d) British money · e) notes and coins

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **e** · 2 → **c** · 3 → **b** · 4 → **a** · 5 → **d**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. I prefer to pay with ____. → *cash / receipt / spend*  
7. Can I pay with ____ card? → *credit / change / boil*  
8. Here is your ____ — ten pounds. → *change / recipe / fever*  
9. Can I have the ____ please? → *receipt / pan / cold*  
10. That is twenty ____ please. → *pounds / pharmacies / ovens*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **cash**  
7. **credit**  
8. **change**  
9. **receipt**  
10. **pounds**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Change* in a shop = ____. → a) money you get back · b) clothes size · c) a recipe  
12. *Receipt* = ____. → a) proof of payment · b) medicine · c) a starter  
13. *Cash or card?* asks about ____. → a) payment method · b) food · c) health  
14. *Shop assistant* = ____. → a) person who works in a shop · b) doctor · c) chef  
15. *Note* (twenty-pound note) = ____. → a) paper money · b) a recipe · c) a cold

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) money you get back**  
12. **a) proof of payment**  
13. **a) payment method**  
14. **a) person who works in a shop**  
15. **a) paper money**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: When I go shopping

**Objetivo:** leer un texto sobre compras y pago.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-59/reading-money.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: When I go shopping"></audio>

> When I go shopping I usually take both cash and my credit card. Yesterday I bought a book for ten pounds. I paid for it with cash. The total was ten pounds so I gave a twenty-pound note. The shop assistant gave me my change — ten pounds back — and a receipt. I spend a lot of money on books every month.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What did the person buy yesterday? → a) A book · b) Clothes · c) Medicine  
2. How much was the book? → a) Ten pounds · b) Twenty pounds · c) Thirty pounds  
3. How did they pay? → a) With cash · b) Only with credit card · c) They didn't pay  
4. How much change did they get? → a) Ten pounds · b) Twenty pounds · c) No change  
5. What do they spend money on every month? → a) Books · b) Cars · c) Only food

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) A book**  
2. **a) Ten pounds**  
3. **a) With cash**  
4. **a) Ten pounds**  
5. **a) Books**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. They usually take ____. → a) cash and credit card · b) only receipts · c) only change  
7. They gave a ____ note. → a) twenty-pound · b) five-pound · c) fifty-pound  
8. The shop assistant gave ____. → a) change and a receipt · b) only medicine · c) pasta  
9. *Paid for* appears with ____. → a) the book / it · b) clothes only · c) fever  
10. The main topic is ____. → a) shopping and paying · b) cooking · c) health advice

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) cash and credit card**  
7. **a) twenty-pound**  
8. **a) change and a receipt**  
9. **a) the book / it**  
10. **a) shopping and paying**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *I paid for it* uses ____. → a) *pay for* in the past · b) *spend on* · c) *should*  
12. *I spend … on books* uses ____. → a) *spend on* · b) *pay for* only · c) *too*  
13. *Change* here means ____. → a) money back · b) clothes · c) recipe  
14. *Receipt* means ____. → a) proof of payment · b) headache · c) starter  
15. Past of *buy* in the text idea = ____. → a) bought · b) buyed · c) buys

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) *pay for* in the past**  
12. **a) *spend on***  
13. **a) money back**  
14. **a) proof of payment**  
15. **a) bought**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: That is twenty pounds please

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo en la caja.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-59/listening-money.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: That is twenty pounds please"></audio>

> **Shop assistant:** That is twenty pounds please.  
> **Customer:** Can I pay for this with credit card?  
> **Shop assistant:** Yes of course.  
> **Customer:** I prefer to pay with cash. Here you are.  
> **Shop assistant:** You gave me thirty pounds. Here is your change — ten pounds — and your receipt.  
> **Customer:** Thank you.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. How much is the total? → a) Twenty pounds · b) Ten pounds · c) Thirty pounds  
2. Does the shop take credit card? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The dialogue doesn't say  
3. How does the customer prefer to pay? → a) With cash · b) Only with card · c) Not at all  
4. How much did the customer give? → a) Thirty pounds · b) Twenty pounds · c) Ten pounds  
5. How much change does the customer get? → a) Ten pounds · b) Twenty pounds · c) Thirty pounds

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Twenty pounds**  
2. **a) Yes**  
3. **a) With cash**  
4. **a) Thirty pounds**  
5. **a) Ten pounds**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The customer also gets a ____. → a) receipt · b) fever · c) recipe  
7. *Pay for this* means ____. → a) pay for this thing · b) spend on clothes in general · c) rest  
8. The dialogue is at ____. → a) a shop checkout · b) a hospital · c) a kitchen  
9. *Here you are* means ____. → a) giving the money · b) saying goodbye forever · c) ordering food  
10. *Yes of course* means ____. → a) yes, no problem · b) no · c) maybe later

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) receipt**  
7. **a) pay for this thing**  
8. **a) a shop checkout**  
9. **a) giving the money**  
10. **a) yes, no problem**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Can I pay* uses ____. → a) base verb after *can* · b) *to pay* · c) *paying* only  
12. *Change* and *receipt* are ____. → a) different things · b) the same word · c) cooking verbs  
13. *Credit card* is a ____. → a) payment method · b) symptom · c) sequencer  
14. *Prefer to pay with cash* = ____. → a) like cash more · b) only use card · c) need medicine  
15. The dialogue practices ____. → a) paying, change, receipt · b) First/Then/Finally · c) should/shouldn't only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) base verb after *can***  
12. **a) different things**  
13. **a) payment method**  
14. **a) like cash more**  
15. **a) paying, change, receipt**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My purchase

**Objetivo:** escribir sobre una compra con *pay for / spend on*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I paid ____ the book with cash. → *for / on / to*  
2. I spend money ____ books. → *on / for / at*  
3. Here is your ____. → *change / fever / pan*  
4. Can I have the ____ please? → *receipt / cold / oven*  
5. Can I pay with ____ card? → *credit / boil / cut*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **for**  
2. **on**  
3. **change**  
4. **receipt**  
5. **credit**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. I / paid / for / the dinner / with cash.  
7. She / spends / money / on / clothes.  
8. Can I / have / the receipt / please?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I paid for the dinner with cash.  
7. She spends money on clothes.  
8. Can I have the receipt please?

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Pagué la cena en efectivo.  
10. Gasto mucho dinero en ropa.  
11. ¿Puedo pagar con tarjeta?  
12. Aquí tienes el cambio y el recibo.  
13. Párrafo corto (4–6 frases): compra + *paid for* + *spend on* + *change/receipt*.  
14. Usa *cash* y *credit card* en dos frases.  
15. Pregunta el recibo de forma educada.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I paid for the dinner with cash.  
10. I spend a lot of money on clothes.  
11. Can I pay with credit card? / Can I pay for this with credit card?  
12. Here is your change and your receipt.  
13. Yesterday I bought a book. I paid for it with cash. I gave twenty pounds. I got ten pounds change and a receipt. I spend money on books every month.  
14. I prefer cash. Sometimes I use my credit card.  
15. Can I have the receipt please?

</details>
