## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 26 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Usar imperativos: ***Turn left / right***, ***Go straight***, ***Past the…***.
- Situar el destino: ***on the left / on the right***.
- Preguntar: ***How do I get to…?*** / ***Where is…?***.
- Usar puntos de referencia: *corner, traffic lights, bank, supermarket…*.
- Cerrar con ***You can’t miss it*** y cortesía (*Thanks / You’re welcome*).

---

## 1. El imperativo para direcciones

En indicaciones de calle usas la **forma base** del verbo **sin sujeto**:

```
Turn left.
Go straight.
Past the bank.
Cross the street.
```

No hace falta *you*: *Turn left* (no *You turn left*) es lo natural en A1 para dar el camino.

| Imperativo | Significado | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Turn left** | Gira a la izquierda | Turn left at the corner. |
| **Turn right** | Gira a la derecha | Turn right at the traffic lights. |
| **Go straight** | Sigue recto | Go straight for 100 metres. |
| **Past the…** | Pasa el/la… | Past the bank and then turn right. |
| **Cross** | Cruza | Cross the street. |

### Tip

*Past the bank* = *go past the bank* (pasa por delante). En el curso aparece mucho la forma corta ***Past the…***.

## 2. *Turn left / Turn right*

| Frase | Uso |
| :--- | :--- |
| Turn left **at the corner** | Gira a la izquierda **en la esquina**. |
| Turn right **at the traffic lights** | Gira a la derecha **en el semáforo**. |
| Turn left **at the bank** | Gira a la izquierda **en el banco** (punto de referencia). |

> Turn left at the corner.  
> Turn right at the traffic lights.  
> Go straight and turn right at the bank.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-26/go-straight-turn-right.mp3" title="🔊 Go straight and turn right at the bank"></audio>

### No digas

| ❌ | ✅ |
| :--- | :--- |
| *Turn to the left* (demasiado largo en A1 calle) | *Turn left* |
| *Turn in left* | *Turn left* |
| *Turn left in the corner* | *Turn left **at** the corner* |

---

## 3. *Go straight*

*Go straight* = sigue en la misma dirección (no gires).

> Go straight for 100 metres.  
> Go straight for about 200 metres.  
> Go straight along this street.  
> Go straight past the bank.  
> Go straight out of the station.

### Combinaciones útiles

| Estructura | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| Go straight **for** + distancia | Go straight for 200 metres. |
| Go straight **along** + calle | Go straight along this street. |
| Go straight **past** + lugar | Go straight past the supermarket. |
| Go straight **out of** + lugar | Go straight out of the station. |

---

## 4. *Past the…* y puntos de referencia

*Past the bank* = pasa el banco (no entres; continúa).

> Past the bank and then turn right.  
> Past the supermarket and the cinema is on the right.  
> Past the bank on your right.  
> Go straight past the supermarket.

### Vocabulario de calle

| Inglés | Español |
| :--- | :--- |
| corner | esquina |
| traffic lights | semáforo |
| crossing | cruce / paso |
| street | calle |
| station | estación |
| on the left | a la izquierda |
| on the right | a la derecha |
| on your left / right | a tu izquierda / derecha |

Más lugares que ya conoces: *bank, supermarket, cinema, library, pharmacy, park*.

## 5. *On the left / On the right*

Al final de las indicaciones **localizas** el destino:

> The pharmacy is **on the left**.  
> The cinema is **on the right**.  
> It’s on the left, next to the pharmacy.  
> Past the bank **on your right**.

### Con U24

| U24 | U26 |
| :--- | :--- |
| The library is **next to** the pharmacy. | Go straight… The library is **on the left**, next to the pharmacy. |
| The cinema is **opposite** the park. | Past the supermarket… The cinema is **on the right**. |

Primero **mueves** a la persona (*Turn / Go / Past*); luego **anclas** el lugar (*on the left / next to…*).

## 7. Frases de cierre y cortesía

| Frase | Significado |
| :--- | :--- |
| **You can’t miss it.** | No te puedes perder / es fácil de ver. |
| **Thanks!** / **Thank you.** | Gracias. |
| **You’re welcome.** | De nada. |
| **Excuse me.** | Perdona / Disculpa (para preguntar). |

Úsalas al final: dan naturalidad al diálogo de calle.

---

## 8. Errores típicos de hispanohablantes

| ❌ Incorrecto | ✅ Correcto | Explicación |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *You turn left at the corner* (demasiado “frase completa”) | *Turn left at the corner* | Imperativo sin sujeto |
| *Turn left in the corner* | *Turn left **at** the corner* | *at* + esquina/semáforo |
| *Go to straight* | *Go straight* | Sin *to* |
| *Pass the bank* (calco) | *Past the bank* / *Go past the bank* | Forma del curso |
| *The cinema is in the right* | *The cinema is **on** the right* | *on the left/right* |
| *How I get to the library?* | *How **do** I get to the library?* | Auxiliar *do* |
| *Turn to left* | *Turn left* | Sin *to* |

---

## 9. Pronunciación útil

| Forma | Tip A1 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Turn left / right** | *tern left* · *right* con /r/ suave o fuerte según acento. |
| **Go straight** | *streit* (el grupo *str-*). |
| **Past the** | *past thə* (the débil). |
| **traffic lights** | acento en *TRA-* · *lights* = /laɪts/. |
| **corner** | *KOR-ner*. |
| **You can’t miss it** | *kant* (UK) / *kænt* (US) · *miss it* enlazado. |

Practica la secuencia: *Go straight → Turn left → Past the… → It’s on the right.*

## 9b. Mini-diálogos para memorizar

> **A:** Excuse me. How do I get to the park?  
> **B:** Go straight. Turn left at the corner. The park is on the right.  
> **A:** Thanks!  
> **B:** You’re welcome.

> **A:** Where is the pharmacy?  
> **B:** Go straight past the supermarket. The pharmacy is on the left, next to the library. You can’t miss it.

> **A:** How do I get to the library from the station?  
> **B:** Go straight out of the station. Go straight for about 200 metres. Turn left at the corner. Past the bank on your right. The library is on the left.

Cambia solo el destino: fijas el patrón de imperativos.

## 9c. Plantillas para copiar

1. Turn left / right at the \_\_\_\_.  
2. Go straight for \_\_\_\_ metres.  
3. Go straight along this street.  
4. Past the \_\_\_\_ and then turn \_\_\_\_.  
5. The \_\_\_\_ is on the left / right.  
6. How do I get to the \_\_\_\_?  
7. It’s on the left, next to the \_\_\_\_.  
8. You can’t miss it.

### Más frases para memorizar

> Turn left at the corner.  
> Turn right at the traffic lights.  
> Go straight for 100 metres.  
> Go straight along this street.  
> Go straight past the bank.  
> Past the bank and then turn right.  
> Past the supermarket and the cinema is on the right.  
> The pharmacy is on the left.  
> The cinema is on the right.  
> It’s on the left, next to the pharmacy.  
> How do I get to the library?  
> How do I get to the cinema?  
> Cross the street and the bank is there.  
> You can’t miss it.  
> Thanks! You’re welcome.

Lee la lista dos veces. Luego da **4 pasos** para ir de tu casa a un lugar cercano.

### Rutina de 2 minutos

1. Di 2 × *Turn left/right at…*.  
2. Di 2 × *Go straight…*.  
3. Di 1 × *Past the…* + 1 × *on the left/right*.  
4. Pregunta y responde: *How do I get to…?*

Si lo haces con un mapa dibujado, el orden de los pasos se vuelve automático.

### Tour guiado (modelo estación → biblioteca)

> Go straight out of the station. Go straight for about 200 metres. Turn left at the corner. Past the bank on your right. The library is on the left, next to the pharmacy. You can’t miss it.

Grábate leyéndolo. Luego cambia el destino a *cinema* o *pharmacy* sin mirar el texto.

### Frases extra para practicar en voz alta

> Excuse me. How do I get to the supermarket?  
> Go straight along this street.  
> Turn right at the traffic lights.  
> Past the bank and the supermarket is on the left.  
> Where is the cinema?  
> Go straight. Turn left at the corner. Past the supermarket. The cinema is on the right.  
> Cross the street.  
> Thanks! You’re welcome.

Personaliza con **tu** calle. Cuanto más concreto el punto de referencia, más claras las indicaciones.

### Checklist mental

- Imperativo = forma base, sin *you*  
- Girar → *Turn left / right* + *at the…*  
- Recto → *Go straight* (+ *for / along / past*)  
- Pasar → *Past the…*  
- Destino → *on the left / on the right*  
- Pregunta → *How do I get to…?*  
- Cierre → *You can’t miss it* · *Thanks / You’re welcome*

### Secuencia típica de 4 pasos

1. **Sal / sigue:** Go straight out of the station / along this street.  
2. **Gira:** Turn left at the corner.  
3. **Pasa:** Past the bank on your right.  
4. **Localiza:** The library is on the left. You can’t miss it.

Si memorizas solo esta plantilla, ya puedes dar indicaciones A1 en la calle.

## 10. Ejercicios prácticos (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige el imperativo

1. \_\_\_\_ left at the corner. (gira)  
2. \_\_\_\_ straight for 100 metres. (sigue)  
3. \_\_\_\_ the bank and then turn right. (pasa)  
4. \_\_\_\_ right at the traffic lights. (gira)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Turn**  
2. **Go**  
3. **Past**  
4. **Turn**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa

1. Turn left \_\_\_\_ the corner.  
2. Go straight \_\_\_\_ 200 metres.  
3. The cinema is \_\_\_\_ the right.  
4. Past the bank \_\_\_\_ your right.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **at**  
2. **for**  
3. **on**  
4. **on**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Traduce

1. Gira a la izquierda en la esquina.  
2. Sigue recto 100 metros.  
3. Pasa el banco y entonces gira a la derecha.  
4. El cine está a la derecha.  
5. ¿Cómo llego a la biblioteca?  
6. No te puedes perder.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Turn left at the corner.  
2. Go straight for 100 metres.  
3. Past the bank and then turn right.  
4. The cinema is on the right.  
5. How do I get to the library?  
6. You can’t miss it.
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrige

1. *You turn left at the corner.* (versión de indicación de calle)  
2. *Turn left in the corner.*  
3. *Go to straight.*  
4. *The pharmacy is in the left.*  
5. *How I get to the cinema?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Turn** left at the corner.  
2. Turn left **at** the corner.  
3. **Go straight.**  
4. The pharmacy is **on** the left.  
5. How **do** I get to the cinema?
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Ordena los pasos (estación → biblioteca)

a) Past the bank on your right.  
b) Go straight out of the station.  
c) The library is on the left, next to the pharmacy.  
d) Turn left at the corner.  
e) Go straight for about 200 metres.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**b → e → d → a → c**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Empareja

1. Turn left  
2. Go straight  
3. Past the bank  
4. on the right  
5. You can’t miss it  

a) sigue recto  
b) a la derecha  
c) gira a la izquierda  
d) es fácil de encontrar  
e) pasa el banco

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **c** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **e** · 4 → **b** · 5 → **d**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Escribe indicaciones

Escribe 5–7 frases para ir de un punto A a un punto B (reales o inventados). Usa al menos:

- *Go straight*  
- *Turn left* o *Turn right*  
- *Past the…*  
- *on the left* o *on the right*

**Modelo:**

> Go straight along this street. Turn left at the corner. Past the supermarket and the cinema is on the right. You can’t miss it.

### Ejercicio 8 — Elige la opción correcta

1. Turn right \_\_\_\_ the traffic lights. (at / on / in)  
2. Go straight \_\_\_\_ this street. (along / at / to)  
3. The library is \_\_\_\_ the left. (on / in / at)  
4. How \_\_\_\_ I get to the park? (do / does / am)  
5. Past \_\_\_\_ supermarket. (the / a / —)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **at**  
2. **along**  
3. **on**  
4. **do**  
5. **the**
</details>

## 11. Plan de práctica (15 minutos)

1. **3 min** — Repite 8 imperativos en voz alta.  
2. **3 min** — Diálogo *How do I get to…?* (pregunta + 4 pasos).  
3. **4 min** — Dibuja un mapa y escribe indicaciones.  
4. **5 min** — Grábate y comprueba *at / on / for / along*.

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *Turn left / right* + *at the…*  
- [ ] Uso *Go straight* (+ *for / along / past*)  
- [ ] Uso *Past the…*  
- [ ] Localizo con *on the left / right*  
- [ ] Pregunto *How do I get to…?*  
- [ ] Cierro con *You can’t miss it* / *Thanks*
