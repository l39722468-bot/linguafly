## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 27 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Clasificar animales: ***pet / farm / wild***.
- Nombrar mascotas, animales de granja y salvajes.
- Describir con ***dangerous, friendly, cute*** (+ *big / small*).
- Hablar de tus mascotas: *I have… My dog is called…*.
- Contrastar: *We don’t have wild animals at home*.

---

## 1. Tres grupos: *pets, farm, wild*

| Grupo | Significado | Ejemplos |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **pet** | mascota (vive con personas) | dog, cat, fish, rabbit, hamster |
| **farm animal** | animal de granja | cow, horse, sheep, pig, chicken, duck |
| **wild animal** | animal salvaje | lion, tiger, bear, wolf, elephant, snake |

![Esquema pets farm wild animals A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-27/a1-unit27-pets-farm-wild.png)

> A cat is a **pet** animal.  
> A cow lives on a **farm**.  
> A **wild** animal lives in the forest.  
> Pigs and chickens are **farm** animals.  
> Lions are **wild** and dangerous.

### Dos posiciones del adjetivo

```
be + adjective → My dog is friendly.
adjective + noun → a friendly dog
```

> My hamster is **cute** and **friendly**.  
> My pet is a **friendly** dog.  
> A snake can be **dangerous**.  
> The horse is **big** but **friendly**.

### Tip para hispanohablantes

En inglés el adjetivo **no cambia** de género ni número: *a cute cat / cute cats* (no *cutes*).

### Diálogo modelo

> **Ana:** Do you have any pets?  
> **Luis:** Yes. I have two pets: a dog and a cat.  
> **Ana:** What’s your dog like?  
> **Luis:** He’s very friendly. He loves to play.  
> **Ana:** And your cat?  
> **Luis:** She’s small and cute. She likes sleeping on the sofa.  
> **Ana:** Cool! Do you like wild animals?  
> **Luis:** I like elephants at the zoo, but lions are dangerous. We don’t have them at home!

## 5. *He / she* con animales

En inglés es habitual usar ***he/she*** con mascotas (sobre todo si conoces el sexo o tienes vínculo emocional):

> My dog is called Max. **He** is friendly.  
> My cat is cute. **She** likes sleeping.

Con animales genéricos o salvajes suele usarse ***it***:

> A lion is dangerous. **It** lives in the wild.

En A1 ambas formas aparecen; prioriza *he/she* con *my dog / my cat*.

## 7. Errores típicos de hispanohablantes

| ❌ Incorrecto | ✅ Correcto | Explicación |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *My dog is very friend* | *My dog is very **friendly*** | Adjetivo = *friendly* |
| *a dog friendly* | *a **friendly** dog* | Adj. antes del nombre |
| *The lion are dangerous* | *The lion **is** / Lions **are*** | Concordancia |
| *Sheeps* | *sheep* (plural igual) | Plural irregular |
| *a cute* (sin nombre) | *a cute cat / It is cute* | Falta sustantivo o *be* |
| *dangerouss animals* | *dangerous animals* | Sin -s en el adj. |
| *I have a pet dog friendly* | *I have a **friendly** pet dog* / *My pet is a friendly dog* | Orden |

## 8b. Mini-diálogos para memorizar

> **A:** Do you have a pet?  
> **B:** Yes. I have a dog. He’s very friendly.  
> **A:** Is he cute?  
> **B:** Yes! And he loves to play.

> **A:** What’s on your grandma’s farm?  
> **B:** There are cows, chickens and a horse.  
> **A:** Is the horse friendly?  
> **B:** Yes. It’s big but friendly.

> **A:** Are lions pets?  
> **B:** No. Lions are wild and dangerous. We don’t have them at home.

Cambia solo el animal y el adjetivo: fijas la estructura.

> The kitten is so cute.

> A cow lives on a farm.  
> A wild animal lives in the forest.

> The elephant is very big.

> Lions are wild and dangerous.  
> I have two pets. My dog is called Max.  
> My cat is small and very cute.

Lee la lista dos veces. Luego describe **tus** mascotas (o inventadas) con 6 frases.

### Rutina de 2 minutos

1. Di 3 animales *pet*, 3 *farm*, 3 *wild*.  
2. Di 3 frases con *friendly / cute / dangerous*.  
3. Di 2 frases *I have…* + descripción.  
4. Di 1 contraste: *Wild animals… we don’t have them at home.*

Si usas fotos reales de animales, el vocabulario se pega más rápido.

### Tour guiado (modelo)

> I have two pets. My dog is called Max. He is very friendly and he loves to play. My cat is small and very cute. She likes sleeping on the sofa. At my grandma’s farm there are cows, chickens and a horse. The horse is big but friendly. Wild animals like lions and tigers are dangerous — we don’t have them at home.

Grábate leyéndolo y luego improvísalo con **tus** animales.

### Frases extra para practicar en voz alta

> Do you have any pets?  
> Yes, I have a rabbit. It’s cute and friendly.  
> What’s your favourite wild animal?  
> I like elephants. They’re very big.  
> Are snakes dangerous?  
> Yes. A snake can be dangerous.  
> Are chickens pets?  
> No. Chickens are farm animals.  
> The zoo has wild animals.

Personaliza con animales que te gusten. Cuanto más personal, mejor recuerdas *pet / farm / wild*.

### Checklist mental

- Mascota → *pet*  
- Granja → *farm animal*  
- Naturaleza / zoo → *wild*  
- Peligroso → *dangerous*  
- Amigable → *friendly*  
- Mono → *cute*  
- Adj. + noun **o** *be* + adj.  
- Mascotas: a menudo *he/she*

2. Una vaca vive en una granja.  
3. Mi perro es muy amigable.

5. Los leones son salvajes y peligrosos.  
6. Tengo dos mascotas.  
7. Una serpiente puede ser peligrosa.

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrige

1. *My dog is very friend.*  
2. *a dog friendly*  
3. *The lion are dangerous.*  
4. *I have two pet.*  
5. *Sheeps are farm animals.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

2. a **friendly** dog  
3. The lion **is** dangerous. / Lions **are** dangerous.  
4. I have two **pets**.  
5. **Sheep** are farm animals.

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa el texto

### Ejercicio 7 — Escribe sobre animales

- al menos 1 *pet*, 1 *farm*, 1 *wild*  
- los adjetivos *friendly*, *cute* y *dangerous*
