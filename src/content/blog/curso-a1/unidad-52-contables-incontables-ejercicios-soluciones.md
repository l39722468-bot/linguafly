## Lección 1 — Gramática: much / many / a lot of

**Objetivo:** *much* + incontable; *many* + contable plural; *a lot of* con ambos.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I don't have ____ milk left. → *much / many / muchs*  
2. How ____ eggs do you need? → *many / much / manys*  
3. We have ____ water in the fridge. → *a lot of / many / much*  
4. There isn't ____ bread left. → *much / many / a lot of*  
5. She has ____ apples in the basket. → *a lot of / much / many*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **much** — *much* + incontable (*milk*).  
2. **many** — *many* + contable plural (*eggs*).  
3. **a lot of** — afirmativa: *a lot of* + incontable.  
4. **much** — negativa con incontable.  
5. **a lot of** (también aceptable: **many**)

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. We use *much* with ____. → a) uncountable nouns (*milk, water*) · b) countable nouns · c) always  
7. We use *many* with ____. → a) countable nouns (*eggs, apples*) · b) uncountable · c) never with plural  
8. *A lot of* can go with ____. → a) countable and uncountable · b) only countable · c) only uncountable  
9. Which is uncountable? → a) rice · b) apple · c) egg  
10. Correct: ____. → a) How many eggs? · b) How much eggs? · c) How many milk?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) uncountable nouns (*milk, water*)**  
7. **a) countable nouns (*eggs, apples*)**  
8. **a) countable and uncountable**  
9. **a) rice**  
10. **a) How many eggs?**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. I don't have / much / milk / left.  
12. How many / apples / do you need?  
13. We have / a lot of / water / and / bread.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I don't have much milk left.  
12. How many apples do you need?  
13. We have a lot of water and bread.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *Much* is for uncountable; *many* for countable plural. → True / False  
15. *A lot of* works with both countable and uncountable. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: contables e incontables

**Objetivo:** *milk, water, bread, rice* (incontables) vs *apples, eggs* (contables).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *Milk* = ____. → a) uncountable (no plural) · b) countable · c) plural only  
2. *Water* = ____. → a) uncountable · b) countable · c) always *two waters* in A1  
3. *Bread* = ____. → a) uncountable · b) countable · c) *breads* always  
4. *Rice* = ____. → a) uncountable · b) countable · c) *rices*  
5. *Apples* and *eggs* = ____. → a) countable (we can count) · b) uncountable · c) same as milk

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) uncountable (no plural)**  
2. **a) uncountable**  
3. **a) uncountable**  
4. **a) uncountable**  
5. **a) countable (we can count)**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Empareja

6. Empareja: milk / water / bread / apples / eggs → uncountable · uncountable · uncountable · countable · countable  
7. Empareja: rice / sugar / orange / banana / coffee → uncountable · uncountable · countable · countable · uncountable

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **milk → uncountable** · **water → uncountable** · **bread → uncountable** · **apples → countable** · **eggs → countable**  
7. **rice → uncountable** · **sugar → uncountable** · **orange → countable** · **banana → countable** · **coffee → uncountable**

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–13 — Completa / elige

8. I need some ____ and bread. → *milk / eggs / apples*  
9. How many ____ do you want? → *eggs / rice / water*  
10. There is a lot of ____ in the fridge. → *water / apple / egg*  
11. We say ____ for quantity. → a) some rice, a lot of bread · b) two rices · c) three breads (A1)  
12. *Countable* = ____. → a) things we can count (*one apple, two eggs*) · b) always liquid · c) never food  
13. *Uncountable* = ____. → a) no plural form normally (*milk, rice*) · b) always plural · c) only drinks

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **milk**  
9. **eggs**  
10. **water**  
11. **a) some rice, a lot of bread**  
12. **a) things we can count (*one apple, two eggs*)**  
13. **a) no plural form normally (*milk, rice*)**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *Milk, water, bread, rice* are uncountable in English. → True / False  
15. *Apples* and *eggs* are countable; we say *two apples*. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Shopping list

**Objetivo:** leer una lista de compra con *much / many / a lot of*.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-52/reading-countable.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Shopping list"></audio>

> I need to buy some food today. We don't have much milk or bread left. I want a lot of water and some rice. For fruit I need many apples and six eggs. We have too much sugar at home so I won't buy any. How many oranges do we need? Maybe four or five.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What does the writer need to buy? → a) Some food · b) Clothes · c) Books  
2. Do they have much milk and bread left? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The text doesn't say  
3. What does he/she want a lot of? → a) Water · b) Milk · c) Bread  
4. Does he/she need rice? → a) Yes, some · b) No · c) A lot only  
5. How many eggs? → a) Six · b) Four · c) Five

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Some food**  
2. **a) No**  
3. **a) Water**  
4. **a) Yes, some**  
5. **a) Six**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Will he/she buy sugar? → a) No · b) Yes · c) Maybe  
7. How many oranges maybe? → a) Four or five · b) Six · c) Many  
8. Which is uncountable in the text? → a) Milk, bread, water, rice, sugar · b) Apples only · c) Eggs only  
9. *Much* is used with ____. → a) milk, bread (uncountable) · b) apples · c) eggs  
10. *Many apples* = ____. → a) countable, plural · b) uncountable · c) wrong

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) No**  
7. **a) Four or five**  
8. **a) Milk, bread, water, rice, sugar**  
9. **a) milk, bread (uncountable)**  
10. **a) countable, plural**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text is about ____. → a) a shopping list, countable and uncountable · b) cooking · c) a restaurant  
12. *A lot of water* means ____. → a) much water · b) many waters · c) a bottle only  
13. *Left* means ____. → a) remaining · b) right · c) bought  
14. *Won't buy* means ____. → a) will not buy · b) want to buy · c) bought  
15. The main topic is ____. → a) shopping for food with much/many · b) a cooking recipe · c) at a restaurant

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) a shopping list, countable and uncountable**  
12. **a) much water**  
13. **a) remaining**  
14. **a) will not buy**  
15. **a) shopping for food with much/many**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Do we have much milk?

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo sobre qué tienen y qué necesitan.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-52/listening-countable.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Do we have much milk?"></audio>

> **A:** Do we have much milk?  
> **B:** No, we don't have much.  
> **A:** How many eggs do we need?  
> **B:** Six eggs and a lot of bread. We have some rice but not much water.  
> **A:** I'll buy water and many apples.  
> **B:** Good. Don't buy too much sugar; we have a lot already.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Do they have much milk? → a) No · b) Yes · c) A little  
2. How many eggs do they need? → a) Six · b) Five · c) Many  
3. Do they need a lot of bread? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The dialogue doesn't say  
4. Do they have much water? → a) No, not much · b) Yes · c) A lot  
5. What will A buy? → a) Water and many apples · b) Milk and eggs · c) Rice only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) No**  
2. **a) Six**  
3. **a) Yes**  
4. **a) No, not much**  
5. **a) Water and many apples**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. What does B say about sugar? → a) Don't buy too much; we have a lot · b) Buy a lot · c) We need sugar  
7. Do they have some rice? → a) Yes · b) No · c) Not much  
8. The dialogue is about ____. → a) a shopping list, what they have/need · b) cooking · c) a restaurant order  
9. *Much* is used with ____. → a) milk, water, bread (uncountable) · b) eggs · c) apples  
10. *Many apples* = ____. → a) countable, plural · b) uncountable · c) wrong

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Don't buy too much; we have a lot**  
7. **a) Yes**  
8. **a) a shopping list, what they have/need**  
9. **a) milk, water, bread (uncountable)**  
10. **a) countable, plural**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *A lot of bread* means ____. → a) much bread · b) many breads · c) six breads  
12. *I'll buy* means ____. → a) I will buy · b) I bought · c) I want to buy  
13. *Don't buy* means ____. → a) do not buy · b) did not buy · c) will not buy  
14. *Too much sugar* means ____. → a) more than needed · b) no sugar · c) a little sugar  
15. The main idea is ____. → a) checking what they have and need (countable/uncountable) · b) a cooking recipe · c) at the supermarket checkout

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) much bread**  
12. **a) I will buy**  
13. **a) do not buy**  
14. **a) more than needed**  
15. **a) checking what they have and need (countable/uncountable)**

## Lección 5 — Writing: In the fridge

**Objetivo:** escribir con *much / many / a lot of*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I don't have ____ milk left. → *much / many / muchs*  
2. How ____ apples do you need? → *many / much / a lot of*  
3. We have ____ water in the fridge. → *a lot of / many / much*  
4. There isn't ____ bread left. → *much / many / a lot of*  
5. She has ____ eggs and oranges. → *many / much / a lot of*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **much**  
2. **many**  
3. **a lot of**  
4. **much**  
5. **many** (también aceptable: **a lot of**)

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. I don't have / much / milk / left.  
7. How many / eggs / do you need?  
8. We have / a lot of / water / and / bread.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I don't have much milk left.  
7. How many eggs do you need?  
8. We have a lot of water and bread.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. No tenemos mucha leche (*don't have much milk*).  
10. Pregunta: cuántos huevos necesitas (*How many eggs…?*).  
11. Dos frases: mucha agua + muchas manzanas.  
12. Pregunta *Do you have much bread?* y respuesta breve.  
13. Párrafo corto (3–4 frases): qué hay en la nevera.  
14. Lista de compra con *much/many/a lot of*.  
15. Diferencia incontable (*milk, rice*) vs contable (*eggs, apples*) en 2 frases.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. We don't have much milk.  
10. How many eggs do you need?  
11. We have a lot of water. We need many apples.  
12. Do you have much bread? No, we don't.  
13. We have a lot of water and some milk. We don't have much bread left. We have many eggs and apples.  
14. I need some milk and a lot of water. I need many apples and six eggs.  
15. Milk and rice are uncountable. Apples and eggs are countable.

</details>
