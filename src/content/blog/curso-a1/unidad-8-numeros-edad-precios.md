Ya sabes describir el aspecto físico de una persona. En la **Unidad 8 del curso A1** (*Numbers 20–100: Prices & Ages*) aprendes a **decir y preguntar edades, precios y números** del 20 al 100.

Esta guía cubre los **números 20–100**, las preguntas ***How old are you?*** y ***How much is…?***, y vocabulario de **precios y dinero** (*euro, cheap, expensive*). Incluye **audios**, **esquemas** y **ejercicios**.

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 8 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Decir los **números del 20 al 100** en inglés.
- Preguntar y decir la **edad** (*How old…?*).
- Preguntar y decir el **precio** (*How much…?*).
- Usar palabras de **dinero**: *euro, dollar, cheap, expensive*.

## 2. Preguntar y decir la edad (*How old…?*)

| Pregunta | Respuesta |
| :--- | :--- |
| **How old are you?** | I am twenty-five years old. |
| **How old is she?** | She is thirty years old. |
| **How old is he?** | He is forty-two years old. |
| **How old is your brother?** | He is twelve years old. |

![Esquema de How old en inglés A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-8/a1-unit8-how-old.png)

### Estructura

```
How old + am/is/are + sujeto?
Sujeto + am/is/are + número + years old.
```

| Ejemplo | Traducción |
| :--- | :--- |
| How old **are** you? | ¿Cuántos años tienes? |
| I **am** twenty-five years old. | Tengo veinticinco años. |
| How old **is** she? | ¿Cuántos años tiene ella? |
| She **is** thirty years old. | Ella tiene treinta años. |

### Regla de oro

En inglés no dices *I have 25 years*. Dices:

- ✅ **I am twenty-five years old.**
- ❌ *I have twenty-five years.*

## 3. Preguntar y decir el precio (*How much…?*)

| Pregunta | Respuesta |
| :--- | :--- |
| **How much is** the coffee? | It **is** three euros. |
| **How much is** this book? | It **is** thirty euros. |
| **How much are** these shoes? | They **are** fifty euros. |
| **How much is** the milk? | It **is** one euro and fifty cents. |

![Esquema de How much en inglés A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-8/a1-unit8-how-much.png)

### Estructura

```
How much is + singular?
How much are + plural?
It is / They are + precio.
```

| Singular | Plural |
| :--- | :--- |
| How much **is** the book? | How much **are** the apples? |
| The book **is** forty-five euros. | The apples **are** five euros. |

## 5. Texto modelo (del curso)

### En el supermercado

> I am at the supermarket. I need **twenty** apples and ten oranges. The apples are **five euros**. The oranges are **three euros**. I also need a bottle of milk. The milk is **one euro and fifty cents**. The total is **nine euros and fifty cents**. I have **twenty euros**. I am happy.

### Tu plantilla en 6 frases

1. How old are you? — I am \_\_\_\_ years old.
2. How old is your \_\_\_\_? — He/She is \_\_\_\_ years old.
3. How much is the \_\_\_\_? — It is \_\_\_\_ euros.
4. How much are the \_\_\_\_? — They are \_\_\_\_ euros.
5. Is this expensive or cheap? — It is \_\_\_\_.
6. The total is \_\_\_\_ euros.

## 7. Errores típicos de hispanohablantes

| ❌ Incorrecto | ✅ Correcto | Explicación |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *I have 25 years* | *I **am** 25 years **old*** | Edad con *to be*, no *have* |
| *How many years do you have?* | ***How old are you?*** | Pregunta fija de edad |
| *How much cost the book?* | ***How much is** the book?* | Estructura *How much is…?* |
| *How much is these shoes?* | *How much **are** these shoes?* | Plural → are |
| *fourty* | ***forty*** | Ortografía sin *u* |
| *twenty five* (escrito) | ***twenty-five*** | Guion en compuestos |

## 8. Ejercicios prácticos (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Escribe el número en inglés

1. 20 → \_\_\_\_  
2. 40 → \_\_\_\_  
3. 25 → \_\_\_\_  
4. 80 → \_\_\_\_  
5. 100 → \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **twenty**  
2. **forty**  
3. **twenty-five**  
4. **eighty**  
5. **one hundred** / **a hundred**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa con *am / is / are*

1. How old \_\_\_\_ you?  
2. I \_\_\_\_ twenty-five years old.  
3. How old \_\_\_\_ she?  
4. How much \_\_\_\_ the coffee?  
5. How much \_\_\_\_ these shoes?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **are**  
2. **am**  
3. **is**  
4. **is**  
5. **are**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Traduce al inglés

1. ¿Cuántos años tienes?  
2. Tengo treinta años.  
3. ¿Cuánto cuesta el libro?  
4. Cuesta cuarenta y cinco euros.  
5. Estos zapatos son caros.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. How old are you?  
2. I am thirty years old.  
3. How much is the book?  
4. It is forty-five euros. / The book is forty-five euros.  
5. These shoes are expensive.
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrige el error

1. *I have twenty-five years.*  
2. *How much is these apples?*  
3. *She have thirty years old.*  
4. *The book are forty euros.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **am** twenty-five years **old**.  
2. How much **are** these apples?  
3. She **is** thirty years old.  
4. The book **is** forty euros.
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Ordena las palabras

1. old / How / you / are  
2. years / am / I / twenty-five / old  
3. is / How / the / much / coffee

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. How old are you?  
2. I am twenty-five years old.  
3. How much is the coffee?
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Mini diálogo tuyo

Escribe 5 frases: tu edad, la edad de un familiar, el precio de dos objetos y si algo es *cheap* o *expensive*.

**Modelo:**

> I am twenty-five years old. My brother is twelve years old. How much is this book? It is thirty euros. These shoes are expensive.

## 9. Diálogo modelo

> **Clerk:** Hello! Can I help you?  
> **Maya:** Yes. How much is this book?  
> **Clerk:** It is thirty euros.  
> **Maya:** And how much are these shoes?  
> **Clerk:** They are fifty euros.  
> **Maya:** Hmm… the book is cheap. The shoes are expensive.  
> **Clerk:** The total for the book is thirty euros.  
> **Maya:** OK. And… how old is this magazine? Is it new?  
> **Clerk:** Yes! It is new. Only two euros.

## 10. Consejos para practicar

1. **Cuenta de 20 en 20** en voz alta: twenty, forty, sixty, eighty, one hundred.
2. **Practica tu edad** y la de tu familia: *I am… / She is… years old.*
3. **Lee etiquetas de precios** y di: *How much is this? It is… euros.*
4. **Escucha los audios** y repite con la misma entonación.
5. Haz los ejercicios de la [Unidad 8 del curso A1](/curso-a1/unit-8).

## Resumen rápido

| Tema | Lo esencial |
| :--- | :--- |
| Decenas | twenty → one hundred |

| Edad | How old are you? → I am … years old |
| Precio singular | How much **is**…? → It **is** … euros |
| Precio plural | How much **are**…? → They **are** … euros |
| Dinero | cheap / expensive, euro, dollar, pound |
