## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 59 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Usar ***pay for*** + cosa (*pay for the dinner / the bill*).
- Usar ***spend on*** + cosa (*spend money on clothes / food*).
- Formar pasados: ***paid*** / ***spent***.
- Nombrar ***cash, credit card, change, receipt, currency***.
- Preguntar: ***Can I pay for this with credit card?***

---

## 1. *Pay for* vs *spend on*

| Expresión | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **pay for** + thing | pagar por algo concreto | I **paid for** the dinner with cash. |
| **spend** (money) **on** + thing | gastar en… | She **spends** a lot of money **on** clothes. |

> I paid for the dinner with cash.  
> Can I pay for this with credit card?  
> She spends a lot of money on clothes.  
> We spent too much on food last month.

| Correcto | Incorrecto típico |
| :--- | :--- |
| pay **for** the coffee | *pay on the coffee* |
| spend money **on** books | *spend money for books* |
| I paid **for** the bill | *I paid on the bill* |

## 2. Pasados irregulares: *paid* / *spent*

| Presente | Pasado | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| pay | **paid** | I **paid** for the book yesterday. |
| spend | **spent** | We **spent** too much last month. |

> I paid for the bill yesterday.  
> She spends money on books.  
> I spent money on books.

## 3. Vocabulario: cash, card, change, receipt

| Palabra | Significado | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **cash** | efectivo | I paid with cash. |
| **credit card** | tarjeta de crédito | Do you take credit card? |
| **change** | cambio (vuelta) | Here is your change. |
| **receipt** | recibo | Can I have the receipt please? |
| **currency** | moneda (de un país) | pounds, euros, dollars |
| **bill** | cuenta | I paid for the bill. |
| **note** | billete | a twenty-pound note |

> I paid with cash — no credit card.  
> Can I have the receipt please?  
> Here is your change — five pounds back.

## 4. En la caja: frases útiles

| Situación | Frase |
| :--- | :--- |
| Preguntar forma de pago | Can I pay for this with credit card? |
| Ofrecer opciones | Cash or card? |
| Preferir efectivo | I prefer to pay with cash. |
| Dar el cambio | Here is your change — ten pounds. |
| Pedir recibo | Can I have the receipt please? |
| Aceptar tarjeta | Yes, of course. / Do you take credit card? |

## 5. Texto modelo: de compras

> When I go shopping I usually take both cash and my credit card. Yesterday I bought a book for ten pounds. I paid for it with cash. The total was ten pounds so I gave a twenty-pound note. The shop assistant gave me my change — ten pounds back — and a receipt. I spend a lot of money on books every month.

## 7. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I paid on the coffee. | I paid **for** the coffee. | *pay for* |
| She spends money for books. | She spends money **on** books. | *spend on* |
| I payed for dinner. | I **paid** for dinner. | pasado irregular |
| We spended too much. | We **spent** too much. | pasado irregular |
| Can I to pay with card? | Can I **pay** with card? | *can* + base |
| Here is your receipt change. | Here is your **change** / your **receipt**. | son cosas distintas |

## 8. Mini prácticas orales

1. Di cómo pagaste algo ayer (*I paid for… with…*).  
2. Di en qué gastas dinero (*I spend money on…*).  
3. Representa la caja: *Cash or card?* / *Can I have the receipt?*  
4. Explica un cambio: *I gave twenty… Here is my change…*

> I paid for the dinner with cash.  
> Can I pay for this with credit card?  
> She spends a lot of money on food.  
> Can I have the receipt please?

## 9. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — *for* / *on* / verbo

1. I paid \_\_\_\_ the dinner with cash.  
2. She spends a lot of money \_\_\_\_ clothes.  
3. Can I \_\_\_\_ for this with credit card?  
4. We \_\_\_\_ too much on food last month.  
5. I paid \_\_\_\_ the bill yesterday.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **for** · 2. **on** · 3. **pay** · 4. **spent** · 5. **for**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Vocabulario

1. Coins and notes = \_\_\_\_  
2. Card to pay without cash = \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_  
3. Money you get back = \_\_\_\_  
4. Paper that shows what you paid = \_\_\_\_  
5. Money used in a country = \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **cash** · 2. **credit card** · 3. **change** · 4. **receipt** · 5. **currency**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Completa

1. I paid with \_\_\_\_ — no credit card.  
2. Can I have \_\_\_\_ receipt please?  
3. Here is your \_\_\_\_ — five pounds back.  
4. Past of pay = \_\_\_\_  
5. Past of spend = \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **cash** · 2. **the** / **a** · 3. **change** · 4. **paid** · 5. **spent**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Empareja

1. pay for  
2. spend on  
3. cash  
4. change  
5. receipt  
6. credit card  

a) efectivo · b) pagar por · c) tarjeta · d) gastar en · e) vuelta · f) recibo

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **d** · 3 → **a** · 4 → **e** · 5 → **f** · 6 → **c**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrige

1. *I paid on the coffee.*  
2. *She spends money for books.*  
3. *I payed for dinner.*  
4. *We spended too much.*  
5. *Can I to pay with card?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I paid **for** the coffee.  
2. She spends money **on** books.  
3. I **paid** for dinner.  
4. We **spent** too much.  
5. Can I **pay** with card?
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa el diálogo

Assistant: That is twenty pounds please.  
Customer: Can I \_\_\_\_ for this with credit card?  
Assistant: Yes of course.  
Customer: I prefer to pay with \_\_\_\_. Here you are.  
Assistant: You gave me thirty pounds. Here is your \_\_\_\_ — ten pounds — and your \_\_\_\_.  
Customer: Thank you.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**pay** · **cash** · **change** · **receipt**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Completa el texto

When I go shopping I usually take both \_\_\_\_ and my credit card. Yesterday I bought a book for ten pounds. I \_\_\_\_ for it with cash. I gave a twenty-pound note. The shop assistant gave me my \_\_\_\_ — ten pounds back — and a \_\_\_\_. I \_\_\_\_ a lot of money on books every month.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**cash** · **paid** · **change** · **receipt** · **spend**
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Elige la correcta

1. ____  
   a) I paid for the coffee · b) I paid on the coffee · c) I payed for the coffee  
2. ____  
   a) spend money for books · b) spend money on books · c) spend money at books  
3. Past of spend = ____  
   a) spend · b) spent · c) spended  
4. Money you get back = ____  
   a) receipt · b) change · c) currency

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a** · 2. **b** · 3. **b** · 4. **b**
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Traduce

1. Pagué la cena en efectivo.  
2. ¿Puedo pagar esto con tarjeta de crédito?  
3. Ella gasta mucho dinero en ropa.  
4. Gastamos demasiado en comida el mes pasado.  
5. Aquí está tu cambio.  
6. ¿Puedo tener el recibo, por favor?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I paid for the dinner with cash.  
2. Can I pay for this with credit card?  
3. She spends a lot of money on clothes.

### Ejercicio 10 — Escribe tu compra

Escribe 8–10 frases sobre una compra. Incluye:

- 2 × *pay for* (presente o pasado)  
- 2 × *spend/spends/spent on*  
- *cash* o *credit card*  
- *change*  
- *receipt*

**Modelo:** el texto de la sección 5.

## 10. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Repasa *pay for / spend on* y *paid / spent*.  
2. **8 min** — Haz los ejercicios 1–5.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha y repite el diálogo de la caja.  
4. **5–8 min** — Representa una compra en voz alta.

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *pay for* (no *pay on*)  
- [ ] Uso *spend on* (no *spend for*)  
- [ ] Recuerdo *paid* / *spent*  
- [ ] Distingo *cash / credit card / change / receipt*  
- [ ] Pido recibo y entiendo el cambio

## 11. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Memoriza el par: ***pay for*** / ***spend on***.  
2. Practica números de dinero en voz alta (*twenty pounds*).  
3. En la caja, une: precio → pago → change → receipt.  
4. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 59](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-59-dinero-pay-for-spend-on-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 59 del curso A1](/curso-a1/unit-59).
