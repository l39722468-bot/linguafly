## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 21 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Nombrar las **habitaciones** principales de una casa.
- Usar **plurales**: *rooms, bedrooms, bathrooms*.
- Describir con adjetivos: ***bright, small, cozy, modern, large, clean***.
- Decir qué **tiene** tu casa: *We have… / My house has…*.
- Preguntar ***How many rooms does your flat have?***.

### Tips

- *living room* = dos palabras (también *sitting room* en algunos sitios).  
- *bathroom* ≠ solo “baño con bañera”: incluye ducha y aseo.  
- *upstairs / downstairs* = arriba / abajo.  
- *flat* (UK) ≈ *apartment* (US).

## 3. Adjetivos para describir habitaciones

```
The + room + is + adjective
The + room + is + adjective + and + adjective
```

| Adjetivo | Significado | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **bright** | luminoso/a | The kitchen is bright. |
| **small** | pequeño/a | The bathroom is small. |
| **cozy** | acogedor/a | The living room is cozy. |
| **modern** | moderno/a | The kitchen is modern. |
| **large / big** | grande | The garden is big. |
| **clean** | limpio/a | The bathroom is clean. |

### Combinaciones útiles

> The kitchen is **bright and modern**.  
> The bathroom is **small but clean**.  
> The living room is **very cozy**.  
> The garden is **large**.  
> My bedroom is **small but bright**.

En inglés el adjetivo **no cambia** con género ni número: *a small room / two small rooms* (*small*, no *smalls*).

| How many bedrooms do you have? | We have two bedrooms. |
| Is your kitchen big? | No, it's small but bright. |
| Do you have a garden? | Yes, we do. / No, we don't. |
| Where do you sleep? | In the bedroom. |
| Is the living room cozy? | Yes, it is. |

## 5. Más ejemplos en contexto

> My flat has four rooms. We have a kitchen, a living room, a bedroom and a bathroom. The kitchen is bright and modern. The living room is very cozy. The bathroom is small but clean. There isn't a garden, but there is a balcony.

> My house has five rooms. There are three bedrooms upstairs and two bathrooms. Downstairs there is a kitchen and a living room. The garden is big. The hall is small.

### Diálogo modelo

> **Ana:** How many rooms does your flat have?  
> **Luis:** It has four rooms. We have a kitchen, a living room, a bedroom and a bathroom.  
> **Ana:** Is the kitchen big?  
> **Luis:** No, it's small, but it's bright and modern.  
> **Ana:** And the living room?  
> **Luis:** It's very cozy. We have a sofa and a TV.  
> **Ana:** Do you have a garden?  
> **Luis:** No, we don't. We have a balcony.  
> **Ana:** Nice! My house has a big garden.

## 6. Errores típicos de hispanohablantes

| ❌ Incorrecto | ✅ Correcto | Explicación |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *The kitchen is brights* | *The kitchen is **bright*** | Adjetivo sin -s |
| *We has three bedrooms* | *We **have** three bedrooms* | we → have |
| *My house have four rooms* | *My house **has** four rooms* | house → has |
| *There is two bathrooms* | *There **are** two bathrooms* | plural → are |
| *livingroom* (junto) | ***living room*** | Dos palabras |
| *How many rooms your flat have?* | *How many rooms **does** your flat have?* | Auxiliar *does* |
| *The rooms is small* | *The rooms **are** small* | plural → are |

## 7. Pronunciación útil

| Forma | Tip A1 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **kitchen** | *KICH-ən*. |
| **living room** | Acento en *liv-*. |
| **bedroom** | *BED-room* (dos partes claras). |
| **bathroom** | *BATH-room* (BrE /ɑː/ o AmE /æ/). |
| **cozy / cosy** | *CO-zi* (AmE *cozy*, BrE a menudo *cosy*). |
| **bright** | Diptongo /aɪ/. |

Escucha los nombres de las habitaciones y luego las frases completas. Repite: *The kitchen is bright and modern.*

### House vs flat (nota rápida)

Si vives en un **piso**, di *flat* (Reino Unido) o *apartment* (EE. UU.). Si es una **casa** con entradas propias y a menudo jardín, di *house*. Las habitaciones son las mismas; cambia el contenedor:

> My **flat** has three rooms. There isn't a garden.  
> My **house** has five rooms and a big garden.

En preguntas, *How many rooms does your flat/house have?* funciona igual.

## 7b. Mini-diálogos para memorizar

> **A:** Where do you cook?  
> **B:** In the kitchen. It's bright and modern.

> **A:** How many bedrooms do you have?  
> **B:** We have two bedrooms. There are two bathrooms upstairs.

> **A:** Is your living room big?  
> **B:** No, it's small, but it's very cozy.

Cambia solo el adjetivo y el número de habitaciones: fijas la estructura rápido.

## 7c. Plantillas para copiar

1. We have a \_\_\_\_ and a \_\_\_\_.  
2. My house / flat has \_\_\_\_ rooms.  
3. The \_\_\_\_ is \_\_\_\_ (and \_\_\_\_).  
4. There are \_\_\_\_ bedrooms upstairs.  
5. How many rooms does your flat have? — It has \_\_\_\_.

### Más frases para memorizar

> We have a kitchen and a living room.  
> My house has four rooms.  
> The kitchen is bright and modern.  
> The living room is very cozy.  
> The bathroom is small but clean.  
> There are two bathrooms upstairs.  
> The garden is big.  
> How many rooms does your flat have?  
> I sleep in the bedroom.

### Rutina de 2 minutos (todos los días)

1. Nombra en voz alta 8 rooms.  
2. Di tres frases *The … is …*.  
3. Di: *We have … bedrooms. My house/flat has … rooms.*  
4. Responde: *How many rooms does your flat have?*

Si lo haces pegado a la puerta de tu cocina durante una semana, el vocabulario se queda sin esfuerzo.

### Frases extra para practicar en voz alta

> The dining room is small.  
> There is a hall near the door.  
> The stairs are upstairs.  
> We have a garage for the car.  
> The balcony is small but bright.  
> My bedroom is cozy.  
> How many bathrooms do you have? — Two.  
> Is your kitchen modern? — Yes, it is.

Copia y personaliza. Cuanto más real sea la casa que describes, mejor recuerdas *have/has* y los adjetivos.

### Tour guiado de ejemplo (para imitar)

> Welcome to my flat. First, the hall is small. Then the kitchen is bright and modern. The living room is cozy. After that, the bedroom is quiet. The bathroom is small but clean. There is a balcony. There isn't a garage.

Traduce el tour a tu casa cambiando solo los adjetivos y el número de habitaciones. Es un excelente ejercicio de speaking de un minuto.

### Ejercicio 7 — Escribe sobre tu casa

Escribe 6–8 frases: cuántas habitaciones, cuáles son, y 3 adjetivos.

**Modelo:**

> My flat has four rooms. We have a kitchen, a living room, a bedroom and a bathroom. The kitchen is bright and modern. The living room is cozy. The bathroom is small but clean. There isn't a garden, but there is a balcony. I sleep in the bedroom.

### Ejercicio 8 — Elige la opción correcta

1. The living room \_\_\_\_ cozy. (is / are)  
2. We \_\_\_\_ two bathrooms. (have / has)  
3. There \_\_\_\_ a garden. (is / are)  
4. How many rooms \_\_\_\_ your house have? (do / does)  
5. The kitchen is bright \_\_\_\_ modern. (and / but)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is**  
2. **have**  
3. **is**  
4. **does**  
5. **and** (también posible *but* si contrastas; aquí lo natural es *and*)
</details>

1. **3 min** — Repite los 10 nombres de rooms con audio.  
2. **4 min** — Di 6 frases *The … is …*.  
3. **4 min** — Practica *We have / My house has / There are…*.  
4. **4 min** — Escribe y lee tu descripción personal.
