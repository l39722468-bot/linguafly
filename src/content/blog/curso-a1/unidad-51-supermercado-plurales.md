## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 51 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Formar **plurales regulares**: *apple → apples*, *egg → eggs*.
- Usar **-es** cuando la palabra termina en *-o* frecuente del supermercado: *tomatoes, potatoes*.
- Recordar irregulares básicos: *child → children*, *person → people*.
- Usar *fish* (igual en plural como comida) y *pieces of fish*.
- Nombrar **fruit, vegetables, meat, fish**.
- Orientarte en la tienda: ***aisle, shelf, basket, trolley, checkout***.
- Preguntar: ***Where are the vegetables?*** / ***Where is the fruit section?***

## 1. Plurales regulares en el supermercado

| Singular | Plural | Regla |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| apple | **apples** | + **-s** |
| banana | **bananas** | + **-s** |
| egg | **eggs** | + **-s** |
| carrot | **carrots** | + **-s** |
| vegetable | **vegetables** | + **-s** |
| tomato | **tomatoes** | + **-es** |
| potato | **potatoes** | + **-es** |

> I need two **tomatoes** for the salad.  
> We have three **potatoes** in the fridge.  
> There are many **apples** in the fruit section.  
> How many **eggs** do you need?

## 2. Plurales irregulares y casos especiales

| Singular | Plural | Nota |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| child | **children** | irregular |
| person | **people** | irregular (prioridad A1) |
| fish | **fish** | igual como comida |
| piece | **pieces** | *two pieces of fish* |

> There are many **children** in the supermarket today.  
> How many **people** are in the queue?  
> I want two **pieces** of fish.  
> We say **two fish** for two pieces of fish.

## 5. Preguntas útiles en el supermercado

| Pregunta | Respuesta típica |
| :--- | :--- |
| Where **are** the vegetables? | In aisle two. |
| Where **is** the fruit section? | Aisle one, next to the entrance. |
| Where **are** the apples? | On the shelf / In aisle five. |
| Where **is** the checkout? | Over there. |
| How many eggs do you need? | Six, please. |

Recuerda: *vegetables / apples* → **Where are…?**; *fruit section / checkout* → **Where is…?**

> Where are the vegetables? — In aisle two.  
> And the fruit? — Aisle one, next to the entrance.  
> I need some chicken and fish. — Meat and fish are at the back.

## 6. Texto modelo: una compra del sábado

> I go to the supermarket every Saturday. First I get a trolley. The fruit and vegetables are in aisle one. I buy apples, bananas and tomatoes. The meat and fish are at the back. I need some chicken and two pieces of fish. Then I pay at the checkout.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-51/reading-saturday.mp3" title="🔊 I go to the supermarket every Saturday…"></audio>

Marca en el texto:

- plurales: *apples, bananas, tomatoes, pieces*  
- lugares: *aisle one, at the back, checkout*  
- *some chicken* + *two pieces of fish*

## 8. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| two tomatos | two **tomatoes** | *tomato* → *tomatoes* |
| three potatos | three **potatoes** | *potato* → *potatoes* |
| two fishs | two **fish** / two **pieces of fish** | *fish* no cambia (comida) |
| Where is the vegetables? | Where **are** the vegetables? | plural → *are* |
| childs | **children** | irregular |
| persons (prioridad A1) | **people** | forma más natural en A1 |

## 10. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Plurales regulares

1. apple → \_\_\_\_  
2. tomato → \_\_\_\_  
3. potato → \_\_\_\_  
4. egg → \_\_\_\_  
5. banana → \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **apples** · 2. **tomatoes** · 3. **potatoes** · 4. **eggs** · 5. **bananas**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa

1. I need two \_\_\_\_ for the salad. (tomate)  
2. We have three \_\_\_\_ in the fridge. (patata)  
3. There are many \_\_\_\_ in the fruit section. (manzana)  
4. How many \_\_\_\_ do you need? (huevo)  
5. I want two \_\_\_\_ of fish.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **tomatoes**  
2. **potatoes**  
3. **apples**  
4. **eggs**  
5. **pieces**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Irregulares y especiales

1. child → \_\_\_\_  
2. person → \_\_\_\_  
3. Plural de *fish* (comida) → \_\_\_\_  
4. Correcto: ____  

a) two fishs · b) two fish · c) two fisheses

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **children** · 2. **people** · 3. **fish** · 4. **b**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Categorías

Escribe **fruit / vegetables / meat / fish**:

1. banana → \_\_\_\_  
2. carrot → \_\_\_\_  
3. chicken → \_\_\_\_  
4. salmon → \_\_\_\_  
5. tomato → \_\_\_\_  
6. beef → \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **fruit** · 2. **vegetables** · 3. **meat** · 4. **fish** · 5. **vegetables** · 6. **meat**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Tienda

1. The apples are in \_\_\_\_ five.  
2. First I get a \_\_\_\_. (carrito)  
3. Then I pay at the \_\_\_\_.  
4. Meat and fish are at the \_\_\_\_.  
5. Where \_\_\_\_ the vegetables?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **aisle**  
2. **trolley**  
3. **checkout**  
4. **back**  
5. **are**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Corrige

1. *I need two tomatos.*  
2. *Where is the vegetables?*  
3. *I want two fishs.*  
4. *There are many childs.*  
5. *The apples is in aisle five.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I need two **tomatoes**.  
2. Where **are** the vegetables?  
3. I want two **fish** / two **pieces of fish**.  
4. There are many **children**.  
5. The apples **are** in aisle five.
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Empareja

1. aisle  
2. checkout  
3. trolley  
4. basket  
5. shelf  

a) caja · b) carrito · c) pasillo · d) estante · e) cesta

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **c** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **b** · 4 → **e** · 5 → **d**
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Completa el texto

I go to the supermarket every Saturday. First I get a \_\_\_\_. The fruit and vegetables are in \_\_\_\_ one. I buy \_\_\_\_, bananas and \_\_\_\_. The meat and fish are at the \_\_\_\_. I need some \_\_\_\_ and two \_\_\_\_ of fish. Then I pay at the \_\_\_\_.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**trolley** · **aisle** · **apples** · **tomatoes** · **back** · **chicken** · **pieces** · **checkout**
</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Escribe tu compra

Escribe 8–10 frases sobre una compra. Incluye:

- 3 plurales (*apples / tomatoes / eggs…*)  
- 1 × *aisle*  
- 1 × *trolley* o *basket*  
- 1 × *meat* o *fish*

4. **5–8 min** — Escribe tu lista de compra y léela en voz alta.

- [ ] Formo *tomatoes / potatoes / apples / eggs*  
- [ ] Recuerdo *children / people / fish*  
- [ ] Clasifico fruit / vegetables / meat / fish  
- [ ] Uso *aisle, trolley, checkout*  
- [ ] Pregunto *Where are the…?* / *Where is the…?*

---

## 12. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Practica plurales **con lista real** de la nevera, no solo con tablas.  
2. Si confundes *Where is / Where are*, mira el sustantivo: plural → *are*.  
3. *Some meat / some fish* prepara U52–53 (*countable / uncountable*, *some / any*).  
4. Practica en la [Unidad 51 del curso A1](/curso-a1/unit-51).

La **Unidad 52** profundiza en **contables e incontables** (*a banana* vs *some milk*).
