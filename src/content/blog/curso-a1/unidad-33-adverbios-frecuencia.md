En la [Unidad 32](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-32-deportes-hobbies-play-go-do) hablaste de deportes y hobbies (*I play football, I go swimming*). En la **Unidad 33 del curso A1** (*Adverbs of Frequency*) aprendes a decir **con qué frecuencia** haces esas cosas: ***always, usually, often, sometimes, never***.

Encaja con la [rutina diaria](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-13-rutina-diaria) y el [Present Simple](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-5-present-simple-rutinas). Incluye **audios**, **esquemas** y **ejercicios**.

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 33 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Usar ***always, usually, often, sometimes, never***.
- Colocar el adverbio **antes del verbo** en Present Simple.
- Colocar el adverbio **después de** *am / is / are*.
- Hablar de hábitos: *get up, have breakfast, go running…*.
- Preguntar frecuencia: ***How often…?*** (introducción A1).

---

## 1. La escala de frecuencia

De más a menos frecuente:

| Adverbio | Significado | % aproximado |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **always** | siempre | 100% |
| **usually** | normalmente / casi siempre | ~80–90% |
| **often** | a menudo / muchas veces | ~60–70% |
| **sometimes** | a veces | ~30–40% |
| **never** | nunca | 0% |

![Escala adverbios de frecuencia A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-33/a1-unit33-frequency-scale.png)

```
always > usually > often > sometimes > never
```

### Tip

No memorices solo la traducción: asocia cada adverbio a **tu** vida (*I always… / I never…*).

## 2. Posición con Present Simple (verbo normal)

Regla A1: **sujeto + adverbio + verbo + …**

> I **always** get up at 7.  
> I **usually** have breakfast with my family.  
> I **often** go running before work.  
> I **sometimes** meet friends for lunch.  
> I **never** eat late at night.

### Cuidado con la 3.ª persona

El adverbio **no** lleva *-s*. El verbo sí:

| Incorrecto | Correcto |
| :--- | :--- |
| She always **get** up | She always **gets** up |
| He never **eat** meat | He never **eats** meat |
| She often **go** running | She often **goes** running |

---

## 3. Posición con *be* (*am / is / are*)

Con *be*, el adverbio va **después**: **sujeto + am/is/are + adverbio + …**

> She **is never** late.  
> He **is never** late.  
> They **are always** busy.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-33/they-are-always-busy.mp3" title="🔊 They are always busy"></audio>

![Posición del adverbio A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-33/a1-unit33-adverb-position.png)

```
I always get up.     → adverbio ANTES del verbo
She is never late.   → adverbio DESPUÉS de be
```

### Contraste rápido

| Tipo | Orden | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Verbo normal | subject + **adv** + verb | I **often** go running |
| *be* | subject + **be** + adv | She **is** never late |

No digas *She never is late* ni *I get always up* en A1 estándar.

## 5. Preguntar *How often…?*

Pregunta A1 muy útil:

> **How often** do you go running?  
> I **often** go running. / Three times a week.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-33/how-often-do-you.mp3" title="🔊 How often do you go running?"></audio>

### Respuestas posibles

| Pregunta | Respuesta con adverbio | Respuesta con tiempo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| How often do you play football? | I **always** play on Saturdays. | Every Saturday. |
| How often do you go swimming? | I **sometimes** go swimming. | Twice a week. |
| How often do you eat meat? | I **never** eat meat. | Never. |

*Every day / twice a week / at weekends* (U10–U13) + adverbios (U33) = hábitos claros.

## 6. Reading modelo: Emma

![Rutina de Emma A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-33/a1-unit33-emma-routine.png)

> My name is Emma. I **always** get up at 7 in the morning. I **usually** have breakfast with my family. I **often** go running before work. I **sometimes** meet friends for lunch. I **never** eat late at night because I like to sleep early. At weekends I **always** relax and read books.

### Comprueba

1. ¿A qué hora se levanta? → **At 7** (*always*).  
2. ¿Desayuna con la familia? → **Usually**.  
3. ¿Qué hace a menudo antes del trabajo? → **Go running**.  
4. ¿Come tarde por la noche? → **Never**.  
5. ¿Qué hace los fines de semana? → **Always relax and read books**.

## 7. Diálogo modelo

> **A:** How often do you get up early?  
> **B:** I **always** get up at 7. And you?  
> **A:** I **usually** get up at 8. Do you go running?  
> **B:** Yes — I **often** go running before work.  
> **A:** Nice! Do you eat late at night?  
> **B:** No, I **never** eat late. I like to sleep early.  
> **A:** At weekends I **always** relax and read books.  
> **B:** Me too sometimes… but I **often** meet friends!

## 8. Errores típicos de hispanohablantes

| ❌ Incorrecto | ✅ Correcto | Explicación |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *I get always up* | *I **always** get up* | Adv. antes del verbo |
| *She never is late* | *She **is never** late* | Adv. después de *be* |
| *I am always get up* | *I always get up* | No mezcles *be* + verbo |
| *She always go* | *She always **goes*** | 3.ª persona en el verbo |
| *I never don't eat* | *I **never** eat* | *Never* ya es negativo |
| *Always I get up* | *I always get up* | Orden A1 estándar |

### Tip *never*

*Never* = negativo. No uses *don't/doesn't* juntos en A1 básico:

> I **never** eat meat. ✅  
> I don’t never eat meat. ❌

## 9. Pronunciación útil

| Forma | Tip A1 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **always** | *OL-weiz* (2 sílabas). |
| **usually** | *IU-zhu-li* (cuidado con /ʒ/). |
| **often** | UK a menudo *OF-ən* (t muda frecuente). |
| **sometimes** | *SAM-taimz*. |
| **never** | *NE-və*. |

Practica la cadena: *always → usually → often → sometimes → never*.

## 9b. Mini-diálogos para memorizar

> **A:** Do you always have breakfast?  
> **B:** Yes, I usually have breakfast with my family.  
> **A:** How often do you go running?  
> **B:** I often go running before work.

> **A:** Is she ever late?  
> **B:** No. She is never late.  
> **A:** Are they busy?  
> **B:** Yes — they are always busy.

> **A:** Do you eat late at night?  
> **B:** Never. I like to sleep early.  
> **A:** What do you do at weekends?  
> **B:** I always relax and read books.

Cambia solo el adverbio: fijas la **posición**.

### Rutina de 2 minutos

1. Di 1 frase con *always*.  
2. Di 1 con *usually*.  
3. Di 1 con *often*.  
4. Di 1 con *sometimes*.  
5. Di 1 con *never*.  
6. Di 1 con *be* (*is/are* + adverbio).

Si lo unes a deportes (U32), practicas *I often play football / I usually go swimming*.

### Combinar U32 + U33

| Deporte / hobby | + frecuencia |
| :--- | :--- |
| I play football | I **always** play football on Saturdays. |
| I go swimming | I **usually** go swimming on Sundays. |
| I do yoga | I **often** do yoga in the morning. |
| I like reading | I **always** read books at weekends. |

### Mini-historia para reescribir

> I always get up at 7. I usually have breakfast. I often go running. I sometimes meet friends. I never eat late. At weekends I always relax.

Cambia los verbos por los tuyos y mantén los adverbios en el sitio correcto.

### Checklist mental

- Escala: always → usually → often → sometimes → never  
- Verbo normal → adverbio **antes**  
- *be* → adverbio **después**  
- 3.ª persona: *-s* en el **verbo**, no en el adverbio  
- *never* = sin *don't*  
- Pregunta: *How often…?*

## 10. Ejercicios prácticos (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige el adverbio

1. I \_\_\_\_ get up at 7. (100%)  
2. She \_\_\_\_ eats meat. (0%)  
3. We \_\_\_\_ go to the cinema. (a veces)  
4. I \_\_\_\_ go running before work. (a menudo)  
5. I \_\_\_\_ have breakfast with my family. (normalmente)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **always**  
2. **never**  
3. **sometimes**  
4. **often**  
5. **usually**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Posición (ordena)

1. always / I / get up / at 7  
2. never / she / is / late  
3. often / I / go / running  
4. are / they / always / busy  
5. sometimes / we / go / to the cinema

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **I always get up at 7.**  
2. **She is never late.**  
3. **I often go running.**  
4. **They are always busy.**  
5. **We sometimes go to the cinema.**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Traduce

1. Siempre me levanto a las 7.  
2. Ella nunca llega tarde.  
3. A menudo voy a correr.  
4. A veces quedo con amigos.  
5. Normalmente desayuno con mi familia.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **I always get up at 7.**  
2. **She is never late.**  
3. **I often go running.**  
4. **I sometimes meet friends.**  
5. **I usually have breakfast with my family.**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — *be* o verbo normal

1. He \_\_\_\_ never late. (*is*)  
2. They \_\_\_\_ always busy. (*are*)  
3. I \_\_\_\_ always get up early. (— / adverbio solo)  
4. She \_\_\_\_ never eats meat.  
5. We \_\_\_\_ sometimes go to the cinema.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is**  
2. **are**  
3. (nada: *I always get up early*)  
4. (nada: *She never eats meat*)  
5. (nada: *We sometimes go…*)
</details>
