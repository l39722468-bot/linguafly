## Lección 1 — Gramática: repaso

**Objetivo:** *There is/are*, posesivos, *have got*, *wearing*, preposiciones, imperativos.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. There ____ a sofa in the living room. → *is / are / have*  
2. This book ____ mine. → *is / are / have*  
3. She ____ got blue eyes. → *has / have / is*  
4. He ____ wearing a jacket. → *is / am / are*  
5. The cat is ____ the table. → *under / on / next to*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is** — *There is* + singular.  
2. **is** — *This book is mine.*  
3. **has** — *She has got.*  
4. **is** — *He is wearing.*  
5. **under** — *Under* = debajo de.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. There ____ two chairs in the room. → a) are · b) is · c) have  
7. Turn ____ at the corner. → a) left · b) right · c) straight  
8. This ____ my hand. These are my feet. → a) is · b) are · c) have  
9. The bank is ____ the pharmacy. → a) next to · b) under · c) behind  
10. I ____ wearing a red shirt. → a) am · b) is · c) are

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) are**  
7. **a) left**  
8. **a) is**  
9. **a) next to**  
10. **a) am**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. There / is / a / lamp / on / the / desk.  
12. Go / straight / past / the / park.  
13. She / has / got / long / legs / and / big / feet.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. There is a lamp on the desk.  
12. Go straight past the park.  
13. She has got long legs and big feet.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use *There is* for singular and *There are* for plural. → V / F  
15. Imperatives (*Turn left, Go straight*) give directions. → V / F

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: Module 3

**Objetivo:** casa, barrio, animales, cuerpo, ropa.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige

1. Kitchen, bedroom, bathroom ____ rooms. → a) are · b) is · c) have  
2. Next to = ____. → a) beside · b) under · c) behind  
3. Pet = ____ animal that lives with people. → a) an · b) a · c) the  
4. Head, arms, legs ____ body parts. → a) are · b) is · c) have  
5. Shirt, trousers, dress ____ clothes. → a) are · b) is · c) have

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) are**  
2. **a) beside**  
3. **a) an**  
4. **a) are**  
5. **a) are**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Relaciona (escribe la pareja)

6. There is/are · Turn left, Go straight · have/has got · am/is/are wearing → house, rooms, furniture / directions / body parts / clothes  
7. mine · dangerous · friendly · jacket → possessive pronoun / can hurt you / nice, kind / outer clothing

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. There is/are → house, rooms, furniture · Turn left, Go straight → directions · have/has got → body parts · am/is/are wearing → clothes  
7. mine → possessive pronoun · dangerous → can hurt you · friendly → nice, kind · jacket → outer clothing

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. The supermarket is ____ the bank and the library. → *between / under / on*  
9. I have ____ two arms and two hands. → *got / get / have*  
10. She is ____ a red dress. → *wearing / wear / wears*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **between**  
9. **got**  
10. **wearing**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Elige

11. Park, cinema, library are ____ in a neighbourhood. → a) places · b) animals · c) clothes  
12. This is ____. These are plural. → a) singular · b) plural · c) past  
13. Wild animals live ____ the forest. → a) in · b) on · c) at

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) places**  
12. **a) singular**  
13. **a) in**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena las palabras

14. There / are / two / chairs / in / the / room.  
15. I / am / wearing / a / shirt / and / trousers.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. There are two chairs in the room.  
15. I am wearing a shirt and trousers.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Anna's world

**Objetivo:** leer un texto que une casa, mascota, ropa, barrio y direcciones.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-30/reading-anna.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Anna"></audio>

> My name is Anna. I live in a small flat. There is a living room, a kitchen, two bedrooms and a bathroom. There is a sofa in the living room and a table next to the window. My room is small but I have got a bed and a desk. I have got a pet cat. She is very friendly. Today I am wearing a blue shirt and jeans. Near my flat there are a park and a supermarket. Go straight and turn left at the corner.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the name of the writer? → a) Anna · b) Tom · c) She  
2. What rooms are there in the flat? → a) Living room, kitchen, two bedrooms, bathroom · b) Only a bedroom · c) A park  
3. Where is the table? → a) Next to the window · b) Under the bed · c) Behind the door  
4. What has Anna got in her room? → a) A bed and a desk · b) A sofa · c) A park  
5. What pet does Anna have? → a) A cat · b) A dog · c) A rabbit

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Anna**  
2. **a) Living room, kitchen, two bedrooms, bathroom**  
3. **a) Next to the window**  
4. **a) A bed and a desk**  
5. **a) A cat**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Is the cat friendly? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say  
7. What is Anna wearing? → a) A blue shirt and jeans · b) A dress · c) A jacket  
8. What is near the flat? → a) A park and a supermarket · b) A zoo · c) A farm  
9. Which instruction does the text give for directions? → a) Go straight and turn left at the corner · b) Turn right · c) Stop here  
10. The text uses ____ to say what there is in the flat. → a) There is/are · b) I have · c) I am

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Yes**  
7. **a) A blue shirt and jeans**  
8. **a) A park and a supermarket**  
9. **a) Go straight and turn left at the corner**  
10. **a) There is/are**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text uses ____ for clothes. → a) am wearing · b) have got · c) there is  
12. Is Anna's room big or small? → a) Small · b) Big · c) The text doesn't say  
13. The main topic is ____. → a) home, pet, clothes, neighbourhood · b) food · c) work  
14. Which preposition is used for the table? → a) Next to · b) Under · c) Behind  
15. Have got is used for ____ and pet. → a) room/possessions · b) clothes only · c) directions

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) am wearing**  
12. **a) Small**  
13. **a) home, pet, clothes, neighbourhood**  
14. **a) Next to**  
15. **a) room/possessions**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Home, pet, clothes

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo de repaso del Módulo 3.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-30/listening-review.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Review"></audio>

> **A:** What is in your living room?  
> **B:** There is a sofa and there are two chairs. The table is next to the window.  
> **A:** Do you have got a pet?  
> **B:** Yes, I have got a dog. He is very friendly.  
> **A:** What are you wearing?  
> **B:** I am wearing a shirt and trousers. To get to the park go straight and turn left.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is in B's living room? → a) A sofa and two chairs · b) A bed · c) A cat  
2. Where is the table? → a) Next to the window · b) Under the bed · c) Behind the door  
3. Does B have got a pet? → a) Yes, a dog · b) No · c) A cat  
4. Is B's dog friendly? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
5. What is B wearing? → a) A shirt and trousers · b) A dress · c) A jacket

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) A sofa and two chairs**  
2. **a) Next to the window**  
3. **a) Yes, a dog**  
4. **a) Yes**  
5. **a) A shirt and trousers**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. How do you get to the park? → a) Go straight and turn left · b) Turn right · c) Stop here  
7. Which grammar does B use for the living room? → a) There is/are · b) I have · c) I am wearing  
8. Which word means "al lado de"? → a) Next to · b) Under · c) Behind  
9. The dialogue covers ____. → a) house, pet, clothes, directions · b) only food · c) only work  
10. B ____ "There is a sofa". → a) says · b) say · c) saying

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Go straight and turn left**  
7. **a) There is/are**  
8. **a) Next to**  
9. **a) house, pet, clothes, directions**  
10. **a) says**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. B ____ "I have got a dog". → a) says · b) say · c) saying  
12. Go ____ and turn left. → a) straight · b) left · c) right  
13. There ____ two chairs. → a) are · b) is · c) have  
14. I ____ wearing a shirt. → a) am · b) is · c) are  
15. Module 3 review includes ____. → a) There is/are, have got, wearing, directions · b) only grammar · c) only vocabulary

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My world

**Objetivo:** escribir uniendo casa, *have got*, *wearing* e imperativos.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. There ____ a bed in my room. → *is / are / have*  
2. The desk ____ next to the window. → *is / are / have*  
3. I ____ got two arms. → *have / has / am*  
4. She ____ wearing a dress. → *is / am / are*  
5. ____ left at the corner. → *Turn / Go / Stop*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is**  
2. **is**  
3. **have**  
4. **is**  
5. **Turn**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. There / are / two / chairs / in / the / room.  
7. I / have / got / a / friendly / dog.  
8. Go / straight / past / the / park.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. There are two chairs in the room.  
7. I have got a friendly dog.  
8. Go straight past the park.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. Two sentences: what is in your room (*There is/are*).  
10. Where is a piece of furniture (*next to / under / behind*).  
11. One *have got* + one *am wearing*.  
12. 2–3 directions (*Turn left, Go straight…*).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. There is a bed in my room. There are two chairs.  
10. The sofa is next to the window.  
11. I have got blue eyes. I am wearing a blue shirt.  
12. Go straight. Turn left at the corner. The park is on the right.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Short paragraph (4–5 sentences): home + pet + clothes.  
14. *This is mine / That is yours* with two objects.  
15. One animal + what someone is wearing (2 sentences).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
There is a living room in my house. There are two bedrooms. I have got a pet cat. She is very friendly. Today I am wearing a shirt and jeans.

**14.**  
This pen is mine. That book is yours.

**15.**  
My dog is very friendly. My sister is wearing a red dress.

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Bloque | Recuerda |
|--------|----------|
| **Casa** | rooms + furniture + *mine/yours* |
| **Existencia** | *There is / There are* |
| **Lugar** | *in/on/under/behind* + *next to/between/opposite* |
| **Calle** | *Turn left · Go straight · Past the…* |
| **Cuerpo** | *have/has got · this/these · feet* |
| **Ropa** | *am/is/are wearing* |
