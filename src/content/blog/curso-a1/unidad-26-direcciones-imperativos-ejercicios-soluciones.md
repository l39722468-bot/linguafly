## Lección 1 — Gramática: imperativos

**Objetivo:** *Turn left / right*, *Go straight*, *Past the…*, *at the corner*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ____ ____ at the corner. → *Turn left / Go straight / Turn right*  
2. ____ ____ for 100 metres. → *Go straight / Turn left / Past the*  
3. ____ ____ bank and then turn right. → *Past the / Turn left at the / Go straight*  
4. ____ ____ at the traffic lights. → *Turn right / Go straight / Past the*  
5. Turn left ____ ____ ____ corner. → *at the / past the / go to the*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Turn left** — *Turn left* = gira a la izquierda.  
2. **Go straight** — *Go straight* = sigue recto.  
3. **Past the** — *Past the* = pasa el/la.  
4. **Turn right** — *Turn right* = gira a la derecha.  
5. **at the** — *At the corner* = en la esquina.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. We use ____ for instructions or directions. → a) imperative (base form) · b) past tense · c) -ing form  
7. "Turn left" = ____. → a) Gira a la izquierda · b) Sigue recto · c) Pasa el  
8. "Go straight" = ____. → a) Sigue recto · b) Gira a la derecha · c) Para  
9. Past the bank = ____. → a) Pasa el banco · b) Gira en el banco · c) Ve al banco  
10. At the corner = ____. → a) where two streets meet · b) in the park · c) inside the building

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) imperative (base form)**  
7. **a) Gira a la izquierda**  
8. **a) Sigue recto**  
9. **a) Pasa el banco**  
10. **a) where two streets meet**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. Turn / left / at / the / corner.  
12. Go / straight / past / the / bank.  
13. Turn / right / at / the / traffic lights.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. Turn left at the corner.  
12. Go straight past the bank.  
13. Turn right at the traffic lights.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. Imperatives use the base form of the verb (*turn, go, walk*). → V / F  
15. We say "Past the" + place to mean "go past". → V / F

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero** — *Turn left. Go straight.* Sin sujeto.  
15. **Verdadero** — *Past the bank* = pasa el banco.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: indicaciones

**Objetivo:** *turn left/right*, *go straight*, *past the*, *corner*, *traffic lights*, *on the left/right*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige

1. ____ = turn to the left side. → a) Turn left · b) Turn right · c) Go straight  
2. Go straight = ____. → a) continue in the same direction · b) stop · c) turn  
3. Past the bank = ____. → a) go by the bank · b) enter the bank · c) turn at the bank  
4. At the corner = ____. → a) where two streets meet · b) in the middle of the street · c) at the end  
5. Traffic lights = ____. → a) red, green, yellow signals · b) a shop · c) a park

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Turn left**  
2. **a) continue in the same direction**  
3. **a) go by the bank**  
4. **a) where two streets meet**  
5. **a) red, green, yellow signals**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Relaciona (escribe la pareja)

6. Turn left · Turn right · Go straight · Past the bank → gira a la izquierda / gira a la derecha / sigue recto / pasa el banco  
7. corner · traffic lights · crossing → where two streets meet / red, green, yellow / place to cross the street

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. Turn left → gira a la izquierda · Turn right → gira a la derecha · Go straight → sigue recto · Past the bank → pasa el banco  
7. corner → where two streets meet · traffic lights → red, green, yellow · crossing → place to cross the street

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. ____ ____ at the corner. → *Turn left / Go straight / Past the*  
9. ____ ____ for 200 metres. → *Go straight / Turn left / Turn right*  
10. Past ____ supermarket and the cinema is on the left. → *the / bank / corner*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **Turn left**  
9. **Go straight**  
10. **the**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Elige

11. "On the left" = ____. → a) a la izquierda · b) a la derecha · c) recto  
12. "On the right" = ____. → a) a la derecha · b) a la izquierda · c) al final  
13. Cross the street = ____. → a) go to the other side · b) turn left · c) stop

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) a la izquierda**  
12. **a) a la derecha**  
13. **a) go to the other side**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena las palabras

14. Go / straight / and / turn / right / at / the / bank.  
15. The / pharmacy / is / on / the / left.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. Go straight and turn right at the bank.  
15. The pharmacy is on the left.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: To the library

**Objetivo:** leer indicaciones con imperativos (*Go straight*, *Turn left*, *Past the…*).

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-26/reading-library.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: To the library"></audio>

> To get to the library from the station, go straight out of the station. Go straight for about 200 metres. Turn left at the corner. Past the bank on your right. The library is on the left, next to the pharmacy. You can't miss it.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Where do the directions start? → a) From the station · b) From the library · c) From the bank  
2. What do you do first? → a) Go straight out of the station · b) Turn left · c) Past the bank  
3. How far do you go straight? → a) About 200 metres · b) 100 metres · c) 500 metres  
4. Where do you turn left? → a) At the corner · b) At the bank · c) At the pharmacy  
5. Where is the bank? → a) On your right · b) On your left · c) In front of you

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) From the station**  
2. **a) Go straight out of the station**  
3. **a) About 200 metres**  
4. **a) At the corner**  
5. **a) On your right**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Where is the library? → a) On the left, next to the pharmacy · b) On the right · c) At the corner  
7. What does "You can't miss it" mean? → a) It is easy to find · b) It is closed · c) It is far  
8. Which word means "gira"? → a) Turn · b) Go · c) Past  
9. Which phrase means "sigue recto"? → a) Go straight · b) Turn left · c) Past the  
10. The text gives ____. → a) directions to the library · b) directions to the station · c) information about the bank

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) On the left, next to the pharmacy**  
7. **a) It is easy to find**  
8. **a) Turn**  
9. **a) Go straight**  
10. **a) directions to the library**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. What is next to the library? → a) The pharmacy · b) The bank · c) The station  
12. The instructions use the ____ form (*Turn, Go, Past*). → a) imperative · b) past tense · c) question  
13. How many steps are in the directions? → a) Several (straight, turn left, past the bank) · b) One · c) Two  
14. Which place do you go past? → a) The bank · b) The pharmacy · c) The library  
15. At the corner = ____. → a) en la esquina · b) en el banco · c) en la biblioteca

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) The pharmacy**  
12. **a) imperative**  
13. **a) Several (straight, turn left, past the bank)**  
14. **a) The bank**  
15. **a) en la esquina**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: How do I get to the cinema?

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo de direcciones.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-26/listening-cinema.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Cinema directions"></audio>

> **A:** How do I get to the cinema?  
> **B:** Go straight along this street. Turn left at the corner. Past the supermarket and the cinema is on the right.  
> **A:** Thanks!  
> **B:** You're welcome.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What does A ask? → a) How to get to the cinema · b) Where is the supermarket · c) Where is the corner  
2. What do you do first? → a) Go straight along this street · b) Turn left · c) Past the supermarket  
3. Where do you turn left? → a) At the corner · b) At the supermarket · c) At the cinema  
4. What do you go past? → a) The supermarket · b) The cinema · c) The corner  
5. Where is the cinema? → a) On the right · b) On the left · c) At the corner

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) How to get to the cinema**  
2. **a) Go straight along this street**  
3. **a) At the corner**  
4. **a) The supermarket**  
5. **a) On the right**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. B uses ____ to give directions. → a) Go straight, Turn left, Past the · b) Please, Sorry · c) Yes, No  
7. What does A say at the end? → a) Thanks! · b) Turn left · c) Where is it?  
8. The dialogue is ____. → a) about giving directions · b) about shopping · c) about family  
9. Which verb is used for "sigue recto"? → a) Go · b) Turn · c) Past  
10. B ____ directions to A. → a) gives · b) give · c) giving

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Go straight, Turn left, Past the**  
7. **a) Thanks!**  
8. **a) about giving directions**  
9. **a) Go**  
10. **a) gives**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. After the supermarket, the cinema ____ on the right. → a) is · b) are · c) be  
12. Go straight along ____ street. → a) this · b) the · c) a  
13. The ____ order is: straight, then left, then past the supermarket. → a) correct · b) wrong · c) unknown  
14. B says "You're welcome" ____ after Thanks. → a) in response · b) before · c) never  
15. Which form does B use (*Go, Turn, Past*)? → a) Imperative · b) Past tense · c) Question

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) is**  
12. **a) this**  
13. **a) correct**  
14. **a) in response**  
15. **a) Imperative**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: Give directions

**Objetivo:** escribir indicaciones con imperativos y *How do I get to…?*

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ____ ____ at the corner. → *Turn left / Go straight / Turn right*  
2. ____ ____ for 200 metres. → *Go straight / Turn left / Past the*  
3. ____ ____ bank on your right. → *Past the / Turn left at the / Go to the*  
4. The library ____ on the left. → *is / are / be*  
5. Turn ____ at the traffic lights. → *right / left / straight*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Turn left**  
2. **Go straight**  
3. **Past the**  
4. **is**  
5. **right**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. Go / straight / past / the / supermarket.  
7. Turn / right / at / the / corner.  
8. The / pharmacy / is / on / the / right.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. Go straight past the supermarket.  
7. Turn right at the corner.  
8. The pharmacy is on the right.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. Write 2–3 directions from A to B (*Turn left, Go straight, Past the…*).  
10. Write *How do I get to the…?* + a short answer.  
11. Write 4–5 steps (*Turn left/right, Go straight, Past the, At the corner, On the left/right*).  
12. Write *Where is the pharmacy?* + answer with *on the left/right* or *next to…*.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. Go straight for 100 metres. Turn left at the corner. Past the bank. The cinema is on the right.  
10. How do I get to the library? Go straight and turn left at the corner.  
11. Go straight out of the station. Turn left at the corner. Past the bank on your right. The library is on the left.  
12. Where is the pharmacy? It's on the left, next to the bank.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Short paragraph: directions to a place (station → cinema or similar).  
14. 3 imperative sentences: one with *Turn*, one with *Go straight*, one with *Past the*.  
15. Short dialogue: *Thanks* / *You're welcome* after directions.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
To get to the cinema, go straight along the street. Turn right at the corner. Past the supermarket. The cinema is on the left. You can't miss it.

**14.**  
Turn left at the corner. Go straight for 200 metres. Past the bank.

**15.**  
Thanks! You're welcome.

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Tema | Recuerda |
|------|----------|
| **Imperativo** | Forma base, sin sujeto |
| **Girar** | *Turn left / right* **at** the… |
| **Recto** | *Go straight* (*for / along / past*) |
| **Pasar** | *Past the* + lugar |
| **Destino** | *on the left / on the right* |
| **Pregunta** | *How do I get to…?* |
| **Cierre** | *You can't miss it* · *Thanks* |
