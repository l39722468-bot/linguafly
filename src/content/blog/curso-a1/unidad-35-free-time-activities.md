En la [Unidad 34](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-34-and-but-because) uniste ideas con *and / but / because*. En la **Unidad 35 del curso A1** (*Free Time Activities*) aprendes a hablar de tu **tiempo libre**: ***go for a walk***, ***go shopping***, ***watching TV***, ***playing video games***.

Repasas *go + -ing* de la [Unidad 32](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-32-deportes-hobbies-play-go-do) y *like + -ing* de la [Unidad 18](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-18-gustos-like-ing-because). Incluye **audios**, **esquemas** y **ejercicios**.

> **Repaso útil:** [Unidad 34 — And, but, because](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-34-and-but-because) · [Unidad 32 — Sports & Hobbies](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-32-deportes-hobbies-play-go-do)

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 35 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Usar ***go for a walk*** e ***go shopping***.
- Hablar de gustos con ***like / enjoy + -ing***.
- Usar vocabulario: *watching TV, video games, walking, free time*.
- Preguntar ***What do you do in your free time?***.
- Combinar con frecuencia (U33) y conectores (U34).

---

## 1. Dos patrones clave

| Patrón | Uso | Ejemplos |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **go + frase** | actividades con *go* | go for a walk, go shopping |
| **like / enjoy + -ing** | gustos de ocio | like watching TV, enjoy walking |

![Patrones free time A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-35/a1-unit35-free-time-patterns.png)

```
go for a walk / go shopping
like + watching / playing / walking / going…
```

### Tip

*Go shopping* (sin *to the*). *Go for a walk* (con *for a*). Memoriza las dos frases enteras.

## 2. *Go for a walk* — ir de paseo

> I **go for a walk** every evening.  
> I **go for a walk** in the park.  
> We **go for a walk** every Sunday.  
> She **goes for a walk** every day.  
> At weekends we sometimes **go for a walk** together.  
> In my free time I **go for a walk**.

También: *like **going** for a walk* (= te gusta ir de paseo).

### Contraste útil

| ✅ | ❌ |
| :--- | :--- |
| go shopping | go to shopping |
| go for a walk | go to a walk |
| go to the shops | go the shops |

*Go to the shops* también es correcto (ir a las tiendas). En esta unidad priorizamos ***go shopping***.

### Regla (repaso U18)

Tras *like / love / enjoy / don't like* → **verbo + -ing**.

| ❌ | ✅ |
| :--- | :--- |
| I like watch TV | I like **watching** TV |
| He likes play video games | He likes **playing** video games |
| We enjoy walk | We enjoy **walking** |

## 7. Reading modelo: Mia

![Tiempo libre de Mia A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-35/a1-unit35-mia-free-time.png)

> My name is Mia. In my free time I like **going for a walk** in the park and **watching** TV. I often **watch** films at home. I don't like **going shopping** because it is too busy. My brother likes **playing** video games and **walking** the dog. At weekends we sometimes **go for a walk** together in the morning.

### Comprueba

1. ¿Qué le gusta a Mia? → **Going for a walk and watching TV**.  
2. ¿Le gusta ir de compras? → **No — too busy**.  
3. ¿Qué ve a menudo? → **Films at home**.  
4. ¿Qué le gusta a su hermano? → **Video games and walking the dog**.  
5. ¿Cuándo pasean juntos? → **Sometimes at weekends in the morning**.

Fíjate: *like going / watching / playing* + *go for a walk* + *often / sometimes* (U33) + *because* (U34).

## 8. Diálogo modelo

> **A:** What do you do in your free time?  
> **B:** I like going for a walk in the park and watching TV. And you?  
> **A:** I often watch films at home. Do you like going shopping?  
> **B:** No, I don't — because it's too busy.  
> **A:** What about your brother?  
> **B:** He likes playing video games and walking the dog.  
> **A:** Nice! At weekends we sometimes go for a walk together.

## 9. Errores típicos de hispanohablantes

| ❌ Incorrecto | ✅ Correcto | Explicación |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *go to shopping* | ***go shopping*** | Sin *to* |
| *go to a walk* | ***go for a walk*** | *for a walk* |
| *I like watch TV* | *I like **watching** TV* | *like + -ing* |
| *She go shopping* | *She **goes** shopping* | 3.ª persona |
| *I like play video games* | *I like **playing** video games* | *-ing* |
| *in my free times* | *in my **free time*** | Singular habitual |

## 9b. Pronunciación útil

| Forma | Tip A1 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **walk / walking** | *wok* · *WO-king* (l muda en muchos acentos). |
| **shopping** | *SHO-ping* (doble p). |
| **watching** | *WO-ching*. |
| **video games** | *VI-di-ou geimz*. |
| **free time** | une: *freeˈtime*. |

Practica: *go for a walk → go shopping → like watching TV*.

## 9c. Mini-diálogos para memorizar

> **A:** Do you go for a walk?  
> **B:** Yes, every evening in the park.  
> **A:** Do you go shopping at weekends?  
> **B:** Sometimes — but I don't like it when it's busy.

> **A:** What do you like doing at home?  
> **B:** Watching TV and playing video games.  
> **A:** How often do you watch films?  
> **B:** I often watch films at night.

> **A:** What does your brother like?  
> **B:** Playing video games and walking the dog.  
> **A:** Do you go for a walk together?  
> **B:** Yes — sometimes at weekends.

Cambia solo el día o el lugar: fijas las **estructuras**.

## 9d. Plantillas para copiar

2. She / He goes shopping on \_\_\_\_.  
3. I like watching \_\_\_\_.

5. We enjoy walking in \_\_\_\_.  
6. What do you do in your free time?

8. At weekends we sometimes go for a walk together.

### Más frases para memorizar

> I go for a walk every evening.  
> She goes shopping on Saturdays.  
> We go shopping at weekends.  
> I go for a walk in the park.  
> We go for a walk every Sunday.  
> I like watching TV.  
> She likes watching TV.  
> She likes watching TV at night.  
> He likes playing video games.  
> I like watching TV and playing video games.  
> We enjoy walking in the park.  
> I often watch films at home.  
> I don't like going shopping because it is too busy.  
> My brother likes playing video games and walking the dog.  
> At weekends we sometimes go for a walk together in the morning.  
> In my free time I like going for a walk in the park and watching TV.  
> What do you do in your free time?  
> In my free time I go for a walk.  
> She goes for a walk every day.  
> They like going shopping on Saturdays.

Lee la lista dos veces. Luego describe **tu** free time en 8 frases.

### Rutina de 2 minutos

1. Di 2 frases *go for a walk*.  
2. Di 2 frases *go shopping*.  
3. Di 2 *like + -ing*.  
4. Responde *What do you do in your free time?*

Si lo unes a U33: *I often go for a walk. I sometimes go shopping. I never play video games.*

### Combinar U32 + U33 + U34 + U35

| Unidad | Aporta | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| U32 | play/go/do | I go swimming |
| U33 | frequency | I often… |
| U34 | connectors | …but / because… |
| **U35** | free time phrases | I go for a walk / like watching TV |

Modelo largo:

> In my free time I often go for a walk in the park **but** I don't like going shopping **because** it's too busy. I like watching TV **and** playing video games.

### Mini-historia para reescribir

> In my free time I like going for a walk and watching TV. I often watch films at home. I don't like going shopping because it's too busy. My brother likes playing video games. At weekends we sometimes go for a walk together.

Cambia *park → beach*, *films → series*, *brother → sister*, y mantén *go / like + -ing*.

### Tour guiado (modelo Mia)

> My name is Mia. In my free time I like going for a walk in the park and watching TV. I often watch films at home. I don't like going shopping because it is too busy. My brother likes playing video games and walking the dog. At weekends we sometimes go for a walk together in the morning.

Grábate leyéndolo. Luego improvisa con **tu** familia.

### Frases extra para practicar en voz alta

> What do you do in your free time?  
> I go for a walk every evening.  
> She goes shopping on Saturdays.  
> I like watching TV and playing video games.  
> We enjoy walking in the park.  
> I often watch films at home.  
> I don't like going shopping because it's too busy.  
> At weekends we sometimes go for a walk together.

Personaliza parque, día y actividad.

### Checklist mental

- he/she → *goes*  
- *like / enjoy + -ing*  
- *What do you do in your free time?*  
- Combina con *often / sometimes* y *because*

## 10. Ejercicios prácticos (con soluciones)

2. She \_\_\_\_ shopping on Saturdays.  
3. I like \_\_\_\_ TV.  
4. He likes \_\_\_\_ video games.  
5. We enjoy \_\_\_\_ in the park.

<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **go**  
2. **goes**  
3. **watching**  
4. **playing**  
5. **walking**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Elige la forma correcta

1. go / goes shopping → She \_\_\_\_ shopping.  
2. like watch / like watching → I \_\_\_\_ TV.  
3. go for a walk / go to a walk → I \_\_\_\_ every Sunday.  
4. go shopping / go to shopping → We \_\_\_\_ at weekends.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
