## Lección 1 — Gramática: horarios

**Objetivo:** Present Simple para horarios fijos (*leaves, arrives, departs*).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. The train ____ at nine o'clock. → *leaves / leave / leaving*  
2. The bus ____ at 8. → *departs / depart / departing*  
3. My flight ____ at ten. → *leaves / leave / leaving*  
4. The plane ____ at five o'clock. → *arrives / arrive / arriving*  
5. It ____ London at half past ten. → *arrives in / arrives at / arrive*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **leaves** — horarios = Present Simple.  
2. **departs**  
3. **leaves**  
4. **arrives**  
5. **arrives in** — *arrive in* + ciudad.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. For fixed times we use ____. → a) Present Simple (leaves, arrives) · b) only Past Simple · c) only -ing  
7. *Leaves at nine* talks about ____. → a) schedule, fixed time · b) a wish only · c) past habit only  
8. The train ____ at nine. → a) leaves · b) leave · c) leaving  
9. Correct: ____. → a) The bus departs at 8. · b) The bus departing at 8. · c) The bus depart at 8.  
10. *Arrives* means ____. → a) reaches a place (time) · b) leaves forever · c) stays home

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Present Simple (leaves, arrives)**  
7. **a) schedule, fixed time**  
8. **a) leaves**  
9. **a) The bus departs at 8.**  
10. **a) reaches a place (time)**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. The / train / leaves / at / nine o'clock.  
12. The / plane / arrives / at / five o'clock.  
13. My / flight / departs / at / ten / in the morning.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. The train leaves at nine o'clock.  
12. The plane arrives at five o'clock.  
13. My flight departs at ten in the morning.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. Timetables often use Present Simple: *The train leaves at 9.* → True / False  
15. We say *arrive in London* (city) / *arrive at the station*. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: verbos de viaje

**Objetivo:** *arrive, depart, visit, stay, explore*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *Arrive* = ____. → a) reach a place · b) leave forever · c) only sleep  
2. *Depart* = ____. → a) leave, go away · b) stay · c) visit only  
3. *Visit* = ____. → a) go to see a place or person · b) leave · c) depart  
4. *Stay* = ____. → a) remain in a place · b) fly only · c) depart  
5. *Explore* = ____. → a) discover, look around · b) sleep only · c) depart

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) reach a place**  
2. **a) leave, go away**  
3. **a) go to see a place or person**  
4. **a) remain in a place**  
5. **a) discover, look around**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Empareja

6. Empareja: arrive / depart / visit / stay / explore → reach a place · leave · go to see · remain · discover  
7. Empareja: the train arrives / the plane departs / visit Paris / stay at a hotel / explore the city → reaches the station · leaves the airport · go to see Paris · sleep there · look around

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **arrive → reach a place** · **depart → leave** · **visit → go to see** · **stay → remain** · **explore → discover**  
7. **the train arrives → reaches the station** · **the plane departs → leaves the airport** · **visit Paris → go to see Paris** · **stay at a hotel → sleep there** · **explore the city → look around**

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. The train ____ at ten. → *arrives / arrive / arriving*  
9. I want to ____ the museums. → *visit / visits / visiting*  
10. I ____ at a hotel for three nights. → *stay / stays / staying*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **arrives**  
9. **visit**  
10. **stay**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Definiciones

11. *Depart* means ____. → a) leave · b) arrive · c) stay  
12. *Explore* means ____. → a) look around, discover · b) only sleep · c) only depart  
13. *Stay at a hotel* means ____. → a) sleep there for some days · b) leave forever · c) fly only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) leave**  
12. **a) look around, discover**  
13. **a) sleep there for some days**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *Arrive* = reach a place; *depart* = leave. → True / False  
15. *Visit* and *explore* are useful for city trips. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: London trip

**Objetivo:** leer un plan de viaje con horarios y verbos de viaje.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-43/reading-london.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: London trip"></audio>

> I am going to London next week. My train leaves at eight o'clock in the morning. It arrives in London at half past ten. I stay at a hotel for three nights. I want to visit the museums and explore the city. My flight departs on Sunday at five o'clock.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Where is the writer going? → a) To London · b) To Paris · c) To Madrid  
2. When does the train leave? → a) At eight o'clock · b) At ten · c) At noon  
3. When does it arrive in London? → a) At half past ten · b) At eight · c) At five  
4. Where does the writer stay? → a) At a hotel · b) At school · c) At the airport only  
5. How many nights? → a) Three · b) One · c) Ten

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) To London**  
2. **a) At eight o'clock**  
3. **a) At half past ten**  
4. **a) At a hotel**  
5. **a) Three**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. What does the writer want to do? → a) Visit museums and explore the city · b) Only sleep · c) Only work  
7. When does the flight depart? → a) On Sunday at five · b) On Monday at eight · c) Never  
8. *Departs* means ____. → a) leaves · b) arrives · c) stays  
9. *Explore* means ____. → a) discover, look around · b) only sleep · c) only depart  
10. *Stay at a hotel* means ____. → a) sleep there for some days · b) leave forever · c) fly only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Visit museums and explore the city**  
7. **a) On Sunday at five**  
8. **a) leaves**  
9. **a) discover, look around**  
10. **a) sleep there for some days**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text is about ____. → a) a trip to London · b) only food · c) only sports  
12. *Arrives* means ____. → a) reaches a place · b) leaves forever · c) stays home  
13. *Visit* means ____. → a) go to see · b) depart · c) never go  
14. The train ____ at eight. → a) leaves · b) leave · c) leaving  
15. The main topic is ____. → a) Travel plans: leave, arrive, stay, visit, explore · b) Cooking · c) Music

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) a trip to London**  
12. **a) reaches a place**  
13. **a) go to see**  
14. **a) leaves**  
15. **a) Travel plans: leave, arrive, stay, visit, explore**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Schedules

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo sobre tren, hotel y vuelo.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-43/listening-schedules.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Schedules"></audio>

> **A:** When does your train leave?  
> **B:** It leaves at nine o'clock.  
> **A:** What time does it arrive in London?  
> **B:** At half past eleven. I stay at a hotel for two nights.  
> **A:** Do you visit museums?  
> **B:** Yes, I want to explore the city. My flight departs on Monday at six in the evening.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. When does the train leave? → a) At nine o'clock · b) At eight · c) At noon  
2. When does it arrive in London? → a) At half past eleven · b) At nine · c) At five  
3. Where does B stay? → a) At a hotel · b) At school · c) At the airport only  
4. How many nights? → a) Two · b) Five · c) Ten  
5. Does B visit museums? → a) Yes, and explore the city · b) No · c) Never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) At nine o'clock**  
2. **a) At half past eleven**  
3. **a) At a hotel**  
4. **a) Two**  
5. **a) Yes, and explore the city**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. When does the flight depart? → a) On Monday at six · b) On Sunday at five · c) Never  
7. *Departs* here means ____. → a) schedule, leaves · b) arrives · c) stays  
8. *Explore* means ____. → a) discover, look around · b) only sleep · c) only depart  
9. The dialogue is about ____. → a) travel plans · b) food · c) sports  
10. *Arrive* means ____. → a) reach a place · b) leave forever · c) stay home

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) On Monday at six**  
7. **a) schedule, leaves**  
8. **a) discover, look around**  
9. **a) travel plans**  
10. **a) reach a place**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Leave* means ____. → a) go away / depart · b) arrive · c) stay  
12. *Stay at a hotel* means ____. → a) sleep there · b) fly only · c) depart  
13. A asks about ____. → a) when the train leaves and when it arrives · b) only food · c) only sports  
14. *Visit* means ____. → a) go to see · b) depart · c) never go  
15. The topic is ____. → a) travel: train, hotel, flight, explore · b) cooking · c) music

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) go away / depart**  
12. **a) sleep there**  
13. **a) when the train leaves and when it arrives**

15. **a) travel: train, hotel, flight, explore**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My trip

**Objetivo:** escribir horarios y planes de viaje.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. The train ____ at nine o'clock. → *leaves / leave / leaving*  
2. The plane ____ at five. → *arrives / arrive / arriving*  
3. I ____ at a hotel for three nights. → *stay / stays / staying*  
4. I want to ____ the museums. → *visit / visits / visiting*  
5. My flight ____ at ten. → *departs / depart / departing*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **leaves**  
2. **arrives**  
3. **stay**  
4. **visit**  
5. **departs**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. The / train / leaves / at / nine o'clock.  
7. The / plane / arrives / at / five o'clock.  
8. I / want / to / explore / the / city.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. The train leaves at nine o'clock.  
7. The plane arrives at five o'clock.  
8. I want to explore the city.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Escribe la hora de salida de tu tren/bus/vuelo.  
10. Escribe dónde te alojas y cuántas noches.  
11. Dos frases: *departs* + *arrives*.  
12. Pregunta y respuesta: *What time does the train leave?*  
13. Párrafo corto (4–5 frases) sobre un viaje.  
14. Dos frases: *visit* + *explore*.  
15. Horario de bus: *departs* + *arrive*.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. My train leaves at eight o'clock.  
10. I stay at a hotel for three nights.  
11. My flight departs at ten. It arrives at two o'clock.  
12. What time does the train leave? It leaves at nine.  
13. My train leaves at eight. It arrives in London at noon. I stay at a hotel. I want to visit museums and explore the city.  
14. I want to visit the museums. I want to explore the city.  
15. The bus departs at seven. I arrive at half past eight.

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Verbo | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **leave / depart** | salir | The train **leaves** at 9. |
| **arrive** | llegar | It **arrives in** London at 10:30. |
| **stay** | alojarse | I **stay** at a hotel. |
| **visit** | visitar | I want to **visit** museums. |
| **explore** | explorar | I want to **explore** the city. |
