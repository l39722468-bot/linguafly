## Lección 1 — Gramática: artículos, plurales y demostrativos

**Objetivo:** Usar *a/an*, plurales regulares y *this/that/these/those*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. This is ____ book. → *a / an / the*  
2. This is ____ eraser. → *a / an / the*  
3. I have two ____ on my desk. → *pen / pens / penes*  
4. ____ is my bag. (near) → *This / That / These*  
5. ____ are my books. (near) → *This / That / These*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a** — *book* empieza por consonante.  
2. **an** — *eraser* empieza por vocal.  
3. **pens** — plural regular + *-s*.  
4. **This** — singular + cerca.  
5. **These** — plural + cerca.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. Which is correct?  
   a) She has a apple. · b) She has an apple. · c) She has the apple.  
7. Correct demonstrative (singular, near):  
   a) This is a ruler. · b) These is a ruler. · c) Those is a ruler.  
8. Correct (plural, far):  
   a) This are pens. · b) That are pens. · c) Those are pens.  
9. Plural of "book"?  
   a) books · b) bookes · c) bookies  
10. Which sentence is correct?  
    a) I see a orange on the table. · b) I see an orange on the table. · c) I see the orange on the table.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **b) She has an apple.**  
7. **a) This is a ruler.**  
8. **c) Those are pens.**  
9. **a) books**  
10. **b) I see an orange on the table.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. is / This / a / desk.  
12. are / These / pens.  
13. is / That / an / eraser.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. This is a desk.  
12. These are pens.  
13. That is an eraser.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We say "a eraser" because eraser starts with a vowel. → ?  
15. "These are books" is the correct plural of "This is a book". → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Falso** — Se dice *an eraser* (empieza por vocal).  
15. **Verdadero**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: números, objetos y colores

**Objetivo:** Números 1–10, objetos del aula y colores básicos.

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige la opción correcta

1. Which number comes after "three"? → a) two · b) four · c) five  
2. Which number comes before "nine"? → a) six · b) seven · c) eight  
3. What do you use to write in a notebook? → a) a ruler · b) a book · c) a pen  
4. What do you use to remove pencil marks? → a) a bag · b) an eraser · c) a desk  
5. What color is the sky on a clear day? → a) red · b) green · c) blue  
6. What color is the sun? → a) black · b) yellow · c) white

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b) four**  
2. **c) eight**  
3. **c) a pen**  
4. **b) an eraser**  
5. **c) blue**  
6. **b) yellow**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–10 — Une la palabra con su significado

**7.** one · two · three · four · five → uno · dos · tres · cuatro · cinco  

**8.** six · seven · eight · nine · ten → seis · siete · ocho · nueve · diez  

**9.** pen · book · ruler · eraser · bag → bolígrafo · libro · regla · goma · mochila  

**10.** red · blue · green · yellow · black → rojo · azul · verde · amarillo · negro

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

7. one→uno · two→dos · three→tres · four→cuatro · five→cinco  
8. six→seis · seven→siete · eight→ocho · nine→nueve · ten→diez  
9. pen→bolígrafo · book→libro · ruler→regla · eraser→goma · bag→mochila  
10. red→rojo · blue→azul · green→verde · yellow→amarillo · black→negro

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Completa

11. There are ____ books on the desk. (7) → *six / seven / eight*  
12. I measure the paper with ____. → *a pen / a ruler / an eraser*  
13. The grass is ____. → *red / blue / green*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **seven**  
12. **a ruler**  
13. **green**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Elige la frase correcta

14. Which sentence is correct?  
    a) There are ten pen on the desk. · b) There are ten pens on the desk. · c) There are ten pens in the bag.  
15. Which description is correct?  
    a) It is a black eraser. · b) It is a black erasers. · c) It is an black eraser.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **b) There are ten pens on the desk.**  
15. **a) It is a black eraser.**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Pablo's classroom

**Objetivo:** Leer la descripción de un aula con artículos, números y colores.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-4/reading-pablo.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Pablo"></audio>

> Hi! My name is Pablo. This is my classroom. It is a big room. There is a board on the wall. There are twenty chairs and ten desks. On my desk, there is a blue pen, a red notebook and an eraser. My pencil is green. My bag is black. Are there books on your desk? Yes, there are three books. Is that a ruler? Yes, it is! This is a great classroom!

### Ejercicios 1–8 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Who is speaking? → a) María · b) Pablo · c) The teacher · d) Sofía  
2. What is on the wall? → a) A window · b) A clock · c) A board · d) A desk  
3. How many chairs are there? → a) Ten · b) Three · c) Twenty · d) Four  
4. What is on Pablo's desk? → a) green pen + black notebook · b) blue pen, red notebook and eraser · c) ruler + three books · d) eraser + yellow pencil  
5. What color is the pencil? → a) Blue · b) Red · c) Black · d) Green  
6. What color is the bag? → a) Red · b) Green · c) Black · d) Blue  
7. How many books are there? → a) Two · b) Five · c) Four · d) Three  
8. What does "Yes, it is!" confirm? → a) bag is black · b) there is a ruler · c) pencil is green · d) books on desk

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b) Pablo**  
2. **c) A board**  
3. **c) Twenty**  
4. **b) blue pen, red notebook and eraser**  
5. **d) Green**  
6. **c) Black**  
7. **d) Three**  
8. **b) there is a ruler**

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Verdadero o falso

9. The room is small. → ?  
10. There is a board on the wall. → ?  
11. Pablo's bag is green. → ?  
12. There are three books on the desk. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. **Falso** (*It is a big room*)  
10. **Verdadero**  
11. **Falso** (*My bag is black*)  
12. **Verdadero**

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Comprensión global

13. Main topic:  
    a) description of Pablo's classroom · b) a family story · c) a list of colors · d) classroom instructions  
14. What does "This is a big room" show?  
    a) Demonstrative + verb + article + adjective + noun · b) Plural demonstrative · c) Possessive · d) Article + adjective + noun only  
15. Which demonstrative for plural nearby?  
    a) This · b) That · c) Those · d) These

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

13. **a) description of Pablo's classroom**  
14. **a) Demonstrative + verb + article + adjective + noun**  
15. **d) These**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Classroom objects

**Objetivo:** Escuchar un diálogo sobre objetos del aula, colores y cantidades.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-4/listening-classroom.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Classroom"></audio>

> **Student 1:** What is this?  
> **Student 2:** It is a pencil. It is yellow.  
> **Student 1:** And those?  
> **Student 2:** Those are erasers. There are four erasers.  
> **Student 1:** Is that a bag?  
> **Student 2:** Yes, it is. It is a red bag.  
> **Student 1:** How many books are there?  
> **Student 2:** There are six books on the desk.

### Ejercicios 1–8 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the first object? → a) A book · b) A pencil · c) An eraser · d) A bag  
2. What color is the pencil? → a) Red · b) Blue · c) Yellow · d) Green  
3. What are "those"? → a) Books · b) Bags · c) Pencils · d) Erasers  
4. How many erasers are there? → a) Two · b) Three · c) Four · d) Six  
5. Is that a bag? → a) No, it isn't · b) Yes, it is · c) It is a pencil · d) It is yellow  
6. What color is the bag? → a) Yellow · b) Blue · c) Black · d) Red  
7. How many books are on the desk? → a) Three · b) Four · c) Five · d) Six  
8. Who asks the questions? → a) Student 1 · b) Student 2 · c) The teacher · d) Pablo

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b) A pencil**  
2. **c) Yellow**  
3. **d) Erasers**  
4. **c) Four**  
5. **b) Yes, it is**  
6. **d) Red**  
7. **d) Six**  
8. **a) Student 1**

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Verdadero o falso

9. The pencil is red. → ?  
10. There are four erasers. → ?  
11. The bag is blue. → ?  
12. There are six books on the desk. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. **Falso** (yellow)  
10. **Verdadero**  
11. **Falso** (red)  
12. **Verdadero**

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Comprensión global

    a) Identifying classroom objects by color and quantity · b) school subjects · c) food · d) family  
14. Demonstrative for plural far away:  
    a) This · b) That · c) These · d) Those  
15. Structure of "There are six books":  
    a) There are + number + plural noun · b) There is + article + noun · c) Pronoun + verb + adjective · d) Demonstrative + noun + verb

<details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My desk

**Objetivo:** Escribir con *a/an*, plurales, demostrativos y descripciones del aula.

### Modelo

> This is a blue pen. That is an eraser.  
> These are red books. Those are black bags.  
> This is a yellow pencil. That is a black bag. These are green erasers.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. This is ____ pencil. (*a / an*)  
2. That is ____ eraser. (*a / an*)  
3. These are ____. (plural of *book*)  
4. ____ are my pens. (Near me.)  
5. ____ is a ruler. (Far away.)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a**  
2. **an**  
3. **books**  
4. **These**  
5. **That**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. a / This / pen. / is / blue  
7. books. / Those / red / are  
8. an / orange / That / eraser. / is

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. This is a blue pen.  
7. Those are red books.  
8. That is an orange eraser.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. One or two phrases with *This is a/an…*  
10. Near + far: *This is …. That is ….*  
11. Singular + plural + colors  
12. Three desk objects with *this/that/these/those*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. This is a blue pen. / This is an eraser.  
10. This is a pen. That is a bag.  
11. This is a red book. These are blue pens.  
12. This is a yellow pencil. That is a black bag. These are green erasers.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. My pencil case / bag (4–6 sentences).  
14. My English classroom (4–6 sentences).  
15. My friend's classroom (4–6 sentences; ≥2 negatives).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
This is my pencil case. There is a blue pen. There is an eraser. These are two pencils. That is a red notebook. It is great!

**14.**  
This is my English classroom. It is a big room. There is a board on the wall. These are ten desks. Those are twenty chairs. My bag is black.

**15.**  
This is my friend's classroom. It is not small. There is a board. These are desks. Those are not red bags — they are blue. Is that a ruler? Yes, it is.

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Tema | Recuerda |
|------|----------|
| *a / an* | *a* + consonante · *an* + vocal |
| Plural | *book → books* |
| Cerca | *this* (sg) · *these* (pl) |
| Lejos | *that* (sg) · *those* (pl) |
| Números | *one* … *ten* |
