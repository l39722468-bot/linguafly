## Lección 1 — Gramática: First, Then, Finally

**Objetivo:** secuenciadores e imperativos en recetas.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ____ cut the vegetables. → *First / Finally / Mix*  
2. First cut. ____ mix the ingredients. → *Then / Finally / Bake*  
3. Then fry the onion. ____ add the salt. → *Finally / First / Cut*  
4. ____ boil the water. → *First / Finally / Mix*  
5. First mix. ____ bake for twenty minutes. → *Then / First / Cut*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **First** — primer paso.  
2. **Then** — paso siguiente.  
3. **Finally** — último paso.  
4. **First**  
5. **Then**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Correct order = ____. → a) First, Then, Finally · b) Finally, Then, First · c) Then, First, Finally  
7. Recipe instructions use ____. → a) imperative (base verb) · b) *to* + verb · c) *-ing* only  
8. *Finally* means ____. → a) last step · b) first step · c) never  
9. *First boil the water* is ____. → a) correct · b) needs *to* · c) needs subject *you*  
10. After *Then* we often use ____. → a) a base verb · b) *to* + verb · c) *will*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) First, Then, Finally**  
7. **a) imperative (base verb)**  
8. **a) last step**  
9. **a) correct**  
10. **a) a base verb**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *Finally cut the vegetables. First bake.* (ordena)  
12. *To mix the eggs.* (imperativo)  
13. *You should to boil the water.* → usa imperativo de receta  
14. *Then first add salt.*  
15. *Bake the onion in a pan.* (elige el verbo correcto)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **First** cut the vegetables. **Finally** bake.  
12. **Mix** the eggs.  
13. **Boil** the water.  
14. **Then** add salt. / **First** add salt.  
15. **Fry** the onion in a pan.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: cocina y recetas

**Objetivo:** *cut, mix, fry, boil, bake* + *pan, pot, oven, recipe, ingredients*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja

1. cut  
2. mix  
3. fry  
4. boil  
5. bake  

a) cook in hot oil in a pan · b) cook in the oven · c) use a knife · d) combine ingredients · e) cook in hot water

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **c** · 2 → **d** · 3 → **a** · 4 → **e** · 5 → **b**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. Use a ____ to fry onions. → *pan / pot / oven*  
7. Boil water in a ____. → *pot / oven / knife*  
8. Bake bread in the ____. → *oven / pan / pot*  
9. A ____ is a list of steps. → *recipe / pan / salt*  
10. ____ are what you need for a recipe. → *Ingredients / Ovens / Finally*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **pan**  
7. **pot**  
8. **oven**  
9. **recipe**  
10. **Ingredients**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. Fry = ____. → a) cook in oil in a pan · b) cook in the oven · c) cut  
12. Boil = ____. → a) cook in hot water · b) mix · c) bake  
13. Bake = ____. → a) cook in the oven · b) fry · c) cut  
14. *Knife* goes with ____. → a) cut · b) bake · c) boil  
15. *Serve* means ____. → a) put food on a plate for people · b) cut · c) boil

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) cook in oil in a pan**  
12. **a) cook in hot water**  
13. **a) cook in the oven**  
14. **a) cut**  
15. **a) put food on a plate for people**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Today I cook pasta

**Objetivo:** leer una receta simple con secuenciadores.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-57/reading-cooking.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Today I cook pasta"></audio>

> Today I cook pasta. First, I boil water. Then I put the pasta in the water. Next, I cook the sauce. After that, I mix the pasta and the sauce. Finally, I eat it. It is delicious!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What does the person cook? → a) Pasta · b) Bread · c) Eggs  
2. What is the first step? → a) Boil water · b) Eat · c) Mix the sauce  
3. When does the person mix pasta and sauce? → a) After cooking the sauce · b) First · c) Never  
4. What is the last step? → a) Eat it · b) Boil water · c) Cut tomatoes  
5. Is the pasta delicious? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Pasta**  
2. **a) Boil water**  
3. **a) After cooking the sauce**  
4. **a) Eat it**  
5. **a) Yes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. *First* appears with ____. → a) boil water · b) eat · c) mix  
7. *Finally* appears with ____. → a) eat it · b) boil · c) put pasta  
8. *Next* and *After that* mean something like ____. → a) Then · b) First · c) Never  
9. The person puts pasta ____. → a) in the water · b) in the oven · c) in a pan with oil  
10. The main topic is ____. → a) cooking pasta step by step · b) a restaurant · c) a supermarket

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) boil water**  
7. **a) eat it**  
8. **a) Then**  
9. **a) in the water**  
10. **a) cooking pasta step by step**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Boil* here means ____. → a) cook in hot water · b) fry · c) bake  
12. *Mix* means ____. → a) combine · b) cut · c) serve only  
13. *Finally* = ____. → a) last · b) first · c) never  
14. Imperative example from recipes = ____. → a) Boil the water · b) I am boiling · c) To boil  
15. The text uses sequence words like ____. → a) First, Then, Next, Finally · b) only Finally · c) only because

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) cook in hot water**  
12. **a) combine**  
13. **a) last**  
14. **a) Boil the water**  
15. **a) First, Then, Next, Finally**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My recipe

**Objetivo:** escribir una receta corta con secuenciadores y verbos de cocina.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ____ cut the onions. → *First / Finally / Oven*  
2. Then ____ the eggs and flour. → *mix / pan / recipe*  
3. ____ the water in a pot. → *Boil / Bake / Cut*  
4. ____ the cake in the oven. → *Bake / Fry / Boil*  
5. ____ add salt and serve. → *Finally / First / Knife*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **First**  
2. **mix**  
3. **Boil**  
4. **Bake**  
5. **Finally**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. First / boil / the water.  
7. Then / mix / the ingredients.  
8. Finally / bake / for twenty minutes.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. First boil the water.  
7. Then mix the ingredients.  
8. Finally bake for twenty minutes.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Primero corta las verduras.  
10. Luego mezcla los ingredientes.  
11. Finalmente hornea durante veinte minutos.  
12. Primero hierve el agua. Luego fríe la cebolla.  
13. Párrafo corto (4–6 frases): pasta o verduras con *First / Then / Finally*.  
14. Usa *pan* o *pot* o *oven* en una frase.  
15. Termina con *serve* o *eat*.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

10. Then mix the ingredients.  
11. Finally bake for twenty minutes.

14. Fry the onion in a pan. / Bake the bread in the oven.
