## Lección 1 — Gramática: adjetivos

**Objetivo:** *dangerous, friendly, cute*; *pet / farm / wild*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. A lion is ____. → *dangerous / friendly / cute*  
2. My dog is very ____. → *friendly / dangerous / wild*  
3. The kitten is so ____. → *cute / dangerous / wild*  
4. A cat is a ____ animal. → *pet / farm / wild*  
5. A cow lives on a ____. → *farm / wild / pet*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **dangerous** — *Dangerous* = peligroso.  
2. **friendly** — *Friendly* = amigable.  
3. **cute** — *Cute* = mono, adorable.  
4. **pet** — *Pet* = mascota.  
5. **farm** — *Farm* = granja.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. A ____ animal lives in the forest. → a) wild · b) pet · c) farm  
7. Tigers are ____. → a) dangerous · b) cute · c) friendly  
8. Rabbits are ____. → a) cute · b) dangerous · c) wild  
9. Pigs and chickens are ____ animals. → a) farm · b) wild · c) pet  
10. We use ____ before the noun to describe animals. → a) adjectives · b) verbs · c) nouns

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) wild**  
7. **a) dangerous**  
8. **a) cute**  
9. **a) farm**  
10. **a) adjectives**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. The / elephant / is / very / big.  
12. My / hamster / is / cute / and / friendly.  
13. A / snake / can / be / dangerous.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. The elephant is very big.  
12. My hamster is cute and friendly.  
13. A snake can be dangerous.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use adjectives like *dangerous, friendly, cute* to describe animals. → V / F  
15. Pets live with people; wild animals live in nature. → V / F

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: pets, farm, wild

**Objetivo:** clasificar animales y usar adjetivos.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige

1. A ____ lives in your house. → a) pet · b) farm · c) wild  
2. Dog, cat, fish are ____. → a) pets · b) farm animals · c) wild animals  
3. Cow, horse, sheep live on a ____. → a) farm · b) house · c) forest  
4. Lion, tiger, bear are ____ animals. → a) wild · b) pets · c) farm  
5. A ____ animal can hurt you. → a) dangerous · b) friendly · c) cute

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) pet**  
2. **a) pets**  
3. **a) farm**  
4. **a) wild**  
5. **a) dangerous**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Relaciona (escribe la pareja)

6. pet · farm · wild · cute → animal that lives with people / place with cows, pigs, chickens / lives in nature / nice to look at  
7. dog · pig · wolf · rabbit → pet / farm / wild / pet or wild

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. pet → animal that lives with people · farm → place with cows, pigs, chickens · wild → lives in nature · cute → nice to look at  
7. dog → pet · pig → farm · wolf → wild · rabbit → pet or wild

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. The zoo has ____ animals. → *wild / pets / farm*  
9. I have a ____ cat. It is very friendly. → *pet / wild / farm*  
10. Chickens and ducks are ____ animals. → *farm / wild / pet*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **wild**  
9. **pet**  
10. **farm**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Elige

11. Friendly means ____. → a) nice, kind · b) scary · c) big  
12. Dangerous means ____. → a) can hurt you · b) very small · c) funny  
13. Where do wild animals live? → a) In the forest or jungle · b) In a house · c) On a farm

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) nice, kind**  
12. **a) can hurt you**  
13. **a) In the forest or jungle**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena las palabras

14. My / pet / is / a / friendly / dog.  
15. Lions / are / wild / and / dangerous.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. My pet is a friendly dog.  
15. Lions are wild and dangerous.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: My pets

**Objetivo:** leer sobre mascotas, granja y animales salvajes.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-27/reading-pets.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: My pets"></audio>

> I have two pets. My dog is called Max. He is very friendly and he loves to play. My cat is small and very cute. She likes sleeping on the sofa. At my grandma's farm there are cows, chickens and a horse. The horse is big but friendly. Wild animals like lions and tigers are dangerous – we don't have them at home.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. How many pets does the writer have? → a) Two · b) One · c) Three  
2. What is the name of the dog? → a) Max · b) She · c) The cat  
3. Is Max friendly or dangerous? → a) Friendly · b) Dangerous · c) Wild  
4. How is the cat described? → a) Small and cute · b) Big and dangerous · c) Wild  
5. Where is the farm? → a) At grandma's · b) At home · c) At the zoo

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Two**  
2. **a) Max**  
3. **a) Friendly**  
4. **a) Small and cute**  
5. **a) At grandma's**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. What animals are on the farm? → a) Cows, chickens, horse · b) Lions and tigers · c) Dogs and cats  
7. Is the horse friendly? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say  
8. Are lions and tigers dangerous? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say  
9. Which word means "mascota"? → a) Pet · b) Wild · c) Farm  
10. Which word means "peligroso"? → a) Dangerous · b) Friendly · c) Cute

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Cows, chickens, horse**  
7. **a) Yes**  
8. **a) Yes**  
9. **a) Pet**  
10. **a) Dangerous**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. Where does the cat like to sleep? → a) On the sofa · b) On the farm · c) In the forest  
12. Do we have lions at home? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The text doesn't say  
13. The main topic is ____. → a) pets, farm and wild animals · b) food · c) family  
14. What does Max love? → a) To play · b) To sleep · c) To eat  
15. Which adjective describes the cat? → a) Cute · b) Dangerous · c) Wild

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) On the sofa**  
12. **a) No**  
13. **a) pets, farm and wild animals**  
14. **a) To play**  
15. **a) Cute**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Do you have a pet?

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo sobre mascotas y animales.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-27/listening-pets.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Pets"></audio>

> **A:** Do you have a pet?  
> **B:** Yes, I have a dog. He is very friendly.  
> **A:** What about your sister?  
> **B:** She has a cat. It is very cute.  
> **A:** I like farm animals. My uncle has a farm with cows and chickens.  
> **B:** Wild animals are dangerous. We don't have them at home.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Does B have a pet? → a) Yes, a dog · b) No · c) A cat  
2. Is B's dog friendly? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
3. What pet does B's sister have? → a) A cat · b) A dog · c) A rabbit  
4. How is the sister's cat described? → a) Very cute · b) Dangerous · c) Wild  
5. Who has a farm? → a) A's uncle · b) B · c) B's sister

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Yes, a dog**  
2. **a) Yes**  
3. **a) A cat**  
4. **a) Very cute**  
5. **a) A's uncle**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. What animals are on the uncle's farm? → a) Cows and chickens · b) Dogs and cats · c) Lions  
7. Are wild animals dangerous? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
8. Do we have wild animals at home? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The audio doesn't say  
9. The dialogue is ____. → a) about pets and animals · b) about food · c) about work  
10. Which word means "amigable"? → a) Friendly · b) Dangerous · c) Cute

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Cows and chickens**  
7. **a) Yes**  
8. **a) No**  
9. **a) about pets and animals**  
10. **a) Friendly**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. A ____ farm animals. → a) likes · b) like · c) liking  
12. B says wild animals ____ dangerous. → a) are · b) is · c) be  
13. The cat is ____. → a) very cute · b) very dangerous · c) very big  
14. What does A ask first? → a) Do you have a pet? · b) Where is the farm? · c) Are lions dangerous?  
15. Farm animals include ____. → a) cows and chickens · b) lions and tigers · c) dogs and cats

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) likes**  
12. **a) are**  
13. **a) very cute**  
14. **a) Do you have a pet?**  
15. **a) cows and chickens**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: Describe animals

**Objetivo:** escribir sobre mascotas y animales con adjetivos.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. A lion ____ dangerous. → *is / are / be*  
2. My ____ is very friendly. → *dog / farm / wild*  
3. Cows live ____ a farm. → *on / in / at*  
4. The kitten ____ so cute. → *is / are / be*  
5. Tigers ____ wild animals. → *are / is / be*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is**  
2. **dog**  
3. **on**  
4. **is**  
5. **are**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. I / have / a / pet / dog.  
7. Wild / animals / are / dangerous.  
8. The / horse / on / the / farm / is / friendly.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I have a pet dog.  
7. Wild animals are dangerous.  
8. The horse on the farm is friendly.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. Describe an animal (pet, farm or wild) with an adjective.  
10. Which animal is dangerous and why?  
11. One farm animal + one wild animal (two sentences).  
12. *Do you have a pet?* + yes/no and a short description.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. My dog is very friendly.  
10. A snake can be dangerous because it can bite.  
11. A cow lives on a farm. A lion is a wild animal.  
12. Do you have a pet? Yes, I have a cute cat.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Short paragraph (4–5 sentences): your pet or a favourite animal.  
14. 3 sentences: one *dangerous*, one *friendly*, one *cute*.  
15. Difference between pet, farm animal and wild animal (2–3 sentences).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
I have a pet dog. His name is Max. He is very friendly and cute. He lives in my house.

**14.**  
A tiger is dangerous. My dog is friendly. The rabbit is cute.

**15.**  
A pet lives with people. Farm animals live on a farm. Wild animals live in nature.

</details>
