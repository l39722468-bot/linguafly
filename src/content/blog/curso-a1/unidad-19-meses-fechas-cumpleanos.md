Tras hablar de hobbies en la [Unidad 18](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-18-gustos-like-ing-because), en la **Unidad 19 del curso A1** (*Months & Dates*) aprendes a situar eventos en el **calendario**: meses, **ordinales**, *in* / *on* y la pregunta clave ***When is your birthday?***.

Esta guía cubre los **12 meses**, los **ordinales 1st–31st**, las preposiciones ***in* (mes)** y ***on* (fecha/día)**, y frases de **cumpleaños** y fiestas. Incluye **audios**, **esquemas** y **ejercicios** (más de 2000 palabras). Combina bien con los [días de la semana](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-12-dias-semana) y los [números](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-8-numeros-edad-precios).

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 19 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Nombrar los **12 meses** del año.
- Usar **ordinales** (*first, second, third… 31st*).
- Decir fechas con ***in* + mes** y ***on* + fecha**.
- Preguntar y responder ***When is your birthday?***.
- Usar ***I was born in/on…*** y situar fiestas (*Christmas, Halloween…*).

### Tips de escritura y mayúsculas

- Los meses van con **mayúscula**: *January*, no *january*.  
- No llevan artículo: *in May* (no *in the May*).  
- Abreviaturas comunes: Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec.

### Fiestas útiles (mes)

| Fiesta | Mes típico |

| **New Year** | January |
| **Valentine's Day** | February |
| **Easter** | March or April |
| **Halloween** | October (31st) |
| **Christmas** | December |

## 2. Ordinales: 1st–31st

Los días del mes se dicen con **ordinales** (1.º, 2.º, 3.º…).

| Número | Ordinal | Se escribe |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | first | **1st** |
| 2 | second | **2nd** |
| 3 | third | **3rd** |
| 4 | fourth | **4th** |
| 5 | fifth | **5th** |
| 6 | sixth | **6th** |
| 7 | seventh | **7th** |
| 8 | eighth | **8th** |
| 9 | ninth | **9th** |
| 10 | tenth | **10th** |
| 11 | eleventh | **11th** |
| 12 | twelfth | **12th** |
| 13 | thirteenth | **13th** |
| 20 | twentieth | **20th** |

| 22 | twenty-second | **22nd** |
| 23 | twenty-third | **23rd** |
| 30 | thirtieth | **30th** |
| 31 | thirty-first | **31st** |

### Regla práctica

- **1 / 21 / 31** → *-st* (*first / twenty-first / thirty-first*)  
- **2 / 22** → *-nd* (*second / twenty-second*)  
- **3 / 23** → *-rd* (*third / twenty-third*)  
- **El resto** → *-th* (*fourth, fifth, twentieth…*)

En fechas suele ir con **the**: *the 15th of May*.

## 3. *In* + mes · *On* + fecha (y día)

| Preposición | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **in** | mes / año | in January · in 2010 |
| **on** | fecha / día de la semana | on the 15th of March · on Monday |

![Esquema in mes y on fecha en inglés A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-19/a1-unit19-in-on.png)

### Ejemplos con *in*

> My birthday is **in** May.  
> Christmas is **in** December.  
> We have holiday **in** August.  
> New Year is **in** January.  
> I was born **in** February.

### Ejemplos con *on*

> My birthday is **on** the 5th of April.  
> I was born **on** the 15th of March.  
> It's **on** the 3rd of July.  
> Her birthday is **on** the 21st of May.  
> The meeting is **on** Monday.  
> Halloween is **on** the 31st of October.

### Dos formas de decir la fecha (A1)

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| **the + ordinal + of + month** | the 15th of May |
| **month + ordinal** | May 15th |

Ambas se entienden. En el curso practicas mucho *the 15th of May*.

## 4. *When is your birthday?*

```
When is your birthday?
It's in + mes.
It's on the + ordinal + of + mes.
```

| Pregunta / respuesta | Significado |
| :--- | :--- |
| **When is your birthday?** | ¿Cuándo es tu cumpleaños? |
| It's **in** June. | Es en junio. |
| It's **on** the 1st of June. | Es el 1 de junio. |
| When is **his** birthday? | ¿Cuándo es su cumpleaños? |
| It's on the 20th of May. | Es el 20 de mayo. |

### *I was born…*

> I **was born** in February.  
> I **was born** on the 15th of March.  
> She was born in July.  
> He was born on the 3rd of December.

*Was born* = nací / nació. En A1 lo usamos como expresión fija con *in/on*.

## 5. Más ejemplos en contexto

> **Ana:** When is your birthday?  
> **Luis:** It's on the 10th of March. And yours?  
> **Ana:** Mine is in August — on the 31st of August.  
> **Luis:** Nice! I was born in February.  
> **Ana:** Christmas is in December. Do you like December?  
> **Luis:** Yes. And Halloween is on the 31st of October. It's fun!

### Calendario mental rápido

> January → New Year  
> February → Valentine's Day  
> March / April → Easter (often)  
> October → Halloween (31st)  
> December → Christmas

### Conectar con otras unidades

> On Monday I have class. (*U12: on + día*)  
> My birthday is on the 5th of April. (*U19: on + fecha*)  
> In May I like swimming. (*U18 + U19*)  
> I was born in 2010. (*in + año*; los números de U8 ayudan)

## 6. Errores típicos de hispanohablantes

| ❌ Incorrecto | ✅ Correcto | Explicación |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *on January* | ***in** January* | Mes → *in* |
| *in the 15th of March* | ***on** the 15th of March* | Fecha → *on* |
| *in Monday* | ***on** Monday* | Día → *on* |
| *My birthday is the May* | *My birthday is **in** May* | Sin *the* con mes |
| *15 March* (solo número cardinal al hablar) | *the **15th** of March* | Ordinal |
| *When your birthday?* | *When **is** your birthday?* | Falta *is* |
| *I born in February* | *I **was** born in February* | *was born* |

## 7. Pronunciación útil

| Forma | Tip A1 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **January / February** | *Jan-yu-eri* / *Feb-ru-eri* (a veces se “come” una sílaba). |
| **February** | Practica despacio: *Feb-ru-ary*. |
| **first / third** | *-st / -rd* claras. |
| **birthday** | *BERTH-day*. |
| **the 15th** | Di *the fifteenth*. |

Escucha los 12 meses en orden y repítelos dos veces. Luego di tu cumpleaños completo en voz alta.

### Truco de memoria para ordinales

Memoriza primero el “núcleo raro”: *first, second, third, fifth, eighth, ninth, twelfth*. El resto suele ser número + *-th* (*sixth, tenth, fourteenth*). Para el 21–23 y el 31, repite la misma lógica del 1–3: *twenty-first, twenty-second, twenty-third, thirty-first*.

Practica en voz alta cinco fechas reales de tu vida (cumpleaños, fiestas, inicio de curso). Si las dices cada día durante una semana, *in/on* deja de confundirse.

## 7b. Mini-diálogos para memorizar

> **A:** When is your birthday?  
> **B:** It's in May.  
> **A:** What date?  
> **B:** On the 21st of May.

> **A:** When were you born? / When is your birthday?  
> **B:** I was born on the 3rd of July.  
> **A:** Nice! I was born in December.

> **A:** When is Christmas?  
> **B:** It's in December.  
> **A:** And Halloween?  
> **B:** On the 31st of October.

Cambia solo el mes y el ordinal: fijas *in/on* sin memorizar diálogos largos.

## 7c. Plantillas para copiar

1. My birthday is in \_\_\_\_.  
2. My birthday is on the \_\_\_\_ of \_\_\_\_.  
3. I was born in \_\_\_\_. / I was born on the \_\_\_\_ of \_\_\_\_.  
4. When is your birthday? — It's on the \_\_\_\_ of \_\_\_\_.  
5. Christmas is in \_\_\_\_. Halloween is on the \_\_\_\_ of \_\_\_\_.

### Más frases para memorizar

> January is the first month. December is the twelfth month.  
> My birthday is in June.  
> My birthday is on the first of June.  
> She was born on the 15th of March.  
> We have holiday in August.  
> The meeting is on Monday.  
> Valentine's Day is in February.  
> When is your birthday? — It's on the 20th of May.

Lee la lista dos veces. Luego tapa el inglés y traduce desde el español. Grábate diciendo **tu** fecha real: es la frase más útil de la unidad.

### Mapa *in / on / at* (A1, versión corta)

| Preposición | Tiempo |
| :--- | :--- |
| **in** | meses, años, partes del día (*in the morning*) |
| **on** | fechas, días de la semana |
| **at** | horas (*at eight*) — de U10 |

No mezcles: *in May* / *on May 5th* / *at 5 o'clock*.

### Rutina rápida de 2 minutos

1. Di los meses 1–6.  
2. Di los meses 7–12.  
3. Di: *My birthday is in …* y *My birthday is on the … of …*.  
4. Pregunta en voz alta: *When is your birthday?* y responde por 3 personas (tú, un amigo, un familiar).

Si puedes, escribe esas cuatro líneas en una nota del móvil y léelas mañana otra vez.

## 8. Ejercicios prácticos (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — *in* o *on*

1. My birthday is \_\_\_\_ May.  
2. My birthday is \_\_\_\_ the 5th of April.  
3. Christmas is \_\_\_\_ December.  
4. The meeting is \_\_\_\_ Monday.  
5. I was born \_\_\_\_ February.  
6. Halloween is \_\_\_\_ the 31st of October.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **in**  
2. **on**  
3. **in**  
4. **on**  
5. **in**  
6. **on**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Ordinales

Escribe el ordinal: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 21, 22, 23, 31.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 21st, 22nd, 23rd, 31st
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Completa

1. \_\_\_\_ is your birthday?  
2. It's on the \_\_\_\_ of March. (10)  
3. I was \_\_\_\_ in July.  
4. November is the \_\_\_\_ month. (11)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **When**  
2. **10th** / **tenth**  
3. **born**  
4. **11th** / **eleventh**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Traduce al inglés

2. Es en mayo.  
3. Es el 5 de abril.  
4. Nací en febrero.  
5. La Navidad es en diciembre.  
6. Halloween es el 31 de octubre.

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrige el error

1. *My birthday is on January.*  
2. *I was born in the 15th of March.*  
3. *When your birthday?*  
4. *Christmas is on December.*  
5. *The meeting is in Monday.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. My birthday is **in** January.  
2. I was born **on** the 15th of March.  
3. When **is** your birthday?  
4. Christmas is **in** December.  
5. The meeting is **on** Monday.
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Empareja mes y fiesta / dato

1. January  
2. February  
3. October  
4. December  

a) Christmas  
b) New Year  
c) Valentine's Day  
d) Halloween

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **c** · 3 → **d** · 4 → **a**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Escribe sobre ti y tu familia

Escribe 6–8 frases: tu cumpleaños (mes y fecha), cuándo naciste, y el cumpleaños de 2 personas. Añade una fiesta.

**Modelo:**

> My birthday is in May. It's on the 12th of May. I was born on the 12th of May. My sister's birthday is in August — on the 3rd of August. My dad was born in January. Christmas is in December. Halloween is on the 31st of October. When is your birthday?
