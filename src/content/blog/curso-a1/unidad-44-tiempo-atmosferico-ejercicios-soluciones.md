## Lección 1 — Gramática: It is + clima

**Objetivo:** *It is* + adjetivo de tiempo (*sunny, cold…*) y *It is raining / snowing*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. It ____ sunny today. → *is / are / am*  
2. It ____ raining now. → *is / are / am*  
3. It ____ cold in winter. → *is / are / am*  
4. It ____ hot in summer. → *is / are / am*  
5. It ____ cloudy this morning. → *is / are / am*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is** — *It is* + adjetivo.  
2. **is** — *It is raining.*  
3. **is**  
4. **is**  
5. **is**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. The pattern is ____. → a) It is + adjective · b) I is + adjective · c) They is + adjective  
7. *It is raining* means ____. → a) Rain is falling now · b) The sun shines · c) It is never wet  
8. Correct for low temperature: ____. → a) It is cold. · b) It are cold. · c) It am cold.  
9. *It is sunny* means ____. → a) The sun is shining · b) It snows · c) It rains  
10. *It is snowing* means ____. → a) Snow falls · b) It is hot · c) It is sunny only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) It is + adjective**  
7. **a) Rain is falling now**  
8. **a) It is cold.**  
9. **a) The sun is shining**  
10. **a) Snow falls**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. It / is / sunny / today.  
12. It / is / raining / now.  
13. It / is / cold / in / winter.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. It is sunny today.  
12. It is raining now.  
13. It is cold in winter.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. For weather we use *It is*, not *I is* or *They is*. → True / False  
15. *It is hot* means the temperature is high. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: el tiempo

**Objetivo:** *sunny, rainy, cloudy, snowy, hot, cold*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *Sunny* = ____. → a) The sun is shining · b) It rains · c) It snows  
2. *Rainy* = ____. → a) It rains; the weather is wet · b) The sun shines · c) It is never wet  
3. *Cloudy* = ____. → a) There are clouds in the sky · b) It is always hot · c) Snow falls  
4. *Snowy* / *It is snowing* = ____. → a) Snow falls · b) It is hot · c) It is sunny only  
5. *Hot* = ____. → a) The temperature is high or warm · b) The temperature is low · c) It snows

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) The sun is shining**  
2. **a) It rains; the weather is wet**  
3. **a) There are clouds in the sky**  
4. **a) Snow falls**  
5. **a) The temperature is high or warm**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Empareja y completa

6. *Cold* means ____. → a) The temperature is low · b) The temperature is high · c) The sun shines  
7. Empareja: sunny / rainy / cloudy / snowy / hot / cold → the sun shines · it rains · clouds · snow · high temperature · low temperature  
8. Empareja: It is sunny / It is rainy / It is cold / It is hot / It is snowy → take sunglasses · take an umbrella · wear a coat · wear shorts · snow on the ground

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) The temperature is low**  
7. **sunny → the sun shines** · **rainy → it rains** · **cloudy → clouds** · **snowy → snow** · **hot → high temperature** · **cold → low temperature**  
8. **It is sunny → take sunglasses** · **It is rainy → take an umbrella** · **It is cold → wear a coat** · **It is hot → wear shorts** · **It is snowy → snow on the ground**

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–13 — Completa / elige

9. It is ____ today — the sun is shining. → *sunny / rainy / snowy*  
10. Take an umbrella — it is ____. → *rainy / sunny / hot*  
11. Wear a coat — it is ____. → *cold / hot / sunny*  
12. Low temperature = ____. → a) cold · b) hot · c) sunny  
13. *It is snowing* means ____. → a) Snow falls · b) It is hot · c) It is sunny only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. **sunny**  
10. **rainy**  
11. **cold**  
12. **a) cold**  
13. **a) Snow falls**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *Cloudy* means there are clouds in the sky. → True / False  
15. Sunny, rainy, cloudy, hot and cold are weather words. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Today and seasons

**Objetivo:** leer sobre el tiempo hoy, ayer y en las estaciones.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-44/reading-weather.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Weather"></audio>

> Today it is sunny and hot. I like this weather. Yesterday it was cloudy and rainy. It was raining in the afternoon. In winter it is cold and sometimes it is snowing. In summer it is hot and sunny. I prefer sunny weather because I can go outside.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the weather like today? → a) Sunny and hot · b) Cold and snowy · c) Only rainy  
2. What was it like yesterday? → a) Cloudy and rainy · b) Sunny and hot · c) Snowy  
3. When was it raining yesterday? → a) In the afternoon · b) At night only · c) Never  
4. What is winter like? → a) Cold and sometimes snowy · b) Always hot · c) Always sunny  
5. What is summer like? → a) Hot and sunny · b) Always snowy · c) Always cold

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Sunny and hot**  
2. **a) Cloudy and rainy**  
3. **a) In the afternoon**  
4. **a) Cold and sometimes snowy**  
5. **a) Hot and sunny**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. What weather does the writer prefer? → a) Sunny weather · b) Rainy weather · c) Snowy weather  
7. Why? → a) Because the writer can go outside · b) Because it is cold · c) The text doesn't say  
8. *It was raining* means ____. → a) Rain falls / fell · b) The sun shines · c) It is hot  
9. *Sunny* means ____. → a) The sun shines · b) It snows · c) It rains  
10. *Cloudy* means ____. → a) There are clouds in the sky · b) It is always hot · c) Snow falls

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Sunny weather**  
7. **a) Because the writer can go outside**  
8. **a) Rain falls / fell**  
9. **a) The sun shines**  
10. **a) There are clouds in the sky**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text is about ____. → a) Weather at different times · b) Only food · c) Only sports  
12. *Hot* means ____. → a) High temperature · b) Low temperature · c) Snow  
13. *Cold* means ____. → a) Low temperature · b) High temperature · c) Sunny only  
14. *It is snowing* means ____. → a) Snow falls · b) It is hot · c) It is sunny only  
15. The main topic is ____. → a) Weather: sunny, rainy, cloudy, hot, and cold · b) Cooking · c) Music

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) Weather at different times**  
12. **a) High temperature**  
13. **a) Low temperature**  
14. **a) Snow falls**  
15. **a) Weather: sunny, rainy, cloudy, hot, and cold**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Weather talk

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo sobre el tiempo.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-44/listening-weather.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Weather talk"></audio>

> **A:** What is the weather like today?  
> **B:** It is sunny and hot.  
> **A:** Great. Yesterday it was cloudy and it was raining.  
> **B:** Yes, I had an umbrella. In winter it is cold and sometimes it is snowing.  
> **A:** I prefer sunny weather.  
> **B:** Me too.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What is the weather like today? → a) Sunny and hot · b) Cold and snowy · c) Only rainy  
2. What was it like yesterday? → a) Cloudy and raining · b) Sunny and hot · c) Snowy  
3. What did B have yesterday? → a) An umbrella · b) Sunglasses · c) Shorts  
4. What is winter like? → a) Cold and sometimes snowy · b) Always hot · c) Always sunny  
5. What weather does A prefer? → a) Sunny weather · b) Rainy weather · c) Snowy weather

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Sunny and hot**  
2. **a) Cloudy and raining**  
3. **a) An umbrella**  
4. **a) Cold and sometimes snowy**  
5. **a) Sunny weather**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Does B agree? → a) Yes. B says “Me too.” · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
7. *Sunny* means ____. → a) The sun shines · b) It rains · c) It snows  
8. *It was raining* means ____. → a) Rain falls / fell · b) The sun shines · c) It is hot  
9. The dialogue is about ____. → a) The weather · b) Food · c) Sports  
10. *Hot* means ____. → a) High temperature · b) Low temperature · c) Snow

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Yes. B says “Me too.”**  
7. **a) The sun shines**  
8. **a) Rain falls / fell**  
9. **a) The weather**  
10. **a) High temperature**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Cold* means ____. → a) Low temperature · b) High temperature · c) Sunny only  
12. *Cloudy* means ____. → a) There are clouds in the sky · b) It is always hot · c) Snow falls  
13. A's first question is ____. → a) What the weather is like today · b) What time it is · c) Where the hotel is  
14. *It is snowing* means ____. → a) Snow falls · b) It is hot · c) It is sunny only  
15. The topic is ____. → a) Weather: sunny, rainy, cold, and snowy · b) Cooking · c) Music

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) Low temperature**  
12. **a) There are clouds in the sky**  
13. **a) What the weather is like today**  
14. **a) Snow falls**  
15. **a) Weather: sunny, rainy, cold, and snowy**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My weather

**Objetivo:** escribir frases sobre el tiempo.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. It ____ sunny today. → *is / are / am*  
2. It ____ raining now. → *is / are / am*  
3. It is ____ in winter. → *cold / hot / sunny*  
4. It is ____ in summer. → *hot / cold / snowy*  
5. It is ____ this morning — there are clouds. → *cloudy / sunny / hot*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is**  
2. **is**  
3. **cold**  
4. **hot**  
5. **cloudy**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. It / is / sunny / today.  
7. It / is / raining / now.  
8. It / is / cold / in / winter.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. It is sunny today.  
7. It is raining now.  
8. It is cold in winter.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Escribe el tiempo de hoy con *It is…*.  
10. Escribe el invierno con *cold* / *snowy*.  
11. Dos frases: ayer + hoy.  
12. Pregunta y respuesta: *What is the weather like?*  
13. Párrafo corto (4 frases) sobre el tiempo.  
14. Escribe qué tiempo prefieres y por qué.  
15. Dos tips: qué llevar si hace frío / si llueve.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. It is sunny and hot today.  
10. It is cold and snowy in winter.  
11. Yesterday it was rainy. Today it is sunny.  
12. What is the weather like? It is sunny.  
13. Today it is sunny and hot. In winter it is cold. Sometimes it is rainy and cloudy. I like sunny weather.  
14. I prefer sunny weather because I can go outside.  
15. When it is cold, wear a coat. When it is rainy, take an umbrella.

</details>
