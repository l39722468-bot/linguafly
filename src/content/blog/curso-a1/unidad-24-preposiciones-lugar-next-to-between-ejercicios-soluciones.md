## Lección 1 — Gramática: *There is/are* + preposiciones

**Objetivo:** *next to, between, in front of, opposite* con *There is/are*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. The bank is ____ the pharmacy. → *next to / between / opposite*  
2. The supermarket is ____ the bank and the library. → *between / next to / in front of*  
3. There is a tree ____ the house. → *in front of / behind / opposite*  
4. The cinema is ____ the park. → *opposite / next to / between*  
5. There ____ a cafe next to the supermarket. → *is / are / have*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **next to** — *next to = al lado de*.  
2. **between** — *between = entre (dos cosas)*.  
3. **in front of** — *in front of = delante de*.  
4. **opposite** — *opposite = enfrente de*.  
5. **is** — *There is* + singular.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. We use ____ for two things. → a) between · b) next to · c) in front of  
7. The bus stop ____ opposite the school. → a) is · b) are · c) be  
8. There are two shops ____ the bank and the pharmacy. → a) between · b) next to · c) opposite  
9. The car is ____ the house. → a) in front of · b) behind · c) between  
10. My flat is ____ the cinema. → a) next to · b) between · c) in front of

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) between** — *Between A and B.*  
7. **a) is**  
8. **a) between**  
9. **a) in front of**  
10. **a) next to**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. There / is / a / park / next to / the / library.  
12. The / pharmacy / is / between / the / bank / and / the / supermarket.  
13. The / bus stop / is / in front of / the / school.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. There is a park next to the library.  
12. The pharmacy is between the bank and the supermarket.  
13. The bus stop is in front of the school.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. Next to means beside, near. → ?  
15. Opposite and in front of can be different: opposite = across from. → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero** — *opposite* suele ser al otro lado de la calle.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: *next to, between, in front of, opposite*

**Objetivo:** significado y uso de las cuatro preposiciones.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la opción correcta

1. The shop ____ the bank. → a) is next to · b) is between · c) is opposite  
2. Between = ____. → a) in the middle of two things · b) beside · c) across from  
3. In front of = ____. → a) before something, facing it · b) behind · c) between  
4. Opposite = ____. → a) facing, across from · b) next to · c) between  
5. The library ____ the cinema and the pharmacy. → a) is between · b) is next to · c) is opposite

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) is next to**  
2. **a) in the middle of two things**  
3. **a) before something, facing it**  
4. **a) facing, across from**  
5. **a) is between**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Relaciona

**6.** Preposición → significado  

| Preposición | Significado |
|-------------|-------------|
| next to | ? |
| between | ? |
| in front of | ? |
| opposite | ? |

**7.** Frase → preposición  

| Frase | Preposición |
|-------|-------------|
| The cafe is beside the bank | ? |
| The shop is in the middle of two streets | ? |
| The car is before the house | ? |
| The park is across from the school | ? |

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**6.** next to → beside · between → in the middle of two · in front of → before, facing · opposite → across from  

**7.** The cafe is beside the bank → next to · The shop is in the middle of two streets → between · The car is before the house → in front of · The park is across from the school → opposite

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. There is a pharmacy ____ the supermarket. → *next to / between / opposite*  
9. The cinema is ____ the park and the library. → *between / next to / in front of*  
10. There is a bench ____ the tree. → *in front of / behind / between*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **next to**  
9. **between**  
10. **in front of**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Elige

11. The ____ is opposite the station. → a) bus stop · b) tree · c) book  
12. We use between ____ two nouns with and. → a) with · b) without · c) after  
13. The supermarket ____ next to the bank. → a) is · b) are · c) be

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) bus stop**  
12. **a) with** — *between A and B*.  
13. **a) is**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena las palabras

14. There / is / a / park / opposite / the / cinema.  
15. The / library / is / in front of / the / school.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. There is a park opposite the cinema.  
15. The library is in front of the school.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: My neighbourhood

**Objetivo:** leer sobre lugares del barrio y su posición.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-24/reading-neighbourhood.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Neighbourhood"></audio>

> My neighbourhood is nice. There is a supermarket next to the bank. There is a pharmacy between the bank and the supermarket. There is a park in front of the library. The cinema is opposite the park. I like to walk in the park. There are two cafes next to the cinema. The bus stop is in front of the library.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. How is the neighbourhood described? → a) Nice · b) Big · c) Small  
2. What is next to the bank? → a) A supermarket · b) A pharmacy · c) A park  
3. Where is the pharmacy? → a) Between the bank and the supermarket · b) Next to the park · c) Opposite the cinema  
4. What is in front of the library? → a) A park · b) A cinema · c) A bank  
5. What is opposite the park? → a) The cinema · b) The library · c) The bank

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Nice**  
2. **a) A supermarket**  
3. **a) Between the bank and the supermarket**  
4. **a) A park**  
5. **a) The cinema**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. What does the writer like to do in the park? → a) To walk · b) To run · c) To sit  
7. How many cafes are next to the cinema? → a) Two · b) One · c) Three  
8. Where is the bus stop? → a) In front of the library · b) Next to the bank · c) Between the pharmacy and the supermarket  
9. Which word means "al lado de"? → a) Next to · b) Between · c) Opposite  
10. Which word means "entre"? → a) Between · b) Next to · c) In front of

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) To walk**  
7. **a) Two**  
8. **a) In front of the library**  
9. **a) Next to**  
10. **a) Between**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The main topic is ____. → a) places in the neighbourhood and their position · b) the writer's family · c) food  
12. What is in front of the library? → a) A park · b) A bus stop · c) A cinema  
13. Which word means "enfrente de"? → a) Opposite · b) Next to · c) Between  
14. The pharmacy is ____ the bank and the supermarket. → a) between · b) next to · c) opposite  
15. Which preposition is used for "delante de"? → a) In front of · b) Behind · c) Between

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) places in the neighbourhood and their position**  
12. **a) A park** (también hay una parada delante; el texto presenta primero el parque)  
13. **a) Opposite**  
14. **a) between**  
15. **a) In front of**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: Places in my neighbourhood

**Objetivo:** escribir ubicaciones con las cuatro preposiciones.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. The pharmacy ____ next to the bank. → *is / are / be*  
2. The supermarket is ____ the bank and the library. → *between / next to / opposite*  
3. There is a park ____ the library. → *in front of / behind / between*  
4. The ____ is opposite the park. → *cinema / bank / pharmacy*  
5. There ____ a bus stop in front of the school. → *is / are / have*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is**  
2. **between**  
3. **in front of**  
4. **cinema**  
5. **is**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. There / is / a / cafe / next to / the / supermarket.  
7. The / library / is / between / the / pharmacy / and / the / cinema.  
8. The / bus stop / is / in front of / the / library.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. There is a cafe next to the supermarket.  
7. The library is between the pharmacy and the cinema.  
8. The bus stop is in front of the library.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. Where is a place (*next to / between / in front of / opposite*).  
10. *There is…* + preposition in your neighbourhood.  
11. Two sentences: position of two places.  
12. *Where is the…?* + answer with a preposition.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

10. There is a park in front of the library.  
11. The pharmacy is next to the bank. The cinema is opposite the park.  
12. Where is the pharmacy? It is next to the bank.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Short paragraph (4–5 sentences): places in your neighbourhood + positions.  
14. 3 sentences: pharmacy, supermarket, cinema (*next to, between, opposite*).  
15. *Where is the bus stop?* + answer with *in front of*.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**14.**  
The pharmacy is next to the bank. The supermarket is between the pharmacy and the library. The cinema is opposite the park.

**15.**  
Where is the bus stop? It is in front of the library.

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Preposición | Recuerda |
|-------------|----------|
| **next to** | al lado de (+ *to*) |
| **between** | entre A **and** B |
| **in front of** | delante de (+ *of*) |
| **opposite** | enfrente / al otro lado |
| **There is/are** | presenta + ubicación |
| **Where is…?** | pregunta de lugar |
