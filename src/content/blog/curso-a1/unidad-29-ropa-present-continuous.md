## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 29 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Formar ***am / is / are + wearing***.
- Nombrar: ***shirt, trousers, dress, shoes, hat, jacket***.
- Añadir colores: *a blue shirt, black shoes…*.
- Preguntar: ***What are you wearing today?***.
- Usar plurales típicos: ***trousers, shoes***.

---

## 1. Vocabulario de ropa

| Inglés | Español | Nota A1 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **shirt** | camisa | a shirt |
| **trousers** | pantalones | siempre plural |
| **dress** | vestido | a dress |
| **shoes** | zapatos | plural (par) |
| **hat** | sombrero / gorro | a hat |
| **jacket** | chaqueta | a jacket |
| **skirt** | falda | a skirt |
| **jeans** | vaqueros | plural |

![Vocabulario clothes A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-29/a1-unit29-clothes.png)

### Tip: *trousers* y *shoes*

En inglés no dices *a trouser* en A1 estándar. Dices:

> I am wearing blue **trousers**.  
> I am wearing black **shoes**.

*Jeans* también es plural: *He is wearing jeans.*

## 2. Present Continuous: *am / is / are + wearing*

| Sujeto | Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I | **am wearing** | I am wearing a shirt. |
| he / she / it | **is wearing** | She is wearing a dress. |
| you / we / they | **are wearing** | They are wearing hats. |

```
subject + am/is/are + wearing + clothes
```

![Esquema I am wearing Present Continuous A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-29/a1-unit29-wearing.png)

> I **am wearing** a shirt.  
> She **is wearing** a dress.  
> They **are wearing** hats.  
> We **are wearing** white shirts.  
> He **is wearing** a jacket and shoes.  
> I **am wearing** blue trousers.  
> I **am wearing** black shoes.  
> I **am wearing** a red shirt.

### Contracciones (muy frecuentes)

| Completa | Contracción |
| :--- | :--- |
| I am wearing | **I’m** wearing |
| She is wearing | **She’s** wearing |
| They are wearing | **They’re** wearing |

En A1 practica primero la forma completa; luego las contracciones.

## 3. Preguntas: *What are you wearing?*

```
What + am/is/are + subject + wearing…?
```

> **What are you wearing** today?  
> What **is** she wearing?  
> What **are** they wearing?

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-29/what-are-you-wearing.mp3" title="🔊 What are you wearing today?"></audio>

### Respuestas modelo

> I am wearing a white shirt and blue trousers.  
> She is wearing a red dress and black shoes.  
> He is wearing a jacket and a hat. He is wearing brown shoes.

### Diálogo modelo

> **A:** What are you wearing today?  
> **B:** I am wearing a white shirt and blue trousers.  
> **A:** Nice. Is your sister wearing a dress?  
> **B:** Yes, she is wearing a red dress and black shoes.  
> **A:** What about your brother?  
> **B:** He is wearing a jacket and a hat. He is wearing brown shoes.

## 5. Present Continuous vs Present Simple (ropa)

| Tiempo | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Present Continuous** | ahora / hoy (puesto) | I **am wearing** a blue shirt today. |
| **Present Simple** | hábito / general | I **wear** jeans at the weekend. |

En esta unidad el foco es **ahora**: *What are you wearing today?*

También: *It is cold. Wear a jacket.* (imperativo, consejo puntual).

### Negativa y corta (intro)

| Tipo | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| Negativa | I’m **not** wearing a hat. / She **isn’t** wearing shoes. |
| Pregunta sí/no | **Are** you wearing a jacket? — Yes, I am. / No, I’m not. |
| Pregunta *Is* | **Is** she wearing a dress? — Yes, she is. |

---

## 7. Errores típicos de hispanohablantes

| ❌ Incorrecto | ✅ Correcto | Explicación |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *I wearing a shirt* | *I **am** wearing a shirt* | Falta *am/is/are* |
| *She am wearing a dress* | *She **is** wearing a dress* | She → is |
| *I am wear a shirt* | *I am **wearing** a shirt* | *-ing* |
| *a blue trousers* | ***blue trousers*** / *a pair of blue trousers* | *trousers* plural |
| *I am wearing a shoes* | *I am wearing **shoes*** / *black shoes* | *shoes* plural |
| *What you are wearing?* | *What **are you** wearing?* | Orden pregunta |
| *I wear a shirt now* (si es “ahora”) | *I **am wearing** a shirt* | Ahora → Continuous |

| Forma | Tip A1 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **wearing** | *WEAR-ing* /ˈweərɪŋ/. |
| **trousers** | *TRAU-zəz* (UK) · a veces *pants* (US = pantalones). |
| **jacket** | *JA-kit*. |
| **shoes** | /ʃuːz/. |
| **I’m / She’s / They’re** | Practica la contracción enlazada a *wearing*. |

En el curso usamos ***trousers*** (UK). En EE. UU. *pants* suele significar pantalones.

## 8b. Mini-diálogos para memorizar

> **A:** What are you wearing today?  
> **B:** I’m wearing a blue shirt and grey trousers.  
> **A:** Nice shoes!  
> **B:** Thanks. They’re black.

> **A:** Is she wearing a dress?  
> **B:** Yes. She’s wearing a red dress and white shoes.  
> **A:** And a hat?  
> **B:** Yes — a small hat.

> **A:** What is he wearing?  
> **B:** He’s wearing a jacket and jeans.  
> **A:** Is he wearing a shirt under the jacket?  
> **B:** Yes. A green shirt.

Cambia solo colores y prendas: fijas *am/is/are wearing*.

Lee la lista dos veces. Luego describe **tu ropa de hoy** en 6–8 frases.

### Rutina de 2 minutos

1. Di 3 frases *I am wearing…*.  
2. Di 2 frases *She/He is wearing…*.  
3. Pregunta y responde: *What are you wearing today?*  
4. Añade 2 colores distintos.

Si te miras o miras una foto, el Continuous se vuelve natural.

### Tour guiado (modelo)

> Today I am wearing a blue shirt and grey trousers. I am wearing black shoes. My sister is wearing a red dress and white shoes. She is wearing a small hat. My brother is wearing a jacket and jeans. He is wearing a green shirt under the jacket. We are all ready for the party.

Grábate leyéndolo. Luego improvisa con **tu** familia o amigos.

### Frases extra para practicar en voz alta

> What are you wearing today?  
> I’m wearing a white shirt and blue trousers.  
> Is your sister wearing a dress?  
> Yes, she’s wearing a red dress and black shoes.  
> He is wearing a jacket and a hat.  
> It is cold. Wear a jacket.  
> My shoes are black.  
> She is wearing a small hat.  
> We are wearing white shirts.  
> They are wearing hats.

Personaliza con tu armario. Cuanto más concreto el color, mejor.

### Checklist mental

- Ahora / hoy → *am/is/are + wearing*  
- I → **am**; he/she → **is**; you/we/they → **are**  
- *trousers* y *shoes* → plural  
- color + prenda → *a blue shirt*  
- Pregunta → *What are you wearing?*  
- UK: *trousers* (US informal: *pants*)

### Ejercicio 7 — Escribe tu outfit

Escribe 8–10 frases sobre tu ropa de hoy (o de una foto). Usa:

- *I am wearing…* (mín. 3)  
- *She/He is wearing…* (mín. 1)  
- al menos 4 prendas distintas  
- al menos 3 colores
