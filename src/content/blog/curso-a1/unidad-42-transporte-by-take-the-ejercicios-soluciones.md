## Lección 1 — Gramática: by / take the

**Objetivo:** *by + transporte* (sin artículo) y *take the + transporte*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I go to work ____ bus. → *by / the / a*  
2. We travel ____ plane. → *by / the / an*  
3. I ____ the bus every day. → *take / takes / taking*  
4. She goes ____ car. → *by / the / a*  
5. He ____ the train to London. → *takes / take / taking*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **by** — *by bus* (sin *the*).  
2. **by**  
3. **take**  
4. **by**  
5. **takes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *Go by bus* means ____. → a) using the bus to travel · b) buying a bus · c) walking only  
7. *Take the bus* means ____. → a) using the bus as a passenger · b) driving a plane · c) never travel  
8. After *by* we use ____. → a) transport without an article (by car, by train) · b) the always · c) a always  
9. Correct: ____. → a) I go by train. · b) I go by the train. · c) I go train by.  
10. With *take* we usually ____. → a) use “the” before bus or train · b) never use the · c) use -ing only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) using the bus to travel**  
7. **a) using the bus as a passenger**  
8. **a) transport without an article (by car, by train)**  
9. **a) I go by train.**  
10. **a) use “the” before bus or train**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. I / go / to work / by / bus.  
12. She / takes / the / train / every Monday.  
13. We / travel / by / plane / to Spain.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I go to work by bus.  
12. She takes the train every Monday.  
13. We travel by plane to Spain.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We say *by bus* (not *by the bus*). → True / False  
15. We say *take the train* (with *the*). → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: transporte

**Objetivo:** *bus, train, plane, taxi, bicycle*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *Bus* = ____. → a) a large road vehicle that carries many people · b) a plane · c) a bike  
2. *Train* = ____. → a) a long vehicle that travels on rails · b) a taxi · c) a bicycle  
3. *Plane* = ____. → a) a vehicle that flies in the air · b) a bus · c) a train  
4. *Taxi* = ____. → a) a car you pay to take you somewhere · b) a free bike · c) a train  
5. *Bicycle* = ____. → a) a two-wheeled vehicle that you pedal · b) a plane · c) a bus

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) a large road vehicle that carries many people**  
2. **a) a long vehicle that travels on rails**  
3. **a) a vehicle that flies in the air**  
4. **a) a car you pay to take you somewhere**  
5. **a) a two-wheeled vehicle that you pedal**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Empareja

6. Empareja: bus / train / plane / taxi / bicycle → many passengers on the road · on rails · flies · you pay the driver · two wheels and pedals  
7. Empareja: by bus / by train / by plane / by taxi / by bicycle → take the bus · take the train · fly · pay for a car ride · cycle

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **bus → many passengers on the road** · **train → on rails** · **plane → flies** · **taxi → you pay the driver** · **bicycle → two wheels and pedals**  
7. **by bus → take the bus** · **by train → take the train** · **by plane → fly** · **by taxi → pay for a car ride** · **by bicycle → cycle**

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. I go ____ bicycle. → *by / the / a*  
9. She takes ____ train. → *the / a / —*  
10. We travel by ____. → *plane / planesing / the*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **by**  
9. **the**  
10. **plane**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Definiciones

11. Which vehicle flies? → a) Plane. · b) Bus. · c) Bicycle.  
12. Which has two wheels and pedals? → a) Bicycle. · b) Train. · c) Plane.  
13. Which do you pay a driver for? → a) A taxi. · b) A bicycle. · c) Walking.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) Plane.**  
12. **a) Bicycle.**  
13. **a) A taxi.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *By car* and *by taxi* do not need *the*. → True / False  
15. *Take the bus* uses *the*. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: How we travel

**Objetivo:** leer cómo viaja una familia.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-42/reading-transport.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: How we travel"></audio>

> I go to work by bus every day. My sister takes the train to London. We sometimes travel by plane for holidays. Last week I went to the airport by taxi. My brother goes to school by bicycle. He likes it because it is fast and cheap.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. How does the writer go to work? → a) By bus. · b) By plane. · c) By train.  
2. What does the sister take? → a) The train. · b) The bus. · c) The taxi.  
3. When do they travel by plane? → a) For holidays. · b) Every day. · c) Never.  
4. How did the writer go to the airport last week? → a) By taxi. · b) By bicycle. · c) By bus.  
5. How does the brother go to school? → a) By bicycle. · b) By plane. · c) By train.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) By bus.**  
2. **a) The train.**  
3. **a) For holidays.**  
4. **a) By taxi.**  
5. **a) By bicycle.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Why does the brother like the bicycle? → a) Because it is fast and cheap. · b) Because it is slow. · c) The text doesn't say.  
7. *Go by bus* means ____. → a) Using the bus. · b) Buying a bus. · c) Flying.  
8. *Take the train* means ____. → a) Using the train. · b) Driving a plane. · c) Walking.  
9. A plane ____. → a) flies in the air · b) travels only on rails · c) has pedals  
10. A taxi is ____. → a) a car you pay for · b) a free bike · c) a train

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Because it is fast and cheap.**  
7. **a) Using the bus.**  
8. **a) Using the train.**  
9. **a) flies in the air**  
10. **a) a car you pay for**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text is about ____. → a) Different types of transport. · b) Only food. · c) Only sports.  
12. A bicycle is ____. → a) A vehicle with two wheels that you pedal. · b) A plane. · c) A train.  
13. A bus is ____. → a) A road vehicle that carries many people. · b) A plane. · c) A bike.  
14. A train is ____. → a) A vehicle that travels on rails. · b) A taxi. · c) A bicycle.  
15. The main topic is ____. → a) How people travel. · b) Cooking. · c) Music.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) Different types of transport.**  
12. **a) A vehicle with two wheels that you pedal.**  
13. **a) A road vehicle that carries many people.**  
14. **a) A vehicle that travels on rails.**  
15. **a) How people travel.**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Commute

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo sobre transporte diario.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-42/listening-commute.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Commute"></audio>

> **A:** How do you go to work?  
> **B:** I take the bus.  
> **A:** I go by car. What about your sister?  
> **B:** She travels by train to London.  
> **A:** Do you ever go by plane?  
> **B:** Yes, for holidays. Last time I went to the airport by taxi.  
> **A:** My son goes to school by bicycle.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. How does B go to work? → a) By bus; B takes the bus. · b) By plane. · c) By bicycle.  
2. How does A go to work? → a) By car. · b) By train. · c) By bus.  
3. How does B's sister travel? → a) By train. · b) By bus. · c) By taxi.  
4. When does B go by plane? → a) For holidays. · b) Every day. · c) Never.  
5. How did B go to the airport last time? → a) By taxi. · b) By bicycle. · c) By train.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) By bus; B takes the bus.**  
2. **a) By car.**  
3. **a) By train.**  
4. **a) For holidays.**  
5. **a) By taxi.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. How does A's son go to school? → a) By bicycle. · b) By plane. · c) By taxi.  
7. *Take the bus* means ____. → a) Use the bus. · b) Buy a bus. · c) Fly.  
8. *Travel by train* means ____. → a) Use the train. · b) Drive a plane. · c) Walk.  
9. The dialogue is about ____. → a) How people travel. · b) Food. · c) Sports.  
10. A plane ____. → a) flies · b) only pedals · c) only rails

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) By bicycle.**  
7. **a) Use the bus.**  
8. **a) Use the train.**  
9. **a) How people travel.**  
10. **a) flies**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. A taxi is ____. → a) A car you pay for. · b) A free bike. · c) A train.  
12. A bicycle is ____. → a) A two-wheeled vehicle that you pedal. · b) A plane. · c) A bus.  
13. Who travels by train to London? → a) B’s sister. · b) A’s son. · c) Nobody.  
14. A bus is ____. → a) A road vehicle that carries many people. · b) A plane. · c) A bike.  
15. The topic is ____. → a) Transport. · b) Cooking. · c) Music.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) A car you pay for.**  
12. **a) A two-wheeled vehicle that you pedal.**  
13. **a) B’s sister.**  
14. **a) A road vehicle that carries many people.**  
15. **a) Transport.**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My transport

**Objetivo:** escribir sobre cómo viajas.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I go to work ____ bus. → *by / the / a*  
2. She ____ the train every Monday. → *takes / take / taking*  
3. We travel by ____. → *plane / the / a*  
4. He goes ____ bicycle. → *by / the / an*  
5. I take ____ bus every day. → *the / a / —*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **by**  
2. **takes**  
3. **plane**  
4. **by**  
5. **the**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. I / go / to work / by / bus.  
7. She / takes / the / train / every Monday.  
8. We / travel / by / plane / for holidays.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I go to work by bus.  
7. She takes the train every Monday.  
8. We travel by plane for holidays.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Escribe cómo vas al trabajo/colegio con *by*.  
10. Escribe sobre un familiar con *takes the…*.  
11. Dos frases: diario + vacaciones.  
12. Pregunta y respuesta: *How do you go to work?*  
13. Párrafo corto (4 frases) sobre transporte en tu familia.  
14. Dos frases sobre *take the bus*.  
15. Una frase en pasado + una en presente.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I go to work by bus.  
10. My sister takes the train to London.  
11. I go to school by bus. We travel by plane for holidays.  
12. How do you go to work? I take the bus.  
13. I go to work by bus. My sister takes the train. We travel by plane for holidays. My brother goes by bicycle.  
14. I take the bus every day. It is cheap and fast.  
15. I went to the airport by taxi. I go to work by bus.

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| **by + transporte** | I go **by bus** / **by car** / **by plane**. |
| **take the + transporte** | I **take the bus** / She **takes the train**. |
| Sin *the* | by bus, by train, by taxi, by bicycle |
| Con *the* | take **the** bus, take **the** train |
