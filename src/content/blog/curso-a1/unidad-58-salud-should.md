## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 58 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Dar consejos con ***should + verbo base***.
- Dar consejos negativos con ***shouldn't***.
- Nombrar síntomas: ***headache, fever, cold***.
- Hablar de ***medicine, pharmacy, doctor, rest***.
- Decir qué hacer (y qué no) cuando estás ***ill / sick***.

---

## 1. *Should / shouldn't* + verbo base

| Forma | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **should** | consejo | You **should** see a doctor. |
| **shouldn't** | consejo negativo | You **shouldn't** go to work. |
| should + **base verb** | sin *to* | You should **rest**. |

> You should see a doctor if you have a fever.  
> You should take medicine for a headache.  
> You should drink a lot of water when you have a cold.  
> You shouldn't go to work if you are very ill.

## 2. Vocabulario: síntomas y lugares

| Palabra | Significado | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **headache** | dolor de cabeza | I have a headache. |
| **fever** | fiebre | I have a slight fever. |
| **cold** | resfriado | I think I have a cold. |
| **medicine** | medicina | Take some medicine. |
| **pharmacy** | farmacia | Go to the pharmacy. |
| **doctor** | médico/a | See a doctor. |
| **rest** | descansar | You should rest. |
| **ill / sick** | enfermo/a | I feel ill today. |

> You need medicine from the pharmacy.

## 3. Consejos típicos por síntoma

| Situación | Consejo |
| :--- | :--- |
| headache | You should take medicine or rest. |
| fever | You should see a doctor. / You should rest. |
| cold | You should drink water and rest. |
| very ill | You shouldn't go to work. |
| fever gets worse | You should see a doctor. |

> He should go to the pharmacy for medicine.  
> Go to the pharmacy for medicine.  
> You shouldn't go to work when you are ill.

## 6. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| You should to rest. | You should **rest**. | sin *to* |
| He shoulds see a doctor. | He **should** see… | sin *-s* |
| You shouldn't to go. | You shouldn't **go**. | verbo base |
| I have headache. | I have **a** headache. | artículo *a* |
| I have fever. | I have **a** fever. | artículo *a* |
| Go to pharmacy. | Go to **the** pharmacy. | *the* + pharmacy |

## 8. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — *should* / *shouldn't*

1. You \_\_\_\_ see a doctor if you have a fever.  
2. You \_\_\_\_ take medicine for a headache.  
3. You \_\_\_\_ go to work if you are very ill.  
4. He \_\_\_\_ go to the pharmacy for medicine.  
5. You \_\_\_\_ drink a lot of water when you have a cold.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **should** · 2. **should** · 3. **shouldn't** · 4. **should** · 5. **should**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Vocabulario

1. Pain in your head = \_\_\_\_  
2. High body temperature = \_\_\_\_  
3. Illness with cough / runny nose = \_\_\_\_  
4. Place to buy medicine = \_\_\_\_  
5. Something you take when ill = \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **headache** · 2. **fever** · 3. **cold** · 4. **pharmacy** · 5. **medicine**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Completa

1. I have \_\_\_\_ headache.  
2. You should \_\_\_\_ at home. (descansar)  
3. Go to \_\_\_\_ pharmacy for medicine.  
4. You shouldn't \_\_\_\_ to work when you are ill.  
5. If the fever gets worse I should see a \_\_\_\_.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a** · 2. **rest** · 3. **the** · 4. **go** · 5. **doctor**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Empareja

1. should  
2. shouldn't  
3. pharmacy  
4. headache  
5. fever  
6. cold  

a) consejo negativo · b) farmacia · c) consejo · d) resfriado · e) dolor de cabeza · f) fiebre

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **c** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **b** · 4 → **e** · 5 → **f** · 6 → **d**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrige

1. *You should to rest.*  
2. *He shoulds see a doctor.*  
3. *I have headache.*  
4. *You shouldn't to go to work.*  
5. *Go to pharmacy.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. You should **rest**.  
2. He **should** see a doctor.  
3. I have **a** headache.  
4. You shouldn't **go** to work.  
5. Go to **the** pharmacy.
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa el diálogo

A: I have a bad \_\_\_\_.  
B: You \_\_\_\_ take some medicine.  
A: Where can I get it?  
B: At the \_\_\_\_. Or if you have a \_\_\_\_ you should see a \_\_\_\_.  
A: I don't have a fever. Just a headache.  
B: Then you should \_\_\_\_ and drink water. You \_\_\_\_ work on the computer all day.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**headache** · **should** · **pharmacy** · **fever** · **doctor** · **rest** · **shouldn't**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Completa el texto

I feel \_\_\_\_ today. I have a headache and a slight \_\_\_\_. I think I have a \_\_\_\_. I should \_\_\_\_ at home and drink a lot of water. I should take some \_\_\_\_ from the pharmacy. I \_\_\_\_ go to work today.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**ill** · **fever** · **cold** · **rest** · **medicine** · **shouldn't**
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Elige la correcta

1. After should we use ____  
   a) to + verb · b) base verb · c) -ing  
2. Shouldn't = ____  
   a) should not · b) must · c) can not  
3. Where do you buy medicine? ____  
   a) hospital only · b) pharmacy · c) restaurant  
4. For a headache you ____  
   a) should take medicine or rest · b) shouldn't drink water · c) must fly

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b** · 2. **a** · 3. **b** · 4. **a**
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Traduce

1. Deberías ver a un médico.  
2. No deberías ir a trabajar.  
3. Tengo dolor de cabeza.  
4. Deberías tomar medicina.  
5. Ve a la farmacia.  
6. Deberías descansar y beber agua.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. You should see a doctor.  
2. You shouldn't go to work.  
3. I have a headache.  
4. You should take medicine.  
5. Go to the pharmacy.  
6. You should rest and drink water.
</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Escribe tu consejo

Escribe 8–10 frases. Incluye:

- 2 síntomas (*headache / fever / cold*)  
- 3 × *should*  
- 2 × *shouldn't*  
- *medicine* o *pharmacy*  
- *doctor* o *rest*

**Modelo:** el texto de la sección 4.

## 9. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Repasa *should/shouldn't* y la tabla de síntomas.  
2. **8 min** — Haz los ejercicios 1–5.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha y repite el diálogo.  
4. **5–8 min** — Dale consejos a un amigo “enfermo” en voz alta.

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *should* + verbo base  
- [ ] Uso *shouldn't* sin *to*  
- [ ] Nombró *headache, fever, cold*  
- [ ] Relaciono *medicine* con *pharmacy*  
- [ ] Doy 3 consejos de salud

## 10. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Recuerda: *should* ≠ *must* (consejo vs obligación fuerte).  
2. Practica *I have a…* con artículo *a*.  
3. Une síntoma + consejo en una frase.  
4. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 58](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-58-salud-should-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 58 del curso A1](/curso-a1/unit-58).
