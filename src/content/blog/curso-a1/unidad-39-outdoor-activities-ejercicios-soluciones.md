## Lección 1 — Gramática: planes futuros

**Objetivo:** Present Continuous para planes ya decididos (*I'm going camping…*).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ going camping next week. → *'m / is / are*  
2. She is ____ hiking on Saturday. → *going / go / goes*  
3. We are ____ cycling on Sunday. → *going / go / goes*  
4. He ____ fishing tomorrow. → *'s going / go / going*  
5. They are ____ to the mountains. → *going / go / goes*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **'m** (*am*)  
2. **going**  
3. **going**  
4. **'s going** (*is going*)  
5. **going**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Present Continuous here talks about ____. → a) plans already decided · b) past habits · c) only now this second  
7. The tense for these plans is ____. → a) Present Continuous · b) Past Simple · c) Present Perfect  
8. *I'm going camping next week* is ____. → a) a future plan · b) a past story · c) a command  
9. After *going* we often use ____. → a) an activity ending in -ing · b) only past verbs · c) nothing  
10. Correct: ____. → a) We're going hiking next week. · b) We going hike. · c) We goes hiking.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) plans already decided**  
7. **a) Present Continuous**  
8. **a) a future plan**  
9. **a) an activity ending in -ing**  
10. **a) We're going hiking next week.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. I'm / going / camping / next week.  
12. She / is / going / cycling / on Sunday.  
13. They / are / going / fishing / tomorrow.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I'm going camping next week.  
12. She is going cycling on Sunday.  
13. They are going fishing tomorrow.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *I'm going camping next week* can mean a future plan. → True / False  
15. We often add a time word: *tomorrow, on Saturday, next week*. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: outdoor

**Objetivo:** *hiking, camping, cycling, fishing, tent, trail, backpack*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *Hiking* = ____. → a) walking in nature or the mountains · b) sleeping in a tent · c) riding a bike  
2. *Camping* = ____. → a) sleeping outdoors in a tent · b) catching fish · c) driving a car  
3. *Cycling* = ____. → a) riding a bicycle · b) walking · c) swimming  
4. *Fishing* = ____. → a) catching fish · b) camping only · c) hiking only  
5. *Outdoor* = ____. → a) outside, in nature · b) inside a house · c) only at school

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) walking in nature or the mountains**  
2. **a) sleeping outdoors in a tent**  
3. **a) riding a bicycle**  
4. **a) catching fish**  
5. **a) outside, in nature**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Empareja

6. Empareja: hiking / camping / cycling / fishing / next week → walk in the mountains · tent · bicycle · fish · future  
7. Empareja objeto → actividad: tent / trail / bike / rod / backpack → camping · hiking · cycling · fishing · hiking/camping

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **hiking → walk in the mountains** · **camping → tent** · **cycling → bicycle** · **fishing → fish** · **next week → future**  
7. **tent → camping** · **trail → hiking** · **bike → cycling** · **rod → fishing** · **backpack → hiking/camping**

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. I am ____ camping next week. → *going / go / goes*  
9. She is going ____ on Saturday. → *hiking / hike / hikes*  
10. We are ____ cycling next week. → *going / go / goes*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **going**  
9. **hiking**  
10. **going**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Definiciones

11. A *trail* is ____. → a) a path for walking · b) a car · c) a fish  
12. A *tent* is ____. → a) a shelter used for camping · b) a bike · c) a book  
13. *Next weekend* is ____. → a) future time · b) past time · c) never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) a path for walking**  
12. **a) a shelter used for camping**  
13. **a) future time**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena

14. I / am / going / hiking / on Saturday.  
15. We / are / going / cycling / next week.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. I am going hiking on Saturday.  
15. We are going cycling next week.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Family outdoors

**Objetivo:** leer planes outdoor de una familia.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-39/reading-outdoors.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Family outdoors"></audio>

> I'm going camping next week with my family. We love outdoor activities. My brother is going hiking on Saturday, and my sister is going cycling on Sunday. My dad is going fishing tomorrow. We have a tent and backpacks. Next weekend, we are all going to the mountains.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the writer doing next week? → a) Going camping · b) Going shopping · c) Staying home  
2. Who is the writer going with? → a) With the family · b) Alone · c) With classmates only  
3. What is the brother doing on Saturday? → a) Going hiking · b) Going fishing · c) Going cycling  
4. What is the sister doing on Sunday? → a) Going cycling · b) Going hiking · c) Going camping  
5. When is dad going fishing? → a) Tomorrow · b) Next year · c) Never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Going camping**  
2. **a) With the family**  
3. **a) Going hiking**  
4. **a) Going cycling**  
5. **a) Tomorrow**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. What equipment do they have? → a) A tent and backpacks · b) Only a car · c) Nothing  
7. Where are they going next weekend? → a) To the mountains · b) To the beach only · c) To school  
8. The text is about ____. → a) outdoor activities and future plans · b) food · c) work  
9. Which activity is walking in nature? → a) Hiking · b) Fishing · c) Cycling  
10. They ____ outdoor activities. → a) love · b) hate · c) ignore

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) A tent and backpacks**  
7. **a) To the mountains**  
8. **a) outdoor activities and future plans**  
9. **a) Hiking**  
10. **a) love**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. A *backpack* is ____. → a) a bag carried on your back · b) a tent · c) a bike  
12. Plans use ____. → a) I'm going / He is going · b) I go always past · c) I went only  
13. When are they all going to the mountains? → a) Next weekend · b) Yesterday · c) Never  
14. How many outdoor activities are named? → a) Four: camping, hiking, cycling, and fishing · b) One · c) Ten  
15. *Outdoor* means ____. → a) outside, in nature · b) indoors · c) underwater only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) a bag carried on your back**  
12. **a) I'm going / He is going**  
13. **a) Next weekend**  
14. **a) Four: camping, hiking, cycling, and fishing**  
15. **a) outside, in nature**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Weekend plans

**Objetivo:** escuchar planes outdoor del fin de semana.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-39/listening-plans.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Weekend plans"></audio>

> **A:** What are you doing next weekend?  
> **B:** I'm going camping with my friends.  
> **A:** Nice! I'm going hiking on Saturday.  
> **B:** My brother is going cycling on Sunday. We love outdoor activities.  
> **A:** Me too. Next week, I'm going fishing.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What is B doing next weekend? → a) Going camping · b) Going shopping · c) Working  
2. Who is B going with? → a) With friends · b) Alone · c) With teachers  
3. What is A doing on Saturday? → a) Going hiking · b) Going fishing · c) Going cycling  
4. What is B's brother doing on Sunday? → a) Going cycling · b) Going hiking · c) Going camping  
5. When is A going fishing? → a) Next week · b) Yesterday · c) Never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Going camping**  
2. **a) With friends**  
3. **a) Going hiking**  
4. **a) Going cycling**  
5. **a) Next week**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. They ____ outdoor activities. → a) love · b) hate · c) ignore  
7. A says ____ after hearing they love outdoor activities. → a) Me too. · b) Never · c) Goodbye  
8. A's first reaction to camping is ____. → a) Nice! · b) Boring · c) No  
9. The dialogue is about ____. → a) future outdoor plans · b) past jobs · c) food only  
10. *I'm going camping* here is ____. → a) a future plan · b) a past habit · c) a command

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) love**  
7. **a) Me too.**  
8. **a) Nice!**  
9. **a) future outdoor plans**  
10. **a) a future plan**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. A's first question is ____. → a) What are you doing next weekend? · b) Where were you? · c) Who are you?  
12. *Cycling* means ____. → a) riding a bicycle · b) walking · c) sleeping  
13. How many activities are mentioned? → a) Four: camping, hiking, cycling, and fishing · b) One · c) Zero  
14. B says I ____ camping. → a) 'm going · b) go always · c) went  
15. *Me too* means ____. → a) I agree / the same for me · b) I disagree · c) goodbye

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) What are you doing next weekend?**  
12. **a) riding a bicycle**  
13. **a) Four: camping, hiking, cycling, and fishing**  
14. **a) 'm going**  
15. **a) I agree / the same for me**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My outdoor plans

**Objetivo:** escribir planes outdoor con Present Continuous.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ going camping next week. → *'m / is / are*  
2. She is ____ hiking on Saturday. → *going / go / goes*  
3. We are ____ fishing tomorrow. → *going / go / goes*  
4. We love ____ activities. → *outdoor / indoors / indoor*  
5. He ____ going cycling on Sunday. → *'s / am / are*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **'m**  
2. **going**  
3. **going**  
4. **outdoor**  
5. **'s**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. I'm / going / camping / next week.  
7. They / are / going / to the mountains / next weekend.  
8. My dad / is / going / fishing / tomorrow.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I'm going camping next week.  
7. They are going to the mountains next weekend.  
8. My dad is going fishing tomorrow.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Escribe tu plan: *I'm going… next week.*  
10. Escribe sobre un familiar: *My brother/sister is going…*  
11. Dos planes: mañana + próximo fin de semana.  
12. Frase con *love outdoor activities* + un hobby.  
13. Párrafo corto (4 frases) sobre planes outdoor.  
14. Tres personas: *He / She / They are going…*  
15. Pregunta y respuesta: *What are you doing next weekend?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I'm going camping next week.  
10. My brother is going hiking on Saturday.  
11. I'm going cycling tomorrow. We are going camping next weekend.  
12. We love outdoor activities. I like hiking.  
13. I'm going camping next weekend. My friend is going hiking on Saturday. We have a tent. We love outdoor activities.  
14. He is going fishing tomorrow. She is going cycling on Sunday. They are going camping next week.  
15. What are you doing next weekend? I'm going hiking.

</details>
