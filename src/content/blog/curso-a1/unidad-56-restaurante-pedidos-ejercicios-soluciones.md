## Lección 1 — Gramática: pedidos educados

**Objetivo:** *I would like…*, *Can I have…?*, *would like to + verb*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ like the soup please. → *would / will / want*  
2. Can I ____ the bill please? → *have / to have / having*  
3. I would like ____ order a starter. → *to / for / –*  
4. We ____ like to see the menu. → *would / will / can*  
5. For dessert I ____ like ice cream. → *would / will / want*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **would** — *I would like* = forma educada.  
2. **have** — *Can I have…?*  
3. **to** — *would like to* + verbo.  
4. **would**  
5. **would**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Polite way to order = ____. → a) I would like… · b) I want… · c) Give me…  
7. *Can I have the bill?* = ____. → a) a polite request for the bill · b) order food · c) see the menu  
8. After *would like* we use ____. → a) *to* + verb or a noun · b) verb only · c) *-ing* only  
9. *Starter* = ____. → a) first course before main · b) dessert · c) drink  
10. *Main course* = ____. → a) the main dish of the meal · b) starter · c) dessert

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) I would like…**  
7. **a) a polite request for the bill**  
8. **a) *to* + verb or a noun**  
9. **a) first course before main**  
10. **a) the main dish of the meal**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. I / would like / the / chicken / please.  
12. Can / I / have / the / menu / please?  
13. For / dessert / I would like / ice cream.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I would like the chicken please.  
12. Can I have the menu please?  
13. For dessert I would like ice cream.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *I would like* is more polite than *I want*. → True / False  
15. At a restaurant we say *Can I have the bill?* to pay. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: restaurante

**Objetivo:** *menu, starter, main course, dessert, bill, waiter*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *Menu* = ____. → a) a list of food and drinks · b) the bill · c) the table  
2. *Starter* = ____. → a) first course, e.g. soup or salad · b) main dish · c) dessert  
3. *Main course* = ____. → a) the main dish of the meal · b) starter · c) dessert  
4. *Dessert* = ____. → a) a sweet dish at the end · b) starter · c) drink  
5. *Bill* = ____. → a) the paper showing what you must pay · b) menu · c) starter

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) a list of food and drinks**  
2. **a) first course, e.g. soup or salad**  
3. **a) the main dish of the meal**  
4. **a) a sweet dish at the end**  
5. **a) the paper showing what you must pay**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Empareja

6. Empareja: menu / starter / main course / dessert / bill → list of dishes · first course · main dish · sweet at end · what you pay  
7. Empareja: soup / chicken / ice cream / waiter / table → often a starter · main course · dessert · serves food · where you sit

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **menu → list of dishes** · **starter → first course** · **main course → main dish** · **dessert → sweet at end** · **bill → what you pay**  
7. **soup → often a starter** · **chicken → main course** · **ice cream → dessert** · **waiter → serves food** · **table → where you sit**

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–13 — Completa / elige

8. Can I see the ____ please? → *menu / bill / dessert*  
9. For ____ I would like soup. → *starter / main course / dessert*  
10. I would like the ____ please. → *bill / menu / starter*  
11. Order of courses: ____. → a) starter, main course, dessert · b) dessert, main, starter · c) main, starter, dessert  
12. *Waiter* = ____. → a) a person who serves food in a restaurant · b) chef · c) customer  
13. To pay at a restaurant you ask for ____. → a) the bill · b) the menu · c) the starter

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **menu**  
9. **starter**  
10. **bill**  
11. **a) starter, main course, dessert**  
12. **a) a person who serves food in a restaurant**  
13. **a) the bill**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. Dessert comes after the main course. → True / False  
15. The menu shows what food you can order. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Tonight at a restaurant

**Objetivo:** leer sobre una comida en el restaurante.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-56/reading-restaurant.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Tonight at a restaurant"></audio>

> We are at a restaurant tonight. The waiter brings the menu. I would like a soup for starter. My friend wants salad. For main course I order chicken and rice. We have water to drink. After dinner we would like dessert. I have ice cream. Then we ask for the bill and pay.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Where are they tonight? → a) At a restaurant · b) At home · c) At a cafe  
2. What does the waiter bring? → a) The menu · b) The bill · c) The food  
3. What starter does the writer want? → a) Soup · b) Salad · c) Chicken  
4. What main course does he/she order? → a) Chicken and rice · b) Soup · c) Ice cream  
5. What do they drink? → a) Water · b) Coffee · c) Juice

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) At a restaurant**  
2. **a) The menu**  
3. **a) Soup**  
4. **a) Chicken and rice**  
5. **a) Water**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. What dessert does the writer have? → a) Ice cream · b) Salad · c) Soup  
7. After dessert they ____. → a) ask for the bill and pay · b) order more food · c) leave without paying  
8. *Starter* means ____. → a) first course · b) main dish · c) dessert  
9. *Bill* means ____. → a) what you pay · b) menu · c) starter  
10. *I would like* means ____. → a) a polite way to order · b) I want now · c) give me

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Ice cream**  
7. **a) ask for the bill and pay**  
8. **a) first course**  
9. **a) what you pay**  
10. **a) a polite way to order**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text is about ____. → a) a meal at a restaurant · b) cooking at home · c) a supermarket  
12. *Main course* means ____. → a) main dish · b) starter · c) dessert  
13. Who wants salad for starter? → a) The friend · b) The writer · c) The waiter  
14. *Dessert* means ____. → a) sweet at the end of the meal · b) starter · c) drink  
15. What is the order of the meal? → a) Starter, main course, dessert · b) Dessert, main, starter · c) Main, bill, dessert

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) a meal at a restaurant**  
12. **a) main dish**  
13. **a) The friend**  
14. **a) sweet at the end of the meal**  
15. **a) Starter, main course, dessert**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Are you ready to order?

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo de pedido en el restaurante.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-56/listening-restaurant.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Are you ready to order?"></audio>

> **Waiter:** Are you ready to order?  
> **Customer:** Yes. I would like the soup for starter please.  
> **Waiter:** And for main course?  
> **Customer:** I would like the fish please.  
> **Waiter:** Anything to drink?  
> **Customer:** Water please.  
> **Waiter:** Dessert?  
> **Customer:** No thank you. Can I have the bill please?  
> **Waiter:** Of course.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What does the customer order for starter? → a) Soup · b) Salad · c) Fish  
2. What main course does the customer want? → a) Fish · b) Chicken · c) Soup  
3. What does the customer drink? → a) Water · b) Coffee · c) Wine  
4. Does the customer want dessert? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The dialogue doesn't say  
5. What does the customer ask for at the end? → a) The bill · b) The menu · c) More water

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Soup**  
2. **a) Fish**  
3. **a) Water**  
4. **a) No**  
5. **a) The bill**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. *I would like* means ____. → a) a polite way to order · b) I want now · c) give me  
7. *Starter* means ____. → a) first course · b) main course · c) dessert  
8. The dialogue is at ____. → a) a restaurant · b) a cafe · c) home  
9. *Can I have the bill?* means ____. → a) a request to pay · b) order food · c) ask for the menu  
10. *Main course* means ____. → a) main dish · b) starter · c) dessert

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) a polite way to order**  
7. **a) first course**  
8. **a) a restaurant**  
9. **a) a request to pay**  
10. **a) main dish**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Who says *Are you ready to order?* → a) The waiter · b) The customer · c) Both  
12. *Bill* means ____. → a) what you pay · b) menu · c) starter  
13. *Of course* means ____. → a) yes, certainly · b) no · c) maybe  
14. *Dessert* means ____. → a) sweet at the end · b) starter · c) drink  
15. The dialogue is about ____. → a) ordering food and asking for the bill · b) cooking at home · c) buying at the supermarket

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) The waiter**  
12. **a) what you pay**  
13. **a) yes, certainly**  
14. **a) sweet at the end**  
15. **a) ordering food and asking for the bill**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My order

**Objetivo:** escribir pedidos educados en el restaurante.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ like the soup please. → *would / will / want*  
2. Can I ____ the bill? → *have / to have / having*  
3. For ____ I would like salad. → *starter / main course / dessert*  
4. We would like ____ see the menu. → *to / for / –*  
5. For dessert I ____ like ice cream. → *would / will / can*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **would**  
2. **have**  
3. **starter**  
4. **to**  
5. **would**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. I / would like / the / chicken / please.  
7. Can / I / have / the / menu / please?  
8. For / dessert / I would like / ice cream.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I would like the chicken please.  
7. Can I have the menu please?  
8. For dessert I would like ice cream.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Pedir la carta de forma educada.  
10. Qué quisieras de entrante (*I would like…*).  
11. Dos frases: plato principal y postre.  
12. Pedir la cuenta de forma educada.  
13. Párrafo corto (3–4 frases): pedido completo.  
14. Diferencia *I want* e *I would like* en 2–3 frases.  
15. Diálogo corto: carta → platos → cuenta.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. Can I have the menu please?  
10. I would like the soup for starter please.  
11. For main course I would like the fish. For dessert I would like ice cream.  
12. Can I have the bill please?  
13. I would like the soup for starter. For main course I would like the chicken. Water please. Can I have the bill please?  
14. I would like is more polite than I want. At a restaurant we say I would like the soup please.  
15. Customer: Can I have the menu please? Waiter: Here you are. Customer: I would like the chicken and the bill please.

</details>
