## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 43 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Usar ***Present Simple*** para horarios (*The train leaves at…*).
- Decir ***leave / depart*** (salir) y ***arrive*** (llegar).
- Usar ***visit, stay, explore*** en planes de viaje.
- Preguntar: ***When does your train leave?*** / ***What time does it arrive?***
- Describir un viaje: salida → llegada → hotel → museos → vuelo de vuelta.

---

## 1. Present Simple para horarios

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| The train **leaves** at… | The train **leaves** at nine o'clock. |
| The bus **departs** at… | The bus **departs** at half past seven. |
| The plane **arrives** at… | The plane **arrives** at three o'clock. |
| It **arrives in** London at… | It **arrives in** London at half past ten. |

![Horarios Present Simple A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-43/a1-unit43-schedules.png)

```
Present Simple (schedules)
→ The train leaves / arrives / departs at + time
→ 3.ª persona: leaves, arrives, departs (con -s)
```

### Tip

Aunque hables de **mañana** o la **próxima semana**, el horario fijo va en Present Simple: *My train leaves at eight tomorrow.* No digas *is leaving* para el horario tipográfico del billete (en A1 priorizamos Present Simple).

### Tercera persona

| Sujeto | Verbo |
| :--- | :--- |
| the train / the bus / the plane / it | leave**s**, arrive**s**, depart**s** |
| I / you / we / they | leave, arrive, depart |

## 2. Verbos de viaje

| Verbo | Significado | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **leave** | salir | The train **leaves** at eight. |
| **depart** | partir/salir | My flight **departs** on Sunday. |
| **arrive** | llegar | It **arrives** in London at 10:30. |
| **visit** | visitar | I want to **visit** the museums. |
| **stay** | quedarse | I **stay** at a hotel for three nights. |
| **explore** | explorar | I want to **explore** the city. |

![Verbos de viaje A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-43/a1-unit43-travel-verbs.png)

### arrive in / arrive at

| Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| arrive **in** + ciudad | arrive **in** London |
| arrive **at** + hora | arrives **at** half past ten |
| arrive **at** + lugar | arrive **at** the station |

### stay at

> I **stay at** a hotel for two nights.  
> She **stays at** a hotel near the museum.

## 8. Errores típicos de hispanohablantes

| ❌ Incorrecto | ✅ Correcto | Explicación |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *The train **leave** at nine* | *The train **leaves** at nine* | 3.ª persona *-s* |
| *The train **is leave** at nine* | *The train **leaves** at nine* | Present Simple |
| *arrive **to** London* | *arrive **in** London* | *in* + ciudad |
| *stay **in** a hotel* (A1 tip) | *stay **at** a hotel* | *at* + hotel (muy frecuente) |
| *My flight **depart** on Sunday* | *My flight **departs** on Sunday* | *-s* |
| *I want **visit** museums* | *I want **to** visit museums* | *want to* + verbo |

## 12. Plan de práctica (15 minutos)

2. **3 min** — Practica *visit / stay / explore*.  
3. **4 min** — Reescribe el reading con tu ciudad.

## 13. Consejos para practicar

1. **Mira un horario real** (tren/avión) y di 5 frases *leaves/arrives at…*.  
2. **Pareja:** uno pregunta horarios; el otro responde + *stay/visit/explore*.  
3. **Combina** U42: *I go by train. The train leaves at eight.*  
4. Prepárate para la [Unidad 44](/curso-a1/unit-44): *The Weather* (*It is sunny / rainy…*).
