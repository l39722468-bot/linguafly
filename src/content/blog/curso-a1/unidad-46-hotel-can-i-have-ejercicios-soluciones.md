## Lección 1 — Gramática: peticiones

**Objetivo:** *Can I have/get…?*, *Is there…?*, *I would like…*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. Can I ____ a key? → *have / has / having*  
2. ____ there a restaurant? → *Is / Are / Am*  
3. Can I ____ room service? → *get / gets / getting*  
4. Is there ____ Wi-Fi? → *any / an / a*  
5. I would ____ a double room. → *like / likes / liking*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **have** — *Can I have…?*  
2. **Is** — *Is there…?*  
3. **get**  
4. **any**  
5. **like**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *Can I have…?* is a ____. → a) polite request · b) past story · c) refusal  
7. *Is there…?* asks about ____. → a) the existence of something · b) only the past · c) a sport  
8. At reception you ____. → a) ask for a key and check in · b) only cook · c) only fly  
9. Correct: ____. → a) Can I have a key? · b) Can I has a key? · c) Can I having a key?  
10. *Room service* means ____. → a) food or help brought to your room · b) only the lobby · c) only the key

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) polite request**  
7. **a) the existence of something**  
8. **a) ask for a key and check in**  
9. **a) Can I have a key?**  
10. **a) food or help brought to your room**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. Can / I / have / a / key / for / room / five?  
12. Is / there / a / double / room / available?  
13. I / would / like / room / service, / please.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. Can I have a key for room five?  
12. Is there a double room available?  
13. I would like room service, please.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *Can I have…?* is a polite way to ask for something. → True / False  
15. *Is there Wi-Fi?* asks if Wi-Fi exists in the hotel. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: hotel

**Objetivo:** *reception, room service, single/double room, key, booking, check in*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *Reception* = ____. → a) a desk where you check in · b) only the beach · c) only the plane  
2. *Room service* = ____. → a) food or help brought to your room · b) only the lobby · c) only the key  
3. *Single room* = ____. → a) a room for one person · b) a room for ten people · c) only the restaurant  
4. *Double room* = ____. → a) a room for two people, often with one bed · b) a room for one person · c) only Wi-Fi  
5. *Key* = ____. → a) something used to open your room · b) only a password · c) only a booking

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) a desk where you check in**  
2. **a) food or help brought to your room**  
3. **a) a room for one person**  
4. **a) a room for two people, often with one bed**  
5. **a) something used to open your room**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Empareja

6. Empareja: reception / room service / single room / double room / key → check in/get a key · order food to your room · one person · two people · open the room  
7. Empareja: hotel / available / check in / booking / lobby → place to stay · free to use · register when you arrive · reservation · entrance area

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **reception → check in/get a key** · **room service → order food to your room** · **single room → one person** · **double room → two people** · **key → open the room**  
7. **hotel → place to stay** · **available → free to use** · **check in → register when you arrive** · **booking → reservation** · **lobby → entrance area**

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–13 — Completa / elige

8. Can I have a ____ for room five? → *key / keysing / lobby*  
9. I go to ____ to check in. → *reception / restauranting / beach*  
10. Is there a ____ room available? → *double / doubles / doubling*  
11. *Available* means ____. → a) free; available for use · b) closed forever · c) only past  
12. *Check in* means ____. → a) register when you arrive at the hotel · b) leave forever · c) only cook  
13. Where do you get your key? → a) at reception · b) at the beach · c) on the plane

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **key**  
9. **reception**  
10. **double**  
11. **a) free; available for use**  
12. **a) register when you arrive at the hotel**  
13. **a) at reception**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. A single room is for one person. → True / False  
15. A booking is a reservation. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Check-in

**Objetivo:** leer un check-in en el hotel.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-46/reading-hotel.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Check-in"></audio>

> I am at the hotel today. I go to reception to check in. Can I have a key for room twelve? The receptionist says yes. Is there a restaurant? Yes, there is. I would like room service tonight. I have a single room. It is clean and quiet.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Where is the writer? → a) At the hotel · b) At school · c) At the beach  
2. What does the writer ask for? → a) A key for room twelve · b) A plane ticket · c) A bike  
3. Is there a restaurant? → a) Yes, there is · b) No · c) The text doesn't say  
4. What does the writer want tonight? → a) Room service · b) A taxi only · c) Nothing  
5. What type of room? → a) A single room · b) A double room · c) A suite only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) At the hotel**  
2. **a) A key for room twelve**  
3. **a) Yes, there is**  
4. **a) Room service**  
5. **a) A single room**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. How is the room? → a) It is clean and quiet · b) It is dirty · c) It is noisy  
7. Where does the writer go first? → a) To reception · b) To the beach · c) To school  
8. A *receptionist* is ____. → a) a person at reception · b) a plane · c) a key  
9. *Can I have…?* is ____. → a) a polite request · b) a refusal · c) a past tense  
10. Room twelve is ____. → a) room number twelve · b) a restaurant · c) Wi-Fi only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) It is clean and quiet**  
7. **a) To reception**  
8. **a) a person at reception**  
9. **a) a polite request**  
10. **a) room number twelve**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text is about ____. → a) hotel check-in and a hotel stay · b) only food · c) only sports  
12. *Is there…?* is ____. → a) asking whether something exists · b) only past · c) a sport  
13. *Quiet* means ____. → a) not noisy · b) always loud · c) always dirty  
14. A *key* is ____. → a) something used to open your room · b) only a password forever · c) only a booking  
15. The main topic is ____. → a) staying at a hotel, checking in and getting a room · b) cooking · c) music

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) hotel check-in and a hotel stay**  
12. **a) asking whether something exists**  
13. **a) not noisy**  
14. **a) something used to open your room**  
15. **a) staying at a hotel, checking in and getting a room**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Reception

**Objetivo:** escuchar un check-in en recepción.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-46/listening-check-in.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Reception"></audio>

> **Guest:** Hello. I have a booking.  
> **Receptionist:** Welcome. Can I have your name?  
> **Guest:** John.  
> **Receptionist:** Here is your key for room eight.  
> **Guest:** Is there Wi-Fi in the hotel?  
> **Receptionist:** Yes, there is. The password is on the key card.  
> **Guest:** Can I have room service?  
> **Receptionist:** Yes. Call reception.  
> **Guest:** Thank you.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What does the guest say first? → a) I have a booking · b) I need a taxi · c) Goodbye  
2. What room is the key for? → a) Room eight · b) Room twelve · c) Room one  
3. Is there Wi-Fi? → a) Yes, there is · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
4. Where is the password? → a) On the key card · b) On the plane · c) At school  
5. What else does the guest ask for? → a) Room service · b) A bike · c) A ticket

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) I have a booking**  
2. **a) Room eight**  
3. **a) Yes, there is**  
4. **a) On the key card**  
5. **a) Room service**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. How do you get room service? → a) Call reception · b) Go to the beach · c) Fly  
7. A *booking* is a ____. → a) reservation · b) key only · c) password only  
8. The dialogue is at ____. → a) hotel reception · b) school · c) the beach  
9. *Can I have…?* is ____. → a) a polite request · b) a refusal · c) past simple  
10. A *key card* is ____. → a) a key card, sometimes with the password · b) only a plane · c) only food

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Call reception**  
7. **a) reservation**  
8. **a) hotel reception**  
9. **a) a polite request**  
10. **a) a key card, sometimes with the password**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. The guest ends with ____. → a) Thank you · b) Goodbye forever · c) Never  
12. *Welcome* is ____. → a) a greeting used when someone arrives · b) a refusal · c) a password  
13. For room service, ____. → a) phone reception · b) leave the hotel · c) buy a plane  
14. A *password* is ____. → a) a secret code used for Wi-Fi or another service · b) a room number only · c) a key forever  
15. The topic is ____. → a) hotel check-in and hotel requests · b) cooking · c) music

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) Thank you**  
12. **a) a greeting used when someone arrives**  
13. **a) phone reception**  
14. **a) a secret code used for Wi-Fi or another service**  
15. **a) hotel check-in and hotel requests**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: At the hotel

**Objetivo:** escribir peticiones y un mini diálogo de check-in.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. Can I ____ a key? → *have / has / having*  
2. ____ there Wi-Fi? → *Is / Are / Am*  
3. I would ____ room service. → *like / likes / liking*  
4. I go ____ reception to check in. → *to / for / at*  
5. Here is your ____. → *key / keysing / lobby*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **have**  
2. **Is**  
3. **like**  
4. **to**  
5. **key**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. Can / I / have / a / key / for / room / five?  
7. Is / there / a / double / room / available?  
8. I / go / to / reception / to / check / in.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. Can I have a key for room five?  
7. Is there a double room available?  
8. I go to reception to check in.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Pide una llave para una habitación.  
10. Pregunta si hay Wi-Fi.  
11. Dos frases: booking + tipo de habitación.  
12. Mini diálogo: room service.  
13. Párrafo corto (3–4 frases) de check-in.  
14. Tres peticiones/preguntas de hotel.  
15. Escribe lo que quieres: habitación + baño.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. Can I have a key for room ten?  
10. Is there Wi-Fi in the hotel?  
11. I have a booking. I would like a double room.  
12. Can I have room service? Yes, call reception.  
13. I go to reception to check in. Can I have a key for room eight? Is there a restaurant? Yes, there is.  
14. Can I have a key? Is there Wi-Fi? I would like room service.  
15. I would like a single room. Is there a bathroom in the room?

</details>
