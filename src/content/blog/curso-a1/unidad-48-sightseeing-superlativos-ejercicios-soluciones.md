## Lección 1 — Gramática: superlativos

**Objetivo:** *the most famous*, *the oldest*, *the biggest*, *the tallest*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. This is ____ famous monument in the city. → *the most / the more / most*  
2. It is ____ old church in town. → *the oldest / the older / oldest*  
3. That is ____ big museum here. → *the biggest / the bigger / biggest*  
4. We saw ____ tall statue in the square. → *the tallest / the taller / tallest*  
5. This is ____ beautiful building in the city. → *the most beautiful / the more beautiful / most beautiful*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **the most** — adjetivo largo: *the most + adj.*  
2. **the oldest** — adjetivo corto: *the + -est*.  
3. **the biggest** — *big → bigger → the biggest* (doble g).  
4. **the tallest**  
5. **the most beautiful**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Superlative = ____. → a) the most / the -est · b) more / -er · c) present only  
7. *Famous* → superlative = ____. → a) the most famous · b) the famousest · c) the more famous  
8. *Old* → superlative = ____. → a) the oldest · b) the most old · c) the older  
9. We use ____ before superlative adjectives. → a) the · b) a · c) no article  
10. *The most famous monument* means ____. → a) the monument that is most famous · b) a monument · c) the only monument always

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) the most / the -est**  
7. **a) the most famous**  
8. **a) the oldest**  
9. **a) the**  
10. **a) the monument that is most famous**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. This is / the / most / famous / monument / in / the city.  
12. It is / the / oldest / church / in / town.  
13. We / did / a / guided tour / of / the museum.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. This is the most famous monument in the city.  
12. It is the oldest church in town.  
13. We did a guided tour of the museum.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. Superlatives compare one thing with all others (*the most famous*, *the oldest*). → True / False  
15. Long adjectives use *the most*; short adjectives use *the -est*. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: turismo y monumentos

**Objetivo:** *museum, monument, statue, guided tour, sightseeing, square, guide*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *Museum* = ____. → a) a place with art, history or objects · b) a monument · c) a statue  
2. *Monument* = ____. → a) a building or structure to remember someone/something · b) a museum · c) a guided tour  
3. *Statue* = ____. → a) a figure of a person or animal in stone/metal · b) a monument only · c) a museum  
4. *Guided tour* = ____. → a) a visit with a guide who explains · b) a museum only · c) a statue  
5. *Sightseeing* = ____. → a) visiting famous places · b) staying at a hotel · c) shopping only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) a place with art, history or objects**  
2. **a) a building or structure to remember someone/something**  
3. **a) a figure of a person or animal in stone/metal**  
4. **a) a visit with a guide who explains**  
5. **a) visiting famous places**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Empareja

6. Empareja: museum / monument / statue / guided tour / square → art and history · remember someone/something · figure in stone · guide explains · open place in town  
7. Empareja: the most famous / the oldest / church / building / city → superlative · superlative of old · religious building · structure · town/city

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **museum → art and history** · **monument → remember someone/something** · **statue → figure in stone** · **guided tour → guide explains** · **square → open place in town**  
7. **the most famous → superlative** · **the oldest → superlative of old** · **church → religious building** · **building → structure** · **city → town/city**

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–13 — Completa / elige

8. We visited ____ museum yesterday. → *the / a / this*  
9. There is a ____ in the square. → *statue / museum / guided tour*  
10. We did a ____ of the old town. → *guided tour / monument / statue*  
11. *Square* = ____. → a) an open place in a town · b) a museum · c) a statue  
12. *Guide* = ____. → a) a person who shows and explains · b) a monument · c) a museum  
13. Where do you often see statues? → a) In squares, parks, near monuments · b) Only in museums · c) At home only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **the**  
9. **statue**  
10. **guided tour**  
11. **a) an open place in a town**  
12. **a) a person who shows and explains**  
13. **a) In squares, parks, near monuments**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. A guided tour has a guide who explains the place. → True / False  
15. A museum has collections; a monument is a structure. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Yesterday in the city

**Objetivo:** leer sobre un día de sightseeing.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-48/reading-sightseeing.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Yesterday in the city"></audio>

> Yesterday we did sightseeing in the city. We visited the most famous museum. It is the oldest building in town. In the square there is a tall statue. We did a guided tour of the monument. The guide explained the history. It was very interesting. We took photos.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What did they do yesterday? → a) Sightseeing in the city · b) Stay at a hotel · c) Go to the beach  
2. What museum did they visit? → a) The most famous · b) The smallest · c) The newest  
3. What is the building? → a) The oldest in town · b) The newest · c) The biggest  
4. Where is the statue? → a) In the square · b) In the museum · c) At home  
5. What did they do at the monument? → a) A guided tour · b) Shopping · c) Swimming

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Sightseeing in the city**  
2. **a) The most famous**  
3. **a) The oldest in town**  
4. **a) In the square**  
5. **a) A guided tour**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. What did the guide explain? → a) The history · b) The price · c) The food  
7. Was it interesting? → a) Yes, very interesting · b) No · c) The text does not say  
8. *Guided tour* means ____. → a) a visit with a guide who explains · b) a museum only · c) a statue  
9. *The most famous* is ____. → a) a superlative · b) a comparative · c) the present only  
10. *Statue* means ____. → a) a figure in stone or metal · b) a museum · c) a monument only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) The history**  
7. **a) Yes, very interesting**  
8. **a) a visit with a guide who explains**  
9. **a) a superlative**  
10. **a) a figure in stone or metal**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text is about ____. → a) sightseeing: museum, monument, guided tour · b) a hotel · c) the beach  
12. *The oldest* is ____. → a) the superlative of old · b) a comparative · c) the present  
13. *Square* means ____. → a) an open place in town · b) a museum · c) a monument  
14. *They took photos* means ____. → a) they made photographs · b) they bought photos · c) they lost photos  
15. The main topic is ____. → a) sightseeing with museum, statue and guided tour · b) shopping · c) hotel

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) sightseeing: museum, monument, guided tour**  
12. **a) the superlative of old**  
13. **a) an open place in town**  
14. **a) they made photographs**  
15. **a) sightseeing with museum, statue and guided tour**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Sightseeing today

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo sobre un día de turismo.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-48/listening-sightseeing.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Sightseeing today"></audio>

> **B:** We did sightseeing. We visited the most famous museum. It is the oldest building in the city.  
> **A:** Did you do a guided tour?  
> **B:** Yes. We saw a big statue in the square. The guide explained the monument.  
> **A:** Was it good?  
> **B:** Yes, it was very interesting.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What did B do today? → a) Sightseeing · b) Shopping · c) Stay at a hotel  
2. What museum did B visit? → a) The most famous · b) The smallest · c) The newest  
3. What is the building? → a) The oldest in the city · b) The biggest · c) The most beautiful  
4. Did B do a guided tour? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The dialogue does not say  
5. Where did they see the statue? → a) In the square · b) In the museum · c) At home

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Sightseeing**  
2. **a) The most famous**  
3. **a) The oldest in the city**  
4. **a) Yes**  
5. **a) In the square**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. What did the guide explain? → a) The monument · b) The price · c) The food  
7. Was it good? → a) Yes, very interesting · b) No · c) The dialogue does not say  
8. *The most famous* is ____. → a) a superlative · b) a comparative · c) the present  
9. *Guided tour* means ____. → a) a visit with a guide · b) a museum only · c) a statue  
10. *Statue* means ____. → a) a figure in stone or metal · b) a museum · c) a monument only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) The monument**  
7. **a) Yes, very interesting**  
8. **a) a superlative**  
9. **a) a visit with a guide**  
10. **a) a figure in stone or metal**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. The dialogue is about ____. → a) sightseeing, museum, guided tour · b) a hotel · c) shopping  
12. *The oldest* is ____. → a) the superlative of old · b) a comparative · c) the present  
13. *Square* means ____. → a) an open place in town · b) a museum · c) a monument  
14. *We saw* is ____. → a) the past of we see · b) the present · c) the future

## Lección 5 — Writing: My sightseeing day

**Objetivo:** escribir sobre sightseeing con superlativos.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. This is ____ most famous monument in the city. → *the / a / this*  
2. It is ____ oldest church in town. → *the / a / this*  
3. We ____ the museum yesterday. → *visited / visit / visiting*  
4. There is a ____ in the square. → *statue / museum / guided tour*  
5. We did ____ guided tour of the monument. → *a / the / an*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **the**  
2. **the**  
3. **visited**  
4. **statue**  
5. **a**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. This is / the / most famous / monument / in / the city.  
7. We / did / a / guided tour / of / the museum.  
8. It is / the / oldest / church / in / town.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. This is the most famous monument in the city.  
7. We did a guided tour of the museum.  
8. It is the oldest church in town.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Escribe el monumento o museo más famoso de tu ciudad (*the most famous*).  
10. Escribe: hiciste una visita guiada ayer (*did a guided tour*).  
11. Dos frases: visitaste el edificio más antiguo y viste una estatua en la plaza.  
12. Pregunta *Did you do sightseeing?* y respuesta breve.  
13. Párrafo corto (3–4 frases): turismo ayer — museo, monumento, visita guiada, estatua.  
14. 2–3 frases con *the most famous* y *the oldest*.  
15. Qué viste en la plaza y si hiciste visita guiada.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. This is the most famous monument in my city.  
10. We did a guided tour of the museum yesterday.  
11. I visited the oldest building in town. There was a statue in the square.  
12. Did you do sightseeing? Yes, we visited the museum.  
13. Yesterday we did sightseeing. We visited the most famous museum. We did a guided tour. There was a tall statue in the square.  
14. This is the most famous monument. It is the oldest church in town.  
15. There was a big statue in the square. We did a guided tour of the monument.

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| **the + -est** | the **oldest** church |
| **the most + adj.** | the **most famous** museum |
| **museum / monument** | We visited the museum. |
| **statue / square** | a statue in the square |
| **guided tour** | We did a guided tour. |
