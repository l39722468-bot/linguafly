Ya sabes describir tu rutina y decir la hora. En la **Unidad 11 del curso A1** (*Places in Town*) aprendes a hablar de **lugares en la ciudad** y a decir **dónde están**.

Esta guía cubre el **vocabulario de lugares**, ***There is / There are***, ***Where is…?***, ***Is there a…?*** y las preposiciones ***next to, opposite, between, near***. Incluye **audios**, **esquemas** y **ejercicios**.

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 11 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Nombrar **lugares** de la ciudad en inglés.
- Usar ***There is / There are*** para decir qué hay.
- Preguntar ***Where is…?*** e ***Is there a…?***.
- Ubicar sitios con ***next to, opposite, between, near***.

## 2. There is / There are

Para decir **qué hay** en un lugar:

| Forma | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **There is** | singular | There **is** a bank near here. |
| **There are** | plural | There **are** two parks in my town. |

## 4. Preposiciones de lugar

| Preposición | Español | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **next to** | al lado de | The bank is **next to** the library. |
| **opposite** | enfrente de | The park is **opposite** the library. |
| **between** | entre | The pharmacy is **between** the bank and the post office. |
| **near** | cerca de | There is a café **near** here. |
| **in** | en | in my street / in the centre |

```
The + lugar + is + preposición + the + lugar
```

## 5. Texto modelo (del curso)

### My town

> My town is small but there are many interesting places. **There is a supermarket** in the main street. **Next to** the supermarket is the library. The library is open from nine to five. **Opposite** the library there is a park. **There is a pharmacy between** the bank and the post office. I like my town.

### Tu plantilla en 6 frases

1. My town / street is \_\_\_\_ (small / big).
2. There is a \_\_\_\_ in \_\_\_\_.
3. There are \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_ in my town.
4. The \_\_\_\_ is next to the \_\_\_\_.
5. The \_\_\_\_ is opposite the \_\_\_\_.
6. The \_\_\_\_ is between the \_\_\_\_ and the \_\_\_\_.

## 6. Preguntas útiles

| Pregunta | Respuesta modelo |
| :--- | :--- |
| Is there a bank near here? | Yes, there is. / No, there isn't. |
| Where is the post office? | It is next to the library. |
| Where is the supermarket? | It is in the main street. |
| Are there any parks? | Yes, there are two parks. |
| Is the café opposite the bank? | Yes, it is. / No, it isn't. |

---

## 7. Errores típicos de hispanohablantes

| ❌ Incorrecto | ✅ Correcto | Explicación |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *There are a bank* | *There **is** a bank* | Singular → is |
| *There is two parks* | *There **are** two parks* | Plural → are |
| *Where is the shops?* | *Where **are** the shops?* | Plural → are |
| *Is there pharmacy?* | *Is there **a** pharmacy?* | Falta *a/an* |
| *next the bank* | ***next to** the bank* | Completa: next **to** |
| *between the bank the post office* | *between the bank **and** the post office* | between A **and** B |

## 8. Ejercicios prácticos (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Completa con *is* o *are*

1. There \_\_\_\_ a bank near here.  
2. There \_\_\_\_ two parks in my town.  
3. Where \_\_\_\_ the supermarket?  
4. Where \_\_\_\_ the shops?  
5. Is there \_\_\_\_ pharmacy near here? (a / an)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is**  
2. **are**  
3. **is**  
4. **are**  
5. **a**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Elige la preposición correcta

1. The bank is (next to / between) the library.  
2. The park is (opposite / between) the library.  
3. The pharmacy is (next to / between) the bank and the post office.  
4. There is a café (near / between) here.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **next to**  
2. **opposite**  
3. **between**  
4. **near**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Traduce al inglés

1. Hay un banco cerca de aquí.  
2. ¿Dónde está correos?  
3. ¿Hay una farmacia cerca?  
4. El parque está enfrente de la biblioteca.  
5. Hay dos supermercados en mi calle.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. There is a bank near here.  
2. Where is the post office?  
3. Is there a pharmacy near here?  
4. The park is opposite the library.  
5. There are two supermarkets in my street.
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrige el error

1. *There are a supermarket in my street.*  
2. *Where is the shops?*  
3. *Is there pharmacy near here?*  
4. *The bank is next the library.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. There **is** a supermarket in my street.  
2. Where **are** the shops?  
3. Is there **a** pharmacy near here?  
4. The bank is next **to** the library.
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Ordena las palabras

1. is / There / a / bank / here / near  
2. the / Where / post office / is  
3. next to / is / The / library / supermarket / the

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. There is a bank near here.  
2. Where is the post office?  
3. The library is next to the supermarket. / The supermarket is next to the library.
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Describe tu barrio

Escribe 5–6 frases sobre tu calle o pueblo. Usa *There is/are*, al menos **dos** lugares y **dos** preposiciones (*next to, opposite, between, near*).

**Modelo:**

> My street is small. There is a supermarket and a café. The café is next to the bank. There is a park opposite the library. The pharmacy is between the bank and the post office. I like my town.

## 9. Diálogo modelo

> **Tourist:** Excuse me. Is there a pharmacy near here?  
> **Local:** Yes, there is. It's between the bank and the post office.  
> **Tourist:** And where is the library?  
> **Local:** The library is next to the supermarket, in the main street.  
> **Tourist:** Is there a park opposite the library?  
> **Local:** Yes! There is a small park opposite the library.  
> **Tourist:** Perfect. Thank you!  
> **Local:** You're welcome.

---

## 10. Consejos para practicar

1. **Dibuja un mapa simple** de tu barrio y descríbelo en inglés.
2. **Practica *There is/are*** mirando por la ventana.
3. **Pregunta en voz alta**: *Where is the…? Is there a…?*
4. **Escucha los audios** y repite con la misma entonación.
5. Haz los ejercicios de la [Unidad 11 del curso A1](/curso-a1/unit-11).
