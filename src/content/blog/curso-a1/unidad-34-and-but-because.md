## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 34 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Usar ***and*** para **sumar** ideas.
- Usar ***but*** para **contrastar**.
- Usar ***because*** para dar una **razón**.
- Unir dos oraciones cortas en una más larga.
- Hablar de gustos y hábitos con más detalle (Leo: sport, music, pizza…).

---

## 1. Tres conectores, tres trabajos

| Conector | Trabajo | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **and** | suma / añade | I like coffee **and** tea. |
| **but** | contraste | I like tea **but** I don't like coffee. |
| **because** | razón / causa | I stay at home **because** I am tired. |

![Esquema and but because A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-34/a1-unit34-and-but-because.png)

```
and      → + (same direction)
but      → ≠ (contrast)
because  → why (reason)
```

### Tip

Pregúntate: ¿estoy **añadiendo**, **oponiéndome** o **explicando por qué**? Esa pregunta elige el conector.

## 2. *And* — sumar ideas

Une dos cosas del mismo “sentido”:

> I like coffee **and** tea.  
> I like pizza **and** pasta.  
> I like dogs **and** cats.  
> I like sport **and** music.  
> At weekends I meet friends **and** we go to the cinema.

### Patrones útiles

| Patrón | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| noun **and** noun | pizza and pasta |
| verb phrase **and** verb phrase | I meet friends and we play video games |
| adjective **and** adjective | easy and tasty |

No hace falta repetir el sujeto si es el mismo y el verbo es paralelo: *I like tea and coffee.*

## 3. *But* — contraste

Une dos ideas que **no van en la misma dirección**:

> I play football **but** I don't like watching it on TV.  
> I like tea **but** I don't like coffee.  
> I like swimming **but** I don't like running.  
> She is tall **but** her brother is short.  
> He is fast **but** he doesn't like running.  
> I don't like fish **but** I eat it sometimes…

### Tip

Tras *but* suele aparecer un **cambio**: positivo → negativo, o al revés.

> I like it **but** it's expensive.  
> It's difficult **but** I like it.

## 4. *Because* — dar una razón

Responde a ***Why…?***

> I stay at home **because** I am tired.  
> We eat pasta **because** we like it.  
> I like pizza **because** it is easy to make and tasty.  
> I'm tired **because** I didn't sleep well.  
> We are happy **because** we passed the exam.  
> I like it **because** it's cheap.  
> She is sad **because** she lost her phone.  
> I eat fish sometimes **because** it's healthy.

### Regla A1

***Because* + sujeto + verbo** (oración completa):

| ❌ | ✅ |
| :--- | :--- |
| because tired | because **I am** tired |
| because healthy | because **it is** healthy |
| because like it | because **I like** it |

Ya viste *because* con gustos en la [Unidad 18](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-18-gustos-like-ing-because). Aquí lo generalizas a cualquier razón.

## 5. De dos frases cortas a una larga

![Diagrama de conectores A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-34/a1-unit34-connectors-diagram.png)

| Frases cortas | Conector | Frase unida |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I like coffee. I like tea. | **and** | I like coffee **and** tea. |
| I like tea. I don't like coffee. | **but** | I like tea **but** I don't like coffee. |
| I stay at home. I am tired. | **because** | I stay at home **because** I am tired. |

### Mini-práctica mental

1. ¿Misma dirección? → **and**  
2. ¿Contraste? → **but**  
3. ¿Por qué? → **because**

### Comprueba

1. ¿Qué le gusta? → **Sport and music**.  
2. ¿Le gusta ver fútbol en la tele? → **No**.  
3. ¿Por qué le gusta la pizza? → **Easy to make and tasty**.  
4. ¿Come pescado? → **Sometimes, because it's healthy**.  
5. ¿Qué hace el fin de semana? → **Meet friends; cinema or video games**.

Cuenta los conectores del texto: verás **and**, **but** y **because** trabajando juntos.

## 8. Diálogo modelo

> **A:** Do you like football?  
> **B:** Yes, I play football **but** I don't like watching it on TV.  
> **A:** Why?  
> **B:** **Because** I prefer to play outside.  
> **A:** And food? Do you like pizza?  
> **B:** Yes, **because** it's easy to make **and** tasty.  
> **A:** Do you like fish?  
> **B:** No, **but** I eat it sometimes **because** it's healthy.  
> **A:** Nice. At weekends I meet friends **and** we go to the cinema.

## 9. Errores típicos de hispanohablantes

| ❌ Incorrecto | ✅ Correcto | Explicación |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *I like tea and I don't like coffee* (sin contraste claro) | *I like tea **but** I don't like coffee* | Contraste → **but** |
| *I stay home because tired* | *…because **I am** tired* | *because* + oración |
| *Because I am tired. I stay home.* (dos frases sueltas A1) | *I stay home **because** I am tired.* | Une en una frase |
| *I like pizza but is tasty* | *I like pizza **because** it is tasty* | Razón ≠ contraste |
| *I like coffee but tea* | *I like coffee **and** tea* | Suma → **and** |

### *Why…? / Because…*

> **Why** do you stay at home?  
> **Because** I am tired. / I stay at home **because** I am tired.

En writing A1, la segunda opción (frase completa) es más clara.

---

## 9b. Pronunciación útil

| Forma | Tip A1 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **and** | a menudo /ən/ débil entre palabras: *tea and coffee*. |
| **but** | /bʌt/; no alargues la *u*. |
| **because** | *bi-COZ* (acento en la 2.ª). |
| **tasty** | *TEIS-ti*. |
| **healthy** | *HEL-zi* (th sonora). |

Practica el trío: *and → but → because* en tres frases seguidas.

### Rutina de 2 minutos

1. Di 2 frases con *and*.  
2. Di 2 frases con *but*.  
3. Di 2 frases con *because*.  
4. Une las tres en un mini-texto (como Leo).

Si lo combinas con U33: *I often play football but I never watch it on TV because I prefer to play outside.*

### Combinar U18 + U32 + U33 + U34

| Unidad | Aporta | Ejemplo unido |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| U18 | like + -ing | I like swimming |
| U32 | play/go/do | I play football |
| U33 | frequency | I often play football |
| **U34** | connectors | …**but** I don't watch it **because** I prefer to play outside |

### Mini-historia para reescribir

> I like sport and music. I play football but I don't watch it on TV. I like pizza because it's tasty. I don't like fish but I eat it sometimes because it's healthy. At weekends I meet friends and we go to the cinema.

Cambia *football → tennis*, *pizza → pasta*, *cinema → gym*, y mantén los conectores.
