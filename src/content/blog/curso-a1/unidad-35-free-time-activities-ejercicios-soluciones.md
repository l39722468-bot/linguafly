## Lección 1 — Gramática: go / like + -ing

**Objetivo:** *go for a walk*, *go shopping*, *goes* y *like/enjoy + -ing*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ for a walk every evening. → *go / goes / going*  
2. She ____ shopping on Saturdays. → *goes / go / going*  
3. I like ____ TV. → *watching / watch / watches*  
4. He enjoys ____ video games. → *playing / play / plays*  
5. We like ____ in the park. → *walking / walk / walks*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **go** — *I go for a walk…*  
2. **goes** — tercera persona.  
3. **watching** — *like + -ing*.  
4. **playing**  
5. **walking**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. We say ____ for a walk. → a) go · b) play · c) do  
7. After *like* / *enjoy* we use ____. → a) verb + -ing · b) to + verb always · c) bare infinitive only  
8. We say ____ shopping. → a) go · b) play · c) make  
9. She likes ____ films. → a) watching · b) watch · c) watches  
10. He ____ for a walk every Sunday. → a) goes · b) go · c) going

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) go**  
7. **a) verb + -ing**  
8. **a) go**  
9. **a) watching**  
10. **a) goes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. I / go / for a walk / every evening.  
12. She / likes / watching / TV.  
13. We / go / shopping / at weekends.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I go for a walk every evening.  
12. She likes watching TV.  
13. We go shopping at weekends.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We say *go for a walk* and *go shopping* (not *play shopping*). → True / False  
15. After *like* we often use *-ing*: *I like watching TV*. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: tiempo libre

**Objetivo:** *go for a walk*, *go shopping*, *watching TV*, *video games*, *social media*, *free time*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *Go for a walk* = ____. → a) walk for pleasure · b) buy things · c) watch TV  
2. *Go shopping* = ____. → a) buy things in shops · b) walk for fun · c) play games  
3. *Watching TV* = ____. → a) look at television · b) buy clothes · c) walk outside  
4. *Video games* = ____. → a) games on a computer or console · b) films · c) books  
5. *Walking* = ____. → a) move on foot · b) drive a car · c) sleep

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) walk for pleasure**  
2. **a) buy things in shops**  
3. **a) look at television**  
4. **a) games on a computer or console**  
5. **a) move on foot**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Empareja

6. Empareja actividad → idea: go for a walk / go shopping / watching TV / video games / walking → outside on foot · buy things · at home · play on a screen · -ing after like  
7. Empareja: go for a walk / go shopping / watching TV / video games / social media → take a walk · shop · view television · electronic games · online social networks

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **go for a walk → outside on foot** · **go shopping → buy things** · **watching TV → at home** · **video games → play on a screen** · **walking → -ing after like**  
7. **go for a walk → take a walk** · **go shopping → shop** · **watching TV → view television** · **video games → electronic games** · **social media → online social networks**

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. I ____ for a walk every Sunday. → *go / goes / going*  
9. She likes ____ TV at night. → *watching / watch / watches*  
10. We ____ shopping at weekends. → *go / goes / going*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **go**  
9. **watching**  
10. **go**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Definiciones

11. *Social media* means ____. → a) Facebook, Instagram, etc. · b) only TV · c) only books  
12. *Free time* means ____. → a) time when you do not work · b) time at school · c) time sleeping  
13. He likes ____ video games. → a) playing · b) play · c) plays

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) Facebook, Instagram, etc.**  
12. **a) time when you do not work**  
13. **a) playing**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena

14. I / like / watching / TV / and / playing / video games.  
15. We / go / for a walk / every Sunday.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. I like watching TV and playing video games.  
15. We go for a walk every Sunday.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Mia

**Objetivo:** leer sobre el tiempo libre de Mia.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-35/reading-mia.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Mia"></audio>

> My name is Mia. In my free time I like going for a walk in the park and watching TV. I often watch films at home. I don't like going shopping because it is too busy. My brother likes playing video games and walking the dog. At weekends we sometimes go for a walk together in the morning.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the writer's name? → a) Mia · b) Maya · c) Emma  
2. What does Mia like in her free time? → a) Going for a walk and watching TV · b) Only shopping · c) Only video games  
3. Does she like going shopping? → a) No · b) Yes · c) Sometimes  
4. Why doesn't she like shopping? → a) Because it is too busy · b) Because it is expensive · c) The text doesn't say  
5. What does her brother like? → a) Playing video games and walking the dog · b) Only shopping · c) Only watching TV

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Mia**  
2. **a) Going for a walk and watching TV**  
3. **a) No**  
4. **a) Because it is too busy**  
5. **a) Playing video games and walking the dog**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. When do they go for a walk together? → a) Sometimes at weekends in the morning · b) Every evening · c) Never  
7. Where does Mia like walking? → a) In the park · b) At the beach · c) In the city centre  
8. What does she often watch at home? → a) Films · b) Sport · c) News  
9. The text is about ____. → a) free-time activities · b) work · c) school  
10. Which phrase means "tiempo libre"? → a) Free time · b) Busy time · c) Work time

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Sometimes at weekends in the morning**  
7. **a) In the park**  
8. **a) Films**  
9. **a) free-time activities**  
10. **a) Free time**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. Mia often ____ films at home. → a) watches · b) watch · c) watching  
12. Her brother likes ____ the dog. → a) walking · b) walk · c) walks  
13. Mia likes ____ TV. → a) watching · b) watch · c) watches  
14. At weekends they sometimes ____ for a walk. → a) go · b) goes · c) going  
15. Which activity does Mia like? → a) Go for a walk · b) Go shopping · c) Drive

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) watches**  
12. **a) walking**  
13. **a) watching**  
14. **a) go**  
15. **a) Go for a walk**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Free time

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo sobre el tiempo libre.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-35/listening-free-time.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Free time"></audio>

> **A:** What do you do in your free time?  
> **B:** I like going for a walk and watching TV. I often watch films.  
> **A:** Do you go shopping often?  
> **B:** Not really. I don't like shopping because it's too busy. I prefer playing video games at home.  
> **A:** What about weekends?  
> **B:** I sometimes go for a walk with my family in the morning.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What does B do in free time? → a) Going for a walk and watching TV · b) Only shopping · c) Only work  
2. Does B watch films often? → a) Yes, often · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
3. Does B go shopping often? → a) No · b) Yes · c) Every day  
4. Why doesn't B like shopping? → a) Because it is too busy · b) Because it is cheap · c) The audio doesn't say  
5. What does B prefer at home? → a) Playing video games · b) Cooking · c) Sleeping

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Going for a walk and watching TV**  
2. **a) Yes, often**  
3. **a) No**  
4. **a) Because it is too busy**  
5. **a) Playing video games**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. When does B go for a walk with family? → a) Sometimes at weekends in the morning · b) Every night · c) Never  
7. The dialogue is ____. → a) about free-time activities · b) about food · c) about school  
8. B often ____ films. → a) watches · b) watch · c) watching  
9. When A asks about shopping, B says ____. → a) Not really · b) Always · c) Yes, every day  
10. A ____ about free time, shopping and weekends. → a) asks · b) ask · c) asking

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Sometimes at weekends in the morning**  
7. **a) about free-time activities**  
8. **a) watches**  
9. **a) Not really**  
10. **a) asks**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. B prefers ____ video games at home. → a) playing · b) play · c) plays  
12. B sometimes ____ for a walk with the family. → a) goes · b) go · c) going  
13. B likes ____ TV. → a) watching · b) watch · c) watches  
14. We say ____ for a walk. → a) go · b) play · c) do  
15. At weekends B sometimes goes for a walk ____. → a) with the family in the morning · b) alone at night · c) at work

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) playing**  
12. **a) goes**  
13. **a) watching**  
14. **a) go**  
15. **a) with the family in the morning**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My free time

**Objetivo:** escribir sobre tu tiempo libre con *go* y *like + -ing*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ for a walk every evening. → *go / goes / going*  
2. She ____ shopping on Saturdays. → *goes / go / going*  
3. I like ____ TV. → *watching / watch / watches*  
4. We enjoy ____ in the park. → *walking / walk / walks*  
5. He likes ____ video games. → *playing / play / plays*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **go**  
2. **goes**  
3. **watching**  
4. **walking**  
5. **playing**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. I / go / for a walk / every evening.  
7. She / likes / watching / TV / and / playing / video games.  
8. We / go / shopping / at weekends.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I go for a walk every evening.  
7. She likes watching TV and playing video games.  
8. We go shopping at weekends.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Escribe qué te gusta hacer en tu free time (usa *like + -ing*).  
10. Escribe una frase con *go for a walk* + frecuencia.  
11. Escribe: algo que te gusta + algo que no te gusta.  
12. Pregunta y respuesta: *What do you do in your free time?*  
13. Escribe un párrafo corto (4–5 frases) sobre tu free time.  
14. Tres frases: *watching TV*, *walking*, *go shopping*.  
15. Escribe sobre otra persona: *My sister/brother likes…*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I like going for a walk and watching TV.  
10. I go for a walk every Sunday.  
11. I like watching TV. I don't like going shopping.  
12. What do you do in your free time? I like walking and watching TV.  
13. In my free time I like going for a walk. I often watch TV. I like playing video games too. I don't like going shopping.  
14. I like watching TV. I enjoy walking. I go shopping on Saturdays.  
15. My sister likes going shopping and watching TV.

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Patrón | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| **go for a walk** | I go for a walk every evening. |
| **go shopping** | We go shopping at weekends. |
| **goes** (he/she) | She goes shopping on Saturdays. |
| **like + -ing** | I like watching TV. |
| **don't like + -ing** | I don't like going shopping. |
