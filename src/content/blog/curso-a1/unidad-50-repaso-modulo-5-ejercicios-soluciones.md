## Lección 1 — Gramática: repaso

**Objetivo:** *take the / go by*, *It is*, *was/were*, *the most*, *need to*.

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Completa

1. I ____ train to London. → *take the / by the / take*  
2. We ____ bus to the airport. → *go by / go by the / take*  
3. It ____ sunny today. → *is / are / was*  
4. I ____ on the beach last week. → *was / were / am*  
5. This is ____ most famous monument in the city. → *the / a / this*  
6. I ____ pack my suitcase tonight. → *need to / need / needs to*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **take the** — *take the* + train/bus.  
2. **go by** — *go by* + bus/car/plane (sin *the*).  
3. **is**  
4. **was**  
5. **the**  
6. **need to**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–10 — Elige

7. Correct: ____. → a) I go by bus · b) I go by the bus · c) I take bus  
8. Weather: ____ sunny. → a) It is · b) There is · c) Is it (statement)  
9. Past of *is/am* = ____. → a) was · b) were · c) is  
10. Superlative: ____ famous. → a) the most · b) more · c) most (alone)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

7. **a) I go by bus**  
8. **a) It is**  
9. **a) was**  
10. **a) the most**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. I / take the / train / to / work.  
12. It is / hot / in / summer.  
13. We / were / in / the mountains / last year.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I take the train to work.  
12. It is hot in summer.  
13. We were in the mountains last year.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We say *take the train / take the bus*; we say *go by car / go by plane*. → True / False  
15. *Need to* and *don't need to* are followed by a base verb. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: repaso

**Objetivo:** aeropuerto, hotel, vacaciones, sightseeing, packing.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *Check-in* = ____. → a) where you get a boarding pass · b) the plane · c) luggage only  
2. *Reception* = ____. → a) the desk where you check in at a hotel · b) your room · c) a key  
3. *Souvenir* = ____. → a) a thing you buy to remember a place · b) a beach · c) a ticket  
4. *Guided tour* = ____. → a) a visit with a guide who explains · b) a museum only · c) a statue  
5. *Toiletries* = ____. → a) toothbrush, soap, shampoo, etc. · b) clothes · c) a suitcase

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) where you get a boarding pass**  
2. **a) the desk where you check in at a hotel**  
3. **a) a thing you buy to remember a place**  
4. **a) a visit with a guide who explains**  
5. **a) toothbrush, soap, shampoo, etc.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Empareja

6. Empareja: sunny / single room / sightseeing / pack / boarding pass → weather · one person · visit places · put in suitcase · get at check-in  
7. Empareja: take the train / was/were / the most famous / need to / Can I have…? → transport · past of be · superlative · necessity · polite request

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **sunny → weather** · **single room → one person** · **sightseeing → visit places** · **pack → put in suitcase** · **boarding pass → get at check-in**  
7. **take the train → transport** · **was/were → past of be** · **the most famous → superlative** · **need to → necessity** · **Can I have…? → polite request**

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–13 — Completa / elige

8. I ____ the bus to the airport. → *take / go by / by*  
9. It is ____ today – take an umbrella. → *rainy / sunny / hot*  
10. We ____ the museum yesterday. → *visited / visit / visiting*  
11. *Essential items* = ____. → a) things you need · b) optional things · c) heavy things only  
12. *Gate* = ____. → a) where you board the plane · b) check-in · c) passport  
13. *Room service* = ____. → a) food or help to your room · b) reception · c) a key

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **take**  
9. **rainy**  
10. **visited**  
11. **a) things you need**  
12. **a) where you board the plane**  
13. **a) food or help to your room**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. With seasons we use *in*: *in summer*, *in winter*. → True / False  
15. Module 5 covers travel: airport, hotel, holidays, sightseeing, packing. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Last month

**Objetivo:** leer un viaje que mezcla temas del Módulo 5.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-50/reading-review.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Last month"></audio>

> Last month I went on holiday. I took the train to the airport. The weather was sunny. I was at the hotel for one week. I did sightseeing – I visited the most famous museum. I needed to pack my suitcase with clothes and toiletries. I didn't need to take so many things. It was great.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. When did he/she go on holiday? → a) Last month · b) Last week · c) Yesterday  
2. How did he/she go to the airport? → a) By train (*took the train*) · b) By bus · c) By car  
3. How was the weather? → a) Sunny · b) Rainy · c) Cold  
4. Where was he/she for one week? → a) At the hotel · b) At the airport · c) At home  
5. What museum did he/she visit? → a) The most famous · b) The smallest · c) The newest

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Last month**  
2. **a) By train (*took the train*)**  
3. **a) Sunny**  
4. **a) At the hotel**  
5. **a) The most famous**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. What did he/she need to pack? → a) Clothes and toiletries · b) Only clothes · c) Only toiletries  
7. Did he/she need to take so many things? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The text does not say  
8. *Took the train* means ____. → a) went by train · b) went by bus · c) went by car  
9. *The most famous* is ____. → a) a superlative · b) a comparative · c) the present only  
10. *Didn't need to* means ____. → a) no necessity in the past · b) obligation · c) future only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Clothes and toiletries**  
7. **a) No**  
8. **a) went by train**  
9. **a) a superlative**  
10. **a) no necessity in the past**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text is about ____. → a) a trip: transport, hotel, sightseeing, packing · b) only the airport · c) only a hotel  
12. *Was* is ____. → a) the past of is/am · b) the present · c) the future  
13. *Sightseeing* means ____. → a) visiting places as a tourist · b) staying at a hotel · c) packing  
14. *Toiletries* means ____. → a) toothbrush, soap, shampoo, etc. · b) clothes · c) a suitcase  
15. The main topic is ____. → a) a complete trip: transport, weather, hotel, sightseeing, packing · b) only packing · c) only the airport

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) a trip: transport, hotel, sightseeing, packing**  
12. **a) the past of is/am**  
13. **a) visiting places as a tourist**  
14. **a) toothbrush, soap, shampoo, etc.**  
15. **a) a complete trip: transport, weather, hotel, sightseeing, packing**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Review trip

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo de repaso del Módulo 5.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-50/listening-review.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Review trip"></audio>

> **A:** How did you go to the airport?  
> **B:** I took the bus. The weather was rainy yesterday.  
> **A:** Were you at the hotel last week?  
> **B:** Yes. I was there. I visited the most famous museum. I need to pack my suitcase tonight.  
> **A:** Don't forget your passport.  
> **B:** I won't.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. How did B go to the airport? → a) By bus (*took the bus*) · b) By train · c) By car  
2. How was the weather yesterday? → a) Rainy · b) Sunny · c) Cold  
3. Was B at the hotel last week? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The dialogue does not say  
4. What did B visit? → a) The most famous museum · b) The beach · c) The mountains  
5. When does B need to pack? → a) Tonight · b) Tomorrow · c) Last week

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) By bus (*took the bus*)**  
2. **a) Rainy**  
3. **a) Yes**  
4. **a) The most famous museum**  
5. **a) Tonight**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. What does A say not to forget? → a) Passport · b) Suitcase · c) Ticket  
7. *Took the bus* means ____. → a) went by bus · b) went by train · c) went by car  
8. *Was* is ____. → a) the past of is/am · b) the present · c) the future  
9. *The most famous* is ____. → a) a superlative · b) a comparative · c) the present  
10. *Need to pack* means ____. → a) necessity to put things in a suitcase · b) past only · c) advice only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Passport**  
7. **a) went by bus**  
8. **a) the past of is/am**  
9. **a) a superlative**  
10. **a) necessity to put things in a suitcase**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. The dialogue covers ____. → a) transport, weather, hotel, sightseeing, packing · b) only the airport · c) only a hotel  
12. *I won't* means ____. → a) I will not forget · b) I forgot · c) I forget  
13. *Were you…?* is ____. → a) a past question with *you* · b) the present · c) the future  
14. *The weather was rainy* means ____. → a) It was rainy · b) It is rainy · c) It will be rainy  
15. Module 5 review is ____. → a) travel: airport, transport, weather, hotel, holidays, sightseeing, packing · b) only grammar · c) only vocabulary

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) transport, weather, hotel, sightseeing, packing**  
12. **a) I will not forget**  
13. **a) a past question with *you***  
14. **a) It was rainy**  
15. **a) travel: airport, transport, weather, hotel, holidays, sightseeing, packing**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My trip

**Objetivo:** escribir un viaje con estructuras del Módulo 5.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ the bus to work. → *take / go by / by*  
2. It ____ rainy today. → *is / are / was*  
3. They ____ in the mountains last year. → *were / was / are*  
4. This is ____ oldest church in town. → *the / a / this*  
5. You ____ take so many shirts. → *don't need to / need to / needs to*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **take**  
2. **is**  
3. **were**  
4. **the**  
5. **don't need to**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. I / go by / bus / to / the airport.  
7. It was / sunny / last week.  
8. I / need to / take / clothes / and / toiletries.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I go by bus to the airport.  
7. It was sunny last week.  
8. I need to take clothes and toiletries.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Cómo vas al aeropuerto (*take the / go by*).  
10. El tiempo ayer (*It was…*).  
11. Dos frases: dónde estuviste (*was/were*) y qué visitaste.  
12. Petición en el hotel + qué necesitas meter en la maleta.  
13. Párrafo corto (3–4 frases): transporte, tiempo, hotel/turismo, equipaje.  
14. 2–3 frases: *was/were*, *the most famous*, *need to / don't need to*.  
15. Qué debes hacer en el aeropuerto (*must*) + pregunta para el hotel (*Is there…?*).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I take the train to the airport.  
10. It was sunny yesterday.  
11. I was on the beach last week. I visited the old town.  
12. Can I have a key? I need to take toiletries.  
13. I took the train to the airport. The weather was sunny. I was at the hotel for one week. I need to pack my suitcase.  
14. We were in the most famous museum. I don't need to take so many things.  
15. You must show your passport at check-in. Is there Wi-Fi in the hotel?

</details>
