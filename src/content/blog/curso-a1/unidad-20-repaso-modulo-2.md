La **Unidad 20 del curso A1** (*Module 2 Review*) cierra el Módulo 2: **vida diaria y rutinas**. No introduce gramática nueva: **consolida** lo de las unidades 11–19 para que puedas describir un día, pedir en un café, hablar de gustos y situar fechas sin dudar.

Esta guía es un **mapa de repaso** con tablas, checklist, audios y ejercicios. Úsala para detectar huecos y volver a la unidad concreta si algo falla.

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 20 — Module 2 Review](/curso-a1/unit-20)  
> **Última unidad del módulo:** [Unidad 19 — Meses y fechas](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-19-meses-fechas-cumpleanos)

---

## Qué deberías poder hacer al terminar el Módulo 2

- Describir **lugares** (*There is/are*) y **días** (*on Monday*).  
- Contar una **rutina** (*First / Then / After that*) con hora (*at seven*).  
- Usar **Present Simple** completo: afirmativa, *don't/doesn't*, *Do/Does…?*.  
- Pedir en un café: *Can I have…? / I'd like…*.  
- Hablar de **comidas** y gustos: *have breakfast*, *like + -ing*, *Why? Because…*.  
- Decir **meses, fechas y cumpleaños** (*in May / on the 10th of May*).

## 1. Present Simple: el núcleo del módulo

| | I / you / we / they | he / she / it |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Afirmativa | I **get up** at seven. | She **gets up** at seven. |
| Negativa | I **don't** like cooking. | He **doesn't** like reading. |
| Pregunta | **Do** you like music? | **Does** she like music? |
| Sí / No | Yes, I **do**. / No, I **don't**. | Yes, she **does**. / No, she **doesn't**. |

### Checklist Present Simple

- [ ] Tras *don't/doesn't* y *Do/Does* → verbo **sin -s**  
- [ ] No mezclo *don't* con *he/she/it*  
- [ ] Respuestas cortas con *do/does/don't/doesn't*

## 2. Tiempo: *in / on / at*

| Preposición | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **at** | hora | at seven · at half past eight |
| **on** | día / fecha | on Monday · on the 10th of May |
| **in** | mes / año / parte del día* | in May · in December · in the morning |

\* *in the morning / afternoon / evening* (pero *at night*).

## 5. Comidas y gustos

### Comidas del día

> I **have breakfast** at seven.  
> She **has lunch** at one.  
> We **have dinner** at half past eight.  
> I like cheese. She doesn't like meat. (*U17*)

### *Like + -ing* + *Why? Because…*

> I **like swimming because** it's fun.  
> I **like reading**. Why? **Because** it's relaxing.  
> They **don't like cooking**.  
> What kind of music do you like? — I like pop and rock.

## 6. Texto modelo de repaso (todo junto)

> I get up at seven o'clock. First I have breakfast — toast and milk. Then I go to work. On Saturday I don't work. I like reading because it's relaxing. I don't like running.  
> At the cafe I say: Can I have a coffee, please? I'd like a sandwich too.  
> My birthday is on the 10th of May. Christmas is in December.  
> There is a park near my house. What do you do on Monday?

Léelo en voz alta. Luego tapa el texto y reconstruye 6–8 frases con tu vida real.

### Cómo usar este repaso (método corto)

1. Haz el checklist del Present Simple.  
2. Completa las plantillas con datos tuyos.  
3. Escucha 8–10 audios y repite.  
4. Escribe tu “página de módulo” (ejercicio 6).  
5. Marca en la autoevaluación qué bloque falló y vuelve solo a esa guía.

No hace falta releer las nueve unidades enteras si solo fallas en *in/on* o en *doesn't*: el mapa del principio te lleva al artículo exacto.

## 7. Errores típicos del Módulo 2 (repaso)

| ❌ | ✅ |
| :--- | :--- |
| *He don't like reading* | He **doesn't** like reading |
| *Do she like music?* | **Does** she like music? |
| *I like swim* | I like **swimming** |
| *My birthday is in the 10th of May* | … **on** the 10th of May |
| *on January* | **in** January |
| *at Monday* | **on** Monday |
| *Give me a coffee* | **Can I have** a coffee, please? |
| *I eat breakfast* (único foco) | I **have** breakfast |

## 8. Diálogo modelo de cierre

> **Ana:** What do you do on Monday?  
> **Luis:** I get up at seven. I have breakfast at half past seven. Then I go to work.  
> **Ana:** Do you like your job?  
> **Luis:** Yes, I do. I like working with people because it's fun.  
> **Ana:** Nice! Would you like a coffee?  
> **Luis:** Yes — can I have a coffee and a sandwich, please?  
> **Ana:** When is your birthday?  
> **Luis:** On the 10th of May. And yours?  
> **Ana:** In August — on the 3rd of August.  
> **Luis:** Cool. On Saturday I don't work. I like reading and watching films.

## 9. Mini-diálogos por bloque

### Rutina

> **A:** What time do you get up?  
> **B:** At seven. First I have breakfast. After that I go to work.

### Gustos

> **A:** Do you like swimming?  
> **B:** Yes, I do. Why? Because it's fun.  
> **A:** Does she like cooking?  
> **B:** No, she doesn't.

### Café

> **A:** Can I have a tea, please?  
> **B:** Would you like something to eat?  
> **A:** I'd like a sandwich.

### Fechas

> **A:** When is your birthday?  
> **B:** It's on the 15th of March. Christmas is in December.

Practica un bloque al día durante cuatro días: rutina → gustos → café → fechas.

### Conexión U10–U19 (tiempo y hábitos)

Si ya viste horas en unidades anteriores, este módulo las une con días, meses y hábitos:

> at seven (hora) · on Monday (día) · in May (mes) · on the 10th of May (fecha)  
> I have breakfast at seven on Monday.  
> My birthday is on the 10th of May. In August we have holiday.

Esa “escalera” de preposiciones es una de las piezas más útiles para salir del Módulo 2 con seguridad.

2. He \_\_\_\_ like reading.  
3. \_\_\_\_ you like music?  
4. \_\_\_\_ she like music?

### Ejercicio 2 — *in / on / at*

1. I get up \_\_\_\_ seven.  
2. I don't work \_\_\_\_ Saturday.  
3. My birthday is \_\_\_\_ May.  
4. My birthday is \_\_\_\_ the 10th of May.  
5. Christmas is \_\_\_\_ December.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

2. **on**  
3. **in**  
4. **on**  
5. **in**

### Ejercicio 3 — Completa

1. First I wake up. \_\_\_\_ I have breakfast. \_\_\_\_ that I go to work.  
2. Can I \_\_\_\_ a coffee, please?  
3. I \_\_\_\_ like swimming because it's fun.  
4. She \_\_\_\_ lunch at one.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Then** / **After**  
2. **have**  
3. **like** (*I like swimming…*)  
4. **has**
</details>

2. Ella no lee / no le gusta leer.  
3. ¿Qué haces el lunes?  
4. Me gustaría un bocadillo.  
5. Mi cumpleaños es el 10 de mayo.  
6. Me gusta nadar porque es divertido.

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrige

1. *He don't like films.*  
2. *I like swim.*  
3. *My birthday is in the 5th of April.*  
4. *Give me a tea.*  
5. *Does you like music?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. He **doesn't** like films.  
2. I like **swimming**.  
3. My birthday is **on** the 5th of April.  
4. **Can I have** a tea, please? / **I'd like** a tea.  
5. **Do** you like music?
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Escribe tu “página de módulo”

Escribe 10–12 frases que junten: rutina + un día de la semana + un pedido de café + 2 gustos con *because* + tu cumpleaños.

**Modelo breve:**

> I get up at seven. On Monday I go to work. I have lunch at one. I like reading because it's relaxing. I don't like running. At the cafe I say: Can I have a coffee, please? I'd like a cookie too. My birthday is on the 12th of May. In August we have holiday. There is a park near my house.

### Ejercicio 7 — Autoevaluación rápida

Marca ✅ o ❌:

1. Sé usar *don't* y *doesn't*.  
2. Sé preguntar con *Do/Does*.  
3. Distingo *in / on / at*.  
4. Puedo pedir en un café con *please*.  
5. Uso *like + -ing* y *Because…*.  
6. Sé decir mi cumpleaños en inglés.

Si marcas ❌ en alguno, vuelve a la guía de esa unidad (tabla del mapa) 10 minutos y rehaz solo ese bloque.

## 12. Plan de repaso (30–40 minutos)

1. **8 min** — Present Simple: 10 frases (afirmativa / negativa / pregunta).  
2. **6 min** — *in / on / at*: 8 frases de tiempo.  
3. **6 min** — Diálogo de cafetería en voz alta.  
4. **6 min** — Gustos: 5 *like + -ing* + *because*.  
5. **6 min** — Fechas y cumpleaños.  
6. **resto** — Escribe el texto modelo personalizado y léelo.

### Checklist final del Módulo 2

- [ ] Rutina con hora y secuenciadores  
- [ ] Present Simple completo (I… / he…)  
- [ ] Días (*on*) y fin de semana  
- [ ] Pedidos educados en el café  
- [ ] Comidas: *have breakfast/lunch/dinner*  
- [ ] *like + -ing* + *Why? Because…*  
- [ ] Meses, ordinales, *When is your birthday?*  
- [ ] *There is/are* básico
