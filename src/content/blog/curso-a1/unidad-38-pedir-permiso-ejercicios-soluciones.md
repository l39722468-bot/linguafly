## Lección 1 — Gramática: Can I…? May I…?

**Objetivo:** pedir permiso con *Can I…?* y *May I…?*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ____ I use the phone? → *Can / Do / Am*  
2. May I ____ the window? → *open / opens / opening*  
3. Can I borrow ____ pen? → *your / you / yours*  
4. ____ I ask a question? → *May / Do / Am*  
5. No, ____ can't right now. → *you / your / yours*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Can** (*May* también vale).  
2. **open** — *May I* + verbo base.  
3. **your**  
4. **May** (*Can* también vale).  
5. **you**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. More formal permission: ____. → a) May I…? · b) I want · c) Give me  
7. After *Can I / May I* we use ____. → a) base verb (open, borrow) · b) -ing only · c) past tense  
8. *No, you can't* is a ____. → a) denial/refusal · b) greeting · c) thank you  
9. *Can I sit here?* — *sit* ____ correct. → a) is · b) are · c) am  
10. *Can I…?* is a ____. → a) request · b) order always · c) past story

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) May I…?**  
7. **a) base verb (open, borrow)**  
8. **a) denial/refusal**  
9. **a) is**  
10. **a) request**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. Can / I / use / the / phone?  
12. May / I / ask / a / question?  
13. No, / you / can't / right / now.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. Can I use the phone?  
12. May I ask a question?  
13. No, you can't right now.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *Can I…?* and *May I…?* both ask for permission. → True / False  
15. After *Can I / May I* we use the base verb (*open*, not *opens*). → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: frases educadas

**Objetivo:** *please, excuse me, borrow, of course, permission*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *Please* = ____. → a) polite word for requests · b) a refusal · c) a goodbye  
2. *Excuse me* = ____. → a) to get attention politely · b) to say no · c) to say thank you  
3. *Borrow* = ____. → a) use and return · b) keep forever · c) break  
4. *Of course* = ____. → a) yes, sure · b) no · c) maybe never  
5. *Permission* = ____. → a) allowed to do something · b) a school subject · c) a book

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) polite word for requests**  
2. **a) to get attention politely**  
3. **a) use and return**  
4. **a) yes, sure**  
5. **a) allowed to do something**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Empareja

6. Empareja: Can I…? / May I…? / Please / Of course / Excuse me → informal request · formal request · polite · yes, sure · get attention  
7. Empareja verbo → objeto típico: borrow / use / open / sit / ask → pen, book · bathroom, phone · door, window · here · question

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **Can I…? → informal request** · **May I…? → formal request** · **Please → polite** · **Of course → yes, sure** · **Excuse me → get attention**  
7. **borrow → pen, book** · **use → bathroom, phone** · **open → door, window** · **sit → here** · **ask → question**

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. Can I use the bathroom, ____? → *please / pleaseing / pleased*  
9. ____ me, may I come in? → *Excuse / Excuses / Excused*  
10. Yes, ____ course. → *of / off / on*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **please**  
9. **Excuse**  
10. **of**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Definiciones

11. A *request* is ____. → a) asking for something · b) saying goodbye · c) a number  
12. *Right now* means ____. → a) at this moment · b) yesterday · c) never  
13. Which is more formal? → a) May I ask a question? · b) Give me a question · c) I want question

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) asking for something**  
12. **a) at this moment**  
13. **a) May I ask a question?**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena

14. Can / I / borrow / your / eraser?  
15. Excuse / me, / may / I / come / in?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. Can I borrow your eraser?  
15. Excuse me, may I come in?

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Permission

**Objetivo:** leer sobre frases educadas en clase y en el trabajo.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-38/reading-permission.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Permission"></audio>

> In class we use polite phrases. Can I use the bathroom? May I ask a question? At work we say please and thank you. Excuse me—can I borrow your pen? Of course. When we ask for permission, we are polite.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Where do we use polite phrases first in the text? → a) In class · b) At home · c) At the park  
2. Which question is about the bathroom? → a) Can I use the bathroom? · b) May I ask a question? · c) Can I borrow your pen?  
3. Which question is more formal in the text? → a) May I ask a question? · b) Give me a question · c) I want a question  
4. At work we say ____. → a) please and thank you · b) only goodbye · c) nothing  
5. *Excuse me* helps to ____. → a) start a polite request · b) say no · c) close a door

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) In class**  
2. **a) Can I use the bathroom?**  
3. **a) May I ask a question?**  
4. **a) please and thank you**  
5. **a) start a polite request**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. What does someone want to borrow? → a) a pen · b) a car · c) a house  
7. The answer to borrowing the pen is ____. → a) Of course · b) Never · c) No  
8. The text is about ____. → a) polite requests and permission · b) sports · c) food  
9. When we ask for ____, we are polite. → a) permission · b) money only · c) food only  
10. The last sentence says we are ____. → a) polite · b) angry · c) late

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) a pen**  
7. **a) Of course**  
8. **a) polite requests and permission**  
9. **a) permission**  
10. **a) polite**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. *Borrow* means ____. → a) use and return · b) keep forever · c) break  
12. We ____ permission. → a) ask for · b) ask to · c) ask of  
13. A friendly yes is ____. → a) Of course · b) Go away · c) Never  
14. The text shows examples ____. → a) both in the text · b) only at home · c) only in parks  
15. *Polite* means ____. → a) respectful; having good manners · b) rude · c) loud

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) use and return**  
12. **a) ask for**  
13. **a) Of course**  
14. **a) both in the text**  
15. **a) respectful; having good manners**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: In class

**Objetivo:** escuchar peticiones educadas en clase.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-38/listening-classroom.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: In class"></audio>

> **A:** Excuse me. Can I use the bathroom?  
> **B:** Yes, of course.  
> **A:** May I borrow your pen, please?  
> **B:** Sure. Here you are.  
> **A:** Thank you.  
> **B:** Can you close the door, please?  
> **A:** Of course. We use please and thank you in class.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What does A ask first? → a) to use the bathroom · b) to open a window · c) to leave school  
2. What is B's first answer? → a) Yes, of course. · b) No · c) Maybe later  
3. What does A ask next? → a) to borrow a pen · b) to borrow a book · c) to sit down  
4. What does B say about the pen? → a) Sure. Here you are. · b) Never · c) Buy one  
5. What does A say after getting the pen? → a) Thank you. · b) Goodbye · c) Sorry

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) to use the bathroom**  
2. **a) Yes, of course.**  
3. **a) to borrow a pen**  
4. **a) Sure. Here you are.**  
5. **a) Thank you.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. What does B ask A to do? → a) to close the door · b) to open the window · c) to leave  
7. Where do they use please and thank you? → a) in class · b) only at home · c) never  
8. How does A start? → a) Excuse me · b) Hey you · c) Give me  
9. *May I…?* is ____ than *Can I…?* in many situations. → a) more formal · b) less polite · c) incorrect  
10. *Here you are* means ____. → a) giving something · b) saying goodbye · c) refusing

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) to close the door**  
7. **a) in class**  
8. **a) Excuse me**  
9. **a) more formal**  
10. **a) giving something**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. May I borrow your pen, ____? → a) please · b) pleased · c) pleasing  
12. The dialogue is about ____. → a) polite requests in class · b) sports · c) food  
13. *Of course* means ____. → a) yes, of course · b) no · c) never  
14. How many permission requests does A make? → a) two: the bathroom and a pen · b) one · c) five  
15. Polite words in the dialogue include ____. → a) please, thank you, and excuse me · b) only goodbye · c) only no

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) please**  
12. **a) polite requests in class**  
13. **a) yes, of course**  
14. **a) two: the bathroom and a pen**  
15. **a) please, thank you, and excuse me**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: Polite requests

**Objetivo:** escribir peticiones de permiso y respuestas educadas.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ____ I sit here? → *Can / Do / Am*  
2. May I ____ a question? → *ask / asks / asking*  
3. Yes, ____ course. → *of / off / on*  
4. Excuse ____, can I come in? → *me / my / I*  
5. Can I borrow ____ book? → *your / you / yours*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Can** (*May* también vale).  
2. **ask**  
3. **of**  
4. **me**  
5. **your**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. Can / I / sit / here?  
7. No, / you / can't / right / now.  
8. May / I / open / the / window?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. Can I sit here?  
7. No, you can't right now.  
8. May I open the window?

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Escribe una petición con *Can I…?*  
10. Escribe una petición más formal con *May I…?*  
11. Mini diálogo: pedir un boli + respuesta positiva.  
12. Empieza con *Excuse me* + *please*.  
13. Diálogo corto (4–5 líneas) en clase.  
14. Tres peticiones diferentes.  
15. Una negativa educada + una petición de ayuda.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. Can I use the bathroom?  
10. May I ask a question?  
11. Can I borrow your pen? Yes, of course. Here you are.  
12. Excuse me, can I use the computer, please?  
13. Can I open the window? Yes, of course. Thank you. Can you close it, please? Sure.  
14. Can I use the bathroom? May I ask a question? Can I borrow your book?  
15. No, you can't right now. Can you help me, please?

</details>
