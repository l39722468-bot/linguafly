## Lección 1 — Gramática: in + estación

**Objetivo:** preposición *in* con *spring, summer, autumn, winter*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. It is hot ____ summer. → *in / on / at*  
2. The flowers bloom ____ spring. → *in / on / at*  
3. It is cold ____ winter. → *in / on / at*  
4. The leaves fall ____ autumn. → *in / on / at*  
5. I go to the beach ____ summer. → *in / on / at*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **in** — *in* + estación.  
2. **in**  
3. **in**  
4. **in**  
5. **in**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Before seasons we use ____. → a) in · b) on · c) at  
7. Correct: ____. → a) in spring · b) on spring · c) at spring  
8. *In autumn* means ____. → a) during autumn · b) never autumn · c) only winter  
9. The four seasons are ____. → a) spring, summer, autumn, winter · b) only summer · c) Monday–Friday  
10. It is snowy ____ winter. → a) in · b) on · c) at

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) in**  
7. **a) in spring**  
8. **a) during autumn**  
9. **a) spring, summer, autumn, winter**  
10. **a) in**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. It / is / hot / in / summer.  
12. The / flowers / bloom / in / spring.  
13. It / is / cold / in / winter.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. It is hot in summer.  
12. The flowers bloom in spring.  
13. It is cold in winter.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We say *in summer* (not *on summer*). → True / False  
15. The four seasons are spring, summer, autumn and winter. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: estaciones

**Objetivo:** *spring, summer, autumn, winter, nature, flowers, leaves*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *Spring* = ____. → a) the season after winter, when flowers bloom · b) the cold season · c) only snow  
2. *Summer* = ____. → a) the hot, sunny season · b) the cold season · c) only autumn  
3. *Autumn* = ____. → a) the season when leaves fall · b) the hot beach season · c) only spring  
4. *Winter* = ____. → a) the cold season, associated with snow · b) the hot season · c) only flowers  
5. *Nature* = ____. → a) plants, animals, weather, and things not made by people · b) only cars · c) only schools

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) the season after winter, when flowers bloom**  
2. **a) the hot, sunny season**  
3. **a) the season when leaves fall**  
4. **a) the cold season, associated with snow**  
5. **a) plants, animals, weather, and things not made by people**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Empareja

6. Empareja: spring / summer / autumn / winter / nature → flowers bloom · hot and sunny · leaves fall · cold and snow · trees, animals, and plants  
7. Empareja: in spring / in summer / in autumn / in winter / nature → warm weather and new leaves · holidays and the beach · orange leaves · snow and a coat · parks and forests

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **spring → flowers bloom** · **summer → hot and sunny** · **autumn → leaves fall** · **winter → cold and snow** · **nature → trees, animals, and plants**  
7. **in spring → warm weather and new leaves** · **in summer → holidays and the beach** · **in autumn → orange leaves** · **in winter → snow and a coat** · **nature → parks and forests**

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–13 — Completa / elige

8. It is hot ____ summer. → *in / on / at*  
9. Flowers bloom ____. → a) in spring · b) on spring · c) at spring  
10. I like ____ in every season. → *nature / natures / naturings*  
11. Which season is cold and snowy? → a) Winter · b) Summer · c) Spring  
12. Which season do leaves fall? → a) Autumn · b) Summer · c) Spring  
13. Parks and forests are part of ____. → a) nature · b) only hotels · c) only tickets

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **in**  
9. **a) in spring**  
10. **nature**  
11. **a) Winter**  
12. **a) Autumn**  
13. **a) nature**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *Autumn* is when leaves fall. → True / False  
15. *In spring* uses the preposition *in*. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Four seasons

**Objetivo:** leer sobre las cuatro estaciones y la naturaleza.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-45/reading-seasons.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Four seasons"></audio>

> I love all four seasons. In spring, the flowers bloom and the weather is warm. In summer, it is hot and sunny; I go to the beach. In autumn, the leaves fall and the trees are orange and yellow. In winter, it is cold and sometimes it snows. I like nature in every season.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What does the writer love? → a) All four seasons · b) Only winter · c) Only summer  
2. What happens in spring? → a) The flowers bloom and the weather is warm · b) It always snows · c) Leaves fall  
3. Where does the writer go in summer? → a) To the beach · b) To school only · c) Nowhere  
4. What happens in autumn? → a) The leaves fall, and the trees are orange and yellow · b) Flowers bloom · c) It is always hot  
5. What is winter like? → a) It is cold and sometimes snowy · b) Always hot · c) Always sunny

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) All four seasons**  
2. **a) The flowers bloom and the weather is warm**  
3. **a) To the beach**  
4. **a) The leaves fall, and the trees are orange and yellow**  
5. **a) It is cold and sometimes snowy**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. What does the writer like in every season? → a) Nature · b) Only hotels · c) Only cars  
7. Summer is ____. → a) hot and sunny · b) always snowy · c) always cold  
8. In spring, ____. → a) flowers bloom · b) leaves fall · c) it always snows  
9. In autumn, ____. → a) leaves fall · b) flowers bloom · c) it is always hot  
10. Winter is ____. → a) cold and snow · b) always hot · c) always sunny

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Nature**  
7. **a) hot and sunny**  
8. **a) flowers bloom**  
9. **a) leaves fall**  
10. **a) cold and snow**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text is about ____. → a) the four seasons and nature · b) only food · c) only sports  
12. *Nature* means ____. → a) trees, flowers, animals, and other things not made by people · b) only cars · c) only tickets  
13. Before seasons we use ____. → a) in · b) on · c) at  
14. Summer is ____. → a) the hot season · b) the cold season · c) only autumn  
15. The main topic is ____. → a) seasons and nature · b) cooking · c) music

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) the four seasons and nature**  
12. **a) trees, flowers, animals, and other things not made by people**  
13. **a) in**  
14. **a) the hot season**  
15. **a) seasons and nature**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Favourite season

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo sobre estaciones favoritas.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-45/listening-seasons.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Favourite season"></audio>

> **A:** What is your favourite season?  
> **B:** I like summer. It is hot and sunny in summer.  
> **A:** I prefer spring. The flowers bloom in spring.  
> **B:** In autumn, the leaves fall. In winter, it is cold and snowy.  
> **A:** I love nature in all seasons.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What is B's favourite season? → a) Summer · b) Winter · c) Autumn  
2. What is summer like for B? → a) Hot and sunny · b) Cold and snowy · c) Always rainy  
3. What does A prefer? → a) Spring · b) Winter · c) Autumn  
4. What happens in spring? → a) The flowers bloom · b) Leaves fall · c) It always snows  
5. What happens in autumn? → a) The leaves fall · b) Flowers bloom · c) It is always hot

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Summer**  
2. **a) Hot and sunny**  
3. **a) Spring**  
4. **a) The flowers bloom**  
5. **a) The leaves fall**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. What is winter like? → a) Cold and snowy · b) Always hot · c) Always sunny  
7. What does A love? → a) Nature in all seasons · b) Only winter · c) Only cars  
8. Before summer we use ____. → a) in · b) on · c) at  
9. The dialogue is about ____. → a) seasons and nature · b) food · c) sports  
10. In spring, ____. → a) flowers bloom · b) leaves fall · c) it always snows

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Cold and snowy**  
7. **a) Nature in all seasons**  
8. **a) in**  
9. **a) seasons and nature**  
10. **a) flowers bloom**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Winter is ____. → a) cold and snow · b) always hot · c) always sunny  
12. *Nature* includes ____. → a) trees, flowers, and animals · b) only hotels · c) only tickets  
13. A's first question is ____. → a) “What is your favourite season?” · b) “What time is it?” · c) “Where is the hotel?”  
14. In autumn, ____. → a) leaves fall · b) flowers bloom · c) it is always hot

## Lección 5 — Writing: My seasons

**Objetivo:** escribir sobre estaciones y naturaleza.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. It is hot ____ summer. → *in / on / at*  
2. Flowers bloom ____ spring. → *in / on / at*  
3. It is cold ____. → a) in winter · b) on winter · c) at winter  
4. Leaves fall ____ autumn. → *in / on / at*  
5. My favourite season is ____. → *spring / springs / springing*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **in**  
2. **in**  
3. **a) in winter**  
4. **in**  
5. **spring** (u otra estación válida)

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. It / is / hot / in / summer.  
7. The / flowers / bloom / in / spring.  
8. It / is / cold / in / winter.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. It is hot in summer.  
7. The flowers bloom in spring.  
8. It is cold in winter.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Escribe el verano con *It is… in summer*.  
10. Escribe tu estación favorita y por qué.  
11. Dos frases: spring + autumn.  
12. Pregunta y respuesta: *What is your favourite season?*  
13. Párrafo corto (4–5 frases) sobre las cuatro estaciones.  
14. Dos actividades: summer + winter.  
15. Cuatro frases: una por estación.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. It is hot and sunny in summer.  
10. My favourite season is summer because it is hot.  
11. In spring, the flowers bloom. In autumn, the leaves fall.  
12. What is your favourite season? My favourite season is summer.  
13. In spring, the flowers bloom. In summer, it is hot. In autumn, the leaves fall. In winter, it is cold. I like nature in every season.  
14. In summer, I go to the beach. In winter, I stay at home when it is snowing.  
15. In spring, it is warm. In summer, it is hot. In autumn, the leaves fall. In winter, it is cold.

</details>
