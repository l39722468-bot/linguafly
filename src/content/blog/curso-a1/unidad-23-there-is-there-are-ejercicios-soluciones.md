## Lección 1 — Gramática: *There is / There are*

**Objetivo:** afirmativa, negativa, preguntas y preposiciones básicas.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. There ____ a book on the table. → *is / are / have*  
2. There ____ two chairs in the room. → *are / is / have*  
3. The cat is ____ the table. → *under / on / behind*  
4. There ____ a pen in my bag. → *isn't / aren't / is*  
5. The sofa is ____ the door. → *behind / in / on*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is** — *There is* + singular.  
2. **are** — *There are* + plural.  
3. **under** — *under = debajo de*.  
4. **isn't** — *There isn't* + singular.  
5. **behind** — *behind = detrás de*.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. There ____ a window in the room. → a) is · b) are · c) have  
7. There ____ any milk in the fridge. → a) isn't · b) aren't · c) is  
8. The book is ____ the desk. → a) on · b) under · c) behind  
9. Is ____ a bathroom upstairs? → a) there · b) here · c) it  
10. Are ____ any apples? → a) there · b) here · c) they

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) is**  
7. **a) isn't** — *milk* incontable → *isn't*.  
8. **a) on**  
9. **a) there** — *Is there…?*  
10. **a) there** — *Are there…?*

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. There / is / a / pen / on / the / desk.  
12. There / are / three / books / under / the / bed.  
13. The / bag / is / behind / the / chair.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. There is a pen on the desk.  
12. There are three books under the bed.  
13. The bag is behind the chair.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use There is for singular and There are for plural. → ?  
15. In questions we say Is there…? or Are there…? → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: *in, on, under, behind*

**Objetivo:** situar objetos con preposiciones de lugar.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la opción correcta

1. The book is ____ the bag. → a) in · b) on · c) under  
2. On = ____. → a) on top of something · b) inside · c) below  
3. Under = ____. → a) below something · b) on top · c) behind  
4. Behind = ____. → a) at the back of · b) in front of · c) inside  
5. There is a lamp ____ the table. → a) on · b) under · c) behind

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) in**  
2. **a) on top of something**  
3. **a) below something**  
4. **a) at the back of**  
5. **a) on**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Relaciona

**6.** Preposición → significado  

| Preposición | Significado |
|-------------|-------------|
| in | ? |
| on | ? |
| under | ? |
| behind | ? |

**7.** Frase → preposición  

| Frase | Preposición |
|-------|-------------|
| The keys are in the bag | ? |
| The cup is on the table | ? |
| The ball is under the bed | ? |
| The cat is behind the sofa | ? |

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**6.** in → inside, within · on → on top of · under → below · behind → at the back  

**7.** The keys are in the bag → in · The cup is on the table → on · The ball is under the bed → under · The cat is behind the sofa → behind

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. There is a phone ____ the desk. → *on / under / behind*  
9. The shoes are ____ the bed. → *under / on / in*  
10. There are two pillows ____ the bed. → *on / under / behind*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **on**  
9. **under**  
10. **on**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Elige

11. Where is the clock? It ____ the wall. → a) on · b) under · c) behind  
12. The ____ is behind the tree. → a) car · b) in · c) on  
13. There is water ____ the bottle. → a) in · b) on · c) under

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) on**  
12. **a) car**  
13. **a) in**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena las palabras

14. There / is / a / book / in / my / bag.  
15. The / dog / is / under / the / table.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. There is a book in my bag.  
15. The dog is under the table.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: My room

**Objetivo:** leer una descripción con *There is/are* y preposiciones.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-23/reading-room.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Room"></audio>

> My room is small but I have everything. There is a bed in the middle. There are two pillows on the bed. There is a desk next to the window. There is a book on the desk and a lamp. Under the bed there are my shoes and a bag. Behind the door there is a wardrobe. I like my room.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. How is the room described? → a) Small · b) Big · c) Dark  
2. What is in the middle of the room? → a) A bed · b) A desk · c) A wardrobe  
3. How many pillows are on the bed? → a) Two · b) One · c) Three  
4. Where is the desk? → a) Next to the window · b) Behind the door · c) Under the bed  
5. What is on the desk? → a) A book and a lamp · b) Shoes · c) Pillows

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Small**  
2. **a) A bed**  
3. **a) Two**  
4. **a) Next to the window**  
5. **a) A book and a lamp**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. What is under the bed? → a) Shoes and a bag · b) A desk · c) A wardrobe  
7. Where is the wardrobe? → a) Behind the door · b) Next to the window · c) Under the bed  
8. Does the writer like his room? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say  
9. Which word means "debajo de"? → a) Under · b) On · c) Behind  
10. Which word means "detrás de"? → a) Behind · b) Under · c) On

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Shoes and a bag**  
7. **a) Behind the door**  
8. **a) Yes**  
9. **a) Under**  
10. **a) Behind**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text uses ____ to describe what is in the room. → a) There is / There are · b) I have · c) It is  
12. What is next to the window? → a) A desk · b) A bed · c) A wardrobe  
13. The main topic is ____. → a) description of a room and what is in it · b) the writer's family · c) shopping  
14. Are there ____ pillows on the bed? → a) two · b) one · c) three  
15. Which preposition is used for the shoes? → a) Under · b) On · c) Behind

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) There is / There are**  
12. **a) A desk**  
13. **a) description of a room and what is in it**  
14. **a) two**  
15. **a) Under**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: What's in your bag?

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo sobre qué hay y dónde está.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-23/listening-bag.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Bag"></audio>

> **A:** What is in your bag?  
> **B:** There is a book, a pen and my keys.  
> **A:** Is there a phone?  
> **B:** Yes, there is. It is under the book.  
> **A:** Where is the chair?  
> **B:** It is behind the table. There are two chairs in the room. There isn't a sofa in my room.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is in B's bag? → a) A book, a pen and keys · b) A phone only · c) A sofa  
2. Is there a phone in the bag? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
3. Where is the phone? → a) Under the book · b) On the table · c) Behind the chair  
4. Where is the chair? → a) Behind the table · b) Under the bed · c) In the bag  
5. How many chairs are in the room? → a) Two · b) One · c) Three

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) A book, a pen and keys**  
2. **a) Yes**  
3. **a) Under the book**  
4. **a) Behind the table**  
5. **a) Two**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Is there a sofa in B's room? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The audio doesn't say  
7. Which word does B use for "debajo de"? → a) Under · b) Behind · c) On  
8. Which word does B use for "detrás de"? → a) Behind · b) Under · c) In  
9. The dialogue is ____. → a) about where things are · b) about food · c) about work  
10. B ____ There is for singular. → a) says · b) say · c) saying

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) No**  
7. **a) Under**  
8. **a) Behind**  
9. **a) about where things are**  
10. **a) says**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. There ____ a sofa. → a) isn't · b) aren't · c) is  
12. A ____ about the bag and the room. → a) asks · b) ask · c) asking  
13. The chairs are ____. → a) in the room · b) in the bag · c) under the table  
14. What does A ask first? → a) What is in your bag? · b) Where is the sofa? · c) How many chairs?  
15. B uses ____ to say there is no sofa. → a) There isn't · b) There aren't · c) There is

## Lección 5 — Writing: What's in the room?

**Objetivo:** escribir con *There is/are*, negativas y preposiciones.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. There ____ a desk in my room. → *is / are / have*  
2. There ____ two windows. → *are / is / have*  
3. The cat is ____ the table. → *under / on / behind*  
4. The bag ____ behind the chair. → *is / are / be*  
5. There ____ a sofa in the room. → *isn't / aren't / is*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is**  
2. **are**  
3. **under**  
4. **is**  
5. **isn't**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. There / is / a / lamp / on / the / desk.  
7. There / are / three / books / under / the / bed.  
8. The / wardrobe / is / behind / the / door.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. There is a lamp on the desk.  
7. There are three books under the bed.  
8. The wardrobe is behind the door.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. What is on your desk (*There is/are*).  
10. Where is an object (*under / on / behind*).  
11. Two sentences: *There is* + *There are* in your room.  
12. *Is there a…?* + your answer (*Yes, there is / No, there isn't*).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. There is a book and a pen on my desk.  
10. The phone is under the table.  
11. There is a bed in my room. There are two chairs.  
12. Is there a bathroom? Yes, there is.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Short paragraph (4–5 sentences): what is in a room + where (*in, on, under, behind*).  
14. 3 sentences with *There isn't / There aren't*.  
15. *Are there any…?* + your answer.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
There is a bed in my room. There are two pillows on the bed. The desk is next to the window. The shoes are under the bed.

**14.**  
There isn't a sofa in my room. There aren't any plants. There isn't a TV.

**15.**  
Are there any chairs? Yes, there are two.

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Tema | Recuerda |
|------|----------|
| Singular | *There **is** / isn't · Is there…?* |
| Plural | *There **are** / aren't · Are there…?* |
| Respuestas | *Yes, there is/are. No, there isn't/aren't.* |
| Preposiciones | *in · on · under · behind* |
| Tip | *any* en negativas/preguntas (plural/incontable) |
