## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 28 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Usar ***have got*** y ***has got*** para el cuerpo.
- Distinguir ***this*** (singular) y ***these*** (plural).
- Nombrar: ***head, face, arms, legs, hands, feet*** (+ eyes, nose, mouth, fingers).
- Describir: *long legs, blue eyes, big feet, tall…*.
- Recordar el plural irregular ***foot → feet***.

### Tip clave

## 2. *Have got / Has got*

| Sujeto | Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I / you / we / they | **have got** | I have got two arms. |
| he / she / it | **has got** | She has got long legs. |

```
I/you/we/they + have got + …
he/she/it + has got + …
```

![Esquema have got has got A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-28/a1-unit28-have-has-got.png)

> I **have got** two arms.  
> We **have got** two hands.  
> She **has got** long legs.  
> He **has got** blue eyes.  
> I **have got** a head and a face.  
> She **has got** long arms.  
> I **have got** two arms and two legs.  
> She **has got** a beautiful face.

### *Have got* ≈ *have*

En A1 verás las dos:

| Con *got* | Sin *got* (también correcta) |
| :--- | :--- |
| I have got two arms. | I have two arms. |
| She has got long legs. | She has long legs. |

El curso practica sobre todo ***have got / has got*** (muy frecuente en UK).

### Negativa y pregunta (intro rápida)

| Tipo | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| Negativa | I **haven’t got** blue eyes. / She **hasn’t got** long arms. |
| Pregunta | **Have** you **got** long legs? / **Has** she **got** blue eyes? |

En esta unidad el foco principal es la **afirmativa** + vocabulario del cuerpo.

## 3. *This / These*

| Forma | Número | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **This** | singular | This is my hand. |
| **These** | plural | These are my feet. |

```
This + is + singular
These + are + plural
```

> **This** is my hand.  
> **This** is my head.  
> **These** are my feet.  
> **These** are my arms.  
> This is my hand and these are my feet.

Señala una sola parte → *This is…*. Señala dos o más → *These are…*.

## 5. Adjetivos con el cuerpo

| Adjetivo | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| **long** | She has got long legs / long arms. |
| **short** | He has got short legs. |
| **big** | My feet are big. / big blue eyes |
| **small** | She has got small hands. |
| **blue** | He has got blue eyes. |
| **tall / short** | She is tall. / He is short. |
| **beautiful** | She has got a beautiful face. |

Orden típico: **adjetivo + parte del cuerpo**

> long legs · blue eyes · big feet · a beautiful face

### Texto modelo (Tom)

> My name is Tom. I have got a head and a face. On my face I have got two eyes, a nose and a mouth. I have got two arms and two hands. This is my left hand. These are my fingers. I have got two legs and two feet. My sister has got long legs and big blue eyes. She is tall.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-28/brother-short.mp3" title="🔊 My brother has got a big head and short legs. He is short"></audio>

### Diálogo modelo

> **A:** What have you got on your face?  
> **B:** I have got two eyes, a nose and a mouth.  
> **A:** This is my hand.  
> **B:** Yes, and these are my arms. I have got long arms.  
> **A:** She has got big feet.  
> **B:** My brother has got a big head and short legs. He is short.

## 7. Errores típicos de hispanohablantes

| ❌ Incorrecto | ✅ Correcto | Explicación |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *I has got two arms* | *I **have** got two arms* | I → have |
| *She have got long legs* | *She **has** got long legs* | She → has |
| *This are my feet* | ***These** are my feet* | Plural → these |
| *These is my hand* | ***This** is my hand* | Singular → this |
| *two foots* | *two **feet*** | Plural irregular |
| *I have got two arm* | *I have got two **arms*** | Plural con número |
| *She has got eyes blue* | *She has got **blue eyes*** | Adj. antes del nombre |

## 8. Pronunciación útil

| Forma | Tip A1 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **have got** | En habla rápida: *I’ve got* = *aiv got*. |
| **has got** | *She’s got* = *shiz got*. |
| **this / these** | *this* corto · *these* con /iː/. |
| **foot / feet** | *fut* · *fiit*. |
| **face / eyes** | *feis* · *aiz*. |
| **fingers** | *FING-gəz*. |

Practica el par: *This is my hand* → *These are my hands*.

## 8b. Mini-diálogos para memorizar

> **A:** This is my hand.  
> **B:** These are my feet.  
> **A:** Have you got long arms?  
> **B:** Yes, I have got long arms.

> **A:** What have you got on your face?  
> **B:** Two eyes, a nose and a mouth.  
> **A:** Has she got blue eyes?  
> **B:** Yes. She has got big blue eyes. She’s tall.

> **A:** These are my fingers.  
> **B:** And this is my left hand.  
> **A:** My brother has got short legs.  
> **B:** He is short.

Cambia solo la parte del cuerpo: fijas *have/has got* y *this/these*.

### Más frases para memorizar

> I have got two arms.  
> She has got long legs.  
> This is my hand.  
> These are my feet.  
> I have got a face and two eyes.  
> He has got blue eyes.  
> We have got two hands.  
> These are my arms.  
> This is my head.  
> I have got a head and a face.  
> She has got long arms.  
> This is my hand and these are my feet.  
> I have got two arms and two legs.  
> She has got a beautiful face.  
> I wash my hands every day.  
> My feet are big.  
> My sister has got long legs and big blue eyes. She is tall.  
> My brother has got a big head and short legs. He is short.

Lee la lista dos veces. Luego descríbete en **8 frases** con *have got* y *this/these*.

### Rutina de 2 minutos

1. Di 4 frases *I have got…*.  
2. Di 2 frases *She/He has got…*.  
3. Señala: *This is…* / *These are…* (4 veces).  
4. Di el plural: *one foot → two feet*.

Si te miras en un espejo o señales tu cuerpo, el vocabulario se fija más rápido.

### Tour guiado (modelo)

> My name is Tom. I have got a head and a face. On my face I have got two eyes, a nose and a mouth. I have got two arms and two hands. This is my left hand. These are my fingers. I have got two legs and two feet. My sister has got long legs and big blue eyes. She is tall.

Grábate leyéndolo. Luego cambia a **tu** descripción sin mirar.

### Frases extra para practicar en voz alta

> This is my head.  
> These are my arms.  
> I have got two legs and two feet.  
> She has got small hands.  
> He has got blue eyes.  
> What have you got on your face?  
> I wash my hands every day.  
> My feet are big. I wear size 42.  
> She is tall. He is short.  
> These are my fingers.

Personaliza con tu familia: *My mum has got… My dad has got…*.

### Checklist mental

- I/you/we/they → **have got**  
- he/she/it → **has got**  
- singular cercano → **This is**  
- plural cercano → **These are**  
- pie → **foot / feet**  
- adj. + body part → *long legs, blue eyes*  
- cara → eyes + nose + mouth
