## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 55 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Usar ***too + adjetivo*** (*too big, too small, too expensive*).
- Usar ***adjetivo + enough*** (*big enough, long enough*).
- Entender que ***not big enough*** ≈ *too small*.
- Pedir tallas: ***Do you have this in size M?***
- Preguntar: ***Where is the changing room?***
- Hablar de ***sale*** (rebajas) y si algo ***fits***.

---

## 1. *Too* + adjetivo

***Too*** = demasiado (más de lo necesario / no queda bien).

| Forma | Significado | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **too** big | demasiado grande | This shirt is **too** big. |
| **too** small | demasiado pequeña | This is **too** small. |
| **too** expensive | demasiado caro | This jacket is **too** expensive. |
| **too** long | demasiado larga | These trousers are **too** long. |

Orden: ***too + adjetivo*** (antes del adjetivo).

> This shirt is too big.  
> The trousers are too small.  
> This jacket is too expensive.

## 2. Adjetivo + *enough*

***Enough*** = suficientemente (la cantidad/talla es suficiente).

| Forma | Significado | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| big **enough** | suficientemente grande | It's big **enough** now. |
| long **enough** | suficientemente larga | Is it long **enough**? |
| cheap **enough** | suficientemente barato | It's cheap **enough**. |

Orden: ***adjetivo + enough*** (después del adjetivo).

> It isn't big enough.  
> Is it long enough?  
> It's big enough now.

| Frase | ≈ |
| :--- | :--- |
| not big enough | too small |
| not small enough | too big |

## 4. Vocabulario de tienda de ropa

| Palabra | Significado | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **size** | talla | Do you have this in size M? |
| **changing room** | probador | Where is the changing room? |
| **sale** | rebajas | There is a sale this week. |
| **on sale** | en rebajas | Is it on sale? |
| **fit** | quedar (bien) | It doesn't fit. / It fits. |
| **try on** | probarse | Try this. |
| **shop assistant** | dependiente/a | The shop assistant says yes. |
| **I'll take it** | me lo llevo | I'll take it. |

> Where is the changing room? — Over there.  
> Is it on sale? — Yes, twenty pounds.  
> It doesn't fit.

## 5. Texto modelo: en la tienda

![Changing room dialogue A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-55/a1-unit55-changing-room.png)

> I am at the clothing store. There is a sale this week. I want a new shirt. Do you have this in size M? The shop assistant says yes. I go to the changing room. The shirt is too big. I need size S. She brings a smaller one. It is big enough now. I buy it. The total is twenty pounds because it's on sale.

- *sale / size M / changing room*  
- *too big* → *size S*  
- *big enough now*

## 7. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| enough big | **big enough** | *enough* va después |
| big too | **too** big | *too* va antes |
| This shirt too big. | This shirt **is** too big. | falta *is* |
| It isn't enough big. | It isn't **big enough**. | orden |
| changing room where? | **Where is** the changing room? | pregunta completa |
| in M size | in **size** M | *size* + letra |

## 9. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — *too* o completa con adjetivo + *enough*

1. This shirt is \_\_\_\_ big.  
2. It isn't \_\_\_\_ enough. (grande)  
3. The trousers are \_\_\_\_ small.  
4. Is it \_\_\_\_ enough? (larga)  
5. This jacket is \_\_\_\_ expensive.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **too** · 2. **big** · 3. **too** · 4. **long** · 5. **too**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Orden de palabras

1. shirt / too / This / is / big → \_\_\_\_  
2. big / isn't / It / enough → \_\_\_\_  
3. the / Where / changing room / is → \_\_\_\_  
4. size / this / Do you have / in / M → \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. This shirt is too big.  
2. It isn't big enough.  
3. Where is the changing room?  
4. Do you have this in size M?
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Tallas y tienda

1. S = \_\_\_\_  
2. M = \_\_\_\_  
3. L = \_\_\_\_  
4. XL = \_\_\_\_  
5. Place to try on clothes = \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_  
6. Reduced prices = \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **small** · 2. **medium** · 3. **large** · 4. **extra large** · 5. **changing room** · 6. **sale**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Empareja

1. too big  
2. big enough  
3. not big enough  
4. sale  
5. changing room  
6. size  

a) queda bien · b) no queda (demasiado pequeña) · c) rebajas · d) no queda (demasiado grande) · e) S/M/L/XL · f) probador

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **d** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **b** · 4 → **c** · 5 → **f** · 6 → **e**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrige

1. *This shirt is enough big.*  
2. *It isn't enough big.*  
3. *Do you have this in M size?*  
4. *Where the changing room is?*  
5. *This is too much small.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. This shirt is **big enough**. / This shirt is **too big**. (según significado)  
   → si querías “demasiado”: **too big**; si “suficiente”: **big enough**.  
2. It isn't **big enough**.  
3. Do you have this in **size M**?  
4. **Where is** the changing room?  
5. This is **too** small.
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa el diálogo

A: Do you have this in \_\_\_\_ L?  
B: Yes, here you are.  
A: Where is the \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_?  
B: Over there.  
A: This is \_\_\_\_ small. Do you have it in \_\_\_\_?  
B: Yes. Try this.  
A: It's \_\_\_\_ enough now. I'll \_\_\_\_ it. Is it on \_\_\_\_?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**size** · **changing** · **room** · **too** · **XL** · **big** · **take** · **sale**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Completa el texto

I am at the clothing store. There is a \_\_\_\_ this week. Do you have this in \_\_\_\_ M? I go to the \_\_\_\_ room. The shirt is \_\_\_\_ big. I need size S. It is big \_\_\_\_ now. The total is twenty pounds because it's on \_\_\_\_.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**sale** · **size** · **changing** · **too** · **enough** · **sale**
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Elige la correcta

1. ____  
   a) too big · b) big too · c) enough too  
2. ____  
   a) enough big · b) big enough · c) big to enough  
3. Not big enough ≈ ____  
   a) too big · b) too small · c) just right  
4. XL = ____  
   a) small · b) medium · c) extra large

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a** · 2. **b** · 3. **b** · 4. **c**
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Traduce

1. Esta camisa es demasiado grande.  
2. No es suficientemente grande.  
3. ¿Tienen esto en talla M?  
4. ¿Dónde está el probador?  
5. ¿Está en rebajas?  
6. Me lo llevo.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. This shirt is too big.  
2. It isn't big enough. / It's not big enough.  
3. Do you have this in size M?  
4. Where is the changing room?  
5. Is it on sale?  
6. I'll take it.
</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Escribe tu visita a la tienda

Escribe 8–10 frases. Incluye:

- 2 × *too* + adjetivo  
- 2 × *(not) … enough*  
- 2 tallas (*size S/M/L/XL*)  
- *changing room*  
- *sale* o *on sale*  
- *I'll take it* o *It doesn't fit*

**Modelo:** el texto de la sección 5.

## 10. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Repasa *too / enough* y el orden de palabras.  
2. **8 min** — Haz los ejercicios 1–5.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha y repite 8 audios (incluye el diálogo).  
4. **5–8 min** — Representa una compra de ropa en voz alta.

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *too* antes del adjetivo  
- [ ] Uso *enough* después del adjetivo  
- [ ] Relaciono *not big enough* con *too small*  
- [ ] Pido *size S/M/L/XL*  
- [ ] Pregunto por *changing room* y *sale*

## 11. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Memoriza el orden: ***too ADJ*** / ***ADJ enough***.  
2. Si no te queda: *too big / too small / doesn't fit*.  
3. Si te queda: *It's big enough* / *It fits*.  
4. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 55](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-55-tienda-ropa-too-enough-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 55 del curso A1](/curso-a1/unit-55).
