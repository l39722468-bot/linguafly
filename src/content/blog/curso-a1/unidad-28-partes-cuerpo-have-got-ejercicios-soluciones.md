## Lección 1 — Gramática: have/has got; this/these

**Objetivo:** *have got / has got* y *this / these* con el cuerpo.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ got two arms. → *have / has / am*  
2. She ____ got long legs. → *has / have / is*  
3. ____ is my hand. → *This / These / That*  
4. ____ are my feet. → *These / This / That*  
5. I have got a ____ and two eyes. → *face / leg / arm*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **have** — *I have got* = tengo.  
2. **has** — *She has got* = tiene.  
3. **This** — *This* = este/esta (singular).  
4. **These** — *These* = estos/estas (plural).  
5. **face** — *Face* = cara.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. He ____ got blue eyes. → a) has · b) have · c) is  
7. We ____ got two hands. → a) have · b) has · c) are  
8. ____ are my arms. → a) These · b) This · c) It  
9. ____ is my head. → a) This · b) These · c) They  
10. I have ____ two legs and two feet. → a) got · b) get · c) have

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) has**  
7. **a) have**  
8. **a) These**  
9. **a) This**  
10. **a) got**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. I / have / got / a / head / and / a / face.  
12. She / has / got / long / arms.  
13. This / is / my / hand / and / these / are / my / feet.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I have got a head and a face.  
12. She has got long arms.  
13. This is my hand and these are my feet.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use *have got* for I/you/we/they and *has got* for he/she/it. → V / F  
15. *This* is for singular and *these* for plural. → V / F

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: partes del cuerpo

**Objetivo:** *head, arms, legs, hands, feet, face* (+ *eyes, nose, mouth*).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige

1. I have got a ____. → a) head · b) arm · c) leg  
2. We have got two ____. → a) arms · b) heads · c) faces  
3. She has got long ____. → a) legs · b) hands · c) feet  
4. I wash my ____ every day. → a) hands · b) legs · c) arms  
5. My ____ are big. I wear size 42. → a) feet · b) hands · c) face

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) head**  
2. **a) arms**  
3. **a) legs**  
4. **a) hands**  
5. **a) feet**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Relaciona (escribe la pareja)

6. head · face · arm · leg → part with brain / front of head with eyes, nose, mouth / from shoulder to hand / from hip to foot  
7. I have two · I have five fingers on each · My · I stand on my two → arms and two legs / hand / face has eyes, nose, mouth / feet

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. head → part with brain · face → front of head with eyes, nose, mouth · arm → from shoulder to hand · leg → from hip to foot  
7. I have two → arms and two legs · I have five fingers on each → hand · My → face has eyes, nose, mouth · I stand on my two → feet

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. He has got a ____ and two eyes. → *face / head / hand*  
9. She has got small ____. → *hands / legs / arms*  
10. I have got two ____ and two feet. → *legs / arms / hands*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **face**  
9. **hands**  
10. **legs**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Elige

11. Hand – plural is ____. → a) hands · b) handes · c) feets  
12. Foot – plural is ____. → a) feet · b) foots · c) footes  
13. On my ____ I have got eyes, a nose and a mouth. → a) face · b) head · c) hand

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) hands**  
12. **a) feet** — no *foots*.  
13. **a) face**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena las palabras

14. I / have / got / two / arms / and / two / legs.  
15. She / has / got / a / beautiful / face.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. I have got two arms and two legs.  
15. She has got a beautiful face.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: My name is Tom

**Objetivo:** leer una descripción del cuerpo con *have got* y *this/these*.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-28/reading-tom.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Tom"></audio>

> My name is Tom. I have got a head and a face. On my face I have got two eyes, a nose and a mouth. I have got two arms and two hands. This is my left hand. These are my fingers. I have got two legs and two feet. My sister has got long legs and big blue eyes. She is tall.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the name of the writer? → a) Tom · b) Sister · c) Face  
2. What has Tom got on his face? → a) Two eyes, a nose, a mouth · b) Two arms · c) Two feet  
3. How many arms does Tom have got? → a) Two · b) One · c) Three  
4. What does "This is my left hand" mean? → a) Esta es mi mano izquierda · b) Estas son mis piernas · c) Tengo dos pies  
5. What are "These" in the text? → a) My fingers · b) My legs · c) My eyes

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Tom**  
2. **a) Two eyes, a nose, a mouth**  
3. **a) Two**  
4. **a) Esta es mi mano izquierda**  
5. **a) My fingers**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. How many legs and feet does Tom have got? → a) Two legs and two feet · b) One leg and one foot · c) Three legs  
7. What has Tom's sister got? → a) Long legs and big blue eyes · b) Short arms · c) Small feet  
8. Is Tom's sister tall? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say  
9. Which word means "cabeza"? → a) Head · b) Face · c) Hand  
10. Which word means "pies"? → a) Feet · b) Legs · c) Hands

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Two legs and two feet**  
7. **a) Long legs and big blue eyes**  
8. **a) Yes**  
9. **a) Head**  
10. **a) Feet**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. We use ____ for singular and ____ for plural. → a) This / These · b) That / Those · c) I / We  
12. Tom has got ____ hands. → a) two · b) one · c) three  
13. The main topic is ____. → a) body parts and have got · b) family · c) animals  
14. Which part of the body has fingers? → a) Hands · b) Feet · c) Head  
15. Have got is used for ____ of the body. → a) describing · b) eating · c) running

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) This / These**  
12. **a) two**  
13. **a) body parts and have got**  
14. **a) Hands**  
15. **a) describing**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: On your face

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo sobre partes del cuerpo.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-28/listening-body.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Body"></audio>

> **A:** What do you have got on your face?  
> **B:** I have got two eyes, a nose and a mouth.  
> **A:** This is my hand.  
> **B:** Yes, and these are my arms. I have got long arms.  
> **A:** She has got big feet.  
> **B:** My brother has got a big head and short legs. He is short.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What does A ask about first? → a) What do you have got on your face? · b) Where are your arms? · c) How many feet?  
2. What has B got on his face? → a) Two eyes, a nose, a mouth · b) Two arms · c) Big feet  
3. What does A say about "This"? → a) This is my hand · b) This is my head · c) This is my foot  
4. What are "these" for B? → a) My arms · b) My legs · c) My feet  
5. Has B got long or short arms? → a) Long · b) Short · c) The audio doesn't say

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) What do you have got on your face?**  
2. **a) Two eyes, a nose, a mouth**  
3. **a) This is my hand**  
4. **a) My arms**  
5. **a) Long**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Who has got big feet? → a) She (a girl) · b) B · c) Brother  
7. What has B's brother got? → a) A big head and short legs · b) Long arms · c) Big feet  
8. Is B's brother tall or short? → a) Short · b) Tall · c) The audio doesn't say  
9. The dialogue is ____. → a) about body parts · b) about animals · c) about food  
10. Which word means "mano"? → a) Hand · b) Head · c) Feet

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) She (a girl)**  
7. **a) A big head and short legs**  
8. **a) Short**  
9. **a) about body parts**  
10. **a) Hand**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. We use ____ for singular body part. → a) This · b) These · c) They  
12. We use ____ for plural body parts. → a) These · b) This · c) It  
13. B says these ____ my arms. → a) are · b) is · c) be  
14. The brother ____ got short legs. → a) has · b) have · c) is  
15. Have got is used for ____ and description. → a) possession · b) action · c) place

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) This**  
12. **a) These**  
13. **a) are**  
14. **a) has**  
15. **a) possession**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: Describe the body

**Objetivo:** escribir con *have/has got* y *this/these*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ got two arms. → *have / has / am*  
2. She ____ got a beautiful face. → *has / have / is*  
3. ____ is my hand. → *This / These / That*  
4. ____ are my feet. → *These / This / That*  
5. He has got two ____ and two feet. → *legs / arms / hands*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **have**  
2. **has**  
3. **This**  
4. **These**  
5. **legs**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. I / have / got / a / head / and / two / eyes.  
7. This / is / my / face / and / these / are / my / hands.  
8. She / has / got / long / legs / and / big / feet.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I have got a head and two eyes.  
7. This is my face and these are my hands.  
8. She has got long legs and big feet.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. What have you got on your face?  
10. *This is my…* and *These are my…* (body parts).  
11. Describe a person (*has got* + body parts) in two sentences.  
12. *Have you got…?* + your answer.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I have got two eyes, a nose and a mouth on my face.  
10. This is my hand. These are my feet.  
11. She has got long legs. She has got blue eyes.  
12. Have you got blue eyes? Yes, I have.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Short paragraph (4–5 sentences): your body with *have got*.  
14. 3 sentences: *This is… / These are…* for body parts.  
15. Difference: *This is…* vs *These are…* (one sentence each).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
I have got a head and a face. I have got two arms and two hands. I have got two legs and two feet.

**14.**  
This is my head. These are my arms. These are my legs.

**15.**  
This is my hand. These are my hands (two).

</details>
