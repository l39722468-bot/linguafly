## Lección 1 — Gramática: play, go, do

**Objetivo:** elegir el verbo correcto con deportes y hobbies.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ football every Saturday. → *play / go / do*  
2. She ____ swimming on Mondays. → *goes / plays / does*  
3. We ____ yoga in the morning. → *do / go / play*  
4. They ____ tennis at the club. → *play / go / do*  
5. My sister ____ painting and reading. → *likes / go / do*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **play** — *Play football, play tennis.*  
2. **goes** — *Go swimming, go running.*  
3. **do** — *Do yoga, do exercise.*  
4. **play**  
5. **likes** — hobbies con *like*, no *play/go/do*.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. We use ____ with football, tennis, basketball. → a) play · b) go · c) do  
7. We use ____ with swimming, running, cycling. → a) go · b) play · c) do  
8. We use ____ with yoga, exercise, gymnastics. → a) do · b) play · c) go  
9. Correct: ____ football. → a) play · b) go · c) do  
10. Correct: ____ swimming. → a) go · b) play · c) do

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) play**  
7. **a) go**  
8. **a) do**  
9. **a) play**  
10. **a) go**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. I / play / football / on / Saturdays.  
12. She / goes / swimming / every / week.  
13. We / do / yoga / in / the morning.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I play football on Saturdays.  
12. She goes swimming every week.  
13. We do yoga in the morning.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *Play* is for ball sports: *play football, play tennis*. → V / F  
15. *Go* is with *-ing* (*go swimming*); *do* is for yoga, exercise. → V / F

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: deportes y hobbies

**Objetivo:** *football, tennis, swimming, painting, reading*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige

1. Football = ____. → a) team sport with a ball · b) in the water · c) with a racket  
2. Tennis = ____. → a) sport with racket and ball · b) in the pool · c) team sport  
3. Swimming = ____. → a) sport in water · b) with a ball · c) with a bike  
4. Painting = ____. → a) making pictures with colours · b) reading books · c) playing sport  
5. Reading = ____. → a) looking at books and understanding · b) painting · c) swimming

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) team sport with a ball**  
2. **a) sport with racket and ball**  
3. **a) sport in water**  
4. **a) making pictures with colours**  
5. **a) looking at books and understanding**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Relaciona (escribe la pareja)

6. football · tennis · swimming · yoga · painting → play / play / go / do / like or enjoy  
7. football · tennis · swimming · painting · reading → fútbol / tenis / natación / pintura / lectura

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. football → play · tennis → play · swimming → go · yoga → do · painting → like / enjoy  
7. football → fútbol · tennis → tenis · swimming → natación · painting → pintura · reading → lectura

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. I ____ football with my friends. → *play / go / do*  
9. She ____ swimming every weekend. → *goes / plays / does*  
10. He likes ____ and reading. → *painting / football / swimming*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **play**  
9. **goes**  
10. **painting**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Elige

11. Basketball: we ____. → a) play · b) go · c) do  
12. Running: we ____ running. → a) go · b) play · c) do  
13. Exercise: we ____ exercise. → a) do · b) play · c) go

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

13. **a) do**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena las palabras

## Lección 3 — Reading: An active family

**Objetivo:** leer sobre deportes y hobbies de una familia.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-32/reading-jake.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Jake"></audio>

> My name is Jake. I love sport. I play football every Saturday with my team. On Sundays I go swimming at the pool. My sister does yoga every morning. She doesn't like football but she likes reading and painting. My mum plays tennis twice a week. We are an active family.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the writer's name? → a) Jake · b) Jack · c) John  
2. When does Jake play football? → a) Every Saturday · b) Every Sunday · c) Every day  
3. When does he go swimming? → a) On Sundays · b) On Saturdays · c) Every morning  
4. What does his sister do every morning? → a) Yoga · b) Football · c) Swimming  
5. Does his sister like football? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The text doesn't say

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Jake**  
2. **a) Every Saturday**  
3. **a) On Sundays**  
4. **a) Yoga**  
5. **a) No**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. What hobbies does his sister have? → a) Reading and painting · b) Football and tennis · c) Swimming  
7. What sport does his mum play? → a) Tennis · b) Football · c) Yoga  
8. How often does his mum play tennis? → a) Twice a week · b) Once a week · c) Every day  
9. Where does Jake go swimming? → a) At the pool · b) In the sea · c) At home  
10. Who does Jake play football with? → a) With his team · b) With his sister · c) Alone

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Reading and painting**  
7. **a) Tennis**  
8. **a) Twice a week**  
9. **a) At the pool**  
10. **a) With his team**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text says they are ____. → a) an active family · b) a big family · c) a quiet family  
12. Which word means "equipo"? → a) Team · b) Pool · c) Week  
13. What is the main topic? → a) Sports and hobbies of the family · b) Jake's school · c) Food  
14. Correct: Jake ____ football. → a) plays · b) goes · c) does  
15. Correct: His sister ____ yoga. → a) does · b) plays · c) goes

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) an active family**  
12. **a) Team**  
13. **a) Sports and hobbies of the family**  
14. **a) plays**  
15. **a) does**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Free time

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo sobre tiempo libre.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-32/listening-free-time.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Free time"></audio>

> **A:** What do you do in your free time?  
> **B:** I play football and tennis. I go swimming once a week.  
> **A:** Do you do any exercise?  
> **B:** Yes, I do yoga on Tuesdays and Thursdays.  
> **A:** What about hobbies?  
> **B:** I like reading and painting. I don't have much time but I try to read before bed.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What sports does B play? → a) Football and tennis · b) Swimming only · c) Yoga only  
2. How often does B go swimming? → a) Once a week · b) Twice a week · c) Every day  
3. Does B do yoga? → a) Yes, on Tuesdays and Thursdays · b) No · c) Only on Sundays  
4. What hobbies does B have? → a) Reading and painting · b) Football · c) Swimming  
5. When does B try to read? → a) Before bed · b) In the morning · c) After lunch

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Football and tennis**  
2. **a) Once a week**  
3. **a) Yes, on Tuesdays and Thursdays**  
4. **a) Reading and painting**  
5. **a) Before bed**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Does B have much time for hobbies? → a) No, not much · b) Yes, a lot · c) The audio doesn't say  
7. A asks about ____. → a) free time, exercise, hobbies · b) work · c) family  
8. Correct: B ____ football. → a) plays · b) goes · c) does  
9. Correct: B ____ swimming. → a) goes · b) plays · c) does  
10. Correct: B ____ yoga. → a) does · b) plays · c) goes

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

12. The dialogue is ____. → a) about free time and activities · b) about work · c) about food  
13. B ____ reading and painting. → a) likes · b) like · c) liking

## Lección 5 — Writing: My sports and hobbies

**Objetivo:** escribir con *play / go / do* y *like + -ing*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ football on Saturdays. → *play / go / do*  
2. She ____ swimming every week. → *goes / plays / does*  
3. We ____ yoga in the morning. → *do / play / go*  
4. He ____ tennis twice a week. → *plays / goes / does*  
5. I ____ reading and painting. → *like / play / go*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **play**  
2. **goes**  
3. **do**  
4. **plays**  
5. **like**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. I / play / football / and / go / swimming.  
7. She / does / yoga / every / morning.  
8. They / like / reading / and / painting.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I play football and go swimming.  
7. She does yoga every morning.  
8. They like reading and painting.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. What sport do you do? (*play / go / do*)  
10. A hobby you like (*reading, painting…*).  
11. One *play* + one *go* or *do*.  
12. *What do you do in your free time?* + your answer.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I play football every Saturday.  
10. I like reading and painting.  
11. I play tennis. I go swimming once a week.  
12. What do you do in your free time? I play football and go swimming.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Short paragraph (4–5 sentences): your sports and hobbies.  
14. 3 sentences: *play…*, *go…*, *do…*.  
15. What a family member does (*play/go/do* or *like*).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
I play football on Saturdays. I go swimming once a week. I do yoga sometimes. I like reading before bed.

**14.**  
I play tennis. I go swimming. I do yoga.

**15.**  
My sister does yoga and likes reading.

</details>
