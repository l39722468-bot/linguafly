## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 56 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Pedir con ***I would like…*** / ***I'd like…*** (más educado que *I want*).
- Usar ***Can I have… please?***
- Decir ***I would like to order…*** / ***We would like to see the menu***.
- Nombrar ***menu, starter, main course, dessert, bill, waiter***.
- Seguir el orden de platos y pedir la **cuenta**.

---

## 1. Pedidos educados: *I would like* / *Can I have*

| Fórmula | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **I would like** + noun | pedir plato/bebida | I would like the soup please. |
| **I'd like** + noun | forma corta | I'd like the chicken please. |
| **I would like to** + verb | intención | I would like **to** order a starter. |
| **We would like to** + verb | plural | We would like **to** see the menu. |
| **Can I have** … **please?** | petición | Can I have the bill please? |

> I would like the soup please.  
> I would like the chicken please.  
> Can I have the menu please?  
> Can I have the bill please?

## 2. *Would like to* + verbo

| Frase | Significado |
| :--- | :--- |
| I would like **to order** a starter. | Quisiera pedir un entrante. |
| We would like **to see** the menu. | Quisiéramos ver la carta. |
| For dessert I would like ice cream. | De postre quisiera helado. |

> We would like to see the menu.

## 3. Vocabulario: menú y platos

| Palabra | Significado | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **menu** | carta | Can I see the menu please? |
| **starter** | entrante | For starter I would like soup. |
| **main course** | plato principal | For main course I order chicken. |
| **dessert** | postre | For dessert I would like ice cream. |
| **bill** | cuenta | Can I have the bill please? |
| **waiter** | camarero | The waiter brings the menu. |
| **table** | mesa | We sit at the table. |
| **order** | pedir / pedido | Are you ready to order? |

Orden típico: **starter → main course → dessert** → **bill**.

> For starter I would like soup.  
> For main course I would like the fish please.  
> Water please.  
> No thank you. (si no quieres postre)

## 7. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I like the soup please. (pedido) | I **would** like the soup please. | *would like* = pedido educado |
| Can I to have the bill? | Can I **have** the bill? | *can* + verbo base |
| I would like order a starter. | I would like **to** order… | falta *to* |
| the dessert for starter | For **starter**… / For **dessert**… | platos distintos |
| Give me the bill. (brusco) | Can I have the bill **please**? | más educado en A1 |

---

## 8. Mini prácticas orales

1. Pide la carta y un entrante.  
2. Pide plato principal + bebida.  
3. Acepta o rechaza postre.  
4. Pide la cuenta.

> We would like to see the menu.  
> For starter I would like soup.  
> I would like the chicken please.  
> Can I have the bill please?

## 9. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Completa

1. I \_\_\_\_ like the soup please.  
2. Can I \_\_\_\_ the bill please?  
3. I would like \_\_\_\_ order a starter.  
4. We \_\_\_\_ like to see the menu.  
5. For dessert I \_\_\_\_ like ice cream.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **would** · 2. **have** · 3. **to** · 4. **would** · 5. **would**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Vocabulario

1. List of food and drinks = \_\_\_\_  
2. First course = \_\_\_\_  
3. Main dish = \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_  
4. Sweet at the end = \_\_\_\_  
5. What you pay = \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **menu** · 2. **starter** · 3. **main course** · 4. **dessert** · 5. **bill**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Orden de la comida

Ordena: dessert · starter · bill · main course · menu

1. \_\_\_\_ (primero miras)  
2. \_\_\_\_  
3. \_\_\_\_  
4. \_\_\_\_  
5. \_\_\_\_ (al final pagas)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **menu** · 2. **starter** · 3. **main course** · 4. **dessert** · 5. **bill**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Empareja

1. I would like…  
2. Can I have the bill?  
3. starter  
4. dessert  
5. waiter  
6. Are you ready to order?  

a) postre · b) pedir educado · c) camarero · d) cuenta · e) entrante · f) pregunta del camarero

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **d** · 3 → **e** · 4 → **a** · 5 → **c** · 6 → **f**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrige

1. *I want the soup.* (hazlo más educado)  
2. *Can I to have the menu?*  
3. *I would like order chicken.*  
4. *Give me the bill.* (más educado)  
5. *For starter I would like ice cream.* (¿plato correcto?)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **would like** the soup please.  
2. Can I **have** the menu?  
3. I would like **to** order chicken.  
4. Can I have the bill **please**?  
5. Ice cream es **dessert**, no starter → For **dessert** I would like ice cream.
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa el diálogo

Waiter: Are you ready to \_\_\_\_?  
Customer: Yes. I would \_\_\_\_ the soup for \_\_\_\_ please.  
Waiter: And for \_\_\_\_ course?  
Customer: I would like the \_\_\_\_ please.  
Waiter: Anything to \_\_\_\_?  
Customer: Water please.  
Waiter: Dessert?  
Customer: No thank you. Can I have the \_\_\_\_ please?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**order** · **like** · **starter** · **main** · **fish** · **drink** · **bill**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Completa el texto

We are at a restaurant tonight. The \_\_\_\_ brings the \_\_\_\_. I would like soup for \_\_\_\_. For \_\_\_\_ course I order chicken and rice. After dinner we would like \_\_\_\_. Then we ask for the \_\_\_\_ and pay.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**waiter** · **menu** · **starter** · **main** · **dessert** · **bill**
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Elige la correcta

1. Polite order: ____  
   a) Give me chicken · b) I would like the chicken please · c) I want chicken now  
2. After *would like* to express action: ____  
   a) to + verb · b) verb-ing · c) only noun always  
3. To pay you ask for: ____  
   a) the menu · b) the bill · c) the starter  
4. Order of courses: ____  
   a) dessert, main, starter · b) starter, main course, dessert · c) main, dessert, starter

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b** · 2. **a** · 3. **b** · 4. **b**
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Traduce

1. Quisiera la sopa, por favor.  
2. ¿Puedo ver la carta?  
3. De entrante quisiera ensalada.  
4. De plato principal quisiera pollo.  
5. ¿Puedo tener la cuenta, por favor?  
6. No, gracias. (al postre)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I would like the soup please.  
2. Can I see the menu please? / Can I have the menu please?  
3. For starter I would like salad.  
4. For main course I would like chicken.  
5. Can I have the bill please?  
6. No thank you.
</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Escribe tu pedido

Escribe 8–10 frases de una cena. Incluye:

- *menu* / *waiter*  
- 1 × *I would like…* (starter)  
- 1 × *I would like…* (main course)  
- bebida  
- postre o *No thank you*  
- *Can I have the bill please?*

**Modelo:** el diálogo de la sección 6.

## 10. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Repasa fórmulas educadas y el orden de platos.  
2. **8 min** — Haz los ejercicios 1–5.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha y repite el diálogo completo.  
4. **5–8 min** — Representa un pedido en voz alta (tú = cliente y camarero).

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *I would like… please*  
- [ ] Uso *Can I have… please?*  
- [ ] Distingo starter / main course / dessert  
- [ ] Pido *menu* y *bill*  
- [ ] Respondo a *Anything to drink?* / *Dessert?*

---

## 11. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Prioriza ***would like*** sobre *want* en restaurante.  
2. Memoriza el bloque: *menu → starter → main → dessert → bill*.  
3. Practica el diálogo completo hasta fluidez.  
4. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 56](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-56-restaurante-pedidos-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 56 del curso A1](/curso-a1/unit-56).

La **Unidad 57** entra en la cocina: ***First, Then, Finally*** y verbos *cut, mix, fry, boil, bake*.
