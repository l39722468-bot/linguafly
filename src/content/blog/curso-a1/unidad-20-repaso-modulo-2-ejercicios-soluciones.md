## Lección 1 — Gramática: Present Simple, tiempo y días

**Objetivo:** repasar afirmativa, *don't/doesn't*, *Do/Does*, *in/on/at* y *like + -ing*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ at seven. → *get up / gets up / getting up*  
2. She ____ breakfast at eight. → *have / has / having*  
3. They ____ like cooking. → *don't / doesn't / not*  
4. What ____ you do on Monday? → *do / does / are*  
5. He ____ like reading. He prefers films. → *doesn't / don't / not*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **get up** — *I* + forma base.  
2. **has** — 3.ª persona.  
3. **don't** — *they don't like*.  
4. **do** — *What do you do?*  
5. **doesn't** — *he doesn't like*.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. I ____ coffee and toast for breakfast. → a) have · b) has · c) having  
7. We ____ dinner at half past eight. → a) have · b) has · c) haves  
8. ____ she like music? → a) Does · b) Do · c) Is  
9. My birthday is ____ the 10th of May. → a) on · b) in · c) at  
10. I ____ swimming. Why? Because it's fun. → a) like · b) likes · c) to like

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) have**  
7. **a) have**  
8. **a) Does**  
9. **a) on** — *on* + fecha.  
10. **a) like**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. I / get up / at / seven / o'clock.  
12. She / doesn't / like / getting up / early.  
13. Can / I / have / a / coffee, / please?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I get up at seven o'clock.  
12. She doesn't like getting up early.  
13. Can I have a coffee, please?

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. Present Simple is used for routines and habits. → ?  
15. We use "on" for dates and days (on Monday, on the 5th). → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero** — *on* + día o fecha.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: rutinas, comidas, gustos, fechas

**Objetivo:** integrar vocabulario del Módulo 2.

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige la opción correcta

1. I have ____ at seven. → a) breakfast · b) lunch · c) dinner  
2. Half past eight = ____. → a) 8:30 · b) 8:15 · c) 8:45  
3. On ____ I don't work. → a) Saturday · b) Monday · c) Tuesday  
4. Can I ____ a tea, please? → a) have · b) has · c) having  
5. I ____ reading. Why? Because it's relaxing. → a) like · b) likes · c) to like  
6. My ____ is on the 15th of March. → a) birthday · b) day · c) week

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) breakfast**  
2. **a) 8:30**  
3. **a) Saturday**  
4. **a) have**  
5. **a) like**  
6. **a) birthday**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–8 — Relaciona

**7.** Comida / día → momento  

| Inglés | Momento |
|--------|---------|
| breakfast | ? |
| lunch | ? |
| dinner | ? |
| Monday | ? |
| Saturday | ? |

**8.** Estructura → uso  

| Estructura | Uso |
|------------|-----|
| I like + -ing | ? |
| in + month | ? |
| on + date | ? |
| at + time | ? |
| Can I have…? | ? |

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**7.** breakfast → morning · lunch → midday · dinner → evening · Monday → first day of week · Saturday → weekend  

**8.** I like + -ing → hobbies · in + month → January · on + date → the 5th of May · at + time → seven o'clock · Can I have…? → cafe request

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Completa

9. What ____ you do on Sunday? → *do / does / are*  
10. I'd ____ a sandwich. → *like / would / want*  
11. We have ____ in August. → *holiday / work / school*  
12. ____ do you like it? Because it's fun. → a) Why · b) Because · c) What

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. **do**  
10. **like** — *I'd like…*  
11. **holiday**  
12. **a) Why**

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Contexto

13. When is your birthday? → a) It's on the 10th of May. · b) I like cake. · c) At eight.  
14. She ____ lunch at one o'clock. → a) has · b) have · c) having  
15. Module 2 includes ____. → a) routines, time, meals, likes, dates · b) only grammar · c) only vocabulary

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

13. **a) It's on the 10th of May.**  
14. **a) has**  
15. **a) routines, time, meals, likes, dates**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Sam's day

**Objetivo:** leer un texto integrado (rutina + gustos + fecha).

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-20/reading-sam.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Sam"></audio>

> My name is Sam. I get up at seven o'clock on weekdays. I have breakfast at half past seven: coffee and toast. I don't like getting up early, but I start work at nine. On Saturday I sleep until nine. I like reading and listening to music. My birthday is on the 22nd of June. I usually have a small party with friends. We have dinner together at eight.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the writer's name? → a) Sam · b) Tom · c) Ben  
2. What time does he get up on weekdays? → a) At seven o'clock · b) At half past seven · c) At nine  
3. What does he have for breakfast? → a) Coffee and toast · b) Tea and eggs · c) Juice and fruit  
4. Does he like getting up early? → a) No · b) Yes · c) The text doesn't say  
5. What time does he start work? → a) At nine · b) At seven · c) At eight

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Sam**  
2. **a) At seven o'clock**  
3. **a) Coffee and toast**  
4. **a) No**  
5. **a) At nine**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. On ____ he sleeps until nine. → a) Saturday · b) Monday · c) Sunday  
7. What does he like doing? → a) Reading and listening to music · b) Only watching films · c) Only cooking  
8. When is his birthday? → a) On the 22nd of June · b) On the 22nd of July · c) In June  
9. What does he do on his birthday? → a) He has a small party with friends · b) He works · c) The text doesn't say  
10. What time do they have dinner together? → a) At eight · b) At seven · c) At nine

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Saturday**  
7. **a) Reading and listening to music**  
8. **a) On the 22nd of June**  
9. **a) He has a small party with friends**  
10. **a) At eight**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text talks about ____. → a) routine, likes and birthday · b) only work · c) only food  
12. He uses ____ for breakfast time. → a) at + time (at half past seven) · b) on + day · c) in + month  
13. Which day does he mention for sleeping late? → a) Saturday · b) Sunday · c) Friday  
14. Sam says "I don't like" ____. → a) getting up early · b) reading · c) music  
15. This text is ____ of Module 2. → a) a review (routine + likes + dates) · b) only grammar · c) only vocabulary

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) routine, likes and birthday**  
12. **a) at + time (at half past seven)**  
13. **a) Saturday**  
14. **a) getting up early**  
15. **a) a review (routine + likes + dates)**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Maya's Monday

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo integrado del Módulo 2.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-20/listening-maya.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Maya"></audio>

> **A:** Hi Maya. What do you do on Monday morning?  
> **B:** I get up at seven. I have breakfast at half past seven. I like coffee and fruit.  
> **A:** Do you like cooking?  
> **B:** Yes, I do. I have lunch at one at home and I cook sometimes.  
> **A:** When is your birthday?  
> **B:** It's on the 3rd of December. I usually have dinner with my family at eight.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What day does A mention first? → a) Monday · b) Tuesday · c) Saturday  
2. What time does B get up? → a) At seven · b) At half past seven · c) At eight  
3. What does B have for breakfast? → a) Coffee and fruit · b) Tea and toast · c) Juice only  
4. Does B like cooking? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
5. What time does she have lunch? → a) At one · b) At twelve · c) At two

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Monday**  
2. **a) At seven**  
3. **a) Coffee and fruit**  
4. **a) Yes**  
5. **a) At one**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. When is B's birthday? → a) On the 3rd of December · b) On the 13th of December · c) In December  
7. What does she have dinner with on her birthday? → a) With her family · b) With friends · c) Alone  
8. What time does she have dinner? → a) At eight · b) At seven · c) At nine  
9. The dialogue includes ____. → a) routine, meals, likes, birthday · b) only time · c) only food  
10. B says she cooks ____. → a) sometimes · b) every day · c) never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) On the 3rd of December**  
7. **a) With her family**  
8. **a) At eight**  
9. **a) routine, meals, likes, birthday**  
10. **a) sometimes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. Where does she have lunch? → a) At home · b) At work · c) In a cafe  
12. Which question does A ask about birthday? → a) When is your birthday? · b) What do you do? · c) Do you like cooking?  
13. B uses ____ for breakfast. → a) like + coffee and fruit · b) don't like · c) hate  
14. Her birthday is ____. → a) in December · b) in June · c) in March  
15. This is ____ of Module 2. → a) an integrated review · b) only listening · c) only grammar

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) At home**  
12. **a) When is your birthday?**

## Lección 5 — Writing: My day and birthday

**Objetivo:** escribir un texto integrado del Módulo 2.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ up at seven. → *get / gets / getting*  
2. She ____ breakfast at half past eight. → *has / have / having*  
3. I ____ reading. → *like / likes / to like*  
4. My birthday is ____ the 5th of June. → *on / in / at*  
5. Can ____ have a tea? → *I / you / we*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **get**  
2. **has**  
3. **like**  
4. **on**  
5. **I** — *Can I have…?*

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. I / get up / at / seven / o'clock.  
7. I / like / reading / and / listening to / music.  
8. My / birthday / is / on / the / 15th / of / March.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I get up at seven o'clock.  
7. I like reading and listening to music.  
8. My birthday is on the 15th of March.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. Your morning routine: get up, breakfast, one activity.  
10. Two things you like + one you don't like, with *because*.  
11. *When is your birthday?* + answer + what you do that day.  
12. A cafe request (*Can I have…?* or *I'd like…*).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I get up at seven o'clock. I have breakfast at half past seven. I have coffee and toast. Then I read the news.  
10. I like reading because it's relaxing. I like swimming because it's fun. I don't like getting up early because I'm tired.  
11. When is your birthday? My birthday is on the 10th of May. I usually have a party with my family.  
12. Can I have a coffee, please? / I'd like a tea and a sandwich, please.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Integrated paragraph (5–6 sentences): routine, meals, a like, birthday.  
14. *What do you do on Saturday?* + 2–3 sentences.  
15. *She gets up at seven. She has breakfast at half past seven. She likes coffee.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
I get up at seven and have breakfast at half past seven. I have lunch at two. I like reading in the evening. My birthday is on the 15th of March. I usually have dinner with my family on that day.

**14.**  
What do you do on Saturday? I get up late. I have breakfast and then I meet my friends.

**15.**  
She gets up at seven o'clock. She has breakfast at half past seven. She likes coffee.

</details>
