## Lección 1 — Gramática: *in* / *on* y *When is your birthday?*

**Objetivo:** *in* + mes, *on* + fecha/día y preguntar el cumpleaños.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. My birthday is ____ January. → *in / on / at*  
2. I was born ____ the 15th of March. → *on / in / at*  
3. ____ is your birthday? → *When / What / Where*  
4. It's ____ the 3rd of July. → *on / in / at*  
5. We have holiday ____ August. → *in / on / at*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **in** — *in* + mes.  
2. **on** — *on* + fecha.  
3. **When** — *When is your birthday?*  
4. **on** — *on the 3rd of July*.  
5. **in** — *in* + mes.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. Christmas is ____ December. → a) in · b) on · c) at  
7. Her birthday is ____ the 21st of May. → a) on · b) in · c) at  
8. ____ is his birthday? It's on the 1st of June. → a) When · b) What · c) Where  
9. The meeting is ____ Monday. → a) on · b) in · c) at  
10. New Year is ____ January. → a) in · b) on · c) at

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) in**  
7. **a) on**  
8. **a) When**  
9. **a) on** — *on* + día de la semana.  
10. **a) in**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. When / is / your / birthday?  
12. My / birthday / is / on / the / 5th / of / April.  
13. I / was born / in / February.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. When is your birthday?  
12. My birthday is on the 5th of April.  
13. I was born in February.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. We use "in" for months (in January). → ?  
15. We use "on" for dates (on the 10th). → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero** — *in* + mes.  
15. **Verdadero** — *on* + fecha.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: meses y ordinales

**Objetivo:** los 12 meses y ordinales (*1st–31st*).

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige la opción correcta

1. The first month of the year is ____. → a) January · b) February · c) December  
2. Valentine's Day is in ____. → a) February · b) March · c) April  
3. 1st = ____. → a) first · b) one · c) oneth  
4. 2nd = ____. → a) second · b) two · c) twoth  
5. 3rd = ____. → a) third · b) three · c) threeth  
6. 21st = ____. → a) twenty-first · b) twenty-one · c) twenty-oneth

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) January**  
2. **a) February**  
3. **a) first**  
4. **a) second**  
5. **a) third**  
6. **a) twenty-first**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–8 — Relaciona

**7.** Número → ordinal  

| Número | Ordinal |
|--------|---------|
| 1st | ? |
| 2nd | ? |
| 3rd | ? |
| 4th | ? |
| 5th | ? |

**8.** Mes → posición  

| Mes | Posición |
|-----|----------|
| January | ? |
| June | ? |
| September | ? |
| December | ? |
| July | ? |

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**7.** 1st → first · 2nd → second · 3rd → third · 4th → fourth · 5th → fifth  

**8.** January → 1st month · June → 6th month · September → 9th month · December → 12th month · July → 7th month

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Completa

9. the ____ of March (10) → *10th / tenth / ten*  
10. My birthday is on the ____ of August. (31) → *31st / thirty-first / thirty-one*  
11. Halloween is in ____. (31st) → *October / November / September*  
12. Easter is often ____ March or ____. → a) in / April · b) on / May · c) at / June

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. **10th**  
10. **31st**  
11. **October**  
12. **a) in / April**

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Contexto

13. How do we write the date in English? → a) the 15th of May / May 15th · b) 15 May · c) May 15  
14. November is ____ month. → a) the 11th · b) the 9th · c) the 10th  
15. When is your ____? It's on the 20th of May. → a) birthday · b) name · c) day

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

13. **a) the 15th of May / May 15th**  
14. **a) the 11th**  
15. **a) birthday**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Lucy's family birthdays

**Objetivo:** Leer sobre cumpleaños y fechas en una familia.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-19/reading-lucy.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Lucy"></audio>

> Hi. I'm Lucy. My birthday is on the 12th of March. I was born in 1995. My brother Tom was born on the 3rd of July. His birthday is in summer. We have a party for him every year. My mum's birthday is in December, on the 25th. Yes, Christmas Day! So we celebrate two things on that day.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the writer's name? → a) Lucy · b) Tom · c) Mum  
2. When is Lucy's birthday? → a) On the 12th of March · b) On the 3rd of July · c) On the 25th of December  
3. When was Tom born? → a) On the 3rd of July · b) On the 12th of March · c) In December  
4. Tom's birthday is ____. → a) in summer · b) in winter · c) in spring  
5. When is mum's birthday? → a) On the 25th of December · b) On the 12th of March · c) On the 3rd of July

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Lucy**  
2. **a) On the 12th of March**  
3. **a) On the 3rd of July**  
4. **a) in summer**  
5. **a) On the 25th of December**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Mum's birthday is on Christmas Day. → a) True · b) False  
7. What do they do for Tom every year? → a) They have a party · b) They go to school · c) The text doesn't say  
8. Lucy was born ____. → a) in 1995 · b) in March · c) on the 12th  
9. On the 25th of December they ____. → a) celebrate Christmas and mum's birthday · b) only celebrate Christmas · c) have a party for Tom  
10. The text uses ____ for months. → a) in · b) on · c) at

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) True**  
7. **a) They have a party**  
8. **a) in 1995** — *I was born in 1995.*  
9. **a) celebrate Christmas and mum's birthday**  
10. **a) in**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. The text uses ____ for dates (12th, 3rd, 25th). → a) on the … of · b) in the · c) at the  
12. How many people are mentioned with birthdays? → a) Three (Lucy, Tom, mum) · b) Two · c) One  
13. Main topic: → a) Birthdays and dates in a family · b) Christmas · c) Parties  
14. Which date is written with ordinal 12th? → a) Lucy's birthday · b) Tom's birthday · c) Mum's birthday  
15. The phrase "When is your birthday?" is ____. → a) a question to ask someone's birthday · b) an answer · c) a month

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) on the … of**  
12. **a) Three (Lucy, Tom, mum)**  
13. **a) Birthdays and dates in a family**  
14. **a) Lucy's birthday**  
15. **a) a question to ask someone's birthday**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Jake and A's birthdays

**Objetivo:** Escuchar un diálogo sobre cumpleaños y fechas.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-19/listening-birthdays.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Birthdays"></audio>

> **A:** When is your birthday, Jake?  
> **B:** It's on the 8th of November.  
> **A:** Oh, so in autumn!  
> **B:** Yes. And you?  
> **A:** My birthday is on the 22nd of June.  
> **B:** Nice, summer! Do you have a party?  
> **A:** Sometimes. Last year I had a party on the 21st because the 22nd was Monday.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. When is Jake's birthday? → a) On the 8th of November · b) On the 22nd of June · c) On the 21st  
2. November is ____. → a) autumn · b) summer · c) winter  
3. When is A's birthday? → a) On the 22nd of June · b) On the 8th of November · c) On the 21st  
4. June is ____. → a) summer · b) autumn · c) spring  
5. Does A have a party every year? → a) Sometimes · b) Always · c) Never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) On the 8th of November**  
2. **a) autumn**  
3. **a) On the 22nd of June**  
4. **a) summer**  
5. **a) Sometimes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. Last year A had the party ____. → a) on the 21st (not the 22nd) · b) on the 22nd · c) in July  
7. Why did they have the party on the 21st? → a) Because the 22nd was Monday · b) Because Jake couldn't come · c) The audio doesn't say  
8. The question "When is your birthday?" is ____. → a) used to ask for a date · b) used to ask the time · c) used to ask the place  
9. Dates are said with ____. → a) on the + ordinal + of + month · b) in the + number · c) at + day  
10. 8th is read ____. → a) eighth · b) eight · c) eightth

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) on the 21st (not the 22nd)**  
7. **a) Because the 22nd was Monday**  
8. **a) used to ask for a date**  
9. **a) on the + ordinal + of + month**  
10. **a) eighth**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. 22nd is read ____. → a) twenty-second · b) twenty-two · c) twentieth-second  
12. Main topic: → a) Birthdays and dates · b) Weather · c) Work  
13. Which question does A ask first? → a) When is your birthday? · b) Do you have a party? · c) What day is it?  
14. A had a ____ last year. → a) party on the 21st · b) party on the 22nd · c) meeting  
15. The dialogue is ____. → a) about birthdays and when they are · b) about work · c) about school

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) twenty-second**  
12. **a) Birthdays and dates**  
13. **a) When is your birthday?**  
14. **a) party on the 21st**  
15. **a) about birthdays and when they are**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My birthday

**Objetivo:** Escribir fechas, cumpleaños y *I was born…*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. My birthday is ____ January. → *in / on / at*  
2. It's ____ the 5th of April. → *on / in / at*  
3. ____ is your birthday? → *When / What / Where*  
4. I was born ____ the 12th of June. → *on / in / at*  
5. Christmas is ____ December. → *in / on / at*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **in**  
2. **on**  
3. **When**  
4. **on**  
5. **in**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. When / is / your / birthday?  
7. My / birthday / is / on / the / 10th / of / May.  
8. I / was born / in / February.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. When is your birthday?  
7. My birthday is on the 10th of May.  
8. I was born in February.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. When is your birthday? (date + month).  
10. *When is your birthday?* + your answer.  
11. Three important dates: one with *in* (month) and two with *on* (date).  
12. Write: *My birthday is on the 3rd of July.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. My birthday is on the 10th of May.  
10. When is your birthday? My birthday is on the 22nd of June.  
11. Christmas is in December. My birthday is on the 5th of April. The meeting is on the 20th of March.  
12. My birthday is on the 3rd of July.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Four sentences: your birthday, a family/friend birthday, a party in a month.  
14. Write the ordinals in words: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 21st.  
15. *Christmas is in December. New Year is on the 1st of January.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
My birthday is on the 15th of March. My brother's birthday is in July. We have a party in August. Christmas is in December.

**14.**  
first, second, third, twenty-first

**15.**  
Christmas is in December. New Year is on the 1st of January.

</details>
