## Lección 1 — Gramática: *doesn't* y *Does he/she…?*

**Objetivo:** Negativa con *doesn't*, preguntas *Does he/she/it…?* y *-s/-es/-ies* en 3.ª persona.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. He ____ like getting up early. → *don't / doesn't / not*  
2. She ____ to work at nine. → *go / goes / going*  
3. ____ he play football? → *Do / Does / Is*  
4. Does she cook? No, she ____. → *does / doesn't / don't*  
5. My dog ____ meat. It likes it. → *eats / eat / eating*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **doesn't** — *he/she/it* + *doesn't*.  
2. **goes** — *she* + *-es* (*go → goes*).  
3. **Does** — *Does he…?*  
4. **doesn't** — *No, she doesn't.*  
5. **eats** — *it* + *-s*.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige la opción correcta

6. She ____ TV every night. → a) watch · b) watches · c) watching  
7. He ____ study English. He works in an office. → a) don't · b) doesn't · c) not  
8. ____ the cat like milk? Yes, it does. → a) Do · b) Does · c) Is  
9. Maria ____ her homework after dinner. → a) do · b) does · c) doing  
10. The ____ flies in the sky. → a) bird · b) birds · c) dog

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **b) watches** — *-ch* → *-es*  
7. **b) doesn't**  
8. **b) Does**  
9. **b) does**  
10. **a) bird** — *The bird flies* (*fly → flies*)

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena las palabras

11. He / doesn't / like / getting up early.  
12. Does / she / work / on / Saturdays?  
13. My / brother / goes / to bed / at / eleven.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. He doesn't like getting up early.  
12. Does she work on Saturdays?  
13. My brother goes to bed at eleven.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. With he, she, it we use "doesn't" for the negative. → ?  
15. After "Does" we use the verb in infinitive (Does he play?). → ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **Verdadero**  
15. **Verdadero** — *Does he play?* (no *plays*).

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: rutinas y animales

**Objetivo:** Rutinas en 3.ª persona, hábitos de animales y profesiones.

### Ejercicios 1–6 — Elige la opción correcta

1. She ____ at seven every morning. → a) wakes up · b) wake up · c) waking up  
2. The ____ sleeps in the day and hunts at night. → a) cat · b) owl · c) dog  
3. He ____ reports every week. → a) writes · b) write · c) writing  
4. The ____ barks when it sees a stranger. → a) dog · b) cat · c) bird  
5. My ____ starts work at eight. She is a nurse. → a) sister · b) brother · c) father  
6. The ____ doesn't eat meat. It eats grass. → a) cat · b) cow · c) dog

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) wakes up**  
2. **b) owl**  
3. **a) writes**  
4. **a) dog**  
5. **a) sister**  
6. **b) cow**

</details>

### Ejercicios 7–10 — Relaciona

**7.** Animal → acción  

| Animal | Acción |
|--------|--------|
| dog | ? |
| cat | ? |
| bird | ? |
| fish | ? |
| horse | ? |

**8.** Profesión → rutina  

| Profesión | Acción |
|-----------|--------|
| teacher | ? |
| doctor | ? |
| nurse | ? |
| engineer | ? |
| chef | ? |

**9.** Pregunta → respuesta  

| Pregunta | Respuesta |
|----------|-----------|
| Does he get up early? | ? |
| Does she work on Sunday? | ? |
| Does the cat like milk? | ? |
| Does your brother study? | ? |
| Does Maria cook? | ? |

**10.** 3.ª persona → infinitivo  

| 3.ª persona | Infinitivo |
|-------------|------------|
| goes | ? |
| watches | ? |
| studies | ? |
| has | ? |
| finishes | ? |

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**7.** dog → barks · cat → meows · bird → flies · fish → swims · horse → runs  

**8.** teacher → teaches · doctor → helps patients · nurse → works in a hospital · engineer → designs · chef → cooks  

**9.** Does he get up early? → Yes, he does. · Does she work on Sunday? → No, she doesn't. · Does the cat like milk? → Yes, it does. · Does your brother study? → Yes, he does. · Does Maria cook? → No, she doesn't.  

**10.** goes → go · watches → watch · studies → study · has → have · finishes → finish

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Completa

11. She ____ breakfast at eight. → *have / has / having*  
12. He ____ like cooking. He eats in restaurants. → *don't / doesn't / not*  
13. ____ your mother work? Yes, she does. → *Do / Does / Is*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **has**  
12. **doesn't**  
13. **Does**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Contexto

14. You want to describe your sister's routine. You ____.  
    a) She gets up at seven. She has breakfast. She goes to work. · b) I get up at seven. · c) Do you get up early?  
15. You want to ask if your brother plays tennis. You ____.  
    a) Does your brother play tennis? · b) Do you play tennis? · c) He plays tennis.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **a) She gets up at seven. She has breakfast. She goes to work.**  
15. **a) Does your brother play tennis?**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Tom's routine

**Objetivo:** Leer la rutina de Tom en 3.ª persona (*he / doesn't*).

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-15/reading-tom.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Tom"></audio>

> Tom is my brother. He gets up at six o'clock every morning. He doesn't like getting up late. He has breakfast at half past six and leaves for work at seven. He works in a hospital. He is a doctor. He doesn't work on Sundays. On Sunday he stays at home and reads or watches TV. He goes to bed at ten.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Who is Tom? → a) The writer's brother · b) A doctor · c) A teacher  
2. What time does he get up? → a) At six · b) At seven · c) At eight  
3. Does he like getting up late? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say  
4. Where does he work? → a) In a hospital · b) In a school · c) In an office  
5. What ____ is he? → a) job · b) name · c) time

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) The writer's brother** (también es doctor, pero la pregunta pide quién es)  
2. **a) At six**  
3. **b) No**  
4. **a) In a hospital**  
5. **a) job** — *He is a doctor.*

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–9 — Comprensión

6. He works on Sunday. → a) True · b) False  
7. On Sunday he ____ at home. → a) stays · b) stay · c) staying  
8. What does he do on Sunday? → a) He reads or watches TV. · b) He works. · c) He gets up at six.  
9. What time does he go to bed? → a) At nine · b) At ten · c) At eleven

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **b) False**  
7. **a) stays**  
8. **a) He reads or watches TV.**  
9. **b) At ten**

</details>

### Ejercicios 10–15 — Detalles

10. Main topic:  
    a) Tom's daily routine · b) A hospital · c) Food  
11. He ____ for work at seven. → a) leaves · b) leave · c) leaving  
12. Does he have breakfast at seven? → a) No, at half past six · b) Yes · c) The text doesn't say  
13. The text uses ____ to describe Tom. → a) he + verb with -s · b) I + verb · c) you + verb  
14. What does he do at home on Sunday? → a) He reads or watches TV. · b) He works. · c) He has breakfast.  
15. How many days does he work a week? → a) Six · b) Seven · c) Five

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

10. **a) Tom's daily routine**  
11. **a) leaves**  
12. **a) No, at half past six**  
13. **a) he + verb with -s**  
14. **a) He reads or watches TV.**  
15. **a) Six** (no trabaja los domingos)

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Ben's sister

**Objetivo:** Escuchar un diálogo sobre la rutina de otra persona (*Does she…?*).

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-15/listening-sister.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Ben's sister"></audio>

> **Anna:** Tell me about your sister. What does she do?  
> **Ben:** She is a teacher. She works in a primary school.  
> **Anna:** Does she get up early?  
> **Ben:** Yes, she does. She gets up at six because she starts work at eight. She doesn't like being late.  
> **Anna:** Does she work on Saturdays?  
> **Ben:** No, she doesn't. She rests on the weekend.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. Who does Ben talk about? → a) His sister · b) His brother · c) His mother  
2. What does she do? → a) She is a teacher. · b) She is a doctor. · c) She is a nurse.  
3. Where does she work? → a) In a primary school · b) In a hospital · c) In an office  
4. Does she get up early? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
5. What time does she start work? → a) At seven · b) At eight · c) At nine

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) His sister**  
2. **a) She is a teacher.**  
3. **a) In a primary school**  
4. **a) Yes**  
5. **b) At eight**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. She likes being late. → a) True · b) False  
7. Does she work on Saturdays? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
8. Main topic of the dialogue:  
   a) Ben's sister: her job and routine · b) Food · c) A school  
9. She ____ up at six. → a) gets · b) get · c) getting  
10. What does she do on the weekend? → a) She rests. · b) She works. · c) She gets up at six.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **b) False**  
7. **b) No**  
8. **a) Ben's sister: her job and routine**  
9. **a) gets**  
10. **a) She rests.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. Why does she get up early? → a) Because she starts work at eight. · b) Because she likes it. · c) The audio doesn't say  
12. The dialogue uses ____ to talk about the sister. → a) she + verb -s · b) I + verb · c) you + verb  
13. Does she start work at seven? → a) No, at eight · b) Yes · c) The audio doesn't say  
14. What does she do on Saturday? → a) She rests. · b) She works. · c) She gets up at six.  
15. "Does she get up early?" and "Does she work on Saturdays?" are ____ questions. → a) Yes/No with Does · b) What time · c) Where

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) Because she starts work at eight.**  
12. **a) she + verb -s**  
13. **a) No, at eight**  
14. **a) She rests.**  
15. **a) Yes/No with Does**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: Someone's routine

**Objetivo:** Escribir rutinas en 3.ª persona con *doesn't* y *Does he/she…?*

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. He ____ like coffee. → *don't / doesn't / not*  
2. She ____ to work at nine. → *go / goes / going*  
3. ____ she work on Sundays? → *Does / Do / Is*  
4. Does he play football? Yes, he ____. → *does / don't / doesn't*  
5. Does the cat ____ milk? → *like / likes / liking*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **doesn't**  
2. **goes**  
3. **Does**  
4. **does**  
5. **like** — tras *Does* → forma base.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena las palabras

6. He / doesn't / like / getting up early.  
7. Does / she / work / on / Saturdays?  
8. My / brother / goes / to bed / at / ten.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. He doesn't like getting up early.  
7. Does she work on Saturdays?  
8. My brother goes to bed at ten.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–12 — Escribe frases guiadas (ejemplos)

9. Two sentences about a relative (*he/she* + *-s*).  
10. *Does your mother work?* + your answer.  
11. Three sentences: what he/she does + one *doesn't*.  
12. *Does he get up early?* + Yes/No answer.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. My sister gets up at seven. She has breakfast at half past seven.  
10. Does your mother work? Yes, she does.  
11. He gets up at six. He has coffee for breakfast. He doesn't like tea.  
12. Does he get up early? No, he doesn't.

</details>

### Ejercicios 13–15 — Párrafos cortos (ejemplos)

13. Someone's routine (4–5 sentences) with one *doesn't*.  
14. An animal's habits (*it* + 3rd person).  
15. Two *Does he/she…?* questions with Yes and No answers.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

**13.**  
My brother gets up at seven. He has breakfast at half past seven. He goes to work at nine. He has dinner at eight. He doesn't like getting up early.

**14.**  
The cat sleeps all day. It eats in the morning. It doesn't like dogs. It plays with a ball.

**15.**  
Does she like coffee? Yes, she does. Does he work on Sundays? No, he doesn't.

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Tema | Recuerda |
|------|----------|
| Negativa | *he/she/it* + **doesn't** + verbo |
| Pregunta | **Does** + *he/she/it* + verbo…? |
| Respuestas | *Yes, he does.* / *No, she doesn't.* |
| Ortografía | *goes, watches, studies, has* |
| Atención | Tras *doesn't/Does* → verbo **sin -s** |
