En la [Unidad 31](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-31-can-habilidad) dijiste qué **puedes** hacer (*I can swim*). En la **Unidad 32 del curso A1** (*Sports & Hobbies*) aprendes a hablar de **deportes y hobbies** con la tríada ***play / go / do***.

Combina bien con *like + -ing* de la [Unidad 18](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-18-gustos-like-ing-because) y con los [días de la semana](/blog/curso-a1/unidad-12-dias-semana). Incluye **audios**, **esquemas** y **ejercicios**.

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 32 (A1)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Usar ***play*** con deportes de pelota.
- Usar ***go + -ing*** (*go swimming, go running*).
- Usar ***do*** con *yoga, exercise…*.
- Hablar de hobbies: ***painting, reading*** (+ *like*).
- Combinar con días y frecuencia: *every Saturday, twice a week*.

---

## 1. La regla *play / go / do*

| Verbo | Con qué | Ejemplos |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **play** | deportes de pelota / juegos | play football, play tennis, play basketball |
| **go** | actividades en **-ing** | go swimming, go running, go cycling |
| **do** | actividades sin pelota / “hacer” | do yoga, do exercise, do gymnastics |

![Esquema play go do A1](/blog/curso-a1/unit-32/a1-unit32-play-go-do.png)

```
play + sport (ball)
go + -ing activity
do + yoga / exercise / …
```

### Tip

No digas *play swimming* ni *go football*. Elige el verbo según el tipo de actividad.

## 8. Errores típicos de hispanohablantes

| ❌ Incorrecto | ✅ Correcto | Explicación |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *play swimming* | ***go** swimming* | -ing → go |
| *go football* | ***play** football* | pelota → play |
| *do tennis* | ***play** tennis* | pelota → play |
| *play yoga* | ***do** yoga* | yoga → do |
| *She go swimming* | *She **goes** swimming* | 3.ª persona |
| *I play the football* | *I play football* | Sin *the* (A1 general) |
| *I do painting* (A1) | *I **like** painting* | Hobby → like + -ing |

## 9. Pronunciación útil

| Forma | Tip A1 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **play / plays** | *plei* · *pleiz*. |
| **go / goes** | *gou* · *gouz*. |
| **do / does** | *duː* · *daz*. |
| **swimming** | *SWI-ming* (doble m). |
| **football** | UK *FUT-bool*. |
| **yoga** | *IOU-ga*. |

Practica el trío: *play football → go swimming → do yoga*.

### Rutina de 2 minutos

1. Di 2 frases *play…*.  
2. Di 2 frases *go …ing*.  
3. Di 2 frases *do…*.  
4. Di 2 hobbies con *like + -ing*.

Si lo unes a días (*on Monday, every Saturday*), practicas U12 + U32 a la vez.

### Combinar *can* (U31) con *play / go / do* (U32)

En la unidad anterior practicaste habilidad (*I can swim*). Aquí hablas de **hábito**: *I go swimming*. Las dos ideas van juntas en la vida real:

| Habilidad (can) | Hábito (play/go/do) |
| :--- | :--- |
| I can swim. | I go swimming on Sundays. |
| She can play tennis. | She plays tennis twice a week. |
| We can do yoga. | We do yoga in the morning. |
| He can run. | He goes running in the park. |

Modelo corto:

> I can swim very well. On Sundays I go swimming at the pool.  
> My mum can play tennis. She plays tennis twice a week.  
> My sister can paint. She likes painting and reading.

Así unes **capacidad** + **rutina** sin mezclar las formas: *can* no lleva *-s*; *plays / goes / does* sí.

### Mini-historia para reescribir

Lee y sustituye los deportes por los tuyos:

> Every Saturday I play football with my friends. On Sunday I go swimming. On Monday I do exercise at home. In the evening I like reading. My brother plays basketball at school. My dad goes cycling at the weekend. We are an active family.

Cambia *football → tennis*, *swimming → running*, *exercise → yoga*, y vuelve a decirlo en voz alta. El verbo (*play / go / do*) debe quedarse bien elegido.

### Tour guiado (modelo)

> My name is Jake. I love sport. I play football every Saturday with my team. On Sundays I go swimming at the pool. My sister does yoga every morning. She doesn’t like football but she likes reading and painting. My mum plays tennis twice a week. We are an active family.

Grábate leyéndolo. Luego improvisa con **tu** familia.

### Frases extra para practicar en voz alta

> What sports do you play?  
> I play football and tennis.  
> Do you go swimming?  
> Yes, I do — every weekend.  
> Does she do yoga?  
> Yes, she does.  
> I go running in the park.  
> We do exercise every day.  
> She likes reading and painting.  
> We’re an active family.

Personaliza con tus hobbies reales. Cuanto más concreto el día, mejor.

### Checklist mental

- Pelota → **play**  
- Actividad *-ing* (swim/run/cycle) → **go**  
- Yoga / exercise → **do**  
- Painting / reading → **like + -ing**  
- he/she → *plays / goes / does*  
- Pregunta → *What sports do you play?*

### Combina U18 + U31 + U32

| Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| Gusto | I **like** swimming. |
| Habilidad | I **can** swim. |
| Actividad | I **go** swimming on Sundays. |

Las tres juntas dan un speaking A1 completo sobre ocio.

## 11. Plan de práctica (15 minutos)

1. **3 min** — Tabla mental: 5 *play*, 5 *go*, 3 *do*.
