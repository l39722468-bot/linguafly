## Lección 1 — Gramática: repaso

**Objetivo:** *can*, *play/go/do*, frecuencia, *and/but/because*, planes (*I'm going…*).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ swim very well. → *can / cans / canning*  
2. She ____ football every Saturday. → *plays / play / playing*  
3. We ____ swimming every week. → *go / goes / going*  
4. He ____ yoga in the morning. → *does / do / doing*  
5. I ____ going camping next week. → *'m / is / are*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **can**  
2. **plays**  
3. **go**  
4. **does**  
5. **'m** (*am*)

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. I ____ have breakfast at 8. → a) always · b) never never · c) at all  
7. I like tea ____ I don't like coffee. → a) but · b) and · c) because  
8. We ____ football (ball sport). → a) play · b) go · c) do  
9. Future plan form: ____. → a) am/is/are going + -ing · b) only past · c) will always required here  
10. *Because* introduces a ____. → a) reason · b) contrast · c) greeting

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) always**  
7. **a) but**  
8. **a) play**  
9. **a) am/is/are going + -ing**  
10. **a) reason**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Ordena

11. I / can / play / the / guitar / very well.  
12. She / always / goes / running / on Mondays.  
13. I / am / going / camping / next weekend / because / I / love / nature.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I can play the guitar very well.  
12. She always goes running on Mondays.  
13. I am going camping next weekend because I love nature.

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Verdadero o falso

14. *Play* + ball sports; *go* + -ing activities; *do* + yoga/exercise. → True / False  
15. *Can I…?* asks for permission; *I'm going camping* can be a future plan. → True / False

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. **True**  
15. **True**

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: repaso

**Objetivo:** deportes, frecuencia, permiso, conectores y planes.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige el significado

1. *Play* often goes with ____. → a) sports with a ball · b) only yoga · c) only camping  
2. *Go* often goes with ____. → a) activities ending in -ing · b) only football · c) only piano  
3. *Do* often goes with ____. → a) yoga / exercise / gymnastics · b) only tennis · c) only drums  
4. *Always* means ____. → a) 100% of the time · b) 0% · c) never  
5. *May I…?* is often ____ than *Can I…?*. → a) more formal · b) less polite · c) incorrect

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) sports with a ball**  
2. **a) activities ending in -ing**  
3. **a) yoga / exercise / gymnastics**  
4. **a) 100% of the time**  
5. **a) more formal**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–7 — Empareja

6. Empareja: play / go / do / can / going → football, tennis · swimming, camping · yoga · ability · future plan  
7. Empareja: and / but / because / very well / of course → addition · contrast · reason · high level · yes, sure

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **play → football, tennis** · **go → swimming, camping** · **do → yoga** · **can → ability** · **going → future plan**  
7. **and → addition** · **but → contrast** · **because → reason** · **very well → high level** · **of course → yes, sure**

</details>

### Ejercicios 8–10 — Completa

8. I ____ have breakfast at 8. → *always / never never / at*  
9. Can I ____ your pen? → *borrow / borrows / borrowing*  
10. We are ____ camping next week. → *going / go / goes*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

8. **always**  
9. **borrow**  
10. **going**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–13 — Definiciones

11. *Never* means ____. → a) 0% · b) 100% · c) sometimes  
12. *Excuse me* is ____. → a) a polite way to start a request · b) a refusal · c) a sport  
13. Outdoor activities include ____. → a) hiking, camping, fishing · b) only homework · c) only TV

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) 0%**  
12. **a) a polite way to start a request**  
13. **a) hiking, camping, fishing**

</details>

### Ejercicios 14–15 — Ordena

14. I / usually / go / swimming / on Fridays.  
15. We / are / going / camping / because / we / love / nature.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

14. I usually go swimming on Fridays.  
15. We are going camping because we love nature.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Tom

**Objetivo:** leer un texto que mezcla habilidad, deportes, frecuencia, planes y permiso.

### Texto

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-40/reading-tom.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Tom"></audio>

> My name is Tom. I can play the guitar quite well and I play football every Saturday. I always do yoga in the morning because I like it. Next week I'm going camping with my friends. Can I use the bathroom? Yes, of course — we use polite phrases every day.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la respuesta correcta

1. What is the writer's name? → a) Tom · b) Tim · c) Ted  
2. Can he play the guitar? → a) Yes, quite well. · b) No · c) Not at all  
3. When does he play football? → a) Every Saturday. · b) Never · c) Every Monday  
4. When does he do yoga? → a) Always in the morning. · b) Never · c) Only at night  
5. Why does he do yoga? → a) Because he likes it. · b) Because he hates it. · c) The text doesn't say

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Tom**  
2. **a) Yes, quite well.**  
3. **a) Every Saturday.**  
4. **a) Always in the morning.**  
5. **a) Because he likes it.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Comprensión

6. What is he doing next week? → a) Going camping. · b) Going shopping. · c) Staying home  
7. Who is he going with? → a) With friends. · b) Alone · c) With teachers  
8. Which permission question appears? → a) Can I use the bathroom? · b) May I leave forever? · c) Can I drive?  
9. *Because* introduces ____. → a) a reason · b) a contrast · c) a greeting  
10. With football we use ____. → a) play · b) go · c) do

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Going camping.**  
7. **a) With friends.**  
8. **a) Can I use the bathroom?**  
9. **a) a reason**  
10. **a) play**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Detalles

11. *And* connects ____. → a) “I can play…” and “I play football”; two positive ideas · b) only contrast · c) only refusal  
12. *Of course* means ____. → a) yes, sure · b) no · c) never  
13. The text reviews ____. → a) several Module 4 topics · b) only past tense · c) only food  
14. *I'm going camping* is ____. → a) a future plan · b) a past habit · c) a command  
15. Polite phrases include ____. → a) please, thank you, Can I…? · b) only goodbye · c) only no

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) “I can play…” and “I play football”; two positive ideas**  
12. **a) yes, sure**  
13. **a) several Module 4 topics**  
14. **a) a future plan**  
15. **a) please, thank you, Can I…?**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Review

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo de repaso del Módulo 4.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a1/unit-40/listening-review.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Review"></audio>

> **A:** Can you swim?  
> **B:** Yes, I can. I go swimming every week.  
> **A:** I always play tennis on Saturdays, but I don't like football.  
> **B:** I'm going camping next weekend because I love nature. May I borrow your torch?  
> **A:** Of course.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Can B swim? → a) Yes. · b) No. · c) The audio doesn't say  
2. How often does B go swimming? → a) Every week. · b) Never. · c) Once a year  
3. What does A always play on Saturdays? → a) Tennis. · b) Football. · c) Yoga  
4. Does A like football? → a) No. · b) Yes. · c) Always  
5. Which connector shows contrast? → a) but · b) and · c) because

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Yes.**  
2. **a) Every week.**  
3. **a) Tennis.**  
4. **a) No.**  
5. **a) but**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. What is B doing next weekend? → a) Going camping. · b) Going shopping. · c) Working  
7. Why is B going camping? → a) Because B loves nature. · b) Because B hates nature. · c) The audio doesn't say  
8. What does B want to borrow? → a) A torch. · b) A car. · c) A house  
9. Which permission form does B use? → a) May I borrow…? · b) Give me · c) I take  
10. A's answer is ____. → a) Of course. · b) Never. · c) Goodbye

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Going camping.**  
7. **a) Because B loves nature.**  
8. **a) A torch.**  
9. **a) May I borrow…?**  
10. **a) Of course.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. With swimming we often use ____. → a) go + swimming · b) play swimming · c) do football  
12. *Always* is ____. → a) an adverb of frequency · b) a sport · c) a place  
13. A *torch* is ____. → a) a light used for camping or in the dark · b) a ball · c) a book  
14. The dialogue reviews ____. → a) can, play/go, frequency, but/because, plans, permission · b) only past · c) only food  
15. *I'm going camping* uses ____. → a) Present Continuous for the future · b) Past Simple · c) Present Perfect

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) go + swimming**  
12. **a) an adverb of frequency**  
13. **a) a light used for camping or in the dark**  
14. **a) can, play/go, frequency, but/because, plans, permission**  
15. **a) Present Continuous for the future**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: Module 4

**Objetivo:** escribir frases que combinen los temas del Módulo 4.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ cook very well. → *can / cans / canning*  
2. She ____ football every Saturday. → *plays / play / playing*  
3. We ____ swimming on Mondays. → *go / goes / going*  
4. I ____ do yoga in the morning. → *always / never never / at*  
5. I like tea ____ I don't like coffee. → *but / and / because*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **can**  
2. **plays**  
3. **go**  
4. **always**  
5. **but**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. I'm / going / camping / next week.  
7. Can / I / borrow / your / pen, / please?  
8. I / do / yoga / in the morning / because / I / like / it.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I'm going camping next week.  
7. Can I borrow your pen, please?  
8. I do yoga in the morning because I like it.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Escribe una habilidad con *can* + *very/quite well*.  
10. Escribe un deporte con *play* + frecuencia.  
11. Dos frases: *and* + *but*.  
12. Un plan outdoor con Present Continuous.  
13. Párrafo corto (4–5 frases) mezclando can, frecuencia, plan y permiso.  
14. Mini diálogo de permiso.  
15. Frase con *because*.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I can cook very well.  
10. I play football every Saturday.  
11. I like tea and coffee. I play tennis, but I don't like football.  
12. I'm going camping next week.  
13. I can swim well. I always go swimming on Mondays because I love it. Next weekend I'm going hiking. Can I use the bathroom? Yes, of course.  
14. Can I borrow your book? Yes, of course. Here you are.  
15. I do yoga because I like it and it is relaxing.

</details>
