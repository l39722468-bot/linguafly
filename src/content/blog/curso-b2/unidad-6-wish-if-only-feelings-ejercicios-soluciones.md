## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** wish + past simple / were / could / would; if only + past perfect

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I wish I ___ calmer before exams. → *am / were / had been*
2. If only I ___ told her the truth! → *tell / told / had told*
3. I wish my neighbours ___ stop playing loud music. → *will / would / can*
4. I wish I ___ express my feelings better. → *can / could / will*
5. She wishes she ___ more sleep last night. → *gets / got / had got*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **were** · 2. **had told** · 3. **would** · 4. **could** · 5. **had got**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. If only you ___ here now! → *are / were / had been*
2. I wish he ___ so shy. → *isn't / weren't / hadn't been*
3. She regrets ___ silent for so long. → *stay / staying / to stay*
4. I wish they ___ me about the problem. → *tell / told / had told*
5. If only it ___ stop raining! → *will / would / can*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **were** · 2. **weren't** · 3. **staying** · 4. **had told** · 5. **would**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *I wish I am taller.*
2. *If only you tell me earlier.*
3. *I wish they will be quiet.*
4. *She wishes she can dance.*
5. *I wish I have slept more.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I wish I **were** taller.
2. If only you **had told** me earlier.
3. I wish they **would** be quiet.
4. She wishes she **could** dance.
5. I wish I **had slept** more.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** feelings & emotions

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. anxious → *ansioso · aliviado · orgulloso*
2. calm → *tranquilo · furioso · celoso*
3. express feelings → *expresar sentimientos · reservar · posponer*
4. apologise → *disculparse · cancelar · despegar*
5. flatmate → *compañero/a de piso · pasajero · guía*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **ansioso** · 2. **tranquilo** · 3. **expresar sentimientos** · 4. **disculparse** · 5. **compañero/a de piso**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

1. relieved ≈ ___ → *aliviado / avergonzado / furioso*
2. frustrated ≈ ___ → *frustrado / agradecido / emocionado*
3. embarrassed ≈ ___ → *avergonzado / seguro / calmado*
4. if only ≈ ___ → *ojalá (más enfático) / porque / aunque*
5. wish + would = ___ → *queja / deseo pasado / certeza*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **aliviado** · 2. **frustrado** · 3. **avergonzado** · 4. **ojalá (más enfático)** · 5. **queja**

</details>

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 2× wish (presente irreal) y 1× if only (pasado).
2. Completa: I wish I ___ (be) calmer.
3. Completa: If only I ___ (tell) her sooner!
4. Completa: I wish they ___ (stop) making noise.
5. Completa: I wish I ___ (can) sleep better.
6. Wish + would — queja sobre un vecino.
7. Regret + gerund en una frase.
8. Corrige: *I wish I am taller.*
9. Corrige: *If only you tell me.*
10. Párrafo sobre expresar emociones.
11. Traduce: Ojalá pudiera expresarme mejor.
12. Traduce: Ojalá me lo hubieras dicho antes.
13. Diálogo con wish / if only.
14. Diferencia wish vs if only (1 frase).
15. Autochequeo: past simple vs past perfect after wish.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Model using wish/if only correctly.
2. **were**
3. **had told**
4. **would stop**
5. **could**
6. I wish my neighbours **would** turn down the music.
7. I regret **staying** silent.
8. I wish I **were** taller.
9. If only you **had told** me.
10. Open feelings paragraph.
11. I wish I **could** express myself better.
12. If only you **had told** me earlier.
13. Open dialogue.
14. If only is often more emphatic than wish.
15. Self-check.

</details>
