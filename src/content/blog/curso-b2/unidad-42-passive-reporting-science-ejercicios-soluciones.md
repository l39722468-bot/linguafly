Este cuaderno reúne los **ejercicios de la Unidad 42 del curso B2** (*Passive Reporting Structures + Scientific Discoveries*). Practicarás reconocimiento, transformación y producción con tareas cerradas o criterios verificables. Cada actividad indica qué debes escribir, qué opciones puedes usar o qué información debes localizar.

> **Guía teórica:** [Passive Reporting & Science B2](/blog/curso-b2/unidad-42-passive-reporting-science)<br>
> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 42 — Passive Reporting & Science](/curso-b2/unit-42)

Trabaja en este orden: responde sin mirar, abre la solución, identifica la causa de cada error y repite la frase correcta en voz alta. En writing y speaking, compara primero la estructura obligatoria y después el contenido.

**Recuerda antes de empezar:**
- Conserva una cláusula completa en **It is said that + subject + verb**.
- Destaca la entidad con **subject + is/are said + to-infinitive**.
- Usa **to be doing** para un proceso simultáneo y **to have + V3** para un hecho anterior.
- La estructura atribuye una afirmación; no demuestra por sí sola que sea cierta.

## Lección 1 — Gramática y chunks

**Objetivo:** seleccionar y construir correctamente it + passive + that y subject + passive + infinitivo simple, continuo o perfecto.

No respondas por parecido visual. Localiza la pista de significado o tiempo y comprueba la cadena completa: modal, auxiliar, infinitivo, participio, objeto y concordancia.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — It + passive + that-clause

Conserva el sujeto y el tiempo dentro de la cláusula introducida por *that*.

1. People say the vaccine works.
   Opciones: *It is said that the vaccine works. · It says the vaccine to work.*
2. Researchers think the experiment was successful.
   Opciones: *It is thought that the experiment was successful. · It thought that the experiment succeeds.*
3. Experts know that the sample contains ice.
   Opciones: *It is known that the sample contains ice. · It knows the sample to contain ice.*
4. Analysts estimate that the project cost €4 million.
   Opciones: *It is estimated that the project cost €4 million. · It estimates to cost €4 million.*
5. Corrige: *It is reported the findings to be reliable.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **It is said that the vaccine works.** — La pasiva impersonal es *It is said that + clause*.
2. **It is thought that the experiment was successful.** — El pasado de la información permanece en la cláusula.
3. **It is known that the sample contains ice.** — *Known* atribuye conocimiento establecido.
4. **It is estimated that the project cost €4 million.** — La cláusula completa conserva sujeto y verbo.
5. **It is reported that the findings are reliable.** — El marco con *it* necesita *that + clause*.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Sujeto + reporting passive + infinitive

Elige el infinitivo según simultaneidad, proceso en curso o anterioridad.

6. People believe the drug is effective.
   Opciones: *The drug is believed to be effective. · The drug is believed to have effective.*
7. People say the lab is analysing data now.
   Opciones: *The lab is said to be analysing data. · The lab is said to have analysed data now.*
8. People report that the scientist made a discovery last year.
   Opciones: *The scientist is reported to have made a discovery. · The scientist is reported to make a discovery.*
9. People think the samples were contaminated earlier.
   Opciones: *The samples are thought to have been contaminated. · The samples are thought to be contaminating.*
10. People expect the findings will be published soon.
   Opciones: *The findings are expected to be published soon. · The findings are expected to publish soon.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **The drug is believed to be effective.** — Estado simultáneo: *to be*.
7. **The lab is said to be analysing data.** — Proceso actual: *to be + -ing*.
8. **The scientist is reported to have made a discovery.** — Hecho anterior: *to have + V3*.
9. **The samples are thought to have been contaminated.** — Anterioridad pasiva: *to have been + V3*.
10. **The findings are expected to be published soon.** — Los findings reciben la acción: infinitivo pasivo.

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Concordancia y reparación

Cada frase contiene un único error objetivo. Reescríbela completa.

11. The researchers *is believed* to be checking the evidence.
12. The treatment is said *that it works*.
13. She is thought *to discovered* the species.
14. The laboratory is reported *developing* a vaccine.
15. The results are expected *to publish* next month.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **The researchers are believed to be checking the evidence.** — El sujeto plural exige *are*.
12. **The treatment is said to work.** — No se mezclan los dos marcos.
13. **She is thought to have discovered the species.** — El hecho anterior exige *to have + V3*.
14. **The laboratory is reported to be developing a vaccine.** — El proceso simultáneo exige *to be + -ing*.
15. **The results are expected to be published next month.** — Se necesita infinitivo pasivo.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** comprender y usar vocabulario de *Scientific Discoveries* en situaciones concretas.

Aprende cada palabra con su colocación. Las alternativas cercanas obligan a decidir por definición y contexto, no por parecido.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Traducción precisa

Elige el único significado que corresponde a la palabra.

1. hypothesis
   Opciones: *explicación comprobable · resultado interpretado · avance decisivo*
2. evidence
   Opciones: *datos que apoyan una conclusión · tratamiento médico · objeto en órbita*
3. findings
   Opciones: *resultados interpretados de un estudio · instrucciones del método · participantes*
4. breakthrough
   Opciones: *avance decisivo · cualquier descubrimiento pequeño · teoría sin probar*
5. innovation
   Opciones: *idea o método nuevo y útil · hallazgo natural · repetición de un ensayo*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **explicación comprobable**
2. **datos que apoyan una conclusión**
3. **resultados interpretados de un estudio**
4. **avance decisivo**
5. **idea o método nuevo y útil**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige por definición

Completa cada definición con una de las tres opciones.

6. A controlled procedure used to test a hypothesis is an ___.
   Opciones: *experiment · invention · finding*
7. A systematic process of asking and testing questions is the ___.
   Opciones: *scientific method · clinical result · medical care*
8. Research into patterns and causes of disease in populations is ___.
   Opciones: *epidemiology · astronomy · pedagogy*
9. A study evaluating an intervention with participants is a ___.
   Opciones: *clinical trial · laboratory claim · diagnosis*
10. Something identified that was previously unknown is a ___.
   Opciones: *discovery · invention · expectation*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **experiment**
7. **scientific method**
8. **epidemiology**
9. **clinical trial**
10. **discovery**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Completa el chunk

Escribe la expresión exacta que completa la situación; todas pertenecen al inventario de la unidad.

11. Before drawing a conclusion, scientists must ___.
12. The team designed an experiment to ___.
13. Researchers will ___ after independent review.
14. The laboratory plans to ___ next year.
15. The result could ___ in disease detection.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **gather evidence**
12. **test the hypothesis**
13. **publish the findings**
14. **conduct a clinical trial**
15. **represent a breakthrough**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: A cautiously reported breakthrough

**Objetivo:** comprender la idea global, localizar datos y reconocer los objetivos en contexto.

> It is reported that a research team has tested a new treatment for a rare disease. The lead scientist is believed to have formed the original hypothesis after reviewing earlier findings. The laboratory is said to be analysing data from a controlled experiment, while an independent group checks the evidence. The treatment is thought to be safe at the tested dosage, but its long-term effects are not yet known. It is expected that a larger clinical trial will begin next year. The first study is estimated to have cost four million euros. Journalists have called the result a breakthrough, although the researchers say that more evidence is needed before that claim can be supported. Their findings are expected to be published after the review.

Lee una vez para captar la situación y otra para localizar detalles. También puedes escuchar el audio. Todas las respuestas se comprueban directamente en el texto.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión global

Responde con la información explícita del texto o audio.

1. What is the research team reported to have tested?
2. Who is believed to have formed the hypothesis?
3. What is the laboratory analysing?
4. When is a larger clinical trial expected to begin?
5. How much is the first study estimated to have cost?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **A new treatment for a rare disease.**
2. **The lead scientist.**
3. **Data from a controlled experiment.**
4. **Next year.**
5. **Four million euros.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles y chunks exactos

Completa con las palabras exactas del texto o audio.

6. Complete: The laboratory is said ___ data.
7. Complete: The first study is estimated ___ €4 million.
8. What is not yet known?
9. Which group checks the evidence?
10. When are the findings expected to be published?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **to be analysing**
7. **to have cost**
8. **The treatment's long-term effects.**
9. **An independent group.**
10. **After the review.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma, significado y secuencia

Elige, explica u ordena usando la evidencia presentada.

11. Choose: *to have formed* is simultaneous / anterior.
12. Choose: *to be analysing* is completed / in progress.
13. Put in order: hypothesis — experiment — larger trial — publication.
14. Why do the researchers avoid confirming a breakthrough?
15. Does passive reporting prove that the treatment is safe?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **anterior**
12. **in progress**
13. **hypothesis → experiment → larger trial → publication**
14. **Because more evidence is needed.**
15. **No; it attributes the claim, and long-term effects are unknown.**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Dr Martinez reports trial news

**Objetivo:** identificar información y expresiones completas a velocidad natural.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b2/unit-42/listening-workbook.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Dr Martinez reports trial news"></audio>

Escucha dos veces antes de abrir el guion: la primera para entender la situación y la segunda para anotar detalles.

<details>
<summary>Leer guion después de escuchar</summary>

> Hello, I'm Dr Martinez, and I work in medical research. It is said that our new treatment could help thousands of patients, but that claim still needs evidence. Our team is believed to have discovered a promising approach during an earlier experiment. The laboratory is reported to be analysing the latest data now, and an external group is thought to be checking our findings. It is expected that the clinical trial will finish next year. One researcher is reported to have left the project last month, but the study is continuing. The results are expected to be published after independent review. We describe the work as an innovation, not yet as a breakthrough.

</details>

Responde con la información explícita del texto o audio.

1. Who is speaking?
2. Who could the treatment help?
3. What is the team believed to have discovered?
4. When is the clinical trial expected to finish?
5. Who left the project?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Dr Martinez.**
2. **Thousands of patients.**
3. **A promising approach.**
4. **Next year.**
5. **One researcher.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles y chunks exactos

Completa con las palabras exactas del texto o audio.

6. Complete: The laboratory is reported ___ the data.
7. Complete: An external group is thought ___ the findings.
8. Complete the anterior event: A researcher is reported ___.
9. When will the results be published?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma, significado y secuencia

Elige, explica u ordena usando la evidencia presentada.

13. Put in order: earlier experiment — current analysis — trial completion — publication.

15. What does he call the work instead of a breakthrough?

<details>

## Lección 5 — Writing y Speaking

**Objetivo:** producir mensajes controlados con it + passive + that y subject + passive + infinitivo simple, continuo o perfecto.

### Ejercicios 1–10 — Writing con modelo

Respeta la extensión y usa todas las expresiones indicadas. Cada tarea incluye una respuesta o un modelo completo: puedes cambiar los detalles, pero conserva las estructuras evaluadas.

1. Transforma: *People say that the vaccine works.*
2. Transforma: *People believe the lab is analysing data.*
3. Transforma el hecho anterior: *People report that Ana made a discovery.*
4. Pasa a plural: *The finding is thought to be reliable.*
5. Corrige: *The results are expected to publish soon.*
6. Redacta 35–45 palabras con *It is said that, hypothesis, evidence*.
7. Escribe 35–45 palabras con *is reported to have, breakthrough, findings*.
8. Redacta 45–55 palabras contrastando *to be analysing / to have analysed*.
9. Escribe 50–60 palabras con *scientific method, conduct an experiment, clinical trial, publish findings*.
10. Escribe 100–120 palabras como noticia científica prudente. Incluye tres marcos con *it*, cuatro con sujeto, dos infinitivos perfectos y ocho términos científicos.

<details>
<summary>Ver modelos de writing</summary>

1. **It is said that the vaccine works.** / **The vaccine is said to work.**
2. **The lab is believed to be analysing data.**
3. **Ana is reported to have made a discovery.**
4. **The findings are thought to be reliable.**
5. The results **are expected to be published** soon.
6. Modelo: **It is said that** the new treatment targets a rare disease. Researchers formed a clear **hypothesis**, but they need stronger **evidence** before presenting the early result as reliable in an international peer-reviewed medical research journal.
7. Modelo: The lead scientist **is reported to have made** a major **breakthrough**. However, the full **findings** have not passed independent review, so the team describes the result cautiously while other laboratories repeat the experiment under controlled conditions.
8. Modelo: The laboratory **is said to be analysing** the latest samples now, so the process is still under way. A separate team **is believed to have analysed** the earlier data already, which places that action before the present report during the current stage of the study.
9. Modelo: The **scientific method** requires researchers to form a hypothesis and **conduct an experiment** under controlled conditions. If the evidence is promising, they may organise a **clinical trial**. They should **publish the findings** only after careful analysis and review. They must also report limitations so other laboratories can reproduce the procedure.
10. Modelo: **It is reported that** a university team has developed a possible treatment. The lead researcher **is believed to have formed** the **hypothesis** after examining earlier **findings**. The laboratory **is said to be conducting** a controlled **experiment**, and an independent group **is thought to be checking** the **evidence**. **It is expected that** a **clinical trial** will begin next year. The first study **is estimated to have cost** €3 million. The treatment **is believed to be** safe at the tested dosage, but long-term effects remain unknown. **It is claimed that** the result is a **breakthrough**; however, the researchers call it an **innovation** until they can publish the findings and repeat the experiment.

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Speaking con guion y pistas

Prepara durante 30 segundos y habla sin leer. Después compara tus estructuras con el guion o las pistas concretas.

11. Pronuncia los dos marcos de reporte con la misma información.
12. Explica *to be doing / to have done* en 30 segundos.
13. Role-play entre periodista y científica.
14. Presenta tres colocaciones del método científico.
15. Informa durante 60 segundos sobre un estudio con seis reportes y ocho términos U42.

<details>
<summary>Ver guiones y pistas de speaking</summary>

11. Guion modelo: **It is believed that the treatment works. The treatment is believed to work.**
12. Guion modelo: **The lab is said to be analysing** data now. **The lab is said to have analysed** earlier data already.
13. A: What is reported? B: The team **is believed to have made** a discovery. A: Is it confirmed? B: No; more evidence is needed.
14. Guion modelo: Researchers **form a hypothesis**, **conduct an experiment**, **gather evidence** and then **publish findings**.
15. Pistas: it is said/thought/expected · is believed to be · is reported to have · is said to be analysing · hypothesis · experiment · evidence · findings · trial · discovery · innovation · breakthrough.

</details>

## Cómo revisar tus resultados

Clasifica cada fallo como **significado**, **forma**, **tiempo**, **concordancia**, **orden** o **vocabulario**. Copia la corrección mínima y crea una frase nueva con el mismo patrón. Después vuelve a intentarlo sin opciones.

Para consolidar, repite las expresiones mañana, dentro de tres días y una semana después. Reduce el apoyo en cada revisión: primero opciones, después una pista en español y finalmente una situación de 45 segundos.
