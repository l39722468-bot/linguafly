Haz cada bloque **sin mirar** la solución. Luego comprueba y lee la explicación.

**Recuerda antes de empezar:**
- Contraste: **although / despite / whereas / however**
- Razón-propósito: **because of / in order to / so that / as a result**
- Casa: **break down / bring in / bring up**
- Redes: **call off / carry on / come up with**

## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** usar correctamente linkers of contrast + reason/purpose + phrasal verbs BE/BREAK/BRING + CALL/CARRY/COME.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige la opción correcta (mix U21–24)

Completa cada frase con **una** de las tres opciones. Mezcla de contraste, razón/propósito y phrasals.

1. ___ Maya felt nervous, she joined the workshop.  
   a) Although · b) Because of · c) In order to
2. She used a tripod ___ capture sharp photos.  
   a) in order to · b) despite · c) call off
3. When the boiler ___, she brought in a plumber.  
   a) broke down · b) called off · c) came across
4. Her livestream was ___, but she came up with a story series.  
   a) called off · b) brought about · c) due to
5. ___ the setbacks, she made progress.  
   a) Despite · b) So that · c) Carry on

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Although** — contraste + cláusula.
2. **a) in order to** — propósito + infinitivo.
3. **a) broke down** — máquina averiada.
4. **a) called off** — cancelar evento.
5. **a) Despite** — *despite* + noun.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa (mix)

Escribe la forma correcta según la pista.

1. ___ heavy traffic, Leo almost arrived late. (a causa de)
2. Sam ___ the idea of an open-plan kitchen. (mencionó)
3. Nora ___ a useful format while scrolling. (encontró)
4. Meditation is calm, ___ goal-setting is dynamic. (mientras que)
5. They ___ a backup plan after the crash. (ejecutaron)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Because of** — U22
2. **brought up** — U23
3. **came across** — U24
4. **whereas** — U21
5. **carried out** — U24

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige el error (mix)

Cada frase tiene **un** error típico del bloque. Reescribe la frase correcta.

1. *Despite she felt nervous, she joined.*
2. *Because of he was late, he missed the light.*
3. *They brought up a plumber for the leak.*
4. *I came up a hashtag for the challenge.*
5. *We carried out posting stories all week.* (continuar)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Although** she felt nervous… / **Despite** her nerves… — U21
2. **Because** he was late… / **Because of** the traffic… — U22
3. They **brought in** a plumber… — U23
4. I **came up with** a hashtag… — U24
5. We **carried on** posting… — U24

</details>

4. **engagement** → a) interacción · b) ampliación · c) resiliencia
5. **milestone** → a) hito · b) trípode · c) troll

<details>

</details>

4. hashtag ≈ ___

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases claras con linkers of contrast + reason/purpose + phrasal verbs BE/BREAK/BRING + CALL/CARRY/COME.

Escribe tus respuestas. Luego compara con los modelos.

1. Escribe **una** frase con cada foco: *although* · *despite* · *in order to* · *break down* · *call off* · *come up with*.
2. Completa: ___ Maya felt nervous, she joined.
3. Completa: She used a tripod ___ capture sharp photos.
4. Completa: When the boiler ___, she brought in a plumber.
5. Completa: Her livestream was ___.
6. Corrige: *Despite she felt nervous…*
7. Corrige: *I came up a story series.*
8. Matching mental: U21 contraste · U22 propósito · U23 casa · U24 redes — escribe un ejemplo de cada uno.
9. Copia y memoriza: *Despite the setbacks, she made progress and stepped further out of her comfort zone.*
10. Mini-historia (6–8 frases) mezclando al menos un linker de contraste, uno de propósito y dos phrasals.
11. Traduce: *A pesar de los contratiempos, siguió estudiando.*
12. Traduce: *Cancelaron el directo, pero ideó una serie de stories.*
13. Explica en **una** frase cuándo eliges *although* frente a *because of*.
14. Escribe una frase mixta con *engagement* + *despite* o *as a result*.
15. Autochequeo del bloque — marca sí/no:
    - ¿Distingo *despite* vs *although*?
    - ¿Distingo *in order to* vs *so that*?
    - ¿Distingo *break down* vs *call off*?
    - ¿Uso *come up with* (con *with*)?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Modelos:
   - **Although** I felt nervous, I joined.
   - **Despite** the setbacks, I continued.
   - I used a tripod **in order to** get sharp photos.
   - The boiler **broke down**.
   - The live was **called off**.
   - I **came up with** a story series.
2. **Although**
3. **in order to**
4. **broke down**
5. **called off**
6. **Although** she felt nervous… / **Despite** her nerves…
7. I **came up with** a story series.
8. U21 although… · U22 in order to… · U23 broke down… · U24 called off / came up with…
9. **Despite the setbacks, she made progress and stepped further out of her comfort zone.**
10. Modelo: Although Maya felt nervous, she joined the workshop. She used a tripod in order to capture sharp photos. When the boiler broke down, she brought in a plumber. Her livestream was called off, but she came up with a story series. Despite the setbacks, she carried on and made progress.
11. **Despite** the setbacks, she **carried on** studying.
12. They **called off** the livestream, but she **came up with** a story series.
13. ***Although*** = contraste; ***because of*** = causa (+ sustantivo).
14. Modelo: **Despite** the crash, **engagement** recovered. / She posted stories. **As a result**, engagement rose.
15. Las cuatro respuestas deberían ser **sí**. Si fallas alguna, repasa la teoría U21–24.

</details>
