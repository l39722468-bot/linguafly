## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** wish, would rather, mixed conditionals, participle clauses

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I wish I ___ booked the tickets earlier. → *have / had / has*
2. I would rather ___ by train than fly. → *travel / to travel / travelling*
3. If we had left earlier, we ___ at the museum now. → *are / would be / will be*
4. ___ read the guide, we chose the eco tour. → *Having / Have / Had*
5. It is time we ___ our carbon footprint. → *reduce / reduced / reducing*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **had** · 2. **travel** · 3. **would be** · 4. **Having** · 5. **reduced**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. I wish my brother ___ help more at home. → *will / would / can*
2. She would prefer ___ with family. → *celebrate / to celebrate / celebrating*
3. If only they ___ the early bus! → *take / took / had taken*
4. ___ by the campaign, they recycle more. → *Encourage / Encouraged / Encouraging*
5. I'd rather you ___ drive so fast. → *don't / didn't / not*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **would** · 2. **to celebrate** · 3. **had taken** · 4. **Encouraged** · 5. **didn't**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *I wish I have booked earlier.*
2. *I would rather to travel by bus.*
3. *If we leave early, we would be there now.*
4. *Having read, we chose the tour.* (add pp)
5. *It is time we reduce emissions.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I wish I **had booked** earlier.
2. I would rather **travel** by bus.
3. If we **had left** early, we **would be** there now.
4. Having **read** the guide, we chose the tour.
5. It is time we **reduced** emissions.

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Una frase con cada: wish, would rather, mixed conditional, participle, it's time.
2. Completa: I wish I ___ (book) earlier.
3. Completa: I would rather ___ (travel) by train.
4. Completa: If we had left earlier, we ___ (be) at the museum now.
5. Completa: ___ read the guide, we chose the eco tour.
6. Completa: It is time we ___ (reduce) our footprint.
7. Mini-historia (6 frases) mezclando U6–9.
8. Corrige: *I wish I have booked.*
9. Corrige: *I would rather to stay.*
10. Matching: estructura → unidad (6–9).
11. Traduce: Ojalá hubiera reservado antes.
12. Traduce: Preferiría no conducir tan rápido.
13. Autochequeo con mapa U10.
14. Shadow reading + listening una vez más.
15. Siguiente paso: teoría U11 en el curso.
16. Enlace /curso-b2/unit-10.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open — one of each U6–9 structure.
2. **had booked**
3. **travel**
4. **would be**
5. Having **read**
6. **reduced**
7. Open mixed paragraph.
8. I wish I **had booked** earlier.
9. I would rather **stay** at home.
10. wish→U6; rather→U7; mixed→U8; participle→U9.
11. I wish I **had booked** earlier.
12. I would rather you **didn't** drive so fast.
13. Self-check vs review map.
14. Practice again without solutions.
