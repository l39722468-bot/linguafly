## Lección 1 — Gramática Mixta

### Subsección 1: Mixed Conditionals — Elige la forma correcta

**Instrucciones:** Elige la opción correcta (a o b) para completar cada oración.

**1.** If cybersecurity had been a priority in the 1990s, data breaches ______ so common today.
- a) wouldn't be
- b) wouldn't have been

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**a) wouldn't be** — El resultado es presente ("today"), así que usamos `would + infinitive`.

</details>

**2.** If the company ______ better infrastructure, the server wouldn't have crashed during the launch.
- a) had
- b) would have

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**a) had** — En mixed conditionals, la cláusula if usa el pasado simple (no "would").

</details>

**3.** If engineers had invested more in renewable energy tech, we ______ so many environmental challenges now.
- a) wouldn't face
- b) wouldn't have faced

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**a) wouldn't face** — El resultado es presente ("now"), así que usamos `would + infinitive`.

</details>

**4.** If developers tested their code more thoroughly, that bug ______ millions of users last year.
- a) wouldn't have affected
- b) wouldn't affect

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**a) wouldn't have affected** — El resultado es pasado ("last year"), así que usamos `would have + past participle`.

</details>

**5.** If AI ______ developed earlier, healthcare would be more advanced now.
- a) had been
- b) was

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**a) had been** — La cláusula if se refiere a una condición hipotética del pasado, así que usamos past perfect.

</details>

### Subsección 2: Passive + Reporting — Transforma o identifica

**Instrucciones:** Transforma las oraciones al passive voice o identifica la estructura correcta.

**1.** Transforma al passive: *The team designed the algorithm to detect fraud.*
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*The algorithm was designed to detect fraud.*

</details>

**2.** Transforma al passive: *Hackers compromised the data last week.*
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*The data was compromised by hackers last week.*

</details>

**3.** Completa el passive reporting: *It is ______ that the server was hacked by an insider.*
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*It is **believed** that the server was hacked by an insider.*

</details>

**4.** Transforma al passive: *The company has raised $50 million in funding.*
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*$50 million has been raised in funding by the company.*

</details>

**5.** Identifica el error: *It is report that several vulnerabilities have been discover.*
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*It is **reported** that several vulnerabilities have been **discovered**.* — Faltan los participios: "reported" y "discovered".

</details>

### Subsección 3: Modals + Phrasal Verbs — Completa o corrige

**Instrucciones:** Completa con el modal correcto o corrige el error.

**1.** The system ______ have crashed due to the overload. (Certeza ~95%)
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*The system **must** have crashed due to the overload.* — "Must" indica certeza casi total.

</details>

**2.** The breach ______ have been accidental — too much data was stolen. (Imposibilidad)
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*The breach **can't** have been accidental.* — "Can't" indica imposibilidad.

</details>

**3.** Corrige: *The new update could have rolled out next month.*
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*The new update **could be rolled out** next month.* — "Rolled out" necesita la estructura pasiva: `could be + past participle`.

</details>

**4.** Complete: *We ______ into some compatibility issues during testing.*
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*We **ran** into some compatibility issues during testing.* — El phrasal verb es "run into" (encontrar un problema).

</details>

**5.** Corrige: *The team came up an innovative solution.*
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*The team **came up with** an innovative solution.* — El phrasal verb es "come up with" (idear/inventar).

</details>

### Subsección 4: Linkers + Cleft Sentences — Elige o corrige

**Instrucciones:** Elige el linker correcto o corrige la cleft sentence.

**1.** The software is fast. ______, it is highly affordable.
- a) However
- b) Furthermore

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**b) Furthermore** — Se añade información positiva, no se contrasta.

</details>

**2.** The server was overloaded. ______, it crashed during peak hours.
- a) Consequently
- b) Nevertheless

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**a) Consequently** — Se expresa una consecuencia lógica.

</details>

**3.** Completa la cleft: ______ the algorithm that identified the security flaw.
- a) It is
- b) It was

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**b) It was** — El evento pasó en el pasado, así que usamos "was".

</details>

**4.** Corrige: *It was during the launch when the system crashed.*
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*It was during the launch **that** the system crashed.* — En cleft sentences se usa "that", no "when".

</details>

**5.** The product had bugs. ______, it was a commercial success.
- a) As a result
- b) Nevertheless

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**b) Nevertheless** — Se expresa concesión: a pesar de los bugs, tuvo éxito.

</details>

### Subsección 3: Uso correcto — Elige

**Instrucciones:** Elige la opción que usa correctamente el término.

**1.** ¿Cuál es correcto?
- a) *The AI uses machine learning to learning patterns.*
- b) *The AI uses machine learning to learn patterns.*

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**b)** — Después de "to" se usa el infinitivo: "to learn".

</details>

**2.** ¿Cuál es correcto?
- a) *Blockchain ensures transparent and immutable transactions.*
- b) *Blockchain ensures transparent and mutable transactions.*

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**a)** — Blockchain garantiza transacciones **inmutables** (no cambiables).

</details>

**3.** ¿Cuál es correcto?
- a) *The data privacy was violated by the company.*
- b) *Data privacy was violated by the company.*

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**b)** — "Data privacy" como concepto general no lleva artículo definido.

</details>

**4.** ¿Cuál es correcto?
- a) *Cloud computing allows to access remote servers.*
- b) *Cloud computing allows remote access to powerful servers.*

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**b)** — "Allow" no permite un sujeto explícito con "to": se usa `allow + noun`.

</details>

**5.** ¿Cuál es correcto?
- a) *The automation has reduced significantly manufacturing costs.*
- b) *The automation has significantly reduced manufacturing costs.*

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**b)** — El adverbio "significantly" va antes del verbo principal, no entre el verbo y su complemento.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading

Lee el siguiente texto sobre ciberseguridad y responde las preguntas.

> **The Rise of AI-Powered Cybersecurity**
>
> In recent years, artificial intelligence has revolutionised the field of cybersecurity. Traditional firewalls and antivirus software, which relied on known threat signatures, are no longer sufficient. Modern cyberattacks are too sophisticated for these conventional tools.
>
> AI-driven security systems, on the other hand, can analyse millions of data points in real time. The algorithm was designed to identify patterns that human analysts might miss. Furthermore, machine learning allows these systems to improve over time, adapting to new types of threats.
>
> It is reported that companies using AI-powered cybersecurity have reduced their breach response time by 60%. Nevertheless, experts warn that AI can also be used by hackers to create more advanced malware. It is therefore essential that organisations invest in both offensive and defensive AI capabilities.
>
> One of the biggest challenges in cybersecurity is data privacy. While AI systems need vast amounts of data to function effectively, collecting and processing this data raises significant ethical concerns. It is believed that stricter regulations will be necessary to balance innovation with user protection.
>
> Looking ahead, the development of quantum computing could both strengthen and undermine encryption. If quantum computers become widely available, current encryption methods would need to be completely redesigned. Consequently, researchers are already working on quantum-resistant encryption protocols.

### Preguntas

**1.** According to the text, why are traditional firewalls no longer sufficient?
- a) They are too expensive
- b) Modern attacks are too sophisticated for them
- c) They have been banned by regulations
- d) They only work with old operating systems

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**b)** — *Modern cyberattacks are too sophisticated for these conventional tools.*

</details>

**2.** What can AI-driven security systems analyse?
- a) Only known threat signatures
- b) Employee emails only
- c) Millions of data points in real time
- d) Physical security cameras

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**c)** — *AI-driven security systems can analyse millions of data points in real time.*

</details>

**3.** How much have companies reduced their breach response time using AI?
- a) 30%
- b) 50%
- c) 60%
- d) 80%

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**c)** — *Companies using AI-powered cybersecurity have reduced their breach response time by 60%.*

</details>

**4.** What is one concern experts have about AI in cybersecurity?
- a) It is too slow for real-time analysis
- b) It can be used by hackers to create advanced malware
- c) It requires too much electricity
- d) It cannot detect phishing attacks

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**b)** — *Experts warn that AI can also be used by hackers to create more advanced malware.*

</details>

**5.** What ethical concern does the text mention about AI and data privacy?
- a) AI systems collect data without consent
- b) AI systems need vast amounts of data, raising ethical concerns
- c) AI systems sell data to third parties
- d) AI systems cannot protect personal data

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**b)** — *While AI systems need vast amounts of data to function effectively, collecting and processing this data raises significant ethical concerns.*

</details>

**6.** What could quantum computing do to encryption?
- a) Make it completely unnecessary
- b) Both strengthen and undermine it
- c) Replace it with blockchain
- d) Make it faster and cheaper

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**b)** — *The development of quantum computing could both strengthen and undermine encryption.*

</details>

**7.** What structure does the sentence "It is reported that companies using AI-powered cybersecurity have reduced..." use?
- a) Passive voice
- b) Passive reporting
- c) Mixed conditional
- d) Cleft sentence

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**b)** — Passive reporting: "It is reported that..."

</details>

**8.** What modal of deduction does "It is believed that stricter regulations will be necessary" express?
- a) Certainty
- b) Possibility
- c) Obligation
- d) Ability

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**b)** — "It is believed" indica posibilidad, no certeza absoluta.

</details>

**9.** Find the phrasal verb in: "Modern cyberattacks are no longer sufficient." What is the synonym?
- a) They are no longer adequate
- b) They are no longer expensive
- c) They are no longer popular
- d) They are no longer legal

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**a)** — "Sufficient" means "adequate" (suficiente/adecuado).

</details>

**10.** Which linker is used to add information in the text?
- a) Nevertheless
- b) Consequently
- c) Furthermore
- d) On the other hand

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**c)** — "Furthermore" adds information (the third paragraph begins with it).

</details>

**11.** Which linker shows contrast in the text?
- a) Furthermore
- b) Consequently
- c) It is reported that
- d) Nevertheless

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**d)** — "Nevertheless" shows contrast/concession.

</details>

**12.** What is the main topic of the text?
- a) The history of computers
- b) AI's role in modern cybersecurity
- c) How to build a firewall
- d) The dangers of social media

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**b)** — The text discusses how AI has revolutionised cybersecurity.

</details>

**13.** Complete: If quantum computers become widely available, current encryption methods ______ need to be redesigned.
- a) would
- b) will
- c) can
- d) must

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**a)** — Second conditional: "would" for hypothetical future.

</details>

**14.** What does "adapt" mean in "adapting to new types of threats"?
- a) Ignoring
- b) Changing to suit new conditions
- c) Copying
- d) Destroying

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**b)** — "Adapt" means to change to suit new conditions (adaptarse).

</details>

**15.** According to the text, why are researchers working on quantum-resistant encryption?
- a) Because current encryption is too slow
- b) Because quantum computers could undermine current encryption
- c) Because governments require it
- d) Because it is cheaper than traditional encryption

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**b)** — *If quantum computers become widely available, current encryption methods would need to be completely redesigned.*

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing y Speaking

### Writing — Ejercicios

**1.** Escribe una mixed conditional (pasado→presente) sobre el impacto de no haber inventado internet.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*If internet had not been invented, the world would be a very different place today.*

</details>

**2.** Transforma al passive reporting: *People think that AI will replace many jobs.*
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*It is thought that AI will replace many jobs.*

</details>

**3.** Escribe una cleft sentence enfatizando cuándo ocurrió un fallo del sistema: (el fallo ocurrió durante la actualización)
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*It was during the update that the system failure occurred.*

</details>

**4.** Escribe un párrafo de 3-4 oraciones sobre ciberseguridad usando al menos 3 linkers diferentes.
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*Cybersecurity is a growing concern for businesses worldwide. Furthermore, the number of phishing attacks has increased dramatically in recent years. However, many companies still fail to invest adequately in protection. Consequently, data breaches continue to affect millions of users every year.*

</details>

**5.** Corrige: *If we would invest more in cybersecurity, breaches wouldn't have increased.*
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*If we **invested** more in cybersecurity, breaches **wouldn't have increased**.* — En la cláusula if no se usa "would"; y el resultado es pasado, así que "wouldn't have increased".

</details>

**6.** Escribe una oración usando el phrasal verb "phase out" en contexto de tecnología obsoleta.
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*The company plans to phase out its older software by the end of the year.*

</details>

**7.** Transforma al passive: *The developers will deploy the new update next week.*
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*The new update will be deployed next week.*

</details>

**8.** Escribe una cleft sentence enfatizando qué causó un problema: (la sobrecarga del servidor causó el problema)
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*It was the server overload that caused the problem.*

</details>

**9.** Completa: The system ______ have crashed due to the overload. (Certeza ~75%)
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*The system **should** have crashed due to the overload.* — "Should" indica alta probabilidad (~75%).

</details>

**10.** Escribe una mixed conditional (presente→pasado) sobre un error de codificación que causó un fallo.
<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*If the code were written more carefully, the bug wouldn't have caused the crash.*

</details>

## Tip de la semana

Cuando practiques gramática mixta, intenta **mezclar estructuras en un mismo párrafo**. No escribas solo conditionals, o solo passive: combínalas. Los exámenes B2 reales te obligan a alternar entre estructuras en el mismo ejercicio, así que la práctica integrada es la clave del éxito.

### Preguntas frecuentes (FAQs)

**¿Qué gramática se practica en la Unidad 56?**
Practica integrada de conditionals (mixed, third), passive voice, passive reporting, modals of deduction, phrasal verbs tech, linkers y cleft sentences en contexto de tecnología.

**¿Cómo diferencia esta unidad de un repaso normal?**
Las unidades de gramática mixta obligan a elegir entre múltiples estructuras en el mismo ejercicio, simulando un examen real donde debes identificar la forma correcta sin contexto previo.

**¿Qué vocabulario tecnológico cubre?**
Artificial intelligence, machine learning, data privacy, cybersecurity, algorithm, automation, blockchain, cloud computing, IoT, digital transformation.

**¿Cómo debo preparar esta unidad?**
Lee primero la teoría de cada estructura, luego completa los ejercicios sin mirar las pistas. Si fallas, identifica la familia gramatical antes de repetir.

**¿Dónde practico la Unidad 56?**
En la Unidad 56 del curso B2 y en esta misma guía, que incluye teoría y ejercicios con soluciones.
