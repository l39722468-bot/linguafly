## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** convince / ask / tell / want + object + to + infinitive

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. She convinced me ___ join the club. → *to / for / -*
2. He asked her ___ wait outside. → *to / for / -*
3. They told us ___ be quiet. → *to / for / -*
4. I want my brother ___ take up cycling. → *to / for / -*
5. The coach advised him ___ rest. → *to / for / -*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **to** · 2. **to** · 3. **to** · 4. **to** · 5. **to**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. My parents allowed me ___ camp overnight. → *to / for / -*
2. She persuaded him ___ try rock climbing. → *to / for / -*
3. Would you like me ___ help you? → *to / for / -*
4. The guide told the group ___ follow the path. → *to / for / -*
5. He wants his friends ___ join the trip. → *to / for / -*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **to** · 2. **to** · 3. **to** · 4. **to** · 5. **to**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *She convinced me join the team.*
2. *He asked her for wait.*
3. *They told us to don't shout.*
4. *I want that my sister takes photos.*
5. *She allowed me camp there.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. She convinced me **to join** the team.
2. He asked her **to wait**.
3. They told us **not to shout**.
4. I want my sister **to take** photos.
5. She allowed me **to camp** there.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Leo and leisure

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b2/unit-4/reading-workbook.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Leo and leisure"></audio>

> Leisure time is precious. Last summer a friend convinced Leo to try rock climbing. She asked him to book the equipment in advance and told him to arrive early. Leo wants his sister to take up photography with him. Their parents allowed them to travel alone for the weekend. He would like his friends to join the cycling club too.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Friend convinced Leo to try ___ → *rock climbing / cooking / sleeping*
2. Asked him to ___ equipment → *book / booking / booked*
3. Told him to ___ early → *arrive / arriving / arrived*
4. Wants sister to take up ___ → *photography / finance / pollution*
5. Parents allowed them to ___ → *travel alone / stay home / skip class*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **rock climbing** · 2. **book** · 3. **arrive** · 4. **photography** · 5. **travel alone**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Convince + obj + ? → *to + inf / gerund / past*
7. Would like friends to ___ → *join / joining / joined*
8. Leisure vocab? → *yes / none*
9. Structure tell + obj + to? → *yes / no*
10. Already joined photography? → *False / True / not stated*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **to + infinitive** · 7. **join cycling club** · 8. **climbing, camping, cycling** · 9. **yes in text** · 10. **Not stated / implied wanting**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Write convince + obj + to.
12. Write ask + obj + to.
13. Main idea? → *leisure / work / environment*
14. Allowed + obj + to example?
15. Course link → */curso-b2/unit-4*

<summary>Ver solución</summary>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 4 frases: convince, ask, tell, want + obj + to.
2. Completa: She convinced me ___ (join) the club.
3. Completa: He asked her ___ (wait) outside.
4. Completa: They told us ___ (be) quiet.
5. Completa: I want my friend ___ (take up) photography.
6. Completa: My parents allowed me ___ (travel) alone.
7. Corrige: *She persuaded him join the team.*
8. Corrige: *He asked me for help her.*
9. Párrafo sobre un hobby de ocio.
10. Traduce: Me convenció para que me uniera.
11. Traduce: Quiero que mi hermana venga conmigo.
12. Diálogo: ask + tell + want.
13. Usa *would like + obj + to*.
14. Usa *advise + obj + to*.
15. Autochequeo: siempre *to* antes del infinitivo.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open — four correct object + infinitive sentences.
2. **to join**
3. **to wait**
4. **to be**
5. **to take up**
6. **to travel**
7. She persuaded him **to join** the team.
8. He asked me **to help** her.
9. Open leisure paragraph.
10. She convinced me **to join**.
11. I want my sister **to come** with me.
12. Open dialogue.
13. I would like you **to join** us.
14. My tutor advised me **to read** more.
15. Self-check.

</details>
