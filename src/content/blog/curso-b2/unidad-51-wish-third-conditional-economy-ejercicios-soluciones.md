Este cuaderno reúne los **ejercicios de la Unidad 51 del curso B2** (*Global Economic Crisis*). Practicarás reconocimiento, transformación y producción con tareas cerradas o criterios verificables. Cada actividad indica qué debes escribir, qué opciones puedes usar o qué información debes localizar.

Trabaja en este orden: responde sin mirar, abre la solución, identifica la causa de cada error y repite la frase correcta en voz alta. En writing y speaking, compara primero la estructura obligatoria y después el contenido.

**Recuerda antes de empezar:**
- Clasifica primero: lamento pasado (*wish/if only*) o hipótesis pasada (*third conditional*).
- Comprueba cada cadena completa: *had + V3* en la cláusula *if*; *would have + V3* en el resultado.
- Distingue *third* de *mixed conditional* por la pista temporal del resultado.
- Conecta cada término económico con una colocación y un contexto.

## Lección 1 — Gramática y chunks

**Objetivo:** seleccionar y construir correctamente *wish/if only + past perfect* y el *third conditional* mediante pistas de función y tiempo.

No respondas por parecido visual. Localiza la pista de significado o tiempo y comprueba la cadena completa: auxiliar, participio y concordancia.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Wish/If Only: lamento sobre el pasado

Construye el lamento con *wish/if only + past perfect*.

1. I wish I ___ (invest) in that company ten years ago.
2. If only the government ___ (act) sooner to stop the crisis.
3. She wishes she ___ (not / sell) her shares during the crash.
4. If only we ___ (see) the signs of the recession earlier.
5. He wishes he ___ (study) economics at university.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **had invested** — *wish + past perfect* (lamento pasado).
2. **had acted** — *if only + past perfect*.
3. **hadn't sold** — negación con *hadn't + V3*.
4. **had seen** — *if only + past perfect*.
5. **had studied** — *wish + past perfect*.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Third Conditional: hipótesis pasada

Recupera la estructura completa: *if + past perfect, would have + V3*.

6. If the bank ___ (not / fail), the crisis wouldn't have been so severe.
7. They would have avoided bankruptcy if they ___ (manage) their debt better.
8. If the government ___ (provide) a stimulus package, the recovery would have been faster.
9. We ___ (not / lose) our jobs if the factory hadn't closed.
10. If I had bought gold, I ___ (make) a huge profit.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **hadn't failed** — cláusula *if* con past perfect negativo.
7. **had managed** — condición pasada.
8. **had provided** — condición pasada.
9. **wouldn't have lost** — resultado del third conditional.
10. **would have made** — resultado del third conditional.

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Distingue third de mixed

Elige la estructura correcta según la pista temporal del resultado.

11. If I had bought gold, I ___ rich now. (would be / would have been)
12. If I had bought gold, I ___ a profit last year. (would make / would have made)
13. If she had studied economics, she ___ a banker today. (would be / would have been)
14. If they had diversified, they ___ everything in the crash. (wouldn't lose / wouldn't have lost)
15. If we had saved more, we ___ a house now. (would buy / would have bought)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **would be** — Mixed (pasado → presente), pista *now*.
12. **would have made** — Third conditional, resultado pasado.
13. **would be** — Mixed (pasado → presente), pista *today*.
14. **wouldn't have lost** — Third conditional, resultado pasado.
15. **would buy** — Mixed (pasado → presente), pista *now*.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** comprender y usar vocabulario de *economía, banca e inversión* en situaciones concretas.

Aprende cada palabra con su colocación. Las alternativas cercanas obligan a decidir por definición y contexto, no por parecido.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Traducción precisa

Elige el único significado que corresponde a la palabra.

1. recession
   Opciones: *periodo de declive económico · subida de precios · tipo de interés*
2. inflation
   Opciones: *subida general de precios · quiebra · paquete de estímulo*
3. bankruptcy
   Opciones: *quiebra legal de una empresa · préstamo · dividendo*
4. portfolio
   Opciones: *cartera de inversiones · cuenta de ahorro · hipoteca*
5. dividend
   Opciones: *pago a los accionistas · comisión · deuda*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **periodo de declive económico**
2. **subida general de precios**
3. **quiebra legal de una empresa**
4. **cartera de inversiones**
5. **pago a los accionistas**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige por definición

Completa cada definición con una de las tres opciones.

6. The general rise in prices across an economy is called ___.
   Opciones: *inflation · recession · currency*
7. A loan secured against property is a ___.
   Opciones: *mortgage · portfolio · dividend*
8. Money lent by a bank that must be repaid with interest is a ___.
   Opciones: *loan · dividend · bankruptcy*
9. The percentage charged for borrowing money is the ___.
   Opciones: *interest rate · stimulus · currency*
10. A government spending package to boost the economy is a ___.
   Opciones: *stimulus package · mortgage · portfolio*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **inflation**
7. **mortgage**
8. **loan**
9. **interest rate**
10. **stimulus package**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Completa el chunk

Escribe la expresión exacta que completa la situación; todas pertenecen al inventario de la unidad.

11. The country needs to reduce its national ___.
12. Many companies are facing ___.
13. She has a diversified ___ of shares.
14. The government announced a new ___.
15. There is a lack of ___ confidence.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **debt**
12. **bankruptcy**
13. **portfolio**
14. **stimulus package**
15. **consumer**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: The 2008 financial crisis

**Objetivo:** comprender la idea global, localizar datos y reconocer las estructuras objetivo en contexto.

> The 2008 global financial crisis began with a housing market bubble fuelled by subprime mortgages. Banks had lent to high-risk borrowers, and when property prices collapsed, defaults soared. If only regulators had monitored lending practices more closely, the crash wouldn't have been so devastating. Many homeowners wish they had read the fine print before signing adjustable-rate mortgages. Governments responded with massive bailouts and stimulus packages. If the central banks hadn't injected liquidity, the recession would have deepened into a depression. The crisis revealed that financial stability depends on transparency, diversification and prudent oversight.

Completa con las palabras exactas del texto.

6. Completa: Banks ___ to high-risk borrowers.
7. Completa: If only regulators ___ lending practices more closely.
8. Completa: The crash ___ so devastating.
9. Completa: If the central banks hadn't injected liquidity, the recession ___.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma, significado y secuencia

12. Identifica: *would have deepened* es el resultado de un ___ conditional.
13. Ordena: bubble → defaults → bailouts → recovery.
14. Why does the author mention the fine print?
15. Nombra dos estructuras de lamento o hipótesis presentes en el texto.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

13. **bubble → defaults → bailouts → recovery**.
14. **To show that homeowners didn't understand the risk of adjustable rates.**
15. **Wish/if only + past perfect y third conditional.**

## Lección 5 — Writing y Speaking

**Objetivo:** producir mensajes controlados con *wish/if only* y *third conditional* junto con vocabulario económico.

### Ejercicios 1–10 — Writing con modelo

Respeta la extensión y usa todas las expresiones indicadas.

1. Completa: *I wish I ___ (save) more money during the boom.*
2. Transforma: *I didn't diversify my portfolio. I lost everything.* → Third conditional.
3. Transforma: *The government didn't act sooner. The recession deepened.* → Third conditional.
4. Escribe 35–45 palabras con un *wish* y dos términos económicos.
5. Escribe 35–45 palabras con un *third conditional* y dos términos económicos.
6. Escribe 45–55 palabras combinando un *wish*, un *third conditional* y tres términos económicos.
7. Describe un arrepentimiento financiero en 40–50 palabras.
8. Escribe 50–60 palabras conectando cinco términos económicos.
9. Escribe 80–100 palabras como resumen de una crisis hipotética evitada.
10. Escribe 100–120 palabras combinando dos *wish*, dos *third conditionals* y diez términos económicos.

<details>
<summary>Ver modelos de writing</summary>

1. I wish I **had saved** more money during the boom.
2. If I **had diversified** my portfolio, I **wouldn't have lost** everything.
3. If the government **had acted** sooner, the recession **wouldn't have deepened**.
4. Modelo: I wish I had invested in renewable energy before the boom. Inflation eroded my savings, and the recession exposed how fragile my portfolio was.
5. Modelo: If the central bank had lowered interest rates last year, the recovery would have been faster and unemployment wouldn't have peaked so high.
6. Modelo: I wish I had read the fine print before signing the mortgage. If I had chosen a fixed rate, I wouldn't have faced bankruptcy when interest rates surged. Diversification saved what little remained.
7. Modelo: My biggest financial regret is not buying property in 2024. I wish I had taken out a mortgage when prices were low. If I had acted then, I would have built equity instead of paying rising rent during the recession.
8. Modelo: The recession exposed deep vulnerabilities. Inflation surged, interest rates climbed, and consumer confidence collapsed. Many households wished they had managed debt better. A targeted stimulus package stabilised the currency, but recovery depends on diversified portfolios and prudent oversight.
9. Modelo: If regulators had monitored subprime lending more closely, the housing bubble wouldn't have formed. Banks wouldn't have faced bankruptcy, and the government wouldn't have needed a massive bailout. The recession would have been avoided, consumer confidence would have remained strong, and the stock market would have continued its steady growth.
10. Modelo: The 2026 recession exposed deep vulnerabilities in the banking sector. I wish regulators had monitored lending practices more closely, and many households wish they hadn't taken out such large loans during the boom. If the central bank had raised interest rates earlier, inflation wouldn't have reached record levels. If the government had provided a targeted stimulus package, the recovery would have been faster. The stock market crashed, bankruptcy soared, and consumer confidence collapsed. Diversified portfolios weathered the storm far better than concentrated investments, proving that debt management, diversification and prudent oversight remain the best defences against economic volatility.

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Speaking con guion y pistas

Prepara durante 30 segundos y habla sin leer.

11. Expresa un arrepentimiento financiero personal usando *wish*.
12. Describe una decisión económica hipotética con *third conditional*.
13. Role-play entre inversor y asesor sobre una cartera mal diversificada.
14. Conecta cinco términos económicos en un monólogo de 60 segundos.
15. Resume la lección en 90 segundos usando dos *wish*, dos *third conditionals* y diez términos.

<details>
<summary>Ver guiones y pistas de speaking</summary>

11. Guion modelo: I wish I had saved more during the boom. If only I had diversified my portfolio before the crash.
12. Guion modelo: If I had bought property when prices were low, I would have built equity instead of facing rising rent during the recession.
13. A: I lost a fortune. B: If you had diversified across sectors, you wouldn't have lost everything. A: I wish I had read the fine print. B: Always check adjustable-rate clauses.
14. Guion modelo: The recession pushed inflation to record highs. Interest rates climbed, consumer confidence collapsed, and many households faced bankruptcy. A stimulus package stabilised the currency, but recovery depends on diversified portfolios and prudent debt management.
15. Pistas: usa el mismo inventario del writing 10; marca cuatro estructuras en tus notas y comprueba cada una al terminar.

</details>

## Cómo revisar tus resultados

Clasifica cada fallo como **significado**, **forma**, **tiempo**, **concordancia**, **orden** o **vocabulario**. Copia la corrección mínima y crea una frase nueva con el mismo patrón. Después vuelve a intentarlo sin opciones.

Para consolidar, repite las expresiones mañana, dentro de tres días y una semana después. Reduce el apoyo en cada revisión: primero opciones, después una pista en español y finalmente una situación de 45 segundos.
