La **Unidad 59** prepara para las pruebas de **Writing** y **Speaking** del examen **Cambridge B2 First**. En Writing, debes producir textos de 140–190 palabras con estructura clara, registro adecuado y variedad gramatical. En Speaking, debes demostrar fluidez, precisión léxica y capacidad interactiva.

## 1. Writing Part 1: Essay

El **essay** es obligatorio en la Part 1 del Writing. Recibirás un tema y dos prompts que debes cubrir. El texto debe tener **140–190 palabras**.

### 1.1 Estructura del Essay

Un essay B2 sigue una estructura de **5 partes**:

| Parte | Función | Palabras aprox. |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| Introducción | Presentar el tema + postura | 20–30 |
| Argumento 1 | Primer argumento + ejemplo | 35–50 |
| Argumento 2 | Segundo argumento + ejemplo | 35–50 |
| Contraste / Argumento 3 | Contraargumento o tercer punto | 30–40 |
| Conclusión | Resumen + opinión final | 20–30 |

### 1.2 Introducción

La introducción debe ser breve y clara. Presenta el tema y expresa tu postura en una o dos oraciones.

**Modelo:**

> *Nowadays, many people believe that university education should be free for everyone. In this essay, I will discuss whether this is a realistic and beneficial idea.*

**Phrases útiles para introducir el tema:**

| Expresión | Ejemplo |
|-----------|---------|
| Nowadays, it is commonly believed that... | *Nowadays, it is commonly believed that technology improves education.* |
| There is a growing debate about... | *There is a growing debate about climate change policies.* |
| One of the most discussed issues today is... | *One of the most discussed issues today is social media addiction.* |

### 1.3 Párrafos del cuerpo

Cada párrafos del cuerpo debe contener:
1. Una **idea principal** (topic sentence)
2. Un **argumento** que la desarrolle
3. Un **ejemplo** concreto

**Estructura de un párrafos:**

> *Firstly, I believe that free university education would reduce social inequality. If students from low-income backgrounds could study without financial pressure, they would have the same opportunities as wealthier students. For example, in countries like Germany, where public universities charge no tuition fees, the number of students from disadvantaged backgrounds has increased significantly.*

### 1.4 Conclusión

La conclusión resume tus puntos principales y reafirma tu postura.

> *In conclusion, while free university education may seem expensive, I believe it is a worthwhile investment for any society, as it promotes equality and economic growth.*

### 1.5 Linkers esenciales

| Tipo | Linkers | Ejemplo |
|------|---------|---------|
| Adición | moreover, furthermore, in addition | *Moreover, it benefits the economy.* |
| Contraste | however, on the other hand, although | *However, some people disagree.* |
| Causa | because, since, due to | *Since education is a right, it should be free.* |
| Ejemplo | for instance, such as, for example | *For instance, Finland provides free education.* |
| Conclusión | in conclusion, to sum up, overall | *In conclusion, the benefits outweigh the costs.* |

### 1.6 Registro formal

En un essay debes usar un **registro formal**. Evita:

| ❌ Informal | ✅ Formal |
|-------------|----------|
| a lot of | many / numerous |
| kids | children |
| get | obtain / acquire |
| nice | pleasant / enjoyable |
| really | extremely / highly |

### 1.7 Errores comunes en el Essay

1. **No responder a ambos prompts** del examen
2. **Exceder 190 palabras** (pierde tiempo)
3. **Usar registro informal** (contracciones, slang)
4. **No usar linkers** variados
5. **Repetir la misma idea** en distintos párrafos
6. **No tener conclusión clara**

## 2. Writing Part 2: Review

El **review** evalúa tu opinión sobre un producto, servicio, lugar o experiencia. Es la opción más común de la Part 2.

### 2.1 Estructura del Review

| Parte | Función | Palabras aprox. |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| Título | Atraer al lector | 3–6 palabras |
| Introducción | Presentar lo reseñado | 20–30 |
| Párrafos de opinión | Ventajas / desventajas | 50–70 |
| Recomendación final | Conclusión + recomendación | 20–30 |

### 2.2 Modelo de Review

> **A Hidden Gem in Barcelona**
>
> Last summer, I visited a small café called *La Terraza* in the heart of Barcelona. It turned out to be one of the best dining experiences I have ever had.
>
> The first thing that impressed me was the atmosphere. The décor was modern yet cosy, and the staff were incredibly friendly. The menu offered a wide variety of dishes, from traditional Spanish tapas to innovative fusion cuisine. I particularly enjoyed the grilled octopus, which was perfectly cooked.
>
> On the downside, the prices were slightly above average, and the café can get very crowded during weekends. However, I think the quality of the food and service makes up for it.
>
> All in all, I would highly recommend *La Terraza* to anyone visiting Barcelona. It is an ideal spot for a relaxed lunch or a special dinner with friends.

### 2.3 Useful Expressions para Reviews

| Función | Expresiones |
|---------|-------------|
| Opinión personal | I think / I believe / In my opinion / Personally |
| Fortalezas | The best thing about... / What I liked most was... / One advantage is... |
| Debilidades | On the downside / The only drawback was... / Unfortunately... |
| Recomendación | I would definitely recommend... / It is worth visiting... / I suggest... |
| Experiencia | I had the opportunity to... / I was lucky enough to... |

## 3. Writing Part 2: Formal Email / Letter

El **formal email/letter** se escribe a una persona o institución. Debe mantener un tono respetuoso y estructura clara.

### 3.1 Estructura

| Parte | Función |
|-------|---------|
| Saludo | Dear Sir/Madam / Dear Mr + apellido |
| Introducción | Expresar el motivo de la carta |
| Cuerpo | Detallar la solicitud, queja o petición |
| Cierre | Expresar esperanza de respuesta |
| Despedida | Yours faithfully (sin nombre) / Yours sincerely (con nombre) |

### 3.2 Modelo de Formal Letter

> Dear Sir or Madam,
>
> I am writing to enquire about the English language course advertised on your website. I would be grateful if you could provide me with some further information.
>
> Firstly, I would like to know the exact dates of the summer course and whether accommodation is included in the price. Additionally, could you tell me if there are any discounts available for students who book early?
>
> I am particularly interested in improving my writing skills, so I would also appreciate it if you could confirm whether the course focuses on academic or creative writing.
>
> I look forward to hearing from you at your earliest convenience.
>
> Yours faithfully,
> María López

### 3.3 Useful Phrases para Formal Letters

| Función | Expresión |
|---------|-----------|
| Petición | I would be grateful if you could... / I would like to enquire about... / Could you please...? |
| Queja | I am writing to complain about... / I am disappointed with... / I wish to express my dissatisfaction... |
| Sugerencia | I would suggest that... / It might be advisable to... / Perhaps you could consider... |
| Agradecimiento | Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter. / I appreciate your help. |

## 4. Speaking Part 1: Interview

La Part 1 dura **3–4 minutos**. El examinador te hará preguntas personales sobre ti, tu vida y tus experiencias.

### 4.1 Tipos de Preguntas

| Tema | Ejemplos de preguntas |
|------|----------------------|
| Trabajo / Estudios | What do you do for a living? / What are you studying at the moment? |
| Hobbies | What do you enjoy doing in your free time? / Have you got any hobbies? |
| Futuro | What are your plans for the future? / Where do you see yourself in five years? |
| Experiencias | Have you ever travelled abroad? / What was the best holiday you've ever had? |

### 4.2 Model Answers

**Pregunta:** *What do you enjoy doing in your free time?*

> *Well, I'm really into photography, actually. I enjoy taking pictures of nature and landscapes, especially during weekends when I go hiking. It helps me relax and be creative at the same time.*

**Pregunta:** *What are your plans for the future?*

> *In the near future, I'm planning to improve my English so that I can apply for a job in an international company. I'd also like to travel more, perhaps visit some English-speaking countries to practise the language in real-life situations.*

**Consejos para la Part 1:**
- No responda con monosílabos; amplía la respuesta
- Usa tiempos variados (presente, pasado, futuro)
- Añade detalles personales

## 5. Speaking Part 2: Individual Long Turn

La Part 2 es un monólogo de **1–2 minutos**. Recibirás dos fotos o imágenes y deberás compararlas, describirlas o especular.

### 5.1 Estructura del Long Turn

| Paso | Función | Tiempo |
|------|---------|--------|
| 1. Describir | Describir las imágenes | 30 seg |
| 2. Comparar | Señalar similitudes y diferencias | 30 seg |
| 3. Especular / Recomendar | Dar tu opinión o recomendar | 30 seg |

### 5.2 Modelo de Long Turn

**Tema:** *These are two photos of people doing different sports outdoors.*

> *In the first photo, I can see a group of young people playing football in a park. They seem to be enjoying themselves, and the weather looks warm and sunny. In the second picture, there is a woman swimming in a lake surrounded by mountains. It looks like a very peaceful and natural setting.*
>
> *Both activities take place outdoors, but they are quite different. While football is a team sport and requires a lot of energy, swimming is an individual activity and can be very relaxing. The environments are also different: the first is in an urban park, whereas the second is in a remote natural area.*
>
> *I personally prefer swimming in natural settings because it helps me disconnect from daily stress. However, I think both activities are great for staying healthy and enjoying nature.*

### 5.3 Useful Phrases para Part 2

| Función | Expresiones |
|---------|-------------|
| Describir | In the first picture, I can see... / What strikes me about this image is... |
| Comparar | Both photos show... / While the first image..., the second one... / On the one hand... on the other hand... |
| Especular | It looks like... / I imagine that... / Perhaps... / They might be... |
| Recomendar | I would recommend... / I think this activity is ideal for... |

## 6. Speaking Part 3 & 4: Collaborative Task and Discussion

### 6.1 Part 3: Collaborative Task (4 minutos)

Recibirás un prompt con información y deberás **interactuar con tu compañero** durante 3 minutos, seguidos de una pregunta al final.

**Estrategias:**
- **Inicia** la conversación con confianza
- **Escucha** activamente a tu compañero
- **Añade ideas** y reacciona a las de tu compañero
- Usa phrases como: *That's a good point. / I see what you mean, but... / I agree, and I'd also add that...*

### 6.2 Part 4: Discussion (4 minutos)

El examinador hará preguntas más generales relacionadas con el tema. Debes **opinar con razones**.

**Modelo de pregunta:** *Do you think it is important for young people to learn a foreign language?*

> *Absolutely. I think it's essential nowadays because we live in a globalised world. Speaking another language opens doors to new cultures, job opportunities, and even different ways of thinking. Besides, studies have shown that learning languages improves cognitive abilities and memory.*

### 6.3 Useful Phrases para Parts 3 & 4

| Función | Expresiones |
|---------|-------------|
| Opinar | In my opinion... / From my point of view... / I believe that... |
| Añadir | What's more... / On top of that... / Another thing worth mentioning is... |
| Disagree cortésmente | I see your point, but... / I'm not entirely sure about that. / That may be true, however... |
| Dar ejemplos | For example... / To give you an example... / Take... as an example. |
| Concluir | So basically... / All things considered... / To sum up what we've discussed... |

## 7. Useful Phrases: Writing and Speaking

### 7.1 Writing — Linkers y Connectores

| Tipo | Expresiones |
|------|-------------|
| Adición | moreover, furthermore, in addition, besides, what is more |
| Contraste | however, nevertheless, on the other hand, although, despite |
| Causa-efecto | due to, as a result of, consequently, therefore, thus |
| Ejemplo | for instance, for example, such as, to illustrate, namely |
| Conclusión | in conclusion, to sum up, overall, all in all, taking everything into account |

### 7.2 Speaking — Interaction Phrases

| Función | Expresiones |
|---------|-------------|
| Opinar | I think... / In my view... / Personally, I feel that... |
| Preguntar | What do you think about...? / How do you feel about...? / Don't you think...? |
| Estar de acuerdo | I completely agree. / That's exactly what I think. / You're absolutely right. |
| Disagree | I'm not so sure about that. / I see your point, but I tend to think... |
| Resumir | So, to summarise... / Basically, we both agree that... |

## 8. Modelo de Essay Completo

**Tema:** *Some people believe that children should learn to cook at school. Others think it is better to learn at home. Discuss both views and give your opinion.*

> Nowadays, there is an ongoing debate about whether cooking should be taught in schools or at home. In this essay, I will examine both perspectives and give my own opinion.
>
> On the one hand, supporters of cooking classes in schools argue that it is a valuable life skill. If children learn to prepare healthy meals at school, they will be better equipped to look after themselves as adults. For example, in Japan, home economics is a compulsory subject, and as a result, Japanese teenagers are generally more independent in the kitchen.
>
> On the other hand, many people believe that cooking is best learned within the family. Parents can pass down traditional recipes and spending time cooking together strengthens family bonds. Furthermore, not all schools have the budget or facilities to offer proper cooking lessons.
>
> In my opinion, although learning at home has clear advantages, schools should still offer basic cooking classes. This way, every child, regardless of their family background, would have the opportunity to acquire this essential skill.
>
> *(162 words)*

## 9. Errores Comunes en Writing y Speaking

| # | Error | Corrección |
|---|-------|------------|
| 1 | *I have 25 years.* | *I am 25 years old.* |
| 2 | *The reason is because...* | *The reason is that...* |
| 3 | *Despite of the cost...* | *Despite the cost... / In spite of the cost...* |
| 4 | *I would like to know that...* | *I would like to know whether...* |
| 5 | *He suggested me to go.* | *He suggested that I should go.* |
| 6 | *The informations are...* | *The information is...* (uncountable) |
| 7 | *I am agree with you.* | *I agree with you.* |
| 8 | *In my opinion, I think...* | Use one or the other, not both. |
