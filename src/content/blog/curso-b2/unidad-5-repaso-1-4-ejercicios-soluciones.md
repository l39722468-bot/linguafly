## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** wish, future tenses, gerund vs infinitive, verb + object + inf

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I wish I ___ started revising earlier. → *have / had / has*
2. By Friday she ___ have submitted both essays. → *will / is / would*
3. She enjoys ___ case studies. → *analyse / analysing / to analyse*
4. Her professor persuaded her ___ present. → *to / for / -*
5. If I had read the guide, I ___ understand now. → *will / would / am*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **had** · 2. **will** · 3. **analysing** · 4. **to** · 5. **would**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. ___ reviewed units 1–4, she feels ready. → *Having / Have / Had*
2. He avoids ___ tasks until the last day. → *leave / leaving / to leave*
3. I am going to ___ the draft tonight. → *finish / finishing / to finish*
4. I wish we ___ more time for leisure. → *have / had / would have*
5. She told him ___ check the data again. → *to / for / -*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Having** · 2. **leaving** · 3. **finish** · 4. **had** · 5. **to**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *By Friday I will submit both essays.*
2. *I wish I have more discipline.*
3. *She enjoys to analyse data.*
4. *Having review the notes, she left.*
5. *If she studied B1, she would pass now.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. By Friday I **will have submitted** both essays.
2. I wish I **had** more discipline.
3. She enjoys **analysing** data.
4. Having **reviewed** the notes, she left.
5. If she **had studied** B1, she **would pass** now.

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Una frase de cada: wish, future perfect, gerund, obj + inf, participle.
2. Completa: I wish I ___ (start) revising earlier.
3. Completa: By Friday she ___ (submit) both essays.
4. Completa: She enjoys ___ (analyse) data.
5. Completa: Her professor persuaded her ___ (present).
6. Completa: ___ (review) units 1–4, she feels ready.
7. Mixed conditional sobre B2.
8. Corrige: *By Friday I will submit both.*
9. Corrige: *I wish I have more time.*
10. Mini-historia (5 frases) mezclando U1–4.
11. Traduce: Ojalá hubiera empezado antes.
12. Traduce: Para el viernes habrá entregado los ensayos.
13. Matching: estructura → unidad.
14. Autochequeo con el mapa U5.
15. Escribe Having + pp + main clause.
16. Enlace al curso U5.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open — one of each structure from U1–4.
2. **had started**
3. **will have submitted**
4. **analysing**
5. **to present**
6. Having **reviewed**
7. If I **had studied** mixed conditionals, I **would feel** confident now.
8. By Friday she **will have submitted** both essays.
9. I wish I **had** more time.
10. Open mixed paragraph.
11. I wish I **had started** earlier.
12. By Friday she **will have submitted** the essays.
13. wish→U1; future perfect→U2; gerund→U3; obj+inf→U4.
14. Self-check vs review map.
15. Having **finished** the exercises, I took a break.
16. **/curso-b2/unit-5**

</details>
