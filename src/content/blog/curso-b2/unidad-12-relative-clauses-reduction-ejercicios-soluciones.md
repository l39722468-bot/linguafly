## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** -ing / -ed reduced relative clauses

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. People ___ the premiere waited outside. → *attending / attended / attend*
2. The soundtrack ___ last month won an award. → *recorded / recording / record*
3. Volunteers ___ at the exhibition greeted guests. → *helping / helped / help*
4. The compost ___ in the garden is ready. → *produced / producing / produce*
5. -ing reduction usually has ___ meaning. → *active / passive / future*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **attending** · 2. **recorded** · 3. **helping** · 4. **produced** · 5. **active**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. -ed reduction usually has ___ meaning. → *passive / active / question*
2. People who are attending → People ___. → *attending / attended / attend*
3. The film which was recorded → The film ___. → *recorded / recording / record*
4. Seedlings ___ in the greenhouse grew fast. → *growing / grown / grow*
5. Tickets ___ online sold out. → *bought / buying / buy*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **passive** · 2. **attending** · 3. **recorded** · 4. **growing** · 5. **bought**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *People who attending the show waited.*
2. *The soundtrack recording last month won.*
3. *Volunteers helped at the show greeted us.* (reduce)
4. *The compost producing in the garden is ready.*
5. *Guests who are waiting outside…* (reduce)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. People **attending** the show waited.
2. The soundtrack **recorded** last month won.
3. Volunteers **helping** at the show greeted us.
4. The compost **produced** in the garden is ready.
5. Guests **waiting** outside…

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 2 reducciones -ing y 2 -ed.
2. Completa: People ___ (attend) the premiere waited.
3. Completa: The soundtrack ___ (record) last month won.
4. Completa: Volunteers ___ (help) at the show smiled.
5. Completa: The compost ___ (produce) is ready.
6. Explica -ing vs -ed en 1 frase.
7. Corrige: *People who attending waited.*
8. Corrige: *The film recording last year won.* (passive idea)
9. Párrafo entertainment/gardening con reduced relatives.
10. Traduce: La gente que asiste al estreno esperó fuera.
11. Traduce: El compost producido en el jardín está listo.
12. Reduce: The guests who are waiting…
13. Reduce: The tickets which were sold online…
14. Autochequeo activo/pasivo.
15. Enlace teoría U12.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open — two -ing and two -ed.
2. **attending**
3. **recorded**
4. **helping**
5. **produced**
6. -ing ≈ active; -ed ≈ passive.
7. People **attending** waited.
8. The film **recorded** last year won.
9. Open paragraph.
10. People **attending** the premiere waited outside.
11. The compost **produced** in the garden is ready.
12. Guests **waiting**…
13. Tickets **sold** online…
14. Self-check.
15. [Guía U12](/blog/curso-b2/unidad-12-relative-clauses-reduction)

</details>
