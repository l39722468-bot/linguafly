## Lección 1 — Gramática y Chunks

### 1.1 Linkers of Contrast

**Elige el linker correcto: although / despite / whereas / however**

1. ______ the summit was a success, several delegates expressed frustration.
2. ______ the economic downturn, the government maintained its spending levels.
3. ______ the US promotes free trade, the EU tends to adopt protectionist measures.
4. The treaty was signed in 2015; ______, implementation has been disappointing.
5. ______ numerous calls for reform, the parliament took no action.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

1. **Although** — *although* + clause (sujeto + verbo: *the summit was a success*)
2. **Despite** — *despite* + noun (*the economic downturn*)
3. **Whereas** — *whereas* compara dos situaciones
4. **however** — *however* conecta oraciones completas con punto y coma
5. **Despite** — *despite* + noun (*numerous calls for reform*)

</details>

### 1.2 Linkers of Reason / Purpose / Result

**Elige el linker correcto: because / so that / therefore / as a result**

1. The economy contracted sharply; ______, unemployment reached 12%.
2. The ambassador was recalled ______ she publicly criticised the government.
3. Troops were deployed ______ violence could be contained.
4. The government raised taxes. ______, public trust declined further.
5. The UN imposed an arms embargo ______ the conflict was escalating rapidly.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

1. **therefore** — *therefore* conecta consecuencia lógica entre oraciones
2. **because** — *because* + clause introduciendo la razón
3. **so that** — *so that* + clause con modal (*could*) para expresar propósito
4. **As a result** — *as a result* introduce la consecuencia
5. **because** — *because* + clause (razón: *the conflict was escalating*)

</details>

### 1.3 Linkers of Addition

**Elige el linker correcto: moreover / furthermore / in addition / not only**

1. ______ the president refused to negotiate, ______ the opposition threatened to boycott.
2. The country faces rising inflation. ______, its national debt has doubled.
3. ______ to military intervention, economic sanctions were imposed.
4. The peace deal included territorial concessions. ______, it established a framework for power sharing.
5. ______ did the ambassador resign, but also the entire cabinet stepped down.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

1. **Not only... but also** — estructura *not only* + inversión + *but also*
2. **Moreover** — *moreover* añade un argumento adicional con coma
3. **In addition** — *in addition to* + noun (*military intervention*)
4. **Furthermore** — *furthermore* añade información adicional formal
5. **Not only** — *Not only* + did + sujeto + verbo (inversión obligatoria)

</details>

### 1.4 Mixed Linkers

**Elige el linker correcto: although / as a result / moreover / despite / in order to / nevertheless**

1. The talks collapsed. ______, both sides returned to their positions.
2. ______ the economic benefits, many residents opposed the construction.
3. The agreement was praised internationally. ______, critics warned it was unenforceable.
4. ______ several attempts at mediation, the conflict continued unabated.
5. The government increased border patrols ______ prevent illegal crossings.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

1. **As a result** — *as a result* introduce consecuencia
2. **Despite** — *despite* + noun (*the economic benefits*)
3. **Nevertheless** — *nevertheless* expresa contraste entre oraciones completas
4. **Despite** — *despite* + noun (*several attempts at mediation*)
5. **in order to** — *in order to* + infinitivo para expresar propósito

</details>

### 2.2 Definitions: Elige la collocation correcta

**Lee la definición y elige la collocation (a–e):**

1. A formal agreement between two countries signed by their leaders. → (a) foreign policy (b) sign a treaty (c) trade agreement
2. The total set of strategies a country uses in its international dealings. → (a) foreign policy (b) bilateral relations (c) human rights
3. An agreement focused on commerce between nations. → (a) trade agreement (b) political alliance (c) peace talks
4. A coalition of parties working together for shared political goals. → (a) nuclear disarmament (b) political alliance (c) referendum
5. Conversations aimed at ending a armed conflict. → (a) peace talks (b) regime change (c) cross-border

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

1. **(b) sign a treaty**
2. **(a) foreign policy**
3. **(a) trade agreement**
4. **(b) political alliance**
5. **(a) peace talks**

</details>

### 2.3 Chunks: Completa con la collocation adecuada

**Completa cada frase con una de las collocations de la lista:**

*hold elections — conduct negotiations — maintain stability — economic sanctions — diplomatic ties*

1. The ambassador was expelled and ______ between the two countries were severed.
2. After months of stalemate, both parties agreed to ______ again.
3. The government plans to ______ in the spring of next year.
4. The peacekeeping force was deployed to ______ in the region.
5. ______ were imposed in response to the annexation of territory.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

1. **diplomatic ties** — *severed diplomatic ties* es una collocation frecuente
2. **conduct negotiations** — *conduct negotiations* es el verbo formal correcto
3. **hold elections** — *hold elections* = convocar elecciones
4. **maintain stability** — *maintain stability in the region*
5. **Economic sanctions** — *economic sanctions were imposed*

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading

Lee el siguiente texto sobre diplomacia internacional y responde las preguntas.

![International Diplomacy](/blog/curso-b2/unit-54/diplomacy.png)

International diplomacy is a complex and often unpredictable process. Although the United Nations was established to maintain global peace, its effectiveness has been questioned on numerous occasions. Despite having the authority to impose sanctions and authorise military intervention, the Security Council is frequently paralysed by the veto power of its permanent members.

Moreover, the rise of non-state actors has complicated traditional diplomatic frameworks. In addition to nation-states, organisations such as the WTO, the EU, and regional blocs now play significant roles in international relations. Furthermore, bilateral relations between major powers — such as the US, China, and Russia — often determine the outcome of multilateral negotiations.

In recent years, economic sanctions have become one of the most widely used tools of foreign policy. Although they are intended to pressure regimes into compliance, critics argue that sanctions disproportionately affect civilian populations. Nevertheless, proponents maintain that targeted economic measures remain the most humane alternative to military action.

As a result of these dynamics, diplomats must navigate an increasingly fragmented landscape. Conduct negotiations, reach agreements, and maintain stability require not only political skill but also a deep understanding of cultural and historical contexts. Consequently, training in international relations has never been more important.

## Lección 5 — Writing y Speaking

### Writing (Ejercicios 1–10)

**Ejercicio 1:** Transforma estas oraciones simples en una sola oración usando *although*.

The economy was growing. Unemployment remained high.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**Although** the economy was growing, unemployment remained high.

</details>

**Ejercicio 2:** Reescribe esta oración usando *despite* + noun.

Although the government raised taxes, the deficit increased.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**Despite** the tax increase, the deficit increased.

*o bien:* **Despite** the government's tax increase, the deficit increased.

</details>

**Ejercicio 3:** Conecta estas dos oraciones con *therefore*.

Trade volumes fell. The government introduced stimulus measures.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

Trade volumes fell. **Therefore**, the government introduced stimulus measures.

</details>

**Ejercicio 4:** Escribe una oración de propósito usando *in order to*.

Use: stabilise — region — deploy troops

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**In order to** stabilise the region, troops were deployed.

</details>

**Ejercicio 5:** Escribe una oración con *not only... but also* incluyendo inversión.

Use: ambassador resign — foreign minister resign

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**Not only did the ambassador resign**, but the foreign minister also stepped down.

</details>

**Ejercicio 6:** Reescribe usando *consequently*.

The ceasefire collapsed. Civilian casualties increased dramatically.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

The ceasefire collapsed. **Consequently**, civilian casualties increased dramatically.

</details>

**Ejercicio 7:** Escribe un párrafo de 40–60 palabras sobre por qué los acuerdos comerciales son importantes. Usa al menos 3 collocations y 2 linkers.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**Modelo de respuesta:**

International trade agreements are essential for economic growth. Moreover, they help maintain stability between neighbouring nations. When countries reach an agreement on tariffs, bilateral relations tend to improve. Furthermore, trade agreements create jobs and reduce poverty. Therefore, governments should prioritise diplomatic negotiations to reach mutually beneficial deals.

*Collocations usadas:* trade agreements, maintain stability, reach an agreement, bilateral relations, diplomatic negotiations. *Linkers usadas:* moreover, furthermore, therefore.

</details>

**Ejercicio 8:** Corrige los 5 errores en este párrafo.

> Despite it was a difficult time, the government held elections. Moreover of the protests, the referendum went ahead. Both the economy and is fragile. In addition for trade, diplomatic ties were strengthened. Therefore, the president acted quickly, so that public confidence could be restored.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

> **Although** it was a difficult time, the government held elections. **Despite** the protests, the referendum went ahead. **Both** the economy **and** the currency are fragile. **In addition to** trade, diplomatic ties were strengthened. **Therefore**, the president acted quickly **so that** public confidence could be restored.

*Errores corregidos:*
1. ~~Despite it was~~ → **Although it was** (although + clause)
2. ~~Moreover of~~ → **Despite** (despite + noun)
3. ~~and is~~ → **and the currency are** (both... and parallel)
4. ~~In addition for~~ → **In addition to** (preposición correcta)
5. ~~Therefore,~~ before the cause → reordered (therefore conecta consecuencia)

</details>

**Ejercicio 9:** Escribe un informe formal de 80–100 palabras sobre una crisis diplomática. Usa: *despite*, *in order to*, *as a result*, *bilateral relations*, *impose sanctions*, *reach an agreement*.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**Modelo de respuesta:**

The diplomatic crisis between the two nations escalated rapidly in January. Despite several rounds of mediation, neither side was willing to compromise on territorial claims. In order to de-escalate tensions, the UN Security Council convened an emergency session. As a result, both countries agreed to a temporary ceasefire. However, reaching a lasting agreement will require sustained diplomatic engagement. The international community has called on both governments to maintain stability and avoid imposing sanctions that could further damage bilateral relations.

</details>

**Ejercicio 10:** Completa este email formal con los linkers y collocations correctos.

> Dear Minister,
>
> I am writing to inform you that ______ [although/despite] our best efforts, the trade negotiations have ______ [broken down / hold elections]. ______ [Moreover / Despite] the economic benefits of a deal, both sides remain divided on key issues. ______ [In order to / Because of] prevent further escalation, I recommend that ______ [bilateral relations / trade agreement] be prioritised at the upcoming summit.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

> Dear Minister,
>
> I am writing to inform you that **although** our best efforts, the trade negotiations have **broken down**. **Despite** the economic benefits of a deal, both sides remain divided on key issues. **In order to** prevent further escalation, I recommend that **bilateral relations** be prioritised at the upcoming summit.

</details>

### Speaking (Ejercicios 11–15)

**Ejercicio 11:** Prepárate para hablar durante 2 minutos sobre el siguiente tema. Usa al menos 3 collocations y 2 linkers.

*"Should countries impose economic sanctions to resolve international conflicts?"*

Estructura sugerida:
- Opinión personal
- Argumento a favor (con linker de adición)
- Argumento en contra (con linker de contraste)
- Conclusión (con linker de resultado)

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**Modelo de respuesta (guion):**

In my opinion, economic sanctions can be an effective tool in international relations, although they must be used carefully. **Moreover**, they offer a non-violent alternative to military intervention. **For instance**, when the UN imposed sanctions on a regime that violated human rights, it sent a strong diplomatic message.

**However**, I also believe that sanctions can disproportionately affect ordinary citizens. **Despite** their intended targets being governments, it is often civilians who suffer the most. **Furthermore**, history shows that sanctions rarely achieve their stated goals quickly.

**Consequently**, I think sanctions should be part of a broader strategy that includes diplomatic ties and peace talks. **In order to** resolve conflicts effectively, nations must reach agreements through multilateral negotiations rather than relying solely on economic pressure.

</details>

**Ejercicio 12:** Practica esta respuesta exprés (30–45 segundos). Usa *although*, *therefore* y *reach an agreement*.

*"What do you think is the biggest challenge in international diplomacy?"*

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**Modelo de respuesta:**

The biggest challenge in international diplomacy is achieving consensus among nations with conflicting interests. Although organisations like the UN provide a platform for dialogue, reaching an agreement on issues such as climate change or nuclear disarmament remains extremely difficult. Different political systems, economic priorities, and cultural perspectives often create deadlocks. Therefore, I believe that patience, compromise, and sustained bilateral relations are essential to overcome these obstacles.

</details>

**Ejercicio 13:** Debate en pareja. Tema: *"Should the EU impose sanctions on countries that violate human rights?"*

Usa estas frases en tu debate:
- *"Not only... but also"*
- *"Nevertheless"*
- *"In order to"*
- *"bilateral relations"*

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**Modelo de debate:**

**A:** I think the EU should impose sanctions. Not only would this demonstrate a commitment to human rights, but it would also pressure violators to change their behaviour.

**B:** I see your point. Nevertheless, history shows that sanctions often harm civilians more than governments. Moreover, they can damage bilateral relations.

**A:** That's a fair point. In order to avoid that, perhaps the EU should impose targeted sanctions on specific individuals rather than broad economic measures.

**B:** I agree. Furthermore, diplomatic ties should be maintained so that dialogue remains possible.

</details>

**Ejercicio 14:** Describe esta situación usando al menos 4 collocations.

*"Two neighbouring countries have been in conflict for years. A mediator has been appointed to help them negotiate."*

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**Modelo de respuesta:**

Two neighbouring countries have been in conflict for years, despite numerous attempts at mediation. The dispute has affected bilateral relations and led to the severing of diplomatic ties. Both nations have imposed economic sanctions on each other, which has damaged trade and increased instability. Recently, a mediator was appointed to conduct negotiations and help the parties reach an agreement. The ultimate goal is to establish power sharing arrangements and maintain stability in the region. Moreover, both governments have expressed willingness to sign a treaty if progress is made.

*Collocations usadas:* bilateral relations, diplomatic ties, economic sanctions, conduct negotiations, reach an agreement, power sharing, maintain stability, sign a treaty, moreover.

</details>

**Ejercicio 15:** Graba una presentación de 2 minutos sobre un tratado internacional conocido (Paris,Geneva, etc.). Estructura:

1. Contexto histórico (although / despite)
2. Contenido principal (moreover / in order to)
3. Resultados (therefore / as a result)
4. Valoración personal (nevertheless)

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**Modelo de presentación:**

The Geneva Conventions, signed in 1949, represent one of the most important achievements in international humanitarian law. Although warfare has evolved significantly since then, the conventions remain the foundation of the rules of armed conflict. Despite their age, they continue to provide essential protections for civilians and prisoners of war.

Moreover, the conventions establish clear guidelines for the treatment of wounded soldiers and regulate the use of certain weapons. In order to ensure compliance, an international monitoring system was created. Furthermore, nations that violate the conventions can face diplomatic isolation and economic sanctions.

As a result, the Geneva Conventions have saved countless lives. Therefore, they remain a cornerstone of international relations. Nevertheless, critics argue that enforcement mechanisms need to be strengthened to address modern challenges such as cyber warfare and asymmetric conflicts.

</details>
