## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** wish / if only + past perfect, participle clauses, mixed conditionals

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I wish I ___ studied harder for the exam. → *have / had / has*
2. If only we ___ more time yesterday. → *have / had / has*
3. She regrets ___ so much junk food. → *eat / eating / to eat*
4. ___ by her coach, she signed up immediately. → *Motivating / Motivated / Having motivate*
5. If he had taken the medicine, he ___ better now. → *feels / would feel / will feel*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **had** · 2. **had** · 3. **eating** · 4. **Motivated** · 5. **would feel**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. I wish I ___ afford a personal trainer. → *can / could / will*
2. ___ finished the course, she updated her CV. → *Having / Have / Had*
3. If only you ___ me sooner! → *tell / told / had told*
4. Encouraged ___ the results, he set new goals. → *by / with / for*
5. I wish they ___ stop interrupting me. → *will / would / can*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **could** · 2. **Having** · 3. **had told** · 4. **by** · 5. **would**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *I wish I have studied more.*
2. *If only he takes the job.* (past regret)
3. *Motivating by her mentor, she applied.*
4. *If she studied harder, she would pass now.* (mixed)
5. *Having finish the test, she left.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I wish I **had studied** more.
2. If only he **had taken** the job.
3. **Motivated** by her mentor, she applied.
4. If she **had studied** harder, she **would pass** now.
5. Having **finished** the test, she left.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Nina's growth plan

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

> Last year Nina regretted starting so many online courses and never finishing them. I wish I had been more disciplined, she says. Motivated by a podcast on personal growth, she enrolled in a B2 programme. If she had studied mixed conditionals in B1, she would understand them better now. Having completed the diagnostic test, she set clear goals for the month. Encouraged by her progress, she plans to join a study group next week.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Who regret not finishing courses? → *Nina / James / Sofia*
2. What does she wish about discipline? → *had been more disciplined / will study / can fly*
3. Motivated by what? → *a podcast / rain / a flight*
4. Mixed conditional in text? → *yes / no*
5. Participle: Having completed… → *diagnostic test / airport / hotel*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Nina** · 2. **had been more disciplined** · 3. **a podcast** · 4. **If she had studied… she would understand** · 5. **diagnostic test**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Encouraged by what? → *progress / traffic / rain*
7. Plans to join what? → *study group / hiking club / embassy*
8. Structure for past regret? → *wish + past perfect / will / going to*
9. Structure for mixed? → *mixed / only first / only zero*
10. Went to study group already? → *False / True*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **her progress** · 7. **study group** · 8. **wish + past perfect** · 9. **if + past perfect, would + base** · 10. **False**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 2 frases con *I wish I had…* (arrepentimiento).
2. Completa: I wish I ___ (study) harder last year.
3. Completa: If only she ___ (tell) me the truth.
4. Completa: ___ by her mentor, she applied for the course.
5. Escribe un mixed conditional sobre tu progreso en inglés.
6. Completa: Having ___ (finish) the test, she relaxed.
7. Usa *regret + gerund* en una frase.
8. Corrige: *I wish I have more time.*
9. Corrige: *Motivating by the coach, she signed up.*
10. Párrafo (4 frases): metas de desarrollo personal.
11. Traduce: Ojalá hubiera empezado antes.
12. Traduce: Si hubiera practicado más, entendería mejor ahora.
13. Escribe *I wish they would…* (queja educada).
14. Participle clause: *Encouraged by…, I…*
15. Autochequeo: 1× wish, 1× participle, 1× mixed.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Model: I wish I had started earlier. I wish I had been more consistent.
2. **had studied**
3. **had told**
4. **Motivated**
5. Open — If I had…, I would… now.
6. Having **finished**
7. I regret wasting so much time.
8. I wish I **had** more time.
9. **Motivated** by the coach, she signed up.
10. Open paragraph with goals vocabulary.
11. I wish I **had started** earlier. / If only I **had started** earlier.
12. If I **had practised** more, I **would understand** better now.
13. I wish they **would** reply faster.
14. Encouraged by my progress, I set new goals.
15. Self-check vs theory.

</details>
