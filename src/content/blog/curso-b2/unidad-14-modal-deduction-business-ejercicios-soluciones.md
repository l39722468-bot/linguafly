## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** must have / might have / can't have + past participle

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. She ___ missed the deadline. (almost sure) → *must have / might have / can't have*
2. They ___ changed the launch date. (possible) → *might have / must have / can't have*
3. He ___ sent the invoice yet. (almost impossible) → *can't have / must have / might have*
4. The designer ___ finished early. (almost sure) → *must have / can't have / will*
5. Structure: modal + ___ + past participle. → *have / had / has*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **must have** · 2. **might have** · 3. **can't have** · 4. **must have** · 5. **have**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. Must have = ___. → *strong certainty past / future plan*
2. Can't have = ___. → *strong impossibility past / permission*
3. Might have = ___. → *possibility past / obligation*
4. She must have ___ the pitch. → *rewritten / rewrite / rewriting*
5. They can't have ___ the merger yet. → *announced / announce / announcing*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **strong certainty past** · 2. **strong impossibility past** · 3. **possibility past** · 4. **rewritten** · 5. **announced**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *She must missed the deadline.*
2. *They might changed the date.*
3. *He can't have send the invoice.*
4. *She must has finished early.*
5. *They must have change the brand.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. She must **have missed** the deadline.
2. They might **have changed** the date.
3. He can't have **sent** the invoice.
4. She must **have** finished early.
5. They must have **changed** the brand.

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe must have, might have y can't have (2 cada uno).
2. Completa: She ___ (must have miss) the deadline.
3. Completa: They ___ (might have change) the launch date.
4. Completa: He ___ (can't have send) the invoice yet.
5. Completa: The designer ___ (must have finish) early.
6. Explica must have vs can't have.
7. Corrige: *She must missed the meeting.*
8. Corrige: *They might have change the brand.*
9. Párrafo business/fashion con deducciones.
10. Traduce: Debe de haber perdido la fecha límite.
11. Traduce: No puede haber enviado la factura todavía.
12. Diálogo tras una reunión de lanzamiento.
13. Matching: certainty levels.
14. Autochequeo: modal + have + pp.
15. Enlace teoría U14.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open — two of each.
2. **must have missed**
3. **might have changed**
4. **can't have sent**
5. **must have finished**
6. Must have = almost sure; can't have = almost impossible.
7. She must **have missed** the meeting.
8. They might have **changed** the brand.
9. Open paragraph.
10. She **must have missed** the deadline.
11. He **can't have sent** the invoice yet.
12. Open dialogue.
13. must have > might have; can't have = negation of must have.
14. Self-check.
15. [Guía U14](/blog/curso-b2/unidad-14-modal-deduction-business)

</details>
