## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** -ing / -ed participle clauses; Having + past participle

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ___ by the noise, residents protested. → *Disturbing / Disturbed / Having disturb*
2. ___ planted trees, the council cut pollution. → *Having / Have / Had*
3. ___ through the park, I saw less litter. → *Walk / Walking / Walked*
4. ___ by the results, schools joined the programme. → *Encourage / Encouraged / Encouraging*
5. ___ finished the audit, experts made recommendations. → *Having / Have / Had*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Disturbed** · 2. **Having** · 3. **Walking** · 4. **Encouraged** · 5. **Having**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. -ing clause = usually ___ meaning. → *active / passive / past*
2. -ed clause = usually ___ meaning. → *passive / active / future*
3. Having + pp = action ___ the main verb. → *before / after / during*
4. Same ___ in both clauses. → *subject / object / tense*
5. Disturbed by… = cláusula ___ → *-ed / -ing / infinitive*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **active** · 2. **passive** · 3. **before** · 4. **subject** · 5. **-ed**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *Disturbing by noise, they protested.*
2. *Having plant trees, air improved.*
3. *Walked through the park, I noticed birds.*
4. *Encouraging by reports, the mayor acted.*
5. *Having finish the audit, they left.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Disturbed** by noise, they protested.
2. Having **planted** trees, air improved.
3. **Walking** through the park, I noticed birds.
4. **Encouraged** by reports, the mayor acted.
5. Having **finished** the audit, they left.

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe una cláusula -ing, -ed y Having + pp.
2. Completa: ___ by the noise, residents protested.
3. Completa: ___ planted trees, air quality improved.
4. Completa: ___ through the park, I saw less litter.
5. Completa: ___ finished the audit, experts spoke.
6. Explica mismo sujeto en 1 frase.
7. Corrige: *Disturbing by noise, they left.*
8. Corrige: *Having plant trees, we left.*
9. Párrafo sobre medio ambiente local.
10. Traduce: Al terminar la auditoría, publicaron el informe.
11. Traduce: Caminando por el río, recogieron basura.
12. Encouraged by… + main clause.
13. Disturbed by… + main clause.
14. Autochequeo: -ing activo / -ed pasivo.
15. Enlace teoría U9.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open — three participle clause types.
2. **Disturbed**
3. Having **planted**
4. **Walking**
5. Having **finished**
6. The participle clause and main clause share the same subject.
7. **Disturbed** by the noise, they left.
8. Having **planted** trees, we left.
9. Open environment paragraph.
10. Having **finished** the audit, they published the report.
11. **Walking** along the river, they collected litter.
12. Encouraged by the results, the mayor acted.
13. Disturbed by pollution, citizens protested.
14. Self-check.
15. [Guía U9](/blog/curso-b2/unidad-9-participle-clauses-environment)

</details>
