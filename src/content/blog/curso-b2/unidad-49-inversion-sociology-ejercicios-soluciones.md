Este cuaderno reúne los **ejercicios de la Unidad 49 del curso B2** (*Inversion for Emphasis + Sociology & Cultural Shifts*). Practicarás reconocimiento, transformación y producción con tareas cerradas o criterios verificables. Cada actividad indica qué debes escribir, qué opciones puedes usar o qué información debes localizar.

> **Guía teórica:** [Inversion & Sociology B2](/blog/curso-b2/unidad-49-inversion-sociology)<br>
> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 49 — Inversion & Sociology](/curso-b2/unit-49)

Trabaja en este orden: responde sin mirar, abre la solución, identifica la causa de cada error y repite la frase correcta en voz alta. En writing y speaking, compara primero la estructura obligatoria y después el contenido.

**Recuerda antes de empezar:**
- Tras una expresión negativa frontal, invierte **auxiliar + sujeto**.
- Si no hay auxiliar, añade **do/does/did** y devuelve el verbo principal a base.
- Con **only when/after**, la inversión ocurre en la cláusula principal.
- Conserva las parejas **hardly/scarcely...when** y **no sooner...than**.

## Lección 1 — Gramática y chunks

**Objetivo:** seleccionar y construir correctamente inversión enfática con expresiones negativas, only, not only, not until y pares temporales.

No respondas por parecido visual. Localiza la pista de significado o tiempo y comprueba la cadena completa: modal, auxiliar, infinitivo, participio, objeto y concordancia.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Expresiones negativas frontales

Mueve el auxiliar existente o añade *do/does/did*; no construyas una pregunta.

1. I have never seen such rapid change.
   Opciones: *Never have I seen such rapid change. · Never I have seen such rapid change.*
2. We seldom witness this diversity.
   Opciones: *Seldom do we witness this diversity. · Seldom witness we this diversity.*
3. Society rarely changes so quickly.
   Opciones: *Rarely does society change so quickly. · Rarely society changes so quickly.*
4. We knew little about the trend.
   Opciones: *Little did we know about the trend. · Little did we knew about the trend.*
5. Corrige: *Under no circumstances you should stereotype residents.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Never have I seen such rapid change.** — Se invierten *have* y el sujeto.
2. **Seldom do we witness this diversity.** — Simple Present necesita *do* y base.
3. **Rarely does society change so quickly.** — Tercera persona: *does + subject + base*.
4. **Little did we know about the trend.** — Simple Past necesita *did* y base.
5. **Under no circumstances should you stereotype residents.** — El modal pasa delante del sujeto.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Only, not only y not until

Decide en qué cláusula ocurre la inversión y comprueba si *only* modifica circunstancia o sujeto.

6. We realised the impact only then.
   Opciones: *Only then did we realise the impact. · Only then we realised the impact.*
7. We understood after the census appeared.
   Opciones: *Only after the census appeared did we understand. · Only after did the census appear we understood.*
8. Demographics changed, and identity changed too.
   Opciones: *Not only did demographics change, but identity did too. · Not only demographics changed, but identity too.*
9. Women gained the vote only in that decade.
   Opciones: *Not until that decade did women gain the vote. · Not until did that decade women gained.*
10. Choose the sentence with no inversion.
   Opciones: *Only researchers understood the table. · Only then researchers understood. · Never society had changed.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **Only then did we realise the impact.** — La circunstancia frontal activa inversión.
7. **Only after the census appeared did we understand.** — La subordinada mantiene orden normal; se invierte la principal.
8. **Not only did demographics change, but identity did too.** — *Not only* frontal invierte la primera cláusula.
9. **Not until that decade did women gain the vote.** — La principal usa *did + subject + base*.
10. **Only researchers understood the table.** — *Only* modifica el sujeto *researchers*.

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Sucesión inmediata

Forma las parejas fijas y distribuye Past Perfect y Past Simple.

11. The programme ended; new trends emerged immediately.
   Opciones: *Hardly had the programme ended when new trends emerged. · Hardly did the programme end than trends emerged.*
12. Broadband arrived; remote work increased immediately.
   Opciones: *No sooner had broadband arrived than remote work increased. · No sooner broadband arrived when work increased.*
13. Complete: Scarcely ___ the census begun ___ the system failed.
   Opciones: *had / when · did / than · has / that*
14. Choose the correct pair.
   Opciones: *Hardly...when · Hardly...than · No sooner...when*
15. Corrige: *No sooner had the survey ended when analysts published the data.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **Hardly had the programme ended when new trends emerged.** — *Hardly + Past Perfect...when + Past Simple*.
12. **No sooner had broadband arrived than remote work increased.** — *No sooner...than*.
13. **had / when** — *Scarcely* comparte patrón con *hardly*.
14. **Hardly...when** — Las parejas no se intercambian.
15. **No sooner had the survey ended than analysts published the data.** — *No sooner* exige *than*.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** comprender y usar vocabulario de *Sociology & Cultural Shifts* en situaciones concretas.

Aprende cada palabra con su colocación. Las alternativas cercanas obligan a decidir por definición y contexto, no por parecido.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Traducción precisa

Elige el único significado que corresponde a la palabra.

1. sociology
   Opciones: *estudio de sociedades e instituciones · estudio de la mente individual · recuento de votos*
2. demographics
   Opciones: *características estadísticas de una población · opiniones personales · normas gramaticales*
3. social change
   Opciones: *transformación de estructuras o normas · traslado individual diario · moda de una semana*
4. identity
   Opciones: *cómo una persona o grupo se define · velocidad de internet · tamaño de una muestra*
5. diversity
   Opciones: *variedad dentro de un grupo · uniformidad obligatoria · dato aislado*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **estudio de sociedades e instituciones**
2. **características estadísticas de una población**
3. **transformación de estructuras o normas**
4. **cómo una persona o grupo se define**
5. **variedad dentro de un grupo**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige por definición

Completa cada definición con una de las tres opciones.

6. An official operation that counts a population is a ___.
   Opciones: *census · trend · generation*
7. A sustained direction of change is a ___.
   Opciones: *trend · fad · tradition*
8. A short-lived fashion is a ___.
   Opciones: *fad · value · census*
9. Movement to live in another place is ___.
   Opciones: *migration · modernisation · connectivity*
10. Growth in the proportion living in cities is ___.
   Opciones: *urbanisation · globalisation · digitisation*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **census**
7. **trend**
8. **fad**
9. **migration**
10. **urbanisation**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: A town interprets rapid social change

**Objetivo:** comprender la idea global, localizar datos y reconocer los objetivos en contexto.

> Never before had Bellford changed as rapidly as it did after the new rail line opened. Migration increased, neighbourhoods became more diverse and local businesses served a wider community. Only after the latest census was published did researchers identify the scale of the demographic shift. Not only had the population grown, but its age profile had changed too. Seldom do social trends have a single cause, so the report considered employment, housing and improved digital connectivity. Hardly had one online service become popular when another replaced it. No sooner had broadband reached the surrounding villages than remote work began to influence migration. Under no circumstances should these figures be used to stereotype residents. Only by interviewing different generations can sociologists explain how globalisation and urbanisation are shaping local identity.

Lee una vez para captar la situación y otra para localizar detalles. También puedes escuchar el audio. Todas las respuestas se comprueban directamente en el texto.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión global

Responde con la información explícita del texto o audio.

1. What caused Bellford to change rapidly?
2. What source revealed the demographic shift?
3. Which three areas did the report consider besides demographics?
4. What reached the villages before remote work increased?
5. What must sociologists combine with figures?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **The opening of a new rail line.**
2. **The latest census.**
3. **Employment, housing and digital connectivity.**
4. **Broadband.**
5. **Interviews with different generations.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles y chunks exactos

Completa con las palabras exactas del texto o audio.

6. Complete: Never before ___ Bellford changed so rapidly.
7. Complete: Only after the census appeared ___ researchers identify the scale.
8. Complete the pair: Hardly... ___; No sooner... ___.
9. What two processes are shaping identity?
10. What must figures not be used to do?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **had**
7. **did**
8. **when; than**
9. **Globalisation and urbanisation.**
10. **Stereotype residents.**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma, significado y secuencia

Elige, explica u ordena usando la evidencia presentada.

11. Choose: *Only after...did researchers identify* inverts subordinate / main clause.
12. Choose: *Only by interviewing* expresses person / method.
13. Put in order: rail line — migration — census — interviews.
14. Does the report attribute change to one cause?
15. Why is *Never before had Bellford changed* not a question?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **main clause**
12. **method**
13. **rail line → migration → census → interviews**
14. **No; it explicitly says social trends seldom have a single cause.**
15. **It is a declarative sentence with emphatic inversion.**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing y Speaking

**Objetivo:** producir mensajes controlados con inversión enfática con expresiones negativas, only, not only, not until y pares temporales.

### Ejercicios 1–10 — Writing con modelo

Respeta la extensión y usa todas las expresiones indicadas. Cada tarea incluye una respuesta o un modelo completo: puedes cambiar los detalles, pero conserva las estructuras evaluadas.

1. Invierte: *I have never seen such rapid change.*
2. Invierte: *We seldom observe this trend.*
3. Corrige: *Only then did researchers understood.*
4. Une con *not only*: population grew; identity changed.
5. Completa: *No sooner had broadband arrived ___ remote work increased.*
6. Escribe 35–45 palabras con *never, seldom, demographics*.
7. Redacta 35–45 palabras con *only after, only by, census*.
8. Escribe 45–55 palabras con *not only, not until, diversity*.
9. Escribe 50–60 palabras con *hardly...when, no sooner...than, trend, fad*.
10. Escribe 100–120 palabras sobre un cambio social. Incluye ocho inversiones distintas y diez términos U49.

<details>
<summary>Ver modelos de writing</summary>

1. **Never have I seen such rapid change.**
2. **Seldom do we observe this trend.**
3. **Only then did researchers understand.**
4. **Not only did the population grow, but identity changed too.**
5. No sooner had broadband arrived **than** remote work increased.
6. Modelo: **Never have local demographics changed** so quickly. **Seldom do researchers attribute** such a shift to one cause, so the report compares migration, employment and housing before explaining how the population's age profile has developed since the railway opened.
7. Modelo: **Only after the census was published did analysts identify** the trend. **Only by interviewing residents can they explain** why it occurred, because population figures reveal scale but cannot show how different generations interpret the change.
8. Modelo: **Not only did migration increase diversity, but it also changed** local services. **Not until researchers compared neighbourhoods did they recognise** how uneven the effects were. Some communities gained transport and employment, whereas others faced higher housing costs, weaker access to public facilities or fewer benefits.
9. Modelo: **Hardly had one online platform become a trend when** a competing service attracted younger users. **No sooner had researchers labelled the first change a lasting trend than** participation declined. The episode showed why a brief **fad** should not be treated as permanent social change without evidence collected over a longer period.
10. Modelo: **Never before had the community experienced** such rapid **social change**. **Rarely do demographics reveal** one simple cause. **Only after the census appeared did researchers identify** increased **migration** and **urbanisation**. **Not only had the population grown, but diversity had increased too**. **Seldom did older residents describe** the shift in the same way as younger groups. **Hardly had broadband reached** the villages **when** remote work expanded. **No sooner had a digital inclusion programme begun than** every place was filled. **Only by combining interviews and statistics can sociologists explain** how **globalisation**, a changing **generation gap** and local **traditions** shape **identity**. Under no circumstances should one striking **trend** be used to stereotype an entire **society**.

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Speaking con guion y pistas

Prepara durante 30 segundos y habla sin leer. Después compara tus estructuras con el guion o las pistas concretas.

11. Pronuncia cuatro inversiones con auxiliares distintos.
12. Explica *only when* en 30 segundos.
13. Role-play entre analista y periodista.
14. Contrasta los pares temporales.
15. Analiza 60 segundos un cambio social con ocho inversiones y ocho términos U49.

<details>
<summary>Ver guiones y pistas de speaking</summary>

11. Guion modelo: **Never have I seen; seldom do we witness; rarely does society change; little did we know.**
12. Guion modelo: The clause after **only when** keeps normal order. The main clause inverts: **Only when the census appeared did we understand**.
13. A: When did you identify the trend? B: **Only after the census appeared did we recognise it**. A: Was there one cause? B: **Rarely do trends have one cause**.
14. Guion modelo: **Hardly had the programme begun when** places filled. **No sooner had it begun than** volunteers requested more sessions.
15. Pistas: never/seldom/only/not only/not until/hardly/no sooner/under no circumstances · census · demographics · migration · diversity · urbanisation · globalisation · identity · trend.

</details>

## Cómo revisar tus resultados

Clasifica cada fallo como **significado**, **forma**, **tiempo**, **concordancia**, **orden** o **vocabulario**. Copia la corrección mínima y crea una frase nueva con el mismo patrón. Después vuelve a intentarlo sin opciones.

Para consolidar, repite las expresiones mañana, dentro de tres días y una semana después. Reduce el apoyo en cada revisión: primero opciones, después una pista en español y finalmente una situación de 45 segundos.
