## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** would rather + inf; would rather + subject + past; would prefer; It's time + past

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I would rather ___ at home this year. → *stay / to stay / staying*
2. She would prefer ___ by train. → *travel / to travel / travelling*
3. It is high time we ___ our cousins. → *visit / visited / visiting*
4. I would rather my sister ___ early. → *comes / came / come*
5. He would rather not ___ abroad alone. → *travel / to travel / travelling*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **stay** · 2. **to travel** · 3. **visited** · 4. **came** · 5. **travel**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. Would you rather ___ or fly? → *drive / to drive / driving*
2. I would prefer tea ___ coffee. → *to / than / over*
3. It is about time he ___ a job. → *find / found / finding*
4. She would rather her parents ___ with them. → *stay / stayed / staying*
5. I'd rather you ___ tell anyone yet. → *don't / didn't / not*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **drive** · 2. **to** · 3. **found** · 4. **stayed** · 5. **didn't**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *I would rather to stay home.*
2. *She would prefer travel by bus.*
3. *It is time we leave.*
4. *I would rather my brother comes early.*
5. *Would you rather to cook?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I would rather **stay** home.
2. She would prefer **to travel** by bus.
3. It is time we **left**.
4. I would rather my brother **came** early.
5. Would you rather **cook**?

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** family

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. grandparents → *abuelos · vecinos · colegas*
2. gathering → *reunión familiar · vuelo · maleta*
3. relative → *pariente · pasajero · guía*
4. decorate → *decorar · cancelar · posponer*
5. celebrate → *celebrar · posponer / retrasar*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **abuelos** · 2. **reunión familiar** · 3. **pariente** · 4. **decorar** · 5. **celebrar**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

1. would rather ≈ ___ → *preferiría / debería / podría*
2. would prefer ≈ ___ → *preferiría (con to) / odiaría / debería*
3. it's high time ≈ ___ → *ya es hora de / nunca / tal vez*
4. nuclear family ≈ ___ → *familia nuclear / familia extendida / solo amigos*
5. extended family ≈ ___ → *familia extendida / familia nuclear / vecinos*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **preferiría** · 2. **preferiría (con to)** · 3. **ya es hora de** · 4. **familia nuclear** · 5. **familia extendida**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Tom's family plans

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b2/unit-7/reading-workbook.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Tom's family plans"></audio>

> Family gatherings need planning. Tom would rather cook at home than eat in a noisy restaurant. He would prefer to celebrate his birthday with close relatives only. It is high time they called their cousins in Canada — it has been three years. He would rather his brother helped with the decorations. His mother would prefer to have the party in the garden.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Tom would rather ___ at home → *cook / to cook / cooking*
2. Would prefer celebration with ___ → *close relatives / strangers / nobody*
3. It is high time they ___ cousins → *called / call / calling*
4. Would rather brother helped with ___ → *decorations / flights / reports*
5. Mother would prefer party in ___ → *garden / airport / office*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **cook** · 2. **close relatives** · 3. **called** · 4. **decorations** · 5. **garden**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Rather + bare inf? → *yes / no*
7. Prefer + to inf? → *yes / no*
8. It's time + past = ? → *should have happened / future certain*
9. Called cousins recently? → *False / True*
10. Find would rather / prefer / it's time.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **yes** · 7. **yes** · 8. **should have happened** · 9. **False (three years)** · 10. **See text**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Write would rather + subject + past.
12. Family vocab? → *yes / none*
13. Main idea? → *family / travel / environment*
14. Rather not eat in noisy ___ → *restaurant / park / trail*
15. Course link → */curso-b2/unit-7*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **Model OK** · 12. **cousins, relatives, gathering** · 13. **family preferences** · 14. **restaurant** · 15. **/curso-b2/unit-7**

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe would rather, would prefer e It's time en contexto familiar.
2. Completa: I would rather ___ (stay) at home.
3. Completa: She would prefer ___ (travel) by train.
4. Completa: It is high time we ___ (visit) our cousins.
5. Completa: I would rather my brother ___ (help) me.
6. Would rather not + inf.
7. Prefer tea ___ coffee.
8. Corrige: *I would rather to cook.*
9. Corrige: *It is time we leave.*
10. Párrafo sobre preferencias familiares.
11. Traduce: Preferiría quedarme en casa.
12. Traduce: Ya es hora de que llamemos a mi tía.
13. I'd rather you didn't…
14. Diálogo en una reunión familiar.
15. Autochequeo: rather (sin to) vs prefer (con to).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open — three structures in family context.
2. **stay**
3. **to travel**
4. **visited**
5. **helped**
6. I would rather not **go** abroad alone.
7. I would prefer tea **to** coffee.
8. I would rather **stay** at home.
9. It is time we **left**.
10. Open family paragraph.
11. I would rather **stay** at home.
12. It is high time we **called** my aunt.
13. I'd rather you **didn't** drive so fast.
14. Open dialogue.
15. Self-check.

</details>
