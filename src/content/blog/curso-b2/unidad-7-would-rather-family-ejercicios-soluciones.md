## Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Would rather / would prefer

1. I would rather ___ (shop) online tonight.
2. He would prefer ___ (buy) local.
3. I would rather my sister ___ (come) early.
4. She would prefer tea ___ coffee.
5. I would rather ___ (not travel) alone.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **shop** · 2. **to buy** · 3. **came** · 4. **to** · 5. **not travel**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — It's time

1. It's time he ___ (get) his budget under control.
2. It's high time they ___ (start) apologising.
3. It's about time we ___ (call) our cousins.
4. It's time you ___ (learn) to cook.
5. It's time the children ___ (go) to bed.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **got** · 2. **started** · 3. **called** · 4. **learned** · 5. **went**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Corrige el error

1. *I would rather to stay home.*
2. *She would prefer travel by bus.*
3. *It is time we leave.*
4. *I would rather my brother comes early.*
5. *Would you rather to cook?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I would rather **stay** home.
2. She would prefer **to travel** by bus.
3. It is time we **left**.
4. I would rather my brother **came** early.
5. Would you rather **cook**?
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Elige la opción correcta

1. Would you rather ___ or fly? → *drive / to drive / driving*
2. I would prefer tea ___ coffee. → *to / than / over*
3. It is about time he ___ a job. → *find / found / finding*
4. She would rather her parents ___ with them. → *stay / stayed / staying*
5. I'd rather you ___ tell anyone yet. → *don't / didn't / not*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **drive** · 2. **to** · 3. **found** · 4. **stayed** · 5. **didn't**
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Escribe tu propio texto

Escribe 8–10 frases sobre tu propia familia. Incluye:

- 2 frases con **would rather + infinitivo**.
- 1 frase con **would rather + sujeto + pasado**.
- 2 frases con **would prefer + to + infinitivo**.
- 2 frases con **it's (high) time + pasado**.
- Al menos 4 palabras de vocabulario de family.

**Modelo:** usa el texto sobre Tom como referencia de estilo.

---

## Tip del profesor

Antes de hablar, pregúntate: ¿estoy expresando **mi** preferencia (would rather + infinitivo), la preferencia sobre **otra persona** (would rather + sujeto + pasado), lo mismo en registro más formal (would prefer + to), o **urgencia** por algo que llevamos posponiendo (it's time + pasado)? Practicar esta clasificación mental antes de construir la frase evita casi todos los errores de esta unidad, especialmente la tentación de añadir *to* después de rather.

## Resumen rápido

| Estructura | Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| would rather | + infinitivo sin *to* | I would rather shop online. |
| would rather | + sujeto + pasado | I'd rather you came early. |
| would prefer | + to + infinitivo | He would prefer to buy local. |
| it's (high) time | + sujeto + pasado | It's time he got control. |
| Vocabulario | family | sibling, in-laws, gathering... |
