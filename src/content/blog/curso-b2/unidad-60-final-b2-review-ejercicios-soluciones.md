## Simulacro de Examen — Use of English

## Part 1: Multiple-choice cloze (5 ejercicios)

**For questions 1–5, read the text below and decide which answer (A, B, C or D) best fits each gap.**

> Last summer, I decided to take a sabbatical from work. I had been feeling (1) _______ for months, and I knew something had to change. I booked a one-way ticket to Thailand, (2) _______ I hoped would help me rediscover my passion for life. The first few days were (3) _______ — I barely knew anyone and I missed my routine terribly. But gradually, I began to (4) _______ myself. I joined a cooking class, met travellers from all over the world, and for the first time in years, I felt genuinely (5) _______. The experience taught me that sometimes you need to leave everything behind to find what truly matters.

1. **A** burning out &emsp; **B** running out &emsp; **C** making out &emsp; **D** carrying out
2. **A** what &emsp; **B** which &emsp; **C** that &emsp; **D** where
3. **A** frightening &emsp; **B** daunting &emsp; **C** shocking &emsp; **D** astonishing
4. **A** enjoy &emsp; **B** amuse &emsp; **C** entertain &emsp; **D** adapt
5. **A** fulfilled &emsp; **B** satisfied &emsp; **C** completed &emsp; **D** achieved

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **A — burning out**. "Burn out" = agotarse. "Running out" = quedarse sin algo; "making out" = besarse/deducir; "carrying out" = ejecutar.
2. **B — which**. Relative pronoun referring to the whole clause: "a one-way ticket to Thailand, **which** I hoped would help…"
3. **B — daunting**. Daunting = intimidante, desalentador. Es la palabra más natural para describir algo que genera inseguridad al principio.
4. **D — adapt**. "Adapt oneself" = adaptarse. En el contexto de un viaje, es la opción correcta.
5. **A — fulfilled**. Fulfilled = realizado. Complementa la idea de "encontrar lo que realmente importa".

</details>

## Part 2: Open cloze (5 ejercicios)

**For questions 6–10, read the text below and think of the word which best fits each gap. Use only one word in each gap.**

> Learning a foreign language is one of the most rewarding things you can do. It opens doors (6) _______ new cultures, friendships and career opportunities. However, it requires (7) _______ and persistence. Many people start enthusiastically but give (8) _______ after a few weeks when progress seems slow. The key is to set realistic goals and celebrate small (9) _______ along the way. Remember: fluency is not (10) _______ you arrive, but the journey itself.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **to** — "open doors to" = collocación fija.
7. **patience** — La oración requiere un sustantivo abstracto que搭配 con "persistence".
8. **up** — "give up" = abandonar.
9. **victories / wins** — "small victories" o "small wins" = logros pequeños.
10. **where** — "Fluency is not where you arrive" = la fluidez no es un destino, sino un proceso.

</details>

## Part 3: Word formation (5 ejercicios)

**Read the text below. Use the word given in capitals at the end of some lines to form a word that fits in the gap.**

> Environmental protection has become one of the most (11) _______ issues of our time. Scientists have been warning for decades that our planet is in (12) _______. The (13) _______ of plastic in the oceans is particularly alarming. Despite growing (14) _______ among the public, governments have been slow to act. It is (15) _______ that we change our habits before it is too late.

11. URGENT
12. DANGER
13. POLLUTE
14. AWARE
15. ESSENTIAL

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **urgent** — Forma adjetivo de "urgency": *most urgent issues*.
12. **danger** — "In danger" = en peligro.
13. **pollution** — Sustantivo de "pollute": *the pollution of the oceans*.
14. **awareness** — Sustantivo de "aware": *growing awareness among the public*.
15. **essential** — Adjetivo de "essence": *It is essential that…*

</details>

## Part 4: Key word transformations (5 ejercicios)

**Complete the second sentence so that it has a similar meaning to the first sentence, using the word given. Do not change the word given. You must use between two and five words, including the word given.**

16. "I haven't seen you for ages!" she said.
→ She said she **(NOT SEE)** me for ages.

17. People believe that the CEO is resigning.
→ The CEO **(SAID)** to be resigning.

18. If I were you, I would accept the offer.
→ If I **(IN)** your shoes, I would accept the offer.

19. She finished the project and then left the office.
→ She didn't leave the office **(UNTIL)** she had finished the project.

20. "You must not park here," the sign said.
→ The sign said that parking **(FORBIDDEN)**.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

16. **hadn't seen** — Reported speech: present perfect → past perfect.
17. **is said** — Passive reporting: "is said to be".
18. **were in** — "If I were in your shoes" = variante de "if I were you".
19. **until** — "didn't leave until" = no se fue hasta que.
20. **was forbidden** — Passive + past simple. "Parking was forbidden" = el estacionamiento estaba prohibido.

</details>

## Simulacro de Examen — Reading

## Text 1: Multiple choice (5 ejercicios)

**Read the text about public libraries and answer questions 21–25.**

> Public libraries have undergone a remarkable transformation in recent decades. Once seen primarily as repositories of books, they have evolved into vibrant community hubs that offer far more than lending services. Today's libraries provide free access to the internet, digital literacy courses, language workshops, and even creative spaces equipped with 3D printers. Far from becoming obsolete in the digital age, libraries have reinvented themselves. A 2024 report found that library usage among young adults increased by 25% over the past five years, driven partly by the demand for quiet study spaces and partly by the expansion of digital services. Libraries also play a crucial role in social inclusion, offering support to immigrants, elderly people, and those without access to technology at home.

21. What does the author suggest about libraries?
- A) They are outdated institutions.
- B) They have successfully adapted to modern needs.
- C) They should focus only on books.
- D) They are less popular than they used to be.

22. The word "repositories" in line 2 is closest in meaning to:
- A) shops
- B) storages
- C) classrooms
- D) offices

23. Library usage among young adults has increased because of:
- A) cheaper book prices
- B) the closure of other community centres
- C) the availability of study spaces and digital services
- D) new library buildings

24. Which group is NOT mentioned as benefiting from libraries?
- A) immigrants
- B) elderly people
- C) children under five
- D) people without home technology

25. What is the overall tone of the passage?
- A) critical
- B) nostalgic
- C) optimistic
- D) sarcastic

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

21. **B** — The text emphasises adaptation and growth, not obsolescence.
22. **B** — Repositories = almacenes/lugares de almacenamiento.
23. **C** — Explicitly stated: "driven partly by the demand for quiet study spaces and partly by the expansion of digital services."
24. **C** — Children under five are not mentioned.
25. **C** — The author presents libraries positively: "remarkable transformation", "vibrant community hubs".

</details>

## Text 2: Matching (5 ejercicios)

**Read the five short texts (26–30). What is each person mainly expressing? Match each text with the correct option (A–H).**

26. "I was convinced the presentation went terribly, but my manager praised me afterwards. I guess we're always our own harshest critics."

27. "The flight was delayed by six hours, which meant I missed the opening ceremony. I tried to make the best of it, but honestly, I was furious."

28. "I've been offered a promotion, but it means relocating to another city. I'm torn — it's a great opportunity, but my family is here."

29. "My colleague always takes credit for my ideas in meetings. I've decided to speak up next time it happens."

30. "I used to think working from home was the dream, but now I realise I miss the social interaction of the office."

**Options:**

A. Expressing frustration
B. Reflecting on a past assumption
C. Showing determination
D. Feeling conflicted
E. Admitting self-criticism
F. Complaining about unfairness
G. Celebrating success
H. Expressing regret

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

26. **E** — "We're always our own harshest critics" = autocrítica.
27. **A** — "I was furious" = frustración.
28. **D** — "I'm torn" = dividido/conflictuado.
29. **C** — "I've decided to speak up" = determinación.
30. **B** — "I used to think… but now I realise" = suposición pasada.

</details>

## Text 3: True / False / Not Given (5 ejercicios)

**Read the text and decide if the statements are True, False or Not Given.**

> Artificial intelligence has been making headlines for several years now. While proponents argue that AI will revolutionise healthcare, education and transportation, critics warn of job displacement and ethical concerns. One area where AI has already made significant inroads is in medical diagnostics. Studies have shown that AI systems can detect certain cancers with accuracy comparable to — and sometimes exceeding — that of human doctors. However, the technology is not without limitations. AI systems are only as good as the data they are trained on, and biased data can lead to biased outcomes. Furthermore, the "black box" problem — the difficulty of understanding how AI reaches its conclusions — remains a significant barrier to trust.

31. AI has been a topic of public interest for many years. — **True / False / Not Given**
32. Everyone agrees that AI will have a positive impact on society. — **True / False / Not Given**
33. AI is better than doctors at detecting all types of cancer. — **True / False / Not Given**
34. AI systems can produce unfair results if trained on biased data. — **True / False / Not Given**
35. Scientists have fully solved the "black box" problem. — **True / False / Not Given**

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

31. **True** — "has been making headlines for several years".
32. **False** — Critics are mentioned: "critics warn of job displacement and ethical concerns".
33. **False** — The text says "certain cancers", not all.
34. **True** — "biased data can lead to biased outcomes".
35. **False** — The text says it "remains a significant barrier", meaning it has not been solved.

</details>

## Simulacro de Examen — Writing

## Task 1: Essay (220–260 words)

**Topic:** Some people believe that university education should be free for everyone. Others argue that students should pay for their own education. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Useful expressions:**
- *It is often argued that…*
- *On the other hand…*
- *From my perspective…*
- *A compelling argument in favour of…*
- *To sum up…*

<details><summary>Ver modelo de respuesta</summary>

> It is often argued that higher education should be funded entirely by the state, while others believe that students should bear the cost themselves. This essay will discuss both perspectives before presenting my own view.
>
> Those in favour of free university education contend that it promotes equality and social mobility. If students do not have to worry about tuition fees, individuals from disadvantaged backgrounds are more likely to pursue a degree. Furthermore, an educated population benefits society as a whole, as graduates tend to contribute more in taxes and civic engagement.
>
> On the other hand, opponents argue that making education free places an unfair burden on taxpayers who did not attend university. They also suggest that students who pay for their own education tend to be more motivated and take their studies more seriously. Moreover, governments have limited budgets and must prioritise spending on healthcare and infrastructure.
>
> From my perspective, a balanced approach is the most sensible solution. Governments could offer means-tested grants to students from low-income families while allowing those who can afford it to pay tuition fees. This would ensure that financial barriers do not prevent talented students from accessing education, while still maintaining a sense of personal responsibility.
>
> In conclusion, while free education has clear advantages, a hybrid model that combines public funding with personal contribution is likely to be the fairest and most sustainable option.

*(238 words)*

</details>

## Task 2: Review (220–260 words)

**Topic:** You recently stayed at a hotel. Write a review for a travel website describing your experience, what you liked and what could be improved.

<details><summary>Ver modelo de respuesta</summary>

> **Grand Horizon Hotel — A Mixed Experience**
>
> Last month, I spent four nights at the Grand Horizon Hotel in Barcelona, and while there were some highlights, the experience was not without its drawbacks.
>
> On the positive side, the location was unbeatable. The hotel is situated just a five-minute walk from La Rambla, which made it incredibly easy to explore the city on foot. The lobby was modern and stylish, and the staff at reception were genuinely friendly and helpful. Our room had a stunning view of the harbour, which made the mornings particularly enjoyable.
>
> However, there were several areas that fell short of expectations. The breakfast buffet, while extensive, was often crowded, and the food was lukewarm at best. More concerning was the noise from the street below, which made it difficult to sleep before midnight. For a hotel in this price range, better soundproofing would be expected. Additionally, the Wi-Fi was frustratingly slow, which was a real inconvenience for a business traveller like myself.
>
> In summary, the Grand Horizon Hotel offers a fantastic location and charming staff, but the noisy rooms and poor dining experience let it down. I would recommend it for a short city break, provided you bring earplugs and are prepared for the occasional inconvenience.

*(208 words)*

</details>

## Simulacro de Examen — Speaking

## Part 1: Interview (2 minutes)

**Practice answering these questions:**

1. What do you do in your free time?
2. Have you ever travelled abroad? Where?
3. What would you like to improve about yourself?

<details><summary>Ver pistas de respuesta</summary>

1. *In my free time, I enjoy… / I'm quite keen on… / I tend to…*
2. *Yes, I've been to… / I went to… last year. It was… / I haven't had the chance yet, but I'd love to…*
3. *I'd like to become more… / I think I need to work on… / If I could change one thing, it would be…*

</details>

## Part 2: Long turn (1 minute per photo)

**You see two photos showing people doing sports. Say which sport you think is more popular and why.**

<details><summary>Ver modelo</summary>

> *I think the first photo, which shows people playing football, depicts a more popular sport. Football is by far the most widely played sport in the world, with millions of fans and players across every continent. It's also relatively cheap — all you need is a ball and some open space. The second photo shows people playing tennis, which is also popular, but it requires more equipment and access to a court, which limits who can play. Personally, I prefer football because it's more social and you can play it almost anywhere.*

</details>

## Part 3: Collaborative task (2 minutes)

**Your friend says: "I think learning online is better than learning in a classroom." Discuss and decide.**

<details><summary>Ver guion</summary>

- *I see your point, but…*
- *That's true, although I also think…*
- *I agree that… However, don't you think…?*
- *Let me put it another way…*
- *I'm not sure I agree with that, because…*
- *So, in conclusion, we both agree that…*

</details>

## Part 4: Discussion (2 minutes)

1. Do you think technology has changed the way we learn languages?
2. Is it important for young people to learn a foreign language? Why?

<details><summary>Ver pistas de respuesta</summary>

1. *Absolutely. Apps like Duolingo have made it… / I'd say technology has been a double-edged sword… / On the one hand… on the other hand…*
2. *I think it's essential, because… / It depends on the country, but in general… / From a personal perspective, learning a language has helped me…*

</details>

## FAQs

<details><summary>¿Qué cubre el repaso final del curso B2?</summary>

Todas las estructuras gramaticales B2: conditionals, passive voice, passive reporting, reported speech, modals, phrasal verbs, linkers, collocations, inversion, cleft sentences, wish/regret, word formation y exam strategies.

</details>

<details><summary>¿Cómo debo estudiar el repaso final?</summary>

Haz el simulacro de examen primero para identificar debilidades. Después repasa las estructuras donde fallaste y vuelve a intentarlo.

</details>

<details><summary>¿Cuánto tiempo dura el simulacro?</summary>

60 minutos para Use of English y Reading. 30 minutos para Writing. Practica con cronómetro para simular condiciones reales.

</details>

<details><summary>¿Qué estructuras son más importantes para el examen?</summary>

Passive voice, modals of deduction, conditionals, reported speech, key word transformations y word formation son las más evaluadas.

</details>

<details><summary>¿Dónde practico la Unidad 60?</summary>

En la Unidad 60 del curso B2 y en esta misma guía, que incluye teoría, simulacro de examen y ejercicios con soluciones.

</details>
