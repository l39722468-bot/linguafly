Has llegado al final del segundo bloque del curso B2, y es el momento de comprobar si realmente dominas las cuatro estructuras que has aprendido en las últimas unidades, o si solo las recuerdas cuando están aisladas cada una en su propia lección. La **Unidad 15** es un **repaso integrado**: en lugar de practicar cada tema por separado, vas a encontrarlos **mezclados**, tal y como aparecerían en una conversación real, un examen oficial o un texto auténtico.

Vas a repasar **relative clauses** (defining y non-defining), **reduced relative clauses** (-ing / -ed), **modals of obligation and necessity** (must, have to, need to, needn't, don't have to) y **modals of deduction** en pasado (must have, might have, can't have). El objetivo no es aprender gramática nueva, sino entrenar tu capacidad para **reconocer qué estructura toca en cada momento** — una habilidad clave del nivel B2.

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 15 (B2)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Repasar **defining / non-defining relative clauses** (who/which/that/whose/where).
- Repasar **reduced relative clauses** con -ing (activo) y -ed (pasivo).
- Repasar **must / have to / need to / needn't / don't have to**.
- Repasar **must have / might have / can't have** para deducciones pasadas.
- Reconocer rápidamente **qué estructura corresponde** a cada frase, sin pistas previas.
- Repasar vocabulario mezclado de **culture, entertainment, money y business**.

## 1. Mapa del repaso: las cuatro unidades en una tabla

Antes de mezclar las estructuras, es útil tener una vista panorámica de qué aprendiste en cada unidad, para poder identificar patrones cuando aparezcan combinados.

![Review map](/blog/curso-b2/unit-15/review-map.png)

| Unidad | Gramática | Señal para reconocerla | Vocabulario |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **11** | Relative clauses | who / which / that / whose / where (con o sin comas) | culture & urban life |
| **12** | Reduced relatives | participio -ing (activo) o -ed (pasivo) sin pronombre relativo | entertainment & gardening |
| **13** | Modals of obligation | must / have to / need to / needn't / don't have to + verbo base | money & volunteering |
| **14** | Modals of deduction | modal + have + participio (sobre el pasado) | business & fashion |

La clave para no confundirlas está en la **estructura visible** de la frase: si ves un pronombre relativo, es la Unidad 11; si ves un participio sin pronombre, es la Unidad 12; si ves un modal + verbo base, es la Unidad 13; si ves un modal + *have* + participio, es la Unidad 14.

## 2. Relative clauses: recordatorio rápido (Unidad 11)

Recuerda el contraste esencial: **defining** (sin comas, información imprescindible) frente a **non-defining** (con comas, información extra). *That* solo aparece en defining clauses, nunca tras una coma.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b2/unit-15/review-relative.mp3" title="🔊 Review: relative clauses"></audio>

> The festival**, which is annual,** attracts thousands. (non-defining — información extra sobre un festival ya identificado)  
> The volunteers **who organise it** work all year. (defining — identifica de qué voluntarios hablamos)

**Recuerda:** who (personas), which (cosas), whose (posesión), where (lugar). Ninguno de los cuatro cambia de forma; lo que cambia es la puntuación según sea defining o non-defining.

---

## 3. Reduced relative clauses: recordatorio rápido (Unidad 12)

Recuerda que reducir una relative clause significa eliminar el pronombre relativo y el verbo *be*, dejando solo el participio: **-ing** para significado activo, **-ed** para significado pasivo.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b2/unit-15/review-reduced.mp3" title="🔊 Review: reduced relatives with -ing"></audio>

> Volunteers **helping** at the exhibition greeted the audience. ← Volunteers **who were helping**… (-ing, activo)

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b2/unit-15/review-reduced-ed.mp3" title="🔊 Review: reduced relatives with -ed"></audio>

> The compost **produced** in the garden is ready. ← The compost **that is produced**… (-ed, pasivo)

**Recuerda:** solo se reduce cuando el pronombre relativo es sujeto de la cláusula, nunca objeto; y nunca con modales (*who can sing* no se reduce).

---

## 4. Modals of obligation: recordatorio rápido (Unidad 13)

Recuerda la diferencia de origen: **must** (opinión del hablante) frente a **have to** (norma externa); y sobre todo, no confundas **needn't** (no hace falta) con **mustn't** (prohibido) — son opuestos.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b2/unit-15/review-obligation.mp3" title="🔊 Review: modals of obligation"></audio>

> You **must save** all receipts for the budget. (obligación, opinión del hablante)  
> You **needn't pay** today. (sin obligación, libertad de elección)

**Recuerda:** el pasado de *must* es **had to**; el pasado negativo es **didn't have to**; y **needn't have + participio** describe algo que hiciste sin que fuera necesario.

---

## 5. Modals of deduction: recordatorio rápido (Unidad 14)

Recuerda la estructura fija **modal + have + participio**, y el matiz de certeza: **must have** (casi seguro que sí), **might have** (posible), **can't have** (casi imposible).

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b2/unit-15/review-deduction.mp3" title="🔊 Review: modals of deduction"></audio>

> She **must have missed** the deadline. (evidencia a favor)  
> He **can't have sent** the invoice yet. (evidencia en contra)

**Recuerda:** el negativo lógico de *must have* es **can't have**, nunca *mustn't have*. Y no confundas la deducción de presente (*must be*) con la de pasado (*must have been*).

## Errores típicos al mezclar las cuatro estructuras

| ❌ Incorrecto | ✅ Correcto | De qué unidad viene el error |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *The festival, that is annual,…* | *The festival, **which** is annual,…* | U11 — *that* no va tras coma |
| *People attend the premiere waited outside.* | *People **attending** the premiere…* | U12 — falta el -ing |
| *You mustn't pay today* (sin obligación) | *You **needn't** pay today.* | U13 — mustn't ≠ needn't |
| *He mustn't have sent the invoice.* | *He **can't have sent** the invoice.* | U14 — negativo lógico es can't have |
| *The soundtrack recording last month…* | *The soundtrack **recorded** last month…* | U12 — significado pasivo → -ed |
| *I must to submit the budget.* | *I **must** submit the budget.* | U13 — must + verbo base, sin *to* |
| *She must missed the deadline.* | *She **must have missed** the deadline.* | U14 — falta *have* + participio |
| *The volunteers which organise it…* | *The volunteers **who** organise it…* | U11 — which no se usa con personas |

El patrón general: cuando mezclas varias estructuras, el cerebro tiende a "contagiar" la forma de una a otra (usar *that* donde no toca, olvidar *have* donde sí toca). La solución es siempre la misma: identificar primero **qué estructura es** antes de completarla.

## Reading: A busy week

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b2/unit-15/reading-mix.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: A busy week"></audio>

> It has been a busy week for everyone. The festival, which is annual, attracted thousands of visitors to the old district, and volunteers helping at the exhibition greeted them with a smile. You must save your receipts, the charity reminded everyone, because the budget needs to be submitted by Friday — although volunteers don't have to work every single day. Meanwhile, at the fashion house nearby, the designer must have finished the new collection early, since the fabric samples were already on the runway rack days before the launch. Someone might have changed the launch date at the last minute, but nobody can have forgotten about it completely, given how much the whole team had prepared. By the end of the week, the district that hosted the festival looked exhausted but proud, the compost produced in the community garden was finally ready, and everyone agreed that a small city can still surprise you with culture, generosity, and a touch of fashion, all in the same seven days.

Este texto mezcla las cuatro estructuras del repaso de forma natural: identifica mentalmente, frase por frase, a qué unidad pertenece cada una antes de continuar.

## Diálogo: Catching up after a long week

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b2/unit-15/dialogue-mix.mp3" title="🔊 Dialogue: Catching up after a long week"></audio>

> **Nora:** Did you go to the festival? I heard it's annual.  
> **Iker:** Yes, the festival, which is annual, is next to my neighbourhood. It was amazing.  
> **Nora:** Were there many volunteers helping there?  
> **Iker:** Definitely — the volunteers helping at the exhibition were incredibly kind.  
> **Nora:** By the way, do you have to submit your budget this week?  
> **Iker:** I must save all my receipts first, but I don't have to submit it until Friday.  
> **Nora:** And the new collection? Is it ready for the launch?  
> **Iker:** The designer must have finished it early — I saw the samples already on the runway.  
> **Nora:** Interesting. Do you think they changed the launch date?  
> **Iker:** They might have changed it, honestly, but nobody has confirmed anything.  
> **Nora:** One thing's for sure — you can't have missed all the excitement this week!  
> **Iker:** True. It's been a busy week, but I needn't complain — it was worth it.

### Ejercicio 1 — Identifica la unidad

Para cada frase, indica si pertenece a U11 (relative), U12 (reduced), U13 (obligation) o U14 (deduction).

1. *The museum, which protects local heritage, is free on Sundays.*  
2. *People attending the premiere waited outside.*  
3. *You needn't pay interest if you repay early.*  
4. *He can't have sent the invoice yet.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **U11** (relative clause, non-defining) · 2. **U12** (reduced, -ing) · 3. **U13** (modal of obligation) · 4. **U14** (modal of deduction)
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa con la forma correcta

1. The festival, ___ is annual, attracts thousands. (which)  
2. Volunteers ___ (help) at the exhibition greeted everyone.  
3. You ___ save all receipts for the budget. (must)  
4. She ___ (miss) the deadline. (must have)  
5. The compost ___ (produce) in the garden is ready.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **which** · 2. **helping** · 3. **must** · 4. **must have missed** · 5. **produced**
</details>

1. *The festival, that is annual, attracts thousands.*  
2. *People attend the premiere waited outside.*  
3. *You mustn't pay today* (sin obligación).  
4. *He mustn't have sent the invoice.*

<summary>Ver solución</summary>

## Tip del profesor

Cuando te enfrentes a una frase y no sepas qué estructura usar, hazte esta pregunta en orden: **¿hay un pronombre relativo (who/which/that/whose/where)?** Si sí, es Unidad 11 (o Unidad 12 si falta el pronombre y ves solo un participio). **¿Hay un modal + verbo base?** Es Unidad 13. **¿Hay un modal + have + participio?** Es Unidad 14. Este simple diagrama de decisión, aplicado con calma, resuelve casi cualquier duda de este repaso.

# Parte II — Ejercicios

## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** relative clauses, reduced relatives, obligation modals, past deduction

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. The festival, ___ is annual, starts soon. → *which / that / who*
2. People ___ the premiere waited outside. → *attending / attended / attend*
3. You ___ save receipts for the audit. → *must / might have / can't*
4. She ___ missed the client call. (deduction) → *must have / must / have to*
5. The compost ___ in the garden is ready. → *produced / producing / produce*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **which** · 2. **attending** · 3. **must** · 4. **must have** · 5. **produced**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. Non-defining → use ___ , not that. → *which/who / that only*
2. Reduced -ing ≈ ___ meaning. → *active / passive*
3. Needn't ≈ ___. → *don't have to / must have*
4. Can't have + pp = ___. → *almost impossible past / obligation*
5. Whose marks ___. → *possession / place / time*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **which/who** · 2. **active** · 3. **don't have to** · 4. **almost impossible past** · 5. **possession**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *The district, that we visited, is nice.*
2. *People who are attending → reduce*
3. *You must to wear a badge.*
4. *She must missed the deadline.*
5. *Volunteers helped at the show greeted us.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The district **that** we visited is nice. (defining, no commas)
2. People **attending**…
3. You must **wear** a badge.
4. She must **have missed** the deadline.
5. Volunteers **helping** at the show greeted us.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

1. must have + pp ≈ ___ → *deducción fuerte pasado / permiso*
