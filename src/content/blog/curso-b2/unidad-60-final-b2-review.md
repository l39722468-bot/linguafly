## Mapa de decisiones: grammar families overview

Antes de entrar en detalles, este es el mapa mental que debes dominar. Cada familia gramatical responde a una función comunicativa distinta:

| Familia gramatical | Función principal | Ejemplo rápido |
|---|---|---|
| Conditionals | Hipótesis, consecuencias | *If I **had studied**, I **would have passed**.* |
| Passive voice | Enfocar la acción, no el agente | *The report **was written** by the team.* |
| Passive reporting | Informar sin comprometer al hablante | *It **is said** that he is guilty.* |
| Reported speech | Transmitir lo que otro dijo | *She **said** she **had finished**.* |
| Modals | Obligación, deducción, consejo | *You **must** see this film.* |
| Phrasal verbs | Verbos compuestos de significado idiomático | *I **came across** an old photo.* |
| Linkers | Conectar ideas con precisión | *Despite being tired, she kept working.* |
| Inversion | Énfasis, formalidad | *Never **have** I **seen** such beauty.* |
| Cleft sentences | Destacar un elemento de la oración | *It was John **who** broke the window.* |
| Wish / Regret | Deseos, arrepentimiento | *I wish I **had known** earlier.* |
| Collocations | Combinaciones naturales de palabras | *make a decision*, no *do a decision* |
| Word formation | Derivación de palabras | *decide → decision → decisive → decisively* |

## 1. Conditionals: completo repaso

### Tabla de conditionals

| Tipo | Estructura | Ejemplo | Uso |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zero** | If + present, present | *If you heat water to 100°C, it boils.* | Hechos científicos, verdades generales |
| **First** | If + present, will + base | *If it rains, we'll cancel the picnic.* | Situaciones reales, probabilidades futuras |
| **Second** | If + past, would + base | *If I won the lottery, I'd travel the world.* | Hipótesis irreales, situaciones improbables |
| **Third** | If + past perfect, would have + pp | *If she had studied, she would have passed.* | Arrepentimiento, consecuencias pasadas irreal |
| **Mixed** | Combina tiempos | *If I had taken that job, I would be living in London now.* | Mezcla de pasado y presente |

### Conditionals con otros modales

- *If you **should** see Paul, tell him to call me.* (First conditional formal)
- *If I **were** you, I **would** apologise.* (Consejo)
- *If you **had** listened to me, you **wouldn't be** in this mess.* (Mixed)

## 2. Passive voice: todos los tiempos

| Tiempo | Activa | Pasiva |
|---|---|---|
| **Present Simple** | They make cars in Germany. | Cars **are made** in Germany. |
| **Present Continuous** | She is writing the report. | The report **is being written**. |
| **Past Simple** | Shakespeare wrote Hamlet. | Hamlet **was written** by Shakespeare. |
| **Past Continuous** | They were repairing the road. | The road **was being repaired**. |
| **Present Perfect** | They have arrested the suspect. | The suspect **has been arrested**. |
| **Past Perfect** | Someone had stolen my bike. | My bike **had been stolen**. |
| **Future Simple** | They will announce the results. | The results **will be announced**. |
| **Future Perfect** | They will have finished by June. | It **will have been finished** by June. |
| **Modals** | You must submit the form. | The form **must be submitted**. |

### Passive con dos complementos

- *They gave **me** a present.* → *I was given a present.* (más natural) / *A present was given to me.*
- *She told **him** the truth.* → *He was told the truth.*

## 3. Passive reporting y reported speech

### Passive reporting (formal)

| Estructura | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| It is said/believed/thought + that | *It is believed that the earth is warming.* |
| It is said/believed/thought + to + infinitive | *He is believed to be the richest man in town.* |
| S + is/was + said/believed + to + infinitive | *She is said to have left the country.* |

### Reported speech (común)

| Directo | Reported |
|---|---|
| "I **am** tired," she said. | She said she **was** tired. |
| "I **have finished**," he said. | He said he **had finished**. |
| "I **will call** you," she said. | She said she **would call** me. |
| "I **can** help," he said. | He said he **could** help. |
| "Do you **like** pizza?" she asked. | She asked if I **liked** pizza. |

**Cambio de pronombres y tiempos:** backshift automático. Said → told si hay objeto directo: *She **told me** she was tired.*

## 4. Modals: obligation, deduction, advice

### Tabla de modals

| Modal | Obligación | Deducción | Consejo |
|---|---|---|---|
| **must** | Tengo que estudiar. | Must be true. | — |
| **have to** | Tengo que ir al médico. | — | — |
| **mustn't** | No puedes tocar esto. | — | — |
| **should** | Deberías hacer ejercicio. | — | You should see a doctor. |
| **ought to** | Deberías ser más paciente. | — | You ought to apologise. |
| **might / may** | — | He might be at home. | — |
| **could** | — | That could be true. | You could try yoga. |
| **would** | — | — | I would start early. |
| **needn't** | No tienes que preocuparte. | — | — |
| **didn't need to** | No necesitó ir (pero fue). | — | — |

### Deducción con tiempos

- *He **must be** at home.* (presente — deducción lógica)
- *He **must have been** at home.* (pasado — deducción lógica sobre el pasado)
- *She **might be** late.* (posibilidad presente)
- *She **might have missed** the bus.* (posibilidad pasada)

## 6. Linkers: contrast, reason, addition, purpose

### Tabla de linkers

| Función | Linkers formales | Linkers informales |
|---|---|---|
| **Contraste** | however, nevertheless, although, despite, whereas | but, though, even though |
| **Razón** | because, since, as, due to, owing to | 'cause |
| **Adición** | furthermore, moreover, in addition, what's more | also, and, besides |
| **Propósito** | in order to, so as to, with the aim of | to, so that |
| **Resultado** | therefore, consequently, as a result, thus | so |
| **Concesión** | although, even though, despite, in spite of | even if |
| **Comparación** | similarly, likewise, in the same way | just like, just as |
| **Ejemplo** | for example, for instance, such as, namely | like |

### Errores comunes con linkers

- ~~Despite~~ **he was tired**, he kept working. → **Despite being tired**, he kept working. / **Although he was tired**, he kept working.
- **Due to** ~~that~~ the rain, the match was cancelled.
- She is not only intelligent **but also** hardworking. (correlativo)

## 7. Inversion, cleft sentences, wish / regret

### Inversion

Se invierte el orden auxiliar-sujeto después de adverbios negativos o restrictivos:

| Expresión | Ejemplo con inversión |
|---|---|
| Never | Never **have** I **seen** such a mess. |
| Rarely / Seldom | Rarely **does** he **arrive** on time. |
| Not only... but also | Not only **did** she **win**, but she also broke the record. |
| Hardly / Scarcely... when | Hardly **had** I **sat down** when the phone rang. |
| No sooner... than | No sooner **had** they **left** than it started raining. |
| Under no circumstances | Under no circumstances **should** you **open** that door. |
| Only then | Only then **did** he **realise** his mistake. |
| So + adjective + be | So beautiful **was** the sunset **that** we stopped to watch. |

### Cleft sentences

Para enfatizar un elemento de la oración:

| Tipo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| **It is/was + énfasis + who/that** | *It was John **who** broke the window.* |
| **What + sujeto + verb + es** | *What I need **is** a holiday.* |
| **The thing/person + that** | *The person **that** I admire most is my mother.* |

### Wish / Regret

| Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| Wish + past simple | Deseo presente irreal | *I wish I **had** more free time.* |
| Wish + past perfect | Arrepentimiento pasado | *I wish I **had studied** harder.* |
| Wish + would | Queja / cambio futuro | *I wish you **would stop** making noise.* |
| Regret + gerund | Arrepentimiento por algo hecho | *I regret **telling** her the secret.* |
| Regret + to infinitive | Noticia desagradable formal | *I regret **to inform** you that your application has been refused.* |

## 8. Collocations y redes de vocabulario

### Collocations esenciales B2

| Verbo | Collocations |
|---|---|
| **make** | a decision, a mistake, progress, an effort, a difference |
| **do** | homework, exercise, research, a job, damage |
| **take** | a photo, notes, a break, a chance, action |
| **have** | a look, a chat, an effect, an influence, a problem |
| **give** | advice, a speech, a chance, permission, an example |

### Redes de vocabulario: feelings

- **Positive:** delighted, thrilled, pleased, content, relieved, grateful
- **Negative:** frustrated, devastated, overwhelmed, anxious, exhausted
- **Intensity scale:** annoyed → irritated → furious / happy → ecstatic

### Redes de vocabulario: work & business

- **Verbs:** recruit, promote, resign, retire, sack, lay off
- **Nouns:** colleague, employer, employee, boss, manager, CEO
- **Adjectives:** qualified, experienced, reliable, ambitious, competent

## 9. Reading: simulated exam passage

**Read the text below and answer the questions that follow.**

> **The Art of Slowing Down**
>
> In a world that celebrates productivity and speed, choosing to slow down can feel almost rebellious. Yet research consistently shows that the quality of our experiences improves dramatically when we resist the urge to rush. A 2023 study at the University of Cambridge found that participants who were asked to eat a chocolate bar slowly reported 40% more enjoyment than those who ate it at their normal pace. The implications extend far beyond chocolate. People who walk slowly through a city notice architectural details that speed-walkers miss entirely. Musicians who practise deliberately — focusing on one passage at a time — advance faster than those who simply repeat pieces from start to finish. Slowness, it turns out, is not the enemy of efficiency but often its secret ally. The challenge is not to eliminate busyness from our lives, but to ensure that when we do move quickly, it is by choice rather than compulsion.

**Questions:**

1. What is the main idea of the text?
2. According to the Cambridge study, what happened to participants who ate slowly?
3. What does the author compare eating chocolate to?
4. The word "compulsion" in the last sentence is closest in meaning to ___
5. What tone does the author adopt throughout the text?

## 11. Errores más comunes en B2

| # | Error | Correction | Regla |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ~~If I would have known~~ | If I **had known** | Third conditional: past perfect, no "would" en la cláusula if |
| 2 | ~~He suggested me to go~~ | He suggested **I (should) go** / He suggested **going** | Suggest + that + subjuntivo / gerundio |
| 3 | ~~Despite of being tired~~ | **Despite being tired** | Despite + gerund / noun, nunca "despite of" |
| 4 | ~~The music was being listened~~ | The music **was being listened to** | Phrasal verb pasivo: la preposición se queda al final |
| 5 | ~~I wish I would have more time~~ | I wish I **had** more time | Wish + past simple para deseos presentes |
| 6 | ~~He is believed being rich~~ | He is believed **to be** rich | Passive reporting + infinitivo |
| 7 | ~~Although it rained, but we went out~~ | **Although** it rained, we went out | Although y but no se usan juntos |
| 8 | ~~The number of students are~~ | The number of students **is** | "The number of" es singular |
| 9 | ~~I used to go often to school~~ | I used to **often go** to school | Frecuencia: adverbio entre "used to" y el verbo |
| 10 | ~~She told me that had she finished~~ | She told me that **she had finished** | Reported speech: no inversión en cláusulas that |

## 13. Resumen rápido: master summary table

| Estructura | Clave para recordar | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **Conditionals** | Backshift en tipos 2 y 3 | If I were you, I'd go. |
| **Passive** | Agente con "by" opcional | It was built in 1900. |
| **Passive reporting** | Said + to + infinitive | He is said to be a genius. |
| **Reported speech** | Backshift automático | She said she was tired. |
| **Modals** | Must = deducción; have to = obligación | He must be tired. |
| **Phrasal verbs** | Separables e inseparables | Turn it off. / I ran into her. |
| **Linkers** | Although + clause; despite + gerund | Although tired, I worked. |
| **Inversion** | Never + aux + suj + verbo | Never have I seen this. |
| **Cleft** | It was X who/that Y | It was John who said it. |
| **Wish** | Past simple = presente; past perfect = pasado | I wish I knew. |
| **Collocations** | make vs do por convención | Make a decision. |
| **Word formation** | Prefijos y sufijos B2 | un-, re-, -tion, -ness, -ful |
