### Lección 1 — Gramática Mixta

#### Ejercicio 1: Mixed Conditionals

Complete each sentence with the correct mixed conditional form.

1. If the patient \_\_\_\_\_\_ (take) the medication on time, she \_\_\_\_\_\_ (not feel) so unwell now.
2. If the government \_\_\_\_\_\_ (invest) more in mental health services, the waiting lists \_\_\_\_\_\_ (not be) so long last year.
3. If I \_\_\_\_\_\_ (not skip) breakfast every day, I \_\_\_\_\_\_ (not have) so much low energy in the mornings.
4. Had the surgeon \_\_\_\_\_\_ (perform) the operation earlier, the patient \_\_\_\_\_\_ (make) a full recovery by now.
5. If the vaccine \_\_\_\_\_\_ (be) available globally, the pandemic \_\_\_\_\_\_ (not affect) so many people in 2020.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

1. **had taken / wouldn't feel** — Past condition → present result.
2. **had invested / wouldn't have been** — Past condition → past result with present relevance.
3. **didn't skip / wouldn't have** — Present condition → past result.
4. **performed / would have made** — Inverted past condition → past result.
5. **were / wouldn't have affected** — Present unreal condition → past result.

</details>

#### Ejercicio 2: Passive + Reporting

Rewrite each sentence in the passive voice or passive reporting form.

1. Scientists have developed a new treatment for migraine.
   → A new treatment for migraine \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.
2. People believe that stress causes heart disease.
   → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ that stress causes heart disease.
3. The hospital carried out the procedure yesterday.
   → The procedure \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ by the hospital yesterday.
4. Researchers estimate that 2 billion people are overweight.
   → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ that 2 billion people are overweight.
5. A team of specialists will treat the patient next week.
   → The patient \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ by a team of specialists next week.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

1. **has been developed** — Present perfect passive.
2. **It is believed** — Passive reporting structure.
3. **was carried out** — Past simple passive.
4. **It is estimated** — Passive reporting structure.
5. **will be treated** — Future simple passive.

</details>

#### Ejercicio 3: Modals + Phrasal Verbs

Choose the correct option.

1. You (must / mustn't) drink alcohol while taking this medication.
2. The doctor (might / must) have recommended surgery — we're not sure yet.
3. She (has to / is having to) fast for twelve hours before the blood test.
4. Could you (bring up / carry out) the topic of mental health at the meeting?
5. If you want to improve your fitness, you should (work out / look into) regularly.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

1. **mustn't** — Prohibition.
2. **might** — Possibility (we're not sure).
3. **has to** — External obligation (the doctor requires it).
4. **bring up** — Introduce a topic. *Carry out* means to perform a test.
5. **work out** — Exercise. *Look into* means to investigate.

</details>

#### Ejercicio 4: Linkers + Wish/Regret

Complete each sentence with the correct linker or wish/regret form.

1. \_\_\_\_\_\_ the treatment was expensive, it was highly effective. (contrast)
2. I wish I \_\_\_\_\_\_ (start) exercising when I was younger.
3. \_\_\_\_\_\_ the new regulations, sugar content in food has been reduced. (cause)
4. She regrets \_\_\_\_\_\_ (not attend) the health screening last year.
5. \_\_\_\_\_\_, mental health services have improved dramatically in recent years. (addition)

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

1. **Although / Even though** — Concession / contrast.
2. **had started** — Past regret (*wish* + past perfect).
3. **Due to / Because of** — Cause.
4. **not attending** — *Regret* + gerund.
5. **Furthermore / Moreover** — Addition.

</details>

#### Ejercicio 8: Sentence Transformation

Rewrite each sentence using the word in brackets.

1. Doctors say that people should exercise more. (recommended)
   → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ that people should exercise more.
2. The hospital performed the operation last Tuesday. (passive)
   → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ last Tuesday.
3. She can't eat dairy products. (allergic)
   → She \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ dairy products.
4. The patient is certain to recover fully. (bound)
   → The patient \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ recover fully.
5. It's a good idea to have a check-up every year. (advisable)
   → It is \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ a check-up every year.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

1. **It is recommended** — Passive reporting.
2. **The operation was performed (by the hospital)** — Past simple passive.
3. **is allergic to** — Collocation: *be allergic to*.
4. **is bound to** — *Be bound to* = certain to.
5. **advisable to have** — Formal advice structure.

</details>

The concept of "wellbeing" has moved from the fringes of healthcare to the mainstream in recent years. While traditional medicine has long focused on treating disease, a growing body of research suggests that addressing mental, emotional, and social factors is equally important. It is now widely accepted that poor mental health can lead to physical illness, and vice versa.

In the UK, the NHS has begun integrating mental health services into primary care. Patients who visit their GP with symptoms of anxiety or depression can now be referred to in-house therapists rather than waiting for specialist appointments. It is believed that this approach reduces recovery times and improves patient outcomes.

However, critics argue that the system is still underfunded. A recent report estimated that mental health services receive only 11% of the total NHS budget, despite accounting for 23% of the overall disease burden. "If the government were to allocate funding proportionally," said Dr. James Carter, a health policy researcher, "we would see a dramatic improvement in outcomes."

Despite these challenges, there is reason for optimism. Community-based programmes, which focus on prevention rather than treatment, have shown promising results. In one trial, participants who engaged in weekly group therapy and physical activity reported a 45% reduction in symptoms of depression after six months. These findings suggest that if such programmes were rolled out nationally, the impact on public health could be transformative.

#### Ejercicio 26: Formal Report — Rewrite

Rewrite the following informal paragraph as a formal health report extract.

> *So basically, lots of people are getting diabetes now. We think it's because of bad food and not enough exercise. The government should do something about it. If they spent more money on teaching people about healthy eating, fewer people would get sick.*

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**Formal version:**

*It is estimated that the prevalence of diabetes has increased significantly in recent years. This rise is thought to be largely caused by poor nutrition and insufficient physical activity. It is recommended that the government should allocate greater funding to public health education. If resources were directed towards promoting healthier dietary habits, it is believed that the incidence of diabetes-related conditions could be substantially reduced.*

#### Ejercicio 27: Conditionals — Write Sentences

Write a mixed conditional sentence for each situation.

1. Past action → present result: You didn't go to the doctor. You still feel ill.
2. Present situation → past result: Your diet is unhealthy. You gained weight last year.
3. Unreal past condition → past result: The hospital invested in new equipment. More patients survived.
4. Inverted conditional: You didn't take the vaccine. You caught the virus.
5. Unreal present → past: The health service is well-funded. Waiting times were shorter last year.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

1. If I **had gone** to the doctor, I **wouldn't still feel** ill.
2. If my diet **weren't** so unhealthy, I **wouldn't have gained** weight last year.
3. If the hospital **had invested** in new equipment, more patients **would have survived**.
4. **Had I taken** the vaccine, I **wouldn't have caught** the virus.
5. If the health service **were** well-funded, waiting times **would have been** shorter last year.

</details>

#### Ejercicio 32: Linkers — Health Essay

Combine each pair of sentences with an appropriate linker.

1. Regular exercise improves physical health. It also benefits mental health.
2. The treatment was expensive. It was worth it because it saved his life.
3. She followed the doctor's advice. She didn't recover as quickly as expected.

#### Ejercicio 33: Health Vocabulary — Error Correction

Find and correct the error in each sentence.

4. He has a surgery on his knee next month.
5. The symptoms was very mild at first.

1. ...diagnosed **with** diabetes... (*diagnose* + *with*)
2. She **has** a prescription... OR She was **given** a prescription... (*take a prescription* is incorrect)
3. The doctor recommended **that I rest** for a week. / The doctor recommended **resting** for a week. (*recommend* + gerund / *that* clause, not *recommend someone to do*)
4. He **is having** surgery... OR He **is scheduled for** surgery... (*have surgery*, not *a surgery*)
5. The symptoms **were** very mild... (*symptoms* is plural)

</details>

#### Ejercicio 34: Speaking — Role Play Prompts

Prepare a 2-minute spoken response for **one** of the following prompts. Use at least **three** different B2 structures from this unit.

**Prompt A:** *Describe a time when you or someone you know had to deal with a health problem. What happened? What was the treatment? How do you wish the situation had been handled differently?*

**Prompt B:** *Some people argue that prevention is better than cure. Do you agree? What could the government do to improve public health? Use conditionals and modals in your answer.*

**Prompt C:** *Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of a national health service like the NHS. Use linkers of contrast and addition.*

<details><summary>Ver modelo de resposta (Prompt A)</summary>

A few years ago, a close friend of mine was diagnosed with severe anaemia. She had been feeling exhausted for months, but she hadn't gone to the doctor because she thought it was just stress. By the time she was diagnosed, her iron levels were dangerously low. She was prescribed iron supplements and had to go through regular blood tests for six months.

Looking back, she wishes she had sought medical advice sooner. If she had gone to her GP earlier, the treatment would have been much simpler. It is believed that early diagnosis in cases like this leads to significantly better outcomes. I think this experience taught both of us the importance of not ignoring persistent symptoms.

</details>

#### Ejercicio 35: Writing — Health Report Structure

Write a short health report (150–200 words) on the topic: *"The Impact of Screen Time on Teenage Mental Health."*

Use the following structure:
- **Introduction:** State the purpose of the report.
- **Findings:** Present two or three key facts (use passive voice and passive reporting).
- **Conclusions:** Use modals of deduction.
- **Recommendations:** Use modals of obligation and advice.

<details><summary>Ver modelo de resposta</summary>

**The Impact of Screen Time on Teenage Mental Health**

This report examines the effects of excessive screen time on the mental health of teenagers aged 13–17.

It is estimated that teenagers spend an average of seven hours per day on screens outside of schoolwork. Recent studies suggest that more than four hours of recreational screen time is associated with increased levels of anxiety and depression. It is believed that social media use is a particularly significant factor, as it can lead to disrupted sleep patterns and low self-esteem.

It is concluded that excessive screen time is likely to have a negative impact on teenage mental health. If current trends continue, the rate of mental health issues among adolescents could rise significantly.

It is recommended that parents should set clear boundaries around screen use, and that schools should incorporate digital wellbeing into their curricula. Furthermore, the government ought to fund further research into this area.

*(164 words)*

</details>

#### Ejercicio 36: Speaking — Discuss and Compare

Work with a partner (or write a response) comparing **two** healthcare systems you know about (e.g., NHS vs. the US system). Discuss:

- How they are funded
- The role of prevention vs. treatment
- What you wish could be improved

Use passive reporting, mixed conditionals, and at least two phrasal verbs.

<details><summary>Ver modelo de resposta</summary>

The NHS in the UK provides healthcare free at the point of use, funded through taxation. In contrast, the US system relies heavily on private insurance. It is believed that the NHS model ensures greater equity, but it is also thought to suffer from long waiting times due to underfunding.

If the US system were to adopt a more preventive approach, it could reduce the enormous costs associated with chronic disease. Likewise, if the NHS were better funded, patients wouldn't have to go through such long delays for treatment.

I wish both systems would invest more in mental health services. It is widely reported that mental health is the area most neglected in both countries. From what I've looked into, community-based programmes could be set up at relatively low cost and could make a significant difference.

</details>

## Tips para esta unidad

- **Mixed conditionals** son la estructura más discriminadora en B2. Dominar la diferencia entre *If I had... (I would be...)* y *If I were... (I would have been...)* te separa del nivel A2-B1.
- **Passive reporting** aparece en casi every Reading & Use of English task. Memoriza las estructuras *It is believed that...* y *...is thought to have...*.
- **Phrasal verbs** de salud (*carry out, cut down on, go through*) son altamente productivos en Writing y Speaking.
