Este cuaderno reúne los **ejercicios de la Unidad 52 del curso B2** (*Passive Voice, Passive Reporting & Reported Speech*). Responde primero sin mirar la clave y comprueba después qué auxiliar, participio o estructura de reporte controla cada respuesta.

> **Guía teórica:** [Passive Voice y Reported Speech B2](/blog/curso-b2/unidad-52-passive-reported-speech-human-rights)<br>
> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 52 — Passive Voice y Reported Speech](/curso-b2/unit-52)

**Recuerda:** la pasiva exige **be + V3**; el passive reporting usa **that + clause** o **to + infinitive**; y el reported speech cambia tiempos y pronombres cuando el verbo introductor está en pasado.

Quien busca **ejercicios passive voice B2** o **reported speech ejercicios B2** está en el texto: cada apartado lo explica en contexto, dentro de la frase o del ejemplo.

## Lección 1 — Gramática: Passive Voice

Completa o transforma la frase.

1. The court ___ (examine) the evidence. → The evidence ___ by the court.
2. They will announce the verdict tomorrow. → The verdict ___ tomorrow.
3. The police should have protected the witness. → The witness ___ by the police.
4. Someone had altered the document. → The document ___ .
5. Corrige: *The suspect was arrest yesterday.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **examines; is examined** — present simple passive: *is + V3*.
2. **will be announced** — future passive.
3. **should have been protected** — modal perfect passive.
4. **had been altered** — past perfect passive.
5. **The suspect was arrested yesterday.** — se necesita el participio *arrested*.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Gramática y vocabulario: Reporting

Elige la opción correcta.

1. ___ is believed that the trial was fair. (*It / He*)
2. The defendant is alleged ___ stolen the funds. (*to have / having*)
3. The lawyer said, “I have seen the report.” → The lawyer said that she ___ the report. (*had seen / has seen*)
4. “Where was the witness?” → The judge asked where the witness ___ . (*was / had*)
5. The judge told the witness ___ the truth. (*to tell / telling*)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **It** — estructura impersonal: *It is believed that...*.
2. **to have** — acción anterior: *to have + V3*.
3. **had seen** — backshift de *have seen*.
4. **was** — pregunta indirecta sin inversión.
5. **to tell** — orden con *tell + object + to-infinitive*.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: A human-rights report

> The commission reported that three detainees had been denied legal advice. It is believed that the procedure was changed after an independent review. The minister said that new safeguards would be introduced, while the victims' lawyer insisted that the recommendations be implemented without delay. The report concluded that complaints must be investigated impartially and that witnesses should be protected from retaliation.

Responde en inglés.

1. What had been denied to the detainees?
2. What is believed about the procedure?
3. What did the minister promise?
4. What did the lawyer insist on?
5. How should complaints be investigated?

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Legal advice had been denied.** — aparece en la primera frase.
2. **It is believed that it was changed after an independent review.**
3. **He said that new safeguards would be introduced.**
4. **That the recommendations be implemented without delay.**
5. **They should be investigated impartially.**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: A court briefing

Escucha o lee este guion y completa.

> The spokesperson explained that the hearing had been postponed because new evidence was being examined. She added that the defendant would be informed immediately and that the witness could be interviewed again. The judge warned reporters not to publish the witness's name.

1. Why had the hearing been postponed?
2. What would happen to the defendant?
3. Who could be interviewed again?
4. What did the judge warn reporters not to do?
5. Cambia a pasiva: *Officials are examining new evidence.*

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Because new evidence was being examined.**
2. **The defendant would be informed immediately.**
3. **The witness could be interviewed again.**
4. **They were warned not to publish the witness's name.**
5. **New evidence is being examined.** — present continuous passive.

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing y Speaking

### Writing

Escribe un email formal de 120–140 palabras para informar a un director de que una auditoría legal ha terminado. Incluye: dos pasivas, un ejemplo de passive reporting y una recomendación indirecta.

<details><summary>Modelo</summary>

**Subject: Legal audit completed**

The audit has been completed and all case files have been reviewed. It is believed that the new filing procedure has reduced delays. The auditors reported that two minor inconsistencies had been identified; they will be corrected this week. I recommend that the team be reminded to record every interview immediately. The final report will be circulated once the corrections have been checked. Please let me know if any further evidence is required.

</details>

### Speaking

Explica durante un minuto cómo debe protegerse a un testigo. Usa *must be*, *should be*, *is believed to* y un verbo de reporte.

<details><summary>Pista y modelo</summary>

**Witnesses must be protected from retaliation. Their identities should be kept confidential, and they are believed to be safer when interviews are recorded. The lawyer said that every complaint should be investigated promptly.**

</details>

## Lección 1 — Gramática y chunks

### 1.1 Passive voice: completa el tiempo verbal

Completa cada oración con la forma pasiva correcta del verbo entre paréntesis.

**1.** The defendant __________ (charge) with fraud.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**is charged**

*The defendant **is charged** with fraud.* — Present simple passive.

</details>

**2.** The law __________ (pass) by parliament in 2018.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**was passed**

*The law **was passed** by parliament in 2018.* — Past simple passive.

</details>

**3.** The evidence __________ (examine) by the forensic team.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**has been examined**

*The evidence **has been examined** by the forensic team.* — Present perfect passive.

</details>

**4.** A new verdict __________ (announce) next week.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**will be announced**

*A new verdict **will be announced** next week.* — Future simple passive.

</details>

**5.** The rights __________ (should / respect) by all authorities.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**should have been respected**

*The rights **should have been respected** by all authorities.* — Past modal passive.

</details>

### 1.2 Passive reporting: transforma a passive reporting

Transforma cada oración activa en passive reporting usando "It is/was said that…" o la estructura personal con "to".

**1.** People say that the suspect fled the country.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

- **It is said that the suspect fled the country.**
- **The suspect is said to have fled the country.**

</details>

**2.** Experts believe that the law is unconstitutional.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

- **It is believed that the law is unconstitutional.**
- **The law is believed to be unconstitutional.**

</details>

**3.** Reports claim that evidence was destroyed.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

- **It is claimed that evidence was destroyed.**
- **Evidence is claimed to have been destroyed.**

</details>

**4.** The media reported that the judge was biased.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

- **It was reported that the judge was biased.**
- **The judge was reported to have been biased.**

</details>

**5.** Analysts expect that the verdict will be unanimous.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

- **It is expected that the verdict will be unanimous.**
- **The verdict is expected to be unanimous.**

</details>

### 1.3 Reported speech: transforma a estilo indirecto

Transforma cada cita directa en reported speech con backshift adecuado.

**1.** "I am not guilty," said the defendant.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**The defendant said (that) he was not guilty.**

</details>

**2.** "I saw the victim last week," the witness stated.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**The witness stated (that) he/she had seen the victim the previous week.**

</details>

**3.** "I will cooperate fully with the investigation," the suspect promised.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**The suspect promised (that) he/she would cooperate fully with the investigation.**

</details>

**4.** "Where is the evidence stored?" the lawyer asked.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**The lawyer asked where the evidence was stored.**

</details>

**5.** "Give me access to the documents," the judge ordered.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**The judge ordered (him/her) to give him/her access to the documents.**

</details>

### 1.4 Mixed: elige la forma correcta

Selecciona la opción correcta (a o b) para completar cada oración.

**1.** It __________ that the trial will be fair.
a) is believed
b) is believed to

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

**a) is believed**

*It is believed that the trial will be fair.* — Con "that + clause", se usa la estructura impersonal sin "to".

</details>

**2.** The man is said __________ the scene.
a) to have left
b) having left

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**a) to have left**

*The man is said to have left the scene.* — Después de "is said" se usa "to + infinitive" (perfecto para pasado).

</details>

**3.** She said that she __________ the evidence.
a) has seen
b) had seen

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**b) had seen**

*She said that she had seen the evidence.* — Backshift: present perfect → past perfect.

</details>

**4.** The law __________ in 2020.
a) was being passed
b) was passed

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**b) was passed**

*The law was passed in 2020.* — Past simple passive para una acción puntual en el pasado.

</details>

**5.** It __________ that the evidence had been tampered with.
a) was reported
b) reported

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**a) was reported**

*It was reported that the evidence had been tampered with.* — Passive reporting: "it + be + V3 + that".

</details>

**1.** Identify all instances of passive reporting in the dialogue.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

- *It is believed that…*
- *It is said to confirm…*
- *It was reported that…*
- *Internal documents are said to have been leaked…*
- *It is expected that…*

</details>

**2.** Why does the lawyer use "It was reported that" instead of "They said"?

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*It was reported that* is more formal and impersonal, appropriate for legal and journalistic contexts. *They said* is informal and vague about the source.

</details>

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

*We are hopeful* is personal and informal. *It is hoped* is impersonal and formal, more appropriate for official statements.

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing y Speaking

### Writing

**1.** Write a short news report (3–5 sentences) about a human rights case using at least 3 passive voice constructions.

<details><summary>Ver modelo</summary>

A human rights tribunal **was convened** in Geneva to investigate allegations of abuse. Testimony **has been collected** from over 100 witnesses. It **is believed** that the crimes **were committed** over a period of five years. The tribunal **is expected to issue** its findings by the end of the month.

</details>

**2.** Rewrite the following active sentences in passive voice: "The police arrested the suspect. They charged him with theft. A judge will hear the case."

<details><summary>Ver modelo</summary>

The suspect **was arrested** by the police. He **was charged** with theft. The case **will be heard** by a judge.

</details>

**3.** Transform this direct quote into reported speech: "I have committed no crime," the defendant told the court.

<details><summary>Ver modelo</summary>

The defendant told the court that he **had committed** no crime.

</details>

**4.** Write a formal paragraph (50–70 words) about freedom of speech using at least 2 passive reporting structures.

<details><summary>Ver modelo</summary>

Freedom of speech **is considered** a fundamental human right in democratic societies. It **is believed that** this right **is protected** by the constitutions of most nations. However, it **is reported that** in many countries, journalists **are persecuted** for expressing their opinions. It **is hoped that** international pressure **will lead** to greater protection.

</details>

**5.** Rewrite this informal sentence in formal legal style using passive reporting: "People think the government broke the law."

<details><summary>Ver modelo</summary>

**It is believed that** the government **violated** the law. / The government is believed to have violated the law.

</details>

**6.** Write a sentence using past modal passive about a human rights violation: "should have been respected."

<details><summary>Ver modelo</summary>

The fundamental rights of the detainees **should have been respected** by the authorities at all times.

</details>

**7.** Write a sentence using conditional passive about a legal scenario: "would be dismissed."

<details><summary>Ver modelo</summary>

The case **would be dismissed** if insufficient evidence is presented to the court.

</details>

**8.** Transform this sentence into perfect conditional passive: "If the lawyer had objected, the jury would have been sequestered."

<details><summary>Ver modelo</summary>

If the lawyer had objected, the jury **would have been sequestered**. (The sentence is already in correct perfect conditional passive form.)

</details>

**9.** Write a 3-sentence legal summary using: was established, has been alleged, is expected to be.

<details><summary>Ver modelo</summary>

A special commission **was established** to investigate the matter. It **has been alleged** that funds were diverted from public accounts. The final report **is expected to be** published next quarter.

</details>

**10.** Write a formal complaint letter opening (2–3 sentences) using passive voice and reported speech: "We have been told that our complaint was ignored."

<details><summary>Ver modelo</summary>

We **have been informed** that our initial complaint, filed on 15 March 2026, **was not addressed** by the relevant department. It **has been brought to our attention** that similar cases **have been overlooked** in the past. We respectfully request that this matter be reviewed immediately.

</details>

**1.** Prepare a 1-minute monologue about a human rights topic. Use at least 5 passive/reporting constructions.

<details><summary>Consejos y modelo</summary>

Suggested chunk list to include:
- *It is believed that…*
- *The law was passed in…*
- *It is reported that…*
- *Rights have been violated…*
- *It is expected that…*

Model opening: *Human rights are protected by international law, but it is believed that many countries violate these rights systematically. It is reported that thousands of people are detained without trial each year. It is hoped that international tribunals will hold perpetrators accountable.*

</details>

**2.** Role-play a courtroom dialogue (judge + lawyer). Use passive voice, passive reporting, and reported speech. (2–3 minutes)

<details><summary>Script sugerido</summary>

> **Judge:** Has the evidence been examined?
> **Lawyer:** Yes, Your Honour. It has been examined by two independent experts.
> **Judge:** And what is the conclusion?
> **Lawyer:** It is believed that the documents are authentic.
> **Judge:** Was the defendant present when the evidence was collected?
> **Lawyer:** The defendant was not present. He is said to have been abroad at the time.

</details>

**3.** Explain the difference between "It is said that he fled" and "He is said to have fled" in your own words.

<details><summary>Respuesta modelo</summary>

Both convey the same meaning — that there is a claim that the man left the country. The first structure (*It is said that…*) emphasises the information, while the second (*He is said to have fled*) emphasises the person. Both are formal and appropriate for legal contexts.

</details>

## Tip del profesor

En inglés formal y periodístico, la passive voice y la passive reporting no son solo "opciones gramaticales": son herramientas de precisión y neutralidad. En un tribunal o en un informe de derechos humanos, el agente a menudo se omite deliberadamente para enfocarse en los hechos y las víctimas. Domina estas estructuras y tu inglés sonará profesional y creíble.

## Preguntas frecuentes

### ¿Cuándo uso la passive voice en inglés?
Cuando el receptor de la acción es más importante que el agente, o cuando el agente es desconocido: *The defendant was convicted.*

### ¿Cómo se forma el passive con diferentes tiempos?
Subject + be (time) + past participle. *is done, was done, has been done, will be done.*

### ¿Qué diferencia hay entre reported speech y passive reporting?
Reported speech transforms direct quotes. Passive reporting attributes information to sources.

### ¿Cuándo uso "it is said that" vs "the man is said to"?
Both correct. First emphasizes information; second emphasizes person. Passive with "to" needs infinitive.
