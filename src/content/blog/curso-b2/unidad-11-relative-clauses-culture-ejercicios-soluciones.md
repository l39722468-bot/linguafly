## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** defining / non-defining relative clauses; who / which / that / whose / where

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. The district ___ we visited has a mural. → *that / , which / who*
2. The festival, ___ is annual, attracts thousands. → *which / that / who*
3. My neighbour, ___ family runs the festival, is friendly. → *whose / who / which*
4. The landmark ___ we met is near the skyscraper. → *where / which / that*
5. Non-defining clauses need ___. → *commas / no commas / that*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **that** · 2. **which** · 3. **whose** · 4. **where** · 5. **commas**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. In non-defining clauses, avoid ___. → *that / which / who*
2. Defining clauses give ___ information. → *essential / extra / optional*
3. People ___ live here love the nightlife. → *who / which / where*
4. The mural ___ won the prize is huge. → *which/that / who / whose*
5. The community ___ heritage is rich organises walks. → *whose / who / which*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **that** · 2. **essential** · 3. **who** · 4. **which/that** · 5. **whose**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *The festival that is annual, attracts thousands.*
2. *My neighbour who family runs it is kind.*
3. *The place which we met is famous.*
4. *The district, that we visited, has a mural.*
5. *People which live here are friendly.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The festival, **which** is annual, attracts thousands.
2. My neighbour, **whose** family runs it, is kind.
3. The place **where** we met is famous.
4. The district **that** we visited has a mural.
5. People **who** live here are friendly.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Sara in the city

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b2/unit-11/reading-workbook.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Sara in the city"></audio>

> Last weekend Sara explored a new district in the city. The district that she visited has a famous mural by a local artist. The festival, which is annual, attracts thousands of visitors from abroad. Her neighbour, whose family runs the festival, gave her a free ticket. The landmark where they met is near a glass skyscraper. Heritage and tradition still matter in this modern community. Nightlife around the square is busy but friendly.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Who explored a district? → *Sara / Omar / Lena*
2. Defining: The district ___ she visited → *that / , which*
3. The festival, which is ___, attracts thousands → *annual / cancelled / quiet*
4. Whose family runs the festival? → *neighbour / designer / donor*
5. Landmark is near a ___ → *skyscraper / runway / compost*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Sara** · 2. **that** · 3. **annual** · 4. **her neighbour's** · 5. **skyscraper**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Heritage and ___ still matter → *tradition / invoice / merger*
7. Nightlife around the square is ___ → *busy but friendly / empty / silent*
8. Non-defining marker in text? → *commas / no relative*
9. Vocab: mural by a ___ artist → *local / foreign only / unknown*
10. Went abroad for the festival? → *False / True*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **tradition** · 7. **busy but friendly** · 8. **commas with which** · 9. **local** · 10. **False (visitors from abroad)**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

12. Write one defining + one non-defining.
13. Key vocab? → *yes / none*
14. Tone? → *curious / angry*
15. Course link → */curso-b2/unit-11*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **See text** · 12. **Model OK** · 13. **district, mural, heritage** · 14. **curious / cultural** · 15. **/curso-b2/unit-11**

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 2 defining y 2 non-defining relative clauses sobre tu ciudad.
2. Completa: The district ___ (that) I love has a mural.
3. Completa: The festival, ___ (which) is annual, starts in May.
4. Completa: My friend, ___ (whose) sister is an artist, invited me.
5. Completa: The square ___ (where) we meet is busy at night.
6. Explica defining vs non-defining en 1 frase.
7. Corrige: *The festival that is annual, is famous.*
8. Corrige: *People which live here are kind.*
9. Párrafo (6 frases) con culture vocab + relatives.
10. Traduce: El barrio que visitamos tiene un mural.
11. Traduce: El festival, que es anual, atrae a miles.
12. Usa whose + where en un diálogo corto.
13. Lista 5 palabras: district, landmark, heritage, mural, nightlife.
14. Autochequeo: ¿puedo quitar that en non-defining? (no)
15. Enlace teoría U11.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open — 2 defining + 2 non-defining.
2. **that**
3. **which**
4. **whose**
5. **where**
6. Defining = essential (no commas); non-defining = extra (commas).
7. The festival, **which** is annual, is famous.
8. People **who** live here are kind.
9. Open culture paragraph.
10. The district **that** we visited has a mural.
11. The festival, **which** is annual, attracts thousands.
12. Open dialogue.
13. Self-check vocab aloud.
14. No — non-defining does not use *that*.
15. [Guía U11](/blog/curso-b2/unidad-11-relative-clauses-culture)

</details>
