## Unidad 54 — Linkers y Collocations: Política y Relaciones Internacionales

La **Unidad 54** integra **linkers** de contraste, causa, propósito y adición con **collocations** del ámbito de la política y las relaciones internacionales. Cada conector sitúa la información correctamente; cada colocación aporta precisión léxica al registro formal.

## 1. Linkers of Contrast

Los linkers de contraste expresan oposición, concesión o diferencia entre dos ideas. En textos políticos son esenciales para presentar argumentos equilibrados y análisis críticos.

### 1.1 Although / Even though

**Although** y **even though** introducen una clause (sujeto + verbo) que contrasta con la idea principal. *Even though* es más enfático.

| Ejemplo | Traducción |
| --- | --- |
| **Although** the president promised reforms, no legislation was passed. | Aunque el presidente prometió reformas, no se aprobó ninguna ley. |
| **Even though** the opposition boycotted the vote, the referendum went ahead. | Aunque la oposición boicoteó la votación, el referéndum siguió adelante. |
| **Although** both nations signed the accord, implementation remains uncertain. | Aunque ambas naciones firmaron el acuerdo, la implementación sigue siendo incierta. |

### 1.2 Despite / In spite of

**Despite** e **in spite of** van seguidos de un **noun** o un **gerund** (no de una clause completa).

| Ejemplo | Traducción |
| --- | --- |
| **Despite** the economic sanctions, the regime continued its nuclear programme. | A pesar de las sanciones económicas, el régimen continuó su programa nuclear. |
| **In spite of** diplomatic efforts, the peace talks broke down. | A pesar de los esfuerzos diplomáticos, las conversaciones de paz se rompieron. |
| **Despite** mounting pressure, the government refused to hold elections. | A pesar de la creciente presión, el gobierno se negó a convocar elecciones. |

> **Error común:** ~~Despite the government resigned~~ → **Despite the government's resignation** / **Although the government resigned**

### 1.3 Whereas / While

**Whereas** y **while** comparan dos situaciones o destacan una diferencia. *Whereas* es más formal y habitual en writing académico.

| Ejemplo | Traducción |
| --- | --- |
| **Whereas** the US favour free trade, the EU often promotes protectionist policies. | Mientras que EE.UU. favorece el libre comercio, la UE a menudo promueve políticas proteccionistas. |
| **While** some delegates supported the resolution, others demanded amendments. | Mientras algunos delegados apoyaron la resolución, otros exigieron enmiendas. |

### 1.4 However / Nevertheless

**However** y **nevertheless** conectan oraciones completas y expresan contraste. Van precedidos de punto y coma o punto, y seguidos de coma.

| Ejemplo | Traducción |
| --- | --- |
| The treaty was widely praised; **however**, its enforcement mechanisms are weak. | El tratado fue ampliamente elogiado; sin embargo, sus mecanismos de cumplimiento son débiles. |
| The sanctions have not achieved their goal. **Nevertheless**, they remain in place. | Las sanciones no han logrado su objetivo. No obstante, siguen en vigor. |

### 1.5 Errores frecuentes: Linkers de contraste

| Error | Correcto | Explicación |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ~~Despite it was raining~~ | **Although it was raining** | *Despite* + noun/gerund; *although* + clause |
| ~~Although of the crisis~~ | **Despite the crisis** | *Although* requires a clause, not a noun |
| ~~Whereas he is poor, he is happy~~ | **Although he is poor, he is happy** | *Whereas* compara dos hechos; para concesión usa *although* |
| ~~However the results~~ | **However, the results** | *However* lleva coma después |
| ~~In spite of the treaty was signed~~ | **In spite of the treaty being signed** | *In spite of* + gerund, no clause |

## 2. Linkers of Reason, Purpose and Result

Estos conectores establecen relaciones causales, de propósito o de consecuencia. Son indispensables en análisis políticos y editoriales.

### 2.1 Because / Since / As

Introducen la **causa** o razón de algo. *Because* es el más directo; *since* y *as* son más formales.

| Ejemplo | Traducción |
| --- | --- |
| **Because** the economy stagnated, the government introduced austerity measures. | Debido a que la economía se estancó, el gobierno introdujo medidas de austeridad. |
| **Since** both parties refused to compromise, negotiations collapsed. | Dado que ambas partes se negaron a comprometerse, las negociaciones fracasaron. |
| **As** public trust eroded, calls for early elections intensified. | Como la confianza pública se deterioró, las llamadas a elecciones anticipadas se intensificaron. |

### 2.2 So / So that

**So** introduce una consecuencia. **So that** introduce un propósito (acompañado de *can/could/will/would*).

| Ejemplo | Traducción |
| --- | --- |
| The president raised taxes, **so** public unrest grew. | El presidente subió impuestos, por lo que la inquietud pública creció. |
| The delegation negotiated **so that** a ceasefire could be established. | La delegación negoció para que pudiera establecerse un alto el fuego. |
| Funding was increased **so that** humanitarian aid could reach civilians. | Se aumentó el financiamiento para que la ayuda humanitaria pudiera llegar a los civiles. |

### 2.3 In order to

Expresa propósito con infinitivo. Más formal que *so that*.

| Ejemplo | Traducción |
| --- | --- |
| **In order to** stabilise the region, troops were deployed along the border. | Con el fin de estabilizar la región, se desplegaron tropas a lo largo de la frontera. |
| **In order to** prevent further escalation, the UN sent envoys. | Con el fin de prevenir una mayor escalada, la ONU envió enviados. |

### 2.4 Therefore / Consequently / As a result

Conectan una oración completa con su consecuencia lógica. Formalidad creciente.

| Ejemplo | Traducción |
| --- | --- |
| The resolution was vetoed. **Therefore**, alternative measures were considered. | La resolución fue vetada. Por lo tanto, se consideraron medidas alternativas. |
| Trade volumes dropped sharply. **Consequently**, unemployment rose. | Los volúmenes comerciales cayeron drásticamente. En consecuencia, el desempleo aumentó. |
| The ceasefire collapsed. **As a result**, civilian casualties increased. | El alto el fuego colapsó. Como resultado, las bajas civiles aumentaron. |

### 2.5 Errores frecuentes: Linkers de causa, propósito y resultado

| Error | Correcto | Explicación |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ~~Because of the economy collapsed~~ | **Because the economy collapsed** | *Because* + clause; *because of* + noun |
| ~~So that to improve relations~~ | **In order to improve relations** | *So that* + clause con modal; no + infinitivo directo |
| ~~Therefore, the government acted quickly~~ | **The government acted quickly. Therefore, ...** | *Therefore* conecta ideas; no inicia una oración que es causa |
| ~~As a result of the talks failed~~ | **As a result, the talks failed** | *As a result* es linker entre cláusulas; *as a result of* + noun |
| ~~Due to the minister resigned~~ | **Due to the minister's resignation** | *Due to* + noun; para clause usa *because* |

## 3. Linkers of Addition and Listing

Añaden información, refuerzan argumentos o enumeran puntos. Clave en ensayos formales y discursos políticos.

### 3.1 Moreover / Furthermore / What's more

Añaden información o argumentos adicionales. *Moreover* y *furthermore* son formales; *what's more* es ligeramente menos formal.

| Ejemplo | Traducción |
| --- | --- |
| The country faces inflation. **Moreover**, its debt has reached record levels. | El país enfrenta inflación. Además, su deuda ha alcanzado niveles récord. |
| The peace deal was fragile. **Furthermore**, neither side trusted the other. | El acuerdo de paz era frágil. Además, ninguna parte confiaba en la otra. |
| The opposition won the popular vote. **What's more**, it secured a majority in parliament. | La oposición ganó el voto popular. Además, obtuvo mayoría en el parlamento. |

### 3.2 In addition / Besides

**In addition** añade un punto nuevo de forma formal. **Besides** añade un argumento que refuerza la idea anterior.

| Ejemplo | Traducción |
| --- | --- |
| **In addition** to trade tariffs, the US imposed travel restrictions. | Además de los aranceles comerciales, EE.UU. impuso restricciones de viaje. |
| **Besides** the economic costs, the conflict caused a massive refugee crisis. | Además de los costes económicos, el conflicto causó una crisis migratoria masiva. |

### 3.3 Not only... but also / Both... and

**Not only... but also** enfatiza una idea inesperada. **Both... and** iguala dos elementos.

| Ejemplo | Traducción |
| --- | --- |
| **Not only** did the ambassador resign, **but also** the foreign minister stepped down. | No solo renunció el embajador, sino que también dimitió el ministro de Asuntos Exteriores. |
| **Both** France **and** Germany supported the proposed sanctions. | Tanto Francia como Alemania apoyaron las sanciones propuestas. |

### 3.4 Errores frecuentes: Linkers de adición

| Error | Correcto | Explicación |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ~~Moreover of the evidence~~ | **Moreover, the evidence** | *Moreover* es adverbio, va con coma, no + *of* |
| ~~Not only the president but also~~ | **Not only did the president... but also** | *Not only* antepuesto invierte el orden (aux + sujeto) |
| ~~Besides to the treaty~~ | **Besides the treaty** | *Besides* + noun; no + *to* |
| ~~In addition for~~ | **In addition to** | La preposición correcta es *to* |
| ~~Both the economy and is weak~~ | **Both the economy and the currency are weak** | *Both... and* enlaza elementos paralelos de misma categoría |

## 4. Collocations: Politics and International Relations

Las **collocations** son combinaciones de palabras que suenan naturales en inglés. En política y relaciones internacionales, usarlas correctamente marca la diferencia entre un inglés básico y uno de nivel B2+.

| Collocation | Español | Ejemplo en contexto |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **hold elections** | convocar elecciones | The government plans to **hold elections** in March. |
| **impose sanctions** | imponer sanciones | The UN voted to **impose sanctions** on the regime. |
| **reach an agreement** | alcanzar un acuerdo | After weeks of talks, both sides **reached an agreement**. |
| **sign a treaty** | firmar un tratado | Twelve nations **signed a treaty** on nuclear disarmament. |
| **conduct negotiations** | llevar a cabo negociaciones | The envoys **conducted negotiations** behind closed doors. |
| **maintain stability** | mantener la estabilidad | Peacekeepers were deployed to **maintain stability** in the region. |
| **break down talks** | romper/fracasar las conversaciones | The negotiations **broke down** over territorial disputes. |
| **bilateral relations** | relaciones bilaterales | Both countries agreed to improve their **bilateral relations**. |
| **foreign policy** | política exterior | The new administration reshaped the nation's **foreign policy**. |
| **trade agreement** | acuerdo comercial | The EU signed a **trade agreement** with Japan. |
| **peace talks** | conversaciones de paz | **Peace talks** resumed after a brief ceasefire. |
| **referendum** | referéndum | The country held a **referendum** on EU membership. |
| **diplomatic ties** | lazos diplomáticos | The crisis led to the severing of **diplomatic ties**. |
| **economic sanctions** | sanciones económicas | **Economic sanctions** were tightened against the regime. |
| **political alliance** | alianza política | The two parties formed a **political alliance** ahead of the vote. |
| **cross-border** | transfronterizo | **Cross-border** cooperation is essential for tackling terrorism. |
| **nuclear disarmament** | desarme nuclear | The treaty aims at achieving **nuclear disarmament** worldwide. |
| **human rights** | derechos humanos | The report highlighted severe **human rights** violations. |
| **regime change** | cambio de régimen | Critics accused the government of pursuing **regime change** abroad. |
| **power sharing** | reparto de poder | The peace deal included provisions for **power sharing**. |

> **Tip:** En inglés formal se dice **conduct negotiations**, no ~~make negotiations~~. Del mismo modo, se dice **hold elections**, no ~~make elections~~.

## 5. Reading: The Paris Climate Agreement

Lee el siguiente texto sobre el Acuerdo de París y responde las preguntas de comprensión.

![The Paris Climate Agreement](/blog/curso-b2/unit-54/paris-agreement.png)

The Paris Climate Agreement, signed in 2015, represents one of the most significant diplomatic achievements of the 21st century. Although nearly 200 nations reached an agreement to limit global warming, critics argued that the targets were not binding enough.

Despite the optimism surrounding the deal, several major economies initially refused to sign. Even though scientific evidence overwhelmingly supported the need for action, political interests delayed implementation. Moreover, developing nations expressed concern that economic sanctions could be imposed if they failed to meet their commitments.

In order to address these concerns, the agreement included provisions for financial assistance. Furthermore, a system of bilateral relations between industrialised and developing countries was established. As a result, the Green Climate Fund was created to help nations transition to renewable energy.

Nevertheless, progress has been slow. While some countries have maintained stability in their emissions targets, others have seen sharp increases. Consequently, the goal of limiting warming to 1.5 degrees Celsius remains under threat.

## 6. Listening y Dialogue

### Dialogue: The Trade Negotiations

**Ambassador Lee:** Good morning, Minister. I understand both sides have made significant progress on the trade agreement.

**Minister García:** Good morning. Yes, although several key issues remain unresolved. In particular, agricultural tariffs and cross-border regulations.

**Ambassador Lee:** Indeed. The US is prepared to reduce tariffs, but only if the EU opens its markets further. Moreover, they want assurances on intellectual property.

**Minister García:** We understand. However, our farmers cannot compete if subsidies are removed overnight. We need a transitional period.

**Ambassador Lee:** That's reasonable. In order to reach an agreement before the summit, perhaps we could propose a phased approach.

**Minister García:** A phased approach might work. Furthermore, both parties could maintain stability by agreeing to review the terms every two years.

**Ambassador Lee:** Excellent. Let's draft a proposal and conduct further negotiations next week.

**Minister García:** Agreed. The consequences of breaking down talks would be severe for both economies.

3. What is Minister García's concern? → **That farmers cannot compete if subsidies are removed overnight**

## 7. Writing — Formal Essays and Reports

En writing de nivel B2, los linkers son esenciales para conectar ideas de forma coherente. Ensayos y reportes sobre temas políticos requieren registro formal.

### Tips para writing formal

- **Abre** con un linker de causa o concesión: *Although the issue is complex, several solutions have been proposed.*
- **Desarrolla** párrafos con *moreover*, *furthermore* o *in addition* para añadir argumentos.
- **Cierra** con *therefore*, *consequently* o *as a result* para sintetizar conclusiones.
- Usa collocations precisas: *conduct negotiations*, *impose sanctions*, *reach an agreement*.

### Modelo de párrafo formal

> Although the peace talks were initially promising, several obstacles emerged. Both sides refused to compromise on key territorial issues. Moreover, external actors complicated the process by funding opposing factions. Consequently, the negotiations broke down after three months. Nevertheless, diplomats remain committed to finding a solution, and further talks are expected to be conducted before the end of the year.

## 8. Speaking

En speaking de nivel B2, los linkers te permiten organizar tus ideas de forma fluida y convencer a tu interlocutor.

### Tips para speaking sobre política

- **Opinión + linker + justificación:** *In my view, although sanctions are necessary, they should be proportionate.*
- **Contraargumento:** *However, one could argue that diplomatic ties are more effective in the long run.*
- **Enumerar:** *Furthermore, we should consider economic, social, and environmental factors.*
- **Concluir:** *Therefore, a multilateral approach is essential.*

### Frases útiles

- "What strikes me is that **although** the treaty was signed, implementation has been minimal."
- "**Moreover**, the economic sanctions have not achieved their stated objectives."
- "**In spite of** these setbacks, I believe **furthermore** negotiations could yield results."
- "**Consequently**, the international community must adopt a more coordinated strategy."

## 9. Errores Cruzados y Estrategia de Examen

En exámenes B2 (First, IELTS, etc.), los errores más comunes con linkers y collocations son:

| Error típico | Correcto | Tipo de error |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ~~Despite it was late, the minister arrived~~ | **Although it was late, the minister arrived** | Despite + clause |
| ~~In order that she could~~ | **In order to** | In order to + infinitivo |
| ~~He made an agreement~~ | **He reached an agreement** | Collocation incorrecta |
| ~~Moreover, the economy is weak~~ (sin coma tras punto) | **The economy is weak; moreover, it is shrinking** | Puntuación con moreover |
| ~~The both countries signed~~ | **Both countries signed** | Article innecesario con *both* |
| ~~So much sanctions~~ | **So many sanctions** | *So many* + countable plural |

## 10. Del Reconocimiento a la Producción B2

### Nivel B2: reconocimiento pasivo

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- |
| Although | *Although the deal was signed, tensions remain.* |
| Despite + noun | *Despite the crisis, growth continued.* |
| Because + clause | *Because the vote failed, elections were called.* |

### Nivel B2+: producción activa

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- |
| Nevertheless | *Nevertheless, the government persisted with reforms.* |
| In order to + infinitivo | *In order to stabilise the region, troops were deployed.* |
| Not only... but also | *Not only was the treaty rejected, but also the minister resigned.* |

> **Clave:** En reconocimiento identificas el linker correcto; en producción lo usas espontáneamente en contextos formales. Practica convirtiendo oraciones simples en oraciones complejas con linkers.

## 11. Resumen Rápido de Teoría

| Categoría | Linkers / Collocations | Uso principal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Contraste** | although, even though, despite, in spite of, whereas, while, however, nevertheless | Expresar oposición o concesión |
| **Causa** | because, since, as | Expresar razón |
| **Propósito** | so that, in order to | Expresar objetivo |
| **Resultado** | so, therefore, consequently, as a result | Expresar consecuencia |
| **Adición** | moreover, furthermore, in addition, besides, not only... but also, both... and, what's more | Añadir información |
| **Collocations** | hold elections, impose sanctions, reach an agreement, sign a treaty, conduct negotiations, bilateral relations, etc. | Registro formal de política |

## Preguntas frecuentes

**¿Cuál es la diferencia entre although, despite y in spite of?**

Although va seguido de clause (sujeto + verbo). Despite e in spite of van seguidos de noun/gerund: Despite the crisis, the economy grew. Although the crisis hit, the economy grew.

**¿Cuándo uso so that vs in order to?**

Ambos expresan propósito. So that va seguido de clause (con modal): She studied hard so that she could pass. In order to va seguido de infinitivo: She studied hard in order to pass.

**¿Qué collocations son más frecuentes en política?**

Hold elections, impose sanctions, reach an agreement, sign a treaty, conduct negotiations, maintain stability, break down talks, bilateral relations.

**¿Puedo usar moreover y furthermore indistintamente?**

Sí, son sinónimos. Moreover añade información nueva; furthermore añade un argumento adicional más formal. Ambos son adecuados para writing académico y formal.

**¿Dónde practico la Unidad 54?**

En la Unidad 54 del curso B2 y en esta misma guía, que incluye teoría y ejercicios con soluciones.
