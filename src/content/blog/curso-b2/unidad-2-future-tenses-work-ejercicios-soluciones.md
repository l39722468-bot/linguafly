## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** will / going to / future perfect / present continuous for plans

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. By next month I ___ have completed the project. → *will / am / would*
2. Look at those clouds — it ___ going to rain. → *is / will / was*
3. I ___ let you know as soon as I hear. → *will / am going to / would*
4. We ___ having a meeting tomorrow at nine. → *are / will / have*
5. She ___ going to apply for the promotion. → *is / will / has*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **will** · 2. **is** · 3. **will** · 4. **are** · 5. **is**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. By 2030 they ___ have worked here twenty years. → *will / are / would*
2. I think prices ___ rise next year. (prediction) → *will / are going to / were*
3. The train ___ at platform three — evidence! → *is going to leave / will leave / leaves*
4. ___ the time we arrive, he will have left. → *By / Until / When*
5. I promise I ___ send the file tonight. → *will / am going to / would*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **will** · 2. **will** · 3. **is going to leave** · 4. **By** · 5. **will**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *By Friday I will complete the report.* (future perfect)
2. *It will rain — look at the sky.*
3. *We will have a call tomorrow.* (fixed plan)
4. *I am going to help you — spontaneous promise*
5. *By next year I will work here ten years.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. By Friday I **will have completed** the report.
2. It **is going to** rain — look at the sky.
3. We **are having** a call tomorrow.
4. I **will** help you.
5. By next year I **will have worked** here ten years.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** work

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. deadline → *fecha límite · ascenso · nómina*
2. quarterly report → *informe trimestral · vuelo · maleta*
3. negotiate → *negociar · despegar · hacer la maleta*
4. target → *objetivo · pasaporte · embarque*
5. approve → *aprobar · cancelar · posponer*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **fecha límite** · 2. **informe trimestral** · 3. **negociar** · 4. **objetivo** · 5. **aprobar**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

1. by next month = ___ → *antes de / durante / después de*
2. exceed ≈ ___ → *superar / perder / retrasar*
3. board meeting ≈ ___ → *reunión de dirección / fiesta / vacaciones*
4. commute ≈ ___ → *desplazamiento al trabajo / equipaje / billete*
5. as soon as ≈ ___ → *tan pronto como / aunque / hasta que*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **antes de** · 2. **superar** · 3. **reunión de dirección** · 4. **desplazamiento al trabajo** · 5. **tan pronto como**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: James at work

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b2/unit-2/reading-workbook.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: James at work"></audio>

> James works in finance. By the end of this quarter he will have delivered three major projects. He is going to negotiate a raise next month because his team exceeded every target. Dark clouds are gathering — it is going to storm during the commute. He will call his manager as soon as the report is approved. He is having a video call with clients tomorrow at ten.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. James works in ___ → *finance / education / travel*
2. By end of quarter he will have ___ → *delivered three projects / flown / camped*
3. Going to negotiate a ___ → *raise / ticket / trail*
4. Evidence for rain? → *dark clouds / sunny sky / snow*
5. Will call when report is ___ → *approved / cancelled / lost*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **finance** · 2. **delivered three projects** · 3. **raise** · 4. **dark clouds** · 5. **approved**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Video call when? → *tomorrow at ten / last year / never*
7. Future perfect example? → *will have delivered / will deliver / delivered*
8. Going to for evidence? → *yes / no*
9. Already negotiated raise? → *False / True*
10. Find will / going to / future perfect.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **tomorrow at ten** · 7. **will have delivered** · 8. **it is going to storm** · 9. **False** · 10. **See text**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Write By + time + will have…
12. Work vocab in text? → *yes / none*
13. Main idea? → *work futures / travel / feelings*
14. Fixed plan structure? → *present continuous / past perfect / wish*
15. Course link → */curso-b2/unit-2*

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe *By + time + will have + pp* sobre tu trabajo o estudios.
2. Completa: By next month I ___ (complete) the project.
3. Completa: Look — it ___ (go) to rain.
4. Completa: I ___ (let) you know as soon as I hear.
5. Escribe un plan fijo con present continuous.
6. Completa: She ___ (go) to apply for the promotion.
7. Promesa espontánea con *will*.
8. Predicción con evidencia → *going to*.
9. Corrige: *By Friday I will finish the report.* (future perfect)
10. Corrige: *We will have a meeting tomorrow.* (plan fijo)
11. Párrafo (4 frases) sobre deadlines en el trabajo.
12. Traduce: Para junio habré terminado el curso.
13. Traduce: Voy a llamar al cliente — ya tengo el número.
14. Diálogo corto: will vs going to.
15. Autochequeo: 1× each future form.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Model: By December I will have finished my course.
2. **will have completed**
3. **is going to** rain
4. **will** let
5. Model: We are meeting the team tomorrow at nine.
6. **is going to** apply
7. Model: Don't worry — I will help you.
8. Model: It is going to storm — take an umbrella.
9. By Friday I **will have finished** the report.
10. We **are having** a meeting tomorrow.
11. Open work paragraph.
12. By June I **will have finished** the course.
13. I **am going to** call the client — I already have the number.
14. Open dialogue.
15. Self-check.

</details>
