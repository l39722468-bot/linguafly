## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** if + past perfect → would + base (now); if + past simple → would have + pp

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. If we had left earlier, we ___ stuck now. → *aren't / wouldn't be / won't be*
2. If I had checked the forecast, I ___ a raincoat. → *pack / would pack / would have packed*
3. If she ___ more organised, she would have caught the flight. → *is / were / had been*
4. We ___ at the beach now if the ferry hadn't been cancelled. → *are / would be / will be*
5. If he had booked online, he ___ waiting in the queue. → *isn't / wouldn't be / won't be*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **wouldn't be** · 2. **would have packed** · 3. **were** · 4. **would be** · 5. **wouldn't be**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. If I ___ taller, I would have joined the team. → *am / were / had been*
2. If they had taken a taxi, they ___ here by now. → *are / would be / will be*
3. If she ___ the alarm, she wouldn't be late now. → *sets / set / had set*
4. Mixed: past → present uses ___ in the if-clause. → *past perfect / past simple / will*
5. If he were braver, he ___ asked for help yesterday. → *will / would / would have*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **were** · 2. **would be** · 3. **had set** · 4. **past perfect** · 5. **would have**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *If we left earlier, we wouldn't be stuck.* (mixed)
2. *If I checked the forecast, I would pack a coat.*
3. *If she was organised, she would have caught it.*
4. *If he had booked, he isn't waiting.*
5. *If I am taller, I would have joined.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. If we **had left** earlier, we wouldn't be stuck.
2. If I **had checked** the forecast, I **would have packed** a coat.
3. If she **were** organised, she **would have caught** it.
4. If he **had booked**, he **wouldn't be** waiting.
5. If I **were** taller, I **would have joined**.

</details>

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

> Travel plans rarely go perfectly. If Clara had left the airport lounge earlier, she would be boarding the plane now. If she had double-checked her passport, she would not be waiting at the embassy today. If her partner were better at packing, they would have avoided the excess baggage fee. They would be hiking in the mountains now if the tour had not been cancelled due to snow.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. If Clara had left lounge earlier, she would be ___ → *boarding / hiking / cooking*
2. If she had checked passport, she wouldn't wait at ___ → *embassy / gym / library*
3. If partner were better at packing, they'd avoid ___ fee → *excess baggage / tuition / scholarship*
4. Would be hiking if tour hadn't been cancelled due to ___ → *snow / sun / wind*
5. Mixed: past perfect → ___ → *present result / past only / zero*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **boarding** · 2. **embassy** · 3. **excess baggage** · 4. **snow** · 5. **present result**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. If she were better at packing = ___ conditional mix → *present → past / past → present*
7. Travel problems? → *yes / none*
8. At the beach now? → *False / True*
9. Underline mixed conditionals.
10. Write If I had…, I would… now.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **present → past** · 7. **passport, queue, cancellation** · 8. **False** · 9. **See text** · 10. **Model OK**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 2 mixed: pasado → presente y presente → pasado.
2. Completa: If we had left earlier, we ___ (not be) stuck now.
3. Completa: If I had checked the forecast, I ___ (pack) a coat.
4. Completa: If she ___ (be) organised, she would have caught the flight.
5. Completa: We ___ (be) at the beach now if the tour hadn't been cancelled.
6. Explica pasado → presente en 1 frase.
7. Corrige: *If we leave early, we wouldn't be stuck.*
8. Corrige: *If I checked the forecast, I would pack a coat.*
9. Párrafo sobre un viaje que salió mal.
10. Traduce: Si hubiéramos salido antes, no estaríamos atrapados.
11. Traduce: Si fuera más alto, habría entrado en el equipo.
12. Usa vocabulario travel: boarding, delay, luggage.
13. Diálogo en el aeropuerto.
14. Autochequeo: tiempos en if y result clauses.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open — one past→present and one present→past mixed.
2. **wouldn't be**
3. **would have packed**
4. **were**
5. **would be**
6. Past unreal condition → present unreal result.
7. If we **had left** earlier, we wouldn't be stuck.
8. If I **had checked** the forecast, I **would have packed** a coat.
9. Open travel paragraph.
10. If we **had left** earlier, we **wouldn't be** stuck.
11. If I **were** taller, I **would have joined** the team.
12. Open with travel vocabulary.
13. Open airport dialogue.
14. Self-check.

</details>
