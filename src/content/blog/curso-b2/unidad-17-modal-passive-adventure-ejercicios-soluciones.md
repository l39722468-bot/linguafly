## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** must/should be + V3; should have been + V3; have/get + object + V3

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. Gear ___ before the climb. (obligation) → *must be tested / must test / must testing*
2. The report ___ earlier. (past criticism) → *should have been published / should publish / should be publish*
3. I ___ my parachute ___ next week. → *am having / serviced · have / service · am having / service*
4. She ___ her wetsuit ___ yesterday. → *had / repaired · has / repair · had / repair*
5. Harnesses ___ before each climb. → *must be inspected / must inspect / must inspecting*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **must be tested** · 2. **should have been published** · 3. **am having / serviced** · 4. **had / repaired** · 5. **must be inspected**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. Modal passive = modal + ___ + V3. → *be / have / to*
2. Should have been + V3 = criticism about the ___. → *past / future / weather*
3. Have something done = someone does it ___ you. → *for / against / without*
4. He ___ his ropes checked every six months. → *has / have / having*
5. They ___ their kayak repaired at the moment. → *are having / have / had*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **be** · 2. **past** · 3. **for** · 4. **has** · 5. **are having**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *Gear must tested before the climb.*
2. *The report should have published earlier.*
3. *I am having my parachute service.*
4. *She had repaired her wetsuit.* (causative)
5. *Harnesses must inspect before climbs.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Gear **must be tested** before the climb.
2. The report **should have been published** earlier.
3. I am having my parachute **serviced**.
4. She **had** her wetsuit **repaired**.
5. Harnesses **must be inspected** before climbs.

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe must be, should be, should have been y have something done (1–2 cada uno).
2. Completa: Gear ___ (must / test) before the climb.
3. Completa: The report ___ (should / publish) earlier.
4. Completa: I ___ my parachute ___ next week. (have / service)
5. Completa: She ___ her wetsuit ___ yesterday. (have / repair)
6. Explica modal passive vs have something done.
7. Corrige: *Gear must tested.*
8. Corrige: *I am having my parachute service.*
9. Párrafo adventure con ambas estructuras.
10. Traduce: Hay que inspeccionar los arneses.
11. Traduce: Me están revisando el paracaídas.
12. Diálogo en el campamento base.
13. Matching: should have been → uso.
14. Autochequeo: object + V3 en causative.
15. Enlace teoría U17.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open — examples of each.
2. **must be tested**
3. **should have been published**
4. **am having / serviced**
5. **had / repaired**
6. Modal passive = obligación impersonal; have sth done = encargas a otro.
7. Gear **must be tested**.
8. I am having my parachute **serviced**.
9. Open paragraph.
10. Harnesses **must be inspected**.
11. I **am having** my parachute **serviced**.
12. Open dialogue.
13. should have been = crítica del pasado.
14. Self-check: object before V3.
15. [Guía U17](/blog/curso-b2/unidad-17-modal-passive-adventure)

</details>
