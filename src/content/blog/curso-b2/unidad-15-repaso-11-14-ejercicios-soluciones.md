## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** relative clauses, reduced relatives, obligation modals, past deduction

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. The festival, ___ is annual, starts soon. → *which / that / who*
2. People ___ the premiere waited outside. → *attending / attended / attend*
3. You ___ save receipts for the audit. → *must / might have / can't*
4. She ___ missed the client call. (deduction) → *must have / must / have to*
5. The compost ___ in the garden is ready. → *produced / producing / produce*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **which** · 2. **attending** · 3. **must** · 4. **must have** · 5. **produced**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. Non-defining → use ___ , not that. → *which/who / that only*
2. Reduced -ing ≈ ___ meaning. → *active / passive*
3. Needn't ≈ ___. → *don't have to / must have*
4. Can't have + pp = ___. → *almost impossible past / obligation*
5. Whose marks ___. → *possession / place / time*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **which/who** · 2. **active** · 3. **don't have to** · 4. **almost impossible past** · 5. **possession**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *The district, that we visited, is nice.*
2. *People who are attending → reduce*
3. *You must to wear a badge.*
4. *She must missed the deadline.*
5. *Volunteers helped at the show greeted us.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The district **that** we visited is nice. (defining, no commas)
2. People **attending**…
3. You must **wear** a badge.
4. She must **have missed** the deadline.
5. Volunteers **helping** at the show greeted us.

</details>

4. deduction → *deducción · seedling · barrio*
5. module review → *repaso de módulo · runway · badge*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

1. must have + pp ≈ ___ → *deducción fuerte pasado / permiso*
2. needn't ≈ ___ → *no es obligatorio / casi seguro*

4. -ed reduction ≈ ___ → *pasiva / activa / futuro*
5. defining clause ≈ ___ → *info esencial / info extra*

<summary>Ver solución</summary>

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Una frase con cada: defining, non-defining, -ing reduction, -ed reduction, must, needn't, must have, might have.
2. Completa: The festival, ___ is annual, starts soon.
3. Completa: People ___ (attend) waited outside.
4. Completa: You ___ save receipts. (obligation)
5. Completa: She ___ (miss) the call. (must have)
6. Mini-historia (8 frases) mezclando U11–14.
7. Corrige: *She must missed the deadline.*
8. Corrige: *People who attending smiled.*
9. Matching: estructura → unidad (11–14).
10. Traduce: Los voluntarios que ayudan saludaron al público.
11. Traduce: No puede haber cambiado la fecha todavía.
12. Autochequeo con mapa U15.
13. Shadow reading + listening otra vez.
14. Siguiente: Unidad 16 en el curso.
15. Enlace /curso-b2/unit-15.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open — one of each structure.
2. **which**
3. **attending**
4. **must**
5. **must have missed**
6. Open mixed paragraph.
7. She must **have missed** the deadline.
8. People **attending** smiled.
9. relatives→11; reduction→12; obligation→13; deduction→14.
10. Volunteers **helping** greeted the audience.
11. They **can't have changed** the date yet.
12. Self-check vs review map.
