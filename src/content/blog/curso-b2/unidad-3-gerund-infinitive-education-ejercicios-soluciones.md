## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** enjoy / mind / avoid + gerund; decide / agree + infinitive; stop + -ing / to

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I enjoy ___ lectures on Fridays. → *attend / attending / to attend*
2. She decided ___ for the scholarship. → *apply / applying / to apply*
3. Would you mind ___ the door? → *open / opening / to open*
4. He avoids ___ classes when possible. → *skip / skipping / to skip*
5. They agreed ___ the research project. → *join / joining / to join*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **attending** · 2. **to apply** · 3. **opening** · 4. **skipping** · 5. **to join**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. She stopped ___ TV and started revising. → *watch / watching / to watch*
2. He stopped ___ a break after three hours. → *take / taking / to take*
3. I aim ___ my degree by 2027. → *finish / finishing / to finish*
4. Do you mind ___ me your notes? → *lend / lending / to lend*
5. He regrets not ___ harder. → *study / studying / to study*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **watching** · 2. **to take** · 3. **to finish** · 4. **lending** · 5. **studying**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *I enjoy to read academic papers.*
2. *She decided applying for the grant.*
3. *Would you mind to wait?*
4. *He stopped to smoke.* (= dejó de fumar)
5. *They agreed joining the team.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I enjoy **reading** academic papers.
2. She decided **to apply** for the grant.
3. Would you mind **waiting**?
4. He stopped **smoking**.
5. They agreed **to join** the team.

</details>

4. major in → *especializarse en · cancelar · posponer*
5. procrastinate → *procrastinar · despegar · reservar*

## Lección 3 — Reading: Sofia at university

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

> University life requires balance. Sofia enjoys attending seminars but avoids missing deadlines. She decided to major in biology and agreed to mentor first-year students. She stopped checking her phone during lectures and started taking detailed notes. Her tutor advised her to read more academic journals. Would you mind sharing your notes after class?

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Sofia enjoys ___ → *attending / to attend / attend*
2. Avoids missing ___ → *deadlines / flights / relatives*
3. Decided to ___ → *major / majoring / majored*
4. Stopped checking phone and started ___ → *taking / take / to take*
5. Tutor advised her to ___ → *read / reading / reads*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **attending seminars** · 2. **deadlines** · 3. **major in biology** · 4. **taking notes** · 5. **read journals**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Mind + ___ structure? → *gerund / infinitive / past*
7. Agreed to ___ → *mentor / mentoring / mentored*
8. Stop + -ing vs stop + to? → *both in text*
9. Already graduated? → *False / True*
10. Underline enjoy / avoid / decide.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **gerund** · 7. **mentor students** · 8. **stopped checking (dejar) / started taking** · 9. **False** · 10. **See text**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Write enjoy + gerund sentence.
12. Education vocab? → *yes / none*
13. Main topic? → *study habits / travel / family*
14. Would you mind + ? → *gerund / infinitive / past*
15. Course link → */curso-b2/unit-3*

<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **Model OK** · 12. **seminars, deadlines, major** · 13. **study habits** · 14. **gerund** · 15. **/curso-b2/unit-3**

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 3 frases: enjoy, decide, avoid + forma correcta.
2. Completa: I enjoy ___ (read) academic articles.
3. Completa: She decided ___ (apply) for the grant.
4. Completa: Would you mind ___ (wait) a moment?
5. Completa: He stopped ___ (procrastinate) and started revising.
6. Stop + to vs stop + -ing — explica con un ejemplo.
7. Completa: They agreed ___ (help) with the project.
8. Corrige: *I enjoy to attend lectures.*
9. Corrige: *She stopped to smoke.* (= dejó de fumar)
10. Párrafo sobre hábitos de estudio.
11. Traduce: Evito perder clases.
12. Traduce: ¿Te importaría abrir la ventana?
13. Escribe *regret + gerund*.
14. Escribe *aim to + inf*.
15. Autochequeo: gerund vs infinitive.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Model using enjoy/decide/avoid correctly.
2. **reading**
3. **to apply**
4. **waiting**
5. stopped **procrastinating**
6. Stop **smoking** = dejar; stop **to rest** = parar con propósito.
7. **to help**
8. I enjoy **attending** lectures.
9. He stopped **smoking**.
10. Open study habits paragraph.
11. I avoid **missing** classes.
12. Would you mind **opening** the window?
13. I regret not **studying** harder.
14. I aim **to finish** my degree by 2027.
15. Self-check.

</details>
