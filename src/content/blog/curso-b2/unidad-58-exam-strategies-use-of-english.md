## Unidad 58 — Estrategias de Examen: Use of English (Teoría y Ejercicios)

La **Unidad 58** prepara para el **Use of English** del examen **Cambridge B2 First**. Cada parte del Use of English exige una estrategia diferente: reconocer collocations en el cloze, recuperar palabras gramaticales en el open cloze, transformar prefijos y sufijos en el word formation, y reconstruir cadenas gramaticales en las key word transformations.

## Teoría — Estrategias para cada parte del Use of English

El Use of English consta de **4 partes** que suman **40 preguntas** en un total de **45 minutos**. Cada parte tiene una pauta distinta y requiere técnicas específicas.

---

### Part 1: Multiple-choice cloze (8 preguntas)

Lees un texto con **8 huecos** y debes elegir la palabra correcta entre **4 opciones** para cada uno. Se evalúa principalmente tu conocimiento de **collocations** (combinaciones frecuentes de palabras) y **fixed phrases** (expresiones fijas).

**Estrategia paso a paso:**

1. **Lee el texto completo primero** sin mirar las opciones. Intenta comprender el significado general.
2. **Identifica el tipo de combinación** que necesitas: ¿verbo + sustantivo? ¿adjetivo + sustantivo? ¿expresión fija?
3. **Lee solo el hueco y su contexto inmediato.** No leas toda la oración otra vez; enfócate en las 2-3 palabras antes y después del hueco.
4. **Prueba cada opción** insertándola en la oración completa. Solo una sonará natural.
5. **Cambia de sección** si te estancas más de 2 minutos en un hueco.

**Ejemplo de collocation:**

> The government has decided to ___ new measures to reduce pollution.
>
> A) make　B) do　C) take　D) put

La respuesta correcta es **C) take** — la collocation es *take measures*.

---

### Part 2: Open cloze (12 preguntas)

Lees un texto con **12 huecos** y debes escribir **exactamente una palabra** en cada uno. No hay opciones: debes recuperar la palabra gramatical correcta del contexto.

**Estrategia paso a paso:**

1. **Lee el párrafo completo** antes de empezar a responder.
2. **Identifica qué tipo de palabra falta** en cada hueco:

| Tipo | Ejemplos | Señales típicas |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| Artículo | *a, an, the, ∅* | Antes de un sustantivo |
| Pronombre | *it, they, which, who, that* | Antes de un verbo o después de una preposición |
| Preposición | *in, on, at, by, with, for* | Antes de un sustantivo o pronombre |
| Auxiliar | *is, was, has, have, had, do, does, did* | Antes de un verbo principal |
| Conjuncción | *and, but, because, although, when* | Entre oraciones o cláusulas |

3. **Lee la oración completa** con tu palabra para confirmar que tiene sentido.
4. **No escribas más de una palabra.** Los artículos no son palabra compuesta; *a* y *an* cuentan como una palabra.

**Ejemplo:**

> She couldn't go to the party ___ she had to work late.

La respuesta es **because** — conjunción que introduce la razón.

---

### Part 3: Word formation (10 preguntas)

Lees un texto con **10 huecos** y una **palabra raíz** entre corchetes que debes transformar para completar el hueco. Puedes añadir prefijos, sufijos o ambas cosas.

**Estrategia paso a paso:**

1. **Lee la oración completa** e identifica qué **parte de la oración** necesitas (sustantivo, adjetivo, verbo, adverbio).
2. **Observa el contexto inmediato del hueco**: ¿hay un artículo antes? → sustantivo. ¿Modifica a un sustantivo? → adjetivo. ¿Modifica a un verbo o adjetivo? → adverbio.
3. **Aplica la transformación adecuada** usando prefijos y sufijos comunes.
4. **Lee la oración completa** con tu palabra para verificar significado y gramática.
5. **Verifica si necesitas plural** en sustantivos o **grado comparativo/superlativo** en adjetivos.

**Ejemplo:**

> The ___ of the new policy was felt immediately. (APPEAR)

La respuesta es **appearance** — necesitas un sustantivo (artículo *the* delante) y se forma con el sufijo *-ance*.

---

### Part 4: Key word transformations (10 preguntas)

Tienes una oración original, una **palabra clave** y una oración incompleta. Debes reescribir la oración usando la palabra clave sin cambiar su forma, para que signifique lo mismo que la original.

**Estrategia paso a paso:**

1. **Lee la oración original** hasta entender completamente su significado.
2. **Identifica la estructura gramatical** clave: pasiva, condicional, reported speech, estructura con *so/such*, comparativos, etc.
3. **Lee la palabra clave** y piensa en qué estructura gramatical exige.
4. **Construye la oración completa** antes de comparar con la incompleta.
5. **Verifica:**

   - ¿La palabra clave aparece exactamente como está (sin cambios)?
   - ¿Las palabras adicionales son correctas?
   - ¿La puntuación y el significado coinciden?

**Ejemplo:**

> Original: "People believe that the suspect left the country last week."
> Key word: **BELIEVED**
> Transformation: The suspect ___ last week.

Respuesta: **is believed to have left the country**

### Collocations frecuentes en el examen B2

Estas combinaciones aparecen constantemente en el Use of English:

| Verbo + sustantivo | Adjetivo + sustantivo | Verbo + preposición |
|---------------------|------------------------|---------------------|
| *take* measures | *significant* impact | *depend on* |
| *make* progress | *considerable* amount | *consist of* |
| *reach* agreement | *major* concern | *belong to* |
| *draw* conclusion | *serious* problem | *result in* |
| *pay* attention | *wide* range | *lead to* |
| *raise* awareness | *strong* evidence | *concentrate on* |
| *carry out* research | *potential* risk | *succeed in* |
| *commit* crime | *fundamental* change | *refer to* |

### Lectura de ejemplo: The Future of Remote Work

> The world of work has undergone a **dramatic** transformation in recent years. Before the pandemic, remote work was considered a **luxury** that only a few companies could afford to offer. However, the **sudden** shift to working from home **proved** that productivity could be maintained — and in many cases, **enhanced** — outside the traditional office environment.

> **Surveys** conducted across multiple industries have **consistently** shown that employees **prefer** flexible working arrangements. The **majority** of workers say they would consider **leaving** their job if remote options were removed entirely. This **represents** a significant challenge for employers who wish to **require** full-time office attendance.

> **Nevertheless**, the remote model is not without its **drawbacks**. Many managers have **expressed concerns** about team cohesion, **communication** gaps, and the **difficulty** of onboarding new staff. Companies are now **experimenting** with hybrid models that attempt to **balance** the benefits of flexibility with the need for **collaboration**.

### Tips para el Listening (conexión con Use of English)

Aunque el Listening es una sección separada, las habilidades del Use of English te ayudan:

- **Collocations en el Listening**: si reconoces combinaciones como *take into account* o *make an effort*, identificarás las respuestas más rápido.
- **Gramática del open cloze**: las palabras gramaticales (*which, although, despite*) aparecen también en los audios como conectores entre ideas.
- **Word formation auditiva**: al escuchar, tu cerebro reconoce patrones de sufijos que refuerzan la comprensión.

---

### Errores comunes en el examen

| # | Error | Cómo evitarlo |
|---|-------|---------------|
| 1 | No leer el texto completo antes de responder en cloze | Lee el texto entero primero; el contexto es clave. |
| 2 | Escribir más de una palabra en el open cloze | Verifica: "¿puedo escribir esta respuesta con una sola palabra?" |
| 3 | No cambiar la parte de la oración en word formation | Identifica primero qué parte de la oración necesitas. |
| 4 | Cambiar la keyword en key word transformations | La keyword debe aparecer exactamente igual en tu respuesta. |
| 5 | No usar el singular/plural correcto en word formation | Revisa el artículo y el contexto: *a problem* vs *problems*. |
| 6 | Ignorar el significado general en cloze | Si la palabra encaja gramaticalmente pero no semánticamente, es incorrecta. |
| 7 | No practicar con cronómetro | El tiempo es limitado: 45 minutos para 40 preguntas. |
| 8 | Olvidar el doble significado en key word transformations | Cada key word transformation puede valer hasta 2 puntos. |

## Ejercicios

### Lección 1 — Part 1: Multiple-choice cloze

#### A. Collocations: verbo + sustantivo

**1.** The company has decided to ___ a new strategy to increase sales.
A) make　B) take　C) do　D) set

**2.** Scientists have been ___ research into the effects of climate change.
A) making　B) doing　C) taking　D) carrying

**3.** It took us three hours to ___ an agreement with the other team.
A) make　B) reach　C) get　D) find

**4.** The government needs to ___ more effort into improving education.
A) give　B) take　C) put　D) make

**5.** She finally managed to ___ a decision after thinking about it all night.
A) do　B) make　C) take　D) have

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

1. **D) set** — *set a strategy* (collocation verbo + sustantivo)
2. **D) carrying** — *carry out research*
3. **B) reach** — *reach an agreement*
4. **C) put** — *put effort into*
5. **B) make** — *make a decision*

</details>

#### B. Collocations: adjetivo + sustantivo

**6.** There is ___ evidence to suggest that exercise improves mental health.
A) strong　B) heavy　C) thick　D) powerful

**7.** The report highlighted the ___ risk of flooding in coastal areas.
A) serious　B) heavy　C) severe　D) big

**8.** She showed a ___ interest in learning about different cultures.
A) high　B) big　C) wide　D) deep

**9.** The ___ of unemployment has increased dramatically this year.
A) rate　B) level　C) number　D) speed

**10.** We need to make a ___ effort to reduce our carbon emissions.
A) huge　B) large　C) strong　D) great

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

6. **A) strong** — *strong evidence*
7. **C) severe** — *severe risk*
8. **C) wide** — *wide interest* (también aceptable: *deep interest*, pero *wide* es la collocation más natural en este contexto)
9. **A) rate** — *rate of unemployment*
10. **D) great** — *great effort*

</details>

#### C. Fixed phrases

**11.** I'm ___ sorry, but I can't come to the meeting tomorrow.
A) much　B) very　C) so　D) truly

**12.** The project was completed ___ schedule, which impressed everyone.
A) in　B) at　C) on　D) by

**13.** She was ___ control of the situation from the very beginning.
A) at　B) in　C) on　D) under

**14.** We need to take this matter ___ consideration before making a final decision.
A) into　B) in　C) to　D) for

**15.** The new shopping centre is ___ the other side of the river.
A) in　B) at　C) on　D) by

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

11. **D) truly** — *truly sorry* (fixed phrase)
12. **C) on** — *on schedule*
13. **B) in** — *in control of*
14. **A) into** — *take into consideration*
15. **C) on** — *on the other side*

</details>

#### D. Mixed cloze

**16.** The teacher asked the students to hand ___ their essays by Friday.
A) in　B) out　C) on　D) up

**17.** We've run ___ milk. Could you go to the shop?
A) off　B) out of　C) down　D) away

**18.** The politician tried to avoid ___ the question directly.
A) answer　B) to answer　C) answering　D) answered

**19.** She has been working here ___ five years now.
A) since　B) for　C) during　D) in

**20.** It's no ___ complaining about the weather — we can't change it.
A) use　B) point　C) worth　D) purpose

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

16. **A) in** — *hand in* (entregar)
17. **B) out of** — *run out of* (quedarse sin)
18. **C) answering** — *avoid + -ing*
19. **B) for** — *for + periodo de tiempo*
20. **A) use** — *it's no use + -ing*

</details>

### Lección 2 — Part 2: Open cloze

#### A. Artículos y pronombres

**1.** The company has decided to launch ___ new product line next month.

**2.** She couldn't find ___ keys anywhere, so she had to call a locksmith.

**3.** ___ is important to learn from your mistakes if you want to improve.

**4.** The students were given ___ assignment to complete over the weekend.

**5.** My brother is a doctor. ___ works at the local hospital.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

1. **a** — *a new product line* (indefinido, primera mención)
2. **her** — *her keys* (pronombre posesivo)
3. **It** — *It is important to...* (pronombre sujeto impersonal)
4. **an** — *an assignment* (indefinido, vocal)
5. **He** — *He works...* (pronombre sujeto)

</details>

#### B. Prepositions

**6.** She has always been interested ___ learning new languages.

**7.** The concert was postponed ___ bad weather conditions.

**8.** We need to come ___ with a solution before Monday.

**9.** The book was written ___ a famous British author.

**10.** I've been looking forward ___ this holiday for months.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

6. **in** — *interested in*
7. **due to** — *postponed due to* (también aceptable: *because of*)
8. **up** — *come up with*
9. **by** — *written by* (agente pasivo)
10. **to** — *looking forward to*

</details>

#### C. Auxiliares y conjunciones

**11.** She ___ have told you about the meeting — it was a secret.

**12.** ___ the rain continued, we decided to stay indoors.

**13.** I don't think he ___ coming to the party tonight.

**14.** The project was delayed ___ some of the materials hadn't arrived.

**15.** She was tired, ___ she kept working until midnight.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

11. **shouldn't** — *shouldn't have told* (no debería haber dicho)
12. **As** — *As the rain continued* (conjunction causale)
13. **is** — *he is coming* (present continuous)
14. **because** — *because some materials hadn't arrived*
15. **but** — *tired, but she kept working* (contrast)

</details>

#### D. Mixed open cloze

**16.** The manager is thinking ___ expanding the business into new markets.

**17.** This is the most difficult test ___ I have ever taken.

**18.** Nobody was ___ to answer the question correctly.

**19.** The movie was ___ interesting that we watched it twice.

**20.** She has been working here ___ she graduated from university.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

16. **about** — *thinking about*
17. **that** — *the most difficult test that...*
18. **able** — *was able to answer*
19. **so** — *so interesting that...*
20. **since** — *since she graduated* (present perfect + since)

</details>

### Lección 3 — Part 3: Word formation

#### A. Formation de sustantivos (-tion, -ment, -ness, -ity)

**1.** The ___ of the project was completed ahead of schedule. (COMPLETE)

**2.** There has been a significant ___ in crime rates this year. (REDUCE)

**3.** Her ___ to detail is what makes her an excellent editor. (ATTEND)

**4.** The ___ of the new building will take two years. (CONSTRUCT)

**5.** He showed great ___ when dealing with the difficult situation. (CALM)

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

1. **completion** — *the completion* (sustantivo de *complete*, sufijo *-ion*)
2. **reduction** — *a reduction* (sustantivo de *reduce*, sufijo *-tion*)
3. **attention** — *attention to detail* (sustantivo de *attend*, sufijo *-tion*)
4. **construction** — *the construction* (sustantivo de *construct*, sufijo *-tion*)
5. **calmness** — *great calmness* (sustantivo de *calm*, sufijo *-ness*)

</details>

#### B. Formation de adjetivos (-ful, -less, -ive, -able)

**6.** The teacher gave us a ___ explanation of the rules. (USE)

**7.** He felt completely ___ when he lost his job. (HELP)

**8.** The new system is much more ___ than the old one. (EFFICIENCY)

**9.** The situation was so ___ that we had to act immediately. (URGE)

**10.** This book is absolutely ___. I couldn't put it down. (FASCINATE)

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

6. **useful** — *a useful explanation* (adjetivo de *use*, sufijo *-ful*)
7. **helpless** — *felt helpless* (adjetivo de *help*, sufijo *-less*)
8. **efficient** — *more efficient* (adjetivo de *efficiency*, sufijo *-ent*)
9. **urgent** — *an urgent situation* (adjetivo de *urge*, sufijo *-ent*)
10. **fascinating** — *absolutely fascinating* (adjetivo de *fascinate*, sufijo *-ing*)

</details>

#### C. Formation de verbos (un-, re-, dis-, en-, -ise, -ify)

**11.** The company decided to ___ the damaged equipment. (REPLACE)

**12.** She tried to ___ her friends from making a bad decision. (COURAGE)

**13.** We need to ___ our subscription before the end of the month. (NEW)

**14.** He chose to ___ the offer because the terms were unacceptable. (ACCEPT)

**15.** The teacher asked us to ___ the results of the experiment. (SIMPLE)

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

11. **replace** — *to replace the equipment* (verbo con prefijo *re-*: *re+place*)
12. **discourage** — *discourage her friends* (verbo con prefijo *dis-*: *dis+courage*)
13. **renew** — *to renew our subscription* (verbo con prefijo *re-*: *re+new*)
14. **reject** — *to reject the offer* (verbo con prefijo *re-*: *re+ject*)
15. **simplify** — *to simplify the results* (verbo con sufijo *-ify*: *simple → simplify*)

</details>

#### D. Mixed word formation

**16.** The ___ between the two countries has lasted for decades. (DIFFER)

**17.** She spoke with such ___ that everyone was impressed. (CONFIDENT)

**18.** The government has ___ increased spending on healthcare. (SIGNIFICANT)

**19.** We were all ___ by the beauty of the sunset. (AMAZE)

**20.** The company's ___ has improved significantly this quarter. (PERFORM)

<details><summary>Verção</summary>

16. **difference** — *the difference* (sustantivo de *differ*, sufijo *-ence*)
17. **confidence** — *with such confidence* (sustantivo de *confident*, sufijo *-ence*)
18. **significantly** — *has significantly increased* (adverbio de *significant*, sufijo *-ly*)
19. **amazed** — *we were amazed* (adjetivo pasivo de *amaze*, sufijo *-ed*)
20. **performance** — *the company's performance* (sustantivo de *perform*, sufijo *-ance*)

</details>

### Lección 4 — Part 4: Key word transformations

#### A. Passive transformations

**1.** "They will build a new hospital in the town centre."
**Key word: GOING**
→ A new hospital ___ in the town centre.

**2.** "Someone stole my bicycle from outside the shop."
**Key word: STOLEN**
→ My bicycle ___ from outside the shop.

**3.** "The police are questioning the suspect right now."
**Key word: BEING**
→ The suspect ___ by the police right now.

**4.** "People speak English in many countries around the world."
**Key word: SPOKEN**
→ English ___ in many countries around the world.

**5.** "The company will announce the results next week."
**Key word: ANNOUNCED**
→ The results ___ next week.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

1. **is going to be built** — *going* + passive (*be going to* + passive)
2. **has been stolen** — *has been stolen* (present perfect passive)
3. **is being questioned** — *is being questioned* (present continuous passive)
4. **is spoken** — *is spoken* (present simple passive)
5. **will be announced** — *will be announced* (future passive)

</details>

#### B. Conditional transformations

**6.** "If you don't study harder, you won't pass the exam."
**Key word: UNLESS**
→ ___ study harder, you won't pass the exam.

**7.** "I didn't wake up early, so I missed the bus."
**Key word: IF**
→ If I ___ earlier, I wouldn't have missed the bus.

**8.** "She didn't have her umbrella, so she got wet."
**Key word: HAD**
→ If she ___ her umbrella, she wouldn't have got wet.

**9.** "You should take an umbrella. It might rain later."
**Key word: IN**
→ Take an umbrella ___ case it rains later.

**10.** "Not studying was a mistake."
**Key word: SHOULD**
→ I ___ studied harder.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

6. **Unless you** — *Unless you study harder...* (*unless* = *if...not*)
7. **had woken** — *If I had woken earlier...* (mixed conditional: third conditional)
8. **had had** — *If she had had her umbrella...* (third conditional)
9. **in** — *in case it rains* (in case + present simple)
10. **should have** — *I should have studied harder* (modal perfect)

</details>

#### C. Reported speech transformations

**11.** "I have finished my homework," said Tom.
**Key word: FINISHED**
→ Tom said that he ___ his homework.

**12.** "Where did you buy that book?" she asked me.
**Key word: WHERE**
→ She asked me where ___ that book.

**13.** "Please don't make so much noise," the teacher said.
**Key word: ASKED**
→ The teacher ___ so much noise.

**14.** "I will help you with your project," she said.
**Key word: PROMISED**
→ She ___ me with my project.

**15.** "You should see a doctor," he said to her.
**Key word: ADVISED**
→ He ___ to see a doctor.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

11. **had finished** — *Tom said that he had finished...* (past perfect en reported speech)
12. **I had bought** — *where I had bought that book* (reported question)
13. **asked us not to make** — *The teacher asked us not to make so much noise* (imperativo negativo)
14. **promised to help** — *She promised to help me...* (promise + infinitivo)
15. **advised her** — *He advised her to see a doctor* (advise + someone + to + infinitivo)

</details>

#### D. Mixed transformations

**16.** "It's possible that she has already left."
**Key word: MAY**
→ She ___ already left.

**17.** "He started learning French two years ago."
**Key word: FOR**
→ He ___ French for two years.

**18.** "The movie was so good that everyone enjoyed it."
**Key word: SUCH**
→ It was ___ movie that everyone enjoyed it.

**19.** "I find it difficult to understand complex instructions."
**Key word: TROUBLE**
→ I have ___ understanding complex instructions.

**20.** "Nobody has ever been to that remote island."
**Key word: NEVER**
→ That remote island ___ .

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

16. **may have** — *She may have already left* (possibilidad en pasado)
17. **has been learning** — *has been learning French for two years* (present perfect continuous)
18. **such a good** — *It was such a good movie that...* (*such + a/an + adj + noun*)
19. **trouble** — *I have trouble understanding* (have trouble + -ing)
20. **has never been visited** — *That remote island has never been visited* (present perfect passive + never)

</details>

### Lección 5 — Writing y Speaking

#### Writing

**1.** Write an essay (140-190 words): "Some people think that remote work is the future of employment, while others believe that working in an office is more productive. Discuss both views and give your opinion."

**2.** Write a review (140-190 words) of a book or film you recently enjoyed. Include: what it was about, why you liked it, and who you would recommend it to.

**3.** Write an email (140-190 words) to a local council proposing improvements to public transport in your area.

**4.** Write a report (140-190 words) for your manager summarising the results of a customer satisfaction survey.

**5.** Write a story (140-190 words) that begins with: "The last time I saw my childhood friend, everything was different."

**6.** Rewrite this sentence using the word in brackets: "The government should spend more money on education." (**BELIEVE**) → ___

**7.** Rewrite this sentence using the word in brackets: "She has been working here since 2019." (**STARTED**) → She ___

**8.** Rewrite this sentence using the word in brackets: "It is not necessary for you to bring food." (**HAVE**) → You ___

**9.** Rewrite this sentence using the word in brackets: "They cancelled the match because of the rain." (**DUE**) → The match ___

**10.** Rewrite this sentence using the word in brackets: "I regret not studying harder for the exam." (**WISH**) → I ___

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**1-5:** Estos son ejercicios de escritura abierta. La respuesta debe seguir las convenciones del formato indicado (essay, review, email, report o story) con un mínimo de 140 palabras y un máximo de 190.

**6.** It is believed that the government should spend more money on education.

**7.** She started working here in 2019.

**8.** You don't have to bring food.

**9.** The match was cancelled due to the rain.

**10.** I wish I had studied harder for the exam.

</details>

#### Speaking

**1.** Describe a place you would like to visit in the future. You should say: where it is, how you would get there, what you would do there, and explain why you want to visit it.

**2.** Do you prefer working alone or in a team? Why? (Mini discussion: give reasons and examples)

**3.** You are arranging to meet a friend. Use these prompts: suggest a day, propose a place, offer an alternative, agree on a time.

**4.** Talk about a skill you would like to learn. Why is it useful? How would you learn it? How long do you think it would take?

**5.** Express your opinion: "Social media has had a negative effect on communication skills." Do you agree? Justify your answer.

<details><summary>Ver solução</summary>

**1-5:** Estos son ejercicios de speaking abiertos. Practica hablando en voz alta durante 1-2 minutos por respuesta. Usa vocabulario variado, conectores (*firstly, moreover, in conclusion*) y estructuras gramaticales complejas (condicionales, passive, reported speech).

Consejos clave:
- Usa conectores: *firstly, furthermore, in addition, however, on the other hand, in conclusion*
- Estructuras a incluir: condicionales, passive voice, comparativos, reported speech
- Fluidez > perfección: es mejor continuar hablando con un pequeño error que quedarse en silencio

</details>

### Preguntas frecuentes

**¿Qué partes tiene el Use of English?**
Cuatro partes: Part 1 (multiple-choice cloze), Part 2 (open cloze), Part 3 (word formation), Part 4 (key word transformations).

**¿Cuánto tiempo debo dedicar a cada parte?**
Parte 1: ~12 min. Parte 2: ~12 min. Parte 3: ~10 min. Parte 4: ~16 min. Practica con cronómetro.

**¿Cómo mejoro en word formation?**
Aprende los prefijos y sufijos comunes: un-, re-, dis-, -tion, -ment, -ness, -ful, -less, -ive, -able. Lee el contexto antes de transformar.

**¿Qué estrategia uso para key word transformations?**
Lee la oración original, identifica la estructura gramatical, lee la keyword y construye la cadena completa antes de comparar con la original.

**¿Dónde practico la Unidad 58?**
En la Unidad 58 del curso B2 y en esta misma guía, que incluye teoría y ejercicios con soluciones.
