## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** passive in all tenses: is/was/is being/has been/had been/will be + V3

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. Ancient monuments ___ every year. (present) → *are visited / visit / is visiting*
2. The castle ___ in the twelfth century. (past) → *was built / built / is built*
3. The site ___ right now. (present continuous) → *is being restored / is restored / restores*
4. The fresco ___ already. (present perfect) → *has been unveiled / unveiled / is unveiling*
5. The artefacts ___ when we arrived. (past perfect) → *had been moved / were moved / move*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **are visited** · 2. **was built** · 3. **is being restored** · 4. **has been unveiled** · 5. **had been moved**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. Future: The ruins ___ by 2030. → *will be excavated / excavate / are excavating*
2. Future perfect: It ___ by next month. → *will have been opened / will open / opened*
3. Past continuous: It ___ when the fire started. → *was being digitised / digitised / is digitised*
4. Passive = ___ + past participle. → *be / have / do*
5. Use *by* when the ___ matters. → *agent / adjective / adverb*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **will be excavated** · 2. **will have been opened** · 3. **was being digitised** · 4. **be** · 5. **agent**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *The castle built in 1200.* (add be)
2. *The site is restore right now.*
3. *The fresco have been unveiled.*
4. *Monuments is visited every year.*
5. *The artefacts had moved already.* (passive)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The castle **was built** in 1200.
2. The site **is being restored** right now.
3. The fresco **has been unveiled**.
4. Monuments **are visited** every year.
5. The artefacts **had been moved** already.

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 1 pasiva en presente, pasado, continuous, present perfect y future.
2. Completa: Monuments ___ (visit) every year.
3. Completa: The castle ___ (build) in 1200.
4. Completa: The site ___ (restore) right now.
5. Completa: The fresco ___ (unveil) already.
6. Explica qué cambia entre tiempos en la pasiva.
7. Corrige: *The castle built in 1200.*
8. Corrige: *The site is restore now.*
9. Párrafo heritage con 4 pasivas distintas.
10. Traduce: El manuscrito ha sido restaurado.
11. Traduce: Las ruinas serán excavadas.
12. Diálogo en un museo / archivo.
13. Matching: tiempo → forma de be.
14. Autochequeo: ¿solo cambia be?
15. Enlace teoría U16.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open — one of each tense.
2. **are visited**
3. **was built**
4. **is being restored**
5. **has been unveiled**
6. Solo cambia **be**; el past participle se mantiene.
7. The castle **was built** in 1200.
8. The site **is being restored** now.
9. Open heritage paragraph.
10. The manuscript **has been restored**.
11. The ruins **will be excavated**.
12. Open dialogue.
13. present→is/are; cont→being; perfect→has been; future→will be.
14. Yes — conjugate **be** only.
15. [Guía U16](/blog/curso-b2/unidad-16-passive-all-tenses-heritage)

</details>
