## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** gerund vs infinitive (enjoy/finish + -ing; want/need/decide + to)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I enjoy ___ at the weekend. → *hiking / to hike / hike*
2. She wants ___ a museum. → *to visit / visiting / visit*
3. We finished ___ the room. → *painting / to paint / paint*
4. They decided ___ camping. → *to go / going / go*
5. I avoid ___ all day. → *sitting / to sit / sit*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **hiking** · 2. **to visit** · 3. **painting** · 4. **to go** · 5. **sitting**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. I need ___ photography. → *to practise / practising / practise*
2. He suggested ___ to the cinema. → *going / to go / go*
3. We hope ___ the exam. → *to pass / passing / pass*
4. Keep ___! → *practising / to practise / practise*
5. She plans ___ a picnic. → *to have / having / have*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **to practise** · 2. **going** · 3. **to pass** · 4. **practising** · 5. **to have**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *I enjoy to hike.*
2. *She wants visiting a museum.*
3. *We finished to paint.*
4. *They decided going camping.*
5. *I need practising more.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I enjoy **hiking**.
2. She wants **to visit** a museum.
3. We finished **painting**.
4. They decided **to go** camping.
5. I need **to practise** more.

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 2× -ing (enjoy/finish) + 2× to (want/decide).
2. Completa: I enjoy ___ (hike).
3. Completa: She wants ___ (visit) a museum.
4. Completa: We finished ___ (paint).
5. Completa: They decided ___ (go) camping.
6. Corrige: *I enjoy to hike.*
7. Corrige: *She wants visiting a museum.*
8. Usa *hiking* y *museum* en 2 frases.
9. Escribe: I need to practise photography.
10. Párrafo (3–4 frases) sobre hobbies con -ing y to.
11. Traduce: Disfruto haciendo senderismo.
12. Traduce: Quiere visitar un museo.
13. Pregunta Do you enjoy…? + respuesta.
14. Escribe 1× avoid + -ing.
15. Autochequeo: enjoy/finish → -ing; want/need/decide → to.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open 2+2.
2. **hiking**
3. **to visit**
4. **painting**
5. **to go**
6. I enjoy **hiking**.
7. She wants **to visit** a museum.
8. I enjoy hiking. She wants to visit a museum.
9. OK.
10. Open hobbies paragraph.
11. I enjoy hiking.
12. She wants to visit a museum.
13. Do you enjoy cycling? — Yes, I enjoy cycling.
14. I avoid sitting all day.
15. Self-check.

</details>
