## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** practicar repaso PPC, Past Perfect, Past Simple vs Present Perfect.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

Elige la opción correcta. Solo una es válida.

1. Laura ___ been working as a teacher for five years. → *has / had / was*
2. She ___ never been to Asia before that trip. → *had / has / have*
3. Last year she ___ to travel to Japan. → *decided / has decided / decide*
4. She ___ just booked another trip. → *has / had / did*
5. Her flight ___ already left when she arrived. → *had / has / have*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **has** · 2. **had** · 3. **decided** · 4. **has** · 5. **had**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Foco U5 = ____. → a) repaso PPC, Past Perfect, Past Simple vs Present Perfect · b) only A1 · c) skip
7. Forma clave U5 es ____. → a) correcta según teoría · b) always Past Simple · c) only will
8. Reading U5 usa ____. → a) foco de la unidad · b) only numbers · c) unrelated
9. Listening U5 practica ____. → a) el mismo foco · b) only spelling · c) only articles
10. Cuaderno U5 tiene ____. → a) 5×15 ejercicios · b) 3 lecciones · c) sin soluciones

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a)** · 7. **a) correcta según teoría** · 8. **a) foco de la unidad** · 9. **a) el mismo foco** · 10. **a) 5×15 ejercicios**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige el error

Cada frase tiene **un** error. Reescribe la frase correcta.

11. *Laura have been working for five years.*
12. *She has never been to Asia before the trip.* (falta contraste pasado)
13. *Last year she has decided to travel.*
14. *Her flight has already left when she arrived.*
15. *She had just booked another trip.* (mezcla tiempos)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. Laura **has** been working for five years.
12. She **had** never been to Asia before that trip.
13. Last year she **decided** to travel.
14. Her flight **had already left** when she arrived.
15. She **has just booked** another trip.

</details>
