## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** present perfect, PP continuous, past perfect, futures (U2–7 review)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ___ lived here since 2015. → *have / has / am*
2. She ___ been studying all morning. → *has / have / is*
3. When I arrived, they ___ already left. → *had / have / were*
4. I ___ Paris last year. → *visited / have visited / had visited*
5. I'm ___ to visit my parents. → *going / go / will*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **have** · 2. **has** · 3. **had** · 4. **visited** · 5. **going**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. Present perfect: have/has + ___ → *V3 / -ing / infinitive*
2. PP continuous: have/has been + ___ → *-ing / V3 / to*
3. Past perfect: had + ___ → *V3 / past / -ing*
4. since + ___ / for + duration → *point in time / duration / both*
5. Going to ≈ ___ → *plan / regret / passive*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **V3** · 2. **-ing** · 3. **V3** · 4. **point in time / duration** · 5. **plan**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *I have live here since 2015.*
2. *She has been study all morning.*
3. *When I arrived, they have left.*
4. *I have visited Paris last year.*
5. *I'm going visit my parents.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **have lived** here since 2015.
2. She **has been studying** all morning.
3. When I arrived, they **had** already left.
4. I **visited** Paris last year.
5. I'm **going to visit** my parents.

</details>

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

5. duration → *duración · sequence · report*

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe PP, PP continuous, past perfect, going to.
2. Completa: I ___ lived here since 2015.
3. Completa: She ___ been studying.
4. Completa: When I arrived, they ___ left.
5. Completa: I ___ Paris last year.
6. Completa: I'm ___ to visit my parents.
7. Corrige: *I have visited Paris last year.*
8. Corrige: *She has been study all morning.*
9. Usa since y for en 2 frases.
10. Escribe: When I arrived, they had already left.
11. Mini-historia (4 frases) con tiempos distintos.
12. Traduce: Vivo aquí desde 2015.
13. Traduce: Voy a visitar a mis padres.
14. Explica since vs for.
15. Escribe 1× will (prediction).
16. Autochequeo: last year → past simple.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open four.
2. **have**
3. **has**
4. **had**
5. **visited**
6. **going**
7. I **visited** Paris last year.
8. has been **studying**
9. Open.
10. OK.
11. Open.
12. I have lived here since 2015.
13. I'm going to visit my parents.
14. since=point; for=duration.
15. OK.
16. Self-check.

</details>
