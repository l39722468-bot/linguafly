## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** articles (a/an, the, zero article)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I went to ___ old church. → *an / a / the*
2. She works in ___ bank. → *a / an / —*
3. We visited ___ cathedral in Seville. → *the / a / an*
4. I go to ___ by bus. (institution) → *school / the school / a school*
5. I need ___ hour to get there. → *an / a / the*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **an** · 2. **a** · 3. **the** · 4. **school** · 5. **an**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. There is ___ museum opposite the park. → *a / an / the*
2. They built ___ office block. → *an / a / the*
3. Life in ___ city can be stressful. → *the / a / —*
4. They are building ___ new stadium. → *a / an / the*
5. Is there ___ library near here? → *a / an / the*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a** · 2. **an** · 3. **the** · 4. **a** · 5. **a**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *I went to a old church.*
2. *We visited a cathedral in Seville.* (única conocida)
3. *I go to the school by bus.* (como alumno)
4. *I need a hour.*
5. *She works in an bank.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I went to **an** old church.
2. We visited **the** cathedral…
3. I go to **school** by bus.
4. I need **an** hour.
5. She works in **a** bank.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** places: buildings

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. library → *biblioteca · coffee · myself*
2. hospital → *hospital · salad · dilemma*
3. cathedral → *catedral · soup · prefer*
4. stadium → *estadio · bread · either*
5. museum → *museo · salt · myself*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **biblioteca** · 2. **hospital** · 3. **catedral** · 4. **estadio** · 5. **museo**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

1. a/an = ___ → *uno no específico / único / institución*
2. the = ___ → *específico/conocido / nunca / cero*
3. go to school = ___ → *sin artículo / the / an*
4. an before ___ → *vowel sound / consonant only / plural*
5. bank ≈ ___ → *banco / salad / myself*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **uno no específico** · 2. **específico/conocido** · 3. **sin artículo** · 4. **vowel sound** · 5. **banco**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 2× a/an + 2× the + 1× zero article.
2. Completa: ___ old church.
3. Completa: ___ bank in the centre.
4. Completa: ___ cathedral in Seville.
5. Completa: I go to ___. (alumno)
6. Corrige: *a old church*
7. Corrige: *I need a hour.*
8. Usa *library* y *stadium* en 2 frases con artículos.
9. Escribe: There is a museum opposite the park.
10. Mini-diálogo en la ciudad (a/the).
11. Traduce: Fuimos a la catedral.
12. Traduce: Trabaja en un banco.
13. Explica go to school vs go to the school.
14. Escribe 1× an + vowel sound.
15. Autochequeo: sonido vocálico → an; específico → the.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open 2+2+1.
2. **an**
3. **a**
4. **the**
5. **school**
6. **an** old church
7. **an** hour
8. Is there a library…? They're building a stadium.
9. OK.
10. Open dialogue.
11. We visited the cathedral.
12. She works in a bank.
13. school = institution; the school = the building.
14. an hour / an old church / an office…
15. Self-check.

</details>
