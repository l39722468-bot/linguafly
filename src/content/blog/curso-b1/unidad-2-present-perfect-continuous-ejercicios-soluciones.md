## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** have/has been + -ing

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. She ___ been studying for five years. → *has / have / had*
2. I ___ been attending classes since September. → *have / has / had*
3. ___ long have you been studying? → *How / What / When*
4. They have been working ___ all day. → *for / since / ago*
5. He has been teaching here ___ ten years. → *for / since / yesterday*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **has** · 2. **have** · 3. **How** · 4. **for** (all day = duration context) · 5. **for**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Foco U2 = ____. → a) have/has been + -ing · b) only A1 · c) skip
7. Forma clave U2 es ____. → a) correcta según teoría · b) always Past Simple · c) only will
8. Reading U2 usa ____. → a) foco de la unidad · b) only numbers · c) unrelated
9. Listening U2 practica ____. → a) el mismo foco · b) only spelling · c) only articles
10. Cuaderno U2 tiene ____. → a) 5×15 ejercicios · b) 3 lecciones · c) sin soluciones

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a)** · 7. **a) correcta según teoría** · 8. **a) foco de la unidad** · 9. **a) el mismo foco** · 10. **a) 5×15 ejercicios**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *She have been studying.*
12. *How long you been working?*
13. *I have been knowing her for years.*
14. *He has been study since Monday.*
15. *They has been waiting.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. She **has** been studying.
12. **How long have** you been working?
13. I **have known** her for years.
14. He has been **studying** since Monday.
15. They **have** been waiting.

</details>
