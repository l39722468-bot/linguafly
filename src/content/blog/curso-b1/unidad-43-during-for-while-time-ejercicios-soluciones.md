## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** during/for/while + time vocabulary

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I fell asleep ___ the film. → *during / while / for*
2. We lived there ___ three years. → *for / during / while*
3. I met her ___ I was travelling. → *while / during / for*
4. She phoned ___ the meeting. → *during / while / for*
5. The phone rang ___ we were eating. → *while / during / for*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **during** · 2. **for** · 3. **while** · 4. **during** · 5. **while**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. during + ___ → *noun / clause / duration*
2. for + ___ → *duration / noun event / clause*
3. while + ___ → *clause / noun only / duration*
4. during the film ≈ ___ → *in the film (time) / for the film / since the film*
5. for three years = ___ → *duration / starting point / range*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **noun** · 2. **duration** · 3. **clause** · 4. **in the film (time)** · 5. **duration**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *during I was travelling*
2. *for the film* (during screening)
3. *while the meeting* (noun only)
4. *I met her during I travelled*
5. *we lived there during three years*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **while** I was travelling / **during** the trip
2. **during** the film
3. **during** the meeting / **while** we were in the meeting
4. I met her **while** I was travelling
5. We lived there **for** three years

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** time

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. moment → *momento · depend · interested*
2. period → *período · afraid · proud*
3. duration → *duración · good · worried*
4. meanwhile → *mientras tanto · pay · married*
5. throughout → *a lo largo de · agree · apply*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **momento** · 2. **período** · 3. **duración** · 4. **mientras tanto** · 5. **a lo largo de**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

1. during → ___ → *noun event / clause / duration number*
2. for → ___ → *how long / when started / from…to*
3. while → ___ → *subject+verb / noun only / duration*
4. meanwhile ≈ ___ → *at the same time / for three years / since*
5. throughout the day ≈ ___ → *all day long / for Monday / since morning*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **noun event** · 2. **how long** · 3. **subject+verb** · 4. **at the same time** · 5. **all day long**

</details>
