## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** non-defining relative clauses (who/which + commas, no that)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. My sister, ___ lives in Madrid, is a teacher. → *who / which / that*
2. The river, ___ flows through the city, is polluted. → *which / who / that*
3. Recycling, ___ helps reduce pollution, is important. → *which / who / that*
4. Climate change, ___ affects us all, is global. → *which / who / that*
5. Can I use that here? → *No / Yes / Sometimes*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **who** · 2. **which** · 3. **which** · 4. **which** · 5. **No**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. Madrid, ___ is busy, is my home city. → *which / who / that*
2. My brother, ___ works in a bank, recycles. → *who / which / that*
3. Plastic, ___ is hard to recycle, pollutes. → *which / who / that*
4. Non-defining needs ___ → *commas / no commas / that*
5. Defining allows ___ → *that / only who / only which*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **which** · 2. **who** · 3. **which** · 4. **commas** · 5. **that**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *My sister that lives in Madrid, is…*
2. *The river that flows…, is polluted.* (extra info)
3. *Recycling, that helps…*
4. *Climate change who affects…*
5. *No commas needed?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. My sister, **who** lives… (commas, no that)
2. The river, **which** flows…,
3. Recycling, **which** helps…
4. Climate change, **which** affects…
5. Non-defining **needs commas**.

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 2× who + 2× which con comas (sin that).
2. Completa: My sister, ___ lives…
3. Completa: The river, ___ flows…
4. Completa: Recycling, ___ helps…
5. ¿Puedo usar that? ___
6. Corrige: *My sister that lives…,*
7. Corrige: *Recycling, that helps…*
8. Usa pollution y recycle en 2 frases non-defining.
9. Escribe: Climate change, which affects us all,…
10. Mini-texto (3 frases) con comas.
11. Traduce: Mi hermana, que vive en Madrid,…
12. Traduce: El río, que atraviesa la ciudad,…
13. Explica defining vs non-defining en 1 frase.
14. Escribe 1× which + commas.
15. Autochequeo: extra info → commas; no that.

2. **who**
3. **which**
4. **which**
5. **No**
6. **who** + commas
7. **which**
8. Open.
9. OK.
10. Open.
