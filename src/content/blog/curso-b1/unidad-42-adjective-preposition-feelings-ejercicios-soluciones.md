## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** adjective + preposition (interested in, afraid of, good at…)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I'm interested ___ learning English. → *in / on / at*
2. She's afraid ___ spiders. → *of / from / about*
3. He's good ___ maths. → *at / in / on*
4. I'm proud ___ my son. → *of / for / about*
5. I'm worried ___ the exam. → *about / of / for*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **in** · 2. **of** · 3. **at** · 4. **of** · 5. **about**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. tired ___ waiting → *of / from / about*
2. responsible ___ the project → *for / of / to*
3. different ___ each other → *from / than / of*
4. fed up ___ the rain → *with / of / about*
5. married ___ someone → *to / with / of*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **of** · 2. **for** · 3. **from** · 4. **with** · 5. **to**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *interested on English*
2. *afraid from spiders*
3. *good in maths*
4. *proud for my son*
5. *worried of the exam*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. interested **in** English
2. afraid **of** spiders
3. good **at** maths
4. proud **of** my son
5. worried **about** the exam

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 3× adj + preposition (interested in, afraid of, good at).
2. Completa: interested ___ English.
3. Completa: afraid ___ spiders.
4. Completa: good ___ maths.
5. Completa: proud ___ my son.
6. Corrige: *interested on English*
7. Corrige: *good in maths*
8. Usa worried about y fed up with en 2 frases.
9. Escribe: She's afraid of spiders.
10. Mini-diario de sentimientos (4 frases).
11. Traduce: Estoy interesado en aprender inglés.
12. Traduce: Estoy orgulloso de mi hijo.
13. Explica interested in vs good at en 1 frase.
14. Escribe 1× responsible for.
15. Autochequeo: bloque adj+prep completo.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open three.
2. **in**
3. **of**
4. **at**
5. **of**
6. interested **in**
7. good **at**
8. Open.
9. OK.
10. Open.
11. I'm interested in learning English.
12. I'm proud of my son.
13. in=topic; at=skill.
14. OK.
15. Self-check.
