## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** passive, modal passive, reported statements/questions/commands (U16–19)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. English ___ all over the world. → *is spoken / speaks / is speaking*
2. The report ___ yesterday. → *was published / published / is published*
3. Applications ___ be submitted online. → *must / must to / must be*
4. She said she ___ send the email. → *would / will / sends*
5. He asked me if I ___ finished. → *had / have / did*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is spoken** · 2. **was published** · 3. **must** · 4. **would** · 5. **had**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. Passive = be + ___ → *past participle / infinitive / -ing*
2. Modal passive = modal + ___ + V3 → *be / to be / being*
3. Reported statement: said (that) + ___ → *backshift / present / infinitive*
4. Reported question: asked if + ___ → *clause / to / infinitive*
5. Told + person + ___ → *to infinitive / that / -ing*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **past participle** · 2. **be** · 3. **backshift** · 4. **clause** · 5. **to infinitive**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *The report was publish yesterday.*
2. *Applications must to be submitted.*
3. *She said she will send.*
4. *He asked me did I finish.*
5. *The teacher told us be quiet.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The report **was published** yesterday.
2. Applications **must be submitted**.
3. She said she **would** send the email.
4. He asked me **if I had** finished.
5. The teacher told us **to be** quiet.

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 2× passive + 2× reported.
2. Completa: English ___ spoken here.
3. Completa: The report ___ published yesterday.
4. Completa: Applications ___ be submitted.
5. Completa: She said she ___ send the email.
6. Completa: He asked if I ___ finished.
7. Corrige: *must to be submitted*
8. Corrige: *She said she will send.*
9. Usa told to en 2 frases.
10. Escribe: The teacher told us to be quiet.
11. Mini-diálogo (4 frases) passive/reported.
12. Traduce: El informe se publicó ayer.
13. Traduce: Me preguntó si había terminado.
14. Explica backshift en 1 frase.
15. Escribe 1× modal passive.
16. Autochequeo: passive = be + V3.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open four.
2. **is**
3. **was**
4. **must**
5. **would**
6. **had**
7. must **be submitted**
8. she **would** send
9. Open.
10. OK.
11. Open.
12. The report was published yesterday.
13. He asked me if I had finished.
14. Past intro verb → backshift tenses.
15. OK.
16. Self-check.

</details>
