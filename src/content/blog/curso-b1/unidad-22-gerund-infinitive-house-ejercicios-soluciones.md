## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** like/love/prefer; remember/forget/try (+ -ing or to)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. Remember ___ the door. (reminder) → *to lock / locking / lock*
2. I remember ___ it. (memory) → *locking / to lock / lock*
3. I prefer ___ near a park. → *living / to live / live*
4. Try ___ the kitchen first. → *cleaning / to clean / clean*
5. I like ___ in the kitchen. → *cooking / to cook / cook*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **to lock** · 2. **locking** · 3. **living** · 4. **cleaning** · 5. **cooking**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. She forgot ___ off the dishwasher. → *turning / to turn / turn*
2. Don't forget ___ the door. → *to lock / locking / lock*
3. We love ___ evenings in the garden. → *spending / to spend / spend*
4. Try ___ the vacuum cleaner. → *restarting / to restart / restart*
5. I prefer ___ in a quiet flat. → *living / to live / live*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **turning** · 2. **to lock** · 3. **spending** · 4. **restarting** · 5. **living**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *Remember locking the door.* (reminder)
2. *I remember to lock it yesterday.* (memory)
3. *Try to cleaning first.*
4. *She forgot to turning it off.*
5. *I prefer live near a park.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Remember **to lock** the door.
2. I remember **locking** it.
3. Try **cleaning** / Try **to clean**…
4. She forgot **turning** / forgot **to turn**…
5. I prefer **living** / **to live**…

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe remember to + remember -ing + prefer + try -ing.
2. Completa: Remember ___ (lock) the door. (reminder)
3. Completa: I remember ___ (lock) it. (memory)
4. Completa: I prefer ___ (live) near a park.
5. Completa: Try ___ (clean) the kitchen first.
6. Corrige: *Remember locking the door.* (si es recordatorio)
7. Usa *fridge* y *dishwasher* en 2 frases.
8. Escribe: She forgot turning off the washing machine.
9. Escribe: I like cooking in the kitchen.
10. Mini-diálogo en casa (remember/forget).
11. Traduce: No olvides cerrar con llave.
12. Traduce: Prefiero vivir en un piso tranquilo.
13. Explica remember to vs remember -ing en 1 frase.
14. Escribe 1× love + -ing.
15. Autochequeo: reminder = to; memory/experiment = -ing.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open four structures.
2. **to lock**
3. **locking**
4. **living / to live**
5. **cleaning**
6. Remember **to lock**…
7. The fridge is new. She forgot turning off the dishwasher.
8. OK.
9. OK.
10. Open dialogue.
11. Remember to lock the door.
12. I prefer living / to live in a quiet flat.
13. to = don't forget; -ing = I recall doing it.
14. We love spending evenings in the garden.
15. Self-check.

</details>
