## Qué aprenderás

- Formar **If + present, will…**
- Usar **when / as soon as / until / before / after** + present
- Vocabulario: *sunny, rainy, storm, forecast, temperature…*

![First conditional](/blog/curso-b1/unit-11/first-conditional.png)

---

## 1. Mapa rápido

| Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **If + present, will** | futuro posible | If it **rains**, I **will** stay at home. |
| **when + present, will** | cuando ocurra | **When** I arrive, I **will** call you. |
| **as soon as** | en cuanto | **As soon as** the storm passes, I **will** call. |
| **until** | hasta que | We **will** stay indoors **until** the rain stops. |

---

## 2. First conditional

> If it **rains** tomorrow, I **will** stay at home.  
> If you **come** early, we **will** have time for coffee.  
> If it's **sunny** on Saturday, we **will** have a barbecue.

## 3. Future time clauses

> **When** I arrive, I **will** call you.  
> **As soon as** the storm passes, I **will** call you.  
> We **will** stay indoors **until** the rain stops.  
> **Before** we leave, I **will** check the forecast.  
> **After** the storm, we **will** go for a walk.

## 8. Ejercicios

### 1 — Completa
1. If it ___ tomorrow, I will stay at home. (*rains*)  
2. When I ___, I will call you. (*arrive*)  
3. As soon as the storm ___, I will call. (*passes*)  
4. We will stay indoors until the rain ___. (*stops*)  
5. If it's sunny, we ___ have a barbecue. (*will*)

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **rains** · 2. **arrive** · 3. **passes** · 4. **stops** · 5. **will**
</details>

### 2 — Corrige
1. *If it will rain, I stay home.* → If it **rains**, I **will** stay…  
2. *When I will arrive, I call you.* → When I **arrive**, I **will** call…  
3. *As soon as it will pass…* → As soon as it **passes**…

### 3 — Vocab
1. Day with no clouds = ___ · 2. Strong rain + thunder = ___ · 3. How hot/cold = ___ · 4. Pronóstico = ___

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **sunny** · 2. **storm** · 3. **temperature** · 4. **forecast**
</details>
