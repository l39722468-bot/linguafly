## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** phrasal verbs 2 (find out, give up, look into, fill in, hand in)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I need to find ___ the opening times. → *out / up / in*
2. Don't give ___. → *up / out / in*
3. We'll look ___ the refund. → *into / after / off*
4. Please fill ___ this form. → *in / out / up*
5. Hand ___ your receipt. → *in / out / up*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **out** · 2. **up** · 3. **into** · 4. **in** · 5. **in**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. She ___ out the price yesterday. → *found / gave / filled*
2. He ___ up smoking last year. → *gave / found / handed*
3. They ___ into my request. → *looked / filled / handed*
4. ___ in the form, please. → *Fill / Find / Give*
5. ___ in your receipt at the checkout. → *Hand / Find / Give*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **found** · 2. **gave** · 3. **looked** · 4. **Fill** · 5. **Hand**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *Find the opening times out the.*
2. *Don't give the up.*
3. *Fill the form.* (phrasal)
4. *Hand your receipt.* (phrasal)
5. *Look the refund into.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Find out** the opening times.
2. Don't **give up**.
3. **Fill in** the form.
4. **Hand in** your receipt.
5. **Look into** the refund.

</details>
