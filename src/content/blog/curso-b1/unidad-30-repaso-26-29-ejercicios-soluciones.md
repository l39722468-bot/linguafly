## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** quantifiers, both/either/neither, articles y reflexives (mix U26–29)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. There isn't ___ milk left. → *much / many / few*
2. ___ tea ___ coffee is fine. → *Either…or / Both…and / Neither…nor*
3. We visited ___ cathedral. → *the / a / an*
4. She taught ___ Spanish. → *herself / himself / itself*
5. ___ options look good. → *Both / Either / Neither*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **much** · 2. **Either…or** · 3. **the** · 4. **herself** · 5. **Both**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. Add ___ salt. → *a little / a few / many*
2. Neither Tom ___ Maria wants to go. → *nor / or / and*
3. I went to ___ old church. → *an / a / the*
4. I hurt ___ when I fell. → *myself / yourself / himself*
5. How ___ eggs are there? → *many / much / little*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a little** · 2. **nor** · 3. **an** · 4. **myself** · 5. **many**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *There isn't many milk.*
2. *Either tea and coffee.*
3. *We visited a cathedral.* (específica)
4. *She taught herself him Spanish.*
5. *Both option look good.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. There isn't **much** milk.
2. **Either** tea **or** coffee.
3. We visited **the** cathedral.
4. She taught **herself** Spanish.
5. **Both options** look good.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** food, choices, buildings, experiences (mix)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. much/many → *cuantificadores · only reflexives*
2. either…or → *elección · cathedral only*
3. the cathedral → *artículo the · myself*
4. herself → *reflexivo · soup only*
5. vegetables / library / choice → *vocab mix · wrong*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **cuantificadores** · 2. **elección** · 3. **artículo the** · 4. **reflexivo** · 5. **vocab mix**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

1. few + ___ → *countable / uncountable / reflexives*
2. neither…nor → *ni…ni / o…o / the*
3. an hour → *vowel sound / consonant / plural*
4. by myself → *alone/without help / many / the*
5. a lot of → *both countable & uncountable / only the*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **countable** · 2. **ni…ni** · 3. **vowel sound** · 4. **alone/without help** · 5. **both countable & uncountable**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Una frase de cada tipo: quantifier, either…or, the, reflexive, both.
2. Completa: There isn't ___ milk.
3. Completa: ___ tea ___ coffee.
4. Completa: We visited ___ cathedral.
5. Completa: She taught ___.
6. Completa: ___ options look good.
7. Completa: Add ___ salt.
8. Corrige: *There isn't many milk.*
9. Corrige: *Neither Tom or Maria…*
10. Corrige: *I hurt me.*
11. Mini-historia (5 frases) mezclando U26–U29.
12. Matching: cuantificador / either / artículo / reflexivo.
13. Traduce: No queda mucha leche.
14. Traduce: Se enseñó español.
15. Autochequeo con el mapa de la Unidad 30.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open — one of each.
2. **much**
3. **Either…or**
4. **the**
5. **herself**
6. **Both**
7. **a little**
8. There isn't **much** milk.
9. Neither Tom **nor** Maria…
10. I hurt **myself**.
11. Open mixed paragraph.
12. much=quantifier; either=choice; the=article; herself=reflexive.
13. There isn't much milk left.
14. She taught herself Spanish.
15. Self-check vs review map.

</details>
