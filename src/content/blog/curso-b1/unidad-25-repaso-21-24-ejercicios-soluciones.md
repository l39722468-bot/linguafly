## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** gerund/infinitive y phrasal verbs (mix U21–24)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I enjoy ___. → *cycling / to cycle / cycle*
2. She wants ___ a sofa. → *to buy / buying / buy*
3. Remember ___ off the lights. → *to turn / turning / turn*
4. Please fill ___ the form. → *in / out / up*
5. I found ___ the discount. → *out / up / in*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **cycling** · 2. **to buy** · 3. **to turn** · 4. **in** · 5. **out**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. Don't give ___. → *up / out / in*
2. Turn ___ the lights. → *off / after / into*
3. We finished ___. → *painting / to paint / paint*
4. They decided ___ camping. → *to go / going / go*
5. Hand ___ the receipt. → *in / out / up*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **up** · 2. **off** · 3. **painting** · 4. **to go** · 5. **in**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *I enjoy to cycle.*
2. *She wants buying a sofa.*
3. *Remember turning off the lights.* (reminder)
4. *Find the discount.* (phrasal)
5. *Fill the form.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I enjoy **cycling**.
2. She wants **to buy** a sofa.
3. Remember **to turn** off…
4. **Find out** the discount.
5. **Fill in** the form.

</details>

3. receipt → *recibo · camping · sofa*
4. look after → *cuidar · museum · refund*
5. find out → *averiguar · photography · garden*

<details>

<details>

</details>

5. Hand it in at the ___ → *desk / cinema*

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Una frase: enjoy -ing, want to, remember to, turn off, find out, fill in.
2. Completa: I enjoy ___.
3. Completa: She wants ___ a sofa.
4. Completa: Remember ___ off the lights.
5. Completa: Please fill ___ the form.
6. Completa: I found ___ the discount.
7. Completa: Don't give ___.
8. Corrige: *I enjoy to cycle.*
9. Corrige: *She wants buying a sofa.*
10. Corrige: *Fill the form.*
11. Mini-historia (5 frases) mezclando U21–U24.
12. Matching: -ing / to / phrasal daily / phrasal shopping.
13. Traduce: Disfruto montando en bici.
14. Traduce: Averigüé el descuento.
15. Autochequeo con el mapa de la Unidad 25.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open — one of each.
2. **cycling** (etc.)
3. **to buy**
4. **to turn**
5. **in**
6. **out**
7. **up**
8. I enjoy **cycling**.
9. She wants **to buy** a sofa.
10. **Fill in** the form.
11. Open mixed paragraph.
12. enjoy=-ing; want=to; turn off=daily; find out/fill in=shopping.
13. I enjoy cycling.
14. I found out the discount.
15. Self-check vs review map.

</details>
