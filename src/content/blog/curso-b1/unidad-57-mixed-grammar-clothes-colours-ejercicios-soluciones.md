## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** passive, would rather, relatives, comparatives, although

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. This dress ___ made of cotton. → *is / was / makes*
2. I'd ___ wear blue. → *rather / better / prefer*
3. The shirt ___ I bought is too small. → *which / who / where*
4. This jacket is ___ expensive than that one. → *more / most / much*
5. ___ it was expensive, I bought it. → *Although / Because / Unless*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is** · 2. **rather** · 3. **which** · 4. **more** · 5. **Although**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. is made of = ___ → *passive present / active / past simple*
2. would rather + ___ → *bare inf / to inf / -ing*
3. which for things = ___ → *relative / passive / modal*
4. more + adj = ___ → *comparative / superlative / passive*
5. Although = ___ → *contrast / reason / condition*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **passive present** · 2. **bare inf** · 3. **relative** · 4. **comparative** · 5. **contrast**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *This dress was made of cotton.* (present fact)
2. *I'd rather to wear blue.*
3. *The shirt who I bought…*
4. *This jacket is expensiver…*
5. *Because it was expensive, I bought it.* (contrast)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. This dress **is made of** cotton.
2. I'd **rather wear** blue.
3. The shirt **which** I bought…
4. This jacket is **more expensive**…
5. **Although** it was expensive, I bought it.

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe passive + would rather + relative + although.
2. Completa: This dress ___ made of cotton.
3. Completa: I'd ___ wear blue.
4. Completa: The shirt ___ I bought is too small.
5. Completa: This jacket is ___ expensive.
6. Completa: ___ it was expensive, I bought it.
7. Corrige: *I'd rather to wear blue.*
8. Corrige: *The shirt who I bought…*
9. Usa try on y fashion en 2 frases.
10. Mini-diálogo (4 frases) en una tienda.
11. Traduce: Preferiría llevar azul.
12. Traduce: Aunque era caro, lo compré.
13. Explica would rather vs had better.
14. Escribe 1× superlative.
15. Autochequeo: relative which/who/where.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open four.
2. **is**
3. **rather**
4. **which**
5. **more**
6. **Although**
7. I'd **rather wear** blue.
8. The shirt **which** I bought…
9. Open.
10. Open.
11. I'd rather wear blue than red.
12. Although it was expensive, I bought it.
13. Rather=preference; had better=strong advice.
14. OK.
15. Self-check.

</details>
