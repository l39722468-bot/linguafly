La **Unidad 1 del curso B1** (*Repaso A2 → B1*) es el puente: Present Perfect, comparativos, modales, *who/which*, *used to* y vocabulario de **feelings**, con el texto de Maria en Londres.

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 1 (B1)

- Reactivar **Present Perfect** (*I have lived…*, *She has never been…*).
- Repasar **comparativos**, **modales** (*must*, *have to*, *can*) y **going to**.
- Usar **relative clauses** básicas: *who* (personas) / *which* (cosas).
- Reconocer *used to* (hábitos pasados) y un primer *has been + -ing* / *had + pp*.
- Hablar de **sentimientos** e **información personal** (*confident*, *anxious*, *proud*…).
- Leer y entender un texto B1 corto (Maria en Londres).

## 1. De A2 a B1: qué cambia

| Nivel | Enfoque típico |
| :--- | :--- |
| **A2** | Formas claras, frases cortas, un tiempo por vez |
| **B1** | Combinar tiempos, explicar *why*, describir feelings, textos más densos |

No “olvidas” A2: lo **enciendes**. Si una estructura falla aquí, vuelve a su unidad A2 y luego sigue el curso B1.

## 2. Present Perfect (repaso rápido)

**have/has + past participle** — experiencia o situación con conexión al presente.

| Sujeto | Auxiliar | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I / you / we / they | **have** | I **have lived** here for three years. |
| he / she / it | **has** | She **has never been** to Japan. |

> I have lived here for three years.  
> She has never been to Japan.  
> We have visited many countries together.

## 3. Primer vistazo: Present Perfect Continuous

En el repaso aparece *has/have been + -ing* (duración hasta ahora). Lo profundizas en la **Unidad 2**.

> She **has been studying** since nine o’clock.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b1/unit-1/has-been-studying.mp3" title="🔊 She has been studying since nine o'clock."></audio>

Idea: *continuous* = acción en progreso / duración; *simple* = resultado o experiencia. Más en [Present Perfect Continuous](/blog/gramatica/present-perfect-continuous).

## 5. Modales: *must*, *have to*, *can*

| Modal | Uso en el repaso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **must** | obligación fuerte / norma | You **must** wear a seatbelt in the car. |
| **have to** | obligación (externa) | I **have to** work early tomorrow. |
| **can** | capacidad | She **can** speak Spanish very well. |

## 7. *used to* + infinitive

Hábitos o situaciones del **pasado** que ya no son verdad:

> I **used to** play tennis when I was young.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b1/unit-1/used-to-play.mp3" title="🔊 I used to play tennis when I was young."></audio>

En el reading de Maria: *I used to miss my family* / *I got used to living alone*. El trío *used to / be used to / get used to* se estudia a fondo en la **Unidad 9**.

## 8. Condicional 1 y Past Perfect (contacto)

| Estructura | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| First conditional | If it rains tomorrow, I **will** stay at home. |
| Past Perfect | He **had finished** his homework before I arrived. |

## 9. Vocabulario: personal info + feelings

| Palabra | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| confident | seguro/a de ti | I feel more **confident** now. |
| nervous / anxious | nervioso / preocupado | At first I felt **anxious**. |

| embarrassed | avergonzado | I felt **embarrassed**. |
| stressed | estresado | Opposite of *relaxed*. |
| amazing | increíble (positivo) | The trip was **amazing**. |

Profesiones / datos: *journalist*, *architect*, *mechanic*, *degree*, *passport*, *library*, *friendly*, *clever*, *professional*.

## 10. Reading: Maria in London

![Maria en Londres](/blog/curso-b1/unit-1/london-scene.png)

> My name is Maria and I am from Spain. I have been living in London for two years because I work as a journalist. At first I felt very nervous and anxious because I didn’t know anyone. But now I have many friends and I feel much more confident. I used to miss my family a lot, but I got used to living alone. My sister is an architect and she is very proud of her job. We both love travelling and we have visited many countries together.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b1/unit-1/reading-maria.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Maria in London"></audio>

Comprueba: ¿De dónde es? ¿Cuánto lleva en Londres? ¿Cómo se sentía al principio? ¿Y ahora? ¿Qué hace su hermana?

## 13. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Present Perfect

1. I \_\_\_\_ lived here for three years. (*have / has*)  
2. She \_\_\_\_ never been to Japan.  
3. We \_\_\_\_ visited many countries together.  
4. He \_\_\_ finished before I arrived. (Past Perfect)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **have** · 2. **has** · 3. **have** · 4. **had**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Modales y comparativos

1. You \_\_\_\_ wear a seatbelt. (*must / can*)  
2. I \_\_\_\_ work early tomorrow. (*have to / has to*)  
3. This hotel is \_\_\_\_ expensive \_\_\_\_ that one.  
4. Next weekend I \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_ visit my parents. (*going to*)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **must** · 2. **have to** · 3. **more** … **than** · 4. **am going to**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — *who* / *which* / *used to*

1. The man \_\_\_\_ helped me was kind.  
2. The book \_\_\_\_ I bought is interesting.  
3. I \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_ play tennis when I was young.  
4. If it rains, I \_\_\_\_ stay at home.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **who** · 2. **which** · 3. **used to** · 4. **will**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Feelings / jobs

1. Opposite of *confident* ≈ \_\_\_\_ / \_\_\_\_  
2. Satisfied with a result → you feel \_\_\_\_  
3. Designs buildings → \_\_\_\_  
4. Writes for a newspaper → \_\_\_\_  
5. Document to travel abroad → \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **nervous** / **anxious** · 2. **satisfied** · 3. **architect** · 4. **journalist** · 5. **passport**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Reading (Maria)

1. Maria is from \_\_\_\_.  
2. She has been living in London for \_\_\_\_ years.  
3. At first she felt \_\_\_\_ and \_\_\_\_.  
4. Now she feels more \_\_\_\_.  
5. Her sister is an \_\_\_\_.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Spain** · 2. **two** · 3. **nervous** and **anxious** · 4. **confident** · 5. **architect**
</details>

## 14. Errores frecuentes

| Evita | Mejor |
| :--- | :--- |
| *She have lived…* | She **has** lived… |
| *more expensive that* | more expensive **than** |
| *must to wear* | **must** wear / **have to** wear |
| *The man which helped me* | The man **who** helped me |
| *I use to play* | I **used to** play |
| *enough big to* | **big enough** to |

## 15. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Haz el repaso **en voz alta**: B1 pide fluidez, no solo reconocer formas.  
2. Escribe 6–8 frases sobre ti: dónde has vivido, cómo te sentiste al cambiar de ciudad, qué *used to* hacer.  
3. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 1](/blog/curso-b1/unidad-1-repaso-a2-b1-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 1 del curso B1](/curso-b1/unit-1).  
4. Siguiente: **Present Perfect Continuous** (Unidad 2) con *for/since* y contexto de educación.
