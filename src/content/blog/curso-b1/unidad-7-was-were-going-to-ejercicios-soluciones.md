## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** practicar was/were going to (planes no realizados).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

Elige la opción correcta. Solo una es válida.

1. I ___ going to call you yesterday but I forgot. → *was / were / am*
2. They ___ going to travel to Spain but cancelled. → *was / were / are*
3. She ___ going to study medicine but changed her mind. → *was / were / is*
4. We ___ going to watch a film but we were too tired. → *was / were / are*
5. He ___ going to buy a new car but lost his job. → *was / were / is*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **was** · 2. **were** · 3. **was** · 4. **were** · 5. **was**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

Completa cada frase con la forma correcta.

1. She ___ going to attend, but she came. (neg.) → *wasn't / weren't / isn't*
2. They ___ going to tell him. (neg.) → *wasn't / weren't / aren't*
3. What ___ you going to do before the rain started? → *was / were / are*
4. You ___ going to come to the party, right? → *was / were / are*
5. My parents ___ going to visit us but got sick. → *was / were / are*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **wasn't** · 2. **weren't** · 3. **were** · 4. **were** · 5. **were**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige el error

Cada frase tiene **un** error. Reescribe la frase correcta.

1. *I am going to call you yesterday.*
2. *They was going to travel.*
3. *She weren't going to attend.*
4. *What was you going to do?*
5. *We was going to have a party.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **was going to** call you yesterday.
2. They **were going to** travel.
3. She **wasn't going to** attend.
4. What **were** you going to do?
5. We **were going to** have a party.

</details>
