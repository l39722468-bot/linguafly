## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** phrasal verbs 1 (turn on/off, put on, take off, hurry up, calm down, look after)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. Please turn ___ the lights. → *off / on / after*
2. ___ on your coat. → *Put / Take / Look*
3. Take ___ your shoes. → *off / on / up*
4. ___ up or we'll be late. → *Hurry / Calm / Turn*
5. Can you look ___ my bag? → *after / off / up*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **off** · 2. **Put** · 3. **off** · 4. **Hurry** · 5. **after**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. Please ___ down. → *calm / hurry / turn*
2. Turn ___ the radio. → *on / after / in*
3. Put ___ your jacket. → *on / off / after*
4. Who looks ___ the dog? → *after / off / up*
5. Don't forget to turn ___ the TV. → *off / after / in*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **calm** · 2. **on** · 3. **on** · 4. **after** · 5. **off**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *Turn the lights off it.*
2. *Put on it.*
3. *Look the bag after.*
4. *Hurry the up.*
5. *Take off your shoes off.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Turn **off** the lights. / Turn **them off**.
2. Put **it on**.
3. **Look after** the bag.
4. **Hurry up**.
5. **Take off** your shoes.

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 5 frases: turn off, put on, take off, hurry up, look after.
2. Completa: Please turn ___ the lights.
3. Completa: ___ on your coat.
4. Completa: Take ___ your shoes.
5. Completa: ___ up or we'll be late.
6. Completa: Look ___ my bag.
7. Corrige: *Put on it.*
8. Corrige: *Look the bag after.*
9. Usa *calm down* en 1 frase.
10. Mini-diálogo de mañana (2 phrasals).
11. Traduce: Apaga las luces.
12. Traduce: Ponte el abrigo.
13. Traduce: ¿Puedes cuidar mi bolso?
14. Escribe Turn on… + Turn off…
15. Autochequeo: partículas on/off/up/after.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open five sentences.
2. **off**
3. **Put**
4. **off**
5. **Hurry**
6. **after**
7. Put **it on**.
8. **Look after** the bag.
9. Please calm down.
10. Open dialogue.
11. Turn off the lights.
12. Put on your coat.
13. Can you look after my bag?
14. Turn on the radio. Turn off the TV.
15. Self-check.

</details>
