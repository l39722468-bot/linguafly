## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 4 (B1)

- Elegir **Past Simple** con tiempo definido (*yesterday*, *last…*, *ago*).
- Elegir **Present Perfect** con *ever/never/just/already/yet* y *for/since*.
- Preguntar *Have you ever…?* vs *When did you…?*
- Usar **time expressions** con el tiempo correcto.
- Leer un texto de viajes que mezcla ambos tiempos.

## 1. La regla rápida

| Tiempo | Idea | Señales típicas |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Past Simple** | pasado **terminado** y concreto | yesterday, last week/night, ago, in 2019 |
| **Present Perfect** | experiencia / resultado **hasta ahora** | ever, never, just, already, yet, for, since |

> I **went** to Paris **yesterday**.  
> She **has never been** to Japan.  
> I **have just finished** my homework.

## 2. Past Simple: tiempo acabado

| Expresión | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| yesterday | I **went** to Paris yesterday. |
| last week / month / year / night | They **visited** London last week. · I **went** to the cinema last night. |
| ago | I met him two years ago. |
| in + año | I **met** him in 2019. |
| When…? | **When did** you see him last? |

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b1/unit-4/went-last-night.mp3" title="🔊 I went to the cinema last night."></audio>

Si la frase **nombra cuándo** (y ese cuándo ya terminó) → Past Simple.

## 3. Present Perfect: conexión presente

| Expresión | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ever** | ¿alguna vez? | Have you **ever** been to Italy? |
| **never** | nunca (experiencia) | She has **never** been to Japan. |
| **just** | acabo de | I have **just** finished / We have **just** arrived. |
| **already** | ya (afirmativa) | She has **already** booked the hotel. |
| **yet** | todavía (neg. / preg.) | They have **not** finished **yet**. / Have you finished **yet**? |
| **for** | duración | He has lived here **for** five years. |
| **since** | punto de inicio | …**since** 2019 / **since** Monday |

## 4. Vocabulario: Time expressions

![Time expressions](/blog/curso-b1/unit-4/time-expressions.png)

| Con Past Simple | Con Present Perfect |
| :--- | :--- |
| yesterday | ever / never |
| last week / month / year / night | just / already / yet |
| ago | for / since |
| in 2019 | this week / this morning* |

\* *this morning* puede ir con ambos según si la mañana “sigue abierta” o ya cerró el día.

---

## 5. Truco: *When* vs *ever*

| Pregunta | Tiempo | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Have you ever** been to Paris? | Present Perfect | experiencia de vida |
| **When did** you go to Paris? | Past Simple | pide fecha / momento |

En el curso también aparece un eco de Past Perfect: *He **had** already left when I called* (acción antes de otra pasada) — refuerzo de la [U3](/blog/curso-b1/unidad-3-past-perfect).

## 9. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige el tiempo

1. I \_\_\_\_ to Paris yesterday. (*went / have been*)  
2. She \_\_\_\_ never been to Japan. (*has / had*)  
3. They \_\_\_\_ London last week. (*visited / have visited*)  
4. I \_\_\_\_ just finished my homework. (*have / did*)  
5. When \_\_\_\_ you see him last? (*did / have*)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **went** · 2. **has** · 3. **visited** · 4. **have** · 5. **did**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — *just / already / yet / ever*

1. Have you \_\_\_\_ been to Italy?  
2. She has \_\_\_\_ booked the hotel.  
3. They have not finished \_\_\_\_.  
4. We have \_\_\_\_ arrived.  
5. Have you finished the report \_\_\_\_?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **ever** · 2. **already** · 3. **yet** · 4. **just** · 5. **yet**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Time expressions

1. Past Simple: yesterday / last week / \_\_\_\_ (*ago*)  
2. Present Perfect duración: \_\_\_\_ five years  
3. Present Perfect inicio: \_\_\_\_ 2019  
4. “Anoche” = \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_  
5. “El mes pasado” = \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **ago** · 2. **for** · 3. **since** · 4. **last night** · 5. **last month**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrección

1. *I have gone to Paris yesterday.* → \_\_\_\_  
2. *She never was to Japan.* → \_\_\_\_  
3. *Did you ever been to Italy?* → \_\_\_\_  
4. *They didn’t finish yet.* → \_\_\_\_ (más natural con PP)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **went** to Paris yesterday.  
2. She **has never been** to Japan.  
3. **Have** you ever **been** to Italy?  
4. They **haven’t finished** yet.
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Reading

1. Last year the person visited \_\_\_\_ countries.  
2. They have never been to \_\_\_\_ yet.  
3. Yesterday they finished a book about \_\_\_\_.  
4. They have already booked a flight to \_\_\_\_.  
5. They have just received the \_\_\_\_.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **five** · 2. **Asia** · 3. **Japan** · 4. **Tokyo** · 5. **photos**
</details>

## 10. Errores frecuentes

| Evita | Mejor |
| :--- | :--- |
| *I have seen him yesterday* | I **saw** him yesterday |
| *I went to Japan ever* | I have **never/ever** been… / Have you **ever**…? |
| *She already booked* (sin auxiliar, si quieres PP) | She **has already** booked |
| *Did you finish yet?* (posible, pero…) | **Have** you finished **yet**? |
| *for 2019* | **since** 2019 / **for** five years |

---

## 11. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Subraya la **señal de tiempo** antes de conjugar.  
2. *When* + fecha → Past Simple; *ever/never/just/already/yet* → Present Perfect.  
3. Practica en la [Unidad 4 del curso B1](/curso-b1/unit-4).  
4. La **Unidad 5** es el **repaso 1–4** (PPC, Past Perfect y este contraste).
