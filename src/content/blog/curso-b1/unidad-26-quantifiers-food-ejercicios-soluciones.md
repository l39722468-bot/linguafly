## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** quantifiers (much/many/a lot/few/little)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. There isn't ___ water left. → *much / many / few*
2. There are ___ apples in the fridge. → *many / much / little*
3. Add ___ salt to the soup. → *a little / a few / many*
4. Very ___ people came. → *few / little / much*
5. She drinks ___ of coffee. → *a lot / many / few*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **much** · 2. **many** · 3. **a little** · 4. **few** · 5. **a lot**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. How ___ milk do you need? → *much / many / few*
2. How ___ eggs are there? → *many / much / little*
3. We have ___ time left. → *little / few / many*
4. There are ___ restaurants here. → *a few / a little / much*
5. I don't eat ___ meat. → *much / many / few*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **much** · 2. **many** · 3. **little** · 4. **a few** · 5. **much**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *There isn't many water.*
2. *There are much apples.*
3. *Add a few salt.*
4. *Very little people came.*
5. *How many milk…?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. There isn't **much** water.
2. There are **many** apples.
3. Add **a little** salt.
4. Very **few** people came.
5. **How much** milk…?

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 2× much/many + 2× few/little + 1× a lot of.
2. Completa: There isn't ___ water.
3. Completa: There are ___ apples.
4. Completa: Add ___ salt.
5. Completa: Very ___ people came.
6. Corrige: *There isn't many water.*
7. Corrige: *Add a few salt.*
8. Usa *vegetables* y *soup* en 2 frases con cuantificadores.
9. Escribe: How much milk / How many eggs…
10. Párrafo (3–4 frases) sobre comida con cuantificadores.
11. Traduce: No queda mucha agua.
12. Traduce: Hay muchas manzanas.
13. Pregunta How much…? + respuesta.
14. Escribe 1× a few + countable.
15. Autochequeo: countable → many/few; uncountable → much/little.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open 2+2+1.
2. **much**
3. **many**
4. **a little**
5. **few**
6. There isn't **much** water.
7. Add **a little** salt.
8. There are many vegetables. Add a little soup? / There's a lot of soup.
9. How much milk…? How many eggs…?
10. Open food paragraph.
11. There isn't much water left.
12. There are many apples.
13. How much coffee do you drink? — A lot.
14. There are a few eggs.
15. Self-check.

</details>
