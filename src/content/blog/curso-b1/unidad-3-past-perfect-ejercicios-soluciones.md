## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** had + past participle

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. Before I left, I ___ never been to Asia. → *had / have / was*
2. By the time we arrived, the film ___ already started. → *had / has / have*
3. She ___ saved money for two years. → *had / has / have*
4. When she got to the station, the train ___ already left. → *had / has / have*
5. After he ___ graduated, he found a job. → *had / has / have*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **had** · 2. **had** · 3. **had** · 4. **had** · 5. **had**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Foco U3 = ____. → a) had + past participle · b) only A1 · c) skip
7. Forma clave U3 es ____. → a) correcta según teoría · b) always Past Simple · c) only will
8. Reading U3 usa ____. → a) foco de la unidad · b) only numbers · c) unrelated
9. Listening U3 practica ____. → a) el mismo foco · b) only spelling · c) only articles
10. Cuaderno U3 tiene ____. → a) 5×15 ejercicios · b) 3 lecciones · c) sin soluciones

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a)** · 7. **a) correcta según teoría** · 8. **a) foco de la unidad** · 9. **a) el mismo foco** · 10. **a) 5×15 ejercicios**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *Before I left, I have never been to Asia.*
12. *When she arrived, the train already left.* (orden poco claro)
13. *I had went to Tokyo.*
14. *By the time we arrive, the film had started.*
15. *She had finish her homework.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. Before I left, I **had** never been to Asia.
12. When she arrived, the train **had already left**.
13. I **had gone** to Tokyo.
14. By the time we **arrived**, the film had started.
15. She **had finished** her homework.

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Japan trip

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b1/unit-3/reading-past-perfect.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Japan trip"></audio>

> Last year I had the best trip of my life. Before I left, I had never been to Asia. I had saved money for two years and I had always dreamed of visiting Japan. When I arrived in Tokyo, I couldn't believe it because I had never seen such a beautiful city. By the time I left, I had visited many temples and had tried real Japanese food. That trip was a turning point in my life because I had always been afraid of travelling alone. Now I know I can overcome any challenge.
