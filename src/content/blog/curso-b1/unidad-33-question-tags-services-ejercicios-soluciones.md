## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** question tags (isn't it?, don't you?…)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. It's a nice day, ___? → *isn't it / is it / doesn't it*
2. You like this restaurant, ___? → *don't you / do you / doesn't you*
3. She works at the bank, ___? → *doesn't she / does she / isn't she*
4. They haven't finished, ___? → *have they / haven't they / did they*
5. You're free tomorrow, ___? → *aren't you / are you / don't you*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **isn't it** · 2. **don't you** · 3. **doesn't she** · 4. **have they** · 5. **aren't you**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. He is a doctor, ___? → *isn't he / is he / doesn't he*
2. We need an appointment, ___? → *don't we / do we / aren't we*
3. The cafe isn't open, ___? → *is it / isn't it / does it*
4. You went to the pharmacy, ___? → *didn't you / did you / don't you*
5. Positive → tag is ___ → *negative / positive / zero*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **isn't he** · 2. **don't we** · 3. **is it** · 4. **didn't you** · 5. **negative**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *It's nice, is it?*
2. *You like it, do you?* (confirm)
3. *She works here, isn't she?*
4. *They haven't finished, haven't they?*
5. *You're free, don't you?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. It's nice, **isn't it?**
2. You like it, **don't you?**
3. She works here, **doesn't she?**
4. They haven't finished, **have they?**
5. You're free, **aren't you?**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 5 tags: isn't it, don't you, doesn't she, have they, aren't you.
2. Completa: It's nice, ___?
3. Completa: You like it, ___?
4. Completa: She works here, ___?
5. Completa: They haven't finished, ___?
6. Corrige: *It's nice, is it?*
7. Corrige: *You're free, don't you?*
8. Usa bank y restaurant en 2 frases con tags.
9. Escribe: You're free tomorrow, aren't you?
10. Mini-diálogo (3 tags).
11. Traduce: Hace buen día, ¿verdad?
12. Traduce: Te gusta este restaurante, ¿no?
13. Explica +→− en 1 frase.
14. Escribe 1× didn't you?
15. Autochequeo: copia auxiliar y cambia polaridad.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open five.
2. **isn't it**
3. **don't you**
4. **doesn't she**
5. **have they**
6. **isn't it**
7. **aren't you**
8. Open.
9. OK.
10. Open.
11. It's a nice day, isn't it?
12. You like this restaurant, don't you?
13. + → − tag.
14. You went…, didn't you?
15. Self-check.
