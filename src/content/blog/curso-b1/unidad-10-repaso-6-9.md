      Going to = plan/evidencia; will = opinión, promesa o decisión espontánea;

La **Unidad 10 del curso B1** (*Repaso 6–9*) integra lo aprendido desde los futuros hasta *used to*. Úsala como **chequeo** antes del Módulo 2 (condicionales).

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 10 — Repaso 6–9](/curso-b1/unit-10)  
> **Guías:** [U6](/blog/curso-b1/unidad-6-future-will-going-to) · [U7](/blog/curso-b1/unidad-7-was-were-going-to) · [U8](/blog/curso-b1/unidad-8-modals-deduction) · [U9](/blog/curso-b1/unidad-9-used-to-be-get-used-to)

## Qué repasas

![Mapa de repaso](/blog/curso-b1/unit-10/review-map.png)

| Unidad | Gramática | Vocabulario |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **6** | will / going to / present continuous | Travel & transport |
| **7** | was/were going to | Plans & intentions |
| **8** | must / might / can't | Personal feelings |
| **9** | used to / be used to / get used to | Habits & lifestyle |

---

## 1. Futuros (U6) — recordatorio

> I **am going to** visit my parents next weekend. *(plan)*  
> Wait, I **will** help you. *(espontáneo)*  
> She **is meeting** her boss at 3 PM. *(arreglo)*  
> Look at the clouds! It **is going to** rain. *(evidencia)*  
> We **are flying** to Rome on Friday. *(viaje fijo)*

## 3. Deducción (U8)

> She **must** be happy — she just got promoted.  
> That **can't** be him — he is in Paris this week.  
> I **might** have left my keys at the office.  
> They **must** be at home — the lights are on.

## 4. Used to trio (U9)

> I **used to** play football when I was young.  
> She **is used to** living in a big city.  
> It took me a few months to **get used to** living here.  
> They **got used to** the cold weather after a few months.

## 5. Mini-mapa de decisión

![Ejemplos rápidos](/blog/curso-b1/unit-10/review-examples.png)

1. ¿Plan futuro vigente? → **going to** / continuous  
2. ¿Plan pasado fallido? → **was/were going to**  
3. ¿Hábito pasado habitual? → **used to**  
4. ¿Acostumbrado ahora / adaptándote? → **be / get used to**  
5. ¿Deduciendo? → **must / might / can't**

## 7. Ejercicios integrados

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige la forma

1. I \_\_\_\_ visit my parents next weekend. *(plan)*  
2. She \_\_\_\_ be happy — she got promoted.  
3. I \_\_\_\_ play football when I was young.  
4. They \_\_\_\_ travel last summer but cancelled.  
5. That \_\_\_\_ be him — he's in Paris.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **am going to** · 2. **must** · 3. **used to** · 4. **were going to** · 5. **can't**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Más contraste

1. Wait — I \_\_\_\_ help you.  
2. She \_\_\_\_ living in a big city for years. *(is used to)*  
3. I \_\_\_\_ have left my keys at work.  
4. She \_\_\_\_ meeting her boss at 3.  
5. It took months to \_\_\_\_ living here.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **will** · 2. **is used to** · 3. **might** · 4. **is** · 5. **get used to**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Corrección

1. *I am going to call you yesterday* → \_\_\_\_  
2. *That mustn't be him* (imposible) → \_\_\_\_  
3. *I am used to play tennis* → \_\_\_\_  
4. *Look! It will rain* (nubes negras) → \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **was going to** call you yesterday…  
2. That **can't** be him.  
3. I am used to **playing** / I **used to play**…  
4. It **is going to** rain.
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Matching rápido

A. arrangement fijo · B. plan fallido · C. hábito pasado · D. deducción fuerte  

1. We are flying on Friday. → \_\_\_\_  
2. I was going to go but cancelled. → \_\_\_\_  
3. I used to live in a village. → \_\_\_\_  
4. She must be relieved. → \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **A** · 2. **B** · 3. **C** · 4. **D**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Produce

Escribe una frase con cada estructura: *going to*, *was going to*, *must*, *can't*, *used to*, *get used to*.

<details>
<summary>Modelo</summary>

I **am going to** study tonight. I **was going to** go out but I was tired. She **must** be pleased. That **can't** be true. I **used to** hate mornings. I am **getting used to** waking up early.
</details>

## 8. Checklist antes del Módulo 2

- [ ] Distingo *will / going to / continuous*  
- [ ] Sé narrar un plan cancelado con *was/were going to*  
- [ ] Uso *must / might / can't* con evidencia  
- [ ] No confundo *used to* con *be used to*  
- [ ] Puedo hablar de travel, plans, feelings y habits

---

## Tip del profesor

En el repaso, **no memorices tablas**: inventa mini-historias (viaje cancelado + deducciones + cambio de hábitos). La gramática B1 se fija con contexto.
