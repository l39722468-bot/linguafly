## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** defining relative clauses (who/which/that)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. The woman ___ lives next door is a vet. → *who / which / where*
2. The book ___ I bought is interesting. → *which / who / whose*
3. Animals ___ live in the wild are free. → *that / who / where*
4. The landscape ___ we visited was breathtaking. → *that / who / whose*
5. The bird ___ I saw was an eagle. → *which / who / where*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **who** · 2. **which** · 3. **that** · 4. **that** · 5. **which**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. Anyone ___ loves wildlife should visit. → *who / which / where*
2. The river ___ flows through the valley is clean. → *which / who / whose*
3. The man ___ saw the bear reported it. → *who / which / where*
4. The plants ___ grow here need water. → *that / who / where*
5. Omit object: The book ___ I bought… → *that/which / who / where*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **who** · 2. **which** · 3. **who** · 4. **that** · 5. **that/which**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *The woman which lives next door…*
2. *Animals who live in the wild…*
3. *The book who I bought…*
4. *Anyone which loves wildlife…*
5. *The bird who I saw…*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. The woman **who/that** lives…
2. Animals **that/which** live…
3. The book **which/that** I bought…
4. Anyone **who/that** loves…
5. The bird **which/that** I saw…

</details>

4. forest → *bosque · appointment · worried*
5. bear → *oso · plastic · exciting*

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 2× who + 2× which/that.
2. Completa: The woman ___ lives…
3. Completa: The book ___ I bought…
4. Completa: Animals ___ live…
5. Completa: The bird ___ I saw…
6. Corrige: *The woman which lives…*
7. Corrige: *Animals who live…*
8. Usa wildlife y landscape en 2 frases con relative.
9. Escribe: Anyone who loves wildlife…
10. Párrafo (3–4) sobre naturaleza con who/which/that.
11. Traduce: La mujer que vive al lado…
12. Traduce: El pájaro que vi…
13. Pregunta Which…? + relative.
14. Escribe 1× that omitible (objeto).
15. Autochequeo: people→who; things→which/that.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open 2+2.
2. **who**
3. **which/that**
4. **that/which**
5. **which/that**
6. **who/that**
7. **that/which**
8. Open.
9. OK.
10. Open paragraph.
11. The woman who lives next door…
12. The bird which/that I saw…
13. Open.
14. The book (that) I bought…
15. Self-check.

</details>
