## Qué aprenderás

- **número + nombre** (*two-day*)
- **edad** (*20-year-old*)
- **well-known / long-distance…**
- Vocabulario: flight, luggage, sightseeing…

![Compound adjectives B1](/blog/curso-b1/unit-37/compound-adjectives.png)

## 1. Patrones

| Tipo | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| número + noun | a **two-day** trip |
| edad | a **20-year-old** student |
| adverbio + participle | a **well-known** hotel |
| adj + noun | a **long-distance** flight |

## 7. Ejercicios

1. a ___ trip (2 days)  
2. a ___ student (20 years)  
3. a ___ hotel  
4. a ___ flight  
5. Vocab: equipaje = ___ · retraso = ___

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **two-day** · 2. **20-year-old** · 3. **well-known** · 4. **long-distance** · 5. **luggage** · **delay**
</details>

---

## Tip del profesor

Delante del nombre: **guion** y **singular** (*a five-star hotel*, no *five-stars*).
