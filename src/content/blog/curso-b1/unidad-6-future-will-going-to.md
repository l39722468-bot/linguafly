> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 6 — Future forms](/curso-b1/unit-6)  
> **Antes:** [U5 — Repaso 1–4](/blog/curso-b1/unidad-5-repaso-1-4) · [will vs going to](/blog/gramatica/will-going-to-diferencia)

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 6 (B1)

- Usar **will** (predicción, promesa, decisión espontánea).
- Usar **going to** (plan / intención / evidencia).
- Usar **present continuous** (arreglo fijo con hora/lugar).
- Reconocer **Present Simple** en horarios (*The flight departs…*).
- Aplicarlo al vocabulario de **viajes y transporte**.

![Tres formas de futuro](/blog/curso-b1/unit-6/future-forms.png)

---

## 1. Mapa rápido

| Forma | Uso principal | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **will** | opinión, promesa, espontáneo | I think it **will** rain. · Wait, I **will** help you. |
| **going to** | plan / intención / evidencia | I **am going to** visit my parents. · It **is going to** rain. |
| **am/is/are + -ing** | arreglo fijo | We **are flying** to Rome on Friday. |
| **Present Simple** | horario | The flight **departs** at 10 AM. |

---

## 2. *going to* — planes e intenciones

> I **am going to** visit my parents next weekend.  
> He **is going to** take the train tomorrow.  
> I **am going to** book the tickets next week.

## 3. *will* — predicciones, promesas, espontáneo

> I think it **will** rain tomorrow.  
> Wait, I **will** help you.  
> I promise I **will** call you when I arrive.  
> I **will** probably stay at home tonight.  
> Perhaps we **will** see you there.

## 4. Present continuous — arreglos fijos

Hay cita, billete o acuerdo concreto:

> She **is meeting** her boss at 3 PM.  
> We **are flying** to Rome on Friday.  
> They **are having** a meeting tomorrow at 9.  
> She **is meeting** her sister at the airport at 5 PM.

## 5. Present Simple — horarios

> The flight **departs** at 10 AM.  
> The plane **takes off** at 8 o’clock.

## 10. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige la forma

1. I \_\_\_\_ visit my parents next weekend. (plan)  
2. I think it \_\_\_\_ rain tomorrow.  
3. She \_\_\_\_ meeting her boss at 3 PM.  
4. Wait — I \_\_\_\_ help you.  
5. Look at the clouds! It \_\_\_\_ rain.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **am going to** · 2. **will** · 3. **is** · 4. **will** · 5. **is going to**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Arreglos y horarios

1. We \_\_\_\_ flying to Rome on Friday.  
2. They \_\_\_\_ having a meeting tomorrow at 9.  
3. The flight \_\_\_\_ at 10 AM. (*departs*)  
4. She \_\_\_\_ meeting her sister at the airport at 5.  
5. I promise I \_\_\_\_ call you when I arrive.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **are** · 2. **are** · 3. **departs** · 4. **is** · 5. **will**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Travel vocab

1. Where you wait for a plane = \_\_\_\_  
2. Document to travel abroad = \_\_\_\_  
3. Plane leaves the ground = \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_  
4. Reserve a room = \_\_\_\_  
5. Delay in departure = \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **airport** · 2. **passport** · 3. **takes off** · 4. **book** · 5. **delay**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrección

1. *I will visiting my parents next weekend.* → \_\_\_\_  
2. *Look! It will rain* (nubes negras) → más natural: \_\_\_\_  
3. *The flight is depart at 10.* → \_\_\_\_  
4. *I think I am going to enjoy* (solo opinión) → también: \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **am going to visit**… / I **am visiting**…  
2. It **is going to** rain.  
3. The flight **departs** at 10.  
4. I think I **will** enjoy…
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Reading (Tokyo)

1. The person is flying next \_\_\_\_.  
2. They are meeting colleagues at the airport at \_\_\_\_ AM.  
3. The plane takes off at \_\_\_\_.  
4. They think they \_\_\_\_ enjoy the trip.  
5. They \_\_\_\_ going to visit temples if they have time.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **month** · 2. **6** · 3. **8** · 4. **will** · 5. **are**
</details>

## 11. Errores frecuentes

| Evita | Mejor |
| :--- | :--- |
| *I will to go* | I **will go** / I **am going to** go |
| *She meeting her boss tomorrow* | She **is meeting**… |
| *The flight is depart* | The flight **departs** |
| *I think it is going to rain* (solo opinión, sin evidencia) | I think it **will** rain |
| Usar *will* para todo | Mira si hay **plan**, **arreglo** o **opinión** |

---

## 12. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Pregunta: ¿opinión / promesa? → **will**. ¿plan decidido / evidencia? → **going to**. ¿cita en la agenda? → **continuous**.  
2. Practica con tu próximo viaje real.  
3. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 6](/blog/curso-b1/unidad-6-future-will-going-to-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 6 del curso B1](/curso-b1/unit-6).

La **Unidad 7** introduce **was/were going to** (planes que no se realizaron).
