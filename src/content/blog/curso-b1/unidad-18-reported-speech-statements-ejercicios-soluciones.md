## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** practicar reported speech statements (said/told + backshift).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

Elige la opción correcta. Solo una es válida.

1. "I am busy." → She said she ___ busy. → *was / is / were*
2. "We will call." → They said they ___ call. → *would / will / can*
3. "She has left." → He said she ___ left. → *had / has / have*
4. "I can help." → She told me she ___ help. → *could / can / would*
5. "I will send it." → Tom said he ___ send it. → *would / will / can*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **was** · 2. **would** · 3. **had** · 4. **could** · 5. **would**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

Completa cada frase con la forma correcta.

1. She ___ me the meeting was cancelled. → *told / said / asked*
2. They ___ they could reply in the morning. → *said / told / asked*
3. Sara said she ___ attached the file. → *had / has / have*
4. He said the message ___ already been forwarded. → *had / has / have*
5. Pia said she ___ busy. → *was / is / were*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **told** · 2. **said** · 3. **had** · 4. **had** · 5. **was**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige el error

Cada frase tiene **un** error. Reescribe la frase correcta.

1. *She said me she was tired.*
2. *They said they will call.*
3. *He said she has left.* (backshift)
4. *She told that she was busy.*
5. *Tom said he can help.* (backshift)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. She **told** me she was tired. / She **said** (that)…
2. They said they **would** call.
3. He said she **had** left.
4. She told **me** (that) she was busy. / She **said**…
5. Tom said he **could** help.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

2. reply → *responder · instalar · posponer*
3. forward → *reenviar · reparar · pronunciar*
4. inbox → *bandeja de entrada · CV · storm*
5. subject → *asunto · salario · device*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa / significado

Elige la opción que completa o explica mejor cada ítem.

1. email ≈ ___ → *correo / deadline / accent*

3. chat ≈ ___ → *chat / contract / drizzle*
4. notify ≈ ___ → *notificar / manufacture / binge*
5. share ≈ ___ → *compartir / postpone / regret*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **correo** · 2. **mensaje** · 3. **chat** · 4. **notificar** · 5. **compartir**

</details>
