## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** modal passive (must/should/can/might be + past participle)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. The form ___ be completed. → *must / should / can*
2. The report ___ be sent today. → *should / must / can*
3. The meeting ___ be postponed. → *can / must / should*
4. The interview ___ be cancelled. → *might / must / should*
5. The contract ___ be signed by Friday. → *must / can / might*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **must** · 2. **should** · 3. **can** · 4. **might** · 5. **must**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. Applications must ___ submitted before the deadline. → *be / being / been*
2. Meetings can ___ held online. → *be / being / been*
3. The salary might ___ increased next year. → *be / being / been*
4. Work experience should ___ included in your resume. → *be / being / been*
5. The client must ___ informed about the delay. → *be / being / been*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **be** · 2. **be** · 3. **be** · 4. **be** · 5. **be**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *Must to be finished.*
2. *The form must completed.*
3. *Should be send today.*
4. *Can be postpone.*
5. *Might being cancelled.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. It **must be** finished.
2. The form must **be** completed.
3. Should be **sent** today.
4. Can be **postponed**.
5. Might **be** cancelled.

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 4 modal passives (must/should/can/might).
2. Completa: The form ___ be completed.
3. Completa: The report ___ be sent today.
4. Completa: Applications must ___ submitted.
5. Corrige: *Must to be finished.*
6. Corrige: *The form must completed.*
7. Usa *deadline* y *colleague* en 2 frases con modal passive.
8. Escribe: The contract must be signed by Friday.
9. Escribe: The meeting can be postponed.
10. Mini-párrafo sobre un proceso de selección (interview/resume).
11. Traduce: El informe debería enviarse hoy.
12. Traduce: La entrevista podría cancelarse.
13. Pregunta *Should the report be finished today?* + respuesta.
14. Escribe 1× might be + V3.
15. Autochequeo: modal + be + V3.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Model: must/should/can/might be + V3.
2. **must**
3. **should**
4. **be**
5. It **must be** finished.
6. The form must **be** completed.
7. Applications must be submitted before the deadline. Meetings can be held with colleagues online.
8. OK model.
9. OK model.
10. Open hiring paragraph.
11. The report should be sent today.
12. The interview might be cancelled.
13. Should the report be finished today? — Yes, it should.
14. The interview might be postponed.
15. Self-check.

</details>
