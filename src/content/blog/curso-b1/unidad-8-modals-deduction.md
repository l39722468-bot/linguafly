## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 8 (B1)

- Usar **must** (casi seguro).
- Usar **might** (posible).
- Usar **can't** (imposible).
- Combinar con adjetivos de **sentimientos**.
- Evitar *mustn't* cuando quieres decir “imposible”.

![Modales de deducción](/blog/curso-b1/unit-8/modals-deduction.png)

---

## 1. Mapa rápido

| Modal | Significado | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **must** | casi seguro | She **must** be happy — she got promoted. |
| **might** | posible | I **might** have left my keys at work. |
| **can't** | imposible | That **can't** be him — he's in Paris. |

Escala: **must** (≈90%) → **might** (≈50%) → **can't** (≈0%).

---

## 2. *must* — deducción fuerte

> She **must** be happy — she just got promoted.  
> They **must** be at home — the lights are on.  
> He **must** be tired — he has been working all day.  
> They **must** have arrived by now — their flight landed an hour ago.  
> You **must** be joking!

## 3. *might* — posibilidad

> I **might** have left my keys at the office.  
> This **might** be the right address — let me check.  
> He **might** be at the meeting — he sent an email saying so.  
> She **might** not know the answer — she looks confused.  
> It **might** rain tomorrow — the forecast says so.

## 4. *can't* — imposibilidad

> That **can't** be him — he is in Paris this week.  
> She **can't** be angry — she never gets angry.  
> That **can't** be right — I checked twice.  
> The phone **can't** be ringing — there's nobody here.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b1/unit-8/cant-be-him.mp3" title="🔊 That can't be him — he is in Paris this week."></audio>

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b1/unit-8/cant-be-angry.mp3" title="🔊 She can't be angry — she never gets angry."></audio>

**No** digas *That mustn't be him* para “imposible”: en B1, usa **can't**.

## 6. Reading: the neighbour

![Deducciones sobre el vecino](/blog/curso-b1/unit-8/neighbour.png)

## 9. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige el modal

1. She \_\_\_\_ be happy — she got promoted.  
2. That \_\_\_\_ be him — he's in Paris.  
3. I \_\_\_\_ have left my keys at work.  
4. They \_\_\_\_ be at home — the lights are on.  
5. She \_\_\_\_ be angry — she never gets angry.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **must** · 2. **can't** · 3. **might** · 4. **must** · 5. **can't**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Feelings

1. Worried about the future = \_\_\_\_  
2. Better after a worry = \_\_\_\_  
3. Sure about yourself = \_\_\_\_  
4. Expectations not met = \_\_\_\_  
5. Very enthusiastic = \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **anxious** · 2. **relieved** · 3. **confident** · 4. **disappointed** · 5. **excited**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Reading

1. The neighbour moved into a new \_\_\_\_.  
2. He might have got a \_\_\_\_.  
3. His wife \_\_\_\_ be angry.  
4. She must feel \_\_\_\_.  
5. The writer might invite them for \_\_\_\_.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **house** · 2. **promotion** · 3. **can't** · 4. **relieved** · 5. **coffee**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrección

1. *That mustn't be him* (imposible) → \_\_\_\_  
2. *She can be happy* (casi seguro, evidencia) → \_\_\_\_  
3. *He must to be tired* → \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. That **can't** be him.  
2. She **must** be happy.  
3. He **must be** tired.
</details>

---

## 10. Errores frecuentes

1. *mustn't* ≠ “imposible” → usa **can't**.  
2. *must to be* → **must be**.  
3. Confundir *must* obligación con *must* deducción: mira si hay **evidencia**.  
4. *might* no es “casi seguro”: si lo es, usa **must**.

---

## Tip del profesor

Di en voz alta la evidencia (*the lights are on*, *he's in Paris*). Si la evidencia es fuerte → **must**; débil → **might**; contradice el hecho → **can't**.
