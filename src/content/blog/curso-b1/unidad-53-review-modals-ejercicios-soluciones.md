## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** deduction, had better, would rather, need/needn't (U8, U46–49)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. She ___ be at home — her car is there. → *must / can / should*
2. It ___ rain later. → *might / must / can't*
3. He ___ be in the office — it's Sunday. → *can't / must / might*
4. You ___ better see a doctor. → *had / have / would*
5. You ___ hurry. → *needn't / need / must*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **must** · 2. **might** · 3. **can't** · 4. **had** · 5. **needn't**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. must (deduction) ≈ ___ → *almost certain / impossible / preference*
2. might ≈ ___ → *possible / certain / advice*
3. had better + ___ → *bare infinitive / to inf / -ing*
4. would rather + ___ → *bare infinitive / to inf / -ing*
5. needn't ≈ ___ → *no necessity / obligation / deduction*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **almost certain** · 2. **possible** · 3. **bare infinitive** · 4. **bare infinitive** · 5. **no necessity**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *She must to be at home.*
2. *He can't to be in the office.*
3. *You had better to see a doctor.*
4. *I'd rather to stay.*
5. *You needn't to hurry.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. She **must be** at home.
2. He **can't be** in the office.
3. You **had better see** a doctor.
4. I'd **rather stay**.
5. You **needn't hurry**.

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe must/might/can't + had better + would rather + needn't.
2. Completa: She ___ be at home.
3. Completa: It ___ rain later.
4. Completa: He ___ be in the office.
5. Completa: You ___ better see a doctor.
6. Completa: I'd ___ stay at home.
7. Corrige: *You had better to see a doctor.*
8. Corrige: *You needn't to hurry.*
9. Usa might y can't en 2 frases.
10. Escribe: You needn't hurry.
11. Mini-diálogo (4 frases) con modales.
12. Traduce: Debe estar en casa.
13. Traduce: Preferiría quedarme en casa.
14. Explica must (deduction) vs must (obligation).
15. Escribe 1× would rather you didn't.
16. Autochequeo: had better/rather sin to.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open five.
2. **must**
3. **might**
4. **can't**
5. **had**
6. **rather**
7. had better **see**
8. needn't **hurry**
9. Open.
10. OK.
11. Open.
12. She must be at home.
13. I'd rather stay at home.
14. Deduction=casi seguro; obligation=debes hacer.
15. OK.
16. Self-check.

</details>
