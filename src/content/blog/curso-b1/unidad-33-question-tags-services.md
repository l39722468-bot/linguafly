## Qué aprenderás

- **+ → −** y **− → +**
- Tags con *be / do / have*
- Vocabulario: bank, doctor, restaurant, appointment…

![Question tags B1](/blog/curso-b1/unit-33/question-tags.png)

## 7. Ejercicios

1. It's a nice day, ___?  
2. You like this restaurant, ___?  
3. She works at the bank, ___?  
4. They haven't finished, ___?  
5. Vocab: banco = ___ · cita = ___

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **isn't it** · 2. **don't you** · 3. **doesn't she** · 4. **have they** · 5. **bank** · **appointment**
</details>

---

## Tip del profesor

Copia el auxiliar de la frase principal y cámbiale el signo (+/−). Si no hay auxiliar, usa *do/does/did*.
