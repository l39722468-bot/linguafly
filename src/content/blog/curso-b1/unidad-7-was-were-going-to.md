## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 7 (B1)

- Formar **was/were going to + verbo**.
- Usarlo para **intenciones pasadas no cumplidas**.
- Contrastarlo con **am/is/are going to** (futuro actual).
- Negativas y preguntas: *wasn't / weren't going to*, *Were you going to…?*
- Vocabulario: *cancel, postpone, change your mind, intention…*

---

## 1. Mapa rápido

| Forma | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **was going to** | I / he / she / it | I **was going to** call you but I forgot. |
| **were going to** | you / we / they | They **were going to** travel but cancelled. |
| **wasn't / weren't going to** | negativa | She **wasn't going to** attend, but she came. |
| **Were you going to…?** | pregunta | **Were you going to** come to the party? |

---

## 2. Forma afirmativa

> I **was going to** call you yesterday but I forgot.  
> She **was going to** study medicine but changed her mind.  
> They **were going to** travel to Spain last summer but cancelled.  
> We **were going to** watch a film last night but we were too tired.  
> He **was going to** buy a new car but lost his job.

## 3. Negativa y pregunta

> She **wasn't going to** attend the meeting but changed her mind.  
> They **weren't going to** tell him the truth but he found out.  
> What **were you going to** do before the rain started?  
> You **were going to** come to the party last Saturday, right?

## 4. Contraste con *am/is/are going to*

| Ahora / futuro | Pasado no cumplido |
| :--- | :--- |
| I **am going to** call you tonight. | I **was going to** call you yesterday but I forgot. |
| We **are going to** visit Japan. | We **were going to** visit Japan but we cancelled. |

*Am going to* = plan vigente. *Was going to* = el plan **ya no** se realizó (o cambió).

## 6. Reading: cancelled Japan trip

![Viaje a Japón cancelado](/blog/curso-b1/unit-7/japan-cancelled.png)

> Last year I was going to travel to Japan for my birthday. I had already booked the flight and hotel, but then I changed my mind because I got a new job. My friend was going to come with me too, but she had to postpone her trip. We were going to visit Tokyo and Kyoto. I was going to learn some Japanese before the trip, but I never had time. Maybe next year we will go.

Cuenta las *was/were going to*: viaje, amiga, ciudades, japonés — y las razones (*new job, postpone, never had time*).

## 9. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Completa

1. I \_\_\_\_ going to call you yesterday but I forgot.  
2. They \_\_\_\_ going to travel to Spain but cancelled.  
3. She \_\_\_\_ going to study medicine but changed her mind.  
4. We \_\_\_\_ going to watch a film but we were too tired.  
5. He \_\_\_\_ going to buy a car but lost his job.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **was** · 2. **were** · 3. **was** · 4. **were** · 5. **was**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Negativa / pregunta

1. She \_\_\_\_ going to attend, but she came. (*wasn't*)  
2. They \_\_\_\_ going to tell him. (*weren't*)  
3. What \_\_\_\_ you going to do?  
4. You \_\_\_\_ going to come, right?  
5. He \_\_\_\_ going to apologise, but I insisted. (*wasn't*)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **wasn't** · 2. **weren't** · 3. **were** · 4. **were** · 5. **wasn't**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Vocabulario

1. Decidir no hacer lo planeado = change your \_\_\_\_  
2. Mover a una fecha posterior = \_\_\_\_  
3. Parar un evento = \_\_\_\_  
4. Decidirse = make up your \_\_\_\_  
5. Tener dudas = have second \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **mind** · 2. **postpone** · 3. **cancel** · 4. **mind** · 5. **thoughts**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Reading

1. The trip was planned for \_\_\_\_ year.  
2. Why did the person change their mind? \_\_\_\_  
3. Cities: Tokyo and \_\_\_\_  
4. Did they learn Japanese? \_\_\_\_  
5. Maybe they will go \_\_\_\_ year.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **last** · 2. **new job** · 3. **Kyoto** · 4. **No** · 5. **next**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Contraste

1. Plan vigente: I \_\_\_\_ going to call tonight.  
2. Plan fallido: I \_\_\_\_ going to call yesterday but forgot.  
3. *I am going to visit Japan last year* → corrige: \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **am** · 2. **was** · 3. I **was going to** visit Japan last year (but…).
</details>

## 10. Errores frecuentes

1. *I am going to call you yesterday* → **was going to** (pasado).  
2. *They was going to* → **were** going to.  
3. *I was going to calling* → **call** (infinitivo sin *to* extra: *going to call*).  
4. Usar *will* para un plan cancelado: mejor **was going to** + *but*.

---

## Tip del profesor

Si puedes añadir **but + razón**, casi siempre encaja *was/were going to*. Sin *but*, el oyente puede pensar que el plan aún vale: aclara el cambio.
