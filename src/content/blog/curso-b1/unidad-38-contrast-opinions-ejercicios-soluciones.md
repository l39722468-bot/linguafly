## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** although/however/despite + opinions

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ___ it was raining, we went out. → *Although / However / Despite*
2. I like it. ___, it's expensive. → *However / Although / Despite*
3. ___ I disagree, I respect you. → *Even though / However / Despite*
4. ___ the delay, we arrived. → *Despite / Although / However*
5. Though ≈ ___ → *Although / However / Because of*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Although** · 2. **However** · 3. **Even though** · 4. **Despite** · 5. **Although**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. although + ___ → *clause / noun only / comma phrase*
2. however position → *new sentence / mid-clause like although / before noun*
3. despite + ___ → *noun/-ing / full clause / verb*
4. even though ≈ ___ → *although (stronger) / however / so that*
5. In spite of ≈ ___ → *despite / although / however*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **clause** · 2. **new sentence** · 3. **noun/-ing** · 4. **although (stronger)** · 5. **despite**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *However it was raining, we went.*
2. *Although, it's expensive.* (second clause)
3. *Despite it was raining* (clause)
4. *Even though I disagree, however I respect.*
5. *Although the delay, we arrived.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Although** it was raining… / However, **it was raining.** (new sentence)
2. I like it. **However**, it's expensive.
3. **Despite the rain** / **Despite it raining**
4. Even though… (no however in same link)
5. **Despite the delay** / **Although there was a delay**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 2× although + 1× however + 1× despite.
2. Completa: ___ it was raining, we went out.
3. Completa: I like it. ___, it's expensive.
4. Completa: ___ I disagree, I respect you.
5. Completa: ___ the delay, we arrived.
6. Corrige: *However it was raining, we went.*
7. Corrige: *Despite it was raining.*
8. Usa agree y disagree en 2 frases con contrast.
9. Escribe: Even though I disagree, I respect you.
10. Mini-debate (4 frases) con contrast.
11. Traduce: Aunque llovía, salimos.
12. Traduce: Sin embargo, es caro.
13. Explica although vs however en 1 frase.
14. Escribe 1× in spite of.
15. Autochequeo: although=clause; despite=noun/-ing.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open four.
2. **Although/Though**
3. **However**
4. **Even though**
5. **Despite**
6. **Although**… / **However**, it…
7. **Despite the rain**
8. Open.
9. OK.
10. Open.
11. Although it was raining, we went out.
12. However, it's expensive.
13. although same clause; however new sentence.
14. OK.
15. Self-check.

</details>
