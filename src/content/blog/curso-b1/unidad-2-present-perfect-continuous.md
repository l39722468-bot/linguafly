Tras el [Repaso A2 → B1](/blog/curso-b1/unidad-1-repaso-a2-b1), la **Unidad 2 del curso B1** (*Present Perfect Continuous*) te enseña a hablar de **duración hasta ahora**: ***She has been studying for two hours***. Combina la forma con *for/since*, el contraste con Present Perfect Simple y vocabulario de **education**.

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 2 (B1)

- Formar **have/has been + -ing**.
- Usar **for** (duración) y **since** (punto de inicio).
- Preguntar con **How long have you been…?**
- Elegir **Continuous** (proceso / evidencia) vs **Simple** (resultado / cantidad).
- Aplicarlo al vocabulario de **education** (degree, subject, homework, attend…).

![Fórmula Present Perfect Continuous](/blog/curso-b1/unit-2/ppc-formula.png)

## 1. La fórmula

**Present Perfect Continuous = have/has + been + -ing**

| Sujeto | Auxiliar | + been + -ing | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I / you / we / they | **have been** | studying / working… | They **have been working** all day. |
| he / she / it | **has been** | studying / raining… | She **has been studying** for two hours. |

> She has been studying for two hours.  
> They have been working on the project all day.  
> We have been waiting since 8 o’clock.  
> It has been raining since this morning.

## 2. *for* vs *since*

| Palabra | Va con | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **for** | duración | for **three years**, for **two hours**, for **months** |
| **since** | punto de inicio | since **2019**, since **September**, since **this morning** |

> I have been learning English **for** three years.  
> He has been teaching **since** 2019.

## 4. Continuous vs Simple

![Continuous vs Simple](/blog/curso-b1/unit-2/continuous-vs-simple.png)

| Enfoque | Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Duración / proceso | Continuous | She **has been gardening**. (hands dirty) |
| Evidencia visible | Continuous | Your eyes are red — you **have been crying**. |
| Resultado / cantidad | Simple | I **have read** three books this year. |
| Acciones contadas | Simple | She **has written** ten emails today. |

> Her hands are dirty. She **has been gardening**.  
> Your eyes are red. You **have been crying**.  
> I **have read** three books this year.  
> She **has written** ten emails today.

2. They \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_ working all day.

4. It \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_ raining since this morning.  
5. How long \_\_\_\_ you \_\_\_\_ studying here?

### Ejercicio 2 — *for* / *since*

1. I have been learning English \_\_\_\_ three years.  
2. He has been teaching \_\_\_\_ 2019.  
3. They have been living in Paris \_\_\_\_ last month.  
4. She has been working here \_\_\_\_ five years.  
5. I have been trying to call you \_\_\_\_ an hour.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **for** · 2. **since** · 3. **since** · 4. **for** · 5. **for**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Continuous vs Simple

1. Her hands are dirty. She \_\_\_\_. (*has been gardening / has gardened*)  
2. I \_\_\_\_ three books this year. (*have read / have been reading*) — resultado  
3. Your eyes are red. You \_\_\_\_. (*have been crying / have cried*)  
4. She \_\_\_\_ ten emails today. (*has written / has been writing*)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **has been gardening** · 2. **have read** · 3. **have been crying** · 4. **has written**
</details>

## 10. Errores frecuentes

| Evita | Mejor |
| :--- | :--- |
| *She have been studying* | She **has** been studying |
| *I am been working* | I **have been** working |
| *for 2019* | **since** 2019 |
| *since three years* | **for** three years |
| *How long are you studying?* (duración hasta ahora) | How long **have you been** studying? |
| *I have been knowing her* | I **have known** her (stative) |

---

## 11. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Cuenta tu historia: *I have been studying… for/since…*  
2. Cuando veas **cantidad** (*three books*, *ten emails*), piensa en **Simple**.  
3. Cuando veas **manos sucias / ojos rojos / all day**, piensa en **Continuous**.  
4. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 2](/blog/curso-b1/unidad-2-present-perfect-continuous-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 2 del curso B1](/curso-b1/unit-2).

La **Unidad 3** introduce el **Past Perfect** (*had + past participle*) para secuencias en el pasado.
