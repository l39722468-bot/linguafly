## 4. Past Simple vs Present Perfect (U4)

| Past Simple | Present Perfect |
| :--- | :--- |
| I **went** yesterday | She **has never been**… |
| They **visited** last week | I **have just finished**… |
| When **did** you see…? | She **has already** booked… |

## 6. Reading: Laura

![Laura en el aeropuerto](/blog/curso-b1/unit-5/laura-airport.png)

> My name is Laura and I have been working as a teacher for five years. Last year I decided to travel to Japan. I had never been to Asia before that trip. When I arrived at the airport, my flight had already left because I was delayed. I have just booked another trip for next summer. I am very excited and confident this time everything will go well.

Marca los tiempos: PPC (*have been working*), Past Simple (*decided*, *arrived*), Past Perfect (*had never been*, *had already left*), Present Perfect (*have just booked*), feelings (*excited*, *confident*).

## 9. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige el tiempo

1. She \_\_\_\_ studying for two hours. (*has been / had*)  
2. He \_\_\_\_ finished before I arrived.  
3. I \_\_\_\_ to Paris yesterday. (*went / have been*)  
4. They \_\_\_\_ living in London since January.  
5. By the time we arrived, the film \_\_\_\_ already started.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **has been** · 2. **had** · 3. **went** · 4. **have been** · 5. **had**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Señales

1. yesterday → \_\_\_\_  
2. for two hours (duración hasta ahora) → \_\_\_\_  
3. before I arrived → \_\_\_\_  
4. just / already / yet / never (experiencia ahora) → \_\_\_\_  
5. last week → \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Past Simple · 2. Present Perfect Continuous · 3. Past Perfect · 4. Present Perfect · 5. Past Simple
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Completa

1. When she got to the station, the train \_\_\_\_ already \_\_\_\_.  
2. I \_\_\_\_ just finished my report.  
3. She \_\_\_\_ never been to Japan.  
4. I \_\_\_\_ read three books this year.  
5. Your eyes are red — you \_\_\_\_ been crying.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **had** … **left** · 2. **have** · 3. **has** · 4. **have** · 5. **have**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Vocabulario

1. Opposite of confident ≈ \_\_\_\_  
2. “alguna vez” = \_\_\_\_  
3. “acabo de” = \_\_\_\_  
4. “todavía” (neg.) = \_\_\_\_  
5. University qualification = \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **nervous** · 2. **ever** · 3. **just** · 4. **yet** · 5. **degree**
</details>

## 10. Checklist antes de seguir

- [ ] Sé formar *has/have been + -ing* y usar *for/since*.  
- [ ] Sé formar *had + pp* con *before / by the time / already*.  
- [ ] Distingo *yesterday/last…* (Past Simple) de *ever/just/already/yet* (Present Perfect).  
- [ ] Elijo Simple (cantidad) vs Continuous (proceso/evidencia).  
- [ ] Uso feelings básicos (*confident*, *excited*, *nervous*…).

Si falla un punto, vuelve a su unidad y luego practícalo en el [curso](/curso-b1/unit-5).

## 11. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. En cada frase, pregunta: ¿**hasta ahora**, **antes de otro pasado**, o **fecha acabada**?  
2. Reescribe el texto de Laura marcando cada tiempo.
