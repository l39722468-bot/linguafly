## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** -ed/-ing adjectives (bored/boring, excited/exciting)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I was ___ / the film was ___. → *bored / boring · boring / bored*
2. The news was ___ / I felt ___. → *exciting / excited · excited / exciting*
3. She was ___ about the exam. → *worried / worrying / worry*
4. The match was ___ / we were ___. → *exciting / excited · excited / exciting*
5. The book is ___ / I am ___. → *interesting / interested · interested / interesting*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **bored / boring** · 2. **exciting / excited** · 3. **worried** · 4. **exciting / excited** · 5. **interesting / interested**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige / completa

1. -ed describes ___ → *how you feel / the cause / the place*
2. -ing describes ___ → *the cause / how you feel / the tag*
3. I felt ___ → *excited / exciting / excite*
4. A ___ film → *boring / bored / bore*
5. I'm ___ in wildlife. → *interested / interesting / interest*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **how you feel** · 2. **the cause** · 3. **excited** · 4. **boring** · 5. **interested**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

1. *I was boring because the film was bored.*
2. *The news was excited.*
3. *She was worrying about the exam.* (feeling)
4. *We were exciting.*
5. *I am interesting in the book.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I was **bored** / the film was **boring**.
2. The news was **exciting**.
3. She was **worried**…
4. We were **excited**.
5. I am **interested**…

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

1. Escribe 3 pares -ed/-ing.
2. Completa: I was ___ / film was ___.
3. Completa: news was ___ / I felt ___.
4. Completa: She was ___ about the exam.
5. Completa: book is ___ / I am ___.
6. Corrige: *I was boring.*
7. Corrige: *The news was excited.*
8. Usa worried y surprising en 2 frases.
9. Escribe: The match was exciting. We were excited.
10. Mini-diario de sentimientos (4 frases).
11. Traduce: Estaba aburrido porque la película era aburrida.
12. Traduce: La noticia era emocionante.
13. Explica -ed vs -ing en 1 frase.
14. Escribe 1× interested/interesting.
15. Autochequeo: feeling=-ed; cause=-ing.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Open three pairs.
2. **bored / boring**
3. **exciting / excited**
4. **worried**
5. **interesting / interested**
6. **bored**
7. **exciting**
8. Open.
9. OK.
10. Open.
11. I was bored because the film was boring.
12. The news was exciting.
13. feeling vs cause.
14. OK.
15. Self-check.

</details>
