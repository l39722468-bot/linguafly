## 6. Ejercicios exprés

1. You ___ better see a doctor.  
2. I'd ___ stay at home.  
3. We ran ___ of milk.  
4. You ___ hurry.  
5. It's time ___ go home.

<details><summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **had** · 2. **rather** · 3. **out** · 4. **needn't** · 5. **to**
</details>
