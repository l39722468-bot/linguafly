Tras el [Present Perfect Continuous](/blog/curso-b1/unidad-2-present-perfect-continuous), la **Unidad 3 del curso B1** (*Past Perfect*) te enseña a ordenar el pasado: ***He had finished his homework before I arrived***. Usas *had + past participle* con *before*, *by the time* y *already*, y vocabulario de **past experiences**.

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 3 (B1)

- Formar **had + past participle** (todos los sujetos).
- Marcar la acción **anterior** en una secuencia pasada.
- Usar *before*, *by the time*, *after*, *already*, *never… before*.
- Contar **experiencias pasadas** (*achievement*, *turning point*, *overcome*…).
- Leer un relato con Past Perfect (viaje a Japón).

## 1. La fórmula

**Past Perfect = had + past participle**

| Sujeto | Auxiliar | + past participle | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I / you / he / she / it / we / they | **had** | finished / been / left… | He **had finished** before I arrived. |

> He had finished his homework before I arrived.  
> She had never been to Paris before last year.  
> They had never met before that day.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b1/unit-3/had-never-been.mp3" title="🔊 She had never been to Paris before last year."></audio>

Negativa: *had not / hadn’t* + pp. Pregunta: *Had* + sujeto + pp?

---

## 2. ¿Para qué sirve?

Past Perfect = “ya había ocurrido” **antes** de otro momento pasado.

| Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| Secuencia | He **had finished** before I **arrived**. |
| Experiencia antes de un momento pasado | She **had never been** to Paris before last year. |
| Causa en el pasado | I **had forgotten** my keys, so I couldn’t get in. |
| Resultado ya listo | She **had studied** hard, so she passed. |

No es “más pasado” por moda: solo cuando necesitas marcar **qué fue primero**.

## 3. *before*, *by the time*, *already*

![Secuencia before / by the time](/blog/curso-b1/unit-3/sequence-before.png)

| Marcador | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **before** | anterior a… | …before I arrived |
| **by the time** | para cuando… | By the time we arrived, the film **had already started**. |
| **already** | ya (antes) | the train **had already left** |
| **after** | después de que… | After he **had graduated**, he found a job. |

> By the time we arrived, the film had already started.  
> When she got to the station, the train had already left.  
> I had already eaten when she invited me to dinner.  
> After he had graduated, he found a job.

## 5. Past Perfect vs Past Simple

| Tiempo | Rol | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Past Simple | momento / narración | I **arrived** / she **got** to the station |
| Past Perfect | lo anterior | the film **had started** / the train **had left** |

Si solo cuentas una línea temporal sin “antes”, Past Simple basta: *I finished and then I left.*

## 10. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Forma (*had* + pp)

1. He \_\_\_\_ finished his homework before I arrived.  
2. She \_\_\_\_ never been to Paris before last year.  
3. They \_\_\_\_ never met before that day.  
4. We \_\_\_\_ finished the project before the deadline.  
5. \_\_\_\_ you already eaten when she invited you?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **had** · 2. **had** · 3. **had** · 4. **had** · 5. **Had**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — *before* / *by the time* / *already*

1. By the time we arrived, the film \_\_\_\_ already \_\_\_\_.  
2. When she got to the station, the train \_\_\_\_ already \_\_\_\_.  
3. I \_\_\_\_ already \_\_\_\_ when she invited me.  
4. After he \_\_\_\_ graduated, he found a job.  
5. He \_\_\_\_ left by the time I called.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **had** … **started** · 2. **had** … **left** · 3. **had** … **eaten** · 4. **had** · 5. **had**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Causa / experiencia

1. I \_\_\_\_ forgotten my keys, so I couldn’t get in.  
2. She \_\_\_\_ studied hard, so she passed.  
3. She \_\_\_\_ been to London twice before she moved there.  
4. I \_\_\_\_ never seen such a beautiful sunset before.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1–4. **had** (+ forgotten / studied / been / seen)
</details>

## 11. Errores frecuentes

| Evita | Mejor |
| :--- | :--- |
| *He has finished before I arrived* | He **had** finished before I arrived |
| *When she arrived, the train already left* (orden poco claro) | …the train **had already left** |
| *By the time we arrive, the film had started* (mezcla de tiempos) | By the time we **arrived**, … |
| *I had went* | I **had gone** / **had been** |
| Usar Past Perfect en toda la historia | Solo en lo **anterior**; el resto en Past Simple |

## 12. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Dibuja dos momentos: primero Past Perfect, después Past Simple.  
2. Practica con *by the time* y *already*.  
3. Cuenta un viaje o un examen: qué **habías** hecho antes.
