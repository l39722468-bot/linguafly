> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 9 — Used to / be used to / get used to](/curso-b1/unit-9)

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 9 (B1)

- **used to + verbo** (hábito pasado).
- **be used to + nombre/-ing** (acostumbrado ahora).
- **get used to + nombre/-ing** (proceso de adaptación).
- Negativa y pregunta con *used to* (*didn't use to*, *Did you use to…?*).
- Vocabulario de **habits & lifestyle**.

![Trío used to](/blog/curso-b1/unit-9/used-to-trio.png)

---

## 1. Mapa rápido

| Estructura | Significado | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **used to + verb** | solía (ya no) | I **used to** live in a village. |
| **be used to + -ing/noun** | estar acostumbrado | I **am used to** the noise. |
| **get used to + -ing/noun** | acostumbrarse | I **got used to** living here. |

---

## 2. *used to* — hábitos del pasado

> I **used to** play football when I was young.  
> I **used to** live in a small village.  
> We **used to** have a dog when we were children.  
> He **used to** drink alcohol but now he has stopped.  
> I **didn't use to** like coffee but now I love it.  
> **Did** you **use to** live in London?

Ojo: en negativa/pregunta, **use** (sin *-d*): *didn't **use** to*, *Did you **use** to…?*

---

## 3. *be used to* — acostumbrado ahora

> She **is used to** living in a big city.  
> We **are used to** getting up early.  
> She **is used to** driving in the city.  
> I **am used to** the noise and the busy lifestyle.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-b1/unit-9/is-used-to-living.mp3" title="🔊 She is used to living in a big city."></audio>

Después de *be used to* → **nombre** o **-ing**, nunca infinitivo (*to play* ✗ → *playing* ✓).

---

## 4. *get used to* — el proceso

> It took me a few months to **get used to** living here.  
> I am **getting used to** working from home now.  
> They **got used to** the cold weather after a few months.  
> I will **get used to** living alone soon.

## 6. Reading: village → city

![Del pueblo a la ciudad](/blog/curso-b1/unit-9/village-city.png)

> I used to live in a small village when I was young. I used to walk to school every day and play in the fields after class. Now I live in a big city and I am used to the noise and the busy lifestyle. It took me a few months to get used to living here. I used to hate traffic but now I don't mind it. My parents used to visit me every weekend but they live far now.

## 9. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige la estructura

1. I \_\_\_\_ play football when I was young. (*used to*)  
2. She \_\_\_\_ living in a big city. (*is used to*)  
3. It took months to \_\_\_\_ living here. (*get used to*)  
4. We \_\_\_\_ getting up early. (*are used to*)  
5. They \_\_\_\_ the cold after a few months. (*got used to*)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **used to** · 2. **is used to** · 3. **get used to** · 4. **are used to** · 5. **got used to**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Forma correcta

1. I didn't \_\_\_\_ to like coffee. (*use*)  
2. Did you \_\_\_\_ to live in London? (*use*)  
3. I am used to \_\_\_\_. (*drive* → -ing)  
4. I used to \_\_\_\_ football. (*play*)  
5. He is getting used to \_\_\_\_ from home. (*work*)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **use** · 2. **use** · 3. **driving** · 4. **play** · 5. **working**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Vocabulario

1. Something you do regularly = \_\_\_\_  
2. Start a new activity = \_\_\_\_ up  
3. Stop a habit = \_\_\_\_ up  
4. Way you live = \_\_\_\_  
5. Become familiar with something new = get \_\_\_\_ to

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **habit** · 2. **take** · 3. **give** · 4. **lifestyle** · 5. **used**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Reading

1. He used to live in a small \_\_\_\_.  
2. Now he lives in a big \_\_\_\_.  
3. He is used to the \_\_\_\_.  
4. It took a few \_\_\_\_ to get used to living there.  
5. He used to hate \_\_\_\_.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **village** · 2. **city** · 3. **noise** · 4. **months** · 5. **traffic**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrección

1. *I am used to play tennis* → \_\_\_\_  
2. *I used to living in Paris* → \_\_\_\_  
3. *I didn't used to smoke* → \_\_\_\_

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I am used to **playing** tennis. / I **used to play** tennis.  
2. I **used to live** in Paris.  
3. I didn't **use** to smoke.
</details>

---

## 10. Errores frecuentes

1. *be used to + infinitive* → **-ing** / noun.  
2. *didn't used to* → **didn't use to**.  
3. Mezclar *used to* (pasado) con *be used to* (ahora).  
4. Olvidar que *get used to* es un **proceso** (tiempo, *took me…*).

---

## Tip del profesor

Pregúntate: ¿**ya no** lo hago? → *used to*. ¿**Ya no me cuesta**? → *be used to*. ¿**Todavía me estoy adaptando**? → *get used to* / *getting used to*.
