## Lección 1 — Gramática: irregulares

**Objetivo:** *went, saw, had, made, wrote, bought*; *didn't / Did* + base.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ to the cinema yesterday. → *went / go / goed*  
2. She ____ a film last night. → *saw / see / seen*  
3. We ____ a great time at the party. → *had / have / haved*  
4. He ____ a cake for his birthday. → *made / make / maked*  
5. They ____ some souvenirs. → *bought / buy / buyed*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **went**  
2. **saw** — no *seen* en Past Simple.  
3. **had**  
4. **made**  
5. **bought**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Past of *write* = ____. → a) wrote · b) writed · c) written (here)  
7. *I goed to London* is ____. → a) wrong · b) correct · c) future  
8. After *didn't* we use ____. → a) base (*go*) · b) *went* · c) *going*  
9. *Did you ____ a film?* → a) see · b) saw · c) seen  
10. *Seen* in *I seen a film* is ____. → a) wrong for Past Simple · b) correct · c) better

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) wrote**  
7. **a) wrong**  
8. **a) base (*go*)**  
9. **a) see**  
10. **a) wrong for Past Simple**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I goed to the shop.*  
12. *She buyed a dress.*  
13. *He didn't went home.*  
14. *Did you saw Tom?*  
15. *I seen a film yesterday.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I **went** to the shop.  
12. She **bought** a dress.  
13. He didn't **go** home.  
14. Did you **see** Tom?  
15. I **saw** a film yesterday.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: went, saw, had…

**Objetivo:** memorizar pares base → past.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja

1. go  
2. see  
3. have  
4. make  
5. buy  

a) saw · b) went · c) bought · d) had · e) made

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **d** · 4 → **e** · 5 → **c**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. write → ____  
7. go → ____  
8. see → ____  
9. make → ____  
10. buy → ____  

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **wrote** · 7. **went** · 8. **saw** · 9. **made** · 10. **bought**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Souvenirs* = ____. → a) things you buy on a trip · b) medicine · c) homework  
12. *Market* = ____. → a) place to buy food · b) cinema · c) hospital  
13. *Postcard* = ____. → a) short card you write to someone · b) a cake · c) a film  
14. *Had coffee* = ____. → a) drank coffee · b) bought a car · c) wrote a letter  
15. *Made dinner* = ____. → a) cooked dinner · b) saw a film · c) went shopping

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) things you buy on a trip**  
12. **a) place to buy food**  
13. **a) short card you write to someone**  
14. **a) drank coffee**  
15. **a) cooked dinner**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Last weekend

**Objetivo:** leer un relato con irregulares (+ algún regular).

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-3/reading-irregular.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Last weekend"></audio>

> Last weekend I had a wonderful time. On Saturday morning I went to the market and bought some fresh fruit and vegetables. Then I saw my old friend Tom at the cafe. We had coffee together and talked for hours. In the afternoon I went home and made a delicious cake for my family. My sister wrote a letter to our grandmother and I helped her with it. In the evening we saw a film on TV. It was very good!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Where did they go on Saturday morning? → a) The market · b) London · c) School  
2. Who did they see at the cafe? → a) Tom · b) Sarah · c) Maria  
3. What did they make in the afternoon? → a) A cake · b) Pasta only · c) Nothing  
4. What did the sister write? → a) A letter · b) A book · c) An email only  
5. What did they see in the evening? → a) A film · b) A match · c) A museum

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) The market**  
2. **a) Tom**  
3. **a) A cake**  
4. **a) A letter**  
5. **a) A film**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Irregular past verbs include ____. → a) had, went, bought, saw, made, wrote · b) only walked · c) only am  
7. *Talked* and *helped* are ____. → a) regular · b) irregular · c) future  
8. They bought ____. → a) fruit and vegetables · b) a car · c) tickets only  
9. The cake was for ____. → a) the family · b) Tom only · c) school  
10. The main topic is ____. → a) a wonderful weekend · b) introductions · c) weather

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) had, went, bought, saw, made, wrote**  
7. **a) regular**  
8. **a) fruit and vegetables**  
9. **a) the family**  
10. **a) a wonderful weekend**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Went* is past of ____. → a) go · b) see · c) buy  
12. *Saw* is past of ____. → a) see · b) go · c) write  
13. *Wrote* is past of ____. → a) write · b) make · c) have  
14. *Bought* is past of ____. → a) buy · b) bring · c) bringed  
15. Mix regular + irregular is ____. → a) natural in stories · b) wrong · c) only for A1

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) go**  
12. **a) see**  
13. **a) write**  
14. **a) buy**  
15. **a) natural in stories**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: A trip to London

**Objetivo:** escuchar un relato de viaje con irregulares.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-3/listening-irregular.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: A trip to London"></audio>

> Hi! I want to tell you about my trip last month. I went to London with my family. We had a wonderful time! On the first day we went to the British Museum. I saw many interesting things there. Then we bought some souvenirs from a shop near the museum. My sister wrote postcards to her friends. In the evening we made dinner at our hotel. It was delicious! I really enjoyed the trip.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Where did they go? → a) London · b) Madrid · c) Barcelona  
2. Who did they travel with? → a) Family · b) Alone · c) Only Tom  
3. Where did they go on the first day? → a) The British Museum · b) The beach · c) School  
4. What did they buy? → a) Souvenirs · b) A car · c) Medicine  
5. What did they make in the evening? → a) Dinner · b) A cake only · c) Postcards

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) London**  
2. **a) Family**  
3. **a) The British Museum**  
4. **a) Souvenirs**  
5. **a) Dinner**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The sister wrote ____. → a) postcards · b) a novel · c) homework  
7. Dinner was at ____. → a) the hotel · b) the museum · c) school  
8. They had a ____ time. → a) wonderful · b) terrible · c) boring  
9. *Enjoyed* is ____. → a) regular past · b) irregular · c) present  
10. The listening practices ____. → a) irregular past verbs · b) only *to be* · c) conditionals

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) postcards**  
7. **a) the hotel**  
8. **a) wonderful**  
9. **a) regular past**  
10. **a) irregular past verbs**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Went / saw / bought / wrote / made / had* are ____. → a) irregular past · b) present · c) future  
12. *Last month* marks ____. → a) past · b) future · c) never  
13. After *Did* use ____. → a) base · b) past form · c) *-ing*  
14. *Made dinner* = ____. → a) cooked dinner · b) bought dinner only · c) saw dinner  
15. Trip story order starts with ____. → a) went to London · b) made dinner · c) wrote postcards

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) irregular past**  
12. **a) past**  
13. **a) base**  
14. **a) cooked dinner**  
15. **a) went to London**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My last trip

**Objetivo:** escribir un relato con irregulares.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. Yesterday I ____ to the park. → *went / go / goed*  
2. I ____ many people there. → *saw / see / seen*  
3. We ____ a great time. → *had / have / haved*  
4. She ____ a letter. → *wrote / write / writed*  
5. They ____ souvenirs. → *bought / buy / buyed*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **went** · 2. **saw** · 3. **had** · 4. **wrote** · 5. **bought**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. I / went / to London / last month.  
7. She / bought / a new dress / yesterday.  
8. We / made / dinner / at the hotel.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I went to London last month.  
7. She bought a new dress yesterday.  
8. We made dinner at the hotel.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Fui al mercado ayer.  
10. Vi a un amigo en el café.  
11. Compré fruta fresca.  
12. Hice un pastel para mi familia.  
13. Negativa: no fui a Londres.  
14. Pregunta: ¿Compraste souvenirs?  
15. Párrafo (6–8 frases) de un viaje/fin de semana con 5 irregulares.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I went to the market yesterday.  
10. I saw a friend at the cafe.  
11. I bought fresh fruit.  
12. I made a cake for my family.  
13. I didn't go to London.  
14. Did you buy souvenirs?  
15. Last weekend I went to the market. I bought fruit. Then I saw my friend. We had coffee. In the evening I made dinner and we saw a film. I had a wonderful time!

</details>
