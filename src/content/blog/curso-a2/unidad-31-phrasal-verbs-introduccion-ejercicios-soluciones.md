## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** phrasal verbs básicos: get up, wake up, look for, turn on/off, sit down, stand up.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ at 7 every day. *(dejar de dormir)* → *wake up / get up / look for*
2. Then I ____ and have a shower. *(salir de la cama)* → *get up / wake up / turn on*
3. I ____ my keys every morning. → *look for / look / look at*
4. ____ the light, please. *(encender)* → *Turn on / Turn off / Sit down*
5. ____ the TV before you go to bed. *(apagar)* → *Turn off / Turn on / Stand up*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **wake up** · 2. **get up** · 3. **look for** · 4. **Turn on** · 5. **Turn off**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. A phrasal verb is ____. → a) verb + particle · b) only one word · c) noun + adjective
7. *Wake up* vs *get up*: wake up = ____. → a) stop sleeping · b) leave the house · c) eat breakfast
8. *Look for* needs ____. → a) for · b) at · c) no particle
9. *Turn on* and *turn off* are ____. → a) opposites · b) the same · c) only past tense
10. Unit 31 introduces ____. → a) basic phrasal verbs · b) separable rules only · c) articles

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) verb + particle** · 7. **a) stop sleeping** · 8. **a) for** · 9. **a) opposites** · 10. **a) basic phrasal verbs**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I look my keys every morning.*
12. *Turn the light.* *(encender)*
13. *I get up of bed at 7.*
14. *Please sit.* *(en clase)*
15. *I wake up the bed.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I **look for** my keys every morning.
12. **Turn on** the light.
13. I **get up** at 7.
14. Please **sit down**.
15. I **wake up** at 7. / I **get up** at 7.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** wake up, get up, look for, turn on/off, sit down, stand up, light, keys, phone.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *wake up* ≈ ____ → *stop sleeping · leave home · turn off*
2. *get up* ≈ ____ → *leave the bed · look for keys · sit down*
3. *look for* ≈ ____ → *search · encender · apagar*
4. *turn on* ≈ ____ → *switch on · stand up · wake up*
5. *sit down* ≈ ____ → *take a seat · get up · look for*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **stop sleeping** · 2. **leave the bed** · 3. **search** · 4. **switch on** · 5. **take a seat**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. Every morning I ____ up at seven. → *wake / get / look*
7. Then I ____ up and turn on the light. → *get / wake / sit*
8. I look ____ my keys and my phone. → *for / at / on*
9. Please ____ down — the lesson is starting. → *sit / stand / turn*
10. After breakfast I stand ____ and turn off the lights. → *up / down / on*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **wake** · 7. **get** · 8. **for** · 9. **sit** · 10. **up**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Look for* is ____. → a) inseparable in U31 · b) always separable · c) only past
12. *Turn off the lights* means ____. → a) apagar · b) encender · c) buscar
13. *Stand up* is the opposite of ____. → a) sit down · b) wake up · c) look for
14. Phrasal verbs in U31 are about ____. → a) routines & instructions · b) only shopping · c) only past tense
15. Unit 31 prepares you for ____. → a) separable phrasals (U32) · b) articles only · c) Zero Conditional

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) inseparable in U31** · 12. **a) apagar** · 13. **a) sit down** · 14. **a) routines & instructions** · 15. **a) separable phrasals (U32)**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Morning routine

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-31/reading-phrasal.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Morning routine"></audio>

> Every morning I wake up at seven. Then I get up and turn on the light. I look for my keys and my phone. I sit down to have breakfast. After that I stand up, turn off the lights and leave home.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Wake up time? → a) Seven · b) Eight · c) Before school only
2. After getting up? → a) Turn on the light · b) Turn off the TV · c) Look for the file
3. What does she look for? → a) Keys and phone · b) Bag only · c) Computers
4. Before leaving? → a) Turn off the lights · b) Turn on computers · c) Sit in class
5. Breakfast? → a) Sit down to eat · b) Stand up only · c) Wake up in bed

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Seven** · 2. **a) Turn on the light** · 3. **a) Keys and phone** · 4. **a) Turn off the lights** · 5. **a) Sit down to eat**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The text describes ____. → a) a morning routine · b) a shop dialogue · c) subject questions
7. *Get up* comes after ____. → a) wake up · b) turn off · c) look for file
8. *Turn on* is used with ____. → a) the light · b) the radio in class · c) worksheets
9. Order: stand up, then ____. → a) turn off lights & leave · b) wake up · c) sit for breakfast first
10. Matches unit 31 phrasals: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U35

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) a morning routine** · 7. **a) wake up** · 8. **a) the light** · 9. **a) turn off lights & leave** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Wake up* = ____. → a) stop sleeping · b) leave home · c) search
12. *Look for* always includes ____. → a) for · b) at · c) off
13. Instructions often use ____. → a) Turn on / Sit down · b) Who called you? · c) some / any
14. Learn each phrasal as ____. → a) one block · b) separate words only · c) only in past
15. Reading helps you ____. → a) spot phrasals in context · b) skip particles · c) avoid English

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) stop sleeping** · 12. **a) for** · 13. **a) Turn on / Sit down** · 14. **a) one block** · 15. **a) spot phrasals in context**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: In class and at home

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-31/listening-phrasal.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: In class and at home"></audio>

> Good morning! Please sit down. Don't stand up yet. First, turn on your computers. If you look for the file on your desk, you will find it. When you finish, turn off the computers. At home I wake up early, get up, turn on the radio and look for my bag before school.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. First instruction in class? → a) Sit down · b) Stand up · c) Leave home
2. Computers? → a) Turn on first · b) Never use · c) Turn on radio
3. File? → a) Look for it on the desk · b) Buy at shop · c) Give back
4. When finish in class? → a) Turn off computers · b) Wake up · c) Eat breakfast
5. At home before school? → a) Wake up, get up, radio, look for bag · b) Only sit down · c) Visit cousin

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Sit down** · 2. **a) Turn on first** · 3. **a) Look for it on the desk** · 4. **a) Turn off computers** · 5. **a) Wake up, get up, radio, look for bag**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Listening mixes ____. → a) class instructions + home routine · b) only shopping · c) only questions
7. *Don't stand up yet* means ____. → a) stay seated for now · b) leave immediately · c) turn off lights
8. *Look for the file* is ____. → a) search on desk · b) put on jacket · c) subject question
9. Home routine includes ____. → a) turn on the radio · b) give worksheets back · c) How many tomatoes
10. Unit 31 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Module 6

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) class instructions + home routine** · 7. **a) stay seated for now** · 8. **a) search on desk** · 9. **a) turn on the radio** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Please sit down* is ____. → a) an instruction · b) a subject question · c) a quantity
12. *Turn on your computers* uses ____. → a) turn on · b) look for · c) give back
13. Shadow the audio and ____. → a) mark each phrasal · b) skip particles · c) translate titles only
14. Class + home texts show phrasals are ____. → a) everyday · b) rare · c) only C1
15. U31 opens ____. → a) Module 4 phrasals · b) Module 1 only · c) articles review

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) an instruction** · 12. **a) turn on** · 13. **a) mark each phrasal** · 14. **a) everyday** · 15. **a) Module 4 phrasals**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ up at 7 every day. → *wake / get / look*
2. Then I get up and turn ____ the light. → *on / off / for*
3. I look ____ my keys every morning. → *for / at / on*
4. Please ____ down. → *sit / stand / turn*
5. ____ off the lights before you leave. → *Turn / Look / Wake*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **wake** · 2. **on** · 3. **for** · 4. **sit** · 5. **Turn**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. Please / sit / down.
7. Turn / on / the / light, / please.
8. I / look / for / my / keys / every / morning.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. Please sit down.
7. Turn on the light, please.
8. I look for my keys every morning.

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Una frase con *wake up*.
10. Una frase con *get up* y *turn on*.
11. Una frase con *look for*.
12. Una instrucción con *sit down* o *stand up*.
13. Una instrucción con *turn off*.
14. Explica *wake up* vs *get up* con tu horario.
15. Párrafo (8–10 frases) sobre tu mañana con phrasals.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. I wake up at seven every morning.
10. I get up and turn on the light.
11. I look for my keys every morning.
12. Please sit down.
13. Turn off the lights before you leave.
14. I wake up at 7, but I get up at 7:15.
15. Every morning I wake up at seven. Then I get up and turn on the light. I look for my keys and my phone. I sit down to have breakfast. After that I stand up, turn off the lights and leave home.

</details>
