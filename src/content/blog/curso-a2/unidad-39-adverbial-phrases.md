  - question: ¿Qué es una adverbial phrase?
    answer: >-
      Es un grupo de palabras que funciona como un adverbio: dice cuándo, dónde

  - question: ¿Qué va primero, lugar o tiempo?
    answer: >-
      En inglés suele ir place before time: I study at home in the evening (no

  - question: ¿Cuál es la diferencia entre in the morning y at the moment?

      In the morning = por las mañanas / esta mañana (rutina o momento del día).

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 39 (A2)

- Usar frases de **tiempo** (*in the morning*, *at the moment*…).
- Usar frases de **lugar** (*at home*, *in the park*…).
- Usar frases de **frecuencia** (*once a week*, *every day*…).
- Ordenar la frase: **place before time**.
- Describir rutinas con más precisión.

## 1. Idea clave: un “paquete” que modifica el verbo

Una **adverbial phrase** no es una sola palabra (*often*), sino un **grupo**:

> I study **in the morning**.  
> She works **at home**.  
> We meet **once a week**.

| Tipo | Pregunta | Ejemplos |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Time** | When? | in the morning, at night, at the moment, on Monday |
| **Place** | Where? | at home, in the park, at school, in London |
| **Frequency** | How often? | every day, once a week, twice a month, at weekends |

## 2. Frases de tiempo

| Frase | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **in the morning / afternoon / evening** | parte del día | I run **in the morning**. |
| **at night** | por la noche | She reads **at night**. |
| **at the moment** | ahora mismo | I'm busy **at the moment**. |
| **on Monday / at the weekend** | día / fin de semana | We travel **at the weekend**. |
| **in July / in 2026** | mes / año | They move **in July**. |

> He's not here **at the moment**.  
> I drink coffee **in the morning**.

## 3. Frases de lugar

| Frase | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| **at home / at school / at work** | I stay **at home** on Sundays. |
| **in the park / in the kitchen** | The kids play **in the park**. |
| **in London / in Spain** | She lives **in Spain**. |
| **at the station / at the airport** | Meet me **at the station**. |

Repaso útil: *at* (puntos), *in* (espacios / ciudades), *on* (superficies / días) — ver también preposiciones de tiempo en el curso.

## 4. Frases de frecuencia

| Frase | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **every day / every week** | cada… | I practise **every day**. |
| **once a week** | una vez por semana | He calls **once a week**. |
| **twice a month** | dos veces al mes | We visit Grandma **twice a month**. |
| **at weekends** | los fines de semana | They travel **at weekends**. |
| **from time to time** | de vez en cuando | I cook **from time to time**. |

> I go to the gym **once a week**.  
> She studies English **every day**.

## 5. Orden de palabras: place before time

Cuando hay **lugar** y **tiempo**, el inglés suele poner **primero el lugar**:

| ✅ Natural | ❌ Menos natural |
| :--- | :--- |
| I study **at home** **in the evening**. | I study in the evening at home. |
| She works **in Madrid** **on Mondays**. | She works on Mondays in Madrid. |

Patrón A2:

> Subject + verb (+ object) + **place** + **time**  
> We play football **in the park** **on Saturdays**.

## 6. Mapa rápido

| Quieres decir… | Usa… | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cuándo (parte del día) | in the morning / at night | I run **in the morning**. |
| Ahora mismo | at the moment | I'm working **at the moment**. |
| Dónde | at home / in the park | We meet **in the park**. |
| Frecuencia | once a week / every day | I train **once a week**. |
| Lugar + tiempo | place → time | She studies **at school** **in the afternoon**. |

## 7. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura — My week

Leo wakes up early **in the morning**. He has breakfast **at home** and then goes to class. **At the moment**, he is preparing for an English exam, so he reviews vocabulary **every day**. He meets his friends **in the park** **once a week**. **At weekends**, he stays **at home** **in the evening** and watches films.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-39/reading-week.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: My week"></audio>

### Diálogo — Making plans

**Ana:** Are you free **at the weekend**?  
**Leo:** **In the morning**, yes. **In the afternoon**, I work.  
**Ana:** OK. Let's meet **in the café** **on Saturday morning**.  
**Leo:** Perfect. I go there **once a week** anyway.  
**Ana:** Great. I'm busy **at the moment**, but I'll message you later **at night**.

## 8. Errores frecuentes

1. ~~I study in the evening at home.~~ → **I study at home in the evening.** *(place before time)*  
2. ~~in the night~~ → **at night**  
3. ~~in the moment~~ → **at the moment**  
4. ~~one time a week~~ → **once a week**  
5. ~~in home~~ → **at home**  
6. ~~every days~~ → **every day**
