## Lección 1 — Gramática: repaso mixto

**Objetivo:** Past Simple, comparativos/superlativos, adverbios y preposiciones.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. Yesterday I ____ to the museum. → *went / go / goed*
2. What ____ you do last weekend? → *did / do / does*
3. My brother is ____ than me. → *taller / tallest / more tall*
4. She is ____ tallest in our family. → *the / a / more*
5. She speaks English ____. → *fluently / fluent / fluents*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **went** · 2. **did** · 3. **taller** · 4. **the** · 5. **fluently**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. I get up ____ 7 o'clock ____ the morning. → *at / in · on / at · in / on*
7. I work ____ Monday. → *on / at / in*
8. I went ____ the entrance and walked ____ the rooms. → *into / through · across / out of · at / on*
9. I walked ____ the bridge. → *across / into / out of*
10. It was ____ most interesting film. → *the / a / more*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **at** · **in** · 7. **on** · 8. **into** · **through** · 9. **across** · 10. **the**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I walk yesterday.*
12. *What did you went?*
13. *She is more tall than me.*
14. *He speaks English fluent.*
15. *I wake up on 7 o'clock.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I **walked** yesterday.
12. What did you **go**? / What did you **do**?
13. She is **taller** than me.
14. He speaks English **fluently**.
15. I wake up **at** 7 o'clock.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: repaso

**Objetivo:** formas clave del Módulo 1.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja

1. go →
2. see →
3. good (comparative) →
4. good (superlative) →
5. careful →

a) carefully · b) better · c) went · d) the best · e) saw

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **c** · 2 → **e** · 3 → **b** · 4 → **d** · 5 → **a**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. through = por el ____ → *interior / mes / adjetivo*
7. across = de un ____ al otro → *lado / verbo / año*
8. at noon = ____ → *mediodía / agosto / fluently*
9. hometown = ____ → *ciudad natal / puente / adverbio*
10. fastest = el más ____ → *rápido / interesante / carefully*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **interior** · 7. **lado** · 8. **mediodía** · 9. **ciudad natal** · 10. **rápido**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Ever seen* often with ____. → a) superlatives · b) only *at* · c) only *did you*
12. *Practise every day* helps you speak ____. → a) fluently · b) the tallest · c) into
13. *Meeting at noon* uses ____. → a) *at* · b) *on* · c) *into*
14. *Walked across the bridge* uses ____. → a) *across* · b) *through* only · c) *the most*
15. Module 1 mixes past + comparisons + how + where/when = ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only B1

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) superlatives** · 12. **a) fluently** · 13. **a) *at*** · 14. **a) *across*** · 15. **a) yes**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Anna

**Objetivo:** leer un texto que integra el Módulo 1.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-10/reading-review.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Anna"></audio>

> My name is Anna and I have a busy life. I get up at 7 o'clock in the morning on weekdays. Last weekend I went to the museum. I went into the main entrance and walked through several rooms. My sister is the tallest in our family and she speaks English very fluently. She saw a beautiful film last night and said it was the most interesting film she has ever seen. On Saturday morning I walked across the bridge to get to the other side of the river. I really enjoyed the weekend!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. When does Anna get up on weekdays? → a) At 7 in the morning · b) At noon · c) In August
2. Where did she go last weekend? → a) The museum · b) London only · c) Work
3. Who is the tallest? → a) Her sister · b) Anna · c) Nobody
4. What film opinion? → a) The most interesting she has ever seen · b) The worst · c) No film
5. What did she do on Saturday morning? → a) Walked across the bridge · b) Slept only · c) Worked

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) At 7 in the morning** · 2. **a) The museum** · 3. **a) Her sister** · 4. **a) The most interesting she has ever seen** · 5. **a) Walked across the bridge**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Movement: ____. → a) into / through / across · b) only *at* · c) only *-ly*
7. Time: ____. → a) at / in / on · b) only *into* · c) only *the most*
8. Superlatives: ____. → a) the tallest / the most interesting · b) only taller · c) only well
9. Adverb: ____. → a) fluently · b) biggest · c) across
10. Past irregulars: ____. → a) went / saw · b) walked only · c) am only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) into / through / across** · 7. **a) at / in / on** · 8. **a) the tallest / the most interesting** · 9. **a) fluently** · 10. **a) went / saw**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *On weekdays* + *at 7* + *in the morning* shows ____. → a) mixed time prepositions · b) only movement · c) only adjectives
12. *Went into* / *walked through* = ____. → a) movement · b) comparative · c) *will*
13. *The tallest in our family* uses ____. → a) *in* + group place · b) *of* only wrong here · c) *at*
14. *Speaks… fluently* = manner adverb · a) true · b) false · c) past only
15. Integrated Module 1 text = ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Unit 1

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) mixed time prepositions** · 12. **a) movement** · 13. **a) *in* + group place** · 14. **a) true** · 15. **a) yes**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: My last week

**Objetivo:** escuchar un relato de repaso.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-10/listening-review.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: My last week"></audio>

> Hi! I want to tell you about my last week. On Monday I had a meeting at noon. On Tuesday I went to the cinema and saw a film. It was the most interesting film I have ever seen. On Wednesday I walked through the park and went into a cafe. I speak English fluently now because I practise every day. My brother is taller than me but I am the fastest runner in the family!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Monday: ____. → a) Meeting at noon · b) Cinema · c) Bridge only
2. Tuesday: ____. → a) Cinema / saw a film · b) Only sleep · c) Work forever
3. Film opinion: ____. → a) Most interesting ever seen · b) Worst · c) No opinion
4. Wednesday: ____. → a) Through the park / into a cafe · b) Only *at* · c) Nothing
5. Who is faster? → a) The speaker · b) The brother · c) Nobody

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Meeting at noon** · 2. **a) Cinema / saw a film** · 3. **a) Most interesting ever seen** · 4. **a) Through the park / into a cafe** · 5. **a) The speaker**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Brother comparison: ____. → a) taller than me · b) the tallest only · c) fluently
7. Speaker superlative: ____. → a) the fastest runner · b) the worst · c) across
8. Why fluent? → a) Practise every day · b) Never study · c) Only *into*
9. Past forms: ____. → a) had / went / saw / walked · b) only *am* · c) only *will*
10. Review listening mixes ____. → a) time + past + comparisons + adverb + movement · b) only Unit 1 · c) only B2

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) taller than me** · 7. **a) the fastest runner** · 8. **a) Practise every day** · 9. **a) had / went / saw / walked** · 10. **a) time + past + comparisons + adverb + movement**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *On Monday… at noon* = ____. → a) *on* + *at* · b) *in* + *into* · c) only *-ly*
12. *The most interesting* = ____. → a) long-adj superlative · b) short *-er* · c) Wh-question
13. *Walked through / went into* = ____. → a) movement · b) *the best* · c) *as… as*
14. *Taller than* vs *the fastest* = ____. → a) comparative vs superlative · b) same · c) only past
15. *Fluently* describes ____. → a) how they speak · b) where · c) when only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) *on* + *at*** · 12. **a) long-adj superlative** · 13. **a) movement** · 14. **a) comparative vs superlative** · 15. **a) how they speak**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My weekend

**Objetivo:** escribir un párrafo que mezcle el Módulo 1.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. Last weekend I ____ to the park. → *went / go / goed*
2. I walked ____ the park. → *through / into / at*
3. My sister is ____ than me. → *taller / tallest / more tall*
4. She speaks ____. → *fluently / fluent / fluents*
5. I got up ____ 8 o'clock ____ Saturday. → *at / on · on / at · in / in*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **went** · 2. **through** · 3. **taller** · 4. **fluently** · 5. **at** · **on**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. What / did / you / do / last weekend?
7. It / was / the / most / interesting / film.
8. I / went / into / a cafe.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. What did you do last weekend?
7. It was the most interesting film.
8. I went into a cafe.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Past irregular: fuiste y viste algo.
10. Wh-question + respuesta.
11. Comparativo + superlativo.
12. Un adverbio de modo.
13. *at/on/in* en dos frases.
14. *into/through/across* en dos frases.
15. Párrafo (8–10 frases) tipo Anna / last week.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I went to the cinema and saw a film.
10. What did you do last weekend? I went to the museum.
11. My brother is taller than me. I am the fastest runner in the family.
12. I speak English fluently.
13. I get up at 7 o'clock. I work on Monday. I was born in 1995.
14. I went into a cafe. I walked through the park. I walked across the bridge.
15. Last weekend I went to the museum. I went into the entrance and walked through the rooms. On Saturday I walked across the bridge. My sister is the tallest in our family and she speaks English fluently. I really enjoyed the weekend!

</details>
