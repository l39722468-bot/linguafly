  - question: ¿Cuándo uso -er y cuándo more?
    answer: >-
      Adjetivos cortos (1 sílaba, a veces 2): -er (taller, bigger, hotter).
      Adjetivos largos: more + adjetivo (more interesting, more expensive).
      Siempre puedes cerrar con than.
  - question: ¿Cuál es el comparativo de good y bad?
    answer: >-
      good → better (no *gooder*). bad → worse (no *badder*). Ejemplos: a better
      camera; worse than yesterday.
  - question: ¿Qué significa as… as?
    answer: >-
      Igualdad: She is as tall as her sister = tan alta como su hermana. No
      compares “más”, compares “igual de”.
  - question: ¿Cómo se escribe bigger y happier?
    answer: >-
      big → bigger (consonante doble). happy → happier (y → i + er). nice →
      nicer (solo -r si ya termina en -e).

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 5 (A2)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Formar comparativos cortos con ***-er*** (*taller, bigger, hotter*).
- Formar comparativos largos con ***more + adjective*** (*more interesting*).
- Usar ***than*** para completar la comparación.
- Usar irregulares ***better*** / ***worse***.
- Expresar igualdad con ***as… as*** (introducción).

Los **superlativos** (*the tallest, the most beautiful*) llegan en la [Unidad 6](/curso-a2/unit-6).

## 1. Dos caminos: *-er* o *more*

| Tipo de adjetivo | Comparativo | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Corto** (tall, big, hot, fast, cheap…) | adj + **-er** | taller, bigger, hotter |
| **Largo** (interesting, expensive, beautiful…) | **more** + adj | more interesting |
| Cierre | **than** | taller **than** me |

![Comparatives -er and more than A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-5/a2-unit5-comparatives.png)

> My brother is **taller than** me.  
> This book is **more interesting than** that one.  
> Summer is **hotter than** winter.  
> My car is **more expensive than** yours.

## 2. Adjetivos cortos frecuentes

| Adjetivo | Comparativo | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| tall | **taller** | Maria is taller than me. |
| short | **shorter** | Sofia is shorter than both of us. |
| big | **bigger** | Our house is bigger than grandmother's. |
| long | **longer** | She has longer hair than I do. |
| hot | **hotter** | Summer is hotter than winter. |
| cold | **colder** | Winter is colder than summer. |
| fast | **faster** | She is faster than us. / The new phone is faster. |
| light | **lighter** | The old phone is lighter than the new one. |
| new | **newer** | My phone is newer than yours. |
| quiet | **quieter** | The village is quieter than the city. |
| cheap | **cheaper** | This bag is cheaper than that one. |

## 4. Irregulares: *good → better*, *bad → worse*

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| bad | **worse** | *badder* / *more bad* |

## 5. Ortografía y *as… as* (intro)

![Spelling and as as A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-5/a2-unit5-spelling-asas.png)

### Ortografía de *-er*

| Regla | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| + **-er** | tall → taller |
| termina en **-e** → + **-r** | nice → nicer |
| consonante + **y** → **-ier** | happy → happier |
| CVC corto → doble consonante | big → **bigger**, hot → **hotter** |

### *as… as* = igualdad

| Forma | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **as** + adj + **as** | igual de… que | She is **as tall as** her sister. |

No digas *\*as taller as*. Con igualdad el adjetivo va en forma base.

> She is as tall as her sister.

## 9. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| more taller | **taller** | corto → *-er*, no *more* |
| interestinger | **more interesting** | largo → *more* |
| gooder | **better** | irregular |
| badder | **worse** | irregular |
| bigger that | bigger **than** | *than*, no *that* |
| as taller as | **as tall as** | igualdad → adjetivo base |
| more big | **bigger** | *big* es corto |

## 10. Mini prácticas orales

1. Compara dos personas (*taller / younger / older*).  
2. Compara dos sitios (*bigger / more beautiful / quieter*).  
3. Compara dos objetos (*more expensive / better / lighter*).  
4. Di una igualdad: *as… as*.

> My brother is taller than me.  
> This phone is more expensive than that one.  
> She is as tall as her sister.

## 11. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — *-er* o *more*

1. My brother is \_\_\_\_ than me. (tall)  
2. This book is \_\_\_\_ than that one. (interesting)  
3. Summer is \_\_\_\_ than winter. (hot)  
4. My car is \_\_\_\_ than yours. (expensive)  
5. She runs \_\_\_\_ than me. (fast)  
6. This exercise is \_\_\_\_ than the last. (difficult)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **taller** · 2. **more interesting** · 3. **hotter** · 4. **more expensive** · 5. **faster** · 6. **more difficult**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — *better / worse*

1. This film is \_\_\_\_ than the other. (good)  
2. Today the weather is \_\_\_\_ than yesterday. (bad)  
3. The new phone has a \_\_\_\_ camera. (good)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **better** · 2. **worse** · 3. **better**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Completa con *than*

1. Maria is taller \_\_\_\_ me.  
2. The garden is more beautiful \_\_\_\_ ours.  
3. The new phone is faster \_\_\_\_ my old phone.  
4. Books are better \_\_\_\_ films for me.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1–4. **than**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Empareja

1. tall  
2. big  
3. interesting  
4. good  
5. bad  
6. happy  

a) bigger · b) better · c) taller · d) happier · e) worse · f) more interesting

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **c** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **f** · 4 → **b** · 5 → **e** · 6 → **d**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Completa el texto de Laura

Maria is \_\_\_\_ than me. Sofia is \_\_\_\_ than both of us but \_\_\_\_ than us when we run. Our house is \_\_\_\_ than grandmother's house. The garden is \_\_\_\_ beautiful than ours. The city is \_\_\_\_ interesting than the village. The village is \_\_\_\_ than the city.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**taller** · **shorter** · **faster** · **bigger** · **more** · **more** · **quieter**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa el listening (phones)

The new phone is \_\_\_\_ than the old one. The old one is \_\_\_\_ than the new one. The new phone has a \_\_\_\_ camera. The battery is \_\_\_\_ powerful than before. The new phone is \_\_\_\_ expensive but \_\_\_\_ useful. It is \_\_\_\_ than my old phone.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**bigger** · **lighter** · **better** · **more** · **more** · **more** · **faster**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Corrige

1. *She is more taller than me.*  
2. *This book is interestinger.*  
3. *This film is gooder than that one.*  
4. *My car is more expensive that yours.*  
5. *She is as taller as her sister.*  
6. *This box is more big than that.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. She is **taller** than me.  
2. This book is **more interesting**.  
3. This film is **better** than that one.  
4. My car is more expensive **than** yours.  
5. She is **as tall as** her sister.  
6. This box is **bigger** than that.
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — *as… as*

1. She is \_\_\_\_ tall \_\_\_\_ her sister.  
2. This bag is not \_\_\_\_ cheap \_\_\_\_ that one.  
3. Incorrecto: as more expensive as → Correcto: ____

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **as** · **as**  
2. **as** · **as**  
3. **as expensive as** / o usa *more expensive than*
</details>

7. Ella es tan alta como su hermana.

### Ejercicio 10 — Escribe tus comparaciones

Escribe 8–12 frases. Incluye:

- 3 × *-er* + *than*  
- 2 × *more… than*  
- 1 × *better* o *worse*

## 12. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Repasa *-er* vs *more* y *better/worse*.  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–7.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha a Laura y los móviles; marca cada comparativo.  
4. **5–8 min** — Escribe y graba 8 comparaciones personales.

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *-er* con adjetivos cortos  
- [ ] Uso *more* con adjetivos largos  
- [ ] Cierro con *than*  
- [ ] Recuerdo *better / worse*  
- [ ] Sé formar *as… as* para igualdad

---

## 13. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Pregunta rápida: ¿1 sílaba? → prueba *-er*. ¿3+ sílabas? → *more*.  
2. Di en voz alta pares: *big–bigger, hot–hotter, happy–happier*.  
3. Compara tu ciudad con otra (como en los modelos del curso).  
4. Practica en la [Unidad 5 del curso A2](/curso-a2/unit-5).

La **Unidad 6** entra en **superlativos**: *the tallest*, *the most beautiful*, *in / of*.
