## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** by/take/get to; It's…/going to/will; watch/listen/prefer; shopping; should; wear/too/enough; look like/be like

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. We go ___ train. → *by / with / in*

2. It's ___ to rain. → *going / will going / goes*

3. I'm ___ for sunscreen. → *looking / look / looked*

4. I have ___ headache. → *a / the / —*

5. She ___ like her mother. → *looks / is look / looking*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **by** · 2. **going** · 3. **looking** · 4. **a** · 5. **looks**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *By bus* (no *with bus*): ____. → a) correcto · b) with bus · c) in bus only

7. *Prefer comedies to dramas*: ____. → a) correcto · b) prefer than · c) like watch

8. *You should rest*: ____. → a) consejo salud · b) precio · c) only Past

9. *I've known her for years*: ____. → a) Present Perfect · b) Past only · c) will

10. Unit 50 reviews ____. → a) Module 5 U41–U49 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U51

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) correcto** · 7. **a) correcto** · 8. **a) consejo salud** · 9. **a) Present Perfect** · 10. **a) Module 5 U41–U49**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I go with train.*

12. *listen music*

13. *How many is it?*

14. *I have headache.*

15. *I know her for years.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I go **by** train.
12. **listen to** music.
13. How **much** is it?
14. I have **a** headache.
15. I've **known** her for years.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** repaso integrado U41–U49: viaje, tiempo, ocio, compras, salud, ropa, personas

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. Travel → go ___ bus → *by / with / in*

2. Weather → It's ___ → *sunny / sun / a sun*

3. Shopping → How ___ is it? → *much / many / more*

4. Health → You ___ rest → *should / should to / shoulds*

5. Clothes → ___ on a hat → *Put / Wear / Take*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **by** · 2. **sunny** · 3. **much** · 4. **should** · 5. **Put**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. We ___ sightseeing in Lisbon. → *went / go / will going*

7. I ___ comedies to dramas. → *prefer / prefer than / likes*

8. Scarf cost ___. → *€12 / sunny / platform*

9. Hotel ___ was friendly. → *neighbour / checkout / forecast*

10. Known partner for ten ___. → *years / year / sunny*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **went** · 7. **prefer** · 8. **€12** · 9. **neighbour** · 10. **years**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Checked in* at hotel = ____. → a) registrarse · b) devolución · c) weather only

12. *Sightseeing* = ____. → a) ver lugares turísticos · b) comprar · c) headache

13. *Put on a hat* = ____. → a) ropa · b) precio · c) look like

14. *Get on well* = ____. → a) personas · b) solo tiempo · c) only will

15. Unit 50 next is ____. → a) Making Suggestions U51 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U51

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) registrarse** · 12. **a) ver lugares turísticos** · 13. **a) ropa** · 14. **a) personas** · 15. **a) Making Suggestions U51**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: A weekend in Lisbon

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-50/reading-review.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: A weekend in Lisbon"></audio>

> Last Friday we **took the train** to Lisbon and **checked in** at a small hotel. On Saturday it was **sunny**, so we went **sightseeing**. In the evening we **watched** a film — I **prefer comedies** to dramas. On Sunday I bought a scarf: "**How much is it?**" — €12. Later I had a **headache**, so my friend said: "You **should** rest." Our **neighbour** in the hotel was **friendly**. I've **known** my travel partner **for** ten years — we always **get on** well.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Took the ___ to Lisbon. → a) train · b) receipt · c) pharmacy

2. Saturday was ___. → a) sunny · b) refund · c) shy

3. Watched a ___ in the evening. → a) film · b) fever · c) gate

4. Scarf cost ___. → a) €12 · b) €59 · c) free only

5. Had a ___ later. → a) headache · b) checkout · c) platform

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) train** · 2. **a) sunny** · 3. **a) film** · 4. **a) €12** · 5. **a) headache**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Prefer ___ to dramas. → a) comedies · b) storms · c) receipts

7. Neighbour was ___. → a) friendly · b) foggy · c) cheaper

8. Known partner for ten ___. → a) years · b) weeks only · c) degrees

9. *Checked in* = ____. → a) hotel · b) only shop · c) only Who

10. Matches Module 5 review: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) comedies** · 7. **a) friendly** · 8. **a) years** · 9. **a) hotel** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Took the train* = ____. → a) viaje · b) weather · c) check in only

12. *How much is it?* in text = ____. → a) compras · b) solo salud · c) only Who

13. *You should rest* = ____. → a) salud · b) ocio · c) cheaper

14. Lisbon text mixes ____. → a) M5 topics · b) only did · c) only phrasals

15. Review writing uses ____. → a) integrated M5 structures · b) only articles · c) only should

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) viaje** · 12. **a) compras** · 13. **a) salud** · 14. **a) M5 topics** · 15. **a) integrated M5 structures**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Planning the day

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-50/listening-review.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Planning the day"></audio>

> **Mia:** What's the weather **like** today?
> **Omar:** It's **going to be** hot. **Put on** a hat.
> **Mia:** Shall we go **by bus** to the museum?
> **Omar:** Yes. Then I'd like to **watch** a documentary.
> **Mia:** OK — but first **I'm looking for** sunscreen at the pharmacy.
> **Omar:** Good idea. If you feel sick, you **should** drink water.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Weather today: going to be ___. → a) hot · b) refund · c) shy

2. Go to museum ___ bus. → a) by · b) with · c) in

3. Omar wants to ___ a documentary. → a) watch · b) listen · c) exchange

4. Mia is looking for ___ at pharmacy. → a) sunscreen · b) receipt · c) drama

5. If feel sick → ___ water. → a) drink · b) take off · c) look like

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) hot** · 2. **a) by** · 3. **a) watch** · 4. **a) sunscreen** · 5. **a) drink**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Omar says ___ on a hat. → a) Put · b) Wear on · c) Take

7. *What's the weather like?* = ____. → a) tiempo · b) carácter · c) precio

8. *I'm looking for sunscreen* = ____. → a) compras/salud · b) only films · c) arrive at

9. *You should drink water* = ____. → a) consejo · b) predicción · c) only Past

10. Unit 50 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Put** · 7. **a) tiempo** · 8. **a) compras/salud** · 9. **a) consejo** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Going to be hot* → a) predicción tiempo · b) look like · c) prefer than

12. *By bus to the museum* → a) transporte · b) solo hotel · c) arrive in

13. *Watch a documentary* → a) ocio · b) solo compras · c) listen music

14. Shadow and note ____. → a) M5 mixed structures · b) only some · c) only Who

15. U50 next unit is ____. → a) Making Suggestions U51 · b) Module 6 only · c) U49 skip

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) predicción tiempo** · 12. **a) transporte** · 13. **a) ocio** · 14. **a) M5 mixed structures** · 15. **a) Making Suggestions U51**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. We went ___ bus. → *by / with / in*

2. It's ___ to be hot. → *going / will going / goes*

3. How ___ is this scarf? → *much / many / more*

4. You ___ rest if you have a headache. → *should / should to / shoulds*

5. She ___ like her mother. → *looks / is look / looking*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **by** · 2. **going** · 3. **much** · 4. **should** · 5. **looks**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. train / took / the / we / to / Lisbon

7. comedies / prefer / I / to / dramas

8. on / Put / a / hat / your

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. We took the train to Lisbon.
7. I prefer comedies to dramas.
8. Put on your hat.

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Una frase de viaje (*by*, *take*, *get to* o *checked in*).

10. Una frase del tiempo (*It's…* o *going to*).

11. Una frase de compras o salud.

12. Una frase de ropa (*wear*, *put on*, *too/enough*).

13. Una frase de personas (*look like*, *What's … like?*, *known*).

14. Corrige: *I go with train.*

15. Escribe un mini-relato de fin de semana (8–10 líneas) mezclando temas del Módulo 5.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. We took the train to Lisbon. / We checked in at a small hotel.
10. On Saturday it was sunny. / It's going to be hot.
11. How much is it? — €12. / You should rest.
12. Put on a hat. / This jacket is warm enough.
13. She looks like her mother. / I've known my friend for ten years.
14. I go **by** train.
15. Last Friday we took the train to Lisbon and checked in at a small hotel. On Saturday it was sunny, so we went sightseeing. In the evening we watched a film — I prefer comedies to dramas. On Sunday I bought a scarf: "How much is it?" — €12. Later I had a headache, so my friend said: "You should rest." Our neighbour in the hotel was friendly. I've known my travel partner for ten years — we always get on well.

</details>
