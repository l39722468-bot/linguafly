En la [Unidad 27](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-27-zero-conditional) hablaste de hechos generales. En la **Unidad 28 del curso A2** (*Have to / Don't have to*) hablas de **obligaciones** y de lo que **no es necesario**.

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 28 — Have to / Don't have to](/curso-a2/unit-28)  
> **Antes:** [U27 — Zero Conditional](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-27-zero-conditional)

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 28 (A2)

- Usar **have to / has to** (obligación).
- Usar **don't / doesn't have to** (no necesario).
- Preguntar: **Do / Does … have to…?**
- Hablar de reglas en **trabajo** y **escuela**.
- Distinguir obligación de opción.

![Have to / Don't have to A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-28/a2-unit28-have-to.png)

---

## 1. Idea clave

| Forma | Significado | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **have to / has to** | es necesario / obligación | I **have to** get up early. |
| **don't / doesn't have to** | no es necesario (opcional) | I **don't have to** work on Saturdays. |

> She **has to** wear a uniform at work.  
> You **don't have to** wear a tie today — it's optional.  
> He **doesn't have to** pay the bill today.

## 2. Formas

![Formas de have to](/blog/curso-a2/unit-28/a2-unit28-forms.png)

| Sujeto | Afirmativa | Negativa | Pregunta |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I / you / we / they | **have to** | **don't have to** | **Do** you **have to**…? |
| he / she / it | **has to** | **doesn't have to** | **Does** she **have to**…? |

Después de *have to / has to* va el **infinitivo**: *go, wear, pay, study…*

> **Do** you **have to** wear a uniform? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.  
> **Does** he **have to** pay today? — No, he **doesn't**.

## 3. Obligación vs opcional (cuidado)

| Frase | Idea |
| :--- | :--- |
| You **have to** arrive on time. | Obligación / regla |
| You **don't have to** bring lunch. | No es necesario (puedes, pero no hace falta) |

*Don't have to* **no** es lo mismo que “no debes / está prohibido”. En A2: **no es obligatorio**.

## 4. Trabajo, casa y escuela

| Contexto | *have to* | *don't have to* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Trabajo | get up early · wear a uniform · pay bills | work on Saturdays · cook today |
| Escuela | wear uniform · arrive on time · do homework | bring lunch · pay for books |
| Estudio | study tonight for the exam | — |

> I **have to** study tonight for my exam tomorrow.  
> I **don't have to** cook today because my mother is making dinner.  
> Students **don't have to** pay for books — the school gives them free.

## 6. Listening: reglas en la escuela

> At school, students have to wear uniform. They have to arrive on time. They don't have to bring lunch because the school has a canteen. Students have to do homework every day. They don't have to pay for books — the school gives them free. Teachers have to correct exams and help students.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-28/dialogue-school.mp3" title="🔊 At school, students have to wear uniform…"></audio>

Preguntas rápidas:

1. ¿Uniforme? → *have to wear uniform*  
2. ¿Llegar? → *have to arrive on time*  
3. ¿Traer comida? → *don't have to* (canteen)  
4. ¿Deberes? → *have to do homework every day*  
5. ¿Pagar libros? → *don't have to* (free)

## 7. Mini práctica oral

> I have to…  
> I don't have to…  
> Do you have to…?  
> She has to / doesn't have to…

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-28/practice-four.mp3" title="🔊 I have to… I don't have to… Do you have to…?"></audio>

3. Pregunta a un compañero: *Do you have to…?*

## 8. Vocabulario (del curso)

| Inglés | Idea |
| :--- | :--- |
| obligation / rule / necessary / optional | obligación / regla / necesario / opcional |
| have to / has to / don't have to | tener que / no tener que |
| get up early / arrive on time | levantarse temprano / llegar a tiempo |
| wear a uniform / wear a tie | llevar uniforme / corbata |
| work / pay bills / cook | trabajar / pagar facturas / cocinar |
| homework / exam / study | deberes / examen / estudiar |
| canteen / books / free | cafetería / libros / gratis |
| correct exams / help students | corregir exámenes / ayudar |
| every day / every month / tonight | cada día / cada mes / esta noche |
| on Saturdays / tomorrow | los sábados / mañana |

## 9. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| She **have to** wear a uniform. | She **has to** wear… | *she* → *has to* |
| He **don't have to** pay. | He **doesn't have to** pay. | *he* → *doesn't* |
| Do she have to…? | **Does** she have to…? | *Does* con *he/she/it* |
| I have to **to** go. | I have to go. | Un infinitivo, sin *to* doble |
| Don't have to = prohibido | Don't have to = **no necesario** | No confundir con *mustn't* (más adelante) |

## 10. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — *have to* o *has to*

1. I ____ go to work tomorrow.  
2. She ____ wear a uniform at work.  
3. They ____ arrive on time.  
4. He ____ study tonight.  
5. We ____ pay our bills every month.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **have to** · 2. **has to** · 3. **have to** · 4. **has to** · 5. **have to**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — *don't / doesn't have to*

1. I ____ work on Saturdays.  
2. He ____ pay the bill today. *(optional)*  
3. You ____ wear a tie today. *(optional)*  
4. She ____ cook tonight.  
5. Students ____ bring lunch. *(canteen)*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **don't have to** · 2. **doesn't have to** · 3. **don't have to** · 4. **doesn't have to** · 5. **don't have to**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Preguntas

1. ____ you have to wear a uniform?  
2. ____ she have to get up early?  
3. ____ they have to do homework every day?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Do** · 2. **Does** · 3. **Do**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Completa el texto

I ____ to get up early every day. I ____ to wear a uniform at work. I ____ have to work on Saturdays. I ____ to study tonight for my exam.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**have** · **have** · **don't** · **have**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Completa la escuela

Students ____ to wear uniform. They ____ to arrive on time. They ____ have to bring lunch. They ____ have to pay for books. Teachers ____ to correct exams.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**have** · **have** · **don't** · **don't** · **have**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Empareja

1. have to  
2. don't have to  
3. has to  
4. doesn't have to  
5. Do you have to…?  

a) pregunta · b) obligación (*I/you/we/they*) · c) no necesario (*I/you…*) · d) obligación (*he/she*) · e) no necesario (*he/she*)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **c** · 3 → **d** · 4 → **e** · 5 → **a**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Corrige

1. *She have to wear a uniform.*  
2. *He don't have to pay today.*  
3. *Do she have to study tonight?*  
4. *I have to to get up early.*  
5. *They has to arrive on time.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. She **has to** wear a uniform.  
2. He **doesn't have to** pay today.  
3. **Does** she have to study tonight?  
4. I have to get up early.  
5. They **have to** arrive on time.
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Traduce

1. Tengo que levantarme temprano cada día.  
2. Ella tiene que llevar uniforme.  
3. No tengo que trabajar los sábados.  
4. Él no tiene que pagar hoy.  
5. ¿Tienes que llevar uniforme?  
6. Los estudiantes tienen que llegar a tiempo.  
7. No tienen que traer comida.  
8. Tengo que estudiar esta noche.  
9. No tengo que cocinar hoy.  
10. Los profesores tienen que corregir exámenes.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I have to get up early every day.  
2. She has to wear a uniform.  
3. I don't have to work on Saturdays.  
4. He doesn't have to pay today.  
5. Do you have to wear a uniform?  
6. Students have to arrive on time.  
7. They don't have to bring lunch.  
8. I have to study tonight.  
9. I don't have to cook today.  
10. Teachers have to correct exams.
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Obligación u opción

Marca **O** (obligación) o **Op** (opcional / no necesario):

1. I have to pay my bills every month.  
2. I don't have to work on Saturdays.  
3. Students have to do homework every day.  
4. They don't have to pay for books.  
5. She has to wear a uniform at work.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **O** · 2. **Op** · 3. **O** · 4. **Op** · 5. **O**
</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tus reglas

Escribe 8–12 líneas sobre tu trabajo/estudios/casa:

- 3 × *I have to…*  
- 2 × *I don't have to…*  
- 1 × *he/she has to…*  
- 1 × pregunta *Do you have to…?*  

**Modelo:** el texto del día o las reglas de la escuela.

## 11. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Tabla *have to / has to / don't / doesn't*.  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–7.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha trabajo y escuela; marca obligaciones.  
4. **5–8 min** — Escribe tus reglas (Ejercicio 10).

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *have to / has to* para obligación  
- [ ] Uso *don't / doesn't have to* para lo no necesario  
- [ ] Pregunto con *Do/Does … have to…?*  
- [ ] Distingo obligación y opción  
- [ ] Puedo hablar de reglas en trabajo y escuela

## 12. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. ¿Es **necesario**? → *have to / has to*.
