## 1. Mapa rápido del Módulo 1

![Checklist Módulo 1](/blog/curso-a2/unit-10/a2-unit10-checklist.png)

Objetivo del repaso: **contar una experiencia pasada** con detalle (cuándo, dónde, cómo, comparado con quién).

Modelo mental:

1. **¿Qué pasó?** → Past Simple (*walked / went / saw*)  
2. **¿Preguntas?** → *What/Where/When did…?*  
3. **¿Comparar?** → *-er / more… than* · *the -est / the most*  
4. **¿Cómo?** → adverbio (*fluently, carefully, well*)  
5. **¿Cuándo / dónde te moviste?** → *at/on/in* + *into/through/across*

---

## 2. Past Simple: regulares e irregulares

| Tipo | Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Regular | verbo + **-ed** | I **walked** to the park yesterday. |
| Irregular | forma propia | She **went** to London last year. |
| Negativa | *didn't* + base | I **didn't** go. |
| Pregunta | *did* + sujeto + base | **Did** you walk? / **What did** you do? |

> I walked to the park yesterday.  
> She went to London last year.  
> What did you do yesterday?  
> I went to the cinema yesterday.

## 3. Comparativos y superlativos

| | Comparativo (2) | Superlativo (grupo) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Corto | **taller than** | **the tallest** |
| Largo | **more interesting than** | **the most interesting** |
| Irregulares | *better / worse* | *the best / the worst* |

> My brother is **taller than** me.  
> She is **the tallest** girl in the class.  
> It was **the most interesting** film I have ever seen.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-10/taller-than.mp3" title="🔊 My brother is taller than me"></audio>

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-10/the-tallest.mp3" title="🔊 She is the tallest girl in the class"></audio>

Recuerda: *than* con comparativo; *the* + superlativo; a menudo *in* the class / family / world.

## 4. Adverbios de modo

| Adjetivo | Adverbio | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| fluent | **fluently** | She speaks English **fluently**. |
| quick | **quickly** | She learned **quickly**. |
| careful | **carefully** | She writes **carefully**. |
| good | **well** | She sings **well**. *(no good)* |
| bad | **badly** | He played **badly**. |
| fast / hard | **fast / hard** | He works **hard**. *(sin -ly)* |

> She speaks English fluently.

## 5. Preposiciones de tiempo: *at, on, in*

| Preposición | Usa con | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **at** | hora / noon / night | at 7 o'clock · at noon |
| **on** | día / fecha | on Monday · on weekdays |
| **in** | mes / año / parte del día | in the morning · in August |

> I get up **at** 7 o'clock every morning.  
> I have a meeting **on** Monday.  
> I have breakfast **at** 8 o'clock **in** the morning.

## 6. Preposiciones de movimiento: *into, out of, through, across*

| Preposición | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **into** | entrar / meter | She **went into** the room. |
| **out of** | salir / bajarse | I **got out of** the car. |
| **through** | por el interior | walked **through** the park / rooms |
| **across** | por una superficie | walked **across** the bridge |

> She went into the room and closed the door.  
> I got out of the car and walked away.

## 7. Texto modelo integrado: Anna

Aquí aparece **casi todo el módulo** en un solo párrafo:

> My name is Anna and I have a busy life. I get up at 7 o'clock in the morning on weekdays. Last weekend I went to the museum. I went into the main entrance and walked through several rooms. My sister is the tallest in our family and she speaks English very fluently. She saw a beautiful film last night and said it was the most interesting film she has ever seen. On Saturday morning I walked across the bridge to get to the other side of the river. I really enjoyed the weekend!

| *went to the museum* | Past Simple irregular |
| *went into… walked through…* | movimiento |
| *the tallest… the most interesting* | superlativos |
| *speaks… fluently* | adverbio |
| *saw… said* | irregulares |
| *walked across the bridge* | *across* |

## 8. Listening: mi semana pasada

## 9. Mini práctica oral mixta

> I walked to the park yesterday.  
> She went to London last year.  
> What did you do yesterday?  
> My brother is taller than me.  
> She speaks English fluently.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-10/practice-mixed.mp3" title="🔊 I walked to the park yesterday…"></audio>

Cuenta tu fin de semana en 6–8 frases e incluye al menos:

- 2 Past Simple (*went / walked / saw / had*)  
- 1 Wh-question (*What did you…?*)  
- 1 comparativo o superlativo  
- 1 adverbio (*fluently / carefully / well*)  
- 1 *at/on/in* + 1 *into/through/across*

## 10. Errores frecuentes del módulo

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Tema |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I walk to the park yesterday. | I **walked**… | Past Simple |
| She goed to London. | She **went**… | irregular |
| What you did do yesterday? | **What did you do** yesterday? | Wh + *did* |
| She is taller that me. | taller **than** me | comparativo |
| She is tallest girl. | **the** tallest girl | superlativo |
| She sings good. | She sings **well**. | adverbio |
| I wake up on 7 o'clock. | **at** 7 o'clock | tiempo |
| She went in the room. *(movimiento)* | went **into** the room | movimiento |
| We walked across the tunnel. | walked **through** the tunnel | *through* vs *across* |

## 11. Ejercicios de repaso

### Ejercicio 1 — Past Simple

1. I ____ to the park yesterday. (walk)  
2. She ____ to London last year. (go)  
3. What ____ you ____ yesterday? (do)  
4. I ____ to the cinema yesterday. (go)  
5. She ____ a film last night. (see)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **walked** · 2. **went** · 3. **did** / **do** · 4. **went** · 5. **saw**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Comparativos y superlativos

1. My brother is ____ than me. (tall)  
2. She is ____ girl in the class. (tall)  
3. It was ____ film I have ever seen. (interesting → the most…)  
4. I am ____ runner in the family. (fast)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **taller** · 2. **the tallest** · 3. **the most interesting** · 4. **the fastest**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Adverbios

1. She speaks English ____. (fluent)  
2. She sings ____. (good → well)  
3. He played ____. (bad)  
4. He works ____ every day. (hard)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **fluently** · 2. **well** · 3. **badly** · 4. **hard**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Preposiciones de tiempo

1. I get up ____ 7 o'clock.  
2. I have a meeting ____ Monday.  
3. I have breakfast at 8 ____ the morning.  
4. On Monday I had a meeting ____ noon.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **at** · 2. **on** · 3. **in** · 4. **at**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Preposiciones de movimiento

1. She ____ into the room. (go → past)  
2. I got ____ the car and walked away.  
3. I walked ____ several rooms.  
4. I walked ____ the bridge.  
5. On Wednesday I walked ____ the park and went ____ a cafe.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **went** · 2. **out of** · 3. **through** · 4. **across** · 5. **through** · **into**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa el texto de Anna

I get up ____ 7 o'clock ____ the morning ____ weekdays. Last weekend I ____ to the museum. I went ____ the main entrance and walked ____ several rooms. My sister is ____ in our family and she speaks English very ____. On Saturday I walked ____ the bridge.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**at** · **in** · **on** · **went** · **into** · **through** · **the tallest** · **fluently** · **across**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Completa el listening

____ Monday I had a meeting ____ noon. ____ Tuesday I ____ to the cinema and ____ a film. It was ____ interesting film. ____ Wednesday I walked ____ the park and went ____ a cafe. My brother is ____ than me.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**On** · **at** · **On** · **went** · **saw** · **the most** · **On** · **through** · **into** · **taller**
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Corrige

1. *I walk to the park yesterday.*  
2. *What you did do yesterday?*  
3. *She is taller that me.*  
4. *She sings good.*  
5. *I wake up on 7 o'clock.*  
6. *We walked across the tunnel.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **walked** to the park yesterday.  
2. **What did you do** yesterday?  
3. She is taller **than** me.  
4. She sings **well**.  
5. I wake up **at** 7 o'clock.  
6. We walked **through** the tunnel.
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Elige la correcta

1. ____  
   a) She goed to London. · b) She went to London. · c) She gone to London.  
2. ____  
   a) What did you do yesterday? · b) What you did yesterday? · c) What do you did yesterday?  
3. ____  
   a) the most interesting · b) the more interesting · c) most interestinger *(superlativo largo)*  
4. ____  
   a) at Monday · b) on Monday · c) in Monday  
5. ____  
   a) through the street *(cruzar)* · b) across the street · c) into the street *(cruzar al otro lado)*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b** · 2. **a** · 3. **a** · 4. **b** · 5. **b**
</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Escribe tu semana (texto integrado)

Escribe 10–14 frases sobre *last week* / *last weekend*. Debe incluir:

- Presentación breve (*My name is…*)  
- 3 × Past Simple (mix regular/irregular)  
- 1 × Wh-question  
- 1 × comparativo **y** 1 × superlativo  
- 1 × adverbio de modo  
- 2 × prep. de tiempo (*at/on/in*)  
- 2 × prep. de movimiento (*into/through/across/out of*)

**Modelo:** sección 7 (Anna) o sección 8 (last week).

## 12. Plan de estudio (30–35 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Recorre el mapa del Módulo 1 (tabla inicial).  
2. **10 min** — Ejercicios 1–8.  
3. **8 min** — Escucha a Anna y “last week”; marca cada estructura.  
4. **8–10 min** — Escribe y graba tu texto integrado.

### Checklist final del Módulo 1

- [ ] Cuento el pasado con *-ed* e irregulares clave  
- [ ] Pregunto con *What/Where/When did…?*  
- [ ] Comparo (*-er / more… than*) y elijo el extremo (*the -est / the most*)  
- [ ] Describo *cómo* con adverbios (*fluently, well…*)  
- [ ] Uso *at / on / in* sin mezclarlos  
- [ ] Uso *into / out of / through / across* en un recorrido  
- [ ] Puedo escribir un párrafo que combine **todo**

---

## 13. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. No estudies cada tema aislado: **fuerza la mezcla** (como Anna).  
2. Lista de “mínimos”: *went, saw, had, taller, the tallest, fluently, at, on, in, into, through, across*.  
3. Grábate 60 segundos sobre *last weekend*.  
4. Practica en la [Unidad 10 del curso A2](/curso-a2/unit-10).

La **Unidad 11** abre el **Módulo 2** con **Present Perfect**: *I have been to…*
