En la [Unidad 21](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-21-going-to-planes-intenciones) usaste *going to* para **planes**. En la **Unidad 22 del curso A2** (*Going to: Predictions*) usas la **misma forma** para **predicciones** con evidencia: *Look at the clouds! It's going to rain.*

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 22 — Going to: Predictions](/curso-a2/unit-22)  
> **Antes:** [U21 — Going to: Plans & Intentions](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-21-going-to-planes-intenciones)

---

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 22 (A2)

- Hacer **predicciones** con *going to* basadas en evidencia.
- Usar **Look at…!** / **Be careful!** para señalar lo que ves.
- Expresar opinión con **I think…** y certeza con **I'm sure…**
- Hablar del **tiempo**, resultados y consecuencias.
- Distinguir **plan** (U21) vs **predicción** (U22).

## 1. Idea clave: evidencia → predicción

| Evidencia (ahora) | Predicción |
| :--- | :--- |
| dark clouds | It **is going to** rain. |
| cup near the edge | That cup **is going to** fall. |
| she studies hard | She **is going to** pass the exam. |
| good forecast | It **is going to** be sunny tomorrow. |

Fórmula (igual que en U21): **am / is / are** + **going to** + infinitivo.

---

## 2. Planes vs predicciones

![Planes vs predicciones con going to](/blog/curso-a2/unit-22/a2-unit22-plans-vs-predictions.png)

| | Unidad 21 — Plans | Unidad 22 — Predictions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Idea | ya lo decidí | lo veo / lo sé ahora |
| Ejemplo | I **am going to** travel to Japan. | Look at the clouds! It **is going to** rain. |
| Pregunta mental | ¿Qué tengo planeado? | ¿Qué va a pasar según la evidencia? |

Misma forma. Uso distinto.

## 3. Predicciones con evidencia (*Look at…!*)

> **Look at** the clouds! It **is going to** rain.  
> **Be careful!** That cup **is going to** fall.  
> Look at those dark clouds! I think it **is going to** rain in the evening.  
> She studies hard. She **is going to** pass the exam.

## 4. *I think…* vs *I'm sure…*

| Frase | Nivel | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **I think…** | opinión / algo de duda | I **think** they **are going to** win the match. |
| **I'm sure…** | más certeza | I **'m sure** he **is going to** get the job. |
| **Maybe…** | posibilidad | **Maybe** we should take an umbrella. |

> I **think** it **is going to** be warm and nice.  
> I **'m sure** we **are going to** have a good day at the beach.  
> I **think** it **is going to** rain in the afternoon.  
> I **'m sure** the match **is going to** be exciting tonight.

## 5. Tiempo, resultados y consecuencias

Vocabulario típico de esta unidad:

| Inglés | Uso en predicción |
| :--- | :--- |
| sunny / warm / nice | It is going to be **sunny**. |
| rain / clouds / umbrella | It is going to **rain**. Take an **umbrella**. |
| fall | That cup is going to **fall**. |
| pass / win / get the job | She is going to **pass**. / They are going to **win**. |
| exciting / good | The match is going to be **exciting**. |

Marcadores: *tomorrow, in the afternoon, in the evening, tonight*.

## 6. Texto modelo: un día de predicciones

> Tomorrow the weather is going to be sunny. I think it is going to be warm and nice. I'm sure we are going to have a good day at the beach. Look at the clouds! I think it is going to rain in the afternoon. Maybe we should take an umbrella. I'm sure the match is going to be exciting tonight. My team is going to win!

## 7. Listening: picnic y película

> Hi! I just checked the weather forecast. I think it is going to be sunny tomorrow. I'm sure we are going to have a great day for our picnic. But look at those dark clouds over there. I think it is going to rain in the evening. Maybe we should go home early. I'm sure the film is going to be good tonight. My friend said it is excellent.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-22/dialogue-picnic.mp3" title="🔊 Hi! I just checked the weather forecast…"></audio>

Preguntas rápidas:

1. ¿Qué tiempo mañana? → *sunny*  
2. ¿Plan de mañana? → *picnic*  
3. ¿Qué ve en el cielo? → *dark clouds*  
4. ¿Cuándo va a llover? → *in the evening*  
5. ¿Qué hay esta noche? → *a film* (va a ser buena)

## 10. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Look the clouds! | **Look at** the clouds! | *look at* + objeto |
| It going to rain. | It **is** going to rain. | Falta *is* |
| I think it going to rain. | I think it **is** going to rain. | *is going to* |
| I'm sure they is going to win. | I'm sure they **are** going to win. | *they* → *are* |
| It is going to raining. | It is going to **rain**. | Infinitivo, no *-ing* |
| (plan vs predicción mezclados sin contexto) | Decide: ¿plan mío o evidencia? | Mismo *going to*, uso distinto |

## 11. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige *am / is / are*

1. Look at the clouds! It ____ going to rain.  
2. I think they ____ going to win the match.  
3. She ____ going to pass the exam. She studies hard.  
4. I'm sure we ____ going to have a good day.  
5. Be careful! That cup ____ going to fall.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is** · 2. **are** · 3. **is** · 4. **are** · 5. **is**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa la predicción

Usa: *rain / fall / pass / win / be*

1. Look at the clouds! It is going to ____.  
2. Be careful! That cup is going to ____.  
3. She studies hard. She is going to ____ the exam.  
4. I'm sure my team is going to ____.  
5. Tomorrow the weather is going to ____ sunny.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **rain** · 2. **fall** · 3. **pass** · 4. **win** · 5. **be**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — *I think* o *I'm sure*

Elige la que encaje mejor (ambas pueden ser gramaticales; sigue el modelo del curso):

1. ____ it is going to rain in the afternoon. *(opinión al ver nubes)*  
2. ____ the match is going to be exciting tonight. *(más certeza)*  
3. ____ he is going to get the job. *(certeza)*  
4. ____ it is going to be warm and nice. *(opinión)*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **I think** · 2. **I'm sure** · 3. **I'm sure** · 4. **I think**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Plan o predicción

Marca **P** (plan) o **Pred** (predicción):

1. I am going to travel to Japan next summer.  
2. Look at the clouds! It is going to rain.  
3. We are going to visit grandmother this weekend.  
4. She studies hard. She is going to pass.  
5. Be careful! That cup is going to fall.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **P** · 2. **Pred** · 3. **P** · 4. **Pred** · 5. **Pred**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Completa el texto del tiempo

Tomorrow the weather ____ going to be sunny. I ____ it is going to be warm and nice. I'm ____ we are going to have a good day at the beach. Look ____ the clouds! I think it is going to ____ in the afternoon.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**is** · **think** · **sure** · **at** · **rain**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa el listening

I think it is going to be ____ tomorrow. I'm sure we are going to have a great day for our ____. Look at those dark ____. I think it is going to rain in the ____. I'm sure the ____ is going to be good tonight.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**sunny** · **picnic** · **clouds** · **evening** · **film**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Empareja

1. Look at the clouds!  
2. Be careful!  
3. I think…  
4. I'm sure…  
5. Maybe…  

a) más certeza · b) evidencia → lluvia · c) vaso que cae · d) posibilidad · e) opinión

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **c** · 3 → **e** · 4 → **a** · 5 → **d**
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Corrige

1. *Look the clouds! It going to rain.*  
2. *I think they is going to win.*  
3. *I'm sure we is going to have a good day.*  
4. *It is going to raining.*  
5. *Be careful! That cup are going to fall.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Look at** the clouds! It **is** going to rain.  
2. I think they **are** going to win.  
3. I'm sure we **are** going to have a good day.  
4. It is going to **rain**.  
5. Be careful! That cup **is** going to fall.
</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tus predicciones

Escribe 8–12 líneas:

- 2 × predicción con evidencia (*Look at…! / Be careful!*)  
- 2 × *I think… is/are going to…*  
- 2 × *I'm sure… is/are going to…*  
- 1 × tiempo de mañana · 1 × esta noche  

**Modelo:** el texto del día soleado / picnic.

## 12. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Tabla evidencia → predicción + planes vs predicciones.  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–7.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha el texto del tiempo y el picnic; marca *I think / I'm sure*.  
4. **5–8 min** — Graba tus predicciones (Ejercicio 10).

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *going to* para predicciones con evidencia  
- [ ] Distingo plan (U21) y predicción (U22)  
- [ ] Uso *Look at…!* / *Be careful!*  
- [ ] Diferencio *I think* e *I'm sure*  
- [ ] Puedo hablar del tiempo y de resultados (*pass, win, rain…*)

---

## 13. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Primero mira la **evidencia** (nubes, estudio, pronóstico…).  
2. Luego elige *I think* o *I'm sure*.  
3. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 22](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-22-going-to-predicciones-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 22 del curso A2](/curso-a2/unit-22).

La **Unidad 23** introduce **will / won't** para predicciones generales, promesas y ofertas: *I'll help you.*
