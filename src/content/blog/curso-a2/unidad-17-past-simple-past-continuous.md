En la [Unidad 16](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-16-past-continuous) formaste *was/were + -ing*. En la **Unidad 17 del curso A2** (*Past Simple + Past Continuous*) **combinas** ambos tiempos: *When I was walking, I saw…*

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 17 — Past Simple + Past Continuous](/curso-a2/unit-17)  
> **Antes:** [U16 — Past Continuous](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-16-past-continuous)

---

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 17 (A2)

- Combinar **Past Continuous** (fondo) + **Past Simple** (interrupción).
- Usar **when** y **while**.
- Contar historias con **at first / then / suddenly / finally**.
- Describir acciones **simultáneas** e **interrumpidas**.
- Escribir un relato breve con ambos tiempos.

![Past Simple + Past Continuous](/blog/curso-a2/unit-17/a2-unit17-ps-pc.png)

---

## 1. Idea clave

| Tiempo | Rol en el relato | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Past Continuous** | acción **larga** / fondo | I **was walking**… |
| **Past Simple** | acción **corta** / hecho puntual | … when I **saw** him. |

Imagen mental: una línea larga (estabas haciendo algo) y un punto (pasó algo).

> I **was walking** when I **saw** him.  
> While she **was cooking**, the phone **rang**.

---

## 2. *When* + Past Simple (interrupción)

**When** suele introducir la acción corta:

> I was walking when I **saw** him.  
> He was reading when the door **opened**.  
> They were having dinner when we **arrived**.  
> I was watching TV when the doorbell **rang**.  
> He was walking his dog when he **saw** smoke.

Patrón A2: **was/were + -ing + when + Past Simple**

---

## 3. *While* + Past Continuous (fondo / simultáneo)

**While** suele ir con la acción en curso:

> While she **was cooking**, the phone rang.  
> We **were talking** while we walked around the lake.  
> I **was cooking** dinner while I **was watching** TV.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-17/while-cooking-phone-rang.mp3" title="🔊 While she was cooking, the phone rang"></audio>

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-17/were-talking-while-walked.mp3" title="🔊 We were talking while we walked around the lake"></audio>

Patrón A2: **While + was/were + -ing, + Past Simple** (o dos acciones en curso).

---

## 4. *When* vs *while*

![When vs while](/blog/curso-a2/unit-17/a2-unit17-when-while.png)

| Conector | Prioridad A2 | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **when** | acción corta (Past Simple) | when I saw him / when the phone rang |
| **while** | acción larga (Past Continuous) | while she was cooking / while we were talking |

Ambas frases son posibles a veces, pero en A2 sigue la prioridad de la tabla.

---

## 5. Conectores de relato

| Conector | Idea |
| :--- | :--- |
| **At first** | al principio |
| **Then** | entonces / después |
| **Suddenly** | de repente |
| **Finally** | finalmente |
| **until** | hasta que |

> **At first** I was walking in the park…  
> **Then** suddenly it started to rain.  
> **Finally** the sun came out.

---

## 6. Texto modelo: el parque

> At first last Saturday I was walking in the park when I met an old friend. We were talking while we walked around the lake. Then suddenly it started to rain. We were running to find shelter when a woman offered us her umbrella. We waited under a tree until the rain stopped. Finally the sun came out and we continued our walk.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-17/reading-park.mp3" title="🔊 At first last Saturday I was walking…"></audio>

![Historia del parque](/blog/curso-a2/unit-17/a2-unit17-park-story.png)

| Frase | Continuous | Simple |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I was walking… when I met… | walking | met |
| We were talking while we walked… | talking | walked *(simultáneo / secuencia)* |
| We were running… when a woman offered… | running | offered |
| Suddenly it started to rain | — | started |
| Finally the sun came out | — | came out |

## 7. Listening: el vecino y el humo

> At first yesterday evening I was watching TV when the doorbell rang. It was my neighbour Mr Jones. He was walking his dog when he saw smoke coming from my kitchen. I was cooking dinner while I was watching TV. Suddenly something burned in the oven. Mr Jones helped me open the windows. Then we checked the kitchen together. Finally everything was okay and I thanked him for his help.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-17/dialogue-neighbour.mp3" title="🔊 At first yesterday evening I was watching TV…"></audio>

Preguntas rápidas:

1. ¿Qué hacía cuando sonó el timbre? → *watching TV*  
2. ¿Quién era? → *Mr Jones*  
3. ¿Qué vio el vecino? → *smoke from the kitchen*  
4. ¿Qué pasó de repente? → *something burned in the oven*

## 10. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I walked when I was seeing him. | I **was walking** when I **saw** him. | fondo = Continuous; interrupción = Simple |
| While she cooked, the phone was ringing. *(prioridad A2)* | While she **was cooking**, the phone **rang**. | *while* + Continuous; hecho corto = Simple |
| I was walk when I saw him. | I was **walking** when I saw him. | falta *-ing* |
| When I was seeing him, I walked. | When I **saw** him, I **was walking**. / I was walking when I saw him. | *see* puntual → Simple |
| Suddenly it was starting to rain. | Suddenly it **started** to rain. | *suddenly* + hecho puntual |

## 11. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige Continuous o Simple

1. I ____ (walk) when I ____ (see) him.  
2. While she ____ (cook), the phone ____ (ring).  
3. He ____ (read) when the door ____ (open).  
4. They ____ (have) dinner when we ____ (arrive).  
5. I ____ (watch) TV when the doorbell ____ (ring).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **was walking** · **saw**  
2. **was cooking** · **rang**  
3. **was reading** · **opened**  
4. **were having** · **arrived**  
5. **was watching** · **rang**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — *when* o *while*

1. I was walking ____ I met an old friend.  
2. ____ she was cooking, the phone rang.  
3. We were running ____ a woman offered us her umbrella.  
4. I was cooking dinner ____ I was watching TV.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **when** · 2. **While** · 3. **when** · 4. **while**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Completa el parque

At first I ____ walking in the park when I ____ an old friend. We ____ talking while we walked around the lake. Suddenly it ____ to rain. We ____ running when a woman ____ us her umbrella. Finally the sun ____ out.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**was** · **met** · **were** · **started** · **were** · **offered** · **came**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Completa el vecino

I ____ watching TV when the doorbell ____. Mr Jones ____ walking his dog when he ____ smoke. I ____ cooking dinner while I ____ watching TV. Suddenly something ____ in the oven.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**was** · **rang** · **was** · **saw** · **was** · **was** · **burned**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Conectores

Ordena la historia con: *At first / Then / Suddenly / Finally*

1. ____ I was walking in the park.  
2. ____ I met my friend and we were talking.  
3. ____ it started to rain.  
4. ____ the sun came out and we continued.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **At first** · 2. **Then** · 3. **Suddenly** · 4. **Finally**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Corrige

1. *I walked when I was seeing him.*  
2. *While she cooked, the phone was ringing.* *(prioridad A2)*  
3. *I was watch TV when the doorbell rang.*  
4. *Suddenly it was starting to rain.*  
5. *They was having dinner when we arrived.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **was walking** when I **saw** him.  
2. While she **was cooking**, the phone **rang**.  
3. I was **watching** TV when the doorbell rang.  
4. Suddenly it **started** to rain.  
5. They **were** having dinner when we arrived.
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Transforma

1. I walked in the park. I met a friend. → *was walking when met*  
2. She cooked. The phone rang. → *While… was cooking…*  
3. He walked his dog. He saw smoke. → *was walking when saw*  
4. We had dinner. They arrived. → *were having when arrived*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **was walking** in the park when I **met** a friend.  
2. While she **was cooking**, the phone **rang**.  
3. He **was walking** his dog when he **saw** smoke.  
4. We **were having** dinner when they **arrived**.
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Empareja

1. when  
2. while  
3. Past Continuous  
4. Past Simple  
5. suddenly  

a) fondo / acción larga · b) interrupción / hecho corto · c) a menudo + Simple · d) a menudo + Continuous · e) de repente (hecho puntual)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **c** · 2 → **d** · 3 → **a** · 4 → **b** · 5 → **e**
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Elige la correcta

1. ____  
   a) I was walking when I saw him. · b) I walked when I was seeing him. · c) I was walk when I saw him.  
2. ____  
   a) While she was cooking, the phone rang. · b) While she cooked, the phone was ringing. · c) While she was cook, the phone rang.  
3. ____  
   a) Suddenly it started to rain. · b) Suddenly it was starting to rain. · c) Suddenly it start to rain.  
4. ____  
   a) They were having dinner when we arrived. · b) They was having dinner when we arrived. · c) They were have dinner when we arrived.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a** · 2. **a** · 3. **a** · 4. **a**
</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Traduce

1. Estaba caminando cuando lo vi.  
2. Mientras ella cocinaba, sonó el teléfono.  
3. Él leía cuando se abrió la puerta.  
4. Estaban cenando cuando llegamos.  
5. Estaba viendo la tele cuando sonó el timbre.  
6. De repente empezó a llover.  
7. Al principio estaba caminando en el parque.  
8. Finalmente salió el sol.  
9. Estaba paseando a su perro cuando vio humo.  
10. Estaba cocinando mientras veía la tele.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I was walking when I saw him.  
2. While she was cooking, the phone rang.  
3. He was reading when the door opened.  
4. They were having dinner when we arrived.  
5. I was watching TV when the doorbell rang.  
6. Suddenly it started to rain.  
7. At first I was walking in the park.  
8. Finally the sun came out.  
9. He was walking his dog when he saw smoke.  
10. I was cooking while I was watching TV. / I was cooking dinner while I was watching TV.
</details>

### Ejercicio 11 — Tu relato

Escribe 8–12 frases con:

- 3 × *was/were … when …*  
- 1 × *While …*  
- conectores: *At first / Then / Suddenly / Finally*  

**Modelo:** parque o vecino/cocina.

2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–7.

4. **5–8 min** — Escribe y graba tu relato.
