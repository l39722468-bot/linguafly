## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 45 (A2)

- Vocabulario: **film, series, concert, documentary, news** y **géneros**.
- Verbos: **watch**, **listen to**, **read**.
- Gustos: **like / love / enjoy + -ing**.
- Comparar: **prefer… to…**.
- Entender una **reseña** sencilla y **recomendar**.

## 1. Vocabulario: medios y géneros

| Palabra | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **film / movie** | película | I watched a great **film**. |
| **series** | serie | This **series** has 8 episodes. |
| **documentary** | documental | We saw a **documentary** about space. |
| **concert** | concierto | We're going to a **concert**. |
| **news** | noticias | I read the **news** online. |
| **podcast** | podcast | She listens to a **podcast**. |

Géneros útiles: **comedy**, **drama**, **action**, **romance**, **horror**, **crime**, **science fiction**.

> I love **comedies**. / I don't like **horror** films.

![Genres](/blog/curso-a2/unit-45/a2-unit45-genres.png)

## 2. Verbos: *watch*, *listen to*, *read*

| Verbo | Uso típico | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **watch** | TV, films, series, sports | I **watch** films at the weekend. |
| **listen to** | music, radio, podcasts | I **listen to** music every day. |
| **read** | books, news, magazines | She **reads** the news in the morning. |

Importante: **listen to** (no *listen music*).

> Do you **watch** series on Netflix?  
> I **listen to** rock music.  
> He **reads** comics.

## 3. Gustos: *like* + *-ing*

Tras **like / love / hate / enjoy** suele ir el gerundio (**-ing**):

| Estructura | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| like + -ing | I **like watching** series. |
| love + -ing | She **loves listening** to jazz. |
| hate + -ing | He **hates reading** the news. |
| enjoy + -ing | We **enjoy going** to concerts. |

> I like **watching** documentaries.  
> Do you enjoy **listening** to podcasts?

También existe *like to + infinitive*, pero en A2 prioriza **like + -ing**.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-45/i-like-watching.mp3" title="🔊 I like watching series."></audio>

---

## 4. Preferir: *prefer… to…*

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| prefer + noun | I **prefer** comedies. |
| prefer A to B | I **prefer** comedies **to** dramas. |
| prefer + -ing | I **prefer watching** films at home. |

> I prefer **series** to **films**.  
> She prefers **listening** to music **to** watching TV.

## 6. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura — A short review

Last night I **watched** a **documentary** about the ocean. It was interesting and a bit sad. I usually **prefer comedies**, but this **film** was excellent. My sister **likes watching series** on her phone. She **listens to** podcasts too. On Friday we're going to a **concert**.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-45/reading-review.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: A short review"></audio>

### Diálogo — A recommendation

**Mia:** Have you **seen** that new **series**?  
**Omar:** Not yet. Is it good?  
**Mia:** Yes — I **love watching** crime dramas.  
**Omar:** I **prefer films**, but this series is brilliant.  
**Mia:** Do you want to **watch** episode one tonight?  
**Omar:** Sure!

4. I prefer comedies ___ dramas.

## 7. Errores frecuentes

1. ~~listen music~~ → **listen to music**  
2. ~~I like watch films.~~ → **I like watching films.**  
3. ~~I prefer comedy than drama.~~ → **I prefer comedy to drama.**  
4. ~~see a film~~ *(a veces OK)* → más natural para cine/TV: **watch a film**  
5. ~~a serie~~ → **a series** (singular y plural suelen ser *series*)  
6. ~~the news are~~ → **the news is** *(uncountable)*

## 8. Ejercicios (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige el verbo

1. ___ a film on TV → watch / listen to / read  
2. ___ to a podcast → watch / listen to / read  
3. ___ the newspaper → watch / listen to / read  
4. ___ a football match → watch / listen to / read  
5. ___ the news online → watch / listen to / read

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. watch  
2. listen to  
3. read  
4. watch  
5. read (también *watch the news* en TV)

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa con *to* si hace falta

1. listen ___ music  
2. watch ___ films  
3. listen ___ the radio  
4. read ___ magazines

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. to  
2. — (nada)  
3. to  
4. — (nada)

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — *like* + *-ing*

1. I like ___ (watch) series.  
2. She loves ___ (listen) to jazz.  
3. They enjoy ___ (go) to concerts.  
4. He hates ___ (read) long books.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. watching  
2. listening  
3. going  
4. reading  

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — *prefer… to…*

1. I prefer films ___ series.  
2. She prefers comedy ___ horror.  
3. We prefer ___ (watch) at home ___ going to the cinema.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. to  
2. to  
3. watching / to  

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrige el error

1. I listen music every day.  
2. I like watch documentaries.  
3. I prefer action than romance.  
4. The news are interesting.  
5. This is a good serie.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. I listen to music every day.  
2. I like watching documentaries.  
3. I prefer action to romance.  
4. The news is interesting.  
5. This is a good series.  

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Traduce al inglés

1. Veo películas el fin de semana.  
2. Escucho música todos los días.  
3. Me gusta ver series.  
4. Prefiero las comedias a los dramas.  
5. ¿Has visto esa serie nueva?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. I watch films at the weekend.  
2. I listen to music every day.  
3. I like watching series.  
4. I prefer comedies to dramas.  
5. Have you seen that new series?  

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Empareja

1. documentary  
2. concert  
3. comedy  
4. news  

a) live music event  
b) funny film / show  
c) information about current events  
d) non-fiction film about real life

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1 → d  
2 → a  
3 → b  
4 → c  

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Completa el diálogo

**A:** Do you ___ watching films?  
**B:** Yes. I ___ comedies ___ dramas.  
**A:** Do you ___ to music too?  
**B:** Every day. And I ___ the news in the morning.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

like / prefer / to  
listen  
read  

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Ordena

1. watching / like / I / series  
2. to / listen / music / I  
3. comedies / prefer / I / to / dramas

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. I like watching series.  
2. I listen to music.  
3. I prefer comedies to dramas.  

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu recomendación (8–12 líneas)

Recomienda una película o serie. Incluye:

- 1 *watch / listen to / read*  
- 1 *like / love + -ing*  
- 1 *prefer… to…*  
- 1 género (*comedy / drama / documentary…*)

**Modelo:** la lectura de la sección 6.
