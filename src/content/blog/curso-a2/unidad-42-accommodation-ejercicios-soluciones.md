## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** book; check in / check out; I'd like to book; Is there…? / Is breakfast included?

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I'd like to ____ a double room. → *book / check / stay*
2. Guests ____ in at reception. → *check / book / take*
3. What time is ____? → *check-out / book-out / stay-out*
4. Is breakfast ____? → *included / include / including*
5. ____ there WiFi in the rooms? → *Is / Are / Do*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **book** · 2. **check** · 3. **check-out** · 4. **included** · 5. **Is**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *Check in* = ____. → a) registrarse al llegar · b) marcharse · c) reservar por teléfono only
7. *I'd like to book* (no *I want book*): ____. → a) correcto · b) I like book · c) I want book
8. *Single room* = ____. → a) individual · b) doble · c) suite
9. *Is breakfast included?* pregunta ____. → a) si el desayuno está en el precio · b) hora check-out · c) platform
10. Unit 42 follows ____. → a) Travel U41 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U43

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) registrarse al llegar** · 7. **a) correcto** · 8. **a) individual** · 9. **a) si el desayuno está en el precio** · 10. **a) Travel U41**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I like to book a room.* *(reserva ahora)*
12. *We want check in.*
13. *A individual room, please.*
14. *Breakfast is include.*
15. *What hour is check-out?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **I'd like to book** a room.
12. We **want to check in** / **We'd like to check in**.
13. A **single** room, please.
14. Breakfast is **included**.
15. What **time** is check-out?

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** hotel, hostel, single/double/twin room, reception, key card, room service, reservation.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *reception* ≈ ____ → *recepción · reserva · albergue*
2. *key card* ≈ ____ → *tarjeta de acceso · desayuno · WiFi*
3. *twin room* ≈ ____ → *dos camas individuales · una cama grande · suite*
4. *hostel* ≈ ____ → *alojamiento más barato · hotel de lujo · restaurante*
5. *reservation* ≈ ____ → *reserva · salida · habitación doble*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **recepción** · 2. **tarjeta de acceso** · 3. **dos camas individuales** · 4. **alojamiento más barato** · 5. **reserva**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. One person → a ____ room. → *single / double / twin*
7. Two people, one big bed → a ____ room. → *double / single / hostel*
8. ____ service until 11 p.m. → *Room / Key / Book*
9. They leave bags at ____. → *reception / platform / gate*
10. Free ____ in the rooms. → *WiFi / journey / fare*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **single** · 7. **double** · 8. **Room** · 9. **reception** · 10. **WiFi**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Double room* at Blue Hotel reading = ____. → a) Ana and Leo · b) single guest only · c) hostel
12. *Check out at 10:30* = ____. → a) dejar habitación · b) llegar · c) book weekend
13. *Key card for room 305* = ____. → a) acceso habitación · b) billete · c) souvenir
14. Booking dialogue: ____ euros per night. → a) 80 · b) 2.50 · c) 21
15. Unit 42 next is ____. → a) Holiday Activities U43 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U43

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) Ana and Leo** · 12. **a) dejar habitación** · 13. **a) acceso habitación** · 14. **a) 80** · 15. **a) Holiday Activities U43**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: At the Blue Hotel

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-42/reading-hotel.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: At the Blue Hotel"></audio>

> Ana and Leo book a double room at the Blue Hotel. Breakfast is included. They check in at 3 p.m. The reception gives them a key card for room 305. There is free WiFi and room service until 11 p.m. The next morning they have breakfast downstairs. They check out at 10:30 and leave their bags at reception until their train.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. They book a ____ room. → a) Double · b) Single · c) Twin only
2. Breakfast is ____. → a) Included · b) Not available · c) At the gate
3. Check-in time? → a) 3 p.m. · b) 10:30 · c) 11 p.m.
4. Room number? → a) 305 · b) 214 · c) 7
5. They check out at ____. → a) 10:30 · b) 3 p.m. · c) 8 a.m.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Double** · 2. **a) Included** · 3. **a) 3 p.m.** · 4. **a) 305** · 5. **a) 10:30**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Room service until ____. → a) 11 p.m. · b) 3 p.m. · c) 8 a.m.
7. Breakfast where? → a) Downstairs · b) At the airport · c) On the beach
8. Bags left at ____. → a) Reception · b) Platform 4 · c) Green Park
9. Until their ____. → a) Train · b) Concert · c) Forecast
10. Matches unit 42: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only travel

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) 11 p.m.** · 7. **a) Downstairs** · 8. **a) Reception** · 9. **a) Train** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Book a double room* = ____. → a) reservar · b) get to · c) go sightseeing
12. *Check in at 3 p.m.* = ____. → a) registrarse · b) salir · c) arrive in
13. *Key card* = ____. → a) tarjeta habitación · b) ticket · c) timetable
14. *There is free WiFi* = ____. → a) Is there…? pattern · b) by bus · c) will rain
15. Hotel text writing uses ____. → a) book/check in/out · b) only phrasals · c) only perfect

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) reservar** · 12. **a) registrarse** · 13. **a) tarjeta habitación** · 14. **a) Is there…? pattern** · 15. **a) book/check in/out**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Booking

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-42/listening-hotel.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Booking"></audio>

> Guest: Hi. I'd like to book a room for next weekend.  
> Reception: How many nights?  
> Guest: Two. Do you have a single room?  
> Reception: Yes. Breakfast included. 80 euros per night.  
> Guest: Perfect. Is there a lift?  
> Reception: Yes. And free WiFi.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

2. For ____. → a) Next weekend · b) Last summer · c) Every day
3. How many nights? → a) Two · b) One · c) Three
4. Room type? → a) Single · b) Double · c) Twin
5. Price per night? → a) 80 euros · b) £2.50 · c) Free

<summary>Ver solución</summary>

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Breakfast ____. → a) Included · b) Not included · c) At platform 4
7. Is there a ____. → a) Lift · b) Storm · c) Gate
8. Also free ____. → a) WiFi · b) Ticket · c) Souvenir
9. Guest says ____. → a) Perfect · b) Never · c) Windy
10. Unit 42 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

13. *Is there a lift?* = ____. → a) Is there…? · b) arrive at · c) by train

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I'd like to ____ a room. → *book / check / stay*
2. We'd like to ____ in, please. → *check / book / take*
3. Is breakfast ____? → *included / include / book*
4. What time is ____? → *check-out / check-in / book*
5. ____ there WiFi? → *Is / Are / Do*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **book** · 2. **check** · 3. **included** · 4. **check-out** · 5. **Is**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. book / to / I'd / like / a / room / double
7. there / Is / WiFi / free / ?
8. check / We'd / to / like / in

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I'd like to book a double room.
7. Is there free WiFi?
8. We'd like to check in.

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Una frase con *I'd like to book…*
10. Una frase con *check in* o *check out*.
11. Una frase con *single* o *double room*.
12. Una frase con *Is breakfast included?* o *Is there WiFi?*
13. Una frase con *reception* o *key card*.
14. Corrige: *I listen music in the room.*
15. Diálogo de recepción (8–10 líneas) con estructuras de alojamiento.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. I'd like to book a double room for two nights.
10. They check in at 3 p.m. / They check out at 10:30.
11. Do you have a single room? / Ana and Leo book a double room.
12. Is breakfast included? / Is there WiFi in the rooms?
13. The reception gives them a key card for room 305.
14. I **listen to music** in the room.
15. Ana and Leo book a double room at the Blue Hotel. Breakfast is included. They check in at 3 p.m. The reception gives them a key card for room 305. There is free WiFi and room service until 11 p.m. The next morning they have breakfast downstairs. They check out at 10:30 and leave their bags at reception until their train.

</details>
