## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** I think… / I don't think…; In my opinion…; I agree (with you); I don't think so

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ___ the film is great. → *think / am think / thinking*

2. I don't ___ it's funny. → *think / thinking / thought*

3. In my ___, it's too long. → *opinion / agree / think*

4. I agree ___ you. → *with / to / for*

5. I don't think ___. → *so / yes / that so*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **think** · 2. **think** · 3. **opinion** · 4. **with** · 5. **so**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *I am agree* is ____. → a) wrong → I agree · b) correct · c) I agree you

7. *I agree with you* = ____. → a) acordar · b) discrepar · c) offer

8. *In my opinion,* needs ____. → a) coma often · b) always that after · c) only Past

9. *I don't think so* = ____. → a) discrepar · b) acordar · c) Can I help

10. Unit 53 follows ____. → a) Offers U52 · b) Module 5 only · c) skip U54

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) wrong → I agree** · 7. **a) acordar** · 8. **a) coma often** · 9. **a) discrepar** · 10. **a) Offers U52**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I am agree with you.*

12. *I agree you.*

13. *I think so not.*

14. *In my opinion that it's boring.*

15. *According to me, it's great.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I agree with you.
12. I agree with you.
13. I don't think so.
14. In my opinion, it's boring.
15. In my opinion, / For me, it's great.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** I think, In my opinion, I agree, I disagree, What do you think?, boring, brilliant, catchy

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *brilliant* ≈ ____ → *genial/brillante · aburrido · demasiado largo*

2. *boring* ≈ ____ → *aburrido · emocionante · genial*

3. *catchy* ≈ ____ → *pegadizo (música) · lento · caro*

4. *exciting* ≈ ____ → *emocionante · aburrido · lento*

5. *worth watching* ≈ ____ → *merece la pena verlo · demasiado largo · no lo creo*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **genial/brillante** · 2. **aburrido** · 3. **pegadizo (música)** · 4. **emocionante** · 5. **merece la pena verlo**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. What do you ___ of this restaurant? → *think / agree / opinion*

7. The actors are ___. → *brilliant / brilliantly / brilliance*

8. Episode one is ___. → *slow / slowly / slowness*

9. The ending was ___. → *amazing / amazingly / amaze*

10. Some say it's ___ — I don't think so. → *boring / bore / bored*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **think** · 7. **brilliant** · 8. **slow** · 9. **amazing** · 10. **boring**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *What do you think?* = ____. → a) pedir opinión · b) offer · c) recycle

12. Forum says actors are ____. → a) brilliant · b) checkout · c) platform

13. Lara says *I'm not sure* = ____. → a) duda · b) I agree always · c) No thanks

14. Opinion text uses ____. → a) I think / In my opinion / I agree · b) only Can I · c) only There is

15. Unit 53 next is ____. → a) Feelings U54 · b) Module 5 only · c) skip U54

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) pedir opinión** · 12. **a) brilliant** · 13. **a) duda** · 14. **a) I think / In my opinion / I agree** · 15. **a) Feelings U54**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Forum review

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-53/reading-opinions.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Forum review"></audio>

> What do you think of the new series? In my opinion, episode one is slow, but the story is interesting. I think the actors are brilliant. Some people say it's boring — I don't think so. I agree that it's a bit long, but it's worth watching.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Topic: new ___. → a) series · b) receipt · c) platform

2. Episode one is ___. → a) slow · b) sunny · c) cheaper

3. Actors are ___. → a) brilliant · b) foggy · c) shy

4. Some say it's ___. → a) boring · b) checkout · c) gate

5. Writer agrees it's a bit ___. → a) long · b) short always · c) free

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) series** · 2. **a) slow** · 3. **a) brilliant** · 4. **a) boring** · 5. **a) long**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Writer says *I don't think so* about ____. → a) boring · b) brilliant · c) slow only

7. *Worth watching* = ____. → a) merece la pena · b) demasiado caro · c) recycle

8. *In my opinion* in text = ____. → a) opinión · b) offer · c) request

9. Story is ____. → a) interesting · b) receipt · c) platform

10. Matches unit 53: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) boring** · 7. **a) merece la pena** · 8. **a) opinión** · 9. **a) interesting** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *What do you think of* = ____. → a) pedir opinión · b) Can I help · c) There is

12. *I agree that it's a bit long* = ____. → a) acuerdo parcial · b) I don't think so · c) Could you

13. Forum uses ____. → a) opinion phrases · b) only transport · c) only health

14. *I think the actors are brilliant* = ____. → a) I think + clause · b) I am think · c) I agree you

15. Opinion writing uses ____. → a) I think / In my opinion / I agree · b) only articles · c) only Shall we

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) pedir opinión** · 12. **a) acuerdo parcial** · 13. **a) opinion phrases** · 14. **a) I think + clause** · 15. **a) I think / In my opinion / I agree**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: After a film

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-53/listening-opinions.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: After a film"></audio>

> Lara: What do you think of the film?
> Ben: I think it was exciting.
> Lara: I agree. The ending was amazing.
> Ben: Do you agree that it was too long?
> Lara: I'm not sure. In my opinion, it was fine.
> Ben: Fair enough!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Ben thinks film was ___. → a) exciting · b) boring · c) foggy

2. Lara ___. → a) agrees · b) I don't think so always · c) Can I help

3. Ending was ___. → a) amazing · b) receipt · c) platform

4. Ben asks if it was too ___. → a) long · b) cheap · c) sunny

5. Lara says in her opinion it was ___. → a) fine · b) terrible always · c) never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) exciting** · 2. **a) agrees** · 3. **a) amazing** · 4. **a) long** · 5. **a) fine**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. *I'm not sure* = ____. → a) duda · b) I agree · c) Yes please

7. *Fair enough!* = ____. → a) aceptar diferencia · b) I disagree rude · c) offer

8. *Do you agree that…?* = ____. → a) preguntar acuerdo · b) offer · c) recycle

9. Dialogue about ____. → a) film opinion · b) shopping · c) transport only

10. Unit 53 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) duda** · 7. **a) aceptar diferencia** · 8. **a) preguntar acuerdo** · 9. **a) film opinion** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *I think it was exciting* → a) I think… · b) I am think · c) I agree you

12. *I agree* → a) acordar · b) I don't think so · c) Can I

13. *In my opinion, it was fine* → a) In my opinion… · b) In my opinion that · c) According to me only

14. Shadow and note ____. → a) opinion phrases · b) only some · c) only Who

15. U53 next unit is ____. → a) Feelings U54 · b) Module 5 only · c) U52 skip

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) I think…** · 12. **a) acordar** · 13. **a) In my opinion…** · 14. **a) opinion phrases** · 15. **a) Feelings U54**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ___ it's a good idea. → *think / am think / thinking*

2. In my ___, we should stay. → *opinion / agree / think*

3. I agree ___ you. → *with / to / for*

4. I don't think ___. → *so / yes / that so*

5. What do you ___ of this film? → *think / agree / opinion*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **think** · 2. **opinion** · 3. **with** · 4. **so** · 5. **think**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. think / I / it's / great

7. opinion / In / my / it's / long / too

8. with / I / agree / you

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I think it's great.
7. In my opinion, it's too long.
8. I agree with you.

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Una opinión con *I think…*.

10. Una opinión con *In my opinion…*.

11. Una frase con *I agree*.

12. Una frase con *I don't think so*.

13. Pregunta *What do you think?*

14. Corrige: *I am agree with you.*

15. Escribe una mini reseña (8–10 líneas) sobre una película o serie.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. I think the film is great.
10. In my opinion, it's too long.
11. I agree with you.
12. I don't think so.
13. What do you think of this series?
14. I agree with you.
15. What do you think of the new series? In my opinion, episode one is slow, but the story is interesting. I think the actors are brilliant. Some people say it's boring — I don't think so. I agree that it's a bit long, but it's worth watching.

</details>
