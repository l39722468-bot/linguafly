  - question: ¿Cuándo uso Present Perfect y cuándo Past Simple?
    answer: >-
      Present Perfect para experiencias o resultados sin decir cuándo exacto

  - question: ¿Puedo decir I have been to Paris last year?
    answer: >-
      No. Last year es un tiempo acabado: usa Past Simple — I went to Paris last

En la [Unidad 14](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-14-present-perfect-just) dijiste *I have just…*. En la **Unidad 15 del curso A2** (*Present Perfect vs Past Simple*) aprendes a **elegir el tiempo**: experiencia / resultado cercano vs momento concreto del pasado.

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 15 — Present Perfect vs Past Simple](/curso-a2/unit-15)  
> **Antes:** [U14 — Just](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-14-present-perfect-just)

---

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 15 (A2)

- Usar **Present Perfect** para experiencias sin fecha (*I have been to…*).
- Usar **Past Simple** con momento concreto (*I went… last year*).
- Reconocer **marcadores**: *ever / never / already / yet / just* vs *yesterday / last… / ago*.
- Evitar mezclas como *I have been to Paris last year*.
- Leer y escuchar textos que **mezclan** ambos tiempos.

![Present Perfect vs Past Simple A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-15/a2-unit15-pp-vs-ps.png)

---

## 1. Idea clave

| Tiempo | ¿Cuándo? | Pregunta mental |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Present Perfect** | experiencia / resultado ligado al presente; **sin** momento concreto | ¿Ha pasado alguna vez? ¿Ya / aún / acaba de…? |
| **Past Simple** | acción **acabada** en un momento concreto | ¿Cuándo? (*yesterday, last week, ago…*) |

Regla de oro A2: **mira el marcador de tiempo**. Él casi siempre elige el verbo.

---

## 2. Present Perfect (repaso rápido)

Forma: **have/has + past participle**

> I **have been** to many countries.  
> I **have never been** to Australia.  
> **Have** you **ever been** to Japan?  
> I **have already** booked my next trip.  
> I **have just** arrived.

Marcadores típicos: **ever, never, already, yet, just** (+ *recently* opcional).

## 3. Past Simple (repaso rápido)

Forma: verbo en pasado (*went, visited, ate, saw, read, finished…*) o *did/didn't* en negativas y preguntas.

> Last year I **went** to Japan.  
> I **visited** Tokyo and Kyoto.  
> Two months ago I **travelled** to Italy.  
> I **ate** pizza in Naples.  
> Yesterday she **saw** him.

---

## 4. Marcadores: la clave del contraste

![Marcadores Present Perfect y Past Simple](/blog/curso-a2/unit-15/a2-unit15-time-markers.png)

| Con Present Perfect | Con Past Simple |
| :--- | :--- |
| ever | yesterday |
| never | last week / last month / last year |
| already | … ago (*two days ago, three weeks ago*) |
| yet | in 2020 / on Monday |
| just | when I was a child *(momento cerrado)* |

Parejas típicas:

| Present Perfect | Past Simple |
| :--- | :--- |
| I **have been** to Paris. | I **went** to Paris **last year**. |
| **Have** you **ever** eaten sushi? | **Did** you eat sushi **yesterday**? |
| I **have never** read sci-fi. | I **read** a novel **last month**. |
| I **have already** bought the book. | I **bought** the book **three weeks ago**. |

---

## 5. Errores que hay que evitar

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I have been to Paris last year. | I **went** to Paris last year. | *last year* → Past Simple |
| I went to Paris. *(si quieres experiencia sin fecha)* | I **have been** to Paris. | experiencia → Present Perfect |
| Did you ever go to Japan? *(prioridad A2)* | **Have** you **ever been** to Japan? | *ever* → Present Perfect |
| I have saw him yesterday. | I **saw** him yesterday. | *yesterday* + Past Simple; participio mal |
| She has visited Tokyo last year. | She **visited** Tokyo last year. | *last year* → Past Simple |
| I have finished my book three weeks ago. | I **finished** my book three weeks ago. | *ago* → Past Simple |

> Hi! I am Nina. I love reading. I have read many books in my life. I have never read a science fiction book but I want to try one. Last month I read a great novel by Jane Austen. It was beautiful. Three weeks ago I finished a mystery book. I really enjoyed it. I have already bought my next book for the holidays. I am excited!

3. ¿Cuándo terminó el misterio? → *three weeks ago* (Past Simple)  
4. ¿Qué ha hecho ya para las vacaciones? → *bought her next book* (*already* + Present Perfect)

## 9. Vocabulario y marcadores (del curso)

| Inglés | Idea / uso |
| :--- | :--- |
| ever / never | alguna vez / nunca → Present Perfect |
| already / yet / just | ya / aún / acabar de → Present Perfect |
| yesterday | ayer → Past Simple |
| last week / month / year | la semana / mes / año pasado → Past Simple |
| … ago | hace… → Past Simple |
| travel / trip | viajar / viaje |
| visit | visitar |
| book (a trip / a book) | reservar / libro *(según contexto)* |
| novel / mystery / science fiction | novela / misterio / ciencia ficción |

Participios / pasados útiles: *been, gone, read, bought, booked, went, visited, travelled, ate, saw, finished, enjoyed*.

## 11. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige el tiempo

1. I ____ to Paris last year. *(went / have been)*  
2. I ____ to Paris. It was amazing. *(went / have been)* — *sin fecha*  
3. She ____ him yesterday. *(saw / has seen)*  
4. ____ you ever ____ to Japan? *(Did… go / Have… been)*  
5. I ____ this car two years ago. *(bought / have bought)*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **went** · 2. **have been** · 3. **saw** · 4. **Have… been** · 5. **bought**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Marcador → tiempo

Di Present Perfect (PP) o Past Simple (PS):

1. yesterday  
2. ever  
3. last week  
4. already  
5. three weeks ago  
6. never  
7. just  
8. last year

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 PS · 2 PP · 3 PS · 4 PP · 5 PS · 6 PP · 7 PP · 8 PS
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Completa a Jake

I ____ been to many countries. I have ____ been to Australia. Last year I ____ to Japan. Two months ago I ____ to Italy. I ____ pizza in Naples. I have ____ booked my next trip to Spain.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**have** · **never** · **went** · **travelled** · **ate** · **already**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Completa a Nina

I ____ read many books. I have ____ read a science fiction book. Last month I ____ a great novel. Three weeks ago I ____ a mystery book. I have ____ bought my next book.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**have** · **never** · **read** · **finished** · **already**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrige

1. *I have been to Japan last year.*  
2. *I went to many countries in my life.* *(si quieres experiencia)*  
3. *Did you ever been to Italy?*  
4. *She has saw him yesterday.*  
5. *I have finished the book three weeks ago.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **went** to Japan last year.  
2. I **have been** to many countries in my life.  
3. **Have** you ever **been** to Italy?  
4. She **saw** him yesterday.  
5. I **finished** the book three weeks ago.
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Transforma

1. I visited Tokyo last year. → experiencia sin fecha (*have been / have visited*)  
2. I have been to Italy. → añade *two months ago*  
3. Have you ever read Jane Austen? → pregunta sobre *last month*  
4. I bought a book. → con *already*  
5. I arrived a moment ago. → con *just*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **have visited** / **have been to** Tokyo.  
2. I **travelled / went** to Italy **two months ago**.  
3. **Did** you read Jane Austen **last month**?  
4. I **have already** bought a book.  
5. I **have just** arrived.
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Empareja

1. ever  
2. yesterday  
3. already  
4. … ago  
5. never  

a) Past Simple · b) Present Perfect (experiencia) · c) Present Perfect (progreso) · d) Past Simple · e) Present Perfect (negativa de experiencia)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **c** · 4 → **d** · 5 → **e**
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Elige la correcta

1. ____  
   a) I have been to Paris last year. · b) I went to Paris last year. · c) I have went to Paris last year.  
2. ____  
   a) Have you ever been to Japan? · b) Did you ever been to Japan? · c) Have you ever went to Japan?  
3. ____  
   a) I have already booked my trip. · b) I already booked have my trip. · c) I have booked already my trip. *(prioridad mid)*  
4. ____  
   a) Two months ago I have travelled to Italy. · b) Two months ago I travelled to Italy. · c) Two months ago I have been travel to Italy.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b** · 2. **a** · 3. **a** · 4. **b**
</details>

## 13. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Pregunta: **¿Hay fecha concreta?** Sí → Past Simple. No / ever-never-already-yet-just → Present Perfect.  
2. Practica parejas: *I have been to X* / *Last year I went to X*.  
3. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 15](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-15-present-perfect-vs-past-simple-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 15 del curso A2](/curso-a2/unit-15).
