## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 58 (A2)

- Repaso: **There is / There are**
- Ubicación: **located in**, **situated**, preposiciones de lugar
- Vocabulario: **museum**, **library**, **stadium**, **hospital**, **square**
- Superlativos con lugares (*the tallest*, *the most famous*)
- Describir un lugar o un edificio

## 1. Vocabulario: lugares y edificios

| Palabra | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **museum** | museo | There's a modern **museum**. |
| **library** | biblioteca | The **library** opens at 9. |
| **stadium** | estadio | The **stadium** is huge. |
| **hospital** | hospital | The **hospital** is near the station. |
| **square** | plaza | We meet in the main **square**. |
| **bridge** | puente | There's an old **bridge**. |
| **tower** | torre | The **tower** is very tall. |
| **station** | estación | The train **station** is busy. |

También: **park**, **church**, **theatre / theater**, **shopping centre**, **city centre / downtown**.

---

## 2. *There is / There are* (repaso)

| Forma | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **There is** | singular / incontable | **There is** a museum. |
| **There are** | plural | **There are** three parks. |
| **Is there…?** | pregunta singular | **Is there** a library nearby? |
| **Are there…?** | pregunta plural | **Are there** any cafés? |
| **There isn't / aren't** | negativa | **There isn't** a stadium here. |

> **There is** a hospital next to the park.  
> **There are** two bridges over the river.

## 3. Ubicación: *located in*, *situated*, preposiciones

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| is **located in** | The library is **located in** the city centre. |
| is **situated** near/on… | The hotel is **situated** near the river. |
| **next to / opposite / between** | The café is **opposite** the museum. |
| **in / on / at** | **in** the square / **on** the corner / **at** the station |

> The stadium is **located in** the north of the city.  
> The hospital is **next to** the bus station.

## 4. Superlativos con lugares

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| the + -est | the **tallest** building |
| the most + adj | the **most famous** museum |
| in + place | the oldest church **in** the town |

> It's the **biggest** square in the city.  
> This is the **most modern** library here.

## 6. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura — A city guide

Welcome to Riverton. **There is** a beautiful main **square** in the centre. The art **museum** is **located** next to the square, and **there are** two cafés opposite it. The **library** is the **oldest** building in town. The football **stadium** is in the south. The **hospital** is near the station — easy to find.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-58/reading-guide.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: A city guide"></audio>

### Diálogo — Asking for places

**Tourist:** Excuse me. **Is there** a **library** near here?  
**Local:** Yes. It's **located in** Green Street, opposite the **museum**.  
**Tourist:** And **are there** any good cafés?  
**Local:** **There are** three in the main **square**.  
**Tourist:** Perfect. Is the **stadium** far?  
**Local:** No — it's the **biggest** building in the south.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-58/dialogue-tourist.mp3" title="🔊 Dialogue: Asking for places"></audio>

## 7. Errores frecuentes

1. ~~There is two museums.~~ → **There are** two museums.  
2. ~~It is locate in…~~ → **It is located in…**  
3. ~~the more tall building~~ → **the tallest** building  
4. ~~in the station~~ *(a menudo)* → **at the station**  
5. ~~Is there museums?~~ → **Are there** any museums?  
6. ~~There have a park.~~ → **There is** a park.

## 8. Ejercicios (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Empareja

1. museum  
2. library  
3. stadium  
4. square  
5. hospital  

a) open space in a town where people meet  
b) place for books and study  
c) place for sports events  
d) place for art/history exhibitions  
e) place for medical care

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1 → d  
2 → b  
3 → c  
4 → a  
5 → e  

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — *There is / There are*

1. ___ a stadium near the river.  
2. ___ three bridges in the city.  
3. ___ any hospitals here?  
4. ___ a library opposite the square.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. There is  
2. There are  
3. Are there  
4. There is  

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Ubicación

1. The museum is located ___ the centre.  
2. The café is ___ the library. *(enfrente)*  
3. We meet ___ the main square.  
4. The hotel is situated ___ the river.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. in  
2. opposite  
3. in  
4. near / by / on *(near/by más natural en A2)*  

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Superlativos

1. This is the ___ (tall) building in town.  
2. It's the ___ (famous) museum in the country.  
3. That square is the ___ (big) in the city.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. tallest  
2. most famous  
3. biggest  

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrige el error

1. There is two libraries.  
2. The hospital is locate in the north.  
3. Is there any cafés?  
4. It's the more old church.  
5. There have a stadium.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. There are two libraries.  
2. The hospital is located in the north.  
3. Are there any cafés?  
4. It's the oldest church.  
5. There is a stadium.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Traduce al inglés

1. Hay un museo en el centro.  
2. Hay dos parques cerca.  
3. La biblioteca está situada en Green Street.  
4. Es el edificio más alto de la ciudad.  
5. ¿Hay un hospital cerca?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. There is a museum in the centre.  
2. There are two parks nearby / near here.  
3. The library is located / situated in Green Street.  
4. It's the tallest building in the city.  
5. Is there a hospital nearby?

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Completa el diálogo

**A:** ___ there a museum near here?  
**B:** Yes. It's ___ in the main square.  
**A:** ___ there any cafés?  
**B:** Yes — it's the ___ square in town, so there are many.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

Is  
located (or situated)  
Are  
biggest / most famous / busiest  

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Ordena

1. is / There / a / library  
2. located / is / The / museum / in / the / centre  
3. tallest / the / It's / building

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. There is a library.  
2. The museum is located in the centre.  
3. It's the tallest building.  

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Guía True / False

> There is a beautiful main square in the centre. The art museum is located next to the square.

1. The square is in the centre.  
2. The museum is far from the square.  
3. The text mentions an art museum.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. True  
2. False  
3. True  

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu guía local (8–12 líneas)

Describe tu barrio o ciudad. Incluye:

- 2 *There is / There are*  
- 1 *located in / next to / opposite*  
- 2 lugares (*museum / library / square…*)  
- 1 superlativo

**Modelo:** la lectura de la sección 6.
