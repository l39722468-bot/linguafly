## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** Can I help you? / I'm looking for… / How much…?; refund / exchange; cheaper / more expensive

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ___ I help you? → *Can / Do / Am*

2. I'm ___ for a blue jacket. → *looking / look / looked*

3. How ___ is this shirt? → *much / many / more*

4. I'll ___ it, thanks. → *take / takes / taking*

5. This one is ___ than that. → *cheaper / more cheap / cheap*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Can** · 2. **looking** · 3. **much** · 4. **take** · 5. **cheaper**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *How much is it?* (precio): ____. → a) correcto · b) How many is it? · c) How much are it?

7. *I'm looking for…* (no *I look for* en tienda): ____. → a) correcto · b) I look for · c) I am look for

8. *Refund* = ____. → a) devolución del dinero · b) cambio de talla · c) probador

9. *Exchange* = ____. → a) cambiar el producto · b) solo dinero · c) caja

10. Unit 46 follows ____. → a) Entertainment U45 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U47

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) correcto** · 7. **a) correcto** · 8. **a) devolución del dinero** · 9. **a) cambiar el producto** · 10. **a) Entertainment U45**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *How many is this bag?*

12. *I look for a present.*

13. *This is more cheap than that.*

14. *I want refund.*

15. *Can I help to you?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. How **much** is this bag?
12. I'm **looking for** a present.
13. This is **cheaper** than that.
14. I'd like a **refund**, please.
15. Can I help **you**?

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** department store, checkout, receipt, refund, exchange, fitting room

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *checkout* ≈ ____ → *caja · probador · recibo*

2. *receipt* ≈ ____ → *ticket de compra · farmacia · talla*

3. *refund* ≈ ____ → *devolución de dinero · cambio · estantería*

4. *fitting room* ≈ ____ → *probador · caja · supermercado*

5. *exchange* ≈ ____ → *cambiar artículo · devolver dinero · pagar*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **caja** · 2. **ticket de compra** · 3. **devolución de dinero** · 4. **probador** · 5. **cambiar artículo**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. Keep your ___ for a refund. → *receipt / checkout / bakery*

7. Where is the ___? *(probar ropa)* → *fitting room / pharmacy / platform*

8. I'd like an ___, please. *(otra talla)* → *exchange / headache / forecast*

9. There's a queue at the ___. → *checkout / concert / souvenir*

10. A big ___ in the centre. → *department store / documentary / degree*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **receipt** · 7. **fitting room** · 8. **exchange** · 9. **checkout** · 10. **department store**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *How much* for price: ____. → a) yes · b) How many only · c) How much are it always

12. *Cheaper* (not *more cheap*): ____. → a) correcto · b) more cheap · c) too cheap only

13. Policy: bring ___ within 14 days. → a) receipt · b) platform · c) podcast

14. Grey jacket is ____. → a) €45 · b) Included · c) Foggy

15. Unit 46 next is ____. → a) Health U47 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U47

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) yes** · 12. **a) correcto** · 13. **a) receipt** · 14. **a) €45** · 15. **a) Health U47**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Store policy

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-46/reading-shopping.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Store policy"></audio>

> Our **department store** is open every day from 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. You can pay at the **checkout** by card or cash. If you need a **refund** or an **exchange**, bring your **receipt** within 14 days. Clothes must have the label. We're happy to help — just ask at customer service.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Store open from 10 a.m. to ____. → a) 9 p.m. · b) 9 a.m. · c) midnight only

2. Pay at the ____. → a) checkout · b) fitting room · c) platform

3. Need refund → bring ____. → a) receipt · b) podcast · c) forecast

4. Within ___ days. → a) 14 · b) 40 · c) 305

5. Clothes must have the ____. → a) label · b) degree · c) gate

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) 9 p.m.** · 2. **a) checkout** · 3. **a) receipt** · 4. **a) 14** · 5. **a) label**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Pay by card or ____. → a) cash · b) train · c) drama

7. Ask at customer ____. → a) service · b) weather · c) museum

8. *Department store* = ____. → a) grandes almacenes · b) farmacia · c) playa

9. *Exchange* in policy = ____. → a) refund or exchange · b) only watch · c) only will

10. Matches unit 46: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) cash** · 7. **a) service** · 8. **a) grandes almacenes** · 9. **a) refund or exchange** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Bring your receipt* = ____. → a) ticket de compra · b) weather · c) check in

12. *Within 14 days* = ____. → a) plazo de devolución · b) solo futuro · c) only Past

13. *Refund or exchange* = ____. → a) devolución/cambio · b) only listen · c) only Who

14. Policy uses ____. → a) shopping structures · b) only did · c) only phrasals

15. Shopping text writing uses ____. → a) Can I help / How much · b) only articles · c) only should

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) ticket de compra** · 12. **a) plazo de devolución** · 13. **a) devolución/cambio** · 14. **a) shopping structures** · 15. **a) Can I help / How much**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: At the clothes shop

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-46/listening-shopping.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: At the clothes shop"></audio>

> **Assistant:** Good morning. **Can I help you?**
> **Customer:** Yes. **I'm looking for** a jacket.
> **Assistant:** What size?
> **Customer:** Medium. **How much is** this one?
> **Assistant:** It's €59. That grey one is **cheaper** — €45.
> **Customer:** I'll take the grey one, please.
> **Assistant:** Perfect. The **checkout** is over there.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Customer wants a ____. → a) jacket · b) hotel · c) train

2. Size is ____. → a) Medium · b) Sunny · c) Eight

3. Grey one costs ____. → a) €45 · b) €305 · c) Included

4. This one costs ____. → a) €59 · b) €12 · c) Free only

5. Checkout is ____. → a) over there · b) in Lisbon · c) last year

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) jacket** · 2. **a) Medium** · 3. **a) €45** · 4. **a) €59** · 5. **a) over there**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Grey jacket is ____. → a) cheaper · b) more cheap · c) included

7. Customer says *I'll take* the ____. → a) grey one · b) receipt · c) platform

8. *Can I help you?* = ____. → a) ofrecer ayuda · b) weather · c) check out hotel

9. *How much is this one?* = ____. → a) preguntar precio · b) listen music · c) arrive at

10. Unit 46 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) cheaper** · 7. **a) grey one** · 8. **a) ofrecer ayuda** · 9. **a) preguntar precio** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *I'm looking for a jacket* → a) I'm looking for… · b) I look for · c) prefer than

12. *That grey one is cheaper* → a) comparativo · b) superlativo only · c) will

13. *I'll take it* → a) comprar / quedarse con · b) listen to · c) arrive in

14. Shadow and note ____. → a) Can I help / How much / cheaper · b) only some · c) only Who

15. U46 next unit is ____. → a) Health U47 · b) Module 6 only · c) U45 skip

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) I'm looking for…** · 12. **a) comparativo** · 13. **a) comprar / quedarse con** · 14. **a) Can I help / How much / cheaper** · 15. **a) Health U47**
