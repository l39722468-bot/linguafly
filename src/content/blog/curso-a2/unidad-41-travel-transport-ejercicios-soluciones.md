## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** by + transporte; take the; get to; arrive at / arrive in; on foot.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I go ____ bus. → *by / on / the*
2. ____ the train to the airport. → *Take / By / Get*
3. How do I ____ the station? → *get to / arrive in / by*
4. We ____ at the hotel at 10. → *arrived / got / took*
5. They ____ in Madrid yesterday. → *arrived / got / took*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **by** · 2. **Take** · 3. **get to** · 4. **arrived** · 5. **arrived**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *By bus* (sin *the*): ____. → a) correcto · b) by the bus · c) take bus
7. *Get to* = ____. → a) llegar / cómo llegar · b) solo ciudad · c) on foot only
8. *Arrive at* + estación/aeropuerto: ____. → a) yes · b) arrive in station · c) get in
9. *On foot* (no *by foot*): ____. → a) correcto · b) by foot · c) in foot
10. Unit 41 starts ____. → a) Módulo 5 · b) Módulo 4 only · c) Module 6

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) correcto** · 7. **a) llegar / cómo llegar** · 8. **a) yes** · 9. **a) correcto** · 10. **a) Módulo 5**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I go by the train.*
12. *Take bus 12.*
13. *We arrived to the station.*
14. *They arrived at France.*
15. *How I get to the hotel?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I go **by train**.
12. **Take the bus** 12. / Take bus number 12.
13. We **arrived at** the station.
14. They **arrived in** France.
15. **How do I** get to the hotel?

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** airport, station, platform, ticket, fare, journey, timetable, delay.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *platform* ≈ ____ → *andén · tarifa · retraso*
2. *fare* ≈ ____ → *precio del trayecto · billete · horario*
3. *journey* ≈ ____ → *trayecto · puerta · parada*
4. *timetable* ≈ ____ → *horario · aeropuerto · equipaje*
5. *delay* ≈ ____ → *retraso · llegada · salida*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **andén** · 2. **precio del trayecto** · 3. **trayecto** · 4. **horario** · 5. **retraso**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. The train is on ____ 3. → *platform / journey / fare*
7. I need a return ____. → *ticket / delay / station*
8. What's the ____ to the centre? → *fare / platform / gate*
9. Check the ____. → *timetable / luggage / passenger*
10. There is a 20-minute ____. → *delay / ticket / platform*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **platform** · 7. **ticket** · 8. **fare** · 9. **timetable** · 10. **delay**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Meet me at the airport* uses ____. → a) at · b) in · c) by
12. *Get off at the square* = ____. → a) bajar en la parada · b) llegar a ciudad · c) book room
13. *Trip* vs *journey*: trayecto enfatizado = ____. → a) journey · b) only travel verb · c) fare
14. Reading Leo prefers ____. → a) by train · b) by the bus · c) on bus
15. Unit 41 next is ____. → a) Accommodation U42 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U42

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) at** · 12. **a) bajar en la parada** · 13. **a) journey** · 14. **a) by train** · 15. **a) Accommodation U42**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: To the airport

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-41/reading-travel.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: To the airport"></audio>

> Leo needs to get to the airport. He takes the train from the city station. The journey takes 35 minutes. He buys a ticket at the machine. The train leaves from platform 4. There is no delay today. He arrives at the airport at 8 and looks for the gate. He prefers travelling by train — it's faster than going by bus.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Leo needs to get to the ____. → a) Airport · b) Museum · c) Hotel
2. He takes the ____. → a) Train · b) Bus · c) Taxi only
3. Journey duration? → a) 35 minutes · b) 20 minutes · c) One hour
4. Platform number? → a) 4 · b) 3 · c) 7
5. Is there a delay? → a) No · b) Yes — 20 min · c) Yes — all day

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Airport** · 2. **a) Train** · 3. **a) 35 minutes** · 4. **a) 4** · 5. **a) No**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. He buys a ticket at the ____. → a) Machine · b) Gate · c) Platform 7
7. Arrives at the airport at ____. → a) 8 · b) 9 · c) 10
8. He looks for the ____. → a) Gate · b) Key card · c) Souvenir
9. Prefers travelling ____. → a) By train · b) By bus · c) On foot only
10. Matches unit 41: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only weather

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Machine** · 7. **a) 8** · 8. **a) Gate** · 9. **a) By train** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Takes the train* = ____. → a) take the + transport · b) by train only · c) arrive in
12. *Get to the airport* = ____. → a) cómo llegar · b) arrive in city · c) check in
13. *Arrives at the airport* = ____. → a) arrive at + sitio · b) arrive in airport · c) get to Madrid
14. *By train — faster than by bus* compares ____. → a) medios · b) hotel rooms · c) weather
15. Airport text writing uses ____. → a) travel structures · b) only like -ing · c) only will

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) take the + transport** · 12. **a) cómo llegar** · 13. **a) arrive at + sitio** · 14. **a) medios** · 15. **a) travel structures**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: At the station

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-41/listening-travel.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: At the station"></audio>

> Tourist: Excuse me, how do I get to the museum?  
> Clerk: Take the bus number 7 and get off at Green Park.  
> Tourist: How long is the journey?  
> Clerk: About 20 minutes. A single ticket is £2.50.  
> Tourist: Which stop?  
> Clerk: Ask the driver — or check the timetable on the app.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Tourist wants to get to the ____. → a) Museum · b) Airport · c) Hotel
2. Take bus number ____. → a) 7 · b) 10 · c) 12
3. Get off at ____. → a) Green Park · b) The station · c) Room 305
4. Journey about ____. → a) 20 minutes · b) 35 minutes · c) One hour
5. Single ticket price? → a) £2.50 · b) £80 · c) Free

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Museum** · 2. **a) 7** · 3. **a) Green Park** · 4. **a) 20 minutes** · 5. **a) £2.50**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Clerk says check the ____. → a) Timetable on the app · b) Key card · c) Forecast
7. *How do I get to…?* = ____. → a) pregunta dirección · b) book room · c) weather
8. *Take the bus* uses ____. → a) the · b) by only · c) no verb
9. *Which stop?* asks about ____. → a) parada · b) platform · c) gate
10. Unit 41 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Timetable on the app** · 7. **a) pregunta dirección** · 8. **a) the** · 9. **a) parada** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Question: How do I ____ the museum? → a) get to · b) arrive in · c) by
12. Instruction: ____ the bus number 7. → a) Take · b) By · c) Arrive
13. *Get off at Green Park* = ____. → a) bajar en parada · b) check in · c) go sightseeing
14. Shadow and note ____. → a) get to / take the · b) only should · c) only much
15. U41 next unit is ____. → a) Accommodation U42 · b) Module 6 only · c) U31 skip

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) get to** · 12. **a) Take** · 13. **a) bajar en parada** · 14. **a) get to / take the** · 15. **a) Accommodation U42**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I go ____ train. → *by / the / on*
2. ____ the bus to the centre. → *Take / By / Get*
3. How do I ____ the airport? → *get to / arrive in / by*
4. We ____ at the station at 9. → *arrived / got / took*
5. They travelled ____ plane. → *by / on / the*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **by** · 2. **Take** · 3. **get to** · 4. **arrived** · 5. **by**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. to / get / How / I / do / the / station / ?
7. the / Take / bus / and / at / get off / the park
8. at / arrived / We / airport / the / 7

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. How do I get to the station?
7. Take the bus and get off at the park.
8. We arrived at the airport at 7.

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Una frase con *by* + transporte.
10. Una frase con *take the* + transporte.
11. Una frase con *How do I get to…?*
12. Una frase con *arrive at* o *arrive in*.
13. Una frase con *platform*, *ticket* o *journey*.
14. Corrige: *I go by the bus.*
15. Describe cómo llegas al aeropuerto o estación (8–10 líneas) con estructuras de viaje.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. I go by bus. / He prefers travelling by train.
10. Take the train to the airport. / I take the bus to work.
11. How do I get to the museum? / How do I get to the airport?
12. We arrived at the hotel at nine. / They arrived in London yesterday.
13. The journey takes 35 minutes. / He buys a ticket at the machine. / Platform 4.
14. I go **by bus**.
15. Leo needs to get to the airport. He takes the train from the city station. The journey takes 35 minutes. He buys a ticket at the machine. The train leaves from platform 4. There is no delay today. He arrives at the airport at 8 and looks for the gate. He prefers travelling by train — it's faster than going by bus.

</details>
