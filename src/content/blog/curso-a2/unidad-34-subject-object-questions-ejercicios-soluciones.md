## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** subject questions (sin did) vs object questions (con did).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. Who ____ you yesterday? *(sujeto)* → *called / did call / did you call*
2. Who ____ you ____ yesterday? *(objeto)* → *did / call · called / · did / called*
3. What ____? *(sujeto — pasó algo)* → *happened / did happen / did you happen*
4. What ____ you ____? *(objeto)* → *did / see · saw / · did / saw*
5. Who ____ here? *(sujeto — Present)* → *lives / does live / live*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **called** · 2. **did / call** · 3. **happened** · 4. **did / see** · 5. **lives**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Subject question uses ____. → a) no do/does/did · b) always did · c) only -ing
7. *Who did call you?* is ____. → a) wrong · b) correct subject · c) object only
8. Object question: Who ____ you call? → a) did · b) called · c) does called
9. Answer *Tom called me* → ask ____. → a) Who called you? · b) Who did you call?
10. Unit 34 trains ____. → a) who/what/which S vs O · b) only phrasals · c) only some/any

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) no do/does/did** · 7. **a) wrong** · 8. **a) did** · 9. **a) Who called you?** · 10. **a) who/what/which S vs O**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *Who did call you?*
12. *Who you saw at the cinema?*
13. *What did happened?*
14. *Who does lives here?*
15. *What she want?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. Who **called** you?
12. Who **did you see** at the cinema?
13. What **happened**?
14. Who **lives** here?
15. What **does she want**?

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** subject vs object, who/what/which, phone, party, address, cousin.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *subject question* ≈ ____ → *who does the action · who receives · some/any*
2. *object question* ≈ ____ → *who receives action · no did · hate -ing*
3. *Who called you?* type → ____ → *subject · object · quantity*
4. *Who did you call?* type → ____ → *object · subject · separable*
5. *What happened?* type → ____ → *subject · object · offer*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **who does the action** · 2. **who receives action** · 3. **subject** · 4. **object** · 5. **subject**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. Tom called me. → Who ____ me? → *called / did call / did you call*
7. I called Tom. → Who ____ I ____? → *did / call · called / · did / called*
8. An accident happened. → What ____? → *happened / did happen / did you see*
9. I saw an accident. → What ____ I ____? → *did / see · saw / · did / saw*
10. Maria lives here. → Who ____ here? → *lives / does live / live*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **called** · 7. **did / call** · 8. **happened** · 9. **did / see** · 10. **lives**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. Mark S or O: *What did you buy?* → a) O · b) S · c) neither
12. Mark S or O: *Who wants coffee?* → a) S · b) O · c) quantity
13. Trick: look at the ____. → a) answer · b) title only · c) image alt
14. *Which bus did you take?* is ____. → a) object · b) subject always · c) -ing only
15. Unit 34 follows ____. → a) U33 gerunds · b) only U31 · c) only Module 6

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) O** · 12. **a) S** · 13. **a) answer** · 14. **a) object** · 15. **a) U33 gerunds**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: A message

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-34/reading-questions.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: A message"></audio>

> Someone called me yesterday. Who called me? It was Ana. She asked about the party. What did she want? She wanted my address. Who did I tell? I told my brother. What happened later? We went to the café together.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Who called? → a) Ana · b) Brother · c) Someone unknown
2. About what? → a) The party · b) Shopping list · c) Uniform rules
3. What did she want? → a) My address · b) Cappuccino · c) Worksheets
4. Who did I tell? → a) My brother · b) Ana · c) Cousin's partner
5. Later? → a) Went to café together · b) Stayed home · c) Paid now

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Ana** · 2. **a) The party** · 3. **a) My address** · 4. **a) My brother** · 5. **a) Went to café together**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. *Who called me?* is ____. → a) subject Q · b) object Q · c) some/any
7. *What did she want?* is ____. → a) object Q · b) subject Q · c) phrasal only
8. *What happened later?* is ____. → a) subject Q · b) object Q · c) need to
9. Text mixes ____. → a) S and O questions · b) only -ing · c) only separables
10. Matches unit 34: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U35

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) subject Q** · 7. **a) object Q** · 8. **a) subject Q** · 9. **a) S and O questions** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Subject Q: no ____. → a) did/do/does before main verb · b) who/what · c) answer
12. Object Q: ____ + infinitive. → a) did/do/does · b) only -s · c) only -ing
13. Pair practice: Who saw you? / ____. → a) Who did you see? · b) Who called you did? · c) What happened did?
14. Reading helps label ____. → a) S vs O · b) only spelling · c) only articles
15. After object Q use ____. → a) base verb · b) past after did · c) -ing only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) did/do/does before main verb** · 12. **a) did/do/does** · 13. **a) Who did you see?** · 14. **a) S vs O** · 15. **a) base verb**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Short interview

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-34/listening-questions.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Short interview"></audio>

> Who lives in that flat? My cousin. Who did you visit last weekend? My cousin! What happened there? Nothing special — but what did you eat? Homemade pizza. Who made it? My cousin's partner.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Who lives in the flat? → a) My cousin · b) Partner · c) Brother
2. Who visited last weekend? → a) My cousin · b) Ana · c) Boss
3. What happened there? → a) Nothing special · b) Big accident · c) Busy café only
4. What did they eat? → a) Homemade pizza · b) Some bread · c) Only tea
5. Who made the pizza? → a) Cousin's partner · b) My cousin only · c) Teacher

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) My cousin** · 2. **a) My cousin** · 3. **a) Nothing special** · 4. **a) Homemade pizza** · 5. **a) Cousin's partner**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. *Who lives in that flat?* = ____. → a) subject · b) object · c) quantity
7. *Who did you visit?* = ____. → a) object · b) subject · c) hate waiting
8. *Who made it?* asks about ____. → a) subject (maker) · b) object only · c) how much
9. Interview mixes ____. → a) who + what S/O · b) only phrasals · c) only -ing likes
10. Unit 34 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Module 6

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) subject** · 7. **a) object** · 8. **a) subject (maker)** · 9. **a) who + what S/O** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. ~~Who did live there?~~ → ____. → a) Who lives there? · b) Who did you live? · c) Who lives did?
12. Object: What ____ you eat? → a) did · b) happened · c) lives
13. Shadow and mark ____. → a) S or O on each question · b) only nouns · c) only some/any
14. *What happened there?* no ____. → a) did · b) who · c) what
15. U34 next is ____. → a) some/any/much/many · b) skip articles · c) Module 6 only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) Who lives there?** · 12. **a) did** · 13. **a) S or O on each question** · 14. **a) did** · 15. **a) some/any/much/many**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. Who ____ you this morning? *(sujeto)* → *phoned / did phone / did you phone*
2. What ____ he want? *(objeto)* → *did / wanted / does want*
3. Who ____ you ____ after that? *(objeto)* → *did / call · called / · did / called*
4. What ____? *(sujeto)* → *happened / did happen / did you see*
5. Who ____ here? *(sujeto)* → *lives / does live / live*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **phoned** · 2. **did** · 3. **did / call** · 4. **happened** · 5. **lives**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. Who / called / you / yesterday?
7. Who / did / you / call / yesterday?
8. What / did / you / see?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. Who called you yesterday?
7. Who did you call yesterday?
8. What did you see?

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Una subject question con *Who*.
10. Una object question con *Who did…*.
11. Una subject question con *What happened*.
12. Una object question con *What did you…*.
13. Una pregunta con *Which* (objeto).
14. Mini diálogo (4 líneas) mezclando S y O.
15. Cuestionario (8 preguntas): 3 S + 3 O + what + which.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. Who called you?
10. Who did you call?
11. What happened yesterday?
12. What did you buy?
13. Which bus did you take?
14. Who phoned you? My boss. What did he want? The report. Who did you call? Ana.
15. Who lives here? What happened? Who wants coffee? Who did you see? What did you eat? Which train did you take? Who made it? What did she want?

</details>
