En la [Unidad 23](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-23-will-wont-predicciones-promesas) usaste *will* para promesas. En la **Unidad 24 del curso A2** (*Present Continuous for Future*) hablas de **citas y planes ya fijados**: *I'm meeting John tomorrow.*

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 24 — Present Continuous for Future](/curso-a2/unit-24)  
> **Antes:** [U23 — Will / Won't](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-23-will-wont-predicciones-promesas)

---

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 24 (A2)

- Usar **Present Continuous** para planes futuros ya organizados.
- Hablar de **citas**, **reuniones** y **eventos** en la agenda.
- Preguntar *when / where / what time*.
- Usar marcadores: *tomorrow, next week, on Monday, at 3 o'clock*.
- Situar esta forma junto a *going to* y *will* (vista previa de U25).

![Present Continuous for Future A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-24/a2-unit24-pc-future.png)

---

## 1. Idea clave: plan fijado = agenda

| Situación | Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cita / quedada | *am/is/are* + *-ing* | I **am meeting** John tomorrow. |
| Vuelo / viaje organizado | *is* + *-ing* | She **is flying** to Paris next week. |
| Cena / partido planificado | *are* + *-ing* | They **are having** dinner on Friday. |

Si está en tu **diary** (hora, día, persona o sitio), Present Continuous encaja muy bien.

---

## 2. Forma: *am / is / are* + *-ing*

| Sujeto | Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I | **am** + *-ing* | I **am meeting** the dentist at 10. |
| He / She | **is** + *-ing* | She **is flying** to Paris next week. |
| You / We / They | **are** + *-ing* | We **are playing** tennis this weekend. |

> I **am meeting** John tomorrow at 3 o'clock.  
> She **is flying** to Paris next week.  
> They **are having** dinner with us on Friday.  
> We **are playing** tennis with them this weekend.

---

## 3. Marcadores de tiempo (futuro)

| Marcador | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| tomorrow | I am meeting John **tomorrow**. |
| next week | She is flying to Paris **next week**. |
| on Monday / Tuesday… | **On Monday** I am meeting my boss. |
| this weekend | We are playing tennis **this weekend**. |
| at 3 o'clock / at 10 / at 1 pm | I am meeting him **at 3 o'clock**. |
| in the morning | I am having a dentist appointment **in the morning**. |

Sin marcador de futuro, la misma forma suele significar **ahora**. Con marcador (*tomorrow, on Friday…*), habla del **futuro fijado**.

## 5. Vista previa: tres futuros

![Going to, will y Present Continuous](/blog/curso-a2/unit-24/a2-unit24-three-futures.png)

| Forma | Uso típico | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *going to* | intención / evidencia | I **am going to** travel next summer. |
| *will* | promesa / oferta / predicción general | I **will** help you. |
| Present Continuous | plan ya fijado (diary) | I **am meeting** John tomorrow. |

La **Unidad 25** compara las tres juntas. Aquí practica sobre todo la agenda.

4. ¿Viernes? → *lunch with his sister at 1 pm*

## 10. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I meeting John tomorrow. | I **am** meeting John tomorrow. | Falta *am/is/are* |
| She are flying to Paris. | She **is** flying to Paris. | *she* → *is* |
| I am meet John tomorrow. | I am **meeting** John tomorrow. | Forma *-ing* |
| I will meeting John tomorrow. *(cita fijada)* | I **am meeting** John tomorrow. | Cita en agenda → PC |
| What time you are meeting him? | What time **are you** meeting him? | Orden en pregunta |

## 11. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — *am / is / are*

1. I ____ meeting John tomorrow at 3 o'clock.  
2. She ____ flying to Paris next week.  
3. They ____ having dinner with us on Friday.  
4. We ____ playing tennis this weekend.  
5. He ____ meeting his boss at 9 o'clock.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **am** · 2. **is** · 3. **are** · 4. **are** · 5. **is**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa con *-ing*

Usa: *meeting / flying / having / playing / going*

1. I am ____ John at 3 o'clock.  
2. She is ____ to Paris next week.  
3. They are ____ dinner on Friday.  
4. We are ____ tennis this weekend.  
5. I am ____ to the cinema on Saturday.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **meeting** · 2. **flying** · 3. **having** · 4. **playing** · 5. **going**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Día de Tom

1. On Monday Tom is meeting ____.  
2. On Tuesday he is having a ____ appointment.  
3. On Wednesday he is flying to ____.  
4. On Thursday he is having dinner with his ____.  
5. On Friday he is playing ____ at 5 pm.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **John** · 2. **dentist** · 3. **Paris** · 4. **parents** · 5. **tennis**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Completa la llamada

On Monday I am ____ my boss at 9. On Wednesday I am ____ to Barcelona. On Friday I am ____ lunch with my sister. ____ you free on Saturday?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**meeting** · **flying** · **having** · **Are**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Elige la mejor forma

1. Cita con el dentista mañana a las 10 →  
   a) I will see the dentist. · b) I am seeing / having a dentist appointment tomorrow at 10. · c) Look! I am going to see the dentist.  
2. Promesa espontánea de ayuda →  
   a) I am helping you tomorrow. · b) I will help you. · c) I am going to rain.  
3. Intención de viajar el próximo verano (aún sin billetes) →  
   a) I am going to travel next summer. · b) I am travelling next summer at 3. · c) I will travelling.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b** · 2. **b** · 3. **a**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Preguntas

1. ____ you meeting John tomorrow?  
2. What time ____ you meeting him?  
3. Where ____ she flying to?  
4. ____ you free on Saturday?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Are** · 2. **are** · 3. **is** · 4. **Are**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Empareja

1. appointment  
2. diary  
3. meeting  
4. confirm  
5. free  

a) agenda · b) cita · c) reunión · d) libre · e) confirmar

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **c** · 4 → **e** · 5 → **d**
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Corrige

1. *I meeting John tomorrow.*  
2. *She are flying to Paris.*  
3. *I am meet the dentist at 10.*  
4. *What time you are meeting him?*  
5. *They is having dinner on Friday.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **am** meeting John tomorrow.  
2. She **is** flying to Paris.  
3. I am **meeting** the dentist at 10.  
4. What time **are you** meeting him?  
5. They **are** having dinner on Friday.
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Traduce

1. Mañana quedo con John a las 3.  
2. El martes tengo cita con el dentista a las 10.  
3. El miércoles vuelo a París para una reunión.  
4. El jueves ceno con mis padres.  
5. El viernes juego al tenis a las 5.  
6. ¿Estás libre el sábado?

9. El miércoles vuelo a Barcelona para una conferencia.  
10. El viernes como con mi hermana a la 1.

4. On Thursday I am having dinner with my parents.  
5. On Friday I am playing tennis at 5.  
6. Are you free on Saturday?  
7. What time are you meeting him?

9. On Wednesday I am flying to Barcelona for a conference.  
10. On Friday I am having lunch with my sister at 1.
</details>
