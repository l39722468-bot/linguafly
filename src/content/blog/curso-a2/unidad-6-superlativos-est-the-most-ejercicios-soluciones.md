## Lección 1 — Gramática: the … -est / the most

**Objetivo:** *the tallest*, *the most beautiful*, *the best/worst*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. He is ____ tallest boy in the school. → *the / a / more*
2. This is ____ most interesting book. → *the / a / -est*
3. July is the ____ month of the year. → *hottest / hotter / hot*
4. This is the ____ film I have ever seen. → *best / better / good*
5. She is the ____ intelligent student in the class. → *most / more / -est*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **the** · 2. **the** · 3. **hottest** · 4. **best** · 5. **most**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Superlative of short adj = ____. → a) *the* + *-est* · b) *-er than* · c) *more* only
7. Superlative of long adj = ____. → a) *the most* + adj · b) *-est* · c) *as… as*
8. *good* → ____. → a) the best · b) the goodest · c) the better
9. *bad* → ____. → a) the worst · b) the baddest · c) the worse
10. Comparatives compare ____; superlatives choose ____. → a) two / the extreme · b) many / two · c) none / none

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) *the* + *-est*** · 7. **a) *the most* + adj** · 8. **a) the best** · 9. **a) the worst** · 10. **a) two / the extreme**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *She is most tallest in the class.*
12. *This is the more beautiful place.*
13. *He is the goodest student.*
14. *It is the most hot day.*
15. *This is best restaurant in town.* (falta artículo)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. She is **the tallest** in the class.
12. This is **the most beautiful** place.
13. He is **the best** student.
14. It is **the hottest** day.
15. This is **the** best restaurant in town.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: extremos e in/of

**Objetivo:** *in the world / in town*; *of the year / of all*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja

1. tall → the ____
2. big → the ____
3. beautiful → the ____
4. good → the ____
5. quiet → the ____

a) biggest · b) most beautiful · c) tallest · d) quietest · e) best

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **c** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **b** · 4 → **e** · 5 → **d**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. the highest mountain ____ the world → *in / of / at*
7. the hottest month ____ the year → *of / in / on*
8. the biggest ____ all the towns → *of / in / at*
9. the most peaceful place ____ the world → *in / of / on*
10. delicious → the most ____ → *delicious / deliciouser / best*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **in** · 7. **of** · 8. **of** · 9. **in** · 10. **delicious**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *in* + ____. → a) place · b) only dates · c) only people names
12. *of* + ____. → a) group / period · b) only hours · c) only *did*
13. *Oldest* means ____. → a) most old in age · b) tallest · c) quietest
14. *Peaceful* → superlative ____. → a) the most peaceful · b) the peacefulest · c) more peaceful than only
15. *Ever seen* often appears with ____. → a) superlatives · b) *at noon* · c) *on Monday* only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) place** · 12. **a) group / period** · 13. **a) most old in age** · 14. **a) the most peaceful** · 15. **a) superlatives**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: My hometown

**Objetivo:** leer un texto con muchos superlativos.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-6/reading-superlatives.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: My hometown"></audio>

> My hometown is small but beautiful. It has the oldest church in the region. The main square is the biggest of all the towns near. In summer, July is the hottest month of the year. My grandmother lives in the tallest building in town. She says it is the most beautiful view she has ever seen. The best restaurant in town serves the most delicious food. I think my hometown is the best place in the world!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What is the oldest in the region? → a) The church · b) The park · c) The lake
2. What is the biggest of the towns near? → a) The main square · b) The church · c) August
3. Which month is the hottest? → a) July · b) January · c) December
4. Who lives in the tallest building? → a) Grandmother · b) Maria · c) Tom
5. What serves the most delicious food? → a) The best restaurant · b) The park · c) The church

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) The church** · 2. **a) The main square** · 3. **a) July** · 4. **a) Grandmother** · 5. **a) The best restaurant**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The view is ____ she has ever seen. → a) the most beautiful · b) worse · c) quieter only
7. The hometown is called ____ place in the world. → a) the best · b) the worst · c) the coldest
8. *Of the year* appears with ____. → a) hottest month · b) tallest boy · c) best film only
9. *In the region / in town / in the world* use ____. → a) *in* · b) *of* · c) *at*
10. The main topic is ____. → a) describing a hometown with superlatives · b) Wh-questions · c) adverbs

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) the most beautiful** · 7. **a) the best** · 8. **a) hottest month** · 9. **a) *in*** · 10. **a) describing a hometown with superlatives**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Oldest / biggest / hottest / tallest* are ____. → a) short *-est* · b) *more* · c) *as… as*
12. *Most beautiful / most delicious* are ____. → a) *the most* + long adj · b) *-er* · c) base only
13. *Best* is irregular from ____. → a) good · b) well · c) bad
14. *Ever seen* supports a ____ meaning. → a) superlative experience · b) future plan · c) *at* time
15. Mixing *-est* and *the most* in one text is ____. → a) natural · b) wrong · c) only B1

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) short *-est*** · 12. **a) *the most* + long adj** · 13. **a) good** · 14. **a) superlative experience** · 15. **a) natural**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: The biggest park

**Objetivo:** escuchar una descripción con superlativos.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-6/listening-superlatives.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: The biggest park"></audio>

> Hi! Today I want to tell you about my favourite place in the city. It is the biggest park in town. It has the tallest trees I have ever seen. In the centre there is a lake with the most beautiful swans. The best time to visit is early in the morning when it is the quietest. My sister says it is the most peaceful place in the world. I agree!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What is the favourite place? → a) The biggest park · b) A restaurant · c) A church
2. What are the tallest? → a) Trees · b) Buildings · c) Swans
3. What is on the lake? → a) The most beautiful swans · b) Boats only · c) Nothing
4. When is the best time to visit? → a) Early morning · b) Midnight · c) August only
5. The sister says it is ____. → a) the most peaceful place · b) the worst place · c) the hottest month

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) The biggest park** · 2. **a) Trees** · 3. **a) The most beautiful swans** · 4. **a) Early morning** · 5. **a) the most peaceful place**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. In the morning the park is ____. → a) the quietest · b) the hottest · c) the oldest
7. *I agree* means ____. → a) I think the same · b) I disagree · c) I leave
8. *In town / in the world* use ____. → a) *in* · b) *of* · c) *on*
9. Superlatives in the audio include ____. → a) biggest, tallest, most beautiful, best, quietest, most peaceful · b) only *went* · c) only *did*
10. The listening practices ____. → a) superlatives · b) *at/on/in* only · c) *-ly* adverbs

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) the quietest** · 7. **a) I think the same** · 8. **a) *in*** · 9. **a) biggest, tallest, most beautiful, best, quietest, most peaceful** · 10. **a) superlatives**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Biggest park in town* = ____ + place. → a) *in* · b) *of* · c) *at*
12. *Quietest* is superlative of ____. → a) quiet · b) quite · c) quick
13. *Most beautiful* needs ____. → a) *the* · b) *a* · c) nothing
14. *Best time* uses irregular ____. → a) best · b) goodest · c) better than only
15. *Ever seen* with *tallest trees* emphasises ____. → a) extreme experience · b) past Wh · c) *on Monday*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) *in*** · 12. **a) quiet** · 13. **a) *the*** · 14. **a) best** · 15. **a) extreme experience**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: The best place

**Objetivo:** escribir descripciones con superlativos.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. This is ____ biggest park in town. → *the / a / more*
2. It is the ____ month of the year. → *hottest / hotter / hot*
3. She is the ____ beautiful singer. → *most / more / -est*
4. This is the ____ restaurant in town. → *best / better / good*
5. It is the most peaceful place ____ the world. → *in / of / on*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **the** · 2. **hottest** · 3. **most** · 4. **best** · 5. **in**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. He / is / the / tallest / boy / in / the school.
7. This / is / the / most / interesting / book.
8. July / is / the / hottest / month / of / the year.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. He is the tallest boy in the school.
7. This is the most interesting book.
8. July is the hottest month of the year.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. El edificio más alto de tu ciudad.
10. El mes más frío / más caluroso.
11. El restaurante mejor de tu pueblo (*the best*).
12. El lugar más pacífico (*the most peaceful*).
13. Usa *in the world* y *of the year*.
14. Compara: una frase comparativa + una superlativa.
15. Párrafo (6–8 frases) sobre tu ciudad o parque favorito.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. It has the tallest building in town.
10. January is the coldest month. July is the hottest month of the year.
11. The best restaurant in town is La Luna.
12. It is the most peaceful place in the park.
13. It is the best place in the world. August is the hottest month of the year.
14. My city is bigger than Valencia. It has the biggest park in the region.
15. My favourite place is the biggest park in town. It has the tallest trees. The best time is early morning when it is the quietest. It is the most peaceful place in the world!

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Caso | Forma |
| :--- | :--- |
| Corto | **the** + adj**-est** |
| Largo | **the most** + adj |
| good/bad | **the best** / **the worst** |
| Lugar / grupo | **in** + place · **of** + group |
