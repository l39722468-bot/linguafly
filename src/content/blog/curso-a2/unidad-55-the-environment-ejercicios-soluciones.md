## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** recycle / save / pollute / waste; should / shouldn't for environmental advice

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. You ___ recycle paper. → *should / should to / shoulds*

2. Don't ___ food. → *waste / wastes / wasting*

3. We ___ glass and paper. → *recycle / recycles / recycling*

4. Cars ___ the air. → *pollute / pollutes / pollution*

5. ___ water when you can. → *Save / Saves / Saving*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **should** · 2. **waste** · 3. **recycle** · 4. **pollute** · 5. **Save**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *You should recycle* (no *should to*): ____. → a) correcto · b) You should to recycle · c) You shoulds recycle

7. *Pollution* is ____. → a) sustantivo · b) verbo · c) adjetivo only

8. *Don't waste food* = ____. → a) no desperdiciar · b) reciclar · c) only Past

9. *Save energy* = ____. → a) ahorrar energía · b) contaminar · c) plastic only

10. Unit 55 follows ____. → a) Feelings U54 · b) Module 5 only · c) skip U56

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) correcto** · 7. **a) sustantivo** · 8. **a) no desperdiciar** · 9. **a) ahorrar energía** · 10. **a) Feelings U54**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *You should to recycle.*

12. *Cars pollution the air.*

13. *Don't wastes food.*

14. *Save the water always the.*

15. *We should recycle plastics only one.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. You should recycle.
12. Cars pollute the air.
13. Don't waste food.
14. Save water.
15. We should recycle paper, glass and plastic.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** environment, recycle, pollution, plastic, save water, save energy, reuse

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *environment* ≈ ____ → *medio ambiente · contaminación · plástico*

2. *recycle* ≈ ____ → *reciclar · desperdiciar · contaminar*

3. *pollution* ≈ ____ → *contaminación · reciclar · ahorrar*

4. *reuse* ≈ ____ → *reutilizar · tirar · contaminar*

5. *plastic* ≈ ____ → *plástico · papel · energía*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **medio ambiente** · 2. **reciclar** · 3. **contaminación** · 4. **reutilizar** · 5. **plástico**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. Turn off lights to save ___. → *energy / energetic / energies*

7. Air ___ is a problem. → *pollution / pollute / polluting*

8. We shouldn't buy many ___ bags. → *plastic / plastics / plasticity*

9. Walk or take the ___. → *bus / busy / busily*

10. Small ___ help every day. → *actions / actioning / acted*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **energy** · 7. **pollution** · 8. **plastic** · 9. **bus** · 10. **actions**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Should recycle* paper, glass and ____. → a) plastic · b) platform · c) drama

12. *Save water* when you brush ____. → a) teeth · b) train · c) receipt

13. *Pollution getting worse* in ____. → a) city · b) museum · c) gate

14. Environment text uses ____. → a) should / recycle / save · b) only I think · c) only There is

15. Unit 55 next is ____. → a) Technology U56 · b) Module 5 only · c) skip U56

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) plastic** · 12. **a) teeth** · 13. **a) city** · 14. **a) should / recycle / save** · 15. **a) Technology U56**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Green tips at home

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-55/reading-environment.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Green tips at home"></audio>

> Protecting the environment starts at home. You should recycle paper, glass and plastic. Save water when you brush your teeth, and turn off lights to save energy. Don't waste food. If you can, walk or take the bus — cars pollute the air. Small actions help every day.

5. Cars pollute the ___. → a) air · b) library · c) degree

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) environment** · 2. **a) plastic** · 3. **a) teeth** · 4. **a) energy** · 5. **a) air**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: At the supermarket

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-55/listening-environment.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: At the supermarket"></audio>

> Ana: We shouldn't buy so many plastic bags.
> Leo: You're right. We should reuse this one.
> Ana: Do you recycle at home?
> Leo: Yes — paper and bottles. I also try to save water.
> Ana: Good. Pollution is getting worse in the city.
> Leo: Let's walk more and use the bus.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Ana says shouldn't buy many ___ bags. → a) plastic · b) paper only · c) glass

2. Leo says ___ this bag. → a) reuse · b) waste · c) pollute

3. Leo recycles paper and ___. → a) bottles · b) dramas · c) receipts

4. Leo tries to save ___. → a) water · b) energy only always · c) plastic bags more

5. Pollution worse in the ___. → a) city · b) library · c) stadium only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) plastic** · 2. **a) reuse** · 3. **a) bottles** · 4. **a) water** · 5. **a) city**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Leo suggests walk and use ____. → a) bus · b) car more · c) plastic

9. Dialogue uses ____. → a) environment advice · b) only tech · c) only work

10. Unit 55 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

## Lección 5 — Writing

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. You ___ recycle at home. → *should / should to / shoulds*

2. Don't ___ food. → *waste / wastes / wasting*

3. We should ___ water. → *save / saves / saving*

4. Cars ___ the air. → *pollute / pollutes / pollution*

5. Protect the ___. → *environment / environmental / environ*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **should** · 2. **waste** · 3. **save** · 4. **pollute** · 5. **environment**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. recycle / We / should / glass

7. water / Save / when / you / can

8. pollute / Cars / the / air

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

7. Save water when you can.
8. Cars pollute the air.

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Un consejo con *You should…*.

10. Un consejo con *You shouldn't…*.

11. Una frase con *recycle*.

12. Una frase con *pollution* o *pollute*.

13. Una frase con *save water* o *save energy*.

14. Corrige: *You should to recycle.*

15. Escribe 8–10 líneas sobre qué haces por el medio ambiente.

<details>
