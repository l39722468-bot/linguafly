## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** Past Simple con tiempo; Present Perfect con ever/never; go sightseeing; take a guided tour.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. We ____ sightseeing yesterday. → *went / have gone / go*
2. ____ you ever ____ hiking? → *Have / been · Did / go*
3. I ____ a souvenir last week. → *bought / have bought / buy*
4. She ____ never ____ hiking. → *has / been · have / been*
5. Last year I ____ to Greece. → *went / have been / go*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **went** · 2. **Have / been** · 3. **bought** · 4. **has / been** · 5. **went**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *Last summer* → ____. → a) Past Simple · b) Present Perfect only · c) will
7. *Have you ever…?* = ____. → a) experiencia de vida · b) tiempo concreto · c) solo futuro
8. *We went sightseeing* (no *did sightseeing*): ____. → a) correcto · b) wrong · c) only perfect
9. *I have visited Rome last year* is ____. → a) wrong (use Past Simple) · b) correct · c) B1 only
10. Unit 43 follows ____. → a) Accommodation U42 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U44

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Past Simple** · 7. **a) experiencia de vida** · 8. **a) correcto** · 9. **a) wrong (use Past Simple)** · 10. **a) Accommodation U42**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I have gone to London last summer.*
12. *We did sightseeing all day.*
13. *Did you ever take a guided tour?*
14. *I buyed a souvenir.*
15. *Have you been hiking yesterday?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I **went** to London last summer.
12. We **went sightseeing** all day.
13. **Have you ever taken** a guided tour?
14. I **bought** a souvenir.
15. **Did you go** hiking yesterday?

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** sightseeing, guided tour, beach, hiking, souvenir, sunbathe, local food.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *sightseeing* ≈ ____ → *ver lugares turísticos · senderismo · recuerdo*
2. *guided tour* ≈ ____ → *visita con guía · tomar el sol · playa*
3. *souvenir* ≈ ____ → *recuerdo del viaje · horario · andén*
4. *hiking* ≈ ____ → *senderismo · documental · concierto*
5. *sunbathe* ≈ ____ → *tomar el sol · reservar · llover*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **ver lugares turísticos** · 2. **visita con guía** · 3. **recuerdo del viaje** · 4. **senderismo** · 5. **tomar el sol**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. We took a ____ tour of the museum. → *guided / guide / guiding*
7. They relaxed on the ____. → *beach / station / reception*
8. Try the ____ food. → *local / locate / location*
9. I love ____ in the mountains. → *hiking / hiking to / hike*
10. Did you buy a ____? → *souvenir / memory / series*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **guided** · 7. **beach** · 8. **local** · 9. **hiking** · 10. **souvenir**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. Postcard: We're in ____. → a) Porto · b) Lisbon only · c) Blue Hotel
12. *Haven't bought souvenirs yet* = ____. → a) Present Perfect · b) Past Simple only · c) will
13. Leo's souvenir was ____. → a) A photo book · b) A vase only · c) WiFi
14. Ana loves the ____. → a) Beach · b) Platform · c) Forecast
15. Unit 43 next is ____. → a) The Weather U44 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U44

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) Porto** · 12. **a) Present Perfect** · 13. **a) A photo book** · 14. **a) Beach** · 15. **a) The Weather U44**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: A postcard from Porto

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-43/reading-holiday.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: A postcard from Porto"></audio>

> Hi! We're in Porto. Yesterday we went sightseeing and took a guided tour of the old town. We haven't bought any souvenirs yet, but we have tried the local food — delicious! Tomorrow we want to go to the beach. Have you ever been to Portugal? See you soon!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Where are they now? → a) Porto · b) Cadiz · c) Asturias
2. Yesterday they ____. → a) Went sightseeing · b) Checked out · c) Watched a documentary
3. Guided tour of ____. → a) The old town · b) The airport · c) Room 305
4. Souvenirs yet? → a) Haven't bought any · b) Bought many · c) Never tried food
5. Tomorrow they want the ____. → a) Beach · b) Train · c) Lift

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Porto** · 2. **a) Went sightseeing** · 3. **a) The old town** · 4. **a) Haven't bought any** · 5. **a) Beach**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Local food is ____. → a) Delicious · b) Included · c) Windy
7. Question at end: ____. → a) Have you ever been to Portugal? · b) Will it rain? · c) Is there WiFi?
8. *Yesterday* → ____. → a) Past Simple · b) Present Perfect only · c) going to
9. *Haven't bought yet* → ____. → a) Present Perfect · b) Past Simple · c) will
10. Matches unit 43: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only travel

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Delicious** · 7. **a) Have you ever been to Portugal?** · 8. **a) Past Simple** · 9. **a) Present Perfect** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Went sightseeing* = ____. → a) go sightseeing (past) · b) did sightseeing · c) have sightseeing
12. *Took a guided tour* = ____. → a) visita guiada · b) book room · c) check in
13. *Have tried the local food* = ____. → a) experiencia reciente · b) last year only · c) by bus
14. Postcard mixes ____. → a) Past + Perfect · b) only articles · c) only should
15. Holiday text writing uses ____. → a) vacation structures · b) only did · c) only phrasals

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) go sightseeing (past)** · 12. **a) visita guiada** · 13. **a) experiencia reciente** · 14. **a) Past + Perfect** · 15. **a) vacation structures**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Talking about holidays

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-43/listening-holiday.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Talking about holidays"></audio>

> Ana: Have you ever been hiking?  
> Leo: Yes, I have. I went hiking in Asturias last year.  
> Ana: Nice! Did you buy a souvenir?  
> Leo: Just a photo book. What about you?  
> Ana: I've never been hiking, but I love the beach. We went to Cadiz in August.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Ana asks if Leo has ever been ____. → a) Hiking · b) Shopping · c) Check-in
2. Leo went hiking in ____. → a) Asturias · b) Porto · c) Lisbon
3. When? → a) Last year · b) Yesterday · c) Next weekend
4. Leo's souvenir? → a) A photo book · b) A vase · c) Nothing
5. Ana went to Cadiz in ____. → a) August · b) Winter · c) March

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Hiking** · 2. **a) Asturias** · 3. **a) Last year** · 4. **a) A photo book** · 5. **a) August**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Ana has ____ been hiking. → a) Never · b) Always · c) Yet
7. Ana loves the ____. → a) Beach · b) Drama · c) Storm
8. *Have you ever…?* then *I went… last year* = ____. → a) Perfect + Past detail · b) only Past · c) only will
9. *Did you buy a souvenir?* = ____. → a) Past Simple · b) Present Perfect only · c) going to
10. Unit 43 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Never** · 7. **a) Beach** · 8. **a) Perfect + Past detail** · 9. **a) Past Simple** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Experience Q: ____ you ever been hiking? → a) Have · b) Did · c) Do
12. Past detail: I ____ hiking last year. → a) went · b) have gone · c) going
13. *I've never been hiking* = ____. → a) never + Perfect · b) last year · c) by train
14. Shadow and note ____. → a) Past / Perfect · b) only a/an · c) only Who
15. U43 next unit is ____. → a) The Weather U44 · b) Module 6 only · c) U42 skip

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) Have** · 12. **a) went** · 13. **a) never + Perfect** · 14. **a) Past / Perfect** · 15. **a) The Weather U44**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. We ____ sightseeing yesterday. → *went / have gone / go*
2. ____ you ever ____ to Japan? → *Have / been · Did / go*
3. I ____ a souvenir last week. → *bought / have bought / buy*
4. They ____ a guided tour on Monday. → *took / have taken / take*
5. I ____ never ____ hiking. → *have / been · has / been*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **went** · 2. **Have / been** · 3. **bought** · 4. **took** · 5. **have / been**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. sightseeing / went / We / yesterday
7. ever / you / Have / hiking / been / ?
8. tour / a / took / guided / They

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. We went sightseeing yesterday.
7. Have you ever been hiking?
8. They took a guided tour.

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Una frase con *go sightseeing* o *take a guided tour* (Past Simple).
10. Una frase con *Have you ever…?*
11. Una frase con *last year* o *yesterday* (Past Simple).
12. Una frase con *souvenir* o *beach*.
13. Una frase con *never* + Present Perfect.
14. Corrige: *I have visited Rome last year.*
15. Postal de vacaciones (8–10 líneas) con Past Simple y Present Perfect.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. We went sightseeing yesterday. / We took a guided tour of the old town.
10. Have you ever been to Portugal? / Have you ever been hiking?
11. I went hiking in Asturias last year. / We went to Cadiz in August.
12. Did you buy a souvenir? / Tomorrow we want to go to the beach.
13. I've never been hiking. / We haven't bought any souvenirs yet.
14. I **visited** Rome last year.
15. Hi! We're in Porto. Yesterday we went sightseeing and took a guided tour of the old town. We haven't bought any souvenirs yet, but we have tried the local food — delicious! Tomorrow we want to go to the beach. Have you ever been to Portugal? See you soon!

</details>
