## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** repaso integrado: futuro, condicionales, have to y could.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. Next week I ____ travel to London. → *will / am / have*
2. If it ____, I will take an umbrella. → *rains / will rain / rained*
3. If you heat water, it ____. → *boils / will boil / boiled*
4. I ____ to get up early tomorrow. → *have / has / having*
5. When I was young, I ____ speak two languages. → *could / can / will*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **will** · 2. **rains** · 3. **boils** · 4. **have** · 5. **could**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *If it rains, I will…* is ____. → a) First Conditional · b) Zero · c) have to
7. *If you heat water, it boils* is ____. → a) Zero · b) First · c) could past
8. *Don't have to work on Friday* means ____. → a) optional · b) forbidden · c) past ability
9. *Could you pass me the water?* is ____. → a) polite request · b) Zero · c) will promise
10. Unit 30 reviews ____. → a) Module 3 mix · b) only Unit 1 · c) only phrasal verbs

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) First Conditional** · 7. **a) Zero** · 8. **a) optional** · 9. **a) polite request** · 10. **a) Module 3 mix**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *If it will rains, I will take an umbrella.*
12. *If you heat water, it will boils.* *(general fact)*
13. *She have to get up early.*
14. *When I was young, I can speak two languages.*
15. *Could you to pass me the water?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. If it **rains**, I will take an umbrella.
12. If you heat water, it **boils**.
13. She **has to** get up early.
14. When I was young, I **could** speak two languages.
15. Could you pass me the water?

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** umbrella, meeting, dentist, glasses, slowly, general fact.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *umbrella* ≈ ____ → *use when it rains · a bike · homework*
2. *general fact* ≈ ____ → *always true · tomorrow's plan · polite ask*
3. *dentist* ≈ ____ → *tooth doctor · train station · uniform*
4. *glasses* ≈ ____ → *wear to see/read better · cook · boil*
5. *slowly* ≈ ____ → *not fast · always · yesterday*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **use when it rains** · 2. **always true** · 3. **tooth doctor** · 4. **wear to see/read better** · 5. **not fast**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. If it rains, I will take an ____. → *umbrella / umbrellas / umbrelling*
7. I have to get up early because I have a ____. → *meeting / meet / met*
8. I don't have to ____ on Friday. → *work / working / worked*
9. Tomorrow I will go to the ____. → *dentist / dentists / dentisting*
10. Could you speak more ____, please? → *slowly / slow / slowness*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **umbrella** · 7. **meeting** · 8. **work** · 9. **dentist** · 10. **slowly**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. London trip uses ____. → a) will · b) Zero only · c) couldn't
12. Teeth yellow without brushing is ____. → a) Zero fact · b) First future · c) have to
13. *I could draw well* is ____. → a) past ability · b) request · c) obligation
14. Module 3 asks you to ____. → a) choose the right form · b) use one form only · c) avoid English
15. Unit 30 is ____. → a) integrated review · b) only greetings · c) only Unit 31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) will** · 12. **a) Zero fact** · 13. **a) past ability** · 14. **a) choose the right form** · 15. **a) integrated review**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Next week

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-30/reading-review.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Next week"></audio>

> Next week I will travel to London. If it rains, I will take an umbrella. If you heat water, it boils — that is a general fact. I have to get up early tomorrow because I have a meeting. I don't have to work on Friday. When I was young, I could speak two languages. Could you pass me the water, please?

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Next week? → a) Travel to London · b) Go to dentist · c) Draw well
2. If it rains? → a) Take an umbrella · b) Boil water · c) Work on Friday
3. Heat water? → a) Boils (general fact) · b) Will travel · c) Get up early
4. Friday? → a) Don't have to work · b) Have to wear uniform · c) Forgot watch
5. When young? → a) Could speak two languages · b) Could draw well · c) Will go to dentist

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Travel to London** · 2. **a) Take an umbrella** · 3. **a) Boils (general fact)** · 4. **a) Don't have to work** · 5. **a) Could speak two languages**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The text mixes ____. → a) will / First / Zero / have to / could · b) only Past · c) only Unit 1
7. *Have a meeting* explains ____. → a) get up early · b) boil water · c) draw well
8. *Could you pass me the water* is ____. → a) a request · b) Zero · c) will travel
9. Zero fact is marked as ____. → a) general fact · b) tomorrow only · c) past ability
10. Matches unit 30 review: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Module 6

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) will / First / Zero / have to / could** · 7. **a) get up early** · 8. **a) a request** · 9. **a) general fact** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Label *If it rains, I will…*: ____. → a) First · b) Zero · c) could
12. Label *If you heat water, it boils*: ____. → a) Zero · b) First · c) have to
13. Integrated texts are ____. → a) natural at review · b) wrong · c) only C2
14. Ask what you mean first, then ____. → a) choose form · b) random pick · c) always will
15. Reading helps you ____. → a) tag each structure · b) skip meaning · c) avoid English

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) First** · 12. **a) Zero** · 13. **a) natural at review** · 14. **a) choose form** · 15. **a) tag each structure**

</details>
