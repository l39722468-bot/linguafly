En las [unidades 21–24](/blog/curso-a2) aprendiste tres formas de futuro. En la **Unidad 25 del curso A2** (*Future: Going to vs Will vs Present Continuous*) **eliges** la forma correcta según el contexto.

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 25 — Future Contrast](/curso-a2/unit-25)  
> **Antes:** [U24 — Present Continuous for Future](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-24-present-continuous-futuro)

---

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 25 (A2)

- Contrastar **going to**, **will** y **Present Continuous**.
- Decidir según: intención, evidencia, promesa, espontáneo o cita fijada.
- Mezclar las tres formas en un mismo texto (viaje, boda, planes).
- Revisar marcadores temporales de futuro.
- Evitar mezclas incorrectas (*I will flying*, *I am go to…*).

![Tres formas de futuro A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-25/a2-unit25-three-futures.png)

---

## 1. Mapa rápido de las tres formas

| Forma | Uso principal | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **going to** | plan/intención · evidencia | I **am going to** visit the museum. / Look! It **is going to** rain. |
| **will** | promesa · oferta · espontáneo · predicción general | I **will** call you. / Maybe I **will** see a show. / I think it **will** be great. |
| **Present Continuous** | plan ya fijado (agenda) | I **am flying** to London. / On Saturday I **am having** dinner with my cousin. |

---

## 2. Mapa de decisión

![Mapa de decisión de futuros](/blog/curso-a2/unit-25/a2-unit25-decision-map.png)

1. ¿Está **ya organizado** (hora, reserva, billete en agenda)? → **Present Continuous**.  
2. ¿Es un **plan/intención** o hay **evidencia** ahora? → **going to**.  
3. ¿Es **promesa**, **oferta**, decisión **ahora** o predicción **general**? → **will**.

> She **is going to** fly to London next month. She already has the ticket.  
> Look! The sky is dark. It **is going to** rain soon.  
> We **are meeting** John at 3 o'clock tomorrow. It's fixed.  
> I **will** stay at home if it rains tomorrow.  
> I **will** send you the invitation tomorrow.

## 3. Repaso rápido de cada forma

### Going to (U21–22)

> I **am going to** visit the British Museum — I have already bought the ticket.  
> I **am going to** wear a white dress — I have already chosen it.

### Will (U23)

> When I arrive, I **will** call you and we **will** meet.  
> Maybe I **will** see a show in the West End too.  
> I think the trip **will** be great!

### Present Continuous (U24)

> Next week I **am flying** to London — that is already arranged.  
> On Saturday I **am having** dinner with my cousin — we booked the restaurant last week.  
> Next Saturday I **am getting married** — it is already arranged!

## 4. Misma idea, forma distinta (elige el matiz)

| Situación | Mejor forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Billete / reserva hecha | PC o *going to* | I **am flying** / I **am going to** fly… |
| Decisión en el momento | *will* | OK, I **'ll** call you. |
| Nubes oscuras | *going to* | It **is going to** rain. |
| Opinión general | *will* | I think it **will** be perfect. |
| Cita en el diary | Present Continuous | I **am meeting** John at 3. |
| Promesa | *will* | I **will** send the invitation tomorrow. |

## 5. Texto modelo: viaje a Londres

> Next week I am flying to London — that is already arranged. When I arrive, I will call you and we will meet. I am going to visit the British Museum — I have already bought the ticket. Maybe I will see a show in the West End too. On Saturday I am having dinner with my cousin — we booked the restaurant last week. I think the trip will be great!

| Frase | Forma | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I am flying to London | Present Continuous | ya organizado |
| I will call you / we will meet | *will* | promesa / decisión al llegar |
| I am going to visit the museum | *going to* | plan + ticket |
| Maybe I will see a show | *will* | posibilidad espontánea |
| I am having dinner… | Present Continuous | reserva hecha |
| I think the trip will be great | *will* | predicción general |

## 6. Listening: boda de Anna

> Hi Anna! I am calling about our plans. Next Saturday I am getting married — it is already arranged! I will send you the invitation tomorrow. I am going to wear a white dress — I have already chosen it. Maybe I will have a party the day after too. I think everything will be perfect!

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-25/dialogue-wedding.mp3" title="🔊 Hi Anna! I am calling about our plans…"></audio>

Preguntas rápidas:

1. ¿Qué pasa el sábado? → *getting married* (PC — ya organizado)  
2. ¿Qué hará mañana? → *will send the invitation* (*will* — promesa)  
3. ¿El vestido? → *am going to wear a white dress* (plan + ya elegido)  
4. ¿Fiesta después? → *Maybe I will have a party* (*will* — quizás)  
5. ¿Predicción final? → *everything will be perfect*

## 9. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I will flying to London. | I **am flying** / I **will fly**… | No mezcles *will* + *-ing* |
| I am go to visit the museum. | I **am going to** visit… | *going to* + infinitivo |
| Look! It will rain. *(nubes ahora)* | Look! It **is going to** rain. | evidencia → *going to* |
| I promise I am helping you. | I promise I **will** help you. | promesa → *will* |
| Tomorrow I will meeting John at 3. *(cita fijada)* | Tomorrow I **am meeting** John at 3. | agenda → PC |

## 10. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige la forma

1. Look! The sky is dark. It ____ rain soon. (*going to / will / are*)  
2. We ____ meeting John at 3 tomorrow. It's fixed. (*will / are / are going to*)  
3. I ____ call you when I arrive. (*will / am / am going to*)  
4. She ____ fly to London next month. She already has the ticket. (*will / is going to / is*)  
5. I think the trip ____ be great. (*will / is going to / is*)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **is going to** · 2. **are** · 3. **will** · 4. **is going to** · 5. **will**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Etiqueta la forma

Escribe **GT** (*going to*), **W** (*will*) o **PC** (Present Continuous):

1. Next week I am flying to London.  
2. I will call you when I arrive.  
3. I am going to visit the British Museum.  
4. Maybe I will see a show.  
5. On Saturday I am having dinner with my cousin.  
6. I think the trip will be great.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **PC** · 2. **W** · 3. **GT** · 4. **W** · 5. **PC** · 6. **W**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Completa Londres

Next week I ____ flying to London. When I arrive, I ____ call you. I ____ going to visit the museum — I have already bought the ticket. On Saturday I ____ having dinner with my cousin.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**am** · **will** · **am** · **am**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Completa la boda

Next Saturday I ____ getting married. I ____ send you the invitation tomorrow. I ____ going to wear a white dress. Maybe I ____ have a party the day after. I think everything ____ be perfect!

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**am** · **will** · **am** · **will** · **will**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Empareja uso ↔ forma

1. evidencia ahora  
2. cita fijada  
3. promesa  
4. intención + ticket  
5. predicción general (*I think*)  

a) *will* · b) *going to* (evidencia) · c) Present Continuous · d) *going to* (plan) · e) *will*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **c** · 3 → **a/e** · 4 → **d** · 5 → **a/e**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Elige la mejor opción

1. Prometo enviarte la invitación mañana.  
   a) I am sending you… tomorrow. · b) I will send you… tomorrow. · c) Look! I am going to send…  
2. Reserva del restaurante hecha.  
   a) I will have dinner… · b) I am having dinner… · c) Maybe I will having dinner…  
3. Nubes oscuras.  
   a) It will rain. · b) It is going to rain. · c) It is raining tomorrow.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b** · 2. **b** · 3. **b**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Corrige

1. *I will flying to London next week.*  
2. *I am go to visit the museum.*  
3. *Look! It will rain.* *(ves nubes ahora)*  
4. *I promise I am helping you.*  
5. *Tomorrow I will meeting John at 3.* *(cita fijada)*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **am flying** to London next week. / I **will fly**…  
2. I **am going to** visit the museum.  
3. Look! It **is going to** rain.  
4. I promise I **will** help you.  
5. Tomorrow I **am meeting** John at 3.
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Traduce (elige la forma natural)

1. La próxima semana vuelo a Londres (ya organizado).  
2. Cuando llegue, te llamaré.  
3. Voy a visitar el museo (ya tengo la entrada).  
4. Quizás vea un espectáculo.  
5. El sábado ceno con mi primo (reserva hecha).  
6. Creo que el viaje será genial.  
7. El sábado que viene me caso (ya organizado).  
8. Te enviaré la invitación mañana.  
9. Voy a llevar un vestido blanco (ya lo he elegido).  
10. ¡Mira! Va a llover pronto.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Next week I am flying to London.  
2. When I arrive, I will call you.  
3. I am going to visit the museum.  
4. Maybe I will see a show.  
5. On Saturday I am having dinner with my cousin.  
6. I think the trip will be great.  
7. Next Saturday I am getting married.  
8. I will send you the invitation tomorrow.  
9. I am going to wear a white dress.  
10. Look! It is going to rain soon.
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Mini contraste

Completa con *will / going to / am-is-are + -ing* (una forma lógica):

1. A: The phone is ringing! B: OK, I ____ answer it.  
2. Look at those dark clouds! It ____ rain.  
3. I ____ meeting my boss at 9 tomorrow.  
4. I ____ wear this dress — I have already chosen it.  
5. I think everything ____ be perfect.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **'ll / will** · 2. **is going to** · 3. **am** · 4. **am going to** · 5. **will**
</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu texto mixto

Escribe 8–12 líneas mezclando las **tres** formas:

- 2 × Present Continuous (agenda)  
- 2 × *going to* (plan/evidencia)  
- 2 × *will* (promesa / Maybe / I think)  

**Modelo:** viaje a Londres o boda.
