En la [Unidad 13](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-13-present-perfect-already-yet) hablaste de **progreso** (*already / yet*). En la **Unidad 14 del curso A2** (*Present Perfect: Just*) dices que **acabas de** hacer algo: *I have just…*

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 14 — Present Perfect: Just](/curso-a2/unit-14)  
> **Antes:** [U13 — Already & Yet](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-13-present-perfect-already-yet)

---

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 14 (A2)

- Usar **just** con Present Perfect: *I have just arrived…*
- Colocar **just** entre *have/has* y el past participle.
- Hablar de **acciones recientes** (mensajes, llegadas, noticias, exámenes…).
- Distinguir **just** de *already* y *yet*.
- Usar **recently** de forma opcional.

![Just con Present Perfect A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-14/a2-unit14-just.png)

---

## 1. Idea clave

| Palabra | Significado A2 | Idea |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **just** | acabar de | la acción es **muy reciente** (hace un momento) |

No es lo mismo que *already* (ya / antes de lo esperado) ni que *yet* (aún / todavía). Aquí el foco es: **acaba de pasar**.

> I **have just** arrived. → Acabo de llegar.  
> She **has just** finished her exam. → Acaba de terminar el examen.

---

## 2. Estructura: *have/has + just + past participle*

**Just** va **entre** *have/has* y el participio:

> I **have just** arrived home from work.  
> She **has just** finished her exam.  
> I **have just** eaten lunch.  
> They **have just** left.  
> I **have just** woken up.  
> We **have just** heard the news.  
> I **have just** received a message.

## 3. Posición de *just*

![Posición de just en Present Perfect](/blog/curso-a2/unit-14/a2-unit14-word-position.png)

| Correcto (prioridad A2) | Incorrecto / menos prioritario |
| :--- | :--- |
| I have **just** finished. | *I just have finished.* |
| She has **just** left. | *She has left just.* |
| They have **just** arrived. | *They just arrived have.* |

Regla fácil: **have/has → just → participio**.

---

## 4. *Just* vs *already* vs *yet*

| Marcador | Uso A2 | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **just** | muy reciente (hace un momento) | I have **just** arrived. |
| **already** | ya (antes de ahora / antes de lo esperado) | I have **already** finished my homework. |
| **yet** | aún / todavía (negativas y preguntas) | I haven't finished **yet**. / Have you finished **yet**? |

Comparaciones rápidas:

- *I have **just** eaten.* → Acabo de comer (hace un momento).  
- *I have **already** eaten.* → Ya he comido (está hecho; no hace falta insistir en «hace un segundo»).  
- *I haven't eaten **yet**.* → Aún no he comido.

---

## 5. *Recently* (opcional en A2)

También puedes oír **recently** (= recientemente) con Present Perfect:

> I have **recently** finished my project.  
> She has **recently** moved to Madrid.

**Just** es más **inmediato** (hace un momento). **Recently** cubre un pasado cercano más amplio (días / semanas recientes). En esta unidad, prioriza **just**.

## 10. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I just have arrived. | I have **just** arrived. | *just* tras *have* |
| I have arrived just. | I have **just** arrived. | *just* antes del participio |
| I just arrived. *(si quieres Present Perfect A2)* | I **have just** arrived. | Present Perfect + *just* |
| I have just finish. | I have just **finished**. | hace falta past participle |
| She has just finish her exam. | She has just **finished** her exam. | participio *-ed* / irregular |
| Have you just finished yet? | Have you **just** finished? / Have you finished **yet**? | no mezclar *just* + *yet* sin necesidad |
| I haven't just arrived. *(poco natural A2)* | I have **just** arrived. / I haven't arrived **yet**. | *just* suele ir en afirmativas |

## 11. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Completa con *just*

1. I have ____ arrived home from work.  
2. She has ____ finished her exam.  
3. They have ____ left.  
4. We have ____ heard the news.  
5. He has ____ woken up.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1–5. **just**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Posición

Elige la correcta:

1. ____  
   a) I just have arrived. · b) I have just arrived. · c) I have arrived just.  
2. ____  
   a) She has just finished her exam. · b) She just has finished her exam. · c) She has finished just her exam.  
3. ____  
   a) They have left just. · b) They just have left. · c) They have just left.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b** · 2. **a** · 3. **c**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — *just*, *already* o *yet*

1. I have ____ woken up. It is seven o'clock. *(hace un momento)*  
2. I have ____ finished my homework. *(ya está hecho; progreso)*  
3. I haven't called my grandmother ____.  
4. Have you washed the dishes ____?  
5. She has ____ received a message. *(hace un momento)*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **just** · 2. **already** · 3. **yet** · 4. **yet** · 5. **just**
</details>

| Caso | Forma |
| :--- | :--- |

| *just* vs *already* | muy reciente vs ya (progreso) |
| *yet* | negativas / preguntas (unidad 13) |
| *recently* (opcional) | pasado cercano más amplio |
