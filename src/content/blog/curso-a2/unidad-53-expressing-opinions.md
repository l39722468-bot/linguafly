En la [Unidad 52](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-52-offers-requests) practicaste ofertas y peticiones. En la **Unidad 53 del curso A2** (*Expressing Opinions*) das **tu opinión** y **reaccionas** a la de otros.

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 53 (A2)

- Dar opinión: **I think…**, **I don't think…**, **In my opinion…**
- Reaccionar: **I agree**, **I disagree**, **I don't think so**
- Vocabulario de temas: películas, música, comida, viajes
- Entender opiniones en reseñas / foros
- Escribir 2–3 frases de opinión

## 1. Dar tu opinión

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| **I think…** | **I think** this film is great. |
| **I don't think…** | **I don't think** it's funny. |
| **In my opinion…** | **In my opinion**, it's too long. |
| **I believe…** | **I believe** travel is important. |
| **For me…** | **For me**, pizza is the best. |

> **I think** we should go by train.  
> **In my opinion**, the museum is better than the mall.

## 2. Acordar y discrepar

| Acordar | Discrepar (suave → más claro) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **I agree.** | **I don't think so.** |
| **I agree with you.** | **I'm not sure.** |
| **You're right.** | **I see your point, but…** |
| **Exactly!** | **I disagree.** |

> A: I think the food is delicious.  
> B: **I agree!** / **I don't think so** — it's too spicy for me.

## 3. Vocabulario por tema

| Tema | Opiniones útiles |
| :--- | :--- |
| Películas / series | boring, exciting, funny, too long, brilliant |
| Música | catchy, loud, relaxing, my favourite |
| Comida | delicious, spicy, sweet, healthy, terrible |
| Viajes | expensive, worth it, crowded, amazing |

> **I think** the soundtrack is **catchy**.  
> **In my opinion**, the city is **too crowded** in summer.

## 6. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura — Forum review

**What do you think** of the new series? **In my opinion**, episode one is slow, but the story is interesting. **I think** the actors are brilliant. Some people say it's boring — **I don't think so**. **I agree** that it's a bit long, but it's worth watching.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-53/reading-forum.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Forum review"></audio>

### Diálogo — After a film

**Lara:** **What do you think** of the film?  
**Ben:** **I think** it was exciting.  
**Lara:** **I agree.** The ending was amazing.  
**Ben:** **Do you agree** that it was too long?  
**Lara:** **I'm not sure.** **In my opinion**, it was fine.  
**Ben:** Fair enough!

## 7. Errores frecuentes

1. ~~I am agree.~~ → **I agree.**  
2. ~~I agree you.~~ → **I agree with you.**  
3. ~~In my opinion that the film is bad.~~ → **In my opinion, the film is bad.**  
4. ~~I think yes.~~ → **I think so.** / **Yes, I think so.**  
5. ~~I don't think yes.~~ → **I don't think so.**  
6. ~~According to me…~~ → más natural: **In my opinion…** / **For me…**

## 8. Ejercicios (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Empareja

1. I think…  
2. In my opinion…  
3. I agree.  
4. I don't think so.  
5. What do you think?  

a) ask for an opinion  
b) give an opinion  
c) disagree politely  
d) another way to give an opinion  
e) say you have the same opinion

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1 → b  
2 → d  
3 → e  
4 → c  
5 → a  

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa

1. I ___ the museum is amazing.  
2. I don't ___ it's a good idea.  
3. In my ___, we should stay.  
4. I agree ___ you.  
5. I don't think ___.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. think  
2. think  
3. opinion  
4. with  
5. so  

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Agree o disagree

1. You're right. → ___  
2. I'm not sure. → ___  
3. Exactly! → ___  
4. I see your point, but… → ___

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. agree  
2. soft disagree / unsure  
3. agree  
4. soft disagree  

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrige el error

1. I am agree with you.  
2. I agree you.  
3. I think so not.  
4. In my opinion that it's boring.  
5. According to me, it's great.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. I agree with you.  
2. I agree with you.  
3. I don't think so.  
4. In my opinion, it's boring.  
5. In my opinion, / For me, it's great.

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Traduce al inglés

1. Creo que la película es genial.  
2. En mi opinión, es demasiado larga.  
3. Estoy de acuerdo contigo.  
4. No lo creo.  
5. ¿Qué opinas?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. I think the film is great.  
2. In my opinion, it's too long.  
3. I agree with you.  
4. I don't think so.  
5. What do you think? / What's your opinion?

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa el diálogo

**A:** What do you ___ of this restaurant?  
**B:** I ___ the food is delicious.  
**A:** I ___ — but in my ___, it's expensive.  
**B:** I don't think ___ . It's OK for a special day.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

think  
think  
agree  
opinion  
so  

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Elige la mejor opción

1. ___ the music is too loud.  
   a) I am think  b) I think  c) I thinking  
2. ___ , we should leave early.  
   a) In my opinion  b) I agree you  c) I think so not  
3. A: It's boring. B: ___ — I loved it.  
   a) I agree  b) I don't think so  c) Exactly

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. b  
2. a  
3. b  

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Ordena

1. think / I / it's / great  
2. opinion / In / my / it's / long / too  
3. with / I / agree / you

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. I think it's great.  
2. In my opinion, it's too long.  
3. I agree with you.  

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Temas

Escribe 1 opinión (*I think* / *In my opinion*) para cada tema:

1. A film  
2. A song  
3. A city

<details>
<summary>Soluciones (modelo)</summary>

1. I think the film is exciting.  
2. In my opinion, the song is catchy.  
3. I think the city is too crowded.

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu mini reseña (8–12 líneas)

Elige una película, canción, comida o viaje. Incluye:

- **I think…**  
- **In my opinion…**  
- **I agree** o **I don't think so** (reacción a una idea imaginaria)  
- 2 adjetivos del tema

**Modelo:** la lectura de la sección 6.

## 9. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Formas de opinar + agree/disagree  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–6.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha forum y diálogo.  
4. **5–8 min** — Ejercicio 10.

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *I think / I don't think*  
- [ ] Uso *In my opinion…*  
- [ ] Digo *I agree with you*  
- [ ] Discrepo con *I don't think so*  
- [ ] Evito *I am agree* e *I agree you*

## 10. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Da tu opinión en voz alta sobre 3 cosas cada día.  
2. Lee comentarios cortos en inglés y marca *I think / I agree*.
