## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** phrasal verbs separables; pronombre en el medio (turn it off).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. The TV is on. Please turn ____ off. → *it / on it / off it*
2. Your bag is on the floor. Pick ____ up. → *it / up it / on it*
3. It's cold. Put your coat ____. → *on / on it / it on*
4. We're home. Take your shoes ____. → *off / off them / them off*
5. This is Ana's pen. Give ____ back. → *it / back it / it back*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **it** · 2. **it** · 3. **on** *(Put your coat on / Put on your coat)* · 4. **off** *(Take your shoes off / Take them off)* · 5. **it** *(Give it back)*

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. With a pronoun object, place it ____. → a) in the middle · b) after the particle · c) before the verb only
7. ~~Turn off it~~ is wrong because ____. → a) pronoun must be middle · b) turn is irregular · c) off is inseparable always
8. *Pick up the book* = ____. → a) Pick the book up · b) Pick up book the · c) Pick book for
9. *Put on* vs *take off* are ____. → a) opposites (clothes) · b) the same · c) only inseparable
10. Unit 32 focuses on ____. → a) separable phrasals · b) only look for · c) articles

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) in the middle** · 7. **a) pronoun must be middle** · 8. **a) Pick the book up** · 9. **a) opposites (clothes)** · 10. **a) separable phrasals**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *Turn off it, please.*
12. *Pick up them from the floor.*
13. *Put on it your jacket.*
14. *Give back it tomorrow.*
15. *Take off them before you sit.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. Turn **it** off, please.
12. Pick **them** up from the floor.
13. Put **it** on. / Put your jacket on.
14. Give **it** back tomorrow.
15. Take **them** off before you sit.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** pick up, put down, take off, put on, give back, separable vs inseparable.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *pick up* ≈ ____ → *collect / lift · put down · turn on*
2. *put down* ≈ ____ → *place down · take off · give back*
3. *put on* ≈ ____ → *wear / start · take off · pick up*
4. *take off* ≈ ____ → *remove (clothes) · put on · look for*
5. *give back* ≈ ____ → *return · pick up · sit down*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **collect / lift** · 2. **place down** · 3. **wear / start** · 4. **remove (clothes)** · 5. **return**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. Turn the TV off. = Turn ____ the TV ____. → *off / on · the / it*
7. Pick up the keys. = Pick ____ keys ____. → *the / up · up / the*
8. Put your coat on. = Put ____ coat ____. → *on / your · your / on*
9. Please put ____ your bags ____. → *down / your · your / down*
10. Can you give ____ back? → *it / back it / it back*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **off** the TV *(or: Turn the TV off)* · 7. **the** keys **up** · 8. **your** coat **on** · 9. **down** your bags **here** *(put down your bags)* · 10. **it** back

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Look for* is ____. → a) inseparable · b) separable with pronoun middle · c) only past
12. *Turn off the TV* and *turn the TV off* are ____. → a) both correct · b) only first · c) only second
13. *Give it back* means ____. → a) devolver · b) encender · c) buscar
14. Separable phrasals with names: object can be ____. → a) two positions · b) only after particle · c) never used
15. Unit 32 builds on U31 ____. → a) phrasal basics · b) only could · c) only some/any

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) inseparable** · 12. **a) both correct** · 13. **a) devolver** · 14. **a) two positions** · 15. **a) phrasal basics**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Morning at home

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-32/reading-separable.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Morning at home"></audio>

> Every morning I wake up and get up. I look for my phone. Then I put on my clothes and pick up my bag. Before I leave, I turn the lights off. If I borrow my sister's scarf, I give it back in the evening.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. After looking for the phone? → a) Put on clothes & pick up bag · b) Give back scarf · c) Turn on computers
2. Before leaving? → a) Turn the lights off · b) Sit down in class · c) Order cappuccino
3. Sister's scarf? → a) Give it back in evening · b) Keep it · c) Pick up from floor
4. *Look for* here is ____. → a) inseparable (U31) · b) separable with it · c) only past
5. Separable examples in text? → a) put on, pick up, turn off, give back · b) only wake up · c) only Who called

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Put on clothes & pick up bag** · 2. **a) Turn the lights off** · 3. **a) Give it back in evening** · 4. **a) inseparable (U31)** · 5. **a) put on, pick up, turn off, give back**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. *Turn the lights off* shows ____. → a) separated form · b) inseparable only · c) subject question
7. *Give it back* uses ____. → a) pronoun in middle · b) ~~give back it~~ · c) no object
8. Morning sequence: wake/get up → look for phone → ____. → a) put on & pick up · b) hate waiting · c) any milk
9. Borrowed item returned ____. → a) in the evening · b) before breakfast · c) at the shop
10. Matches unit 32: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U35

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) separated form** · 7. **a) pronoun in middle** · 8. **a) put on & pick up** · 9. **a) in the evening** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Pick up my bag* = Pick ____ bag ____. → a) my / up · b) up / my · c) for / my
12. Pronoun rule: Pick ____ up. → a) it/them · b) up it · c) up them
13. *Put on* + clothes = opposite ____. → a) take off · b) look for · c) wake up
14. Reading mixes U31 + U32 phrasals: ____. → a) natural · b) wrong · c) only C2
15. Mark separable forms when ____. → a) reading aloud · b) skipping meaning · c) only translating titles

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) my / up** · 12. **a) it/them** · 13. **a) take off** · 14. **a) natural** · 15. **a) reading aloud**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: In class

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Headphones? → a) Take off & put down on desk · b) Turn on · c) Look for file
2. Student asks to ____. → a) put on jacket · b) take off jacket · c) give back scarf
3. Pencil? → a) Pick it up from floor · b) On the sofa · c) In a bag of six
4. When finish? → a) Give worksheets back · b) Wake up early · c) Pay now
5. Cold reason for ____. → a) put on jacket · b) sit down only · c) hate waiting

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Take off & put down on desk** · 2. **a) put on jacket** · 3. **a) Pick it up from floor** · 4. **a) Give worksheets back** · 5. **a) put on jacket**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. *Put them down* uses ____. → a) pronoun middle · b) ~~put down them~~ · c) inseparable look for
7. *Give the worksheets back* = ____. → a) Give back the worksheets · b) Who called you · c) some bread
8. Listening is ____. → a) classroom context · b) shopping list · c) café order
9. *Take off your headphones* opposite of ____. → a) put on · b) wake up · c) live here
10. Unit 32 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Module 6

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) pronoun middle** · 7. **a) Give back the worksheets** · 8. **a) classroom context** · 9. **a) put on** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. ~~Pick up them~~ → ____. → a) Pick them up · b) Pick them · c) Them pick up
12. Separable with noun: two positions ____. → a) often OK · b) never OK · c) only in past
13. Practice pairs: put on ↔ ____. → a) take off · b) look for · c) could
14. Shadow and label ____. → a) each separable phrasal · b) only spelling · c) only articles
15. U32 next is ____. → a) gerunds & infinitives · b) Module 6 only · c) articles U36 first skip

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. The music is loud. Turn ____ off. → *it / off it / on*
2. Pick ____ up — it's on the floor. → *it / up it / them*
3. It's raining. Put your coat ____. → *on / on it / off*
4. Take your shoes ____. → *off / off them / on*
5. Please give ____ back. → *it / back it / it back*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **it** · 2. **it** · 3. **on** · 4. **off** · 5. **it**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. Turn / it / off, / please.
7. Pick / up / your / pencil.
8. Give / the / keys / back.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. Turn it off, please.
7. Pick up your pencil. / Pick your pencil up.
8. Give the keys back. / Give back the keys.

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Una frase con *pick up* (nombre).
10. Una frase con *put it on*.
11. Una frase con *take them off*.
12. Dos formas de *turn off the TV*.
13. Una frase con *give it back*.
14. Contrasta *look for* (inseparable) y *pick up* (separable).
15. Párrafo (8–10 frases) mañana o llegada a casa con separables.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. Pick up the book. / Pick the book up.
10. Here's your jacket. Put it on.
11. Take your shoes off. / Take them off.
12. Turn off the TV. / Turn the TV off.
13. Can you give it back, please?
14. I look for my keys (inseparable). I pick my keys up (separable).
15. Every morning I wake up and get up. I look for my phone. Then I put on my clothes and pick up my bag. Before I leave, I turn the lights off. If I borrow my sister's scarf, I give it back in the evening.

</details>
