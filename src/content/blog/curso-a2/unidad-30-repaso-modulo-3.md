  - question: ¿Cómo elijo entre going to, will y Present Continuous?
    answer: >-
      Going to: plan/intención o evidencia. Will: promesa, oferta o predicción
      general. Present Continuous: cita o plan ya fijado en la agenda.
  - question: ¿Zero o First Conditional?
    answer: >-
      Zero (Present + Present) para hechos siempre verdaderos. First (Present +
      will) para posibilidades futuras: If it rains, I will take an umbrella.
  - question: ¿Have to y could en una frase?
    answer: >-
      Have to = obligación. Don't have to = no necesario. Could = habilidad
      pasada o Could you…? para pedir con educación.

## 1. Formas rápidas (cheat sheet)

![Formas rápidas del Módulo 3](/blog/curso-a2/unit-30/a2-unit30-quick-forms.png)

| Tema | Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Going to | *am/is/are going to* + inf. | I **am going to** visit my family. |
| Will | *will / won't* + inf. | I **will** travel to London. |
| PC futuro | *am/is/are* + *-ing* | I **am meeting** John at 3. |
| First | *If* + Present, *will* | If it **rains**, I **will take** an umbrella. |
| Zero | *If* + Present, Present | If you **heat** water, it **boils**. |
| Have to | *have/has to* · *don't/doesn't have to* | I **have to** get up early. |
| Could | *could / couldn't* · *Could you…?* | I **could** speak two languages. |

---

## 2. Futuro (U21–25)

> I **am going to** visit my family next week. *(plan)*  
> Look at the clouds! It **is going to** rain. *(evidencia)*  
> Next week I **will travel** to London. *(predicción / decisión)*  
> I **will** help you. *(promesa)*  
> Tomorrow I **am meeting** my boss at 9. *(cita fijada)*

Regla rápida: agenda fijada → PC · plan/evidencia → *going to* · promesa/general → *will*.

## 3. Condicionales (U26–27)

> If it **rains**, I **will take** an umbrella. *(First — futuro posible)*  
> If I **finish** early, I **will have** lunch with a friend. *(First)*  
> If you **heat** water, it **boils**. *(Zero — hecho general)*  
> If you **don't brush** your teeth, they **get** yellow. *(Zero)*

## 4. Have to y could (U28–29)

> I **have to** get up early tomorrow because I have a meeting.  
> I **don't have to** work on Friday.  
> I **have to** wear glasses when I read.  
> When I was young, I **could** speak two languages.  
> When I was a child, I **could** draw well.  
> **Could you** pass me the water, please?  
> **Could you** speak more slowly, please?

## 5. Texto modelo: la próxima semana

> Next week I will travel to London. If it rains, I will take an umbrella. If you heat water, it boils — that is a general fact. I have to get up early tomorrow because I have a meeting. I don't have to work on Friday. When I was young, I could speak two languages. Could you pass me the water, please?

## 6. Listening: dentista y hechos

> Tomorrow I will go to the dentist. If I finish early, I will have lunch with a friend. If you don't brush your teeth, they get yellow — that is a general fact. I have to wear glasses when I read. I don't have to work tomorrow. When I was a child, I could draw well. Could you speak more slowly, please?

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-30/dialogue-dentist.mp3" title="🔊 Tomorrow I will go to the dentist…"></audio>

Preguntas rápidas:

1. ¿Mañana? → *dentist* (*will*)  
2. ¿Si termina temprano? → *lunch with a friend* (First)  
3. ¿Hecho general? → *don't brush → teeth get yellow* (Zero)  
4. ¿Obligación? → *wear glasses*  
5. ¿De niño? → *could draw well*  
6. ¿Petición? → *speak more slowly*

## 7. Mini práctica oral

## 9. Errores frecuentes del módulo

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Tema |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| If it will rain, I will stay… | If it **rains**, I will stay… | First |
| If you heat water, it will boil. *(hecho)* | … it **boils**. | Zero |
| She have to wear glasses. | She **has to** wear… | *have to* |
| When I was young, I can swim. | … I **could** swim. | *could* |
| I will meeting John tomorrow. *(cita)* | I **am meeting** John… | PC futuro |

## 10. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige la forma

1. I ____ visit my family next week. *(plan ya decidido)* · a) will · b) am going to · c) am  
2. If it ____, I will take an umbrella. · a) will rain · b) rains · c) raining  
3. If you heat water, it ____. · a) will boil · b) boils · c) boiled  
4. She ____ wear glasses when she reads. · a) have to · b) has to · c) don't have to  
5. When I was young, I ____ speak two languages. · a) can · b) could · c) will

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b** · 2. **b** · 3. **b** · 4. **b** · 5. **b**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Etiqueta el tema

Escribe **Fut / First / Zero / Have / Could**:

1. Next week I will travel to London.  
2. If it rains, I will take an umbrella.  
3. If you heat water, it boils.  
4. I don't have to work on Friday.  
5. Could you pass me the water, please?  
6. When I was a child, I could draw well.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Fut** · 2. **First** · 3. **Zero** · 4. **Have** · 5. **Could** · 6. **Could**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Completa Londres

Next week I ____ travel to London. If it ____, I will take an umbrella. If you heat water, it ____. I ____ to get up early tomorrow. I ____ have to work on Friday. When I was young, I ____ speak two languages.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**will** · **rains** · **boils** · **have** · **don't** · **could**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Completa el dentista

Tomorrow I will go to the ____. If I finish early, I will have ____ with a friend. If you don't brush your teeth, they ____ yellow. I have to wear ____ when I read. Could you speak more ____, please?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**dentist** · **lunch** · **get** · **glasses** · **slowly**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Empareja

1. going to  
2. will  
3. Present Continuous futuro  
4. First Conditional  
5. Zero Conditional  
6. don't have to  

a) promesa / predicción general · b) hecho siempre · c) plan/evidencia · d) cita fijada · e) futuro posible · f) no necesario

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **c** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **d** · 4 → **e** · 5 → **b** · 6 → **f**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Corrige

1. *If it will rains, I will take an umbrella.*  
2. *If you heat water, it will boils.* *(hecho)*  
3. *She have to get up early.*  
4. *When I was young, I can speak two languages.*  
5. *Could you to pass me the water?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. If it **rains**, I will take an umbrella.  
2. If you heat water, it **boils**.  
3. She **has to** get up early.  
4. When I was young, I **could** speak two languages.  
5. Could you pass me the water?
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Traduce

2. Si llueve, cogeré un paraguas.

5. No tengo que trabajar el viernes.  
6. Cuando era joven, podía hablar dos idiomas.  
7. ¿Podrías pasarme el agua, por favor?  
8. Mañana iré al dentista.  
9. Si termino temprano, comeré con un amigo.  
10. ¿Podrías hablar más despacio, por favor?

### Ejercicio 8 — Mini contraste futuros

1. Cita mañana a las 9 → ____  
2. Promesa de ayudar → ____  
3. Plan con billete comprado → ____  

a) I will help you. · b) I am meeting my boss at 9. · c) I am going to fly to London.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b** · 2. **a** · 3. **c**
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Completa libre (una lógica)

1. If it rains tomorrow, I ____.  
2. I have to ____ every day.  
3. When I was a child, I could ____.  
4. Could you ____, please?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Respuestas modelo: **will stay at home** · **get up early / study** · **swim / draw** · **help me / open the window**
</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu texto integrado

Escribe 10–14 líneas mezclando el Módulo 3:

- 1 × *will* o *going to*  
- 1 × Present Continuous futuro *(opcional)*  
- 1 × First · 1 × Zero  
- 1 × *have to* · 1 × *don't have to*  
- 1 × *could* pasado · 1 × *Could you…?*  

**Modelo:** el texto de Londres o el del dentista.

## 11. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Cheat sheet del Módulo 3.  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–7.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha ambos textos; etiqueta cada estructura.  
4. **5–8 min** — Escribe tu texto integrado (Ejercicio 10).

### Checklist

- [ ] Elijo *going to / will / PC* según el matiz  
- [ ] Distingo First y Zero Conditional  
- [ ] Uso *have to / don't have to*  
- [ ] Uso *could* (pasado) y *Could you…?*  
- [ ] Puedo escribir un relato que mezcle el módulo
