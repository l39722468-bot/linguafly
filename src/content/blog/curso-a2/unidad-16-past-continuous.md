  - question: ¿Cómo se forma el Past Continuous?
    answer: >-
      Con was/were + verbo en -ing. I/he/she/it + was; you/we/they + were.

  - question: ¿Cuándo uso Past Continuous en A2?
    answer: >-
      Para decir qué estaba pasando en un momento del pasado: I was reading at

En la [Unidad 15](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-15-present-perfect-vs-past-simple) elegiste Present Perfect o Past Simple. En la **Unidad 16 del curso A2** (*Past Continuous*) describes **qué estaba pasando** en un momento del pasado: *I was watching TV…*

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 16 — Past Continuous](/curso-a2/unit-16)  
> **Antes:** [U15 — Present Perfect vs Past Simple](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-15-present-perfect-vs-past-simple)

---

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 16 (A2)

- Formar **was/were + -ing**.
- Usar **afirmativa, negativa y preguntas**.
- Describir **actividades en curso** (*watching, studying, cooking, reading…*).
- Ambientar una escena del pasado (sábado por la noche, tarde en el parque…).
- Preparar el contraste con Past Simple (Unidad 17).

![Past Continuous was/were + -ing](/blog/curso-a2/unit-16/a2-unit16-past-continuous.png)

---

## 1. Idea clave

| Tiempo | Idea A2 | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Past Continuous** | acción **en curso** en un momento del pasado | I **was watching** TV at 8pm. |
| Past Simple *(repaso)* | hecho **acabado** | The doorbell **rang**. |

Piensa: **¿Qué estabas haciendo a las…?** → Past Continuous.

---

## 2. Forma: *was/were + -ing*

| Sujeto | Auxiliar | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I / he / she / it | **was** | I **was** watching TV. |
| you / we / they | **were** | They **were** playing football. |

> I **was watching** TV at 8pm.  
> She **was reading** a book last night.  
> They **were playing** football.  
> We **were cooking** dinner.  
> He **was walking** his dog.

## 3. Negativa y preguntas

![Formas del Past Continuous](/blog/curso-a2/unit-16/a2-unit16-forms.png)

### Negativa

> I **wasn't** studying.  
> She **wasn't** sleeping.  
> We **weren't** working.  
> They **weren't** watching TV.

### Preguntas

> **Was** she reading?  
> **Were** you working?  
> **Was** he walking his dog?  
> **Were** they playing football?

Respuestas cortas:

- *Yes, I was.* / *No, I wasn't.*  
- *Yes, they were.* / *No, they weren't.*

## 4. Ortografía de *-ing* (repaso útil)

| Regla | Base | -ing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| + *-ing* | watch, read, play | watching, reading, playing |
| quita *-e* muda | come, write | coming, writing |
| doble consonante corta | run, swim, get | running, swimming, getting |
| *-ie* → *-y* | lie | lying |

En esta unidad verás mucho: *watching, studying, cooking, reading, running, playing, walking, sleeping, raining, coming*.

## 5. Texto modelo: Saturday night

> Last Saturday night at 8pm I was watching TV in my living room. My sister was studying in her room. My parents were cooking dinner in the kitchen. Suddenly the doorbell rang. It was our neighbour Mr Brown. He was walking his dog when he saw our lights on. He wanted to ask if we had seen his cat. We were all surprised but we helped him look. At that moment the cat was sleeping under the sofa!

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-16/reading-saturday.mp3" title="🔊 Last Saturday night at 8pm I was watching TV…"></audio>

![Escena del sábado a las 8](/blog/curso-a2/unit-16/a2-unit16-saturday-night.png)

| Quién | Estaba… |
| :--- | :--- |
| Narrador | watching TV |
| Sister | studying |
| Parents | cooking dinner |
| Mr Brown | walking his dog |
| Cat | sleeping under the sofa |

Nota: *the doorbell **rang*** es Past Simple (hecho puntual). El ambiente es Past Continuous. En la **Unidad 17** practicarás más este contraste con *when/while*.

## 6. Listening: en el parque

> Yesterday afternoon at 3pm I was reading a book in the park. My friend was running around the lake. Some children were playing football near. Suddenly it started to rain. Everyone was running to find shelter. I was getting wet when a kind woman offered me her umbrella. We waited under a tree until the rain stopped. At that moment the sun was coming out again. I was grateful for her help.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-16/dialogue-park.mp3" title="🔊 Yesterday afternoon at 3pm I was reading…"></audio>

Preguntas rápidas:

1. ¿Qué hacía el hablante a las 3? → *reading a book*  
2. ¿Y su amigo? → *running around the lake*  
3. ¿Los niños? → *playing football*  
4. ¿Qué pasó de repente? → *it started to rain* (Past Simple)

## 9. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I were watching TV. | I **was** watching TV. | *I* → *was* |
| They was playing. | They **were** playing. | *they* → *were* |
| She was study. | She was **studying**. | hace falta *-ing* |
| Was you working? | **Were** you working? | *you* → *were* |
| I watching TV at 8pm. | I **was** watching TV at 8pm. | falta *was* |
| I was watch TV. | I was **watching** TV. | *-ing* |

## 10. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — *was* o *were*

1. I ____ watching TV at 8pm.  
2. They ____ playing football.  
3. She ____ reading a book.  
4. We ____ cooking dinner.  
5. He ____ walking his dog.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **was** · 2. **were** · 3. **was** · 4. **were** · 5. **was**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa con *-ing*

1. I was ____ (watch) TV.  
2. My sister was ____ (study).  
3. My parents were ____ (cook) dinner.  
4. Some children were ____ (play) football.  
5. The cat was ____ (sleep) under the sofa.  
6. My friend was ____ (run) around the lake.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **watching** · 2. **studying** · 3. **cooking** · 4. **playing** · 5. **sleeping** · 6. **running**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Negativa

1. I was studying. → ____  
2. They were working. → ____  
3. She was sleeping. → ____

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **wasn't** studying.  
2. They **weren't** working.  
3. She **wasn't** sleeping.
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Preguntas

1. she / read → ____  
2. you / work → ____  
3. they / play football → ____  
4. he / walk his dog → ____

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Was** she reading?  
2. **Were** you working?  
3. **Were** they playing football?  
4. **Was** he walking his dog?
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Completa el sábado

Last Saturday night at 8pm I ____ watching TV. My sister ____ studying. My parents ____ cooking dinner. Mr Brown ____ walking his dog. The cat ____ sleeping under the sofa.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**was** · **was** · **were** · **was** · **was**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa el parque

Yesterday afternoon at 3pm I ____ reading a book. My friend ____ running around the lake. Some children ____ playing football. I ____ getting wet when a woman offered me her umbrella. The sun ____ coming out again.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**was** · **was** · **were** · **was** · **was**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Corrige

1. *I were watching TV.*  
2. *She was study in her room.*  
3. *Was you working?*  
4. *They was cooking dinner.*  
5. *He walking his dog.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **was** watching TV.  
2. She was **studying** in her room.  
3. **Were** you working?  
4. They **were** cooking dinner.  
5. He **was** walking his dog.
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Empareja

1. I / he / she / it  
2. you / we / they  
3. wasn't  
4. Were you…?  
5. was + -ing  

a) were · b) was · c) negativa de *was* · d) pregunta con *you* · e) acción en curso (I/he/she/it)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **c** · 4 → **d** · 5 → **e**
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Elige la correcta

1. ____  
   a) I was watching TV. · b) I were watching TV. · c) I was watch TV.  
2. ____  
   a) They was playing. · b) They were playing. · c) They were play.  
3. ____  
   a) Was she reading? · b) Were she reading? · c) Was she read?  
4. ____  
   a) We wasn't cooking. · b) We weren't cooking. · c) We not were cooking.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a** · 2. **b** · 3. **a** · 4. **b**
</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Traduce

1. Estaba viendo la tele a las 8.  
2. Ella estaba estudiando en su habitación.  
3. Mis padres estaban cocinando la cena.  
4. Ellos estaban jugando al fútbol.  
5. Él estaba paseando a su perro.  
6. El gato estaba durmiendo debajo del sofá.  
7. ¿Estabas trabajando?  
8. No estaba estudiando.  
9. Ayer a las 3 estaba leyendo un libro en el parque.  
10. Mi amigo estaba corriendo alrededor del lago.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I was watching TV at 8 o'clock / at 8pm.  
2. She was studying in her room.  
3. My parents were cooking dinner.  
4. They were playing football.  
5. He was walking his dog.  
6. The cat was sleeping under the sofa.  
7. Were you working?  
8. I wasn't studying.  
9. Yesterday at 3pm I was reading a book in the park.  
10. My friend was running around the lake.
</details>

### Ejercicio 11 — Tu escena

Escribe 8–12 frases sobre un momento del pasado (anoche / el sábado / ayer a las 3):

- 4 × *I was… / She was…*  
- 2 × *They/We were…*  
- 1 × negativa · 1 × pregunta  

**Modelo:** sábado en casa o tarde en el parque.

2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–7.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha sábado y parque; marca cada *was/were + -ing*.  
4. **5–8 min** — Escribe y graba tu escena.

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *was* con I/he/she/it y *were* con you/we/they  
- [ ] Añado *-ing* al verbo  
- [ ] Sé hacer negativa y pregunta  
- [ ] Puedo describir una escena en curso en el pasado  
- [ ] Distingo ambiente (Past Continuous) de hecho puntual (Past Simple)
