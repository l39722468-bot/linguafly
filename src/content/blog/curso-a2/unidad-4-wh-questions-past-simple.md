## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 4 (A2)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Formar preguntas: ***Wh-word + did + sujeto + base verb***.
- Elegir bien ***What / Where / When / Who / Why / How***.
- Responder con frases largas (*I went to Valencia with my friends*).
- Ordenar una historia con ***First, Then, After that, Finally***.
- Evitar *\*Where you did go?* y *\*What did you went?*

---

## 1. Fórmula: Wh + did + sujeto + base

| Parte | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| Wh-word | **What** |
| did | **did** |
| sujeto | **you** |
| verbo base | **do** |
| complemento | yesterday? |

> **What did you do** yesterday?  
> **Where did you go** last weekend?  
> **When did she leave**?

![Wh-questions Past Simple A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-4/a2-unit4-wh-questions.png)

Tras ***did***, el verbo va en **base** (*do, go, see*), no en pasado (*did, went, saw*).

## 2. Qué pregunta cada Wh-word

| Wh-word | Pregunta por… | Ejemplo | Respuesta típica |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **What** | acción / cosa | What did you do yesterday? | I went to the beach. |
| **Where** | lugar | Where did you go? | I went to Valencia. |
| **When** | tiempo | When did you leave? | On Sunday afternoon. |
| **Who** | persona | Who did you go with? | With Ana and Pablo. |
| **Why** | razón | Why did you leave early? | Because I was tired. |
| **How** | manera / cómo fue | How did the trip go? | It was amazing! |

> Who did you see at the party?  
> Why did he leave early?  
> How did the exam go?  
> What did you buy at the shop?

## 3. Conectores de relato: First, Then, After that, Finally

| Conector | Uso |
| :--- | :--- |
| **First** | primer paso |
| **Then** | siguiente |
| **After that** | después de eso |
| **Finally** | al final |

![Sequence connectors A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-4/a2-unit4-sequence.png)

> First I went to the beach.  
> Then I swam.  
> After that we had lunch.  
> Finally we went home.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-4/sequence-beach.mp3" title="🔊 First I went to the beach…"></audio>

En entrevistas del curso también organizan las preguntas: *First she asked… Then she asked… Finally she asked…*

## 4. Texto modelo: entrevista de trabajo (Carlos)

> Last month my friend Carlos had an interview for a new job. I asked him many questions about it. First I asked what he did to prepare. He said he studied the company online. Then I asked where the interview took place. He said it was at the office in Madrid. I asked when it happened. He said last Tuesday at 10 o'clock. I asked who he met there. He said he met two managers and the HR director. I asked why he wanted the job. He said because it was interesting and near his home. Finally I asked how it went. He said it went very well and he got the job!

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-4/reading-interview.mp3" title="🔊 Last month my friend Carlos…"></audio>

Preguntas equivalentes en forma directa:

| Wh | Pregunta |
| :--- | :--- |
| What | What did he do to prepare? |
| Where | Where did the interview take place? |
| When | When did it happen? |
| Who | Who did he meet there? |
| Why | Why did he want the job? |
| How | How did it go? |

## 5. Listening: un día de preguntas

> Hi! Yesterday I had a really strange day. My sister called me in the morning and asked me many questions. First she asked what I did last weekend. I said I went to the beach. Then she asked where I went exactly. I said I went to Valencia. She asked when I left. I said I left on Sunday afternoon. She asked who I went with. I said I went with my friends Ana and Pablo. She asked why I did not tell her before. I said because I forgot! Finally she asked how the trip went. I said it was amazing!

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-4/dialogue-sister.mp3" title="🔊 Hi! Yesterday I had a really strange day…"></audio>

Parejas pregunta → respuesta:

- What did you do last weekend? → I went to the beach.  
- Where did you go? → Valencia.  
- When did you leave? → On Sunday afternoon.  
- Who did you go with? → Ana and Pablo.  
- Why didn't you tell her? → Because I forgot.  
- How did the trip go? → It was amazing!

## 6. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Where you did go? | **Where did you go?** | *did* delante del sujeto |
| What did you did? | What did you **do**? | base tras *did* |
| What did you went? | What did you **go**? / What did you **do**? | base, no pasado |
| Where did you went? | Where did you **go**? | *go*, no *went* |
| Who you see? | **Who did you see?** | falta *did* |
| How the trip went? | **How did the trip go?** | *did* + base |

## 8. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige el Wh-word

1. \_\_\_\_ did you do yesterday?  
2. \_\_\_\_ did you go last weekend?  
3. \_\_\_\_ did she leave?  
4. \_\_\_\_ did you see at the party?  
5. \_\_\_\_ did he leave early?  
6. \_\_\_\_ did the exam go?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **What** · 2. **Where** · 3. **When** · 4. **Who** · 5. **Why** · 6. **How**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Ordena

1. did / What / you / do / yesterday  
2. go / did / Where / you  
3. you / Who / did / see  
4. did / Why / leave / he / early

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **What did you do yesterday?**  
2. **Where did you go?**  
3. **Who did you see?**  
4. **Why did he leave early?**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Completa con la base correcta

1. What did you \_\_\_\_ yesterday? (do)  
2. Where did she \_\_\_\_? (go)  
3. Who did you \_\_\_\_ at the party? (see)  
4. What did you \_\_\_\_ at the shop? (buy)  
5. How did the trip \_\_\_\_? (go)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **do** · 2. **go** · 3. **see** · 4. **buy** · 5. **go**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Empareja pregunta y respuesta

1. What did you do?  
2. Where did you go?  
3. When did you leave?  
4. Who did you go with?  
5. How did it go?  

a) To Valencia. · b) It was amazing! · c) I went to the beach. · d) On Sunday afternoon. · e) Ana and Pablo.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **c** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **d** · 4 → **e** · 5 → **b**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Conectores

Completa: First / Then / After that / Finally

\_\_\_\_ I went to the beach. \_\_\_\_ I swam. \_\_\_\_ we had lunch. \_\_\_\_ we went home.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**First** · **Then** · **After that** · **Finally**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa la entrevista (Carlos)

First I asked \_\_\_\_ he did to prepare. Then I asked \_\_\_\_ the interview took place. I asked \_\_\_\_ it happened. I asked \_\_\_\_ he met there. I asked \_\_\_\_ he wanted the job. Finally I asked \_\_\_\_ it went.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**what** · **where** · **when** · **who** · **why** · **how**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Corrige

1. *Where you did go?*  
2. *What did you did yesterday?*  
3. *Where did you went?*  
4. *Who you see at the party?*  
5. *How the trip went?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Where did you go?**  
2. What did you **do** yesterday?  
3. Where did you **go**?  
4. **Who did you see** at the party?  
5. **How did the trip go?**
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Completa el listening

First she asked \_\_\_\_ I did last weekend. Then she asked \_\_\_\_ I went. She asked \_\_\_\_ I left. She asked \_\_\_\_ I went with. She asked \_\_\_\_ I did not tell her. Finally she asked \_\_\_\_ the trip went.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**what** · **where** · **when** · **who** · **why** · **how**
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Traduce

1. ¿Qué hiciste ayer?  
2. ¿Dónde fuiste el fin de semana pasado?  
3. ¿Cuándo se fue ella?  
4. ¿A quién viste en la fiesta?  
5. ¿Por qué se fue temprano?  
6. ¿Cómo fue el viaje?  
7. Primero fui a la playa.  
8. Finalmente volvimos a casa.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. What did you do yesterday?  
2. Where did you go last weekend?  
3. When did she leave?  
4. Who did you see at the party?  
5. Why did he/she leave early?  
6. How did the trip go?  
7. First I went to the beach.  
8. Finally we went home.
</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Escribe tu entrevista

Escribe 8–12 frases: incluye las 6 Wh-questions (o sus respuestas) sobre tu último fin de semana / viaje, y usa **First, Then, Finally** (y si puedes *After that*).

**Modelo:**

> Last weekend I went to the beach. First I packed my bag. Then I went to Valencia with Ana and Pablo. After that we had lunch. Finally we went home. It was amazing!

## 9. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Repasa la fórmula y la tabla Wh.  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–7.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha la entrevista y el diálogo de la hermana; repite las preguntas.  
4. **5–8 min** — Graba tus 6 preguntas + respuestas sobre el fin de semana.

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *Wh + did + sujeto + base*  
- [ ] Distingo What / Where / When / Who / Why / How  
- [ ] No pongo pasado tras *did*  
- [ ] Ordeno con First / Then / After that / Finally  
- [ ] Puedo preguntar y responder sobre un viaje corto

---

## 10. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Memoriza el esqueleto: ***did + sujeto + base*** — el Wh solo cambia el tipo de información.  
2. Practica en pareja: uno pregunta, otro responde con *went / saw / had* (U3).  
3. Si fallas el verbo en pasado, vuelve a [U2](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-2-past-simple-verbos-regulares) / [U3](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-3-past-simple-verbos-irregulares).  
4. Practica en la [Unidad 4 del curso A2](/curso-a2/unit-4).

La **Unidad 5** entra en **comparativos**: *bigger than*, *more expensive than*, *as… as*.
