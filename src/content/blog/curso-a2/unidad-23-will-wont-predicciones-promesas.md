En la [Unidad 22](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-22-going-to-predicciones) predijiste con *going to* y evidencia. En la **Unidad 23 del curso A2** (*Will / Won't: Predictions & Promises*) aprendes **will** y **won't** para **promesas**, **ofertas** y **predicciones generales**.

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 23 — Will / Won't](/curso-a2/unit-23)  
> **Antes:** [U22 — Going to: Predictions](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-22-going-to-predicciones)

---

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 23 (A2)

- Usar **will** + infinitivo (afirmativa).
- Usar **won't** (= *will not*) en negativa.
- Hacer preguntas: **Will you…?**
- Expresar **promesas**, **ofertas** y **predicciones**.
- Contrastar con *going to* (U21–22).

![Will y won't A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-23/a2-unit23-will-wont.png)

---

## 1. Idea clave

| Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Promise** | I **will** help you. / I **won't** forget. |
| **Offer** | I **will** buy the drinks. / Maybe I **will** bring some music. |
| **Prediction** | I think the exam **will** be easy. / They **will** probably arrive late. |

Forma: **will / won't** + infinitivo (igual para *I, you, he, she, they…*).

---

## 2. Afirmativa, negativa e interrogativa

| Tipo | Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| + | *will* + infinitivo | She **will** be here tomorrow. |
| − | *won't* + infinitivo | I **won't** forget your birthday. |
| ? | *Will* + sujeto + infinitivo…? | **Will** you come to the party? |

Contracciones: *I'll, you'll, he'll, she'll, we'll, they'll* · *won't* = *will not*.

Respuestas cortas: *Yes, I will. / No, I won't.*

> I **will** help you with that.  
> She **will** be here tomorrow.  
> I **won't** forget. I promise.  
> **Will** you come to the party?  
> They **will** probably arrive late.

## 3. Promesas y ofertas

Frases útiles del curso:

| Función | Frases |
| :--- | :--- |
| Promesa | I **promise**… · I **will** help you · I **won't** forget · I **won't** give up · I **will** be there for you |
| Oferta | I **will** buy the drinks · Maybe I **will** bring… · Don't worry! I **will**… |
| Ánimo | Don't worry! · Everyone **will** have fun |

> I promise I **will** help you with your homework tomorrow.  
> Don't worry! I **will** call you at 6 o'clock.  
> I **won't** be late. I promise!  
> Maybe I **will** bring some cookies too.

## 4. Predicciones generales

Con *will* a menudo usas *I think*, *probably*, *Maybe*:

> I think the exam **will** be easy next week.  
> They **will** probably arrive late.  
> I think the party **will** be great.  
> Everyone **will** have fun.

No hace falta evidencia visual (*Look at the clouds!*). Es una predicción más **general** u opinión.

## 5. *Going to* vs *will* (mapa rápido)

![Going to vs will](/blog/curso-a2/unit-23/a2-unit23-going-to-vs-will.png)

| | *going to* (U21–22) | *will* (U23) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Plan ya decidido | I **am going to** travel to Japan. | — |
| Evidencia ahora | Look! It **is going to** rain. | — |
| Promesa / oferta | — | I **will** help you. |
| Predicción general | — | I think it **will** be easy. |
| Decisión ahora | — | OK, I **'ll** buy the drinks. |

En la Unidad 25 compararás las tres formas de futuro juntas. Aquí domina **will / won't**.

## 7. Listening: fiesta de Sarah

> Hi Sarah! Don't worry about the party tomorrow. I will help you with the preparations. I will buy the drinks and I won't be late. I promise! Maybe I will bring some music too. I think the party will be great. Everyone will have fun. I won't forget the balloons!

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-23/dialogue-sarah.mp3" title="🔊 Hi Sarah! Don't worry about the party…"></audio>

Preguntas rápidas:

1. ¿Qué hay mañana? → *a party*  
2. ¿Qué comprará? → *the drinks*  
3. ¿Llegará tarde? → *No* (*I won't be late*)  
4. ¿Qué más puede traer? → *music*  
5. ¿Qué no olvidará? → *the balloons*

## 10. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I will to help you. | I will help you. | Sin *to* después de *will* |
| She wills be here. | She **will** be here. | *will* no cambia con el sujeto |
| I will not to forget. | I **won't** forget. / I will not forget. | Infinitivo sin *to* |
| Will come you? | **Will you come?** | Orden: *Will* + sujeto |
| I don't will forget. | I **won't** forget. | Negativa = *won't*, no *don't will* |

## 11. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — *will* o *won't*

1. I ____ help you with that. *(promesa +)*  
2. I ____ forget your birthday. I promise.  
3. She ____ be here tomorrow. *(predicción +)*  
4. I ____ be late. I promise!  
5. They ____ probably arrive late.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **will** · 2. **won't** · 3. **will** · 4. **won't** · 5. **will**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa con el verbo

Usa: *help / call / bring / buy / forget*

1. I will ____ you with your homework.  
2. I will ____ you at 6 o'clock.  
3. Maybe I will ____ some cookies.  
4. I will ____ the drinks.  
5. I won't ____ the balloons!

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **help** · 2. **call** · 3. **bring** · 4. **buy** · 5. **forget**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Preguntas

1. ____ you come to the party?  
2. ____ she be here tomorrow?  
3. ____ they arrive late?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Will** you come to the party?  
2. **Will** she be here tomorrow?  
3. **Will** they arrive late?
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Promesa, oferta o predicción

Marca **Pr** (promesa), **O** (oferta) o **Pred** (predicción):

1. I will help you with the preparations.  
2. I think the exam will be easy.  
3. I won't forget. I promise!  
4. Maybe I will bring some music.  
5. They will probably arrive late.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Pr/O** (promesa u oferta de ayuda) · 2. **Pred** · 3. **Pr** · 4. **O** · 5. **Pred**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Completa la carta a Maria

Dear Maria, I promise I ____ help you with your homework tomorrow. Don't worry! I will ____ you at 6 o'clock. I ____ forget. Maybe I will ____ some cookies too. I think the exam ____ be easy next week.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**will** · **call** · **won't** · **bring** · **will**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa a Sarah

I will help you with the ____. I will buy the ____ and I won't be ____. Maybe I will bring some ____ too. I won't forget the ____!

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**preparations** · **drinks** · **late** · **music** · **balloons**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Empareja

1. I promise  
2. Don't worry  
3. won't  
4. Will you…?  
5. probably  

a) pregunta · b) refuerza promesa · c) negativa de *will* · d) tranquiliza · e) predicción incierta

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **d** · 3 → **c** · 4 → **a** · 5 → **e**
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Corrige

1. *I will to help you.*  
2. *She wills be here tomorrow.*  
3. *I don't will forget.*  
4. *Will come you to the party?*  
5. *I will not to be late.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I will help you.  
2. She **will** be here tomorrow.  
3. I **won't** forget.  
4. **Will you come** to the party?  
5. I **won't** be late. / I will not be late.
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Traduce

10. No olvidaré los globos.

### Ejercicio 10 — Tus promesas

Escribe 8–12 líneas (carta o mensaje):

- 2 × *I will…* (promesa/oferta)  
- 2 × *I won't…*  
- 1 × *Will you…?*  
- 1 × predicción (*I think… will…*)  
- Incluye *I promise* o *Don't worry*

**Modelo:** carta a Maria o mensaje a Sarah.

## 13. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. ¿Prometes, ofreces o predices? → elige **will**.  
2. ¿Plan ya decidido o evidencia clara? → vuelve a **going to**.  
3. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 23](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-23-will-wont-predicciones-promesas-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 23 del curso A2](/curso-a2/unit-23).
