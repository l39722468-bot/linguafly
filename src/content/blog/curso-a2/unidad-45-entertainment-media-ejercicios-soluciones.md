## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** watch / listen to / read; like + -ing; prefer… to…

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ films at the weekend. → *watch / listen / read*
2. I listen ____ music every day. → *to / at / for*
3. I like ____ series. *(-ing)* → *watching / watch / to watch*
4. I prefer comedies ____ dramas. → *to / than / over*
5. She loves ____ to jazz. *(-ing)* → *listening / listen / to listen*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **watch** · 2. **to** · 3. **watching** · 4. **to** · 5. **listening**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *Listen to music* (no *listen music*): ____. → a) correcto · b) listen music · c) listen the music only
7. *Like + -ing*: ____. → a) I like watching · b) I like watch · c) I like to watching only
8. *Prefer A to B* (no *than*): ____. → a) correcto · b) prefer than · c) prefer over only
9. *The news is* (uncountable): ____. → a) correcto · b) the news are · c) a news
10. Unit 45 follows ____. → a) Weather U44 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U46

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) correcto** · 7. **a) I like watching** · 8. **a) correcto** · 9. **a) correcto** · 10. **a) Weather U44**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I listen music every day.*
12. *I like watch documentaries.*
13. *I prefer action than romance.*
14. *The news are interesting.*
15. *This is a good serie.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I **listen to** music every day.
12. I like **watching** documentaries.
13. I prefer action **to** romance.
14. The news **is** interesting.
15. This is a good **series**.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** film, series, documentary, concert, news, podcast; comedy, drama, horror.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *documentary* ≈ ____ → *documental · concierto · comedia*
2. *concert* ≈ ____ → *concierto · noticias · podcast*
3. *comedy* ≈ ____ → *comedia · terror · drama*
4. *news* ≈ ____ → *noticias · serie · película*
5. *podcast* ≈ ____ → *audio programa · museo · playa*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **documental** · 2. **concierto** · 3. **comedia** · 4. **noticias** · 5. **audio programa**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. I watched a ____ about the ocean. → *documentary / comedy / concert*
7. We're going to a ____ on Friday. → *concert / forecast / platform*
8. She listens to a ____. → *podcast / souvenir / ticket*
9. I usually prefer ____. *(género divertido)* → *comedies / horrors / storms*
10. Crime ____ (Mia loves). → *dramas / degrees / delays*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **documentary** · 7. **concert** · 8. **podcast** · 9. **comedies** · 10. **dramas**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Watch* for films/TV: ____. → a) yes · b) listen to films · c) read films only
12. *Read* for news online: ____. → a) yes · b) watch news only wrong always · c) listen news
13. Review: film was ____. → a) Excellent · b) Included · c) Windy
14. Omar prefers ____. → a) Films · b) Only podcasts · c) Umbrella
15. Unit 45 next is ____. → a) Shopping U46 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U46

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) yes** · 12. **a) yes** · 13. **a) Excellent** · 14. **a) Films** · 15. **a) Shopping U46**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: A short review

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-45/reading-media.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: A short review"></audio>

> Last night I watched a documentary about the ocean. It was interesting and a bit sad. I usually prefer comedies, but this film was excellent. My sister likes watching series on her phone. She listens to podcasts too. On Friday we're going to a concert.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Last night watched a ____. → a) Documentary · b) Comedy only · c) Forecast
2. About the ____. → a) Ocean · b) Train · c) Hotel
3. Usually prefers ____. → a) Comedies · b) Horror only · c) Storms
4. This film was ____. → a) Excellent · b) Included · c) Foggy
5. Friday plan: ____. → a) Concert · b) Beach · c) Check-out

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Documentary** · 2. **a) Ocean** · 3. **a) Comedies** · 4. **a) Excellent** · 5. **a) Concert**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Sister likes watching series on her ____. → a) Phone · b) Platform · c) Gate
7. She listens to ____. → a) Podcasts · b) Tickets · c) Timetables
8. Film was a bit ____. → a) Sad · b) Sunny · c) Windy
9. *Watched* = ____. → a) Past Simple · b) Present Perfect only · c) going to
10. Matches unit 45: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only travel

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Phone** · 7. **a) Podcasts** · 8. **a) Sad** · 9. **a) Past Simple** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Watched a documentary* = ____. → a) watch · b) listen to · c) get to
12. *Likes watching series* = ____. → a) like + -ing · b) I'd like · c) should
13. *Prefer comedies* (usual) = ____. → a) prefer · b) arrive at · c) by bus
14. Review uses ____. → a) watch/listen/like -ing · b) only articles · c) only should
15. Media text writing uses ____. → a) entertainment structures · b) only did · c) only phrasals

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) watch** · 12. **a) like + -ing** · 13. **a) prefer** · 14. **a) watch/listen/like -ing** · 15. **a) entertainment structures**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ films at the weekend. → *watch / listen / read*
2. I listen ____ music. → *to / at / for*
3. I like ____ series. *(-ing)* → *watching / watch / watches*
4. I prefer comedies ____ dramas. → *to / than / over*
5. She ____ the news in the morning. → *reads / watches / listens*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **watch** · 2. **to** · 3. **watching** · 4. **to** · 5. **reads**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. watching / like / I / series
7. to / listen / music / I
8. comedies / prefer / I / to / dramas

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I like watching series.
7. I listen to music.
8. I prefer comedies to dramas.

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Una frase con *watch*, *listen to* o *read*.
10. Una frase con *like* + -ing.
11. Una frase con *prefer… to…*
12. Una frase con un género (*comedy*, *drama*, *documentary*).
13. Una frase con *series* o *film*.
14. Corrige: *I listen music every day.*
15. Recomienda una película o serie (8–10 líneas) con estructuras de ocio.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. I watch films at the weekend. / I listen to music every day. / She reads the news.
10. I like watching series. / She loves listening to jazz.
11. I prefer comedies to dramas. / Omar prefers films to series (usually).
12. I love watching crime dramas. / I watched a documentary about the ocean.
13. Have you seen that new series? / This film was excellent.
14. I **listen to** music every day.
15. Last night I watched a documentary about the ocean. It was interesting and a bit sad. I usually prefer comedies, but this film was excellent. My sister likes watching series on her phone. She listens to podcasts too. On Friday we're going to a concert.

</details>
