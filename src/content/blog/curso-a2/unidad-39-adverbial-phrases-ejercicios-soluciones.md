## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** time / place / frequency phrases; place before time.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I study ____ the morning. → *in / at / on*
2. I'm busy ____ the moment. → *in / at / on*
3. We meet ____ a week. → *once / one time / one*
4. She works ____ home ____ Mondays. → *at / in · on / at*
5. I cook ____ home ____ the evening. → *at / in · in / at*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **in** · 2. **at** · 3. **once** · 4. **at / on** · 5. **at / in**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *At the moment* = ____. → a) ahora mismo · b) por las mañanas · c) una vez
7. *At night* (no *in the night*): ____. → a) correcto · b) siempre in · c) on night
8. *Place before time*: ____. → a) at home in the evening · b) in the evening at home · c) Who called
9. *Once a week* = ____. → a) frecuencia · b) lugar · c) país
10. Unit 39 follows ____. → a) should U38 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U40

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) ahora mismo** · 7. **a) correcto** · 8. **a) at home in the evening** · 9. **a) frecuencia** · 10. **a) should U38**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I watch TV in the night.*
12. *We meet one time a week.*
13. *She studies in the afternoon at school.*
14. *I'm free in the moment.*
15. *He stays in home on Sundays.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I watch TV **at night**.
12. We meet **once** a week.
13. She studies **at school in the afternoon**.
14. I'm free **at the moment**.
15. He stays **at home** on Sundays.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** in the morning, at night, at the moment, at home, in the park, once a week, at weekends.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *in the morning* ≈ ____ → *parte del día · ahora · frecuencia*
2. *at the moment* ≈ ____ → *ahora mismo · los lunes · en el parque*
3. *once a week* ≈ ____ → *una vez por semana · cada mañana · en casa*
4. *at weekends* ≈ ____ → *fines de semana · por la noche · el momento*
5. *in the park* ≈ ____ → *lugar · tiempo · modal*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **parte del día** · 2. **ahora mismo** · 3. **una vez por semana** · 4. **fines de semana** · 5. **lugar**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. I drink coffee ____ the morning. → *in / at / on*
7. They travel ____ weekends. → *at / in / on*
8. He calls his parents ____ a week. → *once / one / every*
9. Meet me ____ the station. → *at / in / on*
10. She's not here ____ the moment. → *at / in / on*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **in** · 7. **at** · 8. **once** · 9. **at** · 10. **at**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Every day* clasifica como ____. → a) frequency · b) place only · c) subject Q
12. *At home on Sundays* orden: ____. → a) place + time · b) time + place · c) Who called
13. *In July* = ____. → a) time (mes) · b) place · c) phrasal
14. *From time to time* = ____. → a) de vez en cuando · b) ahora mismo · c) once only
15. Unit 39 next is ____. → a) Module 4 review · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U40

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) frequency** · 12. **a) place + time** · 13. **a) time (mes)** · 14. **a) de vez en cuando** · 15. **a) Module 4 review**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: My week

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-39/reading-adverbials.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: My week"></audio>

> Leo wakes up early in the morning. He has breakfast at home and then goes to class. At the moment, he is preparing for an English exam, so he reviews vocabulary every day. He meets his friends in the park once a week. At weekends, he stays at home in the evening and watches films.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Leo wakes up ____. → a) Early in the morning · b) At night only · c) Once a week
2. Breakfast? → a) At home · b) In the park · c) At the station
3. At the moment he ____. → a) Prepares for exam · b) Watches films · c) Meets friends
4. Reviews vocabulary ____. → a) Every day · b) Once a week · c) At weekends only
5. At weekends evenings ____. → a) Stays at home · b) Goes to class · c) Runs in park

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Early in the morning** · 2. **a) At home** · 3. **a) Prepares for exam** · 4. **a) Every day** · 5. **a) Stays at home**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. *In the morning* = ____. → a) time · b) place · c) modal
7. *At the moment* = ____. → a) now · b) every day · c) at weekends
8. *In the park once a week* = ____. → a) place + frequency · b) time only · c) should
9. *At home in the evening* = ____. → a) place before time · b) time before place · c) Who called
10. Matches unit 39: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) time** · 7. **a) now** · 8. **a) place + frequency** · 9. **a) place before time** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Every day* modifies ____. → a) how often · b) where only · c) Who
12. *At weekends* = ____. → a) frequency/time · b) at home · c) a/an
13. *Goes to class* (after breakfast) = routine ____. → a) time sequence · b) only phrasal · c) only should
14. Reading marks ____. → a) adverbial phrases · b) only articles · c) only like
15. Week routine writing uses ____. → a) time/place/frequency · b) only did · c) only much

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) how often** · 12. **a) frequency/time** · 13. **a) time sequence** · 14. **a) adverbial phrases** · 15. **a) time/place/frequency**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Making plans

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-39/listening-adverbials.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Making plans"></audio>

> Ana: Are you free at the weekend?  
> Leo: In the morning, yes. In the afternoon, I work.  
> Ana: OK. Let's meet in the café on Saturday morning.  
> Leo: Perfect. I go there once a week anyway.  
> Ana: Great. I'm busy at the moment, but I'll message you later at night.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Ana asks if Leo is free ____. → a) At the weekend · b) Every day · c) At the moment
2. Leo is free ____. → a) In the morning · b) In the afternoon · c) At night
3. Afternoon? → a) Works · b) Free · c) In the park
4. Meet where/when? → a) In the café on Saturday morning · b) At home at night · c) Once a week only
5. Ana is busy ____. → a) At the moment · b) At the weekend · c) In the park

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) At the weekend** · 2. **a) In the morning** · 3. **a) Works** · 4. **a) In the café on Saturday morning** · 5. **a) At the moment**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. *At the weekend* = ____. → a) time · b) place · c) should
7. *In the morning / In the afternoon* contrast ____. → a) parts of day · b) countries · c) phrasals
8. *I go there once a week* = ____. → a) frequency · b) place only · c) Who called
9. *Message you later at night* = ____. → a) time phrase · b) at home · c) a/an
10. Unit 39 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Module 6

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) time** · 7. **a) parts of day** · 8. **a) frequency** · 9. **a) time phrase** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Free ____ the weekend? → a) at · b) in · c) on
12. Meet ____ the café ____ Saturday morning → a) in / on · b) at / in · c) on / at
13. Busy ____ the moment → a) at · b) in · c) on
14. Shadow and note ____. → a) time/place/frequency · b) only a/an · c) only phrasals
15. U39 closes before ____. → a) Module 4 review U40 · b) Module 6 only · c) U31 skip

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) at** · 12. **a) in / on** · 13. **a) at** · 14. **a) time/place/frequency** · 15. **a) Module 4 review U40**

</details>

4. She works ____ home ____ Mondays. → *at / in · on / at*
5. I cook ____ home ____ the evening. → *at / in · in / at*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **in** · 2. **at** · 3. **once** · 4. **at / on** · 5. **at / in**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. I / study / at / home / in / the / evening.
7. They / play / in / the / park / on / Sundays.
8. She / works / at / school / in / the / morning.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I study at home in the evening.
7. They play in the park on Sundays.
8. She works at school in the morning.

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Una frase con *in the morning*.
10. Una frase con *at the moment*.
11. Una frase con *once a week*.
12. Una frase con *at home*.
13. Una frase con place + time (orden correcto).
14. Corrige: *I study in the evening at home.*
15. Describe tu semana típica (8–10 líneas) con time, place y frequency.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>
