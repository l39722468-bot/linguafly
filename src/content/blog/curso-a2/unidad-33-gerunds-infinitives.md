En la [Unidad 32](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-32-phrasal-verbs-separables) practicaste phrasal verbs separables. En la **Unidad 33 del curso A2** (*Gerunds & Infinitives*) aprendes **qué forma va después** de verbos frecuentes: *-ing* o *to* + infinitivo.

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 33 — Gerunds & Infinitives](/curso-a2/unit-33)  
> **Antes:** [U32 — Phrasal verbs separables](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-32-phrasal-verbs-separables)

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 33 (A2)

- Usar **like / love / hate + -ing**.
- Usar **want / need + to** + infinitivo.
- Usar **would like (I'd like) + to** para deseos educados.
- Hablar de **gustos** y **planes / necesidades**.
- Evitar mezclas típicas (*I want going*, *I'd like swimming*).

## 1. Idea clave: dos “paquetes”

| Paquete | Forma | Verbos de esta unidad |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gerundio** | verbo + **-ing** | like, love, hate |
| **Infinitivo** | **to** + verbo base | want, need, would like |

> I **like swimming**.  
> I **want to swim**.

No es “traducir palabra por palabra”: es **memorizar el patrón** del verbo principal.

## 2. *Like / love / hate* + **-ing**

| Verbo | Ejemplo | Idea |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **like** | I **like reading**. | me gusta |
| **love** | She **loves cooking**. | le encanta |
| **hate** | They **hate waiting**. | odian / no soportan |

También: *I like watching films*, *He loves playing football*, *We hate getting up early*.

**Negación:** *I don't like getting up early.*  
**Pregunta:** *Do you like dancing?*

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-33/hate-waiting.mp3" title="🔊 I hate waiting in long queues."></audio>

## 3. *Want / need* + **to** + infinitivo

| Verbo | Ejemplo | Idea |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **want** | I **want to learn** English. | quiero |
| **need** | You **need to rest**. | necesitas |

> He **wants to buy** a new phone.  
> We **need to leave** now.

**Negación:** *I don't want to go.* / *You don't need to worry.*  
**Pregunta:** *Do you want to come?* / *Do I need to pay now?*

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-33/need-to-study.mp3" title="🔊 I need to study for the exam."></audio>

## 4. *Would like* + **to** (más educado)

*Would like* expresa un **deseo educado** (ahora o en el futuro cercano), no un gusto general.

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| Affirmative | I **would like to order** a coffee. / I'd **like to visit** Rome. |
| Negative | I **wouldn't like to live** alone. |
| Question | **Would you like to sit** here? |

Contraste clave:

| | *like* + -ing | *would like* + to |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Uso | gusto habitual | deseo / oferta educada |
| Ejemplo | I **like drinking** coffee. | I'd **like to drink** a coffee now. |

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-33/would-like-to-order.mp3" title="🔊 I'd like to order a coffee, please."></audio>

## 5. Mapa rápido (esta unidad)

![Likes and plans](/blog/curso-a2/unit-33/a2-unit33-likes-plans.png)

| Quieres decir… | Usa… | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Gusto / aversión | like/love/hate + **-ing** | I love **reading**. |
| Querer / necesitar | want/need + **to** | I want **to travel**. |
| Deseo educado | would like + **to** | I'd like **to visit** London. |

En niveles superiores hay más verbos (*enjoy + -ing*, *decide + to*, etc.). En A2, domina **este set**.

---

## 6. Ortografía del *-ing* (repaso breve)

| Regla | Base | -ing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Normal | read, cook, play | reading, cooking, playing |
| -e final | dance, write | dancing, writing |
| CVC corto | swim, run, get | swimming, running, getting |

> I like **swimming**.  
> She hates **getting** up early.

## 7. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura: gustos y planes

I **love reading** in the evening. I also **like cooking**, but I **hate washing** the dishes. Next year I **want to travel** to Ireland. First I **need to save** money. Tonight I'd **like to watch** a film at home.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-33/reading-plans.mp3" title="🔊 I love reading in the evening…"></audio>

### Diálogo: café

**A:** Do you **like drinking** coffee?  
**B:** Yes, I **love it**. I'd **like to order** a cappuccino.  
**A:** I **want to try** the tea. Do we **need to pay** now?  
**B:** No — we can pay later. I **hate waiting** when it's busy, though!

## 8. Errores frecuentes

1. **Want + -ing:** ~~I want going.~~ → **I want to go.**  
2. **Would like + -ing:** ~~I'd like swimming.~~ → **I'd like to swim.**  
3. **Like + to** (a veces existe, pero en esta unidad practica **like + -ing** para gustos).  
4. **Olvidar *to*:** ~~I need rest.~~ → **I need to rest.** *(need + nombre es otro patrón: I need rest / I need a break)*  
5. **Mezclar personas:** *She like* → **She likes** reading.

## 9. Ejercicios (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige *-ing* o *to* + verbo

1. I like ___ (swim).  
2. She wants ___ (learn) French.  
3. They hate ___ (wait).  
4. We need ___ (leave) early.  
5. I'd like ___ (visit) the museum.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. swimming  
2. to learn  
3. waiting  
4. to leave  
5. to visit  

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa con el verbo correcto

Usa: *like / love / hate / want / need / would like* (una vez cada uno, forma adecuada).

1. I ___ cooking Italian food.  
2. He ___ to buy a new laptop.  
3. We ___ getting up at 5 a.m.  
4. You ___ to wear a coat — it's cold.  
5. ___ you ___ to sit here?  
6. Do you ___ watching football?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones (modelo)</summary>

1. love  
2. wants  
3. hate  
4. need  
5. Would / like  
6. like  

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Corrige el error

1. I want going home.  
2. She like dancing.  
3. I'd like eating pizza.  
4. He need to study more.  
5. They hates waiting.

### Ejercicio 4 — Traduce al inglés

1. Me gusta leer.  
2. Quiero viajar el año que viene.  
3. Necesito descansar.  
4. Me encantaría pedir un café.  
5. Odio esperar en colas.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

### Ejercicio 5 — *Like* vs *would like*

Elige la opción más natural:

1. En general: I ___ coffee in the morning.  
   a) like drinking  b) would like to drink  
2. En el café, ahora: I ___ a cappuccino, please.  
   a) like drinking  b) would like to order  
3. Oferta educada: ___ come with us?  
   a) Do you like  b) Would you like to  
4. Gusto: She ___ films.  
   a) loves watching  b) would like to watch  
5. Plan inmediato: We ___ leave now.  
   a) like  b) need to  

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. a  
2. b  
3. b  
4. a  
5. b  

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Reescribe con el patrón correcto

1. (like / cook) I _______________ every weekend.  
2. (want / buy) She _______________ a new bag.  
3. (hate / get up) He _______________ early.  
4. (need / finish) We _______________ this today.  
5. (would like / speak) I _______________ to the manager.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. like cooking  
2. wants to buy  
3. hates getting up  
4. need to finish  
5. would like to speak  

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Preguntas

Forma preguntas:

1. you / like / dance → _______________  
2. she / want / come → _______________  
3. we / need / pay now → _______________  
4. you / would like / sit here → _______________  
5. they / love / travel → _______________

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Do you like dancing?  
2. Does she want to come?  
3. Do we need to pay now?  
4. Would you like to sit here?  
5. Do they love travelling? / Do they love traveling?  

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Completa el diálogo

**A:** Do you ___ (like / watch) series?  
**B:** Yes, I ___ (love) them. Tonight I'd ___ (like / watch) a comedy.  
**A:** Perfect. I ___ (want / buy) some snacks first.  
**B:** We ___ (need / hurry) — it starts at eight. I ___ (hate / miss) the beginning!

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

like watching  
love  
like to watch  
want to buy  
need to hurry  
hate missing  

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Verdadero o falso (gramática)

1. *Want* normalmente va con *to* + infinitivo.  
2. *Hate* en esta unidad va con *-ing*.  
3. *I'd like swimming* es correcto para un deseo educado.  
4. *Need* puede ir con *to* + infinitivo: *need to rest*.  
5. *Love* + *-ing* expresa un gusto fuerte.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. V  
2. V  
3. F → I'd like to swim  
4. V  
5. V  

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu texto (8–12 líneas)

Escribe sobre tus gustos y un plan. Incluye:

- *like / love / hate* + *-ing* (mín. 3)  
- *want to* y *need to* (mín. 1 cada uno)  
- *I'd like to* (mín. 1)

**Modelo:** el texto de la sección 7.

## 10. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Tabla *-ing* vs *to*.  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–6.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha lectura y café; marca cada patrón.  
4. **5–8 min** — Escribe y graba el Ejercicio 10.

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *like/love/hate + -ing*  
- [ ] Uso *want/need + to*  
- [ ] Uso *would like + to*  
- [ ] Distingo *like* (hábito) y *would like* (deseo)  
- [ ] Evito *I want going* / *I'd like swimming*

## 11. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Aprende **verbos en grupos**, no reglas abstractas largas.  
2. Di en voz alta pares: *I like cooking* / *I'd like to cook tonight*.  
3. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 33](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-33-gerunds-infinitives-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 33 del curso A2](/curso-a2/unit-33).

La **Unidad 34** trabaja **Subject & Object Questions** (*Who called you?* / *Who did you call?*).
