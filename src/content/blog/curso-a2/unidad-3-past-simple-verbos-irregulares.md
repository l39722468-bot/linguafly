En la [Unidad 2](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-2-past-simple-verbos-regulares) añadiste *-ed*. En la **Unidad 3 del curso A2** (*Past Simple: Irregular Verbs*) memoritas las formas especiales: ***went, saw, had, made, wrote, bought***.

No hay regla mágica: **hay que aprenderlas**. Esta guía las organiza con tablas, textos del curso, audios y ejercicios.

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 3 — Past Simple: verbos irregulares](/curso-a2/unit-3)  
> **Antes:** [Unidad 2 — Verbos regulares](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-2-past-simple-verbos-regulares)

---

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 3 (A2)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Usar ***go → went***, ***see → saw***, ***have → had***.
- Usar ***make → made***, ***write → wrote***, ***buy → bought***.
- Contar un **fin de semana** o un **viaje** con irregulares.
- Evitar *\*goed*, *\*buyed*, *\*writed*, *\*maked*.
- Distinguir Past Simple (*saw*) del participio (*seen*) — este último no es el foco aún.

---

## 1. Los 6 irregulares prioritarios

| Base | Past Simple | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **go** | **went** | I **went** to the cinema yesterday. |
| **see** | **saw** | She **saw** a film last night. |
| **have** | **had** | We **had** a great time at the party. |
| **make** | **made** | He **made** a cake for his birthday. |
| **write** | **wrote** | I **wrote** a letter last week. |
| **buy** | **bought** | She **bought** a new dress yesterday. |

Misma forma para todas las personas: *I went / she went / they went*.

## 2. Regular vs irregular

| Tipo | Cómo se forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Regular** (U2) | base + *-ed* | walk → **walked** |
| **Irregular** (U3) | forma especial | go → **went** (no *goed*) |

![Regular vs irregular Past Simple A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-3/a2-unit3-regular-vs-irregular.png)

En un mismo relato mezclas ambos:

> I **went** to the market and **bought** fruit. Then I **talked** to my friend. (*talked* = regular)

---

## 3. Errores típicos (¡no los escribas!)

| Incorrecto | Correcto |
| :--- | :--- |
| I goed to London. | I **went** to London. |
| She seed a film. | She **saw** a film. |
| We haved a great time. | We **had** a great time. |
| He maked a cake. | He **made** a cake. |
| I writed a letter. | I **wrote** a letter. |
| She buyed a dress. | She **bought** a dress. |
| I gone to the shop. | I **went** to the shop. (*gone* ≠ Past Simple aquí) |
| I seen a film yesterday. | I **saw** a film yesterday. |

*Gone* y *seen* son **participios** (aparecen más adelante con *have/has*). En Past Simple afirmativo de esta unidad: **went** y **saw**.

---

## 4. Negativa y preguntas (igual que con regulares)

| Tipo | Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Afirmativa | sujeto + irregular | I **went** to London. |
| Negativa | **didn't** + base | I **didn't go** to London. |
| Pregunta | **Did** + sujeto + base? | **Did** you **go** to London? |

Tras *didn't / Did* vuelve la **base**: *go, see, buy* — no *went, saw, bought*.

## 5. Texto modelo: last weekend

In the evening we saw a film on TV. It was very good!

## 9. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige el pasado

1. I \_\_\_\_ to the cinema yesterday. (go)  
2. She \_\_\_\_ a film last night. (see)  
3. We \_\_\_\_ a great time at the party. (have)  
4. He \_\_\_\_ a cake for his birthday. (make)  
5. I \_\_\_\_ a letter last week. (write)  
6. She \_\_\_\_ a new dress yesterday. (buy)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **went** · 2. **saw** · 3. **had** · 4. **made** · 5. **wrote** · 6. **bought**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Empareja

1. go  
2. see  
3. have  
4. make  
5. write  
6. buy  

a) made · b) went · c) bought · d) saw · e) wrote · f) had

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **d** · 3 → **f** · 4 → **a** · 5 → **e** · 6 → **c**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Elige la correcta

1. ____  
   a) I go to London last year. · b) I went to London last year. · c) I gone to London last year.  
2. Past of buy = ____  
   a) buyed · b) bought · c) buied  
3. Past of write = ____  
   a) writed · b) written · c) wrote

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b** · 2. **b** · 3. **c**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Completa el fin de semana

Last weekend I \_\_\_\_ a wonderful time. On Saturday I \_\_\_\_ to the market and \_\_\_\_ some fruit. Then I \_\_\_\_ my friend Tom. We \_\_\_\_ coffee together. In the afternoon I \_\_\_\_ a cake. My sister \_\_\_\_ a letter. In the evening we \_\_\_\_ a film.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**had** · **went** · **bought** · **saw** · **had** · **made** · **wrote** · **saw**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Completa el viaje

I \_\_\_\_ to London with my family. We \_\_\_\_ a wonderful time. We \_\_\_\_ to the British Museum. I \_\_\_\_ many interesting things. We \_\_\_\_ souvenirs. My sister \_\_\_\_ postcards. In the evening we \_\_\_\_ dinner at our hotel.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**went** · **had** · **went** · **saw** · **bought** · **wrote** · **made**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Negativa / pregunta

1. Affirmative: I went to London. → Negative: I \_\_\_\_ go to London.  
2. Affirmative: She bought a book. → Question: \_\_\_\_ she buy a book?  
3. Did you \_\_\_\_ a film last night? (see)

### Ejercicio 10 — Escribe tu fin de semana / viaje

Escribe 8–12 frases. Incluye al menos:

- 2 × *went*  
- 1 × *saw*  
- 1 × *had*  
- 1 × *made* o *wrote*  
- 1 × *bought*  
- (opcional) 1–2 regulares de la U2 (*talked, visited…*)

**Modelo:** sección 5 (weekend) o sección 6 (London).

## 10. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Memoriza la tabla de 6 verbos en voz alta.  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–7.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha el weekend y el viaje; repite.  
4. **5–8 min** — Escribe y graba tu propio relato.

### Checklist

- [ ] Recito go/went, see/saw, have/had  
- [ ] Recito make/made, write/wrote, buy/bought  
- [ ] Evito *goed / buyed / writed*  
- [ ] Uso *saw* (no *seen*) en Past Simple  
- [ ] Tras *didn't / Did* uso la base
