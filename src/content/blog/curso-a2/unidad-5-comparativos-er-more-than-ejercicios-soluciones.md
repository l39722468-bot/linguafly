## Lección 1 — Gramática: -er / more / better

**Objetivo:** *-er than*, *more… than*, *better/worse*, *as… as*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. My brother is ____ than me. → *taller / more tall / tallest*  
2. This book is ____ interesting than that one. → *more / most / -er*  
3. Summer is ____ than winter. → *hotter / more hot / hottest*  
4. This film is ____ than the other. → *better / more good / gooder*  
5. She is ____ tall ____ her sister. → *as / as · more / than · -er / than*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **taller**  
2. **more**  
3. **hotter**  
4. **better**  
5. **as** · **as**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Short adjectives often take ____. → a) *-er* · b) *more* only · c) *the most*  
7. Long adjectives often take ____. → a) *more* · b) *-er* · c) *-est* only  
8. *Good* → ____. → a) better · b) gooder · c) more good  
9. *Bad* → ____. → a) worse · b) badder · c) more bad  
10. Equality uses ____. → a) as… as · b) *-er* · c) *more* only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) *-er***  
7. **a) *more***  
8. **a) better**  
9. **a) worse**  
10. **a) as… as**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *She is more tall than me.*  
12. *This phone is expensiver than that one.*  
13. *Today is more better than yesterday.*  
14. *He is as taller as his brother.*  
15. *Winter is more cold than summer.* (prefer short form)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. She is **taller** than me.  
12. This phone is **more expensive** than that one.  
13. Today is **better** than yesterday.  
14. He is as **tall** as his brother.  
15. Winter is **colder** than summer.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: comparar

**Objetivo:** adjetivos frecuentes en comparativos.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja

1. tall →  
2. big →  
3. hot →  
4. interesting →  
5. expensive →  

a) more interesting · b) hotter · c) taller · d) more expensive · e) bigger

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **c** · 2 → **e** · 3 → **b** · 4 → **a** · 5 → **d**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. fast → ____  
7. quiet → ____  
8. beautiful → ____  
9. useful → ____  
10. light → ____  

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **faster** · 7. **quieter** · 8. **more beautiful** · 9. **more useful** · 10. **lighter**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Bigger* spelling doubles ____. → a) g · b) nothing · c) b only  
12. *Hotter* doubles ____. → a) t · b) h · c) nothing  
13. *Lighter* means ____. → a) less heavy · b) more expensive · c) taller  
14. *More powerful* describes ____. → a) stronger battery/energy · b) quieter · c) shorter  
15. *Prefer* means ____. → a) like more · b) hate · c) buy only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) g**  
12. **a) t**  
13. **a) less heavy**  
14. **a) stronger battery/energy**  
15. **a) like more**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Laura and her sisters

**Objetivo:** leer un texto lleno de comparativos.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-5/reading-comparatives.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Laura and her sisters"></audio>

> My name is Laura and I have two sisters. My older sister Maria is taller than me and she has longer hair than I do. My younger sister Sofia is shorter than both of us but she is faster than us when we run. Our house is bigger than our grandmother's house but grandmother's garden is more beautiful than ours. I think our city is more interesting than the village where grandmother lives but the village is quieter than the city.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Who is taller than Laura? → a) Maria · b) Sofia · c) Grandmother  
2. Who is shorter but faster? → a) Sofia · b) Maria · c) Laura  
3. Whose house is bigger? → a) Their house · b) Grandmother's · c) Same  
4. Whose garden is more beautiful? → a) Grandmother's · b) Theirs · c) The city's  
5. What is quieter? → a) The village · b) The city · c) The house

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Maria**  
2. **a) Sofia**  
3. **a) Their house**  
4. **a) Grandmother's**  
5. **a) The village**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Comparatives with *-er* include ____. → a) taller, longer, shorter, faster, bigger, quieter · b) only more · c) only better  
7. *More beautiful / more interesting* are ____. → a) long-adjective forms · b) short *-er* · c) superlatives  
8. Maria has ____ hair than Laura. → a) longer · b) shorter · c) no hair  
9. The city is ____ than the village. → a) more interesting · b) quieter · c) shorter  
10. The main topic is ____. → a) comparing people and places · b) past questions · c) irregular verbs only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) taller, longer, shorter, faster, bigger, quieter**  
7. **a) long-adjective forms**  
8. **a) longer**  
9. **a) more interesting**  
10. **a) comparing people and places**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Taller than* needs ____. → a) *than* · b) *that* · c) *as* only  
12. *More beautiful than* = ____. → a) correct · b) *beautifuler* · c) *most beautiful than*  
13. *Quieter* is comparative of ____. → a) quiet · b) quite · c) quick  
14. *Faster than us when we run* compares ____. → a) speed · b) price · c) age only  
15. Mixing *-er* and *more* in one text is ____. → a) natural · b) wrong · c) only for B1

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) *than***  
12. **a) correct**  
13. **a) quiet**  
14. **a) speed**  
15. **a) natural**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Two phones

**Objetivo:** escuchar una comparación de dos objetos.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-5/listening-comparatives.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Two phones"></audio>

> Hi! I want to tell you about my two phones. I have an old phone and a new one. The new phone is bigger than the old one but the old one is lighter than the new one. The new phone has a better camera than the old one. The battery is more powerful than before. I think the new phone is more expensive than the old one but it is much more useful than that one. Overall I prefer the new one because it is faster than my old phone.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Which phone is bigger? → a) The new one · b) The old one · c) Same  
2. Which phone is lighter? → a) The old one · b) The new one · c) Neither  
3. Which camera is better? → a) The new phone's · b) The old phone's · c) Same  
4. Is the new phone more expensive? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The audio doesn't say  
5. Why prefer the new one? → a) Faster · b) Lighter · c) Older

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) The new one**  
2. **a) The old one**  
3. **a) The new phone's**  
4. **a) Yes**  
5. **a) Faster**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The battery is ____ than before. → a) more powerful · b) quieter · c) shorter  
7. The new phone is ____ useful. → a) much more · b) less · c) not  
8. *Better camera* uses irregular ____. → a) better · b) gooder · c) more good  
9. Patterns include ____. → a) bigger / lighter / better / more… / faster · b) only *went* · c) only *did*  
10. The listening practices ____. → a) comparatives · b) Wh-questions only · c) Present Perfect

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) more powerful**  
7. **a) much more**  
8. **a) better**  
9. **a) bigger / lighter / better / more… / faster**  
10. **a) comparatives**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Bigger than* = short adj + ____. → a) *-er than* · b) *more than* · c) *as as*  
12. *More expensive than* = ____. → a) long adj pattern · b) short *-er* · c) superlative  
13. *Lighter* means ____. → a) less heavy · b) more heavy · c) taller  
14. *Prefer the new one* = ____. → a) like it more · b) hate it · c) sell it  
15. *Faster than my old phone* compares ____. → a) speed · b) colour · c) age only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) *-er than***  
12. **a) long adj pattern**  
13. **a) less heavy**  
14. **a) like it more**  
15. **a) speed**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: Compare two things

**Objetivo:** escribir comparaciones claras.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. My city is ____ than Valencia. → *bigger / more big / biggest*  
2. This book is ____ interesting than that film. → *more / most / -er*  
3. Today the weather is ____ than yesterday. → *worse / badder / more bad*  
4. She is ____ tall ____ her sister. → *as / as*  
5. The new phone is ____ than the old one. → *faster / more fast / fastly*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **bigger** · 2. **more** · 3. **worse** · 4. **as** · **as** · 5. **faster**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. My / brother / is / taller / than / me.  
7. This / phone / is / more / expensive / than / that one.  
8. She / is / as / tall / as / her sister.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. My brother is taller than me.  
7. This phone is more expensive than that one.  
8. She is as tall as her sister.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Compara altura de dos personas (*taller*).  
10. Compara dos libros (*more interesting*).  
11. Compara verano e invierno (*hotter / colder*).  
12. Usa *better* con una película o móvil.  
13. Usa *as… as* para igualdad.  
14. Compara tu ciudad con otra (2 frases).  
15. Párrafo (6–8 frases) comparando dos cosas (hermanas, móviles o ciudades).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. My brother is taller than me.  
10. This book is more interesting than that one.  
11. Summer is hotter than winter. Winter is colder than summer.  
12. This film is better than the other. / The new phone has a better camera.  
13. She is as tall as her sister.  
14. My city is bigger than Valencia. Valencia is more beautiful than my city.  
15. I have two phones. The new one is bigger and faster. The old one is lighter. The new camera is better. It is more expensive but more useful. I prefer the new one.

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Caso | Forma |
| :--- | :--- |
| Corto | **-er** than |
| Largo | **more** + adj + than |
| good/bad | **better** / **worse** |
| Igualdad | **as** + adj + **as** |
