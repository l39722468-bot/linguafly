## 6. Lectura integrada

### Reading — A weekend in Lisbon

Last Friday we **took the train** to Lisbon and **checked in** at a small hotel. On Saturday it was **sunny**, so we went **sightseeing**. In the evening we **watched** a film — I **prefer comedies** to dramas. On Sunday I bought a scarf: “**How much is it?**” — €12. Later I had a **headache**, so my friend said: “You **should** rest.” Our **neighbour** in the hotel was **friendly**. I've **known** my travel partner **for** ten years — we always **get on** well.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-50/reading-lisbon.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: A weekend in Lisbon"></audio>

### Diálogo — Planning the day

**Mia:** What's the weather **like** today?  
**Omar:** It's **going to be** hot. **Put on** a hat.  
**Mia:** Shall we go **by bus** to the museum?  
**Omar:** Yes. Then I'd like to **watch** a documentary.  
**Mia:** OK — but first **I'm looking for** sunscreen at the pharmacy.  
**Omar:** Good idea. If you feel sick, you **should** drink water.

## 7. Errores frecuentes del módulo

1. ~~I go with train.~~ → **I go by train.**  
2. ~~What's the weather?~~ → **What's the weather like?**  
3. ~~listen music~~ → **listen to music**  
4. ~~How many is it?~~ *(precio)* → **How much is it?**  
5. ~~I have headache.~~ → **I have a headache.**  
6. ~~I put a jacket.~~ → **I put on** a jacket.  
7. ~~How is she like?~~ → **What's she like?**  
8. ~~I know her for years.~~ → **I've known** her for years.

## 8. Ejercicios mixtos (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Empareja unidad y frase

1. Travel  
2. Weather  
3. Shopping  
4. Health  
5. People  

a) I'd like a refund.  
b) You should rest.  
c) Take the bus.  
d) What's she like?  
e) It's going to rain.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1 → c  
2 → e  
3 → a  
4 → b  
5 → d  

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa

1. How do I ___ to the station?  
2. I'd like to ___ a single room.  
3. Have you ever ___ sightseeing in Rome?  
4. What's the weather ___ ?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. get  
2. book  
3. been (or gone)  
4. like  

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Ocio y gustos

2. I listen ___ music every day.  
3. I like ___ (watch) series.

<details>

3. watching  
4. to  

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Compras y ropa

### Ejercicio 9 — Lectura True / False

> Last Friday we took the train to Lisbon and checked in at a small hotel. On Saturday it was sunny, so we went sightseeing.

1. They travelled by train.  
2. They arrived at a hostel.  
3. Saturday was good for outdoor activities.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. True  
2. False (hotel)  
3. True  

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu relato de viaje (10–14 líneas)

Escribe sobre un viaje o fin de semana. Incluye al menos:

- transporte (*by / take / get to*)  
- alojamiento o actividad  
- 1 frase del tiempo  
- 1 de ocio o compras  
- 1 de salud **o** ropa **o** personas  
- 1 *should* / *too* / *prefer* / *I've known*

**Modelo:** la lectura de la sección 6.

## 9. Plan de estudio (30–35 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Mapa del módulo (sección 1).  
2. **10 min** — Ejercicios 1–6.  
3. **8 min** — Escucha lectura y diálogo.  
4. **7–10 min** — Ejercicio 10.

### Checklist

- [ ] Viaje: *by / take / get to / arrive at*  
- [ ] Tiempo: *It's…* / *going to* / *will*  
- [ ] Ocio: *watch / listen to / like -ing / prefer*  
- [ ] Compras: *looking for / How much / refund*  
- [ ] Salud / ropa / personas: *should*, *too/enough*, *look like / What's … like?*
