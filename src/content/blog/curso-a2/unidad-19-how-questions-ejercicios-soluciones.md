## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** How often / long / much / many.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ____ often do you exercise? → *How / What / When*
2. ____ long do you study? → *How / How much / Who*
3. ____ much water do you drink? → *How / How many / Where*
4. ____ many books have you read? → *How / How much / Who*
5. I go to the gym three ____ a week. → *times / time / timed*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **How** · 2. **How** · 3. **How** · 4. **How** · 5. **times**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *How often* asks about ____. → a) frequency · b) only price · c) only height
7. *How long* asks about ____. → a) duration · b) only countable things · b) colour
8. *How much* + ____. → a) uncountable · b) only people · c) only *-ing*
9. *How many* + ____. → a) countable plural · b) only water · c) only *for*
10. Correct: ____. → a) How many books…? · b) How much books…? · c) How often books…?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) frequency** · 7. **a) duration** · 8. **a) uncountable** · 9. **a) countable plural** · 10. **a) How many books…?**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *How often you exercise?*
12. *How much books have you read?*
13. *How many water do you drink?*
14. *How long you study?*
15. *I go to the gym three time a week.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. How often **do** you exercise?  
12. How **many** books have you read?  
13. How **much** water do you drink?  
14. How long **do** you study?  
15. I go to the gym three **times** a week.  

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** palabras y patrones de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige

1. The unit focus is ____. → a) How often / long / much / many · b) only colours · c) only A1 *to be*
2. Practice includes ____. → a) grammar + skills · b) only titles · c) no English
3. Reading helps you ____. → a) see forms in context · b) avoid verbs · c) skip audio
4. Listening checks ____. → a) comprehension · b) only spelling of *-est* · c) only Module 6
5. Writing produces ____. → a) your own sentences · b) nothing · c) only copy images

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) How often / long / much / many** · 2. **a) grammar + skills** · 3. **a) see forms in context** · 4. **a) comprehension** · 5. **a) your own sentences**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa con ideas de la unidad

6. Key form of this unit: ____.
7. A common signal word from the texts: ____.
8. Affirmative example (your words): ____.
9. Negative or question example: ____.
10. One vocabulary item from the story: ____.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

6–10. Dependen de la unidad: revisa la guía teórica y el reading; respuestas abiertas aceptables si usan el foco correctamente.

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. Forms must agree with the subject: ____. → a) true · b) false · c) never
12. Time markers help choose the tense: ____. → a) true · b) false · c) irrelevant
13. Shadowing the audio is useful: ____. → a) true · b) false · c) only for teachers
14. After mistakes, reopen «Ver solución»: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) never
15. Then retry in the course unit: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only offline forever

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) true** · 12. **a) true** · 13. **a) true** · 14. **a) yes** · 15. **a) yes**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-19/reading-how.mp3" title="🔊 Reading Unidad 19"></audio>

> My name is Jake. I am a student and I have a busy routine. How often do I exercise? I go to the gym three times a week. How long do I study every day? I study for four hours every day. How much water do I drink? I drink about two litres a day. How many books have I read this year? I have read twelve books. I also watch films twice a week. I feel healthy and productive because I have good habits.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. How often does Jake exercise? → a) Three times a week · b) Never · c) Once a year
2. How long does he study? → a) Four hours every day · b) Thirty minutes · c) Two cups
3. How much water? → a) About two litres · b) Twelve books · c) Three languages
4. How many books this year? → a) Twelve · b) Two cups · c) Four hours
5. Films? → a) Twice a week · b) Never · c) Every hour

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Three times a week** · 2. **a) Four hours every day** · 3. **a) About two litres** · 4. **a) Twelve** · 5. **a) Twice a week**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The text matches unit 19: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Unit 1
7. You can find the target forms: ____. → a) yes · b) never · c) only images
8. Sequence/time words appear as needed: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only future
9. Main idea is clear from the first lines: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) impossible
10. Re-read after checking answers: ____. → a) good idea · b) useless · c) forbidden

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) yes** · 7. **a) yes** · 8. **a) yes** · 9. **a) yes** · 10. **a) good idea**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Mark 3 target forms in the text: ____. → a) do it · b) skip · c) delete the text
12. Change one sentence to negative/question: ____. → a) useful · b) wrong always · c) only B2
13. Note any Past Simple / Perfect / Continuous mix: ____. → a) notice it · b) ignore · c) ban English
14. Copy one model sentence aloud: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) never
15. Write a parallel sentence about you: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only tomorrow forever

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11–15. **a)** (práctica abierta; lo importante es usar el foco de la unidad)

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-19/listening-how.mp3" title="🔊 Listening Unidad 19"></audio>

> Hi! I am Lisa. People often ask me about my habits. How often do I exercise? I go running four times a week. How long do I run each time? I run for thirty minutes. How much coffee do I drink a day? I drink two cups every morning. How many languages do I speak? I speak three: English, Spanish and French. I have been learning Spanish for two years. I feel energetic because I exercise regularly and drink enough water.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. How often does Lisa run? → a) Four times a week · b) Never · c) Once
2. How long each time? → a) Thirty minutes · b) Four hours · c) Two years
3. How much coffee? → a) Two cups every morning · b) Twelve books · c) Two litres
4. How many languages? → a) Three · b) Twelve · c) One
5. Spanish for? → a) Two years · b) Thirty minutes · c) Four times

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Four times a week** · 2. **a) Thirty minutes** · 3. **a) Two cups every morning** · 4. **a) Three** · 5. **a) Two years**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Audio supports the same grammar focus: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only music
7. Speakers use natural A2 vocabulary: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Latin
8. You can answer without the script first: ____. → a) try it · b) never · c) impossible always
9. Then check with the script: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) delete audio
10. Repeat difficult sentences: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only teachers

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) yes** · 7. **a) yes** · 8. **a) try it** · 9. **a) yes** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Transcribe one key sentence: ____. → a) useful · b) useless · c) banned
12. Underline the target grammar: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) never
13. Record yourself saying it: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only offline forever
14. Compare with the model: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) skip English
15. Add the sentence to your notebook: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11–15. **a) yes / useful** (práctica abierta)

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa (según el foco)

1. Write one affirmative sentence with the unit focus.
2. Write one negative sentence.
3. Write one question.
4. Add a time marker that fits.
5. Add one vocabulary word from the reading.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

Usa los modelos del reading/listening de esta unidad; cualquier frase correcta con el foco cuenta.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena / reescribe

6. Reorder a model sentence from the reading.
7. Change person (*I* → *she/they*).
8. Change time (yesterday / for / since / how often…).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Compara con el texto modelo; la concordancia sujeto–verbo debe ser correcta.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe

9–14. Six short sentences using the unit focus about your life.
15. Paragraph (6–8 sentences) mixing this unit with one idea from a previous unit.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplo guía)</summary>

15. Escribe un mini-relato como el reading: incluye el foco de la Unidad 19 y una forma de una unidad anterior del Módulo 2.

</details>
