## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** repaso integrado Módulo 4: phrasals, -ing/to, questions, quantifiers, articles, would like, should, adverbials.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ swimming. *(gusto)* → *like / would like / likes*
2. ____ a tea, please. *(pedido)* → *I like / I'd like / Would you like*
3. You ____ rest. *(consejo)* → *should / should to / ought*
4. I study ____ home ____ the evening. → *at / in · in / at*
5. Who ____ you yesterday? *(sujeto)* → *called / did call / did you call*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **like** · 2. **I'd like** · 3. **should** · 4. **at / in** · 5. **called**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *I'd like* vs *I like*: pedido ahora = ____. → a) I'd like · b) I like · c) Who called
7. *Should* + verbo: ____. → a) base sin to · b) to + V · c) -ing only
8. *Place before time*: ____. → a) at home in the evening · b) in the evening at home · c) once a week only
9. *Pick it up* (separable): ____. → a) correcto · b) pick up it · c) Who did you pick
10. Unit 40 next is ____. → a) Travel & Transport U41 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U41

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) I'd like** · 7. **a) base sin to** · 8. **a) at home in the evening** · 9. **a) correcto** · 10. **a) Travel & Transport U41**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I like a coffee, please.*
12. *You should to go.*
13. *I study in the evening at home.*
14. *Pick up it.*
15. *Do you like some cake?* *(oferta)*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I'd like a coffee, please.
12. You should go.
13. I study at home in the evening.
14. Pick it up.
15. Would you like some cake?

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** get up, turn off, pick up; fruit, bread, station, café, cake, park, running.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *get up* ≈ ____ → *levantarse · apagar · recoger*
2. *turn off* ≈ ____ → *apagar · levantarse · pedir*
3. *pick up* ≈ ____ → *recoger · correr · descansar*
4. *some fruit* ≈ ____ → *cantidad afirmativa · any negativa · subject Q*
5. *the station* ≈ ____ → *lugar definido · general · a station first only*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **levantarse** · 2. **apagar** · 3. **recoger** · 4. **cantidad afirmativa** · 5. **lugar definido**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. Ana ____ up early on Saturdays. → *gets / get / getting*
7. She ____ running in the park. → *likes / would like / like*
8. She doesn't need ____ bread. → *some / any / many*
9. You ____ run today. *(consejo negativo)* → *shouldn't / shouldn't to / don't should*
10. She goes to the café ____ a week. → *once / one / every*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **gets** · 7. **likes** · 8. **any** · 9. **shouldn't** · 10. **once**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *At the moment* in reading = ____. → a) now · b) every Saturday · c) once a week
12. *I'd like to go anyway* = ____. → a) deseo · b) gusto -ing · c) Who called
13. *A café near the station* = ____. → a) a + the · b) the only · c) Ø music
14. *Module 4* includes ____. → a) U31–U39 · b) U41–U49 · c) U1–U10 only
15. Unit 40 closes Module 4 before ____. → a) Travel U41 · b) Module 6 only · c) U31 skip

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) now** · 12. **a) deseo** · 13. **a) a + the** · 14. **a) U31–U39** · 15. **a) Travel U41**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: A busy Saturday

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-40/reading-review.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: A busy Saturday"></audio>

> Ana gets up early on Saturdays. She likes running in the park in the morning. At the moment, she is tired, so her friend says: "You should rest. You shouldn't run today." Ana answers: "I'd like to go anyway — just once." Later she buys some fruit. She doesn't need any bread. She sees a café near the station and thinks: "I'd like a tea." She goes there once a week.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Ana gets up early ____. → a) On Saturdays · b) Every day · c) At night
2. She likes ____. → a) Running in the park · b) Swimming · c) Studying all night
3. Friend says she should ____. → a) Rest · b) Run more · c) Buy bread
4. Ana wants to ____. → a) Go anyway — just once · b) Stay home · c) Forget ID
5. Café visit frequency? → a) Once a week · b) Every day · c) Never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) On Saturdays** · 2. **a) Running in the park** · 3. **a) Rest** · 4. **a) Go anyway — just once** · 5. **a) Once a week**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. *Gets up* = ____. → a) phrasal verb · b) should · c) subject Q
7. *Likes running* = ____. → a) like + -ing · b) I'd like · c) any milk
8. *Some fruit / any bread* = ____. → a) quantifiers · b) only articles · c) Who called
9. *In the park in the morning* = ____. → a) place + time · b) time + place · c) ought to
10. Matches Module 4 review: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) phrasal verb** · 7. **a) like + -ing** · 8. **a) quantifiers** · 9. **a) place + time** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *You shouldn't run today* = ____. → a) consejo negativo · b) pedido · c) gusto
12. *A café near the station* = ____. → a) a first / the known · b) Ø only · c) much many
13. *I'd like a tea* = ____. → a) pedido educado · b) I like tea · c) should tea
14. Reading integrates ____. → a) Módulo 4 topics · b) only travel · c) only weather
15. Saturday text writing uses ____. → a) mixed M4 structures · b) only did · c) only phrasals only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) consejo negativo** · 12. **a) a first / the known** · 13. **a) pedido educado** · 14. **a) Módulo 4 topics** · 15. **a) mixed M4 structures**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: At the café

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-40/listening-review.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: At the café"></audio>

> Waiter: Would you like a table?  
> Ana: Yes, please. I'd like a tea and some cake.  
> Waiter: We don't have any chocolate cake today.  
> Ana: OK. I'd like apple cake.  
> Friend: You should try the sandwich too.  
> Ana: Later. I like eating light in the morning.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Waiter offers ____. → a) A table · b) A train · c) An umbrella
2. Ana wants ____. → a) Tea and some cake · b) Only water · c) Chocolate cake
3. Chocolate cake? → a) Don't have any today · b) Have some · c) Many cakes
4. Ana orders ____. → a) Apple cake · b) Sandwich now · c) Bread
5. Eating light ____. → a) In the morning · b) At night · c) Once a week

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) A table** · 2. **a) Tea and some cake** · 3. **a) Don't have any today** · 4. **a) Apple cake** · 5. **a) In the morning**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. *Would you like a table?* = ____. → a) oferta · b) Do you like · c) Who called
7. *Some cake / any chocolate cake* = ____. → a) quantifiers · b) only should · c) only phrasal
8. *You should try the sandwich* = ____. → a) consejo · b) pedido · c) like -ing
9. *I like eating light* = ____. → a) gusto + -ing · b) I'd like · c) subject Q
10. Unit 40 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Module 6

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) oferta** · 7. **a) quantifiers** · 8. **a) consejo** · 9. **a) gusto + -ing** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Offer: ____ you like a table? → a) Would · b) Do · c) Should
12. Order: I'd ____ apple cake. → a) like · b) likes · c) liking
13. Negative stock: don't have ____ cake → a) any · b) some · c) many
14. Shadow and note ____. → a) M4 mixed · b) only a/an · c) only Who
15. U40 closes M4 before ____. → a) Travel U41 · b) Module 6 only · c) U31 skip

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) Would** · 12. **a) like** · 13. **a) any** · 14. **a) M4 mixed** · 15. **a) Travel U41**

</details>
