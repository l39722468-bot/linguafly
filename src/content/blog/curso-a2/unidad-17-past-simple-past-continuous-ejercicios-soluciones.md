## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** when / while + Past Simple & Past Continuous.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I was walking ____ I met a friend. → *when / while / for*
2. We were talking ____ we walked. → *while / when / since*
3. I ____ watching TV when the doorbell rang. → *was / were / have*
4. They ____ running when it started to rain. → *were / was / are*
5. Something ____ in the oven. → *burned / burning / burn*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **when** · 2. **while** · 3. **was** · 4. **were** · 5. **burned**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *when* often introduces ____. → a) a shorter Past Simple event · b) only Present Perfect · c) *will*
7. *while* often goes with ____. → a) Past Continuous · b) only *ago* · c) *the most*
8. Background action = ____. → a) Past Continuous · b) Future · c) imperative
9. Interrupting action = ____. → a) Past Simple · b) only *-ing* · c) *have just*
10. Correct: ____. → a) I was cooking when he called · b) I cooked when I was calling always wrong · c) I am cooking when he called

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) a shorter Past Simple event** · 7. **a) Past Continuous** · 8. **a) Past Continuous** · 9. **a) Past Simple** · 10. **a) I was cooking when he called**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I walked when I was meeting him.* (fondo + interrupción típica)
12. *While I watched TV, the phone was ringing.* (mejor fondo en continuous)
13. *I was walk in the park.*
14. *They was running when it started.*
15. *When I was seeing him, I walked.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I **was walking** when I **met** him.  
12. While I **was watching** TV, the phone **rang**.  
13. I was **walking** in the park.  
14. They **were** running when it started.  
15. When I **saw** him, I **was walking** / I met him while walking.  

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-17/reading-when.mp3" title="🔊 Reading Unidad 17"></audio>

> At first last Saturday I was walking in the park when I met an old friend. We were talking while we walked around the lake. Then suddenly it started to rain. We were running to find shelter when a woman offered us her umbrella. We waited under a tree until the rain stopped. Finally the sun came out and we continued our walk.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Where was the speaker walking? → a) In the park · b) At school · c) In a hospital
2. Who did they meet? → a) An old friend · b) Mr Jones · c) Lisa
3. What started suddenly? → a) Rain · b) A film · c) An exam
4. Who offered an umbrella? → a) A woman · b) Nobody · c) Tom
5. What came out finally? → a) The sun · b) Smoke · c) A dog

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) In the park** · 2. **a) An old friend** · 3. **a) Rain** · 4. **a) A woman** · 5. **a) The sun**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The text matches unit 17: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Unit 1
7. You can find the target forms: ____. → a) yes · b) never · c) only images
8. Sequence/time words appear as needed: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only future
9. Main idea is clear from the first lines: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) impossible
10. Re-read after checking answers: ____. → a) good idea · b) useless · c) forbidden

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) yes** · 7. **a) yes** · 8. **a) yes** · 9. **a) yes** · 10. **a) good idea**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Mark 3 target forms in the text: ____. → a) do it · b) skip · c) delete the text
12. Change one sentence to negative/question: ____. → a) useful · b) wrong always · c) only B2
13. Note any Past Simple / Perfect / Continuous mix: ____. → a) notice it · b) ignore · c) ban English
14. Copy one model sentence aloud: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) never
15. Write a parallel sentence about you: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only tomorrow forever

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11–15. **a)** (práctica abierta; lo importante es usar el foco de la unidad)

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-17/listening-when.mp3" title="🔊 Listening Unidad 17"></audio>

> At first yesterday evening I was watching TV when the doorbell rang. It was my neighbour Mr Jones. He was walking his dog when he saw smoke coming from my kitchen. I was cooking dinner while I was watching TV. Suddenly something burned in the oven. Mr Jones helped me open the windows. Then we checked the kitchen together. Finally everything was okay and I thanked him for his help.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What was the speaker doing? → a) Watching TV · b) Running · c) Swimming
2. Who rang? → a) Mr Jones · b) Emma · c) David
3. What did he see? → a) Smoke from the kitchen · b) A cat under sofa · c) A film
4. What was burning? → a) Something in the oven · b) The park · c) The lake
5. How did it end? → a) Everything was okay · b) They left town · c) It snowed

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Watching TV** · 2. **a) Mr Jones** · 3. **a) Smoke from the kitchen** · 4. **a) Something in the oven** · 5. **a) Everything was okay**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Audio supports the same grammar focus: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only music
7. Speakers use natural A2 vocabulary: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Latin
8. You can answer without the script first: ____. → a) try it · b) never · c) impossible always
9. Then check with the script: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) delete audio
10. Repeat difficult sentences: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only teachers

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) yes** · 7. **a) yes** · 8. **a) try it** · 9. **a) yes** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Transcribe one key sentence: ____. → a) useful · b) useless · c) banned
12. Underline the target grammar: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) never
13. Record yourself saying it: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only offline forever
14. Compare with the model: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) skip English
15. Add the sentence to your notebook: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) never
