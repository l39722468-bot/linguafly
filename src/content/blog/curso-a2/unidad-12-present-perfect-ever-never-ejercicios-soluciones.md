## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** Have you ever…? / never; Yes, I have / No, I haven't.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ____ you ever been to Japan? → *Have / Has / Did*
2. I have ____ eaten sushi. → *never / ever / yet*
3. Yes, I ____. → *have / has / am*
4. No, I ____. → *haven't / hasn't / didn't*
5. Have you ever ____ a horse? → *ridden / rode / ride*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Have** · 2. **never** · 3. **have** · 4. **haven't** · 5. **ridden**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *Ever* usually appears in ____. → a) questions · b) only negatives · c) only Past Simple
7. *Never* means ____. → a) not at any time · b) already · c) yesterday
8. Short answer affirmative = ____. → a) Yes, I have · b) Yes, I did always · c) Yes, I am
9. Short answer negative = ____. → a) No, I haven't · b) No, I don't never · c) No, I wasn't
10. *Have you ever flown…?* uses ____. → a) past participle · b) Past Simple only · c) *-ing*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) questions** · 7. **a) not at any time** · 8. **a) Yes, I have** · 9. **a) No, I haven't** · 10. **a) past participle**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *Did you ever been to Japan?*
12. *I have ever eaten sushi.* (negativa de experiencia)
13. *Yes, I has.*
14. *No, I haven't never.*
15. *Have you ever ate sushi?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **Have** you ever **been** to Japan?
12. I have **never** eaten sushi.
13. Yes, I **have**.
14. No, I **haven't**. / I have **never**…
15. Have you ever **eaten** sushi?

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** formas y palabras de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. past participle of *see* → ____ → *seen / saw / see*
2. past participle of *eat* → ____ → *eaten / ate / eated*
3. past participle of *go* (with *have*) often → ____ → *been/gone / went / going*
4. *experience* ≈ ____ → *something you have done · a clock time · a month name*
5. *twice* = ____ → *two times · never · yesterday*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **seen** · 2. **eaten** · 3. **been/gone** · 4. **something you have done** · 5. **two times**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. I have ____ to Spain. → *been / being / be*
7. She has ____ that film. → *seen / saw / see*
8. Have you ____ sushi? → *eaten / ate / eat*
9. He has ____ to London today. → *gone / went / go*
10. We have ____ lunch. → *eaten / ate / eated*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **been** · 7. **seen** · 8. **eaten** · 9. **gone** · 10. **eaten**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. Present Perfect needs ____. → a) have/has + PP · b) only *-ed* without have · c) *did* + PP
12. Time words like *yesterday/ago* usually take ____. → a) Past Simple · b) Present Perfect always · c) *will*
13. *Have you ever…?* asks about ____. → a) life experience · b) only tomorrow · c) exact clock yesterday
14. *Already/yet/just* typically go with ____. → a) Present Perfect · b) only Future · c) only *to be*
15. A2 travel verbs often: ____. → a) been, seen, eaten, gone · b) only *am* · c) only *-est*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) have/has + PP** · 12. **a) Past Simple** · 13. **a) life experience** · 14. **a) Present Perfect** · 15. **a) been, seen, eaten, gone**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Classmates survey

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-12/reading-ever.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Classmates survey"></audio>

> Have you ever wondered what your friends have or haven't done? I asked my classmates some questions. Have you ever flown in a plane? Most of them said Yes, I have. Have you ever eaten sushi? Some said Yes and some said No, I haven't. Have you ever been to a concert? Many said Yes. One friend said: I have never been to a concert. I would like to go one day. It was interesting to learn about their experiences.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What did the person ask classmates? → a) Experience questions · b) Only ages · c) Only prices
2. Have most flown in a plane? → a) Yes, I have · b) Nobody · c) The text doesn't say
3. Answers about sushi? → a) Some yes, some no · b) All no · c) All yes only
4. Concerts? → a) Many said yes · b) Impossible · c) Only Tom
5. One friend has never been to ____. → a) A concert · b) School · c) Home

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Experience questions** · 2. **a) Yes, I have** · 3. **a) Some yes, some no** · 4. **a) Many said yes** · 5. **a) A concert**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The text practices ____. → a) Have you ever…? / never; Yes, I have / No, I haven't · b) only *at/on/in* · c) only comparatives
7. Past participles appear ____. → a) yes · b) never · c) only adjectives
8. Present Perfect forms use ____. → a) have/has · b) only *did* · c) only *will*
9. The main topic fits unit 12: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Unit 1
10. Reading + audio helps you ____. → a) notice form in context · b) skip grammar · c) avoid English

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Have you ever…? / never; Yes, I have / No, I haven't** · 7. **a) yes** · 8. **a) have/has** · 9. **a) yes** · 10. **a) notice form in context**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Have/has* agrees with ____. → a) the subject · b) the object only · c) never
12. After *have/has* we use ____. → a) past participle · b) base always · c) *-ing* only
13. Negatives often use ____. → a) haven't/hasn't · b) don't been · c) didn't have been
14. Questions invert ____. → a) Have/Has + subject · b) Subject + Have always · c) Did + have + been wrong mix
15. Mixing PP with a Past Simple time phrase is often ____. → a) a common error · b) required · c) the only correct style

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) the subject** · 12. **a) past participle** · 13. **a) haven't/hasn't** · 14. **a) Have/Has + subject** · 15. **a) a common error**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Experience survey

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-12/listening-ever.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Experience survey"></audio>

> Hi! I am doing a survey about experiences. Can I ask you some questions? Great! Have you ever tried sushi? Yes, I have. I tried it in Tokyo last year. It was amazing. Have you ever ridden a horse? No, I haven't. I would like to try one day. Have you ever been to a football match? Yes, I have. Many times! I love football. Thank you for your answers!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What is the person doing? → a) A survey · b) Cooking · c) Sleeping
2. Tried sushi? → a) Yes · b) No · c) Maybe
3. Where did they try sushi? → a) Tokyo last year · b) Never · c) At school only
4. Ridden a horse? → a) No, I haven't · b) Yes always · c) Every day
5. Football match? → a) Yes, many times · b) Never · c) No interest

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) A survey** · 2. **a) Yes** · 3. **a) Tokyo last year** · 4. **a) No, I haven't** · 5. **a) Yes, many times**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Listening checks ____. → a) comprehension of PP forms · b) only spelling of *-est* · c) only *into*
7. Key verbs you hear include past participles: ____. → a) yes · b) never · c) only future
8. The speaker talks about ____. → a) experiences / recent actions · b) only weather maps · c) only Module 6 food
9. Short answers / already-yet-just / contrast appear as relevant: ____. → a) according to unit · b) never · c) only A1
10. The listening practices unit 12: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Unit 60

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) comprehension of PP forms** · 7. **a) yes** · 8. **a) experiences / recent actions** · 9. **a) according to unit** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. You can notice *have/has* + ____. → a) PP · b) only infinitive · c) *-est*
12. Time markers help choose PP vs Past Simple: ____. → a) true · b) false · c) irrelevant
13. *Been/gone/seen/eaten* are ____. → a) past participles · b) present continuous · c) adjectives only
14. Fluency tip: repeat the audio and ____. → a) shadow the forms · b) ignore verbs · c) translate only titles
15. After listening, write 3 sentences with the unit focus: ____. → a) good practice · b) useless · c) only for teachers

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) PP** · 12. **a) true** · 13. **a) past participles** · 14. **a) shadow the forms** · 15. **a) good practice**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I have ____ to Italy. → *been / being / be*
2. She has ____ that film. → *seen / saw / see*
3. Have you ever ____ sushi? → *eaten / ate / eat*
4. I haven't finished ____. → *yet / already / ago*
5. I have ____ arrived. → *just / yesterday / last*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **been** · 2. **seen** · 3. **eaten** · 4. **yet** · 5. **just**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. I / have / been / to / Spain.
7. Have / you / ever / tried / sushi?
8. She / has / just / finished / her exam.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I have been to Spain.
7. Have you ever tried sushi?
8. She has just finished her exam.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Una experiencia con *have been to*.
10. Una pregunta con *Have you ever…?*
11. Una frase con *never*.
12. Una frase con *already* o *yet*.
13. Una frase con *just*.
14. Una frase Past Simple con *last year / ago*.
15. Párrafo (6–8 frases) mezclando el foco de la Unidad 12.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I have been to Spain.
10. Have you ever eaten sushi?
11. I have never ridden a horse.
12. I have already finished / I haven't called her yet.
13. I have just arrived home.
14. Last year I went to Japan. / Two months ago I travelled to Italy.
15. I have been to many countries. I have never been to Australia. Last year I went to Japan. I have already booked my next trip. I am excited!

</details>
