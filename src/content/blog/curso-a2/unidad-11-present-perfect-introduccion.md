faqs:
  - question: ¿Cómo se forma el Present Perfect en A2?
    answer: >-
      have/has + past participle. I/you/we/they → have. He/she/it → has. Ejemplo:
      I have been to Paris. She has seen that film.
  - question: ¿Qué es el past participle?
    answer: >-
      Es la forma del verbo que va después de have/has. En irregulares: be→been,
      see→seen, eat→eaten, go→gone. En regulares suele coincidir con -ed
      (visited, played).
  - question: ¿Cuál es la diferencia entre been to y gone to?
    answer: >-
      have been to = fuiste y ya volviste (experiencia). has gone to = ha ido y
      todavía no está de vuelta (sigue allí o de camino).
  - question: ¿Present Perfect o Past Simple?
    answer: >-
      En esta unidad prioriza experiencias sin fecha concreta (I have been to
      Paris). Con tiempo pasado concreto (Last year…) suele ir Past Simple (I
      went to Japan). La comparación completa llega en la Unidad 15.

Tras el [repaso del Módulo 1](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-10-repaso-modulo-1), el **Módulo 2** abre con experiencias. En la **Unidad 11 del curso A2** (*Present Perfect: Introduction*) aprendes a decir lo que **has vivido**: ***I have been to Paris***.

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 11 — Present Perfect: Introducción](/curso-a2/unit-11)  
> **Antes:** [U10 — Repaso Módulo 1](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-10-repaso-modulo-1)

---

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 11 (A2)

- Formar **have/has + past participle**.
- Usar participios clave: **been, seen, eaten, gone**.
- Hablar de **experiencias** (*I have been to…*, *She has seen…*).
- Distinguir **been to** (experiencia, ya de vuelta) y **gone to** (aún no ha vuelto).
- Evitar *I has been* / *He have gone*.

![Present Perfect have has + past participle](/blog/curso-a2/unit-11/a2-unit11-present-perfect.png)

## 1. La fórmula

**Present Perfect (afirmativa A2) = have/has + past participle**

| Sujeto | Auxiliar | + past participle | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I / you / we / they | **have** | been / seen / eaten… | I **have been** to Paris. |
| he / she / it | **has** | been / seen / eaten… | She **has seen** that film. |

> I have been to Paris.  
> She has seen that film.  
> They have eaten lunch already.  
> He has gone to the supermarket.  
> We have been to London twice.

## 2. ¿Para qué sirve? (idea A2)

Usa Present Perfect para **experiencias de vida** con conexión al presente: “esto forma parte de mi experiencia”.

| Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| Lugares que has visitado | I **have been** to Italy. |
| Cosas que has visto | I **have seen** the Eiffel Tower. |
| Comidas que has probado | I **have eaten** sushi. |
| Alguien que ha salido (y aún no vuelve) | He **has gone** to the supermarket. |

![Experiencias de viaje Present Perfect](/blog/curso-a2/unit-11/a2-unit11-travel-experiences.png)

Todavía no profundizamos en *ever/never/already/yet/just* (U12–U14). Aquí: **forma afirmativa + participios clave**.

## 3. Past participle: *been, seen, eaten, gone*

| Base | Past Simple | Past participle | Con Present Perfect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| be | was/were | **been** | I have **been** to Paris. |
| see | saw | **seen** | She has **seen** that film. |
| eat | ate | **eaten** | I have **eaten** breakfast. |
| go | went | **gone** | He has **gone** home. |

> I have been to Italy.  
> I have eaten breakfast.  
> He has gone home.

No confundas:

| | Past Simple | Present Perfect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| go | I **went** to Japan last year. | He **has gone** to Australia. |
| see | She **saw** a film last night. | She **has seen** that film. |

## 4. *Been to* vs *gone to*

![Been to vs gone to](/blog/curso-a2/unit-11/a2-unit11-been-vs-gone.png)

| Forma | Significado | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **have/has been to** | Fuiste y **ya volviste** (experiencia) | I **have been** to Spain twice. |
| **have/has gone to** | Ha ido y **aún no está de vuelta** | My brother **has gone** to Australia this month. |

> I have been to many countries. *(experiencias; estoy aquí contándolas)*  
> My brother has gone to Australia this month. He will be back in two weeks. *(sigue allí)*

Truco: **been** = experiencia completa · **gone** = se ha marchado (resultado ahora).

---

## 5. *Have* o *has*: tabla rápida

| Correcto | Incorrecto |
| :--- | :--- |
| I **have** been… | *I has been…* |
| She **has** seen… | *She have seen…* |
| They **have** eaten… | *They has eaten…* |
| He **has** gone… | *He have gone…* |
| We **have** been… | *We has been…* |

## 6. Texto modelo: Laura

> My name is Laura. I have been to many countries. I have seen the Eiffel Tower in Paris and the Colosseum in Rome. I have eaten Italian food and French food. Last year I went to Japan. I have never eaten such amazing sushi! My brother has gone to Australia this month. He will be back in two weeks. I love travelling and having new experiences.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-11/reading-laura.mp3" title="🔊 My name is Laura. I have been to many countries…"></audio>

Marca:

- Present Perfect: *have been · have seen · have eaten · has gone*  
- Past Simple con fecha: *Last year I **went** to Japan*  
- *been* (Laura / experiencias) vs *gone* (hermano, aún fuera)

## 9. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I has been to Paris. | I **have** been to Paris. | *I* → *have* |
| She have seen that film. | She **has** seen that film. | *she* → *has* |
| I have went to Italy. | I have **been** to Italy. / I **went** last year. | participio *been* / o Past Simple |
| I have saw that film. | I have **seen** that film. | *see* → *seen* |
| I have ate sushi. | I have **eaten** sushi. | *eat* → *eaten* |
| He have gone home. | He **has** gone home. | *he* → *has* |
| I have gone to Paris. *(si ya volviste y cuentas experiencia)* | I have **been** to Paris. | experiencia → *been* |

## 10. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — *have* o *has*

1. I ____ been to Paris.  
2. She ____ seen that film.  
3. They ____ eaten lunch already.  
4. He ____ gone to the supermarket.  
5. We ____ been to London twice.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **have** · 2. **has** · 3. **have** · 4. **has** · 5. **have**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Past participle

1. be → ____  
2. see → ____  
3. eat → ____  
4. go → ____  

Completa:

5. I have ____ to Italy. (be)  
6. I have ____ breakfast. (eat)  
7. He has ____ home. (go)  
8. She has ____ that film. (see)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **been** · 2. **seen** · 3. **eaten** · 4. **gone**  
5. **been** · 6. **eaten** · 7. **gone** · 8. **seen**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Empareja

1. I / been / Paris  
2. She / seen / film  
3. They / eaten / lunch  
4. He / gone / supermarket  

a) has gone to the supermarket · b) have been to Paris · c) have eaten lunch · d) has seen that film

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **d** · 3 → **c** · 4 → **a**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — *been* o *gone*

1. I have ____ to many countries. *(experiencia; estoy aquí)*  
2. My brother has ____ to Australia this month. He will be back in two weeks.  
3. Tom has ____ to Spain twice.  
4. My sister has ____ to London today. She will come back next week.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **been** · 2. **gone** · 3. **been** · 4. **gone**
</details>

4. *I have saw that film.*  
5. *I have ate sushi.*  
6. *He have gone home.*

### Ejercicio 8 — Elige la correcta

1. ____  
   a) I have been to Paris. · b) I has been to Paris. · c) I had been to Paris. *(forma A2 prioritaria)*  
2. Past participle of *go*: ____  
   a) went · b) gone · c) going  
3. ____  
   a) She have seen the film. · b) She has seen the film. · c) She seen the film.  
4. Experiencia (ya de vuelta): ____  
   a) I have gone to Spain twice. · b) I have been to Spain twice. · c) I has been to Spain twice.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a** · 2. **b** · 3. **b** · 4. **b**
</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Escribe tus experiencias

Escribe 8–12 frases. Incluye:

- 3 × *I have been to…*  
- 2 × *I have seen…* / *I have eaten…*  
- 1 × *… has gone to…* (alguien que aún no ha vuelto)  
- opcional: 1 Past Simple con fecha (*Last year I went…*)

**Modelo:** Laura o Tom.

## 11. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Repasa *have/has* + tabla *been/seen/eaten/gone*.  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–7.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha a Laura y Tom; marca *been* vs *gone*.  
4. **5–8 min** — Escribe y graba tus experiencias.

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *have* con I/you/we/they y *has* con he/she/it  
- [ ] Recuerdo *been, seen, eaten, gone*  
- [ ] Hablo de experiencias con *have been to / have seen / have eaten*  
- [ ] Distingo *been to* y *gone to*  
- [ ] Evito *I has* / *She have* / *have went*

## 12. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Pregunta: ¿cuento una **experiencia** (been) o digo que alguien **sigue fuera** (gone)?  
2. Lista de 8: *Paris, Rome, sushi, that film, supermarket, home, Spain, London* → haz una frase Present Perfect.  
3. Grábate: *I have been to… I have seen… I have eaten…*  
4. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 11](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-11-present-perfect-introduccion-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 11 del curso A2](/curso-a2/unit-11).

La **Unidad 12** añade **ever** y **never**: *Have you ever…? / I have never…*

| :--- | :--- |

| he/she/it | **has** |
| Participios clave | **been · seen · eaten · gone** |
| Experiencia (vuelto) | **been to** |
