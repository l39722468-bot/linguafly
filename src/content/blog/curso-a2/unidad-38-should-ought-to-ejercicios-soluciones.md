## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** should / shouldn't; ought to; Should I…?

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. You ____ drink more water. *(consejo)* → *should / should to / ought*
2. You ____ smoke. *(consejo negativo)* → *shouldn't / shouldn't to / don't should*
3. You ____ to see a doctor. *(formal)* → *ought / ought to / should to*
4. ____ I call her? *(pregunta)* → *Should / Do should / Am*
5. He ____ study all night. → *shouldn't / shouldn't to / oughtn't to*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **should** · 2. **shouldn't** · 3. **ought to** · 4. **Should** · 5. **shouldn't**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *Should* + verbo ____. → a) base (sin to) · b) to + V · c) -ing
7. *Ought to* lleva ____. → a) to · b) no to · c) did
8. *Should* = consejo, no ____. → a) orden fuerte (must) · b) gusto · c) subject Q
9. *Should I…?* pide ____. → a) consejo · b) pedido café · c) Who called
10. Unit 38 follows ____. → a) would like U37 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U39

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) base (sin to)** · 7. **a) to** · 8. **a) orden fuerte (must)** · 9. **a) consejo** · 10. **a) would like U37**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *You should to go.*
12. *You ought rest.*
13. *Do I should call?*
14. *You shouldn't to worry.*
15. *She oughts to leave early.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. You should go.
12. You ought to rest.
13. Should I call?
14. You shouldn't worry.
15. She ought to leave early.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** rest, sleep, medicine, doctor, exam, ID, water, coffee, traffic, practise.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *rest* ≈ ____ → *descansar · correr · pedir*
2. *ID* ≈ ____ → *documento identidad · té · parque*
3. *practise* ≈ ____ → *practicar · dormir · fumar*
4. *traffic* ≈ ____ → *tráfico · postre · menú*
5. *see a doctor* ≈ ____ → *ir al médico · pedir café · Who called*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **descansar** · 2. **documento identidad** · 3. **practicar** · 4. **tráfico** · 5. **ir al médico**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. You ____ sleep seven or eight hours. → *should / should to / ought*
7. You ____ drink so much coffee. → *shouldn't / shouldn't to / don't should*
8. Students ____ arrive on time. → *ought to / ought / should to*
9. ____ I take medicine? → *Should / Do should / Am*
10. You ____ forget your ID. → *shouldn't / shouldn't to / don't ought*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **should** · 7. **shouldn't** · 8. **ought to** · 9. **Should** · 10. **shouldn't**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

13. *Not yet* (medicine) means ____. → a) no medicine now · b) should smoke · c) like coffee
14. *Professional advice* → You ____ to get… → a) ought · b) likes · c) any

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Advice for the exam

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-38/reading-should.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Advice for the exam"></audio>

> Maria has an English exam next week. Her teacher says she should sleep well the night before. She shouldn't study all night. She ought to practise listening with short videos. Her friend Tom says: "You should arrive early. You shouldn't forget your ID." Maria feels nervous, but she knows what to do.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Maria has ____. → a) An English exam next week · b) A doctor appointment · c) A party
2. Teacher says she should ____. → a) Sleep well · b) Study all night · c) Forget ID
3. She shouldn't ____. → a) Study all night · b) Arrive early · c) Practise listening
4. She ought to practise ____. → a) Listening with videos · b) Running · c) Ordering coffee
5. Tom says don't forget ____. → a) ID · b) Cake · c) Umbrella

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) An English exam next week** · 2. **a) Sleep well** · 3. **a) Study all night** · 4. **a) Listening with videos** · 5. **a) ID**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. *She should sleep well* = ____. → a) consejo · b) pedido · c) gusto
7. *She ought to practise* = ____. → a) consejo formal · b) like -ing · c) any milk
8. *You shouldn't forget your ID* = ____. → a) consejo negativo · b) offer · c) subject Q
9. Maria feels ____. → a) nervous · b) happy only · c) at the café
10. Matches unit 38: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) consejo** · 7. **a) consejo formal** · 8. **a) consejo negativo** · 9. **a) nervous** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Should* + sleep = ____. → a) should sleep (no to) · b) should to sleep · c) ought sleep
12. *Ought to practise* = ____. → a) ought to + V · b) ought + V sin to · c) shouldn't to
13. *Shouldn't study all night* = ____. → a) negativa consejo · b) gusto · c) a/an
14. Reading marks ____. → a) should/ought to · b) only phrasals · c) only much
15. Exam advice writing uses ____. → a) should/shouldn't/ought to · b) only did · c) only like

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) should sleep (no to)** · 12. **a) ought to + V** · 13. **a) negativa consejo** · 14. **a) should/ought to** · 15. **a) should/shouldn't/ought to**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: At the doctor

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-38/listening-should.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: At the doctor"></audio>

> Doctor: How do you feel?  
> Patient: Tired. I work a lot.  
> Doctor: You should sleep seven or eight hours. You shouldn't drink so much coffee.  
> Patient: Should I take medicine?  
> Doctor: Not yet. You ought to rest this weekend and drink water.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Patient feels ____. → a) Tired · b) Happy · c) Excited
2. Why tired? → a) Works a lot · b) Studies all night · c) Runs in park
3. Doctor says sleep ____. → a) Seven or eight hours · b) Two hours · c) All night
4. Shouldn't drink ____. → a) So much coffee · b) Water · c) Juice
5. Medicine? → a) Not yet · b) Yes now · c) Every day

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Tired** · 2. **a) Works a lot** · 3. **a) Seven or eight hours** · 4. **a) So much coffee** · 5. **a) Not yet**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. *You should sleep* = ____. → a) consejo salud · b) I'd like · c) Who called
7. *Should I take medicine?* = ____. → a) pide consejo · b) oferta · c) gusto
8. *You ought to rest* = ____. → a) ought to + rest · b) ought rest · c) should to rest
9. *Drink water* (doctor) = ____. → a) recomendación · b) like coffee · c) any bread
10. Unit 38 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Module 6

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) consejo salud** · 7. **a) pide consejo** · 8. **a) ought to + rest** · 9. **a) recomendación** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Advice: You ____ sleep more. → a) should · b) should to · c) ought
12. Negative: You ____ drink so much coffee. → a) shouldn't · b) shouldn't to · c) don't should
13. Question: ____ I take medicine? → a) Should · b) Do should · c) Am
14. Shadow and note ____. → a) should/ought to · b) only a/an · c) only phrasals
15. U38 closes before ____. → a) adverbials U39 · b) Module 6 only · c) U31 skip

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) should** · 12. **a) shouldn't** · 13. **a) Should** · 14. **a) should/ought to** · 15. **a) adverbials U39**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. You ____ drink more water. → *should / should to / ought*
2. You ____ eat so fast. → *shouldn't / shouldn't to / don't should*
3. You ____ to see a doctor. → *ought / ought to / should to*
4. ____ I book the hotel today? → *Should / Do should / Am*
5. Students ____ arrive late. → *shouldn't / shouldn't to / oughtn't*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **should** · 2. **shouldn't** · 3. **ought to** · 4. **Should** · 5. **shouldn't**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. You / should / rest / this / weekend.
7. Should / I / call / a / taxi / ?
8. You / ought / to / practise / every / day.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. You should rest this weekend.
7. Should I call a taxi?
8. You ought to practise every day.

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Un consejo con *should*.
10. Un consejo negativo con *shouldn't*.
11. Un consejo con *ought to*.
12. Una pregunta *Should I…?*
13. Consejo de salud (2 frases).
14. Consejo de examen (2 frases).
15. Consejos para un amigo que visita tu ciudad (8–10 líneas).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. You should drink more water. / You should sleep well the night before.
10. You shouldn't study all night. / You shouldn't drink so much coffee.
11. You ought to practise listening with short videos. / You ought to rest this weekend.
12. Should I take medicine? / Should I call a taxi?
13. You should sleep seven or eight hours. You shouldn't drink so much coffee.
14. You should arrive early. You shouldn't forget your ID. She ought to practise listening with short videos.
15. You should book the hotel early. You shouldn't leave your bag on the train. You ought to check the weather. You should try local food. You shouldn't forget your passport. Should I take a taxi? You should rest after the flight.

</details>
