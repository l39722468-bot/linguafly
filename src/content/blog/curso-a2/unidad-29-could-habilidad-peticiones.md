En la [Unidad 28](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-28-have-to-dont-have-to) hablaste de obligaciones. En la **Unidad 29 del curso A2** (*Could: Past Ability & Polite Requests*) usas **could** para lo que **podías** hacer antes y para pedir con educación.

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 29 — Could](/curso-a2/unit-29)  
> **Antes:** [U28 — Have to / Don't have to](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-28-have-to-dont-have-to)

---

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 29 (A2)

- Usar **could** + infinitivo para **habilidad pasada**.
- Formar **couldn't** (no podía).
- Hacer **peticiones educadas**: *Could you…, please?*
- Contrastar con **can** (ahora).
- Responder a peticiones de forma natural.

## 1. Dos usos de *could*

| Uso | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Past ability** | lo que sabías / podías hacer antes | When I was young, I **could** swim. |
| **Polite request** | pedir con educación | **Could you** pass the salt, please? |

## 2. Habilidad pasada: *could / couldn't*

Forma: **could / couldn't** + infinitivo (igual para todos los sujetos).

> When I was a child, I **could** swim very well.  
> I **could** ride a bike at the age of five.  
> She **could** speak French when she was a child.  
> I **couldn't** speak English when I was young, but now I **can**.  
> When I was young, I **could** run very fast.  
> I **could** play the piano at the age of seven.  
> I **couldn't** cook when I lived with my parents.

Marcadores útiles: *when I was young / a child*, *at the age of…*, *then / now*.

## 3. Peticiones educadas: *Could you…?*

Patrón: **Could you** + infinitivo + **…, please?**

> **Could you** pass me the salt, please?  
> **Could you** help me with this bag?  
> **Could you** open the window? It's hot in here.  
> **Could you** turn down the volume, please?  
> **Could you** repeat that, please?  
> **Could you** tell me the time? I forgot my watch at home.

Respuestas típicas: *Sure. / Of course. / No problem. / Sorry, I can't.*

## 4. *Can* vs *could*

![Can vs could](/blog/curso-a2/unit-29/a2-unit29-can-vs-could.png)

| | *can* | *could* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Habilidad ahora | I **can** swim now. | — |
| Habilidad pasada | — | I **could** swim when I was a child. |
| Petición | Can you…? *(más directo)* | **Could you…?** *(más educado)* |

En esta unidad prioriza: pasado = *could* · petición cortés = *Could you…?*

---

## 5. Texto modelo: niño y peticiones

> When I was a child, I could swim very well. I could ride a bike at the age of five. I couldn't speak English when I was young, but now I can. Could you pass me the salt, please? Could you help me with this bag? Could you open the window? It is hot in here.

## 6. Listening: joven y por favor

> When I was young, I could run very fast. I could play the piano at the age of seven. I couldn't cook when I lived with my parents. Could you turn down the volume, please? Could you repeat that, please? Could you tell me the time? I forgot my watch at home.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-29/dialogue-young.mp3" title="🔊 When I was young, I could run very fast…"></audio>

Preguntas rápidas:

1. ¿De niño/joven? → *run very fast*  
2. ¿Piano? → *at the age of seven*  
3. ¿Cocinar con los padres? → *couldn't*  
4. ¿Peticiones? → *turn down the volume / repeat / tell the time*

## 7. Mini práctica oral

> When I was a child, I could…  
> I couldn't…  
> Could you…, please?  
> Now I can…

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-29/practice-four.mp3" title="🔊 When I was a child, I could… Could you…, please?"></audio>

1. Di **2** habilidades pasadas y **1** *couldn't*.  
2. Haz **3** peticiones con *Could you…?*  
3. Contrasta: *Then I could… / Now I can…*

## 9. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I could **to** swim. | I could swim. | Infinitivo sin *to* |
| She **coulds** speak French. | She **could** speak… | *could* no cambia |
| When I was young, I **can** swim. | … I **could** swim. | Pasado → *could* |
| Pass the salt! *(muy directo)* | **Could you** pass the salt, please? | Petición educada |
| Could you **to** help me? | Could you help me? | Sin *to* |

## 10. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — *could* o *can*

1. When I was young, I ____ swim very well.  
2. Now I ____ speak English.  
3. She ____ speak French when she was a child.  
4. I ____ cook when I lived with my parents. *(negativa)*  
5. ____ you pass the salt, please?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **could** · 2. **can** · 3. **could** · 4. **couldn't** · 5. **Could**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa la petición

1. Could you ____ the salt, please?  
2. Could you ____ me with this bag?  
3. Could you ____ the window?  
4. Could you ____ down the volume, please?  
5. Could you ____ that, please?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **pass** · 2. **help** · 3. **open** · 4. **turn** · 5. **repeat**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Completa el texto

When I was a child, I ____ swim very well. I ____ ride a bike at the age of five. I ____ speak English when I was young, but now I ____.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**could** · **could** · **couldn't** · **can**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Completa el listening

When I was young, I could ____ very fast. I could play the ____ at the age of seven. I ____ cook when I lived with my parents. Could you tell me the ____?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**run** · **piano** · **couldn't** · **time**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Empareja

1. past ability  
2. polite request  
3. present ability  
4. negative past  

a) Could you help me? · b) I can swim now. · c) I could swim then. · d) I couldn't cook.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **c** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **b** · 4 → **d**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Elige

1. Habilidad de niño:  
   a) I can swim when I was a child. · b) I could swim when I was a child. · c) I could to swim when I was a child.  
2. Petición educada:  
   a) Open the window! · b) Could you open the window, please? · c) You open the window.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b** · 2. **b**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Corrige

1. *I could to swim very well.*  
2. *She coulds speak French.*  
3. *When I was young, I can run fast.*  
4. *Could you to help me?*  
5. *I no could cook.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I could swim very well.  
2. She **could** speak French.  
3. When I was young, I **could** run fast.  
4. Could you help me?  
5. I **couldn't** cook.
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Traduce

1. Cuando era niño, podía nadar muy bien.  
2. Podía montar en bici a los cinco años.  
3. No podía hablar inglés de joven, pero ahora puedo.  
4. ¿Podrías pasarme la sal, por favor?  
5. ¿Podrías ayudarme con esta bolsa?  
6. ¿Podrías abrir la ventana?  
7. Cuando era joven, podía correr muy rápido.  
8. Podía tocar el piano a los siete años.  
9. No podía cocinar cuando vivía con mis padres.  
10. ¿Podrías repetir eso, por favor?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. When I was a child, I could swim very well.  
2. I could ride a bike at the age of five.  
3. I couldn't speak English when I was young, but now I can.  
4. Could you pass me the salt, please?  
5. Could you help me with this bag?  
6. Could you open the window?

9. I couldn't cook when I lived with my parents.  
10. Could you repeat that, please?
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Entonces o petición

Marca **P** (pasado) o **R** (request):

1. I could ride a bike at the age of five.  
2. Could you turn down the volume, please?  
3. I couldn't cook then.  
4. Could you tell me the time?  
5. She could speak French when she was a child.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **P** · 2. **R** · 3. **P** · 4. **R** · 5. **P**
</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu texto

Escribe 8–12 líneas:

- 3 × *When I was…, I could…*  
- 1 × *I couldn't…*  
- 1 × contraste *now I can…*  
- 3 × *Could you…, please?*  

**Modelo:** el texto del niño o el del piano.

## 11. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Dos usos de *could* + *can* vs *could*.  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–7.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha los textos; separa pasado y peticiones.  
4. **5–8 min** — Graba tus frases (Ejercicio 10).

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *could* para habilidad pasada  
- [ ] Formo *couldn't*  
- [ ] Pido con *Could you…, please?*  
- [ ] Distingo *can* (ahora) y *could* (pasado / cortesía)  
- [ ] Puedo hablar de mi infancia y pedir ayuda
