## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 44 (A2)

- Vocabulario: **sunny, rainy, cloudy, windy, foggy, storm**.
- Describir ahora: **It's…**
- Predicciones: **It's going to…** / **It will…**
- Hablar de **temperaturas**.
- Entender un **pronóstico** sencillo.

## 1. Vocabulario del tiempo

| Adjetivo / palabra | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **sunny** | soleado | It's **sunny** today. |
| **rainy** | lluvioso | It's a **rainy** morning. |
| **cloudy** | nublado | It's **cloudy**. |
| **windy** | ventoso | It's very **windy**. |
| **foggy** | con niebla | It's **foggy** this morning. |
| **storm / stormy** | tormenta | There's a **storm**. |
| **hot / warm / cool / cold** | calor → frío | It's **hot** in July. |
| **snow / snowy** | nieve | It's **snowing**. |

Verbos útiles: **rain**, **snow**, **shine**.

> It's **raining**. / The sun is **shining**.

## 2. Describir ahora: *It's…*

| Estructura | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| It's + adjective | It's **cold**. / It's **cloudy**. |
| It's + -ing (weather verb) | It's **raining**. / It's **snowing**. |
| There's a + noun | There's a **storm**. |

Preguntas:

> What's the weather **like**?  
> What's the temperature?

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-44/whats-the-weather-like.mp3" title="🔊 What's the weather like?"></audio>

## 3. Predicciones: *going to* y *will*

| Forma | Cuándo | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **It's going to…** | indicios ahora / plan del cielo | Look at those clouds — it's **going to rain**. |
| **It will…** | predicción / pronóstico | Tomorrow **it will be** sunny. |

> It's **going to be** windy this afternoon.  
> Next week **it will be** warmer.

También: **I think it will…** / **It might…** (posibilidad suave).

## 4. Temperaturas

| Expresión | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| It's + number + degrees | It's **18 degrees**. |
| below zero | It's **below zero**. |
| high / low | The **high** will be 25°. |

En muchos países se usa **Celsius**. En conversación: *It's twenty degrees.*

> It's **22 degrees** and sunny — perfect for the beach.

## 5. Mapa rápido

| Quieres decir… | Usa… | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Tiempo ahora | It's + adj / -ing | It's windy. / It's raining. |
| Preguntar | What's the weather like? | What's the weather like? |
| Predicción con indicios | going to | It's going to snow. |
| Pronóstico | will | It will be cloudy. |
| Temperatura | degrees | It's 10 degrees. |

## 6. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura — Weekend forecast

Today it's **cloudy** and **cool** — about **14 degrees**. This afternoon it's **going to rain**, so take an umbrella. Tomorrow **it will be sunny** and warmer: a **high** of **21 degrees**. Perfect for a walk! On Sunday it **might** be **windy**, but there won't be a **storm**.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-44/reading-forecast.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Weekend forecast"></audio>

### Diálogo — Before a trip

**Ana:** What's the weather **like** in Lisbon?  
**Leo:** Today it's **sunny**. Tomorrow **it's going to be** hotter.  
**Ana:** Will it rain?  
**Leo:** I don't think so. The forecast says **it will be** dry all weekend.  
**Ana:** Great — no umbrella then!

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-44/dialogue-trip.mp3" title="🔊 Dialogue: Before a trip"></audio>

### Mini práctica

1. It's ___ today. *(soleado)*  
2. Look — it's ___ to rain.  
3. Tomorrow it ___ be cold.  
4. What's the weather ___ ?

## 7. Errores frecuentes

1. ~~It's a sun.~~ → **It's sunny.** / **The sun is shining.**  
2. ~~It's rain.~~ → **It's raining.** / **It's rainy.**  
3. ~~There is sunny.~~ → **It's sunny.**  
4. ~~It will raining.~~ → **It will rain.** / **It will be raining.**  
5. ~~How is the weather?~~ *(posible)* → más natural: **What's the weather like?**  
6. ~~20 grades~~ → **20 degrees**

## 8. Ejercicios (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige el adjetivo

1. The sun is out → ___  
2. There are many clouds → ___  
3. The wind is strong → ___  
4. You can't see well — thick mist → ___  
5. Lots of rain → ___

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. sunny  
2. cloudy  
3. windy  
4. foggy  
5. rainy  

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa con *It's…*

1. ___ snowing.  
2. ___ very hot today.  
3. ___ a storm outside. *(There's)*  
4. ___ going to be cold tonight.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. It's  
2. It's  
3. There's  
4. It's  

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — *going to* o *will*

1. Look at the sky! It ___ rain.  
2. According to the forecast, tomorrow it ___ be sunny.  
3. Take a coat — it ___ be windy later. *(indicio: viento empieza)*  
4. I think it ___ snow in the mountains next week.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. 's going to / is going to  
2. will  
3. 's going to / is going to  
4. will  

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrige el error

1. It's a sunny.  
2. It's rain now.  
3. What the weather like?  
4. It will raining tomorrow.  
5. It's 15 grades.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. It's sunny.  
2. It's raining now. / It's rainy now.  
3. What's the weather like?  
4. It will rain tomorrow.  
5. It's 15 degrees.  

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Traduce al inglés

1. Hoy está nublado.  
2. Está lloviendo.  
3. Va a hacer viento esta tarde.  
4. Mañana hará sol.  
5. ¿Qué tiempo hace?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. It's cloudy today.  
2. It's raining.  
3. It's going to be windy this afternoon.  
4. It will be sunny tomorrow. / It's going to be sunny tomorrow.  
5. What's the weather like?  

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Empareja

1. sunny  
2. storm  
3. degrees  
4. forecast  
5. foggy  

a) pronóstico  
b) soleado  
c) niebla  
d) tormenta  
e) grados

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1 → b  
2 → d  
3 → e  
4 → a  
5 → c  

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Completa el diálogo

**A:** What's the weather ___ in your city?  
**B:** It's ___ and cold — about 8 ___.  
**A:** ___ it rain later?  
**B:** Yes, it's ___ to rain this evening.  
**A:** Then I ___ take my umbrella.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

like  
cloudy (or rainy/windy) / degrees  
Will / Is  
going  
will / 'll  

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Elige la mejor opción

1. ___ the weather like today?  
   a) How is  b) What's  c) What's is  
2. There's ___ storm coming.  
   a) a  b) an  c) the  
3. Tomorrow it will ___ warmer.  
   a) being  b) be  c) to be

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. b  
2. a  
3. b  

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Ordena

1. sunny / It's / and / warm  
2. rain / going / It's / to  
3. weather / What's / like / the / ?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. It's sunny and warm.  
2. It's going to rain.  
3. What's the weather like?  

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu pronóstico (8–12 líneas)

Describe el tiempo en tu ciudad: hoy + mañana + fin de semana. Incluye:

- 3 adjetivos (*sunny / rainy / cloudy…*)  
- 1 *going to*  
- 1 *will*  
- 1 temperatura (*degrees*)

**Modelo:** la lectura de la sección 6.

## 9. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Vocabulario + *It's…*  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–6.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha forecast y diálogo.  
4. **5–8 min** — Ejercicio 10.

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *It's sunny / raining / cloudy*  
- [ ] Pregunto *What's the weather like?*  
- [ ] Distingo *going to* (indicios) y *will* (pronóstico)  
- [ ] Digo temperaturas con *degrees*  
- [ ] Evito *It's a sun* / *15 grades*
