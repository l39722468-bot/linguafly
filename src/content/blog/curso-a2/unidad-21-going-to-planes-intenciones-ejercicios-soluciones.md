## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** am/is/are + going to + infinitivo (planes e intenciones).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ going to travel next summer. → *am / is / are*
2. She ____ going to study engineering. → *is / am / are*
3. They ____ going to move next month. → *are / is / am*
4. We ____ not going to stay at home. → *are / is / am*
5. ____ you going to come? → *Are / Is / Am*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **am** · 2. **is** · 3. **are** · 4. **are** · 5. **Are**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Form = ____. → a) am/is/are + going to + infinitive · b) will + -ing · c) have + PP
7. U21 focus is ____. → a) plans & intentions · b) only past habits · c) only zero conditional
8. After *going to* use ____. → a) infinitive (visit) · b) *-ing* only · c) past participle
9. *I going to travel* is ____. → a) wrong (need *am*) · b) correct · c) Past Perfect
10. Negatives use ____. → a) am/is/are + not + going to · b) don't going to · c) didn't going to

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) am/is/are + going to + infinitive** · 7. **a) plans & intentions** · 8. **a) infinitive (visit)** · 9. **a) wrong (need *am*)** · 10. **a) am/is/are + not + going to**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I going to travel to Japan.*
12. *She are going to study English.*
13. *They is going to move.*
14. *We going to to visit Paris.*
15. *Is you going to come?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I **am** going to travel to Japan.
12. She **is** going to study English.
13. They **are** going to move.
14. We are going to visit Paris.
15. **Are** you going to come?

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** verbos de planes: travel, visit, study, move, apply, look for.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *travel* ≈ ____ → *go on a trip · stay forever · forget*
2. *move* (house) ≈ ____ → *change home · cook · sleep*
3. *look for a job* ≈ ____ → *try to find work · finish dinner · rain*
4. *apply for* ≈ ____ → *send a job application · buy milk · run*
5. *get married* ≈ ____ → *have a wedding · visit a museum · open a window*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **go on a trip** · 2. **change home** · 3. **try to find work** · 4. **send a job application** · 5. **have a wedding**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. I am going to ____ Japan. → *visit / visiting / visited*
7. She is going to ____ engineering. → *study / studying / studied*
8. They are going to ____ next month. → *move / moving / moved*
9. He is going to ____ for a job. → *look / looking / looked*
10. We are going to ____ married. → *get / getting / got*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **visit** · 7. **study** · 8. **move** · 9. **look** · 10. **get**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. Time markers for plans: ____. → a) next week / soon / this weekend · b) yesterday only · c) *ago* only
12. *I'm going to* shows ____. → a) a decided plan · b) only Past Simple · c) zero obligation
13. Short answer to *Are you going to come?* → ____. → a) Yes, I am / No, I'm not · b) Yes, I do · c) Yes, I have
14. *Going to visiting* is ____. → a) wrong · b) required · c) Past Perfect
15. Unit 21 is about ____. → a) plans & intentions · b) only weather evidence · c) only *will* promises

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) next week / soon / this weekend** · 12. **a) a decided plan** · 13. **a) Yes, I am / No, I'm not** · 14. **a) wrong** · 15. **a) plans & intentions**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Emma's plans

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-21/reading-plans.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Emma's plans"></audio>

> My name is Emma. I am going to travel to Japan next summer. I am going to visit Tokyo and Kyoto. My brother is going to study at university next year. He is going to study engineering. My parents are going to move to a small village next month. They are going to buy a house there. This weekend I am going to visit my grandmother. She is going to cook my favourite food. I love making plans and thinking about the future!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Where is Emma going to travel? → a) Japan · b) Australia · c) Brazil
2. What cities will she visit? → a) Tokyo and Kyoto · b) London only · c) Paris and Rome
3. What is her brother going to study? → a) Engineering · b) Medicine · c) Art
4. Where are her parents going to move? → a) A small village · b) A big city · c) A hotel
5. Who is she going to visit this weekend? → a) Her grandmother · b) Her boss · c) David

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Japan** · 2. **a) Tokyo and Kyoto** · 3. **a) Engineering** · 4. **a) A small village** · 5. **a) Her grandmother**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The text practices ____. → a) going to for plans · b) only Past Continuous · c) only *must*
7. Emma's parents are going to ____. → a) buy a house · b) finish school · c) rain
8. Time words like *next summer* appear: ____. → a) yes · b) never · c) only *ago*
9. After *going to* verbs are ____. → a) infinitives · b) past participles · c) adjectives only
10. Unit focus matches U21: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Unit 1

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) going to for plans** · 7. **a) buy a house** · 8. **a) yes** · 9. **a) infinitives** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *I am going to* needs ____. → a) *am* + going to + infinitive · b) *did* + going · c) *have* + -ing
12. *She is going to cook* is ____. → a) correct · b) wrong · c) Past Perfect
13. Questions invert ____. → a) Am/Is/Are + subject · b) Subject + Are always · c) Did + going to
14. Negatives: ____. → a) aren't / isn't / am not going to · b) don't going to · c) won't going
15. Reading + audio helps you ____. → a) notice form in context · b) skip grammar · c) avoid English

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) *am* + going to + infinitive** · 12. **a) correct** · 13. **a) Am/Is/Are + subject** · 14. **a) aren't / isn't / am not going to** · 15. **a) notice form in context**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: David's plans

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-21/listening-plans.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: David's plans"></audio>

> Hi! My name is David. I am going to tell you about my plans for next year. I am going to finish university in June. After that I am going to look for a job. I am going to apply for jobs in London and Manchester. My girlfriend is going to travel to Australia in September. She is going to work there for six months. We are going to get married when she comes back. This weekend I am going to visit my parents. They are going to celebrate their anniversary. I am excited about all my plans!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. When is David going to finish university? → a) In June · b) In December · c) Never
2. What is he going to do after that? → a) Look for a job · b) Retire · c) Stop studying forever
3. Where will he apply for jobs? → a) London and Manchester · b) Tokyo only · c) A village
4. Where is his girlfriend going to travel? → a) Australia · b) Japan · c) Spain
5. What are they going to do when she comes back? → a) Get married · b) Move alone · c) Cancel plans

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) In June** · 2. **a) Look for a job** · 3. **a) London and Manchester** · 4. **a) Australia** · 5. **a) Get married**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Listening checks ____. → a) going to plans in context · b) only spelling of *-est* · c) only *into*
7. His parents are going to ____. → a) celebrate their anniversary · b) rain · c) finish school
8. David sounds ____. → a) excited about his plans · b) angry · c) bored only
9. *Going to* appears many times: ____. → a) yes · b) never · c) only once
10. Unit 21 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Unit 60

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) going to plans in context** · 7. **a) celebrate their anniversary** · 8. **a) excited about his plans** · 9. **a) yes** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. You can notice *am/is/are going to* + ____. → a) infinitive · b) only PP · c) *-est*
12. Plans often use markers like *next year*: ____. → a) true · b) false · c) irrelevant
13. Shadow the audio and ____. → a) repeat the forms · b) ignore verbs · c) translate titles only
14. After listening, write 3 plan sentences: ____. → a) good practice · b) useless · c) only for teachers
15. U21 is not mainly about ____. → a) evidence-based weather predictions · b) decided plans · c) *am/is/are going to*

<details>

<details>

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

7. Are / you / going / to / come?

7. Are you going to come?
8. She is going to look for a job.

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Un plan de viaje con *I'm going to…*.

11. Una negativa con *aren't going to*.
12. Una pregunta con *Are you going to…?*
13. Un plan familiar (*My parents are going to…*).
