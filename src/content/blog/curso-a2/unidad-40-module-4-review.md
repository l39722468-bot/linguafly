## Qué repasarás en la Unidad 40 (A2)

- **Phrasal verbs** (U31–U32): *get up*, *pick up*, *turn on*…
- **Gerundios / infinitivos** (U33): *like -ing* / *want to* / *would like to*
- **Subject & object questions** (U34): *Who called you?* / *Who did you call?*
- **Some / any / much / many** (U35) y **artículos** (U36)
- **Would like vs like** (U37) y **should / ought to** (U38)
- **Adverbial phrases** (U39): *at the moment*, *once a week*, place before time

## 3. Gerundios, *like* y *would like*

| Idea | Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Gusto | like + **-ing** / noun | I **like cooking**. / I **like** pasta. |
| Querer | want / need + **to** | I **want to travel**. |
| Pedido educado | I'd like (+ noun / **to** + V) | I'd **like** a coffee. / I'd **like to** sit here. |

## 5. Cantidades y artículos

| Tema | Regla corta | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| some / any | *any* en negativas/preguntas (general) | I don't have **any** milk. |
| much / many | *much* incontable / *many* contable | How **much** water? How **many** eggs? |
| a / an / the / Ø | nuevo → *a/an*; conocido → *the*; general → Ø | I like **music**. |

## 7. Lectura integrada

### Reading — A busy Saturday

Ana **gets up** early **on Saturdays**. She **likes running** **in the park** **in the morning**. **At the moment**, she is tired, so her friend says: “You **should** rest. You **shouldn't** run today.” Ana answers: “I'd **like to** go anyway — just once.” Later she buys **some** fruit. She doesn't need **any** bread. She sees **a** café near **the** station and thinks: “I'd **like** a tea.” She goes there **once a week**.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-40/reading-saturday.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: A busy Saturday"></audio>

### Diálogo — At the café

**Waiter:** **Would you like** a table?  
**Ana:** Yes, please. I'd **like** a tea and **some** cake.  
**Waiter:** We don't have **any** chocolate cake today.  
**Ana:** OK. I'd **like** apple cake.  
**Friend:** You **should** try the sandwich too.  
**Ana:** Later. I **like** eating light **in the morning**.

## 8. Errores frecuentes del módulo

1. ~~I like a coffee, please.~~ → **I'd like a coffee, please.**  
2. ~~You should to go.~~ → **You should go.**  
3. ~~I study in the evening at home.~~ → **I study at home in the evening.**  
4. ~~Who you called?~~ → **Who did you call?**  
5. ~~I want going.~~ → **I want to go.**  
6. ~~Pick up it.~~ → **Pick it up.**  
7. ~~in the moment~~ → **at the moment**  
8. ~~I like the music~~ *(general)* → **I like music.**

## 9. Ejercicios mixtos (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige la opción correcta

1. I ___ swimming.  
   a) like  b) would like  
2. ___ a water, please.  
   a) I like  b) I'd like  
3. You ___ smoke.  
   a) shouldn't  b) shouldn't to  
4. ___ called you?  
   a) Who did  b) Who

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. a  
2. b  
3. a  
4. b  

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Phrasal verbs

Completa: *get up / turn off / pick up / look for*

1. Please ___ the lights.  
2. I ___ my keys every morning.  
3. Can you ___ that bag?  
4. What time do you ___ ?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. turn off  
2. look for  
3. pick up  
4. get up  

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Gerund o infinitivo

1. I love ___ (cook).  
2. I want ___ (travel).  
3. I'd like ___ (order) now.  
4. She hates ___ (wait).

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. cooking  
2. to travel  
3. to order  
4. waiting  

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Artículos y cantidades

1. I saw ___ cat. ___ cat was black.  
2. Do you have ___ milk?  
3. How ___ apples do you need?  
4. I like ___ coffee. *(en general)*

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. a / The  
2. any  
3. many  
4. Ø (sin artículo)  

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Subject vs object questions

1. ___ broke the window? *(alguien lo rompió — sujeto)*  
2. ___ did you see? *(objeto)*  
3. ___ happened yesterday?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Who  
2. Who / What  
3. What  

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Should / would like / like

1. ___ some tea? *(oferta)*  
2. I ___ tea. *(gusto)*  
3. You ___ drink more water. *(consejo)*  
4. I'd ___ to sit here.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Would you like  
2. like  
3. should  
4. like  

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Place before time

Ordena:

1. on Mondays / at school / she / studies  
2. in the evening / at home / we / cook  
3. once a week / in the park / they / meet

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. She studies at school on Mondays.  
2. We cook at home in the evening.  
3. They meet in the park once a week.  

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Corrige el error

1. Pick up it.  
2. You ought rest.  
3. Do you like some cake? *(oferta)*  
4. I study in the night.  
5. Who you meet yesterday?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Pick it up.  
2. You ought to rest.  
3. Would you like some cake?  
4. I study at night.  
5. Who did you meet yesterday?  

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Traduce al inglés

1. Me levanto a las siete.  
2. Querría un café, por favor.  
3. Deberías dormir más.  
4. Estudio en casa por la tarde.  
5. ¿Quién te llamó?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. I get up at seven.  
2. I'd like a coffee, please.  
3. You should sleep more.  
4. I study at home in the afternoon / evening.  
5. Who called you?  

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Texto integrado (10–14 líneas)

Escribe sobre un día típico. Incluye al menos:

- 1 phrasal verb  
- 1 *like* + -ing **y** 1 *I'd like*  
- 1 *should* / *shouldn't*  
- 1 artículo *a/an* + 1 *the*  
- 1 frase con **place + time**

**Modelo:** la lectura de la sección 7.

## 10. Plan de estudio (30–35 minutos)

1. **8 min** — Relee el mapa (sección 1) y las tablas 2–6.  
2. **12 min** — Ejercicios 1–8.

4. **5–8 min** — Ejercicio 10.
