## Lección 1 — Gramática: into / out of / through / across

**Objetivo:** movimiento hacia dentro, hacia fuera y cruzando.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. She went ____ the room. → *into / out of / across*
2. He ran ____ the building. → *out of / into / across*
3. We walked ____ the park. → *through / across / out of*
4. They ran ____ the bridge. → *across / into / out of*
5. I put the books ____ my bag. → *into / across / through*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **into** · 2. **out of** · 3. **through** · 4. **across** · 5. **into**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *into* = ____. → a) movement to the inside · b) only time · c) only height
7. *out of* = ____. → a) movement to the outside · b) month · c) comparative
8. *through* = ____. → a) across the inside · b) only surface · c) *at night*
9. *across* = ____. → a) from one side to the other on a surface · b) only rooms · c) *the most*
10. *go into the room* is ____. → a) clear movement in · b) wrong · c) only *go in the room* allowed

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) movement to the inside** · 7. **a) movement to the outside** · 8. **a) across the inside** · 9. **a) from one side to the other on a surface** · 10. **a) clear movement in**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *She went in the room.* (prioriza *into*)
12. *He walked across the tunnel.* (interior)
13. *They ran through the street.* (superficie)
14. *I came out the museum.*
15. *We went into of the cafe.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. She went **into** the room.
12. He walked **through** the tunnel.
13. They ran **across** the street.
14. I came **out of** the museum.
15. We went **into** the cafe.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: entrar, salir y cruzar

**Objetivo:** combinar verbos + preposiciones típicas.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja

1. into
2. out of
3. through
4. across
5. courtyard

a) patio · b) entrar · c) salir · d) por el interior · e) de lado a lado

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **c** · 3 → **d** · 4 → **e** · 5 → **a**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. go ____ a bookshop → *into / across / through*
7. come ____ a park → *out of / into / across*
8. walk ____ several rooms → *through / across / out of*
9. run ____ a bridge → *across / into / out of*
10. cross ____ street (también *across the street*) → *the / into / of*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **into** · 7. **out of** · 8. **through** · 9. **across** · 10. **the**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. Typical with *park/forest/tunnel/rooms*: ____. → a) through · b) across only · c) at
12. Typical with *street/bridge/river/road*: ____. → a) across · b) through only · c) in August
13. *Main entrance* pairs with ____. → a) go into · b) the tallest · c) fluently
14. *Sculpture section* is reached by walking ____ a courtyard. → a) across · b) out of · c) at noon
15. *Cafe* after leaving often uses ____. → a) went into · b) the best of · c) *-est*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) through** · 12. **a) across** · 13. **a) go into** · 14. **a) across** · 15. **a) went into**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: City adventure

**Objetivo:** leer un recorrido urbano.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-9/reading-movement.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: City adventure"></audio>

> Yesterday I had an adventure in the city. First I went into an old bookshop to buy a book. Then I walked through the park to get to the other side. I saw many people running across the bridge. When I came out of the park I crossed the street and went into a cafe. I had coffee and read my new book. It was a great day!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Where did they go first? → a) Into a bookshop · b) Into a museum · c) Across a river only
2. How did they cross the park? → a) Walked through · b) Flew · c) Drove only
3. Where were people running? → a) Across the bridge · b) Into the bag · c) At noon only
4. What happened after the park? → a) Came out, crossed the street, went into a cafe · b) Went home only · c) Nothing
5. What did they do in the cafe? → a) Had coffee and read · b) Worked · c) Slept

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Into a bookshop** · 2. **a) Walked through** · 3. **a) Across the bridge** · 4. **a) Came out, crossed the street, went into a cafe** · 5. **a) Had coffee and read**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Movement verbs include ____. → a) went into, walked through, running across, came out of · b) only *am* · c) only *the most*
7. *To get to the other side* explains ____. → a) why they walked through · b) time only · c) height
8. They bought ____. → a) a book · b) a phone · c) tickets only
9. Sequence: First… Then… When… = ____. → a) order of the route · b) only grammar of *will* · c) superlatives
10. The main topic is ____. → a) a city route with movement prepositions · b) introductions · c) *at/on/in* only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) went into, walked through, running across, came out of** · 7. **a) why they walked through** · 8. **a) a book** · 9. **a) order of the route** · 10. **a) a city route with movement prepositions**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Went into* = past of ____. → a) go into · b) go out of · c) run across
12. *Came out of* = past of ____. → a) come out of · b) go into · c) walk through
13. *Through the park* = ____. → a) interior path · b) month · c) adverb *-ly*
14. *Across the bridge* = ____. → a) surface/side-to-side · b) inside a tunnel · c) *the best*
15. Mixing *into/through/across/out of* in one story is ____. → a) natural · b) wrong · c) only B2

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) go into** · 12. **a) come out of** · 13. **a) interior path** · 14. **a) surface/side-to-side** · 15. **a) natural**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: The museum

**Objetivo:** escuchar un recorrido por un museo.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-9/listening-movement.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: The museum"></audio>

> Hi! Last weekend I went to the museum. I went into the main entrance and walked through several rooms. First I saw the paintings and then I walked across the courtyard to get to the sculpture section. When I came out of the museum I crossed the street and went into a cafe for coffee. I really enjoyed the day!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Where did they go? → a) The museum · b) The beach · c) School
2. How did they enter? → a) Into the main entrance · b) Across a river · c) Out of a car only
3. How did they move inside? → a) Through several rooms · b) Only across a bridge · c) Never
4. How did they reach sculptures? → a) Across the courtyard · b) Into a bag · c) At 7 only
5. What did they do after? → a) Came out, crossed the street, cafe · b) Stayed forever · c) Worked

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) The museum** · 2. **a) Into the main entrance** · 3. **a) Through several rooms** · 4. **a) Across the courtyard** · 5. **a) Came out, crossed the street, cafe**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. First they saw ____. → a) paintings · b) only coffee · c) nothing
7. *Enjoyed the day* means ____. → a) had a good time · b) hated it · c) left early forever
8. *Crossed the street* is similar in idea to ____. → a) went across the street · b) went into the street bag · c) *the tallest*
9. Prepositions practised: ____. → a) into, through, across, out of · b) only *at* · c) only *-ly*
10. The listening practices ____. → a) movement prepositions · b) only Present Perfect · c) only *will*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) paintings** · 7. **a) had a good time** · 8. **a) went across the street** · 9. **a) into, through, across, out of** · 10. **a) movement prepositions**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Enter museum → ____. → a) go into · b) go out of · c) run across only
12. Move inside rooms → ____. → a) walk through · b) walk across only · c) come out of only
13. Leave museum → ____. → a) come out of · b) go into · c) the most
14. Courtyard side-to-side → ____. → a) across · b) into · c) fluently
15. Cafe after leaving → ____. → a) went into a cafe · b) the hottest · c) *on Monday* only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) go into** · 12. **a) walk through** · 13. **a) come out of** · 14. **a) across** · 15. **a) went into a cafe**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My route

**Objetivo:** escribir un recorrido con las cuatro preposiciones.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I went ____ the museum. → *into / across / through*
2. I walked ____ several rooms. → *through / out of / across*
3. I walked ____ the courtyard. → *across / into / out of*
4. I came ____ the museum. → *out of / into / across*
5. I went ____ a cafe. → *into / through / across*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **into** · 2. **through** · 3. **across** · 4. **out of** · 5. **into**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. She / went / into / the room.
7. We / walked / through / the park.
8. They / ran / across / the bridge.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. She went into the room.
7. We walked through the park.
8. They ran across the bridge.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Entra en una tienda.
10. Camina por el interior de un parque.
11. Cruza un puente o una calle.
12. Sal de un edificio.
13. Entra en un café.
14. Mini ruta con First / Then / Finally.
15. Párrafo (6–8 frases) de un día en la ciudad.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I went into a shop.
10. I walked through the park.
11. I ran across the bridge. / I crossed the street.
12. I came out of the building.
13. I went into a cafe.
14. First I went into the museum. Then I walked through the rooms. Finally I came out of the museum.
15. Yesterday I went into a bookshop. Then I walked through the park. People ran across the bridge. I came out of the park and went into a cafe. It was a great day!

</details>
