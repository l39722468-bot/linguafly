  - question: ¿Cuál es la diferencia entre for y since?
    answer: >-
      For + duración (cuánto tiempo): for two years, for a week. Since + punto

  - question: ¿For y since van con Present Perfect?
    answer: >-
      Sí, en A2 los usamos con Present Perfect para situaciones que empezaron en

En la [Unidad 17](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-17-past-simple-past-continuous) combinaste pasados. En la **Unidad 18 del curso A2** (*For & Since*) vuelves al **Present Perfect** para decir **durante cuánto tiempo** o **desde cuándo**.

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 18 — For & Since](/curso-a2/unit-18)  
> **Antes:** [U17 — Past Simple + Past Continuous](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-17-past-simple-past-continuous)

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 18 (A2)

- Usar **for** + duración: *for two years, for a week*.
- Usar **since** + punto de inicio: *since 2020, since last Christmas*.
- Preguntar con **How long…?**
- Hablar de vida, trabajo, amigos y hobbies con Present Perfect.
- Evitar mezclas (*for 2020*, *since five years*).

![For y since con Present Perfect](/blog/curso-a2/unit-18/a2-unit18-for-since.png)

---

## 1. Idea clave

| Palabra | Qué añade | Pregunta mental | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **for** | **duración** (cuánto tiempo) | ¿Cuánto tiempo? | for five years |
| **since** | **punto de inicio** (desde cuándo) | ¿Desde cuándo? | since 2020 |

Ambos van muy bien con **Present Perfect** cuando la situación **sigue** ahora:

> I **have lived** here **for** five years.  
> I **have worked** here **since** 2020.

> I have lived in London **for five years**.  
> They have been married **for ten years**.

## 5. *For* / *since* vs *ago* (repaso rápido)

| Expresión | Tiempo | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **for / since** | Present Perfect (sigue ahora) | I have lived here **for** five years. |
| **ago** | Past Simple (acabado) | I moved here five years **ago**. |

No digas: *I have lived here five years ago.*

| :--- | :--- |

| for six months / a week / a long time | duración |
| since 2020 / 2021 | punto (año) |
| since last Christmas / January / summer | punto |

| married / teacher / university | casado / profesor / universidad |

## 10. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I have lived here since five years. | I have lived here **for** five years. | duración → *for* |
| I have worked here for 2020. | I have worked here **since** 2020. | punto → *since* |
| I live here for five years. | I **have lived** here for five years. | Present Perfect |
| How long do you live here? | How long **have** you **lived** here? | *How long* + Present Perfect |
| I have lived here five years ago. | I moved here five years **ago**. / I have lived here **for** five years. | *ago* ≠ Present Perfect |

## 11. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — *for* o *since*

1. I have lived here ____ two years.  
2. She has worked here ____ 2020.  
3. They have been married ____ ten years.  
4. He has known her ____ last summer.  
5. I have had my dog ____ last Christmas.  
6. I have been learning Spanish ____ six months.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **for** · 2. **since** · 3. **for** · 4. **since** · 5. **since** · 6. **for**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa a Emma

I have lived in London ____ five years. I have worked at the same company ____ 2020. I have known Sara ____ ten years. I have had Max ____ last Christmas. I have been learning Spanish ____ six months. I have loved travelling ____ I was a child.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**for** · **since** · **for** · **since** · **for** · **since**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Completa a Tom

I have lived in Manchester ____ three years. I have worked as a teacher ____ 2021. I have been married ____ five years. I have had my car ____ last January. I have been learning guitar ____ six months. I have loved music ____ I was a teenager.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**for** · **since** · **for** · **since** · **for** · **since**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — *How long…?*

Escribe la pregunta:

1. You live here. *(How long…?)*  
2. She works there.  
3. They are married.  
4. He has his car.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. How long **have you lived** here?  
2. How long **has she worked** there?  
3. How long **have they been** married?  
4. How long **has he had** his car?
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrige

1. *I have lived here since five years.*  
2. *She has worked here for 2020.*  
3. *How long do you live here?*  
4. *I have lived here five years ago.*  
5. *They have been married since ten years.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I have lived here **for** five years.  
2. She has worked here **since** 2020.  
3. How long **have** you **lived** here?  
4. I moved here five years **ago**. / I have lived here **for** five years.  
5. They have been married **for** ten years.
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Empareja

1. for  
2. since  
3. How long…?  
4. five years  
5. 2020  

a) duración · b) punto de inicio · c) pregunta de duración · d) va con *for* · e) va con *since*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → idea duración · 2 → punto · 3 → **c** · 4 → **d** · 5 → **e**  
(1=a, 2=b)
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Elige la correcta

1. ____  
   a) I have lived here for five years. · b) I have lived here since five years. · c) I live here for five years.  
2. ____  
   a) She has worked here since 2020. · b) She has worked here for 2020. · c) She works here since 2020.  
3. ____  
   a) How long have you lived here? · b) How long do you live here? · c) How long you have lived here?  
4. ____  
   a) I have known her for ten years. · b) I have known her since ten years. · c) I know her for ten years.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a** · 2. **a** · 3. **a** · 4. **a**
</details>

1. He vivido en Londres durante cinco años.

9. Trabajo como profesor desde 2021.

1. Pregunta: **¿Duración o punto?** → *for* / *since*.

| Pregunta | **How long + have/has + PP…?** |
| Tiempo verbal | **Present Perfect** (sigue ahora) |
| No mezclar | ~~for 2020~~ · ~~since five years~~ |
