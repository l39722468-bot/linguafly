  - question: ¿Cuándo uso Present Perfect y cuándo Past Continuous?
    answer: >-
      Present Perfect para experiencias o resultados ligados al presente (I have
      been to Paris / I have already finished). Past Continuous para una acción
      en curso en el pasado (I was walking when…).
  - question: ¿For o since?
    answer: >-
      For + duración (for three years). Since + punto de inicio (since 2021).
      Ambos con Present Perfect cuando la situación sigue.
  - question: ¿Qué preguntas How debo dominar?
    answer: >-
      How long (duración), How often (frecuencia), How much (incontable), How
      many (contable) y How long ago (pasado + ago).

## 1. Formas rápidas (cheat sheet)

![Formas rápidas del Módulo 2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-20/a2-unit20-quick-forms.png)

| Tema | Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Present Perfect | have/has + PP | I **have been** to Paris. |
| Ever / Never | ever…? / never + PP | Have you **ever**…? I have **never**… |
| Already / Yet | already mid · yet final | I have **already** finished. / I haven't… **yet**. |
| Just | have/has + just + PP | I have **just** arrived. |
| PP vs PS | sin fecha vs con fecha | I have been to Japan. / Last year I **went**… |
| Past Continuous | was/were + -ing | I **was walking**… |
| When / While | fondo + interrupción | I was walking **when** I met… |
| For / Since | duración / punto | **for** three years / **since** 2021 |
| How…? | long / often / much / many | How **often** do you exercise? |

## 2. Present Perfect (U11–U14)

> I **have been** to Paris twice.  
> Have you **ever** been to Japan?  
> I have **never** been to Australia.  
> I have **already** finished my course.  
> I haven't found a new job **yet**.  
> I have **just** arrived.

## 3. Present Perfect vs Past Simple (U15)

> I **have lived** in Barcelona for three years. *(sigue)*  
> Last week I **met** an old friend. *(fecha concreta)*  
> I **have already visited** five countries this year.  
> I haven't been to Japan **yet**.

Regla: ¿hay momento concreto (*last week, yesterday, ago*)? → Past Simple. ¿Experiencia / resultado / *already-yet-just-ever-never-for-since*? → Present Perfect.

## 4. Past Continuous + when/while (U16–U17)

> Last week I **was walking** in the park when I **met** an old friend.  
> We **were talking** while we walked.  
> Yesterday I **was cooking** dinner when my sister **called**.

## 5. For & Since + How questions (U18–U19)

> I have lived here **for** three years.  
> I have worked as a teacher **since** 2021.  
> **How long** have you lived in Barcelona?  
> **How often** do you go to the gym? — Three times a week.  
> **How many** countries have you visited? — Five.

## 6. Texto modelo: David

> My name is David. I have lived in Barcelona for three years. I have worked as a teacher since 2021. Last week I was walking in the park when I met an old friend. We were talking while we walked. How often do I exercise? I go to the gym three times a week. I have already finished my course this year but I haven't found a new job yet. I feel good because I have a stable life with good friends.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-20/reading-david.mp3" title="🔊 My name is David. I have lived in Barcelona…"></audio>

| Estructura | Ejemplo en David |
| :--- | :--- |
| for | lived… **for** three years |
| since | worked… **since** 2021 |
| was … when | **was walking** when I **met**… |
| while | **were talking** while we walked |
| How often | gym **three times a week** |
| already / yet | **already** finished / haven't found… **yet** |

## 7. Listening: Maria

> Hi! I am Maria. I have lived in Valencia for four years. I have worked at a hospital since 2020. Yesterday I was cooking dinner when my sister called. We were talking while I was finishing the food. How often do I exercise? I go swimming twice a week. I have already visited five countries this year but I haven't been to Japan yet. I feel happy because I have a good job and a wonderful family.

Preguntas rápidas:

1. ¿Cuánto tiempo en Valencia? → *for four years*  
2. ¿Desde cuándo en el hospital? → *since 2020*  
3. ¿Qué hacía cuando llamó su hermana? → *cooking dinner*  
4. ¿Ha estado en Japón? → *No, not yet*

## 9. Errores frecuentes del módulo

| Incorrecto | Correcto |
| :--- | :--- |
| I have been to Paris last year. | I **went** to Paris last year. |
| I was walk when I met him. | I was **walking** when I met him. |
| I have lived here since three years. | I have lived here **for** three years. |
| How many water…? | How **much** water…? |
| I have already finished yet. | I have **already** finished. / I haven't finished **yet**. |
| How long ago have you arrived? | How long ago **did** you arrive? |

## 10. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige la forma

1. I ____ been to Paris twice. *(have / went)*  
2. She ____ watching TV when I called. *(was / were)*  
3. I have lived here ____ five years. *(for / since)*  
4. I have worked here ____ 2021. *(for / since)*  
5. How ____ do you go to the gym? *(long / often / many)*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **have** · 2. **was** · 3. **for** · 4. **since** · 5. **often**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa a David

I have lived in Barcelona ____ three years. I have worked as a teacher ____ 2021. Last week I ____ walking when I ____ an old friend. I go to the gym three times a ____. I have ____ finished my course but I haven't found a new job ____.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**for** · **since** · **was** · **met** · **week** · **already** · **yet**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Completa a Maria

I have lived in Valencia ____ four years. I have worked at a hospital ____ 2020. Yesterday I ____ cooking when my sister ____. I go swimming ____ a week. I have ____ visited five countries but I haven't been to Japan ____.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**for** · **since** · **was** · **called** · **twice** · **already** · **yet**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Empareja tema → unidad

1. ever / never  
2. was/were + -ing  
3. for / since  
4. How much / How many  
5. already / yet  

a) U13 · b) U12 · c) U16 · d) U18 · e) U19

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **c** · 3 → **d** · 4 → **e** · 5 → **a**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrige

1. *I have been to Japan last year.*  
2. *I was walk when I met him.*  
3. *I have lived here since three years.*  
4. *How many water do you drink?*  
5. *I have already finished yet.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **went** to Japan last year.  
2. I was **walking** when I met him.  
3. I have lived here **for** three years.  
4. How **much** water do you drink?  
5. I have **already** finished. / I haven't finished **yet**.
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Elige la correcta

1. ____  
   a) I was walking when I met him. · b) I walked when I was meeting him. · c) I was walk when I met him.  
2. ____  
   a) I have lived here for three years. · b) I have lived here since three years. · c) I live here for three years.  
3. ____  
   a) How often do you exercise? · b) How long do you exercise a week? *(frecuencia)* · c) How many do you exercise?  
4. ____  
   a) I have already finished. · b) I have finished already the course. · c) I have already finish.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a** · 2. **a** · 3. **a** · 4. **a**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Traduce

1. He vivido en Barcelona durante tres años.  
2. Trabajo como profesor desde 2021.  
3. Estaba caminando cuando conocí a un amigo.  
4. Voy al gimnasio tres veces a la semana.  
5. Ya he terminado el curso.  
6. Aún no he encontrado un nuevo trabajo.  
7. ¿Con qué frecuencia haces ejercicio?  
8. Ya he visitado cinco países este año.  
9. Aún no he estado en Japón.  
10. Ayer estaba cocinando cuando llamó mi hermana.

### Ejercicio 8 — Tu texto integrado

Escribe 10–14 frases que incluyan:

- 2 × Present Perfect (*already/yet/ever/never/just*)  
- 1 × *for* · 1 × *since*  
- 2 × *was/were … when …*  
- 2 × *How…?* (pregunta o respuesta)  

**Modelo:** David o Maria.

1. **5 min** — Recorre el mapa U11–U19.  
2. **10 min** — Ejercicios 1–6.  
3. **8 min** — Escucha a David y Maria; marca cada estructura.

### Checklist final del Módulo 2

- [ ] Uso Present Perfect para experiencias y resultados (*ever/never/already/yet/just*)  
- [ ] Eligo Present Perfect vs Past Simple según el marcador  
- [ ] Describo escenas con *was/were + -ing*  
- [ ] Combino con *when/while*  
- [ ] Uso *for* (duración) y *since* (punto)  
- [ ] Pregunto con *How long / often / much / many*  
- [ ] Puedo escribir un párrafo que combine **todo**

## 12. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. No estudies cada tema aislado: **fuerza la mezcla** (como David).  
2. Mínimos: *have been, already, yet, just, was walking when, for, since, How often*.  
3. Grábate 60–90 segundos sobre tu vida ahora + algo que pasó la semana pasada.  
4. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 20](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-20-repaso-modulo-2-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 20 del curso A2](/curso-a2/unit-20).

La **Unidad 21** abre el **Módulo 3** con **Going to**: planes e intenciones (*I'm going to…*).
