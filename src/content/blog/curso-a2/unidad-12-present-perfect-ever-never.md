En la [Unidad 11](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-11-present-perfect-introduccion) formaste *I have been / She has seen*. En la **Unidad 12 del curso A2** (*Present Perfect: Ever & Never*) **preguntas y niegas** experiencias: ***Have you ever…?*** / ***I have never…***

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 12 — Present Perfect: Ever & Never](/curso-a2/unit-12)  
> **Antes:** [U11 — Present Perfect: Introducción](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-11-present-perfect-introduccion)

---

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 12 (A2)

- Preguntar con **ever**: *Have you ever been to Japan?*
- Negar con **never**: *I have never eaten sushi.*
- Responder: **Yes, I have** / **No, I haven't**.
- Colocar *never* entre *have/has* y el past participle.
- Hablar de experiencias (viaje, comida, ocio) en encuestas y diálogos.

![Ever y never Present Perfect A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-12/a2-unit12-ever-never.png)

---

## 1. Repaso rápido de la forma

Sigues usando **have/has + past participle**.

| | Pregunta con *ever* | Negativa con *never* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I / you / we / they | **Have** you **ever** been…? | I **have never** been… |
| he / she / it | **Has** she **ever** tried…? | He **has never** been… |

Participios útiles en esta unidad: *been, eaten, tried, flown, ridden, seen*.

---

## 2. *Ever* en preguntas

**Ever** = “alguna vez (en la vida)”. Va en preguntas, normalmente **después del sujeto**:

> **Have you ever** been to Japan?  
> **Has she ever** tried Italian food?  
> **Have you ever** ridden a horse?  
> **Have you ever** flown in a plane?  
> **Have you ever** eaten sushi?  
> **Have you ever** been to a concert?

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-12/has-she-ever-tried.mp3" title="🔊 Has she ever tried Italian food?"></audio>

Estructura:

**Have/Has + sujeto + ever + past participle + …?**

---

## 3. *Never* en frases

**Never** = “nunca / ni una vez”. Va **entre** *have/has* y el past participle:

> I **have never** eaten sushi.  
> He **has never** been to the beach.  
> I **have never** seen snow.  
> I **have never** been to a concert.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-12/have-never-eaten-sushi.mp3" title="🔊 I have never eaten sushi"></audio>

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-12/has-never-been-beach.mp3" title="🔊 He has never been to the beach"></audio>

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-12/have-never-seen-snow.mp3" title="🔊 I have never seen snow"></audio>

| Correcto | Incorrecto típico |
| :--- | :--- |
| I have **never** eaten sushi. | *I never have eaten sushi.* |
| I have never seen snow. | *I have seen never snow.* |
| He has never been… | *He never has been…* *(prioridad A2: have + never)* |

Con *never* **no** hace falta *not*: *I have never…* (no *I haven't never…*).

---

## 4. Respuestas cortas: *Yes, I have* / *No, I haven't*

![Respuestas cortas Yes I have No I haven't](/blog/curso-a2/unit-12/a2-unit12-short-answers.png)

| Pregunta | Respuesta corta | Ampliación opcional |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Have you ever been to London? | **Yes, I have.** | I have been to London. |
| Have you ever been there? | **No, I haven't.** | I have never been there. |
| Has she ever tried sushi? | **Yes, she has.** / **No, she hasn't.** | — |

> Yes, I have. I have been to London.  
> No, I haven't. I have never been there.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-12/yes-i-have.mp3" title="🔊 Yes, I have. I have been to London"></audio>

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-12/no-i-havent.mp3" title="🔊 No, I haven't. I have never been there"></audio>

Truco: la respuesta corta usa el mismo auxiliar (*have/has*), no el past participle.

---

## 5. *Ever* vs *never* (mapa mental)

| Palabra | Tipo de frase | Posición | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ever** | pregunta | after subject | Have you **ever** flown…? |
| **never** | “negativa” de experiencia | after have/has | I have **never** flown… |

Regla del curso: ***ever* en preguntas · *never* en negativas**.

---

## 6. Texto modelo: encuesta a compañeros

> Have you ever wondered what your friends have or haven't done? I asked my classmates some questions. Have you ever flown in a plane? Most of them said Yes, I have. Have you ever eaten sushi? Some said Yes and some said No, I haven't. Have you ever been to a concert? Many said Yes. One friend said: "I have never been to a concert. I would like to go one day." It was interesting to learn about their experiences.

## 7. Listening: survey

> Hi! I am doing a survey about experiences. Can I ask you some questions? Great! Have you ever tried sushi? Yes, I have. I tried it in Tokyo last year. It was amazing. Have you ever ridden a horse? No, I haven't. I would like to try one day. Have you ever been to a football match? Yes, I have. Many times! I love football. Thank you for your answers!

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-12/dialogue-survey.mp3" title="🔊 Hi! I am doing a survey about experiences…"></audio>

Nota A2: tras *Yes, I have* a veces aparece Past Simple con fecha (*I tried it in Tokyo last year*). La pregunta de experiencia va en Present Perfect; el detalle con tiempo concreto puede ir en pasado.

## 8. Mini práctica oral

> Have you ever been to Japan?  
> I have never eaten sushi.  
> Yes, I have.  
> No, I haven't.

1. Haz 4 preguntas *Have you ever…?* (comida, viaje, deporte, ocio).

3. Di 3 frases *I have never…*

---

## 9. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Ever you have been to Japan? | **Have you ever** been to Japan? | orden: Have + you + ever |
| I never have eaten sushi. | I **have never** eaten sushi. | *never* tras *have* |
| I haven't never been there. | I have **never** been there. / No, I **haven't**. | no dobles negaciones |
| Yes, I do. *(a Have you ever…?)* | **Yes, I have.** | auxiliar *have* |
| No, I don't. | **No, I haven't.** | auxiliar *haven't* |
| Have you never been to Japan? *(pregunta A2 prioritaria)* | **Have you ever** been…? | *ever* en preguntas |
| I have ever been to Japan. *(afirmativa)* | I **have been**… / I **have never**… | *ever* no va en afirmativas normales A2 |

## 10. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — *ever* o *never*

1. ____ have you been to Japan? → *Have you ____ been…*  
2. I have ____ eaten sushi.  
3. Has she ____ tried Italian food?  
4. He has ____ been to the beach.  
5. Have you ____ ridden a horse?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **ever** · 2. **never** · 3. **ever** · 4. **never** · 5. **ever**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Respuestas cortas

1. Have you ever been to London? → ____, I ____. *(sí)*  
2. Have you ever been there? → ____, I ____. *(no)*  
3. Has she ever tried sushi? → Yes, she ____.  
4. Has he ever flown? → No, he ____.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Yes, I have.** · 2. **No, I haven't.** · 3. **has** · 4. **hasn't**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Ordena / coloca *never*

Elige la correcta:

1. ____  
   a) I never have seen snow. · b) I have never seen snow. · c) I have seen never snow.  
2. ____  
   a) Have you ever been to Japan? · b) Ever have you been to Japan? · c) Have ever you been to Japan?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b** · 2. **a**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Completa la encuesta de compañeros

____ you ever flown in a plane? Most said ____, I ____. Have you ever eaten sushi? Some said No, I ____. One friend said: I have ____ been to a concert.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**Have** · **Yes** · **have** · **haven't** · **never**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Completa el listening

Have you ever ____ sushi? ____, I have. Have you ever ____ a horse? ____, I haven't. Have you ever ____ to a football match? Yes, I ____.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**tried** · **Yes** · **ridden** · **No** · **been** · **have**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Corrige

1. *I never have eaten sushi.*  
2. *Ever you have been to Japan?*  
3. *Yes, I do.* *(respuesta a Have you ever…?)*  
4. *No, I don't.*  
5. *I haven't never been there.*  
6. *I have seen never snow.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **have never** eaten sushi.  
2. **Have you ever** been to Japan?  
3. **Yes, I have.**  
4. **No, I haven't.**  
5. I have **never** been there. / No, I haven't.  
6. I have **never** seen snow.
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Empareja

1. Have you ever been to Japan?  
2. I have never eaten sushi.  
3. Yes, I have.  
4. No, I haven't.  
5. Has she ever tried Italian food?  

a) respuesta corta afirmativa · b) pregunta con *ever* (you) · c) negativa con *never* · d) respuesta corta negativa · e) pregunta con *Has… ever*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **c** · 3 → **a** · 4 → **d** · 5 → **e**
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Elige la correcta

1. *Ever* se usa sobre todo en: ____  
   a) preguntas · b) afirmativas · c) solo con *yesterday*  
2. Posición de *never*: ____  
   a) al final · b) entre have/has y el past participle · c) antes del sujeto  
3. ____  
   a) Have you ever flown in a plane? · b) Have you never flown in a plane? *(prioridad A2 pregunta)* · c) Did you ever fly yesterday Present Perfect  
4. Respuesta a *Have you ever…?*: ____  
   a) Yes, I have. · b) Yes, I do. · c) Yes, I ever.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a** · 2. **b** · 3. **a** · 4. **a**
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Traduce

1. ¿Has estado alguna vez en Japón?  
2. Nunca he comido sushi.  
3. ¿Ha probado ella alguna vez comida italiana?  
4. Él nunca ha estado en la playa.  
5. Sí, he estado en Londres.  
6. No, no he. Nunca he estado allí.  
7. ¿Has montado alguna vez a caballo?  
8. Nunca he visto la nieve.  
9. ¿Has volado alguna vez en un avión?  
10. Nunca he ido a un concierto.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. Have you ever been to Japan?  
2. I have never eaten sushi.  
3. Has she ever tried Italian food?  
4. He has never been to the beach.  
5. Yes, I have. I have been to London.  
6. No, I haven't. I have never been there.  
7. Have you ever ridden a horse?  
8. I have never seen snow.  
9. Have you ever flown in a plane?  
10. I have never been to a concert.
</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Haz tu encuesta

Escribe 8–12 líneas:

- 4 × preguntas *Have you ever…?*  
- 2 × *Yes, I have* (+ detalle)  
- 2 × *No, I haven't* / *I have never…*  

**Modelo:** reading (classmates) o listening (survey).

## 11. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Repasa *ever* (preguntas) y *never* (entre have/has y participio).  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–7.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha la encuesta; practica *Yes, I have / No, I haven't*.  
4. **5–8 min** — Graba tu mini-encuesta.

### Checklist

- [ ] Pregunto con *Have/Has… ever…?*  
- [ ] Niego con *have/has never + past participle*  
- [ ] Respondo *Yes, I have* / *No, I haven't*  
- [ ] No digo *I never have* ni *haven't never*  
- [ ] Puedo hacer una encuesta de 4 preguntas

---

## 12. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Plantilla: *Have you ever + past participle…?* → *Yes, I have.* / *No, I haven't. I have never…*  
2. Lista: *been to Japan, eaten sushi, flown, ridden a horse, been to a concert, seen snow, tried Italian food, been to a football match*.  
3. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 12](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-12-present-perfect-ever-never-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 12 del curso A2](/curso-a2/unit-12).

La **Unidad 13** añade **already** y **yet**: *I have already…* / *I haven't… yet*.

## Resumen rápido

| Caso | Forma |
| :--- | :--- |
| Pregunta | **Have/Has + sujeto + ever + PP…?** |
| Nunca | **have/has + never + PP** |
| Sí | **Yes, I have.** / **Yes, she has.** |
| No | **No, I haven't.** / **No, she hasn't.** |
| Posición *never* | entre *have/has* y el participio |
