## 6. Lectura integrada

### Reading — My A2 week

This week I felt **excited** **because** I finished Module 6. **Shall we** celebrate? **Let's** meet in the main **square** — **there is** a nice café **located** opposite the **library**. **In my opinion**, the course was useful, **although** some units were hard. I **work as** a student assistant, **so** I often **have to** **log in** and **send** emails. I also try to **recycle** at home. **Could you** help me print my certificate? That would be great!

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-60/reading-week.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: My A2 week"></audio>

### Diálogo — After the course

**Lara:** How do you **feel** now?  
**Ben:** **Proud** — and a bit tired.  
**Lara:** **I agree.** **Shall we** go for a coffee?  
**Ben:** Yes. **Would you like me to** book a table?  
**Lara:** Please. **Although** it's late, I'm hungry, **so** let's eat too.  
**Ben:** Perfect. I'll **send** you the **location**.

## 7. Errores frecuentes del módulo

1. ~~Let's to go.~~ → **Let's go.**  
2. ~~Can I to help you?~~ → **Can I help you?**  
3. ~~I am agree.~~ → **I agree.**  
4. ~~I am boring.~~ *(si te aburres)* → **I'm bored.**  
5. ~~You should to recycle.~~ → **You should recycle.**  
6. ~~Turn the WiFi.~~ → **Turn on** the WiFi.  
7. ~~I work like a teacher.~~ → **I work as** a teacher.  
8. ~~There is two museums.~~ → **There are** two museums.  
9. ~~Although… but…~~ → **Although…,** … / …, **but** …

## 8. Ejercicios mixtos (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Empareja unidad y frase

1. Suggestions  
2. Offers  
3. Opinions  
4. Environment  
5. Linking words  

a) Can I help you?  
b) You should recycle.  
c) Shall we go?  
d) I think it's great.  
e) It rained, so we stayed in.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1 → c  
2 → a  
3 → d  
4 → b  
5 → e  

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa

1. ___ we watch a film?  
2. ___ I carry your bag?  
3. I ___ this app is useful.  
4. I feel ___ about the exam. *(nervioso)*

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Shall  
2. Can / Shall  
3. think  
4. nervous  

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Tech + work + places

1. ___ the app from the store.  
2. I work ___ a designer.  
3. I study ___ university.  
4. ___ is a museum in the centre.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Download  
2. as  
3. at  
4. There  

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Conectores

1. I like tea ___ coffee.  
2. It's cheap, ___ small.  
3. I'm tired ___ I worked late.  
4. ___ it was late, we went out.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. and  
2. but  
3. because  
4. Although  

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrige el error

1. Let's to stay in.  
2. I am agree with you.  
3. Turn the computer.  
4. There is two libraries.  
5. Although I'm tired, but I'll go.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Let's stay in.  
2. I agree with you.  
3. Turn on the computer.  
4. There are two libraries.  
5. Although I'm tired, I'll go. / I'm tired, but I'll go.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Traduce al inglés

1. ¿Salimos?  
2. ¿Puedo ayudarte?  
3. Creo que es útil.  
4. Deberías reciclar.  
5. Llovió, así que nos quedamos.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Shall we go out?  
2. Can I help you?  
3. I think it's useful.  
4. You should recycle.  
5. It rained, so we stayed (in / at home).

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Completa el diálogo

**A:** How do you ___ ?  
**B:** Excited ___ I finished the course.  
**A:** ___ we celebrate?  
**B:** Yes. ___ you book a table?  
**A:** Of course. There's a café ___ in the square.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

feel  
because  
Shall  
Could / Can  
located (or situated)  

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Elige la mejor opción

1. ___ the window, please?  
   a) Shall we  b) Could you  c) Let's  
2. I'm ___ about the trip.  
   a) exciting  b) excited  c) excite  
3. Log ___ with your password.  
   a) on  b) in  c) up

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. b  
2. b  
3. b  

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Lectura True / False

> Shall we celebrate? Let's meet in the main square — there is a nice café located opposite the library.

1. They suggest meeting in the square.  
2. The café is far from the library.  
3. There is a café in the text.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. True  
2. False (opposite)  
3. True  

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu texto A2 (10–14 líneas)

Escribe un texto integrado. Incluye al menos:

- 1 sugerencia u oferta (*Shall we / Can I / Could you…*)  
- 1 opinión (*I think / In my opinion*)  
- 1 emoción (*I feel / I'm…*)  
- 1 idea de environment **o** technology **o** work/places  
- 2 conectores (*because / so / although / but…*)

**Modelo:** la lectura de la sección 6.
