## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** elegir entre going to, will y Present Continuous futuro.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. Next week I ____ flying to London. (arreglado) → *am / will / going*
2. I ____ going to visit the museum. (ticket ready) → *am / will / do*
3. I ____ call you when I arrive. (promesa) → *will / am / going*
4. Look! It ____ going to rain. → *is / will / are*
5. Maybe I ____ see a show. → *will / am / are*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **am** · 2. **am** · 3. **will** · 4. **is** · 5. **will**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Fixed arrangement → ____. → a) Present Continuous · b) only Past Simple · c) *must have*
7. Decided plan / evidence → ____. → a) going to · b) only *ago* · c) Past Perfect
8. Promise / spontaneous decision → ____. → a) will · b) only *-ing* diary · c) *for/since*
9. *Maybe* often pairs with ____. → a) will · b) Past Continuous · c) *was*
10. Unit 25 trains ____. → a) choosing the right future · b) only irregular Past · c) only articles

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Present Continuous** · 7. **a) going to** · 8. **a) will** · 9. **a) will** · 10. **a) choosing the right future**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *Next week I will flying to London.* (arreglado)
12. *I will going to visit the museum.* (ticket ready)
13. *I am call you tomorrow.* (promesa)
14. *Look! It will going to rain.*
15. *Maybe I am see a show.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. Next week I **am flying** to London.
12. I **am going to** visit the museum.
13. I **will** call you tomorrow.
14. Look! It **is going to** rain.
15. Maybe I **will** see a show.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** arranged, invitation, ticket, ceremony, maybe, perfect.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *arranged* ≈ ____ → *already organised · never planned · yesterday only*
2. *invitation* ≈ ____ → *a request to come to an event · a shoe · rain*
3. *ceremony* ≈ ____ → *formal event (wedding) · breakfast only · Past Perfect*
4. *ticket* ≈ ____ → *pass to enter · cloud · adjective*
5. *maybe* ≈ ____ → *perhaps · definitely never · ago*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **already organised** · 2. **a request to come to an event** · 3. **formal event (wedding)** · 4. **pass to enter** · 5. **perhaps**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. I am ____ to London next week. → *flying / fly / flew*
7. I am going to ____ the museum. → *visit / visiting / visited*
8. I will ____ you tomorrow. → *call / calling / called*
9. It is going to ____. → *rain / raining / rained*
10. Maybe I will ____ a show. → *see / seeing / seen*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **flying** · 7. **visit** · 8. **call** · 9. **rain** · 10. **see**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. Ticket already bought → often ____. → a) going to · b) only Past Continuous · c) *ago*
12. Wedding date fixed → often ____. → a) Present Continuous · b) only *must* · c) zero conditional
13. *I will send the invitation* is a ____. → a) promise/decision · b) Past Perfect · c) evidence cloud
14. Evidence rain → ____. → a) going to · b) only *did* · c) *-est*
15. Unit 25 mixes ____. → a) three futures · b) only one form · c) only A1 greetings

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) going to** · 12. **a) Present Continuous** · 13. **a) promise/decision** · 14. **a) going to** · 15. **a) three futures**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: London trip

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-25/reading-contrast.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: London trip"></audio>

> Next week I am flying to London — that is already arranged. When I arrive, I will call you and we will meet. I am going to visit the British Museum — I have already bought the ticket. Maybe I will see a show in the West End too. On Saturday I am having dinner with my cousin — we booked the restaurant last week. I think the trip will be great!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What is already arranged? → a) Flying to London · b) Buying a house · c) Cancelling
2. What will happen on arrival? → a) Call and meet · b) Sleep forever · c) Move alone
3. Why visit the museum with going to? → a) Ticket already bought · b) No plan · c) Past habit
4. What on Saturday? → a) Dinner with cousin (booked) · b) Exam · c) Moving
5. Overall feeling about the trip? → a) Will be great · b) Cancelled · c) Angry

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Flying to London** · 2. **a) Call and meet** · 3. **a) Ticket already bought** · 4. **a) Dinner with cousin (booked)** · 5. **a) Will be great**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The text contrasts ____. → a) PC / going to / will · b) only Past Simple · c) only articles
7. *Maybe* appears with ____. → a) will · b) Past Perfect · c) *ago*
8. Restaurant booking shows ____. → a) fixed arrangement · b) Past Perfect only · c) *ago* only
9. Arranged flight uses ____. → a) Present Continuous · b) only *won't* · c) *must have*
10. Matches unit 25: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Module 6

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) PC / going to / will** · 7. **a) will** · 8. **a) fixed arrangement** · 9. **a) Present Continuous** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Choose form by ____. → a) meaning (arranged/plan/promise) · b) random · c) word length
12. Mixing three futures in one story is ____. → a) natural · b) forbidden · c) only C2
13. *I will take an umbrella* can be ____. → a) a decision/response · b) Past Continuous · c) *ago*
14. *I am going to visit* shows ____. → a) intention/plan · b) only evidence rain · c) Past Perfect
15. Reading helps you ____. → a) practise choosing forms · b) skip meaning · c) avoid English

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) meaning (arranged/plan/promise)** · 12. **a) natural** · 13. **a) a decision/response** · 14. **a) intention/plan** · 15. **a) practise choosing forms**

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Present Continuous** | plan fijado / agenda (*I'm flying…*) |
| **Going to** | intención / evidencia (*I'm going to visit… / It's going to rain*) |
| **Will** | promesa, oferta, decisión ahora, predicción general |
| **Truco** | arreglado → PC · plan/evidencia → going to · promesa/espontáneo → will |
