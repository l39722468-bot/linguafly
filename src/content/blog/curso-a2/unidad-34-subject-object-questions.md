En la [Unidad 33](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-33-gerunds-infinitives) practicaste gerundios e infinitivos. En la **Unidad 34 del curso A2** (*Subject & Object Questions*) aprendes a preguntar por el **sujeto** o por el **objeto** — y a no mezclar las dos estructuras.

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 34 — Subject & Object Questions](/curso-a2/unit-34)  
> **Antes:** [U33 — Gerundios e infinitivos](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-33-gerunds-infinitives)

---

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 34 (A2)

- Hacer **subject questions** (*Who called you?*) **sin** *do/does/did*.
- Hacer **object questions** (*Who did you call?*) **con** *do/does/did*.
- Usar **who, what, which** en ambos tipos.
- Entender entrevistas y cuestionarios simples.
- Evitar el error típico: ~~Who did call you?~~ / ~~Who you saw?~~

## 1. Idea clave: ¿preguntas por quién/qué hace, o por quién/qué recibe?

Frase base: **Tom called Ana.**

| Preguntas por… | Pregunta | Respuesta |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sujeto** (quién llamó) | **Who called** Ana? | Tom. |
| **Objeto** (a quién llamó) | **Who did** Tom **call**? | Ana. |

Misma idea con *what*:

| | Pregunta | Respuesta |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Sujeto | **What happened?** | An accident. |
| Objeto | **What did** you **see**? | An accident. |

## 2. Subject questions (sin *do/does/did*)

Preguntas por el **sujeto**. El orden es como una frase afirmativa, pero empieza con *Who / What / Which…*

| Tiempo | Estructura | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Present Simple | Who/What + verbo (-s si 3ª) | **Who lives** here? **What causes** this? |
| Past Simple | Who/What + pasado | **Who saw** you? **What happened?** |
| Present Continuous | Who/What + is/are + -ing | **Who is calling**? |
| with *be* | Who/What + am/is/are/was/were | **Who is** the teacher? |

> **Who works** in this office?  
> **What makes** you happy?  
> **Which train** goes to London?

**Importante:** no pongas *do/does/did* delante del verbo principal.

~~Who did call you?~~ → **Who called you?**  
~~Who does live here?~~ → **Who lives here?**

## 3. Object questions (con *do/does/did*)

Preguntas por el **objeto** (o un complemento). Aquí sí usas auxiliar + infinitivo:

| Tiempo | Estructura | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Present | Who/What + do/does + sujeto + infinitivo | **Who do** you **know**? **What does** she **want**? |
| Past | Who/What + did + sujeto + infinitivo | **Who did** you **see**? **What did** they **buy**? |

> **Who did** you **call** last night?  
> **What do** you **need**?  
> **Which film did** you **watch**?

## 4. Truco rápido para elegir

1. Piensa en la **respuesta**.  
2. Si la respuesta **hace** la acción → **subject question** (sin *did*).  
3. Si la respuesta **recibe** la acción / es el objeto → **object question** (con *did*).

| Situación | Pregunta |
| :--- | :--- |
| Alguien te llamó; quieres saber quién | **Who called you?** |
| Tú llamaste a alguien; quieres saber a quién | **Who did you call?** |
| Pasó algo; quieres el suceso | **What happened?** |
| Tú viste algo; quieres el objeto | **What did you see?** |

---

## 5. *Who, what, which*

| Palabra | Uso típico | Subject | Object |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Who** | personas | Who phoned you? | Who did you phone? |
| **What** | cosas / hechos | What happened? | What did you say? |
| **Which** | elección (de un grupo) | Which bus stops here? | Which bus did you take? |

> **Which** students **passed** the exam? *(subject)*  
> **Which** students **did** you **help**? *(object)*

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-34/which-bus.mp3" title="🔊 Which bus did you take?"></audio>

## 6. Present Simple: *does* solo en object questions

Con *he/she/it* en **object questions**:

> **What does** she **want**?  
> **Who does** Tom **like**?

En **subject questions**, la *-s* va en el verbo principal:

> **Who wants** coffee?  
> **What makes** that noise?

## 7. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura: un mensaje

Someone called me yesterday. **Who called** me? It was Ana. She asked about the party. **What did** she **want**? She wanted my address. **Who did** I **tell**? I told my brother. **What happened** later? We went to the café together.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-34/reading-message.mp3" title="🔊 Someone called me yesterday…"></audio>

### Diálogo: entrevista corta

**A:** **Who lives** in that flat?  
**B:** My cousin.  
**A:** **Who did** you **visit** last weekend?  
**B:** My cousin! **What happened** there?  
**A:** Nothing special — but **what did** you **eat**?  
**B:** Homemade pizza. **Who made** it? My cousin's partner.

## 8. Errores frecuentes

1. **Subject + did:** ~~Who did call you?~~ → **Who called you?**  
2. **Object sin auxiliar:** ~~Who you saw?~~ → **Who did you see?**  
3. **Doble pasado:** ~~Who did you saw?~~ → **Who did you see?**  
4. ***-s* + does juntos mal:** ~~Who does wants coffee?~~ → **Who wants coffee?** / **What does she want?**  
5. **Confundir sentido:** *Who saw you?* ≠ *Who did you see?*

## 9. Ejercicios (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — ¿Subject (S) u Object (O)?

1. Who called you? ___  
2. Who did you call? ___  
3. What happened? ___  
4. What did you buy? ___  
5. Who lives here? ___  
6. Who do you live with? ___

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. S  
2. O  
3. S  
4. O  
5. S  
6. O  

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa (*did* o nada + forma verbal)

1. Who ___ (call) you yesterday?  
2. Who ___ you ___ (call) yesterday?  
3. What ___ (happen) at school?  
4. What ___ you ___ (see)?  
5. Who ___ (want) tea?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. called  
2. did / call  
3. happened  
4. did / see  
5. wants  

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Reescribe como pregunta correcta

Respuesta → pregunta:

1. Tom called me. → Who _______________?  
2. I called Tom. → Who _______________?  
3. An accident happened. → What _______________?  
4. I saw an accident. → What _______________?  
5. Maria lives here. → Who _______________?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Who called you?  
2. Who did you call?  
3. What happened?  
4. What did you see?  
5. Who lives here?  

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Elige la opción correcta

1. ___ you yesterday evening?  
   a) Who called  b) Who did call  c) Who you called  
2. ___ at the party?  
   a) Who you see  b) Who did you see  c) Who saw you did  
3. ___ this noise?  
   a) What does make  b) What makes  c) What did makes  
4. ___ she want?  
   a) What  b) What does  c) What do  
5. ___ the blue bus or the red one?  
   a) Which did you take  b) Which you took  c) Which took you did  

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. a  
2. b  
3. b  
4. b  
5. a  

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrige el error

1. Who did call you?  
2. Who you saw at the cinema?  
3. What did happened?  
4. Who does lives here?  
5. What she want?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Who called you?  
2. Who did you see at the cinema?  
3. What happened?  
4. Who lives here?  
5. What does she want?  

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Traduce al inglés

1. ¿Quién te llamó?  
2. ¿A quién llamaste?  
3. ¿Qué pasó?  
4. ¿Qué compraste?  
5. ¿Quién vive aquí?  
6. ¿Qué quiere ella?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Who called you?  
2. Who did you call?  
3. What happened?  
4. What did you buy?  
5. Who lives here?  
6. What does she want?  

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Completa el diálogo

**A:** ___ (Who / phone) you this morning?  
**B:** My boss.  
**A:** ___ (What / he / want)?  
**B:** He wanted the report.  
**A:** ___ (Who / you / call) after that?  
**B:** Ana. ___ (What / happen) then? We finished the report together.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

Who phoned  
What did he want  
Who did you call  
What happened  

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — *Which*

1. ___ train goes to Oxford? *(subject)*  
2. ___ train did you take? *(object)*  
3. ___ students passed? *(subject)*  
4. ___ students did you help? *(object)*

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Which  
2. Which  
3. Which  
4. Which  

*(La diferencia está en el resto de la pregunta: goes vs did you take; passed vs did you help.)*

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Empareja

1. Who broke the window?  
2. Who did you invite?  
3. What caused the delay?  
4. What did the delay cause?  

a) The snow.  
b) Many cancellations.  
c) Tom (he broke it).  
d) My classmates (I invited them).

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1 → c  
2 → d  
3 → a  
4 → b  

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu entrevista (8–10 preguntas)

Escribe un mini cuestionario con:

- 3 **subject questions**  
- 3 **object questions**  
- al menos un *what* y un *which*

**Modelo:** el diálogo de la sección 7.

## 10. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Tabla sujeto vs objeto.  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–6.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha lectura y diálogo; marca S/O.  
4. **5–8 min** — Escribe el cuestionario (Ejercicio 10) y léelo en voz alta.

### Checklist

- [ ] Sé qué es subject vs object question  
- [ ] No uso *did* en *Who called you?*  
- [ ] Uso *did* + infinitivo en *Who did you call?*  
- [ ] Domino *who / what / which* en ambos tipos  
- [ ] Puedo corregir ~~Who did call you?~~ y ~~Who you saw?~~

## 11. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Practica **pares**: *Who saw you?* / *Who did you see?*  
2. Mira siempre la **respuesta** para elegir la estructura.  
3. Practica en la [Unidad 34 del curso A2](/curso-a2/unit-34).

La **Unidad 35** revisa **some, any, much, many** (y cantidades A2).
