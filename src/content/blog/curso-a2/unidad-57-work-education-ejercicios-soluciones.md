## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** work as / work in; study at; have to + infinitivo; deadline / meeting / exam

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I work ___ a designer. → *as / like / in*

2. I work ___ an office. → *in / as / at*

3. I study ___ university. → *at / in / on*

4. I ___ to answer emails today. → *have / has / having*

5. Finish before the ___. → *deadline / dead line / deadlined*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **as** · 2. **in** · 3. **at** · 4. **have** · 5. **deadline**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *Work as* = profesión: ____. → a) correcto · b) work like · c) work at a designer

7. *Study at university* = ____. → a) correcto · b) study in university always · c) study to university

8. *Have to* = ____. → a) obligación · b) opinión · c) only Past

9. *Deadline* = ____. → a) fecha límite · b) reunión · c) examen only

10. Unit 57 follows ____. → a) Technology U56 · b) Module 5 only · c) skip U58

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) correcto** · 7. **a) correcto** · 8. **a) obligación** · 9. **a) fecha límite** · 10. **a) Technology U56**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I work like a nurse.*

12. *I study in university.*

13. *I have to finishing today.*

14. *She works as an office.*

15. *He has to takes three exams.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I work as a nurse.
12. I study at university.
13. I have to finish today.
14. She works in an office.
15. He has to take three exams.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** work as, work in, study at, have to, deadline, meeting, exam, office, graduation

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *receptionist* ≈ ____ → *recepcionista · diseñador · biología*

2. *meeting* ≈ ____ → *reunión · examen · graduación*

3. *exam* ≈ ____ → *examen · reunión · oficina*

4. *graduation* ≈ ____ → *graduación · fecha límite · materia*

5. *subject* ≈ ____ → *asignatura/materia · empresa · laboratorio*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **recepcionista** · 2. **reunión** · 3. **examen** · 4. **graduación** · 5. **asignatura/materia**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. Mia works ___ a receptionist. → *as / in / at*

7. Leo's favourite ___ is biology. → *subject / subjects / subjecting*

8. She has many ___. → *meetings / meeting / meet*

9. He has to take three ___. → *exams / exam / examing*

10. After ___, work in a lab. → *graduation / graduate / graduated*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **as** · 7. **subject** · 8. **meetings** · 9. **exams** · 10. **graduation**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

## Lección 3 — Reading: Two routines

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Mia works as a ___. → a) receptionist · b) designer · c) biologist only

2. She works in a small ___. → a) office · b) stadium · c) pharmacy

3. Has to answer emails before ___. → a) deadline · b) sunny · c) gate

4. Leo studies at ___. → a) university · b) checkout · c) library only tourist

5. Favourite subject is ___. → a) biology · b) drama · c) refund

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) receptionist** · 2. **a) office** · 3. **a) deadline** · 4. **a) university** · 5. **a) biology**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Has to take three ___ this month. → a) exams · b) meetings only · c) receipts

7. After graduation → work in a ___. → a) lab · b) café only · c) gate

8. *Works as* = ____. → a) profesión · b) lugar · c) opinion

9. *Has many meetings* = ____. → a) trabajo · b) estudio only · c) weather

10. Matches unit 57: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) exams** · 7. **a) lab** · 8. **a) profesión** · 9. **a) trabajo** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Work as a receptionist* = ____. → a) work as + job · b) work in job · c) study at

12. *Study at university* = ____. → a) estudiar en · b) trabajar como · c) recycle

13. *Has to answer emails* = ____. → a) have to · b) should only opinion · c) I think

14. Text uses ____. → a) work & education · b) only linking · c) only health

15. Work writing uses ____. → a) work as / study at / have to · b) only articles · c) only Shall we

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) work as + job** · 12. **a) estudiar en** · 13. **a) have to** · 14. **a) work & education** · 15. **a) work as / study at / have to**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: At lunch

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-57/listening-work.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: At lunch"></audio>

> Nora: What do you do?
> Pablo: I work as a designer. I work in an advertising company.
> Nora: Do you have to go to many meetings?
> Pablo: Yes — almost every day. And you?
> Nora: I study at university. I have an exam on Monday.
> Pablo: Good luck!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Pablo works as a ___. → a) designer · b) receptionist · c) biologist

2. Works in ___ company. → a) advertising · b) weather · c) museum

3. Has to go to many ___. → a) meetings · b) exams only · c) receipts

4. Nora studies at ___. → a) university · b) lab · c) checkout

5. Exam on ___. → a) Monday · b) Friday always · c) never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) designer** · 2. **a) advertising** · 3. **a) meetings** · 4. **a) university** · 5. **a) Monday**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

7. *Almost every day* meetings = ____. → a) frecuencia trabajo · b) only study · c) only weather

8. *Good luck!* for ____. → a) exam · b) shopping · c) pollution

9. Dialogue uses ____. → a) work & education · b) only tech · c) only places

10. Unit 57 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) preguntar trabajo** · 7. **a) frecuencia trabajo** · 8. **a) exam** · 9. **a) work & education** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *I work as a designer* → a) work as · b) work like · c) study at

12. *Do you have to go to many meetings?* → a) have to · b) have to going · c) should to

13. *I study at university* → a) study at · b) study in always · c) work as

14. Shadow and note ____. → a) work/education forms · b) only some · c) only Who

15. U57 next unit is ____. → a) Places U58 · b) Module 5 only · c) U56 skip

5. The ___ is on Friday. → *deadline / dead line / deadlined*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **as** · 2. **in** · 3. **at** · 4. **have** · 5. **deadline**
