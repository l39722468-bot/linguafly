## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** have a + symptom; feel sick/tired; should / shouldn't; hurts / pain

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I have ___ headache. → *a / the / —*

2. I feel ___. *(mareado)* → *sick / sickness / sickly*

3. You ___ rest. → *should / should to / shoulds*

4. My throat ___. → *hurts / hurt / hurting*

5. You ___ go to work with a fever. *(no)* → *shouldn't / should / don't should*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a** · 2. **sick** · 3. **should** · 4. **hurts** · 5. **shouldn't**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *I have a headache* (no *I have headache*): ____. → a) correcto · b) I have headache · c) I have the headache only

7. *You should rest* (no *should to*): ____. → a) correcto · b) You should to rest · c) You should resting

8. *Feel sick* = ____. → a) mareado/náuseas · b) solo cansado formal · c) weather

9. *What's the matter?* = ____. → a) ¿Qué te pasa? · b) ¿Cómo es? · c) precio

10. Unit 47 follows ____. → a) Shopping U46 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U48

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) correcto** · 7. **a) correcto** · 8. **a) mareado/náuseas** · 9. **a) ¿Qué te pasa?** · 10. **a) Shopping U46**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I have headache.*

12. *You should to rest.*

13. *I feel me tired.*

14. *My throat pain.*

15. *What's the matter with you health?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I have **a** headache.
12. You should rest.
13. I feel tired.
14. My throat **hurts**. / I've got a sore throat.
15. What's the matter? / What's wrong?

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** headache, fever, cold, sore throat, medicine, pharmacy, doctor

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *headache* ≈ ____ → *dolor de cabeza · fiebre · farmacia*

2. *fever* ≈ ____ → *fiebre · resfriado · medicina*

3. *pharmacy* ≈ ____ → *farmacia · médico · garganta*

4. *sore throat* ≈ ____ → *dolor de garganta · estómago · pierna*

5. *medicine* ≈ ____ → *medicamento · temperatura · vecino*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **dolor de cabeza** · 2. **fiebre** · 3. **farmacia** · 4. **dolor de garganta** · 5. **medicamento**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. I have a ___. *(resfriado)* → *cold / cool / cloudy*

7. Go to the ___. → *pharmacy / checkout / platform*

8. See a ___ if it's worse. → *doctor / drama / degree*

9. Take this ___ every 8 hours. → *medicine / museum / movie*

10. I've got a pain in my ___. → *back / bag / bakery*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **cold** · 7. **pharmacy** · 8. **doctor** · 9. **medicine** · 10. **back**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Have a + symptom* needs ____. → a) a (often) · b) never article · c) only the

12. *Shouldn't* with fever: ____. → a) You shouldn't go running · b) You should to run · c) listen music

13. Label: max ___ tablets a day. → a) three · b) eight · c) thirty

14. Patient also feels ____. → a) tired · b) sunny · c) cheaper

15. Unit 47 next is ____. → a) Clothes U48 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U48

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) a (often)** · 12. **a) You shouldn't go running** · 13. **a) three** · 14. **a) tired** · 15. **a) Clothes U48**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Medicine label

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-47/reading-health.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Medicine label"></audio>

> This **medicine** is for a **cold** or a **sore throat**. Take one tablet every 8 hours with water. **Don't** take more than three tablets a day. If you have a high **fever**, see a **doctor**. Keep out of reach of children. If you feel worse, stop and ask at the **pharmacy**.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Medicine for cold or ____. → a) sore throat · b) checkout · c) platform

2. Take one tablet every ___ hours. → a) 8 · b) 14 · c) 80

3. Don't take more than ___ a day. → a) three · b) ten · c) zero

4. High fever → see a ____. → a) doctor · b) colleague · c) concert

5. Feel worse → ask at the ____. → a) pharmacy · b) museum · c) gate

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) sore throat** · 2. **a) 8** · 3. **a) three** · 4. **a) doctor** · 5. **a) pharmacy**

</details>

9. *Every 8 hours* = ____. → a) dosis / frecuencia · b) only Past · c) only will

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

## Lección 4 — Listening: At the pharmacy

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-47/listening-health.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: At the pharmacy"></audio>

> **Pharmacist:** Hello. How can I help you?
> **Patient:** I **have a headache** and a **sore throat**.
> **Pharmacist:** Do you have a **fever**?
> **Patient:** A little. I also **feel tired**.
> **Pharmacist:** You **should** rest and drink water. Take this **medicine** every 8 hours.
> **Patient:** Thanks. **Should** I see a **doctor**?
> **Pharmacist:** If you feel **worse** tomorrow, yes.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Patient has headache and ____. → a) sore throat · b) checkout · c) platform

2. Has a little ____. → a) fever · b) comedy · c) gate

3. Also feels ____. → a) tired · b) sunny · c) cheaper

4. Should rest and drink ____. → a) water · b) tickets · c) dramas

5. Medicine every ___ hours. → a) 8 · b) 80 · c) 1 only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) sore throat** · 2. **a) fever** · 3. **a) tired** · 4. **a) water** · 5. **a) 8**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. See doctor if feel ___ tomorrow. → a) worse · b) better always · c) sunny

7. Pharmacist says you ___ rest. → a) should · b) should to · c) listens

8. *Have a headache* = ____. → a) síntoma con artículo · b) weather · c) How much

9. *Should I see a doctor?* = ____. → a) pedir consejo · b) precio · c) arrive at

10. Unit 47 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) worse** · 7. **a) should** · 8. **a) síntoma con artículo** · 9. **a) pedir consejo** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *You should rest* → a) should + infinitive · b) should to · c) prefer than

12. *Feel tired* → a) feel + adj · b) feel me · c) have tired

13. *Take this medicine* → a) instrucción · b) listen to · c) arrive in

14. Shadow and note ____. → a) have a / should / pharmacy · b) only some · c) only Who

15. U47 next unit is ____. → a) Clothes U48 · b) Module 6 only · c) U46 skip

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) should + infinitive** · 12. **a) feel + adj** · 13. **a) instrucción** · 14. **a) have a / should / pharmacy** · 15. **a) Clothes U48**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I have ___ cold. → *a / the / —*

2. I feel ___. → *sick / sickness / sickly*

3. You ___ drink water. → *should / should to / shoulds*

4. My back ___. → *hurts / hurt / hurting*

5. Go to the ___. → *pharmacy / checkout / platform*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a** · 2. **sick** · 3. **should** · 4. **hurts** · 5. **pharmacy**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. a / have / I / headache

7. should / You / rest

8. the / Go / to / pharmacy

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I have a headache.
7. You should rest.
8. Go to the pharmacy.

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Una frase con *have a…* (síntoma).

10. Una frase con *feel…*.

11. Una frase con *should* o *shouldn't*.

12. Una frase con *pharmacy* o *doctor*.

13. Una frase con *hurts* o *pain*.

14. Corrige: *You should to rest.*

15. Describe un problema de salud y da consejos (8–10 líneas).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. I have a headache. / I have a cold.
10. I feel sick. / I feel tired.
11. You should rest. / You shouldn't go to work.
12. Go to the pharmacy. / You should see a doctor.
13. My throat hurts. / I've got a pain in my back.
14. You should rest.
15. I have a headache and a sore throat. I feel tired. My throat hurts. You should rest and drink water. Take medicine every 8 hours. You shouldn't go to work. If you feel worse, go to the pharmacy or see a doctor.

</details>
