## 3. Phrasal verbs tecnológicos

| Phrasal verb | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **turn on / turn off** | encender / apagar | **Turn on** your laptop. |
| **log in / log out** | iniciar / cerrar sesión | **Log in** with your password. |

## 6. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura — How to join the class online

Open the **website** and **log in** with your email and **password**. Then **download** the class **app**. Turn on **WiFi** and click the **link** in your email. If you don't **receive** the message, check your spam folder. Please **upload** your homework before Friday.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-56/reading-online.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: How to join the class online"></audio>

### Diálogo — Tech problem

**Sam:** I can't **log in**.  
**Alex:** Did you **turn on** the **WiFi**?  
**Sam:** Yes. I also **downloaded** the app.  
**Alex:** Try another **password**. Or **send** me a screenshot.  
**Sam:** OK. Can you **look up** the help page?  
**Alex:** Sure — I'll send you the **link**.

## 7. Errores frecuentes

1. ~~download an upload~~ → **download** / **upload** (direcciones opuestas)  
2. ~~open the computer~~ *(encender)* → **turn on** the computer  
3. ~~WiFi's password~~ → **the WiFi password** / **the password for the WiFi**  
4. ~~send me to an email~~ → **send me an email** / **send an email to me**  
5. ~~I am online go~~ → **I go online** / **I'm online**  
6. ~~click the website~~ → **open** the website / **go to** the website / **click the link**

2. upload  
3. password  
4. WiFi  
5. screen

b) wireless internet  
c) get a file from the internet  
d) put a file onto the internet  
e) the display on a phone or laptop
