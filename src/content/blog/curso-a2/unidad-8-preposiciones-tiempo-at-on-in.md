En la [Unidad 7](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-7-adverbios-modo-ly) describiste **cómo** se hace algo. En la **Unidad 8 del curso A2** (*Prepositions of Time*) dices **cuándo** ocurre: ***at*** (hora), ***on*** (día/fecha), ***in*** (mes, año, parte del día).

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 8 — Preposiciones de tiempo](/curso-a2/unit-8)  
> **Antes:** [U7 — Adverbios de modo](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-7-adverbios-modo-ly)

---

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 8 (A2)

- Usar **at** con horas y momentos (*at 7 o'clock*, *at noon*, *at night*).
- Usar **on** con días y fechas (*on Monday*, *on the 15th of March*).
- Usar **in** con meses, años, estaciones y partes del día (*in August*, *in 1990*, *in the morning*).
- Combinarlas en horarios reales: *On Friday at 3 pm* / *at 8 o'clock in the morning*.
- Evitar errores típicos: *on 7 o'clock*, *in Monday*, *at August*.

## 1. La regla en una frase

Piensa en el **tamaño** del tiempo:

| Preposición | Tipo de tiempo | Ejemplos |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **at** | punto concreto (hora / momento) | *at 7 o'clock*, *at noon*, *at midnight*, *at night* |
| **on** | día / fecha | *on Monday*, *on Saturday*, *on the 15th of March* |
| **in** | periodo más amplio | *in August*, *in 1990*, *in summer*, *in the morning* |

![Escala de tiempo at on in](/blog/curso-a2/unit-8/a2-unit8-time-scale.png)

Truco rápido: **hora → at · día → on · mes/año/parte del día → in**.

---

## 2. *At*: horas y momentos

Usa **at** con:

- horas: *at 6 o'clock*, *at 7 am*, *at 3 pm*
- *noon* / *midnight*: *at noon*, *at midnight*
- *night*: *at night*
- fin de semana (British): *at the weekend*
- expresiones fijas: *at Christmas*, *at the moment* (cuando las veas en el curso)

> I get up **at** 7 o'clock every morning.  
> The film starts **at** midnight.  
> I have lunch **at** noon.  
> I sleep **at** night.  
> **At** the weekend I relax.

| at 7 o'clock | *on 7 o'clock* / *in 7 o'clock* |

---

## 3. *On*: días y fechas

Usa **on** con:

- días de la semana: *on Monday*, *on Friday*
- partes concretas de un día: *on Saturday morning*, *on Monday evening*
- fechas: *on the 15th of March*, *on 12 May*
- *weekdays* / *weekends* (según variedad): *on weekdays*; en AmE a menudo *on the weekend*

> I have a meeting **on** Monday.  
> My birthday is **on** the 15th of March.  
> **On** weekdays I start work at 9 o'clock.  
> **On** Saturday I sleep until 10 o'clock.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-8/on-monday.mp3" title="🔊 I have a meeting on Monday"></audio>

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-8/on-fifteenth.mp3" title="🔊 My birthday is on the 15th of March"></audio>

En español dices “el lunes”; en inglés no traduzcas *the* con el día: **on Monday** (no *in the Monday*).

---

## 4. *In*: meses, años, estaciones y partes del día

Usa **in** con:

- meses: *in January*, *in August*, *in December*
- años: *in 1990*, *in 2024*
- estaciones: *in summer*, *in winter*
- partes del día: *in the morning*, *in the afternoon*, *in the evening*

> We go on holiday **in** August.  
> I was born **in** 1990.  
> We go to the beach **in** summer.  
> We meet **in** the morning.  
> **In** the afternoon I have meetings.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-8/in-summer.mp3" title="🔊 We go to the beach in summer"></audio>

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-8/in-the-morning.mp3" title="🔊 We meet in the morning"></audio>

Excepción importante: **at night** (no *in the night* como forma básica A2). El resto de partes del día va con **in the…**.

| Parte del día | Preposición |
| :--- | :--- |
| morning | **in** the morning |
| afternoon | **in** the afternoon |
| evening | **in** the evening |
| night | **at** night |

---

## 5. Combinar *at*, *on* e *in*

En horarios reales suelen aparecer **dos** preposiciones:

> I have breakfast **at** 8 o'clock **in** the morning.  
> The meeting is **on** Friday **at** 3 pm.  
> **On** Monday and Wednesday I go to work **at** 8 o'clock **in** the morning.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-8/breakfast-morning.mp3" title="🔊 I have breakfast at 8 o'clock in the morning"></audio>

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-8/meeting-friday.mp3" title="🔊 The meeting is on Friday at 3 pm"></audio>

Orden natural: **día (*on*) → hora (*at*) → periodo (*in*)** cuando hace falta.

## 6. Texto modelo: la semana de Tom

> My name is Tom and I have a busy week. On Monday and Wednesday I go to work at 8 o'clock in the morning. On Tuesday I have a meeting at noon. On Friday I finish early at 3 pm and I go to the gym. At the weekend I relax. On Saturday morning I sleep until 10 o'clock. In the afternoon I meet my friends. In summer I go on holiday in August.

## 7. Listening: un día típico

> Hi! I want to tell you about my typical day. I wake up at 6 o'clock in the morning. I have breakfast at 7 am. On weekdays I start work at 9 o'clock. I have lunch at noon. In the afternoon I have meetings. On Saturday I sleep until 10 o'clock and I meet my friends in the evening. In December I go on holiday for two weeks.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-8/dialogue-typical-day.mp3" title="🔊 Hi! I want to tell you about my typical day…"></audio>

Preguntas rápidas (respóndete en voz alta):

1. ¿A qué hora se despierta? → *at 6 o'clock in the morning*  
2. ¿Cuándo empieza a trabajar? → *On weekdays… at 9 o'clock*  
3. ¿Cuándo va de vacaciones? → *In December*

## 9. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I have dinner on 7 o'clock. | I have dinner **at** 7 o'clock. | hora → *at* |
| I wake up in 6 o'clock. | I wake up **at** 6 o'clock. | hora → *at* |
| The meeting is in Monday. | The meeting is **on** Monday. | día → *on* |
| We go on holiday at August. | We go on holiday **in** August. | mes → *in* |
| I was born on 1990. | I was born **in** 1990. | año → *in* |
| We meet at the morning. | We meet **in** the morning. | parte del día → *in the…* |
| I sleep in night. | I sleep **at** night. | excepción: *at night* |

## 10. Mini prácticas orales

4. Cuenta tu mañana: *I wake up at… in the morning. On weekdays I…*

## 11. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige *at / on / in*

1. I get up ____ 7 o'clock every morning.  
2. I have a meeting ____ Monday.  
3. We go on holiday ____ August.  
4. The film starts ____ midnight.  
5. My birthday is ____ the 15th of March.  
6. I was born ____ 1990.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **at** · 2. **on** · 3. **in** · 4. **at** · 5. **on** · 6. **in**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Partes del día y momentos

1. We meet ____ the morning.  
2. I have lunch ____ noon.  
3. I sleep ____ night.  
4. In the afternoon I have meetings. *(¿correcto?)*  
5. ____ the weekend I relax. (British)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **in** · 2. **at** · 3. **at** · 4. **Sí** (*in the afternoon*) · 5. **At**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Empareja

1. 7 o'clock  
2. Monday  
3. August  
4. noon  
5. the 15th of March  
6. summer  
7. the morning  
8. night  

a) on · b) at · c) in · d) at · e) on · f) in · g) in · h) at  

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b/d/h (*at*)** · 2 → **a/e (*on*)** · 3 → **c/f/g (*in*)** · 4 → **at** · 5 → **on** · 6 → **in** · 7 → **in** · 8 → **at**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Corrige

1. *I have dinner on 7 o'clock.*  
2. *The meeting is in Friday.*  
3. *We go on holiday at August.*  
4. *I was born on 1990.*  
5. *We meet at the morning.*  
6. *I sleep in night.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I have dinner **at** 7 o'clock.  
2. The meeting is **on** Friday.  
3. We go on holiday **in** August.  
4. I was born **in** 1990.  
5. We meet **in** the morning.  
6. I sleep **at** night.
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Elige la correcta

1. ____  
   a) I wake up on 6 o'clock. · b) I wake up at 6 o'clock. · c) I wake up in 6 o'clock.  
2. ____  
   a) I have a meeting on Monday. · b) I have a meeting in Monday. · c) I have a meeting at Monday.  
3. ____  
   a) We go on holiday at August. · b) We go on holiday on August. · c) We go on holiday in August.  
4. What preposition do we use for days? ____  
   a) at · b) on · c) in  
5. We use *in* for: ____  
   a) months, years and seasons · b) only hours · c) only days

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b** · 2. **a** · 3. **c** · 4. **b** · 5. **a**
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Ordena

2. meeting / The / is / on / Friday / at / 3 / pm  
3. go / We / to / the / beach / in / summer

### Ejercicio 10 — Escribe tu semana

Escribe 8–12 frases. Incluye:

- 3 × **at** (hora / noon / night / weekend)  
- 3 × **on** (días o fechas)  
- 3 × **in** (mes, año, parte del día o estación)  
- al menos una combinación: *on… at…* o *at… in the…*

**Modelo:** sección 6 (Tom) o sección 7 (typical day).

## 12. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Repasa la tabla *at / on / in* y la excepción *at night*.  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–7.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha a Tom y el día típico; repite las preposiciones en voz alta.  
4. **5–8 min** — Escribe y graba tu párrafo de la semana.

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *at* con horas, *noon*, *midnight*, *night*  
- [ ] Uso *on* con días y fechas  
- [ ] Uso *in* con meses, años, estaciones y *the morning/afternoon/evening*  
- [ ] Combino *on Friday at 3 pm* / *at 8 in the morning*  
- [ ] Evito *on 7 o'clock* e *in Monday*

## 13. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Pregunta siempre: ¿es **hora**, **día** o **periodo**?  
2. Lista de 12: *7 o'clock, Monday, August, noon, night, morning, 1990, summer, Friday, midnight, weekend, December* → di la preposición.  
3. Grábate: *I wake up at… On Monday I… In summer I…*  
4. Practica en la [Unidad 8 del curso A2](/curso-a2/unit-8).

La **Unidad 9** entra en **preposiciones de lugar y movimiento**: *in, on, at, into, out of, through…*

## Resumen rápido

| Caso | Preposición |
| :--- | :--- |
| Hora / noon / midnight / night | **at** |
| Día / fecha | **on** |
| Mes / año / estación / morning–evening | **in** |
| Fin de semana (BrE) | **at** the weekend |
| Combinación típica | **on** Friday **at** 3 pm |
