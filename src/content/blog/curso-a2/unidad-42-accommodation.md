## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 42 (A2)

- Hablar de **hotel / hostel / apartment**.
- Usar **book**, **check in**, **check out**.
- Pedir **single / double room**.
- Preguntar servicios: **WiFi**, **breakfast included**, **room service**.
- Hacer un diálogo de **reserva** o **recepción**.

## 4. Diálogos modelo

### Reservar (por teléfono / web)

> **Guest:** Hello. I'd like to **book** a room for Friday and Saturday.  
> **Hotel:** Of course. **Single** or **double**?  
> **Guest:** A **double room**, please. Is **breakfast included**?  
> **Hotel:** Yes. May I have your name?

### Check-in

> **Guest:** Good evening. We have a **reservation** under Garcia.  
> **Reception:** Yes — a double room for two nights. May I see your ID?  
> **Guest:** Here you are. What time is **check-out**?  
> **Reception:** 11 a.m. Your room is 214. Here's your **key card**.

## 6. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura — At the Blue Hotel

Ana and Leo **book** a **double room** at the Blue Hotel. **Breakfast is included**. They **check in** at 3 p.m. The **reception** gives them a **key card** for room 305. **There is** free **WiFi** and **room service** until 11 p.m. The next morning they have breakfast downstairs. They **check out** at 10:30 and leave their bags at reception until their train.

### Diálogo — Booking

**Reception:** City Hotel, good morning.  
**Guest:** Hi. I'd like to **book** a room for next weekend.  
**Reception:** How many nights?  
**Guest:** Two. Do you have a **single room**?  
**Reception:** Yes. **Breakfast included**. 80 euros per night.  
**Guest:** Perfect. Is **there** a lift?  
**Reception:** Yes. And free **WiFi**.

## 7. Errores frecuentes

1. ~~I want book a room.~~ → **I'd like to book** a room.  
2. ~~check-in me~~ → **I'd like to check in.**  
3. ~~a individual room~~ → **a single room**  
4. ~~Is there WiFi?~~ ✅ (correcto) / ~~Have WiFi?~~ → **Do you have WiFi?** / **Is there WiFi?**  
5. ~~breakfast include~~ → **breakfast included**  
6. ~~What hour is check out?~~ → **What time is check-out?**

## 8. Ejercicios (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Completa

1. I'd like to ___ a double room.  
2. Guests ___ in at reception.  
3. You ___ out before 11.  
4. Is breakfast ___ ?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. book  
2. check  
3. check / must check  
4. included  

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Single o double

1. One person → ___ room  
2. Two people, one big bed → ___ room  
3. Two single beds → ___ room

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. single  
2. double  
3. twin  

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — *Is there…?* / *Do you have…?*

1. ___ WiFi in the rooms?  
2. ___ a restaurant in the hotel?  
3. ___ any free rooms tonight?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Is there / Do you have  
2. Is there / Do you have  
3. Do you have / Are there  

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrige el error

1. I like to book a room. *(reserva ahora)*  
2. We want check in.  
3. A individual room, please.  
4. Breakfast is include.  
5. What hour is check-out?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. I'd like to book a room.  
2. We want to check in. / We'd like to check in.  
3. A single room, please.  
4. Breakfast is included.  
5. What time is check-out?  

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Traduce al inglés

1. Querría reservar una habitación doble.  
2. ¿El desayuno está incluido?  
3. ¿Hay WiFi?  
4. Querríamos hacer el check-in.  
5. ¿A qué hora es el check-out?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. I'd like to book a double room.  
2. Is breakfast included?  
3. Is there WiFi? / Do you have WiFi?  
4. We'd like to check in.  
5. What time is check-out?  

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Empareja

1. reception  
2. key card  
3. room service  
4. hostel  
5. reservation  

a) reserva  
b) recepción  
c) comida a la habitación  
d) tarjeta de acceso  
e) alojamiento más barato / compartido

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1 → b  
2 → d  
3 → c  
4 → e  
5 → a  

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Completa el diálogo

**A:** Good evening. We have a ___ under Lopez.  
**B:** A ___ room for two nights. May I see your passport?  
**A:** Sure. Is ___ included?  
**B:** Yes. ___ is on the second floor. Here's your ___.  
**A:** Thanks. What time is ___ ?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

reservation / booking  
double  
breakfast  
Your room / Room 2xx  
key card  
check-out  

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Elige la mejor opción

1. I'd like to ___ a room for tonight.  
   a) book  b) check out  c) stay  
2. Guests usually ___ between 2 and 4 p.m.  
   a) check out  b) check in  c) book  
3. A ___ room is for one person.  
   a) double  b) twin  c) single

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. a  
2. b  
3. c  

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Ordena

1. book / to / I'd / like / a / room / single  
2. there / Is / WiFi / free / ?  
3. check / We'd / to / like / in

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. I'd like to book a single room.  
2. Is there free WiFi?  
3. We'd like to check in.  

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu diálogo (8–12 líneas)

Escribe un diálogo en recepción. Incluye:

- *book* o *reservation*  
- *single* o *double room*  
- *breakfast included* o *WiFi*  
- *check in* o *check-out*

**Modelo:** los diálogos de las secciones 4 y 6.
