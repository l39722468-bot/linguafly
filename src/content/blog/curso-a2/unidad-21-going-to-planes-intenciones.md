En la [Unidad 20](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-20-repaso-modulo-2) cerraste el Módulo 2. En la **Unidad 21 del curso A2** (*Going to: Plans & Intentions*) entras en el **Módulo 3** y aprendes a hablar de **planes e intenciones** con *I'm going to…*

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 21 — Going to: Plans & Intentions](/curso-a2/unit-21)  
> **Antes:** [U20 — Repaso Módulo 2](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-20-repaso-modulo-2)

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 21 (A2)

- Usar **going to** para planes e intenciones ya decididos.
- Formar **afirmativa**, **negativa** e **interrogativa**.
- Conjugar *am / is / are* + *going to* + infinitivo.
- Hablar de viajes, estudios, trabajo y familia.
- Evitar errores típicos (*I going to*, *going to to visit*).

## 1. Idea clave

| Uso | Significado | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Plans** | plan que ya tienes | I **am going to** travel to Japan next summer. |
| **Intentions** | intención decidida | She **is going to** look for a new job. |

*Going to* = **ya lo has pensado / decidido** (viaje, estudios, mudanza, fin de semana…).

Más adelante, en la Unidad 22, verás *going to* también para **predicciones con evidencia**. Aquí nos centramos en **planes e intenciones**.

---

## 2. Forma: *am / is / are* + *going to* + infinitivo

![Formas de going to](/blog/curso-a2/unit-21/a2-unit21-forms.png)

| Sujeto | Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I | **am** going to | I **am going to** visit my family. |
| He / She / It | **is** going to | She **is going to** study English. |
| You / We / They | **are** going to | They **are going to** travel to Spain. |

Después de *going to* va el **infinitivo** (*visit, study, travel, buy, move…*), no *-ing*.

> I **am going to** visit Paris.  
> She **is going to** study at university next year.  
> They **are going to** travel to Spain in August.  
> He **is going to** buy a new car soon.  
> We **are going to** get married when she comes back.

## 3. Negativa: *am/is/are not going to*

| Forma | Contracción | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I am not going to… | I'm not going to… | I **am not going to** stay late. |
| he/she is not going to… | isn't going to… | He **isn't going to** buy a car. |
| we/you/they are not going to… | aren't going to… | We **are not going to** stay at home tonight. |

> We **are not going to** stay at home tonight.  
> I **am not going to** work this weekend.  
> She **isn't going to** move this year.

## 4. Preguntas y respuestas cortas

**Orden:** *Am / Is / Are* + sujeto + *going to* + infinitivo…?

> **Are** you **going to** come? — Yes, I **am**. / No, I **am not**.  
> **Is** she **going to** study engineering? — Yes, she **is**.  
> **What are** you **going to** do this weekend?  
> **Where is** he **going to** apply for jobs?

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-21/are-you-going-to-come.mp3" title="🔊 Are you going to come?"></audio>

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-21/what-are-you-going-to-do.mp3" title="🔊 What are you going to do this weekend?"></audio>

Respuestas cortas: *Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.* · *Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.* · *Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.*

## 5. Marcadores de tiempo (futuro)

| Marcador | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| this weekend | This weekend I **am going to** visit my grandmother. |
| next week / month / year | Next year he **is going to** study at university. |
| next summer | I **am going to** travel to Japan **next summer**. |
| soon | He **is going to** buy a new car **soon**. |
| in June / in August / in September | I **am going to** finish university **in June**. |
| tonight | We **are not going to** stay at home **tonight**. |

Estos marcadores ayudan a situar el plan: no hablas del pasado, hablas de lo que **vas a hacer**.

## 7. Listening: David

> Hi! My name is David. I am going to tell you about my plans for next year. I am going to finish university in June. After that I am going to look for a job. I am going to apply for jobs in London and Manchester. My girlfriend is going to travel to Australia in September. She is going to work there for six months. We are going to get married when she comes back. This weekend I am going to visit my parents. They are going to celebrate their anniversary. I am excited about all my plans!

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-21/dialogue-david.mp3" title="🔊 Hi! My name is David. I am going to tell you…"></audio>

Preguntas rápidas:

1. ¿Cuándo termina la universidad? → *in June*  
2. ¿Qué va a hacer después? → *look for a job*  
3. ¿Dónde solicita trabajo? → *London and Manchester*  
4. ¿A dónde viaja su novia? → *Australia* (en septiembre, 6 meses)  
5. ¿Qué van a hacer cuando ella vuelva? → *get married*

## 10. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I going to visit Paris. | I **am** going to visit Paris. | Falta *am/is/are* |
| She are going to study. | She **is** going to study. | *she* → *is* |
| They is going to travel. | They **are** going to travel. | *they* → *are* |
| I am going to **to** visit. | I am going to visit. | Un solo *to* (el de *going to*) |
| I am going to **visiting**. | I am going to **visit**. | Infinitivo, no *-ing* |
| Are going you to come? | **Are you going to** come? | Orden: auxiliar + sujeto |

## 11. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige *am / is / are*

1. I ____ going to visit my family next week.  
2. She ____ going to study English next year.  
3. They ____ going to travel to Spain in August.  
4. He ____ going to buy a new car soon.  
5. We ____ going to stay at home tonight. *(negativa más adelante; aquí solo el verbo)*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **am** · 2. **is** · 3. **are** · 4. **is** · 5. **are**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa con *going to* + verbo

Usa: *visit / study / travel / move / cook*

1. I am going to ____ my grandmother this weekend.  
2. He is going to ____ engineering at university.  
3. They are going to ____ to Japan next summer.  
4. My parents are going to ____ to a small village.  
5. She is going to ____ my favourite food.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **visit** · 2. **study** · 3. **travel** · 4. **move** · 5. **cook**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Negativa

1. We ____ going to stay at home tonight. (*are not*)  
2. I ____ going to work this weekend. (*am not*)  
3. She ____ going to buy a car this year. (*is not / isn't*)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. We **are not** / **aren't** going to stay at home tonight.  
2. I **am not** going to work this weekend.  
3. She **is not** / **isn't** going to buy a car this year.
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Preguntas

Ordena / completa:

1. ____ you going to come?  
2. What ____ you going to do this weekend?  
3. ____ she going to study engineering?  
4. Where ____ they going to travel?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Are** you going to come?  
2. What **are** you going to do this weekend?  
3. **Is** she going to study engineering?  
4. Where **are** they going to travel?
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Completa a Emma

My name is Emma. I ____ going to travel to Japan next summer. I am going to ____ Tokyo and Kyoto. My brother ____ going to study engineering. My parents ____ going to move to a small village. This weekend I am going to ____ my grandmother.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**am** · **visit** · **is** · **are** · **visit**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa a David

I am going to ____ university in June. After that I am going to ____ for a job. I am going to ____ for jobs in London and Manchester. My girlfriend ____ going to travel to Australia. We ____ going to get married when she comes back.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**finish** · **look** · **apply** · **is** · **are**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Empareja

1. I am going to…  
2. She is going to…  
3. They are going to…  
4. Are you going to…?  
5. We are not going to…  

a) pregunta · b) *I* · c) *she* · d) *they* · e) negativa (*we*)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **c** · 3 → **d** · 4 → **a** · 5 → **e**
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Corrige

1. *I going to visit Paris.*  
2. *She are going to study.*  
3. *They is going to travel.*  
4. *I am going to to buy a house.*  
5. *Are going you to come?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I **am** going to visit Paris.  
2. She **is** going to study.  
3. They **are** going to travel.  
4. I am going to buy a house.  
5. **Are you going to** come?
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Traduce

1. Voy a viajar a Japón el próximo verano.  
2. Ella va a estudiar ingeniería.  
3. Ellos van a mudarse el próximo mes.  
4. ¿Vas a venir?  
5. ¿Qué vas a hacer este fin de semana?  
6. No vamos a quedarnos en casa esta noche.  
7. Voy a buscar un trabajo.  
8. Voy a solicitar trabajos en Londres.  
9. Vamos a casarnos cuando ella vuelva.  
10. Este fin de semana voy a visitar a mis padres.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I am going to travel to Japan next summer.  
2. She is going to study engineering.  
3. They are going to move next month.  
4. Are you going to come?  
5. What are you going to do this weekend?  
6. We are not going to stay at home tonight.  
7. I am going to look for a job.  
8. I am going to apply for jobs in London.  
9. We are going to get married when she comes back.  
10. This weekend I am going to visit my parents.
</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tus planes

Escribe 8–12 líneas sobre tus planes:

- 3 × *I am going to…* (viaje / estudio / fin de semana)  
- 1 × *He/She is going to…* (alguien de tu familia)  
- 1 × *They are going to…*  
- 1 × negativa (*I'm not going to…*)  
- 1 × pregunta (*What are you going to…?*)

**Modelo:** Emma o David.

## 12. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Tabla *am/is/are + going to + infinitivo*.  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–7.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha a Emma y David; subraya cada *going to*.  
4. **5–8 min** — Graba tus planes (Ejercicio 10).

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *am/is/are* + *going to* + infinitivo  
- [ ] Formo negativa e interrogativa  
- [ ] Respondo con *Yes, I am / No, I'm not*  
- [ ] Uso marcadores (*next week, this weekend, soon…*)  
- [ ] Puedo hablar de mis planes de viaje, estudio y familia

## 13. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Primero decide el **plan** (¿qué? ¿cuándo?).  
2. Elige *am / is / are* según el sujeto.  
3. Practica en la [Unidad 21 del curso A2](/curso-a2/unit-21).

La **Unidad 22** amplía *going to* a **predicciones** con evidencia: *Look at the clouds! It's going to rain.*
