## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** Can I…? / Would you like me to…? (offers); Could you…? / Can you…? (requests)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ___ I help you? → *Can / Do / Am*

2. Would you like me ___ help? → *to / for / at*

3. ___ you open the window? → *Could / Can to / Do*

4. ___ I make some tea? → *Shall / Should to / Must*

5. Yes, ___. *(aceptar oferta)* → *please / of / course only*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Can** · 2. **to** · 3. **Could** · 4. **Shall** · 5. **please**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *Can I help you?* = ____. → a) offer · b) request · c) opinion

7. *Could you please send…* = ____. → a) request educada · b) offer · c) only Past

8. *Would you like me to* + ____. → a) verbo base · b) to + verbo · c) -ing only

9. *Yes, please* acepta ____. → a) offer · b) only request · c) I disagree

10. Unit 52 follows ____. → a) Suggestions U51 · b) Module 5 only · c) skip U53

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) offer** · 7. **a) request educada** · 8. **a) verbo base** · 9. **a) offer** · 10. **a) Suggestions U51**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *Can I to help you?*

12. *Could you to wait?*

13. *Would you like me help?*

14. *Please you send the email.*

15. *No.* *(rechazo oferta — educado)*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. Can I help you?
12. Could you wait?
13. Would you like me to help?
14. Could you please send the email?
15. No, thanks. / It's OK, thanks.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** Can I help you?, Could you please…?, Yes please, Of course, No thanks

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *Can I help you?* ≈ ____ → *¿Puedo ayudarte? · abre la ventana · estoy de acuerdo*

2. *Could you please…* ≈ ____ → *¿Podrías… por favor? · sí por favor · no gracias*

3. *Would you like me to…* ≈ ____ → *¿Te gustaría que yo…? · ayúdame · opino que*

4. *Yes, please* ≈ ____ → *sí, por favor · por supuesto · no gracias*

5. *Of course* ≈ ____ → *por supuesto/claro · no puedo · me aburro*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **¿Puedo ayudarte?** · 2. **¿Podrías… por favor?** · 3. **¿Te gustaría que yo…?** · 4. **sí, por favor** · 5. **por supuesto/claro**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. Those bags look ___. → *heavy / heavies / heaviness*

7. Take them ___. → *upstairs / upstair / up stair*

8. Open the ___. → *door / drama / degree*

9. Send the report by ___. → *email / train / sunny*

10. Book a meeting ___ for 3. → *room / rain / receipt*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **heavy** · 7. **upstairs** · 8. **door** · 9. **email** · 10. **room**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Can I* for offers: ____. → a) yes · b) only opinions · c) only linking

12. Reception call is an ____. → a) offer · b) request · c) opinion

13. *That would be great* = ____. → a) aceptar · b) rechazar · c) I disagree

14. Office text uses ____. → a) offers & requests · b) only did · c) only phrasals

15. Unit 52 next is ____. → a) Opinions U53 · b) Module 5 only · c) skip U53

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) yes** · 12. **a) offer** · 13. **a) aceptar** · 14. **a) offers & requests** · 15. **a) Opinions U53**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: At the office

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-52/reading-offers.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: At the office"></audio>

> Good morning. Can I help you with those boxes? If you want, Would you like me to call reception? Later, Could you please send me the report by email? And Shall I book a meeting room for 3? Thanks — that would be great.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Can I help with those ___. → a) boxes · b) dramas · c) platforms

2. Would you like me to call ___. → a) reception · b) weather · c) museum

3. Send report by ___. → a) email · b) train · c) gate

4. Shall I book meeting room for ___. → a) 3 · b) 30 · c) midnight

5. Thanks — that would be ___. → a) great · b) refund · c) foggy

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) boxes** · 2. **a) reception** · 3. **a) email** · 4. **a) 3** · 5. **a) great**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. *Could you please* = ____. → a) request cortés · b) offer · c) only Past

7. *Shall I book* = ____. → a) offer · b) opinion · c) recycle

8. Office reading mixes ____. → a) offers & requests · b) only feelings · c) only places

9. *Good morning* starts ____. → a) office context · b) beach · c) exam only

10. Matches unit 52: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) request cortés** · 7. **a) offer** · 8. **a) offers & requests** · 9. **a) office context** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Can I help you with those boxes* = ____. → a) offer · b) I think · c) There is

12. *Would you like me to call reception* = ____. → a) offer · b) request · c) linking only

13. *Could you please send* = ____. → a) request · b) suggestion · c) feel

14. Text uses ____. → a) Can I / Could you / Would you like me to · b) only articles · c) only should recycle

15. Offer writing uses ____. → a) Can I / Would you like me to · b) only I think · c) only although

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) offer** · 12. **a) offer** · 13. **a) request** · 14. **a) Can I / Could you / Would you like me to** · 15. **a) Can I / Would you like me to**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Helping a neighbour

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-52/listening-offers.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Helping a neighbour"></audio>

> Alex: Hi! Those bags look heavy. Can I help you?
> Sam: Yes, please. Thanks!
> Alex: Would you like me to take them upstairs?
> Sam: That would be great.
> Alex: Could you open the door?
> Sam: Of course. Here you are.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Alex offers to help with ___. → a) bags · b) emails · c) exams

2. Sam accepts with ___. → a) Yes, please · b) I disagree · c) In my opinion

3. Alex offers to take bags ___. → a) upstairs · b) to cinema · c) to beach

4. Alex asks Sam to open the ___. → a) door · b) window only · c) app

5. Sam says ___. → a) Of course · b) I don't think so · c) Let's to go

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) bags** · 2. **a) Yes, please** · 3. **a) upstairs** · 4. **a) door** · 5. **a) Of course**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. *Those bags look heavy* = ____. → a) observación antes de offer · b) weather · c) opinion forum

7. *That would be great* = ____. → a) aceptar offer · b) rechazar · c) linking only

8. *Could you open the door?* = ____. → a) request · b) offer · c) suggestion Shall we

9. Dialogue uses ____. → a) offers & requests · b) only opinions · c) only environment

10. Unit 52 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) observación antes de offer** · 7. **a) aceptar offer** · 8. **a) request** · 9. **a) offers & requests** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Can I help you?* → a) offer · b) I think · c) There are

12. *Would you like me to take them upstairs?* → a) offer · b) request · c) opinion

13. *Of course* → a) aceptar request · b) No thanks · c) I am agree

14. Shadow and note ____. → a) offer/request forms · b) only some · c) only Who

15. U52 next unit is ____. → a) Opinions U53 · b) Module 5 only · c) U51 skip

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) offer** · 12. **a) offer** · 13. **a) aceptar request** · 14. **a) offer/request forms** · 15. **a) Opinions U53**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ___ I carry your bag? → *Can / Do / Am*

2. Could you ___ close the door? *(please)* → *please / to / for*

3. Would you like me ___ help? → *to / for / at*

4. ___ you send the file? → *Could / Can to / Do*

5. Yes, ___. → *please / of / course only*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Can** · 2. **please** · 3. **to** · 4. **Could** · 5. **please**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. help / I / Can / you / ?

7. open / Could / you / the / window / ?

8. me / like / Would / you / to / help / ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. Can I help you?
7. Could you open the window?
8. Would you like me to help?

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Una offer con *Can I…?*.

10. Una offer con *Would you like me to…?*.

11. Una request con *Could you…?*.

12. Una respuesta *Yes, please.*

13. Una respuesta *Of course.*

14. Corrige: *Can I to help you?*

15. Escribe un diálogo de cortesía (8–10 líneas) con offers y requests.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. Can I help you?
10. Would you like me to call a taxi?
11. Could you open the window, please?
12. Yes, please.
13. Of course.
14. Can I help you?
15. A: Those bags look heavy. Can I help you? B: Yes, please. A: Would you like me to take them upstairs? B: That would be great. A: Could you open the door? B: Of course. Here you are.

</details>
