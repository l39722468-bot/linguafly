## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 49 (A2)

- **Look like** (apariencia) vs **be like** (carácter).
- Vocabulario de relaciones: **friend**, **colleague**, **neighbour**.
- Adjetivos de **personalidad**.
- Comparar personas (*taller*, *more friendly*).
- **Have known** + *for/since* (cuánto tiempo conoces a alguien).

## 1. Vocabulario: relaciones

| Palabra | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **friend** | amigo/a | She's my best **friend**. |
| **colleague** | compañero/a de trabajo | He's a **colleague** from work. |
| **neighbour** | vecino/a | Our **neighbours** are quiet. |
| **classmate** | compañero/a de clase | We were **classmates**. |
| **partner** | pareja / socio | This is my **partner**. |
| **relationship** | relación | We have a good **relationship**. |
| **get on (well) with** | llevarse bien | I **get on well with** my colleagues. |

> I've got a lot of friends, but only a few close ones.

---

## 2. *Look like* vs *be like*

| Forma | Pregunta / uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **look like** | apariencia física | She **looks like** her sister. |
| **What does he look like?** | ¿cómo es físicamente? | He's tall with dark hair. |
| **be like** | carácter / personalidad | What's he **like**? He's funny. |
| **What's she like?** | ¿cómo es de carácter? | She's kind and patient. |

> He **looks like** a film star, but he's actually very shy.  
> What's your new boss **like**? — She's strict but fair.

## 4. Comparar personas + *have known*

Comparativos (repaso):

> My brother is **taller than** me.  
> She's **more talkative than** her sister.  
> He's the **friendliest** person I know.

Tiempo que conoces a alguien — **Present Perfect**:

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| have/has known + for | I've **known** her **for** ten years. |
| have/has known + since | We've **known** each other **since** school. |

> How long have you known him? — **I've known** him **since** 2018.

## 6. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura — My neighbour Maya

Maya is my **neighbour**. I've **known** her **for** three years. She **looks like** her mother — same smile and dark hair. What's she **like**? She's **friendly**, **funny** and very **kind**. We **get on well**. She's a bit **quieter** than me, but we talk every weekend over coffee.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-49/reading-maya.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: My neighbour Maya"></audio>

### Diálogo — Talking about a colleague

**Leo:** Have you met the new **colleague**?  
**Nora:** Yes. What's he **like**?  
**Leo:** He's **hard-working** and **honest**. A bit **shy** at first.  
**Nora:** What does he **look like**?  
**Leo:** Tall, short hair. He **looks like** my cousin, actually.  
**Nora:** How long have you **known** him?  
**Leo:** Only **for** two weeks — but we already **get on** well.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-49/dialogue-colleague.mp3" title="🔊 Dialogue: Talking about a colleague"></audio>

### Mini práctica

1. She ___ like her sister. *(apariencia)*  
2. What's he ___ ? *(carácter)*  
3. I've ___ her for five years.  
4. He's more ___ than his brother. *(friendly)*

## 7. Errores frecuentes

1. ~~How is she like?~~ → **What's she like?**  
2. ~~She is look like her mum.~~ → **She looks like** her mum.  
3. ~~I know her for 10 years.~~ → **I've known** her for 10 years.  
4. ~~What does she like?~~ *(gustos)* ≠ apariencia/carácter → para gustos OK; para carácter: **What's she like?**  
5. ~~He is more tall than me.~~ → **He's taller than** me.  
6. ~~We have a good relation.~~ → **relationship** (más natural aquí)

## 8. Ejercicios (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Empareja

1. colleague  
2. neighbour  
3. look like  
4. be like / What's … like?  
5. get on well with  

a) personality  
b) appearance  
c) person next door  
d) have a good relationship with  
e) person you work with

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1 → e  
2 → c  
3 → b  
4 → a  
5 → d  

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — *look like* o *be like*

1. She ___ her father — same eyes.  
2. What's your teacher ___ ? — Strict but fair.  
3. What does he ___ ? — Short and blonde.  
4. He doesn't ___ his brother at all. *(apariencia)*

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. looks like  
2. like  
3. look like  
4. look like  

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Personalidad

Elige: friendly / shy / funny / hard-working / talkative

1. She never stops speaking → ___  
2. He doesn't talk much with new people → ___  
3. She always helps others and smiles → ___  
4. He works late every day → ___  
5. Everyone laughs with him → ___

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. talkative  
2. shy  
3. friendly (or kind)  
4. hard-working  
5. funny  

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — *have known*

1. I ___ (know) Ana ___ 2015.  
2. We ___ (know) each other ___ ten years.  
3. How long ___ you ___ (know) him?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. have known / since  
2. have known / for  
3. have / known  

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrige el error

1. How is she like?  
2. She is look like her mum.  
3. I know him for three years.  
4. He is more funny than me.  
5. What's he look like? *(carácter — corrige la pregunta)*

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. What's she like?  
2. She looks like her mum.  
3. I've known him for three years.  
4. He's funnier than me.  
5. What's he like?  

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Traduce al inglés

1. Se parece a su hermana.  
2. ¿Cómo es él (de carácter)?  
3. La conozco desde hace cinco años.  
4. Nos llevamos bien.  
5. Es más tímido que yo.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. She looks like her sister.  
2. What's he like?  
3. I've known her for five years.  
4. We get on well.  
5. He's shyer / more shy than me.  

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Completa el diálogo

**A:** Have you met my new ___ ?  
**B:** Not yet. What's she ___ ?  
**A:** Very ___ and kind.  
**B:** What does she ___ like?  
**A:** Tall, long hair. I've ___ her ___ Monday.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

colleague (or neighbour / friend)  
like  
friendly  
look  
known / since  

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Ordena

1. like / looks / She / her / mother  
2. she / What's / like / ?  
3. known / I've / him / for / years

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. She looks like her mother.  
2. What's she like?  
3. I've known him for years.  

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Comparativos

1. My sister is ___ (friendly) than me.  
2. Tom is ___ (tall) than Sam.  
3. She's the ___ (kind) person in the office.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. more friendly / friendlier  
2. taller  
3. kindest  

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu descripción (8–12 líneas)

Describe a una persona importante para ti. Incluye:

- relación (*friend / colleague / neighbour…*)  
- *look like* o apariencia  
- *What's … like?* + 2 adjetivos de personalidad  
- *I've known … for/since*  
- 1 comparativo

**Modelo:** la lectura de la sección 6.

## 9. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — *look like* / *be like* + vocabulario  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–6.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha lectura y diálogo.  
4. **5–8 min** — Ejercicio 10.

### Checklist

- [ ] Distingo *look like* y *What's … like?*  
- [ ] Uso *friend / colleague / neighbour*  
- [ ] Describo personalidad con adjetivos  
- [ ] Uso *I've known … for/since*  
- [ ] Evito *How is she like?* e *I know her for…*
