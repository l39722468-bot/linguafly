## Lección 1 — Gramática: Past Simple regular

**Objetivo:** *-ed*, misma forma para todas las personas; *didn't / Did* + base.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ to the park yesterday. → *walked / walk / walking*  
2. She ____ her grandmother last week. → *visited / visit / visits*  
3. We ____ football yesterday. → *played / play / playing*  
4. He ____ his homework two hours ago. → *finished / finish / finishes*  
5. They ____ a film last night. → *watched / watch / watching*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **walked**  
2. **visited**  
3. **played**  
4. **finished**  
5. **watched**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Past of *clean* = ____. → a) cleaned · b) cleant · c) cleaning  
7. *I walk yesterday* is ____. → a) wrong · b) correct · c) future  
8. After *didn't* we use ____. → a) base verb · b) *-ed* · c) *-ing* only  
9. *Did you ____?* → a) play · b) played · c) playing  
10. Same form for *I/she/they* = ____. → a) yes for regular past · b) no · c) only with *will*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) cleaned**  
7. **a) wrong**  
8. **a) base verb**  
9. **a) play**  
10. **a) yes for regular past**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I walk yesterday.*  
12. *She didn't visited her friend.*  
13. *Did you played football?*  
14. *He finish his project last week.*  
15. *We watch a film two hours ago.* (pasado)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I **walked** yesterday.  
12. She didn't **visit** her friend.  
13. Did you **play** football?  
14. He **finished** his project last week.  
15. We **watched** a film two hours ago.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: tiempo y acciones

**Objetivo:** *yesterday, last week, ago* + verbos regulares frecuentes.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja

1. yesterday  
2. last week  
3. two hours ago  
4. last night  
5. on Monday  

a) la semana pasada · b) ayer · c) hace dos horas · d) anoche · e) el lunes (pasado en contexto)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **c** · 4 → **d** · 5 → **e**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. walk → ____  
7. visit → ____  
8. play → ____  
9. finish → ____  
10. clean → ____  

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **walked** · 7. **visited** · 8. **played** · 9. **finished** · 10. **cleaned**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Ago* goes ____ the time period. → a) after · b) before · c) never  
12. *Last* + ____. → a) night / week / weekend · b) ago · c) only *to be*  
13. *Talked* is past of ____. → a) talk · b) tell · c) speak  
14. *Rested* means ____. → a) relaxed / didn't work hard · b) ran · c) bought  
15. *Cooked dinner* = ____. → a) prepared dinner · b) watched TV · c) walked

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) after**  
12. **a) night / week / weekend**  
13. **a) talk**  
14. **a) relaxed / didn't work hard**  
15. **a) prepared dinner**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: A busy week

**Objetivo:** leer un relato con varios verbos regulares.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-2/reading-past.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: A busy week"></audio>

> Last week I had a busy week. On Monday I walked to work because my car was broken. On Tuesday I visited my grandmother in the hospital. She was happy to see me. On Wednesday I played football with my friends. We won the match! On Thursday I finished my project at work. My boss was very happy. Yesterday I rested at home and watched a film.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Why did the person walk to work on Monday? → a) The car was broken · b) It was raining · c) No reason  
2. Who did they visit on Tuesday? → a) Grandmother · b) Boss · c) Teacher  
3. What did they do on Wednesday? → a) Played football · b) Cleaned · c) Cooked only  
4. What happened on Thursday? → a) Finished a project · b) Went to London · c) Bought a car  
5. What did they do yesterday? → a) Rested and watched a film · b) Worked all day · c) Nothing

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) The car was broken**  
2. **a) Grandmother**  
3. **a) Played football**  
4. **a) Finished a project**  
5. **a) Rested and watched a film**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Regular past verbs in the text include ____. → a) walked, visited, played, finished, rested, watched · b) only went · c) only am  
7. *Last week* marks ____. → a) past time · b) future · c) present habit only  
8. The boss was happy because ____. → a) the project was finished · b) football · c) the car  
9. *Won the match* is ____. → a) irregular (preview) · b) regular *-ed* · c) present  
10. The main topic is ____. → a) a busy week in the past · b) introductions · c) health advice

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) walked, visited, played, finished, rested, watched**  
7. **a) past time**  
8. **a) the project was finished**  
9. **a) irregular (preview)**  
10. **a) a busy week in the past**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Walked* = ____ + *-ed*. → a) walk · b) went · c) go  
12. *Visited* is ____. → a) regular past · b) present · c) future  
13. *Yesterday* pairs with ____. → a) past verbs · b) *will* · c) *going to* only  
14. Same form: *I finished / She finished* = ____. → a) correct · b) wrong · c) needs *-s*  
15. *Rested* means past of ____. → a) rest · b) run · c) read

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) walk**  
12. **a) regular past**  
13. **a) past verbs**  
14. **a) correct**  
15. **a) rest**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: What I did yesterday

**Objetivo:** escuchar un relato del día anterior.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-2/listening-past.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: What I did yesterday"></audio>

> Hi! I want to tell you about what I did yesterday. In the morning I walked to the shop and picked up some bread. Then I visited my friend Sarah. We played cards and talked for two hours. In the afternoon I cleaned my room and finished my homework. Last night I watched a film on TV. It was very good!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. When is the story about? → a) Yesterday · b) Next week · c) Every day  
2. Where did they walk in the morning? → a) To the shop · b) To school · c) To London  
3. Who did they visit? → a) Sarah · b) The boss · c) Maria  
4. What did they do with Sarah? → a) Played cards and talked · b) Cooked pasta · c) Bought a car  
5. What did they watch last night? → a) A film · b) Football live · c) Nothing

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Yesterday**  
2. **a) To the shop**  
3. **a) Sarah**  
4. **a) Played cards and talked**  
5. **a) A film**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. In the afternoon they ____. → a) cleaned and finished homework · b) only slept · c) flew  
7. *Picked up* here means ____. → a) got / collected · b) played · c) rested  
8. They talked for ____. → a) two hours · b) two days · c) two minutes only  
9. The film was ____. → a) very good · b) terrible · c) not mentioned  
10. The listening practices ____. → a) regular past verbs · b) only *to be* · c) conditionals

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) cleaned and finished homework**  
7. **a) got / collected**  
8. **a) two hours**  
9. **a) very good**  
10. **a) regular past verbs**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Walked / visited / played / cleaned / finished / watched* are ____. → a) regular past · b) present · c) future  
12. *Last night* = ____. → a) past time · b) future · c) never  
13. After *Did* we would use ____. → a) base verb · b) *-ed* · c) *to* + verb  
14. *Talked* is past of ____. → a) talk · b) tell · c) speak  
15. Order of the day: ____. → a) morning → afternoon → last night · b) night first · c) no order

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) regular past**  
12. **a) past time**  
13. **a) base verb**  
14. **a) talk**  
15. **a) morning → afternoon → last night**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My yesterday

**Objetivo:** escribir un día pasado con verbos regulares.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. Yesterday I ____ to the park. → *walked / walk / will walk*  
2. Last week I ____ my friend. → *visited / visit / visiting*  
3. Two hours ago I ____ my homework. → *finished / finish / finishes*  
4. Last night we ____ a film. → *watched / watch / watching*  
5. I ____ my room yesterday. → *cleaned / clean / cleaning*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **walked** · 2. **visited** · 3. **finished** · 4. **watched** · 5. **cleaned**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. I / walked / to work / yesterday.  
7. She / visited / her grandmother / last week.  
8. We / played / football / on Wednesday.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I walked to work yesterday.  
7. She visited her grandmother last week.  
8. We played football on Wednesday.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Ayer caminé a la tienda.  
10. La semana pasada visité a un amigo.  
11. Hace dos horas terminé los deberes.  
12. Anoche vi una película (*watched*).  
13. Negativa: no jugué al fútbol ayer.  
14. Pregunta: ¿Limpiaste tu habitación?  
15. Párrafo (6–8 frases) sobre ayer con *yesterday / last / ago*.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. Yesterday I walked to the shop.  
10. Last week I visited a friend.  
11. I finished my homework two hours ago.  
12. Last night I watched a film.  
13. I didn't play football yesterday.  
14. Did you clean your room?  
15. Yesterday I walked to the shop. Then I visited my friend. We talked for two hours. In the afternoon I cleaned my room. Last night I watched a film. It was good!

</details>
