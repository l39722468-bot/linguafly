## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** was/were + -ing.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ watching TV at 8pm. → *was / were / am*
2. They ____ cooking dinner. → *were / was / are*
3. ____ she studying? → *Was / Were / Did*
4. We ____ not sleeping. → *were / was / did*
5. He ____ walking his dog. → *was / were / is*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **was** · 2. **were** · 3. **Was** · 4. **were** · 5. **was**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Form = ____. → a) was/were + -ing · b) have + PP · c) will + base
7. Use for ____. → a) actions in progress in the past · b) only experiences · c) only future
8. I/he/she/it take ____. → a) was · b) were · c) have
9. You/we/they take ____. → a) were · b) was · c) has
10. *Suddenly* often interrupts with ____. → a) Past Simple · b) only Present Perfect · c) *will*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) was/were + -ing** · 7. **a) actions in progress in the past** · 8. **a) was** · 9. **a) were** · 10. **a) Past Simple**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I were watching TV.*
12. *They was cooking.*
13. *Was they playing?*
14. *She were studying.*
15. *We was running.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I **was** watching TV.  
12. They **were** cooking.  
13. **Were** they playing?  
14. She **was** studying.  
15. We **were** running.  

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-16/reading-continuous.mp3" title="🔊 Reading Unidad 16"></audio>

> Last Saturday night at 8pm I was watching TV in my living room. My sister was studying in her room. My parents were cooking dinner in the kitchen. Suddenly the doorbell rang. It was our neighbour Mr Brown. He was walking his dog when he saw our lights on. He wanted to ask if we had seen his cat. We were all surprised but we helped him look. At that moment the cat was sleeping under the sofa!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. When was the story? → a) Saturday night at 8pm · b) Last year · c) Tomorrow
2. What was the speaker doing? → a) Watching TV · b) Cooking only · c) Running
3. What were parents doing? → a) Cooking dinner · b) Sleeping · c) Flying
4. Who rang the doorbell? → a) Mr Brown · b) Lisa · c) Tom
5. Where was the cat? → a) Under the sofa · b) In the park · c) In London

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Saturday night at 8pm** · 2. **a) Watching TV** · 3. **a) Cooking dinner** · 4. **a) Mr Brown** · 5. **a) Under the sofa**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The text matches unit 16: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Unit 1
7. You can find the target forms: ____. → a) yes · b) never · c) only images
8. Sequence/time words appear as needed: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only future
9. Main idea is clear from the first lines: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) impossible
10. Re-read after checking answers: ____. → a) good idea · b) useless · c) forbidden

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) yes** · 7. **a) yes** · 8. **a) yes** · 9. **a) yes** · 10. **a) good idea**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Mark 3 target forms in the text: ____. → a) do it · b) skip · c) delete the text
12. Change one sentence to negative/question: ____. → a) useful · b) wrong always · c) only B2
13. Note any Past Simple / Perfect / Continuous mix: ____. → a) notice it · b) ignore · c) ban English
14. Copy one model sentence aloud: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) never
15. Write a parallel sentence about you: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only tomorrow forever

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11–15. **a)** (práctica abierta; lo importante es usar el foco de la unidad)

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-16/listening-continuous.mp3" title="🔊 Listening Unidad 16"></audio>

> Yesterday afternoon at 3pm I was reading a book in the park. My friend was running around the lake. Some children were playing football near. Suddenly it started to rain. Everyone was running to find shelter. I was getting wet when a kind woman offered me her umbrella. We waited under a tree until the rain stopped. At that moment the sun was coming out again. I was grateful for her help.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Where was the speaker? → a) In the park · b) At work · c) At school
2. What was the friend doing? → a) Running around the lake · b) Cooking · c) Sleeping
3. What happened suddenly? → a) It started to rain · b) An exam · c) A film
4. Who offered an umbrella? → a) A kind woman · b) Mr Brown · c) Nobody
5. What was coming out again? → a) The sun · b) The cat · c) Smoke

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) In the park** · 2. **a) Running around the lake** · 3. **a) It started to rain** · 4. **a) A kind woman** · 5. **a) The sun**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Audio supports the same grammar focus: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only music
7. Speakers use natural A2 vocabulary: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Latin
8. You can answer without the script first: ____. → a) try it · b) never · c) impossible always
9. Then check with the script: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) delete audio
10. Repeat difficult sentences: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only teachers

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) yes** · 7. **a) yes** · 8. **a) try it** · 9. **a) yes** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Transcribe one key sentence: ____. → a) useful · b) useless · c) banned
12. Underline the target grammar: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) never
13. Record yourself saying it: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only offline forever
14. Compare with the model: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) skip English
15. Add the sentence to your notebook: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11–15. **a) yes / useful** (práctica abierta)

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa (según el foco)

1. Write one affirmative sentence with the unit focus.
2. Write one negative sentence.
3. Write one question.
4. Add a time marker that fits.
5. Add one vocabulary word from the reading.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

Usa los modelos del reading/listening de esta unidad; cualquier frase correcta con el foco cuenta.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena / reescribe

6. Reorder a model sentence from the reading.
7. Change person (*I* → *she/they*).
8. Change time (yesterday / for / since / how often…).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Compara con el texto modelo; la concordancia sujeto–verbo debe ser correcta.

</details>
