## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 38 (A2)

- Dar consejos con **should** / **shouldn't**.
- Usar **ought to** (más formal) con el mismo sentido.
- Preguntar consejo: **Should I…?**
- Hablar de **salud, viaje y estudio**.
- Evitar errores (*You should to go*, *You ought rest*).

![Should and Ought to A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-38/a2-unit38-should-ought-to.png)

---

## 1. Idea clave: consejo, no orden

*Should* y *ought to* **recomiendan**. No son tan fuertes como *must* (obligación).

| Modal | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **should** | consejo habitual | You **should** drink water. |
| **shouldn't** | consejo negativo | You **shouldn't** smoke. |
| **ought to** | consejo (más formal) | You **ought to** see a doctor. |

> You **should** sleep more.  
> You **ought to** sleep more. *(mismo mensaje, tono más formal)*

## 2. Forma de *should*

| Forma | Estructura | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Affirmative | subject + **should** + verbo base | You **should rest**. |
| Negative | subject + **shouldn't** + verbo base | You **shouldn't worry**. |
| Question | **Should** + subject + verbo base? | **Should I call** her? |

**Importante:** *should* **no** lleva *to*.

~~You should to study.~~ → **You should study.**

## 3. Forma de *ought to*

| Forma | Estructura | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Affirmative | subject + **ought to** + verbo base | You **ought to practise** every day. |
| Negative (formal) | subject + **ought not to** + verbo | You **ought not to** be late. |

En A2, usa sobre todo la **afirmativa**. Para negativas, en conversación es más natural *shouldn't*.

**Importante:** *ought* **sí** lleva *to*.

~~You ought rest.~~ → **You ought to rest.**

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-38/you-ought-to-practise.mp3" title="🔊 You ought to practise every day."></audio>

## 4. *Should* vs *ought to* (¿cuál elijo?)

| Situación | Mejor opción | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Conversación cotidiana | **should** | You should take a break. |
| Texto un poco más formal | **ought to** | Students ought to arrive on time. |
| Consejo negativo rápido | **shouldn't** | You shouldn't eat so fast. |
| Pedir consejo | **Should I…?** | Should I buy a ticket online? |

En el examen A2 Key, *should* es el modal de consejo más frecuente. Domínalo primero; *ought to* es un plus.

## 5. Consejos: salud, viaje y estudio

### Salud

> You **should** drink more water.  
> You **shouldn't** stay up so late.  
> You **ought to** see a doctor if it hurts.

### Viaje

> You **should** book the hotel early.  
> You **shouldn't** leave your bag on the train.  
> You **ought to** check the weather before you go.

### Estudio

> You **should** review your notes every day.  
> You **shouldn't** translate word by word.  
> You **ought to** listen to English for 10 minutes a day.

## 6. Pedir y dar consejo (mini diálogos)

**A:** I have a headache. What **should** I do?  
**B:** You **should** rest and drink water. You **shouldn't** look at your phone.

**A:** **Should** I take a taxi to the airport?  
**B:** Yes — you **ought to** leave early. Traffic is bad.

## 7. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura — Advice for the exam

Maria has an English exam next week. Her teacher says she **should** sleep well the night before. She **shouldn't** study all night. She **ought to** practise listening with short videos. Her friend Tom says: “You **should** arrive early. You **shouldn't** forget your ID.” Maria feels nervous, but she knows what to do.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-38/reading-exam.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Advice for the exam"></audio>

### Diálogo — At the doctor

**Doctor:** How do you feel?  
**Patient:** Tired. I work a lot.  
**Doctor:** You **should** sleep seven or eight hours. You **shouldn't** drink so much coffee.  
**Patient:** **Should** I take medicine?  
**Doctor:** Not yet. You **ought to** rest this weekend and drink water.

## 8. Errores frecuentes

1. ~~You should to go.~~ → **You should go.**  
2. ~~You ought rest.~~ → **You ought to rest.**  
3. ~~You shouldn't to worry.~~ → **You shouldn't worry.**  
4. ~~Do I should call?~~ → **Should I call?**  
5. ~~You must rest~~ *(si solo es consejo suave)* → **You should rest.** *(must = más fuerte)*  
6. ~~You oughtn't smoke~~ *(poco natural en A2)* → **You shouldn't smoke.**

*(Opcional A2+/B1: *You'd better…* = consejo urgente / aviso. No es el foco de esta unidad.)*

## 9. Ejercicios (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Completa con *should* o *shouldn't*

2. You ___ smoke.  
3. Students ___ arrive late.  
4. We ___ check the tickets before we leave.

2. shouldn't  
3. shouldn't  
4. should

### Ejercicio 2 — *should* o *ought to*

Elige la forma correcta (ambas pueden ser posibles; usa la pedida):

1. You ___ (ought) practise every day.  
2. You ___ (should) take an umbrella.  
3. She ___ (ought) call her parents.  
4. He ___ (should / neg) eat in class.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. ought to  
2. should  
3. ought to  
4. shouldn't  

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Preguntas con *Should I…?*

Ordena:

1. call / Should / I / a taxi / ?  
2. book / I / Should / the hotel / today / ?  
3. tell / Should / her / I / the truth / ?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

2. Should I book the hotel today?  
3. Should I tell her the truth?  

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrige el error

1. You should to study tonight.  
2. You ought sleep more.  
3. Do I should go now?  
4. You shouldn't to be rude.  
5. She oughts to leave early.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

2. You ought to sleep more.  
3. Should I go now?

### Ejercicio 5 — Traduce al inglés

1. Deberías beber más agua.  
2. No deberías mirar el móvil en clase.  
3. Deberías (formal) ver a un médico.  
4. ¿Debería llamar ahora?  
5. No deberías olvidar el pasaporte.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

### Ejercicio 6 — Empareja situación y consejo

1. I feel sick.  
2. I have an exam tomorrow.  
3. My bag is open on the train.

b) You should close it. You shouldn't leave it open.  
c) You should rest. You ought to see a doctor.  
d) You should practise every day.

<summary>Soluciones</summary>

2 → a  
3 → b  
4 → d  

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tus consejos (8–12 líneas)

Un amigo viene a tu ciudad por primera vez. Escribe **al menos 6 consejos** con:

- 3 × *should*  
- 2 × *shouldn't*  
- 1 × *ought to*

**Modelo:** temas de la sección 5 (viaje / comida / transporte).

## 10. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Tabla *should* / *shouldn't* / *ought to*.  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–6.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha lectura y diálogo; marca cada modal.  
4. **5–8 min** — Escribe el Ejercicio 10.

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *should* + verbo base (sin *to*)  
- [ ] Uso *shouldn't* para consejos negativos  
- [ ] Uso *ought to* (+ *to*)  
- [ ] Pregunto con *Should I…?*  
- [ ] Distingo consejo (*should*) de obligación fuerte (*must*)
