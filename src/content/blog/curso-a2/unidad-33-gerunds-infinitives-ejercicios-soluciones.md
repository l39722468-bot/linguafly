## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** like/love/hate + -ing; want/need/would like + to.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I like ____ (swim). → *swimming / to swim / swim*
2. She wants ____ (learn) French. → *to learn / learning / learn*
3. They hate ____ (wait). → *waiting / to wait / wait*
4. We need ____ (leave) early. → *to leave / leaving / leave*
5. I'd like ____ (visit) the museum. → *to visit / visiting / visit*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **swimming** · 2. **to learn** · 3. **waiting** · 4. **to leave** · 5. **to visit**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *Like* for general taste uses ____. → a) -ing · b) to only · c) past tense
7. *Want* always needs ____. → a) to + infinitive · b) -ing · c) no verb after
8. *Would like* is ____. → a) polite wish · b) only past · c) same as hate + -ing
9. ~~I want going~~ is wrong: use ____. → a) want to go · b) want go · c) want went
10. Unit 33 trains ____. → a) -ing vs to patterns · b) only phrasals · c) only articles

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) -ing** · 7. **a) to + infinitive** · 8. **a) polite wish** · 9. **a) want to go** · 10. **a) -ing vs to patterns**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I want going home.*
12. *She like dancing.*
13. *I'd like eating pizza now.*
14. *He need to study more.*
15. *They hates waiting.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I want **to go** home.
12. She **likes** dancing.
13. I'd like **to eat** pizza now.
14. He **needs** to study more.
15. They **hate** waiting.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** like, love, hate, want, need, would like; cooking, travel, rest, order.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *love* + -ing ≈ ____ → *really enjoy · need to · polite order*
2. *hate* + -ing ≈ ____ → *don't like at all · want now · live here*
3. *want to* ≈ ____ → *wish/plan · general taste · subject question*
4. *need to* ≈ ____ → *must/necessary · optional · past ability*
5. *would like to* ≈ ____ → *polite desire · hate waiting · pick up*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **really enjoy** · 2. **don't like at all** · 3. **wish/plan** · 4. **must/necessary** · 5. **polite desire**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. I love ____ Italian food. → *cooking / cook / to cooking*
7. He wants ____ a new phone. → *to buy / buying / buy*
8. We hate ____ up early. → *getting / get / to get*
9. You need ____ a coat — it's cold. → *to wear / wearing / wear*
10. Would you like ____ sit here? → *to / for / -ing*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **cooking** · 7. **to buy** · 8. **getting** · 9. **to wear** · 10. **to**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. General habit: I ____ coffee in the morning. → a) like drinking · b) would like to drink now
12. At the café now: I ____ a cappuccino. → a) like drinking · b) would like to order
13. *Like* vs *would like*: different ____. → a) meaning/time · b) spelling only · c) always -ing
14. *Need to rest* = ____. → a) necessary · b) optional · c) past ability
15. Unit 33 follows ____. → a) U32 phrasals · b) only Module 1 · c) only some/any

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) like drinking** · 12. **b) would like to order** · 13. **a) meaning/time** · 14. **a) necessary** · 15. **a) U32 phrasals**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Likes and plans

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-33/reading-gerunds.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Likes and plans"></audio>

> I love reading in the evening. I also like cooking, but I hate washing the dishes. Next year I want to travel to Ireland. First I need to save money. Tonight I'd like to watch a film at home.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Evening activity loved? → a) Reading · b) Washing dishes · c) Travel now
2. Dislikes? → a) Washing dishes · b) Cooking · c) Reading
3. Next year plan? → a) Travel to Ireland · b) Watch film tonight · c) Save dishes
4. Before travel must ____. → a) Save money · b) Hate waiting · c) Order tea
5. Tonight? → a) Would like to watch a film · b) Need to leave early · c) Love cooking only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Reading** · 2. **a) Washing dishes** · 3. **a) Travel to Ireland** · 4. **a) Save money** · 5. **a) Would like to watch a film**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Text uses ____. → a) -ing likes + to plans · b) only phrasals · c) only Who called
7. *Love reading* pattern: love + ____. → a) -ing · b) to only · c) did
8. *Need to save* before ____. → a) travel · b) wash dishes · c) sit down
9. *I'd like to watch* is ____. → a) tonight wish · b) general hate · c) subject Q
10. Matches unit 33: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U35

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) -ing likes + to plans** · 7. **a) -ing** · 8. **a) travel** · 9. **a) tonight wish** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Hate washing* = hate + ____. → a) -ing · b) to + base · c) past
12. *Want to travel* ≠ *like travelling* in ____. → a) time/meaning · b) spelling · c) always same
13. After *need* use ____. → a) to + infinitive · b) -ing only · c) did
14. Reading labels: mark ____. → a) -ing vs to · b) only nouns · c) only articles
15. Pair aloud: like cooking / ____. → a) I'd like to cook tonight · b) hate to cook always · c) need cooking

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) -ing** · 12. **a) time/meaning** · 13. **a) to + infinitive** · 14. **a) -ing vs to** · 15. **a) I'd like to cook tonight**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: At the café

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-33/listening-gerunds.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: At the café"></audio>

> Do you like drinking coffee? Yes, I love it. I'd like to order a cappuccino. I want to try the tea. Do we need to pay now? No — we can pay later. I hate waiting when it's busy, though!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Like drinking coffee? → a) Yes, loves it · b) No, hates it · c) Not mentioned
2. Order now? → a) Cappuccino · b) Homemade pizza · c) Some bread
3. Also wants to ____. → a) Try the tea · b) Travel to Ireland · c) Pick up pencil
4. Pay now? → a) No, later · b) Yes, immediately · c) Need to save
5. Hates ____. → a) Waiting when busy · b) Cooking · c) Washing dishes

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Yes, loves it** · 2. **a) Cappuccino** · 3. **a) Try the tea** · 4. **a) No, later** · 5. **a) Waiting when busy**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. *Like drinking* vs *would like to order*: ____. → a) habit vs now · b) same meaning · c) both -ing
7. *Want to try the tea* uses ____. → a) want to · b) hate -ing · c) Who did
8. *Need to pay* asked as ____. → a) question · b) subject only · c) Zero Conditional
9. Listening setting: ____. → a) café · b) classroom · c) flat interview
10. Unit 33 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Module 6

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) habit vs now** · 7. **a) want to** · 8. **a) question** · 9. **a) café** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *I'd like to order* is ____. → a) polite · b) hate + -ing · c) separable phrasal
12. *Love it* refers to ____. → a) coffee (drinking) · b) tea only · c) worksheets
13. *Hate waiting* = hate + ____. → a) -ing · b) to wait only once · c) did wait
14. Shadow and tag ____. → a) each verb pattern · b) only spelling · c) only some/any
15. U33 next is ____. → a) subject & object questions · b) articles skip · c) Module 6 only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) polite** · 12. **a) coffee (drinking)** · 13. **a) -ing** · 14. **a) each verb pattern** · 15. **a) subject & object questions**

</details>
