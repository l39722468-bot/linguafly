## Lección 1 — Gramática: at / on / in

**Objetivo:** elegir la preposición correcta de tiempo.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I get up ____ 7 o'clock. → *at / on / in*
2. I have a meeting ____ Monday. → *on / at / in*
3. We go on holiday ____ August. → *in / on / at*
4. I sleep ____ night. → *at / in / on*
5. We meet ____ the morning. → *in / at / on*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **at** · 2. **on** · 3. **in** · 4. **at** · 5. **in**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Hours use ____. → a) *at* · b) *on* · c) *in*
7. Days/dates use ____. → a) *on* · b) *at* · c) *in*
8. Months/years/seasons use ____. → a) *in* · b) *at* · c) *on*
9. *At the weekend* is ____. → a) common in BrE · b) wrong · c) only AmE *in*
10. *In the morning* is ____. → a) correct · b) *at the morning* · c) *on the morning*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) *at*** · 7. **a) *on*** · 8. **a) *in*** · 9. **a) common in BrE** · 10. **a) correct**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I wake up on 6 o'clock.*
12. *I work in Monday.*
13. *I was born at 1990.*
14. *We meet at the morning.*
15. *My birthday is in the 15th of March.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I wake up **at** 6 o'clock.
12. I work **on** Monday.
13. I was born **in** 1990.
14. We meet **in** the morning.
15. My birthday is **on** the 15th of March.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: horarios y calendarios

**Objetivo:** combinar expresiones de tiempo con *at/on/in*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja

1. at
2. on
3. in
4. at noon
5. in summer

a) meses/estaciones/años · b) mediodía · c) horas · d) días/fechas · e) verano

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **c** · 2 → **d** · 3 → **a** · 4 → **b** · 5 → **e**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. ____ midnight → *at / on / in*
7. ____ weekdays → *on / at / in*
8. ____ December → *in / on / at*
9. ____ Saturday morning → *on / at / in*
10. ____ the evening → *in / at / on*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **at** · 7. **on** · 8. **in** · 9. **on** · 10. **in**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Noon* = ____. → a) 12:00 · b) midnight · c) morning only
12. *Weekdays* = ____. → a) Monday–Friday · b) only Sunday · c) only August
13. *Until 10 o'clock* means ____. → a) up to that time · b) after forever · c) never
14. *Finish early at 3 pm* uses ____. → a) *at* + clock time · b) *on* + hour · c) *in* + hour
15. Combine: *on Friday at 3 pm* is ____. → a) correct · b) wrong · c) needs *in Friday*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) 12:00** · 12. **a) Monday–Friday** · 13. **a) up to that time** · 14. **a) *at* + clock time** · 15. **a) correct**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Tom's week

**Objetivo:** leer un horario con *at/on/in*.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-8/reading-time.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Tom's week"></audio>

> My name is Tom and I have a busy week. On Monday and Wednesday I go to work at 8 o'clock in the morning. On Tuesday I have a meeting at noon. On Friday I finish early at 3 pm and I go to the gym. At the weekend I relax. On Saturday morning I sleep until 10 o'clock. In the afternoon I meet my friends. In summer I go on holiday in August.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. When does Tom go to work on Monday? → a) At 8 in the morning · b) At midnight · c) In August
2. When is the Tuesday meeting? → a) At noon · b) At night · c) On Sunday
3. When does he finish on Friday? → a) At 3 pm · b) At 8 am · c) At noon only
4. What does he do at the weekend? → a) Relax · b) Work all day · c) Nothing said
5. When is his holiday? → a) In August · b) On Monday · c) At 7 o'clock

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) At 8 in the morning** · 2. **a) At noon** · 3. **a) At 3 pm** · 4. **a) Relax** · 5. **a) In August**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. On Saturday morning he sleeps until ____. → a) 10 o'clock · b) noon · c) midnight
7. He meets friends ____. → a) in the afternoon · b) at night only · c) on Tuesday noon
8. *On Monday and Wednesday* uses ____. → a) *on* · b) *at* · c) *in*
9. *In summer* and *in August* use ____. → a) *in* · b) *on* · c) *at*
10. The main topic is ____. → a) a weekly schedule with time prepositions · b) adverbs · c) superlatives

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) 10 o'clock** · 7. **a) in the afternoon** · 8. **a) *on*** · 9. **a) *in*** · 10. **a) a weekly schedule with time prepositions**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *At 8 o'clock in the morning* combines ____. → a) *at* + *in* · b) *on* + *on* · c) only *of*
12. *At the weekend* is ____. → a) BrE common · b) wrong · c) only *in*
13. *On Saturday morning* uses ____. → a) *on* · b) *in* · c) *at*
14. *Go on holiday in August* uses ____. → a) *in* + month · b) *on* + month · c) *at* + month
15. Mixing several prepositions in one week story is ____. → a) natural · b) wrong · c) only B2

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) *at* + *in*** · 12. **a) BrE common** · 13. **a) *on*** · 14. **a) *in* + month** · 15. **a) natural**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: My typical day

**Objetivo:** escuchar un día típico con *at/on/in*.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-8/listening-time.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: My typical day"></audio>

> Hi! I want to tell you about my typical day. I wake up at 6 o'clock in the morning. I have breakfast at 7 am. On weekdays I start work at 9 o'clock. I have lunch at noon. In the afternoon I have meetings. On Saturday I sleep until 10 o'clock and I meet my friends in the evening. In December I go on holiday for two weeks.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. When do they wake up? → a) At 6 in the morning · b) At noon · c) In December
2. When is breakfast? → a) At 7 am · b) At midnight · c) On Monday only
3. When do they start work on weekdays? → a) At 9 · b) At 6 · c) At 3 pm
4. When do they meet friends? → a) In the evening (Saturday) · b) At noon only · c) Never
5. When is the holiday? → a) In December · b) On weekdays · c) At 7 am

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) At 6 in the morning** · 2. **a) At 7 am** · 3. **a) At 9** · 4. **a) In the evening (Saturday)** · 5. **a) In December**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Lunch is ____. → a) at noon · b) on Friday only · c) in summer only
7. Meetings are ____. → a) in the afternoon · b) at night · c) on Sunday morning
8. *On weekdays / on Saturday* use ____. → a) *on* · b) *at* · c) *in*
9. *In the morning / afternoon / evening* and *in December* use ____. → a) *in* · b) *on* · c) *at*
10. The listening practices ____. → a) *at / on / in* · b) only *-ly* · c) only *-est*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) at noon** · 7. **a) in the afternoon** · 8. **a) *on*** · 9. **a) *in*** · 10. **a) *at / on / in***

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *At 6 o'clock* = ____. → a) clock time · b) month · c) day name
12. *On weekdays* = ____. → a) working days · b) only August · c) midnight
13. *In the evening* is ____. → a) correct · b) *at the evening* · c) *on the evening*
14. *In December* = ____. → a) month · b) day · c) hour
15. Typical pattern morning→noon→afternoon→evening uses ____. → a) mixed *at/on/in* · b) only *of* · c) only *did*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) clock time** · 12. **a) working days** · 13. **a) correct** · 14. **a) month** · 15. **a) mixed *at/on/in***

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My schedule

**Objetivo:** escribir horarios con *at/on/in*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I wake up ____ 7 o'clock. → *at / on / in*
2. I work ____ Monday. → *on / at / in*
3. I was born ____ 1995. → *in / on / at*
4. We meet ____ the evening. → *in / at / on*
5. The film starts ____ midnight. → *at / on / in*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **at** · 2. **on** · 3. **in** · 4. **in** · 5. **at**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. I / get up / at / 7 o'clock.
7. I / have / a meeting / on / Friday.
8. We / go / on holiday / in / August.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I get up at 7 o'clock.
7. I have a meeting on Friday.
8. We go on holiday in August.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Hora de levantarte (*at*).
10. Día de trabajo o clase (*on*).
11. Parte del día (*in the…*).
12. Mes de vacaciones (*in*).
13. Fin de semana (*at the weekend* / *on Saturday*).
14. Combina día + hora: *on Friday at 3 pm*.
15. Párrafo (6–8 frases) de tu día o semana típica.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. I wake up at 7 o'clock.
10. I work on Monday.
11. I have meetings in the afternoon.
12. I go on holiday in August.
13. At the weekend I relax. / On Saturday I sleep until 10.
14. The meeting is on Friday at 3 pm.
15. I wake up at 6 o'clock in the morning. On weekdays I start work at 9. I have lunch at noon. In the evening I meet friends. At the weekend I relax. In December I go on holiday.

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Prep. | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **at** | horas / momentos | at 7 · at noon · at night |
| **on** | días / fechas | on Monday · on 15 March |
| **in** | meses / años / partes del día | in August · in 1990 · in the morning |
