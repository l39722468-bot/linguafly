## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** will/won't para predicciones, promesas y ofertas.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ help you. → *will / won't / am*
2. She ____ forget. → *won't / willn't / don't*
3. ____ you come to the party? → *Will / Do / Are*
4. The exam ____ be easy. → *will / going / is*
5. I promise I ____ call you. → *will / am / do*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **will** · 2. **won't** · 3. **Will** · 4. **will** · 5. **will**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Form = ____. → a) will/won't + infinitive · b) am will · c) have + PP
7. Promises often use ____. → a) will · b) Past Continuous · c) *ago*
8. *Won't* means ____. → a) will not · b) want · c) went
9. Offers like *I'll help* use ____. → a) will · b) only *must* · c) Present Perfect
10. Questions invert ____. → a) Will + subject · b) Subject + Will always · c) Did + will

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) will/won't + infinitive** · 7. **a) will** · 8. **a) will not** · 9. **a) will** · 10. **a) Will + subject**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I will to help you.*
12. *She wills come tomorrow.*
13. *I won't to forget.*
14. *Will you to come?*
15. *He will helping us.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I will help you.
12. She **will** come tomorrow.
13. I won't forget.
14. Will you come?
15. He will **help** us.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** promise, offer, forget, help, party, probably.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *promise* ≈ ____ → *say you will definitely do it · cancel · sleep*
2. *offer* ≈ ____ → *say you can help · rain · forget forever*
3. *won't* = ____ → *will not · want · went*
4. *preparations* ≈ ____ → *things you do before an event · finished past only · adjectives*
5. *probably* ≈ ____ → *likely · never · yesterday*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **say you will definitely do it** · 2. **say you can help** · 3. **will not** · 4. **things you do before an event** · 5. **likely**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. I will ____ you. → *help / helping / helped*
7. I won't ____. → *forget / forgetting / forgot*
8. I will ____ the drinks. → *buy / buying / bought*
9. The party will ____ great. → *be / being / been*
10. Everyone will ____ fun. → *have / having / had*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **help** · 7. **forget** · 8. **buy** · 9. **be** · 10. **have**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *I promise* pairs well with ____. → a) will · b) Past Perfect · c) *ago*
12. Spontaneous offers often use ____. → a) will · b) only *going to* evidence · c) *was*
13. *Maybe I will…* softens a ____. → a) prediction / possibility · b) obligation · c) past habit
14. After *will* use ____. → a) infinitive · b) *-ing* required · c) PP required
15. Unit 23 focus: ____. → a) will/won't · b) only *for/since* · c) only phrasal verbs

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) will** · 12. **a) will** · 13. **a) prediction / possibility** · 14. **a) infinitive** · 15. **a) will/won't**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Letter to Maria

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-23/reading-will.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Letter to Maria"></audio>

> Dear Maria, I promise I will help you with your homework tomorrow. Don't worry! I will call you at 6 o'clock. I won't forget. Maybe I will bring some cookies too. I think the exam will be easy next week. I will study hard and I won't give up. I promise I will be there for you!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What does the writer promise? → a) Help with homework · b) Cancel the exam · c) Move house
2. When will they call? → a) At 6 o'clock · b) At midnight only · c) Never
3. What won't they do? → a) Forget · b) Study · c) Call
4. What might they bring? → a) Cookies · b) A car · c) Snow
5. What does the writer also promise? → a) To be there for Maria · b) To cancel · c) To move

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Help with homework** · 2. **a) At 6 o'clock** · 3. **a) Forget** · 4. **a) Cookies** · 5. **a) To be there for Maria**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The text practices ____. → a) will/won't promises & predictions · b) only Past Continuous · c) only *into*
7. *I promise* appears: ____. → a) yes · b) never · c) only Unit 1
8. *Won't* is used for ____. → a) negative future · b) Past Simple · c) plural nouns
9. After *will* verbs are ____. → a) infinitives · b) PP only · c) adjectives only
10. Matches unit 23: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Module 6

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) will/won't promises & predictions** · 7. **a) yes** · 8. **a) negative future** · 9. **a) infinitives** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *I will help* needs ____. → a) will + infinitive · b) will + -ing · c) am will
12. *I won't forget* is ____. → a) correct · b) wrong · c) Past Perfect
13. Questions: ____. → a) Will you…? · b) Do you will…? · c) Are you will…?
14. Promises + *will* are ____. → a) natural · b) forbidden · c) only B2
15. Reading helps you ____. → a) notice will/won't in context · b) skip form · c) avoid English

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) will + infinitive** · 12. **a) correct** · 13. **a) Will you…?** · 14. **a) natural** · 15. **a) notice will/won't in context**

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Afirmativa** | I **will** help you. |
| **Negativa** | I **won't** forget. |
| **Usos** | promesas, ofertas, predicciones generales |
| **Vs going to** | *will* = decisión/promesa ahora · *going to* = plan/evidencia |
