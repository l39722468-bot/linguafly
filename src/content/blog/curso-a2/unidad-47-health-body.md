## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 47 (A2)

- Síntomas: **have a headache**, **feel sick**, **fever**, **cold**.
- Partes del cuerpo básicas.
- Consejos: **should / shouldn't**.
- Vocabulario: **medicine**, **pharmacy**, **doctor**.
- Describir un problema de salud y pedir / dar consejo.

## 1. Vocabulario: síntomas y lugares

| Palabra / frase | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **have a headache** | dolor de cabeza | I **have a headache**. |
| **have a cold** | resfriado | She **has a cold**. |
| **have a fever** | fiebre | He **has a fever**. |
| **have a sore throat** | dolor de garganta | I've got a **sore throat**. |
| **feel sick** | mareado / náuseas | I **feel sick**. |
| **feel better / worse** | mejor / peor | Do you **feel better**? |
| **medicine** | medicamento | Take this **medicine**. |
| **pharmacy** | farmacia | Go to the **pharmacy**. |
| **doctor** | médico | You should see a **doctor**. |

También: **cough**, **toothache**, **backache**, **stomach ache**.

## 3. Hablar de problemas de salud

| Estructura | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| I have a + symptom | I **have a headache**. |
| I feel + adjective | I **feel tired** / **sick**. |
| My + body part + hurts | My **throat hurts**. |
| I've got a pain in my… | I've got a **pain in my back**. |
| What's the matter? | **What's the matter?** |

> What's wrong? / What's the matter?  
> I don't feel well.

## 4. Consejos con *should*

| Forma | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **should** + infinitive | consejo | You **should** rest. |
| **shouldn't** + infinitive | no conviene | You **shouldn't** go to work. |
| I think you should… | más suave | I think you **should** see a doctor. |

> You **should** drink water.  
> You **shouldn't** take medicine without advice.

## 6. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura — Medicine label

This **medicine** is for a **cold** or a **sore throat**. Take one tablet every 8 hours with water. **Don't** take more than three tablets a day. If you have a high **fever**, see a **doctor**. Keep out of reach of children. If you feel worse, stop and ask at the **pharmacy**.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-47/reading-label.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Medicine label"></audio>

### Diálogo — At the pharmacy

**Pharmacist:** Hello. How can I help you?  
**Patient:** I **have a headache** and a **sore throat**.  
**Pharmacist:** Do you have a **fever**?  
**Patient:** A little. I also **feel tired**.  
**Pharmacist:** You **should** rest and drink water. Take this **medicine** every 8 hours.  
**Patient:** Thanks. **Should** I see a **doctor**?  
**Pharmacist:** If you feel **worse** tomorrow, yes.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-47/dialogue-pharmacy.mp3" title="🔊 Dialogue: At the pharmacy"></audio>

## 7. Errores frecuentes

1. ~~I have headache.~~ → **I have a headache.**  
2. ~~I feel me sick.~~ → **I feel sick.**  
3. ~~My head pain.~~ → **My head hurts.** / **I've got a headache.**  
4. ~~You should to rest.~~ → **You should rest.**  
5. ~~I am sick in my stomach.~~ → **I have a stomach ache.** / **My stomach hurts.**  
6. ~~What's the problem with you?~~ → más natural: **What's the matter?** / **What's wrong?**

2. fever  
3. pharmacy  
4. sore throat  
5. medicine  

a) high temperature  
b) pain in the head  
c) tablets / treatment  
d) place to buy medicine  
e) pain when you swallow

2 → a  
3 → d  
4 → e  
5 → c

2. You ___ go running with a fever.

2. shouldn't  
3. should  
4. shouldn't  

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Etiqueta de medicamento

Lee y responde True / False:

> Take one tablet every 8 hours. Don't take more than three a day. If you have a high fever, see a doctor.

1. You can take four tablets a day.  
2. One tablet every 8 hours is OK.  
3. A high fever → see a doctor.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. False  
2. True  
3. True  

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu consejo (8–12 líneas)

Describe un problema de salud y da consejos. Incluye:

- 2 síntomas (*have a…* / *feel…*)  
- 1 parte del cuerpo  
- 2 frases con **should / shouldn't**  
- *pharmacy* o *doctor*

**Modelo:** el diálogo de la sección 6.
