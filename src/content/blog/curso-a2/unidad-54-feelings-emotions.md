## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 54 (A2)

- Estructuras: **I feel…**, **I'm…**, **How do you feel?**
- Vocabulario: **excited**, **worried**, **bored**, **disappointed**, **proud**, **nervous**
- Diferencia *-ed* vs *-ing* (*bored / boring*)
- Hablar de emociones en experiencias cotidianas
- Describir cómo te sientes en 5–8 líneas

## 1. Vocabulario de emociones

| Adjetivo | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **excited** | emocionado/a (ilusión) | I'm **excited** about the trip. |
| **worried** | preocupado/a | She's **worried** about the exam. |
| **bored** | aburrido/a (tú) | I'm **bored** at home. |
| **disappointed** | decepcionado/a | He felt **disappointed**. |
| **proud** | orgulloso/a | We're **proud** of you. |
| **nervous** | nervioso/a | I feel **nervous** before interviews. |
| **happy / sad** | feliz / triste | I feel **happy** today. |
| **angry / tired** | enfadado / cansado | He's **angry**. / I'm **tired**. |

## 2. *I feel…* e *I'm…*

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| I feel + adjective | I **feel nervous**. |
| I'm + adjective | **I'm** proud of my team. |
| How do you feel? | **How do you feel** today? |
| I feel like + -ing | I **feel like** staying in. |

> **How are you feeling?** — A bit tired, but OK.  
> I **feel disappointed** about the result.

## 3. *-ed* vs *-ing* (importante)

| Cómo te sientes (*-ed*) | Cómo es la cosa (*-ing*) |
| :--- | :--- |
| I'm **bored**. | The lesson is **boring**. |
| I'm **excited**. | The news is **exciting**. |
| I'm **interested**. | The book is **interesting**. |
| I'm **tired**. | The work is **tiring**. |

> The film was **boring**, so I felt **bored**.

---

## 4. Preposiciones útiles

| Patrón | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| excited **about** | excited about the concert |
| worried **about** | worried about money |
| proud **of** | proud of my sister |
| disappointed **with / about** | disappointed with the service |
| nervous **about** | nervous about the test |

---

## 5. Mapa rápido

| Quieres… | Usa… | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Decir cómo te sientes | I feel / I'm + adj | I feel proud. |
| Preguntar | How do you feel? | How do you feel? |
| Causa | about / of / with | worried about… |
| Emoción vs causa | -ed / -ing | bored / boring |

## 6. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura — Before the interview

Tomorrow I have a job interview. I feel **nervous**, but also **excited**. My mum says I should be **proud** of my CV. Last week I was **disappointed** about another interview, so I'm a bit **worried**. I don't want to feel **bored** waiting all morning — I'll take a book. How do I feel right now? Hopeful!

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-54/reading-interview.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Before the interview"></audio>

### Diálogo — After an exam

**Mia:** How do you **feel**?  
**Omar:** I'm **relieved** — and a bit **tired**.  
**Mia:** Were you **nervous**?  
**Omar:** Yes. I was also **worried** about the listening part.  
**Mia:** You should be **proud**. You studied a lot.  
**Omar:** Thanks. I was **disappointed** last time, so this feels better.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-54/dialogue-exam.mp3" title="🔊 Dialogue: After an exam"></audio>

### Mini práctica

1. I feel ___ about the trip. *(ilusión)*  
2. I'm ___ about the exam. *(preocupado)*  
3. How do you ___ ?  
4. The film is ___ , so I feel ___. *(aburrido / me aburro)*

## 7. Errores frecuentes

1. ~~I am boring.~~ *(si te aburres)* → **I'm bored.**  
2. ~~I feel me happy.~~ → **I feel happy.**  
3. ~~I'm excite.~~ → **I'm excited.**  
4. ~~proud for you~~ → **proud of you**  
5. ~~worried of the exam~~ → **worried about** the exam  
6. ~~How you feel?~~ → **How do you feel?**

## 8. Ejercicios (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Empareja

1. excited  
2. worried  
3. bored  
4. proud  
5. disappointed  

a) not happy with the result  
b) looking forward / full of energy about something  
c) thinking something bad may happen  
d) nothing interesting to do  
e) happy about someone's success (including yours)

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1 → b  
2 → c  
3 → d  
4 → e  
5 → a  

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa con la emoción

1. The test is tomorrow → I feel ___.  
2. My team won → I'm ___.  
3. Nothing to do at home → I'm ___.  
4. The concert is next week → I'm ___ about it.  
5. We lost the final → I felt ___.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. nervous / worried  
2. proud / happy / excited  
3. bored  
4. excited  
5. disappointed / sad  

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — *-ed* o *-ing*

1. I'm ___ (bore). The lesson is ___ (bore).  
2. She's ___ (excite) about the news. The news is ___ (excite).  
3. This job is ___ (tire). I feel ___ (tire).

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. bored / boring  
2. excited / exciting  
3. tiring / tired  

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Preposiciones

1. excited ___ the party  
2. worried ___ the results  
3. proud ___ my brother  
4. nervous ___ flying

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. about  
2. about  
3. of  
4. about  

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Corrige el error

1. I am boring today.  
2. I feel me sad.  
3. I'm proud for you.  
4. How you feel?  
5. I'm worry about money.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. I'm bored today.  
2. I feel sad.  
3. I'm proud of you.  
4. How do you feel?  
5. I'm worried about money.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Traduce al inglés

1. Estoy emocionado/a por el viaje.  
2. Me siento nervioso/a.  
3. Estoy preocupado/a por el examen.  
4. Estamos orgullosos de ti.  
5. ¿Cómo te sientes?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. I'm excited about the trip. / I feel excited about the trip.  
2. I feel nervous. / I'm nervous.  
3. I'm worried about the exam.  
4. We're proud of you.  
5. How do you feel?

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Completa el diálogo

**A:** How do you ___ ?  
**B:** A bit ___ about the interview.  
**A:** Don't worry — you should be ___ of your work.  
**B:** Thanks. Last time I was ___ , so I want this to go well.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

feel  
nervous / worried  
proud  
disappointed  

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Ordena

1. feel / I / nervous  
2. about / worried / I'm / the / exam  
3. of / proud / We're / you

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. I feel nervous.  
2. I'm worried about the exam.  
3. We're proud of you.  

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Elige la mejor opción

1. The film was ___, so I left.  
   a) bored  b) boring  c) bore  
2. I'm ___ about my driving test.  
   a) nervous  b) nervousing  c) nervously  
3. She's ___ of her daughter.  
   a) proud  b) pride  c) proudly

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. b  
2. a  
3. a  

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Cómo te sientes (8–12 líneas)

Describe un momento reciente. Incluye:

- **I feel / I'm** + 3 emociones  
- 1 preposición (*about / of*)  
- 1 contraste *-ed / -ing*  
- **How do you feel?** (pregunta a alguien) o respuesta

**Modelo:** la lectura de la sección 6.

## 9. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Vocabulario + *I feel / I'm*  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–6.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha lectura y diálogo.  
4. **5–8 min** — Ejercicio 10.

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *I feel…* / *I'm…* con emociones  
- [ ] Distingo *bored / boring*, *excited / exciting*  
- [ ] Uso *about / of* correctamente  
- [ ] Pregunto *How do you feel?*  
- [ ] Evito *I am boring* e *I feel me…*

## 10. Consejos y siguiente paso
