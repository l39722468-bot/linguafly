## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** look like (apariencia); What's … like? (carácter); have known + for/since

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. She ___ like her mother. → *looks / is look / looking*

2. What's he ___ ? *(carácter)* → *like / look / likes*

3. I've ___ him for two years. → *known / knew / know*

4. He's ___ than his brother. *(friendly)* → *friendlier / more friendly / friendlyer*

5. We ___ on well. → *get / gets / getting*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **looks** · 2. **like** · 3. **known** · 4. **friendlier** · 5. **get**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *Look like* = apariencia: ____. → a) correcto · b) What does she like? (gustos) · c) How is she like?

7. *What's she like?* = carácter: ____. → a) correcto · b) apariencia física · c) precio

8. *I've known her for 3 years* = ____. → a) Present Perfect + for · b) Past only · c) will

9. *Get on well* = ____. → a) llevarse bien · b) weather · c) checkout

10. Unit 49 follows ____. → a) Clothes U48 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U50

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) correcto** · 7. **a) correcto** · 8. **a) Present Perfect + for** · 9. **a) llevarse bien** · 10. **a) Clothes U48**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *How is she like?*

12. *She is look like her mum.*

13. *I know her for 10 years.*

14. *He is more tall than me.*

15. *We have a good relation.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **What's she like?**
12. She **looks like** her mum.
13. I've **known** her for 10 years.
14. He's **taller than** me.
15. We have a good **relationship**.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** friend, colleague, neighbour, friendly, funny, kind, get on well

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *neighbour* ≈ ____ → *vecino/a · colega · amigo*

2. *colleague* ≈ ____ → *compañero de trabajo · vecino · turista*

3. *friendly* ≈ ____ → *simpático/a · tímido · alto*

4. *honest* ≈ ____ → *honesto · divertido · callado*

5. *shy* ≈ ____ → *tímido · trabajador · amable*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **vecino/a** · 2. **compañero de trabajo** · 3. **simpático/a** · 4. **honesto** · 5. **tímido**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

7. He's a ___ from work. → *colleague / checkout / concert*

8. Maya is my ___. → *neighbour / pharmacy / platform*

9. She's very ___ . *(kind)* → *kind / kinder only wrong · kind of*

10. He's ___-working. → *hard / hardly / harder*

6. **friend** · 7. **colleague** · 8. **neighbour** · 9. **kind** · 10. **hard**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: My neighbour Maya

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-49/reading-people.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: My neighbour Maya"></audio>

> Maya is my **neighbour**. I've **known** her **for** three years. She **looks like** her mother — same smile and dark hair. What's she **like**? She's **friendly**, **funny** and very **kind**. We **get on well**. She's a bit **quieter** than me, but we talk every weekend over coffee.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Maya is Leo's ___. → a) neighbour · b) checkout · c) platform

2. Known her for ___ years. → a) three · b) thirty · c) zero

3. Looks like her ___. → a) mother · b) museum · c) movie

4. She's friendly, funny and ___. → a) kind · b) cloudy · c) cheaper

5. They get on ___. → a) well · b) sick · c) sunny

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) neighbour** · 2. **a) three** · 3. **a) mother** · 4. **a) kind** · 5. **a) well**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Quieter ___ me. → a) than · b) to · c) over

7. Talk every weekend over ___. → a) coffee · b) train · c) receipt

8. *Looks like* = ____. → a) parecerse físicamente · b) carácter · c) precio

9. *Get on well* = ____. → a) buena relación · b) only Past · c) only will

10. Matches unit 49: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) than** · 7. **a) coffee** · 8. **a) parecerse físicamente** · 9. **a) buena relación** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *I've known her for three years* = ____. → a) tiempo con for · b) weather · c) check in

12. *What's she like?* = ____. → a) carácter · b) solo apariencia · c) only Who

13. *Quieter than me* = ____. → a) comparativo · b) superlativo · c) cheaper

14. Maya text uses ____. → a) look like / be like / known · b) only did · c) only phrasals

15. People writing uses ____. → a) relationships structures · b) only articles · c) only should

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) tiempo con for** · 12. **a) carácter** · 13. **a) comparativo** · 14. **a) look like / be like / known** · 15. **a) relationships structures**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Talking about a colleague

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-49/listening-people.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Talking about a colleague"></audio>

> **Leo:** Have you met the new **colleague**?
> **Nora:** Yes. What's he **like**?
> **Leo:** He's **hard-working** and **honest**. A bit **shy** at first.
> **Nora:** What does he **look like**?
> **Leo:** Tall, short hair. He **looks like** my cousin, actually.
> **Nora:** How long have you **known** him?
> **Leo:** Only **for** two weeks — but we already **get on** well.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Talking about a new ___. → a) colleague · b) checkout · c) forecast

2. He's hard-working and ___. → a) honest · b) sunny · c) cheaper

3. A bit ___ at first. → a) shy · b) sick · c) windy

4. Looks like Leo's ___. → a) cousin · b) concert · c) gate

5. Known him for two ___. → a) weeks · b) years only · c) degrees

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) colleague** · 2. **a) honest** · 3. **a) shy** · 4. **a) cousin** · 5. **a) weeks**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Hair: short ___. → a) hair · b) rains · c) receipts

7. They already get on ___. → a) well · b) worse · c) foggy

8. *What does he look like?* = ____. → a) apariencia · b) carácter · c) precio

9. *How long have you known him?* = ____. → a) Present Perfect · b) only Past · c) will

10. Unit 49 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) hair** · 7. **a) well** · 8. **a) apariencia** · 9. **a) Present Perfect** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *What's he like?* → a) carácter · b) look like · c) prefer than

12. *He looks like my cousin* → a) apariencia · b) carácter · c) arrive in

13. *Only for two weeks* → a) for + period · b) since only wrong here · c) listen music

14. Shadow and note ____. → a) look like / be like / known · b) only some · c) only Who

15. U49 next unit is ____. → a) Module 5 Review U50 · b) Module 6 only · c) U48 skip

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) carácter** · 12. **a) apariencia** · 13. **a) for + period** · 14. **a) look like / be like / known** · 15. **a) Module 5 Review U50**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. She ___ like her sister. → *looks / is look / looking*

2. What's she ___ ? → *like / look / likes*

3. I've ___ him since 2020. → *known / knew / know*

4. He's my ___ from work. → *colleague / neighbour only wrong · friend*

5. We get on ___. → *well / good / better*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **looks** · 2. **like** · 3. **known** · 4. **colleague** · 5. **well**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. like / looks / she / her / mother

7. known / I've / her / for / years / three

8. on / we / get / well

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. She looks like her mother.
7. I've known her for three years.
8. We get on well.

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Una frase con *look like*.

10. Una frase con *What's … like?* (carácter).

11. Una frase con *I've known … for/since*.

12. Una frase con *friend*, *colleague* o *neighbour*.

13. Una frase con un adjetivo de personalidad.

14. Corrige: *I know her for 10 years.*

15. Describe a alguien que conoces (8–10 líneas) con estructuras de personas.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. She looks like her mother. / He looks like my cousin.
10. What's she like? She's friendly and funny.
11. I've known her for three years. / I've known him since 2019.
12. Maya is my neighbour. / He's a colleague from work.
13. She's kind and hard-working. / He's shy but honest.
14. I've **known** her for 10 years.
15. Maya is my neighbour. I've known her for three years. She looks like her mother. What's she like? She's friendly, funny and very kind. We get on well. She's quieter than me, but we talk every weekend over coffee.

</details>
