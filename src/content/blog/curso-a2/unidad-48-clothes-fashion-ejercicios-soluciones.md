## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** wear / put on / take off; too + adj; adj + enough; casual / formal

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I'm ___ a blue dress today. → *wearing / putting / taking*

2. ___ on your coat. → *Put / Wear / Take*

3. ___ off your shoes, please. → *Take / Put / Wear*

4. This shirt is ___ small. → *too / enough / very much*

5. This coat is warm ___. → *enough / too / very*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **wearing** · 2. **Put** · 3. **Take** · 4. **too** · 5. **enough**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *Put on* = acción de ponerse: ____. → a) correcto · b) I put a jacket (solo) · c) I wear on

7. *Too small* (no *too much small*): ____. → a) correcto · b) too much small · c) enough small

8. *Wear* = llevar puesto: ____. → a) estado · b) solo acción · c) weather

9. *Warm enough* = ____. → a) suficientemente abrigado · b) demasiado calor · c) precio

10. Unit 48 follows ____. → a) Health U47 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U49

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) correcto** · 7. **a) correcto** · 8. **a) estado** · 9. **a) suficientemente abrigado** · 10. **a) Health U47**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I put a jacket.*

12. *This is enough big.*

13. *Too much small.*

14. *I wear on a hat.*

15. *It fits to me.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I **put on** a jacket. / I'm **wearing** a jacket.
12. This is **big enough**.
13. **Too small**.
14. I **put on** a hat. / I **wear** a hat.
15. It **fits** me. / It fits.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** outfit, casual, formal, pattern, size, fit, scarf, trainers

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *outfit* ≈ ____ → *conjunto/look · talla · estampado*

2. *casual* ≈ ____ → *informal · formal · demasiado*

3. *formal* ≈ ____ → *elegante/de trabajo · playa · fiebre*

4. *pattern* ≈ ____ → *estampado · material · probador*

5. *scarf* ≈ ____ → *bufanda · cinturón · zapato*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Dress code tip

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-48/reading-clothes.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Dress code tip"></audio>

> For the office, wear a **smart** or **formal outfit**: a shirt, dark trousers and clean shoes. Avoid **casual** clothes like old jeans or trainers. If it's cold, **put on** a jacket. Check that everything **fits** well — not **too tight** and not **too loose**. A simple bag and a belt are enough accessories.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Office: wear ___ or formal outfit. → a) smart · b) sunny · c) cheaper

2. Avoid ___ clothes like old jeans. → a) casual · b) formal only · c) pharmacy

3. If cold, ___ on a jacket. → a) put · b) wear on · c) take

4. Not too tight and not too ___. → a) loose · b) sunny · c) sick

5. Bag and belt are enough ___. → a) accessories · b) symptoms · c) forecasts

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) smart** · 2. **a) casual** · 3. **a) put** · 4. **a) loose** · 5. **a) accessories**

</details>

<details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

## Lección 4 — Listening: Choosing an outfit

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-48/listening-clothes.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Choosing an outfit"></audio>

> **Sam:** What should I **wear** to the party?
> **Alex:** Something **smart casual**. Not too formal.
> **Sam:** Are these jeans OK?
> **Alex:** Yes — but that shirt is **too bright**. Try the blue one.
> **Sam:** Is this jacket **warm enough**?
> **Alex:** Perfect. **Put on** those black shoes and you're ready.

4. Shirt is too ___. → a) bright · b) sunny · c) cheaper

5. Put on black ___. → a) shoes · b) receipts · c) dramas

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I'm ___ jeans today. → *wearing / putting / taking*

2. ___ on your jumper. → *Put / Wear / Take*

3. This T-shirt is ___ big. → *too / enough / very much*

4. These shoes aren't comfortable ___. → *enough / too / very*

5. I love your ___. → *outfit / outfitting / outfits to*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **wearing** · 2. **Put** · 3. **too** · 4. **enough** · 5. **outfit**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. wearing / I'm / a / red / jumper

7. on / Put / your / coat

8. small / is / This / too / shirt

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I'm wearing a red jumper.
7. Put on your coat.
8. This shirt is too small.

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Una frase con *wear* o *I'm wearing…*.

10. Una frase con *put on* o *take off*.

11. Una frase con *too + adjetivo*.

12. Una frase con *adj + enough*.

13. Una frase con *casual* o *formal*.

14. Corrige: *I put a jacket.*

15. Describe tu outfit de hoy (8–10 líneas) con estructuras de ropa.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. I'm wearing jeans. / I wear a coat in winter.
10. Put on your coat. / Take off your shoes.
11. This shirt is too small. / That shirt is too bright.
12. This jacket is warm enough. / Is this smart enough for work?
13. Jeans are casual. / A suit is formal.
14. I **put on** a jacket. / I'm **wearing** a jacket.
15. Today I'm wearing a casual outfit: a white T-shirt, blue jeans and black trainers. I put on a jacket because it's cold. The jacket is warm enough. My shoes are comfortable, not too tight. I love this simple outfit.

</details>
