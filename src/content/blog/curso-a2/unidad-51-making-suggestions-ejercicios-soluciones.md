## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** Shall we…? / Let's… / Why don't we…? / How about + -ing/noun; Would you like to…?

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ___ we go to the park? → *Shall / Do / Am*

2. ___ have lunch. → *Let's / Let / Lets to*

3. Why ___ we watch a film? → *don't / doesn't / not*

4. How about ___ (meet) at 6? → *meeting / meet / to meet*

5. Would you like ___ (come)? → *to come / come / coming*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Shall** · 2. **Let's** · 3. **don't** · 4. **meeting** · 5. **to come**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *Let's go* (no *Let's to go*): ____. → a) correcto · b) Let's to go · c) Let we go

7. *How about + -ing*: ____. → a) correcto · b) How about to go · c) How about go

8. *Would you like to* = ____. → a) invitación · b) precio · c) only Past

9. *Why don't we* = ____. → a) sugerencia suave · b) petición · c) opinión

10. Unit 51 follows ____. → a) Module 5 Review U50 · b) Module 4 only · c) skip U52

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) correcto** · 7. **a) correcto** · 8. **a) invitación** · 9. **a) sugerencia suave** · 10. **a) Module 5 Review U50**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *Let's to watch a film.*

12. *Shall we to leave?*

13. *How about to meet at 5?*

14. *Why we don't go?*

15. *Would you like come?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. Let's watch a film.
12. Shall we leave?
13. How about meeting at 5?
14. Why don't we go?
15. Would you like to come?

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** go for a walk, have a coffee, see a film, hang out, stay in, meet up

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *go for a walk* ≈ ____ → *dar un paseo · quedarse en casa · ver una serie*

2. *have a coffee* ≈ ____ → *tomar un café · ir al cine · llevar bocadillos*

3. *see a film* ≈ ____ → *ver una película · ir a la playa · tren*

4. *stay in* ≈ ____ → *quedarse en casa · salir · invitar*

5. *meet up* ≈ ____ → *quedar / encontrarse · llover · rechazar*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **dar un paseo** · 2. **tomar un café** · 3. **ver una película** · 4. **quedarse en casa** · 5. **quedar / encontrarse**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. Shall we ___ at 5? → *meet up / meeting up / meet to*

7. Why don't we ___ the train? → *take / takes / taking*

8. How about ___ Lara? → *inviting / invite / to invite*

9. Let's ___ in tonight. → *stay / staying / to stay*

10. Would you like to ___ some sandwiches? → *bring / brings / bringing*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **meet up** · 7. **take** · 8. **inviting** · 9. **stay** · 10. **bring**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Shall we* for plans: ____. → a) yes · b) only offers · c) only opinions

12. Beach plan if weather good: ____. → a) Saturday · b) receipt · c) platform

13. Train at ____. → a) 9 · b) 19 · c) midnight only

14. If rains → ___ in. → a) stay · b) checkout · c) refund

15. Unit 51 next is ____. → a) Offers U52 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U52

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) yes** · 12. **a) Saturday** · 13. **a) 9** · 14. **a) stay** · 15. **a) Offers U52**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Weekend plans

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-51/reading-suggestions.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Weekend plans"></audio>

> Shall we do something this weekend? Let's go to the beach on Saturday if the weather is good. Why don't we take the train at 9? How about inviting Lara too? Would you like to bring some sandwiches? If it rains, let's stay in and watch a series instead.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Shall we do something this ___. → a) weekend · b) receipt · c) platform

2. Let's go to the ___ on Saturday. → a) beach · b) pharmacy · c) checkout

3. Why don't we take the ___ at 9? → a) train · b) drama · c) gate

4. How about inviting ___ too? → a) Lara · b) refund · c) forecast

5. If it rains → ___ in. → a) stay · b) go out · c) exchange

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) weekend** · 2. **a) beach** · 3. **a) train** · 4. **a) Lara** · 5. **a) stay**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Would you like to bring some ___. → a) sandwiches · b) receipts · c) dramas

7. Watch a ___ if it rains. → a) series · b) platform · c) degree

8. *Shall we* in text = ____. → a) sugerencia · b) opinión · c) precio

9. *How about inviting* = ____. → a) How about + -ing · b) How about to · c) prefer than

10. Matches unit 51: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) sandwiches** · 7. **a) series** · 8. **a) sugerencia** · 9. **a) How about + -ing** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Let's go to the beach* = ____. → a) plan · b) weather only · c) shopping

12. *Why don't we take the train* = ____. → a) transporte · b) only health · c) only Who

13. *Would you like to bring* = ____. → a) invitación · b) I agree · c) recycle

14. Text uses ____. → a) suggestion structures · b) only did · c) only phrasals

15. Suggestion writing uses ____. → a) Shall we / Let's / How about · b) only articles · c) only should

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) plan** · 12. **a) transporte** · 13. **a) invitación** · 14. **a) suggestion structures** · 15. **a) Shall we / Let's / How about**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Making plans

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-51/listening-suggestions.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Making plans"></audio>

> Eva: Shall we go to the cinema tonight?
> Nico: I'd love to, but I finish work late.
> Eva: How about tomorrow?
> Nico: Good idea. Would you like to meet at 7?
> Eva: Sure. Let's have a coffee first.
> Nico: Perfect.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Eva suggests the ___. → a) cinema · b) beach · c) train station only

2. Nico finishes work ___. → a) late · b) early always · c) never

3. They meet at ___. → a) 7 · b) 9 · c) midnight

4. Eva suggests ___. → a) tomorrow · b) last week · c) never

5. First they ___ have a coffee. → a) Let's · b) Should · c) Must

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) cinema** · 2. **a) late** · 3. **a) 7** · 4. **a) tomorrow** · 5. **a) Let's**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Nico says *I'd love to, but* = ____. → a) rechazo educado · b) I agree · c) precio

7. *How about tomorrow?* = ____. → a) alternativa · b) opinión · c) weather

8. *Would you like to meet at 7?* = ____. → a) invitación · b) recycle · c) There is

9. Dialogue uses ____. → a) suggestions · b) only opinions · c) only linking

10. Unit 51 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) rechazo educado** · 7. **a) alternativa** · 8. **a) invitación** · 9. **a) suggestions** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Shall we go to the cinema* → a) Shall we…? · b) Let's to · c) How about to

12. *Let's have a coffee first* → a) Let's + base · b) Let's to · c) I am agree

13. *Good idea* → a) aceptar · b) I disagree · c) checkout

14. Shadow and note ____. → a) suggestion forms · b) only some · c) only Who

15. U51 next unit is ____. → a) Offers U52 · b) Module 6 only · c) U50 skip

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) Shall we…?** · 12. **a) Let's + base** · 13. **a) aceptar** · 14. **a) suggestion forms** · 15. **a) Offers U52**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ___ we go out? → *Shall / Do / Am*

2. ___ have pizza. → *Let's / Let / Lets to*

3. How about ___ (go) for a walk? → *going / go / to go*

4. Why ___ we call her? → *don't / doesn't / not*

5. Would you like ___ (join) us? → *to join / join / joining*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **Shall** · 2. **Let's** · 3. **going** · 4. **don't** · 5. **to join**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. we / Shall / go / ?

7. about / How / a / coffee / ?

8. like / Would / you / to / come / ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. Shall we go?
7. How about a coffee?
8. Would you like to come?

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Una sugerencia con *Shall we…?*.

10. Una sugerencia con *Let's…*.

11. Una sugerencia con *How about…?*.

12. Una invitación con *Would you like to…?*.

13. Una respuesta de aceptación.

14. Corrige: *Let's to go.*

15. Escribe un diálogo de planes (8–10 líneas) con sugerencias y respuestas.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. Shall we go to the cinema?
10. Let's have lunch.
11. How about meeting at 6?
12. Would you like to come with us?
13. Good idea! / Sure. / I'd love to.
14. Let's go.
15. A: Shall we go to the cinema tonight? B: I'd love to, but I finish work late. A: How about tomorrow? B: Good idea. Would you like to meet at 7? A: Sure. Let's have a coffee first. B: Perfect.

</details>
