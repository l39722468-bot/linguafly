## Lección 1 — Gramática: to be y Present Simple

**Objetivo:** *I am / you are / he-she is* y *live / lives*, *teach / teaches*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa (*to be*)

1. I ____ from Spain. → *am / is / are*  
2. You ____ a student. → *are / am / is*  
3. She ____ twenty-eight years old. → *is / am / are*  
4. My hometown ____ Madrid. → *is / are / am*  
5. I ____ happy to be here. → *am / is / are*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **am**  
2. **are**  
3. **is**  
4. **is**  
5. **am**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa (Present Simple)

6. I ____ in a quiet neighbourhood. → *live / lives / living*  
7. She ____ in a small flat. → *lives / live / living*  
8. He ____ in London. → *lives / live / is live*  
9. They ____ near the beach. → *live / lives / living*  
10. Maria ____ English at a small school. → *teaches / teach / teaching*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **live**  
7. **lives** — 3.ª persona singular.  
8. **lives**  
9. **live**  
10. **teaches**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *You am a teacher.*  
12. *She live near the beach.*  
13. *My hometown are Madrid.*  
14. *My favourite hobby is play football.*  
15. *I live in quiet neighbourhood.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. You **are** a teacher.  
12. She **lives** near the beach.  
13. My hometown **is** Madrid.  
14. My favourite hobby is **playing** football.  
15. I live in **a** quiet neighbourhood.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: hometown y hobbies

**Objetivo:** *hometown, neighbourhood, hobby, favourite, Nice to meet you*.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja

1. hometown  
2. neighbourhood  
3. hobby  
4. favourite  
5. Nice to meet you  

a) pasatiempo · b) favorito · c) ciudad natal · d) primera vez · e) barrio

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **c** · 2 → **e** · 3 → **a** · 4 → **b** · 5 → **d**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. My ____ is Madrid. → *hometown / hobby / teacher*  
7. I live in a quiet ____. → *neighbourhood / favourite / age*  
8. My favourite ____ is reading. → *hobby / hometown / am*  
9. My ____ colour is blue. → *favourite / neighbourhood / teach*  
10. Nice to ____ you! → *meet / live / teach*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **hometown**  
7. **neighbourhood**  
8. **hobby**  
9. **favourite**  
10. **meet**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Hometown* = ____. → a) the city where you grew up · b) your job · c) your age  
12. *Neighbourhood* = ____. → a) the area where you live · b) a country · c) a school subject  
13. *Hobby is* + ____. → a) *-ing* / noun · b) past tense · c) *to* + base only  
14. *Nice to meet you* is for ____. → a) the first time · b) every morning with friends · c) only emails  
15. *I am a student* tells ____. → a) occupation · b) hometown · c) colour

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) the city where you grew up**  
12. **a) the area where you live**  
13. **a) *-ing* / noun**  
14. **a) the first time**  
15. **a) occupation**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Hello! My name is Maria

**Objetivo:** leer una presentación A2 completa.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-1/reading-intro.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Hello! My name is Maria"></audio>

> Hello! My name is Maria. I am twenty-eight years old. My hometown is Madrid, Spain. I live in a quiet neighbourhood with many parks and cafes. My favourite hobby is reading books. I also love walking in my neighbourhood. I am a teacher. I teach English at a small school. Nice to meet you!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. How old is Maria? → a) Twenty-eight · b) Twenty-five · c) Thirty  
2. Where is her hometown? → a) Madrid · b) Barcelona · c) London  
3. What is her favourite hobby? → a) Reading books · b) Football · c) Cooking only  
4. What is her job? → a) Teacher · b) Student · c) Doctor  
5. Does she close with *Nice to meet you*? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Twenty-eight**  
2. **a) Madrid**  
3. **a) Reading books**  
4. **a) Teacher**  
5. **a) Yes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Her neighbourhood has ____. → a) parks and cafes · b) only a beach · c) no parks  
7. She also loves ____. → a) walking in her neighbourhood · b) flying · c) skiing only  
8. She teaches English at ____. → a) a small school · b) a big hospital · c) a museum  
9. *Hometown* in the text = ____. → a) Madrid, Spain · b) Barcelona · c) London  
10. The main topic is ____. → a) Maria's introduction · b) a trip to London · c) past holidays

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) parks and cafes**  
7. **a) walking in her neighbourhood**  
8. **a) a small school**  
9. **a) Madrid, Spain**  
10. **a) Maria's introduction**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *I am a teacher* uses ____. → a) *to be* · b) Past Simple · c) *will*  
12. *I teach English* uses ____. → a) Present Simple · b) Past Continuous · c) *going to*  
13. *Quiet neighbourhood* needs ____. → a) *a* · b) no article · c) *any*  
14. *Favourite hobby is reading* uses ____. → a) *-ing* · b) past *-ed* · c) *to read* only  
15. *Nice to meet you* is a ____. → a) closing greeting · b) past time marker · c) superlative

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) *to be***  
12. **a) Present Simple**  
13. **a) *a***  
14. **a) *-ing***  
15. **a) closing greeting**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Hi! My name is Carlos

**Objetivo:** escuchar una presentación A2 y comparar con Maria.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-1/listening-intro.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Hi! My name is Carlos"></audio>

> Hi! My name is Carlos. I am from Barcelona, Spain. I am twenty-five years old. My hometown is Barcelona. I live in a nice neighbourhood near the beach. My favourite hobby is playing football. I am a student. I study at the university. I am happy to be here. Nice to meet you!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Where is Carlos from? → a) Barcelona · b) Madrid · c) Valencia  
2. How old is he? → a) Twenty-five · b) Twenty-eight · c) Thirty  
3. Where does he live? → a) Near the beach · b) Near a hospital · c) In London  
4. What is his favourite hobby? → a) Playing football · b) Reading books · c) Cooking  
5. Is he a student or a teacher? → a) Student · b) Teacher · c) Doctor

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Barcelona**  
2. **a) Twenty-five**  
3. **a) Near the beach**  
4. **a) Playing football**  
5. **a) Student**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. He studies at ____. → a) the university · b) a small school · c) home only  
7. He says ____. → a) I am happy to be here · b) Goodbye forever · c) See you yesterday  
8. Compared with Maria, Carlos is ____. → a) a student · b) a teacher · c) older than Maria  
9. His neighbourhood is ____. → a) nice / near the beach · b) quiet with parks · c) not mentioned  
10. The listening is about ____. → a) Carlos's introduction · b) past irregular verbs · c) weather

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) the university**  
7. **a) I am happy to be here**  
8. **a) a student**  
9. **a) nice / near the beach**  
10. **a) Carlos's introduction**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Playing football* after *hobby is* is ____. → a) correct · b) needs *play* · c) past tense  
12. *I study* is ____. → a) Present Simple · b) Past Simple · c) Future  
13. *I am from Barcelona* uses ____. → a) *to be* · b) *did* · c) *will*  
14. *Nice to meet you* appears ____. → a) at the end · b) only in the middle · c) never  
15. A2 introductions add ____. → a) hometown, neighbourhood, hobby · b) only name · c) only past verbs

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) correct**  
12. **a) Present Simple**  
13. **a) *to be***  
14. **a) at the end**  
15. **a) hometown, neighbourhood, hobby**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: My introduction

**Objetivo:** escribir tu presentación A2.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. My ____ is Valencia. → *hometown / meet / teach*  
2. I live in a quiet ____. → *neighbourhood / ago / yesterday*  
3. My favourite ____ is swimming. → *hobby / bill / fever*  
4. I ____ a student. → *am / is / are*  
5. Nice to ____ you! → *meet / live / walk*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **hometown**  
2. **neighbourhood**  
3. **hobby**  
4. **am**  
5. **meet**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. My / hometown / is / Madrid.  
7. I / live / in / a quiet neighbourhood.  
8. My / favourite hobby / is / reading.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. My hometown is Madrid.  
7. I live in a quiet neighbourhood.  
8. My favourite hobby is reading.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Di tu nombre y edad.  
10. Di tu *hometown*.  
11. Describe tu *neighbourhood* en una frase.  
12. Di tu *favourite hobby*.  
13. Di si eres *student* o tu trabajo.  
14. Cierra con *Nice to meet you!* o *I am happy to be here!*  
15. Párrafo completo (8–10 frases) con todos los puntos anteriores.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. Hello! My name is Ana. I am twenty-two years old.  
10. My hometown is Valencia.  
11. I live in a quiet neighbourhood.  
12. My favourite hobby is swimming.  
13. I am a student. / I am a teacher.  
14. Nice to meet you! / I am happy to be here!  
15. Hello! My name is Ana. I am twenty-two years old. My hometown is Valencia. I live in a quiet neighbourhood. My favourite hobby is swimming. I am a student. I study at the university. Nice to meet you!

</details>
