En la [Unidad 12](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-12-present-perfect-ever-never) preguntaste *Have you ever…?*. En la **Unidad 13 del curso A2** (*Present Perfect: Already & Yet*) hablas de **progreso**: lo que **ya** has hecho y lo que **aún no**.

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 13 — Present Perfect: Already & Yet](/curso-a2/unit-13)  
> **Antes:** [U12 — Ever & Never](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-12-present-perfect-ever-never)

---

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 13 (A2)

- Usar **already** en afirmativas: *I have already finished…*
- Usar **yet** en negativas: *I haven't… yet*
- Preguntar con **yet**: *Have you… yet? / Has she… yet?*
- Colocar bien las palabras (*already* en medio · *yet* al final).
- Hablar de tareas y obligaciones del día.

![Already y yet Present Perfect A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-13/a2-unit13-already-yet.png)

---

## 1. Idea clave

| Palabra | Significado A2 | Tipo de frase |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **already** | ya (antes de ahora / antes de lo esperado) | afirmativa |
| **yet** | aún / todavía (no ha pasado hasta ahora) | negativa e interrogativa |

No confundas con *ever/never* (experiencias de vida). Aquí: **lista de tareas**.

---

## 2. *Already* en afirmativas

**Already** va **entre** *have/has* y el past participle:

> I **have already** finished my homework.  
> He **has already** eaten breakfast.  
> I **have already** cleaned my room.  
> I **have already** done my chores.

Estructura: **have/has + already + past participle**

---

## 3. *Yet* en negativas

**Yet** va **al final** de la negativa:

> I **haven't** done the shopping **yet**.  
> I **haven't** finished **yet**.  
> She **hasn't** called **yet**.  
> I **haven't** paid the electricity bill **yet**.  
> I **haven't** sent the emails **yet**.

Estructura: **haven't/hasn't + past participle + … + yet**

---

## 4. *Yet* en preguntas

También al **final**:

> **Has she cleaned** her room **yet**?  
> **Have you washed** the dishes **yet**?  
> Have you finished yet?  
> Has he called yet?

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-13/has-she-cleaned-yet.mp3" title="🔊 Has she cleaned her room yet?"></audio>

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-13/have-you-washed-yet.mp3" title="🔊 Have you washed the dishes yet?"></audio>

Respuestas típicas:

- *Yes, I have.* / *Yes, she has.* *(a menudo + already)*  
- *No, I haven't.* / *No, not yet.*

---

## 5. Posición: *already* vs *yet*

![Posición already y yet](/blog/curso-a2/unit-13/a2-unit13-word-position.png)

| Palabra | Posición prioritaria A2 | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **already** | entre *have/has* y el participio | I have **already** finished… |
| **yet** | al final | I haven't finished **yet**. / Have you finished **yet**? |

| Correcto | Incorrecto / menos prioritario A2 |
| :--- | :--- |
| I have already finished. | *I already have finished.* |
| I haven't finished yet. | *I haven't yet finished.* |
| Have you finished yet? | *Have you yet finished?* |

I must do that before dinner. I also haven't called my boss yet. He is waiting for my call. I feel relaxed because I have already completed most of my tasks for today.

## 10. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I already have finished. | I have **already** finished. | *already* tras *have* |
| I haven't yet finished. *(prioridad A2)* | I haven't finished **yet**. | *yet* al final |
| I have finished yet. | I have **already** finished. / I haven't finished **yet**. | *yet* no va en afirmativas A2 |
| I haven't already finished. | I haven't finished **yet**. | negativa → *yet* |
| Have you already finished yet? | Have you finished **yet**? / Have you **already** finished? | no mezclar las dos |
| Did you finish yet? *(si quieres Present Perfect A2)* | **Have** you finished **yet**? | Present Perfect + *yet* |

## 11. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — *already* o *yet*

1. I have ____ finished my homework.  
2. I haven't done the shopping ____.  
3. Has she cleaned her room ____?  
4. He has ____ eaten breakfast.  
5. Have you washed the dishes ____?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **already** · 2. **yet** · 3. **yet** · 4. **already** · 5. **yet**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Posición

Elige la correcta:

1. ____  
   a) I already have finished. · b) I have already finished. · c) I have finished already. *(prioridad A2: mid)*  
2. ____  
   a) I haven't finished yet. · b) I haven't yet finished. · c) I yet haven't finished.  
3. ____  
   a) Have you yet washed the dishes? · b) Have you washed the dishes yet? · c) Yet have you washed the dishes?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b** · 2. **a** · 3. **b**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Completa a Sofia

I have ____ cleaned my room and done the washing. I have ____ finished my homework. But I haven't paid the electricity bill ____. I also haven't called my grandmother ____.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**already** · **already** · **yet** · **yet**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Completa a Lucas

Today I have ____ done my chores and finished the shopping. I have ____ cleaned the kitchen. But I haven't sent the emails ____. I also haven't called my boss ____.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**already** · **already** · **yet** · **yet**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Transforma

1. I finished my homework. *(already + Present Perfect)*  
2. I didn't do the shopping. *(haven't… yet)*  
3. Ask about the dishes. *(Have you… yet?)*  
4. He ate breakfast. *(already)*  
5. She didn't call. *(hasn't… yet)*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I have **already** finished my homework.  
2. I **haven't** done the shopping **yet**.  
3. **Have you washed** the dishes **yet**?  
4. He **has already** eaten breakfast.  
5. She **hasn't** called **yet**.
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Corrige

1. *I already have finished my homework.*  
2. *I haven't yet done the shopping.* *(prioridad A2)*  
3. *I have finished yet.*  
4. *Have you yet cleaned your room?*  
5. *I haven't already called her.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. I have **already** finished my homework.  
2. I haven't done the shopping **yet**.  
3. I have **already** finished. / I haven't finished **yet**.  
4. Have you cleaned your room **yet**?  
5. I haven't called her **yet**.
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Empareja

1. already  
2. yet (negativa)  
3. yet (pregunta)  
4. have + already + PP  
5. haven't + PP + yet  

a) I haven't paid the bill yet. · b) Has she cleaned yet? · c) I have already finished. · d) “ya” en afirmativa · e) “aún” en negativa/pregunta

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **d** · 2 → **e** (neg.) · 3 → **e** (preg.) / **b** · 4 → **c** · 5 → **a**
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Elige la correcta

1. *Already* se usa sobre todo en: ____  
   a) afirmativas · b) solo negativas · c) solo con *never*  
2. *Yet* en A2 va normalmente: ____  
   a) al principio · b) al final · c) antes de *have*  
3. ____  
   a) I have already cleaned the kitchen. · b) I have cleaned already the kitchen. · c) I already cleaned have the kitchen.  
4. ____  
   a) I haven't sent the emails yet. · b) I haven't sent yet the emails. · c) I haven't already sent the emails.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a** · 2. **b** · 3. **a** · 4. **a**
</details>

2. Aún no he hecho la compra.

4. Él ya ha desayunado.

7. Ella aún no ha llamado.  
8. Ya he limpiado mi habitación.  
9. Aún no he pagado la factura de la luz.  
10. Ya he hecho casi todas mis tareas.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

2. I haven't done the shopping yet.  
3. Has she cleaned her room yet?  
4. He has already eaten breakfast.

7. She hasn't called yet.

</details>

## 12. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Repasa: *already* (medio) · *yet* (final).  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–7.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha a Sofia y Lucas; marca *already/yet*.  
4. **5–8 min** — Escribe y graba tu lista del día.

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *already* en afirmativas entre *have/has* y el participio  
- [ ] Uso *yet* al final en negativas y preguntas  
- [ ] No mezclo *already* + *yet* en la misma frase  
- [ ] Puedo describir tareas hechas y pendientes  
- [ ] Distingo *already/yet* de *ever/never*

| :--- | :--- |

| Pregunta | **Have/Has + sujeto + PP + yet?** |
| Posición *already* | entre *have/has* y el participio |
| Posición *yet* | al final |
