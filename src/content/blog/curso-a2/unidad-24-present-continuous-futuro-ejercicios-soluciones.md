## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** Present Continuous para planes fijados / agenda (*I'm meeting…*).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ meeting John at 3. → *am / is / are*
2. She ____ flying to Paris tomorrow. → *is / am / are*
3. We ____ having dinner on Friday. → *are / is / am*
4. ____ you free on Saturday? → *Are / Is / Do*
5. He ____ playing tennis at 5. → *is / am / are*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **am** · 2. **is** · 3. **are** · 4. **Are** · 5. **is**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. U24 Present Continuous often means ____. → a) a fixed future arrangement · b) only past progress · c) Present Perfect
7. Diary language likes ____. → a) day + time · b) *ago* only · c) *for/since* only
8. Form = ____. → a) am/is/are + -ing · b) will + -ing · c) have + PP
9. *I meeting John tomorrow* is ____. → a) wrong (need *am*) · b) correct · c) Past Perfect
10. Appointments and flights often use ____. → a) Present Continuous · b) only zero conditional · c) *must have*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) a fixed future arrangement** · 7. **a) day + time** · 8. **a) am/is/are + -ing** · 9. **a) wrong (need *am*)** · 10. **a) Present Continuous**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I meeting John at 3.*
12. *She are flying to Paris.*
13. *We is having dinner.*
14. *He am playing tennis.*
15. *Is you free on Saturday?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I **am** meeting John at 3.
12. She **is** flying to Paris.
13. We **are** having dinner.
14. He **is** playing tennis.
15. **Are** you free on Saturday?

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** agenda: meeting, appointment, conference, diary, free, arrive.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *appointment* ≈ ____ → *a booked time (doctor/dentist) · a random walk · rain*
2. *diary* ≈ ____ → *calendar of plans · a kitchen · a shoe*
3. *conference* ≈ ____ → *professional meeting/event · breakfast only · Past Simple*
4. *free* (time) ≈ ____ → *available · expensive · cloudy*
5. *confirm* ≈ ____ → *check/agree plans · cancel forever · forget*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a booked time (doctor/dentist)** · 2. **calendar of plans** · 3. **professional meeting/event** · 4. **available** · 5. **check/agree plans**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. I am ____ John at 3. → *meeting / meet / met*
7. She is ____ to Barcelona. → *flying / fly / flew*
8. We are ____ dinner on Friday. → *having / have / had*
9. He is ____ tennis at 5. → *playing / play / played*
10. I am ____ lunch with my sister. → *having / have / had*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **meeting** · 7. **flying** · 8. **having** · 9. **playing** · 10. **having**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *On Monday / at 10* often signals ____. → a) fixed schedule · b) only Past Perfect · c) *ago*
12. Present Continuous future feels ____. → a) arranged · b) impossible · c) only weather evidence
13. Questions about availability: ____. → a) Are you free…? · b) Did you free…? · c) Have you free?
14. After *am/is/are* use ____. → a) *-ing* · b) infinitive required · c) PP required
15. Unit 24 focus: ____. → a) diary future · b) only *will* promises · c) only *for/since*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) fixed schedule** · 12. **a) arranged** · 13. **a) Are you free…?** · 14. **a) *-ing*** · 15. **a) diary future**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Tom's week

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-24/reading-diary.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Tom's week"></audio>

> My name is Tom. This week I am very busy. On Monday I am meeting John at 3 o'clock. On Tuesday I am having a dentist appointment at 10 in the morning. On Wednesday I am flying to Paris for a meeting. On Thursday I am having dinner with my parents. On Friday I am playing tennis with my friend at 5 pm. I love having a full diary!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Who is Tom meeting on Monday? → a) John · b) Lisa · c) His dentist
2. What on Tuesday? → a) Dentist appointment · b) Tennis · c) Flight to London
3. Where is he flying on Wednesday? → a) Paris · b) Tokyo · c) Manchester
4. Who is dinner with on Thursday? → a) His parents · b) His boss · c) Strangers
5. What on Friday at 5? → a) Tennis · b) Exam · c) Moving house

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) John** · 2. **a) Dentist appointment** · 3. **a) Paris** · 4. **a) His parents** · 5. **a) Tennis**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The text practices ____. → a) Present Continuous for fixed plans · b) only *will* · c) only *ago*
7. Days of the week organise ____. → a) his diary · b) Past Perfect · c) zero conditional
8. Times like *at 3 o'clock* appear: ____. → a) yes · b) never · c) only Unit 1
9. Verbs are in ____. → a) *-ing* · b) PP only · c) infinitive only
10. Matches unit 24: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Module 6

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Present Continuous for fixed plans** · 7. **a) his diary** · 8. **a) yes** · 9. **a) *-ing*** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *I am meeting* needs ____. → a) am + -ing · b) will + -ing · c) have + meet
12. *On Wednesday I am flying* is ____. → a) natural for arrangements · b) wrong · c) Past Perfect
13. Questions invert ____. → a) Am/Is/Are + subject · b) Did + -ing · c) Have + are
14. Diary future is common with ____. → a) appointments/flights · b) only *must have* · c) *ago*
15. Reading helps you ____. → a) notice arranged future · b) skip form · c) avoid English

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) am + -ing** · 12. **a) natural for arrangements** · 13. **a) Am/Is/Are + subject** · 14. **a) appointments/flights** · 15. **a) notice arranged future**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Call to Lisa

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

> Hi Lisa! I am calling to confirm our plans for next week. On Monday I am meeting my boss at 9 o'clock. On Wednesday I am flying to Barcelona for a conference. On Friday I am having lunch with my sister at 1 pm. Are you free on Saturday? I am going to the cinema and I would like you to come with me.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Who is calling? → a) Someone confirming plans with Lisa · b) A stranger · c) No one
2. Monday plan? → a) Meeting the boss at 9 · b) Tennis · c) Sleep all day
3. Wednesday plan? → a) Flying to Barcelona · b) Dentist · c) Cooking class
4. Friday plan? → a) Lunch with sister · b) Exam · c) Moving
5. Saturday idea? → a) Cinema (wants Lisa to come) · b) Cancel forever · c) No plans

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Someone confirming plans with Lisa** · 2. **a) Meeting the boss at 9** · 3. **a) Flying to Barcelona** · 4. **a) Lunch with sister** · 5. **a) Cinema (wants Lisa to come)**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Listening checks ____. → a) diary future language · b) only spelling · c) only *into*
7. *Are you free…?* appears: ____. → a) yes · b) never · c) only Past
8. Conference destination: ____. → a) Barcelona · b) Tokyo · c) Village
9. Present Continuous forms appear: ____. → a) yes · b) never · c) once only
10. Unit 24 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Unit 60

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) diary future language** · 7. **a) yes** · 8. **a) Barcelona** · 9. **a) yes** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Notice *am/is/are* + ____. → a) *-ing* · b) only PP · c) *-est*
12. Confirming plans fits U24: ____. → a) true · b) false · c) irrelevant
13. Shadow and ____. → a) repeat diary sentences · b) ignore verbs · c) translate titles only
14. Write 3 arranged plans: ____. → a) good practice · b) useless · c) teachers only
15. U24 is not mainly about ____. → a) spontaneous *I'll help* offers · b) fixed arrangements · c) *-ing* future
