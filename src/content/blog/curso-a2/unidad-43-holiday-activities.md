## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 43 (A2)

- Vocabulario: **sightseeing**, **guided tour**, **beach**, **hiking**, **souvenir**.
- Contar vacaciones con **Past Simple**.
- Hablar de experiencias con **Present Perfect** (*Have you ever…?*).
- Escribir una postal / mini blog de viaje.
- Evitar mezclar *I went* / *I have been* sin criterio.

## 1. Vocabulario de vacaciones

| Palabra | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **sightseeing** | turismo / ver lugares | We went **sightseeing**. |
| **guided tour** | visita guiada | We took a **guided tour**. |
| **beach** | playa | They relaxed on the **beach**. |
| **hiking** | senderismo | I love **hiking** in the mountains. |
| **souvenir** | recuerdo | I bought a **souvenir**. |

| **museum / castle** | museo / castillo | We visited a **castle**. |
| **local food** | comida local | Try the **local food**. |

> We went **sightseeing** in the morning and to the **beach** in the afternoon.

## 2. Past Simple: vacaciones con tiempo concreto

Usa **Past Simple** cuando hay un **tiempo acabado**: *yesterday, last summer, in 2023, two years ago*.

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| Affirmative | We **visited** Lisbon last July. |
| Negative | We **didn't go** hiking. |
| Question | **Did** you **buy** any souvenirs? |

> Last year I **went** to Greece.  
> We **stayed** near the beach and **took** a guided tour.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-43/last-year-greece.mp3" title="🔊 Last year I went to Greece."></audio>

---

## 3. Present Perfect: experiencia (sin tiempo concreto)

Usa **Present Perfect** para decir si **alguna vez** has hecho algo (experiencia de vida), sin decir cuándo.

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| Affirmative | I **have visited** Italy. / I've **been** hiking. |
| Negative | I **haven't been** to Japan. |
| Question | **Have** you **ever** been sightseeing in London? |

> **Have you ever** taken a guided tour?  
> Yes, I **have**. / No, I **haven't**.

## 4. Contraste rápido

| Pregunta mental | Usa | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ¿Cuándo? (fecha / last…) | **Past Simple** | I **went** there **last summer**. |
| ¿Alguna vez en tu vida? | **Present Perfect** | I **have been** there. |
| Experiencia + detalle de cuándo | Perfect → Past | I've been to Rome. I **went** in 2022. |

~~I have gone to Paris last year.~~ → **I went to Paris last year.**  
~~Did you ever visit Rome?~~ *(mejor)* → **Have you ever visited Rome?**

## 6. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura — A postcard from Porto

Hi! We're in Porto. Yesterday we **went sightseeing** and **took** a **guided tour** of the old town. We **haven't bought** any **souvenirs** yet, but we **have tried** the local food — delicious! Tomorrow we want to go to the **beach**. Have you ever **been** to Portugal? See you soon!

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-43/reading-postcard.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: A postcard from Porto"></audio>

### Diálogo — Talking about holidays

**Ana:** **Have you ever** been **hiking**?  
**Leo:** Yes, I **have**. I **went** hiking in Asturias **last year**.  
**Ana:** Nice! Did you buy a **souvenir**?  
**Leo:** Just a photo book. What about you?  
**Ana:** I've **never** been hiking, but I **love** the **beach**. We **went** to Cadiz **in August**.

## 7. Errores frecuentes

1. ~~I have visited Rome last year.~~ → **I visited Rome last year.**  
2. ~~Did you ever go to Japan?~~ → **Have you ever been to Japan?**  
3. ~~We did sightseeing.~~ → **We went sightseeing.**  
4. ~~I have gone to Paris~~ *(si vives fuera y ya volviste; en BrE suele preferirse)* → **I've been to Paris.**  
5. ~~buy a memory~~ → **buy a souvenir**  
6. ~~a guide tour~~ → **a guided tour**

## 8. Ejercicios (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Vocabulario

Completa: *sightseeing / hiking / souvenir / beach / guided tour*

1. We walked around the city and took photos: ___.  
2. A person explained the museum: ___.  
3. We walked in the mountains: ___.  
4. I swam and sunbathed: ___.  
5. I bought a small gift: ___.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. sightseeing  
2. guided tour  
3. hiking  
4. beach  
5. souvenir  

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Past Simple o Present Perfect

1. I ___ (visit) Madrid in 2021.  
2. ___ you ever ___ (try) local food in Mexico?  
3. We ___ (not buy) souvenirs yesterday.  
4. She ___ (be) to Italy three times.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. visited  
2. Have / tried  
3. didn't buy  
4. has been  

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Corrige el error

1. I have gone to London last summer.  
2. We did sightseeing all day.  
3. Did you ever take a guided tour?  
4. I buyed a souvenir.  
5. Have you been hiking yesterday?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. I went to London last summer.  
2. We went sightseeing all day.  
3. Have you ever taken a guided tour?  
4. I bought a souvenir.  
5. Did you go hiking yesterday? / Have you been hiking?  

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Traduce al inglés

1. Fuimos de sightseeing ayer.  
2. ¿Has hecho alguna vez senderismo?  
3. Compramos un recuerdo.  
4. Hicimos una visita guiada el lunes.  
5. Nunca he estado en la playa en invierno.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. We went sightseeing yesterday.  
2. Have you ever been hiking?  
3. We bought a souvenir.  
4. We took a guided tour on Monday.  
5. I have never been to the beach in winter.  

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Empareja

1. Have you ever…?  
2. last summer  
3. guided tour  
4. souvenir  

a) Past Simple time expression  
b) experience question  
c) something you buy to remember the trip  
d) visit with a guide

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1 → b  
2 → a  
3 → d  
4 → c  

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa el diálogo

**A:** ___ you ever ___ to Greece?  
**B:** Yes, I ___. I ___ there last June.  
**A:** What did you do?  
**B:** We ___ sightseeing and we ___ on the beach.  
**A:** Did you buy a ___ ?  
**B:** Yes — a small vase.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

Have / been  
have / went  
went / sunbathed (or relaxed / swam)  
souvenir  

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Elige la mejor opción

1. We ___ a guided tour yesterday.  
   a) took  b) have taken  c) taking  
2. ___ you ever tried Japanese food?  
   a) Did  b) Have  c) Do  
3. They ___ souvenirs yet.  
   a) didn't buy  b) haven't bought  c) don't buy

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. a  
2. b  
3. b  

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Ordena

1. sightseeing / went / We / yesterday  
2. ever / you / Have / hiking / been / ?  
3. tour / a / took / guided / They

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. We went sightseeing yesterday.  
2. Have you ever been hiking?  
3. They took a guided tour.  

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Experiencia + detalle

Completa con Present Perfect + Past Simple:

1. I ___ (be) to Rome. I ___ (go) in 2020.  
2. She ___ (try) paella. She ___ (eat) it in Valencia last year.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. have been / went  
2. has tried / ate  

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu postal (8–12 líneas)

Escribe una postal de vacaciones. Incluye:

- 2 actividades (*sightseeing / beach / hiking / guided tour…*)  
- 1 frase en **Past Simple** con tiempo  
- 1 frase en **Present Perfect** (*Have you ever…?* o *I have…*)  
- 1 *souvenir* (opcional)

**Modelo:** la lectura de la sección 6.
