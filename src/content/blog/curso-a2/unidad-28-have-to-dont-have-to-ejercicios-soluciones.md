## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** have to / has to y don't / doesn't have to (obligación y lo opcional).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ to get up early. → *have / has / having*
2. She ____ to wear a uniform. → *has / have / having*
3. They ____ have to work on Saturdays. → *don't / doesn't / isn't*
4. He ____ to study tonight. → *has / have / having*
5. ____ you have to wear a uniform? → *Do / Does / Are*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **have** · 2. **has** · 3. **don't** · 4. **has** · 5. **Do**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *I/you/we/they* use ____. → a) have to · b) has to · c) having to
7. *He/she/it* uses ____. → a) has to · b) have to · c) haves to
8. *Don't have to* means ____. → a) not necessary · b) forbidden · c) past ability
9. Questions: ____. → a) Do/Does + have to · b) Have to + do · c) Are + have to
10. Unit 28 trains ____. → a) obligation vs optional · b) only Zero · c) only could

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) have to** · 7. **a) has to** · 8. **a) not necessary** · 9. **a) Do/Does + have to** · 10. **a) obligation vs optional**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *She have to wear a uniform.*
12. *He don't have to pay today.*
13. *Do she have to study tonight?*
14. *I have to to get up early.*
15. *They has to arrive on time.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. She **has to** wear a uniform.
12. He **doesn't have to** pay today.
13. **Does** she have to study tonight?
14. I have to get up early.
15. They **have to** arrive on time.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** obligation, uniform, bills, canteen, homework, arrive on time.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *obligation* ≈ ____ → *something necessary · optional · a colour*
2. *uniform* ≈ ____ → *same clothes for work/school · a ticket · rain*
3. *pay bills* ≈ ____ → *pay for electricity/rent etc. · cook dinner · swim*
4. *canteen* ≈ ____ → *school cafeteria · a bus stop · a park*
5. *arrive on time* ≈ ____ → *not be late · forget · melt*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **something necessary** · 2. **same clothes for work/school** · 3. **pay for electricity/rent etc.** · 4. **school cafeteria** · 5. **not be late**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. I have to ____ up early. → *get / getting / got*
7. She has to ____ a uniform. → *wear / wearing / wore*
8. Students have to do ____ every day. → *homework / homeworks / homeworke*
9. They don't have to ____ lunch. → *bring / bringing / brought*
10. Teachers have to ____ exams. → *correct / correcting / corrected*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **get** · 7. **wear** · 8. **homework** · 9. **bring** · 10. **correct**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. Work at 8 → often ____. → a) have to get up early · b) don't have to work · c) could swim
12. *Don't have to cook* means cooking is ____. → a) optional today · b) forbidden · c) always required
13. *Does she have to…?* is a ____. → a) question · b) past tense · c) Zero Conditional
14. After *have to* use ____. → a) infinitive · b) *to* + infinitive again · c) *-ing* only
15. Unit 28 is about ____. → a) rules & obligations · b) only conditionals · c) only past ability

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) have to get up early** · 12. **a) optional today** · 13. **a) question** · 14. **a) infinitive** · 15. **a) rules & obligations**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: A day with obligations

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-28/reading-have-to.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: A day with obligations"></audio>

> I have to get up early every day because I work at 8 o'clock. I have to wear a uniform at work. I don't have to work on Saturdays, so I can rest. I have to pay my bills every month. I don't have to cook today because my mother is making dinner. I have to study tonight for my exam tomorrow.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Why get up early? → a) Work at 8 o'clock · b) School canteen · c) Exam yesterday
2. What at work? → a) Wear a uniform · b) Cook dinner · c) Pay for books
3. Saturdays? → a) Don't have to work · b) Have to study · c) Have to pay bills
4. Why not cook today? → a) Mother is making dinner · b) No uniform · c) No exam
5. Tonight? → a) Study for exam tomorrow · b) Rest on Saturday · c) Pay bills

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Work at 8 o'clock** · 2. **a) Wear a uniform** · 3. **a) Don't have to work** · 4. **a) Mother is making dinner** · 5. **a) Study for exam tomorrow**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The text practices ____. → a) have to / don't have to · b) only Zero · c) only could
7. *Pay bills every month* is ____. → a) an obligation · b) optional · c) a polite request
8. *Don't have to work on Saturdays* means ____. → a) optional · b) forbidden · c) always required
9. *Have to study tonight* is for ____. → a) exam tomorrow · b) canteen · c) uniform only
10. Matches unit 28: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Module 6

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) have to / don't have to** · 7. **a) an obligation** · 8. **a) optional** · 9. **a) exam tomorrow** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *I have to* + ____. → a) infinitive · b) *to* twice · c) *-ing* only
12. *She has to wear* is ____. → a) correct · b) wrong · c) Past Perfect
13. *Don't have to* ≠ *mustn't* at A2: ____. → a) true · b) false · c) irrelevant
14. Obligation vs optional: ask ____. → a) Is it necessary? · b) Is it past? · c) Is it Zero?
15. Reading helps you ____. → a) notice rules in context · b) skip form · c) avoid English

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) infinitive** · 12. **a) correct** · 13. **a) true** · 14. **a) Is it necessary?** · 15. **a) notice rules in context**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: School rules

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-28/listening-have-to.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: School rules"></audio>

> At school, students have to wear uniform. They have to arrive on time. They don't have to bring lunch because the school has a canteen. Students have to do homework every day. They don't have to pay for books — the school gives them free. Teachers have to correct exams and help students.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Students have to ____. → a) Wear uniform · b) Pay for books · c) Skip homework
2. They have to ____. → a) Arrive on time · b) Bring lunch always · c) Correct exams
3. Lunch? → a) Don't have to bring (canteen) · b) Must bring · c) Never eat
4. Homework? → a) Every day · b) Never · c) Only teachers
5. Books? → a) Free from school · b) Must pay · c) Not mentioned

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Wear uniform** · 2. **a) Arrive on time** · 3. **a) Don't have to bring (canteen)** · 4. **a) Every day** · 5. **a) Free from school**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Listening is about ____. → a) school rules · b) dentist · c) London travel
7. Teachers have to ____. → a) correct exams and help · b) wear student uniform · c) bring lunch
8. *Don't have to pay for books* means ____. → a) books are free · b) forbidden to read · c) no school
9. *Have to* appears for students and ____. → a) teachers · b) only rain · c) only could
10. Unit 28 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Unit 60

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) school rules** · 7. **a) correct exams and help** · 8. **a) books are free** · 9. **a) teachers** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Students *have to* + ____. → a) infinitive · b) *-ing* · c) PP
12. *They don't have to bring lunch* is ____. → a) optional · b) obligation · c) past ability
13. Shadow and ____. → a) mark obligation vs optional · b) ignore verbs · c) translate titles only
14. Write 3 school rules: ____. → a) good practice · b) useless · c) teachers only
15. U28 is not mainly about ____. → a) conditionals only · b) have to · c) don't have to

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I ____ to get up early every day. → *have / has / having*
2. She ____ to wear a uniform. → *has / have / having*
3. We ____ have to work on Saturdays. → *don't / doesn't / isn't*
4. ____ they have to do homework? → *Do / Does / Are*
5. He ____ to study tonight. → *has / have / having*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **have** · 2. **has** · 3. **don't** · 4. **Do** · 5. **has**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. I / have / to / get / up / early / every / day.
7. She / doesn't / have / to / work / on / Saturdays.
8. Do / you / have / to / wear / a / uniform?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I have to get up early every day.
7. She doesn't have to work on Saturdays.
8. Do you have to wear a uniform?

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Tres obligaciones con *I have to…*.
10. Dos cosas opcionales con *I don't have to…*.
11. Una regla escolar con *Students have to…*.
12. Una pregunta *Do you have to…?*
13. Una frase con *has to* (he/she).
14. Contrasta obligación y opción en el trabajo.
15. Párrafo (6–8 frases) sobre tus reglas diarias.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. I have to get up early. I have to wear a uniform. I have to pay my bills.
10. I don't have to work on Saturdays. I don't have to cook today.
11. Students have to arrive on time.
12. Do you have to wear a uniform?
13. She has to study tonight for her exam.
14. I have to get up early, but I don't have to work on Saturdays.
15. I have to get up early because I work at eight. I have to wear a uniform. I don't have to work on Saturdays. I have to study tonight for my exam tomorrow!

</details>

## Resumen rápido

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Obligación** | *have to / has to* + infinitivo |
| **No necesario** | *don't / doesn't have to* |
| **Pregunta** | *Do/Does … have to…?* |
| **Cuidado** | *don't have to* ≠ prohibido |
