## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 57 (A2)

- **Work as** / **work in** y **study at** / **study in**
- Present Simple para rutinas laborales y académicas
- Repaso: **have to / don't have to**
- Vocabulario: **office**, **meeting**, **deadline**, **subject**, **exam**, **graduation**
- Describir brevemente tu trabajo o tus estudios

## 3. *Work as / in* y *study at / in*

| Forma | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **work as** + job | profesión | I **work as** a nurse. |
| **work in** + place/field | lugar / sector | I **work in** a hospital. |
| **study at** + school/uni | institución | I **study at** university. |
| **study in** + city/country | ciudad / país | I **study in** Lisbon. |

> She **works as** a designer. She **works in** marketing.  
> He **studies at** a language school **in** Valencia.

## 4. Rutinas + *have to*

Present Simple para hábitos:

> I **start** work at 9.  
> She **studies** in the evening.

Obligación — **have to**:

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| have to / has to | I **have to** finish this today. |
| don't / doesn't have to | We **don't have to** wear a uniform. |
| Do/Does … have to…? | **Do** you **have to** work weekends? |

> I **have to** meet the **deadline**.  
> Students **have to** take an **exam** in June.

Repaso: [Have to / Don't have to](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-28-have-to-dont-have-to).

## 6. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura — Two routines

Mia **works as** a receptionist. She **works in** a small **office** and has many **meetings**. She often **has to** answer emails before the **deadline**. Leo **studies at** university. His favourite **subject** is biology. He **has to** take three **exams** this month. After **graduation**, he wants to work in a lab.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-57/reading-routines.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Two routines"></audio>

### Diálogo — At lunch

**Nora:** What do you do?  
**Pablo:** I **work as** a designer. I **work in** an advertising company.  
**Nora:** Do you **have to** go to many **meetings**?  
**Pablo:** Yes — almost every day. And you?  
**Nora:** I **study at** university. I have an **exam** on Monday.  
**Pablo:** Good luck!

## 7. Errores frecuentes

1. ~~I work like a teacher.~~ → **I work as a teacher.**  
2. ~~I study in university.~~ → **I study at university.**  
3. ~~I have to to go.~~ → **I have to go.**  
4. ~~I work in teacher.~~ → **I work as a teacher.** / **I work in a school.**  
5. ~~a work~~ → **a job** / **work** (incontable)  
6. ~~I am study~~ → **I study** / **I'm studying**

## 8. Ejercicios (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Empareja

1. deadline  
2. meeting  
3. subject  
4. graduation  
5. office  

a) school subject like maths  
b) place where many people work at desks  
c) last day to finish something  
d) ceremony when you finish your studies  
e) planned discussion at work

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1 → c  
2 → e  
3 → a  
4 → d  
5 → b  

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — *as / in / at*

1. I work ___ a waiter.  
2. I work ___ a restaurant.  
3. I study ___ a language school.  
4. I study ___ Berlin.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. as  
2. in  
3. at  
4. in  

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — *have to*

1. I ___ finish this report today.  
2. She ___ wear a uniform. *(obligación)*  
3. We ___ come on Sunday. *(no es necesario)*  
4. ___ you ___ work late?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. have to  
2. has to  
3. don't have to  
4. Do / have to  

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrige el error

1. I work like a doctor.  
2. I study in university.  
3. I have to to study tonight.  
4. I have a work in Madrid.  
5. She work in an office.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. I work as a doctor.  
2. I study at university.  
3. I have to study tonight.  
4. I have a job in Madrid. / I work in Madrid.  
5. She works in an office.

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Traduce al inglés

1. Trabajo de profesor/a.  
2. Estudio en la universidad.  
3. Tengo que terminar esto hoy.  
4. Tenemos una reunión a las 10.  
5. Mi asignatura favorita es historia.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. I work as a teacher.  
2. I study at university.  
3. I have to finish this today.  
4. We have a meeting at 10.  
5. My favourite subject is history.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa el diálogo

**A:** What do you do?  
**B:** I work ___ a nurse. I work ___ a hospital.  
**A:** Do you ___ to work nights?  
**B:** Sometimes. And you?  
**A:** I study ___ university. I have an ___ next week.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

as  
in  
have  
at  
exam  

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Elige la mejor opción

1. The last day to finish a project is the ___.  
   a) meeting  b) deadline  c) subject  
2. After university you have your ___.  
   a) graduation  b) office  c) salary  
3. I ___ as an engineer.  
   a) work  b) study  c) have

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. b  
2. a  
3. a  

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Ordena

1. as / work / I / a / designer  
2. at / study / I / university  
3. have / I / to / early / start

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. I work as a designer.  
2. I study at university.  
3. I have to start early.  

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Lectura True / False

> Mia works as a receptionist in a small office. Leo studies at university and has to take three exams this month.

1. Mia's job is in an office.  
2. Leo doesn't have any exams.  
3. Mia works as a student.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. True  
2. False  
3. False  

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu descripción (8–12 líneas)

Habla de tu trabajo o estudios. Incluye:

- *work as / work in* **o** *study at / study in*  
- 2 palabras de vocabulario (*meeting / deadline / subject / exam…*)  
- 1 *have to / don't have to*  
- 1 rutina en Present Simple

**Modelo:** la lectura de la sección 6.

## 9. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Vocabulario + *work as / study at*  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–6.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha lectura y diálogo.  
4. **5–8 min** — Ejercicio 10.

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *work as* y *work in*  
- [ ] Uso *study at* y *study in*  
- [ ] Aplico *have to / don't have to*  
- [ ] Uso *meeting, deadline, subject, exam*  
- [ ] Evito *work like* y *study in university*
