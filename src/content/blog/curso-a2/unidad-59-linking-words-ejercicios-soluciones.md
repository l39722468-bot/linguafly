## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** and (suma); but / although / however (contraste); because (causa); so (resultado)

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I like tea ___ coffee. → *and / but / because*

2. It's cheap, ___ small. → *but / and / so*

3. I'm tired ___ I worked late. → *because / so / but*

4. It rained, ___ we stayed in. → *so / because / but*

5. ___ the centre is expensive, I enjoy living there. → *Although / Because / So*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **and** · 2. **but** · 3. **because** · 4. **so** · 5. **Although**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *Because* shows ____. → a) causa · b) resultado · c) suma

7. *So* shows ____. → a) resultado · b) causa · c) contraste only

8. *Although* = ____. → a) aunque · b) por eso · c) y

9. *However* often contrasts with ____. → a) previous idea · b) only and · c) only because

10. Unit 59 follows ____. → a) Places U58 · b) Module 5 only · c) skip U60

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) causa** · 7. **a) resultado** · 8. **a) aunque** · 9. **a) previous idea** · 10. **a) Places U58**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I like tea because coffee.*

12. *It rained, because we stayed in.*

13. *Although it's cheap, but it's small.*

14. *So I was tired, I worked late.*

15. *I enjoy living there, however I love my city because.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I like tea and coffee. / I like tea, but not coffee.
12. It rained, so we stayed in.
13. Although it's expensive, it's nice. / It's cheap, but it's small.
14. I was tired because I worked late.
15. I love my city because there are parks and museums.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** and, but, because, so, although, however, therefore

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *because* ≈ ____ → *porque · por eso · pero*

2. *so* ≈ ____ → *así que/por eso · aunque · y*

3. *although* ≈ ____ → *aunque · porque · y*

4. *however* ≈ ____ → *sin embargo · y · porque*

5. *therefore* ≈ ____ → *por lo tanto · pero · y*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **porque** · 2. **así que/por eso** · 3. **aunque** · 4. **sin embargo** · 5. **por lo tanto**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. Parks ___ museums in the city. → *and / but / so*

7. Busy ___ friendly. → *but / because / so*

8. Rained, ___ stayed home. → *so / because / although*

9. Film was long; ___, excellent. → *however / because / and*

10. Tired ___ worked late. → *because / so / and*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **and** · 7. **but** · 8. **so** · 9. **however** · 10. **because**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Because there are parks* explains ____. → a) love city · b) stayed home · c) film long only

12. *Although expensive* = ____. → a) contraste · b) suma · c) resultado

13. Paragraph links ideas with ____. → a) connectors · b) only There is · c) only Can I

14. *We watched a film and cooked* = ____. → a) and · b) but · c) so cause

15. Unit 59 next is ____. → a) Module 6 Review U60 · b) Module 5 only · c) skip U60

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) love city** · 12. **a) contraste** · 13. **a) connectors** · 14. **a) and** · 15. **a) Module 6 Review U60**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: A short paragraph

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-59/reading-linking.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: A short paragraph"></audio>

> I love my city because there are parks and museums. It's busy, but friendly. Although the centre is expensive, I enjoy living there. Last Saturday it rained, so we stayed at home. We watched a film and cooked dinner. The film was long; however, it was excellent.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Loves city ___ parks and museums. → a) because · b) so · c) but only

2. City is busy ___ friendly. → a) but · b) and cause · c) because result

3. ___ centre expensive, enjoys living there. → a) Although · b) So · c) And

4. It rained, ___ stayed at home. → a) so · b) because · c) although wrong order

5. Watched film ___ cooked dinner. → a) and · b) but · c) so

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) because** · 2. **a) but** · 3. **a) Although** · 4. **a) so** · 5. **a) and**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Film was long; ___, excellent. → a) however · b) because · c) and only

7. Last Saturday it ___. → a) rained · b) sunny always · c) free

8. *Because* introduces ____. → a) reason · b) result · c) addition only

9. *So* introduces ____. → a) result · b) reason · c) contrast only

10. Matches unit 59: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) however** · 7. **a) rained** · 8. **a) reason** · 9. **a) result** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *I love my city because* = ____. → a) causa · b) resultado · c) offer

12. *Although the centre is expensive* = ____. → a) contraste · b) suma · c) Can I help

13. *However, it was excellent* = ____. → a) however · b) because · c) and

14. Text uses ____. → a) linking words · b) only places · c) only tech

15. Linking writing uses ____. → a) and/but/because/so/although · b) only articles · c) only Shall we

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) causa** · 12. **a) contraste** · 13. **a) however** · 14. **a) linking words** · 15. **a) and/but/because/so/although**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Weekend plans

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-59/listening-linking.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Weekend plans"></audio>

> Eva: Shall we go out?
> Nico: I'd like to, but I'm tired because I worked late.
> Eva: OK — so let's stay in and watch a series.
> Nico: Good idea. Although I'm tired, I don't want to sleep yet.
> Eva: Perfect.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Eva asks go ___. → a) out · b) shopping only · c) museum

2. Nico is tired ___ worked late. → a) because · b) so · c) and only

3. They decide ___ stay in. → a) to · b) for · c) at

4. Watch a ___ . → a) series · b) receipt · c) gate

5. Although tired, doesn't want ___ yet. → a) sleep · b) recycle · c) checkout

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) out** · 2. **a) because** · 3. **a) to** · 4. **a) series** · 5. **a) sleep**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. *I'd like to, but* = ____. → a) but · b) because · c) so

7. *So let's stay in* = ____. → a) so result · b) because · c) although

8. *Although I'm tired* = ____. → a) although · b) so · c) and

9. Dialogue mixes ___ + linking. → a) suggestions · b) only places · c) only tech

10. Unit 59 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) but** · 7. **a) so result** · 8. **a) although** · 9. **a) suggestions** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Shall we go out?* + *but I'm tired* → a) suggestion + contrast · b) only opinion · c) only There is

12. *Because I worked late* → a) because · b) so · c) however

13. *Good idea* → a) accept · b) I don't think so · c) No thanks

14. Shadow and note ____. → a) linking words · b) only some · c) only Who

15. U59 next unit is ____. → a) Module 6 Review U60 · b) Module 5 only · c) U58 skip

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) suggestion + contrast** · 12. **a) because** · 13. **a) accept** · 14. **a) linking words** · 15. **a) Module 6 Review U60**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I was tired ___ I worked late. → *because / so / but*

2. It rained, ___ we stayed in. → *so / because / although*

3. It's busy ___ friendly. → *but / and / because*

4. ___ it's expensive, I like it. → *Although / So / And*

5. We watched a film ___ cooked dinner. → *and / but / so*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **because** · 2. **so** · 3. **but** · 4. **Although** · 5. **and**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. tired / I'm / because / worked / I / late / .

7. home / stayed / we / so / in / , / rained / it / .

8. friendly / but / busy / it's / , / .

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. I'm tired because I worked late.
7. It rained, so we stayed in.
8. It's busy, but friendly.

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Una frase con *because*.

10. Una frase con *so*.

11. Una frase con *but*.

12. Una frase con *although*.

13. Una frase con *and*.

14. Corrige: *It rained, because we stayed in.*

15. Escribe un párrafo corto (8–10 líneas) usando conectores.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. I stayed home because it rained.
10. It rained, so we stayed at home.
11. It's busy, but friendly.
12. Although the centre is expensive, I enjoy living there.
13. We watched a film and cooked dinner.
14. It rained, so we stayed in.
15. I love my city because there are parks and museums. It's busy, but friendly. Although the centre is expensive, I enjoy living there. Last Saturday it rained, so we stayed at home. We watched a film and cooked dinner. The film was long; however, it was excellent.

</details>
