## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** It's + adj/-ing; going to vs will; What's the weather like?

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. It's ____ today. *(soleado)* → *sunny / sun / a sun*
2. It's ____. *(lloviendo)* → *raining / rainy / rain*
3. Look — it's ____ to rain. → *going / will / go*
4. Tomorrow it ____ be sunny. → *will / going / is*
5. What's the weather ____? → *like / is / are*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **sunny** · 2. **raining** · 3. **going** · 4. **will** · 5. **like**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *It's sunny* (no *It's a sun*): ____. → a) correcto · b) It's a sun · c) There is sunny
7. *Going to* con indicios ahora: ____. → a) yes · b) never with weather · c) only Past
8. *It will rain* = ____. → a) predicción / pronóstico · b) solo ahora · c) only Past
9. *20 degrees* (no *grades*): ____. → a) correcto · b) 20 grades · c) 20 degree
10. Unit 44 follows ____. → a) Holiday U43 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U45

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) correcto** · 7. **a) yes** · 8. **a) predicción / pronóstico** · 9. **a) correcto** · 10. **a) Holiday U43**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *It's a sunny.*
12. *It's rain now.*
13. *What the weather like?*
14. *It will raining tomorrow.*
15. *It's 15 grades.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. It's **sunny**.
12. It's **raining** now. / It's **rainy** now.
13. **What's** the weather like?
14. It **will rain** tomorrow.
15. It's 15 **degrees**.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** sunny, rainy, cloudy, windy, foggy, storm; hot/warm/cool/cold; forecast, degrees.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *cloudy* ≈ ____ → *nublado · ventoso · con niebla*
2. *windy* ≈ ____ → *ventoso · soleado · tormenta*
3. *foggy* ≈ ____ → *con niebla · lluvioso · frío*
4. *storm* ≈ ____ → *tormenta · pronóstico · grados*
5. *forecast* ≈ ____ → *pronóstico · paraguas · playa*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **nublado** · 2. **ventoso** · 3. **con niebla** · 4. **tormenta** · 5. **pronóstico**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. It's very ____ today. *(ventoso)* → *windy / wind / winds*
7. There's a ____ outside. *(tormenta)* → *storm / sunny / degree*
8. About 14 ____. → *degrees / grades / degree*
9. The ____ will be 25°. *(máxima)* → *high / hot / height*
10. Take an ____. *(paraguas)* → *umbrella / storm / forecast*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **windy** · 7. **storm** · 8. **degrees** · 9. **high** · 10. **umbrella**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. Today reading: ____ and cool. → a) Cloudy · b) Sunny only · c) Hot
12. Afternoon going to ____. → a) Rain · b) Book room · c) Sightseeing
13. Tomorrow high of ____. → a) 21 degrees · b) 80 euros · c) Platform 4
14. Sunday might be ____. → a) Windy · b) Included · c) Double
15. Unit 44 next is ____. → a) Entertainment U45 · b) Module 6 only · c) skip U45

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) Cloudy** · 12. **a) Rain** · 13. **a) 21 degrees** · 14. **a) Windy** · 15. **a) Entertainment U45**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Weekend forecast

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-44/reading-weather.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Weekend forecast"></audio>

> Today it's cloudy and cool — about 14 degrees. This afternoon it's going to rain, so take an umbrella. Tomorrow it will be sunny and warmer: a high of 21 degrees. Perfect for a walk! On Sunday it might be windy, but there won't be a storm.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Today it's ____ and cool. → a) Cloudy · b) Sunny · c) Stormy only
2. Temperature today? → a) About 14 degrees · b) 21 degrees · c) 80 euros
3. This afternoon it's going to ____. → a) Rain · b) Snow · c) Check in
4. Tomorrow it will be ____. → a) Sunny and warmer · b) Foggy and cold · c) At the beach
5. Sunday might be ____. → a) Windy · b) Included · c) On platform 4

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Cloudy** · 2. **a) About 14 degrees** · 3. **a) Rain** · 4. **a) Sunny and warmer** · 5. **a) Windy**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Take an ____. → a) Umbrella · b) Key card · c) Souvenir
7. Tomorrow high of ____. → a) 21 degrees · b) 14 degrees · c) £2.50
8. Perfect for a ____. → a) Walk · b) Guided tour · c) Concert
9. There won't be a ____. → a) Storm · b) Train · c) Series
10. Matches unit 44: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only travel

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Umbrella** · 7. **a) 21 degrees** · 8. **a) Walk** · 9. **a) Storm** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *It's going to rain* = ____. → a) indicios / plan cercano · b) Past Simple · c) book room
12. *It will be sunny* = ____. → a) pronóstico · b) solo ahora · c) arrive at
13. *About 14 degrees* = ____. → a) temperatura · b) precio habitación · c) platform
14. Forecast mixes ____. → a) going to + will · b) only phrasals · c) only should
15. Weather text writing uses ____. → a) It's… / predictions · b) only did · c) only much

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) indicios / plan cercano** · 12. **a) pronóstico** · 13. **a) temperatura** · 14. **a) going to + will** · 15. **a) It's… / predictions**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Before a trip

**Objetivo:** escuchar el foco gramatical en contexto.

> Ana: Will it rain?  
> Leo: I don't think so. The forecast says it will be dry all weekend.  
> Ana: Great — no umbrella then!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. They talk about weather in ____. → a) Lisbon · b) Porto · c) Cadiz
2. Today it's ____. → a) Sunny · b) Rainy · c) Foggy
3. Tomorrow going to be ____. → a) Hotter · b) Cooler · c) Windy only
4. Will it rain? → a) Leo doesn't think so · b) Yes — all weekend · c) Yes — storm
5. Forecast: dry all ____. → a) Weekend · b) Month · c) Night only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Lisbon** · 2. **a) Sunny** · 3. **a) Hotter** · 4. **a) Leo doesn't think so** · 5. **a) Weekend**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Ana says no ____. → a) Umbrella then · b) Ticket · c) Souvenir
7. *What's the weather like?* = ____. → a) pregunta tiempo · b) book room · c) get to
8. *Going to be hotter* = ____. → a) going to · b) Past Simple · c) have been
9. *It will be dry* = ____. → a) will · b) went · c) should
10. Unit 44 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only U31

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Question: What's the weather ____? → a) like · b) is · c) are
12. Today it's ____. → a) sunny · b) sun · c) a sun
13. Tomorrow it's going to ____ hotter. → a) be · b) being · c) rain only
14. Shadow and note ____. → a) It's / going to / will · b) only some · c) only Who

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. It's ____ today. *(nublado)* → *cloudy / cloud / clouds*
2. It's ____. *(lloviendo)* → *raining / rainy / rain*
3. It's ____ to be windy. → *going / will / go*
4. Tomorrow it ____ be cold. → *will / going / is*
5. It's 20 ____. → *degrees / grades / degree*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **cloudy** · 2. **raining** · 3. **going** · 4. **will** · 5. **degrees**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. sunny / It's / and / warm
7. rain / going / It's / to
8. weather / What's / like / the / ?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. It's sunny and warm.
7. It's going to rain.
8. What's the weather like?

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Una frase con *It's* + adjetivo (sunny, cloudy…).
10. Una frase con *It's raining* o *It's snowing*.
11. Una frase con *It's going to…*
12. Una frase con *It will…*
13. Una frase con temperatura (*degrees*).
14. Corrige: *It will raining tomorrow.*
15. Pronóstico para tu ciudad (8–10 líneas) con going to y will.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. It's sunny today. / It's cloudy and cool.
10. It's raining. / This afternoon it's going to rain.
11. It's going to be windy this afternoon.
12. Tomorrow it will be sunny. / It will be dry all weekend.
13. It's about 14 degrees. / A high of 21 degrees.
14. It **will rain** tomorrow.
15. Today it's cloudy and cool — about 14 degrees. This afternoon it's going to rain, so take an umbrella. Tomorrow it will be sunny and warmer: a high of 21 degrees. Perfect for a walk! On Sunday it might be windy, but there won't be a storm.

</details>
