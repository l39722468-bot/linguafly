## Lección 1 — Gramática: Wh + did

**Objetivo:** *What/Where/When/Who/Why/How* + *did* + sujeto + base.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ____ did you do yesterday? → *What / Where / Who*  
2. ____ did you go last weekend? → *Where / What / Why*  
3. ____ did she leave? → *When / Who / What*  
4. ____ did you see at the party? → *Who / When / How*  
5. ____ did he leave early? → *Why / Where / What*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **What** · 2. **Where** · 3. **When** · 4. **Who** · 5. **Why**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Formula = ____. → a) Wh + did + subject + base · b) Wh + past verb · c) Did + Wh  
7. *What did you ____?* → a) do · b) did · c) done  
8. *Where did she ____?* → a) go · b) went · c) going  
9. *How did the exam go?* asks about ____. → a) manner/result · b) place · c) person  
10. After *did* we use ____. → a) base verb · b) *-ed* · c) past participle only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Wh + did + subject + base**  
7. **a) do**  
8. **a) go**  
9. **a) manner/result**  
10. **a) base verb**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *What did you did yesterday?*  
12. *Where she went last weekend?*  
13. *When did he left?*  
14. *Who did you saw?*  
15. *Why he leave early?*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. What did you **do** yesterday?  
12. Where **did she go** last weekend?  
13. When did he **leave**?  
14. Who did you **see**?  
15. Why **did** he leave early?

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: tipos de pregunta

**Objetivo:** asociar Wh con el tipo de información.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja

1. What  
2. Where  
3. When  
4. Who  
5. Why  

a) persona · b) razón · c) cosa/acción · d) lugar · e) tiempo

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **c** · 2 → **d** · 3 → **e** · 4 → **a** · 5 → **b**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. ____ did the trip go? → *How / Who / What*  
7. First… Then… ____… → *Finally / Who / Ago*  
8. I asked ____ he wanted the job. → *why / where / who*  
9. ____ did it happen? → *When / Who / How much*  
10. ____ did you buy at the shop? → *What / When / Why*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **How** · 7. **Finally** · 8. **why** · 9. **When** · 10. **What**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *Interview* = ____. → a) a meeting for a job · b) a film · c) a beach  
12. *Prepare* = ____. → a) get ready · b) forget · c) leave  
13. *Take place* = ____. → a) happen · b) buy · c) cook  
14. *Amazing* = ____. → a) wonderful · b) boring · c) early  
15. *Forgot* is past of ____. → a) forget · b) leave · c) ask

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) a meeting for a job**  
12. **a) get ready**  
13. **a) happen**  
14. **a) wonderful**  
15. **a) forget**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Carlos's interview

**Objetivo:** leer un texto con muchas Wh-questions en pasado.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-4/reading-questions.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Carlos's interview"></audio>

> Last month my friend Carlos had an interview for a new job. I asked him many questions about it. First I asked what he did to prepare. He said he studied the company online. Then I asked where the interview took place. He said it was at the office in Madrid. I asked when it happened. He said last Tuesday at 10 o'clock. I asked who he met there. He said he met two managers and the HR director. I asked why he wanted the job. He said because it was interesting and near his home. Finally I asked how it went. He said it went very well and he got the job!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What was the interview for? → a) A new job · b) A holiday · c) School  
2. Where did it take place? → a) Madrid office · b) Valencia beach · c) Home  
3. When did it happen? → a) Last Tuesday at 10 · b) Sunday afternoon · c) Next week  
4. Who did he meet? → a) Two managers and HR · b) Only Ana · c) Nobody  
5. Did he get the job? → a) Yes · b) No · c) The text doesn't say

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) A new job**  
2. **a) Madrid office**  
3. **a) Last Tuesday at 10**  
4. **a) Two managers and HR**  
5. **a) Yes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. To prepare he ____. → a) studied the company online · b) went to the beach · c) forgot  
7. Why did he want the job? → a) Interesting and near home · b) Only money · c) No reason  
8. Sequence words used: ____. → a) First, Then, Finally · b) only ago · c) only will  
9. *How it went* → result: ____. → a) very well · b) badly · c) cancelled  
10. The main topic is ____. → a) asking about an interview · b) comparatives · c) introductions

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) studied the company online**  
7. **a) Interesting and near home**  
8. **a) First, Then, Finally**  
9. **a) very well**  
10. **a) asking about an interview**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Reported *what he did* relates to ____. → a) What did he do? · b) Where is he? · c) How old is he?  
12. *Took place* means ____. → a) happened · b) bought · c) wrote  
13. *Got the job* means ____. → a) was offered/accepted the job · b) lost the job · c) left early  
14. Wh-words in the story cover ____. → a) What/Where/When/Who/Why/How · b) only What · c) only How  
15. After asking, answers use ____. → a) past forms · b) only future · c) only *to be* present

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) What did he do?**  
12. **a) happened**  
13. **a) was offered/accepted the job**  
14. **a) What/Where/When/Who/Why/How**  
15. **a) past forms**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: My sister asked…

**Objetivo:** escuchar un diálogo de preguntas sobre un viaje.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-4/listening-questions.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: My sister asked…"></audio>

> Hi! Yesterday I had a really strange day. My sister called me in the morning and asked me many questions. First she asked what I did last weekend. I said I went to the beach. Then she asked where I went exactly. I said I went to Valencia. She asked when I left. I said I left on Sunday afternoon. She asked who I went with. I said I went with my friends Ana and Pablo. She asked why I did not tell her before. I said because I forgot! Finally she asked how the trip went. I said it was amazing!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. What did they do last weekend? → a) Went to the beach · b) Had an interview · c) Stayed home  
2. Where exactly? → a) Valencia · b) Madrid · c) London  
3. When did they leave? → a) Sunday afternoon · b) Tuesday at 10 · c) Monday morning  
4. Who did they go with? → a) Ana and Pablo · b) Two managers · c) Alone  
5. How did the trip go? → a) Amazing · b) Terrible · c) Cancelled

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Went to the beach**  
2. **a) Valencia**  
3. **a) Sunday afternoon**  
4. **a) Ana and Pablo**  
5. **a) Amazing**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Why didn't they tell the sister before? → a) Forgot · b) Angry · c) No trip  
7. The sister called ____. → a) in the morning · b) at night only · c) next month  
8. Sequence: First / Then / Finally = ____. → a) order of questions · b) future plans · c) comparatives  
9. *Left* is past of ____. → a) leave · b) live · c) like  
10. The listening practices ____. → a) Wh-questions about the past · b) only *-er* · c) *to be* only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Forgot**  
7. **a) in the morning**  
8. **a) order of questions**  
9. **a) leave**  
10. **a) Wh-questions about the past**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *What did you do?* → answer uses ____. → a) past · b) *will* · c) *going to* only  
12. *Where did you go?* asks for ____. → a) place · b) reason · c) person  
13. *Who did you go with?* asks for ____. → a) person(s) · b) time · c) price  
14. *How did the trip go?* asks for ____. → a) result/opinion · b) only colour · c) size  
15. Direct form of *why I did not tell her* ≈ ____. → a) Why didn't you tell her? · b) Where are you? · c) How old are you?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) past**  
12. **a) place**  
13. **a) person(s)**  
14. **a) result/opinion**  
15. **a) Why didn't you tell her?**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: Ask about the past

**Objetivo:** escribir Wh-questions y respuestas cortas.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. ____ did you do last weekend? → *What / Who / How*  
2. ____ did you go? → *Where / Why / How*  
3. ____ did you leave? → *When / Who / What*  
4. ____ did you go with? → *Who / When / How*  
5. ____ did the trip go? → *How / Who / Where*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **What** · 2. **Where** · 3. **When** · 4. **Who** · 5. **How**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. What / did / you / do / yesterday?  
7. Where / did / she / go / last week?  
8. Why / did / he / leave / early?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. What did you do yesterday?  
7. Where did she go last week?  
8. Why did he leave early?

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Pregunta qué hiciste el fin de semana.  
10. Pregunta dónde fuiste.  
11. Pregunta cuándo saliste.  
12. Pregunta con quién fuiste.  
13. Pregunta por qué no lo dijiste.  
14. Pregunta cómo fue el viaje.  
15. Mini diálogo (6–8 líneas): 3 preguntas + respuestas.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. What did you do last weekend?  
10. Where did you go?  
11. When did you leave?  
12. Who did you go with?  
13. Why didn't you tell me?  
14. How did the trip go?  
15. A: What did you do last weekend? B: I went to the beach. A: Where did you go? B: Valencia. A: How did it go? B: It was amazing!

</details>
