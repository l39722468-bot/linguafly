## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 51 (A2)

- Formas: **Shall we…?**, **Let's…**, **Why don't we…?**, **How about…?**
- Invitaciones: **Would you like to…?**
- Respuestas: aceptar, dudar, rechazar con educación.
- Vocabulario de **planes y actividades en grupo**.
- Entender invitaciones y producir un mini diálogo de planes.

## 1. Formas clave para sugerir

| Forma | Estructura | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shall we…?** | Shall we + verbo base | **Shall we** go to the park? |
| **Let's…** | Let's + verbo base | **Let's** have lunch. |
| **Why don't we…?** | Why don't we + verbo base | **Why don't we** watch a film? |
| **How about…?** | How about + -ing / noun | **How about** meeting at 6? |
| **Would you like to…?** | Would you like to + verbo | **Would you like to** come? |

> **Shall we** take the bus?  
> **Let's** stay at home.  
> **How about** a pizza?

## 2. *Would you like to…?* (invitar)

## 3. Responder a una sugerencia

| Tipo | Frases |
| :--- | :--- |
| Aceptar | **Yes, great!** / **Good idea.** / **I'd love to.** / **Sure.** |
| Dudar | **Maybe.** / **I'm not sure.** / **What time?** |
| Rechazar (educado) | **Sorry, I can't.** / **I'd love to, but I'm busy.** / **Maybe another day.** |

> A: Shall we go shopping?  
> B: **I'd love to, but** I have to study.

---

## 4. Vocabulario de planes

| Actividad | Ejemplo de sugerencia |
| :--- | :--- |
| go for a walk | Let's **go for a walk**. |
| have a coffee | Shall we **have a coffee**? |
| see a film / go to the cinema | Why don't we **see a film**? |
| hang out | How about **hanging out** on Saturday? |
| stay in | Let's **stay in** tonight. |
| meet up | Shall we **meet up** at 5? |

## 6. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura — Weekend plans

**Shall we** do something this weekend? **Let's** go to the beach on Saturday if the weather is good. **Why don't we** take the train at 9? **How about** inviting Lara too? **Would you like to** bring some sandwiches? If it rains, **let's** stay in and watch a series instead.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-51/reading-weekend.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Weekend plans"></audio>

### Diálogo — Making plans

**Eva:** **Shall we** go to the cinema tonight?  
**Nico:** **I'd love to, but** I finish work late.  
**Eva:** **How about** tomorrow?  
**Nico:** **Good idea.** **Would you like to** meet at 7?  
**Eva:** Sure. **Let's** have a coffee first.  
**Nico:** Perfect.

## 7. Errores frecuentes

1. ~~Let's to go.~~ → **Let's go.**  
2. ~~Shall we to eat?~~ → **Shall we eat?**  
3. ~~How about to go?~~ → **How about going?**  
4. ~~Why we don't go?~~ → **Why don't we go?**  
5. ~~Would you like come?~~ → **Would you like to come?**  
6. ~~No.~~ *(seco)* → **Sorry, I can't.** / **I'd love to, but…**

---

## 8. Ejercicios (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Empareja

1. Let's…  
2. Shall we…?  
3. How about…?  
4. Would you like to…?  
5. I'd love to, but…  

a) polite invitation  
b) soft alternative (+ -ing / noun)  
c) direct suggestion  
d) polite refusal start  
e) suggestion as a question

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1 → c  
2 → e  
3 → b  
4 → a  
5 → d  

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa

1. ___ we take a taxi?  
2. ___ stay at home.  
3. Why ___ we call her?  
4. How about ___ (go) for a walk?  
5. Would you like ___ (come) with us?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Shall  
2. Let's  
3. don't  
4. going  
5. to come  

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Elige la forma correcta

1. ___ a coffee?  
   a) How about  b) Shall we  c) Let's to  
2. ___ go now!  
   a) How about  b) Let's  c) Would you like  
3. ___ you like to join us?  
   a) Shall  b) Would  c) Let's

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. a  
2. b  
3. b  

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrige el error

1. Let's to watch a film.  
2. Shall we to leave?  
3. How about to meet at 5?  
4. Why we don't eat out?  
5. Would you like come?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Let's watch a film.  
2. Shall we leave?  
3. How about meeting at 5?  
4. Why don't we eat out?  
5. Would you like to come?  

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Traduce al inglés

1. ¿Salimos?  
2. Vamos a tomar un café.  
3. ¿Qué tal si quedamos a las 6?  
4. ¿Te gustaría venir?  
5. Me encantaría, pero estoy ocupado/a.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Shall we go out? / Shall we leave?  
2. Let's have a coffee.  
3. How about meeting at 6?  
4. Would you like to come?  
5. I'd love to, but I'm busy.  

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa el diálogo

**A:** ___ we go shopping?  
**B:** Sorry, I ___. How ___ tomorrow?  
**A:** Good idea. Would you ___ to meet at 11?  
**B:** Sure. ___ have lunch after that.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

Shall  
can't  
about  
like  
Let's  

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Aceptar o rechazar

Escribe una respuesta educada:

1. Shall we go running? *(rechazar — llueve)*  
2. Would you like to come to my party? *(aceptar)*  
3. How about pizza? *(dudar — hora)*

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Sorry, I can't — it's raining. / I'd love to, but it's raining.  
2. Yes, I'd love to. / Sure, great!  
3. Maybe — what time? / I'm not sure. What time?

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Ordena

1. we / Shall / go / ?  
2. about / How / a / coffee / ?  
3. like / Would / you / to / join / us / ?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Shall we go?  
2. How about a coffee?  
3. Would you like to join us?  

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Elige según el tono

1. Más directo → ___ go!  
2. Más pregunta → ___ we leave now?  
3. Con gerundio → How about ___ (stay) in?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Let's  
2. Shall  
3. staying  

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu diálogo de planes (8–12 líneas)

Escribe un diálogo entre dos personas. Incluye:

- **Shall we…?** o **Let's…**  
- **How about…?** o **Why don't we…?**  
- **Would you like to…?**  
- 1 aceptación y 1 rechazo educado (o duda)

**Modelo:** el diálogo de la sección 6.

## 9. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Formas de sugerir + respuestas  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–6.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha lectura y diálogo.  
4. **5–8 min** — Ejercicio 10.

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *Shall we…?* y *Let's…*  
- [ ] Uso *How about + -ing/noun*  
- [ ] Invito con *Would you like to…?*  
- [ ] Acepto / rechazo con educación  
- [ ] Evito *Let's to* y *How about to*

## 10. Consejos y siguiente paso
