## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 19 (A2)

- Preguntar **How long…?** (duración).
- Preguntar **How often…?** (frecuencia).
- Preguntar **How much…?** / **How many…?** (cantidad).
- Preguntar **How long ago…?** (pasado acabado).
- Responder en encuestas y rutinas (gimnasio, estudio, hábitos).

![How questions A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-19/a2-unit19-how-questions.png)

## 1. Mapa rápido de *How…?*

| Pregunta | Pregunta por… | Respuesta típica |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **How long…?** | duración | for four hours / for two years |
| **How often…?** | frecuencia | three times a week / every day |
| **How much…?** | cantidad (incontable) | two litres / £10 |
| **How many…?** | cantidad (contable) | twelve books / three languages |
| **How long ago…?** | cuándo en el pasado | two hours ago / three years ago |

---

## 2. *How long…?* (duración)

> **How long** have you lived here?  
> — For five years. / Since 2020.

> **How long** do you study every day?  
> — For four hours.

> **How long** do you run each time?  
> — For thirty minutes.

## 3. *How often…?* (frecuencia)

> **How often** do you exercise?  
> — Three times a week.

> **How often** do you go to the gym?  
> — Three times a week.

> **How often** do you watch films?  
> — Twice a week.

> **How often** do you go running?  
> — Four times a week.

## 4. *How much…?* vs *How many…?*

![How much vs How many](/blog/curso-a2/unit-19/a2-unit19-much-many.png)

| How much | How many |
| :--- | :--- |
| incontables | contables |
| water, coffee, money, time | books, people, languages, cups |
| How much water do you drink? | How many books have you read? |
| How much does it cost? | How many people are in your class? |

> **How much** water do you drink? — About two litres a day.  
> **How much** coffee do you drink? — Two cups every morning.  
> **How many** books have you read this year? — Twelve.  
> **How many** languages do you speak? — Three.

## 5. *How long ago…?* (pasado acabado)

> **How long ago** did you arrive?  
> — Two hours ago.

> **How long ago** did you meet her?  
> — Ten years ago.

Usa **Past Simple** + respuesta con **ago** (no *for/since* aquí).

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-19/how-long-ago-arrive.mp3" title="🔊 How long ago did you arrive?"></audio>

| How long…? | How long ago…? |
| :--- | :--- |
| duración (sigue / rutina) | cuándo pasó (acabado) |
| How long have you lived here? | How long ago did you move here? |
| For five years. | Five years ago. |

## 6. Texto modelo: Jake

## 7. Listening: Lisa

> Hi! I am Lisa. People often ask me about my habits. How often do I exercise? I go running four times a week. How long do I run each time? I run for thirty minutes. How much coffee do I drink a day? I drink two cups every morning. How many languages do I speak? I speak three: English, Spanish and French. I have been learning Spanish for two years. I feel energetic because I exercise regularly and drink enough water.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-19/dialogue-lisa.mp3" title="🔊 Hi! I am Lisa. People often ask me…"></audio>

Preguntas rápidas:

1. ¿Con qué frecuencia corre? → *four times a week*  
2. ¿Cuánto corre cada vez? → *for thirty minutes*  
3. ¿Cuánto café? → *two cups*  
4. ¿Cuántos idiomas? → *three*

## 10. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| How many water do you drink? | How **much** water…? | *water* incontable |
| How much books…? | How **many** books…? | *books* contable |
| How often have you lived here? | How **long** have you lived here? | duración ≠ frecuencia |
| How long ago have you arrived? | How long ago **did** you arrive? | *ago* → Past Simple |
| How long do you live here? *(si sigue ahora)* | How long **have** you **lived** here? | Present Perfect + *for/since* |

## 11. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige la pregunta

1. ____ do you exercise? — Three times a week.  
2. ____ have you lived here? — For five years.  
3. ____ water do you drink? — Two litres.  
4. ____ books have you read? — Twelve.  
5. ____ did you arrive? — Two hours ago.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **How often** · 2. **How long** · 3. **How much** · 4. **How many** · 5. **How long ago**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — *much* o *many*

1. How ____ coffee do you drink?  
2. How ____ languages do you speak?  
3. How ____ people are in your class?  
4. How ____ money do you need?  
5. How ____ films do you watch a week?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **much** · 2. **many** · 3. **many** · 4. **much** · 5. **many**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Completa a Jake

How ____ do I exercise? Three times a week. How ____ do I study every day? For four hours. How ____ water do I drink? Two litres. How ____ books have I read? Twelve. I watch films ____ a week.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**often** · **long** · **much** · **many** · **twice**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Completa a Lisa

How ____ do I go running? Four times a week. How ____ do I run each time? For thirty minutes. How ____ coffee do I drink? Two cups. How ____ languages do I speak? Three.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**often** · **long** · **much** · **many**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Empareja

1. How long  
2. How often  
3. How much  
4. How many  
5. How long ago  

a) frecuencia · b) duración · c) incontable · d) contable · e) pasado + *ago*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **c** · 4 → **d** · 5 → **e**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Corrige

1. *How many water do you drink?*  
2. *How much books have you read?*  
3. *How often have you lived here?*  
4. *How long ago have you arrived?*  
5. *How long do you live here?* *(sigue viviendo allí)*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. How **much** water do you drink?  
2. How **many** books have you read?  
3. How **long** have you lived here?  
4. How long ago **did** you arrive?  
5. How long **have** you **lived** here?
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Escribe la pregunta

1. I go to the gym three times a week. → ____  
2. I study for four hours every day. → ____  
3. I drink two litres of water a day. → ____  
4. I have read twelve books. → ____  
5. I arrived two hours ago. → ____

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. How often do you go to the gym?  
2. How long do you study every day?  
3. How much water do you drink a day?  
4. How many books have you read?  
5. How long ago did you arrive?
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Elige la correcta

1. ____  
   a) How often do you exercise? · b) How long do you exercise a week? *(si quieres frecuencia)* · c) How many do you exercise?  
2. ____  
   a) How much water…? · b) How many water…? · c) How often water…?  
3. ____  
   a) How many languages do you speak? · b) How much languages do you speak? · c) How long languages do you speak?  
4. ____  
   a) How long ago did you arrive? · b) How long ago have you arrived? · c) How long ago you arrived?

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a** · 2. **a** · 3. **a** · 4. **a**
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Traduce

1. ¿Con qué frecuencia haces ejercicio?  
2. ¿Cuánto tiempo estudias cada día?  
3. ¿Cuánta agua bebes?  
4. ¿Cuántos libros has leído este año?  
5. ¿Hace cuánto llegaste?  
6. Voy al gimnasio tres veces a la semana.  
7. Estudio durante cuatro horas cada día.  
8. Bebo dos litros al día.  
9. Hablo tres idiomas.  
10. Corro durante treinta minutos.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

2. How long do you study every day?  
3. How much water do you drink?  
4. How many books have you read this year?  
5. How long ago did you arrive?  
6. I go to the gym three times a week.  
7. I study for four hours every day.  
8. I drink two litres a day.  
9. I speak three languages.

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu encuesta

Escribe 8–12 líneas:

- 2 × *How often…?* + respuesta  
- 2 × *How long…?* + respuesta  
- 1 × *How much…?* · 1 × *How many…?*  
- 1 × *How long ago…?*  

**Modelo:** Jake o Lisa.

## 12. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Tabla de las 5 preguntas *How…?*  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–7.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha a Jake y Lisa; marca cada *How*.  
4. **5–8 min** — Graba tu mini encuesta.

### Checklist

- [ ] Distingo *How long / often / much / many / long ago*  
- [ ] Uso *much* con incontables y *many* con contables  
- [ ] Respondo frecuencia con *once/twice/… times a week*  
- [ ] Uso *How long ago* + Past Simple + *ago*  
- [ ] Puedo hablar de mi rutina con preguntas y respuestas

## 13. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Primero elige **qué** preguntas (tiempo, frecuencia, cantidad…).  
2. Luego elige la forma *How…?* correcta.
