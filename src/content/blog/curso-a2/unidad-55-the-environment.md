## 1. Vocabulario clave

| Palabra | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **environment** | medio ambiente | Protect the **environment**. |
| **recycle** | reciclar | We **recycle** glass and paper. |
| **pollution** | contaminación | Air **pollution** is a problem. |
| **pollute** | contaminar | Cars **pollute** the air. |
| **plastic** | plástico | Use less **plastic**. |
| **save** | ahorrar / salvar | **Save** water and energy. |
| **waste** | desperdiciar | Don't **waste** food. |
| **reuse** | reutilizar | **Reuse** your bags. |

También: **rubbish / trash**, **bin**, **public transport**, **energy**, **climate**.

## 2. Acciones cotidianas

| Acción | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| recycle | I **recycle** bottles every week. |
| save water | Turn off the tap — **save water**. |
| save energy | Turn off the lights to **save energy**. |
| use less plastic | I try to **use less plastic**. |
| walk / cycle / take the bus | I **go by bike** to work. |
| plant trees | They **plant trees** in the park. |

## 3. Consejos con *should*

| Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| should + verbo | You **should** recycle. |
| shouldn't + verbo | You **shouldn't** waste water. |
| We should… | **We should** use public transport. |

> You **should** take a reusable bag.  
> People **shouldn't** throw plastic in the sea.

## 6. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura — Green tips at home

Protecting the **environment** starts at home. You **should recycle** paper, glass and **plastic**. **Save water** when you brush your teeth, and turn off lights to **save energy**. **Don't waste** food. If you can, walk or take the bus — cars **pollute** the air. Small actions help every day.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-55/reading-tips.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Green tips at home"></audio>

### Diálogo — At the supermarket

**Ana:** We **shouldn't** buy so many plastic bags.  
**Leo:** You're right. We **should** reuse this one.  
**Ana:** Do you **recycle** at home?  
**Leo:** Yes — paper and bottles. I also try to **save water**.  
**Ana:** Good. **Pollution** is getting worse in the city.  
**Leo:** Let's walk more and use the bus.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-55/dialogue-shop.mp3" title="🔊 Dialogue: At the supermarket"></audio>

### Mini práctica

## 7. Errores frecuentes

1. ~~the nature~~ *(a menudo)* → **nature** / **the environment**  
2. ~~You should to recycle.~~ → **You should recycle.**  
3. ~~pollutions~~ → **pollution** (incontable en este uso)  
4. ~~recycle the environment~~ → **protect** the environment / **recycle** plastic  
5. ~~save the water always~~ → **save water**  
6. ~~I'm recycle~~ → **I recycle**

## 8. Ejercicios (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — Empareja

1. recycle  
2. pollution  
3. save  
4. waste  
5. plastic  

a) use less / not throw away energy or water  
b) material in bottles and bags  
c) dirty air, water or land  
d) use again after processing  
e) use badly or throw away useful things

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1 → d  
2 → c  
3 → a  
4 → e  
5 → b  

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa

1. We ___ bottles every week.  
2. ___ water — turn off the tap.  
3. You ___ use public transport.  
4. You ___ throw plastic in the sea.  
5. Cars ___ the air.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. recycle  
2. Save  
3. should  
4. shouldn't  
5. pollute  

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — *should* / *shouldn't*

1. People ___ recycle more.  
2. We ___ waste food.  
3. You ___ walk or cycle when you can.  
4. Factories ___ pollute rivers.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. should  
2. shouldn't  
3. should  
4. shouldn't  

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrige el error

1. You should to recycle.  
2. There are many pollutions in the city.  
3. Save the water always.  
4. I am recycle at home.  
5. Protect the nature environment. *(elige una forma natural)*

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. You should recycle.  
2. There is a lot of pollution in the city.  
3. Always save water. / Save water.  
4. I recycle at home.  
5. Protect the environment. / Protect nature.

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — True / False
