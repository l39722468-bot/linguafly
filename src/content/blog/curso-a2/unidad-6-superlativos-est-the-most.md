En la [Unidad 5](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-5-comparativos-er-more-than) comparaste **dos** cosas (*taller than*). En la **Unidad 6 del curso A2** (*Superlatives*) eliges **el extremo de un grupo**: ***the tallest***, ***the most beautiful***, ***the best*** — con ***in*** / ***of***.

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 6 — Superlativos](/curso-a2/unit-6)  
> **Antes:** [U5 — Comparativos](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-5-comparativos-er-more-than)

---

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 6 (A2)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Formar superlativos cortos: ***the + -est*** (*the biggest, the hottest*).
- Formar superlativos largos: ***the most + adjective***.
- Usar irregulares ***the best*** / ***the worst***.
- Elegir ***in*** (lugar) u ***of*** (grupo).
- Describir tu ciudad o un lugar favorito con varios superlativos.

---

## 1. Comparativo vs superlativo

| Tipo | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Comparativo (U5) | 2 elementos | Maria is **taller than** me. |
| Superlativo (U6) | 1 entre muchos | He is **the tallest** boy in the school. |

![Superlatives the -est and the most A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-6/a2-unit6-superlatives.png)

## 2. Forma: *the … -est* / *the most …*

| Tipo | Forma | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Corto | **the** + adj + **-est** | the tallest, the biggest, the hottest |
| Largo | **the most** + adj | the most beautiful, the most interesting |
| Irregular | formas especiales | **the best**, **the worst** |

> Mount Everest is **the highest** mountain in the world.  
> She is **the most intelligent** student in the class.  
> This is **the coldest** day of the year.  
> He is **the tallest** boy in the school.

## 3. Adjetivos cortos frecuentes

| Adjetivo | Superlativo | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| tall | **the tallest** | the tallest building in town |
| big | **the biggest** | the biggest park in town |
| small | **the smallest** | the smallest room in the hotel |
| high | **the highest** | the highest mountain in the world |
| hot | **the hottest** | the hottest month of the year |
| cold | **the coldest** | the coldest month |
| old | **the oldest** | the oldest church in the region |
| fast | **the fastest** | the fastest runner of all the students |
| quiet | **the quietest** | the quietest time |

Ortografía (como en U5): *big → the biggest*, *hot → the hottest*, *happy → the happiest*.

---

## 4. Adjetivos largos: *the most …*

| Adjetivo | Superlativo | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| beautiful | **the most beautiful** | the most beautiful view |
| interesting | **the most interesting** | the most interesting book |
| expensive | **the most expensive** | the most expensive shop |
| delicious | **the most delicious** | the most delicious food |
| peaceful | **the most peaceful** | the most peaceful place |
| intelligent | **the most intelligent** | the most intelligent student |

> This is **the most interesting** book I have ever read.  
> This is **the most beautiful** film I have ever seen.

## 5. Irregulares: *the best* / *the worst*

| Adjetivo | Superlativo | Incorrecto |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| good | **the best** | *the goodest* |
| bad | **the worst** | *the baddest* |

> This is **the best** film I have ever seen.  
> He is **the worst** of all my friends. *(ejemplo de forma; úsalo con cuidado en contexto real)*  
> The **best** restaurant in town serves the **most delicious** food.

## 6. *in* vs *of*

| Preposición | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **in** | lugar / grupo espacial | in the world · in the city · in my class · in town |
| **of** | conjunto / lista | of the year · of all my friends · of all the towns |

![in and of with superlatives A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-6/a2-unit6-in-of-places.png)

> July is the hottest month **of** the year.  
> The main square is the biggest **of** all the towns near.  
> It is the most peaceful place **in** the world.

## 7. Texto modelo: mi pueblo

> My hometown is small but beautiful. It has the oldest church in the region. The main square is the biggest of all the towns near. In summer, July is the hottest month of the year. My grandmother lives in the tallest building in town. She says it is the most beautiful view she has ever seen. The best restaurant in town serves the most delicious food. I think my hometown is the best place in the world!

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-6/reading-hometown.mp3" title="🔊 My hometown is small but beautiful…"></audio>

Marca: *the oldest · the biggest · the hottest · the tallest · the most beautiful · the best · the most delicious*.

## 10. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| the most tall | **the tallest** | corto → *-est* |
| the beautifulest | **the most beautiful** | largo → *the most* |
| the goodest | **the best** | irregular |
| tallest mountain (sin *the*) | **the** tallest mountain | casi siempre *the* |
| the biggest in all my friends | the biggest **of** all my friends | grupo → *of* |
| the hottest month in the year | the hottest month **of** the year | *of the year* (frecuente) |

---

## 11. Mini prácticas orales

1. Di el edificio / persona **the tallest** de tu entorno.  
2. Di **the most beautiful** lugar de tu ciudad.  
3. Di **the best** restaurante o parque.  
4. Une 4–5 superlativos en un mini-texto (como el del pueblo).

> The tallest person in my family is my brother.  
> The best restaurant in my town is called La Luna.  
> It is the most peaceful place in the world.

## 12. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — *-est* o *the most*

1. Mount Everest is \_\_\_\_ mountain in the world. (high)  
2. She is \_\_\_\_ student in the class. (intelligent)  
3. This is \_\_\_\_ day of the year. (cold)  
4. He is \_\_\_\_ boy in the school. (tall)  
5. This is \_\_\_\_ book I have ever read. (interesting)  
6. July is \_\_\_\_ month of the year. (hot)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **the highest** · 2. **the most intelligent** · 3. **the coldest** · 4. **the tallest** · 5. **the most interesting** · 6. **the hottest**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — *best / worst*

1. This is \_\_\_\_ film I have ever seen. (good)  
2. He is \_\_\_\_ of all my friends. (bad)  
3. The \_\_\_\_ restaurant in town serves pizza. (good)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **the best** · 2. **the worst** · 3. **best** (*the best restaurant*)
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — *in* / *of*

1. the tallest building \_\_\_\_ town  
2. the hottest month \_\_\_\_ the year  
3. the biggest \_\_\_\_ all the towns near  
4. the most peaceful place \_\_\_\_ the world  
5. the most intelligent student \_\_\_\_ the class

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **in** · 2. **of** · 3. **of** · 4. **in** · 5. **in**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Empareja

1. big  
2. beautiful  
3. good  
4. bad  
5. old  
6. hot  

a) the hottest · b) the biggest · c) the best · d) the oldest · e) the most beautiful · f) the worst

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **e** · 3 → **c** · 4 → **f** · 5 → **d** · 6 → **a**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Completa el texto del pueblo

It has the \_\_\_\_ church in the region. The main square is the \_\_\_\_ of all the towns near. July is the \_\_\_\_ month of the year. She lives in the \_\_\_\_ building in town. It is the \_\_\_\_ beautiful view. The \_\_\_\_ restaurant serves the \_\_\_\_ delicious food. My hometown is the \_\_\_\_ place in the world!

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**oldest** · **biggest** · **hottest** · **tallest** · **most** · **best** · **most** · **best**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa el listening (park)

It is the \_\_\_\_ park in town. It has the \_\_\_\_ trees. There are the \_\_\_\_ beautiful swans. The \_\_\_\_ time to visit is early morning when it is the \_\_\_\_. It is the \_\_\_\_ peaceful place in the world.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**biggest** · **tallest** · **most** · **best** · **quietest** · **most**
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Corrige

1. *She is the most tall in the class.*  
2. *This is beautifulest film.*  
3. *This is the goodest restaurant.*  
4. *He is tallest boy in the school.*  
5. *July is the hottest month in the year.*  
6. *the biggest in all my friends*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. She is **the tallest** in the class.  
2. This is **the most beautiful** film.  
3. This is **the best** restaurant.  
4. He is **the** tallest boy in the school.  
5. July is the hottest month **of** the year.  
6. the biggest **of** all my friends
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Comparativo o superlativo

1. Maria is \_\_\_\_ than me. (tall)  
2. He is \_\_\_\_ boy in the school. (tall)  
3. This book is \_\_\_\_ than that one. (interesting)  
4. This is \_\_\_\_ book I have ever read. (interesting)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **taller** · 2. **the tallest** · 3. **more interesting** · 4. **the most interesting**
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Traduce

1. Es el edificio más alto del pueblo.  
2. Es el mes más caluroso del año.  
3. Es la iglesia más antigua de la región.  
4. Es el mejor restaurante de la ciudad.  
5. Es el lugar más tranquilo del mundo.  
6. Es la película más bonita que he visto.  
7. Es el parque más grande del pueblo.  
8. Es el estudiante más inteligente de la clase.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. It is the tallest building in town.  
2. It is the hottest month of the year.  
3. It is the oldest church in the region.  
4. It is the best restaurant in the city / in town.  
5. It is the most peaceful / quietest place in the world.  
6. It is the most beautiful film I have ever seen.  
7. It is the biggest park in town.  
8. He/She is the most intelligent student in the class.
</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Escribe tu ciudad / lugar favorito

Escribe 8–12 frases. Incluye:

- 3 × *the … -est*  
- 2 × *the most …*  
- 1 × *the best*  
- *in* y *of* al menos una vez cada uno

**Modelo:** sección 7 (hometown) o sección 8 (park).

## 13. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Repasa *-est* vs *the most* y *best/worst*.  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–7.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha el pueblo y el parque; marca cada superlativo.  
4. **5–8 min** — Escribe y graba tu descripción.

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *the* + *-est* con adjetivos cortos  
- [ ] Uso *the most* con adjetivos largos  
- [ ] Recuerdo *the best / the worst*  
- [ ] Distingo *in* (lugar) y *of* (grupo)  
- [ ] Puedo describir mi ciudad con 5+ superlativos

## 14. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Si en U5 usaste *-er / more*, aquí pasa a *-est / the most* + **the**.  
2. Memoriza pares: *bigger → the biggest*, *more beautiful → the most beautiful*.  
3. Practica *in the world / in my city / of the year / of all…*.  
4. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 6](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-6-superlativos-est-the-most-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 6 del curso A2](/curso-a2/unit-6).

La **Unidad 7** entra en **adverbios de modo**: *quickly, slowly, well, badly* y *-ly*.
