En la [Unidad 6](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-6-superlativos-est-the-most) elegiste “el más…”. En la **Unidad 7 del curso A2** (*Adverbs of Manner*) describes **cómo** se hace algo: ***quickly, slowly, carefully, well, badly***.

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 7 — Adverbios de modo](/curso-a2/unit-7)  
> **Antes:** [U6 — Superlativos](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-6-superlativos-est-the-most)

---

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 7 (A2)

Al terminar esta unidad serás capaz de:

- Formar adverbios con ***adjective + -ly***.
- Usar excepciones: ***well*** (de *good*), ***badly*** (de *bad*), ***fast***, ***hard***.
- Colocar el adverbio **después del verbo** (o del objeto).
- Contar acciones con *fluently, carefully, beautifully, loudly…*.
- Evitar *\*She sings good* → **She sings well**.

---

## 1. Adjetivo vs adverbio

| Tipo | Pregunta | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Adjetivo** | ¿cómo es? | She is a **careful** driver. |
| **Adverbio** | ¿cómo lo hace? | She drives **carefully**. |

![Adverbs of manner -ly A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-7/a2-unit7-adverbs-manner.png)

Regla prioritaria A2: **adjective + -ly**.

> She runs **quickly**.  
> He speaks **slowly** because he is tired.  
> They work **carefully**.

## 3. Excepciones: *well, badly, fast, hard*

| Adjetivo | Adverbio | Incorrecto típico |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| good | **well** | *She sings good* |
| bad | **badly** | *He played bad* |
| fast | **fast** | *fastly* |
| hard | **hard** | *hardly* (= casi no; otro significado) |

> She sings **well**.  
> He played **badly** in the match.  
> He works **hard** every day.  
> She runs **fast**.

## 4. Ortografía de *-ly*

![Spelling -ly A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-7/a2-unit7-spelling-ly.png)

| Regla | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| + **-ly** | quick → quickly · slow → slowly |
| **-y** → **-ily** | happy → happily · easy → easily |
| **-ful** → **-fully** | careful → carefully · beautiful → beautifully |
| **-le** → **-ly** (cuando aplica) | possible → possibly |

---

## 5. Posición en la frase

En A2, el adverbio de modo suele ir:

1. **Después del verbo:** She runs quickly.  
2. **Después del objeto:** She answered the question correctly. / She speaks English fluently.

| Correcto | Menos natural / incorrecto en A2 |
| :--- | :--- |
| She speaks English **fluently**. | *She fluently speaks English* (menos prioritario aquí) |
| She answered **correctly**. | *She correctly answered* (posible, pero el curso prioriza final) |

## 6. Texto modelo: Maria

> My friend Maria speaks English very fluently. She learned quickly because she practises every day. She reads books slowly to understand everything well. Last week she sang beautifully at the concert. The audience clapped loudly. Maria also writes carefully and she never makes mistakes. She drives very carefully too. I think she does everything well!

## 9. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| She sings good. | She sings **well**. | *good* → *well* |
| He played bad. | He played **badly**. | adverbio *-ly* / *badly* |
| She runs quick. | She runs **quickly**. | falta *-ly* |
| He works hardly. | He works **hard**. | *hardly* ≠ *hard* |
| She speaks fluent English. *(si quieres adverbio)* | She speaks English **fluently**. | adverbio de modo |
| She carefully writes. *(prioridad A2)* | She writes **carefully**. | posición final |

---

## 10. Mini prácticas orales

1. Di cómo hablas inglés (*slowly / fluently / carefully*).  
2. Di cómo estudias o trabajas (*hard / carefully / quickly*).  
3. Cuenta un momento: *At first I … badly. Then I … carefully. Finally I … well.*

> She runs quickly.  
> She sings well.  
> He played badly.  
> They work carefully.

## 11. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Forma el adverbio

1. She runs \_\_\_\_. (quick)  
2. He speaks \_\_\_\_ because he is tired. (slow)  
3. They work \_\_\_\_. (careful)  
4. She answered the question \_\_\_\_. (correct)  
5. He speaks \_\_\_\_ English. (fluent)  
6. Please speak \_\_\_\_ in the library. (quiet)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **quickly** · 2. **slowly** · 3. **carefully** · 4. **correctly** · 5. **fluently** · 6. **quietly**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — *well / badly / hard / fast*

1. She sings \_\_\_\_. (good)  
2. He played \_\_\_\_ in the match. (bad)  
3. He works \_\_\_\_ every day. (hard)  
4. She runs \_\_\_\_. (fast)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **well** · 2. **badly** · 3. **hard** · 4. **fast**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Empareja

1. quick  
2. careful  
3. beautiful  
4. good  
5. bad  
6. happy  

a) carefully · b) well · c) quickly · d) happily · e) badly · f) beautifully

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **c** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **f** · 4 → **b** · 5 → **e** · 6 → **d**
</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Completa el texto de Maria

Maria speaks English very \_\_\_\_. She learned \_\_\_\_. She reads books \_\_\_\_ to understand everything \_\_\_\_. She sang \_\_\_\_. The audience clapped \_\_\_\_. She writes \_\_\_\_ and drives \_\_\_\_. She does everything \_\_\_\_!

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**fluently** · **quickly** · **slowly** · **well** · **beautifully** · **loudly** · **carefully** · **carefully** · **well**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Completa el listening

The teacher moved very \_\_\_\_. At first I danced \_\_\_\_. He explained everything \_\_\_\_ and \_\_\_\_. I practised \_\_\_\_ and by the end I danced \_\_\_\_! My friend sang \_\_\_\_.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

**quickly** · **badly** · **slowly** · **patiently** · **carefully** · **well** · **beautifully**
</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Corrige

1. *She sings good.*  
2. *He played bad.*  
3. *She runs quick.*  
4. *He works hardly every day.* *(si quieres “con esfuerzo”)*  
5. *She speaks fluent.* *(como adverbio de modo)*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. She sings **well**.  
2. He played **badly**.  
3. She runs **quickly**.  
4. He works **hard** every day.  
5. She speaks **fluently**. / She speaks fluent English. *(adjetivo + noun)*
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Adjetivo o adverbio

1. She is a \_\_\_\_ driver. (careful)  
2. She drives \_\_\_\_. (careful)  
3. He is a \_\_\_\_ student. (good)  
4. He studies \_\_\_\_. (good → well)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **careful** · 2. **carefully** · 3. **good** · 4. **well**
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Elige la correcta

1. ____  
   a) She speaks English fluent. · b) She speaks English fluently. · c) She speaks English fluentlyly.  
2. ____  
   a) He works hard. · b) He works hardly. · c) He works hardily. *(esfuerzo)*  
3. Most adverbs of manner add: ____  
   a) -ing · b) -ed · c) -ly

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **b** · 2. **a** · 3. **c**
</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — Traduce

1. Ella corre rápido.  
2. Él habla despacio.  
3. Ella canta bien.  
4. Él jugó mal.  
5. Trabajan con cuidado.  
6. Habla inglés con fluidez.  
7. Por favor, habla bajo en la biblioteca.  
8. El profesor explicó todo con paciencia.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. She runs quickly. / She runs fast.  
2. He speaks slowly.  
3. She sings well.  
4. He played badly.  
5. They work carefully.  
6. She/He speaks English fluently.  
7. Please speak quietly in the library.  
8. The teacher explained everything patiently.
</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Escribe tu día con adverbios

Escribe 8–12 frases. Incluye:

- 4 × adverbios con *-ly*  
- 1 × *well*  
- 1 × *badly* o *hard* / *fast*  
- un mini-relato *At first… Then… Finally…* (como el baile)

**Modelo:** sección 6 (Maria) o sección 7 (dance class).

## 12. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Repasa *-ly* y excepciones *well/badly/fast/hard*.  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–7.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha a Maria y la clase de baile; repite los adverbios.  
4. **5–8 min** — Escribe y graba tu párrafo.

### Checklist

- [ ] Formo adverbios con *-ly*  
- [ ] Uso *well* (no *good*) con verbos  
- [ ] Distingo *hard* y *hardly*  
- [ ] Coloco el adverbio tras el verbo/objeto  
- [ ] Puedo contar una actividad con 5+ adverbios

## 13. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Pregunta: ¿describo **la cosa** (adjetivo) o **la acción** (adverbio)?  
2. Lista de 10: *quick, slow, careful, quiet, easy, beautiful, fluent, loud, patient, happy* → di el adverbio.  
3. Grábate diciendo: *I study carefully. I speak slowly. I want to speak fluently.*  
4. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 7](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-7-adverbios-modo-ly-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 7 del curso A2](/curso-a2/unit-7).

La **Unidad 8** entra en **preposiciones de tiempo**: *at, on, in*.
