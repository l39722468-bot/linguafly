## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** Present Perfect + Past Continuous + How questions (Module 2 mix).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I have lived here ____ three years. → *for / since / ago*
2. I have worked here ____ 2021. → *since / for / on*
3. I ____ walking when I met a friend. → *was / were / have*
4. How ____ do you exercise? → *often / much / many*
5. I haven't found a job ____. → *yet / already / ago*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **for** · 2. **since** · 3. **was** · 4. **often** · 5. **yet**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Module 2 includes ____. → a) Present Perfect and Past Continuous · b) only *to be* · c) only A1 colours
7. *already/yet* with ____. → a) Present Perfect · b) only Past Continuous · c) *will*
8. *when* often mixes ____. → a) Past Continuous + Past Simple · b) only Future · c) only *the most*
9. *How many* needs ____. → a) countable · b) only uncountable · c) only *-ing*
10. Correct mix: ____. → a) I have lived here for 3 years; I was walking when I met her · b) I live here since 3 years yesterday watching · c) I am lived when

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) Present Perfect and Past Continuous** · 7. **a) Present Perfect** · 8. **a) Past Continuous + Past Simple** · 9. **a) countable** · 10. **a) I have lived here for 3 years; I was walking when I met her**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I have lived here since three years.*
12. *I was walk when I met him.*
13. *How often you exercise?*
14. *I have already not found a job yet.*
15. *Yesterday I have cooked when she called.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I have lived here **for** three years.  
12. I was **walking** when I met him.  
13. How often **do** you exercise?  
14. I haven't found a job **yet**. / I have **already**…  
15. Yesterday I **was cooking** when she **called**.  

</details>

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Elige

2. Practice includes ____. → a) grammar + skills · b) only titles · c) no English
3. Reading helps you ____. → a) see forms in context · b) avoid verbs · c) skip audio
4. Listening checks ____. → a) comprehension · b) only spelling of *-est* · c) only Module 6
5. Writing produces ____. → a) your own sentences · b) nothing · c) only copy images

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Present Perfect + Past Continuous + How questions (Module 2 mix)** · 2. **a) grammar + skills** · 3. **a) see forms in context** · 4. **a) comprehension** · 5. **a) your own sentences**

</details>

7. A common signal word from the texts: ____.
8. Affirmative example (your words): ____.

10. One vocabulary item from the story: ____.

## Lección 3 — Reading

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-20/reading-review.mp3" title="🔊 Reading Unidad 20"></audio>

> My name is David. I have lived in Barcelona for three years. I have worked as a teacher since 2021. Last week I was walking in the park when I met an old friend. We were talking while we walked. How often do I exercise? I go to the gym three times a week. I have already finished my course this year but I haven't found a new job yet. I feel good because I have a stable life with good friends.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Where has David lived? → a) Barcelona for three years · b) Valencia · c) London
2. Job since? → a) 2021 as a teacher · b) 2020 hospital · c) Never
3. Park story? → a) Was walking when he met a friend · b) Only slept · c) Flew
4. Exercise frequency? → a) Three times a week · b) Never · c) Once a year
5. New job? → a) Hasn't found one yet · b) Already found Japan · c) Never worked

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Barcelona for three years** · 2. **a) 2021 as a teacher** · 3. **a) Was walking when he met a friend** · 4. **a) Three times a week** · 5. **a) Hasn't found one yet**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The text matches unit 20: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Unit 1
7. You can find the target forms: ____. → a) yes · b) never · c) only images
8. Sequence/time words appear as needed: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only future
9. Main idea is clear from the first lines: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) impossible
10. Re-read after checking answers: ____. → a) good idea · b) useless · c) forbidden

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) yes** · 7. **a) yes** · 8. **a) yes** · 9. **a) yes** · 10. **a) good idea**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Mark 3 target forms in the text: ____. → a) do it · b) skip · c) delete the text
12. Change one sentence to negative/question: ____. → a) useful · b) wrong always · c) only B2
13. Note any Past Simple / Perfect / Continuous mix: ____. → a) notice it · b) ignore · c) ban English
14. Copy one model sentence aloud: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) never
15. Write a parallel sentence about you: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only tomorrow forever

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11–15. **a)** (práctica abierta; lo importante es usar el foco de la unidad)

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-20/listening-review.mp3" title="🔊 Listening Unidad 20"></audio>

> Hi! I am Maria. I have lived in Valencia for four years. I have worked at a hospital since 2020. Yesterday I was cooking dinner when my sister called. We were talking while I was finishing the food. How often do I exercise? I go swimming twice a week. I have already visited five countries this year but I haven't been to Japan yet. I feel happy because I have a good job and a wonderful family.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Where has Maria lived? → a) Valencia for four years · b) Barcelona · c) Manchester
2. Work since? → a) 2020 at a hospital · b) 2021 teacher · c) Never
3. Yesterday? → a) Cooking when sister called · b) Only walked forever · c) Exam only
4. Exercise? → a) Swimming twice a week · b) Gym three times · c) Never
5. Japan? → a) Hasn't been yet · b) Lives there · c) Went yesterday only

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Valencia for four years** · 2. **a) 2020 at a hospital** · 3. **a) Cooking when sister called** · 4. **a) Swimming twice a week** · 5. **a) Hasn't been yet**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Audio supports the same grammar focus: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only music
7. Speakers use natural A2 vocabulary: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Latin
8. You can answer without the script first: ____. → a) try it · b) never · c) impossible always
9. Then check with the script: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) delete audio
10. Repeat difficult sentences: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only teachers

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) yes** · 7. **a) yes** · 8. **a) try it** · 9. **a) yes** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Transcribe one key sentence: ____. → a) useful · b) useless · c) banned
12. Underline the target grammar: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) never
13. Record yourself saying it: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only offline forever
14. Compare with the model: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) skip English
15. Add the sentence to your notebook: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11–15. **a) yes / useful** (práctica abierta)

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa (según el foco)

1. Write one affirmative sentence with the unit focus.
2. Write one negative sentence.
3. Write one question.
4. Add a time marker that fits.
5. Add one vocabulary word from the reading.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

Usa los modelos del reading/listening de esta unidad; cualquier frase correcta con el foco cuenta.

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena / reescribe

6. Reorder a model sentence from the reading.
7. Change person (*I* → *she/they*).
8. Change time (yesterday / for / since / how often…).

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

Compara con el texto modelo; la concordancia sujeto–verbo debe ser correcta.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe

9–14. Six short sentences using the unit focus about your life.
15. Paragraph (6–8 sentences) mixing this unit with one idea from a previous unit.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplo guía)</summary>

15. Escribe un mini-relato como el reading: incluye el foco de la Unidad 20 y una forma de una unidad anterior del Módulo 2.

</details>
