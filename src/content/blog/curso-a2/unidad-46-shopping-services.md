## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 46 (A2)

- Frases de tienda: **Can I help you?**, **I'm looking for…**, **How much…?**
- Vocabulario: **department store**, **checkout**, **refund**, **exchange**.
- Tipos de tiendas y servicios.
- Comparar precios / productos (*cheaper*, *more expensive*).
- Entender políticas sencillas y **pedir una devolución**.

## 1. Vocabulario: tiendas y servicios

| Palabra | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **department store** | grandes almacenes | There's a big **department store** in the centre. |
| **supermarket** | supermercado | I buy food at the **supermarket**. |
| **pharmacy / chemist's** | farmacia | Can you go to the **pharmacy**? |
| **checkout** | caja | There's a long queue at the **checkout**. |
| **receipt** | ticket / recibo | Keep your **receipt**. |
| **refund** | devolución (dinero) | I'd like a **refund**, please. |
| **exchange** | cambio (producto) | Can I **exchange** this for a larger size? |
| **fitting room** | probador | Where is the **fitting room**? |

Tipos útiles: **clothes shop**, **bookshop**, **bakery**, **shoe shop**, **electronics store**.

## 2. Frases clave en la tienda

| Quién | Frase | Uso |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Dependiente | **Can I help you?** | Ofrecer ayuda |
| Dependiente | **How can I help you?** | Más formal / abierto |
| Cliente | **I'm looking for…** | Buscar algo |
| Cliente | **Excuse me.** | Llamar la atención |
| Cliente | **How much is it?** | Preguntar el precio |
| Cliente | **I'll take it.** | Me lo quedo |
| Cliente | **Just looking, thanks.** | Solo miro |

> **A:** Can I help you?  
> **B:** Yes — I'm looking for a blue jacket.

## 3. Precios y comparativos

| Pregunta / frase | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- |
| How much is…? | **How much is** this shirt? |
| How much are…? | **How much are** these shoes? |
| It costs… | It **costs** €40. |
| It's cheaper / more expensive | This one is **cheaper**. That one is **more expensive**. |
| It's on sale / reduced | It's **on sale**. |

> These jeans are **cheaper** than those.  
> The black bag is **more expensive**, but it's better quality.

## 4. Devoluciones: *refund* y *exchange*

| Quieres… | Di… |
| :--- | :--- |
| Dinero de vuelta | I'd like a **refund**, please. |
| Cambiar talla/color | Can I **exchange** this, please? |
| Mostrar el ticket | Here's my **receipt**. |
| Explicar el problema | It's too small. / It doesn't work. |

> Excuse me — I'd like to **exchange** this T-shirt for a larger size. Here's my **receipt**.

## 6. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura — Store policy

Our **department store** is open every day from 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. You can pay at the **checkout** by card or cash. If you need a **refund** or an **exchange**, bring your **receipt** within 14 days. Clothes must have the label. We're happy to help — just ask at customer service.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-46/reading-policy.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Store policy"></audio>

### Diálogo — At the clothes shop

**Assistant:** Good morning. **Can I help you?**  
**Customer:** Yes. **I'm looking for** a jacket.  
**Assistant:** What size?  
**Customer:** Medium. **How much is** this one?  
**Assistant:** It's €59. That grey one is **cheaper** — €45.  
**Customer:** I'll take the grey one, please.  
**Assistant:** Perfect. The **checkout** is over there.

## 7. Errores frecuentes

1. ~~How many is it?~~ → **How much is it?** (precio = *much*)  
2. ~~I look for a shirt.~~ *(en tienda)* → **I'm looking for** a shirt.  
3. ~~Can I help to you?~~ → **Can I help you?**  
4. ~~I want refund.~~ → **I'd like a refund**, please.  
5. ~~This is more cheap.~~ → **This is cheaper.**  
6. ~~How much costs this?~~ → **How much does this cost?** / **How much is this?**

### Ejercicio 9 — Política de tienda

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu diálogo (8–12 líneas)

Escribe un diálogo en una tienda de ropa. Incluye:

- **Can I help you?** / **I'm looking for…**  
- **How much…?**  
- 1 comparativo (*cheaper / more expensive*)  
- **I'll take it** o **I'd like a refund/exchange**

**Modelo:** el diálogo de la sección 6.
