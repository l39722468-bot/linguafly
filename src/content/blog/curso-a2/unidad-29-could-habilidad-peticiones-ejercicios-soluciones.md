## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** could / couldn't (habilidad pasada) y Could you…? (peticiones educadas).

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. When I was young, I ____ swim very well. → *could / can / will*
2. Now I ____ speak English. → *can / could / will*
3. She ____ speak French when she was a child. → *could / can / cans*
4. I ____ cook when I lived with my parents. → *couldn't / can't / won't*
5. ____ you pass the salt, please? → *Could / Can / Do*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **could** · 2. **can** · 3. **could** · 4. **couldn't** · 5. **Could**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Past ability uses ____. → a) could · b) can only · c) will
7. Polite requests often use ____. → a) Could you…? · b) You open! · c) Opened you
8. *Could* changes with he/she? → a) No, same for all · b) Yes, coulds · c) Yes, cans
9. After *could* use ____. → a) infinitive without to · b) to + infinitive · c) *-ing* only
10. Unit 29 trains ____. → a) could past + polite requests · b) only have to · c) only Zero

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) could** · 7. **a) Could you…?** · 8. **a) No, same for all** · 9. **a) infinitive without to** · 10. **a) could past + polite requests**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I could to swim very well.*
12. *She coulds speak French.*
13. *When I was young, I can run fast.*
14. *Could you to help me?*
15. *I no could cook.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I could swim very well.
12. She **could** speak French.
13. When I was young, I **could** run fast.
14. Could you help me?
15. I **couldn't** cook.

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario

**Objetivo:** swim, ride a bike, piano, pass the salt, turn down, repeat, polite.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja / elige

1. *ride a bike* ≈ ____ → *use a bicycle · swim · cook*
2. *pass the salt* ≈ ____ → *give someone the salt · open a window · run fast*
3. *turn down the volume* ≈ ____ → *make sound quieter · repeat · tell time*
4. *polite* ≈ ____ → *kind / respectful · angry · fast*
5. *at the age of five* ≈ ____ → *when you were five · tomorrow · never*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **use a bicycle** · 2. **give someone the salt** · 3. **make sound quieter** · 4. **kind / respectful** · 5. **when you were five**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. When I was a child, I could ____ very well. → *swim / swimming / swam*
7. I could play the ____ at seven. → *piano / pianos / pianing*
8. Could you ____ me the salt? → *pass / passing / passed*
9. Could you ____ down the volume? → *turn / turning / turned*
10. I couldn't ____ when I lived with my parents. → *cook / cooking / cooked*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **swim** · 7. **piano** · 8. **pass** · 9. **turn** · 10. **cook**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. *When I was young* signals ____. → a) past · b) future · c) Zero only
12. *Could you…, please?* is ____. → a) a polite request · b) past ability · c) obligation
13. *Now I can* contrasts with ____. → a) couldn't before · b) will tomorrow · c) have to
14. *Could you repeat that?* asks someone to ____. → a) say again · b) swim · c) pay bills
15. Unit 29 is about ____. → a) could (past + requests) · b) only conditionals · c) only have to

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) past** · 12. **a) a polite request** · 13. **a) couldn't before** · 14. **a) say again** · 15. **a) could (past + requests)**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Childhood and requests

**Objetivo:** comprender un texto con el foco de la unidad.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-29/reading-could.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Childhood and requests"></audio>

> When I was a child, I could swim very well. I could ride a bike at the age of five. I couldn't speak English when I was young, but now I can. Could you pass me the salt, please? Could you help me with this bag? Could you open the window? It is hot in here.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. As a child, what could they do well? → a) Swim · b) Cook · c) Speak English
2. At what age ride a bike? → a) Five · b) Seven · c) Never
3. English when young? → a) Couldn't, but now can · b) Always could · c) Not mentioned
4. What polite requests? → a) Salt, help, open window · b) Pay bills · c) Study exam
5. Why open the window? → a) Hot in here · b) Cold · c) Exam tomorrow

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Swim** · 2. **a) Five** · 3. **a) Couldn't, but now can** · 4. **a) Salt, help, open window** · 5. **a) Hot in here**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The text mixes ____. → a) past could + Could you…? · b) only have to · c) only Zero
7. *When I was a child* introduces ____. → a) past ability · b) school rules · c) scientific facts
8. *Could you pass me the salt* is ____. → a) a request · b) past ability · c) obligation
9. *Now I can* shows ____. → a) present ability · b) only past · c) don't have to
10. Matches unit 29: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Module 6

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) past could + Could you…?** · 7. **a) past ability** · 8. **a) a request** · 9. **a) present ability** · 10. **a) yes**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Past ability: *could* + ____. → a) infinitive · b) to + inf · c) *-ing*
12. *Could you help me?* is ____. → a) polite · b) rude · c) Past Perfect
13. *Couldn't* = ____. → a) could not · b) can not now · c) will not
14. Same word *could*, different ____. → a) context (past vs request) · b) spelling · c) tense always
15. Reading helps you ____. → a) separate past and requests · b) skip form · c) avoid English

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) infinitive** · 12. **a) polite** · 13. **a) could not** · 14. **a) context (past vs request)** · 15. **a) separate past and requests**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Young and please

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. When young, could run ____. → a) Very fast · b) Very slowly · c) Not at all
2. Piano at age ____. → a) Seven · b) Five · c) Ten
3. Cooking with parents? → a) Couldn't · b) Could very well · c) Not mentioned
4. Requests include ____. → a) Volume, repeat, time · b) Salt only · c) Uniform
5. Why ask the time? → a) Forgot watch at home · b) Hot in here · c) Exam tomorrow

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. Listening separates ____. → a) past could vs Could you…? · b) only Zero · c) only have to
7. *Turn down the volume* is ____. → a) a request · b) past ability · c) obligation
8. *At the age of seven* refers to ____. → a) piano · b) swimming at five · c) cooking
9. *Please* appears with requests: ____. → a) yes · b) never · c) only past
10. Unit 29 practice: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Unit 60

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *When I was young, I could…* = ____. → a) past ability · b) request · c) Zero
12. *Could you tell me the time?* = ____. → a) request · b) past ability · c) have to
13. Shadow and ____. → a) label past vs request · b) ignore verbs · c) translate titles only
14. Write 2 past + 2 requests: ____. → a) good practice · b) useless · c) teachers only
15. U29 is mainly about ____. → a) could · b) only First · c) only don't have to

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

## Lección 5 — Writing

**Objetivo:** producir frases con el foco de la unidad.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. When I was a child, I ____ swim well. → *could / can / will*
2. Now I ____ speak English. → *can / could / will*
3. I ____ cook when I lived with my parents. → *couldn't / can't / won't*
4. ____ you help me with this bag, please? → *Could / Do / Are*
5. Could you ____ the window? → *open / opening / opened*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **could** · 2. **can** · 3. **couldn't** · 4. **Could** · 5. **open**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. When / I / was / young, / I / could / run / very / fast.
7. Could / you / pass / me / the / salt, / please?
8. I / couldn't / speak / English / when / I / was / young.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. When I was young, I could run very fast.
7. Could you pass me the salt, please?
8. I couldn't speak English when I was young.

</details>


### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Dos habilidades pasadas (*When I was…, I could…*).
10. Una negativa pasada (*I couldn't…*).
11. Contraste *Then I could… / Now I can…*.
12. Tres peticiones con *Could you…, please?*
13. Una petición sobre volumen o repetir.
14. Una petición educada en un restaurante.
15. Párrafo (6–8 frases) mezclando pasado y peticiones.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

9. When I was a child, I could swim very well. I could ride a bike at five.
10. I couldn't cook when I lived with my parents.
11. I couldn't speak English when I was young, but now I can.
12. Could you pass the salt? Could you help me? Could you open the window?
13. Could you turn down the volume, please?
14. Could you bring me the menu, please?
15. When I was young, I could run very fast. I could play the piano at seven. I couldn't cook then, but now I can. Could you repeat that, please? Could you tell me the time?

</details>
