## Lección 1 — Gramática

**Objetivo:** for (duration) / since (starting point) + Present Perfect.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I have lived here ____ five years. → *for / since / ago*
2. I have worked here ____ 2020. → *since / for / ago*
3. I have known her ____ ten years. → *for / since / at*
4. I have had my dog ____ last Christmas. → *since / for / on*
5. I have been learning Spanish ____ six months. → *for / since / in*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **for** · 2. **since** · 3. **for** · 4. **since** · 5. **for**

</details>


### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. *for* + ____. → a) a period of time · b) only a starting date · c) only *yesterday*
7. *since* + ____. → a) a starting point · b) only a duration like *five years* · c) *ago*
8. Usually with ____. → a) Present Perfect · b) only Future · c) only imperative
9. *since 2020* is ____. → a) correct · b) needs *for* · c) Past Simple only
10. *for five years* is ____. → a) correct · b) needs *since* · c) wrong

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) a period of time** · 7. **a) a starting point** · 8. **a) Present Perfect** · 9. **a) correct** · 10. **a) correct**

</details>


### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *I have lived here since five years.*
12. *I have worked here for 2020.*
13. *I live here for five years.* (duración hasta ahora)
14. *I have known her since ten years.*
15. *I have had my car for last January.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. I have lived here **for** five years.  
12. I have worked here **since** 2020.  
13. I **have lived** here for five years.  
14. I have known her **for** ten years.  
15. I have had my car **since** last January.  

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-18/reading-for.mp3" title="🔊 Reading Unidad 18"></audio>

> My name is Emma. I have lived in London for five years. I have worked at the same company since 2020. I have known my best friend Sara for ten years. We met when we were at university. I have had my dog Max since last Christmas. He is very friendly. I have been learning Spanish for six months. I have loved travelling since I was a child. I feel good because I have a stable life with good friends.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. Where has Emma lived? → a) London for five years · b) Madrid only · c) Nowhere
2. Since when has she worked there? → a) 2020 · b) Yesterday · c) Never
3. How long has she known Sara? → a) Ten years · b) Two days · c) Six months only
4. Since when has she had Max? → a) Last Christmas · b) 2020 · c) Childhood only
5. How long learning Spanish? → a) Six months · b) Ten years · c) Never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) London for five years** · 2. **a) 2020** · 3. **a) Ten years** · 4. **a) Last Christmas** · 5. **a) Six months**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The text matches unit 18: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only Unit 1
7. You can find the target forms: ____. → a) yes · b) never · c) only images
8. Sequence/time words appear as needed: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only future
9. Main idea is clear from the first lines: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) impossible
10. Re-read after checking answers: ____. → a) good idea · b) useless · c) forbidden

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) yes** · 7. **a) yes** · 8. **a) yes** · 9. **a) yes** · 10. **a) good idea**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. Mark 3 target forms in the text: ____. → a) do it · b) skip · c) delete the text
12. Change one sentence to negative/question: ____. → a) useful · b) wrong always · c) only B2
13. Note any Past Simple / Perfect / Continuous mix: ____. → a) notice it · b) ignore · c) ban English
14. Copy one model sentence aloud: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) never
15. Write a parallel sentence about you: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only tomorrow forever

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11–15. **a)** (práctica abierta; lo importante es usar el foco de la unidad)

</details>

4. Car since? → a) Last January · b) Christmas only · c) 2020
5. Learning guitar for? → a) Six months · b) Ten years · c) Never

<summary>Ver solución</summary>

</details>

9. Then check with the script: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) delete audio
10. Repeat difficult sentences: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) only teachers

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

12. Underline the target grammar: ____. → a) yes · b) no · c) never

## Lección 5 — Writing

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa (según el foco)

1. Write one affirmative sentence with the unit focus.
2. Write one negative sentence.
3. Write one question.
4. Add a time marker that fits.
5. Add one vocabulary word from the reading.

<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>
