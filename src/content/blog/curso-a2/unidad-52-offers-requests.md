> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 52 — Making Offers & Requests](/curso-a2/unit-52)  
> **Antes:** [U51 — Making Suggestions](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-51-making-suggestions)

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 52 (A2)

- **Offers:** *Can I…?*, *Would you like me to…?*, *Shall I…?*
- **Requests:** *Can you…?*, *Could you…?*, *Could you please…?*
- Respuestas educadas (aceptar / rechazar).
- Vocabulario en **casa, trabajo y tienda**.
- Producir diálogos cortos de cortesía.

![Offers & Requests A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-52/a2-unit52-offers-requests.png)

## 1. Offers: ofrecer ayuda

| Forma | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Can I…?** | ofrecer hacer algo | **Can I** help you? |
| **Shall I…?** | ofrecer (británico, frecuente) | **Shall I** open the window? |
| **Would you like me to…?** | ofrecer con más cortesía | **Would you like me to** carry that? |
| **Let me…** | ofrecer de forma directa | **Let me** help you. |

> **Can I** get you a drink?  
> **Would you like me to** call a taxi?

## 2. Requests: pedir algo

| Forma | Tono | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Can you…?** | neutro / informal | **Can you** pass the salt? |
| **Could you…?** | más educado | **Could you** open the door? |
| **Could you please…?** | más cortés | **Could you please** wait a minute? |
| **Would you mind + -ing?** | muy educado (A2+) | **Would you mind** helping me?

> **Could you** send me the email?  
> **Can you** help me with this bag?

## 3. Respuestas

### A una oferta

| Aceptar | Rechazar |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Yes, please.** | **No, thanks.** |
| **That would be great.** | **It's OK, thanks.** |
| **Thank you!** | **I can manage.** |

### A una petición

| Aceptar | Rechazar (educado) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Of course.** | **Sorry, I can't right now.** |
| **Sure.** | **I'm afraid I'm busy.** |
| **No problem.** | **Maybe later.** |

> A: Could you help me?  
> B: **Of course.** / **Sorry, I'm busy at the moment.**

## 6. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura — At the office

Good morning. **Can I** help you with those boxes? If you want, **Would you like me to** call reception? Later, **Could you please** send me the report by email? And **Shall I** book a meeting room for 3? Thanks — that would be great.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-52/reading-office.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: At the office"></audio>

### Diálogo — Helping a neighbour

**Alex:** Hi! Those bags look heavy. **Can I** help you?  
**Sam:** **Yes, please.** Thanks!  
**Alex:** **Would you like me to** take them upstairs?  
**Sam:** That would be great.  
**Alex:** **Could you** open the door?  
**Sam:** **Of course.** Here you are.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-52/dialogue-help.mp3" title="🔊 Dialogue: Helping a neighbour"></audio>

### Mini práctica

1. ___ I help you?  
2. Would you like me ___ carry that?  
3. ___ you open the window?  
4. Yes, ___. *(aceptar oferta)*

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-52/practice-four.mp3" title="🔊 Practice four sentences"></audio>

## 7. Errores frecuentes

1. ~~Can I to help you?~~ → **Can I help you?**  
2. ~~Could you to open…?~~ → **Could you open…?**  
3. ~~Would you like me help?~~ → **Would you like me to help?**  
4. ~~Please you help me.~~ → **Could you please help me?**  
5. ~~Yes.~~ *(muy seco a oferta)* → **Yes, please.** / **That would be great.**  
6. ~~No.~~ → **No, thanks.** / **It's OK, thanks.**

## 8. Ejercicios (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — ¿Offer o request?

1. Can I help you?  
2. Could you wait here?  
3. Would you like me to call a taxi?  
4. Can you pass the salt?  
5. Shall I open the window?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. offer  
2. request  
3. offer  
4. request  
5. offer  

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa

1. ___ I carry your bag?  
2. Could you ___ close the door? *(please)*  
3. Would you like me ___ help?  
4. ___ I make some tea?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Can / Shall  
2. please  
3. to  
4. Shall / Can  

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Elige la mejor opción

1. Ofrecer ayuda educada:  
   a) Help me!  b) Would you like me to help?  c) You help.  
2. Pedir con cortesía:  
   a) Open the window.  b) Could you open the window?  c) You must open.  
3. Aceptar una oferta:  
   a) No.  b) Yes, please.  c) Of course not.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. b  
2. b  
3. b  

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrige el error

1. Can I to help you?  
2. Could you to wait?  
3. Would you like me help with this?  
4. Please you send the email.  
5. No. *(rechazo de oferta — hazlo educado)*

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Can I help you?  
2. Could you wait?  
3. Would you like me to help with this?  
4. Could you please send the email?  
5. No, thanks. / It's OK, thanks.

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Traduce al inglés

1. ¿Puedo ayudarte?  
2. ¿Te gustaría que te lleve eso?  
3. ¿Podrías abrir la ventana?  
4. Sí, por favor.  
5. Lo siento, ahora no puedo.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. Can I help you?  
2. Would you like me to carry that?  
3. Could you open the window?  
4. Yes, please.  
5. Sorry, I can't right now.

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa el diálogo

**A:** Those bags look heavy. ___ I help you?  
**B:** Yes, ___.  
**A:** Would you like me ___ take them upstairs?  
**B:** That would be ___.  
**A:** ___ you open the door?  
**B:** Of ___.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

Can  
please  
to  
great  
Could  
course  

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Empareja respuesta

1. Can I help you?  
2. Could you send the file?  
3. Would you like me to call a taxi?  

a) Of course.  
b) Yes, please.  
c) No, thanks — I'll walk.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1 → b (también OK otras)  
2 → a  
3 → c  

</details>

1. help / I / Can / you / ?  
2. open / Could / you / the / window / ?  
3. me / like / Would / you / to / help / ?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

2. Could you open the window?  
3. Would you like me to help?

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu diálogo (8–12 líneas)

Escribe un diálogo con:

- 2 **offers** (*Can I…?* / *Would you like me to…?* / *Shall I…?*)  
- 1 **request** (*Could you…?* / *Can you…?*)  
- 1 aceptación y 1 rechazo educado

**Modelo:** el diálogo de la sección 6.

## 9. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Offer vs request + respuestas  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–6.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha lectura y diálogo.  
4. **5–8 min** — Ejercicio 10.

### Checklist

- [ ] Ofrezco con *Can I…?* / *Would you like me to…?*  
- [ ] Pido con *Could you…?* / *Can you…?*  
- [ ] Respondo *Yes, please* / *No, thanks* / *Of course*  
- [ ] Distingo offer y request  
- [ ] Evito *Can I to* y *Would you like me help*

---

## 10. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Practica en voz alta: 3 ofertas + 3 peticiones al día.  
2. Graba un mini diálogo y comprueba la cortesía.  
3. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 52](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-52-offers-requests-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 52 del curso A2](/curso-a2/unit-52).
