  - question: ¿Se dice by bus o by the bus?
    answer: >-
      Se dice by bus / by train / by plane (sin the). Con take usamos the: take

  - question: ¿Cuál es la diferencia entre get to y arrive at?
    answer: >-
      Get to = llegar a un destino (How do I get to the airport?). Arrive at =
      llegar a un sitio concreto (We arrived at the station at 9). Arrive in se
      usa con ciudades/países (arrive in Madrid).
  - question: ¿Journey, trip o travel?
    answer: >-
      Travel es el verbo/general. Trip es un viaje concreto (a weekend trip).

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 41 (A2)

- Decir el medio: **by bus / by train / by plane / by car**.
- Usar **take the** + transporte.
- Usar **get to** y **arrive at / arrive in**.
- Vocabulario: *airport, station, platform, ticket, fare, journey*.
- Preguntar y explicar rutas sencillas.

## 1. Idea clave: *by* vs *take the*

| Forma | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **by** + transporte | medio en general (sin *the*) | I go **by bus**. |
| **take the** + transporte | coger un medio concreto | I **take the bus** to work. |
| **on foot** | a pie (no *by foot*) | We go **on foot**. |

> She travels **by train**.  
> We **take the underground** to the centre.

~~by the bus~~ → **by bus**  
~~take bus~~ → **take the bus**

## 2. *Get to* y *arrive at / in*

| Verbo | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **get to** + lugar | llegar / cómo llegar | How do I **get to** the station? |
| **arrive at** + sitio | estación, aeropuerto, hotel… | We **arrived at** the airport at 6. |
| **arrive in** + ciudad/país | Madrid, Spain… | They **arrived in** London yesterday. |

> How do I **get to** the museum?  
> Take the bus and **get off** at the square.  
> We **arrived at** the hotel late.

## 3. Vocabulario de viaje

| Palabra | Idea | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **airport** | aeropuerto | Meet me at the **airport**. |
| **station** | estación | The train leaves from the **station**. |
| **platform** | andén | The train is on **platform** 3. |
| **ticket** | billete | I need a return **ticket**. |
| **fare** | tarifa / precio del trayecto | What's the **fare** to the centre? |
| **journey** | trayecto / viaje | It was a long **journey**. |
| **timetable** | horario | Check the **timetable**. |
| **delay** | retraso | There is a 20-minute **delay**. |

Otros útiles: *gate, departure, arrival, luggage, passenger, stop, get on / get off*.

## 4. Preguntas útiles

| Pregunta | Respuesta modelo |
| :--- | :--- |
| How do I **get to** the airport? | Take the train and get off at Airport Central. |
| How long does the **journey** take? | About 40 minutes. |
| Where do I buy a **ticket**? | At the machine / at the ticket office. |
| Which **platform**? | Platform 2. |
| Is there a **delay**? | Yes — 15 minutes. |

> Excuse me, how do I **get to** the city centre?  
> **Take the bus** number 10.

## 5. Mapa rápido

| Quieres decir… | Usa… | Ejemplo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Medio (general) | by + transport | by bus / by plane |
| Coger un medio | take the + transport | take the metro |
| Cómo llegar | get to | get to the hotel |
| Llegar a un sitio | arrive at | arrive at the station |
| Llegar a una ciudad | arrive in | arrive in Paris |
| A pie | on foot | go on foot |

## 6. Lectura y diálogo

### Lectura — To the airport

Leo needs to **get to** the **airport**. He **takes the train** from the city **station**. The **journey** takes 35 minutes. He buys a **ticket** at the machine. The train leaves from **platform** 4. There is no **delay** today. He **arrives at** the airport at 8 and looks for the gate. He prefers travelling **by train** — it's faster than going **by bus**.

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-41/reading-airport.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: To the airport"></audio>

### Diálogo — At the station

**Tourist:** Excuse me, how do I **get to** the museum?  
**Clerk:** **Take the bus** number 7 and **get off** at Green Park.  
**Tourist:** How long is the **journey**?  
**Clerk:** About 20 minutes. A single **ticket** is £2.50.  
**Tourist:** Which stop?  
**Clerk:** Ask the driver — or check the **timetable** on the app.

## 7. Errores frecuentes

1. ~~by the bus~~ → **by bus**  
2. ~~take bus~~ → **take the bus**  
3. ~~by foot~~ → **on foot**  
4. ~~arrive to the station~~ → **arrive at the station** / **get to the station**  
5. ~~arrive at London~~ → **arrive in London**  
6. ~~How I get to…?~~ → **How do I get to…?**

## 8. Ejercicios (con soluciones)

### Ejercicio 1 — *by* o *take the*

1. I usually go ___ metro.  
2. ___ train to Terminal 2.  
3. She hates travelling ___ plane.  
4. We ___ taxi when it's late. *(take)*

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. by  
2. Take the  
3. by  
4. take a / take the  

</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — *get to* / *arrive at* / *arrive in*

1. How do I ___ the stadium?  
2. We ___ Madrid at noon.  
3. The flight ___ the airport late.  
4. When did you ___ Spain?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. get to  
2. arrived in  
3. arrived at  
4. arrive in / arrive in  

</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Vocabulario

Empareja:

1. platform  
2. fare  
3. timetable  
4. journey  
5. delay  

a) horario  
b) andén  
c) retraso  
d) trayecto  
e) precio del billete/trayecto

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1 → b  
2 → e  
3 → a  
4 → d  
5 → c  

</details>

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrige el error

1. I go by the train.  
2. Take bus 12.  
3. We arrived to the station.  
4. They arrived at France.  
5. How I get to the hotel?

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. I go by train.  
2. Take the bus 12. / Take bus number 12.  
3. We arrived at the station.  
4. They arrived in France.  
5. How do I get to the hotel?  

</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Traduce al inglés

1. Voy en autobús.  
2. Coge el tren al aeropuerto.  
3. ¿Cómo llego a la estación?  
4. Llegamos al hotel a las nueve.  
5. El trayecto dura 40 minutos.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. I go by bus.  
2. Take the train to the airport.  
3. How do I get to the station?  
4. We arrived at the hotel at nine.  
5. The journey takes 40 minutes.  

</details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Completa el diálogo

**A:** Which ___ ?  
**B:** Platform 5. The ___ takes about half an hour.  
**A:** Is there a ___ today?  
**B:** No — it's on time.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

platform  
journey  
delay  

</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Elige la mejor opción

1. Meet me ___ the airport.  
   a) in  b) at  c) by  
2. We travelled ___ car.  
   a) by  b) in the  c) on  
3. Please ___ at the next stop.  
   a) get off  b) get to  c) arrive in

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. b  
2. a  
3. a  

</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Ordena

1. to / get / How / I / do / the / station / ?  
2. the / Take / bus / and / at / get off / the park  
3. at / arrived / We / airport / the / 7

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. How do I get to the station?  
2. Take the bus and get off at the park.  
3. We arrived at the airport at 7.  

</details>

### Ejercicio 9 — *journey* / *trip* / *travel*

1. I love to ___ . *(verbo)*  
2. We had a great weekend ___ to Lisbon.  
3. The train ___ was uncomfortable.

<details>
<summary>Soluciones</summary>

1. travel  
2. trip  
3. journey  

</details>

### Ejercicio 10 — Tu viaje (8–12 líneas)

Describe cómo vas del centro al aeropuerto (o a otro sitio real). Incluye:

- *by* o *take the*  
- *get to* o *arrive at*  
- al menos 3 palabras: *ticket / platform / journey / delay / station / airport*

**Modelo:** la lectura de la sección 6.

---

## 9. Plan de estudio (25–30 minutos)

1. **5 min** — Tablas *by* / *take the* / *get to* / *arrive*.  
2. **8 min** — Ejercicios 1–6.  
3. **7 min** — Escucha lectura y diálogo; anota vocabulario.  
4. **5–8 min** — Ejercicio 10.

### Checklist

- [ ] Uso *by bus* (sin *the*)  
- [ ] Uso *take the train*  
- [ ] Pregunto *How do I get to…?*  
- [ ] Distingo *arrive at* / *arrive in*  
- [ ] Conozco *platform, ticket, journey, delay*

---

## 10. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Aprende bloques: *get to the airport*, *take the train*, *arrive at the station*.  
2. Practica preguntar direcciones en voz alta.  
3. Practica en el [cuaderno de ejercicios de la Unidad 41](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-41-travel-transport-ejercicios-soluciones) y en la [Unidad 41 del curso A2](/curso-a2/unit-41).
