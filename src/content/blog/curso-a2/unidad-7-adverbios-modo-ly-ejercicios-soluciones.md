## Lección 1 — Gramática: -ly / well / badly

**Objetivo:** formar y colocar adverbios de modo.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. She runs ____. → *quickly / quick / quicker*
2. He speaks ____ because he is tired. → *slowly / slow / slowest*
3. They work ____. → *carefully / careful / care*
4. She sings ____. → *well / good / goods*
5. He played ____ in the match. → *badly / bad / worse*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **quickly** · 2. **slowly** · 3. **carefully** · 4. **well** · 5. **badly**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Elige

6. Most manner adverbs = ____. → a) adj + *-ly* · b) *-est* · c) *the most*
7. *good* → adverb ____. → a) well · b) goodly · c) goods
8. *bad* → adverb ____. → a) badly · b) bad · c) worse as only form
9. *He runs fast* is ____. → a) correct · b) needs *fastly* · c) wrong
10. *He works hard* is ____. → a) correct · b) needs *hardly* (same meaning) · c) wrong

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) adj + *-ly*** · 7. **a) well** · 8. **a) badly** · 9. **a) correct** · 10. **a) correct**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Corrige

11. *She sings good.*
12. *He runs fastly.*
13. *Please speak quiet in the library.*
14. *She learned quick.*
15. *He hardly works every day.* (querías *con esfuerzo*)

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. She sings **well**.
12. He runs **fast**.
13. Please speak **quietly** in the library.
14. She learned **quickly**.
15. He works **hard** every day. (*hardly* = casi no)

</details>

## Lección 2 — Vocabulario: cómo haces las cosas

**Objetivo:** pares adjetivo → adverbio.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Empareja

1. quick →
2. careful →
3. beautiful →
4. fluent →
5. loud →

a) carefully · b) beautifully · c) quickly · d) loudly · e) fluently

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **c** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **b** · 4 → **e** · 5 → **d**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Completa

6. slow → ____
7. patient → ____
8. good → ____
9. bad → ____
10. quiet → ____

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **slowly** · 7. **patiently** · 8. **well** · 9. **badly** · 10. **quietly**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Elige

11. Adverbs usually go ____. → a) after the verb / object · b) always before *the* · c) only at the start
12. *Fluently* describes ____. → a) speaking a language smoothly · b) height · c) price
13. *Clapped loudly* means ____. → a) clapped with a lot of noise · b) quietly · c) slowly only
14. *Patiently* means ____. → a) without getting angry / waiting calmly · b) fast · c) badly
15. *Practises every day* helps you learn ____. → a) quickly · b) never · c) worst

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) after the verb / object** · 12. **a) speaking a language smoothly** · 13. **a) clapped with a lot of noise** · 14. **a) without getting angry / waiting calmly** · 15. **a) quickly**

</details>

## Lección 3 — Reading: Maria

**Objetivo:** leer un texto rico en adverbios de modo.

### Texto y audio

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-7/reading-adverbs.mp3" title="🔊 Reading: Maria"></audio>

> My friend Maria speaks English very fluently. She learned quickly because she practises every day. She reads books slowly to understand everything well. Last week she sang beautifully at the concert. The audience clapped loudly. Maria also writes carefully and she never makes mistakes. She drives very carefully too. I think she does everything well!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. How does Maria speak English? → a) Fluently · b) Badly · c) Never
2. Why did she learn quickly? → a) She practises every day · b) She never studies · c) She is lazy
3. How does she read? → a) Slowly · b) Loudly · c) Badly
4. How did she sing? → a) Beautifully · b) Badly · c) Quietly only
5. How does she drive? → a) Carefully · b) Fast only · c) Badly

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Fluently** · 2. **a) She practises every day** · 3. **a) Slowly** · 4. **a) Beautifully** · 5. **a) Carefully**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. The audience ____. → a) clapped loudly · b) left early · c) sang badly
7. She understands everything ____. → a) well · b) bad · c) fastly
8. Adverbs in the text include ____. → a) fluently, quickly, slowly, well, beautifully, loudly, carefully · b) only *the best* · c) only *did*
9. *Writes carefully* means ____. → a) with attention · b) without thinking · c) never
10. The main topic is ____. → a) how Maria does things · b) superlatives of places · c) *at/on/in*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) clapped loudly** · 7. **a) well** · 8. **a) fluently, quickly, slowly, well, beautifully, loudly, carefully** · 9. **a) with attention** · 10. **a) how Maria does things**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Fluently* = ____ + *-ly*. → a) fluent · b) fluency · c) fluencer
12. *Well* is the adverb of ____. → a) good · b) bad · c) fast
13. *Beautifully* comes from ____. → a) beautiful · b) beauty only · c) best
14. *Very carefully* intensifies ____. → a) the adverb · b) a noun only · c) *the*
15. Position: *speaks English fluently* puts the adverb ____. → a) after the object · b) before *speaks* only · c) never

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) fluent** · 12. **a) good** · 13. **a) beautiful** · 14. **a) the adverb** · 15. **a) after the object**

</details>

## Lección 4 — Listening: Dance class

**Objetivo:** escuchar un relato con adverbios de modo.

### Audio y guion

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-7/listening-adverbs.mp3" title="🔊 Listening: Dance class"></audio>

> Hi! Yesterday I went to a dance class. The teacher moved very quickly and we had to follow him. At first I danced badly because I couldn't remember the steps. But the teacher explained everything slowly and patiently. Then I practised carefully and by the end I danced well! My friend sang beautifully at the karaoke after. We had a great time!

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Comprensión

1. How did the teacher move? → a) Very quickly · b) Slowly only · c) Badly
2. How did the speaker dance at first? → a) Badly · b) Well · c) Fluently
3. How did the teacher explain? → a) Slowly and patiently · b) Loudly only · c) Never
4. How did they dance by the end? → a) Well · b) Badly · c) Quietly only
5. How did the friend sing? → a) Beautifully · b) Badly · c) Fast

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a) Very quickly** · 2. **a) Badly** · 3. **a) Slowly and patiently** · 4. **a) Well** · 5. **a) Beautifully**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–10 — Detalles

6. They practised ____. → a) carefully · b) never · c) worst
7. After class they went to ____. → a) karaoke · b) the office · c) the beach only
8. Change: badly → ____ by the end. → a) well · b) worse · c) good (adj as adverb)
9. *Couldn't remember* explains dancing ____. → a) badly · b) fluently · c) carefully first
10. The listening practices ____. → a) adverbs of manner · b) only superlatives · c) *on Monday*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. **a) carefully** · 7. **a) karaoke** · 8. **a) well** · 9. **a) badly** · 10. **a) adverbs of manner**

</details>

### Ejercicios 11–15 — Forma

11. *Badly / well* are ____. → a) irregular-style manner adverbs · b) *-est* · c) *the most*
12. *Patiently* = ____ + *-ly*. → a) patient · b) patience · c) path
13. *Quickly* describes ____. → a) speed of moving · b) height · c) age
14. *Danced well* uses ____ after the verb. → a) well · b) good · c) goods
15. Story arc: badly → carefully → ____. → a) well · b) worst · c) the tallest

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

11. **a) irregular-style manner adverbs** · 12. **a) patient** · 13. **a) speed of moving** · 14. **a) well** · 15. **a) well**

</details>

## Lección 5 — Writing: How I do things

**Objetivo:** escribir con adverbios de modo.

### Ejercicios 1–5 — Completa

1. I speak English ____. → *slowly / slow / slower*
2. I work ____ every day. → *hard / hardly / hardiest*
3. Please drive ____. → *carefully / careful / care*
4. She sings ____. → *well / good / goods*
5. He danced ____ at first. → *badly / bad / worse*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **slowly** · 2. **hard** · 3. **carefully** · 4. **well** · 5. **badly**

</details>

### Ejercicios 6–8 — Ordena

6. She / runs / quickly.
7. He / works / hard / every day.
8. They / speak / English / fluently.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

6. She runs quickly.
7. He works hard every day.
8. They speak English fluently.

</details>

### Ejercicios 9–15 — Escribe (modelos)

9. Corre rápido (*fast* / *quickly*).
10. Habla inglés con fluidez.
11. Canta bien / mal.
12. Explica despacio y con paciencia.
13. Conduce con cuidado.
14. Trabaja duro (no *hardly*).
15. Párrafo (6–8 frases) sobre cómo estudias o practicas algo.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución (ejemplos)</summary>

9. She runs fast. / She runs quickly.
10. She speaks English fluently.
11. She sings well. / He played badly.
12. The teacher explained slowly and patiently.
13. She drives carefully.
14. He works hard every day.
15. I study carefully every day. I read slowly. I practise quickly. I want to speak fluently. I work hard. I do everything well!

</details>
