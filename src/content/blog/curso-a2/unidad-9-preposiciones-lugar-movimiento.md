En la [Unidad 8](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-8-preposiciones-tiempo-at-on-in) dijiste **cuándo**. En la **Unidad 9 del curso A2** (*Prepositions of Place & Movement*) describes **dónde te mueves**: ***into***, ***out of***, ***through***, ***across***.

> **Practica en el curso:** [Unidad 9 — Preposiciones de lugar y movimiento](/curso-a2/unit-9)  
> **Antes:** [U8 — Preposiciones de tiempo](/blog/curso-a2/unidad-8-preposiciones-tiempo-at-on-in)

---

## Qué aprenderás en la Unidad 9 (A2)

- Usar **into** para entrar / meter algo dentro.
- Usar **out of** para salir / sacar algo fuera.
- Usar **through** para atravesar el *interior* de un espacio.
- Usar **across** para cruzar una *superficie* (calle, puente, río).
- Distinguir *through* vs *across* y evitar *She went in the room* cuando quieres marcar movimiento claro.

![Preposiciones de movimiento into out of through across](/blog/curso-a2/unit-9/a2-unit9-prepositions-movement.png)

---

## 1. Lugar vs movimiento (repaso rápido)

Antes de las cuatro estrellas de la unidad, recuerda:

| Tipo | Idea | Ejemplos A2 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lugar** (dónde estás) | posición | *in* the room · *on* the table · *at* the door · *under* the bed · *behind* the sofa |
| **Movimiento** (hacia dónde vas) | dirección / trayectoria | *into* · *out of* · *through* · *across* · *to* · *from* |

Esta unidad se centra en el **movimiento**: no solo “estoy en…”, sino “entro / salgo / atravieso / cruzo…”.

---

## 2. La regla en una frase

| Preposición | Movimiento | Imagen mental |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **into** | fuera → **dentro** | entrar en una habitación / meter en una bolsa |
| **out of** | dentro → **fuera** | salir de un edificio / bajarse del coche |
| **through** | de un lado al otro **por dentro** | parque, bosque, túnel, salas |
| **across** | de un lado al otro **por encima / sobre superficie** | calle, puente, río, patio |

Truco: **into / out of** = entrar o salir. **through / across** = cruzar (interior vs superficie).

---

## 3. *Into*: entrar o meter dentro

Usa **into** con movimiento hacia el interior:

> She went **into** the room and closed the door.  
> I put the books **into** my bag.  
> She went **into** the shop to buy bread.  
> I went **into** a cafe.

| Put it **into** the bag. | *Put it in the bag* (posible, pero *into* marca movimiento) |

## 4. *Out of*: salir o sacar fuera

Usa **out of** con movimiento desde el interior hacia fuera:

> He ran **out of** the building when he heard the fire alarm.  
> He got **out of** the car and walked away.  
> When I came **out of** the park…  
> When I came **out of** the museum…

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-9/ran-out-of.mp3" title="🔊 He ran out of the building…"></audio>

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-9/got-out-of-car.mp3" title="🔊 He got out of the car and walked away"></audio>

Parejas útiles:

| Entrar | Salir |
| :--- | :--- |
| go **into** the room | come / go / run **out of** the room |
| get **into** the car | get **out of** the car |
| put **into** the bag | take **out of** the bag |

---

## 5. *Through*: a través del interior

Usa **through** cuando pasas **por dentro** de un espacio (hay “volumen” o un interior):

> We walked **through** the park to get to the other side.  
> They drove **through** the tunnel to reach the city.  
> We walked **through** the forest to reach the lake.  
> I walked **through** several rooms.

Piensa: *park · forest · tunnel · rooms · city centre* → a menudo **through**.

---

## 6. *Across*: de un lado al otro (superficie)

Usa **across** cuando cruzas una **superficie** o vas al otro lado de algo plano/línea:

> The cat ran **across** the street to the other side.  
> We swam **across** the river.  
> People were running **across** the bridge.  
> I walked **across** the courtyard.

## 7. *Through* vs *across*

![Through vs across A2](/blog/curso-a2/unit-9/a2-unit9-through-vs-across.png)

| | **through** | **across** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Idea | por el **interior** | por encima / al **otro lado** de una superficie |
| Ejemplos | through the park / forest / tunnel / rooms | across the street / bridge / river / road |
| Pregunta | ¿paso *dentro* de algo? | ¿cruzo una línea o superficie? |

> We walked **through** the park.  
> The cat ran **across** the street.

No digas *walked across the tunnel* si quieres “por dentro del túnel”: usa **through**.

## 8. Texto modelo: aventura en la ciudad

> Yesterday I had an adventure in the city. First I went into an old bookshop to buy a book. Then I walked through the park to get to the other side. I saw many people running across the bridge. When I came out of the park I crossed the street and went into a cafe. I had coffee and read my new book. It was a great day!

## 9. Listening: el museo

> Hi! Last weekend I went to the museum. I went into the main entrance and walked through several rooms. First I saw the paintings and then I walked across the courtyard to get to the sculpture section. When I came out of the museum I crossed the street and went into a cafe for coffee. I really enjoyed the day!

<audio controls preload="none" src="/audio/blog/curso-a2/unit-9/dialogue-museum.mp3" title="🔊 Hi! Last weekend I went to the museum…"></audio>

Preguntas rápidas:

1. ¿Cómo entra? → *went into the main entrance*  
2. ¿Cómo se mueve dentro? → *walked through several rooms* / *across the courtyard*  
3. ¿Cómo sale? → *came out of the museum*

---

## 11. Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto / confuso | Correcto (prioridad A2) | Por qué |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| She went in the room. *(si marcas movimiento)* | She went **into** the room. | *into* = entrar |
| He ran of the building. | He ran **out of** the building. | falta *out* |
| We walked across the tunnel. | We walked / drove **through** the tunnel. | túnel = interior |
| We walked through the street. *(cruzar al otro lado)* | We walked **across** the street. / We crossed the street. | calle = superficie |
| She went into the bridge. | She walked **across** the bridge. | puente se cruza *across* |
| Get out the car. | Get **out of** the car. | *out of* + objeto |

## 12. Ejercicios

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige *into / out of / through / across*

1. She went ____ the room and closed the door.  
2. He ran ____ the building when he heard the fire alarm.  
3. We walked ____ the park to get to the other side.  
4. The cat ran ____ the street to the other side.  
5. I put the books ____ my bag.  
6. They drove ____ the tunnel to reach the city.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **into** · 2. **out of** · 3. **through** · 4. **across** · 5. **into** · 6. **through**
</details>

### Ejercicio 2 — *through* o *across*

1. We walked ____ the forest.  
2. We swam ____ the river.  
3. I walked ____ several rooms.  
4. People were running ____ the bridge.  
5. They drove ____ the tunnel.  
6. The cat ran ____ the road.

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **through** · 2. **across** · 3. **through** · 4. **across** · 5. **through** · 6. **across**
</details>

### Ejercicio 3 — Empareja

1. into  
2. out of  
3. through  
4. across  

a) leave a place / vehicle · b) enter · c) over a surface (street, bridge) · d) inside something from one side to the other

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1 → **b** · 2 → **a** · 3 → **d** · 4 → **c**
</details>

### Ejercicio 5 — Completa el museo

I went ____ the main entrance and walked ____ several rooms. Then I walked ____ the courtyard. When I came ____ the museum I crossed the street and went ____ a cafe.

<details>

### Ejercicio 6 — Corrige

1. *She went in the room.* *(movimiento claro)*  
2. *He ran of the building.*  
3. *We walked across the tunnel.*  
4. *We walked through the street to the other side.* *(cruzar la calle)*  
5. *Get out the car.*  
6. *She went into the bridge.*

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. She went **into** the room.  
2. He ran **out of** the building.  
3. We walked / drove **through** the tunnel.  
4. We walked **across** the street. / We crossed the street.  
5. Get **out of** the car.  
6. She walked **across** the bridge.
</details>

### Ejercicio 7 — Elige la correcta

1. ____  
   a) She went into the room. · b) She went across the room. *(entrar)* · c) She went out of the room. *(entrar)*  
2. ____  
   a) through = over a surface · b) through = inside something; across = over a surface · c) through and across are the same  
3. ____  
   a) He got out of the car. · b) He got into of the car. · c) He got out the car.  
4. We use *across* for: ____  
   a) tunnels · b) roads / bridges / rivers · c) bags

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. **a** · 2. **b** · 3. **a** · 4. **b**
</details>

### Ejercicio 8 — Ordena

1. into / She / the / shop / went / to / buy / bread  
2. out of / He / the / building / ran / when / the / alarm / sounded  
3. through / We / the / forest / walked / to / reach / the / lake

<details>
<summary>Ver solución</summary>

1. She went into the shop to buy bread.  
2. He ran out of the building when the alarm sounded.  
3. We walked through the forest to reach the lake.
</details>

## 14. Consejos y siguiente paso

1. Pregunta: ¿entro, salgo o cruzo? ¿Cruzo por **dentro** o por una **superficie**?  
2. Lista: *room, bag, car, park, forest, tunnel, street, bridge, river, cafe* → di la preposición típica.  
3. Grábate: *I went into… I walked through… I ran across… I came out of…*

| :--- | :--- |

| Posición (repaso) | *in / on / at / under / behind* |
