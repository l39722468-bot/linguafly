Airbnb, hostales, B&Bs, glamping... El alojamiento alternativo ha revolucionado la forma de viajar. Pero a diferencia de un hotel con recepción 24 horas, en estos alojamientos la comunicación directa con el anfitrión es fundamental. Saber escribir mensajes claros en inglés, resolver problemas y entender las normas de la casa marca la diferencia entre una estancia perfecta y un desastre logístico.

---

## 1. Antes de Reservar: Preguntas al Anfitrión

Antes de confirmar, es normal (y recomendable) hacer preguntas.

- *"Hi, I'm interested in your listing for August 10-15. Is it available?"*
- *"Is the apartment suitable for two adults and a child?"*
- *"Is there parking available nearby?"*
- *"How far is it from the city centre / the beach / the train station?"*
- *"Is there air conditioning / heating?"*
- *"Are pets allowed?"*
- *"Is there a washing machine in the apartment?"*
- *"What's the Wi-Fi speed like? I need to work remotely."*

---

## 2. Confirmar y Coordinar la Llegada

Una vez reservado, necesitas coordinar el check-in.

### Mensajes típicos al anfitrión

- *"Hi [Name]! I've just booked your place. We're arriving on Friday at around 3 pm. Is that okay?"*
- *"Could you send me the check-in instructions and the address?"*
- *"Is there a lockbox or do we need to meet in person?"*
- *"Our flight lands at 10 pm. Is late check-in possible?"*
- *"Where exactly should we pick up the keys?"*

### Vocabulario de check-in

| Término | Significado |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lockbox / Key safe** | Caja de llaves con código |
| **Self check-in** | Check-in autónomo (sin anfitrión) |
| **Smart lock** | Cerradura inteligente (con código) |
| **House manual** | Manual de la casa (instrucciones) |
| **Check-in time** | Hora de entrada |
| **Check-out time** | Hora de salida |
| **Early check-in** | Entrada anticipada |
| **Late check-out** | Salida tardía |

---

## 3. Durante la Estancia: Normas de la Casa

Cada alojamiento tiene sus reglas. Entenderlas evita malentendidos y cargos extra.

### Normas habituales

| Regla en inglés | Significado |
| :--- | :--- |
| **No smoking** | Prohibido fumar |
| **No parties or events** | Prohibidas las fiestas |
| **Quiet hours after 10 pm** | Silencio después de las 22h |
| **Remove your shoes indoors** | Quitarse los zapatos dentro |
| **Sort the recycling** | Separar el reciclaje |
| **Strip the beds before check-out** | Quitar las sábanas antes de salir |
| **Take out the rubbish** | Sacar la basura |
| **Maximum occupancy: 4 guests** | Máximo 4 huéspedes |

---

## 4. Resolver Problemas

Las cosas no siempre salen bien. Saber comunicar un problema educadamente acelera la solución.

### Mensajes al anfitrión

- *"Hi, the hot water isn't working. Could you help?"*
- *"We can't figure out how to use the washing machine. Are there instructions?"*
- *"The Wi-Fi password doesn't seem to work."*
- *"There's a leak under the kitchen sink."*
- *"The heating isn't turning on. It's quite cold tonight."*
- *"We noticed the apartment doesn't look like the photos. The living room is much smaller."*
- *"The neighbours are being very noisy. Is there anything we can do?"*

### Si necesitas escalar el problema

- *"I've contacted the host but haven't received a response in 24 hours."*
- *"I'd like to request a partial refund due to the issues we experienced."*
- *"The listing was significantly different from the description."*

---

## 5. Hostales: Vocabulario Específico

Los hostales tienen una cultura propia, especialmente para mochileros y viajeros jóvenes.

| Término | Significado |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Dorm / Dormitory** | Habitación compartida |
| **Bunk bed** | Litera |
| **Private room** | Habitación privada |
| **Mixed dorm** | Dormitorio mixto |
| **Female-only dorm** | Dormitorio solo para mujeres |
| **Locker** | Taquilla |
| **Common area / Lounge** | Zona común / Sala de estar |
| **Shared kitchen** | Cocina compartida |
| **Laundry room** | Lavandería |

### Frases para hostales

- *"Do you have any beds available in a 4-bed dorm?"*
- *"Is breakfast included?"*
- *"Are the lockers free or do I need to bring a padlock?"*
- *"Is there a curfew?"* (¿Hay hora de cierre?)
- *"Can I store my luggage after check-out?"*
- *"Do you organise any social events or pub crawls?"*

---

## 6. Bed & Breakfast (B&B)

Los B&Bs ofrecen un toque casero y personal que muchos viajeros adoran.

- *"What time is breakfast served?"*
- *"Do you cater for dietary requirements?"* (¿Adaptan el desayuno a dietas especiales?)
- *"Is there a shared bathroom or an en-suite?"* (¿Baño compartido o privado?)
- *"Could you recommend any local restaurants?"*
- *"The room is lovely. Is it possible to extend my stay by one night?"*

---

## 7. El Check-out: Despedirse Bien

Dejar buena impresión es importante, especialmente si quieres una buena reseña recíproca.

### Mensajes de salida

- *"Hi [Name], we're checking out now. Thank you so much for everything!"*
- *"We've left the keys on the kitchen table as instructed."*
- *"The apartment was wonderful. We had a great stay."*
- *"We've taken out the rubbish and stripped the beds."*
- *"Thank you for being such a great host. We'll definitely leave a positive review."*

---

## 8. Escribir Reseñas en Inglés

Las reseñas son el alma de la economía colaborativa. Saber escribir una buena reseña es un acto de generosidad para futuros viajeros.

### Reseña positiva

> *"Fantastic stay! The apartment was exactly as described — clean, well-equipped and in a perfect location, just 5 minutes from the beach. [Host] was incredibly responsive and gave us great local tips. Would definitely stay again. Highly recommended!"*

### Reseña constructiva

> *"The location was great and the apartment was comfortable overall. However, the Wi-Fi was unreliable and the hot water took a long time to come through. Communication with the host was good — they responded quickly when we reported the issues. Would stay again if these points were addressed."*

Con estas frases y vocabulario, serás un huésped modelo en cualquier rincón del mundo.
